Krishna is the ultimate refuge

Bhagavad Geeta

ॐ

“Surrender completely”

This teaches:

  • Letting go of control
  • Trust
  • Inner security
Chapter 9 · Shloka 9.34 →

मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु ।
मामेवैष्यसि युक्त्वैवमात्मानं मत्परायणः ॥9.34॥

Simple meaning: Fix your mind on Me, become My devotee, worship Me and bow down to Me. Thus having united yourself with Me and holding Me as your supreme goal you will surely attain Me.

Chapter 12 · Shloka 12.8 →

मय्येव मन आधत्स्व मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय ।
निवसिष्यसि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संशयः ॥12.8॥

Simple meaning: Fix your mind upon Me alone, and establish your intellect in Me; thereafter you will dwell in Me alone. Of this there is no doubt.
