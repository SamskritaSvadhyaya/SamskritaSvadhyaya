ॐ

You have control only over action, not results

Bhagavad Geeta

ॐ

“Do your duty without attachment to outcome”

This teaches:

  • Freedom from anxiety
  • Focus on effort
  • Inner steadiness
Chapter 2 · Shloka 2.47 →

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥2.47॥

Simple meaning: Your right is to perform your duty alone, never to its fruits. Never let the fruits of action be your motive, nor let your inclination be to inaction.
