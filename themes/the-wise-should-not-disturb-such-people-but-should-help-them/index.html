ॐ

The wise should not disturb such people but should help them progress gradually

Bhagavad Geeta

ॐ
Chapter 3 · Shloka 3.26 →

न बुद्धिभेदं जनयेदज्ञानां कर्मसङ्गिनाम् ।
जोषयेत्सर्वकर्माणि विद्वान्युक्तः समाचरन् ॥3.26॥

Simple meaning: A disciplined, wise person should not unsettle the minds of the ignorant who are attached to fruitive action; rather, by performing actions properly oneself, the wise should encourage them to engage wholeheartedly in all their prescribed duties.

Chapter 3 · Shloka 3.29 →

प्रकृतेर्गुणसम्मूढाः सज्ज‍न्ते गुणकर्मसु ।
तानकृत्स्नविदो मन्दान्कृत्स्नविन्न विचालयेत् ॥3.29॥

Simple meaning: Those who are thoroughly deluded by the modes (gunas) of material nature (Prakriti) become attached to the actions produced by those modes; one who possesses complete knowledge should not unsettle those of incomplete knowledge who are slow to comprehend.
