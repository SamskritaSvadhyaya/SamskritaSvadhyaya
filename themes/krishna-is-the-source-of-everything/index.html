Krishna is the source of everything

Bhagavad Geeta

ॐ

“Everything comes from Him”

This teaches:

  • Nothing is random
  • Everything has a deeper origin
  • Life becomes connected, not fragmented
Chapter 9 · Shloka 9.4 →

मया ततमिदं सर्वं जगदव्यक्तमूर्तिना ।
मत्स्थानि सर्वभूतानि न चाहं तेष्ववस्थितः ॥9.4॥

Simple meaning: This entire universe is pervaded by Me, in My unmanifest form. All beings rest in Me, yet I do not abide in them.

Chapter 9 · Shloka 9.16 →

अहं क्रतुरहं यज्ञः स्वधाहमहमौषधम् ।
मन्त्रोऽहमहमेवाज्यमहमग्निरहं हुतम् ॥9.16॥

Simple meaning: I am the Vedic ritual sacrifice, I am the yajna, I am the ancestral oblation (svadha), I am the medicinal herb, I am the mantra, I indeed am the clarified butter, I am the fire, and I am the oblation.

Chapter 9 · Shloka 9.17 →

पिताहमस्य जगतो माता धाता पितामहः ।
वेद्यं पवित्रमोङ्कार ऋक्साम यजुरेव च ॥9.17॥

Simple meaning: I am the father of this universe, the mother, the sustainer, and the grandfather; the knowable, the purifier, the syllable Om, and also the Rig Veda, the Sama Veda, and the Yajur Veda.

Chapter 9 · Shloka 9.18 →

गतिर्भर्ता प्रभुः साक्षी निवासः शरणं सुहृत् ।
प्रभवः प्रलयः स्थानं निधानं बीजमव्ययम् ॥9.18॥

Simple meaning: I am the goal, the supporter, the lord, the witness, the abode, the refuge, the well wisher, the origin, the dissolution, the foundation, the treasure house, and the imperishable seed.

Chapter 9 · Shloka 9.19 →

तपाम्यहमहं वर्षं निगृह्णम्युत्सृजामि च ।
अमृतं चैव मृत्युश्च सदसच्चाहमर्जुन ॥9.19॥

Simple meaning: O Arjuna, I give heat, and I withhold and send forth the rain; I am immortality and also death, and I am both the manifest and the unmanifest.

Chapter 10 · Shloka 10.2 →

न मे विदुः सुरगणाः प्रभवं न महर्षयः ।
अहमादिर्हि देवानां महर्षीणां च सर्वशः ॥10.2॥

Simple meaning: Neither the hosts of devas nor the great sages know My origin, for I am the source of all the devas and great sages in every respect.

Chapter 10 · Shloka 10.4 →

बुद्धिर्ज्ञानमसम्मोहः क्षमा सत्यं दमः शमः ।
सुखं दुःखं भवोऽभावो भयं चाभयमेव च ॥10.4॥

Simple meaning: Intellect, knowledge, freedom from delusion, forgiveness, truthfulness, control of the senses, control of the mind, happiness, sorrow, birth, death, fear, and also fearlessness…

Chapter 10 · Shloka 10.5 →

अहिंसा समता तुष्टिस्तपो दानं यशोऽयशः ।
भवन्ति भावा भूतानां मत्त एव पृथग्विधाः ॥10.5॥

Simple meaning: …non-violence, equanimity, contentment, austerity, charity, fame, and infamy—these diverse qualities of living beings arise from Me alone.

Chapter 10 · Shloka 10.8 →

अहं सर्वस्य प्रभवो मत्तः सर्वं प्रवर्तते ।
इति मत्वा भजन्ते मां बुधा भावसमन्विताः॥10.8॥

Simple meaning: I am the source of all; everything evolves from Me. Realizing this, the wise, endowed with deep devotion, worship Me.
