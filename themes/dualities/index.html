“Be steady. Learn to be an observer and not a participator”

Chapter 2 · Shloka 2.14 →

मात्रास्पर्शास्तु कौन्तेय शीतोष्णसुखदुःखदाः ।
आगमापायिनोऽनित्यास्तांस्तितिक्षस्व भारत ॥2.14॥

Simple meaning: O Kaunteya, the contacts between the senses and their objects give rise to cold and heat, pleasure and pain. They come and go and are impermanent; therefore, O Bharata, endure them patiently.

Chapter 2 · Shloka 2.48 →

योगस्थः कुरु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा धनञ्जय ।
सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योः समो भूत्वा समत्वं योग उच्यते ॥2.48॥

Simple meaning: O Dhananjaya, perform your duties established in yoga, abandoning attachment and remaining even-minded in success and failure. This equanimity of mind is called yoga.
