Offer everything to Krishna

Bhagavad Geeta · Narayaneeyam

ॐ

“Transform action into offering”

This teaches:

  • Daily life becomes spiritual
  • No need to separate “spiritual vs worldly”
  • Even small actions gain meaning

Bhagavad Geeta

Chapter 9 · Shloka 9.27 →

यत्करोषि यदश्नासि यज्जुहोषि ददासि यत् ।
यत्तपस्यसि कौन्तेय तत्कुरुष्व मदर्पणम् ॥9.27॥

Simple meaning: Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer in sacrifice, whatever you give away, and whatever austerities you perform, O son of Kunti, do that as an offering unto Me.

Chapter 12 · Shloka 12.6 →

ये तु सर्वाणि कर्माणि मयि सन्न्यस्य मत्पराः ।
अनन्येनैव योगेन मां ध्यायन्त उपासते ॥12.6॥

Simple meaning: But those who surrender all actions in Me, having Me as their supreme goal, and worship Me through exclusive yoga by meditating on Me…

Chapter 12 · Shloka 12.7 →

तेषामहं समुद्धर्ता मृत्युसंसारसागरात् ।
भवामि नचिरात्पार्थ मय्यावेशितचेतसाम् ॥12.7॥

Simple meaning: …O Partha, for those whose minds are absorbed in Me, I swiftly become their deliverer from the ocean of mortal worldly existence.

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 33 · Shloka 33.2 →

त्वत्प्रीतये सकलमेव वितन्वतोऽस्य
भक्त्यैव देव नचिरादभृथाः प्रसादम् ।
येनास्य याचनमृतेऽप्यभिरक्षणार्थं
चक्रं भवान् प्रविततार सहस्रधारम् ॥33.2॥

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Lord! Because King Ambarisha performed all his actions with devotion to You, You were pleased and even without him ever asking for it, You stationed Your Sudarshana Chakra – with thousand sharp edges – at his side for his protection.
