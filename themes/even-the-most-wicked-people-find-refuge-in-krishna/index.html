Even the most wicked people find refuge in Krishna

Bhagavad Geeta · Shrimad Bhagavatam

ॐ

Bhagavad Geeta

Chapter 9 · Shloka 9.30 →

अपि चेत्सुदुराचारो भजते मामनन्यभाक् ।
साधुरेव स मन्तव्यः सम्यग्व्यवसितो हि सः ॥9.30॥

Simple meaning: Even if a person of extremely wicked conduct worships Me with single-minded devotion, he must indeed be regarded as righteous, for he has rightly resolved.

Chapter 9 · Shloka 9.32 →

मां हि पार्थ व्यपाश्रित्य येऽपि स्युः पापयोनयः।
स्त्रियो वैश्यास्तथा शूद्रास्तेऽपि यान्ति परां गतिम् ॥9.32॥

Simple meaning: O Partha, taking refuge in Me, of whatever birth they might be—women, vaishyas, and likewise shudras, or sinful people—attain the supreme destination.

Chapter 9 · Shloka 9.33 →

किं पुनर्ब्राह्मणाः पुण्या भक्ता राजर्षयस्तथा ।
अनित्यमसुखं लोकमिमं प्राप्य भजस्व माम् ॥9.33॥

Simple meaning: How much more easily then holy brahmanas and devoted supreme sages attain Me. Having obtained this transient and joyless world, devote yourself unto Me.

Shrimad Bhagavatam

Chapter 6 · Shloka 10.6.38 →

यातुधान्यपि सा स्वर्गमवाप जननीगतिम् ।
कृष्णभुक्तस्तनक्षीराः किमु गावो नु मातरः ॥10.6.38॥

Simple meaning: Even though she was a wicked rakshasi, because her breast milk was drunk by Krishna, she attained the status of a mother and obtained heaven. Then what to speak of the cows and mothers who nurse Him with love?

Chapter 12 · Shloka 10.12.38 →

नैतद् विचित्रं मनुजार्भमायिनः परावराणां परमस्य वेधसः ।
अघोऽपि यत्स्पर्शनधौतपातकः प्रापात्मसाम्यं त्वसतां सुदुर्लभम् ॥10.12.38॥

Simple meaning: This is not at all astonishing—that even Aghasura, attained equality with the Lord simply by His touch— the Supreme Lord, who appears as a human child and is the controller of all beings, such a state which is extremely difficult for the impious to attain.

Chapter 12 · Shloka 10.12.39 →

सकृद् यदङ्गप्रतिमान्तराहिता मनोमयी भागवतीं ददौ गतिम् ।
स एव नित्यात्मसुखानुभूत्यभिव्युदस्तमायोऽन्तर्गतो हि किं पुनः ॥10.12.39॥

Simple meaning: Even a single contact with something connected to the Lord’s form can grant a divine spiritual destination. Then what to speak of those realized souls, whose ignorance is completely destroyed by experiencing eternal spiritual bliss, and who have the Lord fully realized within?
