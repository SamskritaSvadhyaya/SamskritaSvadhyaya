Immortality of the Soul

Bhagavad Geeta · Shrimad Bhagavatam

ॐ

“The body perishes but the Soul does not”

Bhagavad Geeta

Chapter 2 · Shloka 2.18 →

अन्तवन्त इमे देहा नित्यस्योक्ताः शरीरिणः ।
अनाशिनोऽप्रमेयस्य तस्माद्युध्यस्व भारत ॥2.18॥

Simple meaning: These bodies of the eternal, indestructible, and immeasurable, embodied soul are said to have an end. Therefore, O Bharata, fight.

Chapter 2 · Shloka 2.19 →

य एनं वेत्ति हन्तारं यश्चैनं मन्यते हतम् ।
उभौ तौ न विजानीतो नायं हन्ति न हन्यते ॥2.19॥

Simple meaning: He who considers this soul to be a slayer, and he who thinks this soul can be slain—both of them fail to understand the truth. The soul neither slays nor is slain.

Chapter 2 · Shloka 2.20 →

न जायते म्रियते वा कदाचि नायं भूत्वा भविता वा न भूयः ।
अजो नित्यः शाश्वतोऽयं पुराणो न हन्यते हन्यमाने शरीरे ॥2.20॥

Simple meaning: This soul is never born, nor does it ever die; nor, having once existed, does it ever cease to be. It is unborn, eternal, everlasting, and ancient, and is not destroyed when the body is destroyed.

Chapter 2 · Shloka 2.22 →

वासांसि जीर्णानि यथा विहाय नवानि गृह्णाति नरोऽपराणि ।
तथा शरीराणि विहाय जीर्णा न्यन्यानि संयाति नवानि देही ॥2.22॥

Simple meaning: Just as a person casts off worn-out garments and puts on new ones, so the embodied soul casts off worn-out bodies and enters into other new ones.

Chapter 2 · Shloka 2.30 →

देही नित्यमवध्योऽयं देहे सर्वस्य भारत ।
तस्मात्सर्वाणि भूतानि न त्वं शोचितुमर्हसि ॥2.30॥

Simple meaning: O Bharata, this embodied soul dwelling within the body of all beings is eternally invulnerable and can never be slain. Therefore, you ought not to grieve for any living being.

Shrimad Bhagavatam

Chapter 4 · Shloka 10.4.19 →

भुवि भौमानि भूतानि यथा यान्त्यपयान्ति च ।
नायमात्मा तथैतेषु विपर्येति यथैव भूः ॥10.4.19॥

Simple meaning: Just as the earth remains unchanged despite the constant transformation of earthly beings upon it, so too the soul remains unaffected by the changes bodies undergo—birth, death, and all that lies between.

Chapter 4 · Shloka 10.4.22 →

यावध्हतोऽस्मि हन्तास्मीत्यात्मानं मन्यतेऽस्वदृक् ।
तावत्तदभिमान्यज्ञो बाध्यबाधकतामियात् ॥10.4.22॥

Simple meaning: As long as one who does not perceive the true nature of the soul thinks "I am killed" or "I am the killer," that ignorant person, identifying with the body, continues to experience the duality of victim and persecutor.
