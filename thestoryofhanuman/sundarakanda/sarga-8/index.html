Sarga 8

Further description of the aerial car, Pushpaka

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।
वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

असाध्यसाधकस्वामिन् असाध्यं तव किं वद।
 रामदूत कृपासिन्धो मत्कार्यं साधय प्रभो॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 8

Further description of the aerial car, Pushpaka

(Benefit: Overcoming Cheating and Deceit)

(7 Shlokas)

स तस्य मध्ये भवनस्य संस्थितं महद्विमानं मणिवज्रचित्रितम्।
प्रतप्तजाम्बूनदजालकृत्रिमं ददर्श वीरः पवनात्मजः कपिः॥5.8.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Hanuman), तस्य of that (Ravana’s palace), मध्ये in the middle, भवनस्य of the mansion/palace, संस्थितम् situated/located, महत् great/magnificent, विमानम् aerial car, मणि-वज्र-चित्रितम् adorned with gems and diamonds, प्रतप्त-जाम्बूनद-जाल-कृत्रिमम् artificially designed with refined gold lattice-work, ददर्श saw/beheld, वीरः the hero, पवन-आत्मजः the son of the Wind-God, कपिः the monkey (Hanuman)

Anvay: सः पवनात्मजः वीरः कपिः तस्य भवनस्य मध्ये संस्थितं मणिवज्रचित्रितं प्रतप्तजाम्बूनदजालकृत्रिमं महत् विमानं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: He, the heroic monkey and son of the Wind-God, saw a great aerial mansion situated in the middle of that palace, adorned with gems and diamonds, and artificially made with lattice-work of refined molten Jambunada gold.

तदप्रमेयाप्रतिकारकृत्रिमं कृतं स्वयं साध्विति विश्वकर्मणा।
दिवं गतं वायुपथप्रतिष्ठितं व्यराजतादित्यपथस्य लक्ष्मवत्॥5.8.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that (vimana/aerial mansion), अप्रमेय-अप्रतिकार-कृत्रिमम् immeasurable, inimitably artificial i.e., of a kind that cannot be imitated or matched, कृतम् made, स्वयम् by himself, साधु इति saying "excellent!", विश्वकर्मणा by Vishvakarma (the celestial architect), दिवम् गतम् which had reached the sky/gone to the heavens, वायु-पथ-प्रतिष्ठितम् established in the path of wind (i.e., high up, as if placed in the aerial path), व्यराजत it shone brilliantly, आदित्य-पथस्य of the sun's path, लक्ष्मवत् endowed with splendor

Anvay: तत् अप्रमेयाप्रतिकारकृत्रिमं स्वयं विश्वकर्मणा कृतं 'साधु' इति, दिवं गतं आदित्यपथस्य लक्ष्मवत् वायुपथप्रतिष्ठितं व्यराजत।

Simple meaning: That (vimana), immeasurable and of unmatched artificial design, was made by Vishvakarma himself and praised by him. Having risen to the heavens, situated in the path of the wind, it shone with the splendor in the sun’s path.

न तत्र किञ्चिन्न कृतं प्रयत्नतो न तत्र किञ्चिन्न महार्हरत्नवत्।
न ते विशेषा नियताः सुरेष्वपि न तत्र किञ्चिन्न महाविशेषवत्॥5.8.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: न तत्र किञ्चित् there was nothing (that was), न कृतम् प्रयत्नतः not made with effort (i.e., everything was created with great effort), न तत्र किञ्चित् there was nothing, न महा-अर्ह-रत्नवत् not possessing highly precious gems (i.e., everything was adorned with priceless gems), न ते विशेषाः no such splendours, नियताः fixed/established, सुरेषु अपि even among the devas, न तत्र किञ्चित् there was nothing there, न महा-विशेषवत् that did not have some great distinction

Anvay: तत्र न किञ्चित् न प्रयत्नतः कृतं, तत्र न किञ्चित् न महार्हरत्नवत्, ते विशेषाः सुरेषु अपि नियताः न, तत्र न किञ्चित् न महाविशेषवत्।

Simple meaning: There was nothing there that was not made with great effort. There was nothing there that was not adorned with highly valuable gems. Even among the devas, such splendours were not commonly established. There was nothing there that did not possess some extraordinary distinction.

तपः समाधानपराक्रमार्जितं मनः समाधानविचारचारिणम्।
अनेकसंस्थानविशेषनिर्मितं ततस्ततस्तुल्यविशेषदर्शनम्॥5.8.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तपः austerity/penance, समाधान-पराक्रम-अर्जितम् acquired with concentration/composure and valour/might, मनः the mind, समाधान-विचार-चारिणम् one that moves or functions according to steadiness/contentment and reflection/deep thought, अनेक-संस्थान-विशेष-निर्मितम् constructed with many unique layouts, ततः ततः here and there/from place to place, तुल्य-विशेष-दर्शनम् appearing equally splendid in all aspects

तपः-समाधान-पराक्रम-आर्जितम् – acquired through austerity, concentration, and valour

मनः-समाधान-विचार-चारिणम् – functioning in line with steadiness and reflective thought of the mind

Anvay: तपः समाधानपराक्रमार्जितं, मनः समाधानविचारचारिणम्, अनेकसंस्थानविशेषनिर्मितं, ततः ततः तुल्यविशेषदर्शनम्।

Simple meaning: That (structure) was acquired through austerity, mental composure, and valour; It reflected the workings of a mind engaged in steadiness and reflective thought; It was constructed with many unique architectural designs; And in every part, it appeared equally resplendent and distinguished.

विशेषमालम्ब्य विशेषसंस्थितं विचित्रकूटं बहुकूटमण्डितम्।
मनोऽभिरामं शरदिन्दुनिर्मलं विचित्रकूटं शिखरं गिरेर्यथा॥5.8.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: विशेषम् आलम्ब्य resting on a special/unique foundation, विशेष-संस्थितम् specially situated/uniquely positioned, विचित्र-कूटम् having various peaks/artistically designed spires, बहु-कूट-मण्डितम् adorned with many peaks or towers, मनः-अभिरामम् pleasing to the mind, शरत्-इन्दु-निर्मलम् as pure as the autumn moon, विचित्र-कूटम् with varied peaks/spires (repeated for emphasis), शिखरम् peak/summit, गिरेः यथा like that of a mountain

Anvay: विशेषम् आलम्ब्य, विशेषसंस्थितं, विचित्रकूटं, बहुकूटमण्डितं, मनःअभिरामं, शरदिन्दुनिर्मलं, विचित्रकूटं शिखरं गिरेः यथा।

Simple meaning: Resting on a unique base and specially situated, having artistically varied and many adorned peaks, pleasing to the mind and as pure as the autumn moon, that variegated spired top (of the mansion) was like the summit of a mountain.

वहन्ति यं कुण्डलशोभिताननाः महाशना व्योमचरा निशाचराः।
विवृत्तविध्वस्तविशाललोचनाः महाजवा भूतगणाः सहस्रशः॥5.8.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: वहन्ति यम् whom (they) carry/accompany, कुण्डल-शोभित-आननाः with faces adorned by earrings, महा-अशनाः great eaters/ravenous, व्योम-चराः moving through the sky/sky-travelers, निशा-चराः night-roamers (i.e., rakshasas), विवृत्त-विध्वस्त-विशाल-लोचनाः with wide eyes rolling and disordered, महा-जवाः of great speed/swift, भूत-गणाः hordes of goblin-like beings/spirits, सहस्रशः in thousands

Anvay: कुण्डलशोभिताननाः, महाशनाः, व्योमचराः, निशाचराः, विवृत्तविध्वस्तविशाललोचनाः, महाजवाः, भूतगणाः, सहस्रशः, यं वहन्ति…

Simple meaning: Whom thousands of goblin-like spirits of great speed, with wide, disheveled, and rolling eyes, night-roaming, sky-flying, ravenous beings, with faces adorned with earrings—carry or surround.

वसन्तपुष्पोत्करचारुदर्शनं वसन्तमासादपि कान्तदर्शनम्।
स पुष्पकं तत्र विमानमुत्तमं ददर्श तद्वानरवीरसत्तमः॥5.8.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: वसन्त-पुष्प-उत्कर-चारु-दर्शनम् having a charming appearance like a heap of spring flowers, वसन्त-मासात् अपि कान्त-दर्शनम् even more delightful in appearance than the spring month, सः he (Hanuman), पुष्पकम् (the) Pushpaka (aerial chariot), तत्र there, विमानम् उत्तमम् excellent aerial vehicle/mansion, ददर्श saw, तत् वानर-वीर-सत्तमः that best among monkey-heroes

Anvay: सः वानरवीरसत्तमः तत्र वसन्तपुष्पोत्करचारुदर्शनं वसन्तमासात् अपि कान्तदर्शनम् उत्तमं पुष्पकं विमानं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: That best among monkey-heroes, Hanuman, saw there the excellent aerial mansion, Pushpaka, which had the charming appearance of spring flowers, and was even more delightful to look at than the spring season itself.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे अष्टमः सर्गः ॥8॥

Thus ends the eighth sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.

॥ जय श्री हनुमान् ॥
॥ सर्वं श्री कृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
