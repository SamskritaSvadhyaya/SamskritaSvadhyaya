Sarga 15

सर्गः १५ · Hanuman sees Sita

ॐ

Sundarakanda Sarga 15                                                                                                                                                             

॥ॐ॥

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।
वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

असाध्यसाधकस्वामिन् असाध्यं तव किं वद।
 रामदूत कृपासिन्धो मत्कार्यं साधय प्रभो॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 15

Hanuman sees Sita

(Benefit: Gaining happiness and wealth)

(55 Shlokas)

स वीक्षमाणस्तत्रस्थो मार्गमाणश्च मैथिलीम्।
अवेक्षमाणश्च महीं सर्वां तामन्ववैक्षत॥5.15.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Hanuman), वीक्षमाणः looking around, तत्र-स्थः standing there/stationed at that spot, मार्गमाणः च and searching, मैथिलीम् for Maithili, अवेक्षमाणः च and observing attentively, महीम् सर्वाम् the entire earth/ground area (i.e., the surroundings), ताम् अन्ववैक्षत looked for her/searched for her

Anvay: सः तत्रस्थः वीक्षमाणः, मैथिलीं मार्गमाणः, सर्वां महीम् अवेक्षमाणः ताम् अन्ववैक्षत।

Simple meaning: Standing there looking around, searching for Maithili, Hanuman observed the entire area for her.

सन्तानकलताभिश्च पादपैरुपशोभिताम्।
दिव्यगन्धरसोपेतां सर्वतस्समलङ्कृताम्॥5.15.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: सन्तानक-लताभिः च with hanging creepers of Santanaka, पादपैः उपशोभिताम् adorned with trees/beautified by trees, दिव्य-गन्ध-रस-उपेताम् endowed with divine fragrance and essence, सर्वतः on all sides/all around, समलङ्कृताम् beautifully decorated/adorned

Anvay: (सा मही) सन्तानकलताभिः पादपैः उपशोभितां, दिव्यगन्धरसोपेतां, सर्वतः समलङ्कृताम् (आसीत्)।

Simple meaning: That land was adorned with trees draped in hanging creepers, filled with divine fragrance and essence, and beautifully decorated on all sides.

तां स नन्दनसङ्काशां मृगपक्षिभिरावृताम्।
हर्म्यप्रासादसम्बाधां कोकिलाकुलनिस्स्वनाम्॥5.15.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् that (grove/garden/area), सः he (Hanuman), नन्दन-सङ्काशाम् resembling Nandana (the celestial garden of the devas), मृग-पक्षिभिः आवृताम् filled with deer and birds, हर्म्य-प्रासाद-सम्बाधाम् crowded with mansions and palatial buildings, कोकिल-आकुल-निस्स्वनाम् resounding with the cries of many cuckoos

Anvay: सः नन्दनसङ्काशां, मृगपक्षिभिः आवृतां, हर्म्यप्रासादसम्बाधां, कोकिलाकुलनिस्स्वनां तां (महीम् अपश्यत्)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw that place, resembling the celestial Nandana garden, filled with deer and birds, crowded with mansions and palaces, and resounding with the cries of many cuckoos.

काञ्चनोत्पलपद्माभिर्वापीभिरुपशोभिताम्।
बह्वासनकुथोपेतां बहुभूमिगृहायुताम्॥5.15.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: काञ्चन-उत्पल-पद्माभिः with golden lotuses and water-lilies, वापीभिः उपशोभिताम् adorned with ponds/beautified by tanks or pools, बहु-आसन-कुथ-उपेताम् furnished with many seats and soft cushions (कुथ = silken/cushioned seat or cover), बहु-भूमि-गृह-आयुताम् accompanied by many-storied buildings

Anvay: काञ्चनोत्पलपद्माभिः वापीभिः उपशोभितां, बह्वासनकुथोपेतां, बहुभूमिगृहायुतां (तां महीम् अपश्यत्)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw that place adorned with ponds filled with golden lotuses and water-lilies, furnished with many seats and silken cushions, and surrounded by multi-storied mansions.

सर्वर्तुकुसुमै रम्यां फलवद्भिश्च पादपैः।
पुष्पितानामशोकानां श्रिया सूर्योदयप्रभाम्॥5.15.5॥
प्रदीप्तामिव तत्रस्थो मारुतिस्समुदैक्षत।

Word-by-word meaning: सर्व-ऋतु-कुसुमैः with flowers of all seasons, रम्याम् beautiful, फलवत्-भिः च पादपैः and with fruit-bearing trees, पुष्पितानाम् अशोकानाम् of blossomed Ashoka trees, श्रिया with splendour/beauty, सूर्य-उदय-प्रभाम् like the radiance of sunrise, प्रदीप्ताम् इव as if it were blazing/glowing, तत्रस्थः standing there (Hanuman), मारुतिः son of the Wind-God, समुदैक्षत observed intently/looked carefully

Anvay: तत्रस्थः मारुतिः सर्वर्तुकुसुमैः रम्यां, फलवद्भिः पादपैः च, पुष्पितानाम् अशोकानां श्रिया – सूर्योदयप्रभां प्रदीप्ताम् इव – (ताम् अशोकवनिकां) समुदैक्षत।

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman) saw that beautiful grove, blooming with flowers of all seasons and full of fruit-laden trees. The splendor of the blossoming Ashoka trees was like the radiance of the rising sun.

निष्पत्रशाखां विहगैः क्रियमाणामिवासकृत्॥5.15.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: निष्पत्र-शाखाम् with leafless branches, विहगैः by birds, क्रियमाणाम् इव as if being made, असकृत् repeatedly/constantly

Anvay: विहगैः असकृत् निष्पत्रशाखां क्रियमाणाम् इव…

Simple meaning: Branches of the trees as if were repeatedly being stripped of leaves by the birds…

विनिष्पतद्भिः शतशश्चित्रैः पुष्पावतंसकैः।
आमूलपुष्पनिचितैरशोकैश्शोकनाशनैः॥5.15.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: विनिष्पतद्भिः with the falling down/cascading, शतशः in hundreds, चित्रैः colorful/variegated, पुष्प-अवतंसकैः flowers looking like earrings, आमूल-पुष्प-निचितैः covered with flowers from the root to the top, अशोकैः Ashoka trees, शोक-नाशनैः destroyers of sorrow/grief-removers

Anvay: चित्रैः पुष्पावतंसकैः शतशः विनिष्पतद्भिः, आमूलपुष्पनिचितैः शोकनाशनैः अशोकैः (ताम् अशोकवनिकां समुदैक्षत)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw the grove which was filled with Ashoka trees, which were destroyers of sorrow and covered with flowers from root to top. From them, hundreds of colorful flower clusters were continuously falling which looked like earrings hanging from the branches.

पुष्पभारातिभारैश्च स्पृशद्भिरिव मेदिनीम्।
कर्णिकारैः कुसुमितैः किंशुकैश्च सुपुष्पितैः॥5.15.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुष्प-भार-अतिभारैः with an excessive weight of flowers, च and, स्पृशद्भिः इव as if touching, मेदिनीम् the earth, कर्णिकारैः with Karnikara trees, कुसुमितैः blooming/flower-laden, किंशुकैः with Kimshuka trees (Flame of the Forest), सुपुष्पितैः richly blossomed

Anvay: कुसुमितैः कर्णिकारैः सुपुष्पितैः किंशुकैः च, पुष्पभारातिभारैः मेदिनीं स्पृशद्भिः इव (ताम् अशोकवनिकां समुदैक्षत)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw the grove which was adorned with blooming Karnikara and richly flowered Kimshuka trees, whose excessive burden of flowers seemed as if they were touching the ground.

स देशः प्रभया तेषां प्रदीप्त इव सर्वतः।
पुन्नागास्सप्तपर्णाश्च चम्पकोद्दालकास्तथा॥5.15.9॥
विवृद्धमूला बहवश्शोभन्ते स्म सुपुष्पिताः।

Word-by-word meaning: सः देशः that region/place (Ashoka grove), प्रभया तेषाम् by the radiance of those (trees), प्रदीप्तः इव as if illuminated/lit up, सर्वतः on all sides/everywhere, पुन्नागाः Punnaga trees, सप्तपर्णाः च and Saptaparna trees, चम्पक-उद्दालकाः तथा likewise Champaka and Uddalaka trees, विवृद्ध-मूला having well-grown roots (i.e., fully grown), बहवः many, शोभन्ते स्म were shining/appearing beautiful, सुपुष्पिताः in full bloom

Anvay: तेषां प्रभया सः देशः सर्वतः प्रदीप्तः इव। बहवः पुन्नागाः सप्तपर्णाः च चम्पकोद्दालकाः च, विवृद्धमूलाः सुपुष्पिताः शोभन्ते स्म।

Simple meaning: That place (Ashoka grove), illuminated by the radiance of the flowering trees, appeared as if it were ablaze with light on all sides. Many fully grown Punnaga, Saptaparna, Campaka, and Uddalaka trees, all in full bloom, shone beautifully.

शातकुम्भनिभाः केचित् केचिदग्निशिखोपमाः॥5.15.10॥
नीलाञ्जननिभाः केचित् तत्राशोकास्सहस्रशः।

Word-by-word meaning: शातकुम्भ-निभाः resembling refined gold, केचित् some (of the trees), केचित् others, अग्नि-शिखा-उपमाः like flames of fire, नीलाञ्जन-निभाः like dark collyrium (jet black), केचित् some others, तत्र there (in that grove), अशोकाः Ashoka trees, सहस्रशः in thousands

Anvay: तत्र सहस्रशः अशोकाः केचित् शातकुम्भनिभाः, केचित् अग्निशिखोपमाः, केचित् नीलाञ्जननिभाः।

Simple meaning: There, thousands of Ashoka trees were seen— some resembled refined gold, some looked like tongues of flame, and others were dark like collyrium.

नन्दनं विविधोद्यानं चित्रं चैत्ररथं यथा॥5.15.11॥
अतिवृत्तमिवाचिन्त्यं दिव्यं रम्यं श्रिया वृतम्।

Word-by-word meaning: नन्दनम् like Nandana - the heavenly garden of Indra, विविध-उद्यानम् full of various kinds of gardens, चित्रम् beautiful/marvelous/full of wonders, चैत्ररथम् like Chaitraratha - the divine garden of Kubera, यथा like, अतिवृत्तम् exceedingly vast or extending beyond limits, इव as if, अचिन्त्यम् inconceivable/beyond imagination, दिव्यम् divine/celestial, रम्यम् delightful/charming, श्रिया वृतम् enveloped in or endowed with splendor/beauty

Anvay: नन्दनं, चित्रं चैत्ररथं यथा, विविधोद्यानम्, अतिवृत्तम् इव, अचिन्त्यं दिव्यं रम्यं, श्रिया वृतं (सा अशोकवनिका आसीत्)।

Simple meaning: It looked like Nandana, the celestial garden, and like the marvelous Chaitraratha. It was filled with a variety of gardens, appearing boundless and beyond comprehension, divine, exceedingly delightful, and wrapped in celestial splendor.

द्वितीयमिव चाकाशं पुष्पज्योतिर्गणायुतम्॥5.15.12॥
पुष्परत्नशतैश्चित्रं द्वितीयं सागरं यथा।

Word-by-word meaning: द्वितीयम् इव like a second, च and, आकाशम् sky, पुष्प-ज्योतिः-गण-आयुतम् filled with groups of flower-lights (i.e., flower clusters shining like stars or lights), पुष्प-रत्न-शतैः with hundreds of flowers and gems, चित्रम् beautiful/marvelous/adorned, द्वितीयम् a second, सागरम् ocean, यथा like

Anvay: द्वितीयम् आकाशम् इव च, पुष्पज्योतिर्गणायुतं, पुष्परत्नशतैः चित्रं द्वितीयं सागरं यथा (सा अशोकवनिका आसीत्)।

Simple meaning: It appeared like a second sky, filled with clusters of flowers shining like stars or lights, and like a second ocean, splendidly adorned with hundreds of flowers and gems.

सर्वर्तुपुष्पैर्निचितं पादपैर्मधुगन्धिभिः॥5.15.13॥

नानानिनादैरुद्यानं रम्यं मृगगणैर्द्विजैः।
अनेकगन्धप्रवहं पुण्यगन्धं मनोरमम्॥5.15.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: सर्व-ऋतु-पुष्पैः with flowers of all seasons, निचितम् covered/filled, पादपैः with trees, मधु-गन्धिभिः having the sweet fragrance of honey, नाना-निनादैः with various pleasing sounds, उद्यानम् garden, रम्यम् delightful/charming, मृग-गणैः by groups of deer, द्विजैः by birds, अनेक-गन्ध-प्रवहम् carrying many kinds of fragrances, पुण्य-गन्धम् having sacred (pure, auspicious) scent, मनोरमम् lovely/pleasing to the mind

Anvay: मधुगन्धिभिः सर्वर्तुपुष्पैः निचितं पादपैः, मृगगणैः द्विजैः नानानिनादैः रम्यम् उद्यानं (युक्तम् आसीत्)। अनेकगन्धप्रवहं, पुण्यगन्धं, मनोरमम् (अभवत्)।

Simple meaning: The beautiful garden was filled with trees bearing flowers of all seasons, their blossoms sweet with the scent of honey. It echoed with various melodious sounds, made by flocks of birds and herds of deer. It carried many kinds of pleasant fragrances, was divinely scented, and altogether captivating to the senses.

शैलेन्द्रमिव गन्धाढ्यं द्वितीयं गन्धमादनम्।
अशोकवनिकायां तु तस्यां वानरपुङ्गवः॥5.15.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: शैलेन्द्रम् lord of mountains (i.e., a towering or majestic mountain), इव like, गन्ध-आढ्यम् rich in fragrance, द्वितीयम् another, a second, गन्धमादनम् Gandhamadana mountain (a legendary fragrant mountain in the Himalayas), अशोक-वनिकायाम् in the Ashoka grove, तु but/indeed, तस्याम् in that (grove), वानर-पुङ्गवः the foremost among the monkeys (i.e., Hanuman)

Anvay: वानरपुङ्गवः तस्याम् अशोकवनिकायां – गन्धाढ्यं द्वितीयं शैलेन्द्रं गन्धमादनम् इव —

Simple meaning: In that Ashoka grove, the foremost among the monkeys (Hanuman) saw a place that was rich in fragrance, like a lord of mountains, appearing to be a second Gandhamadana…

स ददर्शाविदूरस्थं चैत्यप्रासादमुच्छ्रितम्।
मथ्ये स्तम्भसहस्रेण स्थितं कैलासपाण्डुरम्॥5.15.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Hanuman), ददर्श saw, अविदूरस्थम् situated not far away/nearby, चैत्य-प्रासादम् a towering temple-palace (चैत्य - shrine, प्रासाद - palace or tall structure), उच्छ्रितम् lofty/tall/elevated, मध्ये in the middle, स्तम्भ-सहस्रेण with a thousand pillars, स्थितम् standing/established, कैलास-पाण्डुरम् white like Mount Kailasa (sacred snow-clad mountain of Lord Shiva)

Anvay: …अविदूरस्थं स्तम्भसहस्रेण मध्ये स्थितम् उच्छ्रितं कैलासपाण्डुरं चैत्यप्रासादं सः ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw, not far from him, a lofty temple-like palace, rising high, it stood in the midst of a thousand pillars, white like Mount Kailasa, shining with brilliance.

प्रवालकृतसोपानं तप्तकाञ्चनवेदिकम्।
मुष्णन्तमिव चक्षूंषि द्योतमानमिव श्रिया॥5.15.17॥
विमलं प्रांशुभावत्वादुल्लिखन्तमिवाम्बरम्।

Word-by-word meaning: प्रवाल-कृत-सोपानम् having stairways made of coral, तप्त-काञ्चन-वेदिकम् with altars/platforms of molten gold, मुष्णन्तम् इव चक्षूंषि as if stealing the eyes (dazzling to the eye), द्योतमानम् इव श्रिया as if radiating with brilliance/glory, विमलम् spotless, pure, प्रांशु-भावत्वात् due to its great height, उल्लिखन्तम् इव अम्बरम् as if touching or scraping the sky

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) प्रवालकृतसोपानं तप्तकाञ्चनवेदिकं, चक्षूंषि मुष्णन्तम् इव, श्रिया द्योतमानम् इव, विमलं, प्रांशुभावत्वात् अम्बरम् उल्लिखन्तम् इव (तत् चैत्यप्रासादं) ददर्श।

Simple meaning: (Hanuman) saw a structure with stairways made of coral and altars of molten gold, So dazzling it seemed to steal the eyes, Shining with splendor, pure and radiant, So tall it looked as if it were touching the sky.

ततो मलिनसंवीतां राक्षसीभिस्समावृताम्॥5.15.18॥

उपवासकृशां दीनां निश्श्वसन्तीं पुनः पुनः।
ददर्श शुक्लपक्षादौ चन्द्ररेखामिवामलाम्॥5.15.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, मलिन-संवीताम् clad in soiled or faded garments, राक्षसीभिः समावृताम् surrounded by rakshasis, उपवास-कृशाम् emaciated due to fasting, दीनाम् miserable/wretched, निःश्वसन्तीम् पुनः पुनः sighing again and again, ददर्श (he) saw, शुक्ल-पक्ष-आदौ चन्द्र-रेखाम् इव like the crescent moon at the beginning of the bright fortnight, अ-मलाम् pure/untainted

Anvay: ततः (हनुमान्) मलिनसंवीतां, राक्षसीभिः समावृताम्, उपवासकृशां, दीनां, पुनः पुनः निश्श्वसन्तीं शुक्लपक्षादौ चन्द्ररेखाम् इव अमलां (तां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Then Hanuman saw a woman — clad in soiled garments, surrounded by rakshasis, emaciated from fasting, miserable, sighing again and again, she appeared as pure as the crescent moon at the start of the bright fortnight.

मन्दप्रख्यायमानेन रूपेण रुचिरप्रभाम्।
पिनद्धां धूमजालेन शिखामिव विभावसोः॥5.15.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: मन्द-प्रख्यायमानेन रूपेण with a form whose beauty was faintly visible, रुचिर-प्रभाम् having a radiant glow/beautiful brilliance, पिनद्धाम् enveloped/bound/veiled, धूम-जालेन by a mesh or veil of smoke, शिखाम् इव like a flame, विभावसोः of fire

Anvay: मन्दप्रख्यायमानेन रूपेण – धूमजालेन पिनद्धां रुचिरप्रभां विभावसोः शिखाम् इव – (तां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: She, whose radiance was naturally beautiful, yet whose form appeared dimmed, like a bright flame of a fire obscured by smoke — thus did Hanuman behold her.

पीतेनैकेन संवीतां क्लिष्टेनोत्तमवाससा।
सपङ्कामनलङ्कारां विपद्मामिव पद्मिनीम्॥5.15.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: पीतेन yellow, एकेन single, संवीताम् clothed in/covered with, क्लिष्टेन soiled, उत्तम-वाससा fine garment, स-पङ्काम् with dirt or mud on her, अन्-अलङ्काराम् without ornaments, वि-पद्माम् deprived of lotuses, इव like, पद्मिनीम् a lake of lotuses

Anvay: एकेन क्लिष्टेन पीतेन उत्तमवाससा संवीतां, सपङ्काम्, अनलङ्कारां – विपद्मां पद्मिनीम् इव – (तां हनुमान् ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw her, clothed in a single yellow, soiled and worn-out fine garment, smeared with dirt, devoid of ornaments, like a lake of lotuses bereft of lotuses.

व्रीडितां दुःखसन्तप्तां परिम्लानां तपस्विनीम्।
ग्रहेणाङ्गारकेणेव पीडितामिव रोहिणीम्॥5.15.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: व्रीडिताम् ashamed/embarrassed, दुःख-सन्तप्ताम् scorched/burnt by sorrow/deeply afflicted, परिम्लानाम् faded/wilted, तपस्विनीम् poor woman, ग्रहेण by the planet, अङ्गारकेण by Mars (the red planet, associated with aggression and suffering), इव like, पीडिताम् tormented/afflicted, रोहिणीम् the star Rohini

Anvay: व्रीडितां, दुःखसन्तप्तां, परिम्लानां, तपस्विनीम् – अङ्गारकेण ग्रहेण पीडितां रोहिणीम् इव – (तां हनुमान् ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw her — ashamed, burnt by sorrow, wilted, poor woman, afflicted like the star Rohini tormented by the malefic planet Mars (Angaraka).

अश्रुपूर्णमुखीं दीनां कृशामनशनेन च।
शोकध्यानपरां दीनां नित्यं दुःखपरायणाम्॥5.15.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: अश्रुपूर्ण-मुखीम् with her face full of tears, दीनाम् miserable/distressed, कृशाम् emaciated/thin, अनशनेन च due to not eating/fasting, शोक-ध्यान-पराम् absorbed in thoughts of grief, दीनाम् miserable/distressed, नित्यम् always, दुःख-परायणाम् given over to sorrow, constantly immersed in pain

Anvay: अश्रुपूर्णमुखीं, दीनाम्, अनशनेन कृशां, शोकध्यानपरां, नित्यं दीनां दुःखपरायणां (तां हनुमान् ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw her — her face full of tears, distressed and thin due to fasting, absorbed in grief and sorrowful thoughts, immersed in suffering.

प्रियं जनमपश्यन्तीं पश्यन्तीं राक्षसीगणम्।
स्वगणेन मृगीं हीनां श्वगणाभिवृतामिव॥5.15.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रियम् जनम् beloved people, अपश्यन्तीम् not seeing/unable to see, पश्यन्तीम् seeing, राक्षसी-गणम् the group of rakshasis, स्व-गणेन from her own kind/group, मृगीम् a female deer, हीनाम् separated, श्व-गण-अभिवृताम् as if surrounded by a pack of dogs, इव like

Anvay: प्रियं जनम् अपश्यन्तीं, राक्षसीगणं पश्यन्तीं – स्वगणेन हीनां श्वगणाभिवृतां मृगीम् इव – (तां हनुमान् ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw her — unable to see her dear ones, surrounded instead by a crowd of rakshasis, like a doe separated from her herd encircled by a pack of dogs.

नीलनागाभया वेण्या जघनं गतयैकया।
नीलया नीरदापाये वनराज्या महीमिव॥5.15.25॥
सुखार्हां दुःखसन्तप्तां व्यसनानामकोविदाम्।

Word-by-word meaning: नील-नाग-आभया like the radiance of a dark serpent, वेण्या by the braid of hair, जघनम् गतया that had fallen down over the hips, एकया a single, नीलया dark in color, नीरद-आपाये at the end of the rainy season (when clouds depart), वन-राज्या the line or stretch of forest, महीम् the earth, इव like, सुख-अर्हाम् deserving of happiness, दुःख-सन्तप्ताम् scorched by sorrow, व्यसनानाम् of misfortunes, अ-कोविदाम् inexperienced

Anvay: जघनं गतया एकया नीलनागाभया वेण्या – नीरदापाये नीलया वनराज्या महीम् इव – सुखार्हां दुःखसन्तप्तां व्यसनानाम् अकोविदां (तां हनुमान् ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw her — her single, dark braid resembling a black serpent, falling over her hips, like the dark line of forest upon the earth when rain clouds have disappeared, a woman deserving happiness, yet scorched by sorrow, inexperienced to misfortunes.

तां समीक्ष्य विशालाक्षीमधिकं मलिनां कृशाम्॥5.15.26॥
तर्कयामास सीतेति कारणैरुपपादिभिः।

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her, समीक्ष्य observing carefully, विशाल-अक्षीम् large-eyed, अधिकम् मलिनाम् extremely unkempt/discolored (due to suffering), कृशाम् thin/emaciated, तर्कयामास began to infer/conclude, सीता इति “this is Sita”, कारणैः by reasons, उपपादिभिः  based on logical inference/indicators

Anvay: विशालाक्षीम्, अधिकं मलिनां, कृशां तां समीक्ष्य, उपपादिभिः कारणैः – “सीता इति” तर्कयामास।

Simple meaning: Seeing that large-eyed woman, emaciated and deeply worn, Hanuman reasoned, “This must be Sita,” using all the signs and logical evidence available to him.

ह्रियमाणा तदा तेन रक्षसा कामरूपिणा॥5.15.27॥
यथारूपा हि दृष्टा वै तथारूपेयमङ्गना।

Word-by-word meaning: ह्रियमाणा being carried away (abducted), तदा at that time, तेन रक्षसा by that rakshasa (i.e., Ravana), काम-रूपिणा who could assume any form at will, यथा रूपा of what appearance, हि indeed, दृष्टा was seen (by the vanaras, or as described), वै truly, तथा रूपा of the same appearance, इयम् अङ्गना this lady (woman)

Anvay: तदा तेन कामरूपिणा रक्षसा ह्रियमाणा यथा रूपा दृष्टा हि वै, तथा रूपा इयम् अङ्गना (इति हनुमान् तर्कयामास)।

Simple meaning: The way she looked when she was being carried off by that rakshasa, who could take any form at will — exactly the same is the appearance of this woman.

पूर्णचन्द्राननां सुभ्रूं चारुवृत्तपयोधराम्॥5.15.28॥
कुर्वतीं प्रभयादेवीं सर्वा वितिमिरा दिशः।

Word-by-word meaning: पूर्ण-चन्द्र-आननाम् having a face like the full moon, सु-भ्रूम् with beautiful eyebrows, चारु-वृत्त-पयोधराम् having charmingly rounded breasts, कुर्वतीम् प्रभया radiating such a light that it made, देवीम् the divine lady, सर्वाः all, वि-तिमिराः free of darkness (cleared of gloom), दिशः directions

Anvay: पूर्णचन्द्राननां, सुभ्रूं, चारुवृत्तपयोधरां देवीं, प्रभया सर्वाः दिशः वितिमिराः कुर्वतीं (तां हनुमान् ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw her - face like the full moon, graceful eyebrows, and beautifully rounded bosom. The divine lady, with her radiance, dispelled the darkness from all directions.

तां नीलकेशीं बिम्बोष्ठीं सुमध्यां सुप्रतिष्ठिताम्॥5.15.29॥
सीतां पद्मपलाशाक्षीं मन्मथस्य रतिं यथा।

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her, नील-केशीम् having dark hair, बिम्ब-ओष्ठीम् with lips like the bimba fruit (glossy, red, and full), सु-मध्याम्  with a slender waist, सु-प्रतिष्ठिताम् firmly established (in posture, or in her resolve), सीताम् Sita, पद्म-पलाश-अक्षीम् with eyes like lotus petals, मन्मथस्य of Manmatha (the god of love), रतिम् Rati (his consort; goddess of beauty and passion), यथा like/as

Anvay: नीलकेशीं, बिम्बोष्ठीं, सुमध्यां सुप्रतिष्ठितां पद्मपलाशाक्षीं — मन्मथस्य रतिं यथा — तां सीतां (हनुमान् ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He saw Sita, with dark hair, lips like the bimba fruit, a slender waist, and steady grace, with lotus petal-like eyes, like Rati, the consort of Manmatha, the god of love.

इष्टां सर्वस्य जगतः पूर्णचन्द्रप्रभामिव॥5.15.30॥
भूमौ सुतनुमासीनां नियतामिव तापसीम्।

Word-by-word meaning: इष्टाम् beloved/desired, सर्वस्य of all, जगतः the world, पूर्ण-चन्द्र-प्रभाम् one who shines like the full moon, इव like, भूमौ on the ground, सु-तनुम् slender-bodied, आसीनाम् seated, नियताम् self-restrained/controlled in conduct, इव like, तापसीम् a female ascetic

Anvay: सर्वस्य जगतः इष्टां पूर्णचन्द्रप्रभाम् इव, नियतां तापसीम् इव, भूमौ आसीनां सुतनुम् (तां हनुमान् ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He saw her who was of slender form — she looked like the light of a full moon which is loved by the whole world, like a self-disciplined ascetic seated on the ground.

निःश्वासबहुलां भीरुं भुजगेन्द्रवधूमिव॥5.15.31॥
शोकजालेन महता विततेन न राजतीम्।

Word-by-word meaning: निःश्वास-बहुलाम् sighing deeply, भीरुम् timid/frightened, भुजगेन्द्र-वधूम् like the wife of the king of serpents, इव like, शोक-जालेन by a net (web) of sorrow, महता immense/great, विततेन spread out/enveloping, न not, राजतीम् shining/radiant

Anvay: भीरुं निःश्वासबहुलां भुजगेन्द्रवधूम् इव, महता विततेन शोकजालेन न राजतीम् (तां हनुमान् ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He saw her — who was sighing deeply, like the frightened consort of the serpent king, and was not radiant because of being enveloped in a great net of sorrow.

संसक्तां धूमजालेन शिखामिव विभावसोः॥5.15.32॥
तां स्मृतीमिव सन्दिग्धामृद्धिं निपतितामिव।

Word-by-word meaning: संसक्ताम् enveloped/covered, धूम-जालेन with a mesh or veil of smoke, शिखाम् flame, इव like, विभावसोः of fire, ताम् her, स्मृतीम् memory, इव like, सन्दिग्धाम् uncertain/clouded, ऋद्धिम् prosperity/glory, निपतिताम् fallen, इव as if

Anvay: धूमजालेन संसक्तां – विभावसोः शिखाम् इव –, – सन्दिग्धां स्मृतीम् इव –, – निपतिताम् ऋद्धिम् इव –, तां (हनुमान् ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw her who appeared — like a flame of fire veiled by smoke, like a memory that has become unclear, like prosperity that has fallen.

विहतामिव च श्रद्धामाशां प्रतिहतामिव॥5.15.33॥
सोपसर्गां यथा सिद्धिं बुद्धिं सकलुषामिव।

Word-by-word meaning: विहताम् इव श्रद्धाम् like faith destroyed/shattered, आशाम् प्रतिहताम् इव like hope obstructed/frustrated, स-उपसर्गाम् यथा सिद्धिम् like success/accomplishment afflicted with obstacles, बुद्धिम् स-कलुषाम् इव like intellect tainted

Anvay: विहतां श्रद्धाम् इव, प्रतिहतां आशाम् इव, सोपसर्गां सिद्धिं यथा, सकलुषां बुद्धिम् इव (तां हनुमान् ददर्श)।  

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw her who appeared — like faith that has been shattered, like hope that has been frustrated, like success beset with obstacles, like intellect that has been tainted.

अभूतेनापवादेन कीर्तिं निपतितामिव॥5.15.34॥
रामोपरोधव्यथितां रक्षोहरणकर्शिताम्।

Word-by-word meaning: अभूतेन false/baseless, अपवादेन by slander or false accusation, कीर्तिम् fame, reputation, निपतिताम् fallen/ruined, इव as if, राम-उपरोध-व्यथिताम् distressed due to separation from Rama, रक्षः-हरण-कर्शिताम् emaciated by the abduction by the rakshasa (Ravana)

Anvay: अभूतेन अपवादेन निपतितां कीर्तिम् इव​, रामोपरोधव्यथितां रक्षोहरणकर्शितां (तां हनुमान् ददर्श)।  

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw her who appeared — like fame fallen due to false slander, afflicted by the grief of separation from Rama, worn down and emaciated by the rakshasa's abduction.

अबलां मृगशावाक्षीं वीक्षमाणां ततस्ततः॥5.15.35॥

बाष्पाम्बुपरिपूर्णेन कृष्णवक्राक्षिपक्ष्मणा।
वदनेनाप्रसन्नेन निःश्वसन्तीं पुनः पुनः॥5.15.36॥

Word-by-word meaning: अबलाम् weak (in worldly power), defenseless woman, मृग-शाव-अक्षीम् one with eyes like a fawn’s (tender and fearful), वीक्षमाणाम् glancing around/looking, ततः ततः in all directions/here and there, बाष्प-अम्बु-परिपूर्णेन filled with hot tears, कृष्ण-वक्र-अक्षि-पक्ष्मणा with long, curved black eyelashes, वदनेन with her face, अप्रसन्नेन unhappy/joyless, निःश्वसन्तीम् sighing, पुनः पुनः again and again

Anvay: मृगशावाक्षीं, बाष्पाम्बुपरिपूर्णेन कृष्णवक्राक्षिपक्ष्मणा, अप्रसन्नेन वदनेन ततः ततः वीक्षमाणम् अबलाम् (तां हनुमान् ददर्श)।  

Simple meaning: Hanuman beheld her — a helpless woman with fawn-like eyes, looking anxiously around, here and there, her beautiful eyes full of tears, shaded by curved black lashes, her face joyless, sighing again and again in sorrow.

मलपङ्कधरां दीनां मण्डनार्हाममण्डिताम्।
प्रभां नक्षत्रराजस्य कालमेघैरिवावृताम्॥5.15.37॥

Word-by-word meaning: मल-पङ्क-धराम् bearing dirt and mire, दीनाम् sorrowful/miserable, मण्डन-अर्हाम् worthy of ornamentation, अमण्डिताम् unadorned, प्रभाम् the radiance, नक्षत्र-राजस्य of the king of stars (the moon), काल-मेघैः by dark clouds, इव like, आवृताम् covered, veiled

Anvay: मलपङ्कधरां, दीनां, मण्डनार्हाम्, आमण्डितां – कालमेघैः आवृतां नक्षत्रराजस्य प्रभाम् इव – (तां हनुमान् ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw Sita clad in dust and dirt, miserable, though worthy of royal adornment, yet unadorned, her radiance like that of the moon now veiled by dark clouds.

तस्य संदिदिहे बुद्धिर्मुहुः सीतां निरीक्ष्य तु।
आम्नायानामयोगेन विद्यां प्रशिथिलामिव॥5.15.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य his (Hanuman’s), संदिदिहे became doubtful/wavered, बुद्धिः intellect, conviction, मुहुः again and again, सीताम् (about) Sita, निरीक्ष्य तु having observed/on observing, आम्नायानाम् of the Vedas, अयोगेन due to lack of proper method or connection, विद्याम् knowledge, प्रशिथिलाम् loosened/faded/dulled, इव like

आम्नाय (पुंलिङ्गः)  वेदः

समानार्थकाः — श्रुति वेद आम्नाय त्रयी श्रुति निगम src. Kosha. Ashtadhyayi

Anvay: सीतां निरीक्ष्य तस्य बुद्धिः तु – आम्नायानाम् अयोगेन प्रशिथिलां विद्याम् इव – मुहुः संदिदिहे।

Simple meaning: Hanuman’s mind repeatedly wavered on seeing Sita, like knowledge of the Vedas that becomes ineffective due to lack of proper application.

दुःखेन बुबुधे सीतां हनुमाननलङ्कृताम्।
संस्कारेण यथा हीनां वाचमर्थान्तरं गताम्॥5.15.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुःखेन with difficulty, बुबुधे recognized/understood, सीताम् Sita, हनुमान् Hanuman, अनलङ्कृताम् unadorned/without ornaments, संस्कारेण by refinement or proper usage, यथा just as, हीनाम् lacking, वाचम् speech, अर्थ-अन्तरम् गताम् that has deviated in meaning/changed in significance

Anvay: हनुमान् – संस्कारेण हीनां वाचम् अर्थान्तरं गताम् यथा – अनलङ्कृतां सीतां दुःखेन बुबुधे।

Simple meaning: Hanuman recognized Sita with great difficulty, for she was without any ornaments, just like one tries to understand speech devoid of refinement, which has strayed from its intended meaning.

तां समीक्षय विशालाक्षीं राजपुत्रीमनिन्दिताम्।
तर्कयामास सीतेति कारणैरुपपादिभिः॥5.15.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her, समीक्ष्य having observed carefully, विशालाक्षीम् a woman with large, beautiful eyes, राजपुत्रीम् a royal princess, अनिन्दिताम् blameless/faultless, तर्कयामास he reasoned/inferred, सीता इति "she is Sita", कारणैः by reasons, उपपादिभिः based on logical indications/proofs

Anvay: हनुमान् विशालाक्षीम् अनिन्दितां राजपुत्रीं तां समीक्ष्य, कारणैः उपपादिभिः "सीता इति" तर्कयामास।

Simple meaning: Observing her closely — the wide-eyed, faultless royal princess, Hanuman reasoned logically that she must be Sita, based on several valid indications.

वैदेह्या यानि चाङ्गेषु तदा रामोऽन्वकीर्तयत्।
तान्याभरणजालानि गात्रशोभीन्यलक्षयत्॥5.15.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: वैदेह्याः of Vaidehi, यानि which, च and, अङ्गेषु on the limbs, तदा at that time, रामः Rama, अन्वकीर्तयत् had described in detail, तानि those, आभरण-जालानि sets of ornaments, गात्र-शोभीनि beautifying her body, अलक्षयत् (he) observed/noticed

Anvay: (हनुमान्) वैदेह्याः अङ्गेषु यानि आभरणजालानि तदा रामः अन्वकीर्तयत्, तानि गात्रशोभीनि अलक्षयत्।

Simple meaning: Hanuman noticed the same ornaments on her limbs that Rama had earlier described in detail — those ornaments which enhanced the beauty of Sita’s body.

सुकृतौ कर्णवेष्टौ च श्वदंष्ट्रौ च सुसंस्थितौ।
मणिविद्रुमचित्राणि हस्तेष्वाभरणानि च॥5.15.42॥
श्यामानि चिरयुक्तत्वात् तथा संस्थानवन्ति च।

Word-by-word meaning: सुकृतौ well-made, कर्ण-वेष्टौ earrings, च and, श्वदंष्ट्रौ ornaments worn on the canine-shaped part of the ear (a type of ear-ornament), च and, सुसंस्थितौ well-positioned/properly placed, मणि-विद्रुम-चित्राणि inlaid with gems and red coral, हस्तेषु on the hands, आभरणानि ornaments, च and, श्यामानि dark (in color), चिरयुक्तत्वात् due to long usage, तथा likewise, संस्थानवन्ति of appropriate shape or structure, च and

Anvay: सुकृतौ सुसंस्थितौ कर्णवेष्टौ च श्वदंष्ट्रौ च, हस्तेषु मणिविद्रुमचित्राणि आभरणानि च, चिरयुक्तत्वात् श्यामानि तथा संस्थानवन्ति च।

Simple meaning: Hanuman observed the well-crafted earrings and the canine-shaped ornaments, properly placed, along with hand ornaments decorated with gems and red coral. These were darkened due to long usage, yet still retained their elegant form.

तान्यैवेतानि मन्येऽहं यानि रामोऽन्वकीर्तयत्॥5.15.43॥

तत्र यान्यवहीनानि तान्यहं नोपलक्षये।
यान्यस्या नावहीनानि तानीमानि न संशयः॥5.15.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: तानि एव एतानि मन्ये अहम् I think, these (ornaments) are indeed/precisely those (ornaments), यानि रामः अन्वकीर्तयत् which Rama had described, तत्र यानि अव-हीनानि among those (described by Rama) which are lost/fallen off, तानि अहम् न उपलक्षये those I do not see, यानि अस्याः न अव-हीनानि which (ornaments) of her (Sita’s) have not fallen off, तानि इमानि those, these (here, now visible), न संशयः (there is) no doubt

Anvay: अहं तत्र यानि अवहीनानि तानि न उपलक्षये। यानि अस्याः न अवहीनानि, तानि इमानि (भवन्ति), न संशयः।

Simple meaning: “Of those (ornaments) described (by Rama), the ones that have fallen off, I do not see here. The ones that have not fallen from her — these here — are certainly the same, without any doubt.

पीतं कनकपट्टाभं स्रस्तं तद्वसनं शुभम्।
उत्तरीयं नगासक्तं तदा दृष्टं प्लवङ्गमैः॥5.15.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: पीतम् yellow, कनक-पट्ट-आभम् shining like a plate of gold, स्रस्तम् loosened/fallen down, तत् that, वसनम् garment/clothing, शुभम् auspicious/beautiful, उत्तरीयम् upper garment/upper cloth, नग-आसक्तम् entangled in a tree (नग - tree, आसक्त - attached), तदा at that time, दृष्टम् was seen, प्लवङ्गमैः by the vanaras

Anvay: तदा प्लवङ्गमैः पीतं कनकपट्टाभं तत् शुभं वसनं स्रस्तं नगासक्तम् उत्तरीयं दृष्टम्।

Simple meaning: "At that time, the vanaras saw that beautiful yellow garment, shining like a plate of gold, loosened and entangled in a tree — it was her upper garment.

भूषणानि च मुख्यानि दृष्टानि धरणीतले।
अनयैवापविद्धानि स्वनवन्ति महान्ति च॥5.15.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूषणानि ornaments, च and, मुख्यानि beautiful, दृष्टानि were seen, धरणी-तले on the surface of the earth/ground, अनया एव by this very (lady) (i.e., by Sita herself), अपविद्धानि thrown away/discarded, स्वनवन्ति resonant/tinkling (i.e., producing sound), महान्ति excellent, च and

Anvay: महान्ति स्वनवन्ति च मुख्यानि भूषणानि च धरणीतले दृष्टानि, अनया एव अपविद्धानि।

Simple meaning: "And the beautiful and excellent ornaments, producing tinkling sounds, were seen on the ground; they had indeed been thrown away by this very lady (Sita).

इदं चिरगृहीतत्वाद्वसनं क्लिष्टवत्तरम्।
तथाऽपि नूनं तद्वर्णं तथा श्रीमद्यथेतरत्॥5.15.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: इदम्  this, चिर-गृहीतत्वात् due to being held (worn) for a long time, वसनम् clothing/garment, क्लिष्टवत्तरम् more crumpled/worn-out/creased, तथा अपि even so/nevertheless, नूनम् surely/certainly, तत्-वर्णम् that (same) color, तथा श्रीमत् equally radiant/splendid, यथा इतरत् as the other (one she was wearing earlier)

Anvay: इदं वसनं चिरगृहीतत्वात् क्लिष्टवत्तरम्। तथा अपि नूनं तत् वर्णं तथा श्रीमत् यथा इतरत्।

Simple meaning: "This garment, because it has been worn for a long time, is more crumpled and worn-out. Even so, it surely has the same color and the same splendour as the other one (she had worn before).

इयं कनकवर्णाङ्गी रामस्य महिषी प्रिया।
प्रणष्टापि सती याऽस्य मनसो न प्रणश्यति॥5.15.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: इयम् this (lady), कनक-वर्णाङ्गी golden-complexioned/with a body of golden hue, रामस्य of Rama, महिषी queen/wife, प्रिया beloved, प्रणष्टा अपि though lost/although abducted, सती virtuous/chaste, या who, अस्य of him (Rama), मनसः from the mind, न प्रणश्यति does not vanish/is not forgotten

Anvay: इयं कनकवर्णाङ्गी सती रामस्य प्रिया महिषी, या प्रणष्टा अपि अस्य मनसः न प्रणश्यति।

Simple meaning: "This golden-complexioned lady is the dear queen of Rama, the chaste one who, though lost, is not forgotten from his mind.

इयं सा यत्कृते रामश्चतुर्भिः परितप्यते।
कारुण्येनानृशंस्येन शोकेन मदनेन च॥5.15.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: इयम् this (lady), सा she is the same, यत्-कृते because of whom, रामः Rama, चतुर्भिः by four (things/feelings), परितप्यते is tormented/is afflicted, कारुण्येन by compassion, अनृशंस्येन by gentleness/non-cruelty, शोकेन by grief, मदनेन च and by love (or passion)

Anvay: इयं सा (सीता) या कारणेन रामः चतुर्भिः — कारुण्येन, अनृशंस्येन, शोकेन, मदनेन च — परितप्यते।

Simple meaning: "This is she, for whose sake Rama is tormented by four feelings — compassion, gentleness, grief, and love.

स्त्री प्रणष्टेति कारुण्यादाश्रितेत्यानृशंस्यतः।
पत्नी नष्टेति शोकेन प्रियेति मदनेन च॥5.15.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्त्री प्रणष्टा इति कारुण्यात् “A woman is lost” – thinking thus, due to compassion, आश्रिता इति अनृशंस्यतः “(she is) under my protection” – thinking thus, due to kindness/non-cruelty, पत्नी नष्टा इति शोकेन “my wife is lost” – thinking thus, due to sorrow, प्रिया इति मदनेन च “(she is) my beloved” – thinking thus, due to love

Anvay: स्त्री प्रणष्टा इति कारुण्यात्, आश्रिता इति अनृशंस्यतः, पत्नी नष्टा इति शोकेन, प्रिया इति मदनेन च (रामः परितप्यते)।

Simple meaning: “Thinking "a woman is lost," he (Rama) is afflicted by compassion. Thinking "she was under my protection," he suffers due to kindness (or righteousness). Thinking "my wife is lost," he is tormented by grief. And thinking "she is my beloved," he is distressed by love.

अस्या देव्या यथा रूपमङ्गप्रत्यङ्गसौष्ठवम्।
रामस्य च यथा रूपं तस्येयमसितेक्षणा॥5.15.51॥

Word-by-word meaning: अस्याः देव्याः of this divine lady (Sita), यथा रूपम् such as is the beauty, अङ्ग-प्रत्यङ्ग-सौष्ठवम् the gracefulness of limbs and sub-limbs (minor limbs), रामस्य च and of Rama too, यथा रूपम् such as is his appearance/beauty, तस्य इयम् she (is) his (beloved), असितेक्षणा the dark-eyed one (Sita)

Anvay: अस्याः देव्याः अङ्गप्रत्यङ्गसौष्ठवं यथा रूपं च, रामस्य रूपं यथा। तस्य इयम् असितेक्षणा।

Simple meaning: “Just as is the beauty and gracefulness of this divine lady’s limbs and features, and just as is the appearance of Rama, so is this dark-eyed lady indeed his (Rama’s wife).

अस्या देव्या मनस्तस्मिंस्तस्य चास्यां प्रतिष्ठितम्।
तेनेयं स च धर्मात्मा मुहूर्तमपि जीवति॥5.15.52॥

Word-by-word meaning: अस्याः देव्याः of this divine lady (Sita), मनः mind, तस्मिन् in him (Rama), तस्य his (Rama’s), च and, अस्याम् in her (Sita), प्रतिष्ठितम् is established/set/fixed, तेन because of that, इयम् she, सः च and he (Rama), धर्मात्मा the righteous soul/virtuous one (Rama), मुहूर्तम् अपि even for a moment, जीवति is able to live/survives

Anvay: अस्याः देव्याः मनः तस्मिन् (रामे), तस्य च मनः अस्यां (सीतायां) प्रतिष्ठितम्। तेन कारणेन, इयं (सीता) धर्मात्मा सः (रामः) च मुहूर्तम् अपि जीवति।

Simple meaning: “The mind of this divine lady is set on him (Rama), and his mind is fixed on her. It is because of that (deep love) that this lady and that righteous man are still alive — even for a moment.

दुष्करं कृतवान्रामो हीनो यदनया प्रभुः।
धारयत्यात्मनो देहं न शोकेनावसीदति॥5.15.53॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुष्करम् extremely difficult (to do), कृतवान् has done, रामः Rama, हीनः bereft/separated, यत् that, अनया from her (Sita), प्रभुः the lord/the great one, धारयति is sustaining/bearing, आत्मनः देहम् his own body/life, न not, शोकेन by grief, अवसीदति is overwhelmed/perishes/sinks

Anvay: प्रभुः रामः यत् अनया हीनः आत्मनः देहं धारयति, शोकेन न अवसीदति, (तत्) दुष्करं कृतवान्।

Simple meaning: “Rama, though separated from her (Sita), yet sustains his own life and does not sink under grief. This indeed is an act of great difficulty that he has accomplished.

दुष्करं कुरुते रामो य इमां मत्तकाशिनीम्।
सीतां विना महाबाहुर्मुहूर्तमपि जीवति॥5.15.54॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुष्करम् extremely difficult, कुरुते does/performs, रामः Rama, यः who, इमाम् this (woman), मत्त-काशिनीम् with intoxicating lusture, सीताम् Sita, विना without, महाबाहुः mighty-armed (Rama), मुहूर्तम् even for a moment, अपि even, जीवति lives

Anvay: यः रामः इमां मत्तकाशिनीं सीतां विना मुहूर्तम् अपि जीवति, महाबाहुः दुष्करं कुरुते।

Simple meaning: “The mighty-armed Rama, who lives even for a moment without this radiant Sita, is indeed performing a feat that is extremely difficult.”

एवं सीतां तदा दृष्ट्वा हृष्टः पवनसम्भवः।
जगाम मनसा रामं प्रशशंस च तं प्रभुम्॥5.15.55॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus/in this manner, सीताम् Sita, तदा then, दृष्ट्वा having seen, हृष्टः delighted/rejoiced, पवन-सम्भवः born of the Wind-God (i.e., Hanuman), जगाम went, मनसा in his mind, रामम् to Rama, प्रशशंस praised, च and, तम् him (Rama), प्रभुम् the Lord/the noble one

Anvay: पवनसम्भवः हनुमान् तदा सीताम् एवं दृष्ट्वा हृष्टः, मनसा रामं जगाम, तं प्रभुं च प्रशशंस।

Simple meaning: Having thus seen Sita at that time, Hanuman, the son of the Wind-God, rejoiced. In his mind, he went to Rama and praised that noble Lord.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे पञ्चदशः सर्गः ॥15॥

Thus ends the fifteenth sarga in Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.

॥ जय श्री हनुमान् ॥
॥ सर्वं श्री कृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
