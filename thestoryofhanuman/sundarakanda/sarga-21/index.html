सर्गः २१ · Sita spurns Ravana's submission

ॐ

Sundarakanda Sarga 21                                                                                                                                                             

॥ॐ॥

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।
वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

असाध्यसाधकस्वामिन् असाध्यं तव किं वद।
 रामदूत कृपासिन्धो मत्कार्यं साधय प्रभो॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 21

Sita spurns Ravana's submission

(Benefit: Inculcating Virtues)

(34 Shlokas)

तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा सीता रौद्रस्य रक्षसः।
आर्ता दीनस्वरा दीनं प्रत्युवाच शनैर्वचः॥5.21.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य his (of Ravana), तत्-वचनम् those words/that speech, श्रुत्वा having heard, सीता Sita, रौद्रस्य of the cruel/terrible one, रक्षसः of the rakshasa (Ravana), आर्ता distressed, दीन-स्वरा with a feeble/piteous voice, दीनम् humbly/pitifully, प्रत्युवाच replied, शनैः slowly/gently, वचः words/speech

Anvay: सीता रौद्रस्य तस्य रक्षसः तत् वचनं श्रुत्वा, आर्ता, दीनस्वरा, शनैः दीनं वचः प्रत्युवाच।

Simple meaning: After hearing those harsh words of the cruel rakshasa, the distressed Sita, with a sorrowful and feeble voice, slowly and humbly replied.

दुःखार्ता रुदती सीता वेपमाना तपस्विनी।
चिन्तयन्ती वरारोहा पतिमेव पतिव्रता।
तृणमन्तरतः कृत्वा प्रत्युवाच शुचिस्मिता॥5.21.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुःख-आर्ता afflicted by grief, रुदती weeping, सीता Sita, वेपमाना trembling, तपस्विनी the poor lady, चिन्तयन्ती thinking, वरारोहा of excellent limbs/beautiful form, पतिम् एव only her husband, पतिव्रता devoted to her husband, तृणम् a blade of grass, अन्तरतः कृत्वा placing in between (herself and Ravana), प्रत्युवाच replied, शुचिस्मिता of pure smile (though figurative here — Sita is dignified even in sorrow)

Anvay: दुःखार्ता, रुदती, वेपमाना, तपस्विनी, वरारोहा, पतिव्रता शुचिस्मिता सीता, पतिम् एव चिन्तयन्ती, तृणम् अन्तरतः कृत्वा, प्रत्युवाच।

Simple meaning: Afflicted with sorrow, weeping, and trembling, poor Sita— who was of noble form, and devoted solely to her husband—was constantly thinking of him. Placing a blade of grass between herself and Ravana (as a sign of distance and modesty), the pure-smiled Sita spoke in reply.

निवर्तय मनो मत्तः स्वजने क्रियतां मनः।
न मां प्रार्थयितुं युक्तं सुसिद्धिमिव पापकृत्॥5.21.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: निवर्तय turn away/withdraw, मनः mind, मत्तः from me, स्वजने on your own people (your own women), क्रियताम् let it be directed/placed, मनः mind, न not, माम् me, प्रार्थयितुम् to desire/to court/to ask for, युक्तम् appropriate/fitting, सु-सिद्धिम् a fully accomplished one/success, इव like, पापकृत् a sinner/wrongdoer

Anvay: मत्तः मनः निवर्तय। स्वजने मनः क्रियताम्। पापकृत् सुसिद्धिम् इव – मां प्रार्थयितुं न युक्तम्।

Simple meaning: "Turn your mind away from me and direct it toward your own women. It is not proper for you to desire me—just as it is not right for a sinner to long for blessedness.

अकार्यं न मया कार्यमेकपत्न्या विगर्हितम्।
कुलं सम्प्राप्तया पुण्यं कुले महति जातया॥5.21.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अकार्यम् improper act/unrighteous deed, न  not, मया by me, कार्यम् should be done/committed, एकपत्न्या by a woman devoted to one husband, विगर्हितम् blameworthy/reprehensible, कुलम् family, सम्प्राप्तया attained/born into, पुण्यम् sacred/virtuous, कुले in a family, महति great, जातया born (as)

Anvay: महति कुले जातया, पुण्यं कुलं सम्प्राप्तया, एकपत्न्या मया, विगर्हितम् अकार्यं न कार्यम्।

Simple meaning: "Born in a noble family, having attained a virtuous lineage, I—being devoted to one husband—should not perform any blameworthy or improper act.”

एवमुक्त्वा तु वैदेही रावणं तं यशस्विनी।
राक्षसं पृष्ठतः कृत्वा भूयो वचनमब्रवीत्॥5.21.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus/in this way, उक्त्वा having spoken, तु but/then, वैदेही Vaidehi (Sita), रावणम् to Ravana, तम् that (Ravana), यशस्विनी the illustrious one/renowned woman, राक्षसम् the rakshasa, पृष्ठतः कृत्वा turning (him) to her back/turning away from the rakshasa, भूयः again, वचनम् words/speech, अब्रवीत् spoke

Anvay: यशस्विनी वैदेही, रावणम् एवम् उक्त्वा, तं राक्षसं पृष्ठतः कृत्वा, भूयः वचनम् अब्रवीत्।

Simple meaning: Having said this to Ravana, the noble Sita turned her back on the rakshasa and spoke again.

नाहमौपयिकी भार्या परभार्या सती तव।
साधु धर्ममवेक्षस्व साधु साधुव्रतं चर॥5.21.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, अहम् I, औपयिकी भार्या wedded wife (a wife taken through proper marriage rites), परभार्या सती (I am) another man's virtuous wife, तव your, धर्मम् dharma/righteousness, अवेक्षस्व consider/reflect upon/respect, साधु properly, साधु-व्रतम् noble vow/virtuous conduct, चर practice/follow

Anvay: अहं तव औपयिकी भार्या न (अस्मि), परभार्या सती (अस्मि)। साधु धर्मम् अवेक्षस्व। साधुव्रतं चर।

Simple meaning: "I am not your lawful wife. I am a devoted wife belonging to another. Do what is right—respect dharma and live a virtuous life.

यथा तव तथान्येषां दारा रक्ष्या निशाचर।
आत्मानमुपमां कृत्वा स्वेषु दारेषु रम्यताम्॥5.21.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: यथा just as, तव your, तथा in the same way, अन्येषाम् of others, दाराः wives, रक्ष्याः should be protected/respected, निशाचर O night-wanderer! (Ravana), आत्मानम् yourself, उपमाम् कृत्वा making (yourself) the comparison/taking yourself as the example, स्वेषु in your own, दारेषु wives, रम्यताम् (you should) take pleasure/delight

Anvay: निशाचर! यथा तव दाराः रक्ष्याः, तथा अन्येषां (दाराः अपि रक्ष्याः)। आत्मानम् उपमां कृत्वा स्वेषु दारेषु रम्यताम्।

Simple meaning: "O Ravana! Just as you would want your wives to be protected, so too should you protect the wives of others. Think of yourself in their place and find joy in your own wives.

अतुष्टं स्वेषु दारेषु चपलं चलितेन्द्रियम्।
नयन्ति निकृतिप्रज्ञं परदाराः पराभवम्॥5.21.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: अतुष्टम् one who is unsatisfied, स्वेषु दारेषु with his own wives, चपलम् fickle/unstable, चलित-इन्द्रियम् whose senses are unsteady/uncontrolled, नयन्ति lead to/bring about, निकृतिप्रज्ञम् one who understands only deceit/crooked in understanding, परदाराः other men's wives, पराभवम् destruction/downfall

Anvay: स्वेषु दारेषु अतुष्टं, चपलं, चलितेन्द्रियं, निकृतिप्रज्ञं पुरुषं, परदाराः पराभवं नयन्ति।

Simple meaning: "A man who is unsatisfied with his own wives, fickle, whose senses are unrestrained, and who is crooked in understanding —such a man is led to ruin by (the pursuit of) other men's wives.

इह सन्तो न वा सन्ति सतो वा नानुवर्तसे।
तथाहि विपरीता ते बुद्धिराचारवर्जिता॥5.21.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: इह here, सन्तः good people, न वा सन्ति do (they) not exist, सतः वा or of the noble people, न अनुवर्तसे do you not follow, तथा हि indeed thus, विपरीता perverted/opposed/contrary, ते your, बुद्धिः intellect/mindset, आचार-वर्जिता devoid of good conduct

Anvay: इह सन्तः न वा सन्ति? सतः न वा अनुवर्तसे? तथा हि ते बुद्धिः विपरीता, आचारवर्जिता (अस्ति)।

Simple meaning: Are the good people not here (in Lanka)? Or are you not following those who are good? It must be like that because your mind, devoid of good conduct, is perverse.

वचो मिथ्याप्रणीतात्मा पथ्यमुक्तं विचक्षणैः।
राक्षसानामभावाय त्वं वा न प्रतिपद्यसे॥5.21.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: वचः words, मिथ्या-प्रणीत-आत्मा one whose mind is led (or inclined) to falsehood, पथ्यम् wholesome/beneficial, उक्तम् spoken, विचक्षणैः by the wise/discerning ones, राक्षसानाम् of the rakshasas, अभावाय for the destruction/ruin, त्वम् you, वा indeed/even, न प्रतिपद्यसे do not accept/do not follow

Anvay: मिथ्याप्रणीतात्मा त्वं, विचक्षणैः उक्तं पथ्यं वचः, राक्षसानाम् अभावाय, न प्रतिपद्यसे।

Simple meaning: "You, whose mind is led by falsehood, do not accept the wholesome words spoken by the wise. This will lead to the destruction of the rakshasas.

अकृतात्मानमासाद्य राजानमनये रतम्।
समृद्धानि विनश्यन्ति राष्ट्राणि नगराणि च॥5.21.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: अ-कृत-आत्मानम् one who has not mastered himself/undisciplined, आसाद्य by obtaining/encountering, राजानम् a king, अनये in unrighteousness/misconduct, रतम् engrossed/engaged, समृद्धानि prosperous, विनश्यन्ति perish/get destroyed, राष्ट्राणि kingdoms, नगराणि च and cities

Anvay: अकृतात्मानम्, अनये रतं राजानम् आसाद्य, समृद्धानि राष्ट्राणि नगराणि च विनश्यन्ति।

Simple meaning: "By obtaining a king who lacks self-control and delights in unrighteousness, even flourishing kingdoms and cities meet their destruction.

तथेयं त्वां समासाद्य लङ्का रत्नौघसङ्कुला।
अपराधात्तवैकस्य नचिराद्विनशिष्यति॥5.21.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: तथा likewise/in the same way, इयम् this (Lanka), त्वाम् you, समासाद्य by encountering/upon coming under, लङ्का Lanka, रत्न-ओघ-सङ्कुला filled with heaps of gems/abundant in treasures, अपराधात् due to the offence/wrongdoing, तव your, एकस्य alone/only, न चिरात् not long after/very soon, विनशिष्यति will perish/be destroyed

Anvay: तथा इयं रत्नौघसङ्कुला लङ्का त्वां समासाद्य, तव एकस्य अपराधात् न चिरात् विनशिष्यति।

Simple meaning: "Likewise, this Lanka, filled with heaps of precious gems, having come under your rule, will soon be destroyed due to the offense committed by you alone.

स्वकृतैर्हन्यमानस्य रावणादीर्घदर्शिनः।
अभिनन्दन्ति भूतानि विनाशे पापकर्मणः॥5.21.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्व-कृतैः by one’s own actions, हन्यमानस्य of one who is being destroyed/struck down, रावण O Ravana!, अदीर्घ-दर्शिनः the shortsighted/those who cannot foresee the consequences, अभिनन्दन्ति rejoice/applaud, भूतानि beings/creatures, विनाशे in the destruction, पाप-कर्मणः of a wicked-doer/sinful person

Anvay: रावण! अदीर्घदर्शिनः, स्वकृतैः हन्यमानस्य, पापकर्मणः विनाशे, भूतानि अभिनन्दन्ति।

Simple meaning: "O Ravana! When a shortsighted person, who is sinful is destroyed by his own actions, beings rejoice in his downfall.

एवं त्वां पापकर्माणं वक्ष्यन्ति निकृता जनाः।
दिष्ट्यैतद्व्यसनं प्राप्तो रौद्र इत्येव हर्षिताः॥5.21.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus/in this manner, त्वाम् you, पाप-कर्माणम् wicked-doer/sinful one, वक्ष्यन्ति will say/will speak of, निकृताः जनाः the oppressed people/those wronged by you, दिष्ट्या एतत् व्यसनम् प्राप्तः रौद्रः fortunately this downfall has come upon the fierce one (you, Ravana), इति एव in these very words, हर्षिताः rejoicing/delighted

Anvay: एवं निकृताः जनाः – 'रौद्रः एतत् व्यसनं दिष्ट्या प्राप्तः' – इति एव हर्षिताः पापकर्माणं त्वां वक्ष्यन्ति।

Simple meaning: "Thus oppressed people will rejoice and will indeed say about you, the sinful one: ‘This fierce Ravana has, by fate, finally met with this downfall.’

शक्या लोभयितुं नाहमैश्वर्येण धनेन वा।
अनन्या राघवेणाहं भास्करेण प्रभा यथा॥5.21.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: शक्या able to be tempted/influenced, लोभयितुम् to be tempted/seduced, न अहम् I am not, ऐश्वर्येण by wealth/power, धनेन वा or by riches, अनन्या undivided/devoted solely, राघवेण by Raghava (Rama), अहम् I, भास्करेण by the sun, प्रभा the radiance/light, यथा just as

Anvay: अहम् ऐश्वर्येण वा धनेन वा लोभयितुं न शक्या। अहं राघवेण अनन्या, यथा भास्करेण प्रभा।

Simple meaning: "Neither wealth nor power can tempt me. I am solely devoted to Raghava, just as the radiance is to the sun.

उपधाय भुजं तस्य लोकनाथस्य सत्कृतम्।
कथं नामोपधास्यामि भुजमन्यस्य कस्यचित्॥5.21.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: उपधाय having rested upon/having laid (my head or body), भुजम् the arm, तस्य of him, लोक-नाथस्य the lord of the world (i.e., Rama), सत्कृतम् revered/noble/honored, कथम् नाम how indeed/how possibly, उपधास्यामि can I place (myself upon), भुजम् arm, अन्यस्य of another, कस्यचित् anyone/someone else

Anvay: तस्य लोकनाथस्य सत्कृतं भुजम् उपधाय, (अहं) कथं नाम अन्यस्य कस्यचित् भुजम् उपधास्यामि?

Simple meaning: "Having once rested upon the revered arm of Rama, the lord of the world, how could I ever possibly lie upon the arm of any other man?

अहमौपयिकी भार्या तस्यैव वसुधापतेः।
व्रतस्नातस्य विप्रस्य विद्येव विदितात्मनः॥5.21.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: अहम् I, औपयिकी भार्या lawfully wedded wife (obtained through proper marriage rites), तस्य एव of him alone, वसुधा-पतेः lord of the earth (i.e., Rama), व्रत-स्नातस्य one who has bathed after completing a vow, विप्रस्य of a Brahmana (learned man), विद्या knowledge/sacred learning, इव just like, विदित-आत्मनः of one who knows the Self/has realized the truth

Anvay: विदितात्मनः व्रतस्नातस्य विप्रस्य विद्या इव – अहं वसुधापतेः तस्य एव औपयिकी भार्या।

Simple meaning: "Just as sacred knowledge rightfully belongs to a self-knowing Brahmana purified by a vow—  I am the lawful wife of Rama alone, the lord of the earth.

साधु रावण रामेण मां समानय दुःखिताम्।
वने वासितया सार्धं करेण्वेव गजाधिपम्॥5.21.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: साधु rightly/properly/it is best, रावण O Ravana, रामेण with Rama, माम् me, समानय unite/bring together, दुःखिताम् who is sorrowful, वने वासितया with the one who lives in the forest, सार्धम् together with, करेणु इव​  just as/like (with) the female elephant, गज-अधिपम् the lord of elephants (tusker/elephant king)

Anvay: रावण! दुःखितां मां रामेण सार्धं – वने वासितया करेणु गजाधिपम् इव – समानय साधु।

Simple meaning: "O Ravana! It is right to unite the miserable me with Rama— just as a she-elephant dwelling in the forest is reunited with the elephant king.

मित्रमौपयिकं कर्तुं रामः स्थानं परीप्सता।
वधं चानिच्छता घोरं त्वयासौ पुरुषर्षभः॥5.21.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: मित्रम् friendship/alliance, औपयिकम् proper/suitable/appropriate, कर्तुम् to make/establish, रामः Rama, स्थानम् proper, परीप्सता desiring/seeking, वधम् killing/destruction, च and, अनिच्छता being unwilling, घोरम् terrible/violent, त्वया by you, असौ that Rama, पुरुषर्षभः the best among men

Anvay: घोरं वधम् अनिच्छता, औपयिकं परीप्सता च त्वया असौ पुरुषर्षभः रामः मित्रं कर्तुं स्थानम्।

Simple meaning: "It is proper for you to build a suitable alliance with Rama, the best among men, if you wish to avoid a terrible destruction and do not desire such calamity.

विदितः स हि धर्मज्ञः शरणागतवत्सलः।
तेन मैत्री भवतु ते यदि जीवितुमिच्छसि॥5.21.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: विदितः known, सः he (Rama), हि indeed/surely, धर्मज्ञः knower of dharma (righteousness), शरण-आगत-वत्सलः kind and loving towards those who seek refuge, तेन with him, मैत्री friendship, भवतु let there be/may it be, ते for you, यदि if, जीवितुम् to live, इच्छसि you wish

Anvay: सः धर्मज्ञः शरणागतवत्सलः विदितः हि। यदि जीवितुम् इच्छसि, तेन ते मैत्री भवतु।

Simple meaning: "He is indeed well known as a righteous man and one who is compassionate towards those who surrender to him. If you wish to live, make peace with him (Rama).

प्रसादयस्व त्वं चैनं शरणागतवत्सलम्।
मां चास्मै नियतो भूत्वा निर्यातयितुमर्हसि॥5.21.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रसादयस्व please propitiate/appease, त्वम् you, च and, एनम् him (Rama), शरण-आगत-वत्सलम् who is compassionate towards those who surrender, माम् me, च and, अस्मै to him (Rama), नियतः भूत्वा becoming self-controlled/with a firm mind, निर्यातयितुम् to send back/to release, अर्हसि you ought/you should

Anvay: त्वम् एनं शरणागतवत्सलं प्रसादयस्व। नियतः भूत्वा मां च अस्मै निर्यातयितुम् अर्हसि।

Simple meaning: "You should appease Rama, who is compassionate to those who seek his refuge. With a controlled mind, you ought to release me and send me back to him.

एवं हि ते भवेत् स्वस्ति सम्प्रदाय रघूत्तमे।
अन्यथा त्वं हि कुर्वाणो वधं प्राप्स्यसि रावण॥5.21.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् हि  thus indeed, ते for you, भवेत् will be, स्वस्ति well-being/safety, सम्प्रदाय by handing over/surrendering, रघु-उत्तमे to the best of the Raghus (i.e., Rama), अन्यथा otherwise, त्वम् you, हि indeed/surely, कुर्वाणः doing (contrary to this), वधम् death, प्राप्स्यसि you will obtain/you will incur, रावण O Ravana!

कुर्वाणः - कुर्वाण (कृ + शानच्), प्रथमैकवचनम् src. Dhatupatha, Ashtadhyayi

Anvay: रावण! एवं हि रघूत्तमे सम्प्रदाय ते स्वस्ति भवेत्। अन्यथा कुर्वाणः त्वं वधं प्राप्स्यसि हि।

Simple meaning: "O Ravana, only by surrendering me to Rama, the best of the Raghu lineage, will you ensure your well-being. Otherwise, by doing the opposite, you will surely meet your death.

वर्जयेद्वज्रमुत्सृष्टं वर्जयेदन्तकश्चिरम्।
त्वद्विधं तु न सङ्कृद्धो लोकनाथः स राघवः॥5.21.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: वर्जयेत् may avoid/may spare, वज्रम् the thunderbolt (of Indra), उत्सृष्टम् when hurled, वर्जयेत् may avoid, अन्तकः Yama, the god of death, चिरम् for a long time, त्वत्-विधम् one like you, तु न but not, सङ्कृद्धः when enraged/provoked, लोकनाथः सः राघवः the lord of the world that Raghava (Rama)

Anvay: उत्सृष्टं वज्रं वर्जयेत्, अन्तकः चिरं वर्जयेत्, सः लोकनाथः राघवः सङ्कृद्धः त्वद्विधं तु न (वर्जयेत्)।

Simple meaning: "A hurled thunderbolt might be avoided; even Yama (the god of death) might spare someone for a while. But if provoked, Rama—the Lord of the world—will certainly not spare someone like you.

रामस्य धनुषः शब्दं श्रोष्यसि त्वं महास्वनम्।
शतक्रतुविसृष्टस्य निर्घोषमशनेरिव॥5.21.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: रामस्य of Rama, धनुषः of the bow, शब्दम् the sound, श्रोष्यसि you will hear, त्वम् you, महास्वनम् loud resounding, शतक्रतु-विसृष्टस्य discharged by Shatakratu (one who has performed a hundred ashwamedha yagnas - another name for Indra), निर्घोषम् the roar/thunderclap, अशनेः इव like that of a thunderbolt

Anvay: त्वं – शतक्रतुविसृष्टस्य अशनेः निर्घोषम् इव – रामस्य धनुषः महास्वनं शब्दं श्रोष्यसि।

Simple meaning: "You will hear the loud, thunderous sound of Rama’s bow—like the roar of Indra’s thunderbolt when it is hurled.

इह शीघ्रं सुपर्वाणो ज्वलितास्या इवोरगाः ।
इषवो निपतिष्यन्ति रामलक्ष्मणलक्षणाः॥5.21.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: इह here, शीघ्रम् soon/quickly, सु-पर्वाणः well-feathered (having good wings/shafts – referring to arrows), ज्वलित-आस्याः with flaming mouths, इव like, उरगाः serpents, इषवः arrows, निपतिष्यन्ति will fall/descend, राम-लक्ष्मण-लक्षणाः marked by Rama and Lakshmana (belonging to or associated with them)

Anvay: रामलक्ष्मणलक्षणाः सुपर्वाणः इषवः – ज्वलितास्याः उरगाः इव – शीघ्रम् इह निपतिष्यन्ति।

Simple meaning: "Beautifully feathered arrows marked by Rama and Lakshmana will fall here soonl—like serpents with flaming mouths.

रक्षांसि परिनिघ्नन्तः पुर्यामस्यां समन्ततः।
असंपातं करिष्यन्ति पतन्तः कङ्कवाससः॥5.21.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: रक्षांसि the rakshasas, परि-निघ्नन्तः killing/destroying completely, पुर्याम् अस्याम् in this city (i.e., Lanka), समन्ततः all around/from all sides, असंपातम् impenetrable cover/a blockade (lit. 'no-passage'), करिष्यन्ति will make/will create, पतन्तः as they descend/falling, कङ्क-वाससः arrows tied with the plumes of an eagle

Anvay: कङ्कवाससः समन्ततः पतन्तः, रक्षांसि परिनिघ्नन्तः, अस्यां पुर्याम् असंपातं करिष्यन्ति।

Simple meaning: "The arrows tied with the plumes of an eagle will rain everywhere, while destroying the rakshasas, will create an impenetrable blockade.

राक्षसेन्द्रमहासर्पान् स रामगरुडो महान्।
उद्धरिष्यति वेगेन वैनतेय इवोरगान्॥5.21.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: राक्षसेन्द्र-महासर्पान् the great serpent-like leaders of the rakshasas (i.e., powerful rakshasa chiefs, compared to serpents), सः that (he), राम-गरुडः Rama, (likened to) Garuda (the eagle mount of Vishnu and enemy of serpents), महान् great, उद्धरिष्यति will destroy/uproot/lift up and eliminate, वेगेन with speed/force/swiftness, वैनतेयः the son of Vinata (i.e., Garuda), इव like, उरगान् serpents

Anvay: सः महान् रामगरुडः राक्षसेन्द्रमहासर्पान् – वैनतेयः उरगान् इव – वेगेन उद्धरिष्यति।

Simple meaning: "That great Rama will swiftly uproot the mighty serpent-like rakshasa leaders as Garuda (the son of Vinata) uproots the serpents.

अपनेष्यति मां भर्ता त्वत्तः शीघ्रमरिन्दमः।
असुरेभ्यः श्रियं दीप्तां विष्णुस्त्रिभिरिव क्रमैः॥5.21.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: अपनेष्यति will carry away/will rescue, माम् me, भर्ता (my) husband, त्वत्तः from you, शीघ्रम् swiftly/soon, अरिन्दमः destroyer of enemies (Rama), असुरेभ्यः from the rakshasas, श्रियम् prosperity, दीप्ताम् radiant/brilliant, विष्णुः Vishnu, त्रिभिः इव क्रमैः with three strides (referring to Vishnu's incarnation as Vamana, who retrieved the worlds in three steps)

Anvay: अरिन्दमः भर्ता शीघ्रं त्वत्तः – विष्णुः त्रिभिः क्रमैः असुरेभ्यः दीप्तां श्रियम् इव – माम् अपनेष्यति।

Simple meaning: "My husband, Rama, the destroyer of enemies, will soon rescue me from you — just like Vishnu, with three mighty strides, took back the radiant prosperity from the rakshasas.

जनस्थाने हतस्थाने निहते रक्षसां बले।
अशक्तेन त्वया रक्षः कृतमेतदसाधु वै॥5.21.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: जनस्थाने in Janasthana (a forested region ruled by Ravana’s army), हत-स्थाने where the forces were destroyed, निहते killed/slain, रक्षसाम् बले the army of the rakshasas, अशक्तेन powerless/incapable, त्वया by you, रक्षः O Rakshasa! (Ravana), कृतम् done, एतत् this, असाधु unrighteous/improper/evil, वै indeed/truly

Anvay: रक्षः! जनस्थाने हतस्थाने निहते रक्षसां बले, त्वया अशक्तेन एतत् असाधु कृतं वै।

Simple meaning: "O Rakshasa! What you have done is truly reprehensible. Being powerless yourself, you resorted to this disgraceful act in Janasthana only after the rakshasa army there had been destroyed.

आश्रमं तु तयोः शून्यं प्रविश्य नरसिंहयोः।
गोचरं गतयोर्भ्रात्रोरपनीता त्वयाधम॥5.21.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: आश्रमम् the hermitage, तु but/indeed, तयोः of those two (brothers – Rama and Lakshmana), शून्यम् empty, प्रविश्य having entered, नर-सिंहयोः of the lion-like men (Rama and Lakshmana), गोचरम् गतयोः having gone out for gathering food (literally: gone into the field or range of cows i.e., forest to collect fruits, roots), भ्रात्रोः of the brothers, अपनीता was abducted/was taken away, त्वया by you, अधम O vile one/lowly one/wicked one!

Anvay: अधम! त्वया तयोः नरसिंहयोः भ्रात्रोः गोचरं गतयोः शून्यम् आश्रमं प्रविश्य, (अहम्) अपनीता।

Simple meaning: "O vile one! You abducted me by entering the empty hermitage of those two lion-like brothers (Rama and Lakshmana) when they had gone out to collect food.

न हि गन्धमुपाघ्राय रामलक्ष्मणयोस्त्वया।
शक्यं सन्दर्शने स्थातुं शुना शार्दूलयोरिव॥5.21.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: न हि indeed, it is not (possible), गन्धम् scent/mere presence/aura, उपाघ्राय having smelled/sensed, राम-लक्ष्मणयोः of Rama and Lakshmana, त्वया by you, शक्यम् possible, सन्दर्शने in their presence/in confrontation, स्थातुम् to stand/to remain, शुना by a dog, शार्दूलयोः इव like (in front of) tigers

Anvay: त्वया रामलक्ष्मणयोः – शुना शार्दूलयोः इव – गन्धम् उपाघ्राय, सन्दर्शने स्थातुं शक्यं न हि।

Simple meaning: "For you, even catching the mere scent of Rama and Lakshmana makes it impossible to stand before them—just as a dog cannot stand before tigers.

तस्य ते विग्रहे ताभ्यां युगग्रहणमस्थिरम्।
वृत्रस्येवेन्द्रबाहुभ्यां बाहोरेकस्य विग्रहे॥5.21.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य of him (Of Vritra), ते your, विग्रहे in conflict/fight/battle, ताभ्याम् with those two (Rama and Lakshmana), युग-ग्रहणम् grappling together/combat, अस्थिरम् unstable/unsteady/ineffective, वृत्रस्य of Vritra (the rakshasa), इव like, इन्द्र-बाहुभ्याम् by the arms of Indra, बाहोः of the arm, एकस्य of one (only), विग्रहे in the fight

Anvay: विग्रहे ते ताभ्यां – विग्रहे तस्य वृत्रस्य एकस्य बाहोः इन्द्रबाहुभ्याम् इव – युगग्रहणम् अस्थिरम्।

Simple meaning: "In the battle with those two (Rama and Lakshmana), your combat will be unsteady and futile—just like the struggle of a single arm of Vritra against the two mighty arms of Indra.

क्षिप्रं तव स नाथो मे रामः सौमित्रिणा सह।
तोयमल्पमिवादित्यः प्राणानादास्यते शरैः॥5.21.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्षिप्रम् quickly, soon, तव your, सः that (he), नाथः lord/protector (Rama), मे my, रामः Rama, सौमित्रिणा with the son of Sumitra (Lakshmana), सह together/along with, तोयम् water, अल्पम् small/little, इव like, आदित्यः the sun, प्राणान् lives/vital breaths, अदास्यते will take away/seize, शरैः by arrows

Anvay: मे नाथः सः रामः सौमित्रिणा सह शरैः – आदित्यः अल्पं तोयम् इव – क्षिप्रं तव प्राणान् अदास्यते।

Simple meaning: "My lord Rama, together with Lakshmana (the son of Sumitra), will quickly take away your life by his arrows, just like the sun dries up a small amount of water.

गिरिं कुबेरस्य गतोऽथ आलयं सभां गतो वा वरुणस्य राज्ञः।
असंशयं दाशरथेर्न मोक्ष्यसे महाद्रुमः कालहतोऽशनेरिव॥5.21.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: गिरिम् कुबेरस्य the mountain of Kubera, गतः अथ even if (you) go there, आलयम् to the residence, सभाम् गतः वा or go to the assembly hall, वरुणस्य राज्ञः of King Varuna, असंशयम् without doubt, दाशरथेः from the son of Dasharatha (Rama), न मोक्ष्यसे you will not be released/you will not escape, महा-द्रुमः (just like) a great tree, काल-हतः you who are already truck down by Time (or Death), अशनेः इव like by lightning

Anvay: अथ कालहतः (त्वं) कुबेरस्य गिरिं गतः वा, राज्ञः वरुणस्य सभाम् आलयं गतः वा, (अपि सन्) – महाद्रुमः अशनेः इव – दाशरथेः असंशयं न मोक्ष्यसे।

Simple meaning: "Already marked for death by Time, whether you flee to Kubera's mountain or seek shelter in King Varuna's royal palace and court, you shall not escape from Rama, Dasharatha's son, any more than a great tree can escape ruin once struck by a thunderbolt."

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे एकविंशः सर्गः ॥21॥

Thus ends the twenty-first sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
