Sarga 9

Description of the harem of Ravana

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।
वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

असाध्यसाधकस्वामिन् असाध्यं तव किं वद।
 रामदूत कृपासिन्धो मत्कार्यं साधय प्रभो॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 9

Description of the harem of Ravana

(Benefit: Overcoming Cheating and Deceit)

(73 Shlokas)

तस्यालयवरिष्ठस्य मध्ये विपुलमायतम्।
ददर्श भवनश्रेष्ठं हनुमान् मारुतात्मजः॥5.9.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य of that (Ravana), आलय-वरिष्ठस्य the most superior among mansions/the foremost of abodes, मध्ये in the middle, विपुलम् vast/wide, आयतम् spacious, ददर्श (he) saw, भवन-श्रेष्ठम्  the best of mansions, हनुमान् Hanuman, मारुत-आत्मजः the son of Wind-God

Anvay: मारुतात्मजः हनुमान् तस्य आलयवरिष्ठस्य मध्ये विपुलम् आयतं भवनश्रेष्ठं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the son of the Wind-God, saw, in the midst of that most superior palace of Ravana, another wide and spacious mansion.

अर्धयोजनविस्तीर्णमायतं योजनं हि तत्।
भवनं राक्षसेन्द्रस्य बहुप्रासादसङ्कुलम्॥5.9.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: अर्ध-योजन-विस्तीर्णम् half a yojana wide, आयतम् योजनम् हि तत्  extended (in length) indeed one yojana (long) was that, भवनम् mansion, राक्षस-इन्द्रस्य of the king of the rakshasas, बहु-प्रासाद-सङ्कुलम् filled with many towers/turrets

Anvay: तत् राक्षसेन्द्रस्य भवनम् अर्धयोजनविस्तीर्णं, योजनम् आयतं हि, बहुप्रासादसङ्कुलम्।

Simple meaning: That mansion of the king of the rakshasas was half a yojana wide, indeed a full yojana in length, and was filled with many towers.

मार्गमाणस्तु वैदेहीं सीतामायतलोचनाम्।
सर्वतः परिचक्राम हनूमानरिसूदनः॥5.9.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: मार्गमाणः तु  but (he) searching/while searching, वैदेहीम्  (for) Vaidehi, सीताम् Sita, आयत-लोचनाम् wide-eyed/with large eyes, सर्वतः in all directions/everywhere, परिचक्राम wandered around/roamed, हनूमान् Hanuman, अरि-सूदनः destroyer of enemies

Anvay: अरिसूदनः हनूमान् आयतलोचनां सीतां वैदेहीं मार्गमाणः तु सर्वतः परिचक्राम।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the destroyer of enemies, while searching for Sita, Vaidehi of wide eyes, wandered all around in every direction.

उत्तमं राक्षसावासं हनुमानवलोकयन्।
आससादाथ लक्ष्मीवान् राक्षसेन्द्रनिवेशनम्॥5.9.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: उत्तमम् excellent, राक्षस-आवासम् abode of the rakshasas, हनुमान् Hanuman, अवलोकयन् observing/surveying, आससाद approached/came near, अथ then, लक्ष्मीवान् the illustrious one, राक्षस-इन्द्र-निवेशनम् residence of the king of the rakshasas

Anvay: अथ लक्ष्मीवान् हनुमान् उत्तमं राक्षसावासम् अवलोकयन् राक्षसेन्द्रनिवेशनम् आससाद।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the illustrious one, while observing the excellent abode of the Rakshasas, then approached the residence of the king of the rakshasas.

चतुर्विषाणैर्द्विरदैस्त्रिविषाणैस्तथैव च।
परिक्षिप्तमसम्बाधं रक्ष्यमाणमुदायुधैः॥5.9.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: चतुः-विषाणैः with four-tusked (elephants), द्विरदैः by elephants, त्रि-विषाणैः with three-tusked (elephants), तथा एव​ च and likewise/similarly, परिक्षिप्तम् surrounded, असम्बाधम् spacious/not crowded, रक्ष्यमाणम् being guarded, उद्-आयुधैः by those armed with uplifted weapons

उद् upsarga always means rising up

Anvay: असम्बाधं (भवनं) चतुर्विषाणैः त्रिविषाणैः द्विरदैः परिक्षिप्तं तथा एव च उदायुधैः रक्ष्यमाणम् (आसीत्)।

Simple meaning: That spacious (palace) was surrounded by four-tusked and three-tusked elephants, and was being guarded by warriors with uplifted weapons.

राक्षसीभिश्च पत्नीभी रावणस्य निवेशनम्।
आहृताभिश्च विक्रम्य राजकन्याभिरावृतम्॥5.9.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: राक्षसीभिः च and by rakshasi women, पत्नीभिः by (his) wives, रावणस्य of Ravana, निवेशनम् residence, आहृताभिः च and by those (maidens) brought (by force), विक्रम्य  having overpowered/conquered, राज-कन्याभिः royal maidens (princesses), आवृतम् filled

Anvay: रावणस्य निवेशनं राक्षसीभिः पत्नीभिः च, विक्रम्य आहृताभिः राजकन्याभिः च आवृतम्।

Simple meaning: The residence of Ravana was surrounded by rakshasi women and his wives, as well as by royal maidens who had been brought there after being overpowered.

तन्नक्रमकराकीर्णं तिमिङ्गिलझषाकुलम्।
वायुवेगसमाधूतं पन्नगैरिव सागरम्॥5.9.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that (palace/residence), नक्र-मकर-आकीर्णम्  filled with alligators and crocodiles, तिमिङ्गिल-झष-आकुलम् crowded with whales and large fish, वायु-वेग-समाधूतम् stirred by the force of wind, पन्नगैः इव like with serpents, सागरम् (like) the ocean

Anvay: तत् (रावणस्य निवेशनं) नक्रमकराकीर्णं, तिमिङ्गिलझषाकुलं, वायुवेगसमाधूतं – पन्नगैः सागरम् इव – (आसीत्)।

Simple meaning: It looked like a sea infested with crocodiles and alligators, crowded with whales and other fishes, tossed by the force of winds and teeming with serpents.

या हि वैश्रवणे लक्ष्मीर्या चेन्द्रे हरिवाहने।
सा रावणगृहे सर्वा नित्यमेवानपायिनी॥5.9.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: या हि that which indeed, वैश्रवणे in (the palace of) Vaishravana (i.e., Kubera), लक्ष्मीः prosperity/splendour, या च and that which, इन्द्रे in Indra, हरिवाहने (another name for) in Indra, सा that (splendour), रावण-गृहे in the house of Ravana, सर्वा all (of it), नित्यम् एव always indeed, अनपायिनी un-departing/never fading

हरिवाहनः पुं. हरि-वाहनः - इन्द्र ; src Kosha, Ashtadhyayi

Anvay: हि या लक्ष्मीः वैश्रवणे, या च हरिवाहने इन्द्रे — सा सर्वा लक्ष्मीः रावणगृहे नित्यम् एव अनपायिनी (आसीत्)।

Simple meaning: The prosperity that was in Kubera's (palace), and that which was in Indra — all that splendour was always unfading in Ravana’s house.

या च राज्ञः कुबेरस्य यमस्य वरुणस्य च।
तादृशी तद्विशिष्टा वा ऋद्धी रक्षोगृहेष्विह॥5.9.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: या च and that which, राज्ञः of the king, कुबेरस्य (namely) Kubera, यमस्य of Yama, वरुणस्य च and of Varuna, तादृशी such (kind of), तत्-विशिष्टा वा or superior to that, ऋद्धिः prosperity/affluence, रक्षः-गृहेषु in the houses of the rakshasas, इह here (i.e., in Lanka)

Anvay: या (ऋद्धिः) राज्ञः कुबेरस्य, यमस्य, वरुणस्य च - तादृशी तद्विशिष्टा वा सा ऋद्धिः रक्षोगृहेषु इह (आसीत्)।

Simple meaning: The prosperity that is in houses of the king Kubera, Yama, and Varuna — that kind, or even greater — such affluence existed here in the houses of the rakshasas.

तस्य हर्म्यस्य मध्यस्थं वेश्म चान्यत् सुनिर्मितं।
बहुनिर्यूहसङ्कीर्णं ददर्श पवनात्मजः॥5.9.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य of that (building/palace), हर्म्यस्य of the lofty mansion, मध्य-स्थम् situated in the middle, वेश्म palace, building, च and, अन्यत् another, सुनिर्मितम् well-constructed, बहु-निर्यूह-सङ्कीर्णम् filled with many turrets, ददर्श (he) saw, पवन-आत्मजः the son of the Wind-God

Anvay: पवनात्मजः तस्य हर्म्यस्य मध्यस्थं सुनिर्मितं बहुनिर्यूहसङ्कीर्णम् अन्यत् वेश्म ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the son of the Wind-God, saw another well-constructed palace situated in the middle of that lofty mansion, crowded with many turrets.

ब्रह्मणोऽर्थे कृतं दिव्यं दिवि यद्विश्वकर्मणा।
विमानं पुष्पकं नाम सर्वरत्नविभूषितम्॥5.9.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्रह्मणः अर्थे for Brahma’s purpose, कृतम् made, दिव्यम् divine/celestial, दिवि in heaven, यत् which, विश्वकर्मणा by Vishvakarma (the celestial architect), विमानम् aerial chariot or palace, पुष्पकम् नाम named Pushpaka, सर्व-रत्न-विभूषितम् adorned with all kinds of gems

Anvay: यत् दिवि विश्वकर्मणा ब्रह्मणः अर्थे कृतं दिव्यं सर्वरत्नविभूषितं पुष्पकं नाम विमानम्।

Simple meaning: That divine aerial chariot, which was adorned with all kinds of gems, named Pushpaka, was made by Vishvakarma in heaven for Brahma’s purpose.

परेण तपसा लेभे यत्कुबेरः पितामहात्।
कुबेरमोजसा जित्वा लेभे तद्राक्षसेश्वरः॥5.9.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: परेण तपसा through intense (supreme) austerity, लेभे obtained, यत्  which, कुबेरः Kubera (the god of wealth), पितामहात् from Brahma (the grandfather of all), कुबेरम् Kubera, ओजसा by (his) power/strength, जित्वा having conquered, लेभे obtained, तत् that (same thing), राक्षस-ईश्वरः the lord of the rakshasas (Ravana)

Anvay: यत् कुबेरः परेण तपसा पितामहात् लेभे, तत् राक्षसेश्वरः कुबेरम् ओजसा जित्वा लेभे।

Simple meaning: That which Kubera had obtained from Brahma through supreme austerity, Ravana, the lord of the rakshasas, obtained by overpowering Kubera.

ईहामृगसमायुक्त्तैः कार्तस्वरहिरण्मयैः।
सुकृतैराचितं स्तम्भैः प्रदीप्तमिव च श्रिया॥5.9.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: ईहामृग-समायुक्तैः adorned with carvings of wolves, कार्तस्वर-हिरण्मयैः made of refined gold, सुकृतैः well-crafted/skilfully made, आचितम् studded/filled/arranged, स्तम्भैः with pillars, प्रदीप्तम् shining/glowing, इव as if, श्रिया with splendor/beauty

Anvay: ईहामृगसमायुक्तैः कार्तस्वरहरिणमयैः सुकृतैः स्तम्भैः आचितं – श्रिया प्रदीप्तम् इव – च।

Simple meaning: It was studded with skillfully made pillars of refined gold, and adorned with carvings of wolves, shining as though glowing with splendor.

मेरुमन्दरसङ्काशैरुल्लिखद्भिरिवाम्बरं।
कूटागारैश्शुभाकारैस्सर्वतस्समलङ्कृतम्॥5.9.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: मेरु-मन्दर-सङ्काशैः resembling Mount Meru and Mount Mandara (i.e., extremely lofty and splendid), उल्लिखद्भिः as if scraping or touching, इव as though, अम्बरम् the sky, कूट-अगारैः with multi-storied mansions or lofty domed buildings, शुभ-आकारैः of auspicious and beautiful forms, सर्वतः all around, समलङ्कृतम् beautifully decorated

कूटागार  कूटागारम् नपुं. कूट-अगारम् - छत पर बनी कोठरी src. Kosha.Ashtadhyayi

Anvay: मेरुमन्दरसङ्काशैः शुभाकारैः कूटागारैः – अम्बरम् उल्लिखद्भिः इव – सर्वतः समलङ्कृतम्।

Simple meaning: (It was) beautifully adorned on all sides with auspiciously shaped multi-storied mansions, huge like Mount Meru and Mount Mandara and seemed as if they were scraping the sky.

ज्वलनार्कप्रतीकाशं सुकृतं विश्वकर्मणा।
हेमसोपानसंयुक्तं चारुप्रवरवेदिकम्॥5.9.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: ज्वलन-अर्क-प्रतीकाशम् resembling fire (ज्वलन) and the sun (अर्क) in brilliance, सुकृतम् well-constructed, finely made, विश्वकर्मणा by Vishvakarma (the divine architect), हेम-सोपान-संयुक्तम् equipped with golden stairways, चारु-प्रवर-वेदिकम् having beautiful and excellent altars/platforms

Anvay: विश्वकर्मणा सुकृतं ज्वलनार्कप्रतीकाशं हेमसोपानसंयुक्तं चारुप्रवरवेदिकम्।

Simple meaning: (It was) finely built by Vishvakarma, resembling fire and the sun in its brilliance, equipped with golden stairways, and had beautiful and excellent platforms.

जालवातायनैर्युक्तं काञ्चनैस्स्फाटिकैरपि।
इन्द्रनीलमहानीलमणिप्रवरवेदिकम्॥5.9.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: जाल-वातायनैः with latticed windows, युक्तम् endowed/furnished, काञ्चनैः (made of) gold, स्फाटिकैः अपि and also with crystal (transparent stone), इन्द्रनील-महानील-मणि-प्रवर-वेदिकम् having excellent platforms studded with Indranila (blue sapphire) and Mahanila (great dark blue stones)

Anvay: काञ्चनैः स्फाटिकैः अपि जालवातायनैः युक्तम् इन्द्रनीलमहानीलमणिप्रवरवेदिकम्।

Simple meaning: (It was) decorated with latticed windows made of gold and also crystal, and had excellent platforms studded with Indranila and Mahanila gems.

विद्रुमेण विचित्रेण मणिभिश्च महाघनैः।
निस्तुलाभिश्च मुक्ताभिस्तलेनाभिविराजितम्॥5.9.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: विद्रुमेण with coral, विचित्रेण variegated (beautifully decorated), मणिभिः च and with gems, महा-घनैः huge and dense (valuable), निस्तुलाभिः च and with incomparable (peerless), मुक्ताभिः pearls, तलेन by the floor (surface), अभिविराजितम् brilliantly adorned/resplendent

Anvay: विचित्रेण विद्रुमेण महाघनैः मणिभिः च निस्तुलाभिः मुक्ताभिः तलेन अभिविराजितम्।

Simple meaning: (It was) resplendent with a floor decorated with variegated coral, great dense gems, and incomparable pearls.

चन्दनेन च रक्तेन तपनीयनिभेन च।
सुपुण्यगन्धिना युक्तमादित्यतरुणोपमम्॥5.9.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: चन्दनेन with sandalwood, च and, रक्तेन red-colored, तपनीय-निभेन resembling gold (tapaniya - gold; nibha - appearance), च and, सु-पुण्य-गन्धिना with excellent sacred fragrance, युक्तम् endowed (infused), आदित्य-तरुण-उपमम् resembling the young (rising) sun

Anvay: रक्तेन तपनीयनिभेन च सुपुण्यगन्धिना आदित्यतरुणोपमं चन्दनेन युक्तम्।

Simple meaning: (That palace was) infused with the red sandalwood resembling gold and endowed with a most sacred fragrance, appearing like the young rising sun.

कूटागारैर्वराकारैर्विविधैस्समलङ्कृतम्।
विमानं पुष्पकं दिव्यमारुरोह महाकपिः॥5.9.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: कूट-आगारैः with turrets (lofty, decorated upper chambers), वर-आकारैः having excellent forms (beautiful shapes), विविधैः of various kinds, समलङ्कृतम् well adorned/richly decorated, विमानम् aerial chariot (flying palace), पुष्पकम् Pushpaka (name of the divine chariot), दिव्यम् divine, आरुरोह ascended/climbed, महा-कपिः the great monkey

Anvay: महाकपिः वराकारैः कूटागारैः समलङ्कृतं दिव्यं पुष्पकं विमानम् आरुरोह।

Simple meaning: The great monkey ascended the divine Pushpaka aerial chariot, which was adorned with various lofty and excellent chambers.

तत्रस्थस्स तदा गन्धं पानभक्ष्यान्नसम्भवम्।
दिव्यं सम्मूर्छितं जिघ्रद्रूपवन्तमिवानिलम्॥5.9.20॥

Simple meaning: “The intoxicated bees would again and again long to enjoy these beautiful faces as they would want to enjoy the blooming lotuses”, thus the glorious Hanuman considered with reasoning.

सा तस्य शुशुभे शाला ताभिः स्त्रीभिर्विराजिता।

Simple meaning: That hall of his (Ravana’s), adorned with those women, shone beautifully—like the clear autumn sky splendidly decorated with stars.

Simple meaning: Surrounded by those women, the lord of the rakshasas, shone resplendently —just like the radiant moon appears when surrounded by stars.

याश्च्यवन्तेऽम्बरात् ताराः पुण्यशेषसमावृताः।

Simple meaning: At that time, Hanuman thought, “Those stars that fall from the sky, surrounded by traces of accumulated merit — these must all have gathered here (as these women).”

ताराणामिव सुव्यक्तं महतीनां शुभार्चिषाम्।
प्रभावर्णप्रसादाश्च विरेजुस्तत्र योषिताम्॥5.9.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताराणाम् of stars, इव like, सुव्यक्तम् clearly visible/distinct, महतीनाम् of the great, शुभ-अर्चिषाम् of those with auspicious radiance, प्रभा-वर्ण-प्रसादाः brilliance, color, and clarity (splendor, hue, and charm), च and, विरेजुः shone/radiated, तत्र there, योषिताम् of the women

Simple meaning: There, the brilliance, color, and charm of the women shone clearly—just like that of great, radiant stars.

व्यावृत्तगुरुपीनस्रक्प्रकीर्णवरभूषणाः।
पानव्यायामकालेषु निद्रापहृतचेतसः॥5.9.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: व्यावृत्त-गुरु-पीन-स्रक्-प्रकीर्ण-वर-भूषणाः their heavy, full garlands fallen aside and with their excellent ornaments scattered, पान-व्यायाम-कालेषु during the times of drinking and physical activity(sport/play), निद्रा-अपहृत-चेतसः their minds taken away (overcome) by sleep

Anvay: पानव्यायामकालेषु व्यावृत्तगुरुपीनस्रक्प्रकीर्णवरभूषणाः निद्रापहृतचेतसः।

Simple meaning: Overcome by sleep during the hours of drinking and sport, their heavy, full garlands had slipped off and their fine ornaments lay scattered.

व्यावृत्ततिलकाः काश्चित् काश्चिदुद्भ्रन्तनूपुराः।

Word-by-word meaning: व्यावृत्त-तिलकाः काश्चित् some women with their tilakas (forehead marks) smeared or displaced, काश्चित् उद्भ्रान्त-नूपुराः others with ankle-bells dislodged, पार्श्वे to the side (near them), गलित-हाराः with necklaces slipped off, च and, काश्चित् some, परम-योषितः exceptionally beautiful women

Simple meaning: Some of the exceptionally beautiful women had their tilakas smeared, some had their anklets dislodged, and some had their necklaces fallen to their sides.

मुक्ताहाराऽवृताश्चान्याः काश्चिद्विस्रस्तवाससः।
व्याविद्धरशनादामाः किशोर्य इव वाहिताः॥5.9.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: मुक्ता-हार-आवृताः covered with pearl necklaces, च and, अन्याः other (women), काश्चित् some, विस्रस्त-वाससः with garments loosened or slipping off, व्याविद्ध-रशना-दमाः with displaced waistbands and girdles, किशोर्यः young mares, इव like, वाहिताः been made to carry load

सुकुण्डलधराश्चान्या विच्छिन्नमृदितस्रजः।
गजेन्द्रमृदिताः फुल्ला लता इव महावने॥5.9.48॥

Simple meaning: Other women, wearing lovely earrings and with their garlands torn and crushed, looked like blooming creepers that had been trampled by a mighty elephant in the thick of the forest.

चन्द्रांशुकिरणाभाश्च हाराः कासांचिदुत्कटाः।
हंसा इव बभुस्सुप्ताः स्तनमध्येषु योषिताम्॥5.9.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: चन्द्र-अंशु-किरण-आभाः च​ shining like the rays of the moon, हाराः necklaces, कासांचित् of some women, उत्कटाः extraordinary, हंसाः इव like swans, बभुः appeared, सुप्ताः asleep, स्तन-मध्येषु in the middle of the breasts, योषिताम् of the women

Simple meaning: The extraordinary beautiful necklaces which were shining like moonbeams, in between the women’s breasts looked like sleeping swans.

अपरासां च वैडूर्याः कादम्बा इव पक्षिणः।
हेमसूत्राणि चान्यासां चक्रवाका इवाभवन्॥5.9.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: अपरासाम् of other women, च and, वैडूर्याः cat’s eye gems (vaidurya stones), कादम्बाः इव पक्षिणः like kadamba birds, हेम-सूत्राणि golden chains (threads of gold), च and, अन्यासाम् of some other women, चक्रवाकाः इव like chakravaka birds (ruddy geese), अभवन् appeared/looked like

Simple meaning: For some women, the cat's-eye gems worn appeared like kadamba birds, while for others, their golden chains looked like chakravaka birds resting upon them.

हंसकारण्डवाकीर्णाश्चक्रवाकोपशोभिताः।
आपगा इव ता रेजुर्जघनैः पुलिनैरिव॥5.9.51॥

Word-by-word meaning: हंस-कारण्डव-आकीर्णाः filled with swans and karandava birds, चक्रवाक-उपशोभिताः adorned with chakravaka birds, आपगाः इव like rivers, ताः (योषितः) those (women), रेजुः shone/appeared, जघनैः with their hips, पुलिनैः इव like sandbanks or riverbanks

Simple meaning: The women shone like beautiful rivers filled with swans, karandava birds, and adorned by chakravaka birds, and their hips resembling the sandbanks of the river.

Note: Because they were wearing ornaments which appeared like the swans, chakravaka birds and kadamba birds, the women appeared beautiful like rivers filled with these birds and their hips are compared to the sandy banks.

किङ्किणीजालसङ्कोशास्ता हैमविपुलाम्बुजाः।
भावग्राहा यशस्तीराः सुप्ता नद्य इवाऽऽबभुः॥5.9.52॥

Word-by-word meaning: किङ्किणी-जाल-सङ्कोशाः enclosed by networks of tiny tinkling bells, ताः those (women), हैम-विपुल-अम्बुजाः with large golden ornaments for lotuses, भाव-ग्राहाः gestures resembling crocodiles, यशः-तीराः with splendor for their banks (metaphorically: whose splendor formed their banks), सुप्ताः sleeping, नद्यः rivers, इव like, आबभुः appeared/looked like

Simple meaning: Those sleeping women, with large golden ornaments as lotuses, encircled by nets of jingling bells, with their gestures as crocodiles and fame as the banks, appeared like rivers.

मृदुष्वङ्गेषु कासाञ्चित् कुचाग्रेषु च संस्थिताः।
बभूवुर्भूषणानीव शुभा भूषणराजयः॥5.9.53॥

Word-by-word meaning: मृदुषु अङ्गेषु on the soft limbs (of the women), कासाञ्चित् of some (of the women), कुच-अग्रेषु च and on the tips of their breasts, संस्थिताः resting/lying/placed, बभूवुः appeared, भूषणानि इव like ornaments, शुभाः beautiful, भूषण-राजयः rows of ornaments (jewelry)

अंशुकान्ताश्च कासाञ्चित् मुखमारुतकम्पिताः।

ताः पताका इवोद्धूताः पत्नीनां रुचिरप्रभाः।

शर्कराऽसवगन्धैश्च प्रकृत्या सुरभिः सुखः।

रावणाननशङ्काश्च काश्चिद्रावणयोषितः।
मुखानि स्म सपत्नीनामुपाजिघ्रन् पुनः पुनः॥5.9.58॥

Word-by-word meaning: रावण-आनन-शङ्काः suspecting that it was Ravana’s face, च and, काश्चित् some (of the women), रावण-योषितः Ravana’s wives, मुखानि the faces, स्म indeed (a particle indicating emphasis, often used with past tense), सपत्नीनाम् of their co-wives/rival wives, उपाजिघ्रन् smelled closely/drew near and sniffed, पुनः पुनः again and again

Anvay: काश्चित् रावणयोषितः रावणाननशङ्काः च पुनः पुनः सपत्नीनां मुखानि उपाजिघ्रन् स्म।

Simple meaning: Some of Ravana’s wives, mistaking the faces of their co-wives to be that of Ravana himself, repeatedly leaned forward in their sleep to smell them.

अत्यर्थं सक्तमनसो रावणे ता वरस्त्रियः।
अस्वतन्त्राः सपत्नीनां प्रियमेवाऽचरंस्तदा॥5.9.59॥

Word-by-word meaning: अत्यर्थम् excessively/intensely, सक्त-मनसः their minds being attached/devoted, रावणे to Ravana, ताः those, वर-स्त्रियः noble women/excellent ladies (his chief consorts), अस्वतन्त्राः not independent/lacking self-control (here, emotionally bound), सपत्नीनाम् of co-wives/rival wives, प्रियम् एव acts of affection alone/only loving gestures, अचरन् performed/did, तदा at that time

Anvay: ताः वरस्त्रियः रावणे अत्यर्थं सक्तमनसः अस्वतन्त्राः तदा सपत्नीनां प्रियम् एव अचरन्।

Simple meaning: Those noble women, whose minds were deeply attached to Ravana, in their sleep acted without independence and, at that time, showed only affectionate gestures even toward their co-wives— gestures that would normally be restrained or competitive in waking life.

बाहूनुपनिधायान्याः पारिहार्यविभूषितान्।
अंशुकानि च रम्याणि प्रमदास्तत्र शिश्यिरे॥5.9.60॥

Word-by-word meaning: बाहून् arms, उपनिधाय having placed under (as a pillow or support), अन्याः other women, पारिहार्य-विभूषितान् adorned with bracelets, अंशुकानि garments/upper cloths, च and, रम्याणि beautiful/pleasing, प्रमदाः women/charming ladies, तत्र  there, शिश्यिरे slept/lay down

पारिहार्य  -  पारिहार्यः A bracelet.src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

Anvay: अन्याः प्रमदाः पारिहार्यविभूषितान् बाहून् रम्याणि अंशुकानि च उपनिधाय तत्र शिश्यिरे।

Simple meaning: Some other women slept there, making pillows of their arms which were decorated with bracelets and beautiful clothes.

अन्या वक्षसि चान्यस्यास्तस्याः काचित् पुनर्भुजम्।
अपरा त्वङ्कमन्यस्यास्तस्याश्चाप्यपरा भुजौ॥5.9.61॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्या one woman, वक्षसि on the chest, च and, अन्यस्याः of another woman, तस्याः of that one (woman), काचित् another (woman), पुनः again, भुजम् (another's) arm, अपरा yet another, तु अङ्कम् lap, अन्यस्याः of another woman, तस्याः च अपि and of that woman also, अपरा another one, भुजौ both arms

Anvay: काचित् अन्या अन्यस्याः वक्षसि (शेते), अपरा तस्याः भुजं पुनः (शेते), अपरा अन्यस्याः तु अङ्कं (शेते), अपरा तस्याः च अपि भुजौ (शेते)।

Simple meaning: One woman was lying on the chest of another; yet another rested on her arm. Another lay on the lap of a different woman, and yet another on the arms of that woman too.

ऊरुपार्श्वकटीपृष्ठमन्योन्यस्य समाश्रिताः।
परस्परनिविष्टाङ्ग्यो मदस्नेहवशानुगाः॥5.9.62॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऊरु-पार्श्व-कटी-पृष्ठम् on the thighs, sides, waists, backs, अन्योन्यस्य of each other, समाश्रिताः resting upon/leaning against, परस्पर-निविष्ट-अङ्ग्यः mutually, with limbs intertwined/their bodies placed upon one another, मद-स्नेह-वश-अनुगाः moved by/subject to the influence of intoxication (here, from sleep, pleasure, or drink) and affection/attachment

Anvay: अन्योन्यस्य ऊरुपार्श्वकटीपृष्ठं समाश्रिताः, परस्परनिविष्टाङ्ग्यः, मदस्नेहवशानुगाः (बभूवुः)।

Simple meaning: Leaning on each other’s thighs, sides, waists, and backs, with their limbs closely intertwined, the women lay together, overcome by intoxication and affection.

अन्योन्यभुजसूत्रेण स्त्रीमाला ग्रथिता हि सा।
मालेव ग्रथिता सूत्रे शुशुभे मत्तषट्पदा॥5.9.63॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्योन्य-भुज-सूत्रेण by the string of each other’s arms (i.e., arms acting as the thread), स्त्री-माला garland of women, ग्रथिता strung/woven, हि indeed, सा that (group of women), माला इव like a garland, ग्रथिता सूत्रे strung on a thread, शुशुभे shone/appeared beautiful, मत्त-षट्पदा  filled with intoxicated bees (buzzing around)

Anvay: सा स्त्रीमाला अन्योन्यभुजसूत्रेण ग्रथिता हि – सूत्रे ग्रथिता मत्तषट्पदा माला इव – शुशुभे।

Simple meaning: Those women, holding each other by arms, shone beautifully like a garland strung on a thread and swarmed by intoxicated bees.

लतानां माधवे मासि फुल्लानां वायुसेवनात्।
अन्योन्य मालाग्रथितं संसक्तकुसुमोच्चयम्॥5.9.64॥

Word-by-word meaning: लतानाम् of creepers/vines, माधवे मासि in the month of Madhava (spring), फुल्लानाम्  fully bloomed, वायु-सेवनात्  due to the touch/blowing of wind, अन्योन्य-माला-ग्रथितम् entwined with each other like a garland, संसक्त-कुसुम-उच्चयम् closely connected/clinging, clusters of flowers

Anvay: माधवे मासि वायुसेवनात् फुल्लानां लतानाम् अन्योन्यमालाग्रथितं संसक्तकुसुमोच्चयम् …

Simple meaning: In the month of Madhava (spring), the fully bloomed creepers, stirred by the wind, become entwined with one another — their flower clusters closely mingled, forming garland-like shapes…

व्यतिवेष्टितसुस्कन्धमन्योन्यभ्रमराकुलम्।
आसीद्वनमिवोद्धूतं स्त्रीवनं रावणस्य तत्॥5.9.65॥

Word-by-word meaning: व्यतिवेष्टित-सु-स्कन्धम् with beautiful branches (arms) entwined with each other (व्यतिवेष्टित - entwined सु-स्कन्धम् - having beautiful shoulders/arms like branches), अन्योन्य-भ्रमर-आकुलम् locks of hair or ornaments intertwined with each other appeared like bees swarming over one another (अन्योन्य - with one another, भ्रमर - bees, आकुलम् - teeming/swarming/crowded), आसीत् appeared/was, वनम् इव उद्धूतम्  like a forest swayed/stirred by the wind, स्त्री-वनम् the grove (or collection) of women, रावणस्य तत्  that of Ravana

Anvay: …तत् रावणस्य व्यतिवेष्टितसुस्कन्धं अन्योन्यभ्रमराकुलं स्त्रीवनम् – उद्धूतं वनम् इव – आसीत्।

Simple meaning: …that grove of women belonging to Ravana, with their beautiful arms, locks of hair or ornaments intertwined with each other appeared like bees swarming over one another and looked like a wind-stirred forest.

उचितेष्वपि सुव्यक्तं न तासां योषितां तदा।
विवेकः शक्य आधातुं भूषणाङ्गाम्बरस्रजाम्॥5.9.66॥

Word-by-word meaning: उचितेषु अपि even though they were in their proper places, सु-व्यक्तम् clearly, distinctly, न  not, तासाम् योषिताम् of those women, तदा at that time, विवेकः distinction/separation/discrimination, शक्यः was possible, आधातुम् to establish/to determine, भूषण-अङ्ग-अम्बर-स्रजाम् between ornaments, limbs, garments, and garlands

Anvay: तदा तासां भूषणाङ्गाम्बरस्रजां योषिताम् उचितेषु (स्थितेषु) अपि सुव्यक्तं विवेकः आधातुं शक्यः न (आसीत्)।

Simple meaning: At that time, even though the ornaments, limbs, clothes, and garlands of the women were in their proper places, it was not clearly possible to distinguish one from the other.

रावणे सुखसंविष्टे तास्त्रियो विविधप्रभाः।
ज्वलन्तः काञ्चना दीपाः प्रैक्षन्तानिमिषा इव॥5.9.67॥

Word-by-word meaning: रावणे while Ravana, सुख-संविष्टे lay down comfortably/was resting happily, ताः स्त्रियः those women, विविध-प्रभाः of varied splendour/complexion, ज्वलन्तः shining, glowing, काञ्चनाः दीपाः golden lamps, प्रैक्षन्त appeared (as if) they were watching, अनिमिषाः इव like beings that do not blink (unblinking), or with unblinking gaze

Anvay: रावणे सुखसंविष्टे, ताः विविधप्रभाः स्त्रियः ज्वलन्तः काञ्चनाः दीपाः अनिमिषाः इव प्रैक्षन्त।

Simple meaning: As Ravana lay resting comfortably, those radiant women were being gazed upon by golden lamps, which glowed steadily as if watching them with unblinking eyes.

राजर्षिपितृदैत्यानां गन्धर्वाणां च योषितः।
राक्षसानां च याः कन्यास्तस्य कामवशं गताः॥5.9.68॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजर्षि-पितृ-दैत्यानाम् of the royal sages, ancestors (manes) and daityas, गन्धर्वाणाम् of the gandharvas (celestial musicians), च and, योषितः women, राक्षसानाम् of the rakshasas, च and, याः कन्याः the maidens who, तस्य of him (Ravana), काम-वशम् गताः had come under the sway of lust/were dominated by desire

Anvay: राजर्षिपितृदैत्यानां गन्धर्वाणां राक्षसानां याः योषितः कन्याः च तस्य कामवशं गताः।

Simple meaning: The daughters (maidens) of royal sages, ancestors, daityas, gandharvas, and rakshasas — all those women had fallen under the sway of Ravana’s lust.

युद्धकामेन ताः सर्वा रावणेन हृताः स्त्रियः।
समदा मदनेनैव मोहिताः काश्चिदागताः॥5.9.69॥

Word-by-word meaning: युद्ध-कामेन desirous of war (combat), ताः सर्वाः all those, रावणेन by Ravana, हृताः were abducted, स्त्रियः women, समदाः intoxicated (completely overcome), मदनेन एव  by passion itself (i.e., love/lust god – Kama), मोहिताः deluded/charmed, काश्चित् some (women), आगताः came (willingly)

Anvay: ताः सर्वाः स्त्रियः युद्धकामेन रावणेन हृताः। काश्चित् मदनेन समदाः मोहिताः एव आगताः।

 Simple meaning: All those women were abducted by Ravana, who was eager for conquest. Some of them, however, came to him willingly, completely intoxicated and deluded by passion.

न तत्र काचित् प्रमदा प्रसह्य वीर्योपपन्नेन गुणेन लब्धा।
न चान्यकामापि न चान्यपूर्वा विना वरार्हां जनकात्मजां ताम्॥5.9.70॥

Word-by-word meaning: न तत्र काचित् प्रमदा not a single woman there, प्रसह्य by force, वीर्य-उपपन्नेन endowed with valor (or strength), गुणेन by virtue/qualities, लब्धा was won (acquired), न च अन्य-कामा अपि nor was there any woman who belonged to another (already in love with someone else), न च अन्य-पूर्वा nor any who had been with another man previously, विना except, वर-अर्हाम् worthy of being revered, जनक-आत्मजाम् ताम् that daughter of Janaka (Sita)

Anvay: वरार्हां तां जनकात्मजां विना तत्र काचित् प्रमदा प्रसह्य न, वीर्योपपन्नेन गुणेन वा लब्धा, च अन्यकामा अपि न, च अन्यपूर्वा न।

Simple meaning: Except Sita no other woman among them had been taken there by force. They were won only on account of his valour and virtues. None loved any one earlier and none was married to others.

न चाकुलीना न च हीनरूपा नादक्षिणा नानुपचारयुक्ता।
भार्याभवत् तस्य न हीनसत्त्वा न चापि कान्तस्य न कामनीया॥5.9.71॥

Word-by-word meaning: न च अ-कुलीना not of inferior lineage, न च हीन-रूपा not lacking in beauty, न अ-दक्षिणा not lacking in graciousness or charm, न अनुपचार-युक्ता not devoid of proper attentiveness or courtesy, भार्या अभवत् तस्य became his (Ravana’s) wife, न हीन-सत्त्वा not low in spirit or character, न च अपि nor even, कान्तस्य to the lover (Ravana), न कामनीया not desirable

Anvay: तस्य भार्या अकुलीना न, हीनरूपा न, अदक्षिणा न, अनुपचारयुक्ता न, हीनसत्त्वा न, च कान्तस्य अकामनीया न अभवत्।

Simple meaning: None of his wives was of inferior birth, or lacking in beauty, grace, or courtesy. None of them were low in character, nor was there a single one who was not desirable or beloved to him.

बभूव बुद्धिस्तु हरीश्वरस्य यदीदृशी राघवधर्मपत्नी।
इमा यथा राक्षसराजभार्याः सुजातमस्येति हि साधुबुद्धेः॥5.9.72॥

Word-by-word meaning: बभूव बुद्धिः तु  but the thought arose, हरि-ईश्वरस्य  in the lord of the monkeys (Hanuman), यदि ईदृशी if in such a way, राघव-धर्म-पत्नी (if) the virtuous wife of Rama (had been left happy with her husband), इमाः यथा राक्षस-राज-भार्याः like these the wives of the king of the rakshasas (Ravana), सु-जातम् अस्य the birth of Ravana would have been blessed, इति thus, हि साधु-बुद्धेः of the one with virtuous understanding

Anvay: इमाः राक्षसराजभार्याः यथा राघवधर्मपत्नी ईदृशी, अस्य (रावणस्य​) सुजातम् (भवेत्) –  इति साधुबुद्धेः हरीश्वरस्य, बुद्धिः तु बभूव।

Simple meaning: Following thought occurred to Hanuman with a pious mind: "The birth of Ravana would have been blessed if the virtuous wife of Rama had been left happy with her husband in the same such manner as these wives of the king of rakshasas."

पुनश्च सोऽचिन्तयादात्तरूपो ध्रुवं विशिष्टा गुणतो हि सीता।
अथायमस्यां कृतवान् म​हात्मा लङ्केश्वरः कष्टमनार्यकर्म॥5.9.73॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुनः च and again, सः he (Hanuman), अचिन्तयत् thought, आत्त-रूपः who had assumed a disguised form (Hanuman, in his tiny form), ध्रुवम्  surely, विशिष्टा is exceptional, गुणतः in terms of virtues, हि indeed, सीता Sita, अथ then, अयम् this (Ravana), अस्याम् towards her, कृतवान् has done, महा-आत्मा this great being, लङ्का-ईश्वरः the king of Lanka, कष्टम् a cruel act, अनार्य-कर्म an unrighteous deed

Anvay: सः आत्तरूपः पुनः च अचिन्तयत् — ध्रुवं सीता गुणतः विशिष्टा (इति)। अथ अयं महात्मा लङ्केश्वरः अस्यां (सीतायां) कष्टम् अनार्यकर्म कृतवान्।

Simple meaning: Again, Hanuman, who had assumed a small form, reflected: “Sita is certainly outstanding in virtue. Then this king of Lanka, though great in power, has committed a cruel and unrighteous act against her.”

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे नवमः सर्गः ॥9॥

Thus ends the ninth sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
