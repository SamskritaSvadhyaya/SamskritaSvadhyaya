The message of Kapila

दशकम् १५

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 15

The message of Kapila

(छन्दस् - मालिनी)

मतिरिह गुणसक्ता बन्धकृत्तेष्वसक्ता
त्वमृतकृदुपरुन्धे भक्तियोगस्तु सक्तिम् ।
महदनुगमलभ्या भक्तिरेवात्र साध्या
कपिलतनुरिति त्वं देवहूत्यै न्यगादीः ॥ 15.1 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: मतिः mind, इह in this world, गुण-सक्ता attached to the gunas/worldly objects, बन्ध-कृत् causes bondage, तेषु असक्ता detached from them, तु while, अमृत-कृत् leads to liberation, उपरुन्धे restrains/destroys, भक्ति-योगः तु while devotion/path of devotion, सक्तिम् this worldly attachment, महत्-अनुगम-लभ्या obtainable through the guidance/company of great souls, भक्तिः devotion, एव alone, अत्र in this path, साध्या the ultimate goal to be sought, कपिल-तनुः  incarnating as Kapila, इति thus, त्वम् You, देवहूत्यै unto Devahuti, न्यगादीः taught/proclaimed

Simple Meaning: Taking incarnation in the form of Kapila, You taught Devahuti that a mind attached to the gunas and worldly objects causes bondage, whereas a mind detached from them leads to liberation. You explained that devotional service (bhakti-yoga) destroys these attachments, that true devotion is attained through the association of great souls, and that devotion itself is the ultimate goal to be achieved.

प्रकृतिमहदहङ्काराश्च मात्राश्च भूतानि
अपि हृदपि दशाक्षी पूरुषः पञ्चविंशः ।
इति विदितविभागो मुच्यतेऽसौ प्रकृत्या
कपिलतनुरिति त्वं देवहूत्यै न्यगादीः ॥ 15.2 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रकृति-महत्-अहङ्काराः Prakriti (primordial nature), Mahat (cosmic intellect), and Ahankara (ego), च and, मात्राः the subtle elements (tanmatras), च and, भूतानि the gross elements (pancha mahabhutas), अपि also, हृत् the mind (internal organ/antahkarana), अपि also, दश-अक्षी the ten sense organs (five organs of perception and five organs of action), पूरुषः Purusha (the supreme soul), पञ्च-विंशः the twenty-fifth principle, इति thus, विदित-विभागः one who clearly understands this discrimination/classification, मुच्यते is liberated, असौ that person, प्रकृत्या from Prakriti/Maya, कपिल-तनुः incarnated as Kapila, इति thus, त्वम् You, देवहूत्यै unto Devahuti, न्यगादीः taught/proclaimed

Simple Meaning: Taking incarnation in the form of Kapila, You taught Devahuti that the creation consists of twenty-five principles—Prakriti, Mahat, Ahankara, the five subtle elements (sound, smell, touch, form, taste), the five gross elements (space, air, fire, water, earth), the mind, the ten sense organs (five organs of knowledge - hearing, seeing, touch, taste, smell, and five organs of action - speech, hands, legs, anus, genitals), and Purusha as the twenty-fifth principle. You proclaimed that anyone who clearly understands this distinction becomes liberated from the bondages of Prakriti.

प्रकृतिगतगुणौघैर्नाज्यते पूरुषोऽयं
यदि तु सजति तस्यां तत् गुणास्तं भजेरन् ।
मदनुभजनतत्त्वालोचनैः साऽप्यपेयात्
कपिलतनुरिति त्वं देवहूत्यै न्यगादीः ॥ 15. 3 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रकृति-गत-गुण-ओघैः by the multitude of qualities (gunas) belonging to Prakriti, न आश्यते is not affected/tainted, पूरुषः अयम् this Purusha (the Soul), यदि if, तु however, सजति he becomes attached, तस्याम् to that Prakriti, तत् then, गुणाः the gunas, तम् him, भजेरन् bind/affect, मत्-अनुभजन-तत्त्व-आलोचनैः through devotional worship of Me and inquiry into the ultimate truth, सा अपि even that Prakriti/Maya, अपेयात् disappears/moves away, कपिल-तनुः incarnated as Kapila, इति thus, त्वम् You, देवहूत्यै unto Devahuti, न्यगादीः taught/proclaimed

Simple Meaning: Taking incarnation in the form of Kapila, You taught Devahuti that the Purusha (Soul) is inherently untainted by the attributes and modes of Prakriti. However, if the Soul attaches itself to Prakriti, the gunas bind it to worldly existence. You proclaimed that by engaging in Your devotional service and contemplating the ultimate Truth, even this Prakriti recedes and disappears, bestowing complete liberation.

विमलमतिरुपात्तैरासनाद्यैर्मदङ्गं
गरुडसमधिरूढं दिव्यभूषायुधाङ्कम् ।
रुचितुलिततमालं शीलयेतानुवेलं
कपिलतनुरिति त्वं देवहूत्यै न्यगादीः ॥ 15.4 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: विमल-मतिः one with a pure mind, उपात्तैः by practicing, आसन-आद्यैः yogic postures and other disciplines (pranayama, dharana), मत्-अङ्गम् My divine form, गरुड-समधिरूढम् seated comfortably upon Garuda, दिव्य-भूषा-आयुध-अङ्कम् adorned with divine ornaments, weapons, and sacred marks, रुचि-तुलित-तमालम् whose beauty rivals the dark Tamala tree, शीलयेत-अनुवेलम् should continuously meditate upon, कपिल-तनुः incarnated as Kapila, इति thus, त्वम् You, देवहूत्यै unto Devahuti, न्यगादीः taught/proclaimed

Simple Meaning: Taking incarnation in the form of Kapila, You taught Devahuti that a seeker with a pure mind should practice yogic postures and other disciplines, and continuously meditate on Your divine form. You instructed her to visualize You seated majestically upon Garuda, adorned with celestial ornaments and weapons, and radiating a glorious dark complexioned beauty that rivals the blooming Tamala tree.

मम गुणगणलीलाकर्णनैः कीर्तनाद्यैः
मयि सुरसरिदोघप्रख्यचित्तानुवृत्तिः ।
भवति परमभक्तिः सा हि मृत्योर्विजेत्री
कपिलतनुरिति त्वं देवहूत्यै न्यगादीः ॥ 15.5 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: मम My, गुण-गण-लीला-कर्णनैः by listening to the host of My virtues and divine sports, कीर्तन-आद्यैः through singing My glories and other devotional practices, मयि towards Me, सुर-सरित्-ओघ-प्रख्य-चित्त-अनुवृत्तिः a continuous flow of the mind like the mighty current of the river Ganges, भवति arises/becomes, परम-भक्तिः supreme devotion, सा that devotion, हि indeed, मृत्योः विजेत्री conqueror of death, कपिल-तनुः incarnated as Kapila, इति thus, त्वम् You, देवहूत्यै unto Devahuti, न्यगादीः taught/proclaimed

Simple Meaning: Taking incarnation in the form of Kapila, You taught Devahuti that by constantly listening to and singing the glorious virtues and divine exploits of Mine, a seeker develops an uninterrupted flow of mind towards Me, just like the steady stream of the river Ganges flowing towards the ocean. You proclaimed that this supreme form of devotion alone conquers death and leads to final liberation.

अहह बहुलहिंसासञ्चितार्थैः कुटुम्बं
प्रतिदिनमनुपुष्णन् स्त्रीजितो बाललाली ।
विशति हि गृहसक्तो यातनां मय्यभक्तः
कपिलतनुरितित्वं देवहूत्यै न्यगादीः ॥ 15.6 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: अहह alas, बहुल-हिंसा-सञ्चित-अर्थैः with wealth accumulated through much violence/harm, कुटुम्बम् family, प्रतिदिनम् every day, अनुपुष्णन् maintaining/nourishing, स्त्री-जितः conquered by woman/dominated by attachment to wife, बाल-लाली fondling children, विशति enters, हि indeed, गृह-सक्तः attached to house and worldly life, यातनाम् terrible hellish torments/suffering, मयि अभक्तः devoid of devotion unto Me, कपिल-तनुः incarnated as Kapila!, इति thus, त्वम् You, देवहूत्यै unto Devahuti, न्यगादीः taught/proclaimed

Simple Meaning: Taking incarnation in the form of Kapila, You taught Devahuti that alas, a person who accumulates wealth through cruel and harmful means to maintain his family, who remains subservient to women's charms and love for his children, and stays deeply attached to his household while remaining devoid of devotion to You, inevitably enters into severe torment and suffering.

युवतिजठरखिन्नो जातबोधोऽप्यकाण्डे
प्रसवगलितबोधः पीडयोल्लङ्घ्य बाल्यम् ।
पुनरपि बत मुह्यत्येव तारुण्यकाले
कपिलतनुरिति त्वं देवहूत्यै न्यगादीः ॥ 15.7 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: युवति-जठर-खिन्नः suffering in the womb of a young woman, जात-बोधः अपि even though gaining spiritual awareness, अकाण्डे suddenly/prematurely, प्रसव-गलित-बोधः losing that awareness during the agonizing process of birth, पीडया through pain, उल्लङ्घ्य passing through, बाल्यम् childhood, पुनः अपि once again, बत alas, मुह्यति एव becomes delusionally attached indeed, तारुण्य-काले during youth, कपिल-तनुः incarnated as Kapila, इति thus, त्वम् You, देवहूत्यै unto Devahuti, न्यगादीः taught/proclaimed

Simple Meaning: Taking incarnation in the form of Kapila, You taught Devahuti that while suffering in the mother's womb, the jiva gains spiritual wisdom and awareness, but loses it all during the terrible pain of birth. After passing through the helplessness of childhood, alas, the jiva becomes delusionally attached once again to worldly life during youth.

पितृसुरगणयाजी धार्मिको यो गृहस्थः
स च निपतति काले दक्षिणाध्वोपगामी ।
मयि निहितमकामं कर्म तूदक्पथार्थं
कपिल्तनुरिति त्वं देवहूत्यै न्यगादीः ॥ 15.8 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: पितृ-सुर-गण-याजी worshipper of the ancestors and the host of devas, धार्मिकः righteous, यः who, गृहस्थः householder, सः च he also, निपतति falls back/returns to worldly existence, काले in due course of time, दक्षिण-अध्व-उपगामी traveller on the southern path (Pitriyana), मयि unto Me, निहितम् dedicated, अकामम् desireless, कर्म action/duty, तु but, उदक्-पथ-अर्थम् leads to the northern path (Devayana/liberation), कपिल-तनुः incarnated as Kapila!, इति thus, त्वम् You, देवहूत्यै unto Devahuti, न्यगादीः taught/proclaimed

Simple Meaning: Taking incarnation in the form of Kapila, You taught Devahuti that a righteous householder who performs rituals to worship ancestors and devas with desires travels on the southern path, only to fall back to the cycle of rebirth once his merits are exhausted. However, desireless actions dedicated entirely unto You lead to the northern path of light and ultimate liberation.

इति सुविदितवेद्यां देव हे देवहूतिं
कृतनुतिमनुगृह्य त्वं गतो योगिसङ्घैः ।
विमलमतिरथाऽसौ भक्तियोगेन मुक्ता
त्वमपि जनहितार्थं वर्तसे प्रागुदीच्याम् ॥ 15.9 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, सुविदित-वेद्याम् to her who had well understood all that was to be known, देव O Lord!, हे O, देवहूतिम् unto Devahuti, कृत-नुतिम् who offered her prayers, अनुगृह्य having blessed, त्वम् You, गतः departed, योगि-सङ्घैः along with hosts of sages/yogis, विमल-मतिः with a completely purified mind, अथ असौ thereafter she, भक्ति-योगेन through the path of devotion (bhakti-yoga), मुक्ता attained liberation, त्वम् अपि You too, जन-हित-अर्थम् for the welfare of mankind, वर्तसे reside, प्राक्-उदीच्याम् in the north-eastern direction

Simple Meaning: O Divine Lord! Having thus imparted supreme wisdom to Devahuti—who fully comprehended Your teachings and offered her heartfelt prayers—You blessed her and departed in the company of great yogis. With a completely purified mind, she attained final liberation through bhakti-yoga. For the welfare and redemption of all humanity, You continue to reside even today in the north-eastern region!

परम किमु बहूक्त्या त्वत्पदाम्भोजभक्तिं
सकलभयविनेत्रीं सर्वकामोपनेत्रीम् ।
वदसि खलु दृढं त्वं तद्विधूयामयान् मे
गुरुपवनपुरेश त्वय्युपाधत्स्व भक्तिम् ॥ 15.10 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: परम O Supreme Lord!, किमु बहूक्त्या what is the use of elaborate words/further elaboration, त्वत्-पद-अम्भोज-भक्तिम् devotion unto Your lotus feet, सकल-भय-विनेत्रीम् dispeller of all fears, सर्व-काम-उपनेत्रीम् bestower of all desires, वदसि खलु You indeed proclaim, दृढम् forcefully/emphatically, त्वम् You, तत् therefore, विधूय removing/dispelling, आमयान् मे my afflictions/diseases, गुरुपवनपुर-ईश O Lord of Guruvayur!, त्वयि in You, उपाधत्स्व bestow/establish, भक्तिम् deep devotion

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Lord! What is the need for elaborate words? You have emphatically proclaimed that devotion to Your lotus feet dispels every fear and grants all noble desires. Therefore, O Lord of Guruvayur, please cure all my diseases and afflictions, and grant me firm, unwavering devotion unto You!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
