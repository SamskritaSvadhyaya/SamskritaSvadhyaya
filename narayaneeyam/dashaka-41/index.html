Cremation of Putana

दशकम् ४१

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 41

Cremation of Putana

व्रजेश्वरैः शौरिवचो निशम्य समाव्रजन्नध्वनि भीतचेताः ।
निष्पिष्टनिश्शेषतरुं निरीक्ष्य कञ्चित्पदार्थं शरणं गतस्वाम् ॥41.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: व्रजेश्वरः the Lord of Vraja (Nanda), शौरी-वचः the words of Shauri (Vasudeva), निशम्य having heard, समाव्रजन् returning, अध्वनि on the path, भीत-चेताः with a fearful mind, निष्पिष्ट-निश्शेष-तरुम् crushing completely all trees beneath it, निरीक्ष्य seeing, कञ्चित् पदार्थम् a certain huge entity (Putana's body), शरणम् गतः त्वाम् took refuge in You

Simple Meaning: Having heard the warning words of Vasudeva, Nanda, the Lord of Vraja, was returning home with a mind filled with fear. On the way, seeing a colossal body that had crushed all the trees beneath it in its fall, he was filled with wonder and immediately took refuge in You.

निशम्य गोपीवचनादुदन्तं सर्वेऽपि गोपा भयविस्मयान्धाः ।
त्वत्पातितं घोरपिशाचदेहं देहुर्विदूरेऽथ कुठारकृत्तम् ॥41.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: निशम्य having heard, गोपी-वचनात् from the words of the gopis, उदन्तम् the news/story, सर्वे अपि all of them indeed, गोपाः the cowherds, भय-विस्मय-अन्धाः blinded/overwhelmed by fear and wonder, त्वत्-पातितम् brought down by You, घोर-पिशाच-देहम् the body of the terrifying rakshasi, देहुः burnt, विदूरे far away, अथ then, कुठार-कृत्तम् chopped into pieces with axes

Simple Meaning: Having heard the entire account from the gopis, all the cowherds were completely overwhelmed by fear and wonder. They then chopped the terrifying rakshasi's colossal body—which You had brought down—into pieces using axes, carried it far away, and burnt it.

त्वत्पीतपूतस्तनतच्छरीरात् समुच्चलन्नुच्चतरो हि धूमः ।
शङ्कामधादागरवः किमेष किं चान्दनो गौल्गुलवोऽथवेति ॥41.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-पीत-पूत-स्तन-तत्-शरीरात् from that body of hers whose breasts were purified by Your drinking, समुच्चलन् rising up, उच्चतरः immensely high, हि indeed, धूमः smoke, शङ्काम् अधात् gave rise to the doubt, आगरवः is this from aguru (sandalwood/aloeswood), किम् एष is this, किम् or, चान्दनः from sandal, गौल्गुलवः from gulgulu (frankincense), अथवा or, इति thus

Simple Meaning: As they burnt her corpse, the dense smoke rising high into the sky emitted such a sweet fragrance—because her body and breasts had been purified by the touch of Your holy lips—that the people wondered in amazement, "Is this smoke from burning aguru wood, sandal, or sweet gulgulu resin?"

मदङ्गसङ्गस्य फलं न दूरे क्षणेन तावत् भवतामपि स्यात् ।
इत्युल्लपन् वल्लवतल्लजेभ्यः त्वं पूतनामातनुथाः सुगन्धिम् ॥41.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: मत-अङ्ग-सङ्गस्य of the contact with My body, फलम् fruit or result, न not, दूरे far away, क्षणेन in a moment, तावत् indeed, भवताम् अपि to you all also, स्यात् may it be, इति उल्लपन् thus saying, वल्लव-तल्लजेभ्यः to the noble gopas, त्वम् You, पूतनाम् Putana, आतनुथाः made, सुगन्धिम् fragrant

Simple Meaning: "The result of contact with My body is not far off; in a short while, it will be yours as well." as if saying these words to the noble gopas, You made a sweet and divine fragrance emanate from the body of Putana.

चित्रं पिशाच्या न हतः कुमारः चित्रं पुरैवाकथि शौरिणेदम् ।
इति प्रशंसन् किल गोपलोको भवन्मुखालोकरसे न्यमाङ्क्षीत् ॥41.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: चित्रम् wonderful or amazing, पिशाच्याः by the pishachi/rakshasi, न not, हतः killed, कुमारः child, चित्रम् wonderful, पुरा एव beforehand itself, अकथि was said, शौरिणा इदम् by Vasudeva this, इति thus, प्रशंसन् praising, किल indeed, गोप-लोकः the people of Vraja, भवत्-मुख-आलोक-रसे in the joy of looking at Your face, न्यमाङ्क्षीत् immersed

Simple Meaning: "How wonderful that the child was not killed by the pishachi! How wonderful that Vasudeva had predicted this beforehand!" Thus praising and marveling, the gopas immersed themselves in the pure joy of gazing upon Your beautiful face.

दिनेदिनेऽथ प्रतिवृद्धलक्ष्मीरक्षीणमाङ्गल्यशतो व्रजोऽयम् ।
भवन्निवासादयि वासुदेव प्रमोदसान्द्रः परितो विरेजे ॥41.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: दिने दिने अथ day by day then, प्रतिवृद्ध-लक्ष्मीः with ever increasing prosperity, अक्षीण-माङ्गल्य-शतः with hundreds of undiminished auspicious events, व्रजः अयम् this Vraja, भवत्-निवासात् because of Your residence अयि वासुदेव O Lord Vasudeva, प्रमोद-सान्द्रः dense with joy, परितः everywhere, विरेजे shone brightly

Simple Meaning: O Vasudeva, owing to Your residence, this Vraja shone brightly on all sides, dense with bliss, possessing ever-increasing wealth and prosperity, and celebrating hundreds of undiminished auspicious events day after day.

गृहेषु ते कोमलरूपहासमिथःकथासङ्कुलिताः कमन्यः ।
वृत्तेषु कृत्येषु भवन्निरीक्षासमागताः प्रत्यहमत्यनन्दन् ॥41.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: गृहेषु in the homes, ते Your, कोमल-रूप-हास-मिथः-कथा-सङ्कुलिताः filled with conversations about Your gentle form and sweet smile, कमन्यः the beautiful women, वृत्तेषु completed, कृत्येषु in the household chores, भवत्-निरीक्षा-समागताः assembled to see You, प्रत्यहम् every day, अत्यनन्दन् rejoiced greatly

Simple Meaning: In every home, the beautiful gopi women spent their time talking about Your gentle form and charming smile. Having finished their daily household duties, they gathered every day to gaze upon You and experienced infinite joy.

अहो कुमारो मयि दत्तदृष्टिः स्मितं कृतं मां प्रति वत्सकेन ।
एह्येहि मामित्युपसार्य पाणी त्वयीश किं किं न कृतं वधूभिः ॥41.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: अहो oh, कुमारः the child, मयि on me, दत्त-दृष्टिः has cast His glance, स्मितम् smile, कृतम् made, माम् प्रति towards me, वत्सकेन by the little darling, एहि एहि come come, माम् to me, इति उपसार्य thus extending, पाणी hands, त्वयि to You, ईश O Lord, किम् किम् what all, न not, कृतम् done, वधूभिः by the young women

Simple Meaning: "Oh look! The child has turned His gaze towards me! The little darling smiled at me! Come, come to me!" Saying this and extending their arms out to You, O Lord, what affectionate acts were not performed by those young women?

भवद्वपुःस्पर्शनकौतुकेन करात्करं गोपवधूजनेन ।
नीतस्त्वमाताम्रसरोजमालाव्यालम्बिलोलम्बतुलामलासीः ॥41.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: भवत्-वपुः-स्पर्शन-कौतुकेन out of eagerness to touch Your body, करात् करम् from hand to hand, गोप-वधू-जनेन by the gopi women, नीतः being passed, त्वम् You, आताम्र-सरोज-माला-व्यालम्बि-लोलम्ब-तुलाम्-अलासीः resembled a bee hovering over a garland of reddish lotuses

Simple Meaning: Eager to touch Your tender body, the gopis passed You from hand to hand, and as You moved among their soft hands, You shone like a honeybee hovering along a garland of reddish lotuses.

निपाययन्ती स्तनमङ्कगं त्वां विलोकयन्ती वदनं हसन्ती ।
दशां यशोदा कतमां न भेजे स तादृशः पाहि हरे गदान्माम् ॥41.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: निपाययन्ती while feeding, स्तनम् milk, अङ्कगम् seated on her lap, त्वाम् You, विलोकयन्ती gazing at, वदनम् face, हसन्ती smiling, दशाम् state of bliss, यशोदा Yashoda, कतमाम् which, न not, भेजे attained, सः that, तादृशः such a one, पाहि protect, हरे O Lord Hari, गदात् माम् me from illness

Simple Meaning: While feeding You milk as You sat on her lap, gazing upon Your sweet face and smiling, what state of supreme bliss did Yashoda not attain? O Lord Hari, residing thus in such heavenly divine affection, please protect me from all my ailments!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
