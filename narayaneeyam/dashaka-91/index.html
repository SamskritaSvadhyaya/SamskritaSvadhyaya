॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 91

The glory of Bhakti

श्रीकृष्ण त्वत्पदोपासनमभयतमं बद्धमिथ्यार्थदृष्टेः
मर्त्यस्यार्तस्य मन्ये व्यपसरति भयं येन सर्वात्मनैव ।
यत्तावत् त्वत्प्रणीतानिह भजनविधीनास्थितो मोहमार्गे
धावन्नप्यावृताक्षः स्खलति न कुहचिद्देवदेवाखिलात्मन् ॥91.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री कृष्ण O Lord Krishna, त्वत्-पद-उपासनम् devotion/worship unto Your feet, अभयतमम् the most supreme source of fearlessness, बद्ध-मिथ्यार्थ-दृष्टेः for one whose perception is bound to illusory worldly objects, मर्त्यस्य for a mortal, आर्तस्य for one who is distressed/suffering, मन्ये I consider, येन by which (devotion), भयम् fear, व्यपसरति completely departs/disappears, सर्वात्मना एव entirely/in every way, यत् तावत् because indeed, त्वत्-प्रणीतान् ordained/instituted by You, इह on this path of devotion, भजन-विधीन् the methods of devotional service, आस्थितः adhering to, मोह-मार्गे on the path of worldly delusion, धावन् अपि even while running, आवृत-अक्षः with closed eyes, स्खलति न does not stumble/fall, कुहचित् anywhere/at any time, देव देव O God of gods, अखिलात्मन् O Universal Soul

Simple Meaning: O Lord Krishna! For a mortal who is afflicted with suffering and bound by false perceptions of worldly objects, I consider devotion unto Your lotus feet to be the ultimate source of absolute fearlessness. By adopting this path, fear vanishes completely in every way. Indeed, when one follows the methods of devotional service taught by You, even if he were to run with eyes closed along the path of ignorance, he will never stumble or suffer a downfall anywhere, O God of gods, O Universal Soul of all!

भूमन् कायेन वाचा मुहुरपि मनसा त्वद्बलप्रेरितात्मा
यद्यत् कुर्वे समस्तं तदिह परतरे त्वय्यसावर्पयामि ।
जात्यापीह श्वपाकस्त्वयि निहितमनःकर्मवागिन्द्रियार्थ-
प्राणो विश्वं पुनीते न तु विमुखमनास्त्वत्पदाद्विप्रवर्यः ॥91.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूमन् O Infinite Supreme Lord, कायेन through body, वाचा through speech, मुहुः अपि repeatedly, मनसा through mind, त्वत्-बल-प्रेरित-आत्मा my inner self which is prompted and motivated by Your power alone, यत् यत् whatever, कुर्वे I perform/do, समस्तम् all, तत् इह that here, परतरे supreme beyond all, त्वयि in You, असौ this (myself/my actions), अर्पयामि I surrender/offer, जात्या अपि इह even by birth here, श्वपाकः a outcaste/chandala (dog-eater), त्वयि in You, निहित-मनः-कर्म-वाग्-इन्द्रियार्थ-प्राणः who has dedicated his mind, actions, speech, sense-objects, and life-forces, विश्वम् the entire world, पुनीते purifies, न तु but not, विमुख-मनाः one whose mind is averse, त्वत्-पदात् from Your lotus feet, विप्र-वर्यः even an exalted brahmana

Simple Meaning: O Infinite Lord! Inspired and impelled solely by Your power, whatever actions I perform repeatedly through my body, speech, or mind—all of it I surrender entirely unto You, the Supreme Reality! Even an outcast born in the lowest community, if he has dedicated his mind, actions, speech, senses, and vital forces unto You, purifies the entire world. On the other hand, even an eminent brahmana, if his mind is turned away from Your lotus feet, cannot purify even himself!

भीतिर्नाम द्वितीयाद्भवति ननु मनःकल्पितं च द्वितीयं
तेनैक्याभ्यासशीलो हृदयमिह यथाशक्ति बुद्ध्या निरुन्ध्याम् ।
मायाविद्धे तु तस्मिन् पुनरपि न तथा भाति मायाधिनाथं
तं त्वां भक्त्या महत्या सततमनुभजनीश भीतिं विजह्याम् ॥91.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: भीतिः नाम fear indeed, द्वितीयात् भवति arises from a sense of duality/separateness, ननु surely, मनः कल्पितम् च and created/projected by the mind, द्वितीयम् duality, तेन therefore, ऐक्य-अभ्यास-शीलः one practicing the discipline of non-duality/unity, हृदयम् इह the mind/heart here, यथा-शक्ति to the best of one's ability, बुद्ध्या with intellect, निरुन्ध्याम् one should control/restrain, माया विद्धे तु तस्मिन् but when that mind is pierced/clouded by Maya, पुनः अपि time and again, न तथा भाति it does not shine forth as such, माया-अधिनाथम् the Supreme Controller/Master of Maya, तम् त्वाम् that Lord You, भक्त्या महत्या with intense devotion, सततम् अनुभजन् constantly worshipping, ईश O Lord, भीतिम् विजह्याम् I shall completely transcend all fear

Simple Meaning: Fear arises solely from the perception of duality, and this duality is merely a projection of the mind. Therefore, one who strives to practice the unity of existence must, to the best of his ability, restrain his mind using refined intellect. However, when the mind is repeatedly clouded by Your illusive power (Maya), realizing non-duality becomes difficult. Hence, O Lord, by constantly worshipping You—the supreme Master of Maya—with intense, unswerving devotion, I shall easily overcome and completely transcend all fear!

भक्तेरुत्पत्तिवृद्धी तव चरणजुषां सङ्गमेनैव पुंसाम्
आसाद्ये पुण्यभाजां श्रिय इव जगति श्रीमतां सङ्गमेन ।
तत्सङ्गो देव भूयान्मम खलु सततं तन्मुखादुन्मिषद्भिः
त्वन्माहात्म्यप्रकारैर्भवति च सुदृढा भक्तिरुद्धूतपापा ॥91.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: भक्तः-उत्पत्ति-वृद्धी the origin/birth and growth of devotion, तव चरण जुषाम् of those who serve/take refuge in Your lotus feet, सङ्गमेन एव through company/association alone, पुंसाम् for men, आसाद्ये are attainable, पुण्य-भाजाम् for the virtuous/blessed ones, श्रियः इव just as riches/wealth (arises), जगति in the world, श्रीमताम् सङ्गमेन through association with the wealthy, तत्-सङ्गः that holy company of devotees, देव O Lord, भूयात् मम may it be granted/happen unto me, खलु indeed, सततम् constantly, तत्-मुखात्-उन्मिषद्भिः emanating/blossoming from their mouths, त्वत्-माहात्म्य-प्रकारैः through the various narratives of Your supreme glory, भवति च and becomes, सुदृढा exceptionally firm/unshakable, भक्तिः devotion, उद्धूत-पापा that which destroys/dispels all sins

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Just as in this world material prosperity is attained by associating with the wealthy, the origin and steady growth of devotion in mortals is achieved solely through association with the virtuous devotees who reside at Your lotus feet. Therefore, O Lord, grant that I may constantly enjoy the holy company of such noble souls! For through the diverse descriptions of Your glorious divine attributes constantly flowing from their lips, devotion becomes firmly anchored and dispels every trace of sin.

श्रेयोमार्गेषु भक्तावधिकबहुमतिर्जन्मकर्माणि भूयो
गायन् क्षेमाणि नामान्यपि तदुभयतः प्रद्रुतं प्रद्रुतात्मा ।
उद्यद्धासः कदाचित् कुहचिदपि रुदन् क्वापि गर्जन् प्रगायन्
उन्मादीव प्रनृत्यन्नयि कुरु करुणां लोकबाह्यश्चरेयम् ॥91.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रेयः मार्गेषु among all paths leading to the supreme spiritual good, भक्तौ in devotion, अधिक-बहुमतिः having maximum regard/reverential attachment, जन्म-कर्माणि Your divine births (incarnations) and deeds, भूयः repeatedly, गायन् singing, क्षेमाणि auspicious, नामानि अपि Your holy names as well, तत्-उभयतः from both of these (singing Your deeds and names), प्रद्रुतम् rapidly, प्रद्रुत-आत्मा with a heart melted in intense love, उद्यत्-हासः suddenly bursting into laughter, कदाचित् sometimes, कुहचित् अपि somewhere, रुदन् weeping/crying, क्वापि somewhere, गर्जन् roaring/shouting in ecstasy, प्रगायन् singing aloud, उन्मादी इव like a person intoxicated/mad with divine love, प्रनृत्यन् dancing in bliss, अयि O Lord, कुरु करुणाम् shower Your grace/mercy, लोक-बाह्यः-चरेयम् so that I may wander transcendent to conventional worldly ways

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Having the highest regard for devotion among all paths to liberation, continuously singing Your auspicious names and the accounts of Your divine incarnations, may my heart rapidly melt with love for You! Bursting into spontaneous laughter at times, weeping somewhere in bliss, shouting with ecstasy, singing aloud, and dancing like one intoxicated with divine love, may I—by Your boundless mercy—wander about, entirely unmindful of conventional worldly ways!

भूतान्येतानि भूतात्मकमपि सकलं पक्षिमत्स्यान् मृगादीन्
मर्त्यान् मित्राणि शत्रूनपि यमितमतिस्त्वन्मयान्यानमानि ।
त्वत्सेवायां हि सिद्ध्येन्मम तव कृपया भक्तिदार्ढ्यं विरागः
त्वत्तत्त्वस्यावबोधोऽपि च भुवनपते यत्नभेदं विनैव ॥91.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूतानि एतानि all these living beings, भूत-आत्मकम् अपि सकलम् as well as the entire material universe composed of the five elements, पक्षि-मत्स्यान् मृग-आदीन् birds, fish, animals, and the like, मर्त्यान् human beings, मित्राणि friends, शत्रून् अपि and enemies too, यमित-मतिः with a controlled mind/intellect, त्वत्-मयानि recognizing them as expressions of Your divine form, आनमानि I bow down to them, त्वत्-सेवायाम् हि indeed in Your service/devotion, सिद्ध्येत् मम may there be accomplished for me, तव कृपया by Your grace, भक्ति-दार्ढ्यम् unyielding firmness of devotion, विरागः dispassion/detachment, त्वत्-तत्त्वस्य अवबोधः अपि च and also the direct realization of Your ultimate truth, भुवन-पते O Lord of the universe, यत्न-भेदम् विना एव without any separate/strenuous effort

Simple Meaning: O Lord of the universe! Controlling my mind, I bow down to all living beings—birds, fish, animals, humans, friends, and even enemies—as well as the entire elemental cosmos, seeing them all as manifestations of Your divine presence. By Your boundless grace, may steadfast devotion unto You, deep dispassion toward worldly pleasures, and the ultimate realization of Your divine Truth be naturally accomplished for me through Your service alone, without needing any separate or arduous effort!

नो मुह्यन् क्षुत्तृडाद्यैर्भवसरणिभवैस्त्वन्निलीनाशयत्वात्
चिन्तासातत्यशाली निमिषलवमपि त्वत्पदादप्रकम्पः ।
इष्टानिष्टेषु तुष्टिव्यसनविरहितो मायिकत्वावबोधात्
ज्योत्स्नाभिस्त्वन्नखेन्दोरधिकशिशिरितेनात्मना सञ्चरेयम् ॥91.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: नो मुह्यन् not being delusionally affected/overwhelmed, क्षुत्-तृड्-आद्यैः by hunger, thirst, and other physical afflictions, भव-सरणि-भवैः born of/arising from the path of worldly life, त्वत्-निलीन-आशयत्वात् due to one's mind/heart being deeply merged in You, चिन्ता-सातत्य-शाली constantly absorbed in continuous remembrance of You, निमिष-लवम् अपि even for a fraction of a second, त्वत्-पदात् from Your lotus feet, अप्रकम्पः unswerving/unshakable, इष्ट-अनिष्टेषु amid desirable and undesirable occurrences, तुष्टि-व्यसन-विरहितः free from ecstatic attachments and grieving distress, मायिकत्व-अवबोधात् owing to the clear realization of their illusory/fleeting nature, ज्योत्स्नाभिः by the soothing moonlight rays, त्वत्-नख-इन्दोः emanating from the moon-like nails of Your lotus feet, अधिक-शिशिरितेन-आत्मना with an inner self/mind rendered exceedingly cool and serene, सञ्चरेयम् may I wander/live in this world

Simple Meaning: Remaining completely unperturbed by hunger, thirst, and other physical tribulations inherent to worldly life—because my mind is deeply absorbed in You—may I be continuously filled with thoughts of You, never straying from Your lotus feet even for a fraction of a second! Recognizing all pleasant and unpleasant experiences to be mere creations of Maya, free from giddy pleasures and grieving distress, and with my inner self rendered exceedingly cool and tranquil by the soothing moonlight beaming from Your toe-nails, may I wander blamelessly through this world!

भूतेष्वेषु त्वदैक्यस्मृतिसमधिगतौ नाधिकारोऽधुना चेत्
त्वत्प्रेम त्वत्कमैत्री जडमतिषु कृपा द्विट्सु भूयादुपेक्षा ।
अर्चायां वा समर्चाकुतुकमुरुतरश्रद्धया वर्धतां मे
त्वत्संसेवी तथापि द्रुतमुपलभते भक्तलोकोत्तमत्वम् ॥91.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूतेषु एषु in all these created beings, त्वत्-ऐक्य-स्मृति-समधिगतौ in attaining the constant realization/remembrance of oneness with You, न अधिकारः no eligibility/qualification, अधुना चेत् if not present now, त्वत्-प्रेम supreme love for You, त्वत्-क-मैत्री friendship towards Your devotees, जड-मतिषु कृपा compassion towards the ignorant/unawakened ones, द्विट्सु towards those who hate You, भूयात् may develop in me (four attitudes), उपेक्षा indifference/dispassion, अर्चायाम् वा or in Your consecrated deity form (archa-murti), समर्चा-कुतुकम् the eager delight in worshipping You, उरुतर-श्रद्धया with immense faith, वर्धताम् मे may it flourish in me, त्वत्-संसेवी one who dedicatedly serves You, तथापि nevertheless, द्रुतम् swiftly, उपलभते attains, भक्त-लोक-उत्तमत्वम् the status of the supreme among devotees

Simple Meaning: O Lord! If I am not yet qualified for that highest state of realizing non-dual oneness with You in all beings (the Uttama-Bhagavata state), then may I at least cultivate the fourfold path of a Madhyama-Bhagavata: supreme love for You, friendship toward Your devotees, compassion for the spiritually ignorant, and calm indifference toward the hostile! Or else, let my enthusiastic devotion to worshipping Your deity form flourish with deep and unwavering faith. Indeed, even by worshipping Your holy image, a devotee rapidly ascends to the highest realm of devotion!

आवृत्य त्वत्स्वरूपं क्षितिजलमरुदाद्यात्मना विक्षिपन्ती
जीवान् भूयिष्ठकर्मावलिविवशगतीन् दुःखजाले क्षिपन्ती ।
त्वन्माया माभिभून्मामयि भुवनपते कल्पते तत्प्रशान्त्यै
त्वत्पादे भक्तिरेवेत्यवददयि विभो सिद्धयोगी प्रबुद्धः ॥91.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: आवृत्य obscuring/concealing, त्वत्-स्वरूपम् Your true transcendent nature, क्षिति-जल-मरुत्-आदि आत्मना in the form of earth, water, fire, air, space, and the other material elements, विक्षिपन्ती projecting/manifesting the diverse universe, जीवान् the individual souls (jivas), भूयिष्ठ कर्म-आवलि-विवश-गतीन् whose movements and rebirths are helpless under the weight of endless accumulated karmas, दुःख-जाले in the net of worldly miseries, क्षिपन्ती casting/throwing, त्वत्-माया Your illusory power (Maya), मा अभिभूत् माम् may it not overwhelm me, अयि O Lord, भुवन-पते O Controller of the universe, कल्पते alone is capable, तत्-प्रशान्त्यै for the complete eradication and pacification of that Maya, त्वत्-पादे at Your lotus feet, भक्तिः एव devotion alone, इति अवदत् thus declared, अयि विभो O All-Pervading Lord, सिद्ध-योगी the perfected sage, प्रबुद्धः Prabuddha (one of the Nine Yogendras)

Simple Meaning: O Lord of the universe! Your Maya conceals Your divine, essential nature and projects this vast manifold universe composed of earth, water, air, and other elements. It repeatedly casts the individual souls—helplessly driven by their mountain of past karmas—into the entangling net of worldly suffering. May this formidable Maya never overwhelm me! O All-Pervading Supreme Lord, as the perfected sage Prabuddha declared, intense devotion at Your lotus feet is the sole remedy capable of completely dispelling and destroying this Maya!

दुःखान्यालोक्य जन्तुष्वलमुदितविवेकोऽहमाचार्यवर्यात्
लब्ध्वा त्वद्रूपतत्त्वं गुणचरितकथाद्युद्भवद्भक्तिभूमा ।
मायामेनां तरित्वा परमसुखमये त्वत्पदे मोदिताहे
तस्यायं पूर्वरङ्गः पवनपुरपते नाशयाशेषरोगान् ॥91.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुःखानि आलोक्य having seen/observed the manifold miseries, जन्तुषु in living beings, अलम् उदित-विवेकः अहम् I, with a deeply awakened sense of spiritual discrimination (viveka), आचार्य-वर्यात् from the most noble Guru/spiritual master, लब्ध्वा having received/attained, त्वत्-रूप-तत्त्वम् the supreme truth regarding Your divine form, गुण-चरित-कथा-आदि-उद्भवत्-भक्ति-भूमा with an overflowing abundance of devotion generated through hearing and singing Your divine attributes, deeds, and stories, मायाम् एनाम् this formidable illusive power (Maya), तरित्वा having overcome/crossed over, परम-सुखमये which is of the nature of supreme transcendent bliss, त्वत्-पादे at Your lotus feet, मोदिताहे I shall rejoice/revel in supreme joy, तस्य अयम् पूर्वः अङ्गः this present devotion and prayer of mine is indeed the prelude/preparatory phase of that ultimate liberation, पवनपुर-पते O Lord of Guruvayur (Pavanapura), नाशय अशेष-रोगान् completely destroy all my remaining illnesses and afflictions

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Guruvayur! Having observed the endless miseries that afflict all living beings, I have developed deep detachment and spiritual discrimination. Receiving the knowledge of Your divine, transcendent reality from the most exalted Guru, and brimming with an abundance of devotion born of meditating on Your divine attributes and stories, I shall cross over this formidable Maya and eternally rejoice in Your bliss-filled lotus feet! This present prayer and devotional practice of mine is the prelude to that final beatitude. O Supreme Master, completely destroy all my physical and mental afflictions without a trace!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
