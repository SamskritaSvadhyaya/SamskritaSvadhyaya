Prelude to the first incarnation

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 11

Prelude to the first incarnation

(छन्दस् - उपेन्द्रवज्रा)

क्रमेण सर्गे परिवर्धमाने
कदापि दिव्याः सनकादयस्ते ।
भवद्विलोकाय विकुण्ठलोकं
प्रपेदिरे मारुतमन्दिरेश ॥11.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्रमेण gradually, सर्गे as creation, परिवर्धमाने was expanding/progressing, कदापि once, दिव्याः divine, सनक-आदयः Sanaka and the other sages, ते those, भवत्-विलोकाय to behold You, विकुण्ठ-लोकम् the realm of Vaikuntha, प्रपेदिरे reached/arrived at, मारुत-मन्दिर-ईश O Lord of the home of the Wind - Guruvayoor

Simple Meaning: As the process of creation was gradually expanding, those divine sages—Sanaka, Sanandana, Sanatana, and Sanatkumara—once arrived at Vaikuntha, to see You, O Lord of Guruvayoor!

मनोज्ञनैश्रेयसकाननाद्यैः
अनेकवापीमणिमन्दिरैश्च ।
अनोपमं तं भवतो निकेतं
मुनीश्वराः प्रापुरतीतकक्ष्याः ॥11.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: मनोज्ञ-नैश्रेयस-कानन-आद्यैः with enchanting Naishreyasa-Kanana (the garden by that name which has trees of ultimate liberation/bliss) and other flora, अनेक-वापी-मणि-मन्दिरैः with numerous lakes and jewel-encrusted mansions, च and, अनोपमम् peerless/matchless, तम् that, भवतः Your, निकेतम् abode - Vaikuntha, मुनि-ईश्वराः the great sages, प्रापुः reached, अतीत-कक्ष्याः having passed through the outer gates/courtyards

Simple Meaning: Passing through the successive outer gates, the exalted sages reached Your incomparable abode, Vaikuntha—a realm adorned with Naishreyasa-Kanana and other flora, numerous lakes and mansions encrusted with precious gems.

भवद्दिदृक्षून्भवनं विविक्षून्
द्वाःस्थौ जयस्तान् विजयोऽप्यरुन्धाम् ।
तेषां च चित्ते पदमाप कोपः
सर्वं भवत्प्रेरणयैव भूमन् ॥11.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: भवत्-दिदृक्षून् desiring to see You, भवनम् the inner abode/palace, विविक्षून् desiring to enter, द्वाःस्थौ the two door-keepers, जयः Jaya, तान् them (the sages), विजयः Vijaya, अपि also, अरुन्धाम् stopped/barred, तेषाम् of those (sages), च and, चित्ते in the mind, पदम् स्थानम् a foothold/place, आप attained, कोपः anger, सर्वम् all this, भवत्-प्रेरणया by Your divine inspiration/prompting, एव alone, भूमन् O All-Pervading Supreme Being

Simple Meaning: As the sages—desiring to see You—attempted to enter Your inner palace, the two gatekeepers, Jaya and Vijaya, barred their path. Thereupon, the sages became angry. O All-Pervading Lord, all of this happened solely through Your divine inspiration!

वैकुण्ठलोकानुचितप्रचेष्टौ
कष्टौ युवां दैत्यगतिं भजेतम् ।
इति प्रशप्तौ भवदाश्रयौ तौ
हरिस्मृतिर्नोऽस्त्विति नेमतुस्तान् ॥11.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: वैकुण्ठ-लोक-अनुचित-प्रचेष्टौ acting in a manner completely unsuited for the abode of Vaikuntha, कष्टौ O wicked/blameworthy ones, युवाम् you two, दैत्य-गतिम् the state being of daityas, भजेतम् shall attain/fall into, इति thus, प्रशप्तौ cursed, भवत्-आश्रयौ who were devoted to/dependent on You, तौ those two (Jaya and Vijaya), हरि-स्मृतिः remembrance of Lord Hari, नः for us, अस्तु let there be, इति saying thus, नेमतुः bowed down to, तान् them (the sages)

Simple Meaning: Cursing them, the sages said, “You two have acted in a manner completely unbecoming of Vaikuntha! O sinful ones, take birth as daityas!" Though thus cursed, Your two faithful attendants, Jaya and Vijaya, bowed before the sages, praying: “May the constant remembrance of Lord Hari never leave us, (even in that state of being daityas)!”

तदेतदाज्ञाय भवानवाप्तः
सहैव लक्ष्म्या बहिरम्बुजाक्ष ।
खगेश्वरांसार्पितचारुबाहुः
आनन्दयंस्तानभिराममूर्त्या ॥11.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्-एतत्-आज्ञाय knowing/perceiving all this, भवान् You, अवाप्तः arrived/came out, सह एव along with, लक्ष्म्या Goddess Lakshmi, बहिः outside (to the gate), अम्बुजाक्ष O Lotus-eyed Lord, खगेश्वर-अंस-अर्पित-चारु-बाहुः resting Your beautiful arm on the shoulder of the King of Birds (Garuda), आनन्दयन् delighted/filled with bliss, तान् them (the sages), अभिराम-मूर्त्या with Your beautiful form

Simple Meaning: O Lotus-eyed Lord! Knowing all that had transpired at the gate, You came out along with Goddess Lakshmi. Resting Your beautiful arm upon the shoulder of Garuda, You delighted those sages with the beauty of Your divine form.

प्रसाद्य गीर्भिः स्तुवतो मुनीन्द्रान्
अनन्यनाथावथ पार्षदौ तौ ।
संरम्भयोगेन भवैस्त्रिभिर्माम्
उपेतमित्यात्तकृपं न्यगादीः ॥11.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रसाद्य having pleased/appeased, गीर्भिः with praises/hymns, स्तुवतः who were singing Your praises, मुनीन् इन्द्रान् the foremost of sages, अनन्य-नाथौ who had no other protector than You, अथ then, पार्षदौ तौ those two attendants (Jaya and Vijaya), संरम्भ-योगेन through the yoga of intense devotion characterized by enmity/confrontation (samrambha-yoga), भवैः त्रिभिः in three births, माम् Me, उपेतम् you both shall reach/rejoin, इति thus, आत्त-कृपम् graciously/filled with compassion, न्यगादीः You declared (to the two attendants)

Simple Meaning: After pleasing the exalted sages who were praising You with divine hymns, You declared to Your two attendants - who had no other protector than You - “Through the yoga of intense enmity (samrambha-yoga), you shall return to Me after three births.”

त्वदीयभृत्यावथ काश्यपात्तौ
सुरारिवीरावुदितौ दितौ द्वौ ।
सन्ध्यासमुत्पादनकष्टचेष्टौ
यमौ च लोकस्य यमाविवान्यौ ॥11.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वदीय-भृत्यौ Your two servants (Jaya and Vijaya), अथ thereafter, काश्यपात् from Sage Kashyapa, तौ those two, सुर-अरि-वीरौ powerful enemies of the devas (asuras), उदितौ were born, दितौ in (the womb of) Diti, द्वौ as a pair, सन्ध्या-समुत्पादन-कष्ट-चेष्टौ whose conception during twilight was caused by improper/unseemly conduct, यमौ twins, च and, लोकस्य to the world, यमौ इव like two Yamas - god of death, अन्यौ other/veritable

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, Your two attendants were born as twin brothers from the womb of Diti through Sage Kashyapa—a result of their unseemly passion during the forbidden twilight hour. Emerging as two powerful enemies of the devas, they were like two Yamas - the god of death.

हिरण्यपूर्वः कशिपुः किलैकः
परो हिरण्याक्ष इति प्रतीतः ।
उभौ भवन्नाथमशेषलोकं
रुषा न्यरुन्धां निजवासनान्धौ ॥11.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: हिरण्य-पूर्वः कशिपुः named Hiranyakashipu (having hiranya as the prefix to kashipu), किल indeed, एकः the first/one, परः the other, हिरण्याक्षः Hiranyaksha, इति thus, प्रतीतः known as, उभौ both of them, भवत्-नाथम् having You as their Lord, अशेष-लोकम् the entire universe, रुषा with fierce anger, न्यरुन्धाम् oppressed/tormented, निज-वासना-अन्धौ blinded by their own innate asuri tendencies (vasanas)

Simple Meaning: The first of the twins was known as Hiranyakashipu, and the other was renowned as Hiranyaksha. Blinded by their innate asuri inclinations, both of them tormented the entire universe, which has You as its Lord.

तयोर्हिरण्याक्षमहासुरेन्द्रो
रणाय धावन्ननवाप्तवैरी ।
भवत्प्रियां क्ष्मां सलिले निमज्य
चचार गर्वाद्विनदन् गदावान् ॥11.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: तयोः of those two, हिरण्याक्ष-महा-असुर-इन्द्रः the great asura king Hiranyaksha, रणाय for battle, धावन् rushing about, अनवाप्त-वैरी unable to find a worthy opponent, भवत्-प्रियाम् Your beloved, क्ष्माम् the Earth, सलिले in the water, निमज्य having submerged, चचार wandered around, गर्वात् out of arrogance, विनदन् roaring loudly, गदावान् wielding a mace

Simple Meaning: Among the two, the mighty asura king Hiranyaksha rushed around in search of a worthy adversary to fight. Finding none, he arrogantly submerged Your beloved, the Earth into the waters, and wandered about roaring fiercely wielding his mace.

ततो जलेशात् सदृशं भवन्तं
निशम्य बभ्राम गवेषयंस्त्वाम् ।
भक्तैकदृश्यः स कृपानिधे त्वं
निरुन्धि रोगान् मरुदालयेश ॥11.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, जल-ईशात् from the lord of waters - Varuna, सदृशम् a worthy match/equal, भवन्तम् You, निशम्य hearing about, बभ्राम wandered around, गवेषयन् searching for, त्वाम् You, भक्त-एक-दृश्यः visible only to Your devotees, सः You, कृपा-निधे O Ocean of Compassion, त्वम् You, निरुन्धि eradicate/ward off, रोगान् my illnesses, मरुत्-आलय-ईश O Lord of abode of the Wind - Guruvayoor

Simple Meaning: Hearing from Varuna, the god of waters, that You alone were his equal match in battle, the asura Hiranyaksha wandered everywhere searching for You - who are visible only to Your devotees.  O Ocean of Compassion, O Lord of Guruvayoor, please eradicate all my ailments!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
