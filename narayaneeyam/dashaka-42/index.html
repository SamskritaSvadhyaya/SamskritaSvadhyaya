Dashaka 42

Destruction of Shakatasura

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 42

Destruction of Shakatasura

कदापि जन्मर्क्षदिने तव प्रभो निमन्त्रितज्ञातिवधूमहीसुरा ।
महानसस्त्वां सविधे निधाय सा महानसादौ ववृते व्रजेश्वरी ॥42.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: कदापि once, जन्म-ऋक्ष-दिने on the day of Your birth star, तव Your, प्रभो O Lord, निमन्त्रित-ज्ञाति-वधू-महीसुरा having invited relatives, their wives and brahmanas, महा-अनसः of a big cart, त्वाम् You, सविधे nearby, निधाय placing, सा she, महानस-आदौ in cooking and other tasks, ववृते engaged, व्रजेश्वरी Yashoda the queen of Vraja

Simple Meaning: O Lord, on the occasion of Your birthday, Yashoda, the queen of Vraja, invited all the relatives, their wives and holy brahmanas. Placing You beneath a big handcart parked nearby, she became busy with cooking and other festival preparations.

ततो भवत्त्राणनियुक्तबालकप्रभीतिसङ्क्रन्दनसङ्कुलारवैः ।
विमिश्रमश्रावि भवत्समीपतः परिस्फुटद्दारुचटच्चटारवः ॥42.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, भवत्-त्राण-नियुक्त-बालक-प्रभीति-सङ्क्रन्दन-सङ्कुल-आरवैः with the chaotic noise of intense fear and crying of the children appointed to protect You, विमिश्रम् mixed with, अश्रावि was heard, भवत्-समीपतः from Your vicinity, परिस्फुटत्-दारु-चटत्-चटा-रवः the loud cracking sound of splitting wood

Simple Meaning: Then, from near You was heard a loud cracking noise of breaking wood, mingled with the confused cries and loud weeping of the frightened children who had been appointed to watch over You.

ततस्तदाकर्णनसम्भ्रमश्रमप्रकम्पिवक्षोजभरा व्रजाङ्गनाः ।
भवन्तमन्तर्ददृशुस्समन्ततो विनिष्पतद्दारुणदारुमध्यगम् ॥42.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, तत्-आकर्णन-सम्भ्रम-श्रम-प्रकम्पि-वक्षोज-भराः on hearing that, with their heavy breasts trembling from confusion and haste, व्रज-अङ्गनाः the women of Vraja, भवन्तम् You, अन्तः-ददृशुः saw inside, समन्ततः all around, विनिष्पतत्-दारुण-दारु-मध्यगम् amidst the shattered heavy wooden cart falling everywhere

Simple Meaning: Then, hearing that dreadful sound, the gopis ran in haste, their heavy breasts trembling with agitation. On reaching the spot, they saw You lying safely in the midst of the heavy broken pieces of the wooden cart that had fallen all around.

शिशोरहो किं किमभूदिति द्रुतं प्रधाव्य नन्दः पशुपाश्च भूसुराः ।
भवन्तमालोक्य यशोदया धृतं समाश्वसन्नश्रुजलार्द्रलोचनाः ॥42.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: शिशोः to the child, अहो किम् किम् अभूत् oh, what happened! what happened! इति thus, द्रुतम् quickly, प्रधाव्य running, नन्दः Nanda, पशुपाः च and the gopas, भूसुराः the brahmanas, भवन्तम् You, आलोक्य seeing, यशोदया by Yashoda, धृतम् held, समाश्वसन् were comforted, अश्रु-जल-आर्द्र-लोचनाः with eyes wet with tears

Simple Meaning: "Oh, what happened to the child? What happened?" Crying out thus, Nanda, the gopas, and the brahmanas ran quickly to the spot. Seeing You safely held in the arms of Yashoda, they felt deeply relieved and comforted, their eyes moist with tears.

कस्को नु कौतस्कुत एष विस्मयो विशङ्कटं यच्छकटं विपाटितम् ।
न कारणं किञ्चिदिहेति ते स्थिताः स्वनासिकादत्तकरास्त्वदीक्षकाः ॥42.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: कः कः who who, नु indeed, कौतः how, कुतः from where, एषः this, विस्मयः wonder or mystery, विशङ्कटम् massive, यत् शकटम् which cart, विपाटितम् was shattered, न not, कारणम् cause, किञ्चित् इह anything here, इति thus, ते they, स्थिताः stood, स्व-नासिका-दत्त-कराः with hands placed on their noses, त्वत्-ईक्षकाः those looking at You

Simple Meaning: "Who did this? Where did this miracle come from? How could such a massive cart be completely shattered when there is no apparent cause to be seen here?" Saying thus, all those gazing upon You stood bewildered, placing their fingers on their noses in utter astonishment.

कुमारकस्यास्य पयोधरार्थिनः प्ररोदने लोलपदाम्बुजाहतम् ।
मया मया दृष्टमनो विपर्यगादितीश ते पालकबालका जगुः ॥42.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: कुमारकस्य अस्य of this tender child, पयोधर-अर्थिनः desiring milk, प्ररोदने in crying, लोल-पद-अम्बुज-आहतम् struck by the kicking of His lotus feet, मया मया by me by me, दृष्टम् अनः seen the cart, विपर्यगात् इति turned upside down thus, ईश O Lord, ते Your, पालक-बालकाः guardian playmates, जगुः spoke

Simple Meaning: "While this little child was crying for milk, the cart was struck by the kicking of His lotus feet! I saw it! I saw it turn upside down!" Thus, O Lord, Your young child guardians eagerly declared to everyone.

भिया तदा किञ्चिदजानतामिदं कुमारकाणामतिदुर्घटं वचः ।
भवत्प्रभावाविदुरैरितीरितं मनागिवाशङ्क्यत दृष्टपूतनैः ॥42.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: भिया out of fear, तदा then, किञ्चित् अजानताम् who do not know anything, इदम् this, कुमारकाणाम् of the young boys, अति-दुर्घटम् highly improbable, वचः word, भवत्-प्रभाव-अविदुरैः by those unaware of Your power, इति ईरितम् thus spoken, मनाक्-इव-आशङ्क्यत was doubted slightly, दृष्ट-पूतनैः by those who had seen Putana

Simple Meaning: Since the young boys were terrified and knew nothing, their statement was doubted as improbable by those unaware of Your true power. However, those who had seen the miracle of Putana's salvation suspected that it might indeed be true.

प्रवालताम्रं किमिदं पदं क्षतं सरोजरम्यौ नु करौ विरोजितौ।
इति प्रसर्पत्करुणातरङ्गितास्त्वदङ्गमापस्पृशुरङ्गनाजनाः ॥42.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रवाल-ताम्रम् red like fresh tender shoots, किम् is, इदम् this, पदम् foot, क्षतम् hurt, सरोज-रम्यौ tender and lovely like lotuses, नु indeed, करौ two hands, विरोजितौ pained, इति thus, प्रसर्पत्-करुणा-तरङ्गिताः filled with overflowing waves of compassion, त्वत्-अङ्गम् Your body, आपस्पृशुः touched gently, अङ्गना-जनाः the women of Vraja

Simple Meaning: "Has His foot, red like fresh tender shoots, been hurt? Have His hands, tender and lovely like lotuses, been pained?" Expressing such thoughts, the gopi women, overwhelmed with deep waves of affection and compassion, tenderly touched and caressed Your body.

अये सुतं देहि जगत्पतेः कृपातरङ्गपातात्परिपातमद्य मे ।
इति स्म सङ्गृह्य पिता त्वदङ्गकं मुहुर्मुहुः श्लिष्यति जातकण्टकः ॥42.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: अये oh, सुतम् child, देहि give, जगत्पतेः of the Lord of the universe, कृपा-तरङ्ग-पातात् by the shower of waves of mercy, परिपातम् saved, अद्य today, मे my, इति स्म thus, सङ्गृह्य gathering or taking, पिता father Nanda, त्वत्-अङ्गकम् Your body, मुहुः मुहुः repeatedly, श्लिष्यति embraced, जात-कण्टकः with hair standing on end in thrill

Simple Meaning: "Oh, give me my child! By the merciful grace of the Lord of the universe, He has been saved today!" Saying this, Your father Nanda took Your tender body into his arms and embraced You again and again, his hair standing on end with intense joy.

अनोनिलीनः किल हन्तुमागतः सुरारिरेवं भवता विहिंसितः ।
रजोऽपि नो दृष्टममुष्य तत्कथं स शुद्धसत्त्वे त्वयि लीनवान् ध्रुवम् ॥42.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: अनः-निलीनः hiding inside the cart, किल indeed, हन्तुम्-आगतः who had come to kill You, सुर-अरिः enemy of the suras, एवम् thus, भवता by You, विहिंसितः was slain, रजः-अपि even dust or the guna of rajas, नो not, दृष्टम् अमुष्य seen of his, तत्-कथम् how then, सः he, शुद्ध-सत्त्वे in pure sattva, त्वयि in You, लीनवान् merged, ध्रुवम् surely

Simple Meaning: Concealed within the cart, the asura who had come to kill You was thus slain by You. Yet, not even a trace of his dust (guna of rajas) was seen afterwards—how remarkable! Surely, he merged directly into You, who are the embodiment of pure sattva.

प्रपूजितैस्तत्र ततो द्विजातिभिर्विशेषतो लम्भितमङ्गलाशिषः ।
व्रजं निजैर्बाल्यरसैर्विमोहयन् मरुत्पुराधीश रुजां जहीहि मे ॥42.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रपूजितैः by the well worshiped, तत्र there, ततः then, द्विजातिभिः by the brahmanas, विशेषतः especially, लम्भित-मङ्गल-आशिषः made to receive auspicious blessings, व्रजम् Vraja, निजैः with Your own, बाल्य-रसैः playful childhood charms, विमोहयन् enchanting, मरुत्पुर-धीश O Lord of Guruvayur, रुजाम् disease or ailments, जहीहि remove, मे my

Simple Meaning: Having been bestowed with abundant auspicious blessings by the well-honored brahmanas assembled there, You enchanted all of Vraja with Your delightful childhood playfulness. O Lord of Guruvayur, please remove all my afflictions and diseases!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
