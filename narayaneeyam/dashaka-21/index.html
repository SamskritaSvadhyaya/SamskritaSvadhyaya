Dashaka 21

Worship in different places and forms

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 21

Worship in different places and forms

(छन्दस् - वसन्ततिलका)

मध्योद्भवे भुव इलावृतनाम्नि वर्षे
गौरीप्रधानवनिताजनमात्रभाजि ।
शर्वेण मन्त्रनुतिभिः समुपास्यमानं
सङ्कर्षणात्मकमधीश्वर संश्रये त्वाम् ॥21.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: मध्य-उद्भवे located in the middle, भुवः of the earth, इलावृत-नाम्नि named Ilavrita, वर्षे in the continent/region, गौरी-प्रधान-वनिता-जन-मात्र-भाजि inhabited solely by women led by Gauri (Parvati), शर्वेण by Sharva (Shiva), मन्त्र-नुतिभिः through sacred incantations and hymns, समुपास्यमानम् being intensely worshipped, सङ्कर्षण-आत्मकम् manifesting in the form of Sankarshana, अधीश्वर O Supreme Lord, संश्रये I take refuge, त्वाम् in You

Simple Meaning: In the central continent of the earth named Ilavritavarsha, which is inhabited exclusively by women with Devi Parvati as their leader, You are worshipped by Lord Shiva, through powerful mantras and hymns of praise in Your form as Sankarshana. O Supreme Sovereign, I take refuge in You!

भद्राश्वनामक इलावृतपूर्ववर्षे
भद्रश्रवोभिः ऋषिभिः परिणूयमानम् ।
कल्पान्तगूढनिगमोद्धरणप्रवीणं
ध्यायामि देव हयशीर्षतनुं भवन्तम् ॥21.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: भद्राश्व-नामके named Bhadrashva, इलावृत-पूर्व-वर्षे in the region east of Ilavrita, भद्रश्रवोभिः by Bhadrasravas, ॠषिभिः by the sages, परिणूयमानम् being praised, कल्प-अन्त-गूढ-निगम-उद्धरण-प्रवीणम् expert in recovering the Vedas hidden/lost at the end of the cosmic cycle, ध्यायामि I meditate, देव O Lord, हयशीर्ष-तनुम् the form with the head of a horse - Hayagriva, भवन्तम् on You

Simple Meaning: O Lord, in the region named Bhadrashva, which lies to the east of Ilavrita, You are being praised by the sage Bhadrashravas and his followers. I meditate on You in Your form of Hayagriva with the head of a horse, who displayed great expertise in retrieving the sacred Vedas that were lost at the end of the cosmic cycle.

ध्यायामि दक्षिणगते हरिवर्षवर्षे
प्रह्लादमुख्यपुरुषैः परिषेव्यमाणम् ।
उत्तुङ्गशान्तधवलाकृतिमेकशुद्ध-
ज्ञानप्रदं नरहरिं भगवन् भवन्तम् ॥21.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: ध्यायामि I meditate, दक्षिण-गते located in the south (of Ilavrita), हरिवर्ष-वर्षे in the region of Harivarsha, प्रह्लाद-मुख्य-पुरुषैः by people led by Prahlada, परिषेव्यमाणम् being devoutly served, उत्तुङ्ग-शान्त-धवल-आकृतिम् having a tall, serene, and white form, एक-शुद्ध-ज्ञान-प्रदम् the sole granter of pure spiritual knowledge, नरहरिम् Lord Narahari (Narasimha - Hari also means Lion), भगवन् O Lord, भवन्तम् on You

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Lord, I meditate on You in Your form as Lord Narahari in the realm known as Harivarsha to the south of Ilavrita. There, You possess a towering, serene, and luminous white form, and are reverently served by devotees led by Prahlada. You are the absolute granter of pure, unblemished knowledge—I fix my mind upon You!

वर्षे प्रतीचि ललितात्मनि केतुमाले
लीलाविशेषललितस्मितशोभनाङ्गम् ।
लक्ष्म्या प्रजापतिसुतैश्च निषेव्यमाणं
तस्याः प्रियाय धृतकामतनुं भजे त्वाम् ॥21.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: वर्षे in the region, प्रतीचि in the west (of Ilavrita), ललित-आत्मनि beautiful in nature, केतुमाले in Ketumala, लीला-विशेष-ललित-स्मित-शोभन-अङ्गम् having charming limbs made beautiful by a playful and gentle smile, लक्ष्म्या by Lakshmi, प्रजापति-सुतैः by the sons of Prajapati, च and, निषेव्यमाणम् being served/worshipped, तस्याः Her, प्रियाय for the delight, धृत-काम-तनुम् having assumed the form of Kamadeva, भजे I worship, त्वाम् You

Simple Meaning: In the beautiful region of Ketumala situated in the west of Ilavrita, You have assumed the enchanting form of Kamadeva with charming limbs made beautiful by playful and gentle smile, and are devoutly worshipped by Lakshmi and the sons of Prajapati. I meditate on that form of Yours, which You have taken to bring delight to Your beloved, Lakshmi!

रम्ये ह्युदीचि खलु रम्यकनाम्नि
वर्षे तद्वर्षनाथमनुवर्यसपर्यमाणम् ।
भक्तैकवत्सलममत्सरहृत्सु भान्तं
मत्स्याकृतिं भुवननाथ भजे भवन्तम् ॥21.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: रम्ये in the charming, हि indeed, उदीचि in the north (of Ilavrita), खलु certainly, रम्यक-नाम्नि named Ramyaka, वर्षे in the region, तत्-वर्ष-नाथ-मनु-वर्य-सपर्यमाणम् being worshipped by Vaivasvata Manu who is the ruler of that region, भक्त-एक-वत्सलम् who is supremely affectionate to devotees, अमत्सर-हृत्सु in pure hearts free from malice/jealousy, भान्तम् shining, मत्स्य-आकृतिम् having the form of Matsya, भुवन-नाथ O Lord of the universe, भजे I worship, भवन्तम् You

Simple Meaning: In the charming region called Ramyaka to the north of Ilavrita, You are devoutly worshipped by Vaivasvata Manu, the noble ruler of that realm. O Lord of the universe, You are exceptionally loving toward Your devotees and shine brightly in the hearts of those free from malice. I worship You in Your divine form as Matsya!

वर्षं हिरण्मयसमाह्वयमौत्तराहम्
आसीनमद्रिधृतिकर्मठकामठाङ्गम् ।
संसेवते पितृगणप्रवरोऽर्यमा यं
तं त्वां भजामि भगवन् परचिन्मयात्मन् ॥21.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: वर्षम् in the region, हिरण्मय-समाह्वयम् called Hiranmaya, औत्तराहम् in the north (of Ramyaka), आसीनम् seated, अद्रि-धृति-कर्मठ-कामठ-अङ्गम् having the body of a tortoise capable of supporting the mountain, संसेवते devoutly worships, पितृ-गण-प्रवरः the head of the pitris, अर्यमा Aryama, यम् whom, तम् that, त्वाम् You, भजामि I worship, भगवन् O Lord, पर-चित्-मय-आत्मन् whose essence is pure supreme consciousness

Simple Meaning: In the region named Hiranmaya which is further north to Ramyaka, You reside in the divine form of a tortoise, capable of supporting the Mandara mountain. There, You are devoutly worshipped by Aryama, the leader of the pitris. O Supreme Sovereign of pure consciousness, I offer my worship unto You!

किञ्चोत्तरेषु कुरुषु प्रियया धरण्या
संसेवितो महितमन्त्रनुतिप्रभेदैः ।
दंष्ट्राग्रघृष्टघनपृष्ठगरिष्ठवर्ष्मा
त्वं पाहि बिज्ञनुत यज्ञवराहमूर्ते ॥21.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: किञ्च and further, उत्तरेषु in the northern, कुरुषु in Kuru, प्रियया by Your beloved, धरण्या by Earth, संसेवितः being worshipped, महित-मन्त्र-नुति-प्रभेदैः with exalted mantras and various hymns of praise, दंष्ट्रा-अग्र-घृष्ट-घन-पृष्ठ-गरिष्ठ-वर्ष्मा having a colossal body whose massive back scraped against the clouds with the tip of Your tusk, त्वम् You, पाहि protect, विज्ञ-नुत praised by the wise, यज्ञ-वराह-मूर्ते O Lord in the form of Yagna-Varaha

Simple Meaning: Furthermore, in the northern region of Kuru, You are worshipped by Your beloved consort, Devi Earth, through sacred mantras and sublime hymns. Praised by wise seers, Your immense boar form possesses a majestic body whose back reached up to scrape the clouds as You lifted the earth on the tip of Your tusk. O Lord in the form of Yagna-Varaha, please protect me!

याम्यां दिशं भजति किंपुरुषाख्यवर्षे
संसेवितो हनुमता दृढभक्तिभाजा ।
सीताभिरामपरमाद्भुतरूपशाली
रामात्मकः परिलसन् परिपाहि विष्णो ॥21.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: याम्याम् in the southern, दिशम् direction, भजति in the realm located, किंपुरुष-आख्य-वर्षे in the region named Kimpurusha, संसेवितः worshipped, हनुमता by Hanuman, दृढ-भक्ति-भाजा who possesses unshakeable devotion, सीता-अभिराम-परम-अद्भुत-रूप-शाली possessing a supremely wonderful and enchanting form that captivated Sita, राम-आत्मकः being in the divine form of Rama, परिलसन् shining gloriously, परिपाहि protect completely, विष्णो O Lord Vishnu

Simple Meaning: In the southern region called Kimpurushavarsha, You shine gloriously in Your divine manifestation as Lord Rama, possessing an enchanting and wondrous form which captivated Sita. There, You are devoutly served by Hanuman, who is filled with steadfast devotion. O Supreme Lord Vishnu, please protect me completely!

श्रीनारदेन सह भारतखण्डमुख्यैः
त्वं साङ्ख्ययोगनुतिभिः समुपास्यमानः ।
आकल्पकालमिह साधुजनाभिरक्षी
नारायणो नरसखः परिपाहि भूमन् ॥21.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री नारदेन by divine Narada, सह along with, भारत-खण्ड-मुख्यैः by the prominent inhabitants of Bharatavarsha, त्वम् You, साङ्ख्य-योग-नुतिभिः through the praises of Sankhya and Yoga philosophies, समुपास्यमानः worshipped devoutly, आकल्प-कालम् until the end of the cosmic age, इह here, साधु-जन-अभिरक्षी protector of righteous people, नारायणः Narayana, नर-सखः friend of Nara, परिपाहि protect completely, भूमन् O Abundant One

Simple Meaning: O Abundant One! In the land of Bharatavarsha, You are devoutly worshipped by divine Narada along with the prominent inhabitants of this region through prayers rooted in Sankhya and Yoga philosophies. As Lord Narayana, the eternal friend of Nara, You protect the righteous until the end of the cosmic age. Please protect me completely!

प्लाक्षेऽर्करूपमयि शाल्मल इन्दुरूपं
द्वीपे भजन्ति कुशनामनि वह्निरूपम् ।
क्रौञ्चेऽम्बुरूपमथ वायुमयं च शाके
त्वां ब्रह्मरूपमपि पुष्करनाम्नि लोकाः ॥21.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्लाक्षे in Plaksha island, अर्क-रूपम् in the form of the Sun, अयि O Lord, शाल्मले in Shalmala island, इन्दु-रूपम् in the form of the Moon, द्वीपे in the island, भजन्ति worship, कुश-नामनि named Kusha, वह्नि-रूपम् in the form of Fire, क्रौञ्चे in Krauncha island, अम्बु-रूपम् in the form of Water, अथ then, वायु-मयम् in the form of Wind, च and, शाके in Shaka island, त्वाम् You, ब्रह्म-रूपम् in the form of Brahma, अपि also, पुष्कर-नामनि in the island named Pushkara, लोकाः the people

Simple Meaning: O Lord! People in various islands worship You in different divine forms. In Plaksha island they worship You as the Sun, in Shalmala island as the Moon, in Kusha island as Fire, in Krauncha island as Water, in Shaka island as Wind, and in Pushkara island as Brahma.

सर्वैर्ध्रुवादिभिरुडुप्रकरैर्ग्रहैश्च
पुच्छादिकेष्ववयवेष्वभिकल्प्यमानैः ।
त्वं शिंशुमारवपुषा महतामुपास्यः
सन्ध्यासु रुन्धि नरकं मम सिन्धुशायिन् ॥21.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: सर्वैः by all, ध्रुव-आदिभिः starting with Dhruva (the pole star), उडु-प्रकरैः by clusters of stars, ग्रहैः and by planets, च and, पुच्छ-आदिकेषु in the tail and other, अवयवेषु in the body parts, अभिकल्प्यमानैः being conceived, त्वम् You, शिंशुमार-वपुषा in the cosmic form of Shimshumara, महताम् by the great sages, उपास्यः object of worship, सन्ध्यासु during the twilight times, रुन्धि destroy, नरकम् hell/suffering, मम my, सिन्धु-शायिन् O Lord who reclines on the ocean

Simple Meaning: O Lord who reclines on the ocean! Great sages worship You during the twilight times in Your grand cosmic form of Shimshumara. In this form, all the stars, planets, and constellations, beginning with Dhruva (the pole star), are conceived as different parts of Your body, such as Your tail, limbs and other parts. O Lord, please destroy all my sufferings and protect me from hell!

पातालमूलभुवि शेषतनुं भवन्तं
लोलैककुण्डलविराजिसहस्रशीर्षम् ।
नीलाम्बरं धृतहलं भुजगाङ्गनाभिः
जुष्टं भजे हर गदान् गुरुगेहनाथ ॥21.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: पाताल-मूल-भुवि in the nethermost region of Patala, शेष-तनुम् in the divine form of Ananta Shesha, भवन्तम् You, लोल-एक-कुण्डल-विराजि-सहस्र-शीर्षम् having a thousand heads glowing with a single quivering earring, नील-अम्बरम् wearing blue garments, धृत-हलम् holding a plough, भुजग-अङ्गनाभिः by the serpent maidens, जुष्टम् accompanied, भजे I worship, हर remove, गदान् ailments or diseases, गुरु-गेह-नाथ O Lord of abode of Guru - Guruvayoor

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Guruvayoor! At the lowermost region of Patala, You exist in the form of the thousand-headed serpent Adishesha, adorned with a single swinging earring, wearing blue robes, holding a plough, and accompanied by serpent maidens. I worship You in this form. Please remove all my illnesses and ailments!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
