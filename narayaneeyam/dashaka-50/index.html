Krishna slays Vatsasura and Bakasura

दशकम् ५०

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 50

Krishna slays Vatsasura and Bakasura

तरलमधुकृत्वृन्दे वृन्दावनेऽथ मनोहरे पशुपशिशुभिः साकं वत्सानुपालनलोलुपः ।
हलधरसखो देव श्रीमन् विचेरिथ धारयन् गवलमुरलीवेत्रं नेत्राभिरामतनुद्युतिः ॥50.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: तरल-मधुकृत्-वृन्दे with swarms of hovering bees, वृन्दावने in Vrindavana, अथ then, मनोहरे charming, पशुप-शिशुभिः साकम् together with the cowherd children, वत्स-अनुपालन-लोलुपः eager to tend the calves, हलधर-सखः accompanied by Balarama (holder of the plough), देव O Divine Lord, श्रीमन् O Glorious One, विचेरिथ You wandered, धारयन् holding, गवल-मुरली-वेत्रम् a buffalo-horn bugle, a flute, and a cane, नेत्र-अभिराम-तनु-द्युतिः possessing a radiant form pleasing to the eyes

Simple Meaning: O Glorious Lord! Wandering in the enchanting forest of Vrindavana—resounding with swarms of hovering bees—You eagerly engaged in tending the calves along with Balarama and the other cowherd children. Holding a horn-bugle, a flute, and a cane, You moved about gracefully, Your radiant divine form captivating the eyes of all!

विहितजगतीरक्षं लक्ष्मीकराम्बुजलालितं ददति चरणद्वन्द्वं वृन्दावने त्वयि पावने ।
किमिव न बभौ सम्पत्सम्पूरितं तरुवल्लरीसलिलधरणीगोत्रक्षेत्रादिकं कमलापते ॥50.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: विहित-जगती-रक्षम् providing protection to the entire universe, लक्ष्मी-कर-अम्बुज-लालितम् tenderly caressed by the lotus hands of Goddess Lakshmi, ददति placing, चरण-द्वन्द्वम् Your pair of lotus feet, वृन्दावने in Vrindavana, त्वयि as You, पावने sanctified, किम् इव न बभौ what indeed did not shine, सम्पत्-सम्पूर्तम् filled with opulence and splendour, तरु-वल्लरी-सलिल-धरणी-गोत्र-क्षेत्र-आदिकम् the trees, creepers, waters, land, mountains, fields, and all else, कमला-पते O Consort of Goddess Lakshmi (Kamalapati)

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Kamala! When You placed Your pair of holy feet—which protect the whole world and are ever caressed by the lotus-soft hands of Goddess Lakshmi—upon the soil of Vrindavana and sanctified it, what in that realm did not burst into supreme glory? The trees, creepers, rivers, land, hills, and pastures—everything became overflowing with divine richness and beauty!

विलसदुलपे कान्तारान्ते समीरणशीतले विपुलयमुनातीरे गोवर्धनाचलमूर्धसु ।
ललितमुरलीनादः सञ्चारयन् खलु वात्सकं क्वचन दिवसे दैत्यं वत्साकृतिं त्वमुदैक्षथाः ॥50.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: विलसत्-उलपे where lush green grass shines delightfully, कान्तार-अन्ते inside the forest edges, समीरण-शीतले cooled by gentle breezes, विपुल-यमुना-तीरे on the vast banks of river Yamuna, गोवर्धन-अचल-मूर्धसु on the peaks of the Govardhana hill, ललित-मुरली-नादः creating sweet notes on Your flute, सञ्चारयन् leading and grazing, खलु indeed, वात्सकम् the young calves, क्वचन दिवसे on a certain day, दैत्यम् a daitya, वत्स-आकृतिम् who had taken the form of a calf, त्वम् You, उदैक्षथाः noticed

Simple Meaning: O Lord! On a certain day, as You moved about grazing the young calves amidst the lush lush green grass at the edge of the forest, on the cool breeze swept vast banks of river Yamuna, and upon the high peaks of Mount Govardhana, while playing sweet melodies on Your flute, You noticed a daitya who had disguised himself as a calf.

रभसविलसत्पुच्छं विच्छायतोऽस्य विलोकयन् किमपि वलितस्कन्धं रन्ध्रप्रतीक्षमुदीक्षितम् ।
तमथ चरणे बिभ्रद्विभ्रामयन् मुहुरुच्चकैः कुहचन महावृक्षे चिक्षेपिथ क्षतजीवितम् ॥50.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: रभस-विलसत्-पुच्छम् whose tail was moving rapidly with excitement, विच्छायतः from a hidden position, अस्य of this daitya, विलोकयन् observing, किमपि somewhat, वलित-स्कन्धम् with a turned neck, रन्ध्र-प्रतीक्षम् waiting for an opportune moment to strike, उदीक्षितम् looking at You, तम् him, अथ then, चरणे by his hind legs, बिभ्रत् holding, विभ्रामयन् swirling, मुहुः repeatedly, उच्चकैः high up into the air, कुहचन onto a certain, महा-वृक्षे large tree, चिक्षेपिथ You hurled, क्षत-जीवितम् destroying his life

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Observing this daitya from your hidden position as his tail wagged excitedly and his neck was turned waiting for a chance to attack You, You caught him firmly by his hind legs, swirled him repeatedly high above in the air, and hurled him onto a large tree, instantly ending his life.

निपतति महादैत्ये जात्या दुरात्मनि तत्क्षणं निपतनजवक्षुण्णक्षोणीरुहक्षतकानने ।
दिवि परिमिलत् वृन्दा वृन्दारकाः कुसुमोत्करैः शिरसि भवतो हर्षाद्वर्षन्ति नाम तदा हरे ॥50.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: निपतति when falling down, महा-दैत्ये the great daitya, जात्या by birth, दुरात्मनि evil natured, तत्क्षणम् that very moment, निपतन-जव-क्षुण्ण-क्षोणीरुह-क्षत-कानने where the forest was damaged as trees were crushed by the impact of his heavy fall, दिवि in the sky, परिमिलत्-वृन्दाः gathering in groups, वृन्दारकाः the devas, कुसुम-उत्करैः with showers of flowers, शिरसि upon the head, भवतः Your, हर्षात् out of immense joy, वर्षन्ति नाम showered indeed, तदा then, हरे O Lord Hari

वृन्दारक  वृन्दारकः पुं. - 'देव, सुर'  src. Kosha, Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: O Lord Hari! When that naturally evil and mighty daitya crashed down, crushing trees and causing destruction to the forest by the sheer force of his impact, groups of devas assembled in the skies and joyfully showered beautiful flowers upon Your head.

सुरभिलतमा मूर्धन्यूर्ध्वं कुतः कुसुमावली निपतति तवेत्युक्तो बालैः सहेलमुदैरयः ।
झटिति दनुजक्षेपेणोर्ध्वं गतस्तरुमण्डलात् कुसुमनिकरः सोऽयं नूनं समेति शनैरिति ॥50.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुरभिलतमा most fragrant, मूर्ध्नि upon Your head, ऊर्ध्वम् from above, कुतः from where, कुसुम-आवली the shower of flowers, निपतति is falling, तव Your, इति thus, उक्तः being asked, बालैः by the cowherd boys, स-हेलम् playfully, उदैरयः You replied, झटिति quickly, दनुज-क्षेपेण by the throwing of the danuja, ऊर्ध्वम् upward, गतः gone, तरु-मण्डलात् from the foliage of the trees, कुसुम-निकरः the multitude of flowers, सः अयम् this very same, नूनम् surely, समेति is coming down, शनैः slowly, इति thus

Simple Meaning: When the astonished cowherd boys asked You from where such extremely fragrant flowers were falling upon Your head from the sky, You playfully replied that when You threw the danuja high up, the flowers from the treetops were knocked into the air and were now merely falling back down slowly.

क्वचन दिवसे भूयो भूयस्तरे परुषातपे तपनतनयापाथः पातुं गता भवदादयः ।
चलितगरुतं प्रेक्षामासुर्बकं खलु विस्म्रृतं क्षितिधरगरुच्छेदे कैलासशैलमिवापरम् ॥50.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्वचन दिवसे on a certain day, भूयः भूयस्तरे in excessive and overwhelming, परुष-आतपे intense heat of the sun, तपन-तनया-पाथः the waters of Yamuna - the daughter of the sun god, पातुम् to drink, गताः having gone, भवत्-आदयः You and Your friends, चलित-गरुतम् flapping its wings, प्रेक्षामासुः saw, बकम् a huge crane, खलु indeed, विस्मृतम् forgotten, क्षितिधर-गरुत्-छेदे during the cutting of the wings of the mountains, कैलास-शैलम् इव अपरम् like another Kailasa mountain

Simple Meaning: On a certain day, when the heat of the sun was exceedingly fierce, You and Your companions went to quench Your thirst with the waters of the river Yamuna. There You all saw a gigantic crane flapping its wings, looking like another Kailasa mountain that Indra had somehow forgotten to clip during the ancient cutting of the wings of the mountains.

पिबति सलिलं गोपव्राते भवन्तमभिद्रुतः स किल निगिलन्नग्निप्रख्यं पुनर्द्रुतमुद्वमन् ।
दलयितुमगात् त्रोट्याः कोट्या तदाऽऽशु भवान् विभो खलजनभिदाचुञ्चुश्चञ्चू प्रगृह्य ददार तम् ॥50.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: पिबति while drinking, सलिलम् water, गोप-व्राते the group of cowherd boys, भवन्तम् towards You, अभिद्रुतः rushing, सः he, किल indeed, निगिलन् swallowing You, अग्नि-प्रख्यम् hot as fire, पुनः again, द्रुतम् quickly, उद्वमन् spitting You out, दलयितुम् to tear apart, अगात् approached, त्रोट्याः of his beak, कोट्या with the sharp tip, तदा then, आशु swiftly, भवान् You, विभो O Almighty Lord, खल-जन-भिदा-चुञ्चुः expert in destroying wicked beings, चञ्चू both halves of his beak, प्रगृह्य grabbing hold of, ददार tore apart, तम् him

Simple Meaning: O Almighty Lord! While the cowherd boys were drinking water, that asura rushed towards You and swallowed You up. But feeling as if he had swallowed a burning fire, he quickly vomited You out and tried to split You apart with the sharp edge of his beak. Thereupon, You, who are ever adept at destroying evil beings, caught both halves of his beak with Your hands and tore him asunder in an instant.

सपदि सहजां सन्द्रष्टुं वा मृतां खलु पूतनामनुजमघमप्यग्रे गत्वा प्रतीक्षितुमेव वा ।
शमननिलयं याते तस्मिन् बके सुमनोगणे किरति सुमनोवृन्दं वृन्दावनात् गृहमैयथाः ॥50.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: सपदि immediately, सहजाम् sister, सन्द्रष्टुम् to meet, वा or, मृताम् who had died, खलु indeed, पूतनाम् Putana, अनुजम् अघम् अपि and also his younger brother Aghasura, अग्रे गत्वा going ahead, प्रतीक्षितुम् एव वा or to await his arrival, शमन-निलयम् to the abode of Yama, याते having gone, तस्मिन् बके when that Baka, सुमनाः गणे while groups of devas, किरति were showering, सुमनाः वृन्दम् a heap of flowers, वृन्दावनात् from Vrindavana, गृहम् ऐयथाः You returned home

Simple Meaning: O Lord! When that Bakasura reached the abode of Yama—perhaps to meet his deceased sister Putana or to go ahead and await his younger brother Aghasura—the devas showered a multitude of flowers from above, and You returned home safely from Vrindavana with Your companions.

ललितमुरलीनादं दूरान्निशम्य वधूजनैः स्त्वरितमुपगम्यारादारूढमोदमुदीक्षितः ।
जनितजननीनन्दानन्दः समीरणमन्दिरप्रथितवसते शौरे दूरीकुरुष्व ममामयान् ॥50.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: ललित-मुरली-नादम् the sweet notes of Your flute, दूरात् निशम्य hearing from afar, वधूजनैः by the gopis, त्वरितम् उपगम्य eagerly approaching, आरात् from nearby, आरूढ-मोदम्-उदीक्षितः with overflowing bliss, gazed upon, जनित-जननी-नन्द-आनन्दः who brought immense joy to Your mother Yashoda and father Nanda, समीरण-मन्दिर-प्रथित-वसते O Lord who famously resides in the temple of Guruvayur, शौरे O Lord Krishna, दूरीकुरुष्व please remove, मम आमयान् all my afflictions and illnesses

Simple Meaning: O Lord Krishna! Hearing the charming notes of Your flute from afar, the gopis eagerly rushed to You and gazed upon You from close by with profound bliss, who filled Your mother Yashoda and father Nanda with boundless joy! O Lord who famously resides in the holy temple of Guruvayur! Please cure and eradicate all my illnesses and afflictions.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
