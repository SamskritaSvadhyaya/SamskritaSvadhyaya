॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 37

Prelude to the incarnation of Krishna

सान्द्रानन्दतनो हरे ननु पुरा दैवासुरे सङ्गरे
त्वत्कृत्ता अपि कर्मशेषवशतो ये ते न याता गतिम् ।
तेषां भूतलजन्मनां दितिभुवां भारेण दूरार्दिता
भूमिः प्राप विरिञ्चमाश्रितपदं देवैः पुरैवागतैः॥37.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: सान्द्र-आनन्द-तनो O Embodiment of Dense Bliss, हरे O Lord Hari, ननु indeed, पुरा in ancient times, दैव-असुरे-सङ्गरे in the war between the devas and asuras, त्वत्-कृत्ताः severed/slain by You, अपि even though, कर्म-शेष-वशतः due to the residue of their past karma, ये those who, ते they, न composition/not, याताः attained, गतिम् final liberation, तेषाम् of them, भूतल-जन्मनाम् born on the surface of the earth, दिति-भुवाम् of those asuras (sons of Diti), भारेण by the crushing burden, दूर-आर्दिता deeply distressed, भूमिः Mother Earth, प्राप approached, विरिञ्चम् Lord Brahma, आश्रित-पदम् who is the shelter for all, देवैः along with the devas, पुरा एव आगतैः who had already arrived before her

Simple Meaning: O Lord Hari, the very embodiment of dense supreme bliss! In the ancient wars between the devas and asuras, many asuras were slain by You. Yet, due to the remnants of their past karma, they did not attain liberation and instead took rebirth on earth as tyrannical kings. Deeply distressed by the heavy burden of these asuras, Mother Earth sought refuge in Lord Brahma, who was already surrounded by the devas who had arrived before her seeking relief.

हा हा दुर्जनभूरिभारमथितां पाथोनिधौ पातुकाम्
एतां पालय हन्त मे विवशतां सम्पृच्छ देवानिमान् ।
इत्यादिप्रचुरप्रलापविवशामालोक्य धाता महीं
देवानां वदनानि वीक्ष्य परितो दध्यौ भवन्तं हरे ॥37.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: हा हा alas alas, दुर्जन-भूरि-भार-मथिताम् crushed by the heavy burden of evil men, पाथोनिधौ into the ocean, पातुकाम् about to sink, एताम् this earth, पालय protect, हन्त alas, मे my, विवशताम् helpless state, सम्पृच्छ ask, देवान् इमान् these devas here, इति-आदि-प्रचुर-प्रलाप-विवशाम् who was helpless with numerous such lamentations, आलोक्य seeing, धाता Lord Brahma, महीम् Mother Earth, देवानाम् of the devas, वदनानि the faces, वीक्ष्य looking at, परितः all around, दध्यौ meditated upon, भवन्तम् You, हरे O Lord Hari

Simple Meaning: Alas alas, protect this earth which is about to sink into the ocean, crushed by the unbearable burden of wicked men! Alas, inquire about my helpless condition from these devas present here! Seeing Mother Earth thus overwhelmed with grief and lamenting in so many ways, Lord Brahma looked around at the faces of the devas and began to deeply meditate upon You, O Lord Hari!

ऊचे चाम्बुजभूरमूनयि सुराः सत्यं धरित्र्या वचो
नन्वस्या भवतां च रक्षणविधौ दक्षो हि लक्ष्मीपतिः ।
सर्वे शर्वपुरस्सरा वयमितो गत्वा पयोवारिधिं
नत्वा तं स्तुमहे जवादिति ययुः साकं तवाकेतनम् ॥37.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऊचे spoke, च and, अम्बुजभूः Brahma (born from the lotus), अमून् to those, अयि O, सुराः devas, सत्यम् true, धरित्र्याः of Mother Earth, वचः statement, ननु indeed, अस्याः of her, भवताम् of you all, च and, रक्षण-विधौ in the matter of protection, दक्षः capable/competent, हि indeed, लक्ष्मीपतिः the consort of Lakshmi (Vishnu), सर्वे all of us, शर्व-पुरस्सराः led by Sharva (Shiva), वयम् we, इतः from here, गत्वा having gone, पयो-वारिधिम् to the milk ocean, नत्वा bowing down, तम् Him, स्तुमहे let us praise/pray, जवात् quickly, इति thus, ययुः they went, साकम् together, तव आकेतनम् to Your divine abode

Simple Meaning: Brahma addressed the devas, saying, O devas, the words spoken by Mother Earth are completely true! Indeed, only the Lord of Lakshmi is capable of protecting both her and all of you. Led by Shiva, let us all go at once to the Ocean of Milk, bow down to Him, and offer our prayers. Saying this, they all proceeded together to Your divine abode.

ते मुग्धानिलशालिदुग्धजलधेस्तीरं गताः सङ्गता
यावत्त्वत्पदचिन्तनैकमनसस्तावत् स पाथोजभूः ।
त्वद्वाचं हृदये निशम्य सकलानानन्दयन्नूचिवान्
आख्यातः परमात्मना स्वयमहं वाक्यं तदाकर्ण्यताम् ॥37.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: ते they, मुग्धा-अनिल-शालि-दुग्ध-जलधेः of the milk ocean served by gentle breezes, तीरम् to the shore, गताः having reached, सङ्गताः gathered together, यावत् while, त्वत्-पद-चिन्तन-एक-मनसः with minds focused solely on meditating upon Your lotus feet, तावत् then, सः that, पाथोजभूः Lord Brahma (born from the lotus), त्वत्-वाचम् Your divine message, हृदये in his heart, निशम्य having heard, सकलान् all the devas, आनन्दयन् delighting, ऊचिवान् spoke, आख्यातः informed, परमात्मना by the Supreme Soul, स्वयम् Himself, अहम् I, वाक्यम् words/message, तत् that, आकर्ण्यताम् please listen to

Simple Meaning: Having gathered together on the shores of the Ocean of Milk, where gentle breezes blew, they all focused their minds entirely on meditating upon Your divine feet. Thereupon, Lord Brahma heard Your inner voice within his heart. Delighting all the devas, he spoke to them: I have been directly informed by the Supreme Self Himself. Please listen to His divine message!

जाने दीनदशामहं दिविषदां भूमेश्च भीमैर्नृपैः
तत्क्षेपाय भवामि यादवकुले सोऽहं समग्रात्मना ।
देवा वृष्णिकुले भवन्तु कलया देवाङ्गनाश्चावनौ
मत्सेवार्थमिति त्वदीयवचनं पाथोजभूरूचिवान् ॥37.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: जाने I know, दीन-दशाम् the miserable condition, अहम् I, दिविषदाम् of the devas, भूमेः of the earth, च and, भीमैः by the terrifying, नृपैः kings, तत्-क्षेपाय for removing that suffering, भवामि I shall be born, यादव-कुले in the Yadava dynasty, सः अहम् I Myself, समग्र-आत्मना with My full essence/complete avatar, देवाः the devas, वृष्णि-कुले in the Vrishni clan, भवन्तु let them be born, कलया through partial incarnations, देव-अङ्गनाः च and the wives of the devas, अवनौ on the earth, मत्-सेवा-अर्थम् for the purpose of serving Me, इति thus, त्वदीय-वचनम् Your divine message, पाथोजभूः Lord Brahma, ऊचिवान् spoke

Simple Meaning: Lord Brahma relayed Your divine message to the gods: I am fully aware of the dire distress of the devas and Mother Earth caused by those tyrannical kings. To remove their burden, I Myself shall incarnate in all My complete essence in the Yadava dynasty. Let the devas and their wives be born on earth in their partial incarnations in the Vrishni clan, to serve Me.

श्रुत्वा कर्णरसायनं तव वचः सर्वेषु निर्वापित-
स्वान्तेष्वीश गतेषु तावककृपापीयूषतृप्तात्मसु ।
विख्याते मधुरापुरे किल भवत्सान्निध्यपुण्योत्तरे
धन्यां देवकनन्दनामुदवहद्राजा स शूरात्मजः ॥37.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रुत्वा having heard, कर्ण-रसायनम् nectar to the ears, तव Your, वचः message, सर्वेषु when all, निर्वापित-स्वान्तेषु with their hearts completely pacified, ईश O Lord, गतेषु had departed, तावक-कृपा-पीयूष-तृप्त-आत्मसु with their souls deeply satisfied by the nectar of Your grace, विख्याते in the renowned, मधुरा-पुरे city of Mathura, किल indeed, भवत्-सान्निध्य-पुण्य-उत्तरे exceedingly sanctified by Your divine presence, धन्याम् the blessed, देवक-नन्दनाम् Devaki (the daughter of Devaka), उदवहत् married, राजा King, सः he (Vasudeva), शूर-आत्मजः the son of Shura

Simple Meaning: O Lord, having heard Your divine message, which was sheer nectar to their ears, the devas felt deeply pacified in their hearts and satisfied by the nectar of Your grace, and they departed. Thereafter, in the renowned city of Mathura—a holy realm exceedingly blessed by Your divine presence—the noble Vasudeva, son of King Shura, married the blessed princess Devaki.

उद्वाहावसितौ तदीयसहजः कंसोऽथ सम्मानयन्
एतौ सूततया गतः पथि रथे व्योमोत्थया त्वद्गिरा ।
अस्यास्त्वामतिदुष्टमष्टमसुतो हन्तेति हन्तेरितः
सन्त्रासात् स तु हन्तुमन्तिकगतां तन्वीं कृपाणीमधात् ॥37.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: उद्वाह-अवसितौ at the conclusion of the wedding ceremony, तदीय-सहजः her brother, कंसः Kamsa, अथ then, सम्मानयन् honoring/showing respect to, एतौ these two (Devaki and Vasudeva), सूततया as the chariot driver, गतः proceeding, पथि on the way, रथे on the chariot, व्योम-उत्थया arising from the sky, त्वत्-गिरा by Your divine voice (a celestial voice), अस्याः of her, त्वाम् you, अतिदुष्टम् who are extremely wicked, अष्टम-सुतः eighth son, हन्ता will be the killer, इति thus, हन्त alas, इरिता pronounced, सन्त्रासात् out of extreme fear, सः he (Kamsa), तु indeed, हन्तुम् to kill, अन्तिक-गताम् who was standing nearby, तन्वीम् the slender lady (Devaki), कृपाणीम् sword, अधात् drew

Simple Meaning: At the conclusion of the wedding, Devaki's brother Kamsa, wishing to honor the newlyweds, personally acted as their chariot driver. As they proceeded along the way, Your divine voice echoed from the sky, proclaiming: O wicked Kamsa, the eighth son of this very sister shall be your killer! Stricken with sudden terror upon hearing this prophecy, Kamsa drew his sword to slay the helpless, slender Devaki right there on the chariot.

गृह्णानश्चिकुरेषु तां खलमतिः शौरेश्चिरं सान्त्वनैः
नो मुञ्चन् पुनरात्मजार्पणगिरा प्रीतोऽथ यातो गृहान् ।
आद्यं त्वत्सहजं तथाऽर्पितमपि स्नेहेन नाहन्नसौ
दुष्टानामपि देव पुष्टकरुणा दृष्टा हि धीरेकदा ॥37.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: गृह्णानः seizing, चिकुरेषु by the hair, ताम् her (Devaki), खल-मतिः that evil-minded one (Kamsa), शौरेः of Vasudeva, चिरम् for a long time, सान्त्वनैः by persuasive entreaties, नो not, मुञ्चन् releasing, पुनः again, आत्मज-अर्पण-गिरा by the solemn promise to surrender each child, प्रीतः pleased/satisfied, अथ then, यातः went, गृहान् to his palace, आद्यम् the first, त्वत्-सहजम् Your sibling (child), तथा in that manner, अर्पितम् presented, अपि even though, स्नेहेन out of affection/fondness, न not, अहन् killed, असौ he (Kamsa), दुष्टानाम् of wicked people, अपि even, देव O Lord, पुष्ट-करुणा abundant compassion, दृष्टा seen, हि indeed, धीः mindset, एकदा at times

Simple Meaning: Seizing Devaki by her hair, the evil-minded Kamsa refused to release her despite Vasudeva's prolonged entreaties. However, when Vasudeva solemnly promised to hand over every newborn child to him, Kamsa was satisfied and returned to his palace. When the firstborn child was presented to him as promised, Kamsa chose not to kill the baby out of a momentary feeling of affection. O Lord, even in the minds of the wicked, a sudden flicker of deep compassion is sometimes seen!

तावत्त्वन्मनसैव नारदमुनिः प्रोचे स भोजेश्वरं
यूयं नन्वसुराः सुराश्च यदवो जानासि किं न प्रभो ।
मायावी स हरिर्भवद्वधकृते भावी सुरप्रार्थनात्
इत्याकर्ण्य यदूनदूधुनदसौ शौरेश्च सूनूनहन् ॥37.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: तावत् then, त्वत्-मनसा एव by Your divine will alone, नारद-मुनिः Sage Narada, प्रोचे spoken, सः he, भोज-ईश्वरम् to the king of the Bhojas (Kamsa), यूयम् you all, ननु indeed, असुराः are asuras, सुराः च and the devas, यदवः are the Yadavas, जानासि do you know, किम् whether, न not, प्रभो O Lord/King, मायावी the master of illusion, सः he, हरिः Lord Hari, भवत्-वध-कृते for the purpose of killing you, भावी will appear/be born, सुर-प्रार्थनात् due to the prayers of the devas, इति thus, आकर्ण्य having heard, यदून् the Yadavas, अदूधुनत् tormenting/persecuting, असौ he (Kamsa), शौरेः of Vasudeva, च and, सूनून् sons, अहन् killed

Simple Meaning: Guided by Your divine will, Sage Narada approached Kamsa and warned him: O King, do you not realize that you and your allies are reincarnated asuras, while the Yadavas are devas? Lord Hari, the master of cosmic illusion, is soon to be born in response to the devas' prayers specifically to slay you! Upon hearing this, Kamsa became terrified and began persecuting the Yadavas, while mercilessly killing each newborn son of Vasudeva as they were presented to him.

प्राप्ते सप्तमगर्भतामहिपतौ त्वत्प्रेरणान्मायया
नीते माधव रोहिणीं त्वमपि भोःसच्चित्सुखैकात्मकः ।
देवक्या जठरं विवेशिथ विभो संस्तूयमानः सुरैः
स त्वं कृष्ण विधूय रोगपटलीं भक्तिं परां देहि मे ॥37.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्राप्ते when reached, सप्तम-गर्भताम् state of being seventh pregnancy, अहि-पतौ when Adishesha (the lord of serpents), त्वत्-प्रेरणात् by Your command, मायया by Yogamaya, नीते was transferred, माधव O Madhava, रोहिणीम् to (the womb of) Rohini, त्वम् You, अपि also, भोः O Lord, सत्-चित्-सुख-एक-आत्मकः whose nature is pure Existence, Consciousness, and Bliss, देवक्याः of Devaki, जठरम् the womb, विवेशिथ entered, विभो O All-pervading Lord, संस्तूयमानः being praised, सुरैः by the gods, सः that, त्वम् You, कृष्ण O Krishna, विधूय dispelling/removing, रोग-पटलीम् the multitude of my ailments, भक्तिम् devotion, पराम् supreme, देहि bestow, मे unto me

Simple Meaning: O Madhava! At the seventh pregnancy, when the king of serpents, Aadishesha entered the womb of Devaki,  he was transferred by Yogamaya to the womb of Rohini under Your divine direction. Thereafter, O All-Pervading Lord, You—whose true nature is absolute Existence, Consciousness, and Bliss—entered the womb of Devaki while being gloriously praised by the devas. O Lord Krishna, please dispel the entire multitude of my diseases and bestow upon me supreme devotion to You!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
