Dashaka 57

Balarama slays Pralambasura

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 57

Balarama slays Pralambasura

रामसखः क्वापि दिने कामद भगवन् गतो भवान् विपिनम् ।
सूनुभिरपि गोपानां धेनुभिरभिसंवृतो लसद्वेषः ॥57.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: राम-सखः accompanied by Your brother Balarama, क्वापि दिने on a certain day, कामद O Fulfiller of Desires, भगवन् O Blessed Lord, गतः went, भवान् You, विपिनम् to the forest, सूनुभिः अपि along with the sons, गोपानाम् of the cowherds, धेनुभिः-अभिसंवृतः surrounded by the cows, लसत्-वेषः splendidly dressed

Simple Meaning: O Blessed Lord, O Fulfiller of Desires! On a certain day, You went into the forest accompanied by Your brother Balarama, surrounded by the cows and the sons of the cowherds, looking exceptionally splendid in Your fine attire.

सन्दर्शयन् बलाय स्वैरं वृन्दावनश्रियं विमलाम् ।
काण्डीरैः सह बालैर्भाण्डीरकमागमो वटं क्रीडन् ॥57.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: सन्दर्शयन् pointing out, बलाय to Balarama, स्वैरम् freely, वृन्दावन-श्रियम् the beauty of Vrindavan, विमलाम् pure and pristine, काण्डीरैः सह along with armed, बालैः boys, भाण्डीरकम् आगमः You arrived at Bhandiraka, वटम् the banyan tree, क्रीडन् playing merrily

Simple Meaning: Pointing out the pure and pristine beauty of the Vrindavan forest to Your brother Balarama, You played merrily along the way with the boys who were playfully armed with sticks, and arrived at the Bhandiraka banyan tree.

तावत्तावकनिधनस्पृहयालुर्गोपमूर्तिरदयालुः ।
दैत्यः प्रलम्बनामा प्रलम्बबाहुं भवन्तमापेदे ॥57.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तावत् at that time, तावक-निधन-स्पृहयालुः eager for Your destruction, गोप-मूर्तिः disguised as a cowherd, अदयालुः merciless, दैत्यः daitya, प्रलम्ब-नामा named Pralamba, प्रलम्ब-बाहुम् You who have long arms, भवन्तम् आपेदे approached You

Simple Meaning: At that time, a merciless daitya named Pralamba, who eagerly desired Your destruction, approached You, who have long arms, disguised in the form of a cowherd boy.

जानन्नप्यविजानन्निव तेन समं निबद्धसौहार्दः ।
वटनिकटे पटुपशुपव्याबद्धं द्वन्द्वयुद्धमारब्धाः ॥57.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: जानन् अपि even though knowing his true nature, अविजानन् इव acting as if unaware, तेन समम् with him, निबद्ध-सौहार्दः establishing friendship, वट-निकटे near the banyan tree, पटु-पशुप-व्याबद्धम् arranged by the skilled cowherd boys, द्वन्द्व-युद्धम् wrestling duel, आरब्धाः You started

Simple Meaning: Even though You knew his true identity, You acted as if You were unaware and established a friendly rapport with him. Right near the banyan tree, You started the wrestling duel organized by the skilled cowherd boys.

गोपान् विभज्य तन्वन् सङ्घं बलभद्रकं भवत्कमपि ।
त्वद्बलभीरुं दैत्यं त्वद्बलगतमन्वमन्यथा भगवन् ॥57.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: गोपान् the cowherds, विभज्य dividing, तन्वन् forming, सङ्घम् teams, बलभद्रकम् (consisting) of Balarama's (side), भवत्कम् अपि and (consisting of) Your (side), त्वत्-बल-भीरुम् afraid of Your strength, दैत्यम् the daitya, त्वत्-बल-गतम् joining Your team, अन्वमन्यथा You permitted, भगवन् O Blessed Lord

Simple Meaning: O Blessed Lord, dividing the cowherd boys into two teams—one led by Balarama and the other by Yourself—You allowed the daitya to join Your team, as he was terrified of Your immense strength and preferred to stay on Your side.

कल्पितविजेतृवहने समरे परयूथगं स्वदयिततरम् ।
श्रीदामानमधत्थाः पराजितो भक्तदासतां प्रथयन् ॥57.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: कल्पित-विजेतृ-वहने where carrying the winner was agreed upon, समरे in the game, पर-यूथ-गम् belonging to the opponent team, स्व-दयित-तरम् Your most beloved, श्रीदामानम् Shridama, अधत्थाः You carried, पराजितः defeated, भक्त-दासताम् Your role as the servant of Your devotees, प्रथयन् proclaiming

Simple Meaning: In that game, where the condition was that the defeated player must carry the winner on his back, You intentionally suffered defeat and carried Your beloved friend Shridama, who was on the opposing team, thus demonstrating Your willingness to become a servant to Your devotees.

एवं बहुषु विभूमन् बालेषु वहत्सु वाह्यमानेषु ।
रामविजितः प्रलम्बो जहार तं दूरतो भवद्भीत्या ॥57.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this manner, बहुषु as many, विभूमन् O All-Pervading Lord, बालेषु boys, वहत्सु were carrying, वाह्यमानेषु were being carried, राम-विजितः defeated by Balarama, प्रलम्बः Pralamba, जहार carried away, तम् him Balarama, दूरतः far away, भवत्-भीत्या out of fear of You

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord, while many boys were thus carrying and being carried on each other's backs, Pralamba was defeated in the game by Balarama. Required to carry him, Pralamba swiftly ran far away with Balarama on his back, driven by his intense fear of You.

त्वद्दूरं गमयन्तं तं दृष्ट्वा हलिनि विहितगरिमभरे ।
दैत्यः स्वरूपमागाद्यद्रूपात् स हि बलोऽपि चकितोऽभूत् ॥57.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-दूरम् far away from You, गमयन्तम् carrying away, तम् him Balarama, दृष्ट्वा seeing, हलिनि when Balarama the wielder of the plough, विहित-गरिम-भरे made himself extremely heavy, दैत्यः the daitya, स्वरूपम् आगात् assumed his real form, यत्-रूपात् from which form, सः he, हि indeed, बलः अपि even Balarama, चकितः अभूत् became alarmed

Simple Meaning: Seeing that he was being carried far away from You, Balarama made his body immensely heavy. Unable to bear the burden, the daitya shed his disguise and assumed his terrifying huge real form, seeing which even the mighty Balarama was momentarily startled.

उच्चतया दैत्यतनोस्त्वन्मुखमालोक्य दूरतो रामः ।
विगतभयो दृढमुष्ट्या भृशदुष्टं सपदि पिष्टवानेनम् ॥57.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: उच्चतया owing to the towering height, दैत्य-तनौः of the daitya's body, त्वत्-मुखम् Your face, आलोक्य beholding, दूरतः from a distance, रामः Balarama, विगत-भयः freed from all fear, दृढ-मुष्ट्या with a firm fist, भृश-दुष्टम् the extremely wicked one, सपदि immediately, पिष्टवान् एनम् crushed him

Simple Meaning: Because of being carried by the daitya on his huge body, Balarama could see Your reassuring face even from a distance. Instantly losing all fear, Balarama struck the extremely wicked Pralamba with his powerful fist, crushing him to death on the spot.

हत्वा दानववीरं प्राप्तं बलमालिलिङ्गिथ प्रेम्णा ।
तावन्मिलतोर्युवयोः शिरसि कृता पुष्पवृष्टिरमरगणैः ॥57.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: हत्वा having killed, दानव-वीरम् the mighty danava hero Pralamba, प्राप्तम् returning, बलम् Balarama, आलिलिङ्गिथ You embraced, प्रेम्णा with deep affection, तावत् thereupon, मिलतोः युवयोः as the two of You met, शिरसि upon Your heads, कृता was made, पुष्प-वृष्टिः a rain of flowers, अमर-गणैः by the hosts of devas

Simple Meaning: Having slain the mighty danava hero, Balarama returned to You, and You embraced him with deep affection. As the two of You met, the hosts of devas showered a rain of fragrant flowers upon Your heads from the heavens.

आलम्बो भुवनानां प्रालम्बं निधनमेवमारचयन् ।
कालं विहाय सद्यो लोलम्बरुचे हरे हरेः क्लेशान् ॥57.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: आलम्बः the support, भुवनानाम् of all the worlds, प्रालम्बम् of Pralamba, निधनम् the destruction, एवम् in this manner, आरचयन् bringing about, कालम् time, विहाय leaving aside, सद्यः instantly, लोलम्ब-रुचे O One with the dark luster of a black bee, हरे O Lord Hari, हरेः remove, क्लेशान् my afflictions

Simple Meaning: O Lord, You are the sole support of all the worlds! Having thus brought about the destruction of Pralamba through Balarama without waiting for time to take its course, O One whose luster is as dark and captivating as a black bee, O Lord Hari, please remove my afflictions instantly!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
