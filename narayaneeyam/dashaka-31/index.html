Vamana blesses Bali

दशकम् ३१

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 31

Vamana blesses Bali

प्रीत्या दैत्यस्तव तनुमहःप्रेक्षणात् सर्वथाऽपि
त्वामाराध्यन्नजित रचयन्नञ्जलिं सञ्जगाद ।
मत्तः किं ते समभिलषितं विप्रसूनो वद त्वं
वित्तं भक्तं भवनमवनीं वाऽपि सर्वं प्रदास्ये ॥31.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रीत्या out of affection/delight, दैत्यः the daitya (Bali), तव Your, तनु-महः-प्रेक्षणात् from gazing upon the divine splendor of Your body, सर्वथा अपि in every possible way, त्वाम् You, आराध्यन् worshipping/honoring, अजित O Unconquerable Lord!, रचयन् अञ्जलिः folding his hands in reverence, सञ्जगाद spoke, मत्तः from me, किम् what, ते by You, समभिलषितम् is desired, विप्र-सूनो O son of a Brahmin!, वद Tell, त्वम् You, वित्तम् wealth, भक्तम् food/grain, भवनम् palatial home, अवनीम् land/earth, वा अपि or even, सर्वम् everything, प्रदास्ये I shall grant

महः  - महः पुं. - 'प्रकाश, कान्ति' src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: Filled with immense joy upon gazing at the mesmerizing radiance of Your divine body, King Bali honored You in every way. Folding his hands in deep reverence, O Unconquerable Lord, he said: "O noble Brahmin boy, tell me what You desire from me! Wealth, grains, mansions, land, or even my entire kingdom—whatever You ask for, I shall grant it to You without hesitation!"

तामीक्षणां बलिगिरमुपाकर्ण्य कारुण्यपूर्णः
अप्यस्योत्सेकं शमयितुमना दैत्यवंशं प्रशंसन् ।
भूमिं पादत्रयपरिमितां प्रार्थयामासिथ त्वं
सर्वं देहीति तु निगदिते कस्य हास्यं न वा स्यात् ॥31.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् those, ईक्षणाम् seen/observed (or smooth/promising), बलि-गिरम् words of King Bali, उपाकर्ण्य having heard, कारुण्य-पूर्णः filled with compassion, अपि even so, अस्य his (Bali's), उत्सेकम् pride/arrogance, शमयितु-मनाः wishing to subdue/calm, दैत्य-वंशम् the lineage of the Daityas, प्रशंसन् praising, भूमिम् land, पाद-त्रय-परिमिताम् measured by three strides of My feet, प्रार्थयामासिथ त्वम् You requested, सर्वम् everything, देहि give, इति thus, तु indeed, निगदिते if asked, कस्य whose, हास्यम् laughter/amusement, न वा स्यात् would not be aroused?

Simple Meaning: Hearing those grand and generous words of King Bali, You—though filled with deep compassion for him—wished to subdue his subtle pride. First praising the high noble lineage of the Daityas, You then asked him for merely as much land as could be measured by three steps of Your small feet! After all, to ask for everything would be a source of amusement!

विश्वेशं मां त्रिपदमिह किं याचसे बालिशस्त्वं
सर्वां भूमिं वृणु किममुनेत्यालपत्त्वां स दृप्यन् ।
यस्माद्दर्पात् त्रिपदपरिपूर्त्यक्षमः क्षेपवादान्
बन्धं चासावगमदतदर्होऽपि गाढोपशान्त्यै ॥31.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: विश्वेशम् the lord of the universe, माम् me, त्रि-पदम् three paces of land, इह here, किम् why, याचसे do you beg for?, बालिशः त्वम् you are childish/naive, सर्वम् entire, भूमिम् earth/kingdom, वृणु ask for/choose, किम् अमुना what is the use of this (tiny bit)?, इति thus, आलपत् spoke to, त्वाम् You, सः he (Bali), दृप्यन् proud/haughty, यस्मात् दर्पात् due to which pride, त्रिपद-परिपूर्ति-अक्षमः unable to fulfill the three paces, क्षेप-वादान् taunting/insulting words, बन्धम् confinement/bondage, च and, असौ he, अगमत् underwent/received, अतत्-अर्हः अपि even though he did not deserve such harsh treatment, गाढ-उपशान्त्यै for his complete purification and ultimate spiritual peace

Simple Meaning: Haughty with pride, King Bali addressed You, saying: "I am the ruler of the whole universe! Why do you ask me for a mere three paces of land like a simpleton? Ask for the entire earth!" Because of this underlying pride, he later found himself unable to fulfill even those three paces, earning taunts and bondage of unable to fulfill his words. Yet, though he did not inherently deserve such harsh treatment, You subjected him to it to thoroughly purge his subtle ego and bestow upon him supreme, unwavering peace.

पादत्रय्या यदि न मुदितो विष्टपैर्नापि तुष्येत्
इत्युक्तेऽस्मिन् वरद भवते दातुकामेऽथ तोयम् ।
दैत्याचार्यस्तव खलु परीक्षार्थिनः प्रेरणात्तं
मा मा देयं हरिरयमिति व्यक्तमेवाबभाषे ॥31.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: पाद-त्रय्या by three paces of land, यदि if, न मुदितः is not satisfied, विष्टपैः by the entire world/universe, न अपि also not, तुष्येत् would be pleased, इति thus, उक्ते अस्मिन् when You spoke like this, वरद O Bestower of Boons!, भवते unto You, दातु-कामे wishful of giving, अथ then, तोयम् the ritual water (to solemnize the gift), दैत्य-आचार्यः the preceptor of the daityas (Shukracharya), तव Your, खलु indeed, परीक्षा-अर्थिनः who wished to test (Bali), प्रेरणात् from the promptings/inspiration, तम् to him (Bali), मा मा do not, do not, देयम् give, हरिः अयम् He is Lord Hari Himself, इति thus, व्यक्तम् एव clearly indeed, अबभाषे spoke/warned

Simple Meaning: O Bestower of Boons! When You remarked, "If a person is not satisfied with three paces of land, he will not be content even if he gets the whole universe," King Bali agreed and was about to pour the ritual water into Your hand to solemnize the gift. At that moment, inspired by Your own divine plan to test Bali's unwavering commitment, Shukracharya—the guru of the daityas —warned him explicitly: "Do not give! Do not give anything! He is none other than Lord Hari Himself!"

याचत्येवं यदि स भगवान् पूर्णकामोऽस्मि सोऽहं
दास्याम्येव स्थिरमिति वदन् काव्यशप्तोऽपि दैत्यः ।
विन्ध्यावल्या निजदयितया दत्तपाद्याय तुभ्यं
चित्रं चित्रं सकलमपि स प्रार्पयत्तोयपूर्वम् ॥31.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: याचति asks/begs, एवम् thus, यदि if, सः He, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, पूर्णकामः असि I have fulfilled all my desires/I am truly fortunate, सः अहम् that same I, दास्यामि एव I shall certainly give, स्थिरम् firmly/resolutely, इति thus, वदन् saying, काव्य-शप्तः अपि even though cursed by Shukracharya (Kavya), दैत्यः the daitya (Bali), विन्ध्यावल्या by Vindhyavali, निज-दयितया by his own loving wife, दत्त-पाद्याय to You who were offered foot-washing water, तुभ्यम् unto You, चित्रम् चित्रम् Oh, how wonderful! How marvelous!, सकलम् अपि even everything (his entire kingdom and possessions), सः he (Bali), प्रार्पयत् offered/handed over, तोय-पूर्वम् accompanied by the ritual pouring of water

Simple Meaning: Even when cursed by his guru Shukracharya, the noble King Bali declared resolutely: "If the Supreme Lord Himself is begging from me, then my life's purpose is completely fulfilled! I shall most certainly grant Him His request." Supported by his devoted wife Vindhyavali, who reverently offered You water to wash Your feet, King Bali wondrously poured the ritual water and surrendered his entire kingdom and all his possessions unto You!

निस्सन्देहं दितिकुलपतौ त्वय्यशेषार्पणं तद्-
व्यातन्वाने मुमुचुः ऋषयः सामराः पुष्पवर्षम् ।
दिव्यं रूपं तव च तदिदं पश्यतां विश्वभाजाम्
उच्चैरुच्चैरवृधदवधीकृत्य विश्वाण्डभाण्डम् ॥31.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: निस्सन्देहम् without any hesitation/unhesitatingly, दिति-कुल-पतौ as the leader of Diti's lineage (King Bali), त्वयि unto You, अशेष-अर्पणम् complete total surrender of everything, तत् that, व्यातन्वाने making/offering, मुमुचुः showered, ऋषयः the sages, स-अमराः along with the gods (Devas), पुष्प-वर्षम् a rain of flowers, दिव्यम् divine/miraculous, रूपम् form, तव Your, च and, तत् इदम् that very form, पश्यताम् as they were watching, विश्व-भाजाम् of all the inhabitants of the universe, उच्चैः उच्चैः higher and higher, अवृधत् grew/expanded, अवधीकृत्य enveloping/reaching as its limit, विश्व-अण्ड-भाण्डम् the entire container of the cosmic egg (the universe)

Simple Meaning: When King Bali, the noble leader of the lineage of Diti, surrendered his everything unto You without the slightest hesitation, the holy sages and celestial gods rained divine flowers down from above in sheer delight! And as all the beings in the universe watched in awe, Your compact form began to expand—growing higher and higher, until it encompassed the entire cosmic egg of the universe!

त्वत्पादाग्रं निजपदगतं पुण्डरीकोद्भवोऽसौ
कुण्डीतोयैरसिचदपुनाद्यज्जलं विश्वलोकान् ।
हर्षोत्कर्षात् सुबहु ननृते खेचरैरुत्सवेऽस्मिन्
भेरीं निघ्नन् भुवनमचरज्जाम्बवान् भक्तिशाली ॥31.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-पाद-अग्रम् the tip of Your raised foot, निज-पद-गतम् which had reached his own realm (Brahma-loka/Satya-loka), पुण्डरीक-उद्भवः असौ this Lord Brahma (born from the lotus), कुण्डी-तोयैः with the holy water from his water-pot (Kamandalu), असिचत् washed/anointed, अपुनात् purified, यत्-जलम् which water (becoming the holy river Ganga), विश्व-लोकान् all the worlds, हर्ष-उत्कर्षात् out of intense, overflowing joy, सुबहु immensely, ननृते danced, खे-चरैः along with the sky-dwellers (celestials/Gandharvas), उत्सवे अस्मिन् in this grand cosmic celebration, भेरीम् a large drum, निघ्नन् beating loudly, भुवनम् across the worlds, अचरत् traveled/circled around, जाम्बवान् Jambavan, भक्तिशाली the supreme devotee

Simple Meaning: When the tip of Your raised foot reached Satya-loka, Lord Brahma reverently washed it with the sacred water from his Kamandalu. That holy water transformed into the divine Ganga, purifying all the worlds! In this grand cosmic celebration, celestial beings danced in ecstasy, and the great devotee Jambavan beat a drum with immense joy, traveling around the universe to proclaim Your glory!

तावद्दैत्यास्त्वनुमतिमृते भर्तुरारब्धयुद्धा
देवोपेतैर्भवदनुचरैस्सङ्गता भङ्गमापन् ।
कालात्माऽयं वसति पुरतो यद्वशात् प्राग्जिताः स्मः
किं वो युद्धैरिति बलिगिरा तेऽथ पातालमापुः ॥31.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: तावत् in the meantime, दैत्याः the daityas, तु indeed, अनुमतिम् ऋते without the permission, भर्तुः of their king (Bali), आरब्ध-युद्धाः who initiated a battle, देव-उपेतैः accompanied by the Devas, भवत्-अनुचरैः with Your divine attendants (or forces), सङ्गताः having engaged in conflict, भङ्गम् आपन् met with defeat, काल-आत्मा अयम् He is Time Personified, वसति stands/dwells, पुरतः in front of us, यत्-वशात् under whose control/power, प्राक् earlier, जिताः स्मः we were victorious, किम् वा what is the use, युद्धिः of fighting?, इति thus, बलि-गिरा by the wise words of King Bali, ते they (the daityas), अथ then, पातालम् आपुः retreated to the netherworld (Patala)

Simple Meaning: Meanwhile, without seeking King Bali's permission, the daityas launched an attack against Your divine attendants and the Devas. However, they were utterly defeated in battle. King Bali then restrained them, saying: "The Supreme Lord standing before us is Time Personified! It was solely by His divine will that we conquered the Devas earlier. What is the point of fighting Him now?" Heeding Bali's wise counsel, the daityas ceased fighting and retreated to the netherworld (Patala).

पाशैर्बद्धं पतगपतिना दैत्यमुच्चैरवादीः
तार्त्तीयीकं दिश मम पदं किं न विश्वेश्वरोऽसि ।
पादं मूर्ध्नि प्रणय भगवन्नित्यकम्पं वदन्तं
प्रह्लाद्स्तं स्वयमुपगतो मानयन्नस्तवीत्त्वाम् ॥31.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: पाशैः with ropes/fetters, बद्धम् bound, पतग-पतिना by Garuda (the king of birds), दैत्यम् to the daitya (Bali), उच्चैः sternly/loudly, अवादीः You said, तार्तीयीकम् for the third, दिश show/grant, मम My, पदम् step/place for footstep, किम् are you not, विश्वेश्वरः असि the ruler of the universe?, पादम् Your foot, मूर्ध्नि on my head, प्रणय place, भगवन् O Supreme Lord!, इति thus, अकम्पम् unwaveringly/without trembling, वदन्तम् to him (Bali) who spoke, प्रह्लादः Prahlada, तम् him, स्वयम् himself, उपगतः approaching, मानयन् honoring/praising him, अस्तवीत् त्वाम् prayed to/praised You

Simple Meaning: When Garuda bound King Bali with fetters, You loudly addressed him: "Show Me the place for My third step! Are you not the mighty ruler of the universe who promises to grant all requests?" Without a moment's hesitation or trembling, Bali replied: "O Supreme Lord, please place Your third footstep right upon my head!" Seeing Bali's unwavering devotion, his noble grandfather Prahlada approached, honoring Bali's supreme surrender and singing glorious praises unto You!

दर्पोच्छित्त्यै विहितमखिलं दैत्य सिद्धोऽसि पुण्यैः
लोकस्तेऽस्तु त्रिदिवविजयी वासवत्वं च पश्चात् ।
मत्सायुज्यं भज च पुनरित्यन्वगृह्णा बलिं तं
विप्रैस्सन्तानितमखवरः पाहि वातालयेश ॥31.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: दर्प-उच्छित्त्यै for the complete destruction/eradication of pride, विहितम् was done, अखिलम् all this, दैत्य O daitya (Bali)!, सिद्धः असि you have become perfected/accomplished, पुण्यैः through your immense merits/pious deeds, लोकः realm/abode (Sutala), ते for you, अस्तु let it be, त्रिदिव-विजयी surpassing even the heavens (Tridiva/Svarga), वासवत्वम् the position/post of Indra, च and, पश्चात् later (in the next Manvantara), मत्-सायुज्यम् intimate liberation/oneness with Me (Sayujya), भज obtain/attain, च and, पुनः again, इति thus, अन्वगृह्णाः You blessed, बलिम् King Bali, तम् that, विप्रैः by the holy Brahmins, सन्तानित-मख-वरः who was made to complete that supreme sacrifice (Yajna), पाहि protect me!, वातालयेश O Lord of Guruvayur (Vatalayesha)!

Simple Meaning: "O daitya! All of this was done solely to eradicate your subtle pride. Through your immense merit, you have now attained spiritual perfection! May you reside in the realm of Sutala, which surpasses even heaven in glory and comfort. In the upcoming Manvantara, you shall assume the status of Indra, and ultimately, you will attain complete liberation (Sayujya) by merging into Me!"

Thus, You abundantly blessed the noble King Bali and ensured that his supreme sacrifice was brought to a glorious completion by the holy priests. O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect and heal me!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
