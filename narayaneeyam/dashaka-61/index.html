Krishna blesses the wives of the brahmanas

दशकम् ६१

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 61

Krishna blesses the wives of the brahmanas

ततश्च वृन्दावनतोऽतिदूरतो वनं गतस्त्वं खलु गोपगोकुलैः ।
हृदन्तरे भक्ततरद्विजाङ्गनाः कदम्बकानुग्रहणाग्रहं वहन् ॥61.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः च and then, वृन्दावनतः अतिदूरतः far away from Vrindavan, वनम् गतः त्वम् You went deep into the forest, खलु indeed, गोप-गोकुलैः along with the gopas and herds of cattle, हृद-अन्तरे within Your heart, भक्ततर-द्विज-अङ्गनाः the deeply devoted wives of brahmanas, कदम्बक-अनुग्रहण-आग्रहम् an eager desire to bestow grace, वहन् carrying

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, O Lord, You wandered far away from Vrindavan into a deep forest accompanied by the cowherd boys and herds of cattle. All the while, You carried in Your heart a keen desire to bless a group of deeply devoted wives of the brahmanas.

ततो निरीक्ष्याशरणे वनान्तरे किशोरलोकं क्षुधितं तृषाकुलम् ।
अदूरतो यज्ञपरान् द्विजान् प्रति व्यसर्जयो दीदिवियाचनाय तान् ॥61.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, निरीक्ष्य seeing, अशरणे helpless, वन-अन्तरे deep within the forest, किशोर-लोकम् the group of young boys, क्षुधितम् hungry, तृषा-आकुलम् distressed by thirst, अदूरतः not far away, यज्ञ-परान् engaged in performing yajna, द्विजान् प्रति towards the brahmanas, व्यसर्जयः You sent, दीदिवि-याचनाय to ask for boiled rice (cooked food), तान् them

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, seeing the young cowherd boys hungry, distressed by thirst, and helpless in the deep forest, You sent them to the brahmanas engaged in performing yajna nearby to ask for cooked food.

गतेष्वथो तेष्वभिधाय तेऽभिधां कुमारकेष्वोदनयाचिषु प्रभो ।
श्रुतिस्थिरा अप्यभिनिन्युरश्रुतिं न किञ्चिदूचुश्च महीसुरोत्तमाः ॥61.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: गतेषु when they went, अथः then, तेषु those, अभिधाय having uttered, ते Your, अभिधाम् name, कुमारकेषु young boys, ओदन-याचिषु requesting cooked food, प्रभो O Lord, श्रुति-स्थिराः though firm in knowledge of the Vedas, अपि even, अभिनिन्युः they feigned, अश्रुतिम् not hearing, न not, किञ्चित् anything, ऊचुः spoke, च and, महीसुर-उत्तमाः the top brahmanas

Simple Meaning: O Lord, when the young boys went there, and mentioned Your name, and requested cooked food, those top brahmanas, despite being firmly well-versed in the Vedas, feigned as if they did not hear anything and did not utter a single word to them.

अनादरात् खिन्नधियो हि बालकाः समाययुर्युक्तमिदं हि यज्वसु ।
चिरादभक्ताः खलु ते महीसुराः कथं हि भक्तं त्वयि तैः समर्प्यते ॥61.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अनादरात् due to disrespect, खिन्न-धियः sad at heart, हि indeed, बालकाः the boys, समाययुः returned, युक्तम् natural, इदम् this, हि indeed, यज्वसु among the performers of yajna, चिरात् for a long time, अभक्ताः non-devotees, खलु indeed, ते those, महीसुराः brahmanas, कथम् how, हि indeed, भक्तम् cooked food, त्वयि to You, तैः by them, समर्प्यते could be offered

Simple Meaning: Disappointed by the disrespect, the young boys returned sad at heart. Indeed, this behavior was natural for those ritualists, for those brahmanas had been devoid of devotion (abhakta) for a long time, so how could cooked food (bhakta) be offered to You by them?

Note: The play is on the word “bhakta”. Bhakta means a devotee and also means cooked rice. The poet says that the brahmanas were abhaktas - nondevotees. So how could someone who is not Your devotee, could offer bhakta - food  - to You?

निवेदयध्वं गृहिणीजनाय मां दिशेयुरन्नं करुणाकुला इमाः ।
इति स्मितार्द्रं भवतेरिता गताः ते दारका दारजनं ययाचिरे ॥61.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: निवेदयध्वम् inform, गृहिणी-जनाय the wives, माम् about Me, दिशेयुः they will give, अन्नम् food, करुणा-कुलाः filled with compassion, इमाः these women, इति thus, स्मित-आर्द्रम् with a tender smile, भवता by You, ईरितातः urged, गताः having gone, ते those, दारकाः boys, दार-जनम् the wives, ययाचिरे begged

Simple Meaning: “Tell My situation to the wives, for they are filled with compassion and will surely give food.” Thus urged by You with a tender smile, the boys went to the wives of the brahmanas and requested food from them.

गृहीतनाम्नि त्वयि सम्भ्रमाकुलाः चतुर्विधं भोज्यरसं प्रगृह्य ताः ।
चिरंधृतत्वत्प्रविलोकनाग्रहाः स्वकैर्निरुद्धा अपि तूर्णमाययुः ॥61.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: गृहीत-नाम्नि when Your name was mentioned, त्वयि regarding You, सम्भ्रम-आकुलाः agitated with excited eagerness, चतुर्-विधम् four types of, भोज्य-रसम् delicious food, प्रगृह्य taking, ताः those wives, चिरम्-धृत-त्वत्-प्रविलोकन-आग्रहाः holding a long-cherished desire to see You, स्वकैः by their husbands and relatives, निरुद्धाः restrained, अपि even though, तूर्णम् quickly, आययुः came

Simple Meaning: As soon as Your name was mentioned, those wives became overcome with excitement. Taking along four types of delicious food, and driven by their long-cherished intense desire to see You, they came rushing to You without delay, even though they were restrained by their husbands and family members.

विलोलपिञ्छं चिकुरे कपोलयोः समुल्लसत्कुण्डलमार्द्रमीक्षिते ।
निधाय बाहुं सुहृदंससीमनि स्थितं भवन्तं समलोकयन्त ताः ॥61.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: विलोल-पिञ्छम् with a moving peacock feather, चिकुरे in the hair, कपोलयोः on both cheeks, समुल्लसत्-कुण्डलम् shining earrings, आर्द्रम् with tender love, ईक्षिते in the glance, निधाय having placed, बाहुम् an arm, सुहृत्-अंस-सीमनि on the shoulder of a friend, स्थितम् standing, भवन्तम् You, समलोकयन्त beheld, ताः those wives

Simple Meaning: Those wives beheld You standing with a moving peacock feather in Your hair, glow of the earrings glistening upon Your cheeks, a gentle look of love in Your eyes, and Your arm placed around the shoulder of a friend.

तदा च काचित्त्वदुपागमोद्यता गृहीतहस्ता दयितेन यज्वना ।
तदैव सञ्चिन्त्य भवन्तमञ्जसा विवेश कैवल्यमहो कृतिन्यसौ ॥61.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदा then, च and, काचित् one woman, त्वत्-उपागम-उद्यता who was preparing to come to You, गृहीत-हस्ता having her hand held, दयितेन by her beloved, यज्वना ritualist husband, तदा एव right at that moment, सञ्चिन्त्य thinking deeply, भवन्तम् upon You, अञ्जसा instantly, विवेश entered, कैवल्यम् liberation, अहो Oh, कृतिनी fortunate/blessed, असौ she

Simple Meaning: And then, one particular woman who was eagerly preparing to come to You was held back by her hand by her ritualist husband. Right at that very moment, contemplating deeply upon You, she instantly attained liberation. Oh, how fortunate and blessed she was!

आदाय भोज्यान्यनुगृह्य ताः पुनः त्वदङ्गसङ्गस्पृहयोज्झतीर्गृहम् ।
विलोक्य यज्ञाय विसर्जयन्निमाः चकर्थ भर्तॄनपि तास्वगर्हणान् ॥61.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: आदाय accepting, भोज्यानि the offered food items, अनुगृह्य showering grace upon, ताः those wives, पुनः again, त्वत्-अङ्ग-सङ्ग-स्पृहया with a longing for physical contact with Your body, उज्झतीः abandoning, गृहम् their homes, विलोक्य seeing, यज्ञाय for the performance of yajna, विसर्जयन् sending back, इमाः these women, चकर्थ You made, भर्तॄन् their husbands, अपि even, तासु towards them, अगर्हणान् free from anger or reproach

Simple Meaning: Accepting the delicious food items and showering Your grace upon those wives who were willing to abandon their homes out of a longing for closeness with You, You advised them to return for the completion of the yajna. Furthermore, You made their husbands completely free from anger or resentment toward them.

निरूप्य दोषं निजमङ्गनाजने विलोक्य भक्तिं च पुनर्विचारिभिः
प्रबुद्धतत्त्वैस्त्वमभिष्टुतो द्विजैः मरुत्पुराधीश निरुन्धि मे गदान् ॥61.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: निरूप्य realizing, दोषम् flaw, निजम् their own, अङ्गना-जने in their wives, विलोक्य seeing, भक्तिम् devotion, च and, पुनः विचारिभिः reflecting deeply again, प्रबुद्ध-तत्त्वैः who had realized the ultimate truth, त्वम् You, अभिष्टुतः praised, द्विजैः by those brahmanas, मरुत्पुर-धीश O Lord of Guruvayur, निरुन्धि eradicate, मे my, गदान् diseases

Simple Meaning: Realizing their own shortcoming and seeing the deep devotion in their wives, those brahmanas reflected deeply, came to realize the ultimate truth, and offered hymn of praises unto You. O Lord of Guruvayur, please eradicate all my ailments!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
