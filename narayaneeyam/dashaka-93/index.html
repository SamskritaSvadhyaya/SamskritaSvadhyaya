Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 93

The learnings from the twenty-four teachers in life

बन्धुस्नेहं विजह्यां तव हि करुणया त्वय्युपावेशितात्मा
सर्वं त्यक्त्वा चरेयं सकलमपि जगद्वीक्ष्य मायाविलासम् ।
नानात्वाद्भ्रान्तिजन्यात् सति खलु गुणदोषावबोधे विधिर्वा
व्यासेधो वा कथं तौ त्वयि निहितमतेर्वीतवैषम्यबुद्धेः ॥93.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: बन्धु-स्नेहम् attachment/affection towards relatives and friends, विजह्याम् may I completely abandon, तव हि by Your indeed, करुणया grace/mercy, त्वयि उपावेशित-आत्मा with my mind/self deeply merged/absorbed in You, सर्वम् त्यक्त्वा having renounced everything, चरेयम् may I wander, सकलम् अपि जगत् the entire universe, वीक्ष्य viewing/perceiving, माया-विलासम् as a mere play/manifestation of Maya, नानात्वात् भ्रान्ति जन्यात् arising from the illusion of diversity/multiplicity, सति खलु when it exists indeed, गुण-दोष-अवबोधे the perception of merits and defects/right and wrong, विधिः वा scriptural injunctions (dos), व्यासेधः वा or scriptural prohibitions (don'ts), कथम् तौ how can those two apply, त्वयि निहित मतेः to one whose intellect is firmly fixed in You, वीत-वैषम्य-बुद्धेः to one who is completely freed from the sense of duality/discrimination

Simple Meaning: O Lord! By Your infinite grace, may I sever my attachment to relatives and worldly bonds, merge my mind entirely in You, renounce all possessions, and wander freely, perceiving this entire universe as a mere sport of Your illusive power (Maya)! Since notions of right and wrong, virtue and vice, stem only from the illusory perception of diversity, how can scriptural prescriptions or prohibitions apply to one whose intellect is irrevocably absorbed in You and who is utterly liberated from all sense of duality?

क्षुत्तृष्णालोपमात्रे सततकृतधियो जन्तवः सन्त्यनन्ताः
तेभ्यो विज्ञानवत्त्वात् पुरुष इह वरस्तज्जनिर्दुर्लभैव ।
तत्राप्यात्मात्मनः स्यात्सुहृदपि च रिपुर्यस्त्वयि न्यस्तचेताः
तापोच्छित्तेरुपायं स्मरति स हि सुहृत् स्वात्मवैरी ततोऽन्यः ॥93.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्षुत्-तृष्णा-लोप-मात्रे merely in satisfying/removing hunger and thirst, सतत-कृत-धियः whose minds are constantly fixed/engaged, जन्तवः living beings, सन्ति-अनन्ताः are infinite in number, तेभ्यः than all of them, विज्ञानवत्त्वात् owing to the possession of higher intellect/discrimination, पुरुषः human being, इह in this world, वरः is superior, तत्-जनिः human birth, दुर्लभ-एव is extremely rare indeed, तत्र अपि even among human beings, आत्मा oneself, आत्मनः to oneself, स्यात् becomes, सुहृद् a true friend, अपि च and also, रिपुः an enemy, यः तु who indeed, त्वयि in You, न्यस्त-चेताः with mind completely surrendered/placed, ताप-उच्छित्तेः for the total destruction of all worldly afflictions, उपायम् the means, स्मरति contemplates/remembers, सः हि he alone, सुहृद् is a true friend to himself, स्वात्म-वैरी an enemy to his own self, ततः अन्यः anyone other than him

Simple Meaning: Innumerable living creatures spend their lives entirely preoccupied with satisfying the basic physical urges of hunger and thirst. Blessed with higher intellect and discrimination, the human being is superior to them all, and taking a human birth is exceedingly rare! Yet, even among humans, one can be one's own true friend or one's own worst enemy. He who places his mind in You and continuously reflects on the divine means to extinguish all worldly suffering is indeed his own best friend; anyone else is merely a destroyer of his own self!

त्वत्कारुण्ये प्रवृत्ते क इव नहि गुरुर्लोकवृत्तेऽपि भूमन्
सर्वाक्रान्तापि भूमिर्नहि चलति ततस्सत्क्षमां शिक्षयेयम् ।
गृह्णीयामीश तत्तद्विषयपरिचयेऽप्यप्रसक्तिं समीरात्
व्याप्तत्वञ्चात्मनो मे गगनगुरुवशाद्भातु निर्लेपता च ॥93.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-कारुण्ये when Your grace, प्रवृत्ते operates/begins to flow, कः इव न हि who indeed is not, गुरुः a teacher/guru, लोक-वृत्ते अपि even in the everyday events of the world, भूमन् O Infinite Supreme Being, सर्व-आक्रान्ता अपि though trampled upon, exploited, and oppressed by all, भूमिः the earth, न हि चलति does not deviate or lose her composure, ततः from her, सत्-क्षमाम् true patience and forbearance, शिक्षयेयम् may I learn, गृह्णीयाम् ईश may I absorb/learn, O Lord, तत्-तत्-विषय-परिचये अपि even while coming into contact with various sense-objects, अप्रसक्तिम् detachment/non-attachment, समीरात् from the wind, व्याप्तत्वम् च आत्मनः and the all-pervading nature of the Self, मे to me, गगन-गुरु-वशात् through the wind/space as a teacher (or sky as a teacher), भातु may it shine/be revealed, निर्लेपता च and untainted purity/unaffected nature

Simple Meaning: O Infinite Supreme Being! When Your divine grace begins to flow, what object or creature in this world does not become a spiritual teacher?

स्वच्छः स्यां पावनोऽहं मधुर उदकवद्वह्निवन्मा स्म गृह्णां
सर्वान्नीनोऽपि दोषं तरुषु तमिव मां सर्वभूतेष्ववेयाम् ।
पुष्टिर्नष्टिः कलानां शशिन इव तनोर्नात्मनोऽस्तीति विद्यां
तोयादिव्यस्तमार्ताण्डवदपि च तनुष्वेकतां त्वत्प्रसादात् ॥93.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्वच्छः स्याम् may I be pure/clear, पावनः अहम् may I be purifying to others, मधुरः sweet/soothing, उदकवत् like water, वह्निवत् like fire, मा स्म गृह्णाम् may I not absorb/take on, सर्व-अन्नीनः अपि though accepting food from all sources (indiscriminately), दोषम् defect/taint, तरुषु in pieces of wood, तम् इव like that (fire manifesting differently depending on the log), माम् my own self, सर्व भूतेषु in all living beings, अवेयाम् may I recognize/perceive, पुष्टिः नष्टिः growth and decay, कलानाम् of the digits/phases, शशिनः इव like of the moon, तनोः of the physical body, न आत्मनः अस्ति does not belong to the Self (Atman), इति विद्याम् thus may I know/realize, तोय-आदि-व्यस्त-मार्ताण्डवत् अपि च and like the sun reflected differently in various sheets of water, तनुषु in different physical bodies, एकताम् oneness/unity of the Self, त्वत्-प्रसादात् by Your supreme grace

Simple Meaning: O Lord! By Your divine grace:

स्नेहाद्व्याधात्तपुत्रप्रणयमृतकपोतायितो मा स्म भूवं
प्राप्तं प्राश्नन् सहेय क्षुधमपि शयुवत् सिन्धुवत्स्यामगाधः ।
मा पप्तं योषिदादौ शिखिनि शलभवत् भृङ्गवत्सारभागी
भूयासं किन्तु तद्वद्धनचयनवशान्माहमीश प्रणेशम् ॥93.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्नेहात्-व्याध-आत्त-पुत्र-प्रणय-मृत-कपोतायितः like the pigeon who died due to attachment for its children captured by the hunter, मा स्म भूवम् may I never become, प्राप्तम् that which is obtained naturally, प्राश्नन् eating/consuming, सहेय may I endure, क्षुधम् अपि hunger as well, शयुवत् like the python (Ajagara), सिन्धुवत् स्याम् अगाधः like the ocean, may I be unfathomable and calm, मा पप्तम् may I not fall/perish, योषित्-आदौ in women and other temptations of sense-pleasures, शिखिनि into the fire, शलभवत् like a moth, भृङ्गवत्-सारभागी like the honeybee who extracts the essence, भूयासम् may I become, किन्तु but, तत्-वत् like that bee (which gets trapped) or like the honey-gatherer, धन-चयन-वशात् due to the accumulation of wealth/possessions, मा अहम् let not I, ईश O Supreme Lord, प्रणेशम् may perish

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Lord! By Your divine grace:

मा बद्ध्यासं तरुण्या गज इव वशया नार्जयेयं धनौघं
हर्तान्यस्तं हि माध्वीहर इव मृगवन्मा मुहं ग्राम्यगीतैः ।
नात्यासज्जेय भोज्ये झष इव बलिशे पिङ्गलावन्निराशः
सुप्यां भर्तव्ययोगात् कुरर इव विभो सामिषोऽन्यैर्न हन्यै ॥93.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: मा बद्ध्यासम् may I not be bound/trapped, तरुण्या by a young female (sensual attraction), गजः इव like a male elephant, वशया by a female elephant (used as a lure), न आर्जयेयम् धन-ओघम् may I not hoard/accumulate mountains of wealth, हर्ता अन्यः तम् हि for another steals/takes away that (accumulated wealth), माध्वी-हरः इव like the collector of honey (taking from the bee), मृगवत् मा मुहम् may I not be deluded like the deer, ग्राम्य-गीतैः by enticing sensory music/worldly sounds, न अत्यासज्जेय भोज्ये may I not be excessively attached to delicious foods, झषः इव like a fish, बलिशे caught on a baited hook, पिङ्गलावत् निराशः becoming free of expectations/desires like the courtesan Pingala, सुप्याम् may I sleep peacefully in spiritual contentment, भर्तव्य-योगात् from the burden/anxiety of maintaining possessions or relations, कुररः इव like the Kurara bird (osprey/hawk), विभो O All-Pervading Lord, स-आमिषः अन्यैः न हन्यै (which) holding a piece of meat is attacked by other birds, but escapes harm upon dropping it

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord! By Your grace, may I learn wisdom from these further teachers:

Note: Pingala was a courtesan of Mithila who, after a night of waiting in vain for customers, became disillusioned with worldly desires. She found peace by giving up expectation and turning her mind toward the Supreme Lord. (Shrimad Bhagavatam - Canto 11, Chapter 8, Shloka 22 onwards)

वर्तेय त्यक्तमानः सुखमतिशिशुवन्निस्सहायश्चरेयं
कन्याया एकशेषो वलय इव विभो वर्जितान्योन्यघोषः ।
त्वच्चित्तो नावबुध्यै परमिषुकृदिव क्ष्माभृदायानघोषं
गेहेष्वन्यप्रणीतेष्वहिरिव निवसान्युन्दुरोर्मन्दिरेषु ॥93.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: वर्तेय may I live/conduct myself, त्यक्त-मानः having completely abandoned false pride and ego, सुखम् happily/in supreme peace, अति-शिशुवत् like a tiny innocent child, निस्सहायः without relying on external companions or support, चरेयम् may I wander/roam, कन्यायाः of a maiden, एक-शेषः remaining as the solitary one, वलयः इव like a bangled ornament, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, वर्जित-अन्योन्य-घोषः devoid of mutual clashing sounds (arguments/distractions), त्वत्-चित्तः with mind entirely merged in You, न-अवबुध्यै may I not even perceive/notice, परम् anything else, इषुकृत् इव like the arrow-maker, क्ष्माभृत्-आयान-घोषम् the loud noise of the king's grand royal procession, गेहेषु in dwellings/shelters, अन्य-प्रणीतेषु built/constructed by others, अहिः इव like a serpent, निवसानि may I reside, उन्दुरोः मन्दिरेषु in the burrows/holes made by rats

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord! By Your divine grace, may I learn further lessons of dispassion and inner quietude:

त्वय्येव त्वत्कृतं त्वं क्षपयसि जगदित्यूर्णनाभात् प्रतीयां
त्वच्चिन्ता त्वत्स्वरूपं कुरुत इति दृढं शिक्षये पेशकारात् ।
विड्भस्मात्मा च देहो भवति गुरुवरो यो विवेकं विरक्तिं
धत्ते सञ्चिन्त्यमानो मम तु बहुरुजापीडितोऽयं विशेषात् ॥93.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वयि एव within Yourself alone, त्वत्-कृतम् created by You, त्वम् You, क्षपयसि dissolve/withdraw, जगद् this universe, इति ऊर्णनाभात् thus from the spider, प्रतीयाम् may I realize/understand, त्वत्-चिन्ता constant contemplation on You, त्वत्-स्वरूपम् Your own divine form/nature, कुरुत इति transforms (one into), दृढम् firmly, शिक्षये may I learn, पेशकारात् from the caterpillar/wasp, विड्-भस्म-आत्मा whose end is reduced to feces or ashes, च and, देहः this physical body, भवति becomes, गुरु-वरः an exalted spiritual teacher/Guru, यः which, विवेकम् spiritual discrimination, विरक्तिम् dispassion/detachment, धत्ते bestows/nourishes, सञ्चिन्त्यमानः when deeply reflected upon, मम my, तु but, बहु-रुजा-पीडितः severely afflicted by many diseases, अयम् this (body), विशेषात् especially so

Simple Meaning: O Lord! May I learn further truths from these teachers:

And from this Physical Body, whose ultimate end is only to turn into feces, ashes, or dust, may I gain deep discrimination and detachment! Indeed, this body of mine, being severely afflicted by many diseases, serves as a particularly great spiritual master, constantly reminding me of the transience of worldly existence and impelling me towards absolute surrender at Your lotus feet!

ही ही मे देहमोहं त्यज पवनपुराधीश यत्प्रेमहेतोः
गेहे वित्ते कलत्रादिषु च विवशितास्त्वत्पदं विस्मरन्ति ।
सोऽयं वह्नेश्शुनो वा परमिह परतः साम्प्रतञ्चाक्षिकर्ण-
त्वग्जिह्वाद्या विकर्षन्त्यवशमत इतः कोऽपि न त्वत्पदाब्जे ॥93.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: ही ही alas, alas, मे my, देह-मोहम् delusion/attachment towards the physical body, त्यज please remove/dispel, पवनपुराधीश O Lord of Guruvayur, यत्-प्रेम-हेतोः for the love of which (body), गेहे in home, वित्ते in wealth, कलत्र-आदिषु inside family/wife and children, च and, विवशिताः driven helplessly, त्वत्-पदम् Your divine lotus feet, विस्मरन्ति (people) forget, सः अयम् that very same body, वह्नेः to fire (burnt to ash), शुनः वा or to dogs/beasts (consumed as flesh), परम् at the end/after death, इह in this life, परतः thereafter, साम्प्रतम् च and in the present time, अक्षि-कर्ण-त्वग्-जिह्वा-आद्याः the eyes, ears, skin, tongue, and other sense organs, विकर्षन्ति pull/drag forcibly, अवशम् helplessly, अतः इतः hither and thither, कः अपि न but no one, त्वत्-पद-अब्जे towards Your lotus feet

Simple Meaning: Alas, alas! O Lord of Guruvayur, please dispel my deep-seated delusion and attachment toward this physical body! It is out of obsessive attachment to this body that human beings become helplessly entrapped in thoughts of house, wealth, family, and relations, entirely forgetting Your sacred lotus feet. In the end, this body is destined merely to be consumed by fire as ash or devoured by dogs and jackals as carrion; and even in the present, its unruly sense organs—eyes, ears, skin, tongue—drag the helpless soul mindlessly in every direction. Alas, none among them directs their senses toward Your lotus feet!

दुर्वारो देहमोहो यदि पुनरधुना तर्हि निश्शेषरोगान्
हृत्वा भक्तिं द्रढिष्ठां कुरु तव पदपङ्केरुहे पङ्कजाक्ष ।
नूनं नानाभवान्ते समधिगतममुं मुक्तिदं विप्रदेहं
क्षुद्रे हा हन्त मा मा क्षिप विषयरसे पाहि मां मारुतेश ॥93.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुर्वारः irrepressible/unconquerable, देह-मोहः attachment/delusion regarding this body, यदि पुनः अधुना if indeed even now, तर्हि then, निश्शेष-रोगान् all my diseases without exception, हृत्वा having completely destroyed/removed, भक्तिम् devotion, द्रढिष्ठाम् most unwavering and firm, कुरु pray grant/make, तव पद पङ्केरुहे at Your lotus feet, पङ्कजाक्ष O Lotus-eyed Lord, नूनम् surely, नाना भव-अन्ते at the end of countless births/incarnations, समधिगतम् attained, अमुम् this, मुक्ति-दम् liberation-bestowing, विप्र-देहम् body of a Brahmana, क्षुद्रे in trivial/worthless, हा हन्त alas, मा मा क्षिप do not, oh do not cast/waste, विषय-रसे in the poisonous pleasures of sense-objects, पाहि माम् protect me, मारुतेश O Lord of Guruvayur

Simple Meaning: O Lotus-eyed Lord! If this attachment to my body remains insurmountable even now, then I pray: completely cure all my ailments without exception and grant me unflinching, steadfast devotion at Your lotus feet! Alas, having finally attained this rare, liberation-bestowing Brahmana birth at the end of countless life cycles, pray do not throw me into the pit of petty, transient sense-pleasures! O Lord of Guruvayur, save me!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
