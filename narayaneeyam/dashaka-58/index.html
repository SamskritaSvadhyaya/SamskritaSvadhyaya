Krishna rescues everyone from the wild fire

दशकम् ५८ · Krishna rescues the gopas from the wild fire

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 58

Krishna rescues the gopas from the wild fire

त्वयि विहरणलोले बालजालैः प्रलम्बप्रमथनसविलम्बे धेनवः स्वैरचाराः ।
तृणकुतुकनिविष्टा दूरदूरं चरन्त्यः किमपि विपिनमैषीकाख्यमीषांबभूवुः ॥58.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वयि while You, विहरण-लोले were engrossed in playing, बाल-जालैः with the group of boys, प्रलम्ब-प्रमथन-सविलम्बे delayed due to the destruction of Pralamba, धेनवः the cows, स्वैर-चाराः wandering freely, तृण-कुतुक-निविष्टाः absorbed in the delight of eating grass, दूर-दूरम् far far away, चरन्त्यः straying, किमपि a certain, विपिनम् forest, ऐषीक-आख्यम् named Aishika, ईषांबभूवुः entered

Simple Meaning: While You were absorbed in playing with the cowherd boys and delayed by the killing of Pralamba, the cows, wandering freely and captivated by fresh grass, strayed far away and entered a forest known as Aishika.

अनधिगतनिदाघक्रौर्यवृन्दावनान्तात् बहिरिदमुपयाताः काननं धेनवस्ताः ।
तव विरहविषण्णा ऊष्मलग्रीष्मतापप्रसरविसरदम्भस्याकुलाः स्तम्भमापुः ॥58.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: अनधिगत-निदाघ-क्रौर्य-वृन्दावन-अन्तात् from the interior of Vrindavan which was free from the severity of summer, बहिः इदम् outside this, उपयाताः having reached, काननम् forest, धेनवः ताः those cows, तव-विरह-विषण्णाः distressed by separation from You, ऊष्मल-ग्रीष्म-ताप-प्रसर-विसरदम्भसि due to the lack of water under the intense summer heat, आकुलाः agitated, स्तम्भम् आपुः stood paralyzed

Simple Meaning: Having strayed out of the Vrindavan forest, which was always protected from the harshness of summer, those cows found themselves in a scorching region. Distressed by separation from You and tormented by thirst under the blazing summer heat, they stood paralyzed with agitation.

तदनु सह सहायैर्दूरमन्विष्य शौरे गलितसरणिमुञ्जारण्यसञ्जातखेदम् ।
पशुकुलमभिवीक्ष्य क्षिप्रमानेतुमारात् त्वयि गतवति ही ही सर्वतोऽग्निर्जजृम्भे ॥58.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदनु thereafter, सह सहायैः with Your companions, दूरम् अन्विष्य searching far and wide, शौरे O Shauri, गलित-सरणि-मुञ्ज-अरण्य-सञ्जात-खेदम् lost in the Munja forest and distressed by lost paths, पशु-कुलम् the herd of cows, अभिवीक्ष्य seeing, क्षिप्रम् आनेतुम् to quickly bring back, आरात् near, त्वयि गतवति as You approached, ही ही alas alas, सर्वतः अग्निः fire from all sides, जजृम्भे flared up

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, O Krishna, searching far and wide with Your companions, You spotted the herd of cows lost and distressed in the Munja forest. As You approached to quickly bring them back, alas, a fierce forest fire suddenly flared up from all sides!

सकलहरिति दीप्ते घोरभाङ्कारभीमे शिखिनि विहतमार्गा अर्धदग्धा इवार्ताः ।
अहह भुवनबन्धो पाहि पाहीति सर्वे शरणमुपगतास्त्वां तापहर्तारमेकम् ॥58.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: सकल-हरिति in all directions, दीप्ते blazing, घोर-भाङ्कार-भीमे terrifying with loud crackling sounds, शिखिनि as the forest fire raged, विहत-मार्गाः with their paths blocked, अर्ध-दग्धाः इव as if half-burnt, आर्तः distressed, अहह alas, भुवन-बन्धो O Friend of the Universe, पाहि पाहि protect protect us, इति thus, सर्वे all of them, शरणम् उपगताः took refuge, त्वाम् (in) You, ताप-हर्तारम् एकम् the sole remover of all distress

हरित् (स्त्रीलिङ्गः)  दिक् src. Kosha Ashtadhayi

हरिति is a sati saptami usage

Simple Meaning: As the forest fire raged fiercely in every direction, terrifying everyone with its loud crackling sounds, their escape routes were completely blocked. Distressed and feeling as though they were already half-burnt, they all took refuge in You, shouting, "Alas! O Friend of the Universe! Save us! Save us!"—knowing You to be the sole redeemer from all suffering.

अलमलमतिभीत्या सर्वतो मीलयध्वं दृशमिति तव वाचा मीलिताक्षेषु तेषु ।
क्व नु दवदहनोऽसौ कुत्र मुञ्जाटवी सा सपदि ववृतिरे ते हन्त भाण्डीरदेशे ॥58.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: अलम् अलम् enough, enough of, अति-भीत्या so much of fear, सर्वतः all of you, मीलयध्वम् close, दृशम् eyes, इति thus, तव वाचा by Your words, मीलित-अक्षेषु तेषु when they closed their eyes, क्व नु where indeed, दव-दहनः असौ (was) that forest fire, कुत्र where, मुञ्ज-अटवी सा (was) that Munja forest, सपदि instantly, ववृतिरे ते they found themselves, हन्त lo and behold, भाण्डीर-देशे back in the region of the Bhandiraka tree

Simple Meaning: "Enough, do not be afraid! All of you, just close your eyes," You commanded. As soon as they closed their eyes upon Your word, lo and behold! Where was that forest fire? Where was that Munja forest? In an instant, they suddenly found themselves safe and sound back in the region of the Bhandiraka banyan tree.

जय जय तव माया केयमीशेति तेषां नुतिभिरुदितहासो बद्धनानाविलासः ।
पुनरपि विपिनान्ते प्राचरः पाटलादिप्रसवनिकरमात्रग्राह्यघर्मानुभावे ॥58.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: जय जय victory victory, तव माया Your wondrous divine power, का इयम् what is this, ईश O Supreme Lord, इति thus, तेषाम् of their, नुतिभिः praises, उदित-हासः smiling, बद्ध-नाना-विलासः indulging in various playful activities, पुनः अपि once again, विपिन-अन्ते in the forest, प्राचरः You roamed, पाटल-आदि-प्रसव-निकर-मात्र-ग्राह्य-घर्म-अनुभावे where the heat of summer was felt only through the fragrance of bunches of flowers like the Patala

Simple Meaning: "Victory, victory to You, O Lord! What a mysterious and wondrous power is this Maya of Yours!" Hearing their joyful praises, You smiled and continued Your playful pastimes. Once again, You roamed with them in the forest, where the intensity of summer was felt only by the bunches of fragrant flowers like the Patala.

त्वयि विमुखमिवोच्चैस्तापभारं वहन्तं तव भजनवदन्तः पङ्कमुच्छोषयन्तम् ।
तव भुजवदुदञ्चद्भूरितेजःप्रवाहं तपसमयमनैषीर्यामुनेषु स्थलेषु ॥58.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वयि towards You, विमुखम् इव as if indifferent, उच्चैः immense, ताप-भारम् weight of heat, वहन्तम् bearing, तव भजन-वदन्तः speaking Your praises, पङ्कम् impurities, उच्छोषयन्तम् drying up, तव भुजवत् like Your arms, उदञ्चत्-भूरि-तेजः-प्रवाहम् radiating a vast flow of splendor, तप-समयम् the summer season, अनैषीः You spent, यामुनेषु स्थलेषु in the regions along the banks of the Yamuna

Simple Meaning: The intense heat of the summer season was like the weight of passion that people carry who are indifferent to You. It bore the intense heat like that of devotion unto You, which dries up all impurities; and like Your own arms, it radiated a vast flow of splendor. In this manner, You spent the summer season playing in the idyllic regions along the banks of the Yamuna.

तदनु जलदजालैस्त्वद्वपुस्तुल्यभाभिः विकसदमलविद्युत्पीतवासोविलासैः ।
सकलभुवनभाजां हर्षदां वर्षवेलां क्षितिधरकुहरेषु स्वैरवासी व्यनैषीः ॥58.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदनु thereafter, जलद-जालैः with the clusters of clouds, त्वत्-वपुः-तुल्य-भाभिः possessing a dark brilliance like Your divine body, विकसत्-अमल-विद्युत्-पीत-वासो-विलासैः with flashing bright lightning resembling the charm of Your yellow garments, सकल-भुवन-भाजाम् to all the inhabitants of the world, हर्ष-दाम् granting joy, वर्ष-वेलाम् the rainy season, क्षितिधर-कुहरेषु in the caves of the mountain, स्वैर-वासी living freely, व्यनैषीः You spent

Simple Meaning: Thereafter came the rainy season, bringing great joy to all living beings in the world. The dark rain clouds resembled the luster of Your own divine body, and the brilliant flashes of lightning reflected the charm of Your yellow silk garments. Dwelling freely in the mountain caves, You spent that delightful monsoon season in play.

कुहरतलनिविष्टं त्वां गरिष्ठं गिरीन्द्रः शिखिकुलनवकेकाकाकुभिः स्तोत्रकारी ।
स्फुटकुटजकदम्बस्तोमपुष्पाञ्जलिं च प्रविदधदनुभेजे देव गोवर्धनोऽसौ ॥58.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: कुहर-तल-निविष्टम् seated inside the caves, त्वाम् You, गरिष्ठम् the most supreme Lord, गिरीन्द्रः the king of mountains, शिखि-कुल-नव-केका-काकुभिः with the sweet, fresh calls of flocks of peacocks, स्तोत्र-कारी making praises, स्फुट-कुटज-कदम्ब-स्तोम-पुष्प-अञ्जलिम् च and flower offerings consisting of bunches of Kutaja and Kadamba flowers, प्रविदधत् offering, अनुभेजे worshipped, देव O Lord, गोवर्धनः असौ this Govardhana hill

Simple Meaning: O Lord, as You resided in its caves, the exalted Govardhana hill worshipped You, the most supreme Being. It sang Your praises through the sweet, fresh calls of flocks of peacocks, and offered floral tributes to You with abundant, fully bloomed bunches of Kutaja and Kadamba flowers.

अथ शरदमुपेतां तां भवद्भक्तचेतोविमलसलिलपूरां मानयन् काननेषु ।
तृणममलवनान्ते चारु सञ्चारयन् गाः पवनपुरपते त्वं देहि मे देहसौख्यम् ॥58.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथ then, शरदम् autumn, उपेताम् which had arrived, ताम् that (season), भवत्-भक्त-चेतः-विमल-सलिल-पूराम् with waters as pure and clear as the minds of Your devotees, मानयन् welcoming, काननेषु in the forests, तृणम् grass, अमल-वन-अन्ते in the pristine forest clearings, चारु beautifully, सञ्चारयन् grazing, गाः the cows, पवनपुर-पते O Lord of Guruvayur, त्वम् You, देहि grant, मे to me, देह-सौख्यम् bodily health and well-being

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, You welcomed the arrival of the autumn season, whose waters were as clear and pure as the minds of Your devotees. Roaming happily in the pristine forest clearings, You grazed the cows on the lush green grass. O Lord of Guruvayur, please bless me with good health and freedom from physical suffering!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
