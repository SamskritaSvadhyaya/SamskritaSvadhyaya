Childhood pranks of Krishna

दशकम् ४५

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 45

Childhood pranks of Krishna

अयि सबल मुरारे पाणिजानुप्रचारैः किमपि भवनभागान् भूषयन्तौ भवन्तौ ।
चलितचरणकञ्जौ मञ्जुमञ्जीरशिञ्जाश्रवणकुतुकभाजौ चेरतुश्चारुवेगात् ॥45.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: अयि O, सबल accompanied by Balarama, मुरारे O Krishna - enemy of Mura, पाणि-जानु-प्रचारैः by crawling on hands and knees, किम् अपि what also, भवन-भागान् the various parts of the house, भूषयन्तौ beautifying, भवन्तौ You both, चलित-चरण-कञ्जौ with moving lotus feet, मञ्जु-मञ्जीर-शिञ्जा-श्रवण-कुतुक-भाजौ enjoying the sweet jingling sound of the anklets on Your feet, चेरतुः moved around, चारु-वेगात् with charming speed

Simple Meaning: O Krishna accompanied by Balarama! Crawling on Your hands and knees, You both beautified the various parts of the house. Moving Your lotus feet with charming speed, You both moved around enjoying the sweet jingling sound of the anklets worn on Your feet.

मृदु मृदु विहसन्तावुन्मिषद्दन्तवन्तौ वदनपतितकेशौ दृश्यपादाब्जदेशौ ।
भुजगलितकरान्तव्यालगत्कङ्कणाङ्कौ मतिमहरतमुच्चैः पश्यतां विश्वनृणाम् ॥45.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: मृदु मृदु gently and sweetly, विहसन्तौ smiling, उन्मिषत्-दन्तवन्तौ displaying their newly emerging little teeth, वदन-पतित-केशौ with hair falling over both of Your faces, दृश्य-पादाब्ज-देशौ revealing the beauty of both of Your lotus feet, भुज-गलित-करान्त-व्यालगत्-कङ्कणाङ्कौ with bangles sliding down from both of Your arms to the wrists, मतिम् अहरतम् You both stole the minds, उच्चैः exceedingly, पश्यताम् of those who watched, विश्व-नृणाम् of all the people in the world

Simple Meaning: Smiling gently and sweetly, displaying Your newly emerging little teeth, with locks of hair scattered over Your faces, revealing the beauty of Your lotus feet, and with bangles sliding down from Your arms onto Your wrists, You both exceedingly stole the hearts and minds of all the people in the world who gazed upon You.

अनुसरति जनौघे कौतुकव्याकुलाक्षे किमपि कृतनिनादं व्याहसन्तौ द्रवन्तौ ।
वलितवदनपद्मं पृष्ठतो दत्तदृष्टी किमिव न विदधाथे कौतुकं वासुदेव ॥45.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: अनुसरति when following, जन-ओघे the crowd of people, कौतुक-व्याकुल-आक्षे with eyes eager and filled with curiosity, किम् अपि कृत-निनादम् uttering some sweet indistinct sounds, व्याहसन्तौ laughing aloud, द्रवन्तौ crawling away fast, वलित-वदन-पद्मम् with lotus-like faces turned, पृष्ठतः backwards, दत्त-दृष्टी casting glances, किम् इव न विदधाथे what indeed did You both not create, कौतुकम् wondrous happiness, वासुदेव O Lord Vasudeva

Simple Meaning: O Lord Vasudeva! As crowds of people followed You both with eyes filled with eagerness and delight, You two crawled away fast while making sweet indistinct sounds and laughing aloud. Turning Your lotus faces backward to cast playful glances at them, what boundless joy and wonder did You both not bring to everyone!

द्रुतगतिषु पतन्तावुत्थितौ लिप्तपङ्कौ दिवि मुनिभिरपङ्कैः सस्मितं वन्द्यमानौ ।
द्रुतमथ जननीभ्यां सानुकम्पं गृहीतौ मुहुरपि परिरब्धौ द्राग्युवां चुम्बितौ च ॥45.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्रुत-गतिषु while moving fast, पतन्तौ falling down, उत्थितौ getting up again, लिप्त-पङ्कौ smeared with mud, दिवि in the heavens, मुनिभिः by sages, अ-पङ्कैः by the pure and sinless ones, सस्मितम् with smiles, वन्द्यमानौ being worshipped, द्रुतम् quickly, अथ then, जननीभ्याम् by both the mothers Yashoda and Rohini, स-अनुकम्पम् with deep affection and compassion, गृहीतौ picked up, मुहुः अपि also repeatedly, परिरब्धौ hugged, द्राक् fondly, युवाम् You two, चुम्बितौ च and kissed

Simple Meaning: While moving fast, You two fell down and got up again, covered in mud. Seeing this, the sinless sages in heaven smiled and worshipped You both. Then, both Your mothers quickly picked You both up with deep affection, hugging and kissing You both again and again!

स्नुतकुचभरमङ्के धारयन्ती भवन्तं तरलमति यशोदा स्तन्यदा धन्यधन्या ।
कपटपशुप मध्ये मुग्धहासाङ्कुरं ते दशनमुकुलहृद्यं वीक्ष्य वक्त्रं जहर्ष ॥45.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्नुत-कुच-भरम् with breasts overflowing with milk, अङ्के in her lap, धारयन्ती holding, भवन्तम् You, तरल-मति restless-minded, यशोदा Yashoda, स्तन्य-दा suckling You, धन्य-धन्या the most blessed of all, कपट-पशुप O You disguised as a cowherd, मध्ये in between, मुग्ध-हास-अङ्कुरम् with the sweet first sprouting smile, ते Your, दशन-मुकुल-हृद्यम् enchanting with budding little teeth, वीक्ष्य seeing, वक्त्रम् face, जहर्ष rejoiced with supreme joy

Simple Meaning: Holding You in her lap with her breasts overflowing with milk, the most blessed Yashoda fed You, her mind filled with deep affection. O Lord disguised as a cowherd child! Looking at Your face in between, enchanting with its budding little teeth and the sweet sprouting of Your innocent smile, she rejoiced with supreme joy.

तदनुचरणचारी दारकैस्साकमारान्निलयततिषु खेलन् बालचापल्यशाली ।
भवनशुकविडालान् वत्सकांश्चानुधावन् कथमपि कृतहासैर्गोपकैर्वारितोऽभूः ॥45.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदनु soon after, चरण-चारी walking on Your feet, दारकैः साकम् with other children, अरात् nearby, निलय-ततिषु in the rows of houses, खेलन् playing, बाल-चापल्य-शाली endowed with childish restless playfulness, भवन-शुक-विडालान् house parrots and cats, वत्सकान् च and small calves, अनुधावन् chasing after, कथमपि with great difficulty, कृत-हासैः with smiling and laughing, गोपकैः by the cowherds, वारितः-अभूः You were stopped

Simple Meaning: Soon after learning to walk, You played near the rows of houses with other children, displaying restless childish playfulness. Chasing after pet parrots, cats, and little calves, You were only stopped with great difficulty by the cowherds, who could not help but laugh and smile at Your pranks.

हलधरसहितस्त्वं यत्र यत्रोपयातो विवशपतितनेत्रास्तत्र तत्रैव गोप्यः ।
विगलितगृहकृत्या विस्मृतापत्यभृत्या मुरहर मुहुरत्यन्ताकुला नित्यमासन् ॥45.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: हलधर-सहितः त्वम् You along with Balarama, यत्र यत्र wherever, उपयातः went, विवश-पतित-नेत्राः with their helpless (spellbound) eyes fixed on You, तत्र तत्र एव right in those very places, गोप्यः the gopis, विगलित-गृह-कृत्याः dropping their household chores, विस्मृत-अपत्य-भृत्याः forgetting their children and servants, मुरहर O Krishna - destroyer of Mura, मुहुः repeatedly, अत्यन्त-आकुलाः immensely enchanted and overcome, नित्यम् आसन् always became

Simple Meaning: O Krishna! Wherever You went along with Balarama, the gopis stood in those very places with their spellbound eyes fixed on You. Completely dropping their household chores and forgetting their own children and servants, they were repeatedly and endlessly enchanted by Your irresistible divine beauty!

प्रतिनवनवनीतं गोपिकादत्तमिच्छन् कलपदमुपगायन् कोमलं क्वापि नृत्यन् ।
सदययुवतिलोकैरर्पितं सर्पिरश्नन् क्वचन नवविपक्वं दुग्धमप्यापिबस्त्वम् ॥45.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रतिनव-नवनीतम् fresh butter, गोपिका-दत्तम् offered by the gopis, इच्छन् desiring, कल-पदम् sweet rhythmic tunes, उपगायन् singing softly, कोमलम् gracefully, क्वापि in some places, नृत्यन् dancing, सदय-युवति-लोकैः by the compassionate young gopis, अर्पितम् offered, सर्पिः clarified butter, अश्नन् eating, क्वचन sometimes, नव-विपक्वम् freshly boiled, दुग्धम्-अपि milk also, आपिबः-त्वम् You drank

Simple Meaning: Desiring the fresh butter offered by the gopis, You sang softly with sweet tunes and danced gracefully here and there. Eating the butter offered by those compassionate young gopis, You also drank the freshly boiled milk they lovingly served You sometimes!

मम खलु बलिगेहे याचनं जातमास्तामिह पुनरबलानामग्रतो नैव कुर्वे ।
इति विहितमतिः किं देव सन्त्यज्य याच्ञां दधिघृतमहरस्त्वं चारुणा चोरणेन ॥45.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: मम My, खलु indeed, बलि-गेहे in the house of King Bali, याचनम् begging, जातम् happened, आस्ताम् let that be, इह here, पुनः but, अबलानाम् अग्रतः in front of women, न एव कुर्वे I will never do it at all, इति thus, विहित-मतिः having formed the resolve, किम् is it that, देव O Lord, सन्त्यज्य abandoning, याच्ञाम् begging, दधि-घृतम् butter and ghee, अहरः त्वम् You stole, चारुणा with charming, चोरणेन act of stealing

Simple Meaning: "Indeed, my begging in the house of King Bali was bad enough; I will never beg before women!" O Lord, did You make up Your mind like this and abandon begging altogether, choosing instead to steal butter and ghee through Your charming acts of theft?

तव दधिघृतमोषे घोषयोषाजनानाम् अभजत हृदि रोषो नावकाशं न शोकः ।
हृदयमपि मुषित्वा हर्षसिन्धौ न्यधास्त्वं स मम शमय रोगान् वातगेहाधिनाथ ॥45.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: तव Your, दधि-घृत-मोषे in the stealing of curd and ghee, घोष-योषा-जनानाम् of the gopis of Gokula, अभजत found, हृदि in the heart, रोषः anger, न no, अवकाशम् room, न nor, शोकः grief, हृदयम्-अपि even their hearts, मुषित्वा having stolen, हर्ष-सिन्धौ in the ocean of bliss, न्यधाः त्वम् You immersed them, सः that Lord, मम my, शमय please cure, रोगान् diseases, वातगेहाधिनाथ Lord of Guruvayur

Simple Meaning: When You stole their curd and ghee, neither anger nor grief found any place in the hearts of the gopis of Gokula. Stealing their very hearts as well, You immersed them in an ocean of supreme bliss! O Lord of Guruvayur, please cure all my diseases!

शाखाग्रे विधुं विलोक्य फलमित्य्म्बां च तातं मुहुः संप्रार्थ्याथ तदा तदीयवचसा प्रोत्क्षिप्तबाहौ त्वयि।
चित्रं देव शशी स ते कर्मगात् किं ब्रूमहे संपतः ज्योतिर्मण्डलपूरिताखिलवपुः प्रागा विराड्रूपताम् ॥45.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: शाखा-अग्रे on the tip of the tree branch, विधुम् the moon, विलोक्य seeing, फलम् a fruit, इति thinking, अम्बाम् to Your mother, च and, तातम् to Your father, मुहुः repeatedly, सम्प्रार्थ्य begging, अथ then, तदा at that time, तदीय-वचसा on their words of comfort, प्रोत्क्षिप्त-बाहौ with arms stretched upwards, त्वयि when You were, चित्रम् wondrous, देव O Lord, शशी the moon, सः that, ते Your, करम् अगात् reached Your hand, किम्-ब्रूमहे what can we say, सम्पातात् owing to this arrival, ज्योतिः-मण्डल-पूरित-अखिल-वपुः Your entire body filled with the radiant stellar sphere, प्रागाः You attained, विराट्-रूपताम् the cosmic form

Simple Meaning: Seeing the moon at the tip of a tree branch and thinking it was a fruit, You repeatedly begged Your mother and father for it. When You stretched Your arms upwards at their gentle words, wondrously, O Lord, that moon reached Your hand! What can we say about this miracle? Covered in the brilliant light of the starry stellar sphere, You revealed Your magnificent cosmic form!

किं किं बतेदमिति संभ्रम भाजमेनं ब्रह्मार्णवे क्षणममुं परिमज्ज्य तातम् ।
मायां पुनस्तनयमोहमयीं वितन्वन् आनन्दचिन्मय जगन्मय पाहि रोगात् ॥45.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: किम् किम् बत इदम् इति wondering what this miracle was, सम्भ्रम-भाजम् who was overwhelmed with awe and wonder, एनम् this, ब्रह्म-अर्णवे in the ocean of Supreme Absolute Reality, क्षणम् for a moment, अमुम् that, परिमज्ज्य immersing, तातम् Your father Nanda, मायाम् Your illusory power, पुनः again, तनय-मोह-मयीम् consisting of parental affection for a son, वितन्वन् spreading, आनन्द-चिन्मय O Lord of pure bliss and consciousness, जगन्मय O Lord who pervades the universe, पाहि protect me, रोगात् from disease

Simple Meaning: Immersing Your father Nanda—who was filled with awe and wonder, thinking "What is this miracle!"—in the limitless ocean of Supreme Brahman for a moment, You then spread Your divine Maya of tender fatherly affection over him again. O Lord of pure bliss and consciousness, O Universal Being, please protect me from disease!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
