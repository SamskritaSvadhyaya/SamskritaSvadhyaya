The liberation of Gajendra (Gajendra Moksha)

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 26

The liberation of Gajendra (Gajendra Moksha)

(छन्दस् - शालिनी)

इन्द्रद्युम्नः पाण्ड्यखण्डाधिराजः
त्वद्भक्तात्मा चन्दनाद्रौ कदाचित् ।
त्वत् सेवायां मग्नधीरालुलोके
नैवागस्त्यं प्राप्तमातिथ्यकामम् ॥26.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: इन्द्रद्युम्नः Indradyumna, पाण्ड्य-खण्ड-अधिराजः the ruler of the Pandya kingdom, त्वत्-भक्त-आत्मा greatly devoted to You, चन्दन-अद्रौ on the Malaya mountain (where Sandalwood trees grow), कदाचित् once upon a time, त्वत्-सेवायाम् in Your worship, मग्न-धीः with his mind absorbed, आमुमोच failed to notice/did not see, न एव not at all, अगस्त्यम् Agastya, प्राप्तम् arrived, आतिथ्य-कामम् desiring hospitality

Simple Meaning: Once, Indradyumna, the king of the Pandya region who was Your great devotee, was so absorbed in Your worship on the Malaya mountain that he failed to notice the arrival of Sage Agastya, who had come there expecting hospitality.

कुम्भोद्भूतिः संभृतक्रोधभारः
स्तब्धात्मा त्वं हस्तिभूयं भजेति ।
शप्त्वाऽथैनं प्रत्यगात् सोऽपि लेभे
हस्तीन्द्रत्वं त्वत्स्मृतिव्यक्तिधन्यम् ॥26.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: कुम्भ-उद्भूतिः the sage who was born from a pot - Agastya, संभृत-क्रोध-भारः who became very angry, स्तब्ध-आत्मा arrogant-minded, त्वम् you, हस्ति-भूयम् the state of being an elephant, भज attain, इति thus, शप्त्वा cursing, अथ then, एनम् him, प्रत्यगात् departed, सः अपि he also, लेभे attained, हस्ति-इन्द्रत्वम् the status of a king of elephants, त्वत्-स्मृति-व्यक्ति-धन्यम् blessed with the clear remembrance of You

Simple Meaning: Filled with intense anger, Sage Agastya cursed him saying, "Because you are arrogant, become an elephant!" and then left the place. King Indradyumna was thus reborn as Gajendra, the king of elephants, but he was blessed with the clear remembrance of You.

दुग्धाम्भोधेर्मध्यभाजि त्रिकूटे
क्रीडञ्छैले यूथपोऽयं वशाभिः ।
सर्वान् जन्तूनत्यवर्तिष्ट शक्त्या
त्वद्भक्तानां कुत्र नोत्कर्षलाभः ॥26.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुग्ध-अम्भोधेः of the milk ocean, मध्य-भाजि situated in the middle, त्रिकूटे on the Trikuta, क्रीडन् roaming/sporting, शैले on the mountain, यूथपः अयम् this leader of the herd, वशाभिः with female elephants, सर्वान् all, जन्तून् living beings, अत्यवर्तिष्ट surpassed, शक्त्या by his strength, त्वत्-भक्तानाम् for Your devotees, कुत्र where, न not, उत्कर्ष-लाभः attainment of greatness/superiority

वशा a female elephant, src. Kosha, Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: Sporting with female elephants on Mount Trikuta, which is located in the middle of the ocean of milk, this leader of the herd surpassed all other creatures in sheer power. Where indeed can Your devotees not attain greatness and superiority?

स्वेन स्थेम्ना दिव्यदेशत्वशक्त्या
सोऽयं खेदानप्रजानन् कदाचित् ।
शैलप्रान्ते घर्मतान्तः सरस्यां
यूथैस्सार्धं त्वत्प्रणुन्नोऽभिरेमे ॥26.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्वेन with his own, स्थेम्ना strength, दिव्य-देशत्व-शक्त्या by the power of the divine region, सः अयम् that very elephant, खेदान् fatigue/distress, अप्रजानन् not knowing, कदाचित् once upon a time, शैल-प्रान्ते at the base of the mountain, घर्म-तान्तः exhausted by heat, सरस्याम् in a lake, यूथैः सार्धम् along with his herd, त्वत्-प्रणुन्नः prompted by You, अभिरेमे sported happily

Simple Meaning: By his own strength and by the divine power of that realm, he never experienced any exhaustion or pain. However, one day, feeling weary from the intense heat, he – prompted by You (by Your design)  – entered a lake at the foot of the mountain, where he sported happily along with his herd.

हूहूस्तावद्देवलस्यापि शापात्
ग्राहीभूतस्तज्जले व​र्तमानः ।
जग्राहैनं हस्तिनं पाददेशे
शान्त्यर्थं हि श्रान्तिदोऽसि स्वकानाम् ॥26.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: हूहूः तावत् (gandharva named) Huhu meanwhile, देवलस्य of sage Devala, अपि also, शापात् due to the curse, ग्राही-भूतः having become a crocodile, तत्-जले in that water, वर्तमानः dwelling, जग्राह seized, एनम् this, हस्तिनम् elephant, पाद-देशे at the foot, शान्ति-अर्थम् for ultimate peace, हि indeed, श्रान्ति-दः giver of fatigue/trouble, असि You are, स्वकानाम् to Your own devotees

Simple Meaning: Meanwhile, a gandharva named Huhu, who had been turned into a crocodile due to a curse by Sage Devala, was living in that lake. He grabbed the elephant by his leg. Indeed, You give hardship to Your own devotees only to grant them ultimate peace and liberation.

त्वत्सेवाया वैभवात् दुर्निरोधं
युध्यन्तं तं वत्सराणां सहस्रम् ।
प्राप्ते काले त्वत्पदैकाग्र्यसिध्यै
नक्राक्रान्तं हस्तिवर्यं व्यधास्त्वम् ॥26.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-सेवायाः of Your worship, वैभवात् by the glory, दुर्निरोधम् unyielding/irresistible, युध्यन्तम् fighting, तम् him, वत्सराणाम् of years, सहस्रम् a thousand, प्राप्ते काले when the appointed time arrived, त्वत्-पद-एक-अग्र्य-सिध्यै to grant him one-pointed devotion at Your feet, नक्र-आक्रान्तम् seized by the crocodile, हस्ति-वर्यम् the noble elephant, व्यधाः You made, त्वम् You

Simple Meaning: By the glory of his past worship of You, the noble elephant had fought valiantly and had proved irresistible (for other creatures) for a thousand years. When the right time arrived, You caused the elephant, to be seized by the crocodile, to enable him to focus his mind with absolute concentration solely on Your divine feet.

आर्तिव्यक्तप्राक्तनज्ञानभक्तिः
शुण्डोत्क्षिप्तैः पुण्डरीकैः समर्चन् ।
पूर्वाभ्यस्तं निर्विशेषात्मनिष्ठं
स्तोत्रं श्रेष्ठं सोऽन्वगादीत् परात्मन् ॥26.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: आर्ति-व्यक्त-प्राक्तन-ज्ञान-भक्तिः his past knowledge and devotion revived through intense distress, शुण्डा-उत्क्षिप्तैः lifted up with his trunk, पुण्डरीकैः with white lotus flowers, समर्चन् worshipping, पूर्व-अभ्यस्तम् practiced in his previous life, निर्विशेष-आत्म-निष्ठम् focused on the unconditioned Supreme Being, स्तोत्रम् hymn of praise, श्रेष्ठम् excellent, सः he, अन्वगादीत् recited, परात्मन् O Supreme Soul

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Soul, as his distress reached its peak, Gajendra's past knowledge and devotion were awakened. Lifting white lotus flowers with his trunk, he offered them in worship to You and recited the great hymn of praise focused on the unconditioned Supreme Being, which he had learnt in his previous life.

श्रुत्वा स्तोत्रं निर्गुणस्थं समस्तं
ब्रह्मेशाद्यैर्नाहमित्यप्रयाते ।
सर्वात्मा त्वं भूरिकारुण्यवेगात्
तार्क्ष्यारूढः प्रेक्षितोऽभूः पुरस्तात् ॥26.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रुत्वा hearing, स्तोत्रम् the hymn of praise, निर्गुण-स्थम् addressed to the attribute-less Supreme, समस्तम् in its entirety, ब्रह्म-ईश-आद्यैः by Brahma, Shiva, and others, न अहम् इति thinking "it is not meant for me", अप्रयाते not proceeding, सर्व-आत्मा You who are the Soul of all beings, त्वम् You, भूरि-कारुण्य-वेगात् through the rush of boundless compassion, तार्क्ष्य-आरूढः mounted on Garuda, प्रेक्षितः अभूः became visible, पुरस्तात् before him

Simple Meaning: Hearing that hymn of praise addressed to the attribute-less Supreme, devas like Brahma and Shiva refrained from going to his aid, thinking the prayer was not directed at them specifically. But You, who are the Soul of all beings, mounted Garuda and appeared before Gajendra, driven by a surge of boundless compassion.

हस्तीन्द्रं तं हस्तपद्मेन धृत्वा
चक्रेण त्वं नक्रवर्यं व्यदारीः ।
गन्धर्वेऽस्मिन् मुक्तशापे स हस्ती
त्वत्सारूप्यं प्राप्य देदीप्यते स्म ॥26.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: हस्ति-इन्द्रम् the king of elephants, तम् him, हस्त-पद्मेन with Your lotus-like hand, धृत्वा pulling up/holding, चक्रेण with Your Sudarshana Chakra, त्वम् You, नक्र-वर्यम् the formidable crocodile, व्यदारीः tore apart, गन्धर्वे in the gandharva, अस्मिन् this one, मुक्त-शापे freed from the curse, सः he, हस्ती elephant, त्वत्-सारूप्यम् Your divine form, प्राप्य attaining, देदीप्यते स्म shone brilliantly

Simple Meaning: Lifting up the king of elephants with Your lotus-like hand, You split open the formidable crocodile using Your discus. As the gandharva was freed from his curse and departed, the noble elephant attained Your divine form and shone with luminous glory.

एतद्वृत्तं त्वां च मां च प्रगे यो
गायेत्सोऽयं भूयसे श्रेयसे स्यात् ।
इत्युक्त्वैनं तेन सार्धं गतस्त्वं
धिष्ण्यं विष्णो पाहि वातालयेश ॥26.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतत् वृत्तम् this incident, त्वाम् You, च and, माम् me, च and, प्रगे in the early morning, यः whoever, गायेत् sings/chants, सः अयम् that person, भूयसे for abundant, श्रेयसे supreme spiritual well-being/prosperity, स्यात् will be, इति thus, उक्त्वा having said, एनम् to him, तेन सार्धम् along with him, गतः went, त्वम् You, धिष्ण्यम् to Your divine abode, विष्णो O Vishnu, पाहि protect, वात-आलय-ईश O Lord of the abode of the Wind - Guruvayoor

Simple Meaning: “Whoever chants this sacred episode of you (Gajendra) and Me early in the morning will be blessed with abundant prosperity and ultimate liberation.” Having said this to him, You returned to Your supreme abode along with Gajendra. O Vishnu, O Lord of Guruvayoor, please protect me!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
