Dashaka 62

Krishna prevents the sacrifice for Indra

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 62

Krishna prevents the sacrifice for Indra

कदाचिद्गोपालान् विहितमखसम्भारविभवान्
निरीक्ष्य त्वं शौरे मघवमदमुद्ध्वंसितुमनाः ।
विजानन्नप्येतान् विनयमृदु नन्दादिपशुपान्
अपृच्छः को वाऽयं जनक भवतामुद्यम इति ॥62.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: कदाचित् once, गोपालान् the gopas, विहित-मख-सम्भार-विभवान् who had gathered abundant arrangements for a sacrifice, निरीक्ष्य seeing, त्वम् You, शौरे O Krishna, मघव-मदम् the pride of Indra, उद्ध्वंसितु-मनाः wishing to destroy, विजानन् knowing, अपि although, एतान् these, विनय-मृदु with gentle humility, नन्द-आदि-पशुपान् the cowherds headed by Nanda, अपृच्छः You asked, कः what, वा indeed, अयम् this, जनक O father, भवताम् of yours, उद्यमः preparation, इति thus

Simple Meaning: O Krishna, seeing the gopas with abundant materials gathered for a sacrifice, You wished to shatter the pride of Indra. Although You already knew everything, You humbly and gently asked Nanda and the other cowherds, O father, what is this grand preparation of yours?

बभाषे नन्दस्त्वां सुत ननु विधेयो मघवतो
मखो वर्षे वर्षे सुखयति स वर्षेण पृथिवीम् ।
नृणां वर्षायत्तं निखिलमुपजीव्यं महितले
विशेषादस्माकं तृणसलिलजीवा हि पशवः ॥62.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: बभाषे replied, नन्दः Nanda, त्वाम् to You, सुत O son, ननु indeed, विधेयः to be performed, मघवतः for Indra, मखः sacrifice, वर्षे वर्षे year after year, सुखयति he gladdens, सः he, वर्षेण with rain, पृथिवीम् the earth, नृणाम् of men, वर्ष-आयत्तम् dependent on rain, निखिलम् all, उपजीव्यम् livelihood, महितले on the earth, विशेषात् especially, अस्माकम् for us, तृण-सलिल-जीवाः depending on grass and water for living, हि indeed, पशवः cattle

Simple Meaning: Nanda replied to You, O son, indeed this sacrifice for Indra must be performed year after year. He gladdens the earth with rain. The entire livelihood of human beings on this earth depends on rain, and especially for us, whose cattle depend entirely on grass and water for their survival.

इति श्रुत्वा वाचं पितुरयि भवानाह सरसं
धिगेतन्नो सत्यं मघवजनिता वृष्टिरिति यत् ।
अदृष्टं जीवानां सृजति खलु वृष्टिं समुचितां
महारण्ये वृक्षाः किमिव बलिमिन्द्राय ददते ॥62.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, श्रुत्वा having heard, वाचम् words, पितुः of father, अयि O Lord, भवान् You, आह spoke, सरसम् delightfully, धिक् fie, एतत् this, नो not, सत्यम् true, मघव-जनिता caused by Indra, वृष्टिः rain, इति that, यत् which, अदृष्टम् unseen destiny, जीवानाम् of living beings, सृजति creates, खलु indeed, वृष्टिम् rain, समुचिताम् suitable, महा-रण्ये in the dense forest, वृक्षाः trees, किम् इव what indeed, बलिम् offering, इन्द्राय to Indra, ददते give

Simple Meaning: O Lord, having heard these words of Your father, You spoke charmingly, Fie upon this thought! It is not true that rain is caused by Indra. Indeed, it is the unseen destiny of living beings that brings suitable rainfall. What worship or offering do the trees in the vast forest ever give to Indra?

इदं तावत् सत्यं यदिह पशवो नः कुलधनं
तदाजीव्यायासौ बलिरचलभर्त्रे समुचितः ।
सुरेभ्योऽप्युत्कृष्टा ननु धरणिदेवाः क्षितितले
ततस्तेऽप्याराध्या इति जगदिथ त्वं निजजनान् ॥62.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: इदम् this, तावत् however, सत्यम् true, यत् that, इह here, पशवः cattle, नः our, कुल-धनम् ancestral wealth, तद्-आजीव्याय for their livelihood, असौ this, बलिः offering, अचल-भर्त्रे to the lord of mountains, समुचितः appropriate, सुरेभ्यः अपि even than the devas, उत्कृष्टः superior, ननु indeed, धरणि-देवाः the holy men, क्षिति-तले on this earth, ततः therefore, ते-अपि they also, आराध्याः worshipful, इति thus, जगदिथ You spoke, त्वम् You, निज-जनान् to Your people

Simple Meaning: However, if this is true that cattle are our true wealth, then an offering is appropriate for the lord of mountains, Govardhana, for their sustainment. Moreover, the holy men on earth are superior even to the devas, and so they too should be revered and worshipped. Thus You spoke to Your people.

भवद्वाचं श्रुत्वा बहुमतियुतास्तेऽपि पशुपाः
द्विजेन्द्रानर्चन्तो बलिमददुरुच्चैः क्षितिभृते ।
व्यधुः प्रादक्षिण्यं सुभृशमनमन्नादरयुताः
त्वमादश्शैलात्मा बलिमखिलमाभीरपुरतः ॥62.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: भवत्-वाचम् Your words, श्रुत्वा having heard, बहुमति-युताः with great respect, ते अपि those very, पशुपाः cowherds, द्विजेन्द्रान-अर्चन्तः worshipping the exalted brahmanas, बलिम् अददुः offered oblations, उच्चैः grandly, क्षितिभृते to the mountain Govardhana, व्यधुः performed, प्रादक्षिण्यम् circumambulation, सुभृशम् अनमन् bowed down deeply, आदर-युताः filled with reverence, त्वम् You, आदः ate, शैल-आत्मा assuming the form of the mountain, बलिम् अखिलम् all the offerings, आभीर-पुरतः before the cowherds

Simple Meaning: Having heard Your words with immense respect, those cowherds worshipped the noble brahmanas and offered grand oblations to the mountain Govardhana. Filled with deep reverence, they performed circumambulation and bowed down before it. Thereupon, assuming the massive form of the mountain itself, You consumed all the offered food right in front of the cowherds.

अवोचश्चैवं तान् किमिह वितथं मे निगदितं
गिरीन्द्रो नन्वेष स्वबलिमुपभुङ्क्ते स्ववपुषा ।
अयं गोत्रो गोत्रद्विषि च कुपिते रक्षितुमलं
समस्तानित्युक्ता जहृषुरखिला गोकुलजुषः ॥62.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: अवोचः You spoke, च and, एवम् thus, तान् to them, किम् what, इह here, वितथम् untrue, मे by Me, निगदितम् spoken, गिरि-इन्द्रः the king of mountains, ननु indeed, एषः this, स्व-बलिम् its own offering, उपभुङ्क्ते consumes, स्व-वपुषा in its own form, अयम् this, गोत्रः mountain, गोत्र-द्विषि when the enemy of mountains Indra, च and, कुपिते becomes angry, रक्षितुम् to protect, अलम् capable, समस्तान् all of us, इति thus, उक्ताः being addressed, जहाृषुः rejoiced, अखिलाः all, गोकुल-जुषः the residents of Gokula

Simple Meaning: And You spoke to them thus, Was anything I said untrue? See, this king of mountains is indeed consuming its own offering in its own physical form. Furthermore, even if the enemy of mountains Indra becomes enraged, this mountain is fully capable of protecting us all. Being addressed thus, all the residents of Gokula rejoiced greatly.

परिप्रीता याताः खलु भवदुपेता व्रजजुषो
व्रजं यावत्तावन्निजमखविभङ्गं निशमयन् ।
भवन्तं जानन्नप्यधिकरजसाऽऽक्रान्तहृदयो
न सेहे देवेन्द्रस्त्वदुपरचितात्मोन्नतिरपि ॥62.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: परिप्रीताः immensely delighted, याताः returned, खलु indeed, भवत्-उपेताः accompanied by You, व्रज-जुषः the residents of Vraja, व्रजम् to Vraja, यावत् while, तावत् right then, निज-मख-विभङ्गम् the disruption of his own sacrifice, निशमयन् hearing about, भवन्तम् You, जानन् knowing, अपि despite, अधिक-रजसा-आक्रान्त-हृदयः with a heart overwhelmed by intense mode of passion (rajoguna), न not, सेहे tolerated, देव-इन्द्रः Indra, त्वत्-उपरचित-आत्म-उन्नतिः whose own exalted stature was established by You, अपि even though

Simple Meaning: Accompanied by You, the residents of Vraja returned home filled with immense delight. Meanwhile, hearing about the cancellation of his own sacrifice, Indra, whose exalted stature was established by You, could not tolerate it. Although knowing Your divine nature, his heart was completely overcome by intense passion and pride.

मनुष्यत्वं यातो मधुभिदपि देवेष्वविनयं
विधत्ते चेन्नष्टस्त्रिदशसदसां कोऽपि महिमा ।
ततश्च ध्वंसिष्ये पशुपहतकस्य श्रियमिति
प्रवृत्तस्त्वां जेतुं स किल मघवा दुर्मदनिधिः ॥62.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: मनुष्यत्वम् human form, यातः having attained, मधु-भिद् Vishnu the destroyer of Madhu, अपि even, देवेषु toward the devas, अविनयम् disrespect, विधत्ते shows, चेत् if, नष्टः destroyed, त्रिदश-सदसाम् of all the devas, कः अपि whatever, महिमा glory, ततः च therefore, ध्वंसिष्ये I will destroy, पशुप-हतकस्य of the wretched cowherd, श्रियम् the fortune, इति thus thinking, प्रवृत्तः prepared, त्वाम् You, जेतुम् to conquer, सः he, किल indeed, मघवा Indra, दुर्मद-निधिः a treasure house of arrogance

Simple Meaning: Thinking, even if it is Vishnu, who having assumed a human form, displays disrespect toward the devas, then the glory of the devas is destroyed. Therefore, I will destroy the fortune of this wretched cowherd, that arrogant Indra set out to conquer You.

त्वदावासं हन्तुं प्रलयजलदानम्बरभुवि
प्रहिण्वन् बिभ्राणः कुलिशमयमभ्रेभगमनः ।
प्रतस्थेऽन्यैरन्तर्दहनमरुदाद्यैविंहसितो
भवन्माया नैव त्रिभुवनपते मोहयति कम् ॥62.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-आवासम् Your abode, हन्तुम् to destroy, प्रलय-जलदान् the clouds of doom, अम्बर-भुवि in the sky, प्रहिण्वन् dispatching, बिभ्राणः brandishing, कुलिशम् thunderbolt, अयम् this Indra, अभ्र​-इभ​-गमनः riding the white elephant - Airavata, प्रतस्थे set out, अन्यैः by other devas, अन्तर्-दहन-मरुत्-आद्यैः such as Agni and Vayu, विहसितः secretly mocked, भवत्-माया Your maya, न एव not indeed, त्रिभुवन-पते O Lord of the three worlds, मोहयति delude, कम् whom

Simple Meaning: O Lord of the three worlds, dispatching the clouds of doom into the sky to destroy Your abode Vraja, brandishing his thunderbolt, and riding his white elephant Airavata, Indra set out. Secretly mocked by the other devas like Agni and Vayu, his foolish pride was evident. Whom indeed does Your divine maya not delude?

सुरेन्द्रः क्रुद्धश्चेत् द्विजकरुणया शैलकृपया
अप्यनातङ्कोऽस्माकं नियत इति विश्वास्य पशुपान् ।
अहो किन्नायातो गिरिभिदिति सञ्चिन्त्य निवसन्
मरुद्गेहाधीश प्रणुद मुरवैरिन् मम गदान् ॥62.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुर-इन्द्रः Indra, क्रुद्धः angry, चेत् if, द्विज-करुणया by the mercy of the brahmanas, शैल-कृपया by the grace of the mountain, अपि also, अनातङ्कः free from fear or distress, अस्माकम् our, नियतः absolute, इति thus, विश्वास्य reassuring, पशुपान् the cowherds, अहो Oh, किम् न आयातः why has not arrived, गिरि-भिद् Indra the breaker of mountains, इति thus, सञ्चिन्त्य thinking, निवसन् residing, मरुद्गेह-धीश O Lord of Guruvayur, प्रणुद eradicate, मुर-वैरिन् O enemy of Mura, मम my, गदान् diseases

Simple Meaning: Reassuring the cowherds by saying, Even if Indra becomes enraged, our safety is absolute through the mercy of the brahmanas and the grace of the mountain. You waited, thinking to Yourself, Oh, why has Indra not arrived yet? O Lord of Guruvayur, O enemy of Mura, please eradicate all my diseases!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
