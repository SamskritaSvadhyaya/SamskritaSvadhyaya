Salvation of Paundraka. Destruction of Sudakshina (the son of the king of Kashi) and Vividha (Dvividha)

दशकम् ८३ · Salvation of Paundraka

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 83

Salvation of Paundraka

Destruction of Sudakshina (the son of the king of Kashi) and Vividha (Dvividha)

रामेऽथ गोकुलगते प्रमदाप्रसक्ते
हूतानुपेतयमुनादमने मदान्धे ।
स्वैरं समारमति सेवकवादमूढो
दूतं न्ययुङ्क्त तव पौण्ड्रकवासुदेवः ॥83.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: रामे अथ then while Balarama, गोकुल-गते had gone to Gokula, प्रमदा-प्रसक्ते and was immersed in joy with the gopis, हूत-अनुपेत-यमुना-दमने who subdued the Yamuna river when she did not come at his call, मद-अन्धे blinded by pride and intoxication, स्वैरम् समारमति was sporting freely at will, सेवक-वाद-मूढः deluded by the flattery of his attendants, दूतम् an envoy, न्ययुङ्क्त dispatched, तव to You, पौण्ड्रक-वासुदेवः Paundraka Vaasudeva

Simple Meaning: While Balarama was visiting Gokula, enjoying the company of the damsels and subduing the river Yamuna when she failed to respond to his summons, a king named Paundraka Vaasudeva became utterly deluded by the false praise of his sycophantic courtiers. Imagining himself to be the true Supreme Lord Vaasudeva, he sent an arrogant messenger to You in Dwaraka.

नारायणोऽहमवतीर्ण इहास्मि भूमौ
धत्से किल त्वमपि मामकलक्षणानि ।
उत्सृज्य तानि शरणं व्रज मामिति त्वां
दूतो जगाद सकलैर्हसितः सभायाम् ॥83.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: नारायणः अहम् I am Narayana, अवतीर्णः descended, इह here, अस्मि am, भूमौ on the earth, धत्से You bear, किल indeed, त्वम् अपि You as well, मामक-लक्षणानि my divine insignia and marks, उत्सृज्य having discarded, तानि those, शरणम्-व्रज take refuge, माम् in me, इति thus, त्वाम् to You, दूतः the messenger, जगाद spoke, सकलैः हसितः laughed at by everyone, सभायाम् in the assembly

Simple Meaning: The envoy arrived at Your court in Dwaraka and declared before the whole assembly: "I am Narayana Himself who has descended to this earth. You are falsely displaying my divine insignias. Renounce those symbols immediately and seek refuge in me!" Hearing this arrogant message, everyone present in Your royal assembly burst into laughter.

दूतेऽथ यातवति यादवसैनिकैस्त्वं
यातो ददर्शिथ वपुः किल पौण्ड्रकीयम् ।
तापेन वक्षसि कृताङ्कमनल्पमूल्य-
श्रीकौस्तुभं मकरकुण्डलपीतचेलम् ॥83.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: दूते अथ यातवति when the messenger had returned, यादव-सैनिकैः त्वम् You along with the Yadava warriors, यातः marching forth, ददर्शिथ beheld, वपुः the form, किल indeed, पौण्ड्रकीयम् of Paundraka, तापेन by branding, वक्षसि on his chest, कृत-अङ्कम् bearing the mark of Srivatsa, अनल्प-मूल्य-श्री-कौस्तुभम् wearing an imitation Kaustubha gem of great apparent value, मकर-कुण्डल-पीत-चेलम् adorned with crocodile-shaped earrings and yellow silk garments

Simple Meaning: When the envoy departed, You marched forth accompanied by Your Yadava forces and came face to face with Paundraka. You saw him foolishly imitating Your divine form—he had branded the mark of Srivatsa upon his chest, wore a fake Kaustubha gem, and was dressed in yellow silk robes with crocodile-shaped earrings hanging from his ears.

कालायसं निजसुदर्शनमस्यतोऽस्य
कालानलोत्करकिरेण सुदर्शनेन ।
शीर्षं चकर्तिथ ममर्दिथ चास्य सेनां
तन्मित्रकाशिपशिरोऽपि चकर्थ काश्याम् ॥83.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: काल-आयसम् made of black iron, निज-सुदर्शनम् his own weapon named Sudarshana, अस्यतः throwing/wielding, अस्य of this Paundraka, काल-अनल-उत्कर-किरेण showering mass of flames like the fire of destruction (Kalanala), सुदर्शनेन by Your divine Sudarshana Chakra, शीर्षम् the head, चकर्तिथ You severed, ममर्दिथ You crushed, च and, अस्य his, सेनाम् army, तत्-मित्र-काशि-प-शिरः अपि also the head of his friend the King of Kashi, चकर्थ You hurled, काश्याम् into the city of Kashi

Simple Meaning: When Paundraka hurled his fake Sudarshana chakra made of black iron, You severed his head using Your divine Sudarshana Chakra, which blazed with blazing fires like in the final deluge. After annihilating his entire army, You also severed the head of his ally, the King of Kashi, and hurled it straight into the city of Kashi.

जाल्येन बालकगिराऽपि किलाहमेव
श्रीवासुदेव इति रूढमतिश्चिरं सः ।
सायुज्यमेव भवदैक्यधिया गतोऽभूत्
को नाम कस्य सुकृतं कथमित्यवेयात् ॥83.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: जाल्येन due to foolishness, बालक-गिरा अपि even by the childish words of flatterers, किल indeed, अहम् एव I alone am, श्री-वासुदेवः Lord Vaasudeva, इति thus, रूढ-मतिः with a firmly entrenched conviction, चिरम् for a long time, सः he (Paundraka), सायुज्यम् एव liberation by absorption into the Supreme Lord (Sayujya mukti), भवत्-ऐक्य-धिया through constant contemplation on oneness with You, गतः अभूत् attained, कः नाम who indeed, कस्य whose, सुकृतम् spiritual merit, कथम् इति अवेयात् how can one comprehend

Simple Meaning: Due to his profound foolishness and the deceptive flattery of his sycophants, Paundraka had firmly convinced himself for a long time that he was Lord Vaasudeva. Because his mind was constantly absorbed in thinking of himself as one with You—even out of delusion—he attained Sayujya mukti (merging into Your divine being) upon his death. Who can ever fathom the mysterious workings of spiritual merit and Your boundless grace?

काशीश्वरस्य तनयोऽथ सुदक्षिणाख्यः
शर्वं प्रपूज्य भवते विहिताभिचारः ।
कृत्यानलं कमपि बाणरणातिभीतैः
भूतैः कथञ्चन वृतैः सममभ्यमुञ्चत् ॥83.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: काशी-ईश्वरस्य of the King of Kashi, तनयः son, अथ then, सुदक्षिण-आख्यः named Sudakshina, शर्वम् Lord Shiva (Sharva), प्रपूज्य having worshipped, भवते against You, विहित-अभिचारः performing black magic rites (abhichara), कृत्य-अनलम् a fierce fire-deity (Kritya-agni), कम् अपि an extraordinary, बाण-रण-अति-भीतैः who were deeply terrified by the battle with Banasura, भूतैः with ghost-spirits (bhutas), कथञ्चन with great difficulty, वृतैः assembled, समम् together with, अभ्यमुञ्चत् unleashed

Simple Meaning: Deeply aggrieved by his father's death, Sudakshina, the son of the King of Kashi, performed intense worship of Lord Shiva to conduct a black magic ritual against You. He created a terrifying fiery entity (Kritya) and unleashed it towards Dwaraka, accompanied by ghost-spirits who were persuaded with great difficulty, as they were still terrified from their earlier defeat in Banasura's battle.

तालप्रमाणचरणामखिलं दहन्तीं
कृत्यां विलोक्य चकितैः कथितोऽपि पौरैः ।
द्यूतोत्सवे किमपि नो चलितो विभो त्वं
पार्श्वस्थमाशु विससर्जिथ कालचक्रम् ॥83.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताल-प्रमाण-चरणाम् whose legs were as tall as palm trees, अखिलम् all around/everything, दहन्तीम् burning, कृत्याम् the fiery entity Kritya, विलोक्य seeing, चकितैः by the frightened, कथितः अपि although informed, पौरैः by the citizens of Dwaraka, द्यूत-उत्सवे in a game of dice, किमपि not in the least, नो not, चलितः disturbed or moved, विभो O All-pervading Lord, त्वम् You, पार्श्वस्थम् situated at Your side, आशु swiftly, विससर्जिथ dispatched, कालचक्रम् the Sudarshana Chakra (wheel of time)

Simple Meaning: Seeing the entity Kritya approaching with legs as tall as palm trees and consuming everything in fire, the terrified citizens of Dwaraka rushed to You in panic. O All-pervading Lord, completely undisturbed while enjoying a game of dice, You casually and swiftly dispatched Your divine Sudarshana Chakra, which stood ever-ready at Your side.

अभ्यापतत्यमितधाम्नि भवन्महास्त्रे
हा हेति विद्रुतवती खलु घोरकृत्या।
रोषात् सुदक्षिणमदक्षिणचेष्टितं तं
पुप्लोष चक्रमपि काशिपुरीमधाक्षीत् ॥83.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: अभ्यापतति as it blazed forward and charged, अमित-धाम्नि possessing boundless luster and power, भवत्-महा-अस्त्रे Your supreme weapon (Sudarshana Chakra), हा हा इति crying out "Alas, alas!", विद्रुतवती fled back in fear, खलु indeed, घोर-कृत्या the terrifying entity Kritya, रोषात् in utter fury, सुदक्षिणम् Sudakshina, अदक्षीण-चेष्टितम् who had engaged in foolish and evil deeds, तम् him, पुप्लोष burnt to ashes, चक्रम् अपि the Sudarshana Chakra as well, काशिपुरीम् अधाक्षीत् burnt down the city of Kashi

Simple Meaning: As Your supreme weapon, the Sudarshana Chakra, advanced blazing with boundless effulgence, the terrifying Kritya turned around and fled in panic, screaming "Alas, alas!" and in fury burnt Sudakshina - who had engaged in foolish and evil deeds - to ashes. The Sudarshana Chakra pursued Kritya all the way back, and burnt the entire city of Kashi.

स खलु विविदो रक्षोघाते कृतोपकृतिः पुरा
तव तु कलया मृत्युं प्राप्तुं तदा खलतां गतः ।
नरकसचिवो देशक्लेशं सृजन् नगरान्तिके
झटिति हलिना युध्यन्नद्धा पपात तलाहतः ॥83.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, खलु indeed, विविधः Vivida (the monkey chieftain - Mainda's brother), रक्षः-घाते in the destruction of the rakshasas during Lord Rama's incarnation, कृत-उपकृतिः who had rendered great assistance, पुरा in ancient times, तव Your, तु but, कलया with an aspect (Balarama), मृत्युम् death, प्राप्तुम् to attain, तदा then, खलताम् wickedness or mischievous behavior, गतः succumbed to, नरक-सचिवः a close minister/ally of Narakasura, देश-क्लेशम् great hardship and distress to the countryside, सृजन् causing, नगर-अन्तिके near the city of Raivataka/Dwaraka, झटिति quickly, हलिना with Balarama (the wielder of the plow), युध्यन् fighting, अद्धा truly, पपात fell dead, तल-आहतः struck down by a blow from his palm

Simple Meaning: Long ago, during Your incarnation as Lord Rama, the monkey chief Vivida had rendered great service in defeating the rakshasa forces. However, in order to attain liberation and death at the hands of Your partial incarnation Balarama, he fell into evil ways and became an ally of the Narakasura. Causing great havoc and torment in the countryside near Dwaraka, he engaged in a fierce duel with Balarama and was instantly killed when struck down by a single powerful blow from Balarama's palm.

Note: Vividha is also known as Dvividha in the Shrimad Bhagavatam. Here the poet has used the name Vividha.

साम्बं कौरव्यपुत्रीहरणनियमितं सान्त्वनार्थी कुरूणां
यातस्तद्वाक्यरोषोद्धृतकरिनगरो मोचयामास रामः ।
ते घात्याः पाण्डवेयैरिति यदुपृतनां नामुचस्त्वं तदानीं
तं त्वां दुर्बोधलीलं पवनपुरपते तापशान्त्यै निषेवे ॥83.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: साम्बम् Samba (Your son by Jambavati), कौरव्य-पुत्री-हरण-नियमितम् who was imprisoned for abducting Lakshmanaa (the daughter of Duryodhana, the Kuru king), सान्त्वन-अर्थी seeking a peaceful resolution, कुरूणाम् among the Kurus, यातः having gone, तत्-वाक्य-रोष-उद्धृत-करिनगरः who lifted up and shook the city of Hastinapura (Karinagara) in rage at their insulting words, मोचयामास secured the release of Samba, रामः Balarama (Rama), ते they (the Kauravas), घात्याः were to be slain, पाण्डवेयैः by the Pandavas, इति considering thus, यदु-पृतनाम् the Yadava army, न अमुचः You did not send or dispatch, त्वम् You, तदानीम् at that time, तम् त्वाम् You who are of such, दुर्बोध-लीलम् unfathomable divine pastimes, पवनपुरपते O Lord of Guruvayur, ताप-शान्त्यै for the alleviation of my suffering, निषेवे I worship and serve

Simple Meaning: When Your son Samba was captured and imprisoned for carrying off Duryodhana's daughter Lakshmanaa, Balarama traveled to Hastinapura seeking a peaceful settlement with the Kauravas. Infuriated by their arrogant and insulting insults, Balarama began dragging the entire city of Hastinapura into the Ganges using his plough, forcing them to release Samba and his bride. Knowing that the Kauravas were destined to be slain later by the Pandavas in the great war, You refrained from deploying the Yadava army at that time. O Lord of Guruvayur, whose divine plays are incomprehensible to human intellect, I reverently worship You for the complete relief of my afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
