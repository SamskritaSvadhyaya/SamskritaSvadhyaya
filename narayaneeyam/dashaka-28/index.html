Dashaka 28

The gifts of the ocean

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

 ॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 28

The gifts of the ocean

(छन्दस् - औपच्छन्दसिकम्)

गरलं तरलानलं पुरस्ताज्जलधेरुद्विजगाल कालकूटम् ।
अमरस्तुतिवादमोदनिघ्नो गिरिशस्तन्निपपौ भवत्प्रियार्थम् ॥28.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: गरलम् poison, तरल-अनलम् possessing flickering/wild flames, पुरस्तात् first, जलधेः from the ocean, उद्विजगाल emerged/spewed forth, कालकूटम् the deadly poison Kaalakuta, अमर-स्तुति-वाद-मोद-निघ्नः overwhelmed by joy at the praises of the immortals (devas), गिरिशः Lord Shiva, तत् that poison, निपपौ drank up, भवत्-प्रिय-अर्थम् to please You

Simple Meaning: First, the terrifying poison Kaalakuta spewed forth from the churning ocean, blazing with wild, flickering flames. Pleased by the earnest prayers of the gods, Lord Shiva drank up that deadly poison to bring delight to You.

विमथत्सु सुरासुरेषु जाता सुरभिस्तामृषिषु न्यधास्त्रिधामन् ।
हयरत्नमभूदथेभरत्नं द्युतरुश्चाप्सरसः सुरेषु तानि ॥28.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: विमथत्सु as they continued churning, सुर-असुरेषु the gods and asuras, जाता emerged, सुरभिः Surabhi (the divine wish-fulfilling cow), ताम् her, ऋषिषु among the sages, न्यधाः You placed/entrusted, त्रिधामन् O Lord of three abodes/forms, हय-रत्नम् the jewel among horses (Uchchaihshravas), अभूत् appeared, अथ then, इभ-रत्नम् the jewel among elephants (Airavata), द्युतरुः the celestial tree (Parijata), च and, अप्सरसः the celestial nymphs (Apsaras), सुरेषु to the gods, तानि those (were given)

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Lord of three abodes! As the gods and asuras continued churning, the divine cow Surabhi emerged first, and You entrusted her to the sages for their sacred rites. Next came the glorious horse Uchchaihshravas, the noble elephant Airavata, the heavenly Parijata tree, and the divine Apsaras—all of which were given to the gods.

जगदीश भवत्परा तदानीं कमनीया कमला बभूव देवी ।
अमलामवलोक्य यां विलोलः सकलोऽपि स्पृहयाम्बभूव लोकः ॥28.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: जगदीश O Lord of the Universe, भवत्-परा devoted solely to You, तदानीम् then, कमनीया enchanting/exceedingly beautiful, कमला Goddess Lakshmi, बभूव manifested, देवी the divine goddess, अमलाम् pure/flawless, अवलोक्य beholding, याम् whom, विलोलः agitated with desire, सकलः अपि everyone without exception, स्पृहयाम् बभूव longed for her, लोकः the world (gods, asuras, and beings)

Simple Meaning: O Lord of the Universe, there then manifested Goddess Lakshmi, radiating divine charm and entirely devoted to You alone. Beholding her immaculate and matchless beauty, everyone in the universe—gods, asuras, and celestial beings alike—became captivated with deep longing for her.

त्वयि दत्तहृदे तदैव देव्यै त्रिदशेन्द्रो मणिपीठिकां व्यतारीत् ।
सकलोपहृताभिषेचनीयैः ऋषयस्तां श्रुतिगीर्भिरभ्यषिञ्चन् ॥28.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वयि upon You, दत्त-हृदे whose heart was entirely bestowed/fixed, तदा एव at that very moment, देव्यै to Goddess Lakshmi, त्रिदश-इन्द्रः the king of gods (Indra), मणि-पीठिकाम् a gem-studded throne, व्यतारीत् offered, सकल-उपहृत-अभिषेचनीयैः with all the sacred ritual paraphernalia brought by everyone, ऋषयः the great sages, ताम् her, श्रुति-गीर्भिः with Vedic hymns, अभ्यषिञ्चन् performed the sacred bath/consecration (Abhisheka)

Simple Meaning: To Goddess Lakshmi, whose heart was already completely set on You alone, Indra immediately offered a magnificent, gem-studded throne. Then, using all the holy waters and ritual offerings brought by all the gods and beings, the great sages performed her grand royal consecration while chanting sacred Vedic hymns.

अभिषेकजलानुपातिमुग्धत्वदपाङ्गैरवभूषिताङ्गवल्लीम् ।
मणिकुण्डलपीतचेलहारप्रमुखैस्ताममरादयोऽन्वभूषन् ॥28.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: अभिषेक-जल-अनुपाति-मुग्ध-त्वत्-अपाङ्गैः by Your charming side-glances falling in unison with the sacred bath waters, अवभूषित-अङ्ग-वल्लीम् whose creeper-like slender body was adorned, मणि-कुण्डल-पीतचेल-हार-प्रमुखैः with gem-studded earrings, yellow silk garments, necklaces, and other fine ornaments, ताम् her (Goddess Lakshmi), अमर-आदयः the gods and other celestial beings, अन्वभूषन् adorned/decorated

Simple Meaning: As the ritual bath waters poured over her, Goddess Lakshmi's creeper-like body was already exquisitely embellished by Your loving, side-glances falling upon her. The gods and celestial beings then joyfully adorned her with radiant gem-studded earrings, brilliant yellow silk garments, exquisite necklaces, and other divine ornaments.

वरणस्रजमात्तभृङ्गनादां दधती सा कुचकुम्भमन्दयाना ।
पदशिञ्जितमञ्जुनूपुरा त्वां कलितव्रीलविलासमाससाद ॥28.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: वरण-स्रजम् the garland of choice/wedding garland, आत्त-भृङ्ग-नादाम् echoing with the sweet humming of attracted bees, दधती holding/carrying, सा She (Goddess Lakshmi), कुच-कुम्भ-मन्द-याना moving gracefully and slowly owing to her heavy, pot-like breasts, पद-शिञ्जित-मञ्जु-नूपुरा with delightful anklets jingling sweetly on her feet, त्वाम् towards You, कलित-व्रीड-विलासम् with bashful charm and graceful elegance, आससाद approached/reached

Simple Meaning: Carrying a splendid wedding garland humming with swarms of attracted honeybees, Goddess Lakshmi walked gracefully with slow, gentle steps, her anklets sweet-sounding with every step. Overflowing with bashful charm and exquisite elegance, She approached You to choose You as her divine consort.

गिरिशद्रुहिणादिसर्वदेवान् गुणभाजोऽप्यविमुक्तदोषलेशान् ।
अवमृश्य सदैव सर्वरम्ये निहिता त्वय्यनयाऽपि दिव्यमाला ॥28.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: गिरिश-द्रुहिण-आदि-सर्व-देवान् all the gods beginning with Shiva (Girisha) and Brahma (Druhina), गुण-भाजः possessing great divine virtues/qualities, अपि although, अविमुक्त-दोष-लेशान् not free from small traces of flaws, अवमृश्य having critically evaluated/considered, सदैव always, सर्व-रम्ये in Him who is all-attractive/source of all delight, निहिता was placed, त्वयि upon You, अनया by Her (Goddess Lakshmi), अपि indeed, दिव्य-माला the divine wedding garland

Simple Meaning: Having thoughtfully evaluated Shiva, Brahma, and all the other gods, Goddess Lakshmi observed that despite their exalted virtues, none were completely free from subtle flaws. Finding You alone to be eternally flawless and all-attractive, She lovingly placed the divine garland around Your neck.

उरसा तरसा ममानिथैनां भुवनानां जननीमनन्यभावाम् ।
त्वदुरोविलसत्तदीक्षणश्रीपरिवृष्ट्या परिपुष्टमास विश्वम् ॥28.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: उरसा with Your chest/breast, तरसा eagerly/swiftly, ममानिथ You embraced/welcomed, एनाम् Her (Goddess Lakshmi), भुवनानाम् of all the worlds, जननीम् the mother, अनन्य-भावाम् devoted exclusively to You, त्वत्-उरः-विलसत्-तत्-ईक्षण-श्री-परिवृष्ट्या by showering the abundance of Her auspicious glances from her abode on Your chest, परिपुष्टम् thoroughly nourished/enriched, आस became, विश्वम् the entire universe

Simple Meaning: You eagerly embraced upon Your chest Goddess Lakshmi—the Mother of all the worlds—who is devoted exclusively to You. She showered Her auspicious and radiant glances upon the world from her place upon Your chest. Their merciful shower thoroughly nourished, enriched, and blessed the entire universe.

अतिमोहनविभ्रमा तदानीं मदयन्ती खलु वारुणी निरागात् ।
तमसः पदवीमदास्त्वमेनामतिसम्माननया महासुरेभ्यः ॥28.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: अति-मोहन-विभ्रमा with intensely intoxicating and enchanting charm, तदानीम् then, मदयन्ती intoxicating/exhilarating the minds, खलु indeed, वारुणी Varuni (the goddess of wine/spirituous liquor), निरागात् emerged/appeared, तमसः of the quality of darkness/ignorance (Tamas), पदवीम् the abode/embodiment, अदाः You gave, त्वम् You, एनाम् her, अति-सम्माननया with immense honor/respect, महासुरेभ्यः to the great Asuras

Simple Meaning: Then emerged Varuni, the divine spirit of wine, dazzling with intensely captivating charm and intoxicating the minds of all who looked upon her. Being an embodiment of the guna of darkness (Tamas), You politely and respectfully handed her over to the great Asuras, who accepted her with immense delight.

तरुणाम्बुदसुन्दरस्तदा त्वं ननु धन्वन्तरिरुत्थितोऽम्बुराशेः ।
अमृतं कलशे वहन् कराभ्यामखिलार्तिं हर मारुतालयेश ॥28.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: तरुण-अम्बुद-सुन्दरः beautiful like a fresh monsoon rain-cloud, तदा then, त्वम् You, ननु indeed/surely, धन्वन्तरिः as Dhanvantari, उत्थितः emerged/rose up, अम्बुराशेः from the ocean, अमृतम् the nectar of immortality (Amrita), कलशे in a pot/vessel, वहन् holding/carrying, कराभ्याम् with both hands, अखिल-आर्तिम् all afflictions/sufferings, हर eradicate/take away, मारुतालय-ईश O Lord of Guruvayur

Simple Meaning: O Lord! You then emerged from the ocean as Dhanvantari, glowing with the enchanting dark hue of a fresh rain-bearing cloud, holding in Your hands the vessel containing the divine nectar of immortality. O Lord of Guruvayur, please remove all my afflictions, illnesses, and sorrows!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
