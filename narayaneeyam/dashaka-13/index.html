Dashaka 13

Destruction of Hiranyaksha

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 13

Destruction of Hiranyaksha

(छन्दस् - शिखरिणी)

हिरण्याक्षं तावद्वरद भवदन्वेषणपरं
चरन्तं सांवर्ते पयसि निजजङ्घापरिमिते ।
भवद्भक्तो गत्वा कपटपटुधीर्नारदमुनिः
शनैरूचे नन्दन् दनुजमपि निन्दंस्तव बलम् ॥ 13.1 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: हिरण्याक्षम् Hiranyaksha, तावत् meanwhile, वरद O Bestower of Boons, भवत्-अन्वेषण-परम् intently searching for You, चरन्तम् wandering, सांवर्ते पयसि in the waters of the cosmic deluge, निज-जङ्घा-परिमिते which were only up to his shins, भवत्-भक्तः Your devotee, गत्वा having approached, कपट-पटु-धीः clever in diplomatic/witty deception, नारद-मुनिः Sage Narada, शनैः softly/cunningly, ऊचे spoke, नन्दन् praising, दनुजम् the asura, अपि and, निन्दन् depreciating/downplaying, तव बलम् Your power

Simple Meaning: Meanwhile, O Bestower of Boons, the asura Hiranyaksha was wandering through the deluge waters—which reached only up to his shins—intently searching for You. Your great devotee Sage Narada, master of divine diplomacy and wit, approached him. With clever artifice, Narada spoke to him softly, flattering the asura while cunningly understating Your true might to goad him into action.

स मायावी विष्णुर्हरति भवदीयां वसुमतीं
प्रभो कष्टं कष्टं किमिदमिति तेनाभिगदितः।
नदन् क्वासौ क्वासविति स मुनिना दर्शितपथो
भवन्तं सम्प्रापद्धरणिधरमुद्यन्तमुदकात् ॥ 13.2 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः that, मायावी deceiver/master of illusions, विष्णुः Vishnu, हरति is carrying away, भवदीयाम् your, वसुमतीम् Earth, प्रभो O Lord, कष्टम् कष्टम् how tragic! how distressing!, किम् इदम् what is this!, इति thus, तेन by him (by Narada), अभिगदितः being told, नदन् roaring loudly, क्व असौ where is he?, क्व असौ इति where is he?!, सः he (Hiranyaksha), मुनिना by the sage (Narada), दर्शित-पथः shown the path, भवन्तम् You, सम्प्रापत् reached/encountered, धरणि-धरम् holding/bearing the Earth, उद्यन्तम् rising up, उदकात् from the waters

Simple Meaning: Narada incited him, saying, "O mighty one, how tragic! That master of illusions, Vishnu, is carrying away your Earth! What is this madness?" Enraged and roaring loudly, "Where is he? Where is he?", Hiranyaksha followed the direction pointed out by the sage and rushed forward, confronting You just as You were rising out of the waters bearing the Earth upon Your tusks!

अहो आरण्योऽयं मृग इति हसन्तं बहुतरैः
दुरुक्तैर्विध्यन्तं दितिसुतमवज्ञाय भगवन् ।
महीं दृष्ट्वा दंष्ट्राशिरसि चकितां स्वेन महसा
पयोधावाधाय प्रसभमुदयुङ्क्था मृधविधौ ॥ 13.3 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: अहो Aha!, आरण्यः अयम् मृगः this wild forest beast, इति thus, हसन्तम् laughing mockingly, बहुतरैः दुरुक्तैः with countless taunts/harsh insults, विध्यन्तम् piercing/attacking, दिति-सुतम् the son of Diti (Hiranyaksha), अवज्ञाय ignoring/disregarding, भगवन् O Lord, महीम् the Earth, दृष्ट्वा seeing, दंष्ट्रा-शिरसि on the tip of Your tusk, चकिताम् frightened/trembling, स्वेन महसा by Your own divine power, पयोधौ in the water, आधाय establishing/placing safely, प्रसभम् fiercely/resolutely, उदयुङ्क्थाः You prepared/readied Yourself, मृध-विधौ for the battle

Simple Meaning: Mocking You with laughter, the asura Hiranyaksha taunted, "Aha! Look at this wild forest beast!" and hurled a barrage of cruel insults at You. Disregarding his foolish words, O Lord, You noticed that the Earth trembled with fear upon the tip of Your tusk. Restoring her confidence through Your radiant glory, You gently placed her safely atop the waters using Your power, and then resolutely turned to engage the demon in battle!

गदापाणौ दैत्ये त्वमपि हि गृहीतोन्नतगदो
नियुद्धेन क्रीडन् घटघटरवोद्घुष्टवियता ।
रणालोकौत्सुक्यान्मिलति सुरसङ्घे द्रुतममुं
निरुन्ध्याः सन्ध्यातः प्रथममिति धात्रा जगदिषे ॥ 13.4 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: गदा-पाणौ with mace in hand, दैत्ये as the daitya (stood), त्वम् You, अपि also, हि indeed, गृहीत-उन्नत-गदः wielding a magnificent/raised mace, नियुद्धेन through the mace-fight, क्रीडन् playing/sporting, घट-घट-रव-उद्घुष्ट-वियता with the sky echoing wildly with the violent clashing sounds (ghata-ghata), रण-आलोक-औत्सुक्यात् out of eagerness to witness the battle, मिलति as gathered, सुर-सङ्घे the assembly of demigods, द्रुतम् quickly, अमुम् him (the daitya), निरुन्ध्याः destroy/eliminate, सन्ध्याः prior to dusk/twilight, प्रथमम् before, इति thus, धात्रा by Lord Brahma, जगदिषे You were addressed

Simple Meaning: As the asura stood ready with mace in hand, You too raised Your formidable mace and engaged him in a duel—treating the fierce contest as mere play while the heavens echoed continuously with the thunderous clashing of Your weapons (ghata-ghata). As crowds of demigods gathered in the sky eager to witness the duel, Lord Brahma prayed to You: "O Supreme Lord, please destroy this asura quickly before twilight arrives, for dusk will only increase his powers!"

गदोन्मर्दे तस्मिंस्तव खलु गदायां दितिभुवो
गदाघाताद्भूमौ झटिति पतितायामहह! भोः ।
मृदुस्मेरास्यस्त्वं दनुजकुलनिर्मूलनचणं
महाचक्रं स्मृत्वा करभुवि दधानो रुरुचिषे ॥ 13.5 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: गदा-उन्मर्दे in that intense mace duel, तस्मिन् in that (fight), तव Your, खलु indeed, गदायाम् mace, दिति-भुवः of the son of Diti (Hiranyaksha), गदा-आघातात् due to the heavy blow from the mace, भूमौ on the ground, झटिति instantly, पतितायाम् having fallen, अहह भोः Ah!/O Lord!, मृदु-स्मेर-आस्यः having a gently smiling face, त्वम् You, दनुज-कुल-निर्मूलन-चणम् adept/renowned for eradicating the entire asura race, महा-चक्रम् the great Sudarshana Chakra (disc), स्मृत्वा remembering/summoning, कर-भुवि upon Your hand, दधानः holding, रुरुचिषे You shone brilliantly/looked exceedingly magnificent

Simple Meaning: Ah, Lord! During that fierce mace duel, when Your mace slipped from Your hand and fell to the ground due to a heavy blow from Hiranyaksha's weapon, You remained completely unperturbed, wearing a gentle, serene smile. You then summoned Your invincible Sudarshana Chakra—famed for eradicating the entire asura clan—and as it rested in Your hand, You shone with unmatched, majestic brilliance!

ततः शूलं कालप्रतिमरुषि दैत्ये विसृजति
त्वयि छिन्दत्येनत् करकलितचक्रप्रहरणात् ।
समारुष्टो मुष्ट्या स खलु वितुदंस्त्वां समतनोत्
गलन्माये मायास्त्वयि किल जगन्मोहनकरीः ॥ 13.6 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, शूलम् trident, काल-प्रतिम-रुषि whose anger was like Death/Time (Kala) personified, दैत्ये as the daitya, विसृजति hurled, त्वयि by You, छिन्दति splitting/cleaving into pieces, एनत् this (trident), कर-कलित-चक्र-प्रहरणात् through the strike of the Sudarshana Chakra held in Your hand, समारुष्टः furious/highly enraged, मुष्ट्या with his fist, सः he (Hiranyaksha), खलु indeed, वितुदन् striking/pummeling, त्वाम् You, समतनोत् deployed/unleashed, गलन्-माये as his illusive powers failed, मायाः magical illusions (Maya), त्वयि against You, किल indeed, जगत्-मोहन-करीः that bewilder the whole world

Simple Meaning: Thereupon, the daitya, burning with a rage as fierce as Death personified, hurled his deadly trident at You. With the Sudarshana Chakra held in Your hand, You effortlessly sliced it into pieces mid-air! Enraged beyond measure, Hiranyaksha rushed forward and struck Your chest with his bare fist. When that too proved futile, he unleashed a barrage of terrifying dark illusions—the very magical tricks that delude the entire universe!

भवच्चक्रज्योतिष्कणलवनिपातेन विधुते
ततो मायाचक्रे विततघनरोषान्धमनसम् ।
गरिष्ठाभिर्मुष्टिप्रहृतिभिरभिघ्नन्तमसुरं
स्वपादाङ्गुष्ठेन श्रवणपदमूले निरवधीः ॥ 13.7 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: भवत्-चक्र-ज्योतिः-कण-लव-निपातेन by the mere falling of a tiny spark of light from Your Sudarshana Chakra, विधुते when dispelled/destroyed, ततः thereupon, माया-चक्रे that array of illusions, वितत-घन-रोष-अन्ध-मनसम् whose mind was completely blinded by intense and overwhelming rage, गरिष्ठाभिः with extremely heavy/powerful, मुष्टि-प्रहृतिभिः fist-blows, अभिघ्नन्तम् striking/pounding, असुरम् the asura (Hiranyaksha), स्व-पाद-अङ्गुष्ठेन with the big toe of Your foot, श्रवण-पद-मूले right at the base of his ear, निरवधीः You struck down/put an end to

Simple Meaning: As soon as a single tiny spark from Your dazzling Sudarshana Chakra touched his magical illusions, they were completely shattered! Blinded by overwhelming, blinding rage, the asura charged forward and pounded You with heavy fist-blows. With effortless ease, You simply struck him near the base of his ear with Your big toe, felling the mighty asura and ending his life on the spot!

महाकायः सोऽयं तव चरणपातप्रमथितो
गलद्रक्तो वक्त्रादपतदृषिभिः श्लाघितहतिः ।
तदा त्वामुद्दामप्रमदभरविद्योतिहृदया
मुनीन्द्राः सान्द्राभिः स्तुतिभिरनुवन्नध्वरतनुम् ॥ 13.8 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: महाकायः having a colossal/gigantic body, सः अयम् that very same asura (Hiranyaksha), तव Your, चरण-पात-प्रमथितः crushed/shattered by the strike of Your foot, गलत्-रक्तः gushing blood, वक्त्रात् from his mouth, अपतत् fell down (dead), ऋषिभिः by the sages, श्लाघित-हतिः whose slaying was lauded/praised, तदा then, त्वाम् You, उद्दाम-प्रमद-भर-विद्योति-हृदयाः whose hearts were illuminated with boundless, overflowing joy, मुनीन्द्राः the noble sages/leaders of seers, सान्द्राभिः with profound/fervent, स्तुतिभिः with hymns of praise, अनुवन् extolled/worshipped, अध्वर-तनुम् You whose body embodies the sacred Yajna (Sacrifice)

Simple Meaning: Shattered by the strike of Your foot, that gigantic demon fell dead to the ground, blood gushing from his mouth, his slaying praised by the holy sages. Thereupon, their hearts radiating with boundless joy, the great seers extolled You—the Supreme Being whose form embodies the sacred Yajna—with profound and radiant hymns of devotion!

त्वचि छन्दो रोमस्वपि कुशगणश्चक्षुषि घृतं
चतुर्होतारोऽङ्घ्रौ स्रुगपि वदने चोदर इडा ।
ग्रहा जिह्वायां ते परपुरुष कर्णे च चमसा
विभो सोमो वीर्यं वरद गलदेशेऽप्युपसदः ॥ 13.9 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वचि in Your skin/hide, छन्दः the Vedic meters (Chhandas), रोमसु अपि in Your body hair, कुश-गणः the bunches of sacred Kusha grass, चक्षुषि in Your eyes, घृतम् the clarified butter (Ghee used in oblation), चतुर्होतारः the four chief priests (Hota, Adhvaryu, Udgata, Brahma), अङ्घ्रौ in Your feet, स्रुक् अपि the sacrificial ladle (Sruk), वदने in Your mouth, च and, उदरे in Your stomach, इडा the sacred offering (Ida), ग्रहाः the ceremonial cups (Grahas), जिह्वायाम् on Your tongue, ते Your, परपुरुष O Supreme Being, कर्णे in Your ears, च and, चमसाः the wooden vessels (Chamasas), विभो O All-Pervading Lord, सोमः the divine Soma juice, वीर्यम् Your vital force/seed, वरद O Bestower of Boons, गल-देशे in the region of Your neck, अपि also, उपसदः the sacred rites called Upasads

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Being! O All-Pervading Lord and Bestower of Boons! You are the very personification of the sacred Yajna (Yajna-Varaha). The Vedic meters form Your skin, and the sacred Kusha grass is Your body hair. Ghee resides in Your eyes, and the four chief officiating priests are Your feet. The offering ladle (Sruk) is Your mouth, the Ida offering rests in Your stomach, and the ceremonial cups (Grahas) dwell on Your tongue. The Chamasa vessels are in Your ears, the divine Soma juice is Your vital power, and the Upasad rites abide in Your neck!

मुनीन्द्रैरित्यादिस्तवनमुखरैर्मोदितमना
महीयस्या मूर्त्या विमलतरकीर्त्या च विलसन् ।
स्वधिष्ण्यं सम्प्राप्तः सुखरसविहारी मधुरिपो
निरुन्ध्या रोगं मे सकलमपि वातालयपते ॥ 13.10 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: मुनीन्द्रैः by the great sages, इति-आदि-स्तवन-मुखरैः echoing with these and other sublime hymns of praise, मोदित-मनाः Your mind filled with supreme delight, महीयस्या मूर्त्या with Your glorious, exalted form, विमलतर-कीर्त्या च and with untarnished, spotless fame, विलसन् shining radiantly, स्वधिष्ण्यम् Your own supreme abode (Vaikuntha), सम्प्राप्तः having returned/reached, सुखरस-विहारी reveling in the essence of divine bliss, मधुरिपो O Vanquisher of the demon Madhu, निरुन्ध्याः eradicate/destroy, रोगम् illness/affliction, मे my, सकलम् अपि every single one without exception, वातालयपते O Lord of Guruvayur (Lord of Vatalaya)

Simple Meaning: O Vanquisher of Madhu! O Lord of Guruvayur! Your heart filled with delight by the magnificent hymns chanted by the great sages, You shone resplendently in Your exalted form and stainless glory. Returning to Your supreme abode of Vaikuntha, You revel in pure, unexcelled bliss. O Lord, I pray to You: please completely eradicate every single illness and affliction troubling my body and mind!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
