Kaliya troubles the people of Vrindavana

दशकम् ५४

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 54

Kaliya troubles the people of Vrindavana

त्वत्सेवोत्कस्सौभरिर्नाम पूर्वं कालिन्द्यन्तर्द्वादशाब्दम् तपस्यन् ।
मीनव्राते स्नेहवान् भोगलोले तार्क्ष्यं साक्षादैक्षताग्रे कदाचित् ॥54.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-सेवा-उत्कः eager to serve You, सौभरिः नाम named Saubhari, पूर्वम् in ancient times, कालिन्दी-अन्तः inside the waters of Yamuna, द्वादश-अब्दम् for twelve years, तपस्यन् performing penance, मीन-व्राते towards the shoal of fish, स्नेहवान् having deep affection, भोग-लोले sporting happily, तार्क्ष्यम् son of Triksha - Tarkshya (Garuda), साक्षात् ऐक्षत directly saw, अग्रे in front of him, कदाचित् once

Simple Meaning: In ancient times, a sage named Saubhari, who was eager to serve You, was performing penance under the waters of the Yamuna river for twelve years. He developed a deep affection for a shoal of fish sporting happily there. He once saw Garuda appear right in front of him.

त्वद्वाहं तं सक्षुधं तृक्षसूनुं मीनं कञ्चिज्जक्षतं लक्षयन् सः ।
तप्तश्चित्ते शप्तवानत्र चेत्त्वं जन्तून् भोक्ता जीवितं चापि मोक्ता ॥54.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-वाहम् Your mount, तम् that, सक्षुधम् hungry, तृक्ष-सूनुम् the son of Triksha (Garuda), मीनम् fish, कञ्चित् a certain, जक्षतम् eating, लक्षयन् seeing, सः he (the sage Saubhari), तप्तः चित्ते pained at heart, शप्तवान् cursed, अत्र here in this place, चेत् त्वम् if You, जन्तून् creatures, भोक्ता eat, जीवितम् life, च अपि and also, मोक्ता shall lose

Simple Meaning: Seeing Your mount Garuda, who was hungry, snatching and eating a fish from the water, the sage Saubhari became deeply pained at heart and cursed him: "If you ever eat any creature in this place again, you will instantly lose your life."

तस्मिन् काले कालियः क्ष्वेलदर्पात् सर्पारातेः कल्पितं भागमश्नन् ।
तेन क्रोधात्त्वत्पदाम्भोजभाजा पक्षक्षिप्तस्तद्दुरापं पयोऽगात् ॥54.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मिन् काले at that time, कालियः Kaliya, क्ष्वेल-दर्पात् out of pride in his poison, सर्प-आरातेः from the enemy of serpents - Garuda, कल्पितम् allocated, भागम् अश्नन् eating the offering, तेन by him Garuda, क्रोधात् out of anger, त्वत्-पद-अम्भोज-भाजा who was devoted to Your lotus feet, पक्ष-क्षिप्तः struck with his wings, तत्-दुरापम् unreachable by him, पयः to the water of Yamuna, अगात् went

Simple Meaning: Around that time, the serpent Kaliya, intoxicated with pride over his deadly poison, began eating the offerings meant for Garuda, the enemy of serpents. Enraged by this, Garuda, who was devoted to Your lotus feet, struck Kaliya with his wings. Fleeing in fear, Kaliya took refuge in that pool of the Yamuna river, which was unreachable by Garuda due to the sage's curse.

घोरे तस्मिन् सूरजानीरवासे तीरे वृक्षा विक्षताः क्ष्वेलवेगात् ।
पक्षिव्राताः पेतुरभ्रे पतन्तः कारुण्यार्द्रं त्वन्मनस्तेन जातम् ॥54.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: घोरे तस्मिन् when that terrible one (serpent Kaliya), सूरजा-नीर-वासे resided in the waters of the daughter of the Sun - Yamuna, तीरे on the banks, वृक्षाः trees, विक्षताः were destroyed, क्ष्वेल-वेगात् due to the intensity of the poison, पक्षि-व्राताः flocks of birds, पेतुः fell down dead, अभ्रे in the sky, पतन्तः while flying, कारुण्य-आर्द्रम् melted with compassion, त्वत्-मनः Your mind, तेन by that sight, जातम् became

सूर (पुंलिङ्गः)  सूर्यः, src Kosha, Ashtadhyayi. सूर-जा - daughter of the Sun - Yamuna

Simple Meaning: When that terrible serpent Kaliya resided in the waters of the Yamuna, the trees along the bank withered and perished from the intense fumes of his poison, and flocks of birds flying overhead fell down dead into the water. Seeing this tragic plight, Your heart melted with deep compassion.

काले तस्मिन्नेकदा सीरपाणिं मुक्त्वा याते यामुनं काननान्तम् ।
त्वय्युद्दामग्रीष्मभीष्मोष्मतप्ता गोगोपाला व्यापिबन् क्ष्वेलतोयम् ॥54.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: काले तस्मिन् at that time, एकदा once, सीर-पाणिम् Balarama the holder of the plough, मुक्त्वा leaving behind, याते having gone, यामुनम् along the banks of Yamuna, कानन-अन्तम् deep into the forest, त्वयि when You, उद्दाम-ग्रीष्म-भीष्म-ऊष्म-तप्ताः scorched by the terrifying heat of the intense summer, गो-गोपालाः the cows and cowherd boys, व्यापिबन् drank, क्ष्वेल-तोयम् the poisoned water

Simple Meaning: At that time, when You had gone deep into the forest along the Yamuna leaving Balarama behind, the cows and cowherd boys, severely scorched by the terrifying heat of the intense summer, drank the poisoned water of the river.

नश्यज्जीवान् विच्युतान् क्ष्मातले तान् विश्वान् पश्यन्नच्युत त्वं दयार्द्रः ।
प्राप्योपान्तं जीवयामासिथ द्राक् पीयूषाम्भोवर्षिभिः श्रीकटाक्षैः ॥54.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: नश्यत्-जीवान् losing their lives, विच्युतान् fallen down, क्ष्मा-तले on the ground, तान् them, विश्वान् all, पश्यन् seeing, अच्युत O Infallible Lord, त्वम् You, दया-आर्द्रः melted with compassion, प्राप्य-उपान्तम् reaching near them, जीवयामासिथ revived, द्राक् instantly, पीयूष-अम्भः-वर्षिभिः raining nectar-like waters, श्री-कटाक्षैः with Your auspicious side-long glances

Simple Meaning: O Infallible Lord, seeing all of them lying fallen on the ground having lost their lives, Your heart melted with compassion. Approaching them, You instantly revived them all with Your auspicious glances, which were like showering streams of nectar upon them.

किं किं जातो हर्षवर्षातिरेकः सर्वाङ्गेष्वित्युत्थिता गोपसङ्घाः ।
दृष्ट्वाऽग्रे त्वां त्वत्कृतं तद्विदन्तः त्वामालिङ्गन् दृष्टनानाप्रभावाः ॥54.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: किम् किम् what is this, जातः arisen, हर्ष-वर्ष-अतिरेकः an overwhelming shower of joy, सर्व-अङ्गेषु in all parts of our body, इति thinking thus, उत्थिताः rising up, गोप-सङ्घाः the groups of cowherd boys, दृष्ट्वा seeing, अग्रे in front, त्वाम् You, त्वत्-कृतम् done by You, तत् that miracle, विदन्तः understanding, त्वाम् You, आलिङ्गन् embraced, दृष्ट-नाना-प्रभावाः having witnessed Your various divine powers

Simple Meaning: Exclaiming "What is this extraordinary surge of joy pouring through our entire body?", the cowherd boys woke up and stood. Seeing You standing before them and understanding that this miracle was Your doing—having already witnessed Your various divine powers—they lovingly embraced You.

गावश्चैवं लब्धजीवाः क्षणेन स्फीतानन्दास्त्वां च दृष्ट्वा पुरस्तात् ।
द्रागावव्रुः सर्वतो हर्षबाष्पं व्यामुञ्चन्त्यो मन्दमुद्यन्निनादाः ॥54.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: गावः च एवम् and the cows likewise, लब्ध-जीवाः having regained their lives, क्षणेन in an instant, स्फीत-आनन्दाः filled with immense bliss, त्वाम् You, च and, दृष्ट्वा seeing, पुरस्तात् in front of them, द्राक् immediately, आवव्रुः surrounded, सर्वतः on all sides, हर्ष-बाष्पम् tears of joy, व्यामुञ्चन्त्यः shedding, मन्दम् softly, उद्यत्-निनादाः mooing with delight

Simple Meaning: Likewise, the cows instantly regained their lives and were filled with immense bliss. Seeing You standing right in front of them, they immediately surrounded You on all sides, shedding tears of joy and mooing softly with delight.

रोमाञ्चोऽयं सर्वतो नः शरीरे भूयस्यन्तः काचिदानन्दमूर्छा ।
आश्चर्योऽयं क्ष्वेलवेगो मुकुन्देत्युक्तो गोपैर्नन्दितो वन्दितोऽभूः ॥54.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: रोमाञ्चः अयम् this horripilation, सर्वतः all over, नः our, शरीरे in the body, भूयसी-अन्तः deep within, काचित् an indescribable, आनन्द-मूर्छा ecstasy of bliss, आश्चर्यः अयम् extraordinary indeed, क्ष्वेल-वेगः the effect of the poison, मुकुन्द इति O Mukunda thus, उक्तः addressed, गोपैः by the cowherd boys, नन्दितः hailed, वन्दितः अभूः You were worshipped

Simple Meaning: "A thrill of joy passes all over our body, and an indescribable ecstasy of bliss surges deep within us. Extraordinary indeed is the effect of this poison, O Mukunda!", so said the cowherd boys. You were greatly hailed and reverently worshipped by them.

एवं भक्तान् मुक्तजीवानपि त्वं मुग्धापाङ्गैरस्तरोगांस्तनोषि ।
तादृग्भूतस्फीतकारुण्यभूमा रोगात् पाया वायुगेहाधिवास ॥54.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this manner, भक्तान् Your devotees, मुक्त-जीवन्-अपि even after they had lost their lives, त्वम् You, मुग्ध-अपाङ्गैः with Your charming side-long glances, अस्त-रोगान् free from all afflictions, तनोषि make them, तादृग्-भूत-स्फीत-कारुण्य-भूमा endowed with such boundless and abundant compassion, रोगात् from disease, पायाः protect me, वायुगेहाधिवास O Lord of Guruvayur

Simple Meaning: In this manner, You restored the lives of Your devotees even after they had perished, removing all their afflictions with Your charming side-long glances. O Lord of Guruvayur, who possesses such boundless and abundant compassion, please protect me from disease.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
