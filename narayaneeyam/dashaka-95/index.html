Supremacy of the path of Bhakti

दशकम् ९५

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 95

Supremacy of the path of Bhakti

आदौ हैरण्यगर्भीं तनुमविकलजीवात्मिकामास्थितस्त्वं
जीवत्वं प्राप्य मायागुणगणखचितो वर्तसे विश्वयोने ।
तत्रोद्वृद्धेन सत्त्वेन तु गुणयुगलं भक्तिभावं गतेन
छित्वा सत्त्वं च हित्वा पुनरनुपहितो वर्तिताहे त्वमेव ॥95.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: आदौ in the beginning, हैरण्यगर्भीम् belonging to Hiranyagarbha, तनुम् form, अविकल-जीव-आत्मिकाम् comprising the aggregate of all unmanifested souls, आस्थितः taking on/abiding in, त्वम् You, जीवत्वम् individual soul-hood, प्राप्य assuming/entering into, माया-गुण-गण-खचितः decorated/conditioned by the network of qualities of Maya, वर्तसे You exist, विश्व-योने O Source of the Universe, तत्र there, उद्वृद्धेन by the fully intensified/elevated, सत्त्वेन quality of Sattva, तु indeed, गुण-युगलम् the other two qualities Rajas and Tamas, भक्ति-भावम् state of supreme devotion, गतेन attaining, छित्वा severing/cutting through, सत्त्वम् quality of Sattva, च and, हित्वा transcending/relinquishing, पुनः then, अनुपहितः unconditioned/free from all attributes, वर्तिताहे I shall remain/abide, त्वम् एव as You alone

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Source of the Universe! In the beginning of creation, You assumed the cosmic form of Hiranyagarbha, which contains the collective soul of all living beings. Then, as You became conditioned by the various gunas or attributes of Maya, You appeared as individual living entities. In that state, when the quality of Sattva becomes supremely elevated and transforms into pure devotional service, it cuts through the other two gunas, Rajas and Tamas. By subsequently transcending Sattva as well, I (the jiva) shall remain fully unconditioned and abide as one with You alone!

सत्त्वोन्मेषात् कदाचित् खलु विषयरसे दोषबोधेऽपि भूमन्
भूयोऽप्येषु प्रवृत्तिस्सतमसि रजसि प्रोद्धते दुर्निवारा ।
चित्तं तावद्गुणाश्च ग्रथितमिह मिथस्तानि सर्वाणि रोद्धुं
तुर्ये त्वय्येकभक्तिश्शरणमिति भवान् हंसरूपी न्यगादीत् ॥95.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: सत्त्व-उन्मेषात् by the manifestation of Sattva, कदाचित् sometimes, खलु indeed, विषय-रसे in the pleasures of sense objects, दोष-बोधे अपि even when there is awareness of defect, भूमन् O Infinite Supreme Lord, भूयः अपि then again, एषु in these sense pleasures, प्रवृत्तिः inclination/engagement, स-तमसि when along with Tamas, रजसि Rajas, प्रोद्धते becomes dominant/elevated, दुर्निवारा is difficult to prevent, चित्तम् mind, तावत् indeed, गुणाः the gunas/attributes, च and, ग्रथितम् are intertwined/bound together, इह in this world, मिथः mutually, तानि all those, सर्वाणि all, रोद्धुम् to check/restrain, तुर्ये in Turiya - the Fourth State beyond the three gunas, त्वयि in You, एक-भक्तिः single-pointed devotion, शरणम् the sole refuge, इति thus, भवान् You, हंस-रूपी in the form of a Swan (Hamsa avatar), न्यगादीत् taught/declared

Simple Meaning: O Infinite Supreme Lord! Sometimes, due to a temporary rise of Sattva, one recognizes the inherent flaws and dangers in sense pleasures. Yet, when Rajas coupled with Tamas becomes strong and dominant, the inclination toward these material enjoyments is extremely difficult to prevent. In this world, the mind and the three gunas are mutually intertwined. Therefore, You declared in Your divine Hamsa incarnation that single-pointed devotion unto You, who transcend all gunas in the Supreme Fourth State - Turiya, is the only true way and refuge to restrain them all!

सन्ति श्रेयांसि भूयांस्यपि रुचिभिदया कर्मिणां निर्मितानि
क्षुद्रानन्दाश्च सान्ता बहुविधगतयः कृष्ण तेभ्यो भवेयुः ।
त्वं चाचख्याथ सख्ये ननु महिततमां श्रेयसां भक्तिमेकां
त्वद्भक्त्यानन्दतुल्यः खलु विषयजुषां सम्मदः केन वा स्यात् ॥95.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सन्ति there are, श्रेयांसि spiritual paths or auspicious means, भूयांसि many, अपि even, रुचि-भिदया according to differences in taste or temperament, कर्मिणाम् by those inclined toward ritualistic actions, निर्मितानि prescribed/created, क्षुद्र-आनन्दाः providing small/trivial pleasures, च and, स-अन्ताः finite/impermanent, बहु-विध-गतयः leading to various types of rebirths/destinations, कृष्ण O Krishna, तेभ्यः from those paths, भवेयुः arise, त्वम् You, च and, अचख्याथ taught/declared, सख्ये unto Your friend Uddhava, ननु indeed, महिततमाम् the most glorious/supreme, श्रेयसाम् among all spiritual paths, भक्तिम् devotion, एकाम् alone, त्वत्-भक्ति-आनन्द-तुल्यः equal to the bliss of devotion unto You, खलु indeed, विषय-जुषाम् of those indulging in worldly sensory pleasures, सम्मदः happiness/delight, केन वा by what means, स्यात् could ever be

Simple Meaning: O Krishna! There are indeed many spiritual paths and ritualistic practices prescribed according to the varying tastes and temperaments of actions-oriented people. However, the fruits derived from them yield only small, impermanent pleasures that lead to various temporary realms and rebirths. You explicitly taught Your dear friend Uddhava that pure devotion unto You is single-handedly the most supreme and glorious among all spiritual paths. Indeed, how could the worldly pleasures of those indulging in sensory gratification ever compare to the supreme bliss born of Your devotion!

त्वत्भक्त्या तुष्टबुद्धेः सुखमिह चरतो विच्युताशस्य चाशाः
सर्वाः स्युः सौख्यमय्यः सलिलकुहरगस्येव तोयैकमय्यः ।
सोऽयं खल्विन्द्रलोकं कमलजभवनं योगसिद्धीश्च हृद्याः
नाकाङ्क्षत्येतदास्तां स्वयमनुपतिते मोक्षसौख्येऽप्यनीहः ॥95.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-भक्त्या through devotion unto You, तुष्ट-बुद्धेः of one whose mind is completely satisfied and peaceful, सुखम् happily, इह in this world, चरतः moving/living, विच्युत-आशस्य of one who is free from worldly desires, च and, आशाः directions, सर्वाः all, स्युः become, सौख्य-मय्यः filled with pure bliss, सलिल-कुहरगस्य इव like one submerged in the depths of water, तोय-एक-मय्यः completely surrounded by water alone, सः अयम् such a devotee, खलु indeed, इन्द्र-लोकम् the realm of Indra (heaven), कमलज-भवनम् the abode of Brahma (Kamalaja), योग-सिद्धीः च and extraordinary yogic powers, हृद्याः attractive/alluring, न आकाङ्क्षति does not desire, एतत् आस्ताम् let alone these, स्वयम् अनुपतिते even when it comes naturally to him, मोक्ष-सौख्ये अपि even in the bliss of liberation (Moksha), अनीहः remains without desire

Simple Meaning: For a devotee whose mind is filled with supreme contentment through devotion unto You, who lives happily in this world completely free from desires, all directions become filled with sheer bliss, just as one submerged deep within the ocean finds water everywhere. Indeed, such a devotee does not desire the heavenly realm of Indra, the divine abode of Brahma, or the most alluring powers of yoga. Let alone these, he remains without desire even towards the bliss of liberation that comes to him naturally!

त्वद्भक्तो बाध्यमानोऽपि च विषयरसैरिन्द्रियाशान्तिहेतोः
भक्त्यैवाक्रम्यमाणैः पुनरपि खलु तैर्दुर्बलैर्नाभिजय्यः ।
सप्तार्चिर्दीपितार्चिर्दहति किल यथा भूरिदारुप्रपञ्चं
त्वद्भक्त्योघे तथैव प्रदहति दुरितं दुर्मदः क्वेन्द्रियाणाम् ॥95.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-भक्तः Your devotee, बाध्यमानः अपि although harassed/affected, च and, विषय-रसैः by the pleasures of sense objects, इन्द्रिय-अशान्ति-हेतोः due to the restlessness of the senses, भक्त्या एव by devotion alone, आक्रम्यमाणैः being overcome/dominated, पुनः अपि time and again, खलु indeed, तैः by those sense pleasures, दुर्बलैः which have become weakened, न अभिजय्यः is not overcome/defeated, सप्त-अर्चितः fire (the seven-flamed one), दीपित-अर्चिः with blazing flames, दहति burns/consumes, किल indeed, यथा just as, भूरि-दारु-प्रपञ्चम् a huge pile of firewood, त्वत्-भक्ति-ओघे in the surging flood of devotion unto You, तथा एव in the exact same manner, प्रदहति completely burns away, दुरितम् sins or evil tendencies, दुर्म्दः wild pride/arrogance, क्व where, इन्द्रियाणाम् of the senses

Simple Meaning: Even if Your devotee is occasionally harassed by sensory attractions owing to the natural restlessness of the senses, those sense objects are continuously overpowered by his devotion and rendered weak, so he is never defeated by them. Just as a fire with fully blazing flames completely consumes a massive pile of firewood, the surging flood of devotion unto You burns away all sins and negative tendencies. When devotion shines so brightly, where is the scope for the wild arrogance of the senses to prevail!

चित्तार्द्रीभावमुच्चैर्वपुषि च पुलकं हर्षबाष्पं च हित्वा
चित्तं शुद्ध्येत्कथं वा किमु बहुतपसा विद्यया वीतभक्तेः ।
त्वद्गाथास्वादसिद्धाञ्जनसततमरीमृज्यमानोऽयमात्मा
चक्षुर्वत्तत्त्वसूक्ष्मं भजति न तु तथाऽभ्यस्तया तर्ककोट्या॥95.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: चित्त-आर्द्री-भावम् melting or softening of the heart, उच्चैः intensely or profoundly, वपुषि in the body, च and, पुलकम् goosebumps or horripilation, हर्ष-बाष्पम् tears of joy, च and, हित्वा without, चित्तम् the mind, शुद्ध्येत् can be purified, कथम् वा how indeed, किम् उ what is the use of, बहु-तपसा through severe penances, विद्यया through dry academic learning, वीत-भक्तेः for one who is devoid of devotion, त्वत्-गाथा-आस्वादि-सिद्ध-अञ्जन-सतत-मरीमृज्यमानः being constantly cleansed by the miraculous collyrium of tasting and enjoying the recitals of Your divine exploits, अयम् आत्मा this soul, चक्षुः-वत् like the eye, तत्त्व-सूक्ष्मम् the subtle ultimate truth, भजति perceives/attains, न तु but not, तथा in that manner, अभ्यस्तया by practicing, तर्क-कोट्या through millions of logical arguments and dialectics

Simple Meaning: How can the mind ever be truly purified without the intense melting of the heart, goosebumps on the body, and tears of joy born of devotion! What is the use of severe austerities or vast scholarly knowledge for someone who is devoid of devotion to You! Just as an eye cleansed by corrylium regains its vision, this inner self, when continuously purified by the divine collyrium of relishing Your stories and glories, easily perceives the subtle supreme truth. Such realization can never be attained even through millions of complex logical arguments!

ध्यानं ते शीलयेयं समतनुसुखबद्धासनो नासिकाग्र-
न्यस्ताक्षः पूरकाद्यैर्जितपवनपथश्चित्तपद्मं त्ववाञ्चम्।
ऊर्ध्वाग्रं भावयित्वा रविविधुशिखिनः संविचिन्त्योपरिष्टात्
तत्रस्थं भावये त्वां सजलजलधरश्यामलं कोमलाङ्गम् ॥95.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: ध्यानम् meditation, ते on You, शीलयेयम् may I practice, सम-तनु-सुख-बद्ध-आसनः with body kept straight and seated comfortably in a stable posture, नासिका-अग्र-न्यस्त-अक्षः with eyes focused on the tip of the nose, पूरक-आद्यैः through pranayama techniques such as inhalation and others, जित-पवन-पथः having controlled the breath and channels of vital energy, चित्त-पद्मम् the lotus of the heart, तु indeed, अवाञ्चम् facing downwards, ऊर्ध्व-अग्रम् facing upwards, भावयित्वा visualizing or making it, रवि-विधु-शिखिनः the sun, moon, and fire, संविचिन्त्य contemplated, उपरिष्टात् above them, तत्र-स्थम् seated there, भावये I meditate upon or visualize, त्वाम् You, सजल-जलधर-श्यामलम् dark blue like a rain-bearing cloud, कोमल-अङ्गम् with soft and tender limbs

Simple Meaning: Maintaining a straight posture in a comfortable seat, with eyes fixed softly on the tip of my nose, may I practice meditation upon You! Having regulated my breath and controlled the flow of vital energies through pranayama practices such as inhalation, retention, and exhalation, may I visualize the lotus of my heart, which normally hangs downwards, turned upwards! Contemplating the subtle spheres of the sun, moon, and fire above that lotus, may I meditate upon You seated there, dark-complexioned like a rain-bearing cloud and endowed with soft, exquisite limbs!

आनीलश्लक्ष्णकेशं ज्वलितमकरसत्कुण्डलं मन्दहास-
स्यन्दार्द्रं कौस्तुभश्रीपरिगतवनमालोरुहाराभिरामम् ।
श्रीवत्साङ्कं सुबाहुं मृदुलसदुदरं काञ्चनच्छायचेलं
चारुस्निग्धोरुमम्भोरुहललितपदं भावयेऽहं भवन्तम् ॥95.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: आनील-श्लक्ष्ण-केशम् with dark blue and smooth shining hair, ज्वलित-मकर-सत्-कुण्डलम् with brilliant alligator-shaped ear ornaments, मन्दहास-स्यन्द-आर्द्रम् glowing with a gentle melting smile, कौस्तुभ-श्री-परिगत-वनमाला-उरु-हार-अभिरामम् splendid with the Kaustubha gem, floral garland, and broad necklaces, श्रीवत्स-अङ्कम् bearing the sacred Shrivatsa mark, सुबाहुम् endowed with strong beautiful arms, मृदु-लसत्-उदरम् with a soft and beautiful abdomen, काञ्चन-च्छाया-चेलम् wearing robes possessing the luster of pure gold, चारु-स्निग्ध-ऊरुम् with soft and well-shaped thighs, अम्भोरुह-ललित-पदम् with charming feet soft like lotus flowers, भावये I meditate upon, अहम् I, भवन्तम् You

Simple Meaning: I meditate upon You, whose dark hair is smooth and glistening, whose ear ornaments in the shape of alligators shine brilliantly, and whose face is softened by a gentle, compassionate smile! I visualize You adorned with the shining Kaustubha gem, the fragrant garland of wild flowers, magnificent necklaces across Your chest, bearing the sacred mark of Shrivatsa, mighty arms, a soft and slender waist, and shining golden garments. Your thighs are smooth and beautifully formed, and Your divine lotus feet are captivatingly gentle and exquisite!

सर्वाङ्गेष्वङ्ग रङ्गत्कुतुकमिति मुहुर्धारयन्नीश चित्तं
तत्राप्येकत्र युञ्जे वदनसरसिजे सुन्दरे मन्दहासे
तत्रालीनं तु चेतः परमसुखचिदद्वैतरूपे वितन्वन्
अन्यन्नो चिन्तयेयं मुहुरिति समुपारूढयोगो भवेयम् ॥95.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: सर्व-अङ्गेषु in all the limbs, अङ्ग O Lord, रङ्गत्-कुतुकम् with overflowing delight or eager fascination, इति thus, मुहुः repeatedly, धारयन् focusing or fixing, ईश O Supreme Lord, चित्तम् the mind, तत्र अपि even among those, एकत्र in one spot, युञ्जे I fix, वदन-सरसिजे in the lotus face, सुन्दरे beautiful, मन्द-हासे adorned with a gentle smile, तत्र therein, लीनम् absorbed or merged, तु indeed, चेतः mind, परम-सुख-चित्-अद्वैत-रूपे in the nature of non-dual consciousness and supreme bliss, वितन्वन् expanding/merging, अन्यत् anything else, नो not, चिन्तयेयम् I may contemplate, मुहुः repeatedly, इति thus, समुपारूढ-योगः one who has attained the highest state of yoga, भवेयम् I may become

Simple Meaning: O Lord! O Supreme Lord! May I repeatedly fix my mind with overflowing delight upon all Your divine limbs! Even among them, may I concentrate my attention on one spot—Your beautiful, lotus-like face adorned with a tender smile! Merging my mind completely in that divine vision, may I expand it to realize Your true nature of non-dual consciousness and supreme bliss, contemplating nothing else, and thus attain the highest state of yoga!

इत्थं त्वद्ध्यानयोगे सति पुनरणिमाद्यष्टसंसिद्धयस्ताः
दूरश्रुत्यादयोऽपि ह्यहमहमिकया सम्पतेयुर्मुरारे ।
त्वत्सम्प्राप्तौ विलम्बावहमखिलमिदं नाद्रिये कामयेऽहं
त्वामेवानन्दपूर्णं पवनपुरपते पाहि मां सर्वतापात् ॥95.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: इत्थम् in this manner, त्वत्-ध्यान-योगे सति while practicing meditation upon You, पुनः furthermore, अणिमा-आदि-अष्ट-संसिद्धयः ताः those eight great occult powers beginning with Anima (becoming microscopic), दूर-श्रुति-आदयः अपि clairaudience (hearing distant sounds) and other powers as well, हि indeed, अहम् अहमिकया competing with one another, सम्पतेयुः may come rushing, मुरारे O Slayer of Mura (Krishna), त्वत्-सम्प्राप्तौ in attaining You, विलम्ब-आवहम् causing delay, अखिलम् इदम् all these, न आदिये I do not care for, कामये I long for, अहम् I, त्वाम् एव You alone, आनन्द-पूर्णम् who are filled with supreme bliss, पवनपुर-पते O Lord of Guruvayur, पाहि protect, माम् me, सर्व-तापात् from all afflictions and sufferings

Simple Meaning: O Krishna! O Slayer of Mura! O Lord of Guruvayur! When one practices such meditation upon You, the eight miraculous siddhis beginning with Anima, along with supernatural powers like clairaudience, may eagerly rush towards the devotee, competing with each other to serve him. However, knowing that all these powers only bring delay in reaching and attaining You, I do not value or desire any of them. I desire You alone, who are the embodiment of infinite supreme bliss. O Lord, please protect me from all my afflictions and worldly sufferings!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
