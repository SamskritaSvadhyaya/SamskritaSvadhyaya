Nara-Narayana and Daksha-Yagna

दशकम् १६

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 16

Nara-Narayana and Daksha-Yagna

(छन्दस् - वसन्ततिलका)

दक्षो विरिञ्चतनयोऽथ मनोस्तनूजां
लब्ध्वा प्रसूतिमिह षोडश चाप कन्याः ।
धर्मे त्रयोदश ददौ पितृषु स्वधां च
स्वाहां हविर्भुजि सतीं गिरिशे त्वदंशे ॥ 16.1 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: दक्षः Daksha, विरिञ्च-तनयः son of Brahma, अथ thereafter, मनोः तनूजाम् the daughter of Manu, लब्ध्वा having married/obtained, प्रसूतिम् Prasuti, इह in this world, षोडश sixteen, च and, आप fathered/obtained, कन्याः daughters, धर्मे unto Dharma, त्रयोदश thirteen, ददौ gave, पितृषु unto the ancestors (Pitrus), स्वधाम् Swadha, च and, स्वाहाम् Swaha, हविर्भुजि unto the fire god Agni, सतीम् Sati, गिरिशे unto Lord Shiva, त्वत्-अंशे who is an expansion/part of You

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, Daksha, the son of Brahma, married Prasuti, the daughter of Manu, and fathered sixteen daughters. He gave thirteen of them in marriage to Dharma, Swadha to the ancestors, Swaha to the fire god Agni, and Sati to Lord Shiva, who is an expansion of You.

मूर्तिर्हि धर्मगृहिणी सुषुवे भवन्तं
नारायणं नरसखं महितानुभावम् ।
यज्जन्मनि प्रमुदिताः कृततूर्यघोषाः
पुष्पोत्करान् प्रववृषुर्नुनुवुः सुरौघाः ॥ 16.2 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: मूर्तिः Murti, हि indeed, धर्म-गृहिणी the wife of Dharma, सुषुवे gave birth to, भवन्तम् You, नारायणम् as Narayana, नर-सखम् the companion of Nara, महित-अनुभावम् possessing glorious majesty, यत्-जन्मनि upon whose birth, प्रमुदिताः joyful, कृत-तूर्य-घोषाः sounding musical instruments and trumpets, पुष्प-उत्करान् showers of flowers, प्रववृषुः poured down, नुनुवुः sang praises, सुर-ओघाः the hosts of devas

Simple Meaning: Murti, the virtuous wife of Dharma, gave birth to You in the form of Narayana, along with Your constant companion Nara, possessing immense spiritual majesty. At the moment of Your birth, the overjoyed hosts of devas sounded celestial musical instruments, showered flowers from the heavens, and sang Your divine praises!

दैत्यं सहस्रकवचं कवचैः परीतं
साहस्रवत्सरतपस्समराभिलव्यैः ।
पर्यायनिर्मिततपस्समरौ भवन्तौ
शिष्टैककङ्कटममुं न्यहतां सलीलम् ॥ 16.3 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: दैत्यम् the asura, सहस्र-कवचम् named Sahasrakavacha, कवचैः परीतम् endowed with a thousand armors, साहस्र-वत्सर-तपस्-समर-अभिलव्यैः which could only be destroyed by simultaneously performing a thousand years of penance and a thousand years of battle, पर्याय-निर्मित-तपस्-समरौ engaging alternately in penance and battle, भवन्तौ both of You (Nara and Narayana), शिष्ट-एक-कङ्कटम्-अमुम् him who was left with only one armor, न्यहताम् destroyed/killed, सलीलम् effortlessly/playfully

Simple Meaning: The asura Sahasrakavacha possessed a thousand invincible armors. One who did penance for a thousand years and also fought with the Asura for a thousand years, simultaneously, only could pierce the armors. Engaging in penance and battle by turns, You both (Nara and Narayana) effortlessly destroyed nine hundred and ninety-nine of his armors, leaving him with just one remaining armor!

अन्वाचरन्नुपदिशन्नपि मोक्षधर्मं
त्वं भ्रातृमान् बदरिकाश्रममध्यवात्सीः ।
शक्रोऽथ ते शमतपोबलनिस्सहात्मा
दिव्याङ्गनापरिवृतं प्रजिघाय मारम् ॥ 16. 4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्वाचरन् practicing, उपदिशन् अपि and also teaching/instructing, मोक्ष-धर्मम् the path of liberation/righteousness, त्वम् You, भ्रातृमान् along with Your brother Nara, बदरिकाश्रमम्-मध्यवात्सीः dwelt in Badarikashrama, शक्रः अथ Indra thereafter, ते Your, शम-तपः-बल-निस्सहात्मा unable to tolerate the power of Your peace and penance, दिव्य-अङ्गना-परिवृतम् accompanied by divine maidens/apsaras, प्रजिघाय dispatched, मारम् Kamadeva (the god of love)

Simple Meaning: While practicing and teaching the eternal path of liberation, You dwelt in Badarikashrama along with Your brother Nara. Unable to tolerate the vast spiritual power generated by Your intense penance and tranquility, Indra dispatched Kamadeva accompanied by a host of celestial maidens to disturb Your meditation.

कामो वसन्तमलयानिलबन्धुशाली
कान्ताकटाक्षविशिखैर्विकसद्विलासैः ।
विध्यन्मुहुर्मुहुरकम्पमुदीक्ष्य च त्वां
भीरुस्त्वयाऽथ जगदे मृदुहासभाजा ॥ 16. 5 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: कामः Kamadeva, वसन्त-मलय-अनिल-बन्धु-शाली accompanied by his companions Spring (Vasanta) and the southern breeze (Malayanila), कान्ता-कटाक्ष-विशिखैः with the arrow-like sidelong glances of the beautiful damsels, विकसत्-विलासैः with unfolding charming gestures, विध्यन् मुहुः मुहुः shooting/piercing repeatedly, अकम्पम् immovable/unshakeable, उदीक्ष्य seeing, च and, त्वाम् You, भीरुः terrified/frightened, त्वया अथ by You then, जगदे was addressed, मृदु-हास-भाजा wearing a gentle smile

Simple Meaning: Accompanied by his companions—Spring and the fragrant southern breeze—Kamadeva repeatedly aimed his arrows at You, along with the captivating gestures and sidelong glances of the celestial maidens. However, seeing You completely unmoved and unshakeable, he became terrified. You then spoke to him with a gentle and compassionate smile.

भीत्याऽलमङ्गज वसन्त सुराङ्गना वो
मन्मानसं त्विह जुषध्वमिति ब्रुवाणः ।
त्वं विस्मयेन परितः स्तुवतामथैषां
प्रादर्शयः स्वपरिचारककातराक्षीः ॥ 16.6 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: भीत्या अलम् away with fear, अङ्गज O Kamadeva!, वसन्त O Vasanta!, सुर-अङ्गनाः O celestial maidens!, वः for you all, मत्-मानसम् (projection of) My mind, तु indeed, इह here, जुषध्वम् enjoy/accept hospitality, इति thus, ब्रुवाणः speaking, त्वम् You, विस्मयेन with amazement, परितः all around, स्तुवताम् while they were praising, अथ एषाम् then to them, प्रादर्शयः displayed/revealed, स्व-परिचारक-कातर-अक्षीः charming, timid-eyed maidens serving as Your attendants

Simple Meaning: "O Kamadeva, Vasanta, and celestial maidens! Cease your fear! Please enjoy (the projection of) My mind here," You said to them gently. As they stood praising You in complete amazement, You revealed to them a host of exquisite, timid-eyed maidens serving as Your personal attendants.

सम्मोहनाय मिलिता मदनादयस्ते
त्वद्दासिकापरिमलैः किल मोहमापुः ।
दत्तां त्वया च जगृहुस्त्रपयैव सर्व-
स्वर्वासिगर्वशमनीं पुनरुर्वशीं ताम् ॥ 16.7 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: सम्मोहनाय for causing delusion/enchanting You, मिलिताः gathered together, मदन-आदयः ते Kamadeva and others, त्वत्-दासिका-परिमलैः by the celestial fragrance of Your maidservants, किल indeed, मोहम् आपुः became enchanted/overwhelmed, दत्ताम् presented/given, त्वया by You, च and, जगृहुः they accepted, त्रपया एव with utter humiliation/bashfulness, सर्व-स्वर्-वासि-गर्व-शमनीम् her - who humbled the pride of all the inhabitants of heaven, पुनः moreover, उर्वशीम् Urvashi, ताम् her

Simple Meaning: Kamadeva and his companions, who had gathered with the intent of enchanting You, were instead completely overcome and mesmerized by the divine fragrance and beauty of Your handmaidens! Filled with awe and humiliation, they accepted Urvashi, who humbled the pride of all heavenly beings—the most exquisite nymph among them, created by You —and took her back to Indra.

दृष्ट्वोर्वशीं तव कथां च निशम्य शक्रः
पर्याकुलोऽजनि भवन्महिमावमर्शात् ।
एवं प्रशान्तरमणीयतरावतारात्
त्वत्तोऽधिको वरद कृष्णतनुस्त्वमेव ॥ 16.8 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: दृष्ट्वा seeing, उर्वशीम् Urvashi, तव Your, कथाम् story/account, च and, निशम्य hearing, शक्रः Indra, पर्याकुलः अजनि became deeply agitated/filled with awe, भवत्-महिमा-अवमर्शात् by realizing Your supreme glory, एवम् thus, प्रशान्त-रमणीयतर-अवतारात् than this incarnation which is extremely peaceful and captivating, त्वत्तः अधिकः greater than You, वरद O Bestower of boons!, कृष्ण-तनुः in the form of Krishna, त्वम् एव You Yourself

Simple Meaning: O Bestower of boons! Upon seeing Urvashi and hearing the account of Your glorious deeds, Indra was overcome with awe as he realized Your supreme majesty. Yet, O Lord, even greater and more enchanting than this serene incarnation of Nara-Narayana is You Yourself in Your manifestation as Krishna!

दक्षस्तु धातुरतिलालनया रजोऽन्धो
नात्यादृतस्त्वयि च कष्टमशान्तिरासीत् ।
येन व्यरुन्ध स भवत्तनुमेव शर्वं
यज्ञे च वैरपिशुने स्वसुतां व्यमानीत् ॥ 16.9 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: दक्षः Daksha, तु but, धातुः of Brahma, अति-लालनया due to excessive pampering/indulgence, रजः-अन्धः blinded by rajas (passion/pride), न अत्यादृतः was not very devoted/respectful, त्वयि in You, च and, कष्टम् alas, अशान्तिः आसीत् became filled with arrogance/lack of peace, येन by which, व्यरुन्ध he opposed/hated, सः he, भवत्-तनुम् एव who is an embodiment of You, शर्वम् Lord Shiva, यज्ञे at the sacrifice, च and, वैर-पिशुने manifesting his deep enmity, स्व-सुताम् his own daughter (Sati), व्यमानीत् insulted/humiliated

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Blinded by passion and pride due to the excessive pampering of his father Brahma, Daksha lacked true devotion unto You and became full of arrogance. Alas, because of this haughtiness, he bore hatred towards Lord Shiva, who is verily Your own form, and in a grand sacrifice driven by malice, he deeply humiliated his own daughter Sati.

क्रुद्धेशमर्दितमखः स तु कृत्तशीर्षो
देवप्रसादितहरादथ लब्धजीवः ।
त्वत्पूरितक्रतुवरः पुनराप शान्तिं
स त्वं प्रशान्तिकर पाहि मरुत्पुरेश ॥ 16.10 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्रुद्ध-ईश-मर्दित-मखः his sacrifice destroyed by angry Lord Shiva (or Virabhadra), सः he (Daksha), तु but, कृत्त-शीर्षः having his head severed, देव-प्रसादित-हरात् अथ from Lord Shiva who was appeased by the devas thereafter, लब्ध-जीवः regaining his life, त्वत्-पूरित-क्रतु-वरः having his grand sacrifice completed through Your grace, पुनः आप regained/attained, शान्तिम् peace, सः त्वम् that You, प्रशान्तिकर O Bestower of absolute peace!, पाहि protect (me), मरुत्पुर-ईश O Lord of Guruvayur!

Simple Meaning: His sacrifice destroyed by enraged Lord Shiva and his head severed, Daksha was restored to life when the devas appeased Shiva. Through Your grace, his grand sacrifice was ultimately completed, and he finally attained peace. O Bestower of peace, O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect me!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
