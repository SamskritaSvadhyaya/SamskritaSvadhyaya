Dashaka 7

The Lord commands Brahma to create

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 7

The Lord commands Brahma to create

(छन्दस् - शार्दूलविक्रीडितम्)

एवं देव चतुर्दशात्मकजगद्रूपेण जातः पुनः
तस्योर्ध्वं खलु सत्यलोकनिलये जातोऽसि धाता स्वयम् ।
यं शंसन्ति हिरण्यगर्भमखिलत्रैलोक्यजीवात्मकं
योऽभूत् स्फीतरजोविकारविकसन्नानासिसृक्षारसः ॥7.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, देव O Lord, चतुर्दश-आत्मक-जगत्-रूपेण in the form of the universe consisting of fourteen worlds, जातः born/manifested, पुनः then, तस्य ऊर्ध्वम् above that, खलु indeed, सत्यलोक-निलये in the abode of Satyaloka, जातः असि You were born, धाता Brahma, स्वयम् Yourself, यम् whom, शंसन्ति they praise, हिरण्यगर्भम् as Hiranyagarbha, अखिल-त्रैलोक्य-जीव-आत्मकम् the soul of all living beings in the three worlds, यः who, अभूत् became, स्फीत-रजः-विकार-विकसत्-नाना-सिसृक्ष-रसः having the intense desire for diverse creations expanded by the swelling modification of rajas guna

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Having thus manifested in the form of the universe consisting of fourteen worlds, You Yourself, then incarnated in Satyaloka as Creator Brahma, whom scriptures praise as Hiranyagarbha, the soul of all beings in the three worlds. Inspired by the swelling quality of rajas guna, You developed an intense desire for creating diverse beings.

सोऽयं विश्वविसर्गदत्तहृदयः सम्पश्यमानः स्वयं
बोधं खल्वनवाप्य विश्वविषयं चिन्ताकुलस्तस्थिवान् ।
तावत्त्वं जगतां पते तप तपेत्येवं हि वैहायसीं
वाणीमेनमशिश्रवः श्रुतिसुखां कुर्वंस्तपःप्रेरणाम् ॥7.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः अयम् that Brahma, विश्व-विसर्ग-दत्त-हृदयः having set his heart on the creation of the universe, सम्पश्यमानः searching/looking, स्वयम् himself, बोधम् knowledge, खलु indeed, अनवाप्य not obtaining, विश्व-विषयम् regarding the creation of the universe, चिन्ता-कुलः overcome with anxiety/worried, तस्थिवान् stood/remained, तावत् then, त्वम् You, जगताम् पते O Lord of the worlds, तप तप इति "tapa tapa" (do penance, do penance), एवम् thus, हि indeed, वैहायसीम् coming from the sky/disembodied, वाणीम् voice, एनम् to him, अशिश्रवः You made him hear, श्रुति-सुखाम् pleasant to the ears, कुर्वन् creating/causing, तपः-प्रेरणाम् inspiration for penance

Simple Meaning: O Lord of the worlds! Having set his heart on creating the universe, Brahma looked around to begin, but not obtaining the necessary knowledge regarding how to create, he stood overcome with anxiety. Just then, You made him hear a disembodied voice from the sky sounding pleasant to the ears that uttered "tapa tapa", inspiring him to perform penance.

कोऽसौ मामवदत् पुमानिति जलापूर्णे जगन्मण्डले
दिक्षूद्वीक्ष्य किमप्यनीक्षितवता वाक्यार्थमुत्पश्यता ।
दिव्यं वर्षसहस्रमात्ततपसा तेन त्वमाराधितः
तस्मै दर्शितवानसि स्वनिलयं वैकुण्ठमेकाद्भुतम् ॥7.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: कः असौ who is this, माम् me, अवदत् spoke to, पुमान् person, इति thus, जल-आपूर्णे completely filled with water, जगत्-मण्डले in the sphere of the world, दिक्षु in all directions, उद्वीक्ष्य looking around, किमपि anything, अनीक्षितवता not seeing, वाक्य-अर्थम् meaning of the words, उत्पश्यता discerning/realizing, दिव्यम् divine, वर्ष-सहस्रम् a thousand years, आत्त-तपसा by him who took up penance, तेन by him, त्वम् You, आराधितः were worshipped, तस्मै to him, दर्शितवान् असि You revealed, स्व-निलयम् Your own abode, वैकुण्ठम् Vaikuntha, एक-अद्भुतम् uniquely wonderful

Simple Meaning: "Who is this person that spoke to me?" thinking thus, Brahma looked around in all directions within the cosmic sphere filled completely with water, but saw no one. Realizing the intended meaning of those divine words, he undertook severe penance for a thousand divine years to worship You. Pleased with his devotion, You revealed to him Your own uniquely wonderful abode, Vaikuntha.

माया यत्र कदापि नो विकुरुते भाते जगद्भ्यो बहिः
शोकक्रोधविमोहसाध्वसमुखा भावास्तु दूरं गताः ।
सान्द्रानन्दझरी च यत्र परमज्योतिःप्रकाशात्मके
तत्ते धाम विभावितं विजयते वैकुण्ठरूपं विभो ॥7.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: माया divine illusion (maya), यत्र where, कदापि at any time, नो not, विकुरुते exerts power/creates modifications, भाते shines, जगद्भ्यः from the worlds/universe, बहिः outside, शोक-क्रोध-विमोह-साध्वस-मुखाः beginning with grief, anger, delusion, and fear, भावाः mental states/emotions, तु indeed, दूरम् far away, गताः gone, सान्द्र-आनन्द-झरी continuous stream of intense bliss, च and, यत्र where, परम-ज्योतिः-प्रकाश-आत्मके possessing the nature of supreme luminous light, तत् that, ते Your, धाम abode, विभावितम् realized/revealed, विजयते reigns supreme/triumphs, वैकुण्ठ-रूपम् the form of Vaikuntha, विभो O All-Pervading Lord

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord! Maya never exerts its power in that supreme abode, which shines beyond the material universe. Negative emotional states such as grief, anger, delusion, and fear are kept far away. Where a continuous stream of intense bliss flows and supreme spiritual light shines, that glorious abode of Yours known as Vaikuntha reigns supreme!

यस्मिन्नाम चतुर्भुजा हरिमणिश्यामावदातत्विषो
नानाभूषणरत्नदीपितदिशो राजद्विमानालयाः ।
भक्तिप्राप्ततथाविधोन्नतपदा दीव्यन्ति दिव्या जनाः
तत्ते धाम निरस्तसर्वशमलं वैकुण्ठरूपं जयेत् ॥7.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: यस्मिन् in which, नाम indeed, चतुर्भुजाः four-armed, हरिमणि-श्याम-अवदात-त्विषः having a dark blue complexion like a sapphire, नाना-भूषण-रत्न-दीपित-दिशः illuminating all directions with various jeweled ornaments, राजत्-विमान-आलयाः residing in splendid flying chariots, भक्ति-प्राप्त-तथाविध-उन्नत-पदाः having attained such a exalted position through devotion, दीव्यन्ति sport/shine, दिव्याः divine, जनाः beings, तत् that, ते Your, धाम abode, निरस्त-सर्व-शमलम् devoid of all impurities, वैकुण्ठ-रूपम् of the form of Vaikuntha, जयेत् may it triumph

Simple Meaning: In that supreme realm, divine beings sport splendidly. Having attained such an exalted state purely through devotion, they possess four arms, a dark-blue complexion like that of a sapphire, illuminate all directions with their various jeweled ornaments and stay in magnificent flying chariots. May that pure abode of Vaikuntha, completely free from all impurities, reign supreme!

नानादिव्यवधूजनैरभिवृता विद्युल्लतातुल्यया
विश्वोन्मादनहृद्यगात्रलतया विद्योतिताशान्तरा ।
त्वत्पादांबुजसौरभैककुतुकाल्लक्ष्मीः स्वयं लक्ष्यते
यस्मिन् विस्मयनीयदिव्यविभवं तत्ते पदं देहि मे ॥7.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: नाना-दिव्य-वधू-जनैः by various celestial maidens, अभिवृता surrounded, विद्युत्-लता-तुल्यया resembling a streak of lightning, विश्व-उन्मादन-हृद्य-गात्र-लतया with a slender charming body that fascinates the universe, विद्योतित-आशा-अन्तरा illuminating the space between all directions, त्वत्-पाद-अम्बुज-सौरभ-एक-कुतुकात् solely out of eager longing for the fragrance of Your lotus feet, लक्ष्मीः Goddess Lakshmi, स्वयम् Herself, लक्ष्यते is seen, यस्मिन् in which, विस्मयनीय-दिव्य-विभवम् possessing wondrous divine splendor, तत् that, ते Your, पदम् abode/state, देहि grant, मे to me

Simple Meaning: Surrounded by various celestial maidens, Goddess Lakshmi Herself is seen in that realm, Her slender body charming enough to enchant the entire universe and brilliant like a flash of lightning, illuminating all directions. She resides there solely out of Her eager desire to enjoy the fragrance of Your lotus feet. Please grant me that wonderfully glorious abode of Yours!

तत्रैवं प्रतिदर्शिते निजपदे रत्नासनाध्यासितं
भास्वत्कोटिलसत्किरीटकटकाद्याकल्पदीप्राकृति ।
श्रीवत्साङ्कितमात्तकौस्तुभमणिच्छायारुणं कारणं
विश्वेषां तव रूपमैक्षत विधिस्तत्ते विभो भातु मे ॥7.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र in that place, एवम् thus, प्रतिदर्शिते revealed, निज-पदे in Your own abode, रत्न-आसन-अध्यासितम् seated on a throne set with gems, भास्वत्-कोटि-लसत्-किरीट-कटक-आदि-आकल्प-दीप्र-आकृति having a form shining with ornaments like crown and bracelets glowing with the brilliance of millions of suns, श्रीवत्स-अङ्कितम् marked with the Shrivatsa sign, आत्त-कौस्तुभ-मणि-छाया-अरुणम् rendered reddish by the luster of the Kaustubha gem worn, कारणम् the ultimate cause, विश्वेषाम् of all the universes, तव Your, रूपम् form, ऐक्षत saw, विधिः Brahma, तत् that, ते Your, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, भातु may it shine, मे in my heart/before me

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord! In that abode of Yours thus revealed, Brahma beheld Your divine form, seated on a gem-studded throne, radiant with crowns, bracelets, and ornaments brilliant like millions of suns. Marked with the auspicious Shrivatsa sign and bathed in the reddish glow of the Kaustubha gem that You wore, You appeared as the supreme cause of all the universes. May that glorious form of Yours shine in my heart!

कालाम्भोदकलायकोमलरुचीचक्रेण चक्रं दिशाम्
आवृण्वानमुदारमन्दहसितस्यन्दप्रसन्नाननम् ।
राजत्कम्बुगदारिपङ्कजधरश्रीमद्भुजामण्डलं
स्रष्टुस्तुष्टिकरं वपुस्तव विभो मद्रोगमुद्वासयेत् ॥7.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: काल-अम्भोद-कलाय-कोमल-रुची-चक्रेण by the mass of soft lustrous sheen resembling dark rain clouds and the kalaya flower, चक्रम् the sphere, दिशाम् of all directions, आवृण्वानम् enveloping/covering, उदार-मन्द-हसित-स्यन्द-प्रसन्न-आननम् having a serene face with a gentle and benevolent smile, राजत्-कम्बु-गदा-अरि-पङ्कज-धर-श्रीमत्-भुजा-मण्डलम् possessing glorious arms holding a conch, mace, discus, and lotus, स्रष्टुः to Creator Brahma, तुष्टि-करम् imparting joy, वपुः divine form, तव Your, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, मत्-रोगम् my disease/affliction, उद्वासयेत् may it eradicate/drive away

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord! Enveloping all directions with a soft, radiant luster resembling dark rain clouds and the kalaya flower, Your serene face shone a gentle, benevolent smile. Your glorious arms adorned with conch, mace, discus, and lotus, filled Creator Brahma with immense joy. May that divine form of Yours completely drive away my afflictions!

दृष्ट्वा सम्भृतसम्भ्रमः कमलभूस्त्वत्पादपाथोरुहे
हर्षावेशवशंवदो निपतितः प्रीत्या कृतार्थीभवन् ।
जानास्येव मनीषितं मम विभो ज्ञानं तदापादय
द्वैताद्वैतभवत्स्वरूपपरमित्याचष्ट तं त्वां भजे ॥7.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: दृष्ट्वा having seen, सम्भृत-सम्भ्रमः filled with great awe and reverence, कमल-भूः Brahma (the lotus-born), त्वत्-पाद-पाथोरुहे at Your lotus feet, हर्ष-आवेश-वशंवदः overcome by intense joy, निपतितः fell down/prostrated, प्रीत्या with love, कृत-अर्थी-भवन् feeling fulfilled, जानासि एव You indeed know, मनीषितम् desire/wish, मम my, विभो O Lord, ज्ञानम् knowledge, तत् that, आपादय grant, द्वैत-अद्वैत-भवत्-स्वरूप-परम् regarding Your supreme nature which encompasses both dualism and non-dualism, इति thus, वाचष्ट prayed/spoke, तम् to You, त्वाम् You, भजे I worship

Simple Meaning: Seeing Your glorious form, Lord Brahma (the lotus-born), filled with deep awe and overcome by intense joy, fell prostrate at Your lotus feet with love, feeling supreme fulfillment. He prayed, “O Lord! You already know my innermost desire. Please grant me that knowledge regarding Your true nature, which encompasses both dualism and non-dualism!” I worship You, the supreme Lord who was thus addressed by Brahma.

आताम्रे चरणे विनम्रमथ तं हस्तेन हस्ते स्पृशन्
बोधस्ते भविता न सर्गविधिभिर्बन्धोऽपि सञ्जायते ।
इत्याभाष्य गिरं प्रतोष्य नितरां तच्चित्तगूढः स्वयं
सृष्टौ तं समुदैरयः स भगवन्नुल्लासयोल्लाघताम् ॥7.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: आताम्रे slightly reddish/copper-hued, चरणे at Your feet, विनम्रम् bowed down, अथ then, तम् him (Brahma), हस्तेन with Your hand, हस्ते in his hand, स्पृशन् touching, बोधः spiritual knowledge, ते to you, भविता shall happen/arise, न not, सर्ग-विधिभिः by the acts of creation, बन्धः bondage, अपि even, सञ्जायते will occur, इति thus, आभाष्य having spoken, गिरम् with reassuring words, प्रतोष्य gladdening, नितराम् immensely, तत्-चित्त-गूढः hidden within his mind, स्वयम् Yourself, सृष्टौ in the work of creation, तम् him, समुदैरयः You inspired/directed, सः that, भगवन् O Supreme Lord, उल्लासय arouse/increase, उल्लाघताम् my freedom from disease/wellness

Simple Meaning: As Brahma remained bowed down at Your slightly reddish lotus feet, You lovingly took his hand in Yours and gladdened him with reassuring words: “Knowledge shall be yours, and you will never be bound by the act of creation.” Having comforted him, You entered into his heart and inspired him to begin the work of creation. That You, O Lord, please arouse in me vibrant health and complete relief from illness!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
