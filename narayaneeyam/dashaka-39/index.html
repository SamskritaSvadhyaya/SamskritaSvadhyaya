The story of Yogmaya

दशकम् ३९

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 39

The story of Yogmaya

भवन्तमयमुद्वहन् यदुकुलोद्वहो निस्सरन् ददर्श गगनोच्चलज्जलभरां कलिन्दात्मजाम्।
अहो सलिलसञ्चयः स पुनरैन्द्रजालोदितो जलौघ इव तत्क्षणात् प्रपदमेयतामाययौ ॥39.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: भवन्तम् You, अयम् this, उद्वहन् carrying, यदुकुल-उद्वहः the eminent one of the Yadu clan (Vasudeva), निस्सरन् stepping out/emerging, ददर्श saw, गगन-उच्चलत्-जल-भ्राम् whose waters were swelling high up into the sky, कलिन्द-आत्मजाम् the Yamuna river (daughter of Kalinda - the Sun), अहो Oh/what a wonder!, सलिल-सञ्चयः that accumulation of water, सः that, पुनः but, ऐन्द्रजाल-उदितः produced by magic/illusory, जल-ओघः flood of water, इव like, तत्क्षणात् instantly, प्रपद-मेयताम् measure up to the ankles, आययौ became

कलिन्द   the sun, src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

ऐन्द्रजाल   ऐन्द्रजाल n. (fr. इन्द्र-जाल), magic, sorcery,  src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: Carrying You, Vasudeva, the jewel of the Yadu dynasty, emerged from the prison and saw the river Yamuna, whose dark waters were raging and surging high towards the sky. But oh, what a miracle! That immense volume of water instantly receded—as if a magical illusion had vanished—and shrank down to mere ankle-depth so he could easily cross!

प्रसुप्तपशुपालिकां निभृतमारुदद्बालिकाम् अपावृतकवाटिकां पशुपवाटिकामाविशन् ।
भवन्तमयमर्पयन् प्रसवतल्पके तत्पदात् वहन् कपटकन्यकां स्वपुरमागतो वेगतः ॥39.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रसुप्त-पशुपालिकाम् where the cowherd women (including Yashoda) were fast asleep, निभृतम् softly/quietly, आरुदत्-बालिकाम् with the infant girl crying gently, अपावृत-कवाटिकाम् whose doors were wide open, पशुप-वाटिकाम् the abode/house of the cowherd chief (Nanda), आविशन् entering, भवन्तम् You, अयम् he (Vasudeva), अर्पयन् placing, प्रसव-तल्पके on the bed of childbirth, तत्-पदात् from that place, वहन् carrying, कपट-कन्यकाम् the illusory infant girl (Yogamaya), स्व-पुरम् to his own city (Mathura), आगतः returned, वेगतः swiftly

Simple Meaning: Entering the home of Nanda Gokula—where the doors stood wide open, all the cowherd women were fast asleep, and the infant girl was crying softly—Vasudeva gently laid You upon Yashoda's birthing bed. Then, taking the divine illusory baby girl in exchange, he swiftly returned to his own residence in Mathura.

ततस्त्वदनुजारवक्षपितनिद्रवेगद्रवद्भटोत्करनिवेदितप्रसववार्तयैवार्तिमान् ।
विमुक्तचिकुरोत्करस्त्वरितमापतन् भोजराडतुष्ट इव दृष्टवान् भगिनिकाकरे कन्यकाम् ॥39.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, त्वत्-अनुजा-रव-क्षपित-निद्र-वेग-द्रवत्-भट-उत्कर-निवेदित-प्रसव-वार्तया एव by the news of the birth delivered by the host of guards who rushed up as their deep sleep was dispelled by the crying of Your younger sister (Yogamaya), आर्तिमान् agitated/filled with distress, विमुक्त-चिकुर-उत्करः with his disheveled hair falling loose, त्वरितम् hastily, आपतन् rushing in, भोजराट् the King of the Bhojas (Kamsa), अतुष्टः displeasure/dissatisfied, इव as if, दृष्टवान् saw, भगिनिका-करे in his sister's (Devaki's) hands, कन्यकाम् a female infant

Simple Meaning: Awoken from their deep slumber by the crying sound of Your divine younger sister, the prison guards rushed to inform Kamsa of the birth. Terrified and agitated upon hearing the news, Kamsa bolted into the room with his hair streaming wild and disheveled. Filled with utter despair and hostility, he looked and saw only a baby girl cradled in his sister Devaki's hands.

ध्रुवं कपटशालिनो मधुहरस्य माया भवेत् असाविति किशोरिकां भगिनिकाकरालिङ्गिताम् ।
द्विपो नलिनिकान्तरादिव मृणालिकामाक्षिपन् अयं त्वदनुजामजामुपलपट्टके पिष्टवान् ॥39.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: ध्रुवम् certainly, कपट-शालिनः of the deceitful one, मधु-हरस्य of the destroyer of Madhu (Lord Vishnu), माया illusion, भवेत् must be, असौ this, इति thus thinking, किशोरिकाम् the infant girl, भगिनिका-कर-आलिङ्गिताम् held tightly in the arms of his sister (Devaki), द्विपः an elephant, नलिनिका-अन्तरात् from the midst of a lotus pond, इव like, मृणालिकाम् a tender lotus stalk, आक्षिपन् snatching away, अयम् this (Kamsa), त्वत्-अनुजाम् Your younger sister, अजाम् the unborn divine Maya, उपल-पट्टके on a slab of stone, पिष्टवान् dashed/smashed

Simple Meaning: Thinking that this infant girl must surely be an illusion created by the deceitful Vishnu, Kamsa snatched Your divine younger sister from Devaki's embracing arms, just like an elephant pulls a tender lotus stalk from a pond, and mercilessly dashed her against a slab of stone.

ततः भवदुपासको झटिति मृत्युपाशादिव प्रमुच्य तरसैव सा समधिरूढरूपान्तरा ।
अधस्तलमजग्मुषी विकसदष्टबाहुस्फुरन्महायुधमहो गता किल विहायसा दिद्युते ॥39.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, भवत्-उपासकः Your devotee, झटिति instantly, मृत्यु-पाशात् from the noose of death, इव as if, प्रमुच्य escaping/freed, तरसा directly/swiftly, एव indeed, सा she (Yogamaya), समधिरूढ-रूप-अन्तरा assuming another divine form, अधः-तलम् down to the ground, अजग्मुषी not falling, विकसत्-अष्ट-बाहु-स्फुरत्-महा-आयुधम् gleaming with powerful weapons held in her eight radiant arms, अहो wonder/amazement, गता rising up, किल indeed, विहायसा into the sky, दिद्युते shone radiantly

Simple Meaning: Then, escaping from Kamsa's grip instantly like Your devotee being freed from the noose of death, she did not crash down to the ground. Instead, swiftly assuming another celestial form, she rose into the sky, shining radiantly with powerful weapons gleaming in her eight outstretched arms.

नृशंसतर कंस ते किमु मया विनिष्पिष्टया बभूव भवदन्तकः क्वचन चिन्त्यतां ते हितम् ।
इति त्वदनुजा विभो खलमुदीर्य तं जग्मुषी मरुद्गणपणायिता भुवि च मन्दिराण्येयुषी ॥39.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: नृशंसतर O most cruel, कंस Kamsa, ते for you, किम् what purpose, मया by me, विनिष्पिष्टया being smashed, बभूव has been born, भवत्-अन्तकः your slayer, क्वचन somewhere, चिन्त्यताम् let it be thought of, ते for your, हितम् welfare, इति thus, त्वत्-अनुजा Your younger sister, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, खलम् to the wicked (Kamsa), उदीर्य having spoken, तम् to him, जग्मुषी departing, मरुत्-गण-पणायिता praised by the hosts of devas, भुवि on earth, च and, मन्दिराणि to various shrines, एयुषी attained/established herself

Simple Meaning: O Lord, Your divine younger sister spoke thus to that wicked Kamsa – “O most cruel Kamsa, what do you gain by smashing me? Your slayer has already been born elsewhere, so think now of your own welfare!” Having addressed him in this manner, she departed into the sky while being praised by the devas, and established herself in various shrines across the earth.

प्रगे पुनरगात्मजावचनमीरिता भूभुजा प्रलम्बबकपूतनाप्रमुखदानवा मानिनः ।
भवन्निधनकाम्यया जगति बभ्रमुर्निर्भयाः कुमारकविमारकाः किमिव दुष्करं निष्कृपैः ॥39.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रगे next morning, पुनः again, अग-आत्मजा-वचनम् words of the daughter of the mountain - Yogmaya (Parvati), ईशिता guided/spurred, भूभुजा by the king (Kamsa), प्रलम्ब-बक-पूतना-प्रमुख-दानवाः danavas led by Pralamba, Baka and Putana, मानिनः arrogant, भवत्-निधन-काम्यया with the desire to kill You, जगति across the world, बभ्रमुः wandered, निर्भयाः fearless, कुमारक-विमारकाः killers of infants, किम् what, इव indeed, दुष्करम् impossible/hard to do, निष्कृपैः by the pitiless ones

अग   अग a. [न गच्छतीति; गम्-ड. न. त]. 1 Unable to walk, not going, not in a position to go;   src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

– A mountain; also a stone;  पर्वतः , वृक्षः  src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

(basically something which cannot move - in this context - a mountain.Yogmaya is Devi Parvati, the daughter of the mountain)

Simple Meaning: The next morning, spurred by the prophecy of Yogmaya, King Kamsa dispatched his arrogant danavas like Pralamba, Baka, and Putana. Desiring to kill You, these pitiless slayers of infants wandered fearlessly across the world, for what cruel deed is impossible for those devoid of mercy?

ततः पशुपमन्दिरे त्वयि मुकुन्द नन्दप्रियाप्रसूतिशयनेशये रुदति किञ्चिदञ्चत्पदे ।
विबुध्य वनिताजनैस्तनयसम्भवे घोषिते मुदा किमु वदाम्यहो सकलमाकुलं गोकुलम् ॥39.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, पशुप-मन्दिरे in the house of the chief of cowherds (Nanda), त्वयि when You, मुकुन्द O Lord Mukunda, नन्द-प्रिया-प्रसूति-शयने-शये lying on the delivery bed of Nanda's beloved wife (Yashoda), रुदति whimpering/crying, किञ्चित्-अञ्चत्-पदे kicking Your tiny feet slightly, विबुध्य waking up, वनिता-जनैः by the womenfolk, तनय-सम्भवे the birth of a son, घोषिते being proclaimed, मुदा with joy, किम् what, उ indeed, वदामि can I say, अहो marvel, सकलम् entire, आकुलम् overflowing/agitated with joy, गोकुलम् Gokula

Simple Meaning: O Mukunda! Meanwhile in the home of Nanda, as You lay crying on Yashoda's delivery bed and kicking Your tiny feet, the women woke up and heralded the joyous news of a baby boy's birth. What words can describe that bliss? Ah, all of Gokula was ecstatic and completely overcome with immense joy!

अहो खलु यशोदया नवकलायचेतोहरं भवन्तमलमन्तिके प्रथममापिबन्त्या दृशा ।
पुनः स्तनभरं निजं सपदि पाययन्त्या मुदा मनोहरतनुस्पृशा जगति पुण्यवन्तो जिताः ॥39.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: अहो oh, खलु indeed, यशोदया by Yashoda, नव-कलाय-चेतः-हरम् captivating like a fresh kalaya flower, भवन्तम् You, अलम् abundantly, अन्तिके nearby, प्रथमम् first, आपिबन्त्या drinking in, दृशा with her eyes, पुनः again, स्तन-भरम् her milk-laden breast, निजम् her own, सपदि at once, पाययन्त्या feeding, मुदा with joy, मनोहर-तनु-स्पृशा touching Your enchanting body, जगति in the world, पुण्यवन्तः virtuous/blessed souls, जिताः excelled/surpassed

Simple Meaning: Oh, how blessed was Yashoda! Drinking in Your captivating form—which resembled a fresh kalaya blossom—with her eyes as You lay beside her, she lovingly fed You her milk and stroked Your enchanting body with overwhelming joy. Truly, she surpassed all the virtuous and fortunate souls in the world!

भवत्कुशलकाम्यया स खलु नन्दगोपस्तदा प्रमोदभरसङ्कुलो द्विजकुलाय किन्नाददात् ।
तथैव पशुपालकाः किमु न मङ्गलं तेनिरे जगत्त्रितयमङ्गल त्वमिह पाहि मामामयात् ॥39.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: भवत्-कुशल-काम्यया out of desire for Your well-being, सः he, खलु indeed, नन्द-गोपः Nanda - the cowherd, तदा then, प्रमोद-भर-सङ्कुलः overwhelmed with immense joy, द्विज-कुलाय to the community of brahmanas, किम् what, न not, अददात् gave, तथा एव in the same manner, पशु-पालकाः the cowherds, किम् what, उ indeed, न not, मङ्गलम् auspicious ceremonies, तेनिरे performed, जगत्-त्रितय-मङ्गल O auspiciousness of the three worlds, त्वम् You, इह here, पाहि protect, माम् me, आमयात् from disease/afflictions

Simple Meaning: Overwhelmed with immense joy and desiring Your well-being, what gifts did Nanda Gopala not shower upon the brahmanas? In the very same way, what auspicious celebrations did the cowherds not perform? O You who bring auspiciousness to all the three worlds, please protect me here from all my diseases!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
