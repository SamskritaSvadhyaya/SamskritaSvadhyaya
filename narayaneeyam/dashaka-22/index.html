Dashaka 22

The story of Ajamila

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 22

The story of Ajamila

(छन्दस् - वंशस्थ)

अजामिलो नाम महीसुरः पुरा
चरन् विभो धर्मपथान् गृहाश्रमी ।
गुरोर्गिरा काननमेत्य दृष्टवान्
सुधृष्टशीलां कुलटां मदाकुलाम् ॥22.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: अजामिलः नाम named Ajamila, महीसुरः a brahmana, पुरा long ago, चरन् walking, विभो O All-pervading Lord, धर्म-पथान् in the path of righteousness, गृह-आश्रमी a householder, गुरोः गिरा on the command of his father, काननम् एत्य entering the forest, दृष्टवान् saw, सु-धृष्ट-शीलाम् a woman of immoral character, कुलटाम् an unchaste woman, मद-आकुलाम् given to drinking

Simple Meaning: O All-pervading Lord! Long ago, there lived a householder brahmana named Ajamila who strictly followed the path of righteousness. One day, while visiting the forest on his father's instructions to gather offerings, he saw an unchaste and immoral woman who was given to drinking.

स्वतः प्रशान्तोऽपि तदाहृताशयः
स्वधर्ममुत्सृज्य तया समारमन् ।
अधर्मकारी दशमी भवन् पुनः
दधौ भवन्नामयुते सुते रतिम् ॥22.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्वतः inherently, प्रशान्तः अपि though calm and peaceful, तत्-आहृत-आशयः his mind captivated by her, स्व-धर्मम् उत्सृज्य abandoning his own righteous duties, तया with her, समारमन् indulging in pleasure, अधर्म-कारी engaging in unrighteous acts, दशमी भवन् reaching the tenth decade of life or advanced old age, पुनः again, दधौ placed, भवत्-नाम-युते bearing Your name, सुते in his son, रतिम् affection or deep attachment

Simple Meaning: Although he was inherently calm and virtuous, his mind became deeply captivated by her. Abandoning his own duties, he spent his life indulging in unrighteous acts with her. As he reached extreme old age, he developed deep affection and attachment for his youngest son, who was bore Your name - Narayana.

स मृत्युकाले यमराजकिङ्करान्
भयङ्करांस्त्रीनभिलक्षयन् भिया ।
पुरा मनाक् त्वत्स्मृतिवासनाबलात्
जुहाव नारायणनामकं सुतम् ॥22.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, मृत्यु-काले at the time of death, यमराज-किङ्करान् the messengers of Yama, भयङ्करान् terrifying, त्रीन् three, अभिलक्षयन् perceiving before him, भिया out of fear, पुरा in the past, मनाक् slightly, त्वत्-स्मृति-वासना-बलात् by the strength of past subtle impressions of remembering You, जुहाव called out, नारायण-नामकम् named Narayana, सुतम् to his son

Simple Meaning: At the time of his death, seeing three terrifying messengers of Yama standing before him, he was overcome with fear. Driven by the force of faint past impressions of remembering You, he loudly called out to his son Narayana.

दुराशयस्यापि तदात्वनिर्गत-
त्वदीयनामाक्षरमात्रवैभवात् ।
पुरोऽभिपेतुर्भवदीयपार्षदाः
चतुर्भुजाः पीतपटा मनोरमाः ॥22.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुराशयस्य अपि even of that evil-minded person, तदा तु then, अनिर्गत-त्वदीय-नाम-अक्षर-मात्र-वैभवात् by the sheer majesty of the syllables of Your name uttered at that moment, पुरः before him, अभिपेतुः arrived quickly, भवदीय-पार्षदाः Your divine attendants, चतुर्भुजाः having four arms, पीत-पटाः wearing yellow garments, मनोरमाः enchanting to behold

Simple Meaning: Even though he was a wicked-minded person, the sheer power and glory of the syllables of Your holy name, uttered by him at the moment of death, caused Your four-armed divine attendants—clad in golden yellow garments and enchanting in appearance—to instantly appear before him.

अमुं च संपाश्य विकर्षतो भटान्
विमुञ्चतेत्यारुरुधुर्बलादमी ।
निवारितास्ते च भवज्जनैस्तदा
तदीयपापं निखिलं न्यवेदयन् ॥22.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: अमुम् him, च and, संपाश्य tying with ropes, विकर्षतः pulling forcefully, भटान् the messengers of Yama, विमुञ्चत release him, इति thus saying, आरुरुधुः stopped, बलात् by force, अमी these attendants of Yours, निवारिताः thwarted, ते they, च and, भवत्-जनैः by Your attendants, तदा then, तदीय-पापम् his sins, निखिलम् all of them, न्यवेदयन् declared

Simple Meaning: Seeing the messengers of Yama tying Ajamila with ropes and dragging away forcefully, Your attendants stopped them by saying “Release him”. Thwarted by Your attendants, Yama's messengers then listed all the sins Ajamila had committed in his life.

भवन्तु पापानि कथं तु निष्कृते
कृतेऽपि भो दण्डनमस्ति पण्डिताः ।
न निष्कृतिः किं विदिता भवादृशाम्
इति प्रभो त्वत्पुरुषा बभाषिरे ॥22.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: भवन्तु let there be, पापानि sins, कथम् how, तु indeed, निष्कृते-कृते-अपि even after atonement has been performed, भो O wise ones, दण्डनम् अस्ति is there punishment, पण्डिताः O learned messengers of Yama, न is not, निष्कृतिः atonement, किम् what, विदिता known, भवादृशाम् to noble ones like you, इति thus, प्रभो O Lord, त्वत्-पुरुषाः Your attendants, बभाषिरे spoke

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Your attendants spoke thus to the messengers of Yama: “O learned ones! Granting that he has committed many sins, how can punishment still apply when he has already performed the ultimate atonement (by uttering the holy name of Lord Narayana)? Is such a fundamental rule of atonement not known to wise messengers like you?”

श्रुतिस्मृतिभ्यां विहिता व्रतादयः
पुनन्ति पापं न लुनन्ति वासनाम् ।
अनन्तसेवा तु निकृन्तति द्वयीम्
इति प्रभो त्वत्पुरुषा बभाषिरे ॥22.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रुति-स्मृतिभ्याम् by the Vedas and the Smritis, विहिताः prescribed, व्रत-आदयः vows etc, पुनन्ति purify, पापम् sin, न do not, लुनन्ति destroy, वासनाम् subtle latent impressions, अनन्त-सेवा worship and service of Lord Ananta, तु but, निकृन्तति eradicates, द्वयीम् both, इति thus, प्रभो O Lord, त्वत्-पुरुषाः Your attendants, बभाषिरे spoke

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Your attendants continued: “Vows, penances, and rites prescribed by the Vedas and Smritis can cleanse a person of sin, but they cannot destroy the underlying sinful tendencies and impressions in the mind. However, service to Lord Ananta and serving Him completely eradicates both the sins and their underlying tendencies!”

अनेन भो जन्मसहस्रकोटिभिः
कृतेषु पापेष्वपि निष्कृतिः कृता ।
यदग्रहीन्नाम भयाकुलो हरेः
इति प्रभो त्वत्पुरुषा बभाषिरे ॥22.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: अनेन by this man, भो O wise ones, जन्म-सहस्र-कोटिभिः in thousands of crores of births, कृतेषु committed, पापेषु for the sins, अपि even, निष्कृतिः atonement, कृता has been done, यत् because, अग्रहीत् he uttered, नाम the holy name, भय-आकुलः overwhelmed with fear, हरेः of Lord Hari, इति thus, प्रभो O Lord, त्वत्-पुरुषाः Your attendants, बभाषिरे spoke

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Your attendants spoke further: “O wise ones, even if this man had accumulated sins over billions of lifetimes, complete atonement was instantly achieved the moment he uttered the holy name of Lord Hari, even though he called out while overwhelmed by fear!”

नृणामबुद्ध्यापि मुकुन्दकीर्तनं
दहत्यघौघान् महिमास्य तादृशः ।
यथाग्निरेधांसि यथौषधं गदान्
इति प्रभो त्वत्पुरुषा बभाषिरे ॥22.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: नृणाम् of human beings, अबुद्ध्या अपि even without full awareness or unintentionally, मुकुन्द-कीर्तनम् chanting or praising the name of Mukunda, दहति burns, अघ-ओघान् masses of sins, महिमा अस्य its glory, तादृशः is of such a nature, यथा like, अग्निः fire, एधांसि logs of wood, यथा like, औषधम् medicine, गदान् diseases, इति thus, प्रभो O Lord, त्वत्-पुरुषाः Your attendants, बभाषिरे spoke

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Your attendants declared: “The chanting of Lord Mukunda's name destroys all accumulated sins even when uttered unconsciously or without knowing its full glory. Such is the supreme power of His name—just as fire burns fuel regardless of how it touches it, and just as medicine cures a disease even if swallowed without knowing its properties!”

इतीरितैर्याम्यभटैरपासृते
भवद्भटानां च गणे तिरोहिते ।
भवत्स्मृतिं कंचन कालमाचरन्
भवत्पदं प्रापि भवद्भटैरसौ ॥22.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति इरितैः having been addressed in this way, याम्य-भटैः by the messengers of Yamaraja, अपासृते having departed, भवत्-भटानाम् of Your attendants, च and, गणे the group, तिरोहिते having disappeared, भवत्-स्मृतिम् remembrance of You, कंचन-कालम् for some time, आचरन् practicing, भवत्-पदम् Your supreme abode, प्रापि reached, भवत्-भटैः by Your attendants, असौ he

Simple Meaning: Having been addressed in this manner by Your attendants, the messengers of Yama departed, and Your attendants vanished from sight. Realizing the value of devotion, Ajamila spent his remaining days absorbed in constant divine remembrance of You. At the end of his life, he was escorted to Your supreme eternal abode by Your messengers!

स्वकिङ्करावेदनशङ्कितो यमः
त्वदंघ्रिभक्तेषु न गम्यतामिति ।
स्वकीयभृत्यानशिशिक्षदुच्चकैः
स देव वातालयनाथ पाहि माम् ॥22.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्व-किङ्कर-आवेदन-शङ्कितः alarmed by the report of his own messengers, यमः Yama, त्वत्-अंघ्रि-भक्तेषु towards the devotees of Your lotus feet, न do not, गम्यताम् go, इति thus, स्वकीय-भृत्यान् his own servants, अशिशिक्षत् instructed, उच्चकैः strictly, सः that, देव O Supreme Lord, वात-आलय-नाथ O Lord of the abode of the Wind - Guruvayoor, पाहि protect, माम् me

Simple Meaning: Alarmed by the report brought by his messengers after their encounter with Your attendants, Yamaraja strictly instructed his servants never to go anywhere near the devotees of Your lotus feet. O Lord of Guruvayoor, who is worshipped by Yama himself, please protect me!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
