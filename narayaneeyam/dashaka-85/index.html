Destruction of Jarasandha. Krishna kills Shishupala during the Rajasuya sacrifice. Duryodhana humiliated

॥ॐ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 85

Destruction of Jarasandha

Krishna kills Shishupala during the Rajasuya sacrifice

Duryodhana humiliated

ततो मगधभूभृता चिरनिरोधसंक्लेशितं
शताष्टकयुतायुतद्वितयमीश भूमीभृताम् ।
अनाथशरणाय ते कमपि पूरुषं प्राहिणोत्
अयाचत स मागधक्षपणमेव किं भूयसा ॥85.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः thereafter, मगध-भूभृता by the king of Magadha (Jarasandha), चिर-निरोध-संक्लेशितम् afflicted and distressed by long imprisonment, शत-अष्टक-युत-अयुत-द्वितयम् hundred times eight plus ten thousand times two (twenty thousand and eight hundred - 20,800), ईश O Supreme Lord, भूमीभृताम् of kings, अनाथ-शरणाय to You who are the refuge of the helpless, ते to You, कमपि पूरुषम् a certain messenger, प्राहिणोत् sent, अयाचत he requested, सः he (the messenger), मागध-क्षपणम् एव the destruction of the ruler of Magadha - Jarasandha, किम् भूयसा what more need be said

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, Jarasandha, the king of Magadha, had imprisoned twenty thousand eight hundred kings in his fortress, leaving them deeply distressed by long captivity. Knowing You to be the sole refuge of the helpless, those imprisoned kings dispatched a messenger to Dwaraka. The messenger approached You and pleaded for the destruction of Jarasandha and the liberation of the royal captives. What more can be said!

यियासुरभिमागधं तदनु नारदोदीरितात्
युधिष्ठिरमखोद्यमादुभयकार्यपर्याकुलः ।
विरुद्धजयिनोऽध्वरादुभयसिद्धिरित्युद्धवे
शशंसुषि निजैः समं पुरमियेथ यौधिष्ठिरीम् ॥85.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: यियासुः wishing to set out, अभि-मागधम् against the king of Magadha - Jarasandha, तदनु immediately after that, नारद-उदीरितात् brought by Sage Narada, युधिष्ठिर-मख-उद्यमात् regarding King Yudhishthira's initiative to perform the Rajasuya sacrifice, उभय-कार्य-पर्याकुलः caught in a dilemma between the two tasks, विरुद्ध-जयिनः of one who overcomes all opposition, अध्वरात् through the Rajasuya sacrifice itself, उभय-सिद्धिः both objectives will be accomplished, इति thus, उद्धवे when Uddhava, शशंसुषि advised, निजैः समम् accompanied by Your clan and followers, पुरम् इयेथ You traveled to the city, यौधिष्ठिरीम् of Yudhishthira (Indraprastha)

Simple Meaning: You, who were desiring to march against Jarasandha, were informed by Sage Narada about King Yudhishthira's plans to perform the Rajasuya sacrifice. Facing a dilemma over which task to address first, You consulted Uddhava. He advised that by supporting Yudhishthira's sacrifice, both goals would be fulfilled, as all rival kings—including Jarasandha—would have to be subdued for the Rajasuya to succeed. Accepting his wise counsel, You set out for Indraprastha accompanied by Your retinue.

अशेषदयितायुते त्वयि समागते धर्मजो
विजित्य सहजैर्महीं भवदपाङ्गसंवर्धितैः ।
श्रियं निरुपमां वहन्नहह भक्तदासायितं
भवन्तमयि मागधे प्रहितवान् सभीमार्जुनम् ॥85.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: अशेष-दयिता-युते accompanied by all Your beloved queens, त्वयि when You, समागते had arrived in Indraprastha, धर्मजः Yudhishthira (the son of Dharma), विजित्य having conquered, सहजैः by his brothers (Bhima, Arjuna, Nakula, Sahadeva), महीम् the whole earth, भवत्-अपाङ्ग-संवर्धितैः who were empowered and prospered by Your gracious sidelong glances, श्रियम् unmatched prosperity and imperial sovereignty, निरुपमाम् peerless, वहन् possessing, अहह oh marvel, भक्त-दासायितम् acting as the humble servant of Your devotees, भवन्तम् You, अयि O Lord, मागधे against Jarasandha (the ruler of Magadha), प्रहितवान् dispatched, स-भीम-अर्जुनम् along with Bhima and Arjuna

Simple Meaning: When You arrived in Indraprastha accompanied by all Your queens, King Yudhishthira dispatched his brothers, who were continually empowered by Your merciful glances, to conquer the world in all directions. Achieving peerless royal fortune and imperial sovereignty through the successful conquest of the earth, Yudhishthira was ready for the Rajasuya sacrifice. O Lord, acting out of supreme grace as the humble servant of Your devotees, You yourself accompanied Bhima and Arjuna to Magadha to challenge and eliminate Jarasandha.

गिरिव्रजपुरं गतास्तदनु देव यूयं त्रयो
ययाच समरोत्सवं द्विजमिषेण तं मागधम् ।
अपूर्णसुकृतं त्वमुं पवनजेन संग्रामयन्
निरीक्ष्य सह जिष्णुना त्वमपि राजयुद्ध्वा स्थितः ॥85.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: गिरिव्रज-पुरम् to the city of Girivraja, गताः having gone, तदनु after that, देव O Lord, यूयम् You, त्रयः the three of You (You, Bhima, and Arjuna), ययाच requested, समर-उत्सवम् the duel / festival of single combat, द्विज-मिषेण under the guise of brahmins, तम् मागधम् from that king of Magadha (Jarasandha), अपूर्ण-सुकृतम् whose was short of merits, तु अमुम् but him, पवनजेन with Bhima (the son of the Wind God), संग्रामयन् causing to engage in combat, निरीक्ष्य watching, सह-जिष्णुना along with Arjuna (Jishnu), त्वम् अपि You too, राज-युद्ध्वा causing the kings to fight, स्थितः stood by

Simple Meaning: Reaching the city of Girivraja disguised as holy brahmins, You, Bhima, and Arjuna approached Jarasandha and asked him for a duel in single combat. Since Jarasandha's past merit had run out, he accepted the challenge and chose Bhima as his opponent. Causing Jarasandha to engage in a fierce fight with Bhima, You stood as a spectator watching the royal battle alongside Arjuna.

अशान्तसमरोद्धतं विटपपाटनासंज्ञया
निपात्य जरसस्सुतं पवनजेन निष्पाटितम् ।
विमुच्य नृपतीन् मुदा समनुगृह्य भक्तिं परां
दिदेशिथ गतस्पृहानपि च धर्मगुप्त्यै भुवः ॥85.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: अशान्त-समर-उद्धतम् who was fiercely arrogant and relentless in the unceasing duel, विटप-पाटना-संज्ञया by the sign of splitting a tree twig in two, निपात्य having brought down, जरसः सुतम् the son of Jara - Jarasandha, पवनजेन by the son of Wind - Bhima, निष्पाटितम् tore apart into two halves, विमुच्य liberating, नृपतीन् all the imprisoned kings, मुदा joyfully, समनुगृह्य bestowing Your grace upon them, भक्तिम् पराम् supreme devotion to You, दिदेशिथ You imparted/blessed, गत-स्पृहान् अपि even though they were freed from worldly desires, च and, धर्म-गुप्त्यै for the protection of righteousness (dharma), भुवः of the earth

Simple Meaning: When the fierce duel between Bhima and Jarasandha showed no sign of ending, You picked up a twig, split it down the middle, and threw the two halves in opposite directions. Understanding Your silent signal, Bhima threw Jarasandha to the ground and tore his body apart into two pieces. You then joyfully liberated the twenty thousand eight hundred captive kings, blessed them with supreme devotion, and instructed them to rule their realms righteously for the protection of dharma, even though they had become completely detached from worldly desires through their suffering.

प्रचक्रुषि युधिष्ठिरे तदनु राजसूयाध्वरं
प्रसन्नभृतकीभवत्सकलराजकव्याकुलम् ।
त्वमप्ययि जगत्पते द्विजपदावनेजादिकं
चकर्थ किमु कथ्यते नृपवरस्य भाग्योन्नतिः ॥85.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रचक्रुषि when he undertook and commenced, युधिष्ठिरे King Yudhishthira, तदनु subsequently, राजसूय-अध्वरम् the great Rajasuya sacrifice, प्रसन्न-भृतकी-भवत्-सकल-राजक-व्याकुलम् thronged with countless kings who joyfully acted as willing humble servants, त्वम् अपि You too, अयि O Lord, जगत्पते Master of the Universe, द्विज-पद-अवनेज-आदिकम् tasks such as washing the feet of noble brahmins and revered guests, चकर्थ performed, किमु कथ्यते how can it be described, नृप-वरस्य of that eminent king Yudhishthira, भाग्य-उन्नतिः the supreme height of good fortune

Simple Meaning: Subsequently, when King Yudhishthira commenced the grand Rajasuya sacrifice, the venue was filled with kings from across the world who joyfully served as humble attendants. O Lord and Master of the Universe, You Yourself personally took up the task of washing the feet of visiting brahmins and revered guests. How can the sublime good fortune of King Yudhishthira ever be fully described?

ततः सवनकर्मणि प्रवरमग्र्यपूजाविधिं
विचार्य सहदेववागनुगतः स धर्मात्मजः ।
व्यधत्त भवते मुदा सदसि विश्वभूतात्मने
तदा ससुरमानुषं भुवनमेव तृप्तिं दधौ ॥85.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, सवन-कर्मणि in the sacrificial proceedings, प्रवरम् the supreme, अग्र्य-पूजा-विधिम् the rite of offering the primary worship (agra-puja), विचार्य considering, सहदेव-वाक्-अनुगतः following the advice and statement of Sahadeva, सः he, धर्म-आत्मजः the son of Dharma - Yudhishthira, व्यधत्त offered, भवते to You, मुदा with great joy, सदसि in that assembly, विश्व-भूत-आत्मने to You who are the indwelling soul of all created beings, तदा then, स-सुर-मानुषम् along with devas and human beings, भुवनम् एव the entire universe itself, तृप्तिम् satisfaction and supreme contentment, दधौ attained

Simple Meaning: When the time came to determine who should receive the highest honor and primary worship (agra-puja) during the sacrificial rites, King Yudhishthira followed the wise counsel of Sahadeva and joyfully offered that supreme worship to You, the inner soul of all living beings. Upon You receiving that worship, the entire universe—including all devas, mortals, and living creatures—felt completely satisfied and filled with supreme joy.

ततः सपदि चेदिपो मुनिनृपेषु तिष्ठत्स्वहो
सभाजयति को जडः पशुपदुर्दुरूटं वटुम् ।
इति त्वयि स दुर्वचोविततिमुद्वमन्नासनात्
उदापतदुदायुधः समपतन्नमुं पाण्डवाः ॥85.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, सपदि immediately, चेदिपः the king of Chedi - Shishupala, मुनि-नृपेषु तिष्ठत्सु while great sages and exalted kings were present, अहो alas, सभाजयति honors, कः which, जडः fool, पशुप-दुर्दुरूटम् an uncultured cowherd, वटुम् youth, इति thus, त्वयि regarding You, सः he (Shishupala), दुर्वचः-विततिम् a stream of abusive words, उद्वमन् uttering/vomiting, आसनात् from his seat, उदापतत् jumped up, उदायुधः with weapon raised, समपतन् rushed against, अमुम् him, पाण्डवाः the Pandavas

Simple Meaning: Upon seeing the highest honor bestowed upon You, Shishupala, the king of Chedi, jumped up from his seat with his weapon drawn. He began hurling a torrent of insults at You, shouting in outrage, "Which fool honors this uncultured cowherd youth when so many great sages and kings are present?" Seeing his insolence, the enraged Pandavas immediately rushed to attack him.

निवार्य निजपक्षगानभिमुखस्यविद्वेषिणः
त्वमेव जहृषे शिरो दनुजदारिणा स्वारिणा ।
जनुस्त्रितयलब्धया सततचिन्तया शुद्धधीः
त्वया स परमेकतामधृत योगिनां दुर्लभाम् ॥85.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: निवार्य restraining, निज-पक्षगान् अभिमुखस्य Your own enraged partisans who were advancing against him, विद्वेषिणः of the adversary (Shishupala), त्वम् एव You Yourself, जहृषे severed, शिरः the head, दनुज-दारिणा by that which cuts down danava forces, स्व-अरिणा by Your own Sudarshana Chakra, जनुः-त्रितय-लब्धया accumulated across three successive lifetimes (as Hiranyakashipu, Ravana, and Shishupala), सतत-चिन्तया by intense and continuous contemplation, शुद्ध-धीः whose mind had become completely purified, त्वया with You, सः he (Shishupala), परम् एकताम् अधृत attained supreme oneness, योगिनाम् दुर्लभाम् which is extremely difficult even for great yogis to achieve

Simple Meaning: Restraining Your allies, who were enraged and rushed to attack, You personally severed the head of Your adversary Shishupala using Your Sudarshana Chakra - which cuts down the danava forces. Because his mind had become thoroughly purified through constant, intense contemplation of You across three continuous births as Your enemy, his soul immediately merged into You, attaining that supreme state of liberation which is rare even for exalted yogis to reach.

ततः सुमहिते त्वया क्रतुवरे निरूढे जनो
ययौ जयति धर्मजो जयति कृष्ण इत्यालपन्।
खलः स तु सुयोधनो धुतमनास्सपत्नश्रिया
मयार्पितसभामुखे स्थलजलभ्रमादभ्रमीत् ॥85.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, सुमहिते when that glorious and highly honored, त्वया by You, क्रतु-वरे supreme sacrificial rite (Rajasuya), निरूढे was successfully concluded, जनः all the assembled people, ययौ departed, जयति धर्मजः victory to King Yudhishthira, जयति कृष्णः victory to Krishna, इति आलपन् thus joyfully proclaiming, खलः wicked, सः he, तु but, सुयोधनः Suyodhana (Duryodhana), धुत-मनाः with a mind deeply agitated and envious, सपत्न-श्रिया at the splendour and wealth of his rivals (the Pandavas), मय-अर्पित-सभा-मुखे in the royal assembly hall built and gifted by Maya (Mayasabha), स्थल-जल-भ्रमात् due to confusion between dry land and water, अभ्रमीत् became bewildered and fell into delusion

Simple Meaning: Upon the successful conclusion of that magnificent sacrifice under Your direct guidance, the assembled crowds departed for their homes, joyfully chanting "Victory to King Yudhishthira! Victory to Krishna!" However, the wicked Duryodhana remained behind, his heart consumed with bitter envy at the extraordinary prosperity of the Pandavas. While walking through the magnificent Mayasabha created by the architect Maya, he became utterly confused between solid floor and water, stepping into water mistaking it for dry land and suffering humiliation.

तदा हसितमुत्थितं द्रुपदनन्दनाभीमयोः
अपाङ्गकलया विभो किमपि तावदुज्जृम्भयन् ।
धराभरनिराकृतौ सपदि नाम बीजं वपन्
जनार्दन मरुत्पुरीनिलय पाहि मामामयात् ॥85.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदा then, हसितम् उत्थितम् the laughter that arose, द्रुपदनन्दना-भीमयोः from the daughter of Drupada (Draupadi) and Bhima, अपाङ्ग-कलया by a subtle glance from the corner of Your eyes, विभो O All-pervading Lord, किमपि mysteriously/subtly, तावत् thereupon, उज्जृम्भयन् encouraging and amplifying, धरा-भर-निराकृतौ for the removal of the burden of the Earth, सपदि immediately, नाम indeed, बीजम् the seed, वपन् sowing, जनार्दन O Janardana (tormentor of wicked people), मरुत्पुरी-निलय O Lord residing in Guruvayur, पाहि protect, माम् me, आमयात् from my diseases and suffering

Simple Meaning: When Duryodhana fell in the Mayasabha, Draupadi and Bhima burst into laughter. O All-pervading Lord, with a subtle glance from the corner of Your eyes, You subtly encouraged and fueled that mocking laughter, thereby immediately sowing the seed for the great Mahabharata war that would ultimately relieve the Earth of her heavy burden of evil forces. O Lord Janardana, who dwell in Guruvayur, please protect me from all my afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
