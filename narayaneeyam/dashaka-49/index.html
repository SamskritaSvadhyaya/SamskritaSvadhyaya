Going to Vrindavana

दशकम् ४९ · Move to Vrindavana and a description of the beauty of the place

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 49

Move to Vrindavana and a description of the beauty of the place

भवत्प्रभावाविदुरा हि गोपास्तरुप्रपातादिकमत्र गोष्ठे ।
अहेतुमुत्पातगणं विशङ्क्य प्रयातुमन्यत्र मनो वितेनुः ॥49.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: भवत्-प्रभाव-अविदुराः unaware of Your divine glory, हि indeed, गोपाः the cowherds, तरू-प्रपात-आदिकम् the crashing of trees and other events, अत्र here, गोष्ठे in Vraja/Gokula, अहेतुम् unexplained, उत्पात-गणम् series of ill omens, विशङ्क्य suspecting, प्रयातुम् to depart, अन्यत्र elsewhere, मनः वितेनुः turned their minds (decided)

Simple Meaning: Unaware of Your supreme divine nature, the cowherds considered the falling of the twin trees and other strange incidents as a series of ominous, unexplainable portents in Gokula. Fearing further danger, they decided to move away to another place.

तत्रोपनन्दाभिधगोपवर्यो जगौ भवत्प्रेरणयैव नूनम् ।
इतः प्रतीच्यां विपिनं मनोज्ञं वृन्दावनं नाम विराजतीति ॥49.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there, उपनन्द-अभिध-गोप-वर्यः the elder cowherd named Upananda, जगौ spoke, भवत्-प्रेरणया एव prompted by You alone, नूनम् surely, इतः from here, प्रतीच्याम् to the west, विपिनम् a forest, मनोज्ञम् beautiful, वृन्दावनम् नाम named Vrindavana, विराजति exists, इति thus

Simple Meaning: Surely prompted by Your divine inner guidance, the respected elder cowherd Upananda then addressed the gathering: "West of here lies a beautiful forest called Vrindavana."

बृहद्वनं तत् खलु नन्दमुख्या विधाय गौष्ठीनमथ क्षणेन ।
त्वदन्वितत्वज्जननीनिविष्टगरिष्ठयानानुगता विचेलुः ॥49.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: बृहद्वनम् Brihadvana (the vast forest area of Gokula), तत् that, खलु indeed, नन्द-मुख्याः Nanda and other chief cowherds, विधाय making, गौष्ठीनम् a deserted/abandoned cowpen, अथ then, क्षणेन in a moment, त्वत्-अन्वित-त्वत्-जननी-निविष्ट-गरिष्ठ-यान-अनुगताः following the grand cart carrying You along with Your mother Yashoda, विचेलुः set out/departed

Simple Meaning: Leaving behind their home in Brihadvana (Gokula) abandoned in an instant, Nanda and the other cowherds set out on their journey, following the grand cart carrying You along with Your mother Yashoda.

अनोमनोज्ञध्वनिधेनुपालीखुरप्रणादान्तरतो वधूभिः ।
भवद्विनोदालपिताक्षराणि प्रपीय नाज्ञायत मार्गदैर्घ्यम् ॥49.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अनः-मनोज्ञ-ध्वनि-धेनु-पाली-खुर-प्रणाद-अन्तरतः amidst the pleasant creaking sound of the bullock carts and the clatter of the hooves of the herds of cows, वधूभिः by the cowherd women (Gopis), भवत्-विनोद-आलपित-अक्षराणि Your charming baby talk and sweet words spoken in play, प्रपीय drinking in (listening intently), न अज्ञायत was not felt, मार्ग-दैर्घ्यम् the length/distance of the path

Simple Meaning: Amidst the pleasing hum of the moving carts and the rhythmic clattering of the cow herds' hooves, the Gopis engrossed in listening to Your sweet, playful baby talk, did not feel the tedious length of the journey at all!

निरीक्ष्य वृन्दावनमीश नन्दत्प्रसूनकुन्दप्रमुखद्रुमौघम् ।
अमोदथाः शाद्वलसान्द्रलक्ष्म्या हरिन्मणीकुट्टिमपुष्टशोभम् ॥49.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: निरीक्ष्य beholding, वृन्दावनम् Vrindavana, ईश O Lord, नन्दत्-प्रसून-कुन्द-प्रमुख-द्रुम-ओघम् rich with blossoming jasmine and other splendid trees, अमोदथाः You rejoiced, शाद्वल-सान्द्र-लक्ष्म्या by the lush green beauty of the grassy pastures, हरित्-मणी-कुट्टिम-पुष्ट-शोभम् possessing an enriched splendor like a floor inlaid with emeralds

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Upon beholding Vrindavana—flourishing with blooming jasmine and a variety of magnificent trees, and gleaming with dense, lush grass that mirrored a floor paved with emeralds—You were delighted!

नवाकनिर्व्यूढनिवासभेदेष्वशेषगोपेषु सुखासितेषु ।
वनश्रियं गोपकिशोरपालीविमिश्रितः पर्यगलोकथास्त्वम् ॥49.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: नवाक-निर्व्यूढ-निवास-भेदेषु in the various newly erected shelters arranged in the form of a half moon (semi circle), अशेष-गोपेषु all the cowherds, सुख-आसितेषु comfortably settled, वन-श्रियम् the natural beauty of the forest, गोप-किशोर-पाली-विमिश्रितः accompanied by a band of young cowherd boys, पर्यक्-अलोकथाः You roamed everywhere observing, त्वम् You

Simple Meaning: When all the cowherds had comfortably settled down in their newly constructed shelters made of fresh foliage, You roamed around exploring the exquisite beauty of the forest in the lively company of the young cowherd boys!

अरालमार्गागतनिर्मलापां मरालकूजाकृतनर्मलापाम् ।
निरन्तरस्मेरसरोजवक्त्रां कलिन्दकन्यां समलोकयस्त्वम् ॥49.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: अराल-मार्ग-आगत-निर्मल-अपाम् with pure waters flowing through a winding/crooked course, मराल-कूज-आकृत-नर्म-लापाम् echoing with the sweet cooing of swans sounding like pleasant casual talk, निरन्तर-स्मेर-सरोज-वक्त्राम् having full-blown lotus flowers as her smiling face, कलिन्द-कन्याम् Yamuna (also known as Kalindi - the daughter of Kalinda), समलोकयः You beheld, त्वम् You

Simple Meaning: You gazed upon the river Kalindi (Yamuna) - the daughter of Kalinda, whose pure waters flowed in a winding course, whose bank resonated with the gentle warbling of swans that sounded like playful banter, and who had full-blown lotus flowers as her smiling face!

मयूरकेकाशतलोभनीयं मयूखमालाशबलं मणीनाम् ।
विरिञ्चलोकस्पृशमुच्चशृङ्गैर्गिरिं च गोवर्धनमैक्षथास्त्वम् ॥49.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: मयूर-केका-शत-लोभनीयम् enchanting with hundreds of calls of peacocks, मयूख-माला-शबलम् variegated with clusters of rays, मणीनाम् of precious gems, विरिञ्च-लोक-स्पृशम् reaching up to the abode of Brahma (Satyaloka), उच्च-शृङ्गैः with its lofty peaks, गिरम् च गोवर्धनम् and the Govardhana hill, ऐक्षथाः You saw, त्वम् You

Simple Meaning: You also beheld the Govardhana hill—enchanting with the calls of hundreds of peacocks, radiant with colorful rays reflecting from embedded gems, and raising its towering peaks as if to touch the realm of Lord Brahma!

समं ततो गोपकुमारकैस्त्वं समन्ततो यत्र वनान्तमागाः ।
ततस्ततस्तां कुटिलामपश्यः कलिन्दजां रागवतीमिवैकाम् ॥49.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: समम् together with, ततः then, गोप-कुमारकैः with the young cowherd boys, त्वम् You, समन्ततः everywhere, यत्र wherever, वन-अन्तम् into the interior of the forest, आगाः went, ततः ततः in all those places, ताम् her (the river Yamuna), कुटिलाम् winding/crooked (also playfully clever), अपश्यः You saw, कलिन्दजाम् the river Yamuna (daughter of Kalinda), रागवतीम् इव like a deeply affectionate/loving woman, एकाम् alone/solitude

Simple Meaning: Then, as You wandered into the depths of the forest in every direction together with the young cowherd boys, You saw the river Yamuna appearing at every turn just like a lover following You alone wherever You went!

तथाविधेऽस्मिन् विपिने पशव्ये समुत्सुको वत्सगणप्रचारे ।
चरन् सरामोऽथ कुमारकैस्त्वं समीरगेहाधिप पाहि रोगात् ॥49.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: तथा-विधे अस्मिन् in such a, विपिने forest, पशव्ये suitable for cattle, समुत्सुकः eager, वत्स-गण-प्रचारे in tending/grazing the herds of calves, चरन् wandering around, सरामः along with Balarama, अथ then, कुमारकैः with the young cowherd boys, त्वम् You, समीरगेहाधिप O Lord of Guruvayur (Samiira-geha-adhipa), पाहि protect, रोगात् from illness/disease

Simple Meaning: In that glorious forest of Vrindavana, which was so perfectly suited for cattle, You became eager to tend the calves. Wandering about along with Balarama and the other young cowherd boys, You enjoyed grazing the herds. O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect me from all diseases!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
