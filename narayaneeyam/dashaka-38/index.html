Dashaka 38

Birth of Krishna

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 38

Birth of Krishna

आनन्दरूप भगवन्नयि तेऽवतारे प्राप्ते प्रदीप्तभवदङ्गनिरीयमाणैः ।
कान्तिव्रजैरिव घनाघनमण्डलैर्द्याम् आवृण्वती विरुरुचे किल वर्षवेला ॥38.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: आनन्द-रूप O Embodiment of Bliss, भगवन् O Supreme Lord, अयि O Lord, ते Your, अवतारे when the advent/incarnation, प्राप्ते was approaching, प्रदीप्त-भवत्-अङ्ग-निरीयमाणैः emerging from Your brilliantly glowing form, कान्ति-व्रजैः इव like rays of effulgence, घनाघन-मण्डलैः with dense dark rain-clouds, द्याम् the sky, आवृण्वती enveloping, विरुरुचे shone resplendently, किल indeed, वर्ष-वेला the rainy season

Simple Meaning: O Lord, the very embodiment of supreme bliss! As the auspicious time of Your divine incarnation drew near, the rainy season arrived in all its splendor. The sky became completely enveloped with dense, dark rain-clouds, appearing as though the intense, radiant effulgence emanating from Your divine body had taken form to cover the skies.

आशासु शीतलतरासु पयोदतोयैः आशासिताप्तिविवशेषु च सज्जनेषु ।
नैशाकरोदयविधौ निशि मध्यमायां क्लेशापहस्त्रिजगतां त्वमिहाविरासीः ॥38.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: आशासु in all directions, शीतलतरासु rendered exceedingly cool, पयोद-तोयैः by the waters of the rain-clouds, आशासित-आपत्ति-विवशेषु filled with intense longing for the fulfillment of their hopes, च and, सत्-जनेषु when the virtuous devotees (were expectant), नैशाकर-उदय-विधौ at the time of the moonrise, निशि in the night, मध्यमायाम् at midnight, क्लेश-अपहः O Destroyer of all afflictions, त्रि-जगताम् of the three worlds, त्वम् You, इह here on earth, आविरासीः manifested/appeared

Simple Meaning: With all the directions cooled by refreshing rains and the hearts of the virtuous filled with joyful anticipation for the fulfillment of their long-cherished hopes, You—the remover of all suffering across the three worlds—manifested Your divine self upon this earth at midnight, right at the auspicious moment of the moonrise.

बाल्यस्पृशाऽपि वपुषा दधुषा विभूतीः उद्यत्किरीटकटकाङ्गदहारभासा ।
शङ्खारिवारिजगदापरिभासितेन मेघासितेन परिलेसिथ सूतिगेहे ॥38.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: बाल्य-स्पृशा अपि even though that of an infant, वपुषा with a physical form, दधुषा possessing/displaying, विभूतीः divine powers and opulence, उद्यत्-किरीट-कटक-अङ्गद-हार-भासा radiant with brilliant crown, bracelets, armlets, and necklaces, शङ्ख-अरि-वारिज-गदा-परिभासितेन resplendent with the conch shell, discus (Sudarsana Chakra), lotus flower, and mace, मेघ-असितेन which was dark like a rain-cloud, परिलेसिथ You shone forth, सूति-गेहे in the lying-in chamber (of Devaki)

Simple Meaning: Even with a physical form of an infant, You displayed magnificent divine opulence, radiating brilliant light from Your crown, bracelets, armlets, and necklaces. Shining resplendently with the conch shell, discus, lotus, and mace held in Your hands, and possessing a captivating dark complexion like that of a rain-cloud, You shone forth brilliantly within the birth chamber.

वक्षःस्थलीसुखनिलीनविलासिलक्ष्मीमन्दाक्षलक्षितकटाक्षविमोक्षभेदैः ।
तन्मन्दिरस्य खलकंसकृतामलक्ष्मीम् उन्मार्जयन्निव विरेजिथ वासुदेव ॥38.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: वक्षःस्थली-सुख-निलीन-विलासि-लक्ष्मी-मन्द-अक्ष-लक्षित-कटाक्ष-विमोक्ष-भेदैः by the subtle expressions of side-glances released with bashful shyness by Lakshmi who was comfortably nestled on Your chest, तत्-मन्दिरस्य of that prison cell, खल-कंस-कृत-अलक्ष्मीम् the inauspiciousness caused by the wicked Kamsa, उन्मार्जयन् इव as if wiping away, विरेजिथ You shone resplendently, वासुदेव O Lord Vasudeva

Simple Meaning: Goddess Lakshmi, residing comfortably and blissfully upon Your chest, cast shy and gentle glances around the room. By those auspicious, playful side-glances, You shone resplendently in Devaki's delivery chamber, as if wiping away all the gloom and inauspiciousness brought upon that place by the evil Kamsa, O Lord Vasudeva!

शौरिस्तु धीरमुनिमण्डलचेतसोऽपि दूरस्थितं वपुरुदीक्ष्य निजेक्षणाभ्याम् ॥

आनन्दबाष्पपुलकोद्गमगद्गदार्द्रः तुष्टाव दृष्टिमकरन्दरसं भवन्तम् ॥38.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: शौरिः तु Vasudeva on the other hand, धीर-मुनि-मण्डल-चेतसः अपि even from the minds of wise sages, दूर-स्थितम् situated far beyond reach, वपुः divine form, उदीक्ष्य beholding, निज-ईक्षणाभ्याम् with his own eyes, आनन्द-बाष्प-पुलक-उद्गम-गद्गद-आर्द्रः overwhelmed with tears of joy, goosebumps, and a choked, emotional voice, तुष्टाव praised/offered prayers to, दृष्टि-मकरन्द-रसम् You who are sweet like nectar to the bee of the eyes, भवन्तम् You

Simple Meaning: Vasudeva, beholding with his own eyes Your divine form—which remains beyond the reach of even the minds of wise and steady sages—was overwhelmed with ecstasy. Tears of joy welled in his eyes, his hair stood on end, and his voice choked with emotion as he offered heartfelt prayers to You, who are like pure nectar to the eyes.

देव प्रसीद परपूरुष तापवल्लीनिर्लूनदात्रसमनेत्रकलाविलासिन् ।
खेदानपाकुरु कृपागुरुभिः कटाक्षैः इत्यादि तेन मुदितेन चिरं नुतोऽभूः ॥38.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: देव O Lord, प्रसीद be pleased/show grace, पर-पूरुष O Supreme Being, ताप-वल्ली-निर्लून-दात्र-सम-नेत्र-कला-विलासिन् O You who shine with playful eyes like a sharp sickle that cuts down the creeper of worldly afflictions, खेदान् all our sufferings, अपाकुरु remove, कृपा-गुरुभिः with glance exceedingly heavy with mercy, कटाक्षैः with Your side-glances, इति-आदि thus and in other ways, तेन by him (Vasudeva), मुदितेन by the overjoyed one, चिरम् for a long time, नुतः praised, अभूः You were

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Be pleased with us, O Supreme Being! You whose playful glance acts like a sharp sickle to instantly cut down the tangled vine of all worldly afflictions, please remove our sufferings with Your side-glances overflowing with infinite mercy! In this manner, Vasudeva, filled with supreme joy, praised You for a long time.

मात्रा च नेत्रसलिलास्तृतगात्रवल्यास्तोत्रैरभिष्टुतगुणः करुणालयस्त्वम् ।
प्राचीनजन्मयुगलं प्रतिबोध्य ताभ्यां मातुर्गिरा दधिथ मानुषबालवेषम् ॥38.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: मात्रा by Your mother (Devaki), च and, नेत्र-सलिल-आस्तृत-गात्र-वल्या whose slender body was bathed in tears from her eyes, स्तोत्रैः with hymns of praise, अभिष्टुत-गुणः whose divine attributes were praised, करुण-आलयः abode of Compassion, त्वम् You, प्राचीन-जन्म-युगलम् two previous births, प्रतिबोध्य reminding, ताभ्याम् to both of them, मातुः of Your mother, गिरा by the request/words, दधिथ assumed, मानुष-बाल-वेषम् the form of a human infant

Simple Meaning: Your mother Devaki, her slender form bathed in tears of divine joy flowing from her eyes, offered prayers praising Your transcendent attributes. You, who are the abode of compassion, reminded both Vasudeva and Devaki of their two previous births (as Sutapa-Prisni and Kasyapa-Aditi). Complying to Your mother's gentle plea, You assumed the sweet form of a normal human infant.

त्वत्प्रेरितस्तदनु नन्दतनूजया ते व्यत्यासमारचयितुं स हि शूरसूनुः ।
त्वां हस्तयोरधृत चित्तविधार्यमार्यैः अम्भोरुहस्थकलहंसकिशोररम्यम् ॥38.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-प्रेरितः prompted by You, तदनु thereafter, नन्द-तनूजया with the newborn daughter of Nanda, ते Your, व्यत्यासम् swap/exchange, आरचयितुम् to perform, सः he, हि indeed, शूर-सूनुः the son of Shura (Vasudeva), त्वाम् You, हस्तयोः in his hands, अधृत took/held, चित्त-विधार्यम् contemplated in the heart, आर्यैः by noble sages, अम्भोरुह-स्थ-कलहंस-किशोर-रम्यम् as delightful as a young cygnet resting on a lotus flower

Simple Meaning: Prompted by You, Vasudeva, the son of Shura, to carry out the exchange of You with the newborn infant daughter of Nanda, lovingly took You into his arms. You, who can be only meditated upon in the hearts of noble sages, who looked as enchantingly beautiful as a delicate young cygnet resting upon a lotus blossom in Vasudeva’s arms.

जाता तदा पशुपसद्मनि योगनिद्रा निद्राविमुद्रितमथाकृत पौरलोकम् ।
त्वत्प्रेरणात् किमिव चित्रमचेतनैर्यद्द्वारैः स्वयं व्यघटि सङ्घटितैः सुगाढम् ॥38.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: जाता born, तदा at that time, पशुप-सद्मनि in the house of the cowherd chief (Nanda), योगनिद्रा Yogamaya, निद्रा-विमुद्रितम् deeply asleep, अथ then, अकृत rendered, पौर-लोकम् the citizens/guards of the city, त्वत्-प्रेरणात् by Your command, किम् इव What indeed, चित्रम् wonder/surprise, अ-चेतनैः by inanimate, यत् that, द्वारैः by the gates, स्वयम् automatically/by themselves, व्यघटि opened wide, सङ्घटितैः were locked/bolted, सुगाढम् very firmly

Simple Meaning: By Your divine command, at that very time, Yogamaya was born in the house of Nanda. She cast a deep hypnotic sleep over the entire city of Mathura and its palace guards. What wonder is there in this: even the heavy, inanimate gates that were so firmly locked and bolted opened wide of their own accord!

शेषेण भूरिफणवारितवारिणाऽथ स्वैरं प्रदर्शितपथो मणिदीपितेन ।
त्वां धारयन् स खलु धन्यतमः प्रतस्थे सोऽयं त्वमीश मम नाशय रोगवेगान् ॥38.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: शेषेण by Lord Shesha, भूरि-फण-वारित-वारिणा shielding the pouring rain with his many expanded hoods, अथ then, स्वैरम् freely, प्रदर्शित-पथः having the path illuminated, मणि-दीपितेन by the radiant gems on his hoods, त्वाम् You, धारयन् carrying, सः he (Vasudeva), खलु indeed, धन्यतमः the most fortunate, प्रतस्थे set forth, सः अयम् त्वम् You as such, ईश O Lord, मम my, नाशय destroy, रोग-वेगान् the intensity/force of diseases

Simple Meaning: Then, carrying You, the most fortunate Vasudeva set forth into the night. Lord Shesha accompanied him, shielding You from the pouring rain with his vast array of hoods while illuminating the path with the brilliant glow of the gems on his heads. O Lord of such great powers, please destroy the force of all my afflictions and diseases!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
