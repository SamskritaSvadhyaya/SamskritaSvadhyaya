The Cosmic Evolution

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 5

The Cosmic Evolution

(छन्दस् - शार्दूलविक्रीडितम्)

व्यक्ताव्यक्तमिदं न किञ्चिदभवत्प्राक्प्राकृतप्रक्षये
मायायाम् गुणसाम्यरुद्धविकृतौ त्वय्यागतायां लयम् ।
नो मृत्युश्च तदाऽमृतं च समभून्नाह्नो न रात्रेः स्थितिः
तत्रैकस्त्वमशिष्यथाः किल परानन्दप्रकाशात्मना ॥5.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: व्यक्त-अव्यक्तम् the manifest (the visible universe) and the unmanifest (its subtle cause), इदम् this, न किञ्चित् nothing at all, अभवत् existed, प्राक् in the beginning/prior, प्राकृत-प्रक्षये during the final cosmic dissolution (Prakrita Pralaya), मायायाम् when Maya (the cosmic creative power), गुण-साम्य-रुद्ध-विकृतौ with its three qualities (Sattva, Rajas, Tamas) balanced and all modifications arrested, त्वयि in You, आगतायाम् had attained, लयम् complete dissolution, नो मृत्युः neither death, च and, तदा अमृतम् then immortality, च and, समभूत् existed, न अह्नः neither of day, न रात्रेः nor of night, स्थितिः presence, तत्र there (in that state), एकः alone, त्वम् You, अशिष्यथाः remained, किल indeed, पर-आनन्द-प्रकाश-आत्मना as the self-luminous nature of supreme bliss

Simple Meaning: At the time of the great cosmic dissolution (Prakrita Pralaya), this entire universe—both in its manifest form of visible objects and its unmanifest form of subtle causes—ceased to exist. The cosmic creative power, Maya, with its three modes (Gunas) perfectly balanced and all its creative activities completely halted, dissolved entirely into You. At that time, there was neither death nor immortality, nor was there any distinction between day and night. In that absolute stillness, You alone remained, shining gloriously as the self-luminous reality of supreme bliss.

Note on Chapter Beginning: This verse marks the beginning of Dashaka 5 of the Narayaneeyam, which vividly describes the state of the universe during the great dissolution and the subsequent process of cosmic creation.

कालः कर्म गुणाश्च जीवनिवहा विश्वं च कार्यं विभो
चिल्लीलारतिमेयुषि त्वयि तदा निर्लीनतामाययुः ।
तेषां नैव वदन्त्यसत्त्वमयि भोः शक्त्यात्मना तिष्ठतां
नो चेत् किं गगनप्रसूनसदृशां भूयो भवेत्संभवः ॥5.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: कालः time, कर्म karma (the past actions of souls), गुणः च and the three modes of nature (Sattva, Rajas, Tamas), जीव-निवहाः the multitudes of living beings, विश्वम् the universe, च and, कार्यम् the effect (creation), विभो O all-pervading Lord, चित्-लीला-रतिम् the delight of resting in Your own pure consciousness, एयुषि when You had attained, त्वयि in You, तदा then, निर्लीनताम् complete absorption, आययुः attained, तेषाम् of them, न एव completely not, वदन्ति (the Shrutis) declare, अ-सत्त्वम् non-existence, अयि भोः O Lord, शक्त्ति-आत्मना in the form of subtle potential energies, तिष्ठताम् for they remained, नो चेत् otherwise, किम् how, गगन-प्रसून-सदृशाम् which are like flowers in the sky (impossible things), भूयः again, भवेत् could happen, संभवः creation/manifestation

निवह   निवहः 1 A multitude, collection, quantity, heap;  src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: O all-pervading Lord! When You withdrew everything to rest in the pure delight of Your own consciousness – Time, Karmas (effect actions of the Jivas), Gunas (the three modes of nature of Sattva, Rajas and Tamas), the countless Jivas (souls), and the entire universe – everything dissolved completely into You. O Lord, Shrutis do not say that these entities became totally non-existent; rather, they remained hidden within You as latent, potential energies (Shakti). If they had truly been destroyed into nothingness, how could they ever be created and manifested again? An absolute nothing can no more manifest than a flower can blossom in the empty sky!

एवं च द्विपरार्धकालविगतावीक्षां सिसृक्षात्मिकां
बिभ्राणे त्वयि चुक्षुभे त्रिभुवनीभावाय माया स्वयम् ।
मायातः खलु कालशक्तिरखिलादृष्टं स्वभावोऽपि च
प्रादुर्भूय गुणान्विकास्य विदधुस्तस्यास्सहायक्रियाम् ॥5.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this manner, च and, द्वि-परार्ध-काल-विगतौ when the vast period of two Parardhas (the lifetime of Brahma) had passed, ईक्षाम् the cosmic glance/intention, सिसृक्षा-आत्मिकाम् consisting of the desire to create, बिभ्राणे when You assumed, त्वयि in You, चुक्षुभे became agitated (aroused to action), त्रिभुवनी-भावाय for the creation of the three worlds, माया Maya (the creative energy), स्वयम् spontaneously, मायातः from Maya, खलु indeed, काल-शक्तिः the power of Time, अखिल-अदृष्टम् the accumulated unseen Karmas of all beings, स्वभावः अपि च and the intrinsic nature of beings, प्रादुर्भूय manifesting, गुणान् the three modes of nature (Sattva, Rajas, Tamas), विकास्य causing to expand/unfold, विदधुः rendered, तस्याः to her (Maya), सहाय-क्रियाम् assistance in her work

Simple Meaning: When the vast epoch of two Parardhas had passed, You cast a glance, desiring once more to create the universe. At that moment, Maya stirred into action to bring forth the three worlds. From Maya arose the power of Time, the accumulated unseen Karmas (Adrishta) of all living beings, and their latent inherent natures (Svabhava). Emerging together, they unfolded the three modes of nature (Gunas) and assisted Maya in her grand act of creation.

मायासन्निहितोऽप्रविष्टवपुषा साक्षीति गीतो भवान्
भेदैस्तां प्रतिबिम्बतो विविशिवान् जीवोऽपि नैवापरः ।
कालादिप्रतिबोधिताऽथ भवता संचोदिता च स्वयं
माया सा खलु बुद्धितत्त्वमसृजद्योऽसौ महानुच्यते ॥5.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: माया-सन्निहितः adjacent to Maya/associated with Maya, अप्रविष्ट-वपुषा (but) Your Form not getting affected by Maya, साक्षी इति as the Witness, गीतः sung/declared (in Scriptures), भवान् You, भेदैः through various reflections/limitations, ताम् into that Maya, प्रतिबिम्बतः as a reflection, विविशिवान् having entered, जीवः the individual soul, अपि also, न एव अपरः is truly none other than You, काल-आदि-प्रतिबोधिता awakened by factors like Time, अथ then, भवता by You, संचोदिता impelled, च and, स्वयम् itself, माया Maya, सा that, खलु indeed, बुद्धि-तत्त्वम् the principle of intellect, असृजत् created, यः असौ which, महान् Mahat, उच्यते is called

Simple Meaning: Though associated with Maya, but Your Form not getting affected by it, You are hailed in the scriptures as the Witness (Sakshi). Entering into Maya through its various reflections, You manifest as the individual soul (Jiva), who is truly none other than You. Then, awakened by Time and other factors and impelled by Your divine will, that Maya brought forth principle of intellect (Buddhitattva), which is known as Mahat.

तत्रासौ त्रिगुणात्मकोऽपि च महान् सत्त्वप्रधानः स्वयं
जीवेऽस्मिन् खलु निर्विकल्पमहमित्युद्बोधनिष्पाद्कः ।
चक्रेऽस्मिन् सविकल्पबोधकमहन्तत्त्वं महान् खल्वसौ
सम्पुष्टं त्रिगुणैस्तमोऽतिबहुलं विष्णो भवत्प्रेरणात् ॥5.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there (in that creation), असौ that, त्रिगुण-आत्मकः composed of the three Gunas, अपि although, च and, महान् Mahat-tattva, सत्त्व-प्रधानः predominantly Sattva, स्वयम् by itself, जीवे in the individual soul, अस्मिन् in this, खलु indeed, निर्विकल्पम् subtle/indeterminate, अहम् "I", इति thus, उद्बोध-निष्पादकः bringing about the awareness, चक्रे produced, अस्मिन् from this (Mahat), सविकल्प-बोधकम् producing determinate/differentiated awareness, अहम्-तत्त्वम् the Ahamkara-tattva (ego-principle), महान् Mahat, खलु indeed, असौ that, सम्पुष्टम् well-augmented, त्रिगुणैः by the three Gunas, तमः-अति-बहुलम् with Tamas being predominant, विष्णो O Vishnu, भवत्-प्रेरणात् by Your inspiration/impulse

Simple Meaning: O Vishnu! Although composed of all three Gunas, that cosmic intellect (Mahat-tattva) is intrinsically predominant in Sattva. It brings about the subtle, pure, and indeterminate awareness of "I" (Nirvikalpa Aham) within the individual soul. Driven by Your divine impulse, that Mahat, further augmented by the three Gunas, then produced from itself the ego-principle (Ahamkara-tattva), which generates differentiated self-awareness (Savikalpa) and is heavily dominated by Tamas.

सोऽहं च त्रिगुणक्रमात् त्रिविधतामासाद्य वैकारिको
भूयस्तैजसतामसाविति भवन्नाद्येन सत्त्वात्मना
देवानिन्द्रियमानिनोऽकृत दिशावातार्कपाश्यश्विनो
वह्नीन्द्राच्युतमित्रकान् विधुविधिश्रीरुद्रशारीरकान् ॥5.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः अहम् that Ahamkara-tattva (ego-principle), च and, त्रिगुण-क्रमात् according to the three Gunas (Sattva, Rajas, Tamas), त्रिविधताम् three-fold nature, आसाद्य having attained, वैकारिकः Vaikarika (Sattvika Ahamkara), भूयः further, तैजस-तामसौ Taijasa (Rajasika Ahamkara) and Tamasa (Tamasika Ahamkara), इति thus, भवन् becoming, आद्येन by the first (Vaikarika/Sattvika Ahamkara), सत्त्व-आत्मना which is of the nature of Sattva, देवान् deities, इन्द्रिय-मानिनः presiding over the senses and mind, अकृत created, दिशा-वात-अर्क-पाशि-अश्विनः Dik (quarters), Vata (wind), Arka (sun), Pashi (Varuna), Ashvins (the twin gods), वह्नि-इन्द्र-अच्युत-मित्र-कान् Vahni (fire), Indra, Achyuta (Upendra), Mitra, Prajapati, विधु-विधि-श्रीरुद्र-शारीरकान् Vidhu (Moon), Vidhi (Brahma), Shri Rudra (Shiva), Kshetrajna (the embodied spirit)

Simple Meaning: That ego-principle (Ahamkara-tattva), dividing itself according to the three Gunas, assumed three distinct forms: Vaikarika (Sattvika), Taijasa (Rajasika), and Tamasa (Tamasika). From the first of these—the Vaikarika form, which is Sattvika in nature—it created the presiding deities of the ten senses, the mind, and the inner instruments: the deities of the Quarters, Wind, Sun, Varuna, the twin Ashvins, Fire, Indra, Upendra, Mitra, Prajapati, Moon, Brahma, Shiva, and Kshetrajna.

भूमन् मानसबुद्ध्यहङ्कृतिमिलच्चित्ताख्यवृत्त्यन्वितं
तच्चान्तःकरणं विभो तव बलात् सत्त्वांश एवासृजत् ।
जातस्तैजसतो दशेन्द्रियगणस्तत्तामसांशात्पुनः
तन्मात्रं नभसो मरुत्पुरपते शब्दोऽजनि त्वद्बलात् ॥5.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूमन् O Infinite Supreme Lord, मानस-बुद्धि-अहङ्कृति-मिलत्-चित्त-आख्य-वृत्ति-अन्वितम् comprising the functions named mind, intellect, ego, and memory (chitta), तत्-च-अन्तःकरणम् and that inner instrument (Antahkarana), विभो O all-pervading Lord, तव बलात् by Your power, सत्त्व-अंशः एव the Sattvic aspect (of Ahamkara) alone, असृजत् created, जातः produced, तैजसतः from the Rajasic aspect (Taijasa Ahamkara), दश-इन्द्रिय-गणः the collection of the ten sense organs, तत्-तामस-अंशात् पुनः then from its Tamasic aspect, तन्मात्रम् the subtle element, नभसः of space, मरुत्पुरपते O Lord of Guruvayur, शब्दः sound, अजनि was born, त्वत्-बलात् by Your power

Simple Meaning: Oh Infinite, All-Pervading Lord! By Your divine power, the Sattvic aspect of ego (Vaikarika Ahamkara) produced the fourfold inner instrument (Antahkarana)—consisting of mind (Manas), intellect (Buddhi), ego (Ahamkara), and memory (Chitta). From its Rajasic aspect (Taijasa Ahamkara) arose the ten senses (the five organs of perception and five organs of action). Then, O Lord of Guruvayur, from its Tamasic aspect (Tamasa Ahamkara), the subtle element of sound (Tanmatra)—which gives rise to space—was brought forth by Your will.

शब्दाद्व्योम ततः ससर्जिथ विभो स्पर्शं ततो मारुतं
तस्माद्रूपमतो महोऽथ च रसं तोयं च गन्धं महीम् ।
एवं माधव पूर्वपूर्वकलनादाद्याद्यधर्मान्वितं
भूतग्राममिमं त्वमेव भगवन् प्राकाशयस्तामसात् ॥5.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: शब्दात् from the subtle sound element, व्योम space, ततः from that (space), ससर्जिथ You created, विभो O all-pervading Lord, स्पर्शम् touch (the subtle touch element), ततः from that (touch), मारुतम् air, तस्मात् from that (air), रूपम् form/color (the subtle form element), अतः from that (form), महः fire/light, अथ and, च and, रसम् taste (the subtle taste element), तोयम् water, च and, गन्धम् smell (the subtle smell element), महीम् earth, एवम् in this manner, माधव O Madhava, पूर्व-पूर्व-कलनात् due to the combination/influence of each preceding element, आद्य-आद्य-धर्म-अन्वितम् inheriting the characteristics of each preceding element, भूत-ग्रामम् इमम् this entire group of physical elements, त्वम् एव You alone, भगवन् oh Supreme Lord, प्राकाशयः manifested, तामसात् from the Tamasic aspect of ego

Simple Meaning: O all-pervading Lord! From the subtle sound element (Shabda), You brought forth space (Akasha). From space, You created the subtle touch element (Sparsha), and from touch arose air (Vayu). From air, You created the subtle form/color element (Rupa), and from form came fire/light (Agni). From fire came the subtle taste element (Rasa), which produced water (Jala). Finally, from water came the subtle smell element (Gandha), which manifested the earth (Prithvi). In this manner, O Madhava, by the interplay of each preceding element, You alone manifested this entire system of physical elements from the Tamasic ego, with each succeeding element inheriting the unique attributes of those that came before it.

एते भूतगणास्तथेन्द्रियगणा देवाश्च जाताः पृथक्
नो शेकुर्भुवनाण्डनिर्मितिविधौ देवैरमीभिस्तदा ।
त्वं नानाविधसूक्तिभिर्नुतगुणस्तत्त्वान्यमून्याविशन्
चेष्टाशक्तिमुदीर्य तानि घटयन् हैरण्यमण्डं व्यधाः ॥5.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: एते these, भूत-गणाः groups of physical elements, तथा and, इन्द्रिय-गणाः groups of sense organs, देवाः च and the presiding deities, जाताः having been born, पृथक् as separate entities, नो शेकुः were not able, भुवन-अण्ड-निर्मिति-विधौ in the act of creating the cosmic egg of the universe, देवैः अमीभिः by these presiding deities, तदा then, त्वम् You, नाना-विध-सूक्तिभिः by various kinds of hymns and prayers, नुत-गुणः whose glorious attributes were praised, तत्त्वानि अमूनि into these various cosmic principles, आविशन् entering, चेष्टा-शक्तिम् the capacity for action (motive power), उदीर्य awakening/activating, तानि them, घटयन् uniting/assembling together, हैरण्यम् अण्डम् the golden cosmic egg, व्यधाः created

Simple Meaning: Though all these physical elements, sense organs, and presiding deities had come into existence, they remained separate and isolated, unable to combine on their own to create the cosmic egg of the universe. The presiding deities then praised Your glorious attributes through various hymns. Hearing their prayers, You entered into all these distinct cosmic principles, activated their dormant powers of action, synthesized them together, and brought forth the magnificent golden cosmic egg (Hiranyanda).

अण्डं तत्खलु पूर्वसृष्टसलिलेऽतिष्ठत् सहस्रं समाः
निर्भिन्दन्नकृथाश्चतुर्दशजगद्रूपं विराडाह्वयम् ।
साहस्रैः करपादमूर्धनिवहैर्निश्शेषजीवात्मको
निर्भातोऽसि मरुत्पुराधिप स मां त्रायस्व सर्वामयात् ॥5.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: अण्डम् egg (the golden cosmic egg), तत् that, खलु indeed, पूर्व-सृष्ट-सलिले in the waters created earlier (the Causal Ocean), अतिष्ठत् rested/remained, सहस्रम् a thousand, समाः years, निर्भिन्दन् cleaving/splitting it open, अकृथाः You made, चतुर्दश-जगत्-रूपम् into the form of the fourteen worlds, विराट्-आह्वयम् named the Virat-purusha (the Cosmic Being), साहस्रैः with thousands of, कर-पाद-मूर्ध-निवहैः assemblies of hands, feet, and heads, निश्शेष-जीव-आत्मकः the inner self/soul of all living beings without exception, निर्भातः असि You shone forth, मरुत्-पुर​-अधिप​ O Lord of the city of the Wind - Guruvayoor, सः that Lord, माम् me, त्रायस्व protect/deliver, सर्व-अमयात् from all illnesses and afflictions

Simple Meaning: That golden cosmic egg rested upon the causal waters created earlier for a period of a thousand years. Then, splitting it open, You assumed the cosmic form known as the Virat-purusha, which contains the fourteen worlds within itself. Shining magnificently with thousands of hands, feet, and heads, You manifested as the inner soul of all living beings. O Lord of Guruvayoor! Please protect and deliver me from all physical and mental afflictions!

Note on Chapter Conclusion: This verse concludes Dashaka 5 of the Narayaneeyam, which describes the dissolution of the cosmos, the manifestation of the fundamental elements (Tattvas), and the origin of the Golden Cosmic Egg (Hiranyanda) and the Cosmic Being (Virat Purusha).

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
