Diversification of creation

दशकम् १०

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 10

Diversification of creation

(छन्दस् - वसन्ततिलका)

वैकुण्ठ वर्धितबलोऽथ भवत्प्रसादात्
अम्भोजयोनिरसृजत् किल जीवदेहान् ।
स्थास्नूनि भूरुहमयानि तथा तिरश्चां
जातिं मनुष्यनिवहानपि देवभेदान् ॥10.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: वैकुण्ठ O Vaikuntha (Lord Vishnu), वर्धित-बलः with enhanced strength, अथ then, भवत्-प्रसादात् by Your grace, अम्भोज-योनिः the lotus-born Brahma, असृजत् created, किल indeed, जीव-देहान् physical bodies of living beings, स्थास्नूनि immovable beings, भूरुह-मयानि consisting of trees and plants, तथा as well as, तिरश्चाम् of animals/birds, जातिम् species, मनुष्य-निवहान् multitudes of humans, अपि and also, देव-भेदान् various classes of divine beings

Simple Meaning: O Lord Vaikuntha! Then, with his power enhanced by Your grace, the lotus-born Lord Brahma created the physical forms of all living beings—immovable plant life, the various species of animals and birds, human beings, and the different classes of celestial beings.

मिथ्याग्रहास्मिमतिरागविकोपभीतिः
अज्ञानवृत्तिमिति पञ्चविधां स सृष्ट्वा ।
उद्दामतामसपदार्थविधानदूनः
तेने त्वदीयचरणस्मरणं विशुद्ध्यै ॥10.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: मिथ्या-आग्रह-अस्मि-मति-राग-विकोप-भीतिः error/misconception, egoism/I-ness, attachment, anger, and fear, अज्ञान-वृत्तिम् nature of ignorance, इति thus, पञ्च-विधाम् fivefold, सः he (Brahma), सृष्ट्वा having created, उद्दाम-तामस-पदार्थ-विधान-दूनः distressed by the creation of such intensely dark/ignorant entities, तेने performed, त्वदीय-चरण-स्मरणम् remembrance of Your divine feet, विशुद्ध्यै for purification

मिथ्या-आग्रह- error/misconception, अस्मि-मति- egoism/I-ness, राग- attachment, विकोप- anger, भीतिः fear

दून   दून p. p. 1 Pained, afflicted, fatigued, agitated, src Kosha, Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: Lord Brahma first created the fivefold nature of ignorance consisting of delusion, egoism, attachment, anger, and fear. Distressed by having brought into existence such ignorant and dark entities, he meditated upon Your divine lotus feet to purify himself.

तावत् ससर्ज मनसा सनकं सनन्दं
भूयः सनातनमुनिं च सनत्कुमारम् ।
ते सृष्टिकर्मणि तु तेन नियुज्यमानाः
त्वत्पादभक्तिरसिका जगृहुर्न वाणीम् ॥10.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तावत् thereupon, ससर्ज created, मनसा from his mind, सनकम् Sanaka, सनन्दम् Sanandana, भूयः further, सनातन-मुनिम् Sanatana the sage, च and, सनत्कुमारम् Sanatkumara, ते they, सृष्टि-कर्मणि in the work of creation, तु indeed, तेन by him (Brahma), नियुज्यमानाः being directed/assigned, त्वत्-पाद-भक्ति-रसिकाः immersed in the bliss of devotion to Your feet, न जगृहुः did not accept, वाणीम् his command/words

Simple Meaning: Thereupon, Brahma created from his mind the four sages—Sanaka, Sanandana, Sanatana, and Sanatkumara. However, when directed by him to engage in the work of creation, they—being completely absorbed in the bliss of devotion to Your lotus feet—did not accept his command.

तावत् प्रकोपमुदितं प्रतिरुन्धतोऽस्य
भ्रूमध्यतोऽजनि मृडो भवदेकदेशः ।
नामानि मे कुरु पदानि च हा विरिञ्च
इत्यादौ रुरोद किल तेन स रुद्रनामा ॥10.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तावत् then, प्रकोपम् rising anger, उदितम् which had arisen, प्रतिरुन्धतः restraining, अस्य his (Brahma's), भ्रू-मध्यतः from between the eyebrows, अजनि was born, मृडः Lord Shiva (Mrida), भवत्-एक-देशः an aspect/portion of Yourself, नामानि names, मे for me, कुरु assign, पदानि abodes/places, च and, हा Alas!, विरिञ्च O Brahma, इति thus, आदौ at once/immediately, रुरोद wept/cried, किल indeed, तेन for that reason, सः he, रुद्र-नामा named Rudra

Simple Meaning: Then, as Brahma restrained the fierce anger arising within him, Lord Shiva (Mrida)—who is a partial manifestation of You—was born from between his eyebrows. Crying out, "O Brahma, alas! Please assign me names and abodes!", he wept continuously, and for that reason, he came to be known as Rudra (the one who cried).

एकादशाह्वयतया च विभिन्नरूपं
रुद्रं विधाय दयिता वनिताश्च दत्वा ।
तावन्त्यदत्त च पदानि भवत्प्रणुन्नः
प्राह प्रजाविरचनाय च सादरं तम् ॥10.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: एकादश-आह्वयतया with eleven names, च and, विभिन्न-रूपम् in distinct forms, रुद्रम् Rudra, विधाय having made, दयिताः beloved, वनिताः wives (the Eleven Rudranis), च and, दत्वा having given, तावन्ति as many, अदत्त gave, च and, पदानि abodes, भवत्-प्रणुन्नः prompted/inspired by You, प्राह said, प्रजा-विरचनाय for the creation of beings, च and, सादरम् respectfully, तम् to him (Rudra)

Simple Meaning: Inspired by You, Lord Brahma gave Rudra eleven distinct forms with eleven names, gave him as many wives and abodes, and then respectfully asked him to proceed with the creation of living beings.

रुद्राभिसृष्टभयदाकृतिरुद्रसंघ-
सम्पूर्यमाणभुवनत्रयभीतचेताः ।
मा मा प्रजाः सृज तपश्चर मङ्गलाय
इत्याचष्ट तं कमलभूर्भवदीरितात्मा ॥10.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: रुद्र-अभिसृष्ट-भयद-आकृति-रुद्र-संघ-सम्पूर्यमाण-भुवन-त्रय-भीत-चेताः whose mind became terrified upon seeing the three worlds filled with the host of terrifying Rudras created by Lord Rudra, मा मा do not, do not, प्रजाः living beings, सृज create, तपः चर perform penance, मङ्गलाय for the welfare/auspiciousness of all, इति thus, अचष्ट spoke, तम् to him (Rudra), कमल-भूः the lotus-born Brahma, भवत्-ईरित-आत्मा inspired from within by You

Simple Meaning: Seeing the three worlds rapidly filled with the frightening and fierce forms created by Rudra, Brahma became terrified in his heart. Inspired by You, the lotus-born Lord Brahma said to him, “Do not create any more such beings! Perform penance for the welfare of the universe.”

तस्याथ सर्गरसिकस्य मरीचिरत्रिः
तत्राङिगराः क्रतुमुनिः पुलहः पुलस्त्यः ।
अङ्गादजायत भृगुश्च वसिष्ठदक्षौ
श्रीनारदश्च भगवन् भवदंघ्रिदासः ॥ 10.7 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य of him (Brahma), अथ then, सर्ग-रसिकस्य eager/desirous for creation, मरीचिः Marichi, अत्रिः Atri, तत्र there/thereupon, अङ्गिराः Angiras, क्रतु-मुनिः Kratu the sage, पुलहः Pulaha, पुलस्त्यः Pulastya, अङ्गात् from his limbs, अजायत were born, भृगुः Bhrigu, च and, वसिष्ठ-दक्षौ Vasishtha and Daksha, श्रीनारदः Sage Narada, च and, भगवन् O Lord, भवत्-अङ्घ्रि-दासः the servant at Your feet

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Then, as Lord Brahma remained eager to continue creation, the great sages were born from his various limbs: Marichi, Atri, Angiras, Kratu, Pulaha, Pulastya, Bhrigu, Vasishtha, Daksha, and Sage Narada—who is a dedicated servant at Your feet.

धर्मादिकानभिसृजन्नथ कर्दमं च
वाणीं विधाय विधिरङ्गजसंकुलोऽभूत् ।
त्वद्बोधितैस्सनकदक्षमुखैस्तनूजैः
उद्बोधितश्च विरराम तमो विमुञ्चन् ॥10.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: धर्म-आदिकान् Dharma and others, अभिसृजन् creating, अथ then, कर्दमम् Kardama, च and, वाणीम् Saraswati (devi of speech), विधाय having created, विधिः Brahma, अङ्ग-ज-संकुलः overwhelmed with passion for his mind-born daughter, अभूत् became, त्वत्-बोधितैः inspired/enlightened by You, सनक-दक्ष-मुखैः headed by Sanaka, Daksha, and others, तनूजैः by his sons, उद्बोधितः awakened to righteous judgment, च and, विरराम desisted, तमः darkness/delusion, विमुञ्चन् giving up

Simple Meaning: Then, having created Dharma and others, along with Kardama and Saraswati, Lord Brahma became overwhelmed with passion for his own creation. However, awakened to proper wisdom by his sons—headed by Sanaka, Daksha, and others who were inspired by You—he cast off that delusion.

वेदान् पुराणनिवहानपि सर्वविद्याः
कुर्वन् निजाननगणाच्चतुराननोऽसौ ।
पुत्रेषु तेषु विनिधाय स सर्गवृद्धिम्
अप्राप्नुवंस्तव पदाम्बुजमाश्रितोभूत् ॥10.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: वेदान् the Vedas, पुराण-निवहान् the collection of Puranas, अपि also, सर्व-विद्याः all branches of knowledge, कुर्वन् producing/emanating, निज-आनन-गणात् from the group of his four faces, चतुराननः four-faced Brahma, असौ that, पुत्रेषु in his sons, तेषु in those, विनिधाय entrusting, सः he, सर्ग-वृद्धिम् the growth and expansion of creation, अप्राप्नुवन् not attaining (the desired growth/progress), तव Your, पद-अम्बुजम् lotus feet, आश्रितः अभूत् took refuge in

Simple Meaning: Emanating the Vedas, the collection of Puranas, and all other branches of knowledge from his four faces, the four-faced Lord Brahma taught them to his sons. However, when he saw that the expansion of creation was still not progressing adequately, he took refuge at Your lotus feet.

जानन्नुपायमथ देहमजो विभज्य
स्त्रीपुंसभावमभजन्मनुतद्वधूभ्याम् ।
ताभ्यां च मानुषकुलानि विवर्धयंस्त्वं
गोविन्द मारुतपुरेश निरुन्धि रोगान् ॥10.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: जानन् knowing, उपायम् the means/solution, अथ then, देहम् his body, अजः the unborn Brahma, विभज्य having divided, स्त्री-पुंस-भावम् the nature of male and female, अभजत् assumed, मनु-तत्-वधूभ्याम् in the form of Manu and his wife (Shatarupa), ताभ्याम् by the two of them, च and, मानुष-कुलानि the human race, विवर्धयन् causing to flourish and expand, त्वम् You, गोविन्द O Govinda, मारुत-पुर-ईश O Lord of the city of the Wind - Guruvayoor, निरुन्धि eradicate/ward off, रोगान् my illnesses

Simple Meaning: Then, knowing the proper solution through Your divine grace, the unborn Lord Brahma divided his own body into two halves, assuming male and female forms as Manu and his wife Shatarupa. Through them, the human race was expanded and multiplied. O Lord Govinda, Lord of Guruvayoor, please eradicate all my ailments!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
