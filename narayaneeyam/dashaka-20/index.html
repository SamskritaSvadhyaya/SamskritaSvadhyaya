The Story of Rishabha Deva

दशकम् २०

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 20

The Story of Rishabha Deva

(छन्दस् - आर्द्रा)

प्रियव्रतस्य प्रियपुत्रभूतात्
आग्नीध्रराजादुदितो हि नाभिः ।
त्वां दृष्टवानिष्टदमिष्टिमध्ये
तवैव तुष्ट्यै कृतयज्ञकर्मा ॥20.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रियव्रतस्य of Priyavrata, प्रिय-पुत्र-भूतात् born as the beloved son, आग्नीध्र-राजात् from King Aagnidhra, उदितः born, हि indeed, नाभिः King Nabhi, त्वाम् You, दृष्टवान् saw, इष्ट-दम् the bestower of desires, इष्टि-मध्ये in the middle of the sacrifice, तव You, एव alone, तुष्ट्यै for the pleasing/satisfaction, कृत-यज्ञ-कर्मा performing sacrificial rites

Simple Meaning: King Nabhi was born as the son of King Aagnidhra, who was the beloved son of Priyavrata. While performing sacred sacrificial rites solely to please You, King Nabhi was blessed to behold You, the granter of all desires, appearing right in the middle of his sacrifice.

अभिष्टुतस्तत्र मुनीश्वरैस्त्वं
राज्ञः स्वतुल्यं सुतमर्थ्यमानः ।
स्वयं जनिष्येऽहमिति ब्रुवाणः
तिरोदधा बर्हिषि विश्वमूर्ते ॥20.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: अभिष्टुतः praised, तत्र there, मुनीश्वरैः by the great sages, त्वम् You, राज्ञः of the king, स्व-तुल्यम् equal to Yourself, सुतम् a son, अर्थ्यमानः being requested, स्वयम् Yourself, जनिष्ये I shall be born, अहम् I, इति thus, ब्रुवाणः saying, तिरोदधाः disappeared, बर्हिषि in the sacrificial fire, विश्वमूर्ते O Lord with cosmos as Your form

Simple Meaning: The sages sang Your praises and the king prayed to You for a son like Yourself.  Replying that You Yourself would be born as his son, You disappeared into the sacrificial fire, O Lord with cosmos as Your form!

नाभिप्रियायामथ मेरुदेव्यां
त्वमंशतोऽभूः ॠषभाभिधानः ।
अलोकसामान्यगुणप्रभाव-
प्रभाविताशेषजनप्रमोदः ॥20.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: नाभि-प्रियायाम् in the beloved wife of Nabhi, अथ then, मेरुदेव्याम् in Merudevi, त्वम् You, अंशतः by a partial incarnation, अभूः were born, ऋषभ-अभिधानः named Rishabha, अलोक-सामान्य-गुण-प्रभाव-प्रभावित-अशेष-जन-प्रमोदः causing delight to all people who were influenced by Your extraordinary qualities and power

Simple Meaning: Then, You were born as a partial incarnation named Rishabha to Merudevi, the beloved wife of Nabhi. With Your extraordinary, divine qualities and power, You delighted all the people of the world.

त्वयि त्रिलोकीभृति राज्यभारं
निधाय नाभिः सह मेरुदेव्या ।
तपोवनं प्राप्य भवन्निषेवी
गतः किलानन्दपदं पदं ते ॥20.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वयि in You, त्रिलोकी-भृति the bearer of the three worlds, राज्य-भारम् the burden of the kingdom, निधाय having placed, नाभिः Nabhi, सह together with, मेरुदेव्या Merudevi, तपो-वनम् the forest of penance, प्राप्य having reached, भवत्-निषेवी worshipping You, गतः went/attained, किल indeed, आनन्द-पदम् the abode of bliss, पदम् the realm, ते Your

Simple Meaning: Having entrusted the burden of ruling the kingdom to You (in the incarnation of Rishabha), the protector of the three worlds, King Nabhi retired to the forest of penance along with Merudevi. Devoting himself entirely to Your worship, he indeed attained Your supreme realm of eternal bliss.

इन्द्रस्त्वदुत्कर्षकृतादमर्षात्
ववर्ष नास्मिन्नजनाभवर्षे ।
यदा तदा त्वं निजयोगशक्त्या
स्ववर्षमेनद्व्यदधाः सुवर्षम् ॥20.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: इन्द्रः Indra, त्वत्-उत्कर्ष-कृतात् caused by Your excellence/greatness, अमर्षात् out of jealousy, ववर्ष न did not rain, अस्मिन् in this, अजनाभ-वर्षे realm of Ajanabha, यदा when, तदा then, त्वम् You, निज-योग-शक्त्या through Your own power of yoga, स्व-वर्षम् Your own realm, एनत् this, व्यदधाः made, सु-वर्षम् a land of abundant rain

Simple Meaning: When Indra out of jealousy over Your supremacy withheld rain from this land of Ajanabha, You used Your own divine power of yoga to bestow abundant rain and prosperity upon Your realm.

जितेन्द्रदत्तां कमनीं जयन्तीम्
अथोद्वहन्नात्मरताशयोऽपि ।
अजीजनस्तत्र शतं तनूजान्
एषां क्षितीशो भरतोऽग्रजन्मा ॥20.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: जित-इन्द्र-दत्ताम् presented by Indra, कमनीम् beautiful, जयन्तीम् Jayanti, अथ then, उद्वहन marrying, आत्म-रत-आशयः even though Your mind was absorbed in the Self, अपि even, अजीजनः You begot, तत्र in her, शतम् a hundred, तनूजान् sons, एषाम् of these, क्षितीशः king, भरतः Bharata, अग्र-जन्मा the eldest-born

Simple Meaning: Indra, who was won over by You, presented the beautiful Jayanti to You as a wife. Even though Your mind was ever absorbed in Self, You married her. Through her, You begot a hundred sons, among whom the eldest was Bharata, who became the ruler of the earth.

नवाभवन् योगिवरा नवान्ये
त्वपालयन् भारतवर्षखण्डान् ।
सैका त्वशीतिस्तव शेषपुत्रः
तपोबलात् भूसुरभूयमीयुः ॥20.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: नव nine, अभवन् became, योगि-वराः great yogis, नव nine, अन्ये others, तु indeed, अपालयन् ruled over, भारत-वर्ष-खण्डान् the nine divisions of Bharatavarsha, स-एका one, तु and, अशीतिः eighty, तव Your, शेष-पुत्रः remaining sons, तपो-बलात् by the power of penance, भू-सुर-भूयम् the state of being brahmanas, ईयुः attained

Simple Meaning: Nine of Your sons became great yogis, and nine others ruled over the nine regions of Bharatavarsha. The remaining eighty-one sons, through the power of their intense penance, attained the revered status of brahmanas.

उक्त्वा सुतेभ्योऽथ मुनीन्द्रमध्ये
विरक्तिभक्त्यन्वितमुक्तिमार्गम् ।
स्वयं गतः पारमहंस्यवृत्तिम्
अधाः जडोन्मत्तपिशाचचर्याम् ॥ 20.8 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: उक्त्वा having taught, सुतेभ्यः to Your sons, अथ then, मुनीन्द्र-मध्ये in the assembly of great sages, विरक्ति-भक्ति-अन्वित-मुक्ति-मार्गम् the path to liberation through dispassion and devotion, स्वयम् Yourself, गतः adopting, पारमहंस्य-वृत्तिम् the conduct of the highest order of ascetics, अधाः You assumed, जड-उन्मत्त-पिशाच-चर्याम् the behavior of an inert person, a madman, or an evil spirit

परमहंस   परम—हंस m. an ascetic of the highest order, a religious man who has subdued all his senses by abstract meditation,  src. Kosha, Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: Then, in the presence of great sages, You taught Your sons the divine path to liberation through dispassion and devotion. Thereafter, You Yourself embraced the exalted conduct of a Paramahamsa and lived acting like an inert person, a madman, or an evil spirit.

परात्मभूतोऽपि परोपदेशं
कुर्वन् भवान् सर्वनिरस्यमानः ।
विकारहीनो विचचार कृत्स्नां
महीमहीनात्मरसाभिलीनः ॥20.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: परात्म-भूतः being the Supreme Self, अपि even though, पर-उपदेशम् instruction for others, कुर्वन् imparting, भवान् You, सर्व-निरस्यमानः being disregarded by all, विकार-हीनः free from modifications/attractions, विचचार wandered, कृत्स्नाम् the entire, महीम् earth, अहीन-आत्म-रस-अभिलीनः deeply absorbed in the unceasing bliss of the Self

Simple Meaning: Even though You are the Supreme Self, You imparted divine instruction for the benefit of humanity. Disregarded and mistreated by the ignorant, You remained totally unperturbed and unchanging, wandering across the whole earth while deeply absorbed in the eternal bliss of Your own Self.

शयुव्रतं गोमृगकाकचर्यां
चिरं चरन्नाप्य परं स्वरूपं ।
दवाहृताङ्गः कुटकाचले त्वं
तापान् ममापाकुरु वातनाथ ॥20.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: शयु-व्रतम् the life of a python (staying still and accepting whatever comes), गो-मृग-काक-चर्याम् the lifestyle of a cow, a deer, and a crow, चिरम् for a long time, चरन् practicing, आप्य having attained, परम् supreme, स्व-रूपम् real nature/form, दव-आहृत-अङ्गः having Your body consumed by a forest fire, कुटक-अचले on Kutaka mountain, त्वम् You, तापान् afflictions/sufferings, मम my, अपाकुरु remove, वात-नाथ O Lord of the Wind - Guruvayoor

Simple Meaning: Observing the ways of the life of a python, and living like a cow, a deer, or a crow for a long time, You abided in Your supreme divine nature. Eventually, on Kutaka mountain, You allowed Your physical body to be consumed by a forest fire. O Lord of Guruvayoor, please remove all my afflictions and sufferings!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
