Salvation of Putana

दशकम् ४०

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 40

Salvation of Putana

तदनु नन्दममन्दशुभास्पदं नृपपुरीं करदानकृते गतम्।
समवलोक्य जगाद भवत्पिता विदितकंससहायजनोद्यमः ॥40.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदनु after that, नन्दम् Nanda, अमन्द-शुभ-आस्पदम् the abode of boundless good fortune, नृप-पुरीम् to the king's city (Mathura), कर-दान-कृते for the purpose of paying tax, गतम् who had gone, समवलोक्य seeing, जगाद spoke, भवत्-पिता Your father (Vasudeva), विदित-कंस-सहाय-जन-उद्यमः who knew the plans of Kamsa's evil aides

Simple Meaning: After that, when Nanda, the embodiment of great auspiciousness, arrived in the royal city of Mathura to pay taxes, Your father Vasudeva went to meet him. Fully aware of the dangerous plots being hatched by Kamsa's evil aides, Vasudeva spoke to Nanda with deep concern.

अयि सखे तव बालकजन्म मां सुखयतेऽद्य निजात्मजजन्मवत् ।
इति भवत्पितृतां व्रजनायके समधिरोप्य शशंस तमादरात् ॥40.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: अयि O, सखे friend, तव your, बालक-जन्म birth of a son, माम् me, सुखयते delights, अद्य today, निज-आत्मज-जन्म-वत् just like the birth of my own son, इति thus, भवत्-पितृताम् Your fatherhood, व्रज-नायके on the chief of Vraja (Nanda), समधिरोप्य attributing / superimposing, शशंस he congratulated, तम् him, आदरात् with great affection / respect

Simple Meaning: Vasudeva said, O my friend, the birth of your son today brings me as much joy as if my own son were born! Attributing the chief of Vraja as Your father, Vasudeva warmly congratulated Nanda with deep love and affection.

इह च सन्त्यनिमित्तशतानि ते कटकसीम्नि ततो लघु गम्यताम् ।
इति च तद्वचसा व्रजनायको भवदपायभिया द्रुतमाययौ ॥40.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: इह here, च and, सन्ति there are, अनिमित्त-शतानि hundreds of bad omens, ते for you, कटक-सीम्नि in your settlement (Gokula), ततः therefore, लघु quickly, गम्यताम् you should return, इति thus, च and, तत्-वचसा by his (Vasudeva's) words, व्रज-नायकः the chief of Vraja (Nanda), भवत्-अपाय-भिया fearing harm to You, द्रुतम् hastily, आययौ returned

Simple Meaning: Vasudeva warned him - “Hundreds of ill omens are appearing in your settlement of Gokula, so you should return there immediately!” Moved by these words and filled with fear for Your safety, Nanda quickly set off and rushed back to Vraja.

अवसरे खलु तत्र च काचन व्रजपदे मधुराकृतिरङ्गना ।
तरलषट्पदलालितकुन्तला कपटपोतक ते निकटं गता ॥40.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अवसरे at that time, खलु indeed, तत्र there, च and, काचन a certain, व्रज-पदे in the land of Vraja, मधुर-आकृतिः of exquisite beauty, अङ्गना woman, तरल-षट्पद-लालित-कुन्तला with her hair adorned by hovering black bees (due the smell of the sweet smelling flowers), कपट-पोतक O (You) in the guise of a child, ते Your, निकटम् presence / vicinity, गता went

Simple Meaning: Around that time, a woman of striking beauty, her dark hair surrounded by hovering black bees attracted to her fragrance, entered Vraja. O You in the guise as an infant! she made her way straight to Your side!

सपदि सा हृतबालकचेतना निशिचरान्वयजा किल पूतना ।
व्रजवधूष्विह केयमिति क्षणं विमृशतीषु भवन्तमुपाददे ॥40.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: सपदि immediately, सा she, हृत-बालक-चेतना who robbed infants of their life energy, निशिचर-अन्वय-जा born in the clan of rakshasas, किल indeed, पूतना Putana, व्रज-वधूषु while the women of Vraja, इह here, का इयम् who is she, इति thus, क्षणम् for a moment, विमृशतीषु were wondering, भवन्तम् You, उपाददे took up / picked up

Simple Meaning: That wicked woman was Putana, born of the rakshasa clan, whose cruel nature was to drain the life energy out of infants. While the women of Vraja stood wondering among themselves for a moment as to who this beautiful stranger could be, she picked You up in her arms.

ललितभावविलासहृतात्मभिर्युवतिभिः प्रतिरोद्धुमपारिता ।
स्तनमसौ भवनान्तनिषेदुषी प्रददुषी भवते कपटात्मने ॥40.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: ललित-भाव-विलास-हृत-आत्मभिः with their minds captivated by her lovely charm and graceful gestures, युवतिभिः by the young gopis, प्रतिरोद्धुम् to stop her, अपारिता who were unable, स्तनम् her breast, असौ she, भवन-अन्त-निषेदुषी sitting down inside the house, प्रददुषी gave, भवते to You, कपट-आत्मने who disguised Yourself as a helpless infant

Simple Meaning: Charmed and captivated by her exquisite beauty and graceful gestures, the young gopis were unable to stop her. Sitting down inside the house, she offered her breast to You, who had disguised Yourself as a helpless infant.

समधिरुह्य तदङ्कमशङ्कितस्त्वमथ बालकलोपनरोषितः ।
महदिवाम्रफलं कुचमण्डलं प्रतिचुचूषिथ दुर्विषदूषितम् ॥40.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: समधिरुह्य climbing upon, तत्-अङ्कम् her lap, अशङ्कितः without fear, त्वम् You, अथ then, बालक-लोपन-रोषितः angry at her killing of innocent infants, महत् large, इव like, आम्र-फलम् a mango fruit, कुच-मण्डलम् her breast, प्रतिचुचूषिथ sucked intensely, दुर्विष-दूषितम् smeared with deadly poison

Simple Meaning: Fearlessly climbing upon her lap, and enraged by her cruel destruction of innocent infants, You sucked intensely at her breast—smeared as it was with deadly poison—just like a child eagerly sucking a big ripe mango!

असुभिरेव समं धयति त्वयि स्तनमसौ स्तनितोपमनिस्वना ।
निरपतद्भयदायि निजं वपुः प्रतिगता प्रविसार्य भुजावुभौ ॥40.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: असुभिः एव along with her very life breath, समम् together, धयति drinking / sucking, त्वयि when You were, स्तनम् her breast, असौ she (Putana), स्तनित-उपम-निस्वना making a roar like thunder, निरपतत् fell down, भयदायि terrifying, निजम् her own, वपुः body, प्रतिगता reverting to, प्रविसार्य stretching out, भुजौ arms, उभौ both

Simple Meaning: As You sucked out her very life breath along with her milk, Putana let out a terrifying roar like thunder, reverted to her original monstrous body, and fell dead to the ground with both her arms stretched wide.

भयदघोषणभीषणविग्रहश्रवणदर्शनमोहितवल्लवे ।
व्रजपदे तदुरःस्थलखेलनं ननु भवन्तमगृह्णत गोपिकाः ॥40.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: भयद-घोषण-भीषण-विग्रह-श्रवण-दर्शन-मोहित-वल्लवे where the cowherds were stupefied upon hearing the terrifying roar and seeing her dreadful monstrous body, व्रज-पदे in that land of Vraja, तत्-उरःस्थल-खेलनम् playing on her chest, ननु indeed, भवन्तम् You, अगृह्णत took up / picked up, गोपिकाः the gopis

Simple Meaning: While the cowherds of Vraja stupefied at the sound of her terrifying roar and the sight of her colossal, hideous body, the gopis picked You up as You were fearlessly playing on her chest.

भुवनमङ्गलनामभिरेव ते युवतिभिर्बहुधा कृतरक्षणः ।
त्वमयि वातनिकेतननाथ मामगदयन् कुरु तावकसेवकम् ॥40.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: भुवन-मङ्गल-नामभिः with Your names that bring auspiciousness to the entire universe, एव indeed, ते Your, युवतिभिः by the young gopis, बहुधा in various ways, कृत-रक्षणः protection being performed, त्वम् You, अयि O, वातनिकेतन-नाथ Lord of Guruvayur, माम् me, अगदयन् making free from disease, कुरु make, तावक-सेवकम् Your humble servant

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Guruvayur! After rescuing You, the young gopis performed rituals for Your protection in various ways by chanting Your sacred names only, which bestow auspiciousness upon the whole universe. Please cure me of my illnesses and make me Your dedicated servant!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
