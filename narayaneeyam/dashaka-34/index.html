Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 34

The incarnation as Rama

(Rama Avatara)

गीर्वाणैरर्थ्यमानो दशमुखनिधनं कोसलेष्वृश्यशृङ्गे
पुत्रीयामिष्टिमिष्ट्वा ददुषि दशरथक्ष्माभृते पायसाग्र्यम् ।
तद्भुक्त्या तत्पुरन्ध्रीष्वपि तिसृषु समं जातगर्भासु जातो
रामस्त्वं लक्ष्मणेन स्वयमथ भरतेनापि शत्रुघ्ननाम्ना ॥34.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: गीर्वाणैः by the gods/devas, अर्थ्यमानः being prayed to/requested (to bring about), दश-मुख-निधनम् the destruction of the ten-headed rakshasa Ravana, कोसलेषु in the kingdom of Kosala, ऋश्यशृङ्गे when Rishyashringa, पुत्रीयाम् इष्टिम् the sacrifice for getting progeny (Putrakameshti Yajna), इष्ट्वा having performed, ददुषि offered, दशरथ-क्ष्माभृते unto King Dasharatha, पायस-अग्र्यम् the divine payasam/sacred porridge, तत्-भुक्त्या by the consumption of that, तत्-पुरन्ध्रीषु अपि and among his three wives, तिसृषु across the three of them, समम् equally, जात-गर्भासु who became pregnant, जातः were born, रामः त्वम् You as Rama, लक्ष्मणेन along with Lakshmana, स्वयम् Yourself, अथ then, भरतेन अपि along with Bharata, शत्रुघ्न-नाम्ना and with the one named Shatrughna

Simple Meaning: Earnestly prayed to by the devas to bring about the destruction of the ten-headed demon Ravana, You manifested in the kingdom of Kosala. When the sage Rishyashringa performed the sacred Putrakameshti Yajna for King Dasharatha and handed him the divine payasam, the king distributed it among his three queens. All three became pregnant, and You Yourself were born as Lord Rama, accompanied by Your divine expansions—Lakshmana, Bharata, and Shatrughna.

कोदण्डी कौशिकस्य क्रतुवरमवितुं लक्ष्मणेनानुयातो
यातोऽभूस्तातवाचा मुनिकथितमनुद्वन्द्वशान्ताध्वखेदः ।
नृणां त्राणाय बाणैर्मुनिवचनबलात्ताटकां पाटयित्वा
लब्ध्वास्मादस्त्रजालं मुनिवनमगमो देव सिद्धाश्रमाख्यम् ॥34.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: कोदण्डी bearing the mighty bow (Kodanda), कौशिकस्य of the sage Kaushika (Vishvamitra), क्रतु-वरम् the supreme Vedic sacrifice (Yajna), अवितुम् in order to protect, लक्ष्मणेन अनुयातः followed/accompanied by Lakshmana, यातः अभूः You departed, तात-वाचा by the command of Your father (King Dasharatha), मुनि-कथित-मनु-द्वन्द्व-शान्त-अध्व-खेदः whose fatigue of the journey was removed by the pair of sacred mantras (Bala and Atibala) taught by the sage, नृणाम् त्राणाय for the protection of humanity, बाणैः with Your deadly arrows, मुनि-वचन-बलात् on the strength of the sage's instruction, ताटकाम् the rakshasi Tataka, पाटयित्वा tearing apart/striking down, लब्ध्वा receiving, अस्मात् from him (Vishvamitra), अस्त्र-जालम् an array of divine missiles (Astras), मुनि-वनम्-अगमः You reached the holy hermitage, देव O Supreme Lord!, सिद्धाश्रम-आख्यम् known by the name Siddhashrama

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Lord! Bearing Your formidable bow Kodanda, You set forth at Your father's command alongside Lakshmana to protect the grand sacrifice of Sage Vishvamitra. The sage imparted to You the twin sacred mantras (Bala and Atibala), completely dispelling the weariness of Your journey. Prompted by Vishvamitra's word and acting for the protection of humanity, You struck down the fierce rakshasi Tataka with Your arrows. Receiving an arsenal of divine missiles (Astras) from the delighted sage in return, You arrived at his sacred hermitage known as Siddhashrama.

मारीचं द्रावयित्वा मखशिरसि शरैरन्यरक्षांसि निघ्नन्
कल्यां कुर्वन्नहल्यां पथि पदरजसा प्राप्य वैदेहगेहम् ।
भिन्दानश्चान्द्रचूडं धनुरवनिसुतामिन्दिरामेव लब्ध्वा
राज्यं प्रातिष्ठथास्त्वं त्रिभिरपि च समं भ्रातृवीरैस्सदारैः ॥34.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: मारीचम् the rakshasa Maricha, द्रावयित्वा driving away/hurling far into the ocean, मख-शिरसि at the altar/head of the sacrifice, शरैः with Your arrows, अन्य-रक्षांसि the other demons (Subahu and his hordes), निघ्नन् slaying/destroying, कल्याम् pure/free from curse/restored to life, कुर्वन् making, अहल्याम् Ahalya, पथि on the way, पद-रजसा by the dust of Your lotus feet, प्राप्य reaching, वैदेह-गेहम् the palace of King Janaka (the Lord of Videha), भिन्दानः च and snapping/breaking, चान्द्र-चूडम् belonging to Shiva (who wears the moon on his crest), धनुः the bow, अवनि-सुताम् the daughter of the earth (Sita), इन्दिराम् एव who was Lakshmi Herself, लब्ध्वा obtaining/marrying, राज्यम् to Your kingdom (Ayodhya), प्रातिष्ठथाः त्वम् You set forth/departed, त्रिभिः अपि च समम् along with all three, भ्रातृ-वीरैः heroic brothers, सदारैः accompanied by their respective brides

Simple Meaning: At the altar of Vishvamitra's sacrifice, You hurled Maricha far into the ocean with a single arrow and destroyed the other rakshasas led by Subahu. En route to Mithila, the dust of Your lotus feet redeemed Ahalya, restoring her to life from her stone curse. Arriving at the palace of King Janaka, You effortlessly broke the mighty bow of Lord Shiva and won the hand of Sita—Goddess Lakshmi Herself. Returning triumphantly to Ayodhya, You traveled in joy accompanied by Your three heroic brothers, each united with their brides.

आरुन्धाने रुषान्धे भृगुकुलतिलके संक्रमय्य स्वतेजो
याते यातोऽस्ययोध्यां सुखमिह निवसन् कान्तया कान्तमूर्ते ।
शत्रुघ्नेनैकदाथो गतवति भरते मातुलस्याधिवासं
तातारब्धोऽभिषेकस्तव किल विहतः केकयाधीशपुत्र्या ॥34.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: आरुन्धाने when blocking the path, रुषा-अन्धे blinded by anger, भृगुकुल-तिलके Parasurama, the gem of the Bhrigu dynasty, संक्रमय्य transferring/absorbing, स्व-तेजः His divine power/radiance, याते when he departed, यातः असि You returned, अयोध्याम् to Ayodhya, सुखम् in happiness/peace, इह here, निवसन् dwelling, कान्तया with Your beloved (Sita), कान्त-मूर्ते O Lord of captivating beauty!, शत्रुघ्नेन एकादा अथ once, when along with Shatrughna, गतवति had left, भरते Bharata, मातुलस्य अधिवासम् to the abode of his maternal uncle, तात-आरब्धः initiated by Your father (King Dasharatha), अभिषेकः coronation, तव Your, किल indeed, विहतः was obstructed/thwarted, केकयाधीश-पुत्र्या by Kaikeyi, the daughter of the king of Kekaya

Simple Meaning: O Lord of enchanting beauty! On Your way back, Parasurama—the pride of the Bhrigu clan—confronted You in a blind rage, blocking Your path. After You absorbed his divine potency into Yourself, he departed peacefully, and You returned safely to Ayodhya to live in bliss with Your beloved Sita. Later, while Bharata and Shatrughna were away visiting their maternal uncle, King Dasharatha made grand preparations for Your royal coronation. However, this was thwarted by Queen Kaikeyi, the daughter of the king of Kekaya.

तातोक्त्या यातुकामो वनमनुजवधूसंयुतश्चापधारः
पौरानारुध्य मार्गे गुहनिलयगतस्त्वं जटाचीरधारी।
नावा सन्तीर्य गङ्गामधिपदवि पुनस्तं भरद्वाजमारात्
नत्वा तद्वाक्यहेतोरतिसुखमवसश्चित्रकूटे गिरीन्द्रे ॥34.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: तात्-उक्त्या by the command of Your father (Dasharatha), यातु-कामः desiring/preparing to depart, वनम् for the forest, अनुज-वधू-संयुतः accompanied by Your younger brother (Lakshmana) and Your wife (Sita), चाप-धारः bearing bows and arrows, पौरान् the citizens of Ayodhya, आरुध्य stopping/leaving behind (while they slept), मार्गे along the path, गुह-निलय-गतः त्वम् You reached the abode of Guha (Sringaverapura), जटा-चीर-धारी wearing matted hair and bark garments, नावा by boat, सन्तीर्य crossing over, गङ्गाम् the holy river Ganga, अधि-पदवि further along the path, पुनः again, तम् भरद्वाजम् unto that Sage Bharadvaja, आरात् nearby, नत्वा bowing down/paying obeisance, तत्-वाक्य-हेतोः on the advice/guidance of his word, अति-सुखम्-अवसः You lived in supreme happiness and tranquility, चित्रकूट-गिरि-इन्द्रे on the glorious king of mountains, Chitrakoota

Simple Meaning: Determined to uphold Your father's word, You departed for the forest accompanied by Sita and Lakshmana, bearing Your bows. Leaving the grieving citizens of Ayodhya behind as they slept, You reached the dwelling of Your devoted friend Guha, where You donned bark garments and matted hair. Crossing the sacred river Ganga by boat, You approached Sage Bharadvaja's hermitage along the way. Following his wise counsel, You made Your abode on the serene mountain Chitrakoota, living there in deep peace and joy.

श्रुत्वा पुत्रार्तिखिन्नं खलु भरतमुखात् स्वर्गयातं स्वतातं
तप्तो दत्वाऽम्बु तस्मै निदधिथ भरते पादुकां मेदिनीं च।
अत्रिं नत्वाऽथ गत्वा वनमतिविपुलं दण्डकं चण्डकायं
हत्वा दैत्यं विराधं सुगतिमकलयश्चारु भोः शारभङ्गीम् ॥34.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रुत्वा hearing, पुत्र-आर्ति-खिन्नम् grief-stricken due to sorrow for his son (Rama), खलु indeed, भरत-मुखात् from the lips of Bharata, स्वर्ग-यातम् departed to heaven (passed away), स्व-तातम् Your own father (King Dasharatha), तप्तः deeply pained/overwhelmed with grief, दत्वा अम्बु offering sacred water oblations (Tarpana), तस्मै unto him, निदधिथ You entrusted/placed, भरते in Bharata, पादुकम् the divine sandals, मेदिनीम् च and the governance of the earth (the kingdom), अत्रिम् नत्वा bowing down to Sage Atri, अथ then, गत्वा entering, वनम् अति-विपुलम् the vast forest, दण्डकम् Dandaka, चण्ड-कायम् having a fierce, monstrous body, हत्वा slaying, दैत्यम् विराधम् the daitya Viradha, सुगतिम-अकलयः You bestowed a glorious destination/liberation (Sugati), चारु O charming Lord!, भोः Oh!, शारभङ्गीम् to the sage Sharabhanga

Simple Meaning: Hearing from Bharata's own lips that Your father, King Dasharatha, had passed away in agony over his separation from You, You were overcome with deep grief and offered sacred water oblations (Tarpana) for his soul. Entrusting the kingdom and Your divine sandals (Padukas) to Bharata to rule in Your place, You journeyed forward.

After paying Your respects to Sage Atri, You entered the vast and formidable Dandaka forest. There, You slew the terrifying daitya Viradha, who possessed a colossal body, and later bestowed ultimate liberation (Sugati) upon the revered Sage Sharabhanga.

नत्वाऽगस्त्यं समस्ताशरनिकरसपत्राकृतिं तापसेभ्यः
प्रत्यश्रौषीः प्रियैषी तदनु च मुनिना वैष्णवे दिव्यचापे ।
ब्रह्मास्त्रे चापि दत्ते पथि पितृसुहृदं वीक्ष्य भूयो जटायुं
मोदात् गोदातटान्ते परिरमसि पुरा पञ्चवट्यां वधूट्या ॥34.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: नत्वा bowing down/offering obeisance to, अगस्त्यम् unto Sage Agastya, समस्त-आशर-निकर-सपत्राकृतिम् complete destruction/feathering with arrows of the entire host of asuras (asura-nikara), तापसेभ्यः unto the ascetics/sages, प्रत्यश्रौषीः You promised/vowed, प्रिय-एषी seeking their welfare, तदनु thereafter, च and, मुनिना by the sage (Agastya), वैष्णवे the divine bow belonging to Lord Vishnu, दिव्य-चापे the celestial bow, ब्रह्मास्त्रे च अपि and also the invincible Brahmastra missile, दत्ते being presented/gifted, पथि on the path, पितृ-सुहृदम् the dear friend of Your father (Dasharatha), वीक्ष्य seeing, भूयः again, जटायुम् the great vulture Jatayu, मोदात् with great joy, गोदा-तट-अन्ते on the banks of the river Godavari, परिरमसि You dwelt happily, पुरा in times past, पञ्चवट्यां in Panchavati, वधूट्या along with Your young bride (Sita)

Simple Meaning: Bowing in reverence to Sage Agastya and seeking the welfare of the holy ascetics, You vowed to utterly destroy all the asura hordes that plagued the forest. Pleased, the sage presented You with the celestial bow of Lord Vishnu and the mighty Brahmastra. Journeying onward, You met Your father’s beloved friend, the noble vulture King Jatayu. Setting up camp at Panchavati on the serene banks of the river Godavari, You dwelt in supreme bliss alongside Your beloved Sita.

प्राप्तायाः शूर्पणख्या मदनचलधृतेरर्थनैर्निस्सहात्मा
तां सौमित्रौ विसृज्य प्रबलतमरुषा तेन निर्लूननासाम् ।
दृष्ट्वैनां रुष्टचित्तं खरमभिपतितं दूषणं च त्रिमूर्धं
व्याहिंसीराशरानप्ययुतसमधिकांस्तत्क्षणादक्षतोष्मा ॥34.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्राप्तायाः of the one who arrived, शूर्पणख्याः Shurpanakha, मदन-चल-धृतेः whose self-control was shaken by lust/desire, अर्थनैः by her persistent entreaties and advances, निस्सहात्मा being unable to tolerate/exhausted, ताम् her, सौमित्रौ to the son of Sumitra (Lakshmana), विसृज्य redirecting/sending away, प्रबलतम-रुषा with intense anger, तेन by him (Lakshmana), निर्लून-नासाम् whose nose (and ears) were cut off, दृष्ट्वा seeing, एनाम् her (in this disfigured state), रुष्ट-चित्तम् enraged in mind, खरम् Khara, अभिपतितम् who rushed to attack, दूषणम् च Dushana, त्रिमूर्धम् and Trishiras, व्याहिंसीः You destroyed/annihilated, आशरान् अपि and the asura forces, अयुत-समधिकान् exceeding ten thousand (14,000 asuras), तत्क्षणात् instantly, अक्षत-ऊष्मा with Your prowess/radiant power unblemished and unwaning

Simple Meaning: When Surpanakha approached You, her composure completely shattered by lust, You grew tired of her relentless advances and directed her to Lakshmana. Enraged by her aggressiveness, Lakshmana severed her nose and ears.

Seeing her disfigured state, the asura commander Khara, along with Dushana and Trishiras, flew into a rage and led a fierce assault against You. Yet, with Your undiminished valor, You single-handedly destroyed all three generals and their army of over fourteen thousand asura in an instant.

सोदर्याप्रोक्तवार्ताविवशदशमुखादिष्टमारीचमाया-
सारङ्ग सारसाक्ष्या स्पृहितमनुगतः प्रावधीर्बाणघातम् ।
तन्मायाक्रन्दनिर्यापितभवदनुजां रावणस्तामहार्षीत्
तेनार्तोऽपि त्वमन्तः किमपि मुदमधास्तद्वधोपायलाभात् ॥34.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: सोदर्या-प्रोक्त-वार्ता-विवश-दशमुख-आदिष्ट-मारीच-माया-सारङ्गम् the magical deer Maricha sent by the ten-headed Ravana who was overcome by the report given by his sister, सारसाक्ष्या by the lotus-eyed Sita, स्पृहितम् desired, अनुगतः chasing/following after, प्रावधीः You killed, बाण-घातम् with a stroke of Your arrow, तत्-माया-क्रन्द-निर्यापित-भवद्-अनुजाम् Your younger brother who was dispatched away by his fake distress call, रावणः Ravana, ताम् her (Sita), अहार्षीत् abducted, तेन by that incident, आर्तः अपि even though sorrowful, त्वम् You, अन्तः inside Your heart, किमपि a certain, मुदम् joy, अधाः felt/experienced, तद्-वध-उपाय-लाभात् because You found a way to bring about his destruction

Simple Meaning: Overcome by the news brought by his sister Surpanakha, the ten-headed Ravana ordered Maricha to take the form of a magical deer. Seeing that golden deer, the lotus-eyed Sita desired it, and You pursued it, eventually killing it with an arrow. Using Maricha's deceptive cries of distress, Ravana managed to lure Your younger brother Lakshmana away from the hermitage and abducted Sita. Though You appeared deeply grieved by her loss, in Your heart You felt a subtle joy, knowing that this event provided the very opportunity and means for Ravana's eventual destruction.

भूयस्तन्वीं विचिन्वन्नहृत दशमुखस्त्वद्वधूं मद्वधेन
इत्युक्त्वा याते जटायौ दिवमथ सुहृदः प्रातनोः प्रेतकार्यम् ।
गृह्णानं तं कबन्धं जघनिथ शबरीं प्रेक्ष्य पम्पातटे त्वं
सम्प्राप्तो वातसूनुं भृशमुदितमनाः पाहि वातालयेश ॥34.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूयः further, तन्वीम् the slender-waisted one (Sita), विचिन्वन् searching for, अहृतः abducted, दशमुखः Ravana, त्वत्-वधूम् Your wife, मत्-वधेन while trying to kill me, इति उक्त्वा saying thus, याते having departed, जटायौ Jatayu, दिवम् to heaven, अथ then, सुहृदः of Your friend, प्रातनोः You performed, प्रेत-कार्यम् the funeral rites, गृह्णानम् who was seizing/grabbing, तम् him, कबन्धम् Kabandha, जघनिथ You killed, शबरीम् Shabari, प्रेक्ष्य meeting/seeing, पम्पा-तटे on the banks of Pampa lake, त्वम् You, सम्प्राप्तः met/reached, वातसूनुम् Hanuman (the son of the wind god), भृशम्-उदित-मनाः with a mind greatly delighted, पाहि protect me, वातालयेश O Lord of Guruvayur

Simple Meaning: Continuing Your search for the slender Sita, You met the dying Jatayu, who informed You that Ravana had abducted Your wife after mortally wounding him in battle. When Jatayu passed away to heaven, You performed the sacred funeral rites for Your noble friend. Moving forward, You slain the rakshasa Kabandha who tried to catch You, met the pious devotee Shabari on the banks of Pampa lake, and eventually met Hanuman, the son of the wind god, to Your great delight. O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect me.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
