The Story of Dhruva

दशकम् १७

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 17

The Story of Dhruva

(छन्दस् - वसन्ततिलका)

उत्तानपादनृपतेर्मनुनन्दनस्य
जाया बभूव सुरुचिर्नितरामभीष्टा ।
अन्या सुनीतिरिति भर्तुरनादृता सा
त्वामेव नित्यमगतिः शरणं गताऽभूत् ॥ 17.1 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: उत्तानपाद-नृपतेः of King Uttanapada, मनु-नन्दनस्य the son of Manu, जाया wife, बभूव was, सुरुचिः Suruchi, नितराम् अभीष्टा highly beloved, अन्या the other, सुनीतिः इति named Suniti, भर्तुः by her husband, अनादृता neglected/unloved, सा she, त्वाम् एव You alone, नित्यम् constantly, अगतिः having no other resort, शरणम् गता अभूत् took refuge in

Simple Meaning: King Uttanapada, the son of Manu, had a wife named Suruchi whom he loved dearly. His other wife, Suniti, was completely neglected by him. Having no other helpless resort, Suniti took refuge in You alone at all times.

अङ्के पितुः सुरुचिपुत्रकमुत्तमं तं
दृष्ट्वा ध्रुवः किल सुनीतिसुतोऽधिरोक्ष्यन् ।
आचिक्षिपे किल शिशुः सुतरां सुरुच्या
दुस्सन्त्यजा खलु भवद्विमुखैरसूया ॥ 17.2 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: अङ्के in the lap, पितुः of the father, सुरुचि-पुत्रकम् the young son of Suruchi, उत्तमम् Uttama, तम् him, दृष्ट्वा seeing, ध्रुवः Dhruva, किल indeed, सुनीति-सुतः the son of Suniti, अधिरोक्ष्यन् desiring to climb, आचिक्षिपे was harshly cast away/scolded, किल indeed, शिशुः child, सुतराम् severely, सुरुच्या by Suruchi, दुस्सन्त्यजा hard to abandon, खलु indeed, भवत्-विमुखैः by those averse to You, असूया jealousy/envy

Simple Meaning: Seeing Suruchi's young son, Uttama, sitting lovingly on his father's lap, the child Dhruva—the son of Suniti—also wished to climb up. But he was harshly scolded and pushed away by Suruchi. Truly, envy is impossible to overcome for those who turn their faces away from You!

त्वन्मोहिते पितरि पश्यति दारवश्ये
दूरं दुरुक्तिनिहतः स गतो निजाम्बाम् ।
साऽपि स्वकर्मगतिसन्तरणाय पुंसां
त्वत्पादमेव शरणं शिशवे शशंस ॥ 17.3 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-मोहिते deluded by Your Maya, पितरि while his father, पश्यति looked on, दार-वश्ये submissive to his wife, दूरम् far away, दुरुक्ति-निहतः deeply hurt by harsh words, सः he (Dhruva), गतः went, निज-अम्बाम् to his own mother, सा अपि she too, स्व-कर्म-गति-सन्तरणाय for crossing over the consequences of one's own karma, पुंसाम् for human beings, त्वत्-पादम् एव Your lotus feet alone, शरणम् as refuge, शिशवे to the child, शशंस recommended/declared

Simple Meaning: While his father—completely submissive to his beloved wife and deluded by Your illusion—looked on helplessly, little Dhruva was deeply wounded by Suruchi's harsh words and went weeping to his mother Suniti. Even in her distress, she advised the young child that for anyone seeking to overcome the bonds of karma, devotion to Your lotus feet is the only true refuge!

आकर्ण्य सोऽपि भवदर्चननिश्चितात्मा
मानी निरेत्य नगरात् किल पञ्चवर्षः ।
सन्दृष्टनारदनिवेदितमन्त्रमार्गः
त्वामरराध तपसा मधुकाननान्ते ॥ 17.4 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: आकर्ण्य hearing (this), सः अपि he too, भवत्-अर्चन-निश्चितात्मा with his mind firmly resolved on worshipping You, मानी proud-spirited/high-minded, निरेत्य leaving, नगरात् from the city, किल indeed, पञ्च-वर्षः five years old, सन्दृष्ट-नारद-निवेदित-मन्त्र-मार्गः instructed in the path of sacred mantras by sage Narada who appeared before him, त्वाम् You, आरराध worshipped/propitiated, तपसा through penance, मधु-कानन-अन्ते at the forest of Madhuvan

Simple Meaning: Upon hearing his mother's advice, the high-minded child Dhruva—barely five years old—resolved firmly to worship You. He left the city and journeyed into the forest. Met along the way by Great Sage Narada, who instructed him in the sacred path of divine mantras, Dhruva engaged in intense penance at Madhuvan to propitiate You!

ताते विषण्णहृदये नगरीं गतेन
श्रीनारदेन परिसान्त्वितचित्तवृत्तौ ।
बालस्त्वदर्पितमनाः क्रमवर्धितेन
निन्ये कठोरतपसा किल पञ्चमासान् ॥ 17.5 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताते while his father, विषण्ण-हृदये was sorrowful at heart, नगरीम् to the city, गतेन went, श्री-नारदेन by Sage Narada, परिसान्त्वित-चित्त-वृत्तौ whose mind was comforted, बालः the young child (Dhruva), त्वत्-अर्पित-मनाः with his mind surrendered unto You, क्रम-वर्धितेन with steadily increasing severity, निन्ये spent/passed, कठोर-तपसा through intense penance, किल indeed, पञ्च-मासान् five months

Simple Meaning: Meanwhile, King Uttanapada was filled with deep grief and regret over Dhruva's departure. Sage Narada visited the capital city and consoled the sorrowful king. Away in the forest, young Dhruva, his mind completely absorbed in devotion to You, passed five months performing penances that grew progressively more intense!

तावत्तपोबलनिरुच्छ्-वसिते दिगन्ते
देवार्थितस्त्वमुदयत्करुणार्द्रचेताः ।
त्वद्रूपचिद्रसनिलीनमतेः पुरस्तात्
आविर्बभूविथ विभो गरुडाधिरूढः ॥ 17.6 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: तावत् meanwhile, तपः-बल-निरुच्छ्वसिते suffocated by the power of his penance, दिगन्ते the cosmic quarters/universe, देव-अर्थितः prayed to by the devas, त्वम् You, उदयत्-करुणा-आर्द-चेताः with a heart melting with surging compassion, त्वत्-रूप-चित्-रस-निलीन-मतेः whose mind was completely merged in the bliss of Your divine form, पुरस्तात् in front of, आविर्बभूविथ manifested/appeared, विभो O All-Pervading Lord!, गरुड-अधिरूढः mounted on Garuda

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord! As Dhruva's fiery penance grew so intense that the entire universe felt suffocated, the frightened devas rushed to seek Your protection. With Your heart overflowing with divine compassion, You rode mounted on Garuda and manifested directly before the young child, whose mind was deeply absorbed in the sublime bliss of Your holy form!

त्वद्दर्शनप्रमदभारतरङ्गितं तं
दृग्भ्यां निमग्नमिव रूपरसायने ते ।
तुष्टूषमाणमवगम्य कपोलदेशे
संस्पृष्टवानसि दरेण तथाऽऽदरेण ॥ 17.7 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-दर्शन-प्रमद-भार-तरङ्गितम् him who was overwhelmed by surging waves of supreme bliss upon seeing You, तम् him, दृग्भ्याम् with his eyes, निमग्नम् इव as if completely submerged, रूप-रसायने in the sweet nectar of Your glorious form, ते Your, तुष्टूषमाणम् desiring to sing Your praises, अवगम्य understanding, कपोल-देशे on his cheek, संस्पृष्टवान् असि You touched, दरेण with Your holy conch (Panchajanya), तथा आदरेण with immense affection

Simple Meaning: Beholding Your exquisite form, young Dhruva was completely overwhelmed by waves of ecstatic joy, staring intently as though his eyes were drinking in pure nectar. Knowing that the innocent child wished to hymn Your glory but did not know how, You gently and lovingly touched his cheek with Your holy conch, Panchajanya!

तावद्विबोधविमलं प्रणुवन्तमेनम्
आभाषथास्त्वमवगम्य तदीयभावम् ।
राज्यं चिरं समनुभूय भजस्व भूयः
सर्वोत्तरं ध्रुव पदं विनिवृत्तिहीनम् ॥ 17.8 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: तावत् then/immediately, विबोध-विमलम् pure with divine knowledge, प्रणुवन्तम् singing praises, एनम् him (Dhruva), आभाषथाः You spoke to, त्वम् You, अवगम्य understanding, तदीय-भावम् his innermost desire/heart's wish, राज्यम् the kingdom, चिरम् for a long time, समनुभूय having enjoyed, भजस्व attain, भूयः thereafter, सर्वोत्तरम् supreme above all, ध्रुव O Dhruva/eternal, पदम् abode, विनिवृत्ति-हीनम् free from return (imperishable)

Simple Meaning: Imbued with pure divine wisdom through the touch of Your conch, young Dhruva offered heartfelt prayers unto You. Understanding his inner thoughts, You affectionately blessed him: "O Dhruva! Rule the kingdom righteously for a long duration, and after that, reach My supreme, eternal abode from which no one ever returns!"

इत्यूचिषि त्वयि गते नृपनन्दनोऽसा-
वानन्दिताखिलजनो नगरीमुपेतः ।
रेमे चिरं भवदनुग्रहपूर्णकामः
ताते गते च वनमादृतराज्यभारः ॥ 17.9 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति ऊचिषि having thus spoken, त्वयि when You, गते had departed, नृप-नन्दनः असौ that prince (Dhruva), आनन्दित-अखिल-जनः making all the people joyful, नगरीम्-उपेतः reached the city, रेमे spent time happily, चिरम् for a long time, भवत्-अनुग्रह-पूर्ण-कामः having his desires fulfilled by Your grace, ताते when his father, गते departed, च and, वनम् to the forest, आदृत-राज्य-भारः taking up the responsibility of governing the kingdom

Simple Meaning: After You departed having blessed him, the young prince returned to the capital city, bringing immense joy to all the citizens. With all his desires completely fulfilled by Your grace, he eventually took over the reins of governance when his father retired to the forest, and ruled the kingdom happily for a long duration.

यक्षेण देव निहते पुनरुत्तमेऽस्मिन्
यक्षैः स युद्धनिरतो विरतो मनूक्त्या ।
शान्त्या प्रसन्नहृदयाद्धनदादुपेतात्
त्वद्भक्तिमेव सुदृढामवृणोन्महात्मा ॥ 17.10 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: यक्षेण by a Yaksha, देव O Lord!, निहते when slain, पुनः furthermore, उत्तमे in Uttama, अस्मिन् this, यक्षैः with the Yakshas, सः he (Dhruva), युद्ध-निरतः engaged in battle, विरतः desisted/stopped, मनु-उक्त्या on the counsel of Manu, शान्त्या by peace/forbearance, प्रसन्न-हृदयात् from the pleased heart, धनदात् from Kubera (the giver of wealth), उपेतात् who approached, त्वत्-भक्तिम् एव devotion to You alone, सुदृढाम् unwavering/firm, अवृणोत् chose/prayed for, महात्मा the noble-souled Dhruva

Simple Meaning: O Lord! When his stepbrother Uttama was killed by a Yaksha, Dhruva waged a fierce war against the Yakshas. However, heeding the wise counsel of his grandfather Svayambhuva Manu, he desisted from the battle with forbearance. Pleased by his conduct, Kubera, the lord of wealth, appeared before him and offered him boons. The noble Dhruva asked for nothing other than unwavering and firm devotion unto You!

अन्ते भवत्पुरुषनीतविमानयातो
मात्रा समं ध्रुवपदे मुदितोऽयमास्ते ।
एवं स्वभृत्यजनपालनलोलधीस्त्वं
वातालयाधिप निरुन्धि ममामयौघान् ॥ 17.11 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्ते at the end, भवत्-पुरुष-नीत-विमान-यातः traveling on a celestial vehicle brought by Your attendants (Vishnudutas), मात्रा समम् along with his mother, ध्रुव-पदे in the eternal realm of Dhruva-loka, मुदितः अयम् आस्ते he abides joyfully, एवम् in this manner, स्व-भृत्य-जन-पालन-लोल-धीः त्वम् You who are ever eager to protect Your devotees, वातालय-अधिप O Lord of Guruvayur!, निरुन्धि eradicate/destroy, मम आमय-ओघान् the multitude of my diseases/afflictions

Simple Meaning: At the end of his glorious earthly life, Dhruva ascended to the imperishable realm of Dhruva-loka on a divine aerial chariot brought by Your own messengers, where he now dwells in supreme bliss alongside his mother Suniti. O Lord of Guruvayur, who are ever-eager and compassionate in nurturing and protecting Your devotees, please completely eradicate all my diseases and afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
