Dashaka 77

Krishna bestows grace on Trivakra. Jarasandha’s invasion

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 77

Krishna bestows grace on Trivakra.

Jarasandha’s invasion

सैरन्ध्र्यास्तदनु चिरं स्मरातुराया
यातोऽभूः सुललितमुद्धवेन सार्धम् ।
आवासं त्वदुपगमोत्सवं सदैव
ध्यायन्त्याः प्रतिदिनवाससज्जिकायाः ॥77.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: सैरन्ध्र्याः of Trivakra (Kubja, the maidservant), तदनु thereafter, चिरम् for a long time, स्मर-आतुरायाः who was deeply afflicted with longing and devotion for You, यातः अभूः You went, सुललितम् charmingly, उद्धवेन सार्धम् along with Uddhava, आवासम् to the residence, त्वत्-उपगम-उत्सवम् the blissful celebration of Your visit, सदैव always, ध्यायन्त्याः contemplating upon, प्रति-दिन-वास-सज्जिकायाः who was adorning herself and preparing her home every single day

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, You graciously went to the home of Trivakra (Kubja) accompanied by Your dear friend Uddhava. For a long time, her heart had been filled with devotion and longing for You. Living in constant anticipation of the joyous occasion of Your visit, she had been decorating her house and preparing herself every single day.

उपगते त्वयि पूर्णमनोरथां प्रमदसम्भ्रमकम्प्रपयोधराम् ।
विविधमाननमादधतीं मुदा रहसि तां रमयाञ्चकृषे सुखम् ॥77.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: उपगते त्वयि upon Your arrival, पूर्ण-मनोरथाम् whose desires were completely fulfilled, प्रमद-सम्भ्रम-कम्प्र-पयोधराम् whose breasts trembled with intense delight, विविध-माननम् varied honors, आदधतीम् offering, मुदा with happiness, रहसि in solitude, ताम् her, रमयाञ्चकृषे You gladdened, सुखम् delightfully

Simple Meaning: Upon Your arrival, her desires were completely fulfilled. Trembling with intense delight and excitement, she offered You various honors and warm hospitality with immense joy. In that private setting, You delightfully brought her complete bliss and gratification.

पृष्टा वरं पुनरसाववृणोद्वराकी
भूयस्त्वया सुरतमेव निशान्तरेषु ।
सायुज्यमस्त्विति वदेत् बुध एव कामं
सामीप्यमस्त्वनिशमित्यपि नाब्रवीत् किम् ॥77.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: पृष्टा being asked, वरम् for a boon, पुनः again, असौ she, वराकी poor woman, ववृणोत् chose, भूयः again, त्वया with You, सुरतम् एव union and intimacy only, निशान्तरेषु during the nights, सायुज्यम् अस्तु let there be merging in You, इति thus, वदेत् should ask, बुधः एव a wise person only, कामम् indeed, सामीप्यम् अस्तु let there be proximity or closeness, अनिशम् इति अपि continuously thus also, न अब्रवीत् किम् did she not even say

Simple Meaning: When asked to choose a boon, that poor woman merely requested future intimacy and union with You on other nights. Indeed, only a wise person would ask for final liberation or merging with You. But why did she not even ask for constant proximity to You?

ततो भवान् देव निशासु कासुचिन्मृगीदृशं तां निभृतं विनोदयन् ।
अदादुपश्लोक इति श्रुतं सुतं स नारदात् सात्त्वततन्त्रविद्बबभौ ॥77.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः thereafter, भवान् You, देव O Lord, निशासु कासुचित् on certain nights, मृगीदृशम् ताम् that doe-eyed lady, निभृतम् in secret, विनोदयन् delighting, अदात् granted, उपश्लोकः इति known as Upashloka, श्रुतम् famous, सुतम् a son, सः he, नारदात् from Narada, सात्त्वत-तन्त्र-वित् knower of the Sattvata Tantra, बबभौ became or shone

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, O Lord, delighting that doe-eyed lady in secret on certain nights, You blessed her with a renowned son named Upashloka. By learning from Sage Narada, he became deeply versed in the sacred Sattvata Tantra scriptures.

अक्रूरमन्दिरमितोऽथ बलोद्धवाभ्याम्
अभ्यर्चितो बहु नुतो मुदितेन तेन ।
एनं विसृज्य विपिनागतपाण्डवेय-
वृत्तं विवेदिथ तथा धृतराष्ट्र्चेष्टाम् ॥77.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: अक्रूर-मन्दिरम् इतः going to the house of Akrura, अथ thereafter, बल-उद्धवाभ्याम् along with Balarama and Uddhava, अभ्यर्चितः worshipped, बहु widely, नुतः praised, मुदितेन-तेन by him who was overjoyed, एनम् him, विसृज्य dispatching, विपिन-आगत-पाण्डवेय-वृत्तम् news of the Pandavas who had returned from the forest, विवेदिथ You learned, तथा as well as, धृतराष्ट्र-चेष्टाम् the actions of Dhritarashtra

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, You went to the house of Akrura along with Balarama and Uddhava. Overjoyed, Akrura worshipped and praised You greatly. Sending him to Hastinapura, You learned through him about the condition of the Pandavas who had returned from the forest, as well as the activities of Dhritarashtra.

विघाताज्जामातुः परमसुहृदो भोजनृपतेः
जरासन्धे रुन्धत्यनवधिरुषान्धेऽथ मथुराम् ।
रथाद्यैर्द्योर्लब्धैः कतिपयबलस्त्वं बलयुतः
त्रयोविंशत्यक्षौहिणि तदुपनीतं समहृथाः ॥77.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: विघातात् due to the killing, जामातुः of his son-in-law, परम-सुहृदः dear friend, भोज-नृपतेः King of the Bhojas - Kamsa, जरासन्धे when Jarasandha, अनवधि-रुषा-अन्धे blinded by boundless rage, अथ then, रुन्धति besieged, मथुराम् Mathura, रथ-आद्यैः with chariots and other divine weapons, द्योः लब्धैः obtained from the heavens, कतिपय-बलः possessing a small force, त्वम् You, बल-युतः accompanied by Balarama, त्रयोविंशति-अक्षौहिणि consisting of twenty-three Akshauhinis, तत्-उपनीतम् the army brought by him, समहृथाः You destroyed

Simple Meaning: When Jarasandha, blinded by boundless anger over the death of his son-in-law and dear friend Kamsa, the king of the Bhojas, laid siege to Mathura, You and Balarama stood with only a small force. Equipped with divine chariots and weapons sent down from heaven, You wiped out the entire twenty-three Akshauhini army that he had brought against You.

बद्धं बलादथ बलेन बलोत्तरं त्वं
भूयो बलोद्यमरसेन मुमोचिथैनम् ।
निश्शेषदिग्जयसमाहृतविश्वसैन्यात्
कोऽन्यस्ततो हि बलपौरुषवांस्तदानीम् ॥77.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: बद्धम् bound, बलात् by force, अथ then, बलेन by Balarama, बल-उत्तरम् superior in strength, त्वम् You, भूयः again, बल-उद्यम-रसेन out of eagerness to engage his forces again, मुमोचिथ-एनम् released him, निश्शेष-दिग्जय-समाहृत-विश्व-सैन्यात् from him who had gathered all the armies of the world through complete conquests of all directions, कः अन्यः who else, ततः हि than him indeed, बल-पौरुषवान् possessing power and heroism, तदानीम् at that time

Simple Meaning: When Balarama forcibly bound Jarasandha, who possessed immense strength, You released him out of a desire to engage his forces again in future battles. At that time, who else could compare in power and heroism to Jarasandha, who had brought together armies from across the entire world through his conquests?

भग्नः स लग्नहृदयोऽपि नृपैः प्रणुन्नो
युद्धं त्वया व्यधित षोडशकृत्व एवम् ।
अक्षौहिणीः शिव शिवास्य जघन्थ विष्णो
सम्भूय सैकनवतित्रिशतं तदानीम् ॥77.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: भग्नः defeated, सः he, लग्न-हृदयः अपि though heartbroken, नृपैः by other kings, प्रणुन्नः encouraged, युद्धम् battle, त्वया with You, व्यधित fought, षोडश-कृत्वः sixteen times, एवम् in this manner, अक्षौहिणीः Akshauhinis, शिव शिव what a wonder!, अस्य his, जघन्थ You destroyed, विष्णो O Vishnu, सम्भूय totaling, स-एक-नवति-त्रि-शतम् three hundred and ninety-one, तदानीम् during those times

Simple Meaning: Though defeated and heartbroken, Jarasandha was urged on by other kings and fought with You sixteen more times in this manner. O Lord Vishnu, in total You destroyed three hundred and ninety-one Akshauhinis of his army during those encounters. What a wonder!

अष्टादशेऽस्य समरे समुपेयुषि त्वं
दृष्ट्वा पुरोऽथ यवनं यवनत्रिकोट्या ।
त्वष्ट्रा विधाप्य पुरमाशु पयोधिमध्ये
तत्राऽथ योगबलतः स्वजनाननैषीः ॥77.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: अष्टादशे अस्य समरे when his eighteenth battle, समुपेयुषि was approaching, त्वम् You, दृष्ट्वा seeing, पुरः ahead, अथ then, यवनम् the Yavana king Kalayavana, यवन-त्रि-कोट्या with three crores of Yavana warriors, त्वष्ट्रा by Vishvakarma, विधाप्य having caused to be built, पुरम् a city, आशु quickly, पयोधि-मध्ये in the middle of the ocean, तत्र अथ there then, योग-बलतः by the power of Your Yoga, स्व-जनान् अनैषीः You transported Your own people

Simple Meaning: As Jarasandha was approaching for his eighteenth battle, You saw the Yavana king Kalayavana advancing at the head of thirty million Yavana warriors. You quickly had a city built in the middle of the ocean by Vishvakarma, and through Your divine power of Yoga, You safely transported all Your people there.

पदभ्यां त्वां पद्ममाली चकित इव पुरान्निर्गतो धावमानो
म्लेच्छेशेनानुयातो वधसुकृतविहीनेन शैले न्यलैषीः ।
सुप्तेनांघ्र्याहतेन द्रुतमथ मुचुकुन्देन भस्मीकृतेऽस्मिन्
भूपायास्मै गुहान्ते सुललितवपुषा तस्थिषे भक्तिभाजे ॥77.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: पदभ्याम् on foot, त्वम् You, पद्म-माली wearing a lotus garland, चकितः इव like one who is frightened, पुरात् निर्गतः having left the city, धावमानः running, म्लेच्छ-ईशेन-अनुयातः pursued by the king of Mlecchas, वध-सुकृत-विहीनेन who lacked the merit to be slain by Your own hands, शैले to a mountain, न्यलैषीः You led him, सुप्तेन by him who was sleeping, अंघ्र्या-हतेन and kicked was by Kalayavana, द्रुतम् अथ quickly then, मुचुकुन्देन by Muchukunda, भस्मीकृते अस्मिन् when he was turned to ashes, भूपाय अस्मै to this king, गुहा-अन्ते inside the cave, सुललित-वपुषा with an exceedingly beautiful form, तस्थिषे You presented Yourself, भक्ति-भाजे to Your devoted worshipper

Simple Meaning: Wearing a garland of lotus flowers, You fled on foot from the city as if terrified, pursued by the Mleccha king who lacked the spiritual merit to be slain directly by Your hands. You led him into a mountain cave where King Muchukunda lay sleeping. When the Yavana king kicked him, Muchukunda awoke and instantly burnt him to ashes. You then revealed Your exquisitely beautiful form to that devoted Muchukunda inside the cave.

ऐक्ष्वाकोऽहं विरक्तोऽस्म्यखिलनृपसुखे त्वत्प्रसादैककाङ्क्षी
हा देवेति स्तुवन्तं वरविततिषु तं निस्पृहं वीक्ष्य हृष्यन् ।
मुक्तेस्तुल्यां च भक्तिं धुतसकलमलां मोक्षमप्याशु दत्वा
कार्यं हिंसाविशुद्ध्यै तप इति च तदा प्रात्थ लोकप्रतीत्यै ॥77.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऐक्ष्वाकः अहम् I am born in the Ikshvaku dynasty, विरक्तः अस्मि I am detached, अखिल-नृप-सुखे from all royal pleasures, त्वत्-प्रसाद-एक-काङ्क्षी desiring only Your grace, हा देव O Lord, इति स्तुवन्तम् thus praising You, तम् to him, वर-विततिषु towards the choice of boons, निस्पृहम् desireless, वीक्ष्य seeing, हृष्यन् being pleased, मुक्तेः तुल्याम् equal to liberation, च and, भक्तिम् devotion, धुत-सकल-मलाम् which washes away all impurities, मोक्षम् अपि आशु liberation also swiftly, दत्वा having granted, कार्यम् to be performed, हिंसा-विशुद्ध्यै for purification from the sins of violence, तपः penance, इति and, च then, तदा at that time, प्रात्थ You instructed, लोक-प्रतीत्यै for the guidance of the world

Simple Meaning: Expressing that he was born in the Ikshvaku lineage and completely detached from all worldly royal pleasures, Muchukunda praised You, asking for nothing but Your grace. Seeing him free from any desire for material boons, You were deeply pleased and granted him pure devotion equal to supreme liberation, as well as final release itself. To set an example for the world and cleanse any residual sins from past violence, You advised him to perform penance.

तदनु मथुरां गत्वा हत्वा चमूं यवनाहृतां
मगधपतिना मार्गे सैन्यैः पुरेव निवारितः ।
चरमविजयं दर्पायास्मै प्रदाय पलायितो
जलधिनगरीं यातो वातालयेश्वर पाहि माम् ॥77.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदनु thereafter, मथुराम् to Mathura, गत्वा having gone, हत्वा having destroyed, चमूम् the army, यवन-आहृताम् brought by the Yavana king, मगध-पतिना by the king of Magadha - Jarasandha, मार्गे on the way, सैन्यैः with his forces, पुरा इव as before, निवारितः blocked, चरम-विजयम् a final victory, दर्पाय for his arrogance, अस्मै to him, प्रदाय granting, पलायितः fleeing, जलधि-नगरीम् to the city in the ocean (Dwaraka), यातः went, वातालयेश्वर O Lord of Guruvayur, पाहि माम् protect me

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, returning to Mathura, You destroyed the remaining Yavana army. On Your way back, You were once again intercepted by Jarasandha, the king of Magadha, with his immense forces. Granting him a final victory to satisfy his pride, You feigned flight and departed for Your city built within the ocean. O Lord of Guruvayur, protect me!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
