Krishna subdues Kaliya

दशकम् ५५

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 55

Krishna subdues Kaliya

अथ वारिणि घोरतरं फणिनं प्रतिवारयितुं कृतधीर्भगवन् ।
द्रुतमारिथ तीरगनीपतरुं विषमारुतशोषितपर्णचयम् ॥55.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथ then, वारिणि in the water, घोरतरम् most terrible, फणिनम् serpent Kaliya, प्रतिवारयितुम् to subdue, कृत-धीः having made up Your mind, भगवन् O Lord, द्रुतम् quickly, आरिथ You climbed, तीरग-नीप-तरुम् the Kadamba tree growing on the bank, विष-मारुत-शोषित-पर्ण-चयम् whose leaves had all been dried up by the poisonous wind

Simple Meaning: Then, O Lord, having made up Your mind to subdue that most terrible serpent dwelling in the water, You quickly climbed the Kadamba tree growing on the bank, whose leaves had all been dried up by the poisonous wind emanating from the river.

अधिरुह्य पदाम्बुरुहेण च तं नवपल्लवतुल्यमनोज्ञरुचा ।
ह्रदवारिणि दूरतरं न्यपतः परिघूर्णितघोरतरङ्गगणे ॥55.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: अधिरुह्य having climbed, पद-अम्बुरुहेण with Your lotus feet, च and, तम् that tree, नव-पल्लव-तुल्य-मनोज्ञ-रुचा which possessed a beautiful charm like fresh tender leaves, ह्रद-वारिणि into the water of the pool, दूरतरम् far out, न्यपतः You jumped, परिघूर्णित-घोर-तरङ्ग-गणे where formidable waves were swirling violently

Simple Meaning: Having climbed that tree with Your lotus feet, which possessed a tender beauty resembling fresh young leaves, You jumped far out into the pool whose waters were swirling violently with formidable waves.

भुवनत्रयभारभृतो भवतो गुरुभारविकम्पिविजृम्भिजला ।
परिमज्जयति स्म धनुश्शतकं तटिनी झटिति स्फुटघोषवती ॥55.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: भुवन-त्रय-भार-भृतः of the one who carries the immense weight of all the three worlds, भवतः of You, गुरु-भार-विकम्पि-विजृम्भि-जला with waters violently shaking and swelling due to Your immense weight, परिमज्जयति स्म was submerged, धनुः-शतकम् for a distance of one hundred bows, तटिनी the river Yamuna, झटिति instantly, स्फुट-घोषवती roaring with loud resounding sounds

Simple Meaning: As You, who carry the immense weight of the three worlds, jumped into the waters, the waters of the Yamuna violently surged and swelled. The river instantly submerged its banks up to a distance of one hundred bows, roaring with loud, resounding waves.

अथ दिक्षु विदिक्षु परिक्षुभितभ्रमितोदरवारिनिनादभरैः ।
उदकादुदगादुरगाधिपतिः त्वदुपान्तमशान्तरुषाऽन्धमनाः ॥55.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथ then, दिक्षु in all major directions, विदिक्षु in all intermediate directions, परिक्षुभित-भ्रमित-उदर-वारि-निनाद-भरैः by the overwhelming roar of the agitated and whirling deep waters, उदकात् out of the water, उदगात् emerged, उरग-अधिपतिः the king of serpents Kaliya, त्वत्-उपान्तम् near You, अशान्त-रुषा-अन्ध-मनाः with a mind completely blinded by rage

Simple Meaning: Then, disturbed by the deafening roar of the deep waters swirling and thrashing in every direction, Kaliya, the king of serpents, emerged from the water and approached You, his mind completely blinded by anger.

फणशृङ्गसहस्रविनिस्सृमरज्वलदग्निकणोग्रविषाम्बुधरम् ।
पुरतः फणिनं समलोकयथा बहुशृङ्गिणमञ्जनशैलमिव ॥55.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: फण-शृङ्ग-सहस्र-विनिस्सृमर-ज्वलत्-अग्नि-कण-उग्र-विष-अम्बु-धरम् emitting fierce poisonous spray along with glowing sparks of fire from the tips of his thousand hoods, पुरतः in front of You, फणिनम् that serpent, समलोकयथा You beheld, बहु-शृङ्गिणम् multi-peaked, अञ्जन-शैलम् इव like a mountain of black collyrium

Simple Meaning: You beheld that serpent Kaliya standing in front of You, emitting a deadly spray of poison mixed with fiery sparks from his thousand elevated hoods, appearing just like a multi-peaked mountain of black collyrium.

ज्वलदक्षि परिक्षरदुग्रविषश्वसनोष्मभरः स महाभुजगः ।
परिदश्य भवन्तमनन्तबलं समवेष्टयदस्फुटचेष्टमहो ॥55.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: ज्वलत्-अक्षि with glowing fiery eyes, परिक्षरत्-उग्र-विष-श्वसन-ऊष्म-भरः exhaling an intense heat of deadly poison through his breath, सः that, महा-भुजगः great serpent Kaliya, परिदश्य biting, भवन्तम् You, अनन्त-बलम् possessing infinite strength, समवेष्टयत् constricted tightly, अस्फुट-चेष्टम् rendering You motionless, अहो O wonder

Simple Meaning: With fiery eyes burning bright and breath exhaling the intense, deadly heat of poison, that great serpent Kaliya bit You—who possess infinite strength—and wrapped his coils tightly around You, rendering You completely motionless. What a wonder!

अविलोक्य भवन्तमथाकुलिते तटगामिनि बालकधेनुगणे ।
व्रजगेहतलेऽप्यनिमित्तशतं समुदीक्ष्य गता यमुनां पशुपाः ॥55.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: अविलोक्य not seeing, भवन्तम् You, अथ then, आकुलिते becoming distressed, तटगामिनि present on the bank, बालक-धेनु-गणे the group of young cowherd boys and cows, व्रज-गेह-तले in the homes of Vraja, अपि also, अनिमित्त-शतम् hundreds of bad omens, समुदीक्ष्य deeply observing, गताः went, यमुनाम् to the Yamuna river, पशुपाः the cowherds

Simple Meaning: Not seeing You, the young cowherd boys and the cows present on the bank became deeply distressed. Meanwhile, back in Gokula, the cowherds observed hundreds of evil omens appearing in their homes and rushed in anxiety towards the Yamuna river.

अखिलेषु विभो भवदीयदशाम् अवलोक्य जिहासुषु जीवभरम् ।
फणिबन्धनमाशु विमुच्य जवात् उदगम्यत हासजुषा भवता ॥55.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: अखिलेषु when all, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, भवदीय-दशाम् Your plight, अवलोक्य seeing, जिहासुषु being ready to give up, जीव-भरम् their very lives, फणि-बन्धनम् the coils of the serpent, आशु quickly, विमुच्य breaking free, जवात् with force, उदगम्यत You rose up, हास-जुषा smiling gently, भवता by You

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord, seeing Your plight, all Your loved ones were ready to give up their very lives. Seeing this, You quickly broke free from the coils of the serpent with great force and rose up with a gentle smile upon Your face.

अधिरुह्य ततः फणिराजफणान् ननृते भवता मृदुपादरुचा ।
कलशिञ्जितनूपुरमञ्जुमिलत्करकङ्कणसङ्कुलसङ्क्वणितम् ॥55.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: अधिरुह्य having climbed, ततः then, फणि-राज-फणान् upon the hoods of the king of serpents, ननृते dance was performed, भवता by You, मृदु-पाद-रुचा with the exquisite charm of Your tender feet, कल-शिञ्जित-नूपुर-मञ्जु-मिलत्-कर-कङ्कण-सङ्कुल-सङ्क्वणितम् accompanied by the sweet chiming of anklets harmoniously blending with the delightful jingling of hand bangles

Simple Meaning: Climbing atop the broad hoods of the serpent king Kaliya, You began to dance with Your beautiful soft feet, creating a blissful melody as the sweet chiming of Your anklets blended with the rhythmic jingling of Your wrist bangles.

जहृषुः पशुपास्तुतुषुर्मुनयो ववृषुः कुसुमानि सुरेन्द्रगणाः ।
त्वयि नृत्यति मारुतगेहपते परिपाहि स मां त्वमदान्तगदात् ॥55.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: जहृषुः rejoiced, पशुपाः the cowherds, तुतुषुः were elated, मुनयः the sages, ववृषुः showered, कुसुमानि flowers, सुर-इन्द्र-गणाः the hosts of devas led by Indra, त्वयि when You, नृत्यति were dancing, मारुतगेहपते O Lord of Guruvayur, परिपाहि protect, सः that You, माम् me, त्वम् You, अदान्त-गदात् from intractable disease

Simple Meaning: While You danced atop Kaliya, the cowherds rejoiced, the sages were filled with supreme bliss, and the hosts of devas led by Indra showered flowers upon You. O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect me from this intractable disease.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
