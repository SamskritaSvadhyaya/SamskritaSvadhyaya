Dashaka 29

The incarnation as Mohini

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

 ॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 29

The incarnation as Mohini

(छन्दस् - वसन्ततिलका)

उद्गच्छतस्तव करादमृतं हरत्सु दैत्येषु तानशरणाननुनीय देवान् ।
सद्यस्तिरोदधिथ देव भवत्प्रभावात् उद्यत्स्वयूथ्यकलहा दितिजा बभूवुः ॥29.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: उद्गच्छतः as You emerged, तव Your, करात् from the hands, अमृतम् the nectar of immortality, हरत्सु while seizing/snatching away, दैत्येषु as the Daityas, तान् those, अशरणान् helpless/shelterless, अनुनीय consoling/reassuring, देवान् the Devas, सद्यः immediately, तिरोदधिथ You vanished, देव O Lord, भवत्-प्रभावात् by Your divine power/influence, उद्यत्-स्वयूथ्य-कलहाः engaging in mutual fighting among their own group, दितिजाः the sons of Diti (asuras), बभूवुः became

Simple Meaning: O Lord! As You emerged with the vessel, the asuras forcefully snatched the nectar right out of Your hands. Reassuring the helpless and distressed devas not to lose heart, You suddenly vanished from their sight. Immediately, by the power of Your divine maya, fierce infighting and quarrels broke out among the asuras themselves over the distribution of the nectar!

श्यामां रुचाऽपि वयसाऽपि तनुं तदानीं प्राप्तोऽसि तुङ्गकुचमण्डलभंगुरां त्वम् ।
पीयूषकुम्भकलहं परिमुच्य सर्वे तृष्णाकुलाः प्रतिययुस्त्वदुरोजकुम्भे ॥29.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्यामाम् of a youthful, dark-complexioned maiden, रुचा अपि in complexion/radiance as well, वयसा अपि in age as well, तनुम् body/form, तदानीम् then, प्राप्तः असि You assumed, तुङ्ग-कुच-मण्डल-भंगुराम् bent slightly owing to the weight of high, round breasts, त्वम् You, पीयूष-कुम्भ-कलहम् the quarrel over the nectar pot, परिमुच्य abandoning entirely, सर्वे all (the asuras), तृष्णा-कुलाः filled with overwhelming desire/passion, प्रतिययुः turned/directed their attention, त्वत्-उरोज-कुम्भे towards Your pot-like breasts

Simple Meaning: You then assumed the form of Mohini—a bewitchingly beautiful, dark-complexioned maiden in the prime of her youth, bending slightly under the weight of her full breasts. Enchanted by this dazzling form, all the asuras completely forgot their fierce quarrel over the pot of nectar, their hearts now filled with overwhelming desire for the beauty of Your form!

का त्वं मृगाक्षि विभजस्व सुधामिमामित्यारूढरागविवशानभियाचतोऽमून् ।
विश्वस्यते मयि कथं कुलटाऽस्मि दैत्या इत्यालपन्नपि सुविश्वसितानतानीः ॥29.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: का त्वम् Who are you?, मृगाक्षि O doe-eyed maiden!, विभजस्व divide/distribute, सुधाम् इमाम् this nectar (among us), इति आरूढ-राग-विवशान् thus overwhelmed with intense passion, अभियाचतः begging/entreating, अमून् those (asuras), विश्वस्यते मयि कथम् How can I be trusted?, कुलटा अस्मि I am an unchaste/deceitful woman, दैत्याः O Daityas!, इति आलपन् अपि even while speaking thus, सुविश्वसितान् entirely trusting/full of faith, अतानीः You rendered (them)

Simple Meaning: Overwhelmed with intense passion, the asuras begged You, "O doe-eyed beauty, who are you? Please distribute this nectar among us!" Even as You playfully teased them, saying, "O Daityas, how can you put your faith in me? I am an unchaste, untrustworthy woman!", Your enchanting words only made them trust You completely without a trace of doubt.

मोदात् सुधाकलशमेषु ददत्सु सा त्वं दुश्चेष्टितं मम सहध्वमिति ब्रुवाणा ।
पङ्क्तिप्रभेदविनिवेशितदेवदैत्या लीलाविलासगतिभिः समदाः सुधां ताम् ॥29.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: मोदात् out of joy/infatuation, सुधा-कलशम् the pot of nectar, एषु as they (the asuras), ददत्सु handed over (to You), सा त्वम् You in that enchanting form of Mohini, दुश्चेष्टितम् मम my wicked/unpredictable conduct, सहध्वम् bear with/tolerate, इति Thus, ब्रुवाणा speaking, पङ्क्ति-प्रभेद-विनिवेशित-देव-दैत्या seating the gods and daityas in separate, distinct rows, लीला-विलास-गतिभिः with charming, playful, and graceful movements, समदाः distributed/served, सुधाम् ताम् that divine nectar

Simple Meaning: Delighted and fully enchanted, the asuras willingly handed over the vessel of nectar to You. Playfully teasing them, You said, "You must tolerate whatever unpredictable or wicked things I might do!" You then seated the gods and the asuras in two separate rows. Moving between them with captivating grace, charm, and elegance, You began to distribute that immortal nectar.

अस्मास्वियं प्रणयिणीत्यसुरेषु तेषु जोषं स्थितेष्वथ समाप्य सुधां सुरेषु ।
त्वं भक्तलोकवशगो निजरूपमेत्य स्वर्भानुमर्धपरिपीतसुधं व्यलावीः ॥29.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: अस्मासु towards us, इयम् she, प्रणयिणी is deeply in love/affectionate, इति thus thinking, असुरेषु as the asuras, तेषु those, जोषम् quiet/silent/expectant, स्थितेषु remained, अथ then, समाप्य finishing, सुधाम् the nectar, सुरेषु among the gods, त्वम् You, भक्त-लोक-वशगः submissive to the devotion of Your devotees, निज-रूपम् Your original divine form, एत्य returning to/assuming, स्वर्भानुम् Rahu (Swarbhanu), अर्ध-परिपीत-सुधम् who had half-drunk the nectar, व्यलावीः severed/cut off (his head)

Simple Meaning: Thinking, "She loves us and will surely serve us next," the asuras remained quietly expectant. Meanwhile, You—who are ever-obedient to the love of Your devotees—served all the remaining nectar to the gods. When Rahu secretly sat among the gods and managed to drink half of the nectar, You immediately assumed Your original form and severed his head with Your discus.

त्वत्तः सुधाहरणयोग्यफलं परेषु दत्वा गते त्वयि सुरैः खलु ते व्यगृह्णन् ।
घोरेऽथ मूर्छति रणे बलिदैत्यमायाव्यामोहिते सुरगणे त्वमिहाविरासीः॥29.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्तः from You, सुधा-हरण-योग्य-फलम् the proper fruit/consequence of stealing the nectar, परेषु to the enemies (asuras), दत्वा having given, गते त्वयि when You departed, सुरैः with the gods, खलु indeed, ते they (the asuras), व्यगृह्णन् engaged in fierce battle, घोरे in the terrible, अथ then, मूर्छति escalating/becoming intense, रणे in the battle, बलि-दैत्य-माया-व्यामोहिते deluded/overwhelmed by the magical powers (Maya) of the daitya Bali, सुर-गणे when the host of gods was, त्वम् You, इह here (on the battlefield), आविरसीः manifested/appeared

Simple Meaning: Having dealt the asuras the fitting consequence of snatching the nectar from You, You departed from the scene. The enraged asuras then waged a fierce war against the gods. As the battle turned dreadful and the gods were completely overwhelmed and deluded by the powerful magical illusions (Maya) of King Bali, You manifested right there on the battlefield to protect them!

त्वं कालनेमिमथ मालिमुखाञ्जघन्थ शक्रो जघान बलिजम्भवलान् सपाकान् ।
शुष्कार्द्रदुष्करवधे नमुचौ च लूने फेनेन नारदगिरा न्यरुणो रणं त्वं ॥29.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वम् You, कालनेमिम् Kalanemi, अथ then, मालि-मुखान् Mali and other prominent daityas, जघन्थ killed/vanquished, शक्रः Indra, जघान killed, बलि-जम्भ-वलान् Bali, Jambha, and Vala, स-पाकान् along with Paka, शुष्क-आर्द्र-दुष्कर-वधे whose killing was impossible by anything dry or wet, नमुचौ and when Namuchi, च also, लूने was slain/severed, फेनेन by means of sea-foam, नारद-गिरा at the advice/intervention of Sage Narada, न्यरुणः stopped/brought to an end, रणम् the battle, त्वम् You

Simple Meaning: You then vanquished Kalanemi, Mali, and other mighty daitya leaders, while Indra defeated Bali, Jambha, Vala, and Paka. When Namuchi—who could not be killed by anything wet or dry—was slain by Indra using sea-foam, Sage Narada advised that the objective was fulfilled. Following his words, You brought the fierce battle to a close.

योषावपुर्दनुजमोहनमाहितं ते श्रुत्वा विलोकनकुतूहलवान् महेशः ।
भूतैस्समं गिरिजया च गतः पदं ते स्तुत्वाऽब्रवीदभिमतं त्वमथो तिरोधाः ॥29.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: योषा-वपुः the female form (Mohini), दनुज-मोहनम् which enchanted the asuras (Danujas), आहितम् assumed, ते by You, श्रुत्वा hearing about, विलोकन-कुतूहल-वान् filled with intense curiosity to see it, महेशः Shiva (Mahesha), भूतैः समम् along with his retinue of spirits/Bhutas, गिरिजया च and with Parvati (Girija), गतः went, पदम् ते to Your abode (Vaikuntha), स्तुत्वा praised You, अब्रवीत् अभिमतम् expressed his desire, त्वम् You, अथ then, तिरोधाः disappeared

Simple Meaning: Having heard about the enchanting female form (Mohini) You had assumed to delude the asuras, Shiva was filled with intense curiosity to behold it. Accompanied by Parvati and his retinue of attendants, he went to Your supreme abode. After offering praises to You, he expressed his deep desire to see that enchanting form—whereupon You vanished from his sight!

आरामसीमनि च कन्दुकघातलीलालोलायमाननयनां कमनीं मनोज्ञाम् ।
त्वामेष वीक्ष्य विगलद्वसनां मनोभूवेगादनङ्गरिपुरङ्ग समालिलिङ्ग ॥29.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: आराम-सीमनि at the edge of a garden/grove, च and, कन्दुक-घात-लीला-लोलायमान-नयनाम् whose eyes were darting about playfully due to the game of striking a ball, कमनीम् charming/lovely, मनोज्ञाम् captivating/beautiful, त्वाम् You (in the form of Mohini), एषः this (Shiva), वीक्ष्य having seen, विगलत्-वसनाम् whose garments had slipped or loosened, मनोभू-वेगात् driven by the surge of passion (Kama), अनङ्ग-रिपुः the enemy of Ananga/Kama (Shiva), अङ्ग O Lord!, समालिलिङ्ग embraced

Simple Meaning: While You (in Your enchanting form as Mohini) were playfully playing with a ball at the edge of a garden, Your eyes darted gracefully with every stroke, and Your garments slipped slightly out of place. Seeing You in this intensely captivating and lovely form, Lord Shiva—though he is the conqueror of Kama—was suddenly overcome by a fierce surge of passionate desire and eagerly embraced You, O Lord!

भूयोऽपि विद्रुतवतीमुपधाव्य देवो वीर्यप्रमोक्षविकसत्परमार्थबोधः ।
त्वन्मानितस्तव महत्त्वमुवाच देव्यै तत्तादृशस्त्वमव वातनिकेतनाथ ॥29.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूयः अपि once again, विद्रुतवतीम् who was running away, उपधाव्य chasing after, देवः the divine Shiva, वीर्य-प्रमोक्ष-विकसत्-परमार्थ-बोधः realizing the highest ultimate Truth upon the discharge of his vital energy, त्वत्-मानितः honored/enlightened by You, तव Your, महत्त्वम् greatness/supreme glory, उवाच described/spoke, देव्यै to Goddess Parvati, तत् तादृशः त्वम् You who are of such infinite power and splendor, अव protect (me), वात-निकेत-नाथ O Lord of Guruvayur!

Simple Meaning: As You slipped away and ran ahead, Lord Shiva chased after You again. But as his vital energy discharged, he suddenly broke free from the spell of Your illusion (Maya) and awoke to the highest spiritual Truth. Honored and enlightened by Your supreme power, he returned to Goddess Parvati and glorified Your matchless greatness to her. O Lord of Guruvayur, who possesses such infinite glory, please protect and heal me!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
