Salvation of Nalakubara and Manigriva

दशकम् ४८

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 48

Salvation of Nalakubara and Manigriva

मुदा सुरौघैस्त्वमुदारसम्मदैः उदीर्य दामोदर इत्यभिष्टुतः ।
मृदुदरः स्वैरमुलूखले लगन्नदूरतो द्वौ ककुभावुदैक्षथाः ॥48.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: मुदा joyfully, सुर-ओघैः by the hosts of devas, त्वम् You, उदार-सम्मदैः filled with immense delight, उदीर्य proclaiming, दामोदरः Damodara (the one with a rope around His belly), इति thus, अभिष्टुतः praised, मृदु-उदरः having a soft belly, स्वैरम् freely/at Your own will, उलूखले लगन् attached to/dragging the wooden mortar, अदूरतः nearby, द्वौ two, ककुभौ Kakubha (Arjuna) trees, उदैक्षथाः You looked towards

Simple Meaning: Joyfully praised by the hosts of devas who were filled with immense delight and proclaimed You as "Damodara" (the one bound by a rope around His belly), You freely dragged the wooden mortar attached to You and looked towards two nearby Arjuna trees.

कुबेरसूनुर्नलकूबराभिधः परो मणिग्रीव इति प्रथां गतः ।
महेशसेवाधिगतश्रियोन्मदौ चिरं किल त्वद्विमुखावखेलताम् ॥48.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: कुबेर-सूनुः the son of Kubera, नलकूबर-अभिधः named Nalakubara, परः the other, मणिग्रीवः Manigriva, इति thus, प्रथाम् गतः became well-known, महेश-सेवा-धिगत-श्रिया with wealth obtained through worship of Shiva, उन्मदौ intoxicated with pride, चिरम् for a long time, किल indeed, त्वत्-विमुखौ averse to You, अवखेलताम् sported indulgently

Simple Meaning: One son of Kubera was named Nalakubara, and the other was famous as Manigriva. Intoxicated with pride due to the immense wealth obtained through their worship of Shiva, they both spent a long time indulgently sporting about, completely averse and indifferent to You.

सुरापगायां किल तौ मदोत्कटौ सुरापगायद्बहुयौवतावृतौ ।
विवाससौ केलिपरौ स नारदो भवत्पदैकप्रवणो निरैक्षत ॥48.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुर-आपगायाम् in the celestial river (Ganges), किल indeed, तौ those two, मद-उत्कटौ drunk/intoxicated with pride and wine, सुरा-आप-गायत्-बहु-यौवत-आवृतौ surrounded by many young women singing while drinking wine, विवाससौ clad without clothes (naked), केलि-परौ engrossed in water sports, सः that, नारदः Sage Narada, भवत्-पद-एक-प्रवणः devoted solely to Your lotus feet, निरैक्षत beheld

Simple Meaning: While those two, intoxicated with wine and pride, were playing unclothed in the divine Ganges surrounded by many singing maidens, Sage Narada—who is completely devoted to Your feet—happened to see them.

भिया प्रियालोकमुपात्तवाससं पुरो निरीक्ष्यापि मदान्धचेतसौ ।
इमौ भवद्भक्त्युपशान्तिसिद्धये मुनिर्जगौ शान्तिमृते कुतः सुखम् ॥48.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: भिया out of fear, प्रिया-लोकम् the young women, उपात्त-वाससम् putting on their clothes, पुरः in front, निरीक्ष्य seeing, अपि even though, मद-अन्ध-चेतसौ minds blinded by pride and intoxication, इमौ these two, भवत्-भक्ति-उपशान्ति-सिद्धये for attaining devotion unto You and tranquility of mind, मुनिः the sage Narada, जगौ declared, शान्तिम् ऋते without peace, कुतः where is, सुखम् happiness

Simple Meaning: Seeing Sage Narada appear before them, the young women quickly put on their clothes out of fear. However, these two sons of Kubera, their minds completely blinded by pride and intoxication, remained unabashed and nude. Seeing their state, the great sage declared for their eventual attainment of devotion and tranquility, "Without peace of mind, how can there ever be true happiness?

युवामवाप्तौ ककुभात्मतां चिरं हरिं निरीक्ष्याथ पदं स्वमाप्नुतम् ।
इतीरेतौ तौ भवदीक्षणस्पृहां गतौ व्रजान्ते ककुभौ बभूवतुः ॥48.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: युवाम् you two, अवाप्तौ having attained, ककुभ-आत्मताम् the state of Arjuna trees, चिरम् for a long time, हरिम् Lord Hari, निरीक्ष्य having seen, अथ then, पदम् स्वम् आप्नुतम् shall regain your own celestial abode, इति इरितौ thus cursed/instructed, तौ those two, भवत्-ईक्षण-स्पृहाम् गतौ filled with an eager longing for Your divine vision, व्रज-अन्ते at the outskirts of Vraja, ककुभौ बभूवतुः became twin Arjuna trees

Simple Meaning: "May you both become Arjuna trees for a long time! Then, upon beholding Lord Hari, you shall regain your original divine forms and return to your abode." Having been thus instructed by Narada, the two brothers—now filled with a deep, longing desire for Your divine sight—became twin Arjuna trees at the edge of Vraja.

अतन्द्रमिन्द्रद्रुयुगं तथाविधं समेयुषा मन्थरगामिना त्वया ।
तिरायितोलूखलरोधनिर्धुतौ चिराय जीर्णौ परिपातितौ तरू ॥48.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: अतन्द्रम् continuously/steadfastly, इन्द्र-द्रु-युगम् the pair of majestic Arjuna trees, तथा-विधम् standing in that manner, समेयुषा approaching, मन्थर-गामिना crawling/moving slowly, त्वया by You, तिरायित-उलूखल-रोध-निर्धुतौ shaken down due to the obstruction caused by the sideways mortar, चिराय after a long time, जीर्णौ ancient/decayed, परिपातितौ uprooted/felled, तरू the two trees

Simple Meaning: Crawling along slowly, You approached that pair of majestic Arjuna trees standing firmly there. As You passed between them, the wooden mortar turned sideways and caught against their trunks. Shaken down due to the obstruction caused by the sideways mortar, the two ancient trees fell to the ground!

अभाजि शाखिद्वितयं यदा त्वया तदैव तद्गर्भतलान्निरेयुषा ।
महात्विषा यक्षयुगेन तत्क्षणात् अभाजि गोविन्द भवानपि स्तवैः ॥48.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: अभाजि was shattered/broken, शाखि-द्वितयम् the pair of trees, यदा when, त्वया by You, तदा-एव at that very moment, तत्-गर्भ-तलात् from the inner core of those trees, निरेयुषा emerging, महा-त्विषा possessing magnificent brilliance, यक्ष-युगेन by the pair of Yakshas, तत्-क्षणात् instantly, अभाजि was worshipped/praised, गोविन्द O Govinda, भवान् You, अपि also, स्तवैः with hymns of praise

Simple Meaning: O Govinda! The moment that pair of trees was felled by You, two Yakshas of magnificent brilliance emerged from their trunks. Instantly, they showered You with heartfelt hymns of praise and adoration!

इहान्यभक्तोऽपि समेष्यति क्रमात् भवन्तमेतौ खलु रुद्रसेवकौ ।
मुनिप्रसादाद्भव्दङ्घ्रिमागतौ गतौ वृणानौ खलु भक्तिमुत्तमाम् ॥48.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: इह in this world, अन्य-भक्तः अपि even a devotee of other deities, समेष्यति shall attain, क्रमात् gradually, भवन्तम् You, एतौ these two, खलु indeed, रुद्र-सेवकौ devotees of Lord Shiva, मुनि-प्रसादात् by the grace of Sage Narada, भवत्-अङ्घ्रिम् Your lotus feet, आगतौ reached, गतौ departed, वृणानौ begging/choosing, खलु indeed, भक्तिम् उत्तमाम् supreme devotion

Simple Meaning: Even one who worships other devas attains You in due course. These two, though devotees of Lord Shiva, reached Your lotus feet through the grace of Sage Narada and departed after begging for supreme devotion unto You!

ततस्तरूद्दारणदारुणारवप्रकम्पिसम्पातिनि गोपमण्डले ।
विलज्जितत्वज्जननीमुखेक्षिणा व्यमोक्षि नन्देन भवान् विमोक्षदः ॥48.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, तरू-उद्दारण-दारुण-आरव-प्रकम्पि-सम्पातिनि rushing in trembling panic at the terrifying crashing sound of the falling trees, गोप-मण्डले as the group of cowherds gathered, विलज्जित-त्वत्-जननी-मुख-ईक्षिणा looking at the embarrassed face of Your mother, व्यमोक्षि was untied/released, नन्देन by Nanda, भवान् You, विमोक्षदः the Granter of liberation

Simple Meaning: Then, alarmed by the terrifying crash of the falling trees, the cowherds rushed in panic to the spot. Observing the embarrassed face of Your mother Yashoda, Nanda released You—You who Yourself grants liberation (releases from the bondage of the cycles of life and death)!

महीरुहोर्मध्यगतो बतार्भको हरेः प्रभावादपरिक्षतोऽधुना ।
इति ब्रुवाणैर्गमितो गृहं भवान् मरुत्पुराधीश्वर पाहि मां गदात् ॥48.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: महीरुहोः of the two trees, मध्य-गतः entered between, बत Oh, अर्भकः the child, हरेः प्रभावात् by the power/grace of Lord Hari, अपरिक्षतः-अधुना unharmed now, इति thus, ब्रुवाणैः by the cowherds talking among themselves, गमितः led/taken, गृहम् to the home, भवान् You, मरुत्पुराधीश्वर O Lord of Guruvayur, पाहि protect, माम् me, गदात् from disease/affliction

Simple Meaning: Exclaiming in wonder, "Oh! Though caught between those two falling trees, the child remains completely unharmed today by the divine grace of Lord Hari!", the cowherds carried You back home. O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect me from all my ailments.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
