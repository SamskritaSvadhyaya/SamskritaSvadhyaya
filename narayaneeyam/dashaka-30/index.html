Dashaka 30

The incarnation as Vamana

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

 ॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 30

The incarnation as Vamana

(छन्दस् - वसन्ततिलका)

शक्रेण संयति हतोऽपि बलिर्महात्मा शुक्रेण जीविततनुः क्रतुवर्धितोष्मा ।
विक्रान्तिमान् भयनिलीनसुरां त्रिलोकीं चक्रे वशे स तव चक्रमुखादभीतः ॥30.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: शक्रेण by Indra (Shakra), संयति in battle, हतः अपि although killed, बलिः King Bali, महात्मा the noble-souled/great-minded, शुक्रेण by his guru Shukracharya, जीवित-तनुः whose body was brought back to life, क्रतु-वर्धित-उष्मा whose power and prowess were vastly increased through sacrifices (the Vishvajit Yajna), विक्रान्तिमान् mighty and heroic, भय-निलीन-सुराम् with the gods hiding away in fear, त्रिलोकीम् the three worlds, चक्रे वशे brought under his complete control, सः he (Bali), तव Your, चक्र-मुखत् from even Your Discus (Sudarshana) and other weapons, अभीतः fearless

Simple Meaning: Although King Bali had been slain in battle by Indra, his guru Shukracharya brought him back to life. Restored and energized through solemn sacrifices (Yajnas), the heroic and noble Bali grew so immensely powerful that he conquered all the three worlds, driving the terrified gods into hiding. Rendered fearless even of Your mighty Sudarshana Discus, he established his absolute rule over the universe.

पुत्रार्तिदर्शनवशाददितिर्विषण्णा तं काश्यपं निजपतिं शरणं प्रपन्ना ।
त्वत्पूजनं तदुदितं हि पयोव्रताख्यं सा द्वादशाहमचरत्त्वयि भक्तिपूर्णा ॥30.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुत्र-आर्ति-दर्शन-वशात् owing to seeing the distress and suffering of her sons (the Devas), अदितिः Aditi, विषण्णा deeply grieved/despondent, तम् that, काश्यपम् Sage Kashyapa, निज-पतिम् her own husband, शरणम् प्रपन्ना sought refuge with, त्वत्-पूजनम् Your worship, तत्-उदितम् advised/instructed by him, हि indeed, पयोव्रत-आख्यम् known by the name Payovrata, सा she, द्वादश-अहम् for twelve days, अचरत् observed/performed, त्वयि towards You, भक्ति-पूर्णा filled with complete devotion

Simple Meaning: Seeing the intense suffering and plight of her sons, Mother Aditi was plunged into deep grief. She turned to her husband, the great Sage Kashyapa, for guidance and protection. As instructed by him, she devoutly performed the sacred twelve-day vow known as Payovrata—a ritual of consuming only milk while remaining absorbed in pure, undivided devotion unto You.

तस्यावधौ त्वयि निलीनमतेरमुष्याः श्यामश्चतुर्भुजवपुः स्वयमाविरासीः ।
नम्रां च तामिह भवत्तनयो भवेयं गोप्यं मदीक्षणमिति प्रलपन्नयासीः ॥30.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्याः of that (Payovrata vow), अवधौ at the conclusion/completion, त्वयि in You, निलीन-मतेः whose mind was completely absorbed, अमुष्याः of her (Mother Aditi), श्यामः dark-complexioned, चतुर्भुज-वपुः four-armed form, स्वयं You Yourself, आविरासीः manifested/appeared, नम्राम् bowing down/humble, च and, ताम् to her, इह here, भवत्-तनयः Your son, भवेयम् I shall become, गोप्यम् secret/kept hidden, मद-ईक्षणम् My vision/seeing Me, इति thus, प्रलपन् declaring, अयासीः You departed

Simple Meaning: At the conclusion of her sacred vow, as Mother Aditi remained with her mind totally absorbed in You, You Yourself manifested before her in Your magnificent, four-armed, dark-complexioned form. Reassuring her as she bowed down in reverence, You promised, "I shall take birth as your son. Keep this divine vision of Me a secret!" Having declared this, You vanished from her sight.

त्वं काश्यपे तपसि सन्निदधत्तदानीं प्राप्तोऽसि गर्भमदितेः प्रणुतो विधात्रा ।
प्रासूत च प्रकटवैष्णवदिव्यरूपं सा द्वादशीश्रवणपुण्यदिने भवन्तं ॥30.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वम् You, काश्यपे in Sage Kashyapa, तपसि in his austere penance/ascetic power, सन्निदधत् placing Your divine presence/power, तदानीम् then, प्राप्तः असि entered, गर्भम् the womb, अदितेः of Aditi, प्रणुतः praised, विधात्रा by Lord Brahma (Vidhatra), प्रासूत gave birth to, च and, प्रकट-वैष्णव-दिव्य-रूपम् revealing the divine, radiant form of Lord Vishnu (with four arms, conch, discus, etc.), सा she (Aditi), द्वादशी-श्रवण-पुण्य-दिने on the auspicious day of Shravana Dwadashi, भवन्तम् You

Simple Meaning: You first infused Your divine presence into the austere penance of Sage Kashyapa, and through him, entered the womb of Mother Aditi while Lord Brahma offered prayers of praise to You. On the highly auspicious day of Shravana Dwadashi, Aditi gave birth to You as You revealed Your direct, glorious, and divine Vaishnava form!

पुण्याश्रमं तमभिवर्षति पुष्पवर्षैः हर्षाकुले सुरगणे कृततूर्यघोषे ।
बध्वाऽञ्जलिं जय जयेति नुतः पितृभ्यां त्वं तत्क्षणे पटुतमं वटुरूपमाधाः ॥30.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुण्य-आश्रमम् the holy hermitage, तम् that, अभिवर्षति showering/raining down upon, पुष्प-वर्षैः with rains of divine flowers, हर्षाकुले overwhelmed with joy, सुर-गणे as the assembly of devas, कृत-तूर्य-घोषे sounded their celestial drums and trumpets, बध्वा-अञ्जलिम् with folded hands in reverence, जय जय इति uttering "Victory, victory!", नुतः praised/worshipped, पितृभ्याम् by Your divine parents (Kashyapa and Aditi), त्वम् You, तत्क्षणे at that very moment, पटुतमम् exceedingly attractive/clever/charming, वटु-रूपम् the form of a young Brahmin celibate (celibate boy/brahmachari), आधाः assumed

Simple Meaning: As showers of celestial flowers rained down upon the holy hermitage and the overjoyed gods sounded their divine trumpets in the heavens, Your parents bowed before You with folded hands, chanting "Victory! Victory to You!" At that very moment, You transformed into an exceedingly charming, brilliant young Brahmin boy (Vamana).

तावत्प्रजापतिमुखैरुपनीय मौञ्जीदण्डाजिनाक्षवलयादिभिरर्च्यमानः ।
देदीप्यमानवपुरीश कृताग्निकार्यः त्वं प्रास्थिथा बलिगृहं प्रकृताश्वमेधम् ॥30.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: तावत् thereupon/at that time, प्रजापति-मुखैः by the Prajapatis (creators/patriarchs) led by Sage Kashyapa, उपनीय performing the sacred thread ceremony (Upanayana), मौञ्जी-दण्ड-अजिन-अक्षवलय-आदिभिः with the belt of Munjagrass, the wooden staff (Danda), the deerskin (Ajina), the rosary beads (Akshavalaya), and other sacred articles, अर्च्यमानः being worshipped/honored, देदीप्यमान-वपुः possessing a brilliantly glowing/resplendent body, ईश O Lord!, कृत-अग्नि-कार्यः having performed the sacred fire ritual (Agnikaryam/Homa), त्वम् You, प्रास्थिथाः set forth/departed towards, बलि-गृहम् the place/sacrificial hall of King Bali, प्रकृत-अश्वमेधम् where the Ashvamedha sacrifice was being conducted

Simple Meaning: Thereupon, the Prajapatis led by Kashyapa performed Your sacred thread ceremony (Upanayana), honoring You with sacred articles such as the Munja-grass belt, the ascetic's staff, the deerskin, and rosary beads. Glowing with divine radiance, O Lord, You completed Your sacred fire rituals (Agnikaryam) and set forth with resolute steps toward the sacrificial hall of King Bali, where the great Ashvamedha sacrifice was underway.

गात्रेण भाविमहिमोचितगौरवं प्राक् व्यावृण्वतेव धरणीं चलयन्नायासीः ।
छत्रं परोष्मतिरणार्थमिवादधानो दण्डं च दानवजनेष्विव सन्निधातुम् ॥30.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: गात्रेण by Your body, भावि-महिम-उचित-गौरवम् the weight and dignity appropriate to Your future cosmic grandeur (Trivikrama form), प्राक् beforehand, व्यावृण्वता इव as if revealing/displaying, धरणीम् the earth, चलयन् causing to tremble/shake, आयासीः You walked/advanced, छत्रम् an umbrella, पर-उष्म-तिरण-अर्थम् इव as if to ward off the intense heat of others (or to protect against the pride/heat of enemies), आदधानः holding, दण्डम् a staff/rod of punishment, च and, दानव-जनेषु upon the danavas, इव as if, सन्निधातुम् to mete out punishment/establish discipline

Simple Meaning: As You walked, Your small body seemed to display in advance the immense weight and dignity of the cosmic grandeur You would soon assume, causing the earth to tremble beneath Your footsteps. You held a small umbrella—as if to shield against the overwhelming heat and arrogance of adversaries—and carried a wooden staff (Danda), as if signaling the chastisement and discipline You were about to impose upon the daitya clan!

तां नर्मदोत्तरतटे हयमेधशालामासेदुषि त्वयि रुचा तव रुद्धनेत्रैः ।
भास्वान् किमेष दहनो नु सनत्कुमारो योगी नु कोऽयमिति शुक्रमुखैश्शशङ्के ॥30.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् that, नर्मदा-उत्तर-तटे on the northern bank of the Narmada river, हयमेध-शालाम् the hall of the Ashvamedha (horse) sacrifice, आसेदुषि when You reached/arrived at, त्वयि You, रुचा by the brilliant effulgence, तव Your, रुद्ध-नेत्रैः with their eyes dazzled/blinded, भास्वान् the Sun God (Surya), किम् एषः is this one?, दहनः नु or is it the Fire God (Agni)?, सनत्कुमारः Sanatkumara, योगी नु or a supreme ascetic/yogi?, कः अयम् इति who could this be?, शुक्र-मुखैः by Shukracharya and the other priests/sages, शशङ्के it was wondered/conjectured

Simple Meaning: When You arrived at the Ashvamedha sacrificial hall situated on the northern bank of the Narmada River, Your dazzling divine effulgence completely blinded the eyes of everyone present. Dazzled by Your radiance, Shukracharya and the other officiating priests wondered in amazement: "Is this the Sun God himself? Or is it Agni, the God of Fire? Could this be the great sage Sanatkumara or some supreme Yogi?"

आनीतमाशु भृगुभिर्महसाऽभिभूतैः त्वां रम्यरूपमसुरः पुलकावृताङ्गः ।
भक्त्या समेत्य सुकृती परिणिज्य पादौ तत्तोयमन्वधृत मूर्धनि तीर्थतीर्थम् ॥30.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: आनीतम् brought in/welcomed inside, आशु at once, भृगुभिः by the sages of the Bhrigu lineage (Shukracharya and his disciples), महसा by Your spiritual effulgence, अभिभूतैः who were overwhelmed/dazzled, त्वाम् You, रम्य-रूपम् who possessed an exquisitely charming form, असुरः the asura king (Bali), पुलक-आवृत-अङ्गः with his body covered in goosebumps (out of divine thrill), भक्त्या with deep devotion, समेत्य approaching, सुकृती the virtuous/fortunate one (King Bali), परिणिज्य having washed, पादौ Your two feet, तत्-तोयम् that holy water, अन्वधृत placed/sprinkled, मूर्धनि upon his head, तीर्थ-तीर्थम् the most sacred of all holy waters (which purifies all other sacred tirthas)

Simple Meaning: Overwhelmed and dazzled by Your brilliant effulgence, the Bhrigu sages immediately ushered You into the sacrificial hall. Seeing Your exquisitely captivating form, the virtuous King Bali felt a thrill of divine joy rush through his entire body. Filled with deep devotion, he approached You, reverently washed Your lotus feet, and placed upon his head that holy water—the supreme tirtha capable of sanctifying all sacred waters in the universe!

प्रह्लादवंशजतया क्रतुभिर्द्विजेषु विश्वासतो नु तदिदं दितिजोऽपि लेभे ।
यत्ते पदाम्बु गिरिशस्य शिरोभिलाल्यं स त्वं विभो गुरुपुरालय पालयेथाः ॥30.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रह्लाद-वंशजतया by virtue of being born in the lineage of Prahlada, क्रतुभिः through the performance of Vedic sacrifices, द्विजेषु in the holy Brahmins, विश्वासतः group of pious deeds/deep trust, नु surely, तत् इदम् that rare privilege, दितिजः अपि even though born as a demon (Daitya/son of Diti), लेभे obtained, यत् that, ते Your, पद-अम्बु water that washed the lotus feet, गिरिशस्य of Lord Shiva (Girisha), शिरो-भिलाल्यम् fondly cherished/borne upon the head, सः त्वम् that You, विभो O All-Pervading Lord!, गुरुपुरालय O Lord of Guruvayur!, पालयेथाः please protect me!

Simple Meaning: Was it due to his birth in the holy lineage of Prahlada, his dedicated performance of noble yajnas, or his deep faith in the holy Brahmins? Whatever the cause, even though born as an asura, King Bali obtained the incredible privilege of receiving upon his head, the water that washed Your lotus-foot —the supreme water that Lord Shiva himself fondly cherishes upon his head! O All-Pervading Lord, O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect and heal me!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
