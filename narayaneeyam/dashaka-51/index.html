Krishna slays Aghasura

दशकम् ५१

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 51

Krishna slays Aghasura

कदाचन व्रजशिशुभिः समं भवान् वनाशने विहितमतिः प्रगेतराम् ।
समावृतो बहुतरवत्समण्डलैः सतेमनैर्निरगमदीश जेमनैः ॥51.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: कदाचन on one day, व्रज-शिशुभिः-समम् along with the cowherd boys of Vraja, भवान् You, वन-अशने to eat in the forest, विहित-मतिः having made up the mind, प्रगेतराम् very early in the morning, समावृतः surrounded, बहुतर-वत्स-मण्डलैः by numerous herds of calves, स-तेमनैः with curds, curry, pickles etc, निरगमत् set out, ईश O Lord, जेमनैः with cooked rice

Simple Meaning: O Lord! On a certain day, having decided to have a picnic in the forest together with the cowherd boys of Vraja, You set out very early in the morning surrounded by numerous herds of calves, carrying cooked rice along with delicious condiments.

विनिर्यतस्तव चरणाम्बुजद्वयात् उदञ्चितं त्रिभुवनपावनं रजः ।
महर्षयः पुलकधरैः कलेबरैः उदूहिरे धृतभवदीक्षणोत्सवाः ॥51.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: विनिर्यतः You who had set out, तव Your, चरण-अम्बुज-द्वयात् from the pair of lotus feet, उदञ्चितम् rising up, त्रिभुवन-पावनम् sanctifying all the three worlds, रजः dust, महर्षयः the great sages, पुलक-धरैः experiencing goosebumps of joy, कलेबरैः with their bodies, उदूहिरे bore upon themselves, धृत-भवत्-ईक्षण-उत्सवाः enjoying the grand festival of beholding You

Simple Meaning: As You stepped out, the sacred dust rising from Your lotus feet—which purifies all three worlds—was reverently received by the great sages upon their bodies, as they experienced thrill and divine bliss while enjoying the celestial feast of beholding You.

प्रचारयत्यविरलशाद्वले तले पशून् विभो भवति समं कुमारकैः ।
अघासुरो न्यरुणदघाय वर्तनीं भयानकः सपदि शयानकाकृतिः ॥51.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रचारयति while grazing, अविरल-शाद्वले-तले on the ground dense with soft green grass, पशून् the calves and cattle, विभो O All-pervading Lord, भवति You, समम् along with, कुमारकैः the young cowherd boys, अघ-असुरः the asura named Agha, न्यरुणत् blocked, अघाय to cause harm, वर्तनीम् the path, भयानकः of terrifying appearance, सपदि suddenly, शयानक-आकृतिः in the form of a python

Simple Meaning: O Lord! As You were grazing the calves along with the young cowherd boys on the lush green pastures, the terrifying asura Aghasura suddenly assumed the gigantic form of a python and lay across the path to cause harm to You all.

महाचलप्रतिमतनोर्गुहानिभप्रसारितप्रथितमुखस्य कानने ।
मुखोदरं विहरणकौतुकाद्गताः कुमारकाः किमपि विदूरगे त्वयि ॥51.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: महा-चल-प्रतिम-तनोः whose body resembled a huge mountain, गुहा-निभ-प्रसारित-प्रथित-मुखस्य whose wide opened mouth was like a massive cave, कानने in the forest, मुख-उदरम् the cavity of the mouth, विहरण-कौतुकात् out of playful curiosity, गताः entered, कुमारकाः the young cowherd boys, किमपि while slightly, विदूरगे remaining at a distance, त्वयि You

Simple Meaning: O Lord! While You were lagging slightly behind, the young cowherd boys, mistaking the giant python whose mountain-like body was stretched out and whose wide open mouth resembled a vast mountain cave for a natural cavern, entered inside his mouth out of playful curiosity.

प्रमादतः प्रविशति पन्नगोदरं क्वथत्तनौ पशुपकुले सवात्सके ।
विदन्निदं त्वमपि विवेशिथ प्रभो सुहृज्जनं विशरणमाशु रक्षितुम् ॥51.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रमादतः out of ignorance and unawareness, प्रविशति having entered, पन्नग-उदरम् the belly of the giant snake, क्वथत्-तनौ when their bodies were burning and suffocating inside, पशुप-कुले the group of cowherd boys, स-वात्सके along with the herds of calves, विदन् इदम् knowing this, त्वम् अपि You too, विवेशिथ entered inside, प्रभो O Lord, सुहृत्-जनम् Your dear friends, विशरणम् who were helpless, आशु quickly, रक्षितुम् to protect

Simple Meaning: O Lord! When the helpless cowherd boys and calves unknowingly entered the python's mouth and began to suffocate inside his burning belly, You immediately realized their plight and entered the serpent's mouth Yourself to save Your dear friends.

गलोदरे विपुलितवर्ष्मणा त्वया महोरगे लुठति निरुद्धमारुते ।
द्रुतं भवान् विदलितकण्ठमण्डलो विमोचयन् पशुपपशून् विनिर्ययौ ॥51.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: गल-उदरे within his throat and stomach, विपुलित-वर्ष्मणा by expanding Your body immensely, त्वया by You, महा-उरगे when that giant serpent, लुठति was writhing on the ground, निरुद्ध-मारुते with his breath choked, द्रुतम् quickly, भवान् You, विदलित-कण्ठ-मण्डलः having torn open his throat, विमोचयन् liberating, पशुप-पशून् the cowherd boys and the calves, विनिर्ययौ came out safely

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Expanding Your body immensely within the throat and belly of that great serpent, You choked his breath and caused him to writhe on the ground. You then quickly burst open his throat, bringing out the cowherd boys and calves safely, and emerged victorious from his mouth.

क्षणं दिवि त्वदुपगमार्थमास्थितं महासुरप्रभवमहो महो महत् ।
विनिर्गते त्वयि तु निलीनमञ्जसा नभःस्थले ननृतुरथो जगुः सुराः ॥51.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्षणम् for a moment, दिवि in the sky, त्वत्-उपगम-अर्थम् आस्थितम् waiting for Your arrival and merger into You, महा-असुर-प्रभवम् originating from that great asura, अहो oh wondrous, महः-महत् glowing bright soul light, विनिर्गते when You emerged out, त्वयि into You, तु indeed, निलीनम् merged, अञ्जसा instantly, नभःस्थले in the heavens, ननृतुः danced, अथः then, जगुः sang praises, सुराः the devas

Simple Meaning: O Lord! The dazzling spiritual light originating from that great asura waited in the sky for a moment, and as soon as You emerged from his mouth, it instantly merged into Your divine body. Seeing this miraculous liberation, the devas danced and sang Your praises in the heavens.

सविस्मयैः कमलभवादिभिः सुरैः अनुद्रुतस्तदनु गतः कुमारकैः ।
दिने पुनस्तरुणदशामुपेयुषि स्वकैर्भवानतनुत भोजनोत्सवम् ॥51.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: स-विस्मयैः with amazement and wonder, कमल-भव-आदिभिः led by Brahma who was born from the lotus and others, सुरैः by the devas, अनुद्रुतः followed and praised, तत्-अनु after that, गतः going along, कुमारकैः with the young cowherd boys, दिने when the day, पुनः further, तरुण-दशाम-उपेयुषि reached midday, स्वकैः with Your own companions, भवान् You, अतनुत conducted, भोजन-उत्सवम् the festive picnic lunch

Simple Meaning: Praised and watched with deep wonder by Brahma and the other devas, You then walked along with the young cowherd boys. As the day reached midday, You sat down with Your dear companions and enjoyed the delightful picnic lunch in the forest.

विषाणिकामपि मुरलीं नितम्बके निवेशयन् कबलधरः कराम्बुजे ।
प्रहासयन् कलवचनैः कुमारकान् बुभोजिथ त्रिदशगणैर्मुदा नुतः ॥51.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: विषाणिकाम् the horn bugle, अपि as well as, मुरलीम् the flute, नितम्बके at Your waist belt, निवेशयन् tucking in, कबल-धरः holding a morsel of curd rice, कर-अम्बुजे in Your lotus hand, प्रहासयन् making them laugh, कल-वचनैः with sweet playful words, कुमारकान् the young cowherd boys, बुभोजिथ You enjoyed Your meal, त्रिदश-गणैः by the groups of devas, मुदा with joy, नुतः being praised

Simple Meaning: Tucking Your horn bugle and flute into Your waist belt, and holding a morsel of curd rice in Your lotus hand, You made all the young cowherd boys burst into laughter with Your sweet playful talk. Thus You enjoyed Your picnic meal in the forest while the groups of devas praised You with great joy from the heavens.

सुखाशनं त्विह तव गोपमण्डले मखाशनात् प्रियमिव देवमण्डले ।
इति स्तुतस्त्रिदशवरैर्जगत्पते मरुत्पुरीनिलय गदात् प्रपाहि माम् ॥51.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुख-अशनम् this pleasant lunch, तु इह indeed here, तव Your, गोप-मण्डले in the company of the cowherd boys, मख-अशनात् than receiving sacriﬁcial offerings, प्रियम् इव is more dear to You, देव-मण्डले among the assembly of devas, इति thus, स्तुतः being praised, त्रिदश-वरैः by the highest devas, जगत्-पते O Lord of the universe, मरुत्पुरी-निलय O Lord residing in Guruvayur, गदात् from my diseases, प्रपाहि माम् protect me

Simple Meaning: O Lord of the universe! O Lord residing in Guruvayur! Seeing You eat with such joy, the highest devas praised You saying that this delightful picnic meal in the company of the cowherd boys is far more dear to You than accepting rich sacriﬁcial offerings from the devas. O Supreme Lord, please protect me from all my diseases!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
