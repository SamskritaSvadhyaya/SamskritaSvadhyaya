Dashaka 8

The dawn of creation

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 8

The dawn of creation

(छन्दस् - शालिनी)

एवं तावत् प्राकृतप्रक्षयान्ते
ब्राह्मे कल्पे ह्यादिमे लब्धजन्मा ।
ब्रह्मा भूयस्त्वत्त एवाप्य वेदान्
सृष्टिं चक्रे पूर्वकल्पोपमानाम् ॥8.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, तावत् so far, प्राकृत-प्रक्षय-अन्ते at the end of the total dissolution of Prakriti (Prakrita-pralaya), ब्राह्मे कल्पे in the Brahma-kalpa, हि indeed, आदिमे the very first, लब्ध-जन्मा having attained birth, ब्रह्मा Brahma, भूयः again, त्वत्तः from You, एव alone, आप्य having received, वेदान् the Vedas, सृष्टिम् creation, चक्रे performed, पूर्व-कल्प-उपमानम् similar to the previous cosmic cycle

Simple Meaning: Thus, at the end of the grand dissolution of Prakriti (Prakrita-pralaya), Lord Brahma attained his rebirth in the very first Brahma-kalpa. Receiving the Vedas directly from You once again, he carried out the creation of the universe just as it was in the previous cosmic cycle.

सोऽयं चतुर्युगसहस्रमितान्यहानि
तावन्मिताश्च रजनीर्बहुशो निनाय ।
निद्रात्यसौ त्वयि निलीय समं स्वसृष्टैः
नैमित्तिकप्रलयमाहुरतोऽस्य रात्रिम् ॥8.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः He, अयम् this Brahma, चतुर्युग-सहस्र-मितानि measuring a thousand four-yuga cycles, अहानि days, तावत् मिताः of equal measure, च and, रजनीः nights, बहुशः many times, निनाय spent, निद्राति sleeps, असौ he, त्वयि in You, निलीय having merged, समम् along with, स्व-सृष्टैः his own creations, नैमित्तिक-प्रलयम् Naimittika-pralaya, आहुः they call, अतः therefore, अस्य his, रात्रिम् night

Simple Meaning: One thousand four-yuga cycles - Chaturyuga - make one day for this Brahma, and an equal duration is his night. During his day time he creates. At the end of his day, he sleeps, merging into You along with all the beings created by him. Therefore, sages call this night of Brahma as Naimittika-pralaya.

अस्मादृशां पुनरहर्मुखकृत्यतुल्यां
सृष्टिं करोत्यनुदिनं स भवत्प्रसादात् ।
प्राग्ब्राह्मकल्पजनुषां च परायुषां तु
सुप्तप्रबोधनसमास्ति तदाऽपि सृष्टिः ॥8.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: अस्मादृशाम् for beings like us, पुनः again, अहः-मुख-कृत्य-तुल्याम् similar to the daily morning duties, सृष्टिम् creation, करोति performs, अनुदिनम् every day, सः he, भवत्-प्रसादात् by Your grace, प्राक्-ब्राह्म-कल्प-जनुषाम् of those born in the previous Brahma-kalpa, च and, पर-आयुषाम् of those with long lifespans, तु indeed, सुप्त-प्रबोधन-समा is like awakening from sleep, अस्ति is, तदा also, अपि even, सृष्टिः creation

Simple Meaning: By Your grace, Lord Brahma performs the creation every single day of his life, just like beings like us who perform their daily morning routines. For those beings who were born in the previous Brahma-kalpa and possess a long life, being born in a new Kalpa is like waking up from a deep sleep.

पञ्चाशदब्दमधुना स्ववयोर्धरूपम्
एकं परार्धमतिवृत्य हि वर्ततेऽसौ ।
तत्रान्त्यरात्रिजनितान् कथयामि भूमन्
पश्चाद्दिनावतरणे च भवद्विलासान् ॥8.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: पञ्चाशत् अब्दम् fifty years, अधुना now, स्व-वयः-अर्ध-रूपम् consisting of half of his lifespan, एकम् one, परार्धम् Parardha, अतिवृत्य having completed, हि indeed, वर्तते is, असौ he, तत्र in that, अन्त्य-रात्रि-जनितान् produced during the previous night, कथयामि I shall narrate, भूमन् O Infinite Supreme Lord, पश्चात्-दिन-अवतरणे and during the dawn of the subsequent day, च and, भवत्-विलासान् Your divine sports

Simple Meaning: Lord Brahma has now completed fifty years of his age, which constitutes the first half of his full lifespan known as the first Parardha. O Supreme Lord, I shall now narrate Your divine sports that occurred during the previous night of that period, as well as during the dawn of the day that followed.

दिनावसानेऽथ सरोजयोनिः
सुषुप्तिकामस्त्वयि सन्निलिल्ये ।
जगन्ति च त्वज्जठरं समीयुः
तदेदमेकार्णवमास विश्वम् ॥8.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: दिन-अवसाने at the end of the day, अथ then, सरोज-योनिः the lotus-born Brahma, सुषुप्ति-कामः desiring deep sleep, त्वयि in You, सन्निलिल्ये merged completely, जगन्ति the worlds, च and, त्वत्-जठरम् Your abdomen, समीयुः entered, तदा then, इदम् this, एक-अर्णवम् a single vast ocean, आस became, विश्वम् the universe

Simple Meaning: Then, at the close of his day, the lotus-born Lord Brahma, desiring sleep, merged completely into You. All the worlds also entered Your abdomen, and the entire universe transformed into one single vast ocean.

तवैव वेषे फणिराजि शेषे
जलैकशेषे भुवने स्म शेषे ।
आनन्दसान्द्रानुभवस्वरूपः
स्वयोगनिद्रापरिमुद्रितात्मा ॥8.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: तव Your, एव alone, वेषे form, फणि-राजि on the king of serpents, शेषे on Shesha, जल-एक-शेषे when only water remained, भुवने in the universe, स्म शेषे You rested, आनन्द-सान्द्र-अनुभव-स्वरूपः embodiment of dense bliss and consciousness, स्व-योग-निद्रा-परिमुद्रित-आत्मा with Yourself absorbed in Your own yogic sleep

Simple Meaning: When the entire universe was submerged and only water remained, You rested upon the king of serpents, Shesha, who is a manifestation of Your own self. Being the embodiment of dense bliss and supreme consciousness, You lay there with Your mind absorbed in Your own divine yogic sleep.

कालाख्यशक्तिं प्रलयावसाने
प्रबोधयेत्यादिशता किलादौ ।
त्वया प्रसुप्तं परिसुप्तशक्ति-
व्रजेन तत्राखिलजीवधाम्ना ॥8.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: काल-आख्य-शक्तिम् the power known as time, प्रलय-अवसाने at the end of the dissolution, प्रबोधय awaken, इति thus, आदिशता instructing, किल indeed, आदौ in the beginning, त्वया by You, प्रसुप्तम् slept, परिसुप्त-शक्ति-व्रजेन along with the dormant powers, तत्र there, अखिल-जीव-धाम्ना the abode of all living beings

Simple Meaning: Instructing the power known as Time at the very beginning to awaken You at the end of the dissolution, You slept there as the abode of all living beings, while all other cosmic powers remained dormant within You.

चतुर्युगाणां च सहस्रमेवं
त्वयि प्रसुप्ते पुनरद्वितीये ।
कालाख्यशक्तिः प्रथमप्रबुद्धा
प्राबोधयत्त्वां किल विश्वनाथ ॥8.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: चतुर्युगाणाम् of the four-yugas, च and, सहस्रम् a thousand cycles, एवम् in this manner, त्वयि when You, प्रसुप्ते were sleeping, पुनः on the other hand, अ-द्वितीये without a second, काल-आख्य-शक्तिः the power called time, प्रथम-प्रबुद्धा waking up first, प्राबोधयत् awakened, त्वाम् You, किल indeed, विश्व-नाथ O Lord of the Universe

Simple Meaning: In this manner, while You, the One without a second, slept for a duration of one thousand cycles of four-yugas, the power called Time woke up first and indeed, awakened You, O Lord of the Universe.

विबुध्य च त्वं जलगर्भशायिन्
विलोक्य लोकानखिलान् प्रलीनान् ।
तेष्वेव सूक्ष्मात्मतया निजान्तः
स्थितेषु विश्वेषु ददाथ दृष्टिम् ॥8.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: विबुध्य having awakened, च and, त्वम् You, जल-गर्भ-शायिन् O Lord lying on the waters, विलोक्य seeing, लोकान् the worlds, अखिलान् all, प्रलीनान् dissolved, तेषु in those, एव indeed, सूक्ष्म-आत्मतया in subtle form, निज-अन्तः within Yourself, स्थितेषु remaining, विश्वेषु upon the worlds, ददाथ You cast, दृष्टिम् Your glance

Simple Meaning: O Lord lying on the cosmic waters, upon awakening and seeing all the worlds dissolved and residing in subtle form within Your own self, You cast Your divine glance upon those very worlds.

ततस्त्वदीयादयि नाभिरन्ध्रात्
उदञ्चितं किञ्चन दिव्यपद्मम् ।
निलीननिश्शेषपदार्थमाला-
संक्षेपरूपं मुकुलायमानम् ॥8.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, त्वदीयात् from Your, अयि O Lord, नाभि-रन्ध्रात् navel cavity, उदञ्चितम् arose, किञ्चन a certain, दिव्य-पद्मम् divine lotus, निलीन-निश्शेष-पदार्थ-माला-संक्षेप-रूपम् the condensed form of all the absorbed objects, मुकुलायमानम् appearing as a bud

Simple Meaning: Then, O Lord, from Your navel cavity there arose a miraculous divine lotus, which appeared in the form of a bud and held within itself the condensed essence of all the universe and its objects that had dissolved into You.

तदेतदम्भोरुहकुड्मलं ते
कलेवरात् तोयपथे प्ररूढम् ।
बहिर्निरीतं परितः स्फुरद्भिः
स्वधामभिर्ध्वान्तमलं न्यकृन्तत् ॥8.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् एतत् that very, अम्भोरुह-कुड्मलम् lotus bud, ते Your, कलेवरात् from the body, तोय-पथे into the expanse of waters, प्ररूढम् emerged, बहिः-निरीतम् coming outside, परितः all around, स्फुरद्भिः shining, स्व-धामभिः by its own brilliance, ध्वान्तम् darkness, अलम् completely, न्यकृन्तत् dispelled

Simple Meaning: That lotus bud emerged from Your divine body into the expanse of the cosmic waters. Coming outside, it completely dispelled the surrounding darkness with its own brilliant light shining in all directions.

संफुल्लपत्रे नितरां विचित्रे
तस्मिन् भवद्वीर्यधृते सरोजे ।
स पद्मजन्मा विधिराविरासीत्
स्वयंप्रबुद्धाखिलवेदराशिः ॥8.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: संफुल्ल-पत्रे with fully bloomed petals, नितराम् exceedingly, विचित्रे wonderful, तस्मिन् in that, भवत्-वीर्य-धृते sustained by Your divine power, सरोजे lotus, सः that, पद्म-जन्मा lotus-born, विधिः Brahma, आविः आसीत् appeared, स्वयं-प्रबुद्ध-अखिल-वेद-राशिः with the entire repository of Vedas themselves revealed to him

Simple Meaning: In that lotus which was exceedingly wonderful with fully bloomed petals and sustained by Your divine power, Lord Brahma was born. Upon his emergence, the entire repository of the Vedas themselves revealed to him.

अस्मिन् परात्मन् ननु पाद्मकल्पे
त्वमित्थमुत्थापितपद्मयोनिः ।
अनन्तभूमा मम रोगराशिं
निरुन्धि वातालयवास विष्णो ॥8.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: अस्मिन् in this, परात्मन् O Supreme Soul, ननु indeed, पाद्म-कल्पे Padma-kalpa, त्वम् You, इत्थम् in this manner, उत्थापित-पद्म-योनिः having caused the lotus-born Brahma to arise, अनन्त-भूमा O You of infinite glory, मम my, रोग-राशिम् multitude of diseases, निरुन्धि eradicate, वात-आलय-वास O dweller of the abode of the Wind - Guruvayoor, विष्णो O Lord Vishnu

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Soul of infinite glory! In this Padma-kalpa, You caused the lotus-born Brahma to arise in this manner. O Lord Vishnu who resides in Guruvayoor! Please completely eradicate all my diseases and ailments.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
