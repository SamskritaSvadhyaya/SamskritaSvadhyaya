Dashaka 59

The song of the flute

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 59

The song of the flute

त्वद्वपुर्नवकलायकोमलं प्रेमदोहनमशेषमोहनम् ।
ब्रह्म तत्त्वपरचिन्मुदात्मकं वीक्ष्य सम्मुमुहुरन्वहं स्त्रियः ॥59.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-वपुः Your divine body, नव-कलाय-कोमलम् tender like a fresh Kalaya flower, प्रेम-दोहनम् generating love, अशेष-मोहनम् enchanting everyone without exception, ब्रह्म Brahman, तत्त्व-पर-चित्-मुद्-आत्मकम् the ultimate reality composed of pure consciousness and bliss, वीक्ष्य seeing, सम्मुमुहुः fell deeply in love, अनु-अहम् day after day, स्त्रियः the gopis

Simple Meaning: Beholding Your divine body, tender like a fresh Kalaya flower, generating intense love, enchanting everyone, and embodying the ultimate reality of pure consciousness and bliss, the gopis fell more deeply in love with You day after day.

मन्मथोन्मथितमानसाः क्रमात्त्वद्विलोकनरतास्ततस्ततः ।
गोपिकास्तव न सेहिरे हरे काननोपगतिमप्यहर्मुखे ॥59.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: मन्मथ-उन्मथित-मानसाः with minds churned by love, क्रमात् gradually, त्वत्-विलोकन-रताः engrossed in gazing at You, ततः ततः everywhere, गोपिकाः the gopis, तव Your, न सेहिरे could not bear, हरे O Lord Hari, कानन-उपगतिम् अपि even Your departure to the forest, अहः-मुखे at the break of dawn

Simple Meaning: O Lord Hari, with their minds deeply agitated by love, the gopis were constantly eager to gaze upon You wherever You went. Consequently, they could not bear to part with You even for a moment, finding Your departure to the forest at the break of dawn utterly unbearable.

निर्गते भवति दत्तदृष्टयस्त्वद्गतेन मनसा मृगेक्षणाः ।
वेणुनादमुपकर्ण्य दूरतस्त्वद्विलासकथयाऽभिरेमिरे ॥59.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: निर्गते भवति when You departed, दत्त-दृष्टयः fixing their gaze on You, त्वत्-गतेन मनसा with minds absorbed in You, मृगेक्षणाः the doe-eyed gopis, वेणु-नादम् उपकर्ण्य hearing the music of Your flute, दूरतः from afar, त्वत्-विलास-कथया through conversations about Your playful pastimes, अभिरेमिरे revelled in joy

Simple Meaning: When You departed for the forest, those doe-eyed gopis kept their eyes fixed on You until You vanished from sight. With their minds completely absorbed in You, they heard the melody of Your flute echoing from afar and spent their time delightfully singing and talking about Your enchanting pastimes.

काननान्तमितवान् भवानपि स्निग्धपादपतले मनोरमे ।
व्यत्ययाकलितपादमास्थितः प्रत्यपूरयत वेणुनालिकाम् ॥59.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: कानन-अन्तम् इतवान् having reached the heart of the forest, भवान् अपि You too, स्निग्ध-पादप-तले at the foot of a charming lush tree, मनोरमे delightfully, व्यत्यय-आकलित-पादम्-आस्थितः standing with feet crossed, प्रत्यपूरयत played delightfully, वेणु-नालिकाम् the flute

Simple Meaning: Having reached the deep, charming woods, You stood gracefully under the shade of a lush, beautiful tree with Your feet crossed, playing sweet melodies on Your flute.

मारबाणधुतखेचरीकुलं निर्विकारपशुपक्षिमण्डलम् ।
द्रावणं च दृषदामपि प्रभो तावकं व्यजनि वेणुकूजितम् ॥59.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: मार-बाण-धुत-खे-चरी-कुलम् agitated the women in the sky with Kama's arrows, निर्विकार-पशु-पक्षी-मण्डलम् animals and birds became still and motionless, द्रावणम् च and the hard stones, दृषदाम् अपि even melting, प्रभो O Lord, तावकम् Your, व्यजनि produced such an effect, वेणु-कूजितम् the sweet notes of Your flute

Simple Meaning: O Lord, the sweet melody of Your flute worked astounding wonders! It struck the celestial women with the shafts of love, rendered the naturally restless birds and animals completely motionless in deep ecstasy, and melted even the hard stones.

वेणुरन्ध्रतरलाङ्गुलीदलं तालसञ्चलितपादपल्लवम् ।
तत् स्थितं तव परोक्षमप्यहो संविचिन्त्य मुमुहुर्व्रजाङ्गनाः ॥59.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: वेणु-रन्ध्र-तरल-अङ्गुली-दलम् with tender sprout-like fingers moving swiftly over the holes of the flute, ताल-सञ्चलित-पाद-पल्लवम् with tender leaf-like feet tapping rhythmically to the beat, तत्-स्थितम् that enchanting posture, तव Your, परोक्षम् अपि even when not seen directly, अहो wonder of wonders, संविचिन्त्य contemplating, मुमुहुः व्रज-अङ्गनाः the maidens of Vraja swooned in ecstasy

Simple Meaning: With Your tender sprout-like fingers dancing deftly over the holes of the flute and Your tender feet gently tapping in rhythm, Your stance was mesmerizing. Wonder of wonders, even though You were far out of their sight in the forest, merely visualizing this posture caused the gopis of Vraja to faint in deep devotional ecstasy!

निर्विशङ्कभवदङ्गदर्शिनीः खेचरीः खगमृगान् पशूनपि ।
त्वत्पदप्रणयि काननं च ताः धन्यधन्यमिति नन्वमानयन् ॥59.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: निर्विशङ्क-भवत्-अङ्ग-दर्शिनीः fearlessly gazing at Your divine form, खे-चरीः the women in the sky, खग-मृगान् पशून् अपि the birds, wild beasts, and cows as well, त्वत्-पद-प्रणयि attached to Your lotus feet, काननम् च and the forest, ताः they, धन्य-धन्यम् इति as blessed among the most blessed, ननु अमानयन् indeed praised

Simple Meaning: The gopis considered the celestial women, birds, wild animals, and cows truly blessed, as they could gaze upon Your divine form without any hesitation or fear. They also extolled the forest of Vrindavan as most fortunate, as it was constantly adorned with the touch of Your lotus feet.

आपिबेयमधरामृतं कदा वेणुभुक्तरसशेषमेकदा ।
दूरतो बत कृतं दुराशयेत्याकुला मुहुरिमाः समामुहन् ॥59.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: आपिबेयम् अधर-अमृतम् may I drink the nectar of Your lips, कदा when, वेणु-भुक्त-रस-शेषम् the remaining essence left behind after being enjoyed by the flute, एकदा (at least) once, दूरतः बत कृतम् alas driven far away, दुराशया इति by such impossible desires, आकुलाः distressed, मुहुः again and again, इमाः these (gopis), समामुहन् swooned into deep delusion

Simple Meaning: "When, oh when, will I be blessed to drink the nectar of His lips, that remaining sweetness left behind after being tasted by His flute? Alas, such a desire is surely impossible!" Tormented by these intense longings, the gopis swooned again and again in deep ecstasy.

प्रत्यहं च पुनरित्थमङ्गनाश्चित्तयोनिजनितादनुग्रहात् ।
बद्धरागविवशास्त्वयि प्रभो नित्यमापुरिह कृत्यमूढताम् ॥59.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रत्यहम् च and day after day, पुनः इत्थम् in this manner, अङ्गनाः the maidens, चित्त-योनि-जनितात् caused by the effect of Kama, अनुग्रहात् by the grace, बद्ध-राग-विवशाः overwhelmed by intense love, त्वयि towards You, प्रभो O Lord, नित्यम् आपूः constantly attained, इह कृत्य-मूढताम् complete forgetfulness of their household duties here (in this world)

Simple Meaning: O Lord, in this manner, day after day, through the influence of intense love awakened within them, which became like a blessing on them, the maidens of Vraja became so completely overwhelmed with devotion for You that they lost all awareness of their daily household chores.

रागस्तावज्जायते हि स्वभावात् मोक्षोपायो यत्नतः स्यान्न वा स्यात् ।
तासां त्वेकं तद्द्वयं लब्धमासीत् भाग्यं भाग्यं पाहि मां मारुतेश ॥59.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: रागः attachment, तावत् how much ever, जायते arises, हि indeed, स्वभावात् naturally, मोक्ष-उपायः the path to liberation, यत्नतः through great effort, स्यात् न वा स्यात् may or may not be, तासाम् तु for those gopis however, एकम् तत्-द्वयम् both these came together as one, लब्धम् आसीत् was attained, भाग्यम् भाग्यम् how fortunate, how supreme their luck, पाहि माम् protect me, मारुतेश O Lord of Guruvayur

Simple Meaning: Attachment arises out of natural tendencies. The path to liberation may or may not come even through one’s efforts. But for those gopis, attachment to You served as a path to liberation! Both attachment and liberation came together as one! Oh, how supremely fortunate they were! O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect me!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
