Dashaka 4

The progress of the spiritual aspirant

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 4

The progress of the spiritual aspirant

(छन्दस् - रथोध्दथा)

कल्यतां मम कुरुष्व तावतीं कल्यते भवदुपासनं यया ।
स्पष्टमष्टविधयोगचर्यया पुष्टयाशु तव तुष्टिमाप्नुयाम् ॥4.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: कल्यताम् health (physical and mental fitness), मम my, कुरुष्व please grant/make, तावतीम् that much, कल्यते (by which) can be performed, भवत्-उपासनम् worship of You, यया by which, स्पष्टम् clearly, अष्ट-विध-योग-चर्यया by practicing the eight-fold path of Yoga, पुष्टया robust (fully developed), आशु quickly, तव Your, तुष्टिम् pleasure/satisfaction, आप्नुयाम् may I attain

Simple Meaning: Please grant me at least that much physical and mental health which is necessary to perform Your worship! Armed with that strength, may I quickly please You by diligently practicing the robust, eight-fold path of Yoga (Ashtanga Yoga) and quickly attain Your grace.

ब्रह्मचर्यदृढतादिभिर्यमैराप्लवादिनियमैश्च पाविताः ।
कुर्महे दृढममी सुखासनं पङ्कजाद्यमपि वा भवत्पराः ॥4.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्रह्मचर्य-दृढता-आदिभिः with firm practice of celibacy and others, यमैः by self-restraints, आप्लव-आदि-नियमैः च and by regular bathing and observances of right conduct, पाविताः purified, कुर्महे we assume, दृढम् firmly, अमी these (we), सुख-आसनम् comfortable posture (Sukhasana), पङ्कज-आद्यम् अपि वा or the Lotus posture and others, भवत्-पराः dedicated to You

Simple Meaning: Oh Lord! Having purified ourselves through the practice of self-restraint like firm celibacy, and daily observances such as regular bathing, firmly assume a comfortable posture, such as the Lotus posture (Padmasana) or any other, dedicated to You.

तारमन्तरनुचिन्त्य सन्ततं प्राणवायुमभियम्य निर्मलाः ।
इन्द्रियाणि विषयादथापहृत्यास्महे भवदुपासनोन्मुखाः ॥4.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तारम् the sacred syllable Om, अन्तः internally, अनुचिन्त्य meditating upon, सन्ततम् constantly, प्राण-वायुम् the vital breath, अभियम्य controlling, निर्मलाः purified, इन्द्रियाणि the senses, विषयात् from their sense objects, अथ then, अपहृत्य withdrawing, आस्महे we sit, भवत्-उपासन-उन्मुखाः eagerly turning towards Your worship

Simple Meaning: Oh Lord! Constantly meditating on the sacred syllable Om within our minds and controlling our vital breath, we become purified. Then, withdrawing our senses from all worldly objects, we sit in deep meditation, eagerly devoted to Your worship.

अस्फुटे वपुषि ते प्रयत्नतो धारयेम धिषणां मुहुर्मुहुः ।
तेन भक्तिरसमन्तरार्द्रतामुद्वहेम भवदङ्घ्रिचिन्तका ॥4.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अस्फुटे in the subtle/not clearly visible, वपुषि form, ते Your, प्रयत्नतः with great effort, धारयेम may we fix, धिषणाम् the intellect/mind, मुहुः मुहुः again and again, तेन by that, भक्ति-रसम् the sweet essence of devotion, अन्तः-आर्द्रताम् inner melting of the heart, उद्वहेम may we experience/bear, भवत्-अङ्घ्रि-चिन्तकाः as those who contemplate Your feet

Simple Meaning: On Your subtle, unmanifest form which is difficult to visualize, we shall try to fix our minds with great effort again and again. By doing so, and by contemplating Your divine feet, may we experience a deep devotion that melts our hearts.

विस्फुटावयवभेदसुन्दरं त्वद्वपुः सुचिरशीलनावशात् ।
अश्रमं मनसि चिन्तयामहे ध्यानयोगनिरतास्त्वदाश्रयाः ॥4.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: विस्फुट-अवयव-भेद-सुन्दरम् beautiful with clearly defined, distinct limbs, त्वत्-वपुः Your divine form, सुचिर-शीलनावशात् by long and constant practice, अश्रमम् effortlessly, मनसि in the mind, चिन्तयामहे we contemplate, ध्यान-योग-निरताः deeply absorbed in the yoga of meditation, त्वत्-आश्रयाः having taken refuge in You

Simple Meaning: Having taken refuge in You, being deeply absorbed in the yoga of meditation, and by our long and constant practice, we will be able to contemplate on Your form with every single beautiful limb clearly and vividly defined.

ध्यायतां सकलमूर्तिमीदृशीमुन्मिषन्मधुरताहृतात्मनाम् ।
सान्द्रमोदरसरूपमान्तरं ब्रह्म रूपमयि तेऽवभासते ॥4.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: ध्यायताम् of those who meditate, सकल-मूर्तिम् on the complete form, ईदृशीम् such as this (with all Your limbs clearly defined), उन्मिषत्-मधुरता-हृत-आत्मनाम् whose minds are completely captivated by the blossoming sweetness, सान्द्र-मोद-रस-रूपम् of the nature of intense, concentrated bliss, आन्तरम् internal, ब्रह्म रूपम् infinite Reality (form as Brahman), अयि O Lord, ते Your, अवभासते shines forth (reveals itself)

Simple Meaning: O Lord! To those who meditate on Your complete form in this manner i.e with a clear visualisation of all Your limbs - Saguna form), and their minds utterly captivated by its blossoming sweetness, Your true inner nature—which is the absolute, infinite Brahman, the very embodiment of concentrated, supreme bliss (Nirguna form)— reveals itself.

तत्समास्वदनरूपिणीं स्थितिं त्वत्समाधिमयि विश्वनायक ।
आश्रिताः पुनरतः परिच्युतावारभेमहि च धारणादिकम् ॥4.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्-समास्वादन-रूपिणीम् of the nature of experiencing that (bliss of Brahman), स्थितिम् state, त्वत्-समाधिम् communion with You (Samadhi), अयि विश्वनायक O Lord of the universe, आश्रिताः having attained, पुनः again, अतः from that state, परिच्युतौ upon falling down (distraction), आरभेमहि we shall begin, च and, धारणा-आदिकम् starting with concentration (Dharana) and others

Simple Meaning: O Lord of the universe! Having attained that state of supreme communion with You (Samadhi), which is the blissful experience of Brahman, we may sometimes fall back and be distracted into the worldly plane. Whenever that happens, we shall patiently begin our practice once again, starting from concentration (Dharana), meditation (Dhyana), and the other steps to steady our minds.

इत्थमभ्यसननिर्भरोल्लसत्त्वत्परात्मसुखकल्पितोत्सवाः ।
मुक्तभक्तकुलमौलितां गताः सञ्चरेम शुकनारदादिवत् ॥4.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: इत्थम् in this manner, अभ्यसन-निर्भर-उल्लसत्-त्वत्-परात्म-सुख-कल्पित-उत्सवाः who are celebrating a constant festival of joy arising from the supreme bliss of Your divine Self through continuous practice, मुक्त-भक्त-कुल-मौलिताम् the state of being the crown jewels among the liberated devotees, गताः having attained, सञ्चरेम may we wander (the earth), शुक-नारद-आदि-वत् like Shuka, Narada, and others

Simple Meaning: By practicing in this manner, may we experience a continuous festival of intense joy born from the supreme bliss of Your divine Self. Having thus attained liberation and becoming the crown jewels among Your greatest liberated devotees, may we freely wander this earth, just like the great sages Shuka, Narada, and others.

त्वत्समाधिविजये तु यः पुनर्मङ्क्षु मोक्षरसिकः क्रमेण वा ।
योगवश्यमनिलं षडाश्रयैरुन्नयत्यज सुषुम्नया शनैः ॥4.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-समाधि-विजये upon mastering this absorption in You, तु indeed, यः who, पुनः further, मङ्क्षु instantly, मोक्ष-रसिकः desiring liberation, क्रमेण वा or progressively, योग-वश्यम् controlled through Yoga, अनिलम् the vital breath (prana), षट्-आश्रयैः through the six subtle centers (chakras), उन्नयति raises upwards, अज O Unborn Lord, सुषुम्नया through the Sushumna nadi, शनैः gradually

Simple Meaning: O Unborn Lord! Upon mastering this Samadhi in You, a yogi who desires either immediate liberation or progressive liberation through the higher realms, takes control of the vital breath (prana) through the power of Yoga. He then gradually raises that breath upwards through the Sushumna channel, passing it step by step through the six subtle energy centers (chakras).

लिङ्गदेहमपि सन्त्यजन्नथो लीयते त्वयि परे निराग्रहः ।
ऊर्ध्वलोककुतुकी तु मूर्धतः सार्धमेव करणैर्निरीयते ॥4.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: लिङ्ग-देहम् the subtle body, अपि even, सन्त्यजन् renouncing, अथः then, लीयते merges, त्वयि in You, परे in the Supreme Being, निराग्रहः one who is free from all desires, ऊर्ध्व-लोक-कुतुकी one who desires the higher worlds, तु but, मूर्धतः through the crown of the head (Brahma-randhra), सार्धम् एव along with, करणैः the senses and mind, निरीयते departs (goes forth)

Simple Meaning: A yogi who is free from all desires renounces even his subtle body right here and merges directly into You, the Supreme Being. But a yogi who still holds a desire to experience the higher celestial worlds departs from the body through the crown of the head (Brahma-randhra), carrying his mind and senses along with him.

अग्निवासरवलर्क्षपक्षगैरुत्तरायणजुषा च दैवतैः ।
प्रापितो रविपदं भवत्परो मोदवान् ध्रुवपदान्तमीयते ॥4.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: अग्नि-वासर-वलर्क्ष-पक्ष-गैः by the deities presiding over fire, daytime, and the bright fortnight, उत्तरायण-जुषा by those presiding over the six-month northern course of the sun, च and, दैवतैः by these divine guides, प्रापितः led to, रवि-पदम् the realm of the sun, भवत्-परः one who is solely devoted to You, मोदवान् full of joy, ध्रुव-पद-अन्तम् up to the region of Dhruva (the Pole Star) and beyond, ईयते reaches (travels)

Simple Meaning: Guided step by step by the deities presiding over fire, daytime, the bright fortnight of the moon, and the northern course of the sun (Uttarayana), Your single-minded devotee is led first to the realm of the sun. Filled with joy, he then travels further upward, reaching the region of Dhruva and the higher cosmic realms.

आस्थितोऽथ महरालये यदा शेषवक्त्रदहनोष्मणार्द्यते ।
ईयते भवदुपाश्रयस्तदा वेधसः पदमतः पुरैव वा ॥4.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: आस्थितः staying, अथ then, महः-आलये in Maharloka (the higher cosmic realm), यदा when, शेष-वक्त्र-दहन-ऊष्मणा by the heat of the fire emanating from the mouths of Lord Shesha, अर्द्यते is distressed, ईयते goes, भवत्-उपाश्रयः one who has taken refuge in You, तदा then, वेधसः पदम् to the abode of Brahma (Satya-loka/Brahma-loka), अतः from there, पुरा एव वा or even well before that

Simple Meaning: The devotee staying in Mahar-loka, upon feeling distressed by the intense heat of the destructive fire emanating from the mouths of Lord Shesha at the end of the kalpa, then moves upward to Brahma-loka, the supreme abode of Brahma. Alternatively, by the power of his devotion, he may choose to go directly to Brahma's abode even well before that destruction begins.

तत्र वा तव पदेऽथवा वसन् प्राकृतप्रलय एति मुक्तताम् ।
स्वेच्छया खलु पुरा विमुच्यते संविभिद्य जगदण्डमोजसा ॥4.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there (in Satyaloka), वा or, तव पदे in Your own abode (Vaikuntha), अथवा or else, वसन् residing, प्राकृत-प्रलये during the cosmic dissolution (Prakrita Pralaya), एति attains, मुक्तताम् liberation, स्वेच्छया by his own will, खलु indeed, पुरा before that, विमुच्यते is liberated, संविभिद्य breaking through, जगत्-अण्डम् the cosmic egg, ओजसा by the power (of his yoga)

Simple Meaning: Residing either there in Satyaloka or in Your own abode (Vaikuntha), the devotee attains final liberation at the time of the great cosmic dissolution (Prakrita Pralaya). Alternatively, by the sheer power of his yoga, he can even break through the layers of the cosmic egg by his own will and achieve ultimate liberation well before that time.

तस्य च क्षितिपयोमहोऽनिलद्योमहत्प्रकृतिसप्तकावृतीः ।
तत्तदात्मकतया विशन् सुखी याति ते पदमनावृतं विभो ॥4.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य of that (cosmic egg), च and, क्षिति-पयः-महः-अनिल-द्यो-महत्-प्रकृति-सप्तक-आवृतीः the seven concentric layers consisting of earth, water, fire, air, space, Mahat (the cosmic intellect), and Prakriti (primordial nature), तत्-तत्-आत्मकतया by identifying himself with each of those respective elements, विशन् entering, सुखी blissfully, याति attains, ते Your, पदम् supreme abode, अनावृतम् unconfined (free from all coverings), विभो O all-pervading Lord

Simple Meaning: O all-pervading Lord! Breaking through the cosmic egg, that liberated yogi blissfully passes through its seven concentric covering layers—earth, water, fire, air, space, the cosmic intellect (Mahat), and primordial nature (Prakriti). By identifying his own subtle elements with each of these cosmic layers, he dissolves them step by step and ultimately reaches Your supreme, unconfined, and eternal abode (Vaikuntha), never to return to worldly existence.

अर्चिरादिगतिमीदृशीं व्रजन् विच्युतिं न भजते जगत्पते ।
सच्चिदात्मक भवत् गुणोदयानुच्चरन्तमनिलेश पाहि माम् ॥4.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: अर्चिः-आदि-गतिम् the path of light starting with the deity of flame, ईदृशीम् such as this, व्रजन् traveling by, विच्युतम् fall down (return to rebirth), न भजते never experiences, जगत्पते O Lord of the universe, सत्-चित्-आत्मक O You whose nature is pure Existence and Consciousness, भवत्-गुण-उदयान् the manifestations of Your divine attributes and glories, उच्चरन्तम् who am constantly singing and praising, अनिल-ईश O Lord of Wind -  Guruvayoor, पाहि protect, माम् me

Simple Meaning: O Lord of the universe! O Supreme Being whose very essence is pure Existence and Consciousness (Sat-Chit)! A yogi traveling along this path of light (Archiradi Marga) never falls back into the cycle of birth and death. O Lord of Guruvayoor, please protect me, who is constantly singing and celebrating the glorious attributes of Your divine manifestations!

Note on Chapter Conclusion: This verse concludes Dasaka 4 of the Narayaneeyam, which beautifully outlines the systematic path of Ashtanga Yoga and the progressive liberation (Krama Mukti) of a devotee.
