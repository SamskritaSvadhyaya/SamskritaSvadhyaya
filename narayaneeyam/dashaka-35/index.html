Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 35

The incarnation as Rama (contd.)

(Rama Avatara)

नीतस्सुग्रीवमैत्रीं तदनु हनुमता दुन्दुभेः कायमुच्चैः
क्षिप्त्वाङ्गुष्ठेन भूयो लुलुविथ युगपत् पत्रिणा सप्त सालान् ।
हत्वा सुग्रीवघातोद्यतमतुलबलं वालिनं व्याजवृत्त्या
वर्षावेलामनैषीर्विरहतरलितस्त्वं मतङ्गाश्रमान्ते ॥35.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: नीतः led/brought into, सुग्रीव-मैत्रीम् friendship with Sugriva, तदनु thereafter, हनुमता by Hanuman, दुन्दुभेः of Dundubhi, कायम् the massive skeleton/body, उच्चैः high into the air, क्षिप्त्वा kicking, अङ्गुष्ठेन with Your big toe, भूयः furthermore, लुलुविथ You pierced/felled, युगपत् simultaneously, पत्रिणा with a single arrow, सप्त सालान् seven sala trees, हत्वा killing, सुग्रीव-घात-उद्यतम् who was intent on killing Sugriva, अतुल-बलम् of incomparable strength, वालिनम् Vali, व्याज-वृत्त्या through a disguised/hidden strategy, वर्षा-वेलाम् the rainy season, अनैषीः You spent, विरह-तरलितः agitated by separation from Sita, त्वम् You, मतङ्ग-आश्रम-अन्ते near the hermitage of Sage Matanga

Simple Meaning: Brought into friendship with Sugriva by Hanuman, You demonstrated Your immense power by effortlessly flicking the massive skeleton of Dundubhi high into the air with Your big toe, and then piercing seven sala trees simultaneously with a single arrow. You then struck down Vali, possessing incomparable strength, through a hidden strategy as he prepared to kill Sugriva. Deeply agitated by the pain of separation from Sita, You spent the duration of the rainy season near the hermitage of Sage Matanga.

सुग्रीवेणानुजोक्त्या सभयमभियता व्यूहितां वाहिनीं ताम्
ऋक्षाणां वीक्ष्य दिक्षु द्रुतमथ दयितामार्गणायावनम्राम् ।
सन्देशं चाङ्गुलीयं पवनसुतकरे प्रादिशो मोदशाली
मार्गे मार्गे ममार्गे कपिभिरपि तदा त्वत्प्रिया सप्रयासैः ॥35.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुग्रीवेण by Sugriva, अनुज-उक्त्या through the words of Your younger brother (Lakshmana), सभयम्-अभियता who approached in fear, व्यूहिताम् organized/marshaled, वाहिनीम् the army, ताम् that, ऋक्षाणाम् of the bears and monkeys, वीक्ष्य seeing, दिक्षु in all directions, द्रुतम् quickly, अथ then, दयिता-मार्गणाय for the search of Your beloved Sita, अवनम्राम् bowing down in devotion, सन्देशम् message, च and, अङ्गुलीयम् the signet ring, पवनसुत-करे in the hands of Hanuman (the son of the wind god), प्रादिशः You entrusted, मोदशाली filled with joy, मार्गे मार्गे path after path, ममार्गे was searched, कपिभिः by the monkeys, अपि also, तदा at that time, त्वत्-प्रिया Your beloved Sita, सप्रयासैः with great efforts

Simple Meaning: Reminded by the stern warning of Lakshmana, Sugriva fearfully approached You with a vast and well-organized army of monkeys and bears. Seeing them bowing before You, ready to be deployed in all directions to search for Your beloved, You felt immense joy and handed over a personal message along with Your signet ring into the hands of Hanuman. The dedicated monkeys then painstakingly searched every possible path and region in their effort to find Your beloved Sita.

त्वद्वार्ताकर्णनोद्यद्गरुदुरुजवसम्पातिसम्पातिवाक्य-
प्रोत्तीर्णार्णोधिरन्तर्नगरि जनकजां वीक्ष्य दत्वाङ्गुलीयम् ।
प्रक्षुद्योद्यानमक्षक्षपणचणरणः सोढबन्धो दशास्यं
दृष्ट्वा प्लुष्ट्वा च लङ्कां झटिति स हनुमान् मौलिरत्नं ददौ ते ॥35.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-वार्ता-कर्णन-उद्यत्-गरुत्-उरु-जव-सम्पाति-सम्पाति-वाक्य-प्रोत्तीर्ण-अर्णोधिः who crossed the ocean guided by the words of Sampati whose massive wings regrew upon hearing Your news, अन्तः-नगरि inside the city of Lanka, जनकजाम् the daughter of Janaka (Sita), वीक्ष्य seeing, दत्वा giving, अङ्गुलीयम् the signet ring, प्रक्षुद्य destroying, उद्यानम् the royal garden, अक्ष-क्षपण-चण-रणः who proved his mastery in battle by destroying Akshakumara, सोढ-बन्धः who allowed himself to be bound, दशास्यम् the ten-headed Ravana, दृष्ट्वा confronting/seeing, प्लुष्ट्वा burning, च and, लङ्काम् Lanka, झटिति swiftly, सः he, हनुमान् Hanuman, मौलि-रत्नम् the crown jewel, ददौ handed over, ते to You

Simple Meaning: Hearing the news about You caused Sampati's powerful wings to regrow, and guided by his words, Hanuman crossed the vast ocean. He located Sita in the inner city of Lanka, presented her with Your signet ring, and went on to destroy Ravana's royal garden. Demonstrating supreme prowess, he slew Akshakumara in battle, willingly submitted to being bound so he could confront the ten-headed Ravana face-to-face, and set Lanka ablaze. Returning swiftly, Hanuman placed Sita's precious crown jewel into Your hands.

त्वं सुग्रीवाङ्गदादिप्रबलकपिचमूचक्रविक्रान्तभूमी-
चक्रोऽभिक्रम्य पारेजलधि निशिचरेन्द्रानुजाश्रीयमाणः ।
तत्प्रोक्तां शत्रुवार्तां रहसि निशमयन् प्रार्थनापार्थ्यरोष-
प्रास्ताग्नेयास्त्रतेजस्त्रसदुदधिगिरा लब्धवान् मध्यमार्गम् ॥35.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वम् You, सुग्रीव-अङ्गद-आदि-प्रबल-कपि-चमू-चक्र-विक्रान्त-भूमी-चक्रः along with the vast military force of powerful monkey commanders led by Sugriva and Angada covering the earth, अभिक्रम्य advancing towards, पारे जलधि the shores of the ocean, निशिचर-इन्द्र-अनुज-आश्रीयमाणः sought as refuge by the younger brother of the king of the rakshasas (Vibhishana), तत्-प्रोक्ताम् disclosed by him, शत्रु-वार्ताम् intelligence regarding the enemy, रहसि in secret, निशमयन् listening to, प्रार्थना-पार्थ्य-रोष-प्रास्त-आग्नेय-अस्त्र-तेजः-त्रसत्-उदधि-गिरा on the advice of the lord of the ocean who was terrified by the fierce brilliance of the Agneyastra arrow discharged when Your initial prayers were ignored, लब्धवान् obtained, मध्य-मार्गम् the way across the middle of the sea

Simple Meaning: Accompanied by the formidable monkey army led by Sugriva, Angada, and other brave commanders whose forces seemed to cover the entire earth, You marched to the shores of the ocean. There, Vibhishana, the younger brother of the king of the rakshasas, sought refuge in You, and You listened to the strategic secrets of the enemy he disclosed in confidence. When Your initial peaceful prayers to the lord of the ocean went unanswered, You released the scorching Agneyastra. Terrified by its fiery brilliance, the lord of the ocean appeared and offered You the means to construct a path through the sea.

कीशैराशान्तरोपाहृतगिरिनिकरैः सेतुमाधाप्य यातो
यातून्यामर्द्य दंष्ट्रानखशिखरिशिलासालशस्त्रैः स्वसैन्यैः ।
व्याकुर्वन् सानुजस्त्वं समरभुवि परं विक्रमं शक्रजेत्रा
वेगान्नागास्त्रबद्धः पतगपतिगरुन्मारुतैर्मोचितोऽभूः ॥35.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: कीशैः by the monkeys, आशन-अन्तर-उपाहृत-गिरि-निकरैः with heaps of mountains brought from various directions, सेतुम् a bridge, आधाप्य having caused to be constructed, यातः crossing over, यातूनि the rakshasa forces, अमर्द्य crushing/destroying, दंष्ट्रा-नख-शिखरि-शिला-साल-शस्त्रैः using teeth, nails, mountain peaks, boulders, and trees as weapons, स्वसैन्यैः by Your army, व्याकुर्वन् displaying, सानुजः along with Your younger brother (Lakshmana), त्वम् You, समर-भुवि on the battlefield, परम् supreme, विक्रमम् prowess/valor, शक्रजेत्रा by Indrajit (the conqueror of Indra), वेगात् suddenly, नाग-अस्त्र-बद्धः bound by the deadly Nagastra missile, पतग-पति-गरुत्-मारुतैः by the wind generated by the powerful wings of Garuda (the lord of birds), मोचितः अभूः You were liberated/freed

Simple Meaning: Having a bridge constructed over the ocean with heaps of mountains brought from all directions by the monkeys, You crossed over to Lanka. Your army crushed the rakshasa forces using their teeth, nails, mountain peaks, boulders, and trees as weapons. As You and Your younger brother Lakshmana displayed supreme valor on the battlefield, You were suddenly bound by the deadly Nagastra missile fired by Indrajit. However, You were swiftly liberated from the serpent-bonds by the wind created by the fanning wings of Garuda.

सौमित्रिस्त्वत्र शक्तिप्रहृतिगलदसुर्वातजानीतशैल-
घ्राणात् प्राणानुपेतो व्यकृणुत कुसृतिश्लाघिनं मेघनादम् ।
मायाक्षोभेषु वैभीषणवचनहृतस्तम्भनः कुम्भकर्णं
सम्प्राप्तं कम्पितोर्वीतलमखिलचमूभक्षिणं व्यक्षिणोस्त्वम् ॥35.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: सौमित्रिः the son of Sumitra (Lakshmana), तु अत्र however here, शक्ति-प्रहृति-गलत्-असुः whose life breath was ebbing away due to the strike of the Shakti weapon, वातज-आनीत-शैल-घ्राणात् by smelling (the medicinal herbs on) the mountain brought by the son of the wind god - Hanuman, प्राणान् उपेतः regained his life, व्यकृणुत killed/destroyed, कुसृति-श्लाघिनम् who boasted of his deceitful tricks, मेघनादम् Meghanada (Indrajit), माया-क्षोभेषु during the illusions and turmoil created by deceitful tactics, वैभीषण-वचन-हृत-स्तम्भनः whose stupor/confusion was removed by the words of Vibhishana, कुम्भकर्णम् Kumbhakarna, सम्प्राप्तम् who arrived, कम्पित-उर्वी-तलम् making the surface of the earth tremble, अखिल-चमू-भक्षिणम् who was devouring the whole army, व्यक्षिणोः You destroyed/annihilated, त्वम् You

Simple Meaning: When Lakshmana was struck by the deadly Shakti weapon and his life breath was fading, he was restored to life by smelling the divine herbs from the mountain brought by Hanuman. He then went on to kill Meghanada, who prided himself on his deceptive techniques. When You were temporarily perturbed by the illusions created by the rakshasa forces, Vibhishana's timely words cleared Your mind. You then directly confronted and annihilated Kumbhakarna, who was shaking the very earth as he advanced, devouring whole armies of monkeys.

गृह्णन् जम्भारिसंप्रेषितरथकवचौ रावणेनाभियुद्ध्यन्
ब्रह्मास्त्रेणास्य भिन्दन् गलततिमबलामग्निशुद्धां प्रगृह्णन् ।
देवश्रेणीवरोज्जीवितसमरमृतैरक्षतैः ऋक्षसङ्घैः
लङ्काभर्त्रा च साकं निजनगरमगाः सप्रियः पुष्पकेण ॥35.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: गृह्णन् accepting, जम्भ-अरि-संप्रेषित-रथ-कवचौ the chariot and armor sent by Indra (the enemy of Jambha), रावणेन अभियुद्ध्यन् fighting against Ravana, ब्रह्मास्त्रेण by the Brahmastra missile, अस्य his, भिन्दन् severing, गलततिम् line of heads, अबलाम् Your wife (Sita), अग्नि-शुद्धाम् purified by fire, प्रगृह्णन् accepting back, देव-श्रेणी-वर-उज्जीवित-समर-मृतैः restored to life by the boons of the assembly of gods after dying in battle, अक्षतैः unhurt/unharmed, ऋक्ष-सङ्घैः with the hosts of bears and monkeys, लङ्का-भर्त्रा the king of Lanka - Vibhishana, च and, साकम् together, निज-नगरम् to Your own city (Ayodhya), अगाः You returned, स-प्रियः accompanied by Your beloved Sita, पुष्पकेण by the Pushpaka vimana

Simple Meaning: Accepting the celestial chariot and armor sent by Indra, You engaged in a fierce battle with Ravana and severed his ten heads using the Brahmastra, and accepted Sita back, who proved her purity through fire. The monkey and bear armies were restored to life by the boons of the gods. Accompanied by Your beloved Sita, Vibhishana, and Your allies, You returned to Your own city of Ayodhya on the Pushpaka aerial vehicle.

प्रीतो दिव्याभिषेकैरयुतसमधिकान् वत्सरान् पर्यरंसीः
मैथिल्यां पापवाचा शिव! शिव! किल तां गर्भिणीमभ्यहासीः ।
शत्रुघ्नेनार्दयित्वा लवणनिशिचरं प्रार्दयः शूद्रपाशं
तावद्वाल्मीकिगेहे कृतवसतिरुपासूत सीता सुतौ ते ॥35.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रीतः delighted, दिव्य-अभिषेकैः by the grand coronation ceremonies, अयुत-समधिकान् exceeding ten thousand, वत्सरान् years, पर्यरंसीः You ruled/enjoyed life, मैथिल्याम् regarding the daughter of Mithila (Sita), पाप-वाचा through scandalous words, शिव शिव alas, किल indeed, ताम् her, गर्भिणीम् while pregnant, अभ्यहासीः You abandoned, शत्रुघ्नेन through Shatrughna, अर्दयित्वा having killed, लवण-निशिचरम् the rakshasa Lavana, प्रार्दयः You eliminated, शूद्रपाशम् the vile Shudra (Shambuka), तावत् in the meantime, वाल्मीकि-गेहे in the hermitage of Valmiki, कृत-वसतिः taking up residence, उपासूत gave birth to, सीता Sita, सुतौ ते Your two sons

Simple Meaning: Delighted after Your grand coronation, You ruled the kingdom for over ten thousand years. Alas, when scandalous gossip about Sita spread among the public, You abandoned her in the forest, even though she was pregnant. Later, You had Shatrughna kill the rakshasa Lavana and You Yourself executed Shambuka. In the meantime, Sita found refuge at the hermitage of Sage Valmiki and gave birth to Your twin sons.

वाल्मीकेस्त्वत्सुतोद्गापितमधुरकृतेराज्ञया यज्ञवाटे
सीतां त्वय्याप्तुकामे क्षितिमविशदसौ त्वं च कालार्थितोऽभूः ।
हेतोः सौमित्रिघाती स्वयमथ सरयूमग्ननिश्शेषभृत्यैः
साकं नाकं प्रयातो निजपदमगमो देव वैकुण्ठमाद्यम् ॥35.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: वाल्मीकेः of Valmiki, त्वत्-सुत-उद्गापित-मधुर-कृतेः where Your sweet story was sung by Your two sons, आज्ञया by the instruction, यज्ञ-वाटे at the sacrificial hall, सीताम् Sita, त्वयि आप्तुकामे when You desired to accept back, क्षितिम् the earth, अविशत् असौ she entered, त्वम् You, च and, काल-अर्थितः अभूः were requested by Time (Kala), हेतोः due to a cause, सौमित्रि-घाती abandoning Lakshmana, स्वयम् Yourself, अथ then, सरयू-मग्न-निश्शेष-भृत्यैः along with all Your servants who immersed themselves in the Sarayu river, साकम् together with, नाकम् to heaven, प्रयातः having departed, निज-पदम् Your own abode, अगमः You reached, देव O Lord, वैकुण्ठम् आद्यम् the eternal primeval Vaikuntha

Simple Meaning: At the sacrificial hall, in the direction of Valmiki, Your sweet epic was sung by Your two sons. When You wished to accept Sita back, she chose instead to enter the earth. Later, requested by Kala, You were forced by circumstance to abandon Lakshmana. Thereafter, along with all Your subjects and devoted servants who merged into the holy Sarayu river, You departed from the earth and returned to Your supreme, eternal abode of Vaikuntha.

सोऽयं मर्त्यावतारस्तव खलु नियतं मर्त्यशिक्षार्थमेवं
विश्लेषार्तिर्निरागस्त्यजनमपि भवेत् कामधर्मातिसक्त्या ।
नो चेत् स्वात्मानुभूतेः क्व नु तव मनसो विक्रिया चक्रपाणे
स त्वं सत्त्वैकमूर्ते पवनपुरपते व्याधुनु व्याधितापान् ॥35.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः अयम् that this, मर्त्य-अवतारः human incarnation, तव Your, खलु indeed, नियतम् surely, मर्त्य-शिक्षा-अर्थम् for teaching mankind, एवम् in this manner, विश्लेष-आर्तिः agony of separation, निरागः त्यजनम् abandonment of the innocent, अपि even, भवेत् happens, काम-धर्म-अति-सक्त्या through excessive attachment to desire and righteous duty, नो चेत् otherwise, स्व-आत्मा-अनुभूतेः of the one whose mind is ever merged in self-realization, क्व नु where indeed, तव Your, मनसः of mind, विक्रिया agitation/disturbance, चक्रपाणे O Wielder of the Discus, सः त्वम् that You, सत्त्व-एक-मूर्ते O Embodiment of Pure Sattva, पवनपुरपते O Lord of Guruvayur, व्याधुनु dispel/remove, व्याधि-तापान् the afflictions of disease and suffering

Simple Meaning: This human incarnation of Yours was surely meant to teach mankind a lesson. It showed how excessive attachment to emotional desires and strict adherence to duty can lead to the agony of separation and the abandonment of the innocent. Otherwise, for You whose mind is perpetually immersed in the bliss of the Self, how could there ever be any sorrow or mental disturbance, O Wielder of the Discus? O Lord of Guruvayur, the very embodiment of pure Sattva, please completely eradicate all my diseases and suffering!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
