Journey to Samantapanchaka and meeting with the friends and relatives

दशकम् ८४

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 84

Journey to Samantapanchaka and meeting with the friends and relatives

क्वचिदथ तपनोपरागकाले पुरि निदधत् कृतवर्मकामसूनू ।
यदुकुलमहिलावृतः सुतीर्थं समुपगतोऽसि समन्तपञ्चकाख्यम् ॥84.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्वचित् अथ once on an occasion, तपन-उपराग-काले at the time of a solar eclipse, पुरि in the city of Dvaraka, निदधत् leaving behind as guardians, कृतवर्म-कामसूनू Kritavarma and Aniruddha (the son of Pradyumna - the incarnation of Kamadeva), यदुकुल-महिला-वृतः surrounded by the women of the Yadava clan, सुतीर्थम् to the sacred pilgrimage site, समुपगतः असि You arrived, समन्तपञ्चक-आख्यम् named Samantapanchaka

Simple Meaning: Once, on the auspicious occasion of a solar eclipse, You entrusted the protection of Dwaraka to Kritavarma and Anuruddha, the son of Pradyumna. Accompanied by the members of the Yadava clan and their women, You traveled to the sacred pilgrimage site known as Samantapanchaka.

बहुतरजनताहिताय तत्र त्वमपि पुनन् विनिमज्य तीर्थतोयम् ।
द्विजगणपरिमुक्तवित्तराशिः सममिलथाः कुरुपाण्डवादिमित्रैः ॥84.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: बहुतर-जनता-हिताय for the welfare of the vast gathering of people, तत्र there, त्वम् अपि You as well, पुनन् purifying, विनिमज्य by bathing in, तीर्थ-तोयम् the sacred holy waters of the tirtha, द्विज-गण-परिमुक्त-वित्त-राशिः bestowing immense wealth upon assemblies of noble brahmins, सममिलथाः You met and reunited, कुरु-पाण्डव-आदि-मित्रैः with Your friends and relatives including the Kurus, Pandavas, and other allies

Simple Meaning: For the ultimate spiritual good of the immense gathering of pilgrims, You took a ceremonial holy dip, purifying the sacred waters of Kurukshetra by Your very presence. You distributed vast fortunes of wealth in charity to assemblies of pious brahmins, and joyfully reunited with Your beloved friends and relatives, including the Kurus, the Pandavas, and other royal allies who had assembled there.

तव खलु दयिताजनैः समेता द्रुपदसुता त्वयि गाढभक्तिभारा ।
तदुदितभवदाहृतिप्रकारैः अतिमुमुदे सममन्यभामिनीभिः ॥84.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तव Your, खलु indeed, दयिता-जनैः with Your beloved queens, समेता having met together, द्रुपद-सुता Draupadi (the daughter of King Drupada), त्वयि toward You, गाढ-भक्ति-भारा possessed of deep and intense devotion, तत्-उदित-भवत्-आहृति-प्रकारैः through the accounts narrated by them describing how You married/wedded each of them, अतिमुमुदे was filled with supreme joy, समम् together with, अन्य-भामिनीभिः other royal ladies

Simple Meaning: Reunited with Your beloved queens at Kurukshetra, Draupadi, who possessed deep and unwavering devotion to You, listened intently as each queen narrated the wondrous stories of how You won her hand and wedded her. Hearing these sweet accounts of Your divine marriages, Draupadi and all the other noble ladies present were filled with supreme joy.

तदनु च भगवन् निरीक्ष्य गोपानतिकुतुकादुपगम्य मानयित्वा।
चिरतरविरहातुराङ्गरेखाः पशुपवधूः सरसं त्वमन्वयासीः ॥84.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदनु after that, च and, भगवन् O Blessed Lord, निरीक्ष्य having seen, गोपान् the cowherds of Vrindavan, अति-कुतुकात् out of great affection and eagerness, उपगम्य approaching them, मानयित्वा honoring and comforting them, चिरतर-विरह-आतुर-अङ्ग-रेखाः whose physical forms were emaciated due to long and painful separation from You, पशुप-वधूः the gopis (wives of the cowherds), सरसम् with profound love and affection, त्वम् You, अन्वयासीः met and consoled

Simple Meaning: After meeting the royal families, O Blessed Lord, You saw the gopis and cowherds of Vrindavan who had also traveled to Kurukshetra. Approaching them with immense joy and affection, You honored Nanda and the cowherds. You then affectionately met and consoled the gopis, whose slender forms bore the marks of deep longing and agony caused by their prolonged separation from You since Your departure from Gokula.

सपदि च भवदीक्षणोत्सवेन प्रमुषितमानहृदां नितम्बिनीनाम् ।
अतिरसपरिमुक्तकञ्चुलीके परिचयहृद्यतरे कुचे न्यलैषीः ॥84.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: सपदि immediately, च and, भवत्-ईक्षण-उत्सवेन by the glorious festival of beholding You, प्रमुषित-मान-हृदाम् whose hearts were completely cleansed of any lingering resentment or hurt caused by separation, नितम्बिनीनाम् of those beautiful gopis, अति-रस-परिमुक्त-कञ्चुलीके where their bodices slipped off due to intense surge of emotional love, परिचय-हृद्यतरे which were deeply familiar and endearing to You, कुचे on their breasts, न्यलैषीः You embraced and rested

Simple Meaning: Instantly, upon experiencing the supreme festival of beholding Your divine form again, all feelings of lingering resentment and pain from long separation vanished from the hearts of those exquisite gopis. Overwhelmed by an intense surge of divine love, their garments loosened, and You lovingly embraced them, resting against their hearts that were so intimately known and deeply dear to You.

रिपुजनकलहैः पुनः पुनर्मे समुपगतैरियती विलम्बनाऽभूत् ।
इति कृतपरिरम्भणेत्वयि द्राक् अतिविवशा खलु राधिका निलिल्ये ॥84.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: रिपु-जन-कलहैः on account of conflicts and wars with enemies, पुनः पुनः repeatedly, मे to Me, समुपगतैः which arose, इयती so much, विलम्बना अभूत् delay has occurred, इति saying thus, कृत-परिरम्भणे when You embraced her, त्वयि in You, द्राक् instantly, अति-विवशा completely overwhelmed by ecstatic love, खलु indeed, राधिका Radhika, निलिल्ये merged herself

Simple Meaning: "Because of constant wars with my enemies that kept arising, such a long delay occurred in my coming to meet you," saying thus, You affectionately embraced Radhika. Overwhelmed with profound emotional love upon that embrace, she instantly lost herself in divine ecstasy, merging into Your loving form.

अपगतविरहव्यथास्तदा ता रहसि विधाय ददाथ तत्त्वबोधम् ।
परमसुखचिदात्मकोऽहमात्मेत्युदयतु वः स्फुटमेव चेतसीति ॥84.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: अपगत-विरह-व्यथाः whose agony of separation had completely vanished, तदा then, ताः those gopis, रहसि in solitude, विधाय taking aside, ददाथ You imparted, तत्त्व-बोधम् the supreme spiritual wisdom of Truth, परम-सुख-चित्-आत्मकः अहम् आत्मा इति that I am the Supreme Paramatman, the very essence of ultimate bliss and pure consciousness, उदयतु may this bloom/awaken, वः in all of you, स्फुटम् एव clearly indeed, चेतसि इति in your minds

Simple Meaning: Having thus dispelled their deep agony of separation, You took those gopis aside in solitude and imparted to them the highest spiritual instruction, teaching them: "Recognize that I am the Paramatman residing within your own hearts, the eternal embodiment of pure consciousness and absolute bliss. May this ultimate truth dawn clearly within your minds forever."

सुखरसपरिमिश्रितो वियोगः किमपि पुराऽभवदुद्धवोपदेशैः ।
समभवदमुतः परं तु तासां परमसुखैक्यमयी भवद्विचिन्ता ॥84.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुख-रस-परिमिश्रितः mixed with a taste of divine joy, वियोगः the pangs of separation, किमपि to some extent, पुरा in the past, अभवत् became, उद्धव-उपदेशैः through the spiritual teachings of Uddhava, समभवत् became, अमुतः परम् after this supreme instruction, तु but, तासाम् for those gopis, परम-सुख-ऐक्य-मयी filled with supreme state of oneness and absolute bliss, भवत्-विचिन्ता constant contemplation upon You

Simple Meaning: Earlier, through the profound philosophical instructions brought by Uddhava to Vrindavan, the intense pain of separation experienced by the gopis had reduced to some extent, but was still there. But now, after receiving this direct spiritual realization from You, the very thought of You was enough to transform them into a supreme state of oneness with You and obtain absolute bliss.

मुनिवरनिवहैस्तवाथ पित्रा दुरितशमाय शुभानि पृच्छ्यमानैः ।
त्वयि सति किमिदं शुभान्तरैः रित्युरुहसितैरपि याजितस्तदाऽसौ ॥84.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: मुनिवर-निवहैः by assemblies of exalted sages, तव Your, अथ then, पित्रा by father Vasudeva, दुरित-शमाय for the expiation of sins/purification, शुभानि auspicious sacrificial rites, पृच्छ्यमानैः being asked about, त्वयि सति when You, the Supreme Lord Himself, are present in person, किम् इदम् what is the need for this, शुभ-अन्तरैः with other ritualistic sacrifices, इति thus, उरु-हसितैः with deep smiles/laughter, अपि even so, याजितः made to perform sacrifices, तदा at that time, असौ he (Vasudeva)

Simple Meaning: At that assembly, Your father Vasudeva approached the exalted sages and inquired about auspicious sacrificial rites to expiate past sins. The great sages smiled deeply, amused that Vasudeva was seeking ritualistic purification despite having You, the supreme purifier of all existence, as his own son. Nevertheless, to honor traditional Vedic customs, they guided him in performing the grand sacrifices.

सुमहति यजने वितायमाने प्रमुदितमित्रजने सहैव गोपाः ।
यदुजनमहितास्त्रिमासमात्रं भवदनुषङ्गरसं पुरेव भेजुः ॥84.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुमहति in that magnificent, यजने sacrifice, वितायमाने being performed, प्रमुदित-मित्र-जने where all friends and relatives were filled with joy, सह एव together indeed, गोपाः the cowherds of Gokula, यदु-जन-महिताः honoured and revered by the members of the Yadava clan, त्रि-मास-मात्रम् for a period of three months, भवत्-अनुषङ्ग-रसम् the sweet joy of Your intimate company and presence, पुरा इव just as in the days of Vrindavan, भेजुः enjoyed and experienced

Simple Meaning: While that grand Vedic sacrifice was being performed amidst universal joy and celebration, Nanda and the cowherds of Gokula, highly revered and hosted with immense affection by the Yadavas, stayed at Samantapanchaka for three full months. During this time, they immersed themselves once again in the exquisite bliss of Your constant presence and companionship, just as they had during Your childhood in Vrindavan.

व्यपगमसमये समेत्य राधां दृढमुपगूह्य निरीक्ष्य वीतखेदाम् ।
प्रमुदितहृदयः पुरं प्रयातः पवनपुरेश्वर पाहि मां गदेभ्यः ॥84.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: व्यपगम-समये at the time of departure from Kurukshetra, समेत्य coming together with, राधाम् Radha, दृढम् tightly/deeply, उपगूह्य embracing, निरीक्ष्य observing her, वीत-खेदाम् free from all sorrow and grief, प्रमुदित-हृदयः with a joyful and satisfied heart, पुरम् to the city of Dwaraka, प्रयातः departed, पवनपुरेश्वर O Lord of Guruvayur (Pavanapuresvara), पाहि protect, माम् me, गदेभ्यः from all illnesses and afflictions

Simple Meaning: At the time of departure, You met Radha once again, held her in a tight embrace, and saw that her mind was completely serene and free from the grief of separation. With a heart filled with joy, You returned to Dwaraka along with the Yadavas. O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect me from all my ailments and physical afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
