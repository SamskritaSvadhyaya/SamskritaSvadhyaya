Krishna’s marriage to Rukmini

दशकम् ७९

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 79

Krishna’s marriage to Rukmini

बलसमेतबलानुगतो भवान् पुरमगाहत भीष्मकमानितः ।
द्विजसुतं त्वदुपागमवादिनं धृतरसा तरसा प्रणनाम सा ॥79.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: बल-समेत-बल-अनुगतः accompanied by Balarama and followed by an army, भवान् You, पुरम् अगाहत entered the city, भीष्मक-मानितः honored by King Bhishmaka, द्विज-सुतम् the brahmana youth, त्वत्-उपागम-वादिनम् who informed her of Your arrival, धृत-रसा filled with joy, तरसा quickly, प्रणनाम bowed in reverence, सा she

Simple Meaning: Accompanied by Balarama and followed by an army, You entered the city of Kundina, where You were warmly honored by King Bhishmaka. Upon hearing from the brahmana youth that You had arrived, Rukmini was filled with intense joy and quickly bowed in reverence before him.

भुवनकान्तमवेक्ष्य भवद्वपुर्नृपसुतस्य निशम्य च चेष्टितम् ।
विपुलखेदजुषां पुरवासिनां सरुदितैरुदितैरगमन्निशा ॥79.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: भुवन-कान्तम् the most enchanting in the universe, अवेक्ष्य having seen, भवत्-वपुः Your divine form, नृप-सुतस्य of the prince (Rukmi), निशम्य having heard, च and, चेष्टितम् the wicked plan or action, विपुल-खेद-जुषाम् suffering deep distress, पुर-वासिनाम् of the citizens of Kundina, सरुदितैः उदितैः with lamentations accompanied by tears, अगमत् passed, निशा the night

Simple Meaning: Having beheld Your form, which is the most captivating in the entire universe, and having heard of the foolish, wicked plan of Prince Rukmi to marry Rukmini to Shishupala, the citizens of Kundina spent the night in profound distress, weeping and lamenting their misfortune.

तदनु वन्दितुमिन्दुमुखी शिवां विहितमङ्गलभूषणभासुरा ।
निरगमत् भवदर्पितजीविता स्वपुरतः पुरतः सुभटावृता ॥79.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदनु thereafter, वन्दितुम् to offer worship, इन्दु-मुखी whose face was like the moon - Rukmini, शिवाम् to Goddess Parvati, विहित-मङ्गल-भूषण-भासुरा radiant with auspicious ornaments, निरगमत् set forth, भवत्-अर्पित-जीविता who had dedicated her life to You, स्व-पुरतः from her palace, पुरतः forward, सुभट-आवृता surrounded by brave guards

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, Rukmini, whose face was the moon, whose life was completely surrendered to You, set out from her palace radiant in her auspicious bridal ornaments. Surrounded by brave guards, she proceeded to the temple to offer worship to Goddess Parvati.

कुलवधूभिरुपेत्य कुमारिका गिरिसुतां परिपूज्य च सादरम् ।
मुहुरयाचत तत्पदपङ्कजे निपतिता पतितां तव केवलम् ॥79.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: कुल-वधूभिः उपेत्य accompanied by noble married women, कुमारिका the young princess Rukmini, गिरि-सुताम् the daughter of the mountain (Goddess Parvati), परिपूज्य having worshipped, च and, सादरम् respectfully, मुहुः अयाचत repeatedly prayed, तत्-पद-पङ्कजे at Her lotus feet, निपतिता prostrating herself, पतिताम् husband, तव Your, केवलम् exclusively

Simple Meaning: Accompanied by noble married women, the young princess Rukmini respectfully worshipped Goddess Parvati. Prostrating herself at the lotus feet of the Goddess, she repeatedly prayed with all her heart for one thing alone—to have You, and You only, as her husband.

समवलोककुतूहलसङ्कुले नृपकुले निभृतं त्वयि च स्थिते ।
नृपसुता निरगाद्गिरिजालयात् सुरुचिरं रुचिरञ्जितदिङ्मुखा ॥79.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: समवलोक-कुतूहल-सङ्कुले crowded with kings eager to catch a glimpse of her, नृप-कुले in the assembly of kings, निभृतम् quietly/eagerly waiting, त्वयि You, च and, स्थिते standing, नृप-सुता princess Rukmini, निरगात् came out, गिरिजा-आलयात् from the temple of Parvati, सुरुचिरम् exquisitely beautiful, रुचि-रञ्जित-दिक्-मुखा illuminating all directions with her enchanting radiance

Simple Meaning: While the assembly of kings stood crowded in eager anticipation to catch a glimpse of her, and You too stood quietly waiting, Princess Rukmini emerged from the temple of Parvati. Exquisitely beautiful, she illuminated every direction with her enchanting radiance.

भुवनमोहनरूपरुचा तदा विवशिताखिलराजकदम्बया ।
त्वमपि देव कटाक्षविमोक्षणैः प्रमदया मदयाञ्चकृषे मनाक् ॥79.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: भुवन-मोहन-रूप-रुचा by the radiance of her beauty that bewitched the whole world, तदा then, विवषित-अखिल-राज-कदम्बया by whom the entire host of kings was made helpless with longing, त्वम् You, अपि even, देव O Lord, कटाक्ष-विमोक्षणैः by the casting of her side-long glances, प्रमदया by that enchanting maiden, मदयाञ्चकृषे were enchanted, मनाक् a little bit

Simple Meaning: At that moment, by the radiance of her world-bewitching beauty, she rendered the entire host of assembled kings helpless with longing. O Lord, even You were slightly enchanted by the side-long glances cast by that captivating maiden!

क्वनु गमिष्यसि चन्द्रमुखीति तां सरसमेत्य करेण हरन् क्षणात् ।
समधिरोप्य रथं त्वमपाहृथा भुवि ततो विततो निनदो द्विषाम् ॥79.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्वनु where, गमिष्यसि are you going, चन्द्र-मुखि O moon-faced one, इति thus, ताम् her, सरसम् lovingly, एत्य approaching, करेण by the hand, हरन् taking or seizing, क्षणात् in a moment, समधिरोप्य placing her, रथम् on the chariot, त्वम् You, अपाहृथाः carried her away, भुवि on the earth, ततः then, विततः arose, निनदः a tumultuous roar, द्विषाम् of the enemies

Simple Meaning: Lovingly addressing her, "Where are you going, O moon-faced one?", You approached her, took her hand, and in a moment placed her on Your chariot and carried her away. Immediately, a tumultuous uproar of shock and anger arose among the gathered enemies.

क्व नु गतः पशुपाल इति क्रुधा कृतरणा यदुभिश्च जिता नृपाः ।
न तु भवानुदचाल्यत तैरहो पिशुनकैः शुनकैरिव केसरी ॥79.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्व where, नु indeed, गतः has gone, पशु-पालः the cowherd, इति thus, क्रुधा with anger, कृत-रणाः waging battle, यदुभिः by the Yadavas, च and, जिताः defeated, नृपाः the kings, न not, तु but, भवान् You, उदचाल्यत were shaken or perturbed, तैः by them, अहो wonder, पिशुनकैः by malicious, शुनकैः इव like dogs, केसरी a lion

Simple Meaning: Exclaiming in rage, "Where has that cowherd gone?", the kings waged a fierce battle, but were easily defeated by the Yadavas. You Yourself were not perturbed in the slightest by those malicious kings, just as a majestic lion is completely unmoved by dogs!

तदनु रुक्मिणमागतमाहवे वधमुपेक्ष्य निबध्य विरूपयन् ।
हृतमदं परिमुच्य बलोक्तिभिः पुरमया रमया सह कान्तया ॥79.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदनु thereafter, रुक्मिणम् Rukmi, आगतम् who came, आहवे in battle, वधम् slaying him, उपेक्ष्य refraining from, निबध्य tying him up, विरूपयन् disfiguring him (by shaving his head and mustache), हृत-मदम् crushed of his pride, परिमुच्य releasing him, बल-उक्तिभिः on the intervention and words of Balarama, पुरम् अयाः You went to Your city (Dwaraka), रमया सह along with Goddess Lakshmi, कान्तया Your beloved (Rukmini)

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, when Rukmi rushed forward to fight, You spared his life but tied him up and disfigured him by shaving off his hair and mustache to humiliate him. His pride thus crushed, You released him at the intervention of Balarama, and then proceeded to Your city of Dvaraka accompanied by Your beloved Rukmini, who is none other than Goddess Lakshmi herself.

नवसमागमलज्जितमानसां प्रणयकौतुकजृम्भितमन्मथाम् ।
अरमयः खलु नाथ यथासुखं रहसि तां हसितांशुलसन्मुखीम् ॥79.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: नव-समागम-लज्जित-मानसाम् whose mind was filled with shyness on account of her new union, प्रणय-कौतुक-जृम्भित-मन्मथाम् whose love and desire were heightened by the novelty and joy of affection, अरमयः You delighted, खलु indeed, नाथ O Lord, यथा-सुखम् as You pleased, रहसि in privacy, ताम् her, हसित-अंशु-लसत्-मुखीम् whose face shone brightly with the beams of her sweet smiles

Simple Meaning: O Lord, her mind was filled with shyness during this new union, yet her love and delight were heightened by the joy of Your affection. In privacy, You delighted her —she whose face shone brilliantly with the gentle radiance of her sweet smile.

विविधनर्मभिरेवमहर्निशं प्रमदमाकलयन् पुनरेकदा ।
ऋजुमतेः किल वक्रगिरा भवान् वरतनोरतनोदतिलोलताम् ॥79.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: विविध-नर्मभिः with various playful pleasantries and pleasant jokes, एवम् in this manner, अहः-निशम् day and night, प्रमदम् आकलयन् bestowing delight, पुनः एकदा then on one occasion, ऋजु-मतेः of the simple-minded and straightforward, किल indeed, वक्र-गिरा with hurtful words, भवान् You, वर-तनोः of that exquisite Rukmini, अतनोत् caused, अति-लोलताम् intense agitation and distress

Simple Meaning: Bestowing constant delight upon her day and night through various affectionate pleasantries, You on one occasion addressed that straightforward and simple-minded Rukmini with hurtful words (conveying Your apparent indifference to her), causing her intense mental turmoil and agitation.

तदधिकैरथ लालनकौशलैः प्रणयिनीमधिकं सुखयन्निमाम् ।
अयि मुकुन्द भवच्चरितानि नः प्रगदतां गदतान्तिमपाकुरु ॥79.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्-अधिकैः greater than that, अथ thereafter, लालन-कौशलैः by Your expert ways of fondling and caressing, प्रणयिनीम् Your beloved Rukmini, अधिकम् exceedingly, सुखयन् इमाम् making her happy, अयि मुकुन्द O Mukunda, भवत्-चरितानि Your glorious divine deeds, नः our, प्रगदताम् who recite, गद-तान्तिम् the torment of disease and affliction, अपाकुरु remove completely

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, by Your exceedingly tender and expert ways of affectionate caressing—far exceeding Your playful teasing—You brought immense joy and happiness to Your beloved Rukmini. O Mukunda, please completely remove the torments of illness and physical afflictions from us who recite Your divine deeds!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
