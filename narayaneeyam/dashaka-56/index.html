Dashaka 56

Krishna bestows grace upon Kaliya

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 56

Krishna bestows grace upon Kaliya

रुचिरकम्पितकुण्डलमण्डलः सुचिरमीश ननर्तिथ पन्नगे ।
अमरताडितदुन्दुभिसुन्दरं वियति गायति दैवतयौवते ॥56.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: रुचिर-कम्पित-कुण्डल-मण्डलः with a circle of beautiful ear ornaments trembling delightfully, सुचिरम् for a long time, ईश O Supreme Lord, ननर्तिथ You danced, पन्नगे on the serpent Kaliya, अमर-ताडित-दुन्दुभि-सुन्दरम् echoing beautifully with the beating of celestial drums by the devas, वियति in the sky, गायति singing, दैवत-यौवते while the celestial maidens

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Lord, with Your beautiful ear ornaments moving delightfully as You moved, You danced for a long time upon the hoods of the serpent Kaliya. In the sky above, celestial drums were struck beautifully by the devas while the celestial maidens sang Your praises in chorus.

नमति यद्यदमुष्य शिरो हरे परिविहाय तदुन्नतमुन्नतम् ।
परिमथन् पदपङ्करुहा चिरं व्यहरथाः करतालमनोहरम् ॥56.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: नमति as it bowed down, यत् यत् whichever, अमुष्य his, शिरः head, हरे O Lord Hari, परिविहाय leaving that, तत्-उन्नतम्-उन्नतम् whichever other hood was raised high, परिमथन् trampling down, पद-पङ्करुहा with Your lotus foot, चिरम् for a long time, व्यहरथाः You played, कर-ताल-मनोहरम् keeping delightful rhythm with hand clapping

Simple Meaning: O Lord Hari, whenever one of Kaliya's heads bowed down, You left it and stepped onto whichever other hood was raised high, trampling it down with Your lotus foot. Thus, You played for a long time, keeping a delightful rhythm with the clapping of hands.

त्वदवभग्नविभुग्नफणागणे गलितशोणितशोणितपाथसि ।
फणिपताववसीदति सन्नतास्तदबलास्तव माधव पादयोः ॥56.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-अवभग्न-विभुग्न-फणा-गणे with his collection of hoods broken and bent low by You, गलित-शोणित-शोणित-पाथसि with the waters turned red by the gushing blood, फणि-पतौ when the serpent king Kaliya, अवसीदति was sinking in utter exhaustion, सन्नताः bowed down, तत्-अबलाः his wives, तव Your, माधव O Madhava, पादयोः at the feet

Simple Meaning: O Madhava, when the serpent king Kaliya lay completely exhausted—his array of hoods crushed and bent low by Your steps, and the waters of the river turning crimson with his gushing blood—his wives bowed down in deep surrender at Your lotus feet.

अयि पुरैव चिराय परिश्रुतत्वदनुभावविलीनहृदो हि ताः ।
मुनिभिरप्यनवाप्यपथैः स्तवैर्नुनुवुरीश भवन्तमयन्त्रितम् ॥56.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अयि O Lord, पुरा एव long before itself, चिराय for a long time, परिश्रुत-त्वत्-अनुभाव-विलीन-हृदः हि whose hearts were indeed absorbed (in You) by hearing of Your divine power, ताः those wives of Kaliya, मुनिभिः अपि even by great sages, अनवाप्य-पथैः through paths unattainable, स्तवैः with hymns of praise, नुनुवुः praised, ईश O Supreme Lord, भवन्तम् You, अ-यन्त्रितम् spontaneously and without hesitation

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Lord, long before this incident, the hearts of those wives of Kaliya had already been absorbed in You upon hearing accounts of Your boundless divine power. They now praised You spontaneously and without hesitation, uttering glorious hymns, which are beyond the powers of even the great sages.

फणिवधूगणभक्तिविलोकनप्रविकसत्करुणाकुलचेतसा ।
फणिपतिर्भवताऽच्युत जीवितस्त्वयि समर्पितमूर्तिरवानमत् ॥56.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: फणि-वधू-गण-भक्ति-विलोकन-प्रविकसत्-करुणा-आकुल-चेतसा with a heart overflowing with blossoming compassion upon witnessing the intense devotion of the serpent wives, फणि-पतिः the serpent king Kaliya, भवता by You, अच्युत O Imperishable Lord, जीवितः was granted life, त्वयि unto You, समर्पित-मूर्तिः surrendering his whole body and soul, अवानमत् bowed down low

Simple Meaning: O Imperishable Lord Achyuta, witnessing the deep devotion of the serpent wives, Your heart overflowed with rich compassion and You granted Kaliya his life. Deeply grateful, the serpent king surrendered his entire body and soul unto You and bowed down low in complete reverence.

रमणकं व्रज वारिधिमध्यगं फणिरिपुर्न करोति विरोधिताम् ।
इति भवद्वचनान्यतिमानयन् फणिपतिर्निरगादुरगैः समम् ॥56.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: रमणकम् to Ramanaka, व्रज go, वारिधि-मध्य-गम् situated in the middle of the ocean, फणि-रिपुः Garuda the enemy of serpents, न करोति will not cause, विरोधिताम् enmity, इति thus, भवत्-वचनानि Your words, अतिमानयन् deeply respecting, फणि-पतिः the serpent king Kaliya, निरगात् departed, उरगैः समम् along with his serpent kin

Simple Meaning: Go to Ramanaka island situated in the middle of the ocean, where Garuda, the enemy of serpents, will cause you no harm. Deeply respecting Your words, the serpent king Kaliya departed from the Yamuna along with his family and serpent kin.

फणिवधूजनदत्तमणिव्रजज्वलितहारदुकूलविभूषितः ।
तटगतैः प्रमदाश्रुविमिश्रितैः समगथाः स्वजनैर्दिवसावधौ ॥56.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: फणि-वधू-जन-दत्त-मणि-व्रज-ज्वलित-हार-दुकूल-विभूषितः adorned with shimmering necklaces of glittering gems and fine silk garments presented by the serpent wives, तट-गतैः waiting on the bank, प्रमद-अश्रु-विमिश्रितैः shed with tears of joy, समगथाः You reunited, स्वजनैः with Your loved ones, दिवस-अवधौ at the end of the day

Simple Meaning: Adorned with shimmering silk garments and dazzling necklaces embedded with precious gems presented by the wives of Kaliya, You returned to the bank at sunset and reunited with Your loved ones, who wept tears of pure joy upon seeing You safe.

निशि पुनस्तमसा व्रजमन्दिरं व्रजितुमक्षम एव जनोत्करे ।
स्वपति तत्र भवच्चरणाश्रये दवकृशानुररुन्ध समन्ततः ॥56.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: निशि in the night, पुनः moreover, तमसा because of darkness, व्रज-मन्दिरम् to the homes in Vraja, व्रजितुम् to return, अक्षमे unable, एव indeed, जन-उत्करे when the group of people was, स्वपति were sleeping, तत्र there, भवत्-चरण-आश्रये taking refuge at Your feet, दव-कृशानुः a forest fire, अरुन्ध surrounded, समन्ततः on all sides

Simple Meaning: Night having fallen, the dark rendered it impossible for the people of Vraja to return to their homes. As they lay sleeping there on the riverbank, taking sole refuge in Your feet, a fierce forest fire broke out and trapped them on all sides.

प्रबुधितानथ पालय पालयेत्युदयदार्तरवान् पशुपालकान् ।
अवितुमाशु पपाथ महानलं किमिह चित्रमयं खलु ते मुखम् ॥56.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रबुधितान् waking up, अथ then, पालय पालय इति crying save us! save us!, उदयत्-आर्त-रवान् raising distressed cries, पशु-पालकान् the cowherds, अवितुम् to protect, आशु quickly, पपाथ You drank, महा-अनलम् the huge fire, किम् what, इह here, चित्रम् wonder, अयम् this fire, खलु indeed, ते Your, मुखम् mouth

Simple Meaning: Waking up to find themselves trapped, the terrified cowherds raised distressed cries, calling out, Save us! Save us! To protect them, You instantly swallowed that enormous forest fire. What wonder is there in this, for fire itself is indeed Your mouth!

Note: Fire is the element which emerged from Vishnu’s mouth in the Cosmic Form.

शिखिनि वर्णत एव हि पीतता परिलसत्यधुना क्रिययाऽप्यसौ ।
इति नुतः पशुपैर्मुदितैर्विभो हर हरे दुरितैःसह मे गदान् ॥56.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: शिखिनि in fire, वर्णतः by color, एव alone, हि indeed, पीतता yellowness, परिलसति shines, अधुना now, क्रियया अपि by action also, असौ this (quality), इति thus, नुतः praised, पशुपैः by the cowherds, मुदितैः overjoyed, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, हर take away, हरे O Lord Hari, दुरितैः सह along with my sins, मे my, गदान् diseases

Simple Meaning: The overjoyed cowherds praised You, humorously remarking: Formerly, पीतता (yellow color) was seen in fire only by its quality in color, but now पीतता (the condition of being drunk) can be applied, making the action real as well! O All-Pervading Lord Hari, please destroy my diseases along with all my sins.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
