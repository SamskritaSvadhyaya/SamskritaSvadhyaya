दशकम् ९७

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 97

The story of Markandeya

त्रैगुण्याद्भिन्नरूपं भवति हि भुवने हीनमध्योत्तमं यत्
ज्ञानं श्रद्धा च कर्ता वसतिरपि सुखं कर्म चाहारभेदाः ।
त्वत्क्षेत्रत्वन्निषेवादि तु यदिह पुनस्त्वत्परं तत्तु सर्वं
प्राहुर्नैगुण्यनिष्ठं तदनुभजनतो मङ्क्षु सिद्धो भवेयम् ॥97.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्रैगुण्यात् due to the three gunas or modes of nature, भिन्न-रूपम् of different kinds or manifold, भवति is, हि indeed, भुवने in this world, हीन-मध्य-उत्तमम् inferior or middle or superior, यत् which, ज्ञानम् knowledge, श्रद्धा faith, च and, कर्ता agent or doer, वसतिः place of residence, अपि also, सुखम् happiness, कर्म action, च and, आहार-भेदाः varieties of food, त्वत्-क्षेत्र-त्वत्-निषेवा-आदि Your sacred place and Your service and so on, तु but, यत् which, इह here, पुनः moreover, त्वत्-परम् dedicated to You, तत् that, तु indeed, सर्वम् all, प्राहुः they say, नैर्गुण्य-निष्ठम् beyond the three gunas or transcendental, तत्-अनुभजनतः by worshiping or following that, मङ्क्षु quickly, सिद्धः a liberated or perfected soul, भवेयम् may I become

Simple Meaning: In this world, knowledge, faith, the doer, residence, happiness, action, and varieties of food are all classified as inferior, middle, or superior according to the three modes of nature. However, those things that are dedicated to You—such as Your sacred dwelling places, Your service, and devotion to You—are declared to be completely beyond the gunas and transcendental. By constantly engaging in and worshipping these divine aspects, may I quickly attain liberation and ultimate perfection!

त्वय्येव न्यस्तचित्तः सुखमयि विचरन् सर्वचेष्टास्त्वदर्थं
त्वद्भक्तैः सेव्यमानानपि चरितचरानाश्रयन् पुण्यदेशान् ।
दस्यौ विप्रे मृगादिष्वपि च सममतिर्मुच्यमानावमानः
स्पर्धासूयादिदोषः सततमखिलभूतेषु संपूजये त्वाम् ॥97.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वयि in You, एव alone, न्यस्त-चित्तः with mind completely fixed or surrendered, सुखम् happily, अयि O Lord, विचरन् wandering or moving about, सर्व-चेष्टाः all activities, त्वत्-अर्थम् for Your sake or dedicated to You, त्वत्-भक्तैः by Your devotees, सेव्यमानान् अपि visited or served also, चरित-चरान् previously frequented by holy men, आश्रयण् taking resort to, पुण्य-देशान् holy places or sacred lands, दस्यौ in a thief, विप्रे in a brahmana, मृग-आदिषु in animals like deer and others, अपि even, च and, सम-मतिः with an equal mind, मुच्यमान-अवमानः freed from feeling insulted, स्पर्धा-असूया-आदि-दोषः defects like rivalry and envy and others, सततम् constantly, अखिल-भूतेषु in all living beings, संपूजये I shall worship, त्वाम् You

Simple Meaning: O Lord, with my mind completely surrendered to You alone, living happily and dedicating all my actions for Your sake, I shall visit and reside in holy places frequented and served by Your noble devotees. Possessing an equal mind towards a thief, a brahmana, or animals like a deer, and completely freed from feelings of humiliation as well as flaws like rivalry and jealousy, I shall constantly worship You present in all living beings.

त्वद्भावो यावदेषु स्फुरति न विशदं तावदेवं ह्युपास्तिं
कुर्वन्नैकात्म्यबोधे झटिति विकसति त्वन्मयोऽहं चरेयम् ।
त्वद्धर्मस्यास्य तावत् किमपि न भगवन् प्रस्तुतस्य प्रणाशः
तस्मात्सर्वात्मनैव प्रदिश मम विभो भक्तिमार्गं मनोज्ञम् ॥97.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-भावः the feeling of Your presence or divine awareness of You, यावत् as long as, एषु in these living beings, स्फुरति does not manifest, न not, विशदम् clearly or fully, तावत् until then, एवम् in this manner, हि indeed, उपास्तिम् worship or devotion, कुर्वन् performing, ऐकात्म्य-बोधे when the knowledge of oneness or unity, झटिति quickly, विकसति blossoms or dawns, त्वत्-मयः absorbed in You or filled with You, अहम् I, चरेयम् may I move about, त्वत्-धर्मस्य अस्य of this path of devotion to You, तावत् until then, किमपि not at all, न not, भगवन् O Blessed Lord, प्रस्तुतस्य which has been commenced, प्रणाशः destruction or loss, तस्मात् therefore, सर्व-आत्मना एव with my whole heart and soul, प्रदिश bestow or grant, मम to me, विभो O All-pervading Lord, भक्ति-मार्गम् the path of devotion, मनोज्ञम् captivating

Simple Meaning: O Lord, as long as the clear perception of Your presence in all living beings does not fully manifest in me, may I continue to perform worship in this way. When the realization of divine oneness dawns quickly within me, may I move about completely absorbed in You. O Blessed Lord, not even the slightest effort on this path of devotion is ever wasted or lost once begun. Therefore, O All-pervading Lord, please grant me with all my heart and soul this captivating path of devotion!

तं चैनं भक्तियोगं द्रढयितुमयि मे साध्यमारोग्यमायुः
दिष्ट्या तत्रापि सेव्यं तव चरणमहो भेषजायेव दुग्धम् ।
मार्कण्डेयो हि पूर्वं गणकनिगदितद्वादशाब्दायुरुच्चैः
सेवित्वा वत्सरं त्वां तव भटनिवहैर्द्रावयामास मृत्युम् ॥97.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् च एनम् and this, भक्ति-योगम् path of devotion, द्रढयितुम् to strengthen, अयि O Lord, मे my, साध्यम् goal to be achieved, आरोग्यम् good health, आयुः long life, दिष्ट्या fortunately, तत्र-अपि in that effort also, सेव्यम् to be served or worshipped, तव Your, चरणम् feet, अहो ah or wonderful, भेषजाय इव like medicine, दुग्धम् milk, मार्कण्डेयः Sage Markandeya, हि indeed, पूर्वम् in the past, गणक-निगदित-द्वादश-अब्द-आयुः who was predicted by astrologers to have a life of only twelve years, उच्चैः intensely, सेवित्वा having worshipped, वत्सरम् for a year, त्वाम् You, तव Your, भट-निवहैः by the group of Your attendants, द्रावयामास drove away, मृत्युम् Death or Yama

Simple Meaning: O Lord, in order to strengthen this path of devotion, my immediate goals are good health and a long life. Fortunately, for achieving these as well, worshipping Your feet is the primary means—just as sweet milk is taken as medicine. Indeed, in ancient times, Sage Markandeya was predicted by astrologers to live for only twelve years. But by worshipping You intensely for just one year, he caused Your attendants to drive away Death itself.

मार्कण्डेयश्चिरायुः स खलु पुनरपि त्वत्परः पुष्पभद्रा-
तीरे निन्ये तपस्यन्नतुलसुखरतिः षट् तु मन्वन्तराणि ।
देवेन्द्रः सप्तमस्तं सुरयुवतिमरुन्मन्मथैर्मोहयिष्यन्
योगोष्मप्लुष्यमाणैर्न तु पुनरशकत्त्वज्जनं निर्जयेत् कः ॥97.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: मार्कण्डेयः Sage Markandeya, च and, चिरायुः possessed of long life or immortal, सः he, खलु indeed, पुनरपि again, त्वत्-परः completely devoted to You, पुष्पभद्रा-तीरे on the banks of the Pushpabhadra river, निन्ये spent, तपस्यन् performing penance, अतुल-सुख-रतिः enjoying incomparable bliss, षट् six, तु indeed, मन्वन्तराणि manvantaras, देवेन्द्रः Indra - the lord of devas, सप्तमः the seventh, तम् him, सुर-युवति-मरुत्-मन्मथैः by celestial maidens and spring breezes and the god of love, मोहयिष्यन् desiring to delude or entice, योग-ऊष्म-प्लुष्यमाणैः who were scorched by the power of his yoga, न तु पुनः अशकत् could not succeed at all, त्वत्-जनम् Your devotee, निर्जयेत् can conquer, कः who

Simple Meaning: Blessed with a long life, the sage Markandeya spent six manvantaras in deep penance on the banks of the Pushpabhadra river, completely devoted to You and enjoying matchless spiritual bliss. During the seventh manvantara, Indra tried to distract and delude him using celestial maidens, soft gentle breezes, and the god of love. However, they were all scorched by the intense power of his yogic penance and failed completely. Indeed, who can ever conquer Your true devotee?

प्रीत्या नारायणाख्यस्त्वमथ नरसखः प्राप्तवानस्य पार्श्वं
तुष्ट्या तोष्टूयमानः स तु विविधवरैर्लोभितो नानुमेने ।
द्रष्टुं मायां त्वदीयां किल पुनरवृणोद्भक्तितृप्तान्तरात्मा
मायादुःखानभिज्ञस्तदपि मृगयते नूनमाश्चर्यहेतोः ॥97.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रीत्या with love or affection, नारायण-आख्यः named Narayana, त्वम् You, अथ then, नर-सखः friend of Nara, प्राप्तवान्-असि arrived or appeared, अस्य at his, पार्श्वम् side or presence, तुष्ट्या with joy, तोष्टूयमानः praising or extolling intensely, सः he, तु indeed, विविध-वरैः with various boons, लोभितः tempted, न not, अनुमेने did accept or agree, द्रष्टुम् to see, मायाम् illusive power/Maya, त्वदीयाम् Your, किल indeed, पुनः again, अवृणोत् requested/chose, भक्ति-तृप्त-अन्तरात्मा whose inner soul was completely satisfied with devotion, माया-दुःख-अनभिज्ञः unacquainted with the sorrows caused by Maya, तत् अपि even then, मृगयते sought or desired, नूनम् surely, आश्चर्य-हेतोः out of curiosity or for wonder

Simple Meaning: Pleased with his devotion, You, appearing in the form of Sage Narayana, the friend of Nara, arrived in his presence. Filled with immense joy, Markandeya offered fervent praises to You. Though tempted by You with various divine boons, his heart was completely satisfied with pure devotion alone, and he declined them all. Instead, he requested to behold Your illusive power, Maya. Having never experienced the suffering caused by Maya, he wished to see it merely out of curiosity to witness its extraordinary wonder.

याते त्वय्याशु वाताकुलजलदगलत्तोयपूर्णातिघूर्णत्-
सप्तार्णोराशिमग्ने जगति स तु जले सम्भ्रमन् वर्षकोटीः ।
दीनः प्रैक्षिष्ट दूरे वटदलशयनं कञ्चिदाश्चर्यबालं
त्वामेव श्यामलाङ्गं वदनसरसिजन्यस्तपादाङ्गुलीकम् ॥97.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: याते when departed, त्वयि You, आशु quickly, वात-आकुल-जलद-गलत्-तोय-पूर्ण-अति-घूर्णत् filled with water falling from clouds driven by wind and wildly churning, सप्त-अर्णोराशि-मग्ने submerged in the seven oceans, जगति the world, सः he, तु indeed, जले in the water, सम्भ्रमन् wandering about, वर्ष-कोटीः for crores of years, दीनः distressed, प्रैक्षिष्ट saw, दूरे in the distance, वट-दल-शयनम् lying on a banyan leaf, कञ्चित् a certain, आश्चर्य-बालम् miraculous child, त्वाम् एव You alone, श्यामल-अङ्गम् dark-complexioned, वदन-सरसिज-न्यस्त-पाद-अङ्गुलीकम् placing the toe of Your foot into Your lotus mouth

Simple Meaning: As soon as You departed, the entire world became submerged in the seven oceans, which were wildly churning and filled with torrential water pouring from clouds scattered by fierce winds. Wandering distressed in those vast waters for millions of years, Markandeya saw in the distance an extraordinary child resting on a banyan leaf. That child was none other than You, with Your dark-complexioned body, placing Your toe into Your lotus-like mouth.

दृष्ट्वा त्वां हृष्टरोमा त्वरितमुपगतः स्प्रष्टुकामो मुनीन्द्रः
श्वासेनान्तर्निविष्टः पुनरिह सकलं दृष्टवान् विष्टपौघम् ।
भूयोऽपि श्वासवातैर्बहिरनुपतितो वीक्षितस्त्वत्कटाक्षैः
मोदादाश्लेष्टुकामस्त्वयि पिहिततनौ स्वाश्रमे प्राग्वदासीत् ॥97.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: दृष्ट्वा having seen, त्वाम् You, हृष्ट-रोमा with hair standing on end due to ecstasy, त्वरितम् quickly, उपगतः approaching, स्प्रष्टु-कामः desiring to touch, मुनि-इन्द्रः the chief among sages Sage Markandeya, श्वासेन by Your inhalation, अन्तः-निविष्टः drawn inside, पुनः again, इह here, सकलम् entire, दृष्टवान् saw, विष्टप-ओघम् multitude of worlds, भूयः-अपि once again, श्वास-वातैः by Your exhalation breath, बहिः-अनुपतितः thrown outside, वीक्षितः looked upon, त्वत्-कटाक्षैः by Your compassionate glance, मोदात् with joy, आश्लेष्टु-कामः desiring to embrace, त्वयि when You, पिहित-तनौ concealed Your form, स्व-आश्रमे in his own hermitage, प्राक्-वत् as before, आसीत् remained

Simple Meaning: Seeing You, the great sage Markandeya felt supreme ecstasy with the hair on his body standing on end. He rushed forward desiring to touch You, but was suddenly drawn inside Your body through Your inhalation breath. Inside You, he beheld the entire expanse of all the worlds. Once again, he was blown out through Your exhalation breath and was blessed by Your compassionate glance. Overflowing with joy, he moved to embrace You, but at that moment You concealed Your divine form, and he found himself back in his own hermitage just as before.

गौर्या सार्धं तदग्रे पुरभिदथ गतस्त्वत्प्रियप्रेक्षणार्थी
सिद्धानेवास्य दत्वा स्वयमयमजरामृत्युतादीन् गतोऽभूत् ।
एवं त्वत्सेवयैव स्मररिपुरपि स प्रीयते येन तस्मात्
मूर्तित्रय्यात्मकस्त्वं ननु सकलनियन्तेति सुव्यक्तमासीत् ॥97.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: गौर्या सार्धम् along with Goddess Gauri, तत्-अग्रे in front of him, पुरभिद् Shiva the destroyer of the three cities, अथ then, गतः having gone or arrived, त्वत्-प्रिय-प्रेक्षणार्थी desiring to see Your beloved devotee, सिद्धान् एव already accomplished or attained, अस्य to him, दत्वा having granted, स्वयम् himself, अयम् this Lord Shiva, अजरा-मृत्युता-आदीन् freedom from old age and death and others, गतः अभूत् departed, एवम् thus, त्वत्-सेवया एव purely through Your service or devotion to You, स्मररिपुः अपि Shiva - the enemy of Kama also, सः he, प्रीयते is pleased, येन by which, तस्मात् therefore, मूर्ति-त्रयी-आत्मकः the embodiment of the Trinity, त्वम् You, ननु indeed, सकल-नियन्ता the controller of all, इति thus, सुव्यक्तम् आसीत् became clearly evident

Simple Meaning: Then Lord Shiva, along with Goddess Gauri, arrived before Markandeya out of a desire to see Your beloved devotee. Lord Shiva granted him boon upon boon—such as freedom from old age and death—which Markandeya had already effectively achieved through his devotion to You, and then Lord Shiva departed. Since even Lord Shiva is pleased purely through one's devotion to You, it became crystal clear that You are the true soul of the Divine Trinity and the supreme controller of all!

त्र्यंशेस्मिन् सत्यलोके विधिहरिपुरभिन्मन्दिराण्यूर्ध्वमूर्ध्वं
तेभोऽप्यूर्ध्वं तु मायाविकृतिविरहितो भाति वैकुण्ठलोकः ।
तत्र त्वं कारणाम्भस्यपि पशुपकुले शुद्धसत्त्वैकरूपी
सच्चित्ब्रह्माद्वयात्मा पवनपुरपते पाहि मां सर्वरोगात् ॥97.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्र्यंशे अस्मिन् in this threefold realm, सत्यलोके in Satyaloka, विधि-हरि-पुरभिद्-मन्दिराणि the abodes of Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva, ऊर्ध्वम् ऊर्ध्वम् positioned progressively higher, तेभ्यः अपि ऊर्ध्वम् far higher than all those, तु indeed, माया-विकृति-विरहितः unaffected by any changes of Maya, भाति radiates, वैकुण्ठ-लोकः the divine realm of Vaikuntha, तत्र there, त्वम् You, कारण-अम्भसि in the Causal Ocean, अपि as well as, पशुप-कुले amidst the cowherd clan, शुद्ध-सत्त्व-एक-रूपी the personification of pure sattva, सत्-चित्-ब्रह्म-अद्वय-आत्मा the non-dual Brahman of eternal truth and consciousness, पवनपुरपते O Lord of Guruvayur, पाहि save, माम् me, सर्व-रोगात् from every affliction

Simple Meaning: Within this Satyaloka region comprising three higher spheres, the residences of Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva stand positioned one above the other. Elevated beyond all these lies the luminous world of Vaikuntha, completely untainted by the illusory changes of Maya. There, as well as in the primordial Causal Waters and within the cowherd settlement of Gokula, You reign as the embodiment of unalloyed sattva—the non-dual Absolute Consciousness and Existence. O Lord of Guruvayur, pray protect me from all ailments and diseases!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
