The Story of the Prachetas

दशकम् १९

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 19

The Story of the Prachetas

(छन्दस् - रमणा)

पृथोस्तु नप्ता पृथुधर्मकर्मठः
प्राचीनबर्हिर्युवतौ शतद्रुतौ ।
प्रचेतसो नाम सुचेतसः सुतान्
अजीजनत्त्वत्करुणाङ्कुरानिव ॥19.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: पृथोः of King Prithu, तु indeed, नप्ता great-grandson, पृथु-धर्म-कर्मठः devoted to great righteous deeds, प्राचीनबर्हिः King Prachinabarhis, युवतौ through his young wife, शतद्रुतौ through Shatadruti, प्रचेतसः नाम named the Prachetas, सुचेतसः virtuous-minded, सुतान् sons, अजीजनत् begot, त्वत्-करुणा-अङ्कुरान् इव like the sprouts of Your mercy

Simple Meaning: King Prachinabarhis, the great-grandson of King Prithu, was deeply devoted to performing righteous deeds. Through his young wife Shatadruti, he begot ten virtuous sons known by the group name of  the Prachetas, who were like the very sprouts of Your mercy.

पितुः सिसृक्षानिरतस्य शासनात्
भवत्तपस्याभिरता दशापि ते।
पयोनिधिं पश्चिममेत्य तत्तटे
सरोवरं सन्ददृशुर्मनोहरम् ॥19.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: पितुः of their father, सिसृक्षा-निरतस्य who was intent on creation, शासनात् by the command, भवत्-तपस्या-अभिरताः intent on performing penance unto You, दश अपि all ten of them, ते they, पयोनिधिम् to the ocean, पश्चिमम् western, एत्य having reached, तत्-तटे on its shore, सरोवरम् a lake, सन्ददृशुः saw, मनोहरम् charming

Simple Meaning: Obeying the command of their father, who was keen on furthering creation, all ten brothers set out intent on performing penance unto You. Reaching the western ocean, they saw a charming lake on its shore.

तदा भवत्तीर्थमिदं समागतो
भवो भवत्सेवकदर्शनादृतः ।
प्रकाशमासाद्य पुरः प्रचेतसाम्
उपादिशत् भक्ततमस्तव स्तवम् ॥19.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदा at that time, भवत्-तीर्थम् इदम् this holy lake of Yours, समागतः having arrived, भवः Lord Shiva, भवत्-सेवक-दर्शन-आदृतः delighted to see Your devotees, प्रकाशम् आसाद्य appearing, पुरः in front, प्रचेतसाम् of the Prachetas, उपादिशत् taught, भक्त-तमः the greatest of Your devotees, तव Your, स्तवम् hymn of praise

Simple Meaning: At that time, Lord Shiva, who is the greatest of Your devotees, arrived at this holy lake of Yours. Delighted to meet Your devotees, he manifested in front of the Prachetas and taught them a glorious hymn of praise in Your honor.

स्तवं जपन्तस्तममी जलान्तरे
भवन्तमासेविषतायुतं समाः ।
भवत्सुखास्वादरसादमीष्वियान्
बभूव कालो ध्रुववन्न शीघ्रता ॥ 19.4 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्तवम् hymn of praise, जपन्तः chanting, तम् that, अमी these Prachetas, जल-अन्तरे under the water, भवन्तम् You, आसेविषत worshiped, अयुतम् समाः ten thousand years, भवत्-सुख-आस्वाद-रसात् to experience the bliss of attaining You, अमीषु for them, इयान् so much, बभूव was, कालः time, ध्रुववत् like Dhruva, न शीघ्रता not quickly

Simple Meaning: Chanting that hymn of praise under the water, the Prachetas worshiped You for ten thousand years. For the attainment of the bliss of Your realisation, they had to spend this long period in austerities unlike Dhruva who got it very quickly.

तपोभिरेषामतिमात्रवर्धिभिः
स यज्ञहिंसानिरतोऽपि पावितः ।
पिताऽपि तेषां गृहयातनारद-
प्र​दर्शितात्मा भवदात्मतां ययौ ॥19.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: तपोभिः by the penances, एषाम् of these Prachetas, अतिमात्र-वर्धिभिः growing immensely, सः he, यज्ञ-हिंसा-निरतः अपि though engaged in the slaughter of animals in sacrifices, पावितः was purified, पिता अपि their father also, तेषाम् of them, गृह-यात-नारद-प्र​दर्शित-आत्मा shown the true Self by Narada who visited his house, भवत्-आत्मताम् absorption in You, ययौ attained

Simple Meaning: Purified by the immensely growing penances of his ten sons, their father King Prachinabarhis, though previously engaged in sacrificing animals during rituals, was enlightened. Being shown the truth of the Supreme Self by Sage Narada who visited his house, he attained complete absorption in You.

कृपाबलेनैव पुरः प्रचेतसां
प्रकाशमागाः पतगेन्द्रवाहनः ।
विराजि चक्रादिवरायुधांशुभिः
भुजाभिरष्टाभिरुदञ्चितद्युतिः ॥19.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृपा-बलेन एव purely by the strength of Your mercy, पुरः in front, प्रचेतसाम् of the Prachetas, प्रकाशम् आगाः You appeared, पतग-इन्द्र-वाहनः riding on the king of birds Garuda, विराजि shining, चक्र-आदि-वर-आयुध-अंशुभिः with the brilliance of Your excellent weapons like the discus, भुजाभिः अष्टाभिः with eight arms, उदञ्चित-द्युतिः possessing elevated splendor

Simple Meaning: Purely out of Your boundless mercy, You manifested directly before the Prachetas, riding on Garuda, the king of birds. With Your eight arms radiant with the brilliance of Your divine weapons like the discus, You shone with supreme divine splendor.

प्रचेतसां तावदयाचतामपि
त्वमेव कारुण्यभराद्वरानदाः ।
भवद्विचिन्ताऽपि शिवाय देहिनां
भवत्वसौ रुद्रनुतिश्च कामदा ॥19.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रचेतसाम् to the Prachetas, तावत् even though, अयाचताम् अपि not asking for anything, त्वम् एव You Yourself, कारुण्य-भरात् out of abundance of mercy, वरान् अदाः granted boons, भवत्-विचिन्ता अपि even thinking of You, शिवाय for the welfare, देहिनाम् of embodied beings, भवतु असौ may it be, रुद्र-नुतिः च and this hymn (in praise of You) by Rudra (Lord Shiva), काम-दा granter of all desires

Simple Meaning: Even though the Prachetas did not ask for any boons, You Yourself granted them glorious blessings out of Your abundant mercy. May even thinking of You bring total well-being to all living beings, and may this hymn in Your praise taught by Lord Shiva grant all noble desires.

अवाप्य कान्तां तनयां महीरुहां
तया रमध्वं दशलक्षवत्सरीम् ।
सुतोऽस्तु दक्षो ननु तत्क्षणाच्च मां
प्रयास्यथेति न्यगदो मुदैव तान् ॥19.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: अवाप्य having obtained, कान्ताम् a beautiful wife, तनयाम् the daughter, महीरुहाम् of the trees, तया with her, रमध्वम् enjoy, दशलक्ष-वत्सरीम् for ten lakh years, सुतः a son, अस्तु let there be, दक्षः Daksha, ननु indeed, तत्-क्षणात् च and immediately after that, माम् to Me, प्रयास्यथ You shall attain, इति thus, न्यगदः You said, मुदा एव with great joy, तान् to them

Simple Meaning: “Having obtained the beautiful Marisha, the daughter of the trees, as your wife, enjoy life with her for ten lakh years. A son named Daksha will be born to you, and immediately after that, you shall attain Me.” Being pleased with them, You told them thus.

ततश्च ते भूतलरोधिनस्तरून्
क्रुधा दहन्तो द्रुहिणेन वारिताः ।
द्रुमैश्च दत्तां तनयामवाप्य तां
त्वदुक्तकालं सुखिनोऽभिरेमिरे ॥19.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः च​ and then, ते they (the Prachetas), भू-तल-रोधिनः blocking the surface of the earth, तरून्  the trees, क्रुधा in anger, दहन्तः burning, द्रुहिणेन by Lord Brahma, वारिताः were restrained, द्रुमैः च and by the trees, दत्ताम् given/offered, तनयाम् daughter (Marisha), अवाप्य having obtained, ताम् her, त्वत्-उक्त-कालम् for the duration specified by You, सुखिनः happily, अभिरेमिरे they enjoyed/lived happily

Simple Meaning: And then, coming out of the waters, the Prachetas saw the earth completely covered and choked by dense trees. As they began to burn the trees in anger, Lord Brahma intervened and stopped them. The trees then offered their daughter, Marisha, to the brothers. Accepting her as their wife, they lived happily together for the entire duration of time foretold by You.

अवाप्य दक्षं च सुतं कृताध्वराः
प्रचेतसो नारदलब्धया धिया ।
अवापुरानन्दपदं तथाविधः
त्वमीश वातालयनाथ पाहि माम् ॥19.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: अवाप्य having obtained, दक्षम् Daksha, च and, सुतम् son, कृत-अध्वराः having performed sacrifices, प्रचेतसः the Prachetas, नारद-लब्धया obtained through Narada, धिया with spiritual wisdom, अवापुः attained, आनन्द-पदम् the abode of bliss, तथा-विधः of such nature, त्वम् You, ईश O Lord, वात-आलय-नाथ O Lord of the abode of the Wind - Guruvayoor, पाहि protect, माम् me

Simple Meaning: Having obtained Daksha as their son, and having performed sacred sacrifices, the Prachetas, blessed with spiritual wisdom bestowed by Sage Narada, they attained the supreme abode of ultimate bliss. O Lord of Guruvayoor, please protect me!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
