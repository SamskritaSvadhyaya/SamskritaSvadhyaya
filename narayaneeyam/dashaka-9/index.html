Dashaka 9

Brahma in his creative role

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 9

Brahma in his creative role

(छन्दस् - पृथ्वी)

स्थितस्स कमलोद्भवस्तव हि नाभिपङ्केरुहे
कुतः स्विदिदमम्बुधावुदितमित्यनालोकयन् ।
तदीक्षणकुतूहलात् प्रतिदिशं विवृत्ताननः
चतुर्वदनतामगाद्विकसदष्टदृष्ट्यम्बुजाम् ॥9.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्थितः seated, सः he, कमल-उद्भवः the lotus-born Brahma, तव Your, हि indeed, नाभि-पङ्केरुहे in the lotus from Your navel, कुतः from where, स्वित् possibly, इदम् this lotus, अम्बुधौ in the ocean, उदितम् arose, इति thus, अनालोकयन् not seeing, तत्-ईक्षण-कुतूहलात् out of eagerness to see that source, प्रति-दिशम् in every direction, विवृत्त-आननः turning his face, चतुर्वदनताम् state of having four faces, अगात् attained, विकसत्-अष्ट-दृष्टि-अम्बुजाम् with eight eyes like blooming lotuses

Simple Meaning: Seated on the lotus originating from Your navel, Lord Brahma could not discern where that lotus had arisen from in the middle of the vast ocean. Out of eagerness to discover its source, he turned his face in all directions, thereby acquiring four faces with eight wide-open, lotus-like eyes.

महार्णवविघूर्णितं कमलमेव तत्केवलं
विलोक्य तदुपाश्रयं तव तनुं तु नालोकयन् ।
क एष कमलोदरे महति निस्सहायो ह्यहं
कुतः स्विदिदम्बुजं समजनीति चिन्तामगात् ॥9.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: महा-अर्णव-विघूर्णितम् tossed about in the great ocean, कमलम् lotus, एव alone, तत् that, केवलम् only, विलोक्य seeing, तत्-उपाश्रयम् the support of that lotus, तव Your, तनुम् form, तु but, न आलोकयन् not seeing, कः who, एषः this, कमल-उदरे in the belly of the lotus, महति great, निस्सहायः helpless, हि indeed, अहम् I, कुतः from where, स्वित् possibly, इदम् अम्बुजम् this lotus, समजनि arose, इति thus, चिन्ताम् worry, अगात् he reached

Simple Meaning: Seeing only that lotus being tossed about in the vast cosmic ocean, and unable to see Your divine form which was its support, Lord Brahma became anxious and thought, "Who am I, helpless in the belly of this great lotus? From where could this lotus have possibly originated?"

अमुष्य हि सरोरुहः किमपि कारणं सम्भ्वेत्
 इति स्म कृतनिश्चयस्स खलु नालरन्ध्राध्वना ।
स्वयोगबलविद्यया समवरूढवान् प्रौढधीः
त्वदीयमतिमोहनं न तु कलेवरं दृष्टवान् ॥9.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: अमुष्य of this, हि indeed, सरोरुहः lotus, किमपि some, कारणम् source, सम्भवेत् must exist, इति thus, स्म कृत-निश्चयः having resolved, सः he, खलु indeed, नाल-रन्ध्र-अध्वना through the passage of the hollow lotus stalk, स्व-योग-बल-विद्यया by his own power of yoga and knowledge, समवरूढवान् descended, प्रौढ-धीः the highly intelligent Brahma, त्वदीयम् Your, अति-मोहनम् extremely charming, न not, तु but, कलेवरम् divine body, दृष्टवान् saw

Simple Meaning: Resolving that there must certainly be a source or origin for this lotus, the highly intelligent Brahma descended through the hollow passage of the lotus stalk using his powers of yoga and knowledge. However, he was unable to see Your extremely charming divine body.

ततः सकलनालिकाविवरमार्गगो मार्गयन्
प्रयस्य शतवत्सरं किमपि नैव संदृष्टवान् ।
निवृत्य कमलोदरे सुखनिषण्ण एकाग्रधीः
समाधिबलमादधे भवदनुग्रहैकाग्रही ॥9.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, सकल-नालिका-विवर-मार्ग-गः moving through the entire passage of the hollow stem, मार्गयन् searching, प्रयस्य striving, शत-वत्सरम् for a hundred years, किमपि anything, न एव not at all, संदृष्टवान् saw, निवृत्य returning, कमल-उदरे inside the lotus, सुख-निषण्णः comfortably seated, एकाग्र-धीः with a focused mind, समाधि-बलम् power of intense meditation, आदधे applied, भवत्-अनुग्रह-एक-आग्रही solely eager for Your grace

Simple Meaning: Then, traveling through the entire hollow passage of the lotus stem, he searched for a hundred years with great effort, but found nothing at all. Returning inside the lotus and seating himself comfortably, he focused his mind with single-pointed dedication, relying on the power of meditation with a sole desire to receive Your grace.

शतेन परिवत्सरैर्दृढसमाधिबन्धोल्लसत्-
प्रबोधविशदीकृतः स खलु पद्मिनीसम्भवः ।
अदृष्टचरमद्भुतं तव हि रूपमन्तर्दृशा
व्यचष्ट परितुष्टधीर्भुजगभोगभागाश्रयम् ॥9.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: शतेन for a hundred, परिवत्सरैः years, दृढ-समाधि-बन्ध-उल्लसत्-प्रबोध-विशदीकृतः enlightened by the wisdom arising from firm adherence to deep meditation, सः he, खलु indeed, पद्मिनी-सम्भवः the lotus-born Brahma, अदृष्ट-चरम् never seen before, अद्भुतम् wonderful, तव Your, हि indeed, रूपम् form, अन्तः-दृशा with his inner vision, व्यचष्ट beheld, परितुष्ट-धीः with a gladdened heart, भुजग-भोग-भाग-आश्रयम् resting on a part of the body of the serpent Adishesha

Simple Meaning: After a hundred years of firm and unbroken deep meditation, the lotus-born Lord Brahma became enlightened with true wisdom. With a gladdened heart, he beheld through his inner vision Your wonderful, never-before-seen divine form resting on a portion of the body of the serpent Adishesha.

किरीटमुकुटोल्लसत्कटकहारकेयूरयुङ्-
मणिस्फुरितमेखलं सुपरिवीतपीताम्बरम् ।
कलायकुसुमप्रभं गलतलोल्लसत्कौस्तुभं
वपुस्तदयि भावये कमलजन्मे दर्शितम् ॥9.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: किरीट-मुकुट-उल्लसत्-कटक-हार-केयूर-युक्-मणि-स्फुरित-मेखलम् adorned with a tall crown, glowing bracelets, necklaces, and armlets, a waist-band glittering with gems, सु-परिवीत-पीत-अम्बरम् beautifully wrapped in yellow silk garments, कलाय-कुसुम-प्रभम् (with complexion) brilliant like the Kalaya flower, गल-तल-उल्लसत्-कौस्तुभम् with the Kaustubha gem shining on Your neck, वपुः divine form, तत् that, अयि O Lord, भावये I meditate upon, कमल-जन्मने to the lotus-born Brahma, दर्शितम् shown

Simple Meaning: O Lord, I meditate upon that divine form shown by You to the lotus-born Brahma—with a glowing complexion of the deep blue hue of the Kalaya flower, adorned with a tall crown, radiant bracelets, necklaces, armlets, a girdle sparkling with precious gems, beautifully adorned in yellow silk, and with the brilliant Kaustubha gem glittering upon Your neck.

श्रुतिप्रकरदर्शितप्रचुरवैभव श्रीपते
हरे जय जय प्रभो पदमुपैषि दिष्ट्या दृशोः ।
कुरुष्व धियमाशु मे भुवननिर्मितौ कर्मठाम्
इति द्रुहिणवर्णितस्वगुणबंहिमा पाहि माम् ॥9.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रुति-प्रकर-दर्शित-प्रचुर-वैभव O Lord whose abundant glory is revealed by the multitude of Vedas, श्री-पते O Lord of Lakshmi, हरे O Hari, जय जय victory, victory to You, प्रभो O Sovereign Lord, पदम् उपैषि You have come into, दिष्ट्या by good fortune, दृशोः in range of my vision, कुरुष्व make, धियम् intellect, आशु quickly, मे my, भुवन-निर्मितौ in the creation of the worlds, कर्मठाम् capable and adept, इति thus, द्रुहिण-वर्णित-स्व-गुण-बंहिमा You whose vast array of divine virtues was praised by Brahma, पाहि protect, माम् me

Simple Meaning: “O Lord of Lakshmi, O Lord Hari, whose vast divine glory is revealed by all the Vedas! Victory, victory to You, O Sovereign Lord! By great fortune, You have appeared before my eyes. Please make my mind quickly capable and adept in creating the worlds.” O Lord Vishnu, who was thus praised by Brahma extolling Your immense virtues, please protect me!

लभस्व भुवनत्रयीरचनदक्षतामक्षतां
गृहाण मदनुग्रहं कुरु तपश्च भूयो विधे ।
भवत्वखिलसाधनी मयि च भक्तिरत्युत्कटा
इत्युदीर्य गिरमादधा मुदितचेतसं वेधसम् ॥9.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: लभस्व obtain, भुवन-त्रयी-रचन-दक्षताम् skill in creating the three worlds, अक्षताम् unimpaired/unbroken, गृहाण accept, मत्-अनुग्रहम् My grace, कुरु perform, तपः penance, च and, भूयः again, विधे O Brahma, भवतु let there be, अखिल-साधनी the accomplisher of all goals, मयि in Me, च and, भक्तिः devotion, अति-उत्कटा intensely strong, इति thus, उदीर्य uttering, गिरम् word, आदधाः You made, मुदित-चेतसम् gladdened at heart, वेधसम् Brahma

Simple Meaning: “O Brahma, obtain unimpaired skill in creating the three worlds, accept My grace, and perform penance again. May you have strong devotion unto Me, which accomplishes all goals.” Uttering these words, You made Brahma very glad at heart.

शतं कृततपास्ततः स खलु दिव्यसंवत्सरान्
अवाप्य च तपोबलं मतिबलं च पूर्वाधिकम् ।
उदीक्ष्य किल कम्पितं पयसि पङ्कजं वायुना
भवद्बलविजृम्भितः पवनपाथसी पीतवान् ॥9.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: शतम् a hundred, कृत-तपाः having performed penance, ततः then, सः he, खलु indeed, दिव्य-संवत्सरान् divine years, अवाप्य having attained, च and, तपो-बलम् the power of penance, मति-बलम् the power of intellect, च and, पूर्व-अधिकम् far greater than before, उदीक्ष्य observing, किल indeed, कम्पितम् shaken, पयसि in the water, पङ्कजम् the lotus, वायुना by the wind, भवत्-बल-विजृम्भितः empowered by Your divine strength, पवन-पाथसी both the wind and the water, पीतवान् drank/absorbed

Simple Meaning: Then, having performed penance for a hundred divine years, Lord Brahma gained power of austerity and intellectual capacity far greater than before. Observing that the lotus was being violently shaken in the cosmic waters by fierce winds, he—fully empowered by Your divine strength—absorbed both the wind and the waters.

तवैव कृपया पुनस्सरसिजेन तेनैव सः
प्रकल्प्य भुवनत्रयीं प्रववृते प्रजानिर्मितौ ।
तथाविधकृपाभरो गुरुमरुत्पुराधीश्वर
त्वमाशु परिपाहि मां गुरुदयोक्षितैरीक्षितैः ॥9.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: तव Your, एव indeed, कृपया by grace, पुनः again, सरसिजेन with the lotus, तेन एव with that very same, सः he (Brahma), प्रकल्प्य having fashioned, भुवन-त्रयीम् the three worlds, प्रववृते proceeded, प्रजा-निर्मितौ in the creation of living beings, तथा-विध-कृपा-भरः filled with such immense compassion, गुरु-मरुत्-पुर-अधीश्वर O Lord of the city of the Guru Brihaspati and the Wind - Guruvayoor, त्वम् You, आशु quickly, परिपाहि protect, माम् me, गुरु-दया-उक्षितैः bathed in deep compassion, ईक्षितैः with Your divine glances

Simple Meaning: By Your grace alone, Lord Brahma fashioned the three worlds using that very same lotus and set about creating all living beings. O Lord of Guruvayoor, Who are filled with such abundant grace, please protect me quickly with Your divine glances, overflowing with deep compassion!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
