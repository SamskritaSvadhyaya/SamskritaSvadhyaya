Cosmos as the form of the Lord

दशकम् ६

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 6

Cosmos as the form of the Lord

(छन्दस् - वसन्ततिलका)

एवं चतुर्दशजगन्मयतां गतस्य
पातालमीश तव पादतलं वदन्ति ।
पादोर्ध्वदेशमपि देव रसातलं ते
गुल्फद्वयं खलु महातलमद्भुतात्मन् ॥6.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, चतुर्दश-जगत्-मयताम् the state of being composed of the fourteen worlds, गतस्य of You who attained, पातालम् the Patala world, ईश O Lord, तव Your, पाद-तलम् sole (lower part) of the feet, वदन्ति they say/speak of, पाद-ऊर्ध्व-देशम् the region of the upper part of the feet, अपि also, देव O Lord, रसातलम् the Rasatala world, ते Your, गुल्फ-द्वयम् pair of ankles, खलु indeed, महातलम् the Mahatala world, अद्भुत-आत्मन् O One of wondrous nature

Simple Meaning: OLord of wondrous nature! When You thus assumed the form containing the fourteen worlds within Yourself, the sages described Patala as the soles of Your feet. O Divine One, they speak of Rasatala as the upper part of Your feet, and Mahatala as Your pair of ankles.

जङ्घे तलातलमथो सुतलं च जानू
किञ्चोरुभागयुगलं वितलातले द्वे ।
क्षोणीतलं जघनमम्बरमङ्ग नाभिः
वक्षश्च शक्रनिलयस्तव चक्रपाणे ॥6.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: जङ्घे lower legs/shins, तलातलम् the Talatala world, अथः then, सुतलम् the Sutala world, च and, जानू two knees, किञ्च and further, ऊरु-भाग-युगलम् pair of thighs, वितल-अतले द्वे the two worlds - Vitala and Atala, क्षोणी-तलम् surface of the earth, जघनम् hips/loins, अम्बरम् sky/outer space, अङ्ग O Dear Lord, नाभिः navel, वक्षः chest, च and, शक्र-निलयः abode of Indra - Swarga, तव Your, चक्र-पाणे O Lord who holds the discus in Your hand

Simple Meaning: O Lord holding the discus! Talatala is Your shins, Sutala is Your knees, and the two worlds Vitala and Atala are Your pair of thighs. The surface of the earth - Bhumi is Your hips, outer space Bhuva- is Your navel, and Swarga, the abode of Indra, is Your chest.

ग्रीवा महस्तव मुखं च जनस्तपस्तु
फालं शिरस्तव समस्तमयस्य सत्यम् ।
एवं जगन्मयतनो जगदाश्रितैः
अप्यन्यैर्निबद्धवपुषे भगवन्नमस्ते ॥6.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: ग्रीवा neck, महः Maharloka, तव Your, मुखम् face, च and, जनः Janaloka, तपः Tapoloka, तु indeed, फालम् forehead, शिरः head, तव Your, समस्त-मयस्य composed of everything, सत्यम् Satyaloka, एवम् thus, जगत्-मय-तनो O You whose body consists of the worlds, जगत्-आश्रितैः residing within the worlds, अपि अन्यैः by other components too, निबद्ध-वपुषे to You whose cosmic body is constructed, भगवन् O Lord, नमः ते salutations unto You

Simple Meaning: O Lord whose body consists of all the worlds! Maharloka is Your neck, Janaloka is Your face, Tapoloka is Your forehead, and Satyaloka is the head of Your all-encompassing form. O Lord, salutations unto You whose cosmic body is formed by these and all other components contained within the universe!

त्वद्ब्रह्मरन्ध्रपदमीश्वर विश्वकन्द
छन्दांसि केशव घनास्तव केशपाशाः ।
उल्लासिचिल्लियुगलं द्रुहिणस्य गेहं
पक्ष्माणि रात्रिदिवसौ सविता च नेत्रै ॥6.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-ब्रह्म-रन्ध्र-पदम् the region of Your crown of the head (brahmarandhra), ईश्वर O Supreme Ruler, विश्व-कन्द O Root/Source of the universe, छन्दांसि the Vedic metres, केशव O Keshava, घनाः the clouds, तव Your, केश-पाशाः locks of hair, उल्लासि-चिल्लि-युगलम् gleaming pair of eyebrows, द्रुहिणस्य of Brahma, गेहम् abode, पक्ष्माणि eyelashes, रात्रि-दिवसौ night and day, सविता the Sun, च and, नेत्रे eyes

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Ruler, O Keshava, O You who are the root source of the universe! The Vedic metres are the region at the crown of Your head, and the dark rain clouds are Your locks of hair. Your gleaming pair of eyebrows is the abode of Brahma, Your eyelashes are night and day, and the Sun forms Your eyes.

निश्शेषविश्वरचना च कटाक्षमोक्षः
कर्णौ दिशोऽश्वियुगलं तव नासिके द्वे ।
लोभत्रपे च भगवन्नधरोत्तरोष्ठौ
तारागणाश्च दशनाः शमनश्च दंष्ट्रा ॥6.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: निश्शेष-विश्व-रचना creation of the entire universe without exception, च and, कटाक्ष-मोक्षः casting of Your side-glance, कर्णौ ears, दिशः directions/quarters of space, अश्वि-युगलम् the Ashwini Twins, तव Your, नासिके द्वे pair of nostrils, लोभ-त्रपे greed and modesty, च and, भगवन् O Lord, अधर-उत्तर-ओष्ठौ lower and upper lips, तारा-गणाः groups of stars, च and, दशनाः teeth, शमनः Yama (the god of death), च and, दंष्ट्रा canine teeth/tusks

Simple Meaning: O Lord! The side-glance You cast is the creation of the entire universe. The directions of space are Your ears, and the Ashwini Twins are Your pair of nostrils. Greed and modesty are Your lower and upper lips, the clusters of stars are Your teeth, and Yama, the god of death, is Your canine teeth.

माया विलासहसितं श्वसितं समीरो
जिह्वा जलं वचनमीश शकुन्तपङ्क्तिः ।
सिद्धादयः स्वरगणा मुखरन्ध्रमग्निः
देवा भुजाः स्तनयुगं तव धर्मदेवः ॥6.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: माया divine illusion (maya), विलास-हसितम् playful smile, श्वसितम् breath, समीरः the wind, जिह्वा tongue, जलम् water, वचनम् speech, ईश O Lord, शकुन्त-पङ्क्तिः flock of birds chirping, सिद्ध-आदयः the Siddhas and others, स्वर-गणाः Your vocal chords, मुख-रन्ध्रम् mouth cavity, अग्निः fire, देवाः the devas, भुजाः arms, स्तन-युगम् pair of breasts/chest, तव Your, धर्म-देवः Dharma (the god of righteousness)

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Your playful smile is the divine illusion - Maya, and Your breath is the wind. Your tongue is water, Your speech is the chirping of birds, and the Siddhas and other celestial beings are Your vocal chords. Your mouth cavity is fire, the devas are Your arms, and Dharma, the god of righteousness, is Your pair of breasts.

पृष्ठं त्वधर्म इह देव मनः सुधांशुः
अव्यक्तमेव हृदयंबुजमम्बुजाक्ष ।
कुक्षिः समुद्रनिवहा वसनं तु सन्ध्ये
शेफः प्रजापतिरसौ वृषणौ च मित्रः ॥6.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: पृष्ठम् back, तु indeed, अधर्मः Adharma (unrighteousness), इह here, देव O Lord, मनः mind, सुधांशुः the moon, अव्यक्तम् Unmanifest (Mula-prakriti), एव indeed, हृदय-अम्बुजम् lotus-like heart, अम्बुज-अक्ष O Lotus-eyed One, कुक्षिः abdomen, समुद्र-निवहाः collection of oceans, वसनम् garment, तु indeed, सन्ध्ये the two twilights (dawn and dusk), शेफः organ of generation/male organ, प्रजापतिः Prajapati (the lord of creation), असौ that, वृषणौ testicles, च and, मित्रः Mitra (a form of the sun god)

Simple Meaning: O Lotus-eyed Lord! Adharma is Your back, and the Moon is Your mind. The unmanifest Mula-prakriti is Your lotus-like heart, and the vast oceans form Your abdomen. The two twilights, dawn and dusk, are Your garments. Prajapati is Your organ of generation, and the god Mitra forms Your testicles.

श्रोणीस्थलं मृगगणाः पदयोर्नखास्ते
हस्त्युष्ट्रसैन्धवमुखा गमनं तु कालः ।
विप्रादिवर्णभवनं वदनाब्जबाहु-
चारूरुयुग्मचरणं करुणांबुधे ते ॥6.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रोणी-स्थलम् region of the hips, मृग-गणाः herds of wild animals/deer, पदयोः of the feet, नखाः nails, ते Your, हस्ति-उष्ट्र-सैन्धव-मुखाः animals such as elephants, camels, and horses, गमनम् movement/gait, तु indeed, कालः time, विप्र-आदि-वर्ण-भवनम् the origin of the castes beginning with Brahmanas, वदन-अब्ज-बाहु-चारु-उरु-युग्म-चरणम् the lotus face, arms, beautiful pair of thighs, and feet, करुणा-अम्बुधे O Ocean of Mercy, ते Your

Simple Meaning: O Ocean of Mercy! Herds of wild animals form the region of Your hips, and animals like elephants, camels, and horses are Your fingernails and toenails. Your movement or gait is Time itself. The four social classes - the Brahmanas, Kshatriyas, Vaishyas and Shudras - originated respectively from Your lotus face, arms, beautiful pair of thighs, and feet.

संसारचक्रमयि चक्रधर क्रियास्ते
वीर्यं महासुरगणोऽस्थिकुलानि शैलाः ।
नाड्यस्सरित्समुदयस्तरवश्च रोम
जीयादिदं वपुरनिर्वचनीयमीश ॥6.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: संसार-चक्रम् cycle of worldly existence, अयि चक्रधर O Wielder of the Discus, क्रियाः activities/actions, ते Your, वीर्यम् prowess/valor, महा-असुर-गणः host of great asuras, अस्थि-कुलानि collection of bones, शैलाः mountains, नाड्यः blood vessels/veins, सरित्-समुदयः network of rivers, तरवः trees, च and, रोम body hair, जीयात् may it triumph/be victorious, इदम् this, वपुः cosmic form, अनिर्वचनीयम् indefinable/indescribable, ईश O Lord

Simple Meaning: O Lord, Wielder of the Discus! The cycle of worldly existence is Your continuous action, and the host of great asuras represents Your mighty prowess. The mountains are Your bones, the rivers are Your network of blood vessels, and the trees are the hair on Your body. O Lord, may this indescribable cosmic form of Yours triumph!

ईदृग्जगन्मयवपुस्तव कर्मभाजां
कर्मावसानसमये स्मरणीयमाहुः ।
तस्यान्तरात्मवपुषे विमलात्मने ते
वातालयाधिप नमोऽस्तु निरुन्धि रोगान् ॥6.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: ईदृक् like this, जगत्-मय-वपुः cosmic body consisting of the worlds, तव Your, कर्म-भाजाम् for those bound by karma, कर्म-अवसान-समये at the time of the end of karma/death, स्मरणीयम् to be remembered, आहुः they say, तस्य of that, अन्तरात्म-वपुषे who is the inner soul of all forms, विमल-आत्मने to the Pure Being, ते Your, वात-आलय-अधिप O Lord of the abode of the Wind - Guruvayoor, नमः salutations, अस्तु may there be, निरुन्धि destroy/cure, रोगान् illnesses and afflictions

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Guruvayoor! This cosmic form of Yours, which encompasses the entire universe, should be remembered at the end of life by those bound by action. Salutations unto You, who exist as the inner soul of all beings and are forever pure. Please remove and destroy all my illnesses!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
