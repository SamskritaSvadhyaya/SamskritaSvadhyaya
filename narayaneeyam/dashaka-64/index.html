Dashaka 64

Krishna crowned as Govinda

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 64

Krishna crowned as Govinda; Krishna brings back Nanda from Varuna’s abode

आलोक्य शैलोद्धरणादिरूपं प्रभावमुच्चैस्तव गोपलोकाः ।
विश्वेश्वरं त्वामभिमत्य विश्वे नन्दं भवज्जातकमन्वपृच्छन् ॥64.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: आलोक्य having seen, शैल-उद्धरण-आदि-रूपम् such as the lifting of the mountain, प्रभावम् glory, उच्चैः supreme, तव Your, गोप-लोकाः the cowherd people, विश्वेश्वरम् the Lord of the universe, त्वाम् You, अभिमत्य considering, विश्वे all, नन्दम् to Nanda, भवत्-जातकम् Your horoscope or birth details, अन्वपृच्छन् inquired

Simple Meaning: Having witnessed Your supreme power and glory displayed through feats like the lifting of the mountain, all the cowherd people considered You to be the Lord of the universe Himself, and inquired of Nanda regarding Your horoscope.

गर्गोदितो निर्गदितो निजाय वर्गाय तातेन तव प्रभावः ।
पूर्वाधिकस्त्वय्यनुराग एषामैधिष्ट तावत् बहुमानभारः ॥64.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: गर्ग-उदितः spoken by Sage Garga, निर्गदितः was related, निजाय to his own, वर्गाय group of kinsmen, तातेन by Your father Nanda, तव Your, प्रभावः glory, पूर्व-अधिकः greater than before, त्वयि toward You, अनुरागः love and affection, एषाम् of these cowherds, ऐधिष्ट grew, तावत् thereupon, बहुमान-भारः a immense reverence

Simple Meaning: Your father Nanda related to his kinsmen all that had been foretold by Sage Garga regarding Your divine glory. Hearing this, their deep love and affection for You grew even greater than before, accompanied by an immense reverence.

ततोऽवमानोदिततत्त्वबोधः सुराधिराजः सह दिव्यगव्या।
उपेत्य तुष्टाव स नष्टगर्वः स्पृष्ट्वा पदाब्जं मणिमौलिना ते ॥64.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, अवमान-उदित-तत्त्व-बोधः realization of truth arising from his humiliation, सुर-अधिराजः the king of devas - Indra, सह with, दिव्य-गव्या with the celestial cow - Kamadhenu, उपेत्य approaching, तुष्टाव praised, सः he, नष्ट-गर्वः his pride shattered, स्पृष्ट्वा touching, पद-अभ्जम् lotus foot, मणि-मौलिना with his gem-studded crown, ते Your

Simple Meaning: Then, with the true realization of Your supreme reality dawning upon him through his intense humiliation, Indra, the king of devas, approached You along with Kamadhenu, the celestial cow. His pride shattered, he touched Your lotus feet with his gem-studded crown and began to offer his praises.

स्नेहस्नुतैस्त्वां सुरभिः पयोभिर्गोविन्दनामाङ्कितमभ्यषिञ्चत् ।
ऐरावतोपाहृतदिव्यगङ्गापाथोभिरिन्द्रोऽपि च जातहर्षः ॥64.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्नेह-स्नुतैः flowing with motherly affection, त्वाम् You, सुरभिः Surabhi (Kamadhenu), पयोभिः with streams of milk, गोविन्द-नाम-अङ्कितम् adorned with the title Govinda, अभ्यषिञ्चत् anointed, ऐरावत-उपाहृत-दिव्य-गङ्गा-पाथोभिः with the holy waters of the celestial Ganga brought by Airavata, इन्द्रः Indra, अपि also, च and, जात-हर्षः filled with supreme joy

Simple Meaning: Kamadhenu anointed You with streams of milk flowing out of motherly affection, bestowing upon You the name Govinda. Filled with supreme joy, Indra also performed Your ceremonial bath using the holy waters of the celestial Ganga brought by his elephant Airavata.

जगत्त्रयेशे त्वयि गोकुलेशे तथाऽभिषिक्ते सति गोपवाटः ।
नाकेऽपि वैकुण्ठपदेऽप्यलभ्यां श्रियं प्रपेदे भवतः प्रभावात् ॥64.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: जगत्-त्रय-ईशे the Lord of the three worlds, त्वयि while You, गोकुल-ईशे as the master of Gokula, तथा in that manner, अभिषिक्ते having been anointed, सति were, गोप-वाटः the settlement of the cowherds, नाके in Swarga/heaven, अपि even, वैकुण्ठ-पदे in the abode of Vaikuntha, अपि even, अलभ्याम् unattainable, श्रियम् splendour and prosperity, प्रपेदे attained, भवतः Your, प्रभावात् due to the glory

Simple Meaning: When You, the supreme Lord of the three worlds, were thus anointed as the sovereign ruler of Gokula, the entire settlement of cowherds attained such unprecedented glory, splendour, and prosperity that it could not be equaled even in heaven or in Vaikuntha itself.

कदाचिदन्तर्यमुनं प्रभाते स्नायन् पिता वारुणपूरुषेण ।
नीतस्तमानेतुमगाः पुरीं त्वं तां वारुणीं कारणमर्त्यरूपः ॥64.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: कदाचित् once, अन्तः-यमुनम् inside the waters of Yamuna, प्रभाते in the early morning, स्नायन् bathing, पिता Your father Nanda, वारुण-पूरुषेण by a servant of Varuna, नीतः was taken away, तम् him, आनेतुम् to bring back, अगाः You went, पुरीम् to the city, त्वम् You, ताम् that, वारुणीम् belonging to Varuna, कारण-मर्त्य-रूपः You who have assumed a human form for a specific cause

Simple Meaning: Once, while bathing in the waters of the Yamuna in the early morning, Your father Nanda was captured and taken away by a servant of Varuna. Assuming a human form for a specific divine purpose, You went to that city of Varuna in order to bring him back.

ससम्भ्रमं तेन जलाधिपेन प्रपूजितस्त्वं प्रतिगृह्य तातम् ।
उपागतस्तत्क्षणमात्मगेहं पिताऽवदत्तच्चरितं निजेभ्यः ॥64.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: स-सम्भ्रमम् with great reverence and haste, तेन by him, जल-अधिपेन by the lord of waters- Varuna, प्रपूजितः worshiped, त्वम् You, प्रतिगृह्य taking back, तातम् Your father, उपागतः returned, तत्-क्षणम् at that very moment, आत्म-गेहम् to Your own home, पिता Your father Nanda, अवदत् related, तत्-चरितम् that incident, निजेभ्यः to his own kinsmen

Simple Meaning: Worshiped with immense reverence by Varuna, the lord of waters, You took Your father back and returned that very moment to Your own home. Upon returning, Your father Nanda related that extraordinary incident to his kinsmen.

हरिं विनिश्चित्य भवन्तमेतान् भवत्पदालोकनबद्धतृष्णान् ॥

निरीक्ष्य विष्णो परमं पदं तद्दुरापमन्यैस्त्वमदीदृशस्तान् ॥64.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: हरिम् Lord Hari, विनिश्चित्य having firmly concluded, भवन्तम् You, एतान् these cowherds, भवत्-पद-आलोकन-बद्ध-तृष्णान् intensely longing to see Your supreme abode, निरीक्ष्य seeing, विष्णो O Lord Vishnu, परमम् supreme, पदम् abode, तत् that, दुरापम् unattainable, अन्यैः by others, त्वम् You, अदीदृशः showed, तान् to them

Simple Meaning: O Lord Vishnu, having firmly realized that You are none other than Supreme Lord Hari Himself, the cowherds became intensely filled with the desire to behold Your transcendent realm. Seeing their longing, You granted them the divine vision of Your supreme abode, Vaikuntha, which is otherwise unattainable by anyone else.

स्फुरत्परानन्दरसप्रवाहप्रपूर्णकैवल्यमहापयोधौ ।
चिरं निमग्नाः खलु गोपसङ्घास्त्वयैव भूमन् पुनरुद्धृतास्ते ॥64.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्फुरत्-परा-आनन्द-रस-प्रवाह-प्रपूर्ण-कैवल्य-महा-पयोधौ in the great ocean of liberation which is completely filled with the overflowing current of radiant supreme bliss, चिरम् for a long time, निमग्नाः deeply immersed, खलु indeed, गोप-सङ्घाः the groups of cowherds, त्वया by You, एव alone, भूमन् O Infinite Supreme Lord, पुनः again, उद्धृताः were brought back, ते they

Simple Meaning: O Infinite Supreme Lord, the cowherds remained deeply immersed for a long time in that vast ocean of liberation, which overflowed with the continuous current of supreme divine bliss. Indeed, it was You alone who brought them back to the realm of worldly consciousness.

करबदरवदेवं देव कुत्रावतारे निजपदमनवाप्यं दर्शितं भक्तिभाजाम् ।
तदिह पशुपरूपी त्वं हि साक्षात् परात्मा पवनपुरनिवासिन् पाहि मामामयेभ्यः ॥64.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: कर-बदर-वत् like a gooseberry held in the palm of one's hand, एवम् in this manner, देव O Lord, कुत्र in which, अवतारे incarnation, निज-पदम् Your own supreme abode, अनवाप्यम् unattainable, दर्शितम् was revealed, भक्ति-भाजाम् to Your devoted followers, तत् therefore, इह here, पशुप-रूपी appearing in the form of a cowherd, त्वम् You, हि indeed, साक्षात् manifestly, परात्मा the Supreme Soul, पवनपुर-निवासिन् O Lord residing in Guruvayur, पाहि protect, माम् me, आमयेभ्यः from all afflictions and diseases

Simple Meaning: O Lord, in which other incarnation was Your supreme abode, otherwise unattainable, ever revealed so effortlessly to Your devotees like a gooseberry held in the palm of one's hand? Therefore, You who appear here in the simple form of a cowherd are indeed the Supreme Soul Himself. O Lord residing in Guruvayur, please protect me from all my afflictions and diseases!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
