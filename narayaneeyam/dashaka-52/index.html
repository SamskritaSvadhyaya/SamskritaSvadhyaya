Krishna humbles Brahma’s pride

दशकम् ५२

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 52

Krishna humbles Brahma’s pride

अन्यावतारनिकरेष्वनिरीक्षितं ते भूमातिरेकमभिवीक्ष्य तदाघमोक्षे ।
ब्रह्मा परीक्षितुमनाः स परोक्षभावं निन्येऽथ वत्सकगणान् प्रवितत्य मायाम् ॥52.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्य-अवतार-निकरेषु in all the other avatars, अनिरीक्षितम् unseen, ते Your, भूम-अतिरेकम् extraordinary abundance of glory and power, अभिवीक्ष्य witnessing, तदा अघ-मोक्षे at the time of Aghasura's liberation, ब्रह्मा Lord Brahma, परीक्षितु-मनाः wishing to test You, सः he, परोक्ष-भावम् into disappearance, निन्ये led, अथ then, वत्सक-गणान् the herds of calves, प्रवितत्य expanding, मायाम् his power of illusion

Simple Meaning: Having witnessed the extraordinary glory and power shown by You during the liberation of Aghasura—a glory unseen in any of Your other incarnations—Lord Brahma wished to test Your divine power. He spread his power of illusion and made all the herds of calves vanish from sight.

वत्सानवीक्ष्य विवशे पशुपोत्करे तान् आनेतुकाम इव धातृमतानुवर्ती ।
त्वं सामिभुक्तकबलो गतवांस्तदानीं भुक्तांस्तिरोऽधित सरोजभवः कुमारान् ॥52.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: वत्सान् the calves, अ-वीक्ष्य not finding, विवशे becoming distressed, पशुप-उत्करे when the group of cowherd boys became, तान् them, आनेतु-कामः wishing to fetch, इव as if, धातृ-मत-अनुवर्ती following Brahma's intent, त्वम् You, सामि-भुक्त-कबलः with a half-eaten morsel of food in hand, गतवान् went, तदानीम् at that moment, भुक्तान् who had just finished eating, तिरोऽधित concealed, सरोज-भवः Lord Brahma (born from the lotus), कुमारान् the young cowherd boys

Simple Meaning: When the cowherd boys became deeply distressed at not seeing their calves, You set out to search for them, as if merely playing along with Brahma's plan, with a half-eaten morsel of food still in Your hand. At that moment, Lord Brahma hid the young cowherd boys as well, who had finished eating their lunch.

वत्सायितस्तदनु गोपगणायितस्त्वं शिक्यादिभाण्डमुरलीगवलादिरूपः ।
प्राग्वद्विहृत्य विपिनेषु चिराय सायं त्वं माययाऽथ बहुधा व्रजमाययाथ ॥52.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: वत्सायितः assuming the forms of the calves, तदनु after that, गोप-गणायितः assuming the forms of the cowherd boys, त्वम् You, शिक्य-आदि-भाण्ड-मुरली-गवल-आदि-रूपः taking the forms of the slings, pots, flutes, horns and other articles, प्राग्वत् as before, विहृत्य playing, विपिनेषु in the forests, चिराय for a long time, सायम् in the evening, त्वम् You, मायया by Your divine power, अथ then, बहुधा in many forms, व्रजम् to Vraja, आययाथ returned

Simple Meaning: Thereafter You assumed the forms of the calves, the cowherd boys, and all their possessions such as the ropes, pots, flutes, and horns. Having played in the forests for a long time just as before, You returned to Vraja in the evening in those many forms through Your divine power.

त्वामेव शिक्यगवलादिमयं दधानो भूयस्त्वमेव पशुवत्सकबालरूपः ।
गोरूपिणीभिरपि गोपवधूमयीभिः आसादितोऽसि जननीभिरतिप्रहर्षात् ॥52.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वाम् एव You alone, शिक्य-गवल-आदि-मयम् consisting of the slings, flutes and other items, दधानः holding, भूयः again, त्वम् एव You alone, पशु-वत्सक-बाल-रूपः taking the forms of the cows calves and cowherd boys, गो-रूपाभिः by those in the form of cows, अपि also, गोप-वधू-मयीभिः consisting of the cowherd women, आसादितः असि You were received, जननीभिः by the mothers, अति-प्रहर्षात् with supreme joy

Simple Meaning: Carrying You alone in the form of the slings, flutes, and other items, and being You alone in the forms of the cows, calves, and cowherd boys, You were received with supreme joy by all the mothers, both the cows and the cowherd women.

जीवं हि कञ्चिदभिमानवशात् स्व​कीयं मत्वा तनूज इति रागभरं वहन्त्यः ।
आत्मानमेव तु भवन्तमवाप्य सूनुं प्रीतिं ययुर्न कियतीं वनिताश्च गावः ॥52.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: जीवम् a individual soul, हि indeed, कञ्चित् any particular one, अभिमान-वशात् due to attachment, स्वकीयम् one's own, मत्वा considering, तनूजः son, इति thus, राग-भरम् extreme affection, वहन्त्यः bearing, आत्मानम् एव the Supreme Soul Himself, तु but, भवन्तम् You, अवाप्य obtaining, सूनुम् as son, प्रीतिम् love, ययुः attained, न कियतीम् boundless, वनिताः the women, च and, गावः the cows

Simple Meaning: Women and cows normally feel immense affection for their offspring merely because attachment makes them view a particular individual soul as their own son. But when they received You, the Supreme Soul Himself, as their son, how could their joy and love be anything less than boundless?

एवं प्रतिक्षणविजृम्भितहर्षभारनिश्शेषगोपगणलालितभूरिमूर्तिम् ।
त्वामग्रजोऽपि बुबुधे किल वत्सरान्ते ब्रह्मात्मनोरपि महान् युवयोर्विशेषः ॥52.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this manner, प्रतिक्षण-विजृम्भित-हर्ष-भार-निश्शेष-गोप-गण-लालित-भूरि-मूर्तिम् You whose manifold forms were cherished by all the cowherd people with joy that grew every moment, त्वाम् You, अग्रजः अपि even Your elder brother Balarama, बुबुधे understood, किल indeed, वत्सर-अन्ते at the end of a year, ब्रह्म-आत्मनोः between Brahman and the individual soul, अपि even, महान् great, युवयोः between You two, विशेषः the distinction

Simple Meaning: In this manner, all the cowherd people cherished Your manifold forms with joy that increased every single moment. At the end of one year, even Your elder brother Balarama understood Your true nature, realizing the great distinction between You two, who are like the Supreme Brahman and the individual soul.

वर्षावधौ नवपुरातनवत्सपालान् दृष्ट्वा विवेकमसृणे द्रुहिणे विमूढे ।
प्रादीदृशः प्रतिनवान् मकुटाङ्गदादि भूषांश्चतुर्भुजयुजः सजलाम्बुदाभान् ॥52.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: वर्ष-अवधौ at the completion of a year, नव-पुरातन-वत्स-पालान् the new and the old calves and cowherd boys, दृष्ट्वा having seen, विवेक-मसृणे when his discrimination became dulled, द्रुहिणे when Brahma, विमूढे became completely bewildered, प्रादीदृशः You revealed, प्रतिनवान् each of them transformed, मकुट-अङ्गद-आदि-भूषान् adorned with crowns armlets and other ornaments, चतुर्भुज-युजः endowed with four arms, स-जल-अम्बुद-आभान् shining with the dark brilliance of rain-bearing clouds

Simple Meaning: At the end of one year, when Brahma saw both the new calves and cowherd boys along with the old ones he had hidden, his discrimination failed and he became completely bewildered. Thereupon You revealed to him that each of those forms was in fact Yourself, four-armed, adorned with crowns, armlets, and other ornaments, and glowing with the dark sheen of rain-filled clouds.

प्रत्येकमेव कमलापरिलालिताङ्गान् भोगीन्द्रभोगशयनान् नयनाभिरामान् ।
लीलानिमीलितदृशः सनकादियोगिव्यासेवितान् कमलभूर्भवतो ददर्श ॥52.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रत्येकम् एव each one of them indeed, कमला-परिलालित-अङ्गान् whose limbs were gently caressed by Goddess Lakshmi, भोगी-इन्द्र-भोग-शयनान् resting on the coils of the king of serpents Adisesha, नयन-अभिरामान् enchanting to the eyes, लीला-निमीलित-दृशः with eyes playfully half-closed, सनक-आदि-योगि-व्यासेवितान् worshipped by sages like Sanaka and others, कमलभूः Lord Brahma born from the lotus, भवतः Your forms, ददर्श saw

Simple Meaning: Lord Brahma saw that each one of those forms was You only, with limbs gently caressed by Goddess Lakshmi, resting on the bed of Adisesha, visually captivating, with eyes playfully half-closed, and served by great yogis like Sanaka and others.

नारायणाकृतिमसंख्यतमां निरीक्ष्य सर्वत्र सेवकमपि स्वमवेक्ष्य धाता ।
मायानिमग्नहृदयो विमुमोह यावत् एको बभूविथ तदा कबलार्धपाणिः ॥52.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: नारायण-आकृतिम् forms of Narayana, असंख्यतमाम् countless, निरीक्ष्य having seen, सर्वत्र everywhere, सेवकम् अपि as a servant also, स्वम् himself, अवेक्ष्य seeing, धाता Lord Brahma, माया-निमग्न-हृदयः with his mind submerged in Maya, विमुमोह became deeply bewildered, यावत् while, एकः alone, बभूविथ You became, तदा then, कबल-अर्ध-पाणिः with a half-eaten morsel of food in hand

Simple Meaning: Seeing countless forms of Narayana everywhere, and beholding himself in all directions as a humble servant to each of them, Lord Brahma's mind was completely overwhelmed by Your Maya and he became totally bewildered. Thereupon You merged all those forms back into Yourself and stood alone as before, holding the half-eaten morsel of food in Your hand.

नश्यन्मदे तदनु विश्वपतिं मुहुस्त्वां नत्वा च नूतवति धातरि धाम याते ।
पोतैः समं प्रमुदितैः प्रविशन् निकेतं वातालयाधिप विभो परिपाहि रोगात् ॥52.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: नश्यत्-मदे when his pride was destroyed, तदनु after that, विश्व-पतिम् the Lord of the universe, मुहुः repeatedly, त्वाम् You, नत्वा bowing down to, च and, नूतवति after praising, धातरि when Lord Brahma, धाम in his abode, याते went, पोतैः with the young boys, समम् together, प्रमुदितैः who were joyful, प्रविशन् entering, निकेतम् home, वातालयाधिप O Lord of Guruvayur, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, परिपाहि protect me, रोगात् from disease

Simple Meaning: When Brahma's pride was completely shattered, he bowed down to You, the Lord of the universe, again and again and offered prayers. After he departed for his own abode, You entered Vraja along with the joyful young cowherd boys. O Lord of Guruvayur, O All-Pervading Lord, please protect me from all diseases.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
