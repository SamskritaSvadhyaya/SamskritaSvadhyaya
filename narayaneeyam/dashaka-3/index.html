The Prayer of the Devotee

दशकम् ३

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 3

The Prayer of the devotee

(छन्दस् - शिखरिणी)

पठन्तो नामानि प्रमदभरसिन्धौ निपतिताः
स्मरन्तो रूपं ते वरद कथयन्तो गुणकथाः ।
चरन्तो ये भक्तास्त्वयि खलु रमन्ते परममून्
अहं धन्यान् मन्ये समधिगतसर्वाभिलषितान् ॥3.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: पठन्तः chanting, नामानि names, प्रमद-भर-सिन्धौ in the ocean filled with joy, निपतिताः immersed, स्मरन्तः meditating upon, रूपम् form, ते Your, वरद O giver of boons, कथयन्तः discussing, गुण-कथाः stories of Your virtues, चरन्तः moving about, ये who, भक्ताः devotees, त्वयि in You, खलु indeed, रमन्ते rejoice, परम् supremely, अमून् these, अहम् I, धन्यान् blessed, मन्ये consider, समधिगत-सर्व-अभिलषितान् who have attained all their desires

Simple Meaning: O Giver of boons! Those devotees who chant Your divine names and find themselves immersed in an ocean of joy, who meditate upon Your beautiful form, talk about Your glorious attributes, and move about in this world finding delight in You alone—I consider them to be the most blessed souls, who have truly attained all that is ever worth desiring.

गदक्लिष्टं कष्टं तव चरणसेवारसभरे
अप्यनासक्तं चित्तं भवति बत विष्णो कुरु दयाम् ।
भवत्पादाम्भोजस्मरणरसिको नामनिवहान्
अहं गायं गायं कुहचन विवत्स्यामि विजने ॥3.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: गद-क्लिष्टम् afflicted by disease, कष्टम् alas, तव Your, चरण-सेवा-रस-भरे in the abundance of the joy of serving Your feet, अपि even, अनासक्तम् unattached, चित्तम् mind, भवति becomes, बत what a pity, विष्णो O Vishnu, कुरु show, दयाम् mercy, भवत्-पाद-अम्भोज-स्मरण-रसिकः one who delights in remembering Your lotus feet, नाम-निवहान् multitudes of Your names, अहम् I, गायम् गायम् repeatedly singing, कुहचन somewhere, विवत्स्यामि will dwell, विजने in a solitary place

Simple Meaning: O Lord Vishnu! What a tragedy it is that my mind, constantly tormented by painful disease, is unable to find attachment even in the sweet joy of serving Your lotus feet. Alas, please show mercy on me! Bless me so that, delighting in the constant remembrance of Your lotus feet, I may spend my days in some solitary place, singing Your holy names over and over again.

कृपा ते जाता चेत् किमिव न हि लभ्यं तनुभृतां
मदीयक्लेशौघप्रशमनदशा नाम कियती ।
न के के लोकेऽस्मिन्ननिशमयि शोकाभिरहिता
भवद्भक्ता मुक्ताः सुखगतिमसक्ता विदधते ॥3.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृपा grace, ते Your, जाता is born (obtained), चेत् if, किम् इव what indeed, न not, हि surely, लभ्यम् attainable, तनुभृताम् for embodied beings, मदीय-क्लेश-ओघ-प्रशमन-दशा the state of calming the multitude of my miseries, नाम indeed, कियती how insignificant (by comparison), न not, के के who all, लोके-अस्मिन् in this world, अनिशम् continuously, अयि oh, शोक-अभिरहिताः free from all sorrows, भवत्-भक्ताः Your devotees, मुक्ताः liberated, सुख-गतिम् the path of supreme bliss, असक्ताः unattached (to worldly things), विदधते attain

Simple Meaning: Oh Lord! If Your grace is once obtained, what is there in this world that is impossible for living beings to achieve? Compared to that ultimate power, how tiny and insignificant is the task of curing my bundle of physical illnesses and miseries! Indeed, are there not countless devotees of Yours in this world who, living completely detached from worldly attractions, have been freed from all grief, liberated from bondage, and blessed with the highest state of supreme bliss?

मुनिप्रौढा रूढा जगति खलु गूढात्मगतयो
भवत्पादाम्भोजस्मरणविरुजो नारदमुखाः ।
चरन्तीश स्वैरं सततपरिनिर्भातपरचित्-
सदानन्दाद्वैतप्रसरपरिमग्नाः किमपरम् ॥ 4 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: मुनि-प्रौढाः the great leaders among sages, रूढाः well-established, जगति in the world, खलु indeed, गूढ-आत्म-गतयः whose movements are deeply hidden, भवत्-पाद-अम्भोज-स्मरण-विरुजः cured of all spiritual and physical afflictions by remembering Your lotus feet, नारद-मुखाः led by sages like Narada, चरन्ति wander, ईश O Lord, स्वैरम् freely, सतत-परिनिर्भात-पर-चित्-सदानन्द-अद्वैत-प्रसर-परिमग्नाः immersed in the continuous, radiant flow of Your-self which is of the nature of non-dual supreme consciousness and eternal bliss, किम् अपरम् what else is superior (to this)

Simple Meaning: O Lord! The great, self-realized masters among sages, led by the divine sage Narada, wander freely across the worlds. Cured of all afflictions—both of the body and of worldly existence—simply by the remembrance of Your lotus feet, they remain immersed in the continuous, radiant flow of Your-self which is of the nature of non-dual supreme consciousness and eternal bliss. What greater fortune than this could ever be spoken of?

भवद्भक्तिः स्फीता भवतु मम सैव प्रशमयेत्
अशेषक्लेशौघं न खलु हृदि सन्देहकणिका ।
न चेद्व्यासस्योक्तिस्तव च वचनं नैगमवचो
भवेन्मिथ्या रथ्यापुरुषवचनप्रायमखिलम् ॥3.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: भवत्-भक्तिः devotion to You, स्फीता intense/abundant, भवतु let it become, मम my, सा एव that alone, प्रशमयेत् may cure/quell, अशेष-क्लेश-ओघम् the entire multitude of my afflictions, न not, खलु indeed, हृदि in my heart, सन्देह-कणिका even a speck of doubt, न चेत् if it were not so, व्यासस्य उक्तिः the words of Sage Vyasa, तव Your, च and, वचनम् own words, नैगम-वचः the declarations of the Vedas, भवेत् would become, मिथ्या false, रथ्या-पुरुष-वचन-प्रायम् comparable to the empty gossip of a common man on the street, अखिलम् all of it

Simple Meaning: Let my devotion to You grow abundantly, for that devotion alone will completely cure and destroy my entire multitude of miseries. Of this, there is not even a tiny speck of doubt in my heart. If this were not true, then the sacred words of Sage Vyasa, Your own promises, and the declarations of the Vedas themselves would all become false—reduced to nothing more than the meaningless gossip of an ordinary man on the street!

भवद्भक्तिस्तावत् प्रमुखमधुरा त्वत्गुणरसात्
किमप्यारूढा चेदखिलपरितापप्रशमनी ।
पुनश्चान्ते स्वान्ते विमलपरिबोधोदयमिलन्
महानन्दाद्वैतं दिशति किमतः प्रार्थ्यमपरम् ॥3.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: भवत्-भक्तिः devotion to You, तावत् indeed, प्रमुख-मधुरा sweet from the very beginning, त्वत्-गुण-रसात् due to the essence of Your glorious attributes, किम् अपि even a little degree, आरूढा if grown/intensified, चेत् if, अखिल-परिताप-प्रशमनी the destroyer of all miseries, पुनः and further, च and, अन्ते in the end, स्वान्ते in the mind/heart, विमल-परिबोध-उदय-मिलन् merging with the dawning of pure knowledge, महा-आनन्द-अद्वैतम् the supreme, non-dual state of bliss, दिशति bestows, किम् what, अतः than this, प्रार्थ्यम् to be prayed for, अपरम् other

Simple Meaning: Devotion to You, is sweet from its very beginning because it is filled with the nectar of Your glorious attributes. Once it intensifies and grows deep within the heart, it extinguishes all worldly miseries. Ultimately, it brings about the dawn of pure self-knowledge, merging the devotee into the supreme, non-dual state of absolute bliss. What more could one ever desire for than this?

विधूय क्लेशान् मे कुरु चरणयुग्मं धृतरसं
भवत्क्षेत्रप्राप्तौ करमपि च ते पूजनविधौ ।
भवन्मूर्त्यालोके नयनमथ ते पादतुलसी-
परिघ्राणे घ्राणं श्रवणमपि ते चारुचरिते ॥3.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: विधूय having shaken off, क्लेशान् मे my afflictions, कुरु make, चरण-युग्मम् pair of feet, धृत-रसम् finding delight, भवत्-क्षेत्र-प्राप्तौ in reaching Your temples, करम् अपि and also my hands, च and, ते पूजन-विधौ in the rituals of Your worship, भवत्-मूर्ति-आलोके in gazing at Your divine form, नयनम् my eyes, अथ then, ते पाद-तुलसी-परिघ्राणे in smelling the Tulasi leaves offered at Your feet, घ्राणम् my nose, श्रवणम् अपि and also my ears, ते चारु-चरिते in Your beautiful stories/exploits

Simple Meaning: Please destroy my illnesses and bless that – my feet find delight in walking to Your sacred temples; my hands be occupied in Your worship; my eyes gaze upon Your beautiful form; my nose breathe in the divine fragrance of the Tulasi leaves offered at Your feet; and my ears hear of Your glorious stories.

प्रभूताधिव्याधिप्रसभचलिते मामकहृदि
त्वदीयं तद्रूपं परमसुखचिद्रूपमुदियात् ।
उदञ्चद्रोमाञ्चो गलितबहुहर्षाश्रुनिवहो
यथा विस्मर्यासं दुरुपशमपीडापरिभवान् ॥3.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रभूत-आधि-व्याधि-प्रसभ-चलिते shaken by the force of mental anxieties and physical diseases, मामक-हृदि in my heart, त्वदीयम् Your, तत्-रूपम् that divine form, परम-सुख-चित्-रूपम् of the nature of supreme bliss and pure consciousness, उदियात् may it arise/manifest, उदञ्चत्-रोमाञ्चः with hair standing on end due to ecstasy, गलित-बहु-हर्ष-अश्रु-निवहः shedding a flood of tears of joy, यथा so that, विस्मर्यासम् I may completely forget, दुरुपशम-पीडा-परिभवान् the humiliations and sufferings caused by these hard-to-cure illnesses

प्रभूत (विशेषणम्) बहुलम् src. Kosha. Ashtadhyayi

आधि (पुंलिङ्गः) मनःपीडा src. Kosha. Ashtadhyayi

व्याधि (पुंलिङ्गः) रोगः src. Kosha. Ashtadhyayi

प्रसभः Force, src. Kosha. Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: My heart is tormented by intense mental anxieties and painful physical diseases. In this agitated heart of mine, may Your divine form—which is the very embodiment of supreme bliss and pure consciousness— manifest! Being blessed so, with my hair standing on end in ecstasy and shedding a flood of tears of joy, may I completely forget all the humiliations and unbearable sufferings of these stubborn, painful illnesses.

मरुद्गेहाधीश त्वयि खलु पराञ्चोऽपि सुखिनो
भवत्स्नेही सोऽहं सुबहु परितप्ये च किमिदम् ।
अकीर्तिस्ते मा भूद्वरद गदभारं प्रशमयन्
भवत् भक्तोत्तंसं झटिति कुरु मां कंसदमन ॥3.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: मरुत्-गेह-अधीश O Lord of the abode of the Wind - Guruvayoor, त्वयि towards You, खलु indeed, पराञ्चः अपि even those who are turned away, सुखिनः are happy, भवत्-स्नेही who loves You, सः अहम् that very me, सुबहु excessively, परितप्ये am suffering, च and, किम् इदम् what is this, अकीर्तिः ill repute, ते to You, मा भूत् let it not happen, वरद O Giver of boons, गद-भारम् the heavy burden of my disease, प्रशमयन् curing, भवत्-भक्त-उत्तमसम् the crown jewel among Your devotees, झटिति quickly, कुरु make, माम् me, कंस-दमन O Slayer of Kamsa

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Guruvayoor! O Slayer of Kamsa! What a strange irony this is—even those people who are completely indifferent to You are living happily and comfortably in this world. Yet, here I am, someone who loves You deeply, suffering from this unbearable, agonizing pain! O Giver of boons, please do not let this bring a bad name to You. Quickly cure the heavy burden of my illness, and make me shine as the crown jewel among Your devotees!

किमुक्तैर्भूयोभिस्तव हि करुणा यावदुदियात्
अहं तावद्देव प्रहितविविधार्तप्रलपितः ।
पुरः क्लृप्ते पादे वरद तव नेष्यामि दिवसान्
यथाशक्ति व्यक्तं नतिनुतिनिषेवा विरचयन् ॥3.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: किम् what (is the use), उक्तैः of speaking, भूयोभिः many more words, तव Your, हि indeed, करुणा mercy, यावत् until, उदियात् dawns, अहम् I, तावत् till then, देव O Lord, प्रहित-विविध-आर्त-प्रलपितः offering my cries of distress, पुरः in front of, क्लृप्ते placed/visualized, पादे at Your feet, वरद O Giver of boons, तव Your, नेष्यामि will spend, दिवसान् my days, यथा-शक्तिः according to my capacity, व्यक्तम् clearly, नति-नुति-निषेवाः prostrations, praises, and service, विरचयन् performing

Simple Meaning: O Lord! What is the use of repeating so many words? Until Your divine mercy dawns upon me, I will spend all my days right here, offering my cries of distress to You, with my mind fixed on Your feet placed right in front of me. O Giver of boons! To the best of my strength and capacity, I will spend my time openly performing prostrations, singing Your praises, and offering my worship to You.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
