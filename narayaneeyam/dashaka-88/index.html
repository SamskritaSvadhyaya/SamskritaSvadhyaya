Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 88

Krishna brings the dead children of Vasudeva and Devaki and of the brahmana back to life

Krishna shows Vaikuntha to Arjuna

प्रागेवाचार्यपुत्राहृतिनिशमनया स्वीयषट्सूनुवीक्षां
काङ्क्षन्त्या मातुरुक्त्या सुतलभुवि बलिं प्राप्य तेनार्चितस्त्वम् ।
धातुः शापाद्धिरण्यान्वितकशिपुभवान् शौरिजान् कंसभग्नान्
आनीयैनान् प्रदर्श्य स्वपदमनयथाः पूर्वपुत्रान् मरीचेः ॥88.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्राक् एव earlier indeed, आचार्य-पुत्र-आहृति-निशमनया by hearing about the retrieval/restoration of the son of Your guru, स्वीय-षट्-सूनु-वीक्षाम् to see her own six sons, काङ्क्षन्त्या longing for, मातुः of Your mother (Devaki), उक्त्या by the words/request, सुतल-भुवि in the land of Sutala, बलिम् King Bali, प्राप्य reaching, तेन-अर्चितः त्वम् You who were worshipped by him, धातुः of Brahma, शापात् due to the curse, हिरण्य-अन्वित-कशिपु-भवान् born as the sons of Hiranyakashipu, शौरि-जान् born to Shauri - Vasudeva, कंस-भग्नान् slain/killed by Kamsa, आनीय एनान् bringing these, प्रदर्श्य showing them to her, स्व-पदम् to Your own abode, अनयथाः You led/took, पूर्व-पुत्रान् the former sons, मरीचेः of Sage Marichi

Simple Meaning: Earlier, hearing how You had brought back Your guru’s dead son, Your mother Devaki longed to see her own six infants who had been slain by Kamsa. At her request, You went to the realm of Sutala, where You were reverently worshipped by King Bali. From there, You brought back those six infants—who were originally the sons of Sage Marichi, born as the sons of Hiranyakashipu due to a curse by Lord Brahma, and subsequently reborn as the children of Vasudeva only to be killed by Kamsa. After showing them to Your delighted mother, You elevated them to Your supreme abode.

श्रुतदेव इति श्रुतं द्विजेन्द्रं
बहुलाश्वं नृपतिं च भक्तिपूर्णम् ।
युगपत्त्वमनुग्रहीतुकामो
मिथिलां प्रापिथं तापसैः समेतः ॥88.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रुतदेवः इति known as Shrutadeva, श्रुतम् famous/celebrated, द्विजेन्द्रम् noble brahmana, बहुलाश्वम् Bahulashva, नृपतिम् King, च and, भक्ति-पूर्णम् filled with intense devotion, युगपत् simultaneously, त्वम् You, अनुग्रहीतु-कामः desiring to bless, मिथिलाम् to the city of Mithila, प्रापिथ You reached, तापसैः with great sages/ascetics, समेतः accompanied

Simple Meaning: Desiring to simultaneously bless two deeply devoted souls—a noble and revered brahmana named Shrutadeva and King Bahulashva—You set out and arrived in the city of Mithila, accompanied by a group of great sages.

गच्छन् द्विमूर्तिरुभयोर्युगपन्निकेतम्
एकेन भूरिविभवैर्विहितोपचारः ।
अन्येन तद्दिनभृतैश्च फलौदनाद्यैः
तुल्यं प्रसेदिथ ददथ च मुक्तिमाभ्याम् ॥88.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: गच्छन् going/entering, द्वि-मूर्तिः assuming two identical forms, उभयोः of both, युगपत् simultaneously, निकेतम् to the residence, एकेन by one (King Bahulashva), भूरि-विभवैः with great opulence and grand arrangements, विहित-उपचारः received with grand hospitalities and worship, अन्येन by the other (Shrutadeva), तत्-दिन-भृतैः with items procured/gathered on that very day, च and, फल-ओदनाद्यैः with fruits, cooked rice, and simple offerings, तुल्यम् equally, प्रसेदिथ You were pleased, ददथ च and bestowed, मुक्तिम् liberation, आभ्याम् upon both of them

Simple Meaning: Assuming two identical forms, You simultaneously entered the homes of both devotees. One of them, King Bahulashva, welcomed You with royal magnificence and grand hospitality, while the other, Shrutadeva, offered simple fruits, rice, and water gathered that very day. You were equally pleased with both offerings and blessed both of these great souls with ultimate liberation.

भूयोऽथ द्वारवत्यां द्विजतनयमृतिं तत्प्रलापानपि त्वम्
को वा दैवं निरुन्ध्यादिति किल कथयन् विश्ववोढाप्यसोढाः ।
जिष्णोर्गर्वं विनेतुं त्वयि मनुजधिया कुण्ठितां चास्य बुद्धिं
तत्त्वारूढां विधातुं परमतमपदप्रेक्षणेनेति मन्ये ॥88.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूयः अथ later then, द्वारवत्याम् in Dwaraka, द्विज-तनय-मृतिम् the death of a brahmana's newborn sons, तत्-प्रलापान् अपि and his lamentations as well, त्वम् You, कः वा who indeed, दैवम् fate, निरुन्ध्यात् इति can obstruct/stop, किल thus, कथयन् saying, विश्व-वोढा अपि though You are the upholder and bearer of the universe, असोढाः endured/tolerated, जिष्णोः of Arjuna, गर्वम् pride, विनेतुम् to remove/subdue, त्वयि towards You, मनुज-धिया thinking You to be a mere human, कुण्ठिताम् dulled/blunted, च and, अस्य his, बुद्धिम् mind/intellect, तत्त्व-आरूढाम् realisation of the ultimate Truth, विधातुम् to make, परम-तम-पद-प्रेक्षणेन by revealing Your supreme transcendental realm, इति मन्ये thus I consider/think

Simple Meaning: Later in Dwaraka, when a brahmana repeatedly lost his newborn sons and wept bitterly at Your door, You—the supreme upholder of the entire universe—bore his lamentations quietly and remarked, "Who can obstruct the course of fate?" I believe this blunted Arjuna’s mind into thinking that You are a mere human. To remove his pride and to make him understand the absolute truth, You showed him the Supreme abode, Vaikuntha.

नष्टा अष्टास्य पुत्राः पुनरपि तव तूपेक्षया कष्टवादः
स्पष्टो जातो जनानामथ तदवसरे द्वारकामाप पार्थः ।
मैत्र्या तत्रोषितोऽसौ नवमसुतमृतौ विप्रवर्यप्ररोदं
श्रुत्वा चक्रे प्रतिज्ञामनुपहृतसुतः सन्निवेक्ष्ये कृशानुम् ॥88.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: नष्टाः lost/dead, अष्ट eight, अस्य his (the brahmana's), पुत्राः sons, पुनः अपि तव तु उपेक्षया yet again due to Your indifference, कष्ट-वादः harsh criticism/blame, स्पष्टः जातः explicitly arose, जनानाम् among the people, अथ then, तत्-अवसरे on that occasion, द्वारकाम् आप reached Dwaraka, पार्थः Arjuna (son of Pritha), मैत्र्या out of friendship, तत्र उषितः असौ residing there, नवम-सुत-मृतौ upon the death of the ninth son, विप्र-वर्य-प्ररोदम् the loud lamentation of the noble brahmana, श्रुत्वा hearing, चक्रे प्रतिज्ञाम् made a vow, अनुपहृत-सुतः सन् failing to bring back the son alive, निवेक्ष्ये I shall enter, कृशानुम् fire

Simple Meaning: When eight sons of the brahmana died one after another, the people began to criticize You openly for Your apparent indifference. Around that time, Arjuna arrived at Dwaraka and stayed as Your guest out of friendship. Hearing the heartbreaking grief of the noble brahmana upon the death of his ninth child, Arjuna made a solemn vow: "If I fail to bring him back alive, I shall enter the fire!"

मानी स त्वामपृष्ट्वा द्विजनिलयगतो बाणजालैर्महास्त्रै
रुन्धानः सूतिगेहं पुनरपि सहसा दृष्टनष्टे कुमारे ।
याम्यामैन्द्रीं तथाऽन्याः सुरवरनगरीर्विद्ययाऽऽसाद्य सद्यो
मोघोद्योगः पतिष्यन् हुतभुजि भवता सस्मितं वारितोऽभूत् ॥88.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: मानी proud/arrogant, सः he (Arjuna), त्वाम् You, अपृष्ट्वा without consulting/asking, द्विज-निलय-गतः having gone to the residence of the brahmana, बाण-जालैः with a dense network of arrows, महा-अस्त्रैः with powerful celestial weapons, रुन्धानः covering/encasing, सूति-गेहम् the delivery room, पुनरपि once again, सहसा suddenly, दृष्ट-नष्टे when the newborn child disappeared as soon as it was seen, कुमारे the infant boy, याम्याम् to the city of Yama, ऐन्द्रीम् to the city of Indra, तथा and, अन्याः other, सुर-वर-नगरीः glorious cities of the noble devas, विद्यया-आसाद्य using his divine lore/mystic power to reach, सद्यः immediately, मोघ-उद्योगः his efforts having proved futile, पतिष्यन् about to leap/fall, हुत-भुजि into the blazing fire, भवता by You, स-स्मितम् with a gentle smile, वारितः अभूत् was stopped/prevented

Simple Meaning: When the tenth child was about to be born, overly confident in his own prowess, Arjuna went to the brahmana's house without asking for Your guidance. He encased the delivery room in an impenetrable cage made of celestial arrows and powerful weapons. Yet, as soon as the newborn infant was born, it vanished into thin air! Utilizing his mystic knowledge, Arjuna searched immediately in the realms of Yama, Indra, and the glorious abodes of other devas, but all his efforts ended in complete failure. As he prepared to jump into the flaming fire to fulfill his solemn vow, You gently smiled and stopped him.

सार्धं तेन प्रतीचीं दिशमतिजविना स्यन्दनेनाभियातो
लोकालोकं व्यतीतस्तिमिरभरमथो चक्रधाम्ना निरुन्धन् ।
चक्रांशुक्लिष्टदृष्टिं स्थितमथ विजयं पश्य पश्येति वारां
पारे त्वं प्राददर्शः किमपि हि तमसां दूरदूरं पदं ते ॥88.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: सार्धम् तेन along with him (Arjuna), प्रतीचीम् दिशम् towards the western direction, अति-जविना स्यन्दनेन with extraordinarily swift chariot, अभियातः riding, लोकालोकम् the Lokaloka mountain, व्यतीतः crossing beyond, तिमिर-भरम् अथः the dense mass of darkness thereafter, चक्र-धाम्ना by the brilliant radiance of the Sudarshana chakra, निरुन्धन् dispelling/cutting through, चक्र-अंशु-क्लिष्ट-दृष्टिम् whose eyes were dazzled and blinded by the brilliant rays of the chakra, स्थितम् standing, अथ then, विजयम् unto Arjuna, पश्य पश्य इति look, look, thus, वाराम् पारे beyond the waters of the causal ocean, त्वम् You, प्राददर्शः showed/revealed, किमपि हि a certain transcendent, तमसाम् दूर-दूरम् far, far beyond all darkness of ignorance, पदम् ते abode of Yours

Simple Meaning: Riding alongside Arjuna in Your extraordinarily swift chariot, You traveled toward the western direction and crossed far beyond the Lokaloka mountain. Dispersing the dense mass of cosmic darkness ahead with the brilliant radiance of Your Sudarshana chakra, You spoke to Arjuna—whose eyes were dazzled by the intense rays of the disc—saying, "Look, look!" There, beyond the waters of the cosmic ocean, You revealed Your transcendent supreme abode, situated far, far beyond all darkness of ignorance.

तत्रासीनं भुजङ्गाधिपशयनतले दिव्यभूषायुधाद्यैः
आवीतं पीतचेलं प्रतिनवजलदश्यामलं श्रीमदङ्गम् ।
मूर्तीनामीशितारं परमिह तिसृणामेकमर्थं श्रुतीनां
त्वामेव त्वं परात्मन् प्रियसखसहितो नेमिथ क्षेमरूपम् ॥88.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there, आसीनम् seated, भुजङ्ग-अधिप-शयन-तले upon the bed formed by the lord of serpents (Adishesha), दिव्य-भूषा-आयुधाद्यैः with divine ornaments, weapons, and paraphernalia, आवीतम् surrounded/adorned, पीत-चेलम् wearing yellow garments, प्रतिनव-जलद-श्यामलम् dark like a fresh rain-bearing cloud, श्रीमत्-अङ्गम् with a glorious and radiant form, मूर्तीनाम् ईशितारम् the Supreme Lord of the three forms (Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva), परमम् the ultimate, इह here, तिसृणाम् of the three, एकम् अर्थम् the sole subject/meaning, श्रुतीनाम् of all the Vedas, त्वाम् एव Yourself alone, त्वम् You, परात्मन् O Supreme Being, प्रिय-सख-सहितः together with Your dear friend (Arjuna), नेमिथ bowed down in reverence, क्षेमरूपम् the embodiment of ultimate well-being and peace

Simple Meaning: Seated upon the serpent couch of Adishesha, adorned with divine ornaments and weapons, wearing yellow silk garments, and glowing with a complexion as dark and beautiful as a fresh rain-bearing cloud, was Your own majestic form. You are the Supreme Lord over the cosmic trinity of Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva, and the sole ultimate subject taught by all the Vedas. O Supreme Being, together with Your dear friend Arjuna, You bowed down in reverence before this supreme form of Yourself, the ultimate embodiment of peace and auspiciousness.

युवां मामेव द्वावधिकविवृतान्तर्हिततया
विभिन्नौ सन्द्रष्टुं स्वयमहमहार्षं द्विजसुतान् ।
नयेतं द्रागेतानिति खलु वितीर्णान् पुनरमून्
द्विजायादायादाः प्रणुतमहिमा पाण्डुजनुषा ॥88.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: युवाम् both of you, माम् एव Myself alone, द्वौ two persons, अधिक-विवृत-अन्तर्हिततया through greater manifestation and concealment respectively, विभिन्नौ appearing different, सन्द्रष्टुम् to see clearly, स्वयम् Myself, अहम् I, अहात्षम् kidnapped/carried away, द्विज-सुतान् the sons of the brahmana, नयेतम् take back, द्राक् quickly, एतान् these infants, इति thus, खलु indeed, वितीर्णान् handed over, पुनः again, अमून् these children, द्विजाय to the brahmana, आदाय-आदाः after giving, प्रणुत-महिमा Your glory being praised, पाण्डु-जनुषा by Arjuna (born of Pandu)

Simple Meaning: Lord Maha-Vishnu said, "I carried away the brahmana's children myself because I desired to see both of you, who are non-different from Me, though appearing distinct due to the greater degree of My divine manifestation in Krishna and My concealment in Arjuna. Take these infants back immediately." Receiving the children from Him, You returned them to the brahmana, while Arjuna praised Your transcendent glory with deep devotion.

एवं नानाविहारैर्जगदभिरमयन् वृष्णिवंशं प्रपुष्णन्
ईजानो यज्ञभेदैरतुलविहृतिभिः प्रीणयन्नेणनेत्राः ।
भूभारक्षेपदम्भात् पदकमलजुषां मोक्षणायावतीर्णः
पूर्णं ब्रह्मैव साक्षाद्यदुषु मनुजतारूषितस्त्वं व्यलासीः ॥88.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this manner, नाना-विहारैः through various divine play/pastimes, जगत्-अभिरमयन् delighting the entire world, वृष्णि-वंशम् the Vrishni dynasty, प्रपुष्णन् nourishing and causing to flourish, ईजानः performing, यज्ञ-भेदैः various types of Vedic sacrifices, अतुल-विहृतिभिः through matchless/unrivalled pastimes, प्रीणयन् pleasing, एण-नेत्राः the fawn-eyed women (of Dwaraka), भूभार-क्षेप-दम्भात् under the pretext of removing the burden of the earth, पद-कमल-जुषाम् of those who resort to Your lotus feet, मोक्षणाय for granting complete liberation, अवतीर्णः incarnated, पूर्णम् absolute/full, ब्रह्म एव Brahman/Supreme Reality itself, साक्षात् directly, यदुषु among the Yadus, मनुजता-रूषितः assuming the guise/form of a human being, त्वम् You, व्यलासीः shone forth in all Your splendour

Simple Meaning: In this manner, delighting the entire world through Your diverse divine play, causing the Vrishni dynasty to flourish, performing various grand Vedic sacrifices, and bringing immense joy to the fawn-eyed women of Dwaraka through Your matchless pastimes, You shone forth in supreme splendour. Under the pretext of relieving the heavy burden of the earth, You actually descended to grant ultimate liberation to all who seek refuge at Your lotus feet. You are directly the absolute Brahman, manifesting among the Yadus in human form.

प्रायेण द्वारवत्यामवृतदयि तदा नारदस्त्वद्रसार्द्रः
तस्माल्लेभे कदाचित्खलु सुकृतनिधिस्त्वत्पिता तत्त्वबोधम् ।
भक्तानामग्रयायी स च खलु मतिमानुद्धवस्त्वत्त एव
प्राप्तो विज्ञानसारं स किल जनहितायाधुनाऽऽस्ते बदर्याम् ॥88.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रायेण for the most part, द्वारवत्याम् in Dwaraka, अवृतद्-अयि resided, O Lord, तदा during those days, नारदः Sage Narada, त्वत्-रस-आर्द्रः overflowing with deep devotion towards You, तस्मात् from him (Narada), लेभे obtained, कदाचित् once, खलु indeed, सुकृत-निधिः that reservoir of merit, त्वत्-पिता Your father (Vasudeva), तत्त्व-बोधम् the supreme knowledge of Reality, भक्तानाम् अग्रयायी the foremost among devotees, सः he, च and, खलु indeed, मतिमान् the wise and intelligent, उद्धवः Uddhava, त्वत्त एव from You alone, प्राप्तः obtained, विज्ञान-सारम् the ultimate essence of spiritual wisdom, सः he, किल indeed, जन-हिताय for the welfare of mankind, अधुना now, आस्ते resides, बदर्याम् in Badarikashrama

Simple Meaning: During those days in Dwaraka, Sage Narada—whose heart was constantly overflowing with deep love and devotion for You—resided there for the most part. From him, Your virtuous father Vasudeva, that reservoir of spiritual merit, received the supreme knowledge of divine Truth. Likewise, Uddhava, the wise and foremost among all Your devotees, received the highest essence of spiritual wisdom directly from You. To this day, he resides in Badrikashrama for the welfare and spiritual upliftment of humanity.

सोऽयं कृष्णावतारो जयति तव विभो यत्र सौहार्दभीति-
स्नेहद्वेषानुरागप्रभृतिभिरतुलैरश्रमैर्योगभेदैः ।
आर्तिं तीर्त्वा समस्ताममृतपदमगुस्सर्वतः सर्वलोकाः
स त्वं विश्वार्तिशान्त्यै पवनपुरपते भक्तिपूर्त्यै च भूयाः ॥88.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः अयम् this very, कृष्ण-अवतारः incarnation as Krishna, जयति shines gloriously/triumphs, तव Your, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, यत्र wherein, सौहार्द-भीति-स्नेह-द्वेष-अनुराग-प्रभृतिभिः through various unmatched attitudes such as friendship, fear, affection, hatred, and intense passionate love, अतुलैः through these matchless, अश्रमैः effortless, योग-भेदैः paths of spiritual union, आर्तिम् तीर्त्वा crossing over all afflictions/sorrows, समस्ताम् completely, अमृत-पदम् अगुः reached the state of eternal liberation/immortality, सर्वतः from all directions, सर्व-लोकाः all people, सः त्वम् that very You, विश्व-आर्ति-शान्त्यै for removing all suffering from the world, पवन-पुर-पते O Lord of Guruvayur, भक्ति-पूर्त्यै for the fulfillment of devotion, च and, भूयाः may You shine forth/manifest

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord! Glorious and supreme is this incarnation of Yours as Krishna. In this descent, through matchless and effortless paths of devotion—whether out of friendship, fear, affection, hatred, or intense love—people from all walks of life completely crossed over all worldly afflictions and attained eternal liberation. O Lord of Guruvayur, may that very incarnation of Yours manifest in my heart to eradicate all world suffering and to bring my devotion to absolute perfection!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
