Praise to the Lord in the form of the Vedas

दशकम् ९९

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 99

Praise to the Lord in the form of the Vedas

विष्णोर्वीर्याणि को वा कथयतु धरणेः कश्च रेणून्मिमीते
यस्यैवाङ्घ्रित्रयेण त्रिजगदभिमितं मोदते पूर्णसम्पत्
योसौ विश्वानि धत्ते प्रियमिह परमं धाम तस्याभियायां
त्वद्भक्ता यत्र माद्यन्त्यमृतरसमरन्दस्य यत्र प्रवाहः ॥99.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: विष्णोः of Lord Vishnu, वीर्याणि mighty deeds/exploits, कः who, वा indeed, कथयतु can tell/describe, धरणेः of the earth, कः who, च and, रेणून् the dust particles, मिमीते can count/measure, यस्य of whom, एव alone, अङ्घ्रि-त्रयेण by three steps of His foot, त्रि-जगत् the three worlds, अभिमितम् measured/encompassed, मोदते rejoices, पूर्ण-सम्पत् in full bliss/abundance, यः who, असौ that Lord, विश्वानि all the worlds, धत्ते supports/sustains, प्रियम् dear/beloved, इह here, परमम् supreme, धाम abode, तस्य His, अभियायाम् may I reach/attain, त्वत्-भक्ताः Your devotees, यत्र where, माद्यन्ति rejoice with ecstasy, अमृत-रस-मरन्दस्य of the sweet nectar of divine bliss, यत्र where, प्रवाहः a continuous stream/flow

Simple Meaning: Who indeed can fully describe the mighty deeds of Lord Vishnu, just as who can count all the particles of dust on the earth? By just three steps of His foot, He, who measured the three worlds, bringing them full peace and abundance, He supports all the universes. May I attain His supreme and beloved abode, where Your devotees rejoice in ecstasy and where a continuous stream of divine nectar flows eternally.

आद्यायाशेषकर्त्रे प्रतिनिमिषनवीनाय भर्त्रे विभूतेः
भक्तात्मा विष्णवे यः प्रदिशति हविरादीनि यज्ञार्चनादौ ।
कृष्णाद्यं जन्म यो वा महदिह महतो वर्णयेत्सोऽयमेव
प्रीतः पूर्णो यशोभिस्त्वरितमभिसरेत् प्राप्यमन्ते पदं ते ॥99.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: आद्याय to the Primeval Lord, अशेष-कर्त्रे to the Creator of all, प्रति-निमिष-नवीनाय who appears ever-new at every moment, भर्त्रे to the Lord, विभूतेः of all the opulences, भक्त-आत्मा with a heart full of devotion, विष्णवे to Lord Vishnu, यः who, प्रदिशति offers, हविः-आदीनि oblations and other offerings, यज्ञ-अर्चना-दौ in sacrifices and worship, कृष्ण-आद्यम् starting with Krishna, जन्म birth or incarnations, यः who, वा or, महत् glorious, इह in this world, महतः of the Great Supreme Lord, वर्णयेत् describes, सः he, अयम् this person, एव indeed, प्रीतः pleased, पूर्णः filled, यशोभिः with fame, त्वरितम् swiftly, अभिसरेत् attains, प्राप्यम् the ultimate destination, अन्ते at the end, पदम् abode, ते Your

Simple Meaning: One who with a devoted heart offers oblations and worship in rituals to Lord Vishnu—the Primeval One, the Creator of everything, the Lord who is ever-new at every moment, and the Lord of all opulences—or he who describes in this world the glorious incarnations of the Supreme Being starting with Lord Krishna, becomes completely satisfied and filled with fame. At the end of his life, he swiftly reaches Your supreme supreme abode, the ultimate goal of existence.

हे स्तोतारः कवीन्द्रास्तमिह खलु यथा चेतयध्वे तथैव
व्यक्तं वेदस्य सारं प्रणुवत जननोपात्तलीलाकथाभिः ।
जानन्तश्चास्य नामान्यखिलसुखकराणीति सङ्कीर्तयध्वं
हे विष्णो कीर्तनाद्यैस्तव खलु महतस्तत्त्वबोधं भजेयम् ॥99.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: हे स्तोतारः O hymn chanters, कवीन्द्राः noble poets, तम् Him, इह in this world, खलु indeed, यथा as, चेतयध्वे you understand/comprehend, तथा so, एव alone, व्यक्तम् clearly, वेदस्य of the Veda, सारम् essence, प्रणुवत praise, जनन-उपात्त-लील-कथाभिः through the narratives of His divine playful incarnations, जानन्तः knowing, च and, अस्य His, नामानि names, अखिल-सुख-कराणि bestowers of all happiness, इति thus, सङ्कीर्तयध्वम् sing in praise, हे विष्णो O Vishnu, कीर्तन-आद्यैः through singing praise and other means, तव Your, खलु indeed, महतः of the Great Lord, तत्त्व-बोधम् knowledge of the Supreme Truth, भजेयम् may I attain

Simple Meaning: O noble poets and chanters of hymns! Praise Him in this world according to your understanding, singing the clear essence of the Vedas through tales of His divine playful incarnations. Knowing that His holy names bestow all happiness, sing them with deep devotion. O Lord Vishnu, through singing Your praises and other devotional means, may I attain the knowledge of Your supreme reality.

विष्णोः कर्माणि सम्पश्यत मनसि सदा यैः स धर्मानबध्नाद्
यानीन्द्रस्यैष भृत्यः प्रियसख इव च व्यातनोत् क्षेमकारी ।
वीक्षन्ते योगसिद्धाः परपदमनिशं यस्य सम्यक्प्रकाशं
विप्रेन्द्रा जागरूकाः कृतबहुनुतयो यच्च निर्भासयन्ते ॥99.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: विष्णोः of Lord Vishnu, कर्माणि deeds, सम्पश्यत reflect upon, मनसि in the mind, सदा always, यैः by which, सः He, धर्मान् righteous duties, अबध्नात् established, यानि which, इन्द्रस्य of Indra, एषः this Lord, भृत्यः servant, प्रिय-सखः dear friend, इव like, च and, व्यातनोत् performed, क्षेम-कारी conferring welfare, वीक्षन्ते behold, योग-सिद्धाः perfected yogis, पर-पदम् supreme abode, अनिशम् constantly, यस्य whose, सम्यक्-प्रकाशम् fully illuminated, विप्रेन्द्राः eminent sages, जागरूकाः ever-vigilant, कृत-बहु-नुतयः who offer abundant praises, यत् which, च and, निर्भासयन्ते illumine or declare gloriously

Simple Meaning: Always reflect in your mind upon the mighty deeds of Lord Vishnu, by which He established cosmic order and righteousness. Acting like a humble servant and a dear friend to Indra, He performed auspicious deeds that ensured the protection and welfare of all. Perfected yogis constantly behold His brilliantly illuminated supreme abode, while ever-vigilant and enlightened sages continuously glorify and declare its divine presence.

नो जातो जायमानोऽपि च समधिगतस्त्वन्महिम्नोऽवसानं
देव श्रेयांसि विद्वान् प्रतिमुहुरपि ते नाम शंसामि विष्णो ।
तं त्वां संस्तौमि नानाविधनुतिवचनैरस्य लोकत्रयस्या-
प्यूर्ध्वं विभ्राजमाने विरचितवसतिं तत्र वैकुण्ठलोके ॥99.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: नः among us, जातः anyone born in the past, जायमानः anyone born in the future, अपि even, च and, समधिगतः reached/comprehended, त्वत्-महिम्नः of Your glory, अवसानम् limit/end, देव O Lord, श्रेयांसि as bestowers of ultimate good, विद्वान् knowing, प्रतिमुहुः repeatedly, अपि even, ते Your, नाम names, शंसामि I praise, विष्णो O Vishnu, तम् that, त्वाम् You, संस्तौमि I glorify, नाना-विध-नुति-वचनैः with various kinds of hymns of praise, अस्य of this, लोक-त्रयस्य of the three worlds, अपि even, ऊर्ध्वम् above, विभ्राजमाने shining brilliantly, विरचित-वसतिम् residing, तत्र there, वैकुण्ठ-लोके in the realm of Vaikuntha

Simple Meaning: O Lord Vishnu! No one born in the past, nor anyone to be born in the future, has ever reached the limit of Your supreme glory. Knowing that Your holy names bring ultimate auspiciousness, I repeatedly chant and praise them. I glorify You through various hymns—You who reside in the brilliantly shining realm of Vaikuntha, elevated far above the three worlds!

आपः सृष्ट्यादिजन्याः प्रथममयि विभो गर्भदेशे दधुस्त्वां
यत्र त्वय्येव जीवा जलशयन हरे सङ्गता ऐक्यमापन् ।
तस्याजस्य प्रभो ते विनिहितमभवत् पद्ममेकं हि नाभौ
दिक्पत्रं यत् किलाहुः कनकधरणिभृत् कर्णिकं लोकरूपम् ॥99.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: आपः waters, सृष्टि-आदि-जन्याः created at the beginning of creation, प्रथमम् first, अयि O, विभो All-Pervading Lord, गर्भ-देशे inside the womb or waters, दधुः held, त्वाम् You, यत्र where, त्वयि in You, एव alone, जीवाः individual souls, जल-शयन O Lord sleeping on the waters, हरे O Hari, सङ्गताः merged, ऐक्यम् oneness, आपन् attained, तस्य of that, अजस्य of the Unborn, प्रभो O Lord, ते Your, विनिहितम् placed, अभवत् became, पद्मम् lotus, एकम् one, हि indeed, नाभौ at the navel, दिक्-पत्रम् having the directions as leaves, यत् which, किल indeed, आहुः they say, कनक-धरणि-भृत् Mount Meru, कर्णिकम् as the pericarp or central seed vessel, लोक-रूपम् in the form of the worlds

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord! The primordial waters created at the beginning of creation first held You within their depths. O Lord Hari who sleeps upon the waters, in You all individual souls merged and attained oneness! From the navel of Your unborn form, arose a cosmic lotus whose leaves are the directions and whose central seed vessel is the golden Mount Meru, representing all the worlds.

हे लोका विष्णुरेतद्भुवनमजनयत्तन्न जानीथ यूयं
युष्माकं ह्यन्तरस्थं किमपि तदपरं विद्यते विष्णुरूपम् ।
नीहारप्रख्यमायापरिवृतमनसो मोहिता नामरूपैः
प्राणप्रीत्येकतृप्ताश्चरथ मखपरा हन्त नेच्छा मुकुन्दे ॥99.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: हे लोकाः O people of the world, विष्णुः Lord Vishnu, एतत् this, भुवनम् universe, अजनयत् created, तत् Him, न not, जानीथ do you know, यूयम् you, युष्माकम् within you, हि indeed, अन्तर-स्थम् dwelling inside, किमपि a certain, तत् that, अपरम् distinct, विद्यते exists, विष्णु-रूपम् form of Lord Vishnu, नीहार-प्रख्य-माया-परिवृत-मनसः minds shrouded by mist-like maya/illusion, मोहिताः deluded, नाम-रूपैः by names and forms, प्राण-प्रीति-एक-तृप्ताः satisfied merely with pleasing the senses, चरथ you wander, मख-पराः devoted to rituals, हन्त alas, न no, इच्छा desire, मुकुन्दे for Mukunda

Simple Meaning: O people of the world, Lord Vishnu created this entire universe, yet you do not know Him! Within each of you dwells a distinct divine form of Lord Vishnu as the inner indweller. Shrouded by the mist of illusion and deluded by worldly names and forms, you wander around satisfied merely with sensory pleasures and attached to ritualistic sacrifices. Alas, you have no true desire for Mukunda!

मूर्ध्नामक्ष्णां पदानां वहसि खलु सहस्राणि सम्पूर्य विश्वं
तत्प्रोत्क्रम्यापि तिष्ठन् परिमितविवरे भासि चित्तान्तरेऽपि ।
भूतं भव्यं च सर्वं परपुरुष भवान् किञ्च देहेन्द्रियादिषु
आविष्टोऽप्युद्गतत्वादमृतसुखरसं चानुभुङ्क्षे त्वमेव ॥99.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: मूर्ध्नाम् of heads, अक्ष्णाम् of eyes, पदानाम् of feet, वहसि You possess/bear, खलु indeed, सहस्राणि thousands, सम्पूर्य pervading, विश्वम् the universe, तत् that, प्रोत्क्रम्य exceeding/extending beyond, अपि even, तिष्ठन् standing, परिमित-विवरे in a small space, भासि You shine, चित्त-अन्तरे inside the heart, अपि also, भूतम् the past, भव्यम् the future, च and, सर्वम् all, पर-पुरुष O Supreme Person, भवान् You, किञ्च moreover, देह-इन्द्रिय-आदिषु in the body senses and other adjuncts, आविष्टः entered, अपि although, उद्गतत्वात् because of being transcendent, अमृत-सुख-रसम् the sweet essence of immortal bliss, च and, अनुभुङ्क्षे enjoy, त्वम् You, एव alone

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Being! You possess thousands of heads, eyes, and feet, pervading the entire universe and extending far beyond it. Yet, You shine within the tiny space of the human heart as the inner indweller. You are all that was in the past and all that will be in the future. Moreover, though You have entered into bodies and senses, You remain completely unattached and transcendent, enjoying alone the eternal, sweet essence of supreme bliss!

यत्तु त्रैलोक्यरूपं दधदपि च ततो निर्गतोऽनन्तशुद्ध-
ज्ञानात्मा वर्तसे त्वं तव खलु महिमा सोऽपि तावान् किमन्यत् ।
स्तोकस्ते भाग एवाखिलभुवनतया दृश्यते त्र्यंशकल्पं
भूयिष्ठं सान्द्रमोदात्मकमुपरि ततो भाति तस्मै नमस्ते ॥99.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: यत् which, तु indeed, त्रैलोक्य-रूपम् the form of the three worlds, दधत् bearing or assuming, अपि even though, च and, ततः from that, निर्गतः transcending, अनन्त-शुद्ध-ज्ञान-आत्मा as the embodiment of infinite pure knowledge, वर्तसे You exist, त्वम् You, तव Your, खलु indeed, महिमा glory, सः that, अपि also, तावान् so great, किम अन्यत् what else, स्तोकः small, ते Your, भागः portion, एव alone, अखिल-भुवनतया as the entire cosmos, दृश्यते is seen, त्र्यंश-कल्पम् nearly three-fourths, भूयिष्ठम् greater part, सान्द्र-मोद-आत्मकम् of the nature of intense bliss, उपरि above, ततः beyond that, भाति shines, तस्मै to Him, नमः salutations, ते to You

Simple Meaning: Even while assuming the form of the three worlds, You transcend them all, existing as the embodiment of infinite and pure divine knowledge. Such is Your boundless glory! The entire visible cosmos is but a tiny fraction of Your being, while the vast remainder—nearly three-fourths—shines beyond as the realm of intense, unconditioned bliss. To You, who are that Supreme Being, I offer my humble salutations!

अव्यक्तं ते स्वरूपं दुरधिगमतमं तत्तु शुद्धैकसत्त्वं
व्यक्तं चाप्येतदेव स्फुटममृतरसाम्भोधिकल्लोलतुल्यम् ।
सर्वोत्कृष्टामभीष्टां तदिह गुणरसेनैव चित्तं हरन्तीं
मूर्तिं ते संश्रयेऽहं पवनपुरपते पाहि मां कृष्ण रोगात् ॥99.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: अव्यक्तम् unmanifest, ते Your, स्वरूपम् true nature, दुरधिगम-तमम् most difficult to comprehend, तत् that, तु indeed, शुद्ध-एक-सत्त्वम् pure unalloyed sattva, व्यक्तम् manifest, च and, अपि however, एतत् this, एव alone, स्फुटम् clearly, अमृत-रस-अम्भोधि-कल्लोल-तुल्यम् like waves in an ocean of nectar, सर्व-उत्कृष्टाम् most supreme, अभीष्टाम् most desirable, तत् therefore, इह here, गुण-रसेन् एव by the sweetness of its attributes alone, चित्तम् mind, हरन्तीम् captivating, मूर्तिम् divine form, ते Your, संश्रये I take refuge in, अहम् I, पवनपुर-पते O Lord of Guruvayur, पाहि protect, माम् me, कृष्ण O Krishna, रोगात् from disease

Simple Meaning: Your unmanifest nature, being pure unalloyed sattva, is extremely difficult to comprehend. But this manifest form of Yours (like Krishna) is like the waves rising from an ocean of nectar—supreme, most desirable, and captivating the mind by the sweetness of its attributes. I take refuge in this divine form of Yours, O Lord of Guruvayur! O Krishna, protect me from all my diseases!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
