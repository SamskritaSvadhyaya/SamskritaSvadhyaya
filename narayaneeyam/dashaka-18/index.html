Dashaka 18

The Story of Prithu

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 18

The Story of Prithu

(छन्दस् - प्रहर्षिणी)

जातस्य ध्रुवकुल एव तुङ्गकीर्तेः
अङ्गस्य व्यजनि सुतः स वेननामा ।
यद्दोषव्यथितमतिः स राजवर्यः
त्वत्पादे निहितमना वनं गतोऽभूत् ॥18.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: जातस्य born, ध्रुव-कुले in the family of Dhruva, एव indeed, तुङ्ग-कीर्तेः of high fame, अङ्गस्य of King Anga, व्यजनि was born, सुतः a son, सः he, वेन नामा named Vena, यत्-दोष-व्यथित-मतिः whose mind was deeply pained by his son wicked deeds, सः he, राज-वर्यः that noble king Anga, त्वत्-पादे at Your feet, निहित-मनाः placing his mind, वनम् to the forest, गतः अभूत् went away

Simple Meaning: In the famous lineage of Dhruva, a son named Vena was born to the renowned King Anga. Deeply pained in mind by the wicked deeds of his son Vena, that noble king Anga fixed his mind completely at Your feet and retired to the forest.

पापोऽपि क्षितितलपालनाय वेनः
पौराद्यैरुपनिहितः कठोरवीर्यः ।
सर्वेभ्यो निजबलमेव सम्प्रशंसन्
भूचक्रे तव यजनान्ययं न्यरौत्सीत् ॥18.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: पापः अपि though wicked, क्षिति-तल-पालनाय for protecting the earth, वेनः Vena, पौर-आद्यैः by the citizens and others, उपनिहितः was installed as king, कठोर-वीर्यः of cruel prowess, सर्वेभ्यः to all people, निज-बलम् एव his own power alone, सम्प्रशंसन् praising highly, भूचक्रे on the earth, तव Your, यजनानि sacrifices offered to You, अयम् he, न्यरौत्सीत् stopped/prohibited

Simple Meaning: Even though Vena was wicked and of cruel prowess, he was installed on the throne by the citizens and others for the protection of the earth. Praising his own power above all else, he prohibited all holy sacrifices offered to You on the earth.

सम्प्राप्ते हितकथनाय तापसौघे
मत्तोऽन्यो भुवनपतिर्न कश्चनेति ।
त्वन्निन्दावचनपरो मुनीश्वरैस्तैः
शापाग्नौ शलभदशामनायि वेनः ॥18.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सम्प्राप्ते when they arrived, हित-कथनाय to give beneficial advice, तापस-ओघे a group of sages, मत्तः than me, अन्यः another, भुवन-पतिः lord of the universe, न कश्चन इति there is no one, त्वत्-निन्दा-वचन-परः engaging in words of insult against You, मुनि-ईश्वरैः by those great sages, तैः by them, शाप-अग्नौ in the fire of curse, शलभ-दशाम् to the state of a moth, अनायि was reduced, वेनः Vena

Simple Meaning: When a group of great sages arrived to give him good advice, Vena arrogantly claimed that there was no other lord of the universe superior to him. Engaging in blasphemous words against You, he was destroyed like a moth in the fire of the curse pronounced by those revered sages.

तन्नाशात् खलजनभीरुकैर्मुनीन्द्रैः
तन्मात्रा चिरपरिरक्षिते तदङ्गे ।
त्यक्ताघे परिमथितादथोरुदण्डात्
दोर्दण्डे परिमथिते त्वमाविरासीः ॥18.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्-नाशात् on his destruction, खल-जन-भीरुकैः afraid of wicked people, मुनि-इन्द्रैः by the great sages, तत्-मात्रा by his mother (Sunitha), चिर-परिरक्षिते preserved for a long time, तत्-अङ्गे his body, त्यक्त-अघे freed from sins, परिमथितात् which was churned, अथ then, ऊरु-दण्डात् from the thigh, दोः-दण्डे when the arms were churned, त्वम् You, आविरासीः manifested or appeared

Simple Meaning: Upon Vena's destruction, the great sages became concerned about lawlessness and wicked people arising due to the lack of a ruler. His body had been preserved for a long time by his mother. The sages first churned his thigh, which removed all his sins, and then churned his arms, from which You manifested as King Prithu.

विख्यातः पृथुरिति तापसोपदिष्टैः
सूताद्यैः परिणुतभाविभूरिवीर्यः ।
वेनार्त्या कबलितसम्पदं धरित्रीम्
आक्रान्तां निजधनुषा समामकार्षीः ॥18.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: विख्यातः famed, पृथुः इति as Prithu, तापस-उपदिष्टैः instructed by the sages, सूत-आद्यैः by bards and heralds, परिणुत-भावि-भूरि-वीर्यः whose future immense prowess was praised, वेन-आर्त्या due to the distress caused by Vena, कबलित-सम्पदम् swallowed up her resources, धरित्रीम् the earth, आक्रान्ताम् pursued, निज-धनुषा with your own bow, समाम् अकार्षी You leveled or flattened

Simple Meaning: Renowned as Prithu, You were praised by bards and heralds as instructed by the sages of Your future great prowess. The earth had withdrawn all her resources and crops due to the distress caused by Vena. Pursuing her with Your bow, You leveled the earth to restore her abundance.

भूयस्तां निजकुलमुख्यवत्सयुक्त्यैः
देवाद्यैः समुचितचारुभाजनेषु ।
अन्नादीन्यभिलषितानि यानि तानि
स्वच्छन्दं सुरभितनूमदूदुहस्त्वम् ॥18.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूयः further, ताम् her (the earth), निज-कुल-मुख्य-वत्स-युक्त्यैः with the leader of each group as the calf, देव-आद्यैः by the devas and others, समुचित-चारु-भाजनेषु in suitable beautiful vessels, अन्न-आदीनि food grains and other items, अभिलषितानि desired, यानी तानि whatever they were, स्वच्छन्दम् freely, सुरभि-तनूम् (the earth) in the form of Surabhi (the celestial cow), अदूदुहः त्वम् You milked

Simple Meaning: Further, You made the earth yield like the celestial cow Surabhi. By having the leaders of the devas, sages, and others of each group in the form of calves, in suitable vessels, You allowed them to freely milk from the earth all the food grains and desired treasures they wished for.

आत्मानं यजति मखैस्त्वयि त्रिधामन्
आरब्धे शततमवाजिमेधयागे ।
स्पर्धालुः शतमख एत्य नीचवेषो
हृत्वाऽश्वं तव तनयात् पराजितोऽभूत् ॥18.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: आत्मानम् Yourself, यजति worshiping through, मखैः by sacrifices, त्वयि in You, त्रिधामन् O Lord of three realms, आरब्धे when commenced, शततम-वाजिमेध-यागे the hundredth Ashvamedha sacrifice, स्पर्धालुः envious, शतमखः Indra who performed hundred sacrifices, एत्य coming, नीच-वेषः disguise of a lowly person, हृत्वा having stolen, अश्वम् the sacrificial horse, तव तनयात् from Your son, पराजितः अभूत् was defeated

Simple Meaning: O Lord of three realms, when You commenced Your hundredth Ashvamedha sacrifice to worship Yourself through holy rituals, Indra became jealous. Coming in a disguise of a lowly person, he stole the sacrificial horse, but he was defeated by Your son (Vijitashwa).

देवेन्द्रं मुहुरिति वाजिनं हरन्तं
वह्नौ तं मुनिवरमण्डले जुहूषौ ।
रुन्धाने कमलभवे क्रतोः समाप्तौ
साक्षात्त्वं मधुरिपुमैक्षथाः स्वयं स्वम् ॥18.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: देवेन्द्रम् Indra - the chief of devas, मुहुः repeatedly, इति in this manner, वाजिनम् the sacrificial horse, हरन्तम् stealing, वह्नौ in the fire, तम् him, मुनि-वर-मण्डले when the assembly of great sages, जुहूषौ wished to offer as an oblation, रुन्धाने when stopped, कमलभवे by Brahma born of the lotus, क्रतोः समाप्तौ at the conclusion of the sacrifice, साक्षात् in person, त्वम् You, मधुरिपुम् the enemy of Madhu, ऐक्षथाः saw, स्वयम् स्वम् Your own self

Simple Meaning: When Indra repeatedly stole the sacrificial horse in this manner, the assembly of great sages became furious and prepared to offer Indra himself as an oblation into the sacrificial fire. Brahma intervened and stopped them. At the conclusion of the sacrifice, You saw Your very own original form as Lord Vishnu, the enemy of the asura Madhu, manifested directly in person before You.

तद्दत्तं वरमुपलभ्य भक्तिमेकां
गङ्गान्ते विहितपदः कदापि देव ।
सत्रस्थं मुनिनिवहं हितानि शंसन्
ऐक्षिष्ठाः सनकमुखान् मुनीन् पुरस्तात् ॥18.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्-दत्तम् given by Him, वरम् blessing, उपलभ्य having received, भक्तिम् एकाम् devotion to You alone, गङ्गा-अन्ते on the banks of the Ganges, विहित-पदः residing, कदापि once, देव O Lord, सत्र-स्थम् assembled in a holy gathering, मुनि-निवहम् to the group of sages, हितानि beneficial truths, शंसन् teaching, ऐक्षिष्ठाः You saw, सनक-मुखान् headed by Sanaka, मुनीन् the sages, पुरस्तात् in front of You

Simple Meaning: O Lord, having received the boon of pure devotion from Him, You resided on the banks of the Ganges. Once, while imparting beneficial spiritual teachings to a gathering of sages assembled there, You saw the great sages, headed by Sanaka appear before You.

विज्ञानं सनकमुखोदितं दधानः
स्वात्मानं स्वयमगमो वनान्तसेवी ।
तत्तादृक्पृथुवपुरीश सत्वरं मे
रोगौघं प्रशमय वातगेहवासिन् ॥18.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: विज्ञानम् supreme spiritual wisdom, सनक-मुख-उदितम् imparted by Sanaka and the other sages, दधानः bearing/absorbing, स्व-आत्मानम् Your true self, स्वयम् Himself, अगमः attained, वन-अन्त-सेवी dwelling in the deep forest, तत्-तादृक्-पृथु-वपुः ईश O Lord who took that form of King Prithu, सत्वरम् quickly, मे my, रोग-ओघम् multitude of diseases, प्रशमय relieve/cure, वात-गेह-वासिन् O resident of the abode of the Wind - Guruvayoor

Simple Meaning: Absorbing the divine spiritual wisdom imparted by Sanaka and the other sages, You retired to the deep forest and attained Your true divine nature. O Lord of Guruvayoor, who incarnated in that glorious form of King Prithu, please quickly cure the multitude of diseases afflicting me.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
