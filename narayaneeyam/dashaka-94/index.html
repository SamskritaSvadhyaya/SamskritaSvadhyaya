Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 94

Enlightenment through Bhakti

शुद्धा निष्कामधर्मैः प्रवरगुरुगिरा तत्स्वरूपं परं ते
शुद्धं देहेन्द्रियादिव्यपगतमखिलव्याप्तमावेदयन्ते ।
नानात्वस्थौल्यकार्श्यादि तु गुणजवपुस्सङ्गतोऽध्यासितं ते
वह्नेर्दारुप्रभेदेष्विव महदणुतादीप्तताशान्ततादि ॥94.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: शुद्धाः purified (seekers), निष्काम-धर्मैः through selfless duties/actions performed without desire for rewards, प्रवर-गुरु-गिरा through the sacred words of the noble preceptor, तत्-स्वरूपम् परम् ते that Supreme divine essence/nature of Yours, शुद्धम् absolute/pure, देह-इन्द्रिय-आदि-व्यपगतम् completely free from body, sense organs, and mind, अखिल-व्याप्तम् all-pervading, आवेदयन्ते realize/know, नानात्व-स्थौल्य-कार्श्य-आदि diversity, thickness, thinness, and other such differences, तु but, गुण-ज-वपुः-सङ्गतः अध्यासितम् ते super-imposed upon You due to contact with physical forms born of the Gunas (prakriti), वह्नेः of fire, दारु-प्रभेदेषु इव like in various pieces/shapes of wood, महत्-अणुता-दीप्तता-शान्तता-आदि greatness/largeness, smallness, brightness, dullness/extinction, and so on

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Seekers whose minds have been purified through the selfless performance of duties (nishkama karma) come to realize Your Supreme, unblemished Form through the sacred teachings of a noble preceptor. They know You to be pure, untainted, completely free from the physical body, sense organs, and mind, and all-pervading! The apparent differences such as diversity, largeness, smallness, stoutness, or thinness are merely super-imposed (adhyasita) upon You due to contact with physical forms born of the Gunas. This is just like fire, which is one, but appears large, small, brilliant, or dim depending on the shape and nature of the logs in which it manifests!

आचार्याख्याधरस्थारणिसमनुमिलच्छिष्यरूपोत्तरार-
ण्यावेधोद्भासितेन स्फुटतरपरिबोधाग्निना दह्यमाने ।
कर्मालीवासनातत्कृततनुभुवनभ्रान्तिकान्तारपूरे
दाह्याभावेन विद्याशिखिनि च विरते त्वन्मयी खल्ववस्था ॥94.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: आचार्य-आख्या-धरा-स्थ-अरणि-समनुमिलत्-शिष्य-रूप-उत्तर-अरणि-आवेध-उद्भासितेन (आचार्य-आख्या-धरा-स्थ-अरणि) having the lower fire-stick (arani) known as the Master/Guru, (समनुमिलत्-शिष्य-रूप-उत्तर-अरणि) combining with the upper fire-stick in the form of the disciple, (आवेध-उद्भासितेन) kindled/illuminated by the friction of spiritual instruction and contemplation, स्फुटतर-परिबोध-अग्निना by the brightly blazing fire of direct realization, दह्यमाने being thoroughly burned/consumed, कर्म-आली-वासना-तत्कृत-तनु-भुवन-भ्रान्ति-कान्तार-पूरे (कान्तार-पूरे) in the dense forest-expanse of accumulated (कर्म-आली-वासना) past actions (karmas), latent impressions (vasanas), (तत्कृत-तनु-भुवन-भ्रान्ति) and the illusory manifestations of bodies and worlds generated by them, दाह्य-अभावेन owing to the total absence of anything left to burn, विद्या-शिखिनि च विरते and when that very flame of knowledge subsides/extinguishes itself, त्वत्-मयी खलु अवस्था that ultimate state indeed becomes one with You alone

Simple Meaning: O Lord! When the Guru acts as the lower fire-stick (adhararani) and the disciple as the upper fire-stick (uttararani), the churning friction of spiritual instruction and intense reflection kindles the brilliantly blazing fire of divine Knowledge (jnana). This fire completely consumes the dense, vast forest of accumulated karmas, latent mental impressions (vasanas), and the illusory perceptions of physical bodies and worlds born from them. When everything combustible is thus reduced to ashes, that flame of Knowledge itself naturally subsides, leaving behind only the eternal state of complete absorption in You!

एवं त्वत्प्राप्तितोऽन्यो नहि खलु निखिलक्लेशहानेरुपायो
नैकान्तात्यन्तिकास्ते कृषिवदगदषाड्गुण्यषट्कर्मयोगाः ।
दुर्वैकल्यैरकल्या अपि निगमपथास्तत्फलान्यप्यवाप्ता
मत्तास्त्वां विस्मरन्तः प्रसजति पतने यान्त्यनन्तान् विषादान्॥94.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus/in this manner, त्वत्-प्राप्तितः अन्यः other than attaining You, न हि खलु there is indeed no, अखिल-क्लेश-हानेः for the complete destruction of all afflictions, उपायः remedy/means, न एकान्त-आत्यन्तिकाः are neither absolute nor permanent, ते those (other methods), कृषि-वत् like agriculture, अगद-षैड्गुण्य-षट्कर्म-योगाः medicine, diplomacy/statesmanship (shadgunya), six esoteric/tantric rites (shatkarma), and yogic practices, दुर्-वैकल्यैः owing to severe deficiencies/difficulties in execution, अकल्याः difficult/impossible to accomplish, अपि also, निगम-पथाः the paths of Vedic rituals, तत्-फलानि अपि अवाप्ताः even if their fruits are somehow attained, मत्ताः intoxicated (with pride/pleasure), त्वाम् You, विस्मरन्तः forgetting, प्रसजति पतने when fall (from heavenly realms) inevitably occurs, यान्ति अनन्तान् विषादान् (they) suffer endless/boundless griefs and sorrows

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Thus, there is truly no other way to ensure the complete and permanent destruction of all human afflictions than attaining You! Secular and worldly remedies—such as agriculture, medical treatments, diplomacy, tantric rites, or complex yogic practices—are neither absolute nor everlasting in their relief, much like farming which is subject to uncertainty. Even the elaborate rituals prescribed in the Vedas are extremely hard to perform due to the rigid rules and defects in execution; and even when their heavenly rewards are somehow achieved, people become intoxicated with worldly delights, forget You, and upon the inevitable fall from those elevated realms, plunge into boundless grief and sorrow!

त्वल्लोकादन्यलोकः क्वनु भयरहितो यत् परार्धद्वयान्ते
त्वद्भीतस्सत्यलोकेऽपि न सुखवसतिः पद्मभूः पद्मनाभ ।
एवं भावे त्वधर्मार्जितबहुतमसां का कथा नारकाणां
तन्मे त्वं छिन्धि बन्धं वरद कृपणबन्धो कृपापूरसिन्धो ॥94.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-लोकात् अन्य-लोकः a world/realm other than Your abode (Vaikuntha), क्व-नु where indeed is there, भय-रहितः free from fear, यत् because, परार्ध-द्वय-अन्ते at the end of two parardhas (the lifetime of Brahma), त्वत्-भीतः fearful of You (as Eternal Time), सत्यलोके अपि even in Satyaloka, न सुख-वसतिः there is no peaceful/fearless habitation, पद्म-भूः Lord Brahma (born from the lotus), पद्मनाभ O Lord Padmanabha (Lotus-naveled Lord Vishnu), एवम् भावे such being the case, तु indeed, अधर्म- अर्जित-बहु-तमसाम् for those who have accumulated intense darkness/sin through unrighteous acts, का कथा what to speak of, नारकाणाम् the denizens of hell, तत् therefore, मे my, त्वम् You, छिन्धि sever/cut asunder, बन्धम् bondage, वरद O Bestower of boons, कृपण-बन्धो O Friend of the helpless, कृपा-पूर-सिन्धो O Ocean filled with the flood of mercy

Simple Meaning: O Lord Padmanabha! Is there any realm other than Your divine supreme abode (Vaikuntha) that is completely free from fear? Even Lord Brahma, residing in the highest realm of Satyaloka, lives in fear of You manifesting as Eternal Time at the end of his lifespan of two parardhas, and thus knows no absolute peace or permanence! Such being the plight of even the highest celestial beings, what needs to be said about the miserable fate of sinful souls bound for hell on account of their unrighteous deeds? Therefore, O Bestower of Boons, O Friend of the Helpless, O Ocean overflowing with Mercy, pray sever my bonds of worldly existence!

याथार्थ्यात्त्वन्मयस्यैव हि मम न विभो वस्तुतो बन्धमोक्षौ
मायाविद्यातनुभ्यां तव तु विरचितौ स्वप्नबोधोपमौ तौ ।
बद्धे जीवद्विमुक्तिं गतवति च भिदा तावती तावदेको
भुङ्क्ते देहद्रुमस्थो विषयफलरसान्नापरो निर्व्यथात्मा ॥94.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: याथार्थ्यात् from the standpoint of absolute reality, त्वत्-मयस्य एव हि of one who is indeed a manifestation of You/one with You, मम न not for me, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, वस्तुतः in truth, बन्ध-मोक्षौ bondage and liberation, माया-विद्या-तनुभ्याम् through the medium of Maya (ignorance) and Vidya (knowledge), तव तु by You indeed, विरचितौ created/orchestrated, तौ those two, स्वप्न-बोध-उपमौ comparable to the state of dream and waking, बद्धे between the bound soul, जीवत्-विमुक्तिम् गतवति च and one who has attained jivanmukti (liberation while living), भिदा difference, तावती तावत् is only this much, एकः one (the bound soul), देह-द्रुम-स्थः residing on the tree of the physical body, भुङ्क्ते enjoys/consumes, विषय-फल-रसान् the tastes/juices of the fruits of sense-objects, न अपरः while the other does not, निर्व्यथात्मा remaining as the serene/unafflicted Self

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord! In ultimate reality, since I am essentially non-different from You, there is actually no such thing as true bondage or liberation for me! They are merely illusory states projected by You through Your dual powers of Maya (ignorance) and Vidya (knowledge), exactly like the experiences of dream and waking. The only distinction between a bound soul (baddha) and a living-liberated soul (jivanmukta) is this: both reside on the same tree of the physical body, but the bound soul compulsively tastes the bittersweet fruits of sense-pleasures, whereas the jivanmukta remains an unaffected, serene witness, untouched by sorrow or attachment!

जीवन्मुक्तत्वमेवंविधमिति वचसा किं फलं दूरदूरे
तन्नामाशुद्धबुद्धेर्न च लघु मनसश्शोधनं भक्तितोऽन्यत् ।
तन्मे विष्णो कृषीष्ठास्त्वयि कृतसकलप्रार्पणं भक्तिभारं
येन स्यां मङ्क्षु किञ्चिद् गुरुवचनमिलत्त्वत्प्रबोधस्त्वदात्मा ॥94.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: जीवन्मुक्तत्वम् एवम् विधम् इति that living-liberation (jivanmukti) is of such a nature, वचसा with mere words, किम् फलम् what is the use/benefit, दूरदूरे is very far/remote indeed, तत्-नाम that state itself, विशुद्ध-बुद्धेः for one whose intellect is not pure, न च लघु and there is no easy, मनसः शोधनम् purification of the mind, भक्तितः अन्यत् other than devotion (bhakti), तत् therefore, मे for me, विष्णो O All-Pervading Lord Vishnu, कृषीष्ठाः pray bestow/grant, त्वयि unto You, कृत-सकल-प्रार्पणम् with all actions and self completely surrendered, भक्ति-भारम् an abundance of intense devotion, येन by which, स्याम् I may become, मङ्क्षु quickly, किञ्चित्-गुरु-वचन-मिलत् त्वत्-प्रबोधः gaining divine realization of You aided slightly by the Preceptor's words, त्वत्-आत्मा one with Your Supreme Self

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord Vishnu! What is the use of mere words and verbal descriptions about the nature of living-liberation (jivanmukti)? For one whose mind is not yet completely purified, that exalted state remains unimaginably far away! And there is truly no easy or direct means to purify the mind other than unswerving devotion (bhakti). Therefore, O Lord, pray bestow upon me an intense, overflowing devotion wherein all my actions and very self are totally surrendered unto You—so that, blessed slightly by the words of the Guru, I may swiftly awaken to Your direct realization and become one with You!

शब्द्ब्रह्मण्यपीह प्रयतितमनसस्त्वां न जानन्ति केचित्
कष्टं वन्ध्यश्रमास्ते चिरतरमिह गां बिभ्रते निष्प्रसूतिम् ।
यस्यां विश्वाभिरामास्सकलमलहरा दिव्यलीलावताराः
सच्चित्सान्द्रं च रूपं तव न निगदितं तां न वाचं भ्रियासम् ॥94.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: शब्द-ब्रह्मणि अपि इह even in the verbal Brahman (Vedic lore/scriptural scholarship) in this world, प्रयतित-मनसः those who exert their minds continuously, त्वाम् You, न जानन्ति do not realize/know, केचित् some people, कष्टम् alas, how tragic, वन्ध्य-श्रमाः ते their efforts are completely futile/barren, चिरतरम् इह for a very long time in this world, गाम् a cow, बिभ्रते (they) maintain/nourish, निष्प्रसूतिम् barren/incapable of giving milk, यस्याम् in which (speech/words), विश्व-अभिरामाः enchanting to the entire universe, सकल-मल-हराः destroying all sins and impurities, दिव्य-लीला-अवताराः Your divine sports and incarnations, सत्-चित्-सान्द्रम् च रूपम् and Your nature of dense Existence and Consciousness, तव Yours, न निगदितम् is not glorified/described, ताम् न वाचम् भ्रियासम् may I never utter or possess such speech

Simple Meaning: How tragic it is, O Lord! There are scholars who dedicate their entire lives to studying the vast oceans of Vedic lore and scriptural scholarship (shabda brahman), yet fail to realize You, the Supreme Goal! Their intense labors are utterly futile, exactly like painstakingly maintaining and feeding a barren cow that yields no milk! May I never indulge in or possess such speech which fails to sing of Your enchanting, sin-destroying divine incarnations and Your supreme form of dense Existence and Consciousness (sat-chit-sandra)!

यो यावान् यादृशो वा त्वमिति किमपि नैवावगच्छामि भूमन्
न एवञ्चानन्यभावस्त्वदनुभजनमेवाद्रिये चैद्यवैरिन् ।
त्वल्लिङ्गानां त्वदङ्घ्रिप्रियजनसदसां दर्शनस्पर्शनादिः
भूयान्मे त्वत्प्रपूजानतिनुतिगुणकर्मानुकीर्त्यादरोऽपि ॥94.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः who, यावान् how great/immense, यादृशः वा or of what precise nature, त्वम् You are, इति thus, किमपि न एव अवगच्छामि I do not understand or comprehend at all, भूमन् O Infinite Supreme Lord, न एव च and not, अनन्यभावः single-pointed focus/devotion, त्वत्-अनुभजनम् एव worshipping and serving You alone, आदिये I value/cherish, चैद्य-वैरिन् O Enemy of Chedi (Shishupala) - Krishna, त्वत्-लिङ्गानाम् of Your divine forms and idols, त्वत्-अङ्घ्रि-प्रिय-जन-सदसाम् of the assemblies of devotees beloved to Your lotus feet, दर्शन-स्पर्शन-आदिः beholding, touching, and serving them, भूयात् मे may there be unto me, त्वत्-प्रपूजा-नति-नुति-गुण-कर्म-अनुकीर्ति-आदरः अपि deep reverence and delight in worshipping You, bowing before You, chanting Your praises, and constantly singing of Your divine attributes and deeds

Simple Meaning: O Infinite Supreme Being! O Krishna, the enemy of Shishupala! I cannot fully comprehend who You are, how vast You are, or what Your true, ultimate nature is and also lack single-pointed, wholehearted devotion unto You. May I be blessed with the opportunity to behold and touch Your sacred forms and to revere assemblies of Your beloved devotees. And may I always possess unbounded reverence and joy in worshipping You, bowing down before You, singing Your glories, and constantly recounting Your divine virtues and majestic deeds!

यद्यल्लभ्येत तत्तत्तव समुपहृतं देव दासोऽस्मि तेऽहं
त्वद्गेहोन्मार्जनाद्यं भवतु मम मुहुः कर्म निर्मायमेव ।
सूर्याग्निब्राह्मणात्मादिषु लसितचतुर्बाहुमाराधये त्वां
त्वत्प्रेमार्द्रत्वरूपो मम सततमभिष्यन्दतां भक्तियोगः॥94.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: यत् यत् whatever, लभ्येत may be obtained, तत् तत् that and that, तव unto You, समुपहृतम् is offered, देव O Lord, दासः servant, अस्मि I am, ते Your, अहम् I, त्वत्-गेह-उन्मार्जन-आद्यम् sweeping and cleaning Your temple and such service, भवतु may it be, मम my, मुहुः repeatedly, कर्म action or service, निर्मायम् एव without any deceit or pride, सूर्य-अग्नि-ब्राह्मण-आत्मा-आदिषु in the sun, fire, brahmanas, and in one's own self and other abodes, लसित-चतुर्-बाहुम् shining with four arms, आराधये I worship, त्वाम् You, त्वत्-प्रेम-आर्द्रात्व-रूपः consisting of the tender state of love for You, मम my, सततम् constantly, अभिष्यन्दताम् may flow uninterruptedly, भक्ति-योगः the path of devotional service

Simple Meaning: O Lord! I am Your humble servant, and whatever I obtain, I offer entirely unto You. May I repeatedly engage in acts of service such as sweeping and cleaning Your temple with complete sincerity and freedom from pride. May I worship You, shining with Your magnificent four-armed form, present within the sun, the sacrificial fire, the brahmanas, and inside my own self. And may my devotional service, filled with the tenderness of pure love for You, flow constantly and uninterruptedly forever!

ऐक्यं ते दानहोमव्रतनियमतपस्सांख्ययोगैर्दुरापं
त्वत्सङ्गेनैव गोप्यः किल सुकृतितमा प्रापुरानन्दसान्द्रम् ।
भक्तेष्वन्येषु भूयस्स्वपि बहुमनुषे भक्तिमेव त्वमासां
तन्मे त्वद्भक्तिमेव द्रढय हर गदान् कृष्ण वातालयेश ॥94.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऐक्यम् union or oneness with You, ते Your, दान-होम-व्रत-नियम-तपस्-सांख्य-योगैः through charity, fire rituals, vows, strict observances, penances, the path of knowledge, and yogic practices, दुरापम् difficult to attain, त्वत्-सङ्गेन एव through Your association alone, गोप्यः the gopis, किल indeed, सुकृतितमाः most fortunate/virtuous, प्रापुः attained, आनन्द-सान्द्रम् dense and supreme bliss, भक्तेषु among devotees, अन्येषु other, भूयस्सु अपि even though many, बहुमनुषे You value/treasure highly, भक्तिम् एव devotion alone, त्वम् You, आसाम् of these gopis, तत् therefore, मे my, त्वत्-भक्तिम् एव devotional service unto You alone, द्रढय strengthen, हर remove/dispel, गदान् ailments/diseases, कृष्ण O Krishna, वातालय-ईश O Lord of Guruvayur

Simple Meaning: O Krishna! O Lord of Guruvayur! Union with You is exceedingly difficult to attain through charity, fire sacrifices, religious vows, strict spiritual disciplines, severe penances, intellectual knowledge, or yogic exercises. Yet, the most blessed gopis attained the dense bliss of complete oneness with You through Your association alone. Though there are countless other devotees, You treasure the pure devotion of these gopis above all else. Therefore, please strengthen my devotion unto You and remove all my afflictions and diseases!
