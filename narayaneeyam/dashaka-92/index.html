Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 92

Bhakti in Kaliyuga

वेदैस्सर्वाणि कर्माण्यफलपरतया वर्णितानीति बुध्वा
तानि त्वय्यर्पितान्येव हि समनुचरन् यानि नैष्कर्म्यमीश ।
मा भूद्वेदैर्निषिद्धे कुहचिदपि मनःकर्मवाचां प्रवृत्तिः
दुर्वर्जं चेदवाप्तं तदपि खलु भवत्यर्पये चित्प्रकाशे ॥92.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: वेदैः by the Vedas, सर्वाणि all, कर्माणि prescribed duties/actions, अफल-परतया as being without desire for fruits/rewards (action performed with detachment), वर्णितानि इति are described, बुध्वा having understood/realized thus, तानि those (duties), त्वयि in You, अर्पितानि एव surrendering/offering alone, हि indeed, समनुचरन् executing/performing meticulously, यानि which (path/actions), नैष्कर्म्यम् state of actionlessness/freedom from karmic bondages (naishkarmya), ईश O Lord, मा भूत् may there not be, वेदैः by the Vedas, निषिद्धे in prohibited actions, कुहचित् अपि anywhere/at any time, मनः कर्म वाचाम् of mind, body, and speech, प्रवृत्तिः inclination/engagement, दुर्वर्जम् चेत् if unavoidable, अवाप्तम् happens to occur, तत्-अपि that too, खलु indeed, भवति अर्पये I offer/surrender unto You, चित्-प्रकाशे O Pure Consciousness/Light of Knowledge

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Lord! Realizing that all karmas (duties) prescribed by the Vedas are ultimately intended to be performed without desire for their material fruits, one who offers all such actions unto You attains the supreme state of actionlessness (naishkarmya). May my mind, speech, and body never engage in actions prohibited by the Vedas! However, if any prohibited or sinful action happens to occur accidentally or unavoidably, I surrender even that unto You, O Lord who shines as Pure Consciousness!

यस्त्वन्यः कर्मयोगस्तव भजनमयस्तत्र चाभीष्टमूर्तिं
हृद्यां सत्त्वैकरूपां दृषदि हृदि मृदि क्वापि वा भावयित्वा ।
पुष्पैर्गन्धैर्निवेद्यैरपि च विरचितैः शक्तितो भक्तिपूतैः
नित्यं वर्यां सपर्यां विदधदयि विभो त्वत्प्रसादं भजेयम् ॥92.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः तु अन्यः whereas that other, कर्म-योगः path of action/discipline of duties, तव भजन-मयः which is of the nature of Your devotional worship, तत्र in that path, च and, अभीष्ट-मूर्तिम् the beloved divine form of one's choice (Ishta-devata), हृद्याम् charming/captivating to the heart, सत्त्व-एक-रूपाम् of the nature of unalloyed purity (sattva-guna), दृषदि in a stone image/idol, हृदि in the heart, मृदि in an earthen image, क्वापि वा or anywhere else, भावयित्वा having meditated upon/envisioned, पुष्पैः with flowers, गन्धैः with sandal paste/fragrances, निवेद्यैः अपि and with food offerings (naivedya) as well, च and, विरचितैः arranged/prepared, शक्तितः according to one's capacity, भक्ति-पूतैः purified by intense devotion, नित्यम् daily/constantly, वर्याम् supreme/exalted, सपर्याम् worship/services, विदधत् performing, अयि विभो O All-Pervading Lord, त्वत्-प्रसादम् Your supreme grace, भजेयम् may I attain/enjoy

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord! On the other hand, there is the devotional path of action (Bhagavata-Karma-Yoga), wherein one envisions Your exceedingly charming form of pure sattva in a stone deity, within the heart, in an earthen image, or wherever one prefers. By offering flowers, sandal paste, and food offerings prepared according to one's capacity and purified through unswerving devotion, and by daily performing this noble worship, may I constantly enjoy Your divine grace!

स्त्रीशूद्रास्त्वत्कथादिश्रवणविरहिता आसतां ते दयार्हाः
त्वत्पादासन्नयातान् द्विजकुलजनुषो हन्त शोचाम्यशान्तान् ।
वृत्त्यर्थं ते यजन्तो बहुकथितमपि त्वामनाकर्णयन्तो
दृप्ता विद्याभिजात्यैः किमु न विदधते तादृशं मा कृथा माम् ॥92.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्त्री-शूद्राः women and people of lower class, त्वत्-कथा-आदि-श्रवण-विरहिताः being deprived of hearing Your divine stories and holy names, आसताम् let them be, ते दया-आर्हाः they are indeed worthy of compassion, त्वत्-पाद-आसन्न-यातान् who have reached the close proximity of Your lotus feet (through their high birth and privilege), द्विज-कुल-जनुषः those born in the noble families of the twice-born (brahmanas, kshatriyas, vaishyas), हन्त alas, शोचामि I deeply pity, अशान्तान् who are utterly devoid of peace/restless, वृत्त्यर्थम् solely for the sake of earning a livelihood, ते they, यजन्तः performing elaborate sacrifices/rituals, बहु-कथितम् अपि though Your divine glories are copiously declared in the scriptures, त्वाम् You, अनाकर्णयन्तः refusing to listen to/ignoring, दृप्ताः puffed up with pride, विद्या-अभिजात्यैः due to learning and noble lineage, किमु न विदधते what senseless deeds do they not commit, तादृशम् such a state, मा कृथाः माम् please do not make me

Simple Meaning: Women and people of lower class, are deprived of the opportunity to hear Your divine stories and glories are truly deserving of compassion. But alas! I deeply pity those born into noble, twice-born families who have come so close to Your lotus feet by virtue of their birth and privileges, yet remain devoid of peace! Performing elaborate rituals merely as a trade for earning a livelihood, ignoring You despite Your glories being proclaimed throughout the scriptures, and puffed up with pride over their erudition and noble lineage—what folly do they not commit? O Lord, I pray, never let me fall into such a wretched state!

पापोऽयं कृष्णरामेत्यभिलपति निजं गूहितुं दुश्चरित्रं
निर्लज्जस्यास्य वाचा बहुतरकथनीयानि मे विघ्नितानि ।
भ्राता मे वन्ध्यशीलो भजति किल सदा विष्णुमित्थं बुधांस्ते
निन्दन्त्युच्चैर्हसन्ति त्वयि निहितमतींस्तादृशं मा कृथा माम् ॥92.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: पापः अयम् this sinner, कृष्ण राम इति "Krishna", "Rama" thus, अभिलपति repeatedly utters/chants, निजम् his own, गूहितुम् to conceal, दुश्चरित्रम् bad conduct/wicked deeds, निर्लज्जस्य अस्य of this shameless person, वाचा by the talk/interruption, बहुतर-कथनीयानि many things that ought to be spoken, मे of mine, विघ्नितानि have been disrupted/hindered, भ्राता मे my brother, वन्ध्य-शीलः of useless/futile character, भजति worships, किल indeed, सदा constantly, विष्णुम् Lord Vishnu, इत्थम् in this manner, बुधान् those who deem themselves wise, ते they, निन्दन्ति slander/scoff at, उच्चैः हसन्ति laugh loudly in derision, त्वयि-निहित-मतीन् those who have placed their minds firmly in You, तादृशम् such a state, मा कृथाः माम् please do not make me

Simple Meaning: "This sinner continuously chants 'Krishna, Rama' merely to conceal his wicked deeds!” “Because of this shameless fool's interruptions, my important discussions have been disrupted.” “My brother, devoid of all sense, constantly worships Vishnu!" In this manner, worldly people who pride themselves on their intelligence, slander and mock those who have surrendered their minds unto You. O Supreme Lord, I pray, never let me fall into such a hypocritical or arrogant state!

श्वेतच्छायं कृते त्वां मुनिवरवपुषं प्रीणयन्ते तपोभिः
त्रेतायां स्रुक्स्रुवाद्यङ्कितमरुणतनुं यज्ञरूपं यजन्ते ।
सेवन्ते तन्त्रमार्गैर्विलसदरिगदं द्वापरे श्यामलाङ्गं
नीलं सङ्कीर्तनाद्यैरिह कलिसमये मानुषास्त्वां भजन्ते ॥92.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्वेत्-छायम् of white complexioned form, कृते in the Satya-yuga (Krita-yuga), त्वाम् You, मुनि-वर-वपुषम् wearing the form/appearance of an exalted ascetic/sage, प्रीणयन्ते (people) satisfy/please, तपोभिः through penances and austerities, त्रेतायाम् in the Treta-yuga, स्रुक्-स्रुव-आदि-अङ्कितम् bearing/holding the sacrificial wooden ladles (sruk and sruva) and other sacrificial implements, अरुण-तनुम् having a reddish-golden complexioned form, यज्ञ-रूपम् as the embodiment of Yajna (sacrificial worship), यजन्ते (they) worship You through sacrifices, सेवन्ते (they) serve/worship, तन्त्र-मार्गैः through the methods of Tantras, द्वापरे in the Dvapara-yuga, विलसत्-अरि-गदम् adorned with the shining discus (Sudarshana) and mace (Kaumodaki), श्यामल-अङ्गम् having a dark complexioned form, नीलम् (having) a blue hue, सङ्कीर्तन-आद्यैः through congregational chanting of Your divine names and glories (sankirtana), इह here, कलि-समये in the age of Kali, मानुषाः human beings, त्वाम् You, भजन्ते worship/serve

Simple Meaning: In Satya-yuga, people worship You through intense penances in Your white-complexioned form as an exalted ascetic. In Treta-yuga, they worship You through Vedic sacrifices as the reddish-hued embodiment of Yajna, holding sacrificial ladles. In Dvapara-yuga, they serve Your dark form holding the shining discus and mace according to Tantric methods. And here in the age of Kali, human beings worship You, who assume a blue hue, primarily through the congregational chanting of Your divine names and glories (sankirtana)!

सोऽयं कालेयकालो जयति मुररिपो यत्र सङ्कीर्तनाद्यैः
र्निर्यत्नैरेव मार्गैरखिलद न चिरात्त्वत्प्रसादं भजन्ते ।
जातास्त्रेताकृतादावपि हि किल कलौ सम्भवं कामयन्ते
दैवात्तत्रैव जातान् विषयविषरसैर्मा विभो वञ्चयास्मान् ॥92.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः अयम् that very, कालेय-कालः dark age of Kali, जयति is glorious/triumphant, मुर-रिपो O Enemy of Mura, यत्र in which (age), सङ्कीर्तन-आद्यैः through congregation chanting and easy devotional paths, निर्यत्नैः एव through effortless methods indeed, मार्गैः through ways, अखिल-द O Bestower of everything, न चिरात् without delay/very quickly, त्वत्-प्रसादम् Your divine grace, भजन्ते (people) attain, जाताः those born, त्रेता-कृत-आदौ अपि even in Treta, Satya (Krita), and other Yugas, हि किल indeed, कलौ in the Kali-yuga, सम्भवम् birth, कामयन्ते desire/pray for, दैवात् by good fortune/providence, तत्र एव in that very Kali-yuga, जातान् born, विषय-विष-रसैः by the poisonous pleasures of sense-objects, मा do not, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, वञ्चय अस्मान् deceive/beguile us

Simple Meaning: O Lord Krishna, Enemy of Mura! That very age of Kali is truly glorious, for in this age, O Bestower of all blessings, people effortlessly attain Your divine grace in a very short time through simple paths like the congregational chanting of Your holy names! Indeed, those born even in the golden ages of Satya and Treta desire to take birth in Kali-yuga for this reason. Having been born by great fortune in this very age of Kali, O All-Pervading Lord, pray do not let us be beguiled by the poisonous delights of worldly sense-pleasures!

भक्तास्तावत्कलौ स्युर्द्रमिलभुवि ततो भूरिशस्तत्र चोच्चैः
कावेरीं ताम्रपर्णीमनु किल कृतमालां च पुण्यां प्रतीचीम् ।
हा मामप्येतदन्तर्भवमपि च विभो किञ्चिदञ्चद्रसं त्वयि
आशापाशैर्निबध्य भ्रमय न भगवन् पूरय त्वन्निषेवाम् ॥92.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: भक्ताः तावत् devotees indeed, कलौ in the age of Kali, स्युः will be born/will abound, द्रमिल-भुवि in the land of Dravida (South India), ततः among those places, भूरिशः in large numbers, तत्र च उच्चैः and especially/in great abundance there, कावेरीम् along the banks of the Kaveri, ताम्रपर्णीम् अनु along the Tamraparni, किल indeed, कृतमालाम् च and the Kritamala (Vaigai), पुण्याम् प्रतीचीम् and the holy Pratichi (Pamba/Nila), हा alas, माम् अपि me too, एतत्-अन्तर्भवम् अपि च born within this very sacred land, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, किञ्चित् अञ्चत् रसम् त्वयि possessing at least a tiny spark of devotion/taste toward You, आशा-पाशैः निबध्य binding with the strong ropes of worldly desires, भ्रमय न do not make me wander helplessly, भगवन् O Supreme Lord, पूरय त्वत्-निषेवाम् fulfill and make complete my continuous service/devotion unto You

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord! It is declared in the scriptures that in the Kali Yuga, great devotees of Yours will be born in vast numbers in the Dravida region—especially along the holy banks of rivers such as Kaveri, Tamraparni, Kritamala, and the sacred western region. Alas! Though I too am fortunate enough to be born in this very land and possess at least a modest inclination of love toward You, pray do not leave me bound by the agonizing ropes of worldly desires to wander endlessly in samsara! O Supreme Master, complete my spiritual path and fulfill my ardent desire to be in Your eternal service!

दृष्ट्वा धर्मद्रुहं तं कलिमपकरुणं प्राङ्महीक्षित् परीक्षित्
हन्तुं व्याकृष्टखड्गोऽपि न विनिहतवान् सारवेदी गुणांशात् ।
त्वत्सेवाद्याशु सिद्ध्येदसदिह न तथा त्वत्परे चैष भीरुः
यत्तु प्रागेव रोगादिभिरपहरते तत्र हा शिक्षयैनम् ॥92.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: दृष्ट्वा having seen, धर्म-द्रुहम् the hater/persecutor of Dharma, तम् कलिम् that (personification of) Kali, अपकरुणम् merciless/cruel, प्राक् महीक्षित् परीक्षित् king Parikshit, the former ruler of the earth, हन्तुम् to slay, व्याकृष्ट-खड्गः अपि though having drawn his sword, न विनिहतवान् did not kill him, सार-वेदी who knows the true essence of things, गुण-अंशात् because of Kali's single redeeming merit, त्वत्-सेवा-आदि आशु सिद्ध्येत् (that in Kali Yuga) divine service and devotion unto You easily and quickly yield spiritual fruition, असत् इह न तथा evil/sinful intentions in this age do not bring immediate ill consequences, त्वत्-परे च एषः भीरुः and towards Your dedicated devotees this Kali is afraid, यत् तु प्राक् एव रोग-आदिभिः अपहरते because  he deprives us beforehand through diseases and afflictions, तत्र हा in that matter, alas, शिक्षय एनम् please discipline/restrain him

Simple Meaning: Long ago, when King Parikshit saw merciless Kali tormenting Dharma, he drew his sword to slay him. Yet, being a true knower of essence, he spared Kali's life upon recognizing his great redeeming virtue: in the age of Kali, devotion and service unto You swiftly yield spiritual success, sinful thoughts do not bear fruit unless committed in action. Kali remains terrified of Your devotees, because to keep us under his control, alas, Kali deprives us of devotional opportunities towards You, by afflicting us beforehand with diseases and worldly troubles. O Lord, I pray, discipline and restrain him in this regard!

गङ्गा गीता च गायत्र्यपि च तुलसिका गोपिकाचन्दनं तत्
सालग्रामाभिपूजा परपुरुष तथैकादशी नामवर्णाः ।
एतान्यष्टाप्ययत्नान्यपि कलिसमये त्वत्प्रसादप्रवृद्ध्या
क्षिप्रं मुक्तिप्रदानीत्यभिदधुः ऋषयस्तेषु मां सज्जयेथाः ॥92.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: गङ्गा the holy river Ganga, गीता च and the Bhagavad Gita, गायत्री अपि च as well as the sacred Gayatri mantra, तुलसिका the holy Tulasi plant, गोपिका-चन्दनम् तत् and that Gopichandana (sacred clay), सालग्राम-अभिपूजा worship of the Salagrama stone, परपुरुष O Supreme Being, तथा and, एकादशी the Ekadashi vow/fast, नाम-वर्णाः the letters/syllables of Your holy names, एतानि अष्ट अपि these eight simple practices as well, कलि समये in the age of Kali, त्वत्-प्रसाद-प्रवृद्ध्या through the boundless growth of Your grace, क्षिप्रम् swiftly, मुक्ति-प्रदानि इति grant liberation, अभिदधुः ऋषयः thus declare the ancient sages, तेषु in these (eight simple practices), माम् me, सज्जयेथाः please engage/attach firmly

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Being! The ancient sages declare that even in this age of Kali, these eight simple practices—the sacred Ganges, the Bhagavad Gita, the Gayatri mantra, the holy Tulasi plant, Gopichandana, the worship of the Salagrama stone, the observance of the Ekadashi fast, and the chanting of the syllables of Your divine names—swiftly bestow liberation by augmenting Your supreme grace. O Lord, please keep me steadfastly devoted and engaged in these eight holy practices!

देवर्षीणां पितृणामपि न पुनः ऋणी किङ्करो वा स भूमन् ।
योऽसौ सर्वात्मना त्वां शरणमुपगतस्सर्वकृत्यानि हित्वा ।
तस्योत्पन्नं विकर्माप्यखिलमपनुदस्येव चित्तस्थितस्त्वं
तन्मे पापोत्थतापान् पवनपुरपते रुन्धि भक्तिं प्रणीयाः ॥92.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: देव-ऋषीणाम् to the devas and sages, पितृणाम् अपि and to the pitris (ancestors) as well, न पुनः is no longer, ऋणी a debtor, किङ्करः वा or a bound servant, सः he, भूमन् O Infinite Supreme Lord, यः असौ who, सर्वात्मना with wholehearted/unreserved devotion, त्वाम् You, शरणम् उपगतः has taken refuge in, सर्व-कृत्यानि हित्वा renouncing all obligatory duties/rites, तस्य of such a surrendered soul, उत्पन्नम् that which has arisen, विकर्म अपि even a sinful or prohibited act (committed unintentionally), अखिलम् entirely, अपनुदसि एव You completely wipe away/dispel, चित्त-स्थितः त्वम् residing as You do in his heart, तत् therefore, मे my, पाप-उत्थ-तापान् suffering and afflictions born of past sins, पवनपुर-पते O Lord of Guruvayur, रुन्धि eliminate/suppress, भक्तिम् प्रणीयाः and bestow upon me unswerving devotion

Simple Meaning: O Infinite Supreme Lord! One who renounces all worldly and ritualistic obligations to surrender whole-heartedly and unconditionally to You alone is no longer indebted or bound as a servant to devas, sages, or ancestors. Residing continuously within his heart, You completely wipe away even any accidental sin he might commit. Therefore, O Lord of Guruvayur, pray eliminate all my sufferings arising from past sins and bestow upon me pure, unswerving devotion unto Your lotus feet!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
