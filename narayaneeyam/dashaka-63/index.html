Dashaka 63

Krishna lifts Govardhana

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 63

Krishna lifts Govardhana

ददृशिरे किल तत्क्षणमक्षतः तनितजृम्भितकम्पितदिक्तटाः ।
सुषमया भवदङ्गतुलां गता व्रजपदोपरि वारिधरास्त्वया ॥63.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: ददृशिरे were seen, किल indeed, तत्-क्षणम् at that moment, अक्षतः fully gathered, तनित-जृम्भित-कम्पित-दिक्-तटाः making the quarters shake with expanding thunder, सुषमया in beauty, भवत्-अङ्ग-तुलाम् resemblance to Your dark body, गताः attaining, व्रज-पद-उपरि over the region of Vraja, वारि-धराः the rain-bearing clouds, त्वया by You

Simple Meaning: At that very moment, dense dark rain-bearing clouds that resembled Your own bodily hue, gathered over the region of Vraja, making all directions tremble with the roar of their expanding thunder. They were observed by You.

विपुलकरकमिश्रैस्तोयधारानिपातैः दिशिदिशि पशुपानां मण्डले दण्ड्यमाने ।
कुपितहरिकृतान्नः पाहि पाहीति तेषां वचनमजित श्रृण्वन् मा बिभीतेत्यभाणीः ॥63.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: विपुल-करक-मिश्रैः mixed with large hailstones, तोय-धारा-निपातैः by torrential downpours of water streams, दिशि दिशि in every direction, पशुपानाम् of the cowherds, मण्डले the entire community, दण्ड्यमाने being tormented, कुपित-हरि-कृतात् caused by enraged Indra, नः us, पाहि पाहि protect protect, इति thus, तेषाम् their, वचनम् words, अजित O Unconquerable Lord, श्रृण्वन् hearing, मा बिभीत do not fear, इति thus, अभाणीः You spoke

Simple Meaning: O Unconquerable Lord, when torrential downpours mixed with huge hailstones began pounding in every direction, tormenting the entire community of cowherds, they cried out, Save us! Save us from this destruction caused by the enraged Indra! Hearing their desperate cries, You reassured them saying, Do not fear.

कुल इह खलु गोत्रो दैवतं गोत्रशत्रोर्विहतिमिह स रुन्ध्यात् को नु वः संशयोऽस्मिन् ।
इति सहसितवादी देव गोवर्धनाद्रिं त्वरितमुदमुमूलो मूलतो बालदोर्भ्याम् ॥63.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: कुले in our clan, इह here, खलु indeed, गोत्रः the mountain, दैवतम् divinity, गोत्र-शत्रोः of the enemy of mountains Indra, विहतिम् इह the obstacle or destruction here, सः that mountain, रुन्ध्यात् will obstruct or ward off, कः what, नु indeed, वः your, संशयः doubt, अस्मिन् in this, इति thus, स-हसित-वादी speaking with a smile, देव O Lord, गोवर्धन-अद्रिम Mountain Govardhana, त्वरितम् quickly, उदमुमूलः uprooted, मूलतः from its base, बाल-दोर्भ्याम् with Your young child-like arms

Simple Meaning: O Lord, speaking with a gentle smile, You said, Indeed, this mountain is our clan deity. It will surely ward off any destruction caused by Indra. What doubt can you possibly have in this? Saying so, You swiftly uprooted the entire Govardhana mountain from its base using Your tender young arms.

तदनु गिरिवरस्य प्रोद्धृतस्यास्य तावत् सिकतिलमृदुदेशे दूरतो वारितापे ।
परिकरपरिमिश्रान् धेनुगोपानधस्तात् उपनिदधदधत्था हस्तपद्मेन शैलम् ॥63.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदनु thereafter, गिरिवरस्य of the great mountain, प्रोद्धृतस्य lifted up, अस्य of this, तावत् then, सिकतिल-मृदु-देशे in a soft and sandy area, दूरतः from afar, वारित-आपे where water was kept away, परिकर-परिमिश्रान् along with all their belongings and paraphernalia, धेनु-गोपान् the cows and cowherds, अधस्तात् underneath, उपनिदधत् placing safely, अधत्थाः You held, हस्त-पद्मेन with Your lotus hand, शैलम् the mountain

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, beneath the lifted mountain where the ground was soft, sandy, and kept completely dry from the surrounding rain, You placed all the cowherds, cows, and their belongings safely underneath, and effortlessly held the mountain aloft with Your lotus hand.

भवति विधृतशैले बालिकाभिर्वयस्यैः अपि विहितविलासं केलिलापादिलोले ।
सविधमिलितधेनूरेकहस्तेन कण्डूयति सति पशुपालास्तोषमैषन्त सर्वे ॥63.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: भवति while You, विधृत-शैले were holding the mountain, बालिकाभिः with young girls, वयस्यैः with Your companions, अपि also, विहित-विलासम् engaging in playful sports, केलि-आलाप-आदि-लोले eagerly indulging in playful banter and chats, सविध-मिलित-धेनूः the cows gathered near, एक-हस्तेन with one hand, कण्डूयति सति were scratching/caressed, पशुपालाः the cowherds, तोषम् joy, ऐषन्त experienced, सर्वे all

Simple Meaning: While You held up the mountain, You playfully engaged in lighthearted chatter and joyful banter with the young gopis and Your friends. Caressing the cows with one hand that gathered close to You, You filled all the cowherds with immense joy and delight.

अतिमहान् गिरिरेष तु वामके करसरोरुहि तं धरते चिरम् ।
किमिदमद्भुतमद्रिबलं न्विति त्वदवलोकिभिराकथि गोपकैः ॥63.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: अति-महान् extremely huge, गिरिः mountain, एषः this, तु indeed, वामके in the left, कर-सरोरुहि lotus-like hand, तम् it, धरते holds, चिरम् for a long time, किम् what, इदम् this, अद्भुतम् wonder, अद्रि-बलम् power of the mountain, नु indeed, इति thus, त्वत्-अवलोकिभिः by those who were gazing at You, आकथि was spoken, गोपकैः by the cowherds

Simple Meaning: This mountain is extraordinarily huge, yet He holds it for a long time on His left lotus hand. What a wonder this is! Is it due to the power of the mountain itself that it has picked itself up? Thus spoke the amazed cowherds who were gazing upon You.

अहह धार्ष्ट्यममुष्य वटोर्गिरिं व्यथितबाहुरसाववरोपयेत् ।
इति हरिस्त्वयि बद्धविगर्हणो दिवससप्तकमुग्रमवर्षयत् ॥63.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: अहह alas, धार्ष्ट्यम् arrogance, अमुष्य of this, वटोः young boy, गिरिम् mountain, व्यथित-बाहुः with pained arm, असौ he, अवरोपयेत् will lower down, इति thus, हरिः Indra, त्वयि toward You, बद्ध-विगर्हणः holding deep resentment, दिवस-सप्तकम् for seven days, उग्रम् fiercely, अवर्षयत् poured rain

Simple Meaning: Alas, look at the sheer arrogance of this boy! His arm will soon ache, and he will drop the mountain down! Thinking thus and harboring deep resentment toward You, Indra caused torrential rains to pour down fiercely for seven continuous days.

अचलति त्वयि देव पदात् पदं गलितसर्वजले च घनोत्करे ।
अपहृते मरुता मरुतां पतिः त्वदभिशङ्कितधीः समुपाद्रवत् ॥63.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: अ-चलति without moving, त्वयि while You remained, देव O Lord, पदात् पदम् step by step, गलित-सर्व-जले when all water was exhausted, च and, घन-उत्करे in the mass of clouds, अपहृते blown away, मरुता by the wind, मरुताम् पतिः Indra the lord of devas, त्वत्-अभिशङ्कित-धीः his mind filled with awe and apprehension of You, समुपाद्रवत् fled in fear

Simple Meaning: O Lord, while You remained unmoved without shifting even a single step, the dense mass of clouds completely exhausted all their water and was blown away by the wind. Seeing this, Indra, the lord of devas, his mind filled with awe and deep apprehension regarding Your true power, fled in fear.

शममुपेयुषि वर्षभरे तदा पशुपधेनुकुले च विनिर्गते ।
भुवि विभो समुपाहितभूधरः प्रमुदितैः पशुपैः परिरेभिषे ॥63.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: शमम् उपेयुषि having subsided, वर्ष-भरे the heavy downpour, तदा then, पशुप-धेनु-कुले the group of cowherds and cows, च and, विनिर्गते having come out, भुवि on the ground, विभो O All-pervading Lord, समुपाहित-भूधरः having placed down the mountain, प्रमुदितैः overjoyed, पशुपैः by the cowherds, परिरेभिषे You were embraced

Simple Meaning: O All-pervading Lord, when the heavy downpour subsided and the entire community of cowherds along with their cattle emerged safely, You set the mountain back down on the earth. Thereupon, You were joyfully embraced by the ecstatic cowherds.

धरणिमेव पुरा धृतवानसि क्षितिधरोद्धरणे तव कः श्रमः ।
इति नुतस्त्रिदशैः कमलापते गुरुपुरालय पालय मां गदात् ॥63.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: धरणिम् the earth, एव indeed, पुरा in ancient times, धृतवान् असि You held up, क्षितिधर-उद्धरणे in lifting a mountain, तव Your, कः what, श्रमः exertion, इति thus, नुतः praised, त्रिदशैः by the devas, कमलापते O Lord of Lakshmi, गुरुपुरालय O Lord residing in Guruvayur, पालय protect, माम् me, गदात् from disease

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Lakshmi, You who previously lifted the entire earth (in Your Varaha incarnation), what exertion could there possibly be for You in lifting a mere mountain? Thus praised by the devas, O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect me from all my afflictions and diseases!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
