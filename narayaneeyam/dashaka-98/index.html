॥ॐ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 98

Praise of the Absolute Brahman

(छन्दस् - स्रग्धरा)

यस्मिन्नेतद्विभातं यत इदमभवद्येन चेदं य एतत्
योऽस्मादुत्तीर्णरूपः खलु सकलमिदं भासितं यस्य भासा ।
यो वाचां दूरदूरे पुनरपि मनसां यस्य देवा मुनीन्द्राः
नो विद्युस्तत्त्वरूपं किमु पुनरपरे कृष्ण तस्मै नमस्ते ॥98.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: यस्मिन् in whom, एतत् this universe, विभातम् shines or appears, यतः from whom, इदम् this, अभवत् came into being or originated, येन by whom, च and, इदम् this world, यः who, एतत् is this universe, यः who, अस्मात् from this, उत्तीर्ण-रूपः is of a transcendent nature or beyond, खलु indeed, सकलम् entire, इदम् this, भासितम् is illuminated, यस्य whose, भासा by light or effulgence, यः who, वाचाम् to words, दूर दूरे is far far beyond, पुनः-अपि and also, मनसाम् to minds, यस्य whose, देवाः devas, मुनि-इन्द्राः leaders among sages, नो not, विद्युः know, तत्त्व-रूपम् true nature or form, किमु पुनः अपरे what then of others, कृष्ण O Krishna, तस्मै to Him, नमस्ते salutations to You

Simple Meaning: You are that Supreme Reality in whom this universe shines, from whom it originated, by whom it is sustained, and who Himself is this entire universe. Yet You remain completely transcendent beyond it! Everything in this creation is illuminated by Your effulgence alone. You are far beyond the reach of words and mind, and even the devas and great sages do not fully comprehend Your true nature—what then to speak of ordinary beings? Salutations to You, O Krishna!

जन्माथो कर्म नाम स्फुटमिह गुणदोषादिकं वा न यस्मिन्
लोकानामूतये यः स्वयमनुभजते तानि मायानुसारी ।
विभ्रच्छक्तीररूपोऽपि च बहुतररूपोऽवभात्यद्भुतात्मा
तस्मै कैवल्यधाम्ने पररसपरिपूर्णाय विष्णो नमस्ते ॥98.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: जन्म birth, अथो and also, कर्म action, नाम name, स्फुटम् clearly, इह in this world, गुण-दोष-आदिकम् वा or qualities and defects and others, न not, यस्मिन् in whom, लोकानाम् ऊतये for the protection or welfare of the worlds, यः who, स्वयम् Himself, अनुभजते assumes or accepts, तानि them, माया-अनुसारी following His illusive power or Maya, विभ्रत्-शक्तीः bearing or possessing divine powers, अरूपः अपि though formless, च and, बहुतर-रूपः having countless forms, अवभाति shines or appears, अद्भुत-आत्मा of wondrous nature, तस्मै to Him, कैवल्य-धाम्ने who is the abode of liberation, पर-रस-परिपूर्णाय filled with supreme bliss, विष्णो O Lord Vishnu, नमस्ते salutations to You

Simple Meaning: In Your true transcendent nature, You have no birth, actions, name, or worldly attributes like qualities and defects. Yet, for the protection and elevation of the worlds, You assume all of these through Your divine Maya. Possessing all miraculous powers, You shine in countless forms while remaining formless, displaying Your wondrous and mysterious nature. Salutations to You, O Vishnu, who are the ultimate abode of liberation and overflowing with supreme bliss!

नो तिर्यञ्चन्न मर्त्यं न च सुरमसुरं न स्त्रियं नो पुंमांसं
न द्रव्यं कर्म जातिं गुणमपि सदसद्वापि ते रूपमाहुः ।
शिष्टं यत् स्यान्निषेधे सति निगमशतैर्लक्षणावृत्तितस्तत्
कृच्छ्रेणावेद्यमानं परमसुखमयं भाति तस्मै नमस्ते ॥98.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: नो neither, तिर्यञ्चम् an animal or bird, न nor, मर्त्यम् a human, न nor, च and, सुरम् a deva, असुरम् an asura, न nor, स्त्रियम् a woman, नो nor, पुंमांसम् a man, न nor, द्रव्यम् substance/object, कर्म action, जातिम् class/species, गुणम् अपि or quality, सत् असत् वा अपि or existent or non-existent, ते Your, रूपम् form, आहुः they declare, शिष्टम् what remains, यत् which, स्यात् is, निषेधे-सति when negation is done, निगम-शतैः by hundreds of scriptures, लक्षणा-वृत्तितः through indirect indication, तत् that, कृच्छ्रेण-आवेद्यमानम् known/comprehended with great difficulty, परम-सुख-मयम् filled with supreme bliss, भाति shines, तस्मै to Him, नमस्ते salutations to You

Simple Meaning: The learned declare that Your true form is neither an animal, nor a human, nor a deva, nor an asura, nor a woman, nor a man. It is neither a substance, nor an action, nor a species, nor an attribute, nor anything existing or non-existing in the material sense. When hundreds of scriptures negate everything material, that absolute reality which alone remains—indicated indirectly and known only with great difficulty as pure supreme bliss—shines forth. Salutations to You, that Supreme Being!

मायायां बिम्बितस्त्वं सृजसि महदहङ्कारतन्मात्रभेदैः
भूतग्रामेन्द्रियाद्यैरपि सकलजगत्स्वप्नसङ्कल्पकल्पम् ।
भूयः संहृत्य सर्वं कमठ इव पदान्यात्मना कालशक्त्या
गम्भीरे जायमाने तमसि वितिमिरो भासि तस्मै नमस्ते ॥98.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: मायायाम् in Maya/the illusive power, बिम्बितः reflected, त्वम् You, सृजसि create, महत्-अहङ्कार-तन्मात्र-भेदैः through divisions of mahat, ahankara and tanmaatras (subtle elements), भूत-ग्राम-इन्द्रिय-आद्यैः अपि with groups of physical elements, senses and others, सकल-जगत् the entire world, स्वप्न-सङ्कल्प-कल्पम् like a dream/a mental projection, भूयः again, संहृत्य withdrawing, सर्वम् everything, कमठः इव like a tortoise, पदानि (its) limbs, आत्मना by Yourself, काल-शक्त्या through the power of time, गम्भीरे जायमाने when deep, तमसि darkness, वितिमिरः free from darkness/self-effulgent, भासि You shine, तस्मै to Him, नमस्ते salutations to You

Simple Meaning: Reflected in Your illusive power Maya, You create this entire universe—consisting of the cosmic intelligence, ego, subtle elements, physical elements, and senses—making it appear like a dream or a mental illusion. Then again, through the power of time, You withdraw everything into Yourself just as a tortoise retracts its limbs. When the deep darkness of cosmic dissolution prevails, You alone shine, free from all darkness, as pure self-effulgent consciousness. Salutations to You, that Supreme Lord!

शब्दब्रह्मेति कर्मेत्यणुरिति भगवन् काल इत्यालपन्ति
त्वामेकं विश्वहेतुं सकलमयतया सर्वथा कल्प्यमानम् ।
वेदान्तैर्यत्तु गीतं पुरुषपरचिदात्माभिधं तत्तु तत्त्वं
प्रेक्षामात्रेण मूलप्रकृतिविकृतिकृत् कृष्ण तस्मै नमस्ते ॥98.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: शब्द-ब्रह्म इति as Sabda-Brahman, कर्म इति as Karma, अणुः इति as Anu/atom, भगवन् O Blessed Lord, कालः इति as Kala/Time, आलपन्ति philosophers speak of, त्वाम् You, एकम् the One, विश्व-हेतुम् cause of the universe, सकल-मयतया as being present in all forms, सर्वथा in all ways, कल्प्यमानम् imagined/conceived, वेदान्तैः by the Vedanta/Upanishads, यत् which, तु indeed, गीतम् is sung/praised, पुरुष-पर-चित्-आत्मा-अभिधम् named as Purusha, Parama-Chit-Atman (the Supreme Conscious Self), तत् that, तु indeed, तत्त्वम् Reality, प्रेक्षा-मात्रेण by a mere glance, मूल-प्रकृति-विकृति-कृत् which causes transformations in primordial nature, कृष्ण O Krishna, तस्मै to Him, नमस्ते salutations to You

Simple Meaning: O Lord, philosophers conceive You—the single, ultimate cause of the universe present in all forms—in various ways, calling You Shabda-Brahman, Karma, Atom, or Time. However, that supreme Reality praised in Vedanta under the names Purusha and Parama-Chit-Atman (the Supreme Conscious Self), which triggers all transformations in primordial nature through a mere glance, is You alone. Salutations to You, O Krishna!

सत्त्वेनासत्तया वा न च खलु सदसत्त्वेन निर्वाच्यरूपा
धत्ते यासावविद्या गुणफणिमतिवद्विश्वदृश्यावभासम् ।
विद्यात्वं सैव याता श्रुतिवचनलवैर्यत्कृपास्यन्दलाभे
संसारारण्यसद्यस्त्रुटनपरशुतामेति तस्मै नमस्ते ॥98.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: सत्त्वेन as real/existent, असत्तया वा or as non-existent, न not, च and, खलु indeed, सत्-असत्त्वेन as both real and non-existent, निर्वाच्य-रूपा can be defined/described, धत्ते holds/assumes, या असौ which that, अविद्या Avidya/ignorance, गुण-फणि-मति-वत् like mistaking a rope for a snake due to its attributes, विश्व-दृश्य-अवभासम् the appearance of the visible universe, विद्यात्वम् the nature of Vidya/spiritual knowledge, सा एव that very Avidya, याता attains/transforms into, श्रुति-वचन-लवैः by tiny fragments of scriptural teachings, यत्-कृपा-स्यन्द-लाभे when the flow of Your grace is obtained, संसार-अरण्य-सद्यः-त्रुटन-परशुताम् the state of being an axe that instantly cuts down the dense forest of samsara/worldly existence, एति becomes, तस्मै to Him, नमस्ते salutations to You

Simple Meaning: That ignorance (Avidya) cannot be defined as real, non-existent, or both real and non-existent. Like a rope mistaken for a snake, it projects the false appearance of the visible universe. Yet, when the flow of Your grace is attained, that very Avidya transforms into Vidya (spiritual wisdom) which, with the help of even a small fragment of scriptural teaching, instantly acts like an axe to chop down the dense forest of samsara. Salutations to You, that Supreme Lord!

भूषासु स्वर्णवद्वा जगति घटशरावादिके मृत्तिकाव-
त्तत्त्वे सञ्चिन्त्यमाने स्फुरति तदधुनाप्यद्वितीयं वपुस्ते ।
स्वप्नद्रष्टुः प्रबोधे तिमिरलयविधौ जीर्णरज्जोश्च यद्व-
द्विद्यालाभे तथैव स्फुटमपि विकसेत् कृष्ण तस्मै नमस्ते ॥98.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूषासु in ornaments, स्वर्ण-वत् वा like gold, जगति in the world, घट-शराव-आदिके in pots and pitchers and others, मृत्तिका-वत् like clay, तत्त्वे when truth/reality, सञ्चिन्त्यमाने is reflected upon, स्फुरति shines forth/becomes manifest, तत् that, अधुना अपि even now, अद्वितीयम् non-dual, वपुः form/nature, ते Your, स्वप्न-द्रष्टुः for a dreamer, प्रबोधे upon waking up, तिमिर-लय-विधौ when darkness is removed, जीर्ण-रज्जोः of a worn-out rope, च and, यद्वत् just as, विद्या-लाभे upon attaining spiritual knowledge, तथा एव in the exact same manner, स्फुटम् clearly, अपि even, विकसेत् manifests/dawns, कृष्ण O Krishna, तस्मै to Him, नमस्ते salutations to You

Simple Meaning: Just as gold is the sole underlying reality in various ornaments, or clay in various pots and vessels, when the truth is deeply pondered, Your non-dual form is realized as the true reality behind this entire universe even right now. Just as a dream disappears when the dreamer wakes up, or a snake illusion vanishes from a worn-out rope when light removes darkness, in the exact same way, Your true non-dual nature shines forth clearly upon the dawn of spiritual knowledge. Salutations to You, O Krishna!

यद्भीत्योदेति सूर्यो दहति च दहनो वाति वायुस्तथान्ये
यद्भीताः पद्मजाद्याः पुनरुचितबलीनाहरन्तेऽनुकालम् ।
येनैवारोपिताः प्राङ्निजपदमपि ते च्यावितारश्च पश्चात्
तस्मै विश्वं नियन्त्रे वयमपि भवते कृष्ण कुर्मः प्रणामम् ॥98.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: यत्-भीत्या out of fear of whom, उदेति rises, सूर्यः the sun, दहति burns, च and, दहनः fire, वाति blows, वायुः the wind, तथा and similarly, अन्ये others, यत्-भीताः fearing whom, पद्मज-आद्याः Lord Brahma and the other devas, पुनः again, उचित-बलीन् appropriate offerings/duties, आहरन्ते render/perform, अनुकालम् regularly at appointed times, येन एव by whom alone, आरोपिताः established/elevated, प्राक् earlier, निज-पदम् in their respective positions, अपि even, ते they, च्यावितारः removed/displaced, च and, पश्चात् later, तस्मै to Him, विश्वम् the universe, नियन्त्रे to the Controller, वयम् अपि we also, भवते to You, कृष्ण O Krishna, कुर्मः make, प्रणामम् respectful salutations

Simple Meaning: Out of fear of whom the sun rises, fire burns, the wind blows, and all other cosmic powers perform their functions; fearing whom Lord Brahma and the devas render their respective duties regularly at appointed times; by whom alone they were elevated to their cosmic positions in the past, and by whom they will eventually be removed from office—to You, O Krishna, the Supreme Controller of the entire universe, we offer our humble salutations!

त्रैलोक्यं भावयन्तं त्रिगुणमयमिदं त्र्यक्षरस्यैकवाच्यं
त्रीशानामैक्यरूपं त्रिभिरपि निगमैर्गीयमानस्वरूपम् ।
तिस्रोवस्था विदन्तं त्रियुगजनिजुषं त्रिक्रमाक्रान्तविश्वं
त्रैकाल्ये भेदहीनं त्रिभिरहमनिशं योगभेदैर्भजे त्वाम् ॥98.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्रै-लोक्यम् the three worlds, भावयन्तम् causing to manifest or sustaining, त्रि-गुण-मयम् consisting of the three gunas, इदम् this, त्रि-अक्षरस्य of the three letters A U M (अ-ओ-म्), एक-वाच्यम् the sole denoted entity, त्रि-ईशानाम् of the three lords Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva, ऐक्य-रूपम् the single unified form, त्रिभिः by the three, अपि also, निगमैः by Vedas, गीयमान-स्वरूपम् whose true nature is praised, तिस्रः the three, अवस्थाः states of consciousness, विदन्तम् knowing, त्रि-युग-जनि-जुषम् taking incarnations in the three yugas, त्रि-क्रम्-आक्रान्त-विश्वम् the universe traversed in three steps, त्रै-काल्ये across the three divisions of time - past, present and future, भेद-हीनम् free from any distinction/change, त्रिभिः by the three, अहम् I, अनिशम् continuously, योग-भेदैः paths of yoga, भजे worship, त्वाम् You

Simple Meaning: You, who sustain these three worlds - Swarga, Bhuloka, Patalaloka; You, who are composed of the three gunas (Sattva, Rajas and Tamas); You, who are the sole meaning behind the three-lettered syllable AUM (अ ओ म्); You, who are the unified essence of the three lords - Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva; You, whose divine form is praised by the three Vedas - Rig, Yajur and Sama; You, who know the three states of consciousness - waking, dream and sleep; You, who appear in incarnations in the three yugas - Treta, Dvapara and Kali; You, who traversed the entire universe in three steps; You, who remain completely unchanged across the three divisions of time - past, present, and future —- I continuously worship You using the three paths of yoga (Karma, Bhakti and Gynana).

सत्यं शुद्धं विबुद्धं जयति तव वपुर्नित्यमुक्तं निरीहं
निर्द्वन्द्वं निर्विकारं निखिलगुणगणव्यञ्जनाधारभूतम् ।
निर्मूलं निर्मलं तन्निरवधिमहिमोल्लासि निर्लीनमन्तः
निस्सङ्गानां मुनीनां निरुपमपरमानन्दसान्द्रप्रकाशम् ॥98.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: सत्यम् truth, शुद्धम् pure, विबुद्धम् absolute consciousness, जयति shines gloriously, तव Your, वपुः divine form, नित्य-मुक्तम् eternally free, निर्-ईहम् without desires, निर्-द्व​न्द्वम् without dualities, निर्-विकारम् without a change, निखिल-गुण-गण-व्यञ्जन-आधार-भूतम् the source for the manifestation of all auspicious attributes, निर्-मूलम् rootless/without a cause, निर्-मलम् flawless, तत् that, निर्-अवधि-महिम-उल्लासि radiating boundless/limitless glory, निर्-लीनम् deep within, अन्तः inside, निः-स​ङ्गानाम् of the unattached, मुनीनाम् of the sages, निर्-उपम-परमानन्द-सान्द्र-प्रकाशम् intense effulgence of supreme bliss without a parallel

Simple Meaning: Your divine form shines gloriously as eternally free, without desires, without dualities and without a change. It is the source for the manifestation of all attributes, without a cause and without a flaw. Radiating glory without a limit, it reveals itself deep within the hearts of sages who are without any attachment, as an intense, effulgent light of supreme bliss without a parallel.

दुर्वारं द्वादशारं त्रिशतपरिमिलत्षष्टिपर्वाभिवीतं
सम्भ्राम्यत् क्रूरवेगं क्षणमनु जगदाच्छिद्य सन्धावमानम् ।
चक्रं ते कालरूपं व्यथयतु न तु मां त्वत्पदैकावलम्बं
विष्णो कारुण्यसिन्धो पवनपुरपते पाहि सर्वामयौघात् ॥98.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुर्वारम् irresistible, द्वादश-आरम् having twelve spokes, त्रिशत-परिमिलत्-षष्टि-पर्व-अभिवीतम् composed of three hundred and sixty joints, सम्भ्राम्यत् revolving, क्रूर-वेगम् with terrifying speed, क्षणम् अनु every moment, जगत् the world, आच्छिद्य snatching away, सन्धावमानम् rushing forward, चक्रम् wheel, ते Your, काल-रूपम् in the form of time, व्यथयतु may it torment, न not, तु but, माम् me, त्वत्-पद-एक-अवलम्बम् whose sole refuge is Your feet, विष्णो O Vishnu, कारुण्य-सिन्धो O Ocean of Mercy, पवनपुर-पते O Lord of Guruvayur, पाहि protect me, सर्व-आमय-ओघात् from all afflictions and diseases

Simple Meaning: Your wheel of time revolves with irresistible and terrifying speed, having twelve spokes representing twelve months and three hundred and sixty joints representing days. Rushing forward, it snatches away the lifespan of every creature at every moment. May this time wheel not torment me, for I have taken sole refuge at Your lotus feet! O Lord Vishnu, O Ocean of Mercy, O Lord of Guruvayur! Please protect me from the entire multitude of diseases and afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
