Dashaka 32

The incarnation as Fish (Matsya Avatara)

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 32

The incarnation as Fish

(Matsya Avatara)

पुरा हयग्रीवमहासुरेण षष्ठान्तरान्तोद्यदकाण्डकल्पे ।
निद्रोन्मुखब्रह्ममुखात् हृतेषु वेदेष्वधित्सः किल मत्स्यरूपम् ॥32.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुरा in ancient times, हयग्रीव-महासुरेण by the formidable asura Hayagriva, षष्ठ-अन्तर-अन्त-उद्यत्-अकाण्ड-कल्पे during the premature dissolution (unusual deluge) arising at the end of the sixth Manvantara (Chakshusha Manvantara), निद्रा-उन्मुख-ब्रह्म-मुखात् from the mouth of Lord Brahma who was leaning toward sleep, हृतेषु when stolen/carried away, वेदेषु the holy Vedas, अधित्सः You desired/willed to assume, किल indeed, मत्स्य-रूपम् the form of a Fish (Matsya)

Simple Meaning: Long ago, toward the end of the sixth Manvantara (Chakshusha Manvantara), an untimely dissolution (Akaanda Pralaya) set in. As Lord Brahma felt drowsy and inclined to sleep, the mighty demon Hayagriva stole the holy Vedas directly from his mouth. To retrieve the sacred scriptures and protect the universe, You willed to assume the divine incarnation of a Fish (Matsya)!

सत्यव्रतस्य द्रमिलाधिभर्तुर्नदीजले तर्पयतस्तदानीम् ।
कराञ्जलौ सञ्ज्वलिताकृतिस्त्वमदृश्यथाः कश्चन बालमीनः ॥32.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: सत्यव्रतस्य of King Satyavrata, द्रमिल-अधिभर्तुः the ruler of the Dramila land, नदी-जले in the river water (the Kritamala river), तर्पयतः while performing his morning water oblations (Tarpana), तदानीम् at that time, कर-अञ्जलौ in the cupped palms of his hands, सञ्ज्वलिताकृतिः possessing a brilliantly glowing/radiant form, त्वम् You, अदृश्यथाः appeared, कश्चन a certain/tiny, बाल-मीनः little fish

Simple Meaning: At that time, while King Satyavrata—the devout ruler of the Dramila region—was offering water oblations (Tarpana) in the sacred river, You suddenly appeared in the cupped palms of his hands! Taking the form of a tiny, remarkably radiant little fish, You glowed with divine effulgence.

क्षिप्तं जले त्वां चकितं विलोक्य निन्येऽम्बुपात्रेण मुनिः स्वगेहम् ।
स्वल्पैरहोभिः कलशीं च कूपं वापीं सरश्चानशिषे विभो त्वम् ॥32.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्षिप्तम् thrown back, जले into the water, त्वाम् You, चकितम् appearing frightened/anxious, विलोक्य seeing, निन्ये brought, अम्बु-पात्रेण in a water vessel/jar, मुनिः the king-sage (Satyavrata), स्व-गेहम् to his own abode/hermitage, स्वल्पैः अहोभिः in a few short days, कलशीम् a large pitcher/pot, च and, कूपम् a well, वापीम् a large step-well/pond, सरः च and a massive lake, अनशिषे You pervaded/grew to fill completely, विभो O All-Pervading Lord!, त्वम् You

Simple Meaning: When the king-sage Satyavrata threw You back into the river, You pleaded fearfully to be saved from the larger creatures. Seeing Your plight, the compassionate sage carried You home in a small water pot. But within just a few days, O All-Pervading Lord, You rapidly grew so vast that You completely outgrew the pitcher, then a well, then a large pond, and finally even a wide lake!

योगप्रभावाद्भवदाज्ञयैव नीतस्ततस्त्वं मुनिना पयोधिम् ।
पृष्टोऽमुना कल्पदिदृक्षुमेनं सप्ताहमास्वेति वदन्नयासीः ॥32.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: योग-प्रभावात् through the power of Yoga (spiritual energy), भवत्-आज्ञा एव strictly according to Your command, नीतः carried/transported, ततः from there (the lake), त्वम् You, मुनिना by the sage-king (Satyavrata), पयोधिम् into the vast ocean, पृष्टः being asked, अमुना by him (Satyavrata), कल्प-दिदृक्षुम longing to witness the cosmic dissolution (Pralaya/end of the Kalpa), एनम् unto him, सप्ताहम् for seven days, आस्व wait/remain patient, इति thus, वदन् saying, अयासीः You disappeared/departed

Simple Meaning: Realizing Your divine nature, the sage-king Satyavrata carried You to the vast ocean with the help of spiritual powers, exactly as You instructed. When the eager king asked about the imminent cosmic deluge, longing to witness the end of the Kalpa, You instructed him: "Wait patiently here for seven days!" Having given this command, You vanished into the deep waters.

प्राप्ते त्वदुक्तेऽहनि वारिधारापरिप्लुते भूमितले मुनीन्द्रः ।
सप्तर्षिभिः सार्धमपारवारिण्युद्घूर्णमानः शरणं ययौ त्वाम् ॥32.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्राप्ते when arrived, त्वत्-उक्ते on the day specified by You (the seventh day), अहनि on that day, वारि-धारा-परिप्लुते submerged/inundated by torrential streams of rain, भूमि-तले the surface of the earth, मुनीन्द्रः the great king-sage (Satyavrata), सप्तर्षिभिः सार्धम् along with the seven great Sages (Saptarshis), अपार-वारिणी in the endless/boundless waters, उद्घूर्णमानः being tossed violently/tossed around, शरणम् ययौ sought refuge in, त्वाम् You

Simple Meaning: When the seventh day appointed by You arrived, the entire surface of the earth became completely inundated with torrential downpours of rain. As the great king-sage Satyavrata, along with the seven holy Sages (Saptarshis), found themselves violently tossed about in the boundless, surging ocean of cosmic dissolution, he sought refuge in You alone!

धरां त्वदादेशकरीमवाप्तां नौरूपिणीमारुरुहुस्तदा ते
तत्कम्पकम्प्रेषु च तेषु भूयस्त्वमम्बुधेराविरभूर्महीयान् ॥32.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: धराम् Mother Earth, त्वत्-आदेश-करीम् obeying Your divine command, अवाप्ताम् who arrived/presented herself, नौ-रूपिणीम् in the form of a boat, आरुरुहुः mounted/boarded, तदा then, ते they (King Satyavrata and the Saptarishis), तत्-कम्प-कम्प्रेषु when trembling with fear due to the violent rocking of that boat, च and, तेषु as they were, भूयः once again, त्वम् You, अम्बुधेः from the surging ocean, आविरभूः manifested/appeared, महीयान् in an extraordinarily gigantic/glorious form

Simple Meaning: Obeying Your divine command, Mother Earth herself appeared before them in the form of a sturdy boat. King Satyavrata and the seven holy Sages (Saptarshis) immediately boarded it. However, as the boat began to rock violently upon the turbulent waters, they trembled with apprehension. Just then, You manifested once again from the vast, surging ocean in an extraordinarily colossal, golden Fish form!

झषाकृतिं योजनलक्षदीर्घां दधानमुच्चैस्तरतेजसं त्वाम् ।
निरीक्ष्य तुष्टा मुनयस्त्वदुक्त्या त्वत्तुङ्गशृङ्गे तरणिं बबन्धुः ॥32.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: झष-आकृतिम् the form of a giant fish, योजन-लक्ष-दीर्घाम् extending to a length of a hundred thousand Yojanas, दधानम् possessing/assuming, उच्चैस्तर-तेजसम् endowed with exceedingly brilliant effulgence, त्वाम् You, निरीक्ष्य seeing/beholding, तुष्टाः delighted/overjoyed, मुनयः the sages (Saptarshis), त्वत्-उक्त्या in accordance with Your instructions, त्वत्-तुङ्ग-शृङ्गे onto Your lofty, towering horn, तरणिम् the boat, बबन्धुः tied/secured

Simple Meaning: Beholding Your extraordinarily radiant Fish form that stretched across a hundred thousand Yojanas, the holy sages were filled with immense joy and awe! Following Your divine instructions, tied the boat to the towering horn on Your head.

आकृष्टनौको मुनिमण्डलाय प्रदर्शयन् विश्वजगद्विभागान् ।
संस्तूयमानो नृवरेण तेन ज्ञानं परं चोपदिशन्नचारीः ॥32.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: आकृष्ट-नौकः towing the boat behind You, मुनि-मण्डलाय to the group of sages (the Saptarishis), प्रदर्शयन् revealing/showing, विश्व-जगत्-विभागान् the various regions and divisions of the entire cosmos, संस्तूयमानः being exalted/praised in glorious hymns, नृवरेण तेन by that best among men (King Satyavrata), ज्ञानम् परम् the supreme spiritual wisdom (the Matsya Purana/Atma-jnana), च and, उपदिशन् imparting/instructing, अचारीः You moved/traversed through the cosmic waters

Simple Meaning: Towing the boat safely across the endless waters of dissolution, You revealed the various realms and divisions of the universe to the assembled sages. As King Satyavrata offered heartfelt prayers in Your praise, You imparted the supreme, sacred wisdom (Atma-jnana) unto him, moving through the vast cosmic ocean.

कल्पावधौ सप्तमुनीन् पुरोवत् प्रस्थाप्य सत्यव्रतभूमिपं तम् ।
वैवस्वताख्यं मनुमादधानः क्रोधाद् हयग्रीवमभिद्रुतोऽभूः ॥32.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: कल्प-अवधौ at the conclusion of the Kalpa (the period of cosmic deluge), सप्त-मुनीन् the Seven Sages (Saptarishis), पुरोवत् as before/as in previous cycles, प्रस्थाप्य establishing/stationing, सत्यव्रत-भूमिपम् तम् that King Satyavrata, वैवस्वत-आख्यम् named Vaivasvata, मनुम् as the Manu (progenitor of mankind), आदधानः appointing/making, क्रोधात् in divine wrath, हयग्रीवम् toward the asura Hayagriva, अभिद्रुतः अभूः You rushed/charged

Simple Meaning: At the conclusion of the deluge, You safely established the Seven Sages (Saptarshis) in their respective cosmic positions as in previous cycles. You appointed the devout King Satyavrata as the present Manu—famously known as Vaivasvata Manu, the progenitor of mankind in the current epoch. Then, filled with divine wrath, You charged across the waters to confront the asura Hayagriva!

स्वतुङ्गशृङ्गक्षतवक्षसं तं निपात्य दैत्यं निगमान् गृहीत्वा ।
विरिञ्चये प्रीतहृदे ददानः प्रभञ्जनागारपते प्रपायाः ॥32.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्व-तुङ्ग-शृङ्ग-क्षत-वक्षसम् whose chest was pierced by Your own lofty/sharp horn, तम् that, दैत्यम् daitya (Hayagriva), निपात्य striking down/slaying, निगमान् the sacred Vedas, गृहीत्वा taking back/recovering, विरिञ्चये unto Lord Brahma (Virinchi), प्रीत-हृदे whose heart was filled with joy, ददानः handing over/restoring, प्रभञ्जन-आगार-पते O Lord of place of the Wind - Guruvayur!, प्रपायाः please protect me completely!

Simple Meaning: Slaying the fierce daitya Hayagriva by piercing his chest with Your sharp, towering horn, You recovered the sacred Vedas and joyfully restored them to Lord Brahma, whose heart overflowed with delight and relief! O Lord of Guruvayur, who thus protected the eternal truth, please protect me completely from all my afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
