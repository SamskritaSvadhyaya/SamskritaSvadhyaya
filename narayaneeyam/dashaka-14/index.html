Incarnation as Kapila

दशकम् १४

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 14

Incarnation as Kapila

(छन्दस् - औपच्छन्दसिकम्)

समनुस्मृततावकाङ्घ्रियुग्मः
स मनुः पङ्कजसम्भवाङ्गजन्मा ।
निजमन्तरमन्तरायहीनं
चरितं ते कथयन् सुखं निनाय ॥ 14.1 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: समनुस्मृत-तावक-अङ्घरि-युग्मः constantly contemplating Your pair of lotus feet, सः मनुः that Swayambhuva Manu, पङ्कज-सम्भव-अङ्ग-जन्मा born directly from the body of Lord Brahma (who was born from the lotus), निजम् अन्तरम् his own manvantara period, अन्तराय-हीनम् free from all obstacles, चरितम् ते Your divine glories/deeds, कथयन् relating, सुखम् happily, निनाय spent/passed (the time)

Simple Meaning: Swayambhuva Manu—the son born directly from the body of Lord Brahma—spent his entire manvantara (epoch) in serene happiness. Keeping Your lotus feet constantly in his heart and freely singing Your divine glories, he ruled over his reign smoothly, untouched by any obstacles!

समये खलु तत्र कर्दमाख्यो
द्रुहिणच्छायभवस्तदीयवाचा ।
धृतसर्गरसो निसर्गरम्यं
भगवंस्त्वामयुतं समाः सिषेवे ॥ 14.2 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: समये at that time, खलु indeed, तत्र there/during that period, कर्दम-आख्यः (Prajapati) named Kardama, द्रुहिण-च्छाया-भवः born from the shadow of Lord Brahma (Druhina), तदीय-वाचा by Brahma's instruction, धृत-सर्ग-रसः having a keen desire to engage in creation, निसर्ग-रम्यम् naturally captivating/supreme, भगवन् O Lord, त्वाम् You, अयुतम् समाः for ten thousand years, सिषेवे worshipped/served devotedly

Simple Meaning: During that very epoch, O Supreme Lord, Prajapati named Kardama—who was born from the shadow of Lord Brahma—wished to fulfill his father's command to propagate creation. To gain the required spiritual power and grace, he devoutly served and worshipped You—the naturally enchanting Lord—in intense penance for ten thousand years!

गरुडोपरि कालमेघक्रमं
विलसत्केलिसरोजपाणिपद्मम् ।
हसितोल्लसिताननं विभो त्वं
वपुराविष्कुरुषे स्म कर्दमाय ॥ 14.3 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: गरुड्-उपरि mounted atop Garuda, काल-मेघ-कम् having the lustrous hue of a dark rain-bearing cloud, विलसत्-केलि-सरोज-पाणि-पद्मम् holding a playful, gleaming lotus in Your lotus hand, हसित-उल्लसित-आननम् having a face delightfully illuminated with a radiant smile, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, त्वम् You, वपुः divine form, आविष्कुरुषे स्म revealed/manifested, कर्दमाय unto Sage Kardama

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord! Pleased by his intense penance, You manifested Your divine form before Sage Kardama—seated majestically atop Your mount Garuda, dark and radiant like a fresh raincloud, holding a charming lotus in Your lotus-like hand, Your face beaming with a wondrous, compassionate smile!

स्तुवते पुलकावृताय तस्मै
मनुपुत्रीं दयितां नवापि पुत्रीः ।
कपिलं च सुतं स्वमेव पश्चात्
स्वगतिं चाप्यनुगृह्य निर्गतोऽभूः ॥ 14.4 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्तुवते to him who was singing hymns of praise, पुलक-आवृताय whose body was covered with goosebumps of ecstasy, तस्मै unto that Kardama, मनु-पुत्रीम् Devahuti (the daughter of Manu), दयिताम् as his beloved wife, नव अपि पुत्रीः and nine daughters as well, कपिलम् च सुतम् and Kapila as his divine son, स्वम् एव who was none other than Yourself, पश्चात् later on, स्व-गतिम् च अपि and ultimate liberation/Your own supreme abode (Moksha), अनुगृह्य having bestowed as blessings, निर्गतः अभूः You departed/disappeared

Simple Meaning: As Kardama stood before You, his body thrilled with goosebumps of devotional ecstasy as he sang Your praises, You showered him with abundant blessings. You granted him Devahuti (the daughter of Svayambhuva Manu) as his devoted wife, promised him nine daughters, and assured him that You Yourself would be born as his son, Kapila. Bestowing upon him ultimate liberation (Moksha) at the end of his earthly journey, You then departed from his vision.

स मनुः शतरूपया महिष्या
गुणवत्या सुतया च देवहूत्या ।
भवदीरितनारदोपदिष्टः
समगात् कर्दममागतिप्रतीक्षम् ॥ 14.5 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः मनुः that Swayambhuva Manu, शतरूपया महिष्या along with his queen Shatarupa, गुणवत्या with his virtuous, सुतया च and daughter, देवहूत्या named Devahuti, भवत्-ईरित-नारद-उपदिष्टः directed/advised by Sage Narada who was sent by You, समगात् approached/went to, कर्दमम् Kardama, आगति-प्रतीक्षम् who was eagerly awaiting their arrival

Simple Meaning: Directed by Sage Narada—who acted on Your divine impulse—King Swayambhuva Manu, accompanied by his noble queen Satarupa and their virtuous daughter Devahuti, approached Kardama, who was eagerly awaiting their arrival as foretold by You.

मनुनोपहृतां च देवहूतिं
तरुणीरत्नमवाप्य कर्दमोऽसौ ।
भवदर्चननिवृतोऽपि तस्यां
दृढशुश्रूषणया दधौ प्रसादम् ॥ 14.6 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: मनुना by King Manu, उपहृताम् offered/handed over, च and, देवहूतिम् Devahuti, तरुणी-रत्नम् the gem among young women, अवाप्य having received, असौ that Kardama, भवत्-अर्चन-निवृतः अपि although fully immersed and fulfilled in worshipping You, तस्याम् towards her, दृढ-शुश्रूषणया on account of her deep, steadfast service and devotion, दधौ प्रसादम् felt deeply pleased/showed great favor

Simple Meaning: Having received the virtuous Devahuti—a true gem among women—offered to him by King Manu, Kardama accepted her as his wife. Though he was ever immersed in the bliss of worshipping You and detached from worldly desires, he was so moved by Devahuti's deep, selfless devotion and tireless service that his heart overflowed with affection and grace towards her!

स पुनस्त्वदुपासनप्रभावात्
दयिताकामकृते कृते विमाने ।
वनिताकुलसङ्कुलो नवात्मा
व्यहरद्देवपथेषु देवहूत्या ॥ 14.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Kardama), पुनः furthermore/then, त्वत्-उपासन-प्रभावात् by the power derived from worshipping You, दयिता-काम-कृते created to fulfill the wishes of his beloved wife, कृते विमाने in an aerial mansion/celestial craft (Vimana), वनिता-कुल-सङ्कुलः surrounded by a host of celestial damsels/maidens, नव-आत्मा having assumed a youthful, radiant form, व्यहरत् sported/traveled joyfully, देव-पथेषु through the heavenly paths/celestial skies, देवहूत्या along with Devahuti

Simple Meaning: Through the incredible spiritual powers he had acquired by worshipping You, Kardama created a wondrous, flying aerial mansion (Vimana) to fulfill the heart's desire of his beloved wife, Devahuti. Transforming himself into a youthful, handsome form, he traveled through the skyways with her in absolute delight, served by a retinue of heavenly maidens!

शतवर्षमथ व्यतीत्य सोऽयं
नव कन्याः समवाप्य धन्यरूपाः ।
वनयानसमुद्यतोऽपि कान्ता-
हितकृत्त्वज्जननोत्सुको न्यवात्सीत् ॥ 14.8 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: शत-वर्षम् a hundred years, अथ then, व्यतीत्य after passing, सः अयम् that very same (Kardama), नव कन्याः nine daughters, समवाप्य having begotten/obtained, धन्य-रूपाः of auspicious forms, वन-यान-समुद्यतः अपि although eager to depart for the forest (to pursue asceticism), कान्ता-हित-कृत् taking care of his wife's well-being, त्वत्-जनन-उत्सुकः eager for Your divine advent/incarnation (as Kapila), न्यवात्सीत् stayed back/continued to reside at home

Simple Meaning: After a hundred years passed in that celestial bliss, Kardama was blessed with nine auspicious and radiant daughters. Although he was now ready to renounce worldly life and retreat into the forest for solitude, he stayed back at home—both to ensure the happiness of his beloved wife, Devahuti, and because he was eagerly awaiting Your divine birth as his son!

निजभर्तृगिरा भवन्निषेवा-
निरतायामथ देव देवहूत्याम् ।
कपिलस्त्वमजायथा जनानां
प्रथयिष्यन् परमात्मतत्त्वविद्याम् ॥ 14.9 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: निज-भर्तृ-गिरा according to the words/instruction of her husband, भवन्-निषेवा-निरतायाम् who was wholly devoted to worshipping You, अथ thereafter, देव O Divine Lord!, देवहूत्याम् in (the womb of) Devahuti, कपिलः त्वम् You as Kapila, अजयथाः were born, जनानाम् unto all people/humanity, प्रथयिष्यन् intending to reveal/spread, परमात्म-तत्त्व-विद्याम् the sacred knowledge of the Supreme Self (Sankhya philosophy)

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Thereafter, following the instruction of her husband, Devahuti immersed herself entirely in Your divine worship. Pleased with her devotion, You were born as her son— Kapila—in order to illuminate and propagate the supreme wisdom of the Self (Sankhya philosophy) for the liberation of all mankind!

वनमेयुषि कर्दमे प्रसन्ने
मतसर्वस्वमुपादिशन् जनन्यै ।
कपिलात्मक वायुमन्दिरेश
त्वरितं त्वं परिपाहि मां गदौघात् ॥ 14.10 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: वनम् एयुषि having departed into the forest, कर्दमे when Kardama had, प्रसन्ने with a peaceful mind, मत-सर्वस्वम् the complete essence of divine philosophy (Sankhya wisdom), उपादिशन् instructing/imparting, जनन्यै unto Your mother Devahuti, कपिल-आत्मक O Lord in the form of Kapila!, वायु-मन्दिर-ईश O Lord of Guruvayur!, त्वरितम् quickly, त्वम् You, परिपाहि protect, माम् me, गद-ओघात् from the host of diseases/afflictions

Simple Meaning: When Kardama departed to the forest with a peaceful mind, You, taking the form of Kapila, imparted the supreme essence of spiritual wisdom to Your mother Devahuti. O Lord of Guruvayur! Please protect me quickly from all my diseases and afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
