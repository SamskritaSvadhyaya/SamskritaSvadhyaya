Dashaka 23

The story of Chitraketu

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 23

The story of Chitraketu

(छन्दस् - वसन्ततिलका)

प्राचेतसस्तु भगवन्नपरो हि दक्षः
त्वत्सेवनं व्यधित सर्गविवृद्धिकामः ।
आविर्बभूविथ तदा लसदष्टबाहुः
तस्मै वरं ददिथ तां च वधूमसिक्नीम् ॥23.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्राचेतसः तु the son of Prachetas, भगवन् O Lord, अपरः another, हि indeed, दक्षः Daksha, त्वत्-सेवनम् Your worship, व्यधित performed, सर्ग-विवृद्धि-कामः desiring the expansion of creation, आविर्बभूविथ You appeared, तदा then, लसत्-अष्ट-बाहुः shining with eight arms, तस्मै unto him, वरम् boon, ददिथ You granted, ताम् that, च and, वधूम् bride, असिक्नीम् Asikni

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Another Daksha (not Daksha who was Sati’s father), who was born as the son of the Prachetas, desired to expand the population of creation and performed intense devotion to You. Pleased with his worship, You manifested before him shining brilliantly with eight arms, granted him the boons he desired, and gave him Asikni to be his wife.

तस्यात्मजास्त्वयुतमीश पुनस्सहस्रं
श्रीनारदस्य वचसा तव मार्गमापुः ।
नैकत्रवासमृषये स मुमोच शापं
भक्तोत्तमस्त्वृषिरनुग्रहमेव मेने ॥23.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य आत्मजाः तु his sons indeed, अयुतम् ten thousand, ईश O Supreme Lord, पुनः again, सहस्रम् one thousand, श्री-नारदस्य of sage Narada, वचसा by the words, तव Your, मार्गम् path, आपुः attained, न एकत्र वासम् not staying in one place, ऋषये unto the sage, सः he (Daksha), मुमोच uttered, शापम् a curse, भक्त-उत्तमः तु but the jewel among devotees, ऋषिः the sage Narada, अनुग्रहम् एव a blessing only, मेने considered it

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Lord! Daksha first begot ten thousand sons and later another thousand sons to populate the world. However, guided by the instructions of sage Narada, all of them renounced worldly pursuits and chose the path of devotion leading to You. Enraged by this, Daksha cursed sage Narada that he would never be able to stay in one place for long. But Narada, the foremost among devotees, happily accepted this curse as a blessing (to continuously wander and spread Your glory)!

षष्ट्या ततो दुहितृभिः सृजतः कुलौघान्
दौहित्रसूनुरथ तस्य स विश्वरूपः ।
त्वत्स्तोत्रवर्मितमजापयदिन्द्रमाजौ
देव त्वदीयमहिमा खलु सर्वजैत्रः ॥23.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: षष्ट्या through sixty, ततः thereafter, दुहितृभिः through daughters, सृजतः of Daksha who was creating, कुल-ओघान् multitude of lineages, दौहित्र-सूनुः अथ then the son of his daughter, तस्य his, सः that, विश्वरूपः Vishwarupa, त्वत्-स्तोत्र-वर्मितम् protected by the armor of Your prayer, अजापयत् made him chant, इन्द्रम् Indra, आजौ in battle, देव O Lord, त्वदीय-महिमा Your glory, खलु indeed, सर्व-जैत्रः all-conquering

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, Daksha created various lineages through his sixty daughters. Vishwarupa was born in the lineage of one of these daughters. By bestowing the sacred protective prayer of Your divine armor, Narayana Kavacham, upon Indra, Vishwarupa enabled him to emerge victorious in battle. O Lord, Your divine glory is truly all-conquering!

प्राक्शूरसेनविषये किल चित्रकेतुः
पुत्राग्रही नृपतिरङ्गिरसः प्रभावात् ।
लब्ध्वैकपुत्रमथ तत्र हते सपत्नीसङ्घैः
अमुह्यदवशस्तव माययासौ ॥23.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्राक् long ago, शूरसेन-विषये in the kingdom of Shurasena, किल indeed, चित्रकेतुः Chitraketu, पुत्र-आग्रही longing intensely for a son, नृपतिः king, अङ्गिरसः of Sage Angiras, प्रभावात् by the power and grace, लब्ध्वा having obtained, एक-पुत्रम् a single son, अथ thereafter, तत्र that son, हते having been poisoned/killed, सपत्नी-सङ्घैः by the group of co-wives, अमुह्यत् was bewildered and plunged into grief, अवशः helpless, तव Your, मायया by the power of Maya, असौ he

Simple Meaning: Long ago, in the kingdom of Shurasena, there lived a king named Chitraketu who longed intensely for a son. By the grace and blessing of Sage Angiras, he eventually obtained a single son through his elder queen. However, out of extreme jealousy, his other co-wives poisoned the child to death. Overwhelmed by Your powerful Maya (delusion), the helpless king was plunged into deep grief and delusion.

तं नारदस्तु सममङ्गिरसा दयालुः
सम्प्राप्य तावदुपदर्श्य सुतस्य जीवम् ।
कस्यास्मि पुत्र इति तस्य गिरा विमोहं
त्यक्त्वा त्वदर्चनविधौ नृपतिं न्ययुङ्क्त ॥23.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him (King Chitraketu), नारदः तु Sage Narada indeed, समम् along with, अङ्गिरसा Sage Angiras, दयालुः compassionate, सम्प्राप्य approaching, तावत् then, उपदर्श्य showing/bringing forth, सुतस्य of the dead son, जीवम् the jiva (soul), कस्य अस्मि पुत्रः "Whose son am I?", इति thus, तस्य गिरा by the soul's words, विमोहम् delusion and grief, त्यक्त्वा dispelling/abandoning, त्वत्-अर्चन-विधौ in the practice of Your worship, नृपतिम् the king, न्ययुङ्क्त initiated/directed

Simple Meaning: Moved by deep compassion, Sage Narada along with Sage Angiras visited the grieving king. Sage Narada summoned the soul (jiva) of the deceased child. The soul spoke directly to the king asking, “Whose son am I (in this endless cycle of births)?” Hearing these words from the soul, King Chitraketu broke free from his grief and delusion. The sages then initiated him into the sacred path of worshipping You.

स्तोत्रं च मन्त्रमपि नारदतोऽथ लब्ध्वा
तोषाय शेषवपुषो ननु ते तपस्यन् ।
विद्याधराधिपतितां स हि सप्तरात्रे
लब्ध्वाप्यकुण्ठमतिरन्वभजद्भवन्तम् ॥23.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्तोत्रम् hymnal prayer, च and, मन्त्रम् sacred mantra, अपि also, नारदतः from Sage Narada, अथ thereafter, लब्ध्वा having received, तोषाय for the pleasing, शेष-वपुषः of Your form as Lord Shesha, ननु indeed, ते Your, तपस्यन् performing austerities, विद्याधर-अधिपतिताम् the status of the ruler of Vidyadharas, सः he (Chitraketu), हि indeed, सप्त-रात्रे in seven nights, लब्ध्वा अपि even after attaining, अकुण्ठ-मतिः with unshakeable devotion and devotion undistracted by worldly pleasures, अन्वभजत् worshipped/served continuously, भवन्तम् You

Simple Meaning: Receiving the sacred hymn and mantra from Sage Narada, King Chitraketu performed intense austerities to please You in Your form as Lord Shesha. Within just seven nights, he attained the exalted status of the king of Vidyadharas. Yet, completely unattached to these celestial glories, he remained steadfast in his mind and continued to worship You with unwavering devotion.

तस्मै मृणालधवलेन सहस्रशीर्ष्णा
रूपेण बद्धनुतिसिद्धगणावृतेन ।
प्रादुर्भवन्नचिरतो नुतिभिः प्रसन्नो
दत्वाऽऽत्मतत्त्वमनुगृह्य तिरोदधाथ ॥23.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मै unto him (King Chitraketu), मृणाल-धवलेन as white as a lotus stalk, सहस्र-शीर्ष्णा having a thousand heads, रूपेण in a form, बद्ध-नुति-सिद्ध-गण-आवृतेन surrounded by groups of Siddhas offering praises, प्रादुर्भवन् appearing/manifesting, अचिरतः quickly, नुतिभिः by his prayers, प्रसन्नः being pleased, दत्वा granting, आत्म-तत्त्वम् the knowledge of the Supreme Self, अनुगृह्य blessing him, तिरोदधाथ You vanished from sight

Simple Meaning: Pleased with King Chitraketu's steadfast devotion, You quickly manifested before him in Your glorious divine form as Lord Ananta (Adisesha)—white as a pure lotus stalk, bearing a thousand heads, and surrounded by assemblies of Siddhas singing Your praises. Gratified by his prayers, You bestowed upon him the supreme wisdom of the Self, blessed him, and then vanished from his sight.

त्वद्भक्तमौलिरथ सोऽपि च लक्षलक्षं
वर्षाणि हर्षुलमना भुवनेषु कामम् ।
सङ्गापयन् गुणगणं तव सुन्दरीभिः
सङ्गातिरेकरहितो ललितं चचार ॥23.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-भक्त-मौलिः the crest-jewel among Your devotees, अथ then, सः अपि च he also, लक्ष-लक्षम् lakhs upon lakhs, वर्षाणि for years, हर्षुल-मनाः with a joyful mind, भुवनेषु through the worlds, कामम् at his free will, सङ्गापयन् causing to sing, गुण-गणम् the multitude of Your divine glories, तव Your, सुन्दरीभिः along with beautiful women (celestial damsels), सङ्ग-अतिरेक-रहितः completely free from attachment or lustful desire, ललितम् gracefully/joyfully, चचार wandered

Simple Meaning: That crest-jewel among Your devotees, King Chitraketu, spent hundreds of thousands of years wandering freely across various realms with a heart full of joy. Accompanied by beautiful celestial damsels who sang Your divine glories under his direction, he lived completely unattached to sensual pleasures, moving joyfully wherever he wished, entirely absorbed in You.

अत्यन्तसङ्गविलयाय भवत्प्रणुन्नो
नूनं स रूप्यगिरिमाप्य महत्समाजे ।
निश्शङ्कमङ्ककृतवल्लभमङ्गजारिं
तं शङ्करं परिहसन्नुमयाभिशेपे ॥23.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: अत्यन्त-सङ्ग-विलयाय for the complete destruction/dissolution of all worldly attachments, भवत्-प्रणुन्नः prompted/inspired by You, नूनम् surely/indeed, सः he (Chitraketu), रूप्य-गिरिम् the Silver Mountain - Mount Kailasha, आप्य reaching, महत्-समाजे in the assembly of great sages, निश्शङ्कम् without any hesitation/inhibition, अङ्क-कृत-वल्लभम् holding his beloved consort (Parvati) on his lap, अङ्गज-अरिम् the enemy of Kamadeva (Shiva), तम् Shankara, परिहसन् lightheartedly remarking/smiling at, उमया by Uma - Parvati, अभिशेपे was cursed

Simple Meaning: Surely prompted by Your divine will to completely free him from his subtle traces of worldly attachment, Chitraketu arrived at Mount Kailasha. There, in the midst of a grand assembly of exalted sages, he saw Shiva seated with his beloved Parvati on his lap and mocked at Shiva. Parvati took offense and cursed Chitraketu.

निस्सम्भ्रमस्त्वयमयाचितशापमोक्षो
वृत्रासुरत्वमुपगम्य सुरेन्द्रयोधी ।
भक्त्यात्मतत्त्वकथनैः समरे विचित्रं
शत्रोरपि भ्रममपास्य गतः पदं ते ॥23.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: निस्सम्भ्रमः तु अयम् unperturbed indeed he, अयाचित-शाप-मोक्षः without seeking release from the curse, वृत्रासुरत्वम् उपगम्य attaining the form of Vritrasura, सुरेन्द्र-योधी fighting with the lord of devas Indra, भक्त्या आत्म-तत्त्व-कथनैः through devotion and discourses on the supreme truth of the self, समरे in battle, विचित्रम् wonderfully, शत्रोः अपि even of his enemy, भ्रमम् अपास्य removing the delusion, गतः पदम् ते reached Your supreme abode

Simple Meaning: Unperturbed by the curse and not even seeking redemption from it, Chitraketu took birth as the asura named Vritrasura. While fighting with Indra, the king of devas, he dispelled the delusion of even his enemy through his devotion and teachings on spiritual truth right in the battlefield, and ultimately attained Your supreme divine abode.

त्वत्सेवनेन दितिरिन्द्रवधोद्यताऽपि
तान्प्रत्युतेन्द्रसुहृदो मरुतोऽभिलेभे ।
दुष्टाशयेऽपि शुभदैव भवन्निषेवा
तत्तादृशस्त्वमव मां पवनालयेश ॥23.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-सेवनेन through Your worship/service, दितिः Diti, इन्द्र-वध-उद्यता अपि even though intent on destroying Indra, तान् प्रत्युत on the contrary to those, इन्द्र-सुहृदः friends of Indra, मरुतः the Maruts, अभिलेभे obtained, दुष्ट-आशये अपि even when done with malicious intent, शुभ-दा एव bestows only good results, भवत्-निषेवा worshipping You, तत्-तादृशः त्वम् You of such a merciful nature, अव माम् protect me, पवन-आलय-ईश O Lord of abode of the Wind - Guruvayoor

Simple Meaning: Diti worshipped You with the malicious intention of obtaining a son to kill Indra. Through that very worship of You, she gave birth to the Maruts, who ended up becoming the great friends and allies of Indra. Worshipping You bestows auspicious results even when done with evil motives. O Lord of Guruvayoor, who is possessed of such boundless mercy, please protect me.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
