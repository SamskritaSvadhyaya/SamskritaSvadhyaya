Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 86

Krishna kills Salva and Dantavakra

Krishna saves Draupadi’s honor during the game of dice and from the wrath of Sage Durvasa

Battle of Kurukshetra

साल्वो भैष्मीविवाहे यदुबलविजितश्चन्द्रचूडाद्विमानं
विन्दन् सौभं स मायी त्वयि वसति कुरुंस्त्वत्पुरीमभ्यभाङ्क्षीत् ।
प्रद्युम्नस्तं निरुन्धन्निखिलयदुभटैर्न्यग्रहीदुग्रवीर्यं
तस्यामात्यं द्युमन्तं व्यजनि च समरः सप्तविंशत्यहान्तः ॥86.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: साल्वः King Salva, भैष्मी-विवाहे during the marriage of Rukmini (daughter of Bhishma), यदु-बल-विजितः defeated by the army of the Yadavas, चन्द्रचूडात् from Lord Shiva (the one who has moon on his forehead), विमानम् an aerial chariot, विन्दन् obtaining, सौभम् named Saubha, सः he, मायी practitioner of black magic/sorcery, त्वयि वसति कुरुन् when You were residing in the city of the Kurus - Indraprastha, त्वत्-पुरीम् Your city of Dwaraka, अभ्यभाङ्क्षीत् attacked and damaged, प्रद्युम्नः Pradyumna, तम् निरुन्धन् resisting him, अखिल-यदु-भटैः with all the Yadava warriors, न्यग्रहीत subdued and defeated, उग्र-वीर्यम् who possessed terrifying prowess, तस्य आमात्यम् his minister, द्युमन्तम् named Dyuman, व्यजनि च and there took place, समरः a fierce battle, सप्त-विंशति-अहान्तः lasting for twenty-seven days

Simple Meaning: King Salva, who had been soundly defeated by the Yadava army during Rukmini's marriage, performed severe penance to Lord Shiva and obtained a magical aerial chariot named Saubha. Being a master of sorcery, he waited for the time when You were away at Kurukshetra and launched a fierce attack on Your city of Dvaraka. Pradyumna, leading the brave Yadava warriors, blocked his advance and severely wounded Salva's formidable minister, Dyuman. A terrible battle ensued between the two armies, lasting for twenty-seven days.

तावत्त्वं रामशाली त्वरितमुपगतः खण्डितप्रायसैन्यं
सौभेशं तं न्यरुन्धाः स च किल गदया शार्ङ्गमभ्रंशयत्ते ।
मायातातं व्यहिंसीदपि तव पुरतस्तत्त्वयापि क्षणार्धं
नाज्ञायीत्याहुरेके तदिदमवमतं व्यास एव न्यषेधीत् ॥86.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: तावत् meanwhile / by then, त्वम् You, राम-शाली accompanied by Balarama, त्वरितम् quickly, उपगतः having returned, खण्डित-प्राय-सैन्यम् whose army was almost completely destroyed, सौभ-ईशम् the master of Saubha (Salva), तम् him, न्यरुन्धाः blocked, सः च and he, किल indeed, गदया with his mace, शार्ङ्गम् Your bow Sharnga, अभ्रंशयत् struck down/knocked away, ते from Your hand, माया-तातम् an illusory form of Your father (Vasudeva), व्यहिंसीत् अपि and even killed, तव पुरतः in front of You, तत् that, त्वया अपि by You too, क्षण-अर्धम् for half a moment, न अज्ञायि was not recognized as illusion, इति आहुः एके so say some scholars, तत् इदम् that view, अवमतम् is unauthoritative/disregarded, व्यासः एव Sage Vyasa himself, न्यषेधीत् explicitly refuted

Simple Meaning: Meanwhile, You returned quickly to Dwaraka accompanied by Balarama and confronted Salva, whose army had already been largely decimated by Pradyumna. During the battle, Salva struck Your bow Sharnga with his mace, causing it to slip from Your hand. He then used magic to create an illusory figure of Your father Vasudeva and killed him before Your eyes. Some commentators claim that even You were deluded by this illusion for half a moment; however, Sage Vyasa explicitly refutes this view (holding that You, being the omniscient Lord of Maya, could never be deceived).

क्षिप्त्वा सौभं गदाचूर्णितमुदकनिधौ मङ्क्षु साल्वेऽपि चक्रे-
णोत्कृत्ते दन्तवक्त्रः प्रसभमभिपतन्नभ्यमुञ्चद्गदां ते ।
कौमोदक्या हतोऽसावपि सुकृतनिधिश्चैद्यवत्प्रापदैक्यं
सर्वेषामेष पूर्वं त्वयि धृतमनसां मोक्षणार्थोऽवतारः ॥86.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्षिप्त्वा throwing down, सौभम् the aerial chariot Saubha, गदा-चूर्णितम् pulverized into pieces by Your mace (Kaumodaki), उदक-निधौ into the ocean, मङ्क्षु quickly, साल्वे अपि and when Salva too, चक्रेण उत्कृत्ते was decapitated by Your Sudarshana Chakra, दन्तवक्त्रः Dantavaktra, प्रसभम् furiously/violently, अभिपतन् अभ्यमुञ्चत् rushing forward hurled, गदाम् ते a mace at You, कौमोदक्या by Your mace Kaumodaki, हतः असौ अपि he too being struck and slain, सुकृत-निधिः a reservoir of past spiritual merit, चैद्यवत्-प्रापत्-ऐक्यम् attained oneness with You just like Shishupala (the king of Chedi), सर्वेषाम् एषः for all these beings, पूर्वम् who had previously, त्वयि धृत-मनसाम् fixed their minds continuously on You, मोक्षण-अर्थः अवतारः this incarnation was specifically meant for their liberation

Simple Meaning: Striking the aerial city Saubha with Your mace, You smashed it to pieces and cast it into the ocean, and swiftly decapitated Salva with Your Sudarshana Chakra. Thereupon, his ally Dantavaktra rushed at You furiously, hurling his mace. You struck him down with Your mace Kaumodaki. Though an adversary in form, Dantavaktra possessed immense spiritual merit accumulated through intense concentration on You across births, and he instantly merged into Your divine form, just as Shishupala had done. Indeed, this incarnation of Yours was specifically undertaken to bestow liberation upon those who continuously centered their minds upon You.

त्वय्यायातेऽथ जाते किल कुरुसदसि द्यूतके संयतायाः
क्रन्दन्त्या याज्ञसेन्याः सकरुणमकृथाश्चेलमालामनन्ताम् ।
अन्नान्तप्राप्तशर्वांशजमुनिचकितद्रौपदीचिन्तितोऽथ
प्राप्तः शाकान्नमश्नन् मुनिगणमकृथास्तृप्तिमन्तं वनान्ते ॥86.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वयि आयाते when You had returned to Dwaraka, अथ then, जाते किल when there occurred indeed, कुरु-सदसि in the assembly of the Kauravas, द्यूतके the gambling match, संयतायाः who was seized, क्रन्दन्त्याः who was crying out in distress, याज्ञसेन्याः of Yaajnaseni (Draupadi), सकरुणम् mercifully, अकृथाः You created, चेल-मालाम् अनन्ताम् an endless stream of garments, अन्न-अन्त-प्राप्त-शर्व-अंशज-मुनि-चकित-द्रौपदी-चिन्तितः अथ remembered by Draupadi who was terrified of Sage Durvasa (born of a portion of Lord Shiva) who arrived after all food was exhausted, प्राप्तः arriving there, शाक-अन्नम् अश्नन् eating a tiny leaf of remaining vegetable/food, मुनि-गणम् the entire assembly of sages, अकृथाः तृप्तिमन्तम् You made completely satisfied, वनान्ते in the forest

Simple Meaning: After You returned to Dwaraka, the tragic gambling match took place in the assembly hall of the Kauravas. When Draupadi was dragged into the court and wept piteously for help, You mercifully provided her with an unending stream of robes to protect her modesty. Later, while the Pandavas were in exile in the forest, Sage Durvasa—a part incarnation of Shiva—arrived at their hermitage along with his disciples after all the food had been consumed. Terrified of his curse, Draupadi prayed intensely to You. You appeared instantly, ate a single particle of leftover vegetable stuck to her pot, and thereby magically filled the stomachs of Durvasa and all his disciples with complete satisfaction.

युद्धोद्योगेऽथ मन्त्रे मिलति सति वृतः फल्गुनेन त्वमेकः
कौरव्ये दत्तसैन्यः करिपुरमगमो दूत्यकृत् पाण्डवार्थम् ।
भीष्मद्रोणादिमान्ये तव खलु वचने धिक्कृते कौरवेण
व्यावृण्वन् विश्वरूपं मुनिसदसि पुरीं क्षोभयित्वागतोऽभूः ॥86.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: युद्ध-उद्योगे अथ then during the preparations for war, मन्त्रे मिलति सति when consultations took place, वृतः chosen, फल्गुनेन by Phalguna (Arjuna), त्वम् एकः You alone (unarmed), कौरव्ये to Duryodhana (the scion of Kuru), दत्त-सैन्यः having given Your entire Narayani army, करिपुरम् अगमः You went to Hastinapura (Karipura), दूत्य-कृत् acting as a peace envoy, पाण्डव-अर्थम् on behalf of the Pandavas, भीष्म-द्रोण-आदि-मान्ये which was deeply respected by Bhishma, Drona, and other elders, तव खलु Your indeed, वचने धिक्कृते when Your word was contemptuously rejected, कौरवेण by Duryodhana, व्यावृण्वन् revealing, विश्वरूपम् Your divine Universal Form (Vishvarupa), मुनि-सदसि in the assembly of sages and elders, पुरीम् the entire city of Hastinapura, क्षोभयित्वा shaking and perturbing, आगतः अभूः You returned to the Pandava camp

Simple Meaning: When preparations for war began and consultations were held, Arjuna chose You alone to be on his side unarmed, while Duryodhana accepted Your formidable Narayani army. You then traveled to Hastinapura as a peace envoy on behalf of the Pandavas. Although Your righteous proposal was praised and respected by elders like Bhishma and Drona, it was arrogantly rejected by Duryodhana. In response, You revealed Your terrifying Universal Form in that assembly of sages and elders, thoroughly shaking the entire city with awe and panic before returning.

जिष्णोस्त्वं कृष्ण सूतः खलु समरमुखे बन्धुघाते दयालुं
खिन्नं तं वीक्ष्य वीरं किमिदमयि सखे नित्य एकोऽयमात्मा ।
को वध्यः कोऽत्र हन्ता तदिह वधभियं प्रोज्झ्य मय्यर्पितात्मा
धर्म्यं युद्धं चरेति प्रकृतिमनयथा दर्शयन् विश्वरूपम् ॥86.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: जिष्णोः of Arjuna (Jishnu), त्वम् You, कृष्ण O Krishna, सूतः the charioteer, खलु indeed, समर-मुखे at the front of the battlefield, बन्धु-घाते at the thought of killing his own kinsmen, दयालुम् full of compassion, खिन्नम् despondent and grieving, तम्-वीरम् that heroic warrior Arjuna, वीक्ष्य seeing, किम् इदम् अयि सखे what is this O friend, नित्यः eternal, एकः अयम् आत्मा this supreme soul is one and imperishable, कः वध्यः who is to be killed, कः अत्र हन्ता who here is the killer, तत्-इह therefore in this world, वध-भियम् the fear of killing or being killed, प्रोज्झ्य completely renouncing, मयि अर्पित-आत्मा with your mind surrendered to Me, धर्म्यम् युद्धम् righteous battle, चर perform, इति thus, प्रकृतिम् अनयथा You brought him back to his true nature as a warrior, दर्शयन् revealing, विश्वरूपम् Your Universal Form

Simple Meaning: O Krishna, serving as the charioteer of Arjuna at the forefront of the battlefield, You saw that great hero suddenly overcome with grief and misplaced compassion at the thought of killing his own kinsmen. You instructed him: "What is this weakness, O friend? The Atman is eternal, unchangeable, and one without a second. Who is killed, and who is the killer? Abandon all fear of death, surrender your mind completely to Me, and fulfill your duty by fighting this righteous war." Revealing Your grand Universal Form, You restored his courage and brought him back to his true nature.

भक्तोत्तंसेऽथ भीष्मे तव धरणिभरक्षेपकृत्यैकसक्ते
नित्यं नित्यं विभिन्दत्ययुतसमधिकं प्राप्तसादे च पार्थे ।
निश्शस्त्रत्वप्रतिज्ञां विजहदरिवरं धारयन् क्रोधशाली-
वाधावन् प्राञ्जलिं तं नतशिरसमथो वीक्ष्य मोदादपागाः ॥86.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: भक्त-उत्तमसे in that crest-jewel among devotees, अथ then, भीष्मे when Bhishma, तव Your, धरणि-भर-क्षेप-कृत्य-एक-सक्ते who was singularly devoted to fulfilling Your intent of removing the burden of the Earth, नित्यम्-नित्यम् day after day, विभिन्दति striking down and tormenting, अयुत-समधिकम् more than ten thousand warriors, प्राप्त-सादे च and when he had become thoroughly exhausted, पार्थे Partha (the son of Pritha - Arjuna), निश्शस्त्रत्व-प्रतिज्ञाम् Your solemn vow to remain unarmed, विजहत् abandoning, अरि-वरम् a heavy chariot wheel, धारयन् holding high, क्रोधशाली इव appearing as if filled with intense wrath, आधावन् rushing forward on foot, प्राञ्जलिम् with folded hands, तम् him (Bhishma), नत-शिरसम् with bowed head, अथो then, वीक्ष्य beholding, मोदात् with joy, अपागाः You withdrew and returned to the chariot

Simple Meaning: Bhishma, that supreme jewel among Your devotees, fought relentlessly to fulfill Your ultimate purpose of lightening the Earth's burden, daily slaying over ten thousand warriors and reducing Arjuna to extreme exhaustion. Seeing His friend in grave peril, You broke Your vow of remaining weaponless in the war. Seizing a heavy chariot wheel as Your weapon, You charged furiously toward Bhishma on foot as if consumed by rage. Beholding You rushing toward him, Bhishma dropped his bow, bowed his head, and stood with folded hands in deep devotion. Seeing his surrender, You gladly set aside Your anger and returned to the chariot.

युद्धे द्रोणस्य हस्तिस्थिररणभगदत्तेरितं वैष्णवास्त्रं
वक्षस्याधत्त चक्रस्थगितरविमहाः प्रार्दयत्सिन्धुराजम् ।
नागास्त्रे कर्णमुक्ते क्षितिमवनमयन् केवलं कृत्तमौलिं
तत्रे त्रापि पार्थं किमिव नहि भवान् पाण्डवानामकार्षीत् ॥86.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: युद्धे in the war, द्रोणस्य during the command of Drona, हस्ति-स्थिर-रण-भगदत्त-ईरितम् launched by King Bhagadatta who fought firmly mounted on an elephant, वैष्णव-अस्त्रम् the Vaishnavastra weapon, वक्षसि on Your own chest, आधत्त You received, चक्र-स्थगित-रवि-महाः hiding the brilliance of the sun with Your Sudarshana Chakra, प्रार्दयत्-सिन्धुराजम् You caused the destruction of Jayadratha (the king of the Sindhu region), नाग-अस्त्रे when the Nagastra weapon, कर्ण-मुक्ते was discharged by Karna, क्षितिम् the earth, अवनमयन् causing to sink down (by pressing the chariot floor with Your foot), केवलम् only, कृत्त-मौलिम् with his crown cut off, तत्र एत्र अपि there and also here, पार्थम् Arjuna (Partha), त्रापि saved, किम् इव न हि what indeed did, भवान् You, पाण्डवानाम् अकार्षीत् not do for the sake of the Pandavas

Simple Meaning: During the battle under Drona's command, when King Bhagadatta hurled the invincible Vaishnavastra from atop his elephant, You stepped forward and took the lethal weapon upon Your own chest, transforming it into a garland. Later, by masking the sun's light with Your Sudarshana Chakra, You created an artificial eclipse to enable Arjuna to slay Jayadratha. And when Karna released the deadly Nagastra aimed at Arjuna's neck, You pressed the floor of the chariot with Your foot, causing it to sink five inches into the earth so that the missile merely struck off his crown. What did You not do, O Lord, for the sake of the Pandavas?

युद्धादौ तीर्थगामी स खलु हलधरो नैमिशक्षेत्रमृच्छन्
अप्रत्युत्थायिसूतक्षयकृदथ सुतं तत्पदे कल्पयित्वा ।
यज्ञघ्नं वल्कलं पर्वणि परिदलयन् स्नाततीर्थो रणान्ते
सम्प्राप्तो भीमदुर्योधनरणमशमं वीक्ष्य यातः पुरीं ते ॥86.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: युद्ध-आदौ before the commencement of the war, तीर्थ-गामी setting out on a pilgrimage to sacred places, सः he, खलु indeed, हलधरः Balarama (the holder of the plough), नैमिशक्षेत्रम् ऋच्छन् arriving at the sacred holy grove of Naimisharanya, अप्रत्युत्थायि-सूत-क्षय-कृत् eliminating Romaharshana Suta who failed to rise and pay proper respect, अथ then, सुतम् his son (Ugrashravas), तत्-पदे in his place, कल्पयित्वा establishing/installing, यज्ञ-घ्नम् who destroyed the sacred sacrifices, वल्कलम् the asura named Valkala, पर्वणि on every full moon and new moon day, परिदलयन् slaying/tearing apart, स्नात-तीर्थः having bathed in all the holy waters, रण-अन्ते toward the end of the war, सम्प्राप्तः arriving back, भीम-दुर्योधन-रणम् the fierce mace duel between Bhima and Duryodhana, अशमम् unstoppable / relentless, वीक्ष्य beholding, यातः departed for, पुरीम् ते Your city of Dwaraka

Simple Meaning: Before the war began, Balarama set out on a pilgrimage to various holy places. Upon reaching Naimisharanya, he struck dead Romaharshana Suta for failing to show due reverence, installing Romaharshana's son Ugrashravas in his place as the narrator of the Puranas. He then vanquished the asura Valkala, who disrupted the sacrifices of the sages on every full moon and new moon day, and completed his purification baths across all holy rivers. Returning toward the conclusion of the war, seeing the relentless, intractable mace duel between Bhima and Duryodhana, he departed for Your city of Dwaraka.

Note: The name of the asura is mentioned as Balvala in the Shrimad Bhagavatam. Here the poet mentions as Valkala.

संसुप्तद्रौपदेयक्षपणहतधियं द्रौणिमेत्य त्वदुक्त्या
तन्मुक्तं ब्राह्ममस्त्रं समहृत विजयो मौलिरत्नं च जह्रे ।
उच्छित्यै पाण्डवानां पुनरपि च विशत्युत्तरागर्भमस्त्रे
रक्षन्नङ्गुष्ठमात्रः किल जठरमगाश्चक्रपाणिर्विभो त्वम् ॥86.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: संसुप्त-द्रौपदेय-क्षपण-हत-धियम् the evil minded one who killed the sleeping sons of Draupadi, द्रौणिम् the son of Drona - Ashvatthama, एत्य having confronted, त्वत्-उक्त्या on Your instruction, तत्-मुक्तम् released by him, ब्राह्मम् अस्त्रम् the Brahmashira astra, समहृत withdrawn, विजयः Vijaya (Arjuna), मौलि-रत्नम् च and the gemstone on his head, जह्रे took away, उच्छित्यै for the total destruction, पाण्डवानाम् of the Pandavas, पुनः अपि च and furthermore, विशति उत्तरा-गर्भम् अस्त्रे when the missile entered the womb of Uttara, रक्षन् protecting, अङ्गुष्ठ-मात्रः assuming a thumb-sized form, किल indeed, जठरम् अगाः You entered her womb, चक्रपाणिः holding the Sudarshana Chakra in Your hand, विभो O All-pervading Lord, त्वम् You

Simple Meaning: The evil minded Ashvatthama killed the sons of Draupadi while they were asleep.  Arjuna confronted him and unleashed the Brahmastra on him. But on Your advice, he recalled the weapon, and stripped him of the jewel on his forehead. Driven by malice to destroy the Pandava lineage completely, Ashvatthama redirected the missile into the womb of Uttara to kill her unborn child Parikshit. O All-pervading Lord, holding the Sudarshana Chakra and assuming a thumb-sized form, You entered Uttara's womb and protected the child from the fiery weapon.

धर्मौघं धर्मसूनोरभिदधदखिलं छन्दमृत्युस्स भीष्मः
त्वां पश्यन् भक्तिभूम्नैव हि सपदि ययौ निष्कलब्रह्मभूयम् ।
संयाज्याथाश्वमेधैस्त्रिभिरतिमहितैर्धर्मजं पूर्णकामं
स्म्प्राप्तो द्वरकां त्वं पवनपुरपते पाहि मां सर्वरोगात् ॥86.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: धर्म-ओघम् the profound stream of dharma/righteous duties, धर्म-सूनोः to the son of Dharma - (Yudhishthira), अभिदधत् expounding in full, अखिलम् complete, छन्द-मृत्युः सः he who possessed the power to choose the time of his own death, भीष्मः Bhishma, त्वाम् You, पश्यन् gazing upon, भक्ति-भूम्ना एव हि through the sheer grandeur of intense devotion alone, सपदि immediately, ययौ attained, निष्कल-ब्रह्म-भूयम् the supreme state of the partless, unconditioned Brahman, संयाज्य having made to perform, अथ then, अश्वमेधैः त्रिभिः by the three Ashwamedha sacrifices, अति-महितैः by magnificent and most exalted, धर्मजम् for the one born to Dharma - Yudhishthira, पूर्ण-कामम् whose every desire was now fully fulfilled, सम्प्राप्तः द्वारकाम् त्वम् You returned to Dwaraka, पवनपुरपते O Lord of Guruvayur, पाहि protect, माम् me, सर्व-रोगात् from all diseases and afflictions

Simple Meaning: Expounder of the vast lore of righteousness to King Yudhishthira from his bed of arrows, the great Bhishma—who held power over his own death—fixed his eyes upon You. Through the sheer intensity of his sublime devotion, he shed his mortal frame and immediately merged into the supreme, unconditioned Brahman. Thereafter, You enabled King Yudhishthira to perform three magnificent Ashwamedha sacrifices, whose all the desires were fulfilled by them, before returning to Your abode at Dwaraka. O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect me from all my ailments and diseases!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
