Krishna kills Kamsa

दशकम् ७५

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 75

Krishna kills Kamsa

प्रातः सन्त्रस्तभोजक्षितिपतिवचसा प्रस्तुते मल्लतूर्ये
सङ्घे राज्ञां च मञ्चानभिययुषि गते नन्दगोपेऽपि हर्म्यम् ।
कंसे सौधाधिरूढे त्वमपि सहबलः सानुगश्चारुवेषो
रङ्गद्वारं गतोऽभूः कुपितकुवलयापीडनागावलीढम् ॥75.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रातः in the morning, सन्त्रस्त-भोज-क्षितिपति-वचसा by the command of the terrified king of the Bhojas (Kamsa), प्रस्तुते when commenced, मल्ल-तूर्ये the sounding of the wrestling drums, सङ्घे of the assembly, राज्ञाम् of kings, च and, मञ्चान् to the raised platforms, अभिययुषि having taken their seats, गते having proceeded, नन्दगोपे अपि even Nanda Gopa, हर्म्यम् to the gallery, कंसे when Kamsa, सौध-अधिरूढे had climbed onto his high royal tower, त्वम् You, अपि also, सह-बलः along with Balarama, स-अनुगः accompanied by Your friends, चारु-वेषः beautifully attired, रङ्ग-द्वारम् to the entrance gate of the arena, गतः अभूः arrived, कुपित-कुवलयापीड-नाग-आवलीढम् blocked by the furious elephant Kuvalayapida

Simple Meaning: When morning dawned, the wrestling drums resounded by the order of the terrified Kamsa. As the assembled kings, noblemen, and Nanda Gopa took their seats in the elevated viewing galleries, and Kamsa ascended his high royal balcony, You arrived at the main archway of the arena. Beautifully dressed and accompanied by Balarama and Your devoted cowherd companions, You found the gate blocked by the furious elephant Kuvalayapida.

पापिष्ठापेहि मार्गाद्द्रुतमिति वचसा निष्ठुरक्रुद्धबुद्धेः
अम्बष्ठस्य प्रणोदादधिकजवजुषा हस्तिना गृह्यमाणः ।
केलीमुक्तोऽथ गोपीकुचकलशचिरस्पर्धिनं कुम्भमस्य
व्याहत्यालीयथास्त्वं चरणभुवि पुनर्निर्गतो वल्गुहासी ॥75.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: पापिष्ठ O sinful ones, अपेहि move away, मार्गात् द्रुतम् quickly from the path, इति वचसा with these harsh words, निष्ठुर-क्रुद्ध-बुद्धेः of the cruel and wrathful, अम्बष्ठस्य of the elephant driver (mahout), प्रणोदात् by the prompting, अधिक-जव-जुषा charging with extreme speed, हस्तिना by the elephant, गृह्यमाणः being seized, केली-मुक्तः अथ freeing Yourself effortlessly as if in play then, गोपी-कुच-कलश-चिर-स्पर्धिनम् which were always competing with the pitcher-like breasts of the gopis, कुम्भम् the temple or forehead, अस्य of this elephant, व्याहत्य striking, आलीयथाः त्वम् You concealed Yourself, चरण-भुवि between its legs, पुनः again, निर्गतः emerging, वल्गु-हासी smiling sweetly

Simple Meaning: The cruel and wrathful mahout shouted harshly, "O sinful ones, get out of the way immediately!" and goaded the furious elephant to charge at You with terrific speed. As the elephant wrapped its trunk around You, You slipped out effortlessly as if playing a game. You then struck a mighty blow upon the elephant's massive forehead—which had long vied in rounded beauty with the pitcher-like breasts of the gopis—and playfully hid between its legs before emerging with a sweet smile.

हस्तप्राप्योऽप्यगम्यो झटिति मुनिजनस्येव धावन् गजेन्द्रं
क्रीडन्नापात्य भूमौ पुनरभिपततस्तस्य दन्तं सजीवम् ।
मूलादुन्मूल्य तन्मूलगमहितमहामौक्तिकान्यात्ममित्रे
प्रादास्त्वं हारमेभिर्ललितविरचितं राधिकायै दिशेति ॥75.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: हस्त-प्राप्यः अपि although reachable within hand's length, अगम्यः inaccessible, झटिति swiftly, मुनि-जनस्य इव like to the minds of worldly sages, धावन् running, गजेन्द्रम् the lordly elephant, क्रीडन् playing, आपात्य felling down, भूमौ onto the ground, पुनः again, अभिपततः of him who charged forward, तस्य his, दन्तम् the tusk, सजीवम् while he was still alive, मूलात् from the root, उन्मूल्य ripping out, तत्-मूल-ग-महित-महा-मौक्तिकानि the magnificent large pearls situated at the root of that tusk, आत्म-मित्रे to Your close friend Sridama, प्रादाः त्वम् You gave, हारम् a necklace, एभिः with these, ललित-विरचितम् beautifully crafted, राधिकायै to Radha, दिश-इति present it, saying thus

Simple Meaning: Though remaining right near the elephant's trunk, You remained elusive and ungraspable—just as You are to the minds of worldly sages. Playing playfully with the mighty beast, You threw him to the ground. As he charged at You once more in rage, You ripped out his massive tusk from its root while he was still alive. Gathering the magnificent, precious pearls found at the root of the tusk, You handed them to Your dear friend Shridama, saying, "Craft a beautiful necklace from these pearls and present it to Radha!"

गृह्णानं दन्तमंसे युतमथ हलिना रङ्गमङ्गाविशन्तं
त्वां मङ्गल्याङ्गभङ्गीरभसहृतमनोलोचना वीक्ष्य लोकाः ।
हंहो धन्यो हि नन्दो नहि नहि पशुपालाङ्गना नो यशोदा
नो नो धन्येक्षणाः स्मस्त्रिजगति वयमेवेति सर्वे शशंसुः ॥75.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: गृह्णानम् carrying, दन्तम् the tusk, अंसे on Your shoulder, युतम् अथ accompanied then, हलिना by Balarama who holds the plough, रङ्गम् the arena, अङ्ग O Lord, आवशन्तम् entering, त्वाम् You, मङ्गल्य-अङ्ग-भङ्गी-रभस-हृत-मनः-लोचनाः whose minds and eyes were instantly captivated by the supreme auspicious grace of Your form, वीक्ष्य seeing, लोकाः the people, हंहो Oh, धन्यः हि नन्दः blessed, indeed, Nanda, नहि नहि no no, पशुपाल-अङ्गनाः the cowherd women of Vraja, नो or, यशोदा Yasoda, नो नो no no, धन्य-ईक्षणाः possessors of blessed vision, स्मः are, त्रिजगति in all the three worlds, वयम् एव we ourselves, इति thus, सर्वे all of them, शशंसुः praised

Simple Meaning: O Lord, carrying the huge tusk resting upon Your shoulder and accompanied by Balarama, You stepped into the wrestling arena. The minds and eyes of all the gathered spectators were instantly swept away by the incomparable grace and auspicious beauty of Your form. Overwhelmed with awe, the crowds shouted in ecstasy: "Oh, how blessed is Nanda! No, wait—far more blessed are the cowherd maidens of Vraja! Or rather, Yashoda is the most blessed! No, no—we ourselves are the most fortunate beings in all the three worlds, for today our eyes are blessed to behold this divine beauty!"

पूर्णं ब्रह्मैव साक्षान्निरवधि परमानन्दसान्द्रप्रकाशं
गोपेशु त्वं व्यलासीर्न खलु बहुजनैस्तावदावेदितोऽभूः ।
दृष्ट्वाऽथ त्वां तदेदंप्रथममुपगते पुण्यकाले जनौघाः
पूर्णानन्दा विपापाः सरसमभिजगुस्त्वत्कृतानि स्मृतानि ॥75.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: पूर्णम् ब्रह्म एव the absolute Brahman Himself, साक्षात् directly, निरवधि-परमानन्द-सान्द्र-प्रकाशम् the infinite, dense effulgence of supreme bliss, गोपेशु among the cowherds, त्वम् You, व्यलासीः shone, न खलु not indeed, बहु-जनैः by the vast majority of people, तावत् until then, आवेदितः अभूः was known or recognized, दृष्ट्वा अथ seeing now, त्वाम् You, तदा इदम् प्रथमम् for the very first time then, उपगते when arrived, पुण्य-काले the auspicious moment of spiritual fruition, जन-ओघाः the vast multitudes of people, पूर्ण-आनन्दाः filled with absolute supreme bliss, विपापाः cleansed of all sins, सरसम् joyfully and sweetly, अभिजगुः sang aloud, त्वत्-कृतानि Your divine deeds, स्मृतानि remembered and cherished

Simple Meaning: You, who are directly the supreme, non-dual Para Brahman—the infinite embodiment of dense bliss and absolute consciousness—had lived and shone hitherto among the humble cowherds of Gokula, unrecognized by the wider world. But now, as that supreme auspicious moment of cosmic destiny arrived, the vast multitudes of people in Mathura beheld You for the very first time. Instantly liberated from all past sins and filled with unbounded bliss, they began to sing and recount with deep devotional joy all the miraculous deeds You had performed in Vraja.

चाणूरो मल्लवीरस्तदनु नृपगिरा मुष्टिको मुष्टिशाली
त्वां रामं चाभिपेदे झटझटिति मिथो मुष्टिपातातिरूक्षम् ।
उत्पातापातनाकर्षणविविधरणान्यासतां तत्र चित्रं
मृत्योः प्रागेव मल्लप्रभुरगमदयं भूरिशो बन्धमोक्षान् ॥75.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: चाणूरः Chanura, मल्ल-वीरः the champion wrestler, तदनु thereupon, नृप-गिरा at the command of King Kamsa, मुष्टिकः Mushtika, मुष्टि-शाली skilled in fist-combat, त्वाम् You, रामम् च and Balarama, अभिपेदे confronted, झट-झटिति with sharp cracking sounds, मिथः mutually, मुष्टि-पात-अति-रूक्षम् exceedingly harsh with exchange of heavy blow falls, उत्पात-आपातन-आकर्षण-विविध-रणावि-न्यासताम् while executing various combat maneuvers like lifting, throwing down, and dragging, तत्र there, चित्रम् wonderful and ironic it was, मृत्योः प्राक् एव even prior to his physical death, मल्ल-प्रभुः the chief of wrestlers (Chanura), अगमत् अयम् he underwent, भूरिशः repeatedly, बन्ध-मोक्षान् limbs-locking and releasing (as well as spiritual bondage and liberation)

Simple Meaning: Prompted by King Kamsa's command, the championship wrestler Chanura challenged You, while the powerful Mushtika confronted Balarama. A grueling, harsh duel ensued with rapid cracking sounds of colliding fists. As Chanura engaged in intricate combat techniques—lifting, slamming, and dragging—a wondrous irony unfolded: long before his actual death at Your hands, the master wrestler repeatedly experienced bondings and releases (bandha-moksha), prefiguring the ultimate liberation from worldly bondage that You were about to grant him.

हा धिक् कष्टं कुमारौ सुललितवपुषौ मल्लवीरौ कठोरौ
न द्रक्ष्यामो व्रजामस्त्वरितमिति जने भाषमाणे तदानीम् ।
चाणूरं तं करोद्भ्रामणविगलदसुं पोथयामासिथोर्व्यां
पिष्टोऽभून्मुष्टिकोऽपि द्रुतमथ हलिना नष्टशिष्टैर्दधावे ॥75.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: हा धिक् alas, what a pity, कष्टम् what a tragedy, कुमारौ these two young boys, सुललित-वपुषौ possessing extremely delicate and charming bodies, मल्ल-वीरौ the two veteran champion wrestlers, कठोरौ rock-hard and merciless, न द्रक्ष्यामः we shall not watch this, व्रजामः त्वरितम् let us leave quickly, इति जने when the people, भाषमाणे were speaking thus, तदानीम् at that moment, चाणूरम् तम् that Chanura, कर-उद्भ्रामण-विगलत्-असुम् whose life-breath ebbed away as he was whirled around by Your hands, पोथयामासिथ उर्व्याम् You slammed onto the ground, पिष्टः अभूत् was crushed and slain, मुष्टिकः अपि Mushtika as well, द्रुतम् अथ quickly then, हलिना by Balarama, नष्ट-शिष्टैः by the remaining fleeing wrestlers, दधावे fled away in fear

Simple Meaning: Distressed by the stark contrast in the arena, the spectators lamented, "Alas! What a tragedy! These two young boys have such delicate bodies, while these royal wrestlers are rock-hard and ruthless. We cannot bear to watch this mismatch—let us leave at once!" Even as the crowds spoke thus, You whirled Chanura around by His hands until his life-breath ebbed away, slamming his body to the ground. Simultaneously, Balarama crushed Mushtika, causing the remaining wrestlers in the arena to flee in terror.

कंस संवार्य तूर्यं खलमतिरविदन् कार्यमार्यान् पितॄन्
तानाहन्तुं व्याप्तमूर्तेस्तव च समशिषद्दूरमुत्सारणाय ।
रुष्टो दुष्टोक्तिभिस्त्वं गरुड इव गिरिं मञ्चमञ्चन्नुदञ्चत्-
खड्गव्यावल्गदुस्संग्रहमपि च हठात् प्राग्रहीरौग्रसेनिम् ॥75.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: कंसः Kamsa, संवार्य stopping, तूर्यम् the arena drums and music, खल-मतिः the evil-minded one, अविदन् कार्यम् not knowing what to do, आर्यान् पितॄन् तान् those venerable parents (Vasudeva and Devaki) as well as Nanda, आहन्तुम् to slay, व्याप्त-मूर्तेः of You whose form pervades the entire universe, तव Your, च and, समशिषत् ordered, दूरम् उत्सारणाय for driving away to a far distance, रुष्टः enraged, दुष्ट-उक्तिभिः by these wicked orders, त्वम् You, गरुडः-इव like Garuda jumping onto, गिरिम a mountain, मञ्चम् अञ्चन् leaping onto the high balcony tower, उदञ्चत्-खड्ग-व्यावल्ग-दुस्संग्रहम् अपि च even though he was brandishing a flashing sword, moving rapidly, and extremely difficult to catch, हठात् forcefully, प्राग्रहीः You grabbed hold of, औग्रसेनिम् the son of Ugrasena - Kamsa

Simple Meaning: Panicked by the defeat of his prized wrestlers, the evil-minded Kamsa ordered the arena music to stop. Driven to desperation, he screamed orders to slay Your venerable elders—Vasudeva, Devaki, and Nanda —and to drive You and Balarama far out of the city. Enraged by his wicked words, You leaped onto his high royal balcony tower like Garuda springing upon a mountain peak. Though Kamsa brandished his flashing sword and darted about, making himself difficult to seize, You instantly pounced and grabbed him by the hair with force.

सद्यो निष्पिष्टसन्धिं भुवि नरपतिमापात्य तस्योपरिष्टात्
त्वय्यापात्ये तदैव त्वदुपरि पतिता नाकिनां पुष्पवृष्टिः ।
किं किं ब्रूमस्तदानीं सततमपि भिया त्वद्गतात्मा स भेजे
सायुज्यं त्वद्वधोत्था परम परमियं वासना कालनेमेः ॥75.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: सद्यः instantly, निष्पिष्ट-सन्धिम् whose joints were thoroughly shattered, भुवि onto the ground, नरपतिम् the king (Kamsa), आपात्य throwing down, तस्य उपरिष्टात् upon his body, त्वयि आपात्ये when You jumped down, तदा एव at that very moment, त्वत्-उपरि upon You, पतिता showered down, नाकिनाम् of the devas, पुष्प-वृष्टिः a shower of flowers, किम् किम् ब्रूमः what can we possibly say, तदानीम् at that glorious time, सततम् अपि though always, भिया with fear, त्वत्-गत-आत्मा whose mind was absorbed in You, सः he (Kamsa), भेजे attained, सायुज्यम् liberation of complete oneness with You, त्वत्-वध-उत्था born from being slain by You, परम O Supreme Lord, परम् इयम् वासना surely this was the latent impression, कालनेमेः (from his previous birth as) Kalanemi

Simple Meaning: Dragging King Kamsa down, You slammed him to the earth, shattering his joints, and jumped down upon his chest with immense force. At that very moment, a shower of flowers showered upon You from the devas above. What words can describe the wondrous irony of that moment? Because Kamsa's mind had been incessantly fixed on You, even though, out of fear, he instantly attained sayujya-mukti—complete absorption and oneness with Your divine essence upon being slain by Your hands. O Supreme Lord, this final liberation was truly the fulfillment of his soul's long journey originating from his previous incarnation as Kalanemi.

तद्भ्रातृनष्ट पिष्ट्वा द्रुतमथ पितरौ सन्नमन्नुग्रसेनं
कृत्वा राजानमुच्चैर्यदुकुलमखिलं मोदयन् कामदानैः ।
भक्तानामुत्तमं चोद्धवममरगुरोराप्तनीतिं सखायं
लब्ध्वा तुष्टो नगर्यां पवनपुरपते रुन्धि मे सर्वरोगान् ॥75.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्-भ्रातृन् अष्ट the eight brothers of Kamsa, पिष्ट्वा crushing, द्रुतम् अथ quickly then, पितरौ to Your parents (Vasudeva and Devaki), सन्नमन् bowing down in reverence, उग्रसेनम् Ugrasena, कृत्वा राजानम् having installed as king, उच्चैः immensely, यदुकुलम् अखिलम् the entire Yadu clan, मोदयन् pleasing, काम-दानैः by fulfilling all their desires, भक्तानाम् उत्तमम् the crest-jewel among devotees, च and, उद्धवम् Uddhava, अमर-गुरोः आप्त-नीतिम् who had mastered political science directly from Brihaspati (the preceptor of devas), सखायम् as Your dear friend and counselor, लब्ध्वा having gained, तुष्टः well-pleased, नगर्याम् in the city of Mathura, पवनपुरपते O Lord of Guruvayur, रुन्धि eradicate and remove, मे my, सर्व-रोगान् all diseases and afflictions

Simple Meaning: After slaying the eight brothers of Kamsa, You immediately went to Your parents, Vasudeva and Devaki, and bowed at their feet in reverence, releasing them from their bonds. You then crowned Ugrasena as the king of Mathura and brought boundless joy to the entire Yadu clan by granting all their wishes. Reunited with Your beloved friend Uddhava—the jewel among devotees who had learned diplomacy directly from Brihaspati—You resided happily in Mathura. O Lord of Guruvayur, please completely eradicate all my ailments and sufferings!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
