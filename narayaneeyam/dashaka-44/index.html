The naming ceremony (of Krishna)

दशकम् ४४

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 44

The naming ceremony (of Krishna)

गूढं वसुदेवगिरा कर्तुं ते निष्क्रियस्य संस्कारान् ।
हृद्गतहोरातत्त्वो गर्गमुनिस्त्वत् गृहं विभो गतवान् ॥44.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: गूढम् secretly, वसुदेव-गिरा by the word of Vasudeva, कर्तुम् to perform, ते Your, निष्क्रियस्य who are free from all action, संस्कारान् the sacred rituals, हृद्-गत-होरा-तत्त्वः who had the knowledge of astrology and horoscope in his heart, गर्ग-मुनिः Sage Garga, त्वत्-गृहम् Your house, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, गतवान् went to

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord! In accordance with the request of Vasudeva, Sage Garga, who possessed complete knowledge of astrology and horoscope in his heart, secretly came to Your house to perform the sacred rituals for You, who are eternally free from all action.

नन्दोऽथ नन्दितात्मा वृन्दिष्टं मानयन्नमुं यमिनाम् ।
मन्दस्मितार्द्रमूचे त्वत्संस्कारान् विधातुमुत्सुकधीः ॥44.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: नन्दः Nanda, अथ then, नन्दित-आत्मा with a joyful heart, वृन्दिष्टम् highest among the ascetics, मानयन् honoring, अमुम् him, यमिनाम् among the sages, मन्द-स्मित-आर्द्रम् with a gentle smile and affection, ऊचे spoke, त्वत्-संस्कारान् Your sacred rites, विधातुम् to perform, उत्सुक-धीः eager at heart

Simple Meaning: Then Nanda, filled with immense joy in his heart, honored Sage Garga, who was the most eminent among ascetics and sages. Eager at heart to perform Your sacred rites, he spoke to him with a gentle and affectionate smile.

यदुवंशाचार्यत्वात् सुनिभृतमिदमार्य कार्यमिति कथयन् ।
गर्गो निर्गतपुलकश्चक्रे तव साग्रजस्य नामानि ॥44.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: यदु-वंश-आचार्यत्वात् because of being the preceptor of the Yadu dynasty, सुनिभृतम् very secretly, इदम् this, आर्य O noble one, कार्यम् should be done, इति thus, कथयन् saying, गर्गः Garga, निर्गत-पुलकः with goosebumps arising out of ecstasy, चक्रे performed, तव Your, स-अग्रजस्य along with Your elder brother, नामानि the naming rites

Simple Meaning: Saying, "O noble one! Because I am the priest of the Yadu dynasty, this ceremony must be performed very secretly (so the Kamsa does not suspect anything)," Sage Garga, with goosebumps arising out of ecstasy, gave names to You along with Your elder brother.

कथमस्य नाम कुर्वे सहस्रनाम्नो ह्यनन्तनाम्नो वा ।
इति नूनं गर्गमुनिश्चक्रे तव नाम नाम रहसि विभो ॥44.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: कथम् how, अस्य for Him, नाम name, कुर्वे shall I give, सहस्र-नाम्नः who has a thousand names, हि indeed, अनन्त-नाम्नः or who has infinite names, वा or, इति thus, नूनम् surely, गर्ग-मुनिः Sage Garga, चक्रे gave, तव Your, नाम name, नाम certainly/surely, रहसि in secret, विभो O All-Pervading Lord

Simple Meaning: "How shall I give a name to Him who already possesses a thousand names, or rather infinite names?" Thinking thus, Sage Garga surely gave You Your name, in secret, O All-Pervading Lord!

कृषिधातुणकाराभ्यां सत्तानन्दात्मतां किलाभिलपत् ।
जगदघकर्षित्वं वा कथयदृषिः कृष्णनाम ते व्यतनोत् ॥44.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृषि-धातु-ण-काराभ्याम् by the root krish and the letter na, सत्ता-आनन्द-आत्मताम् the nature of existence and bliss, किल indeed, अभिलपत् expressing, जगत्-अघ-कर्षित्वम् the removal of the sins of the world, वा or, कथयत् indicating, ऋषिः the sage Garga, कृष्ण-नाम the name Krishna, ते to You, व्यतनोत् bestowed

Simple Meaning: Expressing Your true nature as Existence and Bliss through the combination of the verbal root krish and the suffix na, or indicating Your power to remove all the sins of the world, the sage Garga bestowed upon You the name Krishna.

अन्यांश्च नामभेदान् व्याकुर्वन्नग्रजे च रामादीन् ।
अतिमानुषानुभावं न्यगदत्त्वामप्रकाशयन् पित्रे ॥44.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्यान् other, च and, नाम-भेदान् different names, व्याकुर्वन् explaining, अग्रजे to the elder brother, च and, राम-आदीन् Rama and others, अति-मानुष-अनुभावम् superhuman power, न्यगदत् mentioned, त्वाम् You, अ-प्रकाशयन् without revealing, पित्रे to the father

Simple Meaning: While explaining various other names for You, and giving names like Rama to Your elder brother, Sage Garga indicated Your superhuman capabilities to Your father Nanda, yet without fully revealing Your true identity as the Supreme Lord.

स्निह्यति यस्तव पुत्रे मुह्यति स न मायिकैः पुनः शोकैः ।
द्रुह्यति यः स तु नश्येदित्यवदत्ते महत्त्वमृषिवर्यः ॥44.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्निह्यति loves, यः who, तव Your, पुत्रे son, मुह्यति is deluded, सः he, न not, मायिकैः by illusory, पुनः again, शोकैः by griefs, द्रुह्यति harbors malice, यः who, सः he, तु on the other hand, नश्येत् perishes, इति thus, अवदत् stated, ते Your, महत्त्वम् greatness, ऋषि-वर्यः the eminent sage Garga

Simple Meaning: "He who loves your son will never again be deluded by illusory griefs; on the other hand, he who harbors malice towards Him shall perish." Thus did the eminent sage Garga speak of Your immense glory and greatness to Nanda.

जेष्यति बहुतरदैत्यान् नेष्यति निजबन्धुलोकममलपदम् ।
श्रोष्यसि सुविमलकीर्तीरस्येति भवद्विभूतिमृषिरूचे ॥44.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: जेष्यति He will conquer, बहुतर-दैत्यान् numerous daityas, नेष्यति He will lead, निज-बन्धु-लोकम् His own relatives and devotees, अमल-पदम् to the pure supreme abode, श्रोष्यसि you will hear, सुविमल-कीर्तीः the spotless glorious deeds, अस्य His, इति thus, भवत्-विभूतिम् Your divine glory, ऋषिः the sage Garga, ऊचे spoke

Simple Meaning: "He will conquer numerous daityas, He will lead all His relatives and devotees to the pure supreme abode, and you will hear of His pristine, glorious deeds!" Thus did sage Garga reveal Your divine glory to Nanda.

अमुनैव सर्वदुर्गं तरितास्थ कृतास्थमत्र तिष्ठध्वम् ।
हरिरेवेत्यनभिलपन्नित्यादि त्वामवर्णयत् स मुनिः ॥44.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: अमुना एव through Him alone, सर्व-दुर्गम् all dangers and difficult situations, तरितास्थ you all will cross over, कृतास्थम् with firm faith and determination, अत्र here, तिष्ठध्वम् remain and stay, हरिः Lord Hari, एव indeed, इति thus, अनभिलपन् without explicitly uttering, इत्यादि and so on, त्वाम् You, अवर्णयत् described, सः that, मुनिः sage Garga

Simple Meaning: "Through Him alone you all will cross over every danger and difficulty. Therefore, stay here with firm faith and devotion!" Thus, without directly revealing that You were Lord Hari Himself, that Sage Garga described Your glory and reassured Nanda.

गर्गेऽथ निर्गतेऽस्मिन् नन्दितनन्दादिनन्द्यमानस्त्वम् ।
मद्गदमुद्गतकरुणो निर्गमय श्रीमरुत्पुराधीश ॥44.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: गर्गे Sage Garga, अथ then, निर्गते having departed, अस्मिन् when he, नन्दित-नन्द-आदि-नन्द्यमानः who brought joy to and were praised by Nanda and others, त्वम् You, मत्-गदम् my disease, उद्गत-करुणः You with overflowing compassion, निर्गमय please cure and remove completely, श्री-मरुत्पुर-धीश Lord of Guruvayur

Simple Meaning: When Sage Garga departed, You who brought immense joy to Nanda and the residents of Gokula, were looked after by them very lovingly. O Lord of Guruvayur, with Your overflowing compassion, please completely eradicate my disease!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
