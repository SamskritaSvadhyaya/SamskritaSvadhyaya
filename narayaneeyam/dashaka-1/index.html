Dashaka 1

Glory of the Lord

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 1

Glory of the Lord

(छन्दस् - स्रग्धरा)

सान्द्रानन्दावबोधात्मकमनुपमितं कालदेशावधिभ्यां
निर्मुक्तं नित्यमुक्तं निगमशतसहस्रेण निर्भास्यमानम् ।
अस्पष्टं दृष्टमात्रे पुनरुरुपुरुषार्थात्मकं ब्रह्म तत्वं
तत्तावद्भाति साक्षाद् गुरुपवनपुरे हन्त भाग्यं जनानाम् ॥1.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: सान्द्र-आनन्द-अवबोध-आत्मकम् the dense embodiment of pure bliss and consciousness, अनुपमितम् incomparable, काल-देश-अवधिभ्याम् by the limitations of time and space, निर्मुक्तम् free from, नित्य-मुक्तम् ever free, निगम-शत-सहस्रेण by hundreds and thousands of scriptures, निर्भास्यमानम् being illuminated, अस्पष्टम् unmanifest, दृष्ट-मात्रे seen merely, पुनः again, उरु-पुरुषार्थ-आत्मकम् the ultimate goal of human life, ब्रह्म Brahman, तत्वम् the truth, तत् that, तावत् indeed, भाति shines, साक्षात् directly, गुरुपवनपुरे in Guruvayoor, हन्त oh, भाग्यम् fortune, जनानाम् of people

Simple Meaning: The supreme truth of Brahman, which is the dense embodiment of pure bliss and consciousness, incomparable, entirely free from the limitations of time and space, and eternally liberated, is continuously illuminated by hundreds of thousands of vedic scriptures. Though it remains unmanifest and difficult to grasp at first, the moment it is realized, it reveals itself as the ultimate goal of human life. Oh, what a great fortune for the people that this very same Brahman shines directly before our eyes in the sacred town of Guruvayoor!

एवं दुर्लभ्यवस्तुन्यपि सुलभतया हस्तलब्धे यदन्यत्
तन्वा वाचा धिया वा भजति बत जनः क्षुद्रतैव स्फुटेयम् ।
एते तावद्वयं तु स्थिरतरमनसा विश्वपीड़ापहत्यै
निश्शेषात्मानमेनं गुरुपवनपुराधीशमेवाश्रयामः ॥1.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, दुर्लभ-वस्तुनि in an unattainable object, अपि even, सुलभतया with ease, हस्त-लब्धे obtained in hand, यत् that, अन्यत् other, तन्वा by body, वाचा by speech, धिया by mind, वा or, भजति worships, बत alas, जनः people, क्षुद्रता एव baseness alone, स्फुटा clear, इयम् this, एते these, तावत् indeed, वयम् we, तु but, स्थिरतर-मनसा with a very firm mind, विश्व-पीड़ा-अपहत्यै for the removal of worldly miseries, निश्शेष-आत्मानम् the soul of all the beings in the universe, एनम् this, गुरुपवनपुर-अधीशम् the Lord of Guruvayoor, एव alone, आश्रयामः we take refuge in

Simple Meaning: When such a rare supreme bliss, which is otherwise so difficult to attain, is easily available right in front of us within our reach, if people still choose to worship other things with their body, speech, or mind, it is a clear sign of their ignorance and narrow mindedness. However, we shall firmly resolve with an unwavering mind to surrender only to this Lord of Guruvayoor, who is the soul of all the beings in the universe, for the complete eradication of all our worldly afflictions.

सत्त्वं यत्तत् पराभ्यामपरिकलनतो निर्मलं तेन तावत्
भूतैर्भूतेन्द्रियैस्ते वपुरिति बहुशः श्रूयते व्यासवाक्यम् ।
तत् स्वच्छ्त्वाद्यदाच्छादितपरसुखचिद्गर्भनिर्भासरूपं
तस्मिन् धन्या रमन्ते श्रुतिमतिमधुरे सुग्रहे विग्रहे ते ॥1.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सत्त्वम् pure sattva, यत् which, तत् that, पराभ्याम् by the other two gunas (rajas and tamas), अपरिकलनतः due to non-mixture, निर्मलम् completely pure, तेन by that, तावत् indeed, भूतैः by the elements, भूत-इन्द्रियैः by the sense organs, ते your, वपुः body, इति thus, बहुशः repeatedly, श्रूयते is heard, व्यास-वाक्यम् the words of Vyasa, तत् that, स्वच्छत्वात् due to extreme purity, यत् which, आच्छादित-पर-सुख-चित्-गर्भ-निर्भास-रूपम् concealing the pure bliss and consciousness within while reflecting it brilliantly outside, तस्मिन् in that, धन्याः the fortunate ones, रमन्ते rejoice, श्रुति-मति-मधुरे sweet to hear about and to contemplate upon, सुग्रहे easily graspable, विग्रहे in the divine form, ते your

Simple Meaning: Sage Vyasa repeatedly declares in the scriptures that Your divine body is made of absolute, pure sattva guna, completely unmixed with rajas and tamas, and is untouched by the material elements or physical senses. Because of its supreme purity, this form subtly holds the infinite divine bliss and consciousness within while reflecting it beautifully on the outside. The fortunate devotees constantly rejoice in this divine form of Yours, which is sweet to hear about, delightful to contemplate upon, and easily graspable by the mind.

निष्कम्पे नित्यपूर्णे निरवधिपरमानन्दपीयूषरूपे
निर्लीनानेकमुक्तावलिसुभगतमे निर्मलब्रह्मसिन्धौ ।
कल्लोलोल्लासतुल्यं खलु विमलतरं सत्त्वमाहुस्तदात्मा
कस्मान्नो निष्कलस्त्वं सकल इति वचस्त्वत्कलास्वेव भूमन् ॥1.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: निष्कम्पे without disturbance, नित्य-पूर्णे eternally full, निरवधि-परमानन्द-पीयूष-रूपे the nature of boundless supreme bliss nectar, निर्लीन-अनेक-मुक्तावलि-सुभगतमे beautified by many liberated souls who are like pearls merged in it, निर्मल-ब्रह्म-सिन्धौ in the pure ocean of Brahman, कल्लोल-उल्लास-तुल्यम् comparable to the play of a wave, खलु indeed, विमलतरम् extremely pure, सत्त्वम् sattva guna, आहुः they say, तत्-आत्मा having that as the body, कस्मात् why, नो not, निष्कलः without a form (formless), त्वम् you, स-कलः with a form, इति thus, वचः statement, त्वत्-कलासु एव only to your incarnations, भूमन् O omnipresent Lord

Simple Meaning: O omnipresent Lord, the pure ocean of Brahman is without disturbances, eternally full, of the nature of limitless nectar of supreme bliss, and beautifully adorned by countless pearls of liberated souls who have merged into it. Sages say that your extremely pure sattva form is like a brilliant wave rising from this ocean. Since You are that very Brahman itself, why should You not be called Nishkala - without a form? Therefore, descriptions of having Sakala - parts or a physical form apply only to Your various incarnations.

निर्व्यापारोऽपि निष्कारणमज भजसे यत्क्रियामीक्षणाख्यां
तेनैवोदेति लीना प्रकृतिरसतिकल्पाऽपि कल्पादिकाले ।
तस्याः संशुद्धमंशं कमपि तमतिरोधायकं सत्त्वरूपं
स त्वं धृत्वा दधासि स्वमहिमविभवाकुण्ठ वैकुण्ठ रूपं ॥1.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: निर्व्यापारः अपि even though without any actions, निष्कारणम् without any external cause, अज O unborn one, भजसे you accept, यत् which, क्रियाम् action, ईक्षण-आख्याम् known as glances, तेन एव by that alone, उदेति arises, लीना dissolved, प्रकृतिः prakriti, असति-कल्पा अपि even though non-existent as it were, कल्प-आदि-काले at the beginning of a cycle of creation, तस्याः her, संशुद्धम् pure, अंशम् part, कमपि a certain, तम् that, अतिरोधायकम् non-concealing, सत्त्व-रूपम् of the nature of sattva, सः त्वम् that you, धृत्वा assuming, दधासि you bear, स्व-महिम-विभव-अकुण्ठ O You whose glory and power are unimpeded, वैकुण्ठ O Lord of Vaikuntha, रूपम् the divine form

Simple Meaning: O unborn Lord of Vaikuntha, whose divine glory and power are never diminished! Even though You are completely without action by nature, at the beginning of cosmic creation, You effortlessly cast Your creative glance without any external cause. By that glance alone, prakriti, which lay dormant and practically non-existent during the dissolution, is awakened into action. Then, assuming a certain supremely pure aspect of that prakriti which consists of pure sattva and never veils Your true nature, You manifest Your glorious divine form.

तत्ते प्रत्यग्रधाराधरललितकलायावलीकेलिकारं
लावण्यस्यैकसारं सुकृतिजनदृशां पूर्णपुण्यावतारम् ।
लक्ष्मीनिश्शङ्कलीलानिलयनममृतस्यन्दसन्दोहमन्तः
सिञ्चत् सञ्चिन्तकानां वपुरनुकलये मारुतागारनाथ ॥1.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that, ते your, प्रत्यग्र-धाराधर-ललित-कलाय-अवली-केलि-कारम् sporting the beauty of a fresh rain cloud and a cluster of lovely blue kalaya flowers, लावण्यस्य of beauty, एक-सारम् the ultimate essence, सुकृति-जन-दृशाम् to the eyes of righteous people, पूर्ण-पुण्य-अवतारम् the manifestation of their full merits, लक्ष्मी-निश्शङ्क-लीला-निलयनम् the abode of the uninhibited sporting of Lakshmi, अमृत-स्यन्द-सन्दोहम् a torrential flow of nectar, अन्तः inside, सिञ्चत् drenching, सञ्चिन्तकानाम् of those who meditate, वपुः body, अनुकलये I meditate upon, मारुतागार-नाथ O Lord of Guruvayoor

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Guruvayoor, I constantly meditate upon Your divine form, which sports the enchanting bluish hue of a fresh rain cloud and a cluster of lovely kalaya flowers. It is the ultimate essence of all beauty and appears to the eyes of righteous people as the direct fruit of their abundant past merits. It is the home where Goddess Lakshmi sports freely without any hesitation, and it completely drenches the hearts of those who meditate upon it with a torrential downpour of supreme nectar.

कष्टा ते सृष्टिचेष्टा बहुतरभवखेदावहा जीवभाजाम्
इत्येवं पूर्वमालोचितमजित मया नैवमद्याभिजाने ।
नोचेज्जीवाः कथं वा मधुरतरमिदं त्वद्वपुश्चिद्रसार्द्रं
नेत्रैः श्रोत्रैश्च पीत्वा परमरससुधाम्भोधिपूरे रमेरन् ॥1.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: कष्टा painful, ते your, सृष्टि-चेष्टा activity of creation, बहुतर-भव-खेद-आवहा bringing immense worldly miseries, जीव-भाजाम् for the living beings, इति एवम् in this manner, पूर्वम् previously, आलोचितम् thought, अजित O unconquerable one, मया by me, न एवम् not so, अद्य today, अभिजाने I realize, नो चेत् otherwise, जीवाः the living beings, कथम् how, वा or, मधुरतरम् exceedingly sweet, इदम् this, त्वत्-वपुः your body, चित्-रस-आर्द्रम् saturated with the essence of pure consciousness, नेत्रैः with the eyes, श्रोत्रैः च and with the ears, पीत्वा having drunk, परम-रस-सुधा-अम्भोधि-पूरे in the flood of the supreme nectar ocean of bliss, रमेरन् would rejoice

Simple Meaning: O unconquerable Lord! In the past I used to think that Your cosmic activity of creation was cruel and painful, as it brings endless worldly miseries to living beings. But today, I realize that this is not true at all. For if You had not created this universe, how could living beings ever drink in through their eyes and ears - this exceedingly sweet divine form of Yours, which is overflowing with pure consciousness, and endlessly rejoice in the flooding ocean of supreme nectar and bliss?

नम्राणां सन्निधत्ते सततमपि पुरस्तैरनभ्यर्थितान्
अप्यर्थान् कामानजस्रं वितरति परमानन्दसान्द्रां गतिं च ।
इत्थं निश्शेषलभ्यो निरवधिकफल: पारिजातो हरे त्वं
क्षुद्रं तं शक्रवाटीद्रुममभिलषति व्यर्थमर्थिव्रजोऽयम् ॥1.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: नम्राणाम् of those who bow down, सन्निधत्ते present, सततम् अपि always, पुरः in front of, तैः by them, अनभ्यर्थितान् unasked, अपि even, अर्थान् objects of desire, कामान् pleasures, अजस्रम् continuously, वितरति bestows, परम-आनन्द-सान्द्राम् dense with supreme bliss, गतिम् liberation, च and, इत्थम् thus, निश्शेष-लभ्यः accessible to all completely, निर्-अवधिक-फलः yielding limitless fruits, पारिजातः the parijata tree, हरे O Lord Hari, त्वम् you, क्षुद्रम् insignificant, तम् that, शक्र-वाटी-द्रुमम् the tree in Shakra’s (Indra's) garden, अभिलषति desires, व्यर्थम् in vain, अर्थि-व्रजः अयम् this seeker

Simple Meaning: O Lord Hari, You are always present right in front of those who bow down to You, continuously bestowing wealth, desires, and even unasked boons upon them, along with the ultimate liberation filled with supreme bliss. Thus, You are the true Parijata tree, easily accessible to everyone and yielding limitless fruits. Yet, this crowd of worldly seekers foolishly and in vain craves that insignificant celestial tree planted in Indra's garden, which gives only limited rewards.

कारुण्यात् काममन्यं ददति खलु परे स्वात्मदस्त्वं विशेषात्
ऐश्वर्यादीशतेऽन्ये जगति परजने स्वात्मनोऽपीश्वरस्त्वम् ।
त्वय्युच्चैरारमन्ति प्रतिपदमधुरे चेतनाः स्फीतभाग्याः
त्वं चात्माराम एवेत्यतुलगुणगणाधार शौरे नमस्ते ॥1.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: कारुण्यात् out of compassion, कामम् desire, अन्यम् other, ददति give, खलु indeed, परे others, स्व-आत्म-दः giver of your own self, त्वम् you, विशेषात् specially, ऐश्वर्यात् due to lordship, ईशते rule, अन्ये others, जगति in the world, पर-जने over other people, स्व-आत्मनः अपि even over your own self, ईश्वरः ruler, त्वम् you, त्वयि in you, उच्चैः immensely, आरमन्ति rejoice, प्रतिपद-मधुरे in the constant sweetness at every step, चेतनाः living beings, स्फीत-भाग्याः of great fortune, त्वम् you, च and, आत्मा-रामः rejoicing in your own self, एव alone, इति thus, अतुल-गुण-गण-आधार O abode of incomparable virtues, शौरे O Shauri (Krishna), नमस्ते salutations to you

Simple Meaning: The abode of incomparable virtues, O Lord Krishna! Out of compassion other deities may grant the worldly desires of their devotees, but You uniquely give Your very self to Your devotees (give Your own identity to the devotees by merging them with Yourself). Other rulers in this world exercise power only over others due to their lordship, but You are the master of even Your own self. The highly fortunate souls immensely rejoice in You, who are sweet at every step, while You remain ever satisfied in Your own self. Salutations unto You!

ऐश्वर्यं शङ्करादीश्वरविनियमनं विश्वतेजोहराणां
तेजस्संहारि वीर्यं विमलमपि यशो निस्पृहैश्चोपगीतम्।
अङ्गासङ्गा सदा श्रीरखिलविदसि न क्वापि ते सङ्गवार्ता
तद्वातागारवासिन् मुरहर भगवच्छब्दमुख्याश्रयोऽसि ॥1.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऐश्वर्यम् lordship, शङ्कर-आदि-ईश्वर-विनियमनम् ruling over lords like Shankara and others, विश्व-तेजः-हराणाम् of those who rob the luster of the universe, तेजः-संहारि destroying the luster, वीर्यम् valor, विमलम् pure, अपि also, यशः fame, निस्पृहैः च and by desireless sages, उपगीतम् sung, अङ्ग-आसङ्गा clinging to your body, सदा always, श्रीः Goddess Lakshmi, अखिल-वित् असि you are all knowing, न not, क्वापि anywhere, ते your, सङ्ग-वार्ता rumors of attachment, तत् therefore, वात-आगार-वासिन् O dweller of the city of the Wind - Guruvayoor, मुर-हर O slayer of Mura, भगवत्-शब्द-मुख्य-आश्रयः असि you are the primary meaning of the word Bhagavan

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Guruvayoor, O slayer of the asura Mura! Your lordship is such that You rule over even supreme lords like Shankara and others, and Your valor can destroy the strength of those who steal the luster of the universe. Your pure, spotless fame is sung even by liberated sages who have renounced everything. Goddess Lakshmi always clings to Your body, and You are all knowing, yet there is not even a trace of attachment in You. Therefore, You alone are the primary and truest meaning of the word Bhagavan!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
