Krishna bestows grace on Kuchela

दशकम् ८७

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 87

Krishna bestows grace on Kuchela

कुचेलनामा भवतः सतीर्थ्यतां गतः स सान्दीपनिमन्दिरे द्विजः ।
त्वदेकरागेण धनादिनिस्स्पृहो दिनानि निन्ये प्रशमी गृहाश्रमी ॥87.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: कुचेल-नामा Kuchela by name, भवतः Your, सतीर्थ्यताम् गतः companionship/fellow student status, सः he, सान्दीपनि-मन्दिरे in the hermitage of guru Sandipani, द्विजः a brahmana, त्वत्-एक-रागेण with exclusive devotion unto You alone, धन-आदि-निस्स्पृहः free from desire for wealth and other material things, दिनानि days, निन्ये spent, प्रशमी calm, गृहाश्रमी a householder

Simple Meaning: A brahmana named Kuchela had been Your fellow student in the hermitage of Sage Sandipani. With complete self-control and total detachment from wealth and worldly possessions due to his single-minded devotion to You alone, he passed his days peacefully living as a householder.

समानशीलाऽपि तदीयवल्लभा तथैव नो चित्तजयं समेयुषी ।
कदाचिदूचे बत वृत्तिलब्धये रमापतिः किं न सखा निषेव्यते ॥87.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: समान-शीला अपि though of similar virtuous disposition, तदीय-वल्लभा his devoted wife, तथैव in the very same manner, नो not, चित्त-जयम् mastery over the mind, समेयुषी having attained, कदाचित् ऊचे once said, बत alas, वृत्ति-लब्धये for obtaining a livelihood, रमा-पतिः the Lord of Goddess Lakshmi, किम् why, न is not, सखा friend, निषेव्यते approached/worshipped

Simple Meaning: Although his wife was equally virtuous, she had not attained the same absolute mastery over her mind and desires as Kuchela. Alas, distressed by their deep poverty, she once said to him, "Why do you not approach your friend, who is the husband of Lakshmi, to seek a means of livelihood?"

इतीरितोऽयं प्रियया क्षुधार्तया जुगुप्समानोऽपि धने मदावहे ।
तदा त्वदालोकनकौतुकाद्ययौ वहन् पटान्ते पृथुकानुपायनम् ॥87.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति ईरितः अयम् thus requested by her, प्रियया by his beloved wife, क्षुधा-अर्तया who was suffering from intense hunger, जुगुप्समानः अपि although recoiling with disdain, धने for wealth, मद-आवहे which causes pride and arrogance, तदा then, त्वत्-आलोकन-कौतुकात् out of eagerness to see You, ययौ he set out, वहन् carrying, पट-अन्ते tied in the corner of his cloth, पृथुकान् flatten rice/beaten rice, उपायनम् as an offering/gift

Simple Meaning: Thus urged by his beloved wife who was distressed by severe hunger, Kuchela set out to see You. Even though he naturally detested wealth because it generates pride and vanity, he went out of pure eagerness to behold You, carrying a humble gift of beaten rice tied in the corner of his garment.

गतोऽयमाश्चर्यमयीं भवत्पुरीं गृहेषु शैब्याभवनं समेयिवान् ।
प्रविश्य वैकुण्ठमिवाप निर्वृतिं तवातिसम्भावनया तु किं पुनः ॥87.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: गतः अयम् this Kuchela having reached, आश्चर्य-मयीम् wondrous/full of marvels, भवत्-पुरीम् Your city of Dwaraka, गृहेषु among the palaces, शैब्या-भवनम् the mansion of Shaibya - Mitravinda, समेयिवान् reached/entered, प्रविश्य entering, वैकुण्ठम् इव as if entering Vaikuntha, आप reached/experienced, निर्वृतिम् supreme bliss, तव अति-सम्भावनया by Your extraordinary and warm reception, तु indeed, किम् पुनः what to speak of

Simple Meaning: Reaching Your wondrous city of Dwaraka, Kuchela arrived at the palace of Queen Mitravinda. Upon entering, he experienced supreme bliss, as though he had stepped into Vaikuntha itself. What then can be said of the boundless joy he felt when You received him with such extraordinary honor and affection!

प्रपूजितं तं प्रियया च वीजितं करे गृहीत्वाऽकथयः पुराकृतम् ।
यदिन्धनार्थं गुरुदारचोदितैरपर्तुवर्ष तदमर्षि कानने ॥87.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रपूजितम् honoured greatly, तम् him, प्रियया by Your beloved wife, च and, वीजितम् fanned, करे in the hand, गृहीत्वा holding, अकथयः You spoke of, पुरा-कृतम् past events/earlier deeds, यत् that, इन्धन-अर्थम् for the sake of firewood, गुरु-दार-चोदितैः urged by the wife of the guru, अपर्तु-वर्षम् unseasonal rain/storm, तत् that, अमर्षि endured, कानने in the forest

Simple Meaning: Having worshipped and honoured him greatly, and while Your beloved wife fanned him, You took his hand in Yours and fondly recalled Your past days together. You remembered how, sent by Your guru's wife to collect firewood, both of you endured an unseasonal storm and heavy rain together in the forest.

त्रपाजुषोऽस्मात् पृथुकं बलादथ प्रगृह्य मुष्टौ सकृदाशिते त्वया ।
कृतं कृतं नन्वियतेति संभ्रमाद्रमा किलोपेत्य करं रुरोध ते ॥87.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्रपा-जुषः who was feeling embarrassed, अस्मात् from him, पृथुकम् the beaten rice, बलात् अथ forcefully then, प्रगृह्य snatching/taking, मुष्टौ in Your hand, सकृत्-आशिते when eaten once, त्वया by You, कृतम् कृतम् enough, enough, ननु इयता इति surely with this much, संभ्रमात् in great haste/eagerness, रमा Goddess Lakshmi, किला उपेत्य indeed approaching, करम् hand, रुरोध restrained/stopped, ते Your

Simple Meaning: When Kuchela felt extremely shy to offer his humble gift, You forcefully snatched the bundle of beaten rice tied in his garment. After You had eaten just one handful, Lakshmi incarnate Rukmini, rushed forward in eagerness and stopped Your hand, saying enough, enough.

भक्तेषु भक्तेन स मानितस्त्वया पुरीं वसन्नेकनिशां महासुखम् ।
बतापरेद्युर्द्रविणं विना ययौ विचित्ररूपस्तव खल्वनुग्रहः ॥87.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: भक्तेषु among all devotees, भक्तेन by the most devoted, सः he, मानितः त्वया honoured by You, पुरीम् in the city of Dwaraka, वसन् residing, एक-निशाम् for one night, महा-सुखम् in great joy and comfort, बत alas/wonderfully, अपरे-द्युः on the next day, द्रविणम् wealth, विना without, ययौ he departed, विचित्र-रूपः wondrous/mysterious in form, तव Your, खलु indeed, अनुग्रहः grace/blessing

Simple Meaning: Honoured by You—who are the most devoted to Your devotees—Kuchela spent one night in Dwaraka in great joy and comfort. Alas, on the next morning, he set out for home without asking for or receiving any material wealth. Wondrous and mysterious indeed is the nature of Your divine grace!

यदि ह्ययाचिष्यमदास्यदच्युतो वदामि भार्यां किमिति व्रजन्नसौ ।
त्वदुक्तिलीलास्मितमग्नधीः पुनः क्रमादपश्यन्मणिदीप्रमालयम् ॥87.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: यदि if, हि indeed, अयाचिष्यम् I had asked, अदास्यत् अच्युतः Achyuta (Lord Krishna) would have given, वदामि I say, भार्याम् to my wife, किम् what, इति thus, व्रजन् walking back, असौ he, त्वत्-उक्ति-लीला-स्मित-मग्न-धीः with his mind absorbed in Your divine words, playful gestures, and gentle smile, पुनः on the other hand, क्रमात् gradually, अपश्यत् saw, मणि-दीप्रम् आलयम् a magnificent palace gleaming with jewels

Simple Meaning: Walking back home, Kuchela thought to himself, "If I had asked, Krishna would surely have given me wealth. But what shall I tell my wife now?" With his mind completely absorbed in the memory of Your divine conversation, playful charm, and sweet smile, he saw a magnificent palace gleaming with precious gems (in place of his humble hut).

किं मार्गविभ्रंश इति भ्रमन् क्षणं गृहं प्रविष्टः स ददर्श वल्लभाम् ।
सखीपरीतां मणिहेमभूषितां बुबोध च त्वत्करुणां महाद्भुताम् ॥87.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: किम् is this, मार्ग-विभ्रंशः a mistake/confusion in finding the path, इति thinking thus, भ्रमन् bewildered, क्षणम् for a moment, गृहम् the house, प्रविष्टः entering, सः he, ददर्श saw, वल्लभाम् his beloved wife, सखी-परीताम् surrounded by maidservants, मणि-हेम-भूषिताम् adorned with jewels and golden ornaments, बुबोध understood/realised, च and, त्वत्-करुणाम् Your grace/compassion, महा-अद्भुताम् most wondrous and extraordinary

Simple Meaning: Wondering for a moment whether he had lost his way, Kuchela stepped inside the palace. There he saw his beloved wife, surrounded by maidservants and beautifully adorned with gold and precious jewels. In that moment, he fully realized the extraordinary and miraculous nature of Your divine grace.

स रत्नशालासु वसन्नपि स्वयं समुन्नमद्भक्तिभरोऽमृतं ययौ ।
त्वमेवमापूरितभक्तवाञ्छितो मरुत्पुराधीश हरस्व मे गदान् ॥87.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Kuchela), रत्न-शालासु in gem-studded palatial mansions, वसन्-अपि even while living, स्वयम् himself, समुन्नमत्-भक्ति-भरः overflowing with ever-increasing deep devotion, अमृतम् liberation/immortality, ययौ attained, त्वम् You, एवम् in this manner, आपूरित-भक्त-वाञ्छितः fulfilling the innermost desires of Your devotees, मरुत्-पुर-अधीश O Lord of Guruvayur, हरस्व remove/destroy, मे my, गदान् diseases and ailments

Simple Meaning: Living amidst gem-studded palatial mansions, Kuchela remained completely unattached, his heart constantly overflowing with ever-increasing devotion to You, until he ultimately attained eternal liberation. O Lord of Guruvayur, who thus completely fulfills the heart's desires of Your devotees, please destroy all my ailments and diseases!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
