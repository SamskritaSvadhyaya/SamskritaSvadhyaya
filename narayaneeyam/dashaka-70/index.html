Dashaka 70

Salvation of Sudarshana and Destruction of Shankhachuda and Arishta

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 70

Salvation of Sudarshana and

Destruction of Shankhachuda and Arishta

इति त्वयि रसाकुलं रमितवल्लभे वल्लवाः कदापि पुरमम्बिकामितुरम्बिकाकानने ।
समेत्य भवता समं निशि निषेव्य दिव्योत्सवं सुखं सुषुपुरग्रसीद्व्रजपमुग्रनागस्तदा ॥70.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति in this manner, त्वयि when You, रसाकुलम् blissfully, रमित-वल्लभे had sported with Your beloved maidens, वल्लवाः the cowherd men, कदापि once, पुरम् to the shrine, अम्बिका-मितुः of the consort of Ambika - Shiva, अम्बिका-कानने in the forest named Ambikavana, समेत्य having arrived, भवता समम् along with You, निशि at night, निषेव्य having celebrated, दिव्य-उत्सवम् the divine festival, सुखम् peacefully, सुषुपुः slept, अग्रसीत् swallowed, व्रज-पम् the chief of Vraja - Nanda, उग्र-नागः a fierce serpent, तदा then

Simple Meaning: After You had thus blissfully sported with Your beloved maidens, the cowherds once traveled to the holy forest of Ambikavana, home to the sacred shrine of Lord Shiva. Having arrived there along with You, they observed a grand night-long festival in worship of the Lord. Afterwards, as they were sleeping peacefully in the night, a huge and fierce serpent suddenly caught and began to swallow Nanda.

समुन्मुखमथोल्मुकैरभिहतेऽपि तस्मिन् बलात् अमुञ्चति भवत्पदे न्यपति पाहि पाहीति तैः ।
तदा खलु पदा भवान् समुपगम्य पस्पर्श तं बभौ स च निजां तनुं समुपसाद्य वैद्यधरीम् ॥70.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: समुन्मुखम् raising the face up/advancing, अथ then, उल्मुकैः with burning wooden torches, अभिहते अपि even when struck repeatedly, तस्मिन् while that serpent, बलात् by force, अमुञ्चति was not releasing Nanda, भवत्-पदे at Your lotus feet, न्यपति fell prostrate, पाहि पाहि इति crying "Save us, protect us!", तैः by those cowherds, तदा then, खलु indeed, पदा with Your foot, भवान् You, समुपगम्य approaching, पस्पर्श touched, तम् that serpent, बभौ shone resplendently, सः च and he, निजान् his own, तनुम् celestial form, समुपसाद्य regaining, वैद्यधरीम् belonging to a Vidyadhara

Simple Meaning: Even when the cowherds struck the fierce serpent with burning wooden torches, it refused to release its hold on Nanda. Helpless, they threw themselves at Your lotus feet, crying out, "Save us! Protect us!" Thereupon, You approached and touched the serpent with Your divine foot. Instantly, the serpent regained his original celestial body as a Vidyadhara and shone resplendently.

सुदर्शनधर प्रभो ननु सुदर्शनाख्योऽस्म्यहं मुनीन् क्वचिदपाहसं त इह मां व्यधुर्वाहसम् ।
भवत्पदसमर्पणादमलतां गतोऽस्मीत्यसौ स्तुवन् निजपदं ययौ व्रजपदं च गोपा मुदा ॥70.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुदर्शन-धर O bearer of the Sudarshana disc, प्रभो O Lord, ननु indeed, सुदर्शन-आख्यः named Sudarshana, अस्मि am, अहम् I, मुनीन् sages, क्वचित् once, अपाहसम् I ridiculed, ते they, इह in this world, माम् me, व्यधुः made, वाहसम् a serpent or python, भवत्-पद-समर्पणात् by the touch of Your divine foot, अमलताम् purity or liberation from curse, गतः अस्मि I have attained, इति thus, असौ he, स्तुवन् praising You, निज-पदम् to his celestial abode, ययौ departed, व्रज-पदम् to the settlement of Vraja, च and, गोपाः the cowherd men, मुदा with great joy

Simple Meaning: "O Lord, bearer of the Sudarshana disc! I was a Vidyadhara named Sudarshana. Having once mocked some sages for their looks, I was cursed by them to take the body of a python in this world. Now, by the divine touch of Your lotus foot, I am freed from all sin and restored to my pure form." Expressing his deep gratitude and singing Your praises in this manner, he departed to his heavenly realm, while the cowherds returned joyfully to Vraja.

कदापि खलु सीरिणा विहरति त्वयि स्त्रीजनैः जहार धनदानुगः स किल शङ्खचूडोऽबलाः ।
अतिद्रुतमनुद्रुतस्तमथ मुक्तनारीजनं रुरोजिथ शिरोमणिं हलभृते च तस्याददाः ॥70.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: कदापि once, खलु indeed, सीर​-इणा with the wielder of the plough - Balarama, विहरति while sporting, त्वयि You, स्त्रीजनैः along with the women of Vraja, जहार carried away or abducted, धनद-अनुगः a follower of the god of wealth - Kubera, सः he, किल named, शङ्खचूडः Shankhachuda, अबलाः the women, अतिद्रुतम् very swiftly, अनुद्रुतः pursuing, तम् him, अथ then, मुक्त-नारी-जनम् having freed the women, रुरोजिथ You killed or struck down, शिरोमणिम् the crest-jewel, हल-भृते to the wielder of the plough - Balarama, च and, तस्य his, आददाः You gave

Simple Meaning: Once, while You were sporting in the forest along with Balarama and the gopis, a follower of Kubera named Shankhachuda abducted the women. Swiftly pursuing him, You rescued the maidens, struck him down, extracted the shining crest-jewel from his head, and presented it to Balarama.

दिनेषु च सुहृज्जनैस्सह वनेषु लीलापरं मनोभवमनोहरं रसितवेणुनादामृतम् ।
भवन्तममरीदृशाममृतपारणादायिनं विचिन्त्य किमु नालपन् विरहतापिता गोपिकाः ॥70.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: दिनेषु during the daytime, च and, सुहृज्-जनैः सह along with Your cowherd friends, वनेषु in the forests, लीला-परम् engaged in divine sports, मनोभव-मनोहरम् more enchanting than Manmatha, रसित-वेणु-नाद-अमृतम् who pours out the sweet nectar of flute music, भवन्तम् You, अमरी-दृशाम् to the eyes of celestial women, अमृत-पारणा-दायिनम् bestowing a feast of nectar, विचिन्त्य remembering/meditating upon, किमु न आलपन् how did they not converse, विरह-तापिताः agonized by separation, गोपिकाः the gopi maidens

Simple Meaning: During the daytime, when You wandered in the forests with Your cowherd companions, absorbed in play, You charmed everyone as a form more enchanting than Manmatha himself, pouring forth the sweet nectar of Your flute music and offering a feast of heavenly bliss to the eyes of the celestial damsels watching from above. Deeply pained by Your temporary separation during these hours, how passionately did the gopis sing of Your divine deeds and converse among themselves!

भोजराजभृतकस्त्वथ कश्चित् कष्टदुष्टपथदृष्टिररिष्टः ।
निष्ठुराकृतिरपष्ठुनिनादस्तिष्ठते स्म भवते वृषरूपी ॥70.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: भोज-राज-भृतकः a servant of the king of the Bhojas - Kamsa, तु then, अथ thereafter, कश्चित् a certain, कष्ट-दुष्ट-पथ-दृष्टिः whose mind and vision were set on wicked and cruel paths, अरिष्टः Arishtasura, निष्ठुर-आकृतिः of a terrifying form, अपष्ठु-निनादः bellowing with horrible sound, तिष्ठते स्म stood, भवते facing You, वृष-रूपी in the form of a bull

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, a wicked asura whose mind was steeped in evil, named Arishta—a servant of King Kamsa —arrived in Vraja taking the form of a huge bull. Possessing a terrifying appearance and bellowing with horrible, deafening roars that shook the surroundings, he stood facing You.

शाक्वरोऽथ जगतीधृतिहारी मूर्तिमेष बृहतीं प्रदधानः ।
पङ्क्तिमाशु परिघूर्ण्य पशूनां छन्दसां निधिमवाप भवन्तम् ॥70.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: शाक्वरः the bull (Arishta), अथ then, जगती-धृति-हारी shattering the courage and peace of the world, मूर्तिम् form, एषः this (asura), बृहतीम् massive/huge, प्रदधानः assuming, पङ्क्तिम् row/herd, आशु swiftly, परिघूर्ण्य scattering in panic, पशूनाम् of the cattle, छन्दसाम् निधिम् the repository of all Vedic meters or scriptures, अवाप approached, भवन्तम् You

Simple Meaning: Assuming a gigantic form that shattered the peace and courage of the whole world, the fierce bull asura scattered the herds of cattle in all directions. Bellowing wildly, he then charged towards You—the repository of all Vedic meters and wisdom.

तुङ्गशृङ्गमुखमाश्वभियन्तं संगृहय्य रभसादभियं तम् ।
भद्ररूपमपि दैत्यमभद्रं मर्दयन्नमदयः सुरलोकम् ॥70.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: तुङ्ग-शृङ्ग-मुखम् with his raised sharp horns facing forward, आशु swiftly, अभियान्तम् charging furiously, संगृहय्य catching hold of, रभसात् with great force and speed, अभियम् fearlessly, तम् him, भद्र-रूपम् अपि though appearing in an auspicious form of a bull, दैत्यम् the daitya, अ-भद्रम् not bull/evil, मर्दयन् crushing, अमदयः You delighted, सुर-लोकम् the realm of the devas

Simple Meaning: As that wicked daitya—who bore a form of a भद्र (bull) but was अभद्र (not a bull) (can also be interpreted as: who bore भद्रम्  (an auspicious) form of a bull but was अभद्रम्  (evil)— —charged furiously toward You with his sharp horns lowered, You seized him by his horns swiftly and fearlessly. Crushing him to the ground and destroying him, You brought joy and relief to the realm of the devas.

Note: The play is on the word भद्रम् and अभद्रम् 

चित्रमद्य भगवन् वृषघातात् सुस्थिराऽजनि वृषस्थितिरुर्व्याम् ।
वर्धते च वृषचेतसि भूयान् मोद इत्यभिनुतोऽसि सुरैस्त्वम् ॥70.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: चित्रम् a wonder, अद्य today, भगवन् O Lord, वृष-घातात् by the destruction of the bull - Arishta, सुस्थिरा firmly established, अजनि has become, वृष-स्थितिः the position of righteousness, उर्व्याम् on the earth, वर्धते grows, च and, वृष-चेतसि in the mind of the Indra, भूयान् immense, मोदः delight, इति thus, अभिनुतः असि You are praised, सुरैः by the devas, त्वम् You

Simple Meaning: "O Lord, what a wonder! By the slaying of this vrisha (bull), vrisha-stithi (the position of righteousness) has become firmly established on earth, and immense joy fills the heart of Vrisha (Indra)!" Expressing their wonder in this manner through play on words, the devas extolled Your glorious victory.

Note: The play is on the word वृष.

औक्षकाणि परिधावत दूरं वीक्ष्यतामयमिहोक्षविभेदी ।
इत्थमात्तहसितैः सह गोपैर्गेहगस्त्वमव वातपुरेश ॥70.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: औक्षकाणि O herds of oxen, परिधावत run away, दूरम् far off, वीक्ष्यताम् behold, अयम् this one, इह here, उक्ष-विभेदी the slayer of the giant bull, इत्थम् in this manner, आत्त-हसितैः laughing merrily, सह with, गोपैः the cowherd boys, गेह-गः returning home, त्वम् You, अव protect, वातपुर-ईश O Lord of Guruvayur

Simple Meaning: "O herds of oxen, run far away! Behold, here comes the powerful slayer of the giant bull!" Teasing You playfully with such jovial words, Your cowherd companions walked beside You as You returned home to Vraja amidst shared laughter and joy. O Lord of Guruvayur, protect me from all my afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
