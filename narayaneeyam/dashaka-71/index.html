Destruction of Keshi and Vyomasura

दशकम् ७१

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 71

Destruction of Keshi and Vyomasura

यत्नेषु सर्वेष्वपि नावकेशी केशी स भोजेशितुरिष्टबन्धुः ।
त्वां सिन्धुजावाप्य इतीव मत्वा सम्प्राप्तवान् सिन्धुजवाजिरूपः ॥71.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: यत्नेषु in all attempts, सर्वेषु अपि even in every single one, न अवकेशी never failing, केशी (the asura) Keshi, सः he, भोज-ईषितुः of the king of the Bhojas - Kamsa, इष्ट-बन्धुः the dear friend, त्वाम् You, सिन्धु-जा-वाप्यः who is attained by the daughter of sindhu (ocean) - Lakshmi, इति इव thus as if, मत्वा considering, सम्प्राप्तवान् arrived, सिन्धु-ज-वाजि-रूपः taking the form of a horse born in the Sindh region

Simple Meaning: The asura Keshi, a dear friend of King Kamsa whose missions had never failed before, arrived in Vraja taking the form of a horse born in the Sindh region. Thinking that You are easily attainable to the one born in Sindhu (Lakshmi), he approached You as a majestic horse born in the region of Sindhu.

Note: The play is on the word Sindhu. Sindhu means ocean and Sindhu is also a name of a region which is famous for its excellent breed of horses.

गन्धर्वतामेष गतोऽपि रूक्षैर्नादैः समुद्वेजितसर्वलोकः ।
भवद्विलोकावधि गोपवाटीं प्रमर्द्य पापः पुनरापतत्त्वाम् ॥71.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: गन्धर्वताम् the state of a Gandharva or horse, एषः this one (Keshi), गतः अपि even though attaining, रूक्षैः with harsh, नादैः roars or neighs, समुद्वेजित-सर्व-लोकः terrifying all the inhabitants of the world, भवत्-विलोक-अवधि until sighting You, गोप-वाटीम् the cowherd settlement of Vraja, प्रमर्द्य trampling and devastating, पापः the sinful one, पुनः again, आपतत् rushed toward, त्वाम् You

गन्धर्व (पुंलिङ्गः) अश्वः src. Kosha. Ashtadhyayi

गन्धर्व (पुंलिङ्गः) देवगायकः src. Kosha. Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: Even though he assumed the form of a Gandharva (in this case - a horse), he terrified everyone far and wide with his harsh, deafening neighs. Trampling and devastating the cowherd settlement of Vraja, he searched wildly until his eyes caught sight of You, whereupon he charged in Your direction.

Note: Gandharvas - the celestial singers are known for their melodious voices. Gandharva also means a horse. The play is on the word Gandharva. The poet says that the asura assumed a form of Gandharva (double entendo) but his neighing was harsh and terrifying.

तार्क्ष्यार्पिताङ्घ्रेस्तव तार्क्ष्य एष चिक्षेप वक्षोभुवि नाम पादम् ।
भृगोः पदाघातकथां निशम्य स्वेनापि शक्यं तदितीव मोहात् ॥71.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तार्क्ष्य-अर्पित-अङ्घ्रेः of You whose lotus feet are placed upon Tarkshya (Garuda), तव Your, तार्क्ष्यः horse (Keshi), एषः this, चिक्षेप hurled or kicked, वक्षः-भुवि upon Your chest, नाम indeed, पादम् his leg, भृगोः of Sage Bhrigu, पद-आघात-कथाम् the story of the kick, निशम्य having heard, स्वेन अपि by himself also, शक्यम् possible, तत् that, इति इव thinking as if, मोहात् out of sheer delusion

तार्क्ष्यः 1 An epithet of Garuda; —4 A horse. src. Kosha. Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: On Your chest – You, whose lotus feet are placed upon Tarkshya (Garuda) – the tarkshya (the horse - Keshi) hurled his hind leg straight at Your chest. It was as though, having heard how Sage Bhrigu once struck Your chest, this foolish asura, out of delusion, believed that it was possible for him also to do the same!

Note: The play is on the word “Tarkshya”. Tarkshya is another name of Garuda and tarkshya also means a horse.

प्रवञ्चयन्नस्य खुराञ्चलं द्रागमुञ्च चिक्षेपिथ दूरदूरम्
सम्मूर्च्छितोऽपि ह्यतिमूर्च्छितेन क्रोधोष्मणा खादितुमाद्रुतस्त्वाम् ॥71.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रवञ्चयन् dodging or evading, अस्य his, खुर-अञ्चलम् the tip of the hoof, द्राक् swiftly, अमुम् that (asura), चिक्षेपिथ You hurled, दूर-दूरम् far and wide, सम्मूर्च्छितः अपि even though knocked unconscious, हि indeed, अति-मूर्च्छितेन with immensely heightened, क्रोध-उष्मणा heat of rage, खादितुम् to devour You, आद्रुतः charged back, त्वाम् at You

Simple Meaning: Dodging his charging hoof with effortless speed, You seized the asura by his hind legs and hurled him far away. Though knocked unconscious by the force of the throw, he quickly revived and—his fury burning with even greater heat—charged back at You with wide-open jaws, determined to devour You.

त्वं वाहदण्डे कृतधीश्च वाहादण्डं न्यधास्तस्य मुखे तदानीम् ।
तद् वृद्धिरुद्धश्वसनो गतासुः सप्तीभवन्नप्ययमैक्यमागात् ॥71.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वम् You, वाह-दण्डे upon punishing the horse, कृत-धीः having decided, च and, बाहा-दण्डम् Your rod-like forearm, न्यधाः inserted, तस्य into his, मुखे mouth, तदानीम् then, तत्-वृद्धि-रुद्ध-श्वसनः with his breathing choked by the swelling of that arm, गत-असुः with his life gone, सप्ती-भवन् अपि even though being a horse, अयम् he, ऐक्यम् आगात् attained union with You

वाह (पुंलिङ्गः) अश्वः src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

सप्ति (पुंलिङ्गः) अश्वः src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: Resolving to punish the equine asura, You thrust Your left forearm—resembling a formidable club—deep into his gaping mouth. As Your arm expanded within his throat, it completely choked off his breath, causing him to collapse lifeless to the ground. Though he was in the form of a horse, by dying directly at Your hands he attained union with You.

आलम्भमात्रेण पशोः सुराणां प्रसादके नूत्न इवाश्वमेधे ।
कृते त्वया हर्षवशात् सुरेन्द्रास्त्वां तुष्टुवुः केशवनामधेयम् ॥71.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: आलम्भ-मात्रेण by the mere killing, पशोः of the animal, सुराणाम् of the devas, प्रसादके which pleases, नूत्ने in a unique or unprecedented, इव as if, अश्वमेधे Ashvamedha Yagna (horse sacrifice), कृते being performed, त्वया by You, हर्ष-वशात् overcome with joy, सुर-इन्द्राः the leaders of the devas, त्वाम् You, तुष्टुवुः praised, केशव-नामधेयम् addressing You by the name Keshava

Simple Meaning: By merely slaying this horse, it was as if You performed an unprecedented Ashvamedha Yagna (horse sacrifice) that brought immense pleasure to the devas. Filled with joy at the destruction of Keshi, the celestial beings praised You, proclaiming Your renowned name Keshava—the slayer of Keshi.

Note: In the Ashvamedha Yagna, the sacrificial horse goes on a victorious round all over the world, before it is sacrificed. Here, Krishna directly killed/sacrificed the horse. So the poet terms it as a new type of Ashvamedha Yagna.

कंसाय ते शौरिसुतत्वमुक्त्वा तं तद्वधोत्कं प्रतिरुध्य वाचा।
प्राप्तेन केशिक्षपणावसाने श्रीनारदेन त्वमभिष्टुतोऽभूः ॥71.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: कंसाय to Kamsa, ते Your, शौरि-सुतत्वम् identity as the son of Shauri (Vasudeva), उक्त्वा having revealed, तम् him, तत्-वध-उत्कम् eager to kill Vasudeva, प्रतिरुध्य restraining, वाचा with persuasive words, प्राप्तेन arriving, केशि-क्षपण-अवसाने at the end of the destruction of Keshi, श्री नारदेन by Sage Narada, त्वम् You, अभिष्टुतः अभूः were gloriously praised

Simple Meaning: Having first revealed to Kamsa that You were in fact the son of Vasudeva, Narada restrained Kamsa from executing Vasudeva by advising him to wait. Arriving in Vraja just as You finished slaying the asura Keshi, the holy Sage Narada approached You and offered his heartfelt, divine praises.

कदापि गोपैः सह काननान्ते निलायनक्रीडनलोलुपं त्वाम् ।
मयात्मजः प्राप दुरन्तमायो व्योमाभिधो व्योमचरोपरोधी ॥71.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: कदापि once, गोपैः सह along with the cowherd boys, कानन-अन्ते in the deep forest, निलायन-क्रीडन-लोलुपम् eagerly engrossed in playing hide-and-seek, त्वाम् You, मय-आत्मजः the son of Maya, प्राप approached, दुरन्त-मायः a master of deceptive and endless illusions, व्योम-अभिधः named Vyoma, व्योमचर-उपरोधी who was a terror to the celestial gods roaming in the sky

Simple Meaning: Once, while You were eagerly engrossed in playing hide-and-seek with the cowherd boys in the deep forest, a wicked demon named Vyomasura—the son of Maya—approached You. Possessing endless powers of deception and illusion, he was a terrifying foe even to the celestial beings who travel through the skies.

स चोरपालायितवल्लवेषु चोरायितो गोपशिशून् पशूंश्च
गुहासु कृत्वा पिदधे शिलाभिस्त्वया च बुद्ध्वा परिमर्दितोऽभूत् ॥71.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Vyomasura), चोर-पालायित-वल्लवेषु among the cowherd boys playing the game of thieves and guardians, चोरायितः disguising himself as a thief, गोप-शिशून् the cowherd children, पशून् च and the cattle, गुहासु in mountain caves, कृत्वा having placed or hidden, पिदधे sealed, शिलाभिः with giant boulders, त्वया by You, च and, बुद्ध्वा discovering his deception, परिमर्दितः अभूत् was crushed and destroyed

Simple Meaning: Taking advantage of the game where the cowherd boys acted as thieves and guardians, Vyomasura joined in disguised as one of the thieves. Stealthily stealing away the cowherd children and the cattle, he locked them inside deep mountain caves and sealed the entrances with massive boulders. Instantly perceiving his wicked deception, You grabbed the asura, dragged him down, and crushed him to death.

एवं विधैश्चाद्भुतकेलिभेदैरानन्दमूर्च्छामतुलां व्रजस्य ।
पदे पदे नूतनयन्नसीमां परात्मरूपिन् पवनेश पायाः ॥71.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् विधैः with such diverse and astonishing kinds, च and, अद्भुत-केलि-भेदैः of marvelous sports and play, आनन्द-मूर्च्छाम् an overwhelming trance of bliss, अतुलाम् incomparable, व्रजस्य of the land of Vraja, पदे पदे at every step, नूतनयन् ever renewing/making fresh, असीमाम् limitless, पर-आत्म-रूपिन् O Lord whose nature is the Supreme Being, पवनेश O Lord of Guruvayur, पायाः protect me

Simple Meaning: By performing such diverse, marvelous, and miraculous pastimes, You continuously renewed an incomparable and limitless trance of divine bliss at every step for the inhabitants of Vraja. O Lord of Guruvayur, who art the embodiment of the Supreme Soul, protect me!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
