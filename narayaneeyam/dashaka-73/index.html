Krishna’s journey to Mathura

दशकम् ७३

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 73

Krishna’s journey to Mathura

निशमय्य तवाथ यानवार्तां भृशमार्ताः पशुपालबालिकास्ताः ।
किमिदं किमिदं कथं न्वितीमाः समवेताः परिदेवितान्यकुर्वन् ॥73.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: निशमय्य having heard, तव Your, अथ then, यान-वार्ताम् the news of departure to Mathura, भृशम् आर्ताः extremely distressed and heartbroken, पशुपाल-बालिकाः ताः those young cowherd maidens, किम् इदम् what is this, किम् इदम् what is this, कथम् नु how can this be, इति thus, इमाः these gopis, समवेताः gathering together, परिदेवितानि lamentations and wailings, अकुर्वन् made/performed

Simple Meaning: When the young cowherd maidens heard the agonizing news of Your impending departure to Mathura, they were completely overwhelmed with grief. Gathering together in deep distress, they cried out in anguish: "What is this? What is happening? How can this be?" Thus, they wept and lamented bitterly over the thought of losing Your presence.

करुणानिधिरेष नन्दसूनुः कथमस्मान् विसृजेदनन्यनाथाः ।
बत नः किमु दैवमेवमासीदिति तास्त्वद्गतमानसा विलेपुः ॥73.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: करुणा-निधिः an ocean of compassion, एषः this, नन्द-सूनुः son of Nanda, कथम् how, अस्मान् us, विसृजेत् could abandon or leave, अनन्य-नाथाः who have no other protector or Lord, बत alas, नः our, किमु why, दैवम् fate/Providence, एवम् आसीत् has become in this way (so cruel), इति thus, ताः those maidens of Vraja, त्वत्-गत-मानसाः with minds completely absorbed in You, विलेपुः lamented aloud

Simple Meaning: "How could Krishna, the son of Nanda and a veritable ocean of mercy, abandon us who have no other refuge or protector but Him? Alas, why has Providence turned in this way?" Expressing their unbearable anguish in this way, those maidens of Vraja wept bitterly, their minds fully merged in Your divine form and love.

चरमप्रहरे प्रतिष्ठमानः सह पित्रा निजमित्रमण्डलैश्च ।
परितापभरं नितम्बिनीनां शमयिष्यन् व्यमुचः सखायमेकम् ॥73.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: चरम-प्रहरे in the last watch of the night, प्रतिष्ठमानः setting forth, सह with, पित्रा Your father Nanda, निज-मित्र-मण्डलैः च and Your own circle of cowherd friends, परिताप-भारम् the heavy burden of intense grief and agony, नितम्बिनीनाम् of the beautiful maidens of Vraja, शमयिष्यन् desiring to soothe or alleviate, व्यमुचः You dispatched/sent, सखायम् एकम् a messenger friend

Simple Meaning: Setting forth in the last watch of the night along with Your father Nanda and Your close circle of cowherd companions, You wished to soothe the overwhelming grief of the beautiful maidens of Vraja. To comfort their heartbroken spirits, You dispatched a trusted messenger friend to convey Your reassuring words to them.

अचिरादुपयामि सन्निधिं वो भविता साधु मयैव सङ्गमश्रीः ।
अमृताम्बुनिधौ निमज्जयिष्ये द्रुतमित्याश्वसिता वधूरकार्षीः ॥73.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अचिरात् very soon, उपयामि I shall come back, सन्निधिम् to the presence, वः of all of you, भविता there will take place, साधु indeed, मया एव with Me alone, सङ्गम-श्रीः the glory and bliss of reunion, अमृत-अम्बु-निधौ in an ocean of nectar, निमज्जयिष्ये I shall immerse you all, द्रुतम् quickly, इति with these words, आश्वसिताः comforted and reassured, वधूः the maidens of Vraja, अकार्षीः You made

Simple Meaning: Through Your message, You reassured the grieving maidens of Vraja, promising them: "I shall return to your side very soon. The joyful bliss of our reunion will surely be yours again, and I shall quickly immerse you all into an ocean of divine nectar." With these comforting and loving words, You gave solace to their broken hearts.

सविषादभरं सयाच्ञमुच्चैः अतिदूरं वनिताभिरीक्ष्यमाणः ।
मृदु तद्दिशि पातयन्नपाङ्गान् सबलोऽक्रूररथेन निर्गतोऽभूः ॥73.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: स-विषाद-भारम् filled with a heavy burden of sorrow, स-याच्ञम् उच्चैः with loud and piteous entreaties, अति-दूरम् to a great distance, वनिताभिः by the maidens, ईक्ष्यमाणः being intently gazed upon, मृदु tenderly, तत्-दिशि in their direction, पातयन् casting, अपाङ्गान् sidelong glances, स-बलः accompanied by Balarama, अक्रूर-रथेन on Akrura's chariot, निर्गतः अभूः You departed

Simple Meaning: As the maidens of Vraja gazed after You from afar with hearts weighed down by profound sorrow and with loud, piteous entreaties, You cast tender, compassionate side-glances back toward them. Thus, accompanied by Balarama, You departed on Akrura's chariot toward Mathura.

अनसा बहुलेन वल्लवानां मनसा चानुगतोऽथ वल्लभानाम् ।
वनमार्तमृगं विषण्णवृक्षं समतीतो यमुनातटीमयासीः ॥73.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: अनसा with bullock carts, बहुलेन numerous, वल्लवानाम् of the cowherds, मनसा with hearts and minds, च and, अनुगतः followed, अथ then, वल्लभानाम् of (Your) beloved maidens, वनम् through the forest, आर्त-मृगम् with sorrowful animals, विषण्ण-वृक्षम् with grieving trees, समतीतः passing through, यमुना-तटीम् to the banks of the Yamuna River, आयासीः You reached

Simple Meaning: Followed by a large procession of cowherds in bullock carts, and accompanied in spirit by the hearts and minds of Your beloved maidens who could not bear to part from You, You moved onward. Passing through forests where even the animals appeared sorrowful and the trees seemed to droop in grief at Your departure, You finally arrived at the banks of the Yamuna River.

नियमाय निमज्य वारिणि त्वामभिवीक्ष्याथ रथेऽपि गान्दिनेयः ।
विवशोऽजनि किं न्विदं विभोस्ते ननु चित्रं त्ववलोकनं समन्तात् ॥73.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: नियमाय for performing his ritual bath and ablutions, निमज्य having submerged himself, वारिणि in the water, त्वाम् You, अभिवीक्ष्य having seen, अथ then, रथे on the chariot, अपि as well, गान्दिनेयः the son of Gandini - (Akrura), विवशः अजनि became overwhelmed/bewildered, किम् नु इदम् what indeed is this, विभोः ते of You, the All-Pervading Lord, ननु is it not, चित्रम् a wonder, तु indeed, अवलोकनम् vision/appearance, समन्तात् everywhere

Simple Meaning: When Akrura dipped into the Yamuna waters to perform his daily rituals, he saw You present underwater. Astonished, he surfaced, only to see You sitting quietly on the chariot above as before. Submerging again, he saw You once more in the water. Seeing You simultaneously present everywhere, Akrura became bewildered with wonder at Your all-pervading nature.

पुनरेष निमज्य पुण्यशाली पुरुषं त्वां परमं भुजङ्गभोगे ।
अरिकम्बुगदाम्बुजैः स्फुरन्तं सुरसिद्धौघपरीतमालुलोके ॥73.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुनः again, एषः this, निमज्य having submerged himself, पुण्यशाली the fortunate and virtuous Akrura, पुरुषम् the Supreme Person, त्वाम् You, परमम् the Supreme Lord, भुजङ्ग-भोगे on the coils of the serpent Ananta Shesha, अरि-कम्बु-गदा-अम्बुजैः with the discus, conch, mace, and lotus flower, स्फुरन्तम् splendidly shining, सुर-सिद्ध-ओघ-परीतम् surrounded by hosts of devas and celestial perfected beings, आलुलोके beheld

Simple Meaning: Dipping into the water once more, the immensely fortunate Akrura beheld You in Your divine form as the Supreme Lord Mahavishnu, reclining majestically upon the soft coils of the thousand-headed serpent Ananta Shesha. Holding the conch, discus, mace, and lotus flower, You shone with celestial splendour while being reverently worshiped by surrounding hosts of devas and perfected beings.

स तदा परमात्मसौख्यसिन्धौ विनिमग्नः प्रणुवन् प्रकारभेदैः ।
अविलोक्य पुनश्च हर्षसिन्धोरनुवृत्त्या पुलकावृतो ययौ त्वाम् ॥73.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he Akrura, तदा then, परमात्म-सौख्य-सिन्धौ in the ocean of Supreme spiritual bliss, विनिमग्नः deeply immersed, प्रणुवन् praising and offering hymns, प्रकार-भेदैः in various distinct ways, अविलोक्य no longer seeing the vision, पुनः च and again, हर्ष-सिन्धोः अनुवृत्त्या through the flow of that ocean of joy, पुलक-आवृतः covered with goosebumps of ecstasy, ययौ went back, त्वाम् to You

Simple Meaning: Immersed in the boundless ocean of Supreme Bliss upon receiving that divine vision, Akrura offered praises to You in various sweet ways. When the grand form dissolved back into the waters, he emerged from the river still submerged in that ocean of joy, his body covered in goosebumps of pure ecstasy, and returned to Your side at the chariot.

किमु शीतलिमा महान् जले यत् पुलकोऽसाविति चोदितेन तेन ।
अतिहर्षनिरुत्तरेण सार्धं रथवासी पवनेश पाहि मां त्वम् ॥73.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: किम् उ is it that, शीतलिमा extreme cold, महान् great, जले in the water, यत् because of which, पुलकः असौ this thrill or goosebumps, इति thus, चोदितेन questioned, तेन by You, अति-हर्ष-निरुत्तरेण-सार्धम् along with him Akrura who was rendered speechless by intense joy, रथ-वासी seated on the chariot, पवनेश O Lord of Guruvayur, पाहि protect, माम् me, त्वम् You

Simple Meaning: When Akrura returned to the chariot, You playfully questioned him: "Is the water so exceptionally cold that it has given you such thrilling goosebumps?" Speechless with intense bliss and awe, he could offer no answer. O Lord of Guruvayur, seated on that chariot, please protect me from all my afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
