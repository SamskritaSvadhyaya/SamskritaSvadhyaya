Krishna’s reunion with the gopis

दशकम् ६८

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 68

Krishna’s reunion with the gopis

तव विलोकनाद्गोपिकाजनाः प्रमदसङ्कुलाः पङ्कजेक्षण ।
अमृतधारया संप्लुता इव स्तिमिततां दधुस्त्वत्पुरोगताः ॥68.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: तव Your, विलोकनात् from beholding, गोपिका-जनाः the maidens of Vraja, प्रमद-सङ्कुलाः filled with ecstasy, पङ्कज-ईक्षण O lotus-eyed Lord, अमृत-धारया by a stream of nectar, संप्लुताः drenched/bathed, इव as if, स्तिमितताम् stillness/motionlessness, दधुः attained, त्वत्-पुरोगताः standing before You

Simple Meaning: O lotus-eyed Lord, upon beholding You, the gopis were filled with ecstasy. Standing before You, they became motionless, as if they had been drenched in a shower of nectar.

तदनु काचन त्वत्कराम्बुजं सपदि गृह्णती निर्विशङ्कितम् ।
घनपयोधरे सन्निधाय सा पुलकसंवृता तस्थुषी चिरम् ॥68.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदनु thereupon, काचन a certain gopi, त्वत्-कर-अम्बुजम् Your lotus hand, सपदि at once, गृह्णती seizing, निर्विशङ्कितम् without any hesitation, घन-पयोधरे on her full breast, सन्निधाय placing, सा she, पुलक-संवृता hair standing on end in ecstasy, तस्थुषी stood, चिरम् for a long time

Simple Meaning: Thereupon, a certain gopi, without the slightest hesitation, eagerly seized Your lotus hand and placed it upon her breast, standing motionless for a long time as her body thrilled with ecstasy.

तव विभोऽपरा कोमलं भुजं निजगलान्तरे पर्यवेष्टयत् ।
गलसमुद्गतं प्राणमारुतं प्रतिनिरुन्धतीवातिहर्षुला ॥68.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तव Your, विभो O All-pervading Lord, अपरा another gopi, कोमलम् soft, भुजम् arm, निज-गल-अन्तरे around her own neck, पर्यवेष्टयत् wrapped, गल-समुद्गतम् rising up through the throat, प्राण-मारुतम् the breath of life, प्रतिनिरुन्धती इव as if checking/holding back, अति-हर्षुला beside herself with joy

Simple Meaning: O All-pervading Lord, beside herself with immense joy, another gopi wrapped Your soft arm around her neck, as if holding back her very life-breath that was surging up through her throat in sheer ecstasy.

अपगतत्रपा कापि कामिनी तव मुखाम्बुजात् पूगचर्वितम् ।
प्रतिगृहय्य तद्वक्त्रपङ्कजे निदधती गता पूर्णकामताम् ॥68.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अपगत-त्रपा devoid of shyness/shame, कापि a certain, कामिनी passionate maiden, तव Your, मुख-अम्बुजात् from Your lotus mouth, पूग-चर्वितम् betel nut chew, प्रतिगृह्य receiving, तत्-वक्त्र-पङ्कजे in her lotus mouth, निदधती placing, गता attained, पूर्ण-कामताम् the state of total fulfillment of desire

Simple Meaning: Casting aside all shyness in the intensity of her devotion, a certain passionate maiden received the chewed betel nut directly from Your lotus mouth and placed it into her own, thereby attaining the ultimate fulfillment of her deep spiritual longing.

विकरुणो वने संविहाय मामपगतोऽसि का त्वामिह स्पृशेत् ।
इति सरोषया तावदेकया सजललोचनं वीक्षितो भवान् ॥68.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: विकरुणः merciless, वने in the forest, संविहाय having abandoned, माम् me, अपगतः असि You have gone away, का which woman, त्वाम् You, इह here, स्पृशेत् would touch, इति thus, स-रोषया with anger, तावत् then, एकया by one gopi, स-जल-लोचनम् with tear-filled eyes, वीक्षितः looked upon, भवान् You

Simple Meaning: "Leaving me all alone in the dark forest, You vanished so mercilessly! What woman here would ever want to touch You again?" Saying this, one gopi looked upon You with tear-filled eyes that mingled deep affection with anger.

इति मुदाऽऽकुलैर्वल्लवीजनैः सममुपागतो यामुने तटे ।
मृदुकुचाम्बरैः कल्पितासने घुसृणभासुरे पर्यशोभथाः ॥68.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, मुदा-आकुलैः filled with intense delight, वल्लवी-जनैः with the gopi maidens, समम् together, उपागतः arrived, यामुने तटे on the banks of the Yamuna, मृदु-कुच-अम्बरैः with soft upper garments from their breasts, कल्पित-आसने on the seat fashioned for You, घुसृण-भासुरे brilliant with saffron paste, पर्यशोभथाः You shone resplendently

Simple Meaning: Accompanied by the gopi maidens who were filled with intense delight, You arrived at the banks of the Yamuna. Seated upon a place specially prepared for You using their soft upper garments, which were radiant with saffron paste from their bodies, You shone with glorious beauty.

कतिविधा कृपा केऽपि सर्वतो धृतदयोदयाः केचिदाश्रिते ।
कतिचिदीदृशा मादृशेष्वपीत्यभिहितो भवान् वल्लवीजनैः ॥68.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: कति विधा how many kinds, कृपा compassion or grace, के अपि some persons, सर्वतः towards everyone, धृत-दय-उदयाः who show compassion, केचित् some others, आश्रिते towards those who seek refuge or depend on them, कतिचित् a few others, ईदृशाः like this, मादृशेषु अपि even towards those like us, इति thus, अभिहितः addressed, भवान् You, वल्लवी-जनैः by the gopi maidens

Simple Meaning: "How many kinds of compassion are there in this world? Some people extend grace to everyone unconditionally; some show mercy only to those who seek refuge in them; while a few others behave like You, showing no grace even to those like us who are totally devoted to them!" In this manner, You were questioned by the gopi maidens.

अयि कुमारिका नैव शङ्क्यतां कठिनता मयि प्रेमकातरे ।
मयि तु चेतसो वोऽनुवृत्तये कृतमिदं मयेत्यूचिवान् भवान् ॥68.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: अयि कुमारिकाः O young maidens, न एव by no means, शङ्क्यताम् should be suspected, कठिनता hardness or cruelty, मयि in Me, प्रेम-कातरे who am overwhelmed with love, मयि in Me, तु indeed, चेतसः of the mind, वः of all of you, अनुवृत्तये for constant absorption, कृतम् done, इदम् this, मया by Me, इति thus, ऊचिवान् spoke, भवान् You

Simple Meaning: "O young maidens, never suspect cruelty in Me, for I am completely overwhelmed with love for you! This disappearance was brought about by Me only to ensure that your minds remain unceasingly absorbed in thought of Me." Thus You spoke, reassuring them.

अयि निशम्यतां जीववल्लभाः प्रियतमो जनो नेदृशो मम ।
तदिह रम्यतां रम्ययामिनीष्वनुपरोधमित्यालपो विभो ॥68.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: अयि O, निशम्यताम् listen, जीव-वल्लभाः O maidens dearer to Me than life itself, प्रिय-तमः most dear, जनः person, न not, ईदृशः like this, मम to Me, तत् therefore, इह here, रम्यताम् sport in delight, रम्य-यामिनीषु during these enchanting nights, अनुपरोधम् without any restraint or hindrance, इति thus, आलपः You spoke, विभो O All-pervading Lord

Simple Meaning: "O maidens dearer to Me than life itself, listen! No one in this world is as dear to Me as you are. Therefore, sport in delight during these enchanting nights, free from all restraint!" Thus You spoke lovingly to them, O All-pervading Lord.

इति गिराधिकं मोदमेदुरैर्व्रजवधूजनैः साकमारमन् ।
कलितकौतुको रासखेलने गुरुपुरीपते पाहि मां गदात् ॥68.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, गिरा by Your words, अधिकम् immensely, मोद-मेदुरैः rendered thick with joy or ecstatic, व्रज-वधू-जनैः with the maidens of Vraja, साकम् together, आरमन् sporting, कलित-कौतुकः filled with enthusiasm or eager delight, रास-खेलने in the divine Rasaleela dance, गुरुपुरी-पते O Lord of Guruvayur, पाहि protect, माम् me, गदात् from disease

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Guruvayur, having thus made the young maidens of Vraja immensely ecstatic through Your sweet words, You sported with them and engaged with eager delight in the divine Rasaleela dance. Protect me from all my ailments!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
