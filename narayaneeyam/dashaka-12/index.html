Incarnation as boar (Varaha avatara)

दशकम् १२

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 12

Incarnation as boar (Varaha avatara)

(छन्दस् - वसन्ततिलका)

स्वायम्भुवो मनुरथो जनसर्गशीलो
दृष्ट्वा महीमसमये सलिले निमग्नाम् ।
स्रष्टारमाप शरणं भवदङ्घ्रिसेवा-
तुष्टाशयं मुनिजनैः सह सत्यलोके ॥ 12.1 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्वायम्भुवः मनुः Svayambhuva Manu, अथ then, जन-सर्ग-शीलः intent on the creation of mankind, दृष्ट्वा seeing, महीम् the Earth, असमये prematurely/at an improper time, सलिले in the cosmic ocean, निमग्नाम् submerged, स्रष्टारम् the Creator (Brahma), आप reached/took, शरणम् refuge in, भवत्-अङ्घ्रि-सेवा-तुष्ट-आशयम् whose heart was satisfied/purified by service to Your lotus feet, मुनि-जनैः सह along with the company of sages, सत्यलोके in Satyaloka

Simple Meaning: Then, Svayambhuva Manu, who was eager to fulfill his duty of populating the world, saw the Earth submerged prematurely beneath the cosmic ocean. He, along with the assembly of great sages, immediately approached and took refuge in Lord Brahma in Satyaloka—whose mind was fully contented and serene through dedicated service to Your lotus feet.

कष्टं प्रजाः सृजति मय्यवनिर्निमग्ना
स्थानं सरोजभव कल्पय तत् प्रजानाम् ।
इत्येवमेष कथितो मनुना स्वयंभूः
अम्भोरुहाक्ष तव पादयुगं व्यचिन्तीत् ॥ 12.2 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: कष्टम् Alas!/How tragic!, प्रजाः living beings, सृजति (as I am) creating, मयि while I am, अवनिः the Earth, निमग्ना is submerged, स्थानम् a place/habitation, सरोज-भव O Lotus-born Lord (Brahma), कल्पय create/ordain, तत् therefore, प्रजानाम् for the living beings, इति एवं thus, एषः this, कथितः addressed, मनुना by Manu, स्वयंभूः Self-born Brahma, अम्भो-रुह-अक्ष  O Lotus-eyed Lord, तव Your, पाद-युगम् pair of lotus feet, व्यचिन्तीत् pondered/meditated upon

Simple Meaning: Manu lamented to Brahma, "Alas! While I am engaged in populating the world with offspring, the Earth has sunk into the deep waters. O Lotus-born Lord, please ordain a proper dwelling place for these beings!" Being thus petitioned by Manu, the unborn Brahma began to deeply meditate upon Your pair of lotus feet, O Lotus-eyed Lord!

हा हा विभो जलमहं न्यपिबं पुरस्तात्
अद्यापि मज्जति मही किमहं करोमि ।
इत्थं त्वदङ्घ्रियुगलं शरणं यतोऽस्य
नासापुटात् समभवः शिशुकोलरूपी ॥ 12.3 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: हा हा Alas! Alas!, विभो O All-pervading Lord, जलम् the cosmic waters, अहम् I (Brahma), न्यपिबम् drank up, पुरस्तात् earlier/previously, अद्य अपि even today, मज्जति is sinking, मही the Earth, किम् what, अहम् I, करोमि shall do, इत्थम् thus, त्वत्-अङ्घ्रि-युगलम् Your pair of lotus feet, शरणम् refuge, यतः as he took, अस्य of him (Brahma), नासा-पुटात् from the nostril, समभवः You manifested, शिशु-कोल-रूपी in the form of a tiny boar (Shishu-Kola)

Simple Meaning: Brahma lamented, "Alas, O Supreme Lord! Earlier, I drank up the deluge waters to clear the way for creation, yet even now the Earth is sinking again! What should I do?" As he thus surrendered completely unto Your lotus feet, You emerged from his nostril in the form of a tiny boar!

अङ्गुष्ठमात्रवपुरुत्पतितः पुरस्तात्
भोयोऽथ कुम्भिसदृशः समजृम्भथास्त्वम् ।
अभ्रे तथाविधमुदीक्ष्य भवन्तमुच्चैः
विस्मेरतां विधिरगात् सह सूनुभिः स्वैः ॥ 12.4 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: अङ्गुष्ठ-मात्र-वपुः having a body measuring merely the size of a thumb, उत्पतितः springing up/shooting forth, पुरस्तात् right in front (out of Brahma's nostril), भूयः thereupon/immediately after, अथ then, कुम्भि-सदृशः equal in size to an elephant, समजृम्भथाः You expanded/grew, त्वम् You, अभ्रे in the sky, तथा-विधम् growing in such a magnificent manner, उदीक्ष्य beholding, भवन्तम् You, उच्चैः immensely, विस्मेरताम् to wonder/utter astonishment, विधिः Lord Brahma, अगात् reached/went, सह along with, सूनुभिः स्वैः his sons (Sanaka and the other sages)

Simple Meaning: Emerging initially with a tiny body no larger than a thumb, You shot forth into the sky and instantly expanded to the size of a mighty elephant! Seeing You grow so astonishingly high in the heavens, Lord Brahma, along with his sage sons, was struck with utter wonder and immense amazement!

कोऽसावचिन्त्यमहिमा किटिरुत्थितो मे
नासापुटात् किमु भवेदजितस्य माया ।
इत्थं विचिन्तयति धातरि शैलमात्रः
सद्यो भवन् किल जगर्जिथ घोरघोरम् ॥ 12.5 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: कः who, असौ is this, अचिन्त्य-महिमा of inconceivable majesty/glory, किटिः boar, उत्थितः emerged, मे from my, नासा-पुटात् nostril, किमु भवेत् could it be, अजितस्य of the Unconquerable Lord (Vishnu), माया the divine illusion/power (Maya), इत्थम् thus, विचिन्तयति while reflecting/pondering, धातरि Lord Brahma (the Creator), शैल-मात्रः massive as a mountain, सद्यः instantly, भवान् You, किल indeed, जगर्जिथ roared, घोर-घोरम् terribly and fiercely

Simple Meaning: While Lord Brahma was pondering in deep wonder, "Who is this boar of inconceivable prowess that has emerged from my nostril? Could this be the miraculous Maya of the unconquerable Lord Hari?", You instantly expanded to the colossal size of a mountain and let out a fierce, thunderous roar that echoed across the heavens!

तं ते निनादमुपकर्ण्य जनस्तपःस्थाः
सत्यस्थिताश्च मुनयो नुनुवुर्भवन्तम् ।
तत्स्तोत्रहर्षुलमनाः परिणद्य भूयः
तोयाशयं विपुलमूर्तिरवातरस्त्वम् ॥ 12.6 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् that, ते Your, निनादम् thunderous roar, उपकर्ण्य hearing, जन-तपः-स्थाः residing in Janaloka and Tapoloka, सत्य-स्थिताः च and those abiding in Satyaloka, मुनयः the sages, नुनुवुः praised/extolled, भवन्तम् You, तत्-स्तोत्र-हर्षुल-मनाः Your mind delighted by their hymns of praise, परिणद्य roaring again, भूयः repeatedly, तोयाशयम् into the vast expanse of the ocean, विपुल-मूर्तिः with Your colossal/gigantic form, अवातरः You descended, त्वम् You

Simple Meaning: Hearing Your thunderous roar echo through the realms, the exalted sages residing in Janaloka, Tapoloka, and Satyaloka began to sing Your divine praises. Delighted by their sacred hymns, You roared once again, and assuming a colossal form, You plunged directly into the vast depths of the cosmic ocean.

ऊर्ध्वप्रसारिपरिधूम्रविधूतरोमा
प्रोत्क्षिप्तवालधिरवाङ्मुखघोरघोणः ।
तूर्णप्रदीर्णजलदः परिघूर्णदक्ष्णा
स्तोतृन् मुनीन् शिशिरयन्नवतेरिथ त्वम् ॥ 12.7 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऊर्ध्व-प्रसारि-परिधूम्र-विधूत-रोमा bristling up and shaking Your smoky-grey bristles that pointed upwards, प्रोत्क्षिप्त-वालधिः holding Your tail thrown high in the air, अवाङ्मुख-घोर-घोणः pointing Your terrible, ferocious snout downwards, तूर्ण-प्रदीर्ण-जलदः instantly tearing apart the clouds as You descended, परिघूर्णद्-अक्ष्णा with Your rolling, brilliantly flashing eyes, स्तोतृन् the praising, मुनीन् worshipping sages, शिशिरयन् pleasing/showering cool grace upon, अवतेरिथ त्वम् You plunged down (into the waters)

Simple Meaning: With Your smoky-grey bristles standing erect and shaking, Your tail raised high, and Your fierce snout pointed downwards, You tore through the clouds as You descended. Looking upon the chanting sages with Your rolling, brilliant eyes, You filled their hearts with serene delight and grace as You plunged into the deep waters!

अन्तर्जलं तदनुसंकुलनक्रचक्रं
भ्राम्यत्तिमिङ्गिलकुलं कलुषोर्मिमालम् ।
आविश्य भीषणरवेण रसातलस्थान्
आकम्पयन् वसुमतीमगवेषयस्त्वम् ॥ 12.8 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्तर्जलम् deep underwater, तदनु thereafter, संकुल-नक्र-चक्रम् teeming with swarms of crocodiles, भ्राम्यत्-तिमिङ्गिल-कुलम् filled with schools of thrashing whale-eating monsters (timingilas), कलुष-ऊर्मि-मालम् with turbid, violent waves churning in series, आविश्य entering, भीषण-रवेण with a terrifying roar, रसातल-स्थान् the inhabitants of the subterranean world (Rasatala), आकम्पयन् causing to tremble, वसुमतीम् the Earth, अगवेषयः You searched for, त्वम् You

Simple Meaning: Plunging deep underwater into the dark ocean—swarming with ferocious crocodiles, thrashing with gigantic timingila monsters, and churning with violent, muddy waves—You entered with a thunderous, terrifying roar that shook the inhabitants of the subterranean realm (Rasatala) to their core. There, in those depths, You diligently sought out the submerged Earth!

दृष्ट्वाऽथ दैत्यहतकेन रसातलान्ते
संवेशितां झटिति कूटकिटिर्विभो त्वम् ।
आपातुकानविगणय्य सुरारिखेटान्
दंष्ट्राङ्कुरेण वसुधामदधाः सलीलम् ॥ 12.9 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: दृष्ट्वा having seen, अथ then, दैत्य-हतकेन by that vile daitya (Hiranyaksha), रसातल-अन्ते at the bottom of the subterranean realm (Rasatala), संवेशिताम् deposited/hidden, झटिति instantly, कूट-किटिः the extraordinary/divine Boar, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, त्वम् You, आपातुकान् rushing forward to attack, अविगणय्य ignoring/disregarding, सुर-अरि-खेटान् those wretched enemies of the demigods, दंष्ट्रा-अङ्कुरेण with the tip of Your curved tusk, वसुधाम् the Earth, अदधाः You lifted/held up, सलीलम् playfully/effortlessly

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord! Discovering the Earth hidden at the bottom of the abyssal depths of Rasatala by that vile daitya Hiranyaksha, You—in Your extraordinary divine Boar form—completely disregarded the wretched asuri guards rushing to attack You. Instead, You casually and effortlessly scooped up the entire Earth upon the tip of Your majestic tusk!

अभ्युद्धरन्नथ धरां दशनाग्रलग्न
मुस्ताङ्कुराङ्कित इवाधिकपीवरात्मा ।
उद्धूतघोरसलिलाज्जलधेरुदञ्चन्
क्रीडावराहवपुरीश्वर पाहि रोगात् ॥ 12.10 ॥

Word-by-word meaning: अभ्युद्धरन् uplifting/raising up, अथ then, धराम् the Earth, दशन-अग्र-लग्न-मुस्त-अङ्कुर-अङ्कितः इव appearing as if decorated with a tiny sprout of musta grass sticking to the tip of His tusk, अधिक-पीवर-आत्मा having a magnificently colossal/massive body, उद्धूत-घोर-सलिलात् from the ocean whose terrifying waters were wildly agitated, जलधेः from the ocean, उदञ्चन् emerging/rising up, क्रीडा-वराह-वपुः assuming the form of the divine Playful Boar, ईश्वर O Supreme Sovereign Lord, पाहि save/protect (me), रोगात् from disease/my afflictions

Simple Meaning: Uplifting the vast Earth, Your colossal, massive body emerged from the wildly churning, terrifying ocean waters. As You rose up, the immense Earth perched upon the tip of Your tusk looked as delicate and light as a tiny shoot of musta grass! O Supreme Lord who assumed the glorious form of the Playful Boar, please protect me from all my illnesses and afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
