Incident of the Syamantaka gem. Krishna's marriage to Jambavati and Satyabhama

॥ॐ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 80

Incident of the Syamantaka gem

Krishna's marriage to Jambavati and Satyabhama

सत्राजितस्त्वमथ लुब्धवदर्कलब्धं
दिव्यं स्यमन्तकमणिं भगवन्नयाचीः ।
तत्कारणं बहुविधं मम भाति नूनं
तस्यात्मजां त्वयि रतां छलतो विवोढुम् ॥80.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: सत्राजितः from Satrajit, त्वम् You, अथ then, लुब्ध-वत् like a greedy person, अर्क-लब्धम् obtained from the Sun God, दिव्यम् divine, स्यमन्तक-मणिम् the Syamantaka jewel, भगवन् O Lord, अयाचीः asked for, तत्-कारणम् the reason for that, बहुविधम् of many kinds, मम भाति appears to me, नूनम् certainly, तस्य-आत्मजाम् his daughter (Satyabhama), त्वयि रताम् who was devoted to You, छलतः under some pretext, विवोढुम् to marry

Simple Meaning: O Lord, You requested Satrajit for the divine Syamantaka jewel which he had received from the Sun God, appearing almost like a greedy person. The reasons may be many. But to me, the real reason behind Your action appears be a pretext to marry his daughter Satyabhama, who was deeply devoted to You.

अदत्तं तं तुभ्यं मणिवरमनेनाल्पमनसा
प्रसेनस्तद्भ्राता गलभुवि वहन् प्राप मृगयाम् ।
अहन्नेनं सिंहो मणिमहसि मांसभ्रमवशात्
कपीन्द्रस्तं हत्वा मणिमपि च बालाय ददिवान् ॥80.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: अदत्तम् not given, तम् that (jewel), तुभ्यम् to You, मणि-वरम् the precious jewel, अनेन अल्प-मनसा by this small-minded Satrajit, प्रसेनः Prasena, तत्-भ्राता his brother, गल-भुवि on his neck, वहन् wearing, प्राप went to, मृगयाम् hunting, अहन् killed, एनम् him, सिंहः a lion, मणि-महसि in the brilliance of the jewel, मांस-भ्रम-वशात् mistaking it for meat, कपि-इन्द्रः the leader of the monkeys - Jambavan, तम् that lion, हत्वा having killed, मणिम् the jewel, अपि च as well, बालाय to his young child, ददिवान् gave

Simple Meaning: When that narrow-minded Satrajit refused to give You that precious jewel, his brother Prasena wore it around his neck and went into the forest for hunting. A lion killed Prasena, mistaking the brilliant red radiance of the jewel for a piece of meat. Subsequently, Jambavan, the leader of the monkeys, killed the lion and gave the divine jewel to his child to play with.

शशंसुः सत्राजिद्गिरमनु जनास्त्वां मणिहरं
जनानां पीयूषं भवति गुणिनां दोषकणिका ।
ततः सर्वज्ञोऽपि स्वजनसहितो मार्गणपरः
प्रसेनं तं दृष्ट्वा हरिमपि गतोऽभूः कपिगुहाम् ॥80.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: शशंसुः talked about, सत्राजित्-गिरम् अनु following the words of Satrajit, जनाः the people, त्वाम् You, मणि-हरम् as the thief of the jewel, जनानाम् for the people, पीयूषम् nectar, भवति becomes, गुणिनाम् of the virtuous ones, दोष-कणिका even a small speck of fault, ततः thereupon, सर्वज्ञः अपि though omniscient, स्वजन-सहितः accompanied by Your people, मार्गण-परः engaged in search, प्रसेनम् Prasena, तम् that, दृष्ट्वा having seen, हरिमपि the lion also, गतः अभूः You reached, कपि-गुहाम् the cave of Jambavan

Simple Meaning: Following the slanderous accusations of Satrajit, the public began talking that You were the thief of the jewel. For common people, finding even a tiny speck of fault in virtuous souls is like sweet nectar to them. Thereupon, although You are omniscient and knew everything, You went out searching into the forest accompanied by Your people. Seeing the slain Prasena and then the dead lion, You eventually reached the cave of Jambavan.

भवन्तमवितर्कयन्नतिवयाः स्वयं जाम्बवान्
मुकुन्दशरणं हि मां क इह रोद्धुमित्यालपन् ।
विभो रघुपते हरे जय जयेत्यलं मुष्टिभिः
चिरं तव समर्चनं व्यधित भक्तचूडामणिः ॥80.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: भवन्तम् You, अवितर्कयन् failing to recognize, अतिवयाः aged, स्वयम् himself, जाम्बवान् Jambavan, मुकुन्द-शरणम् who has taken refuge in Mukunda, हि indeed, माम् me, कः who, इह here, रोद्धुम् to oppose or stop, इति thus, आलपन् exclaiming, विभो O Omnipresent Lord, रघुपते O Lord of the Raghus - Rama, हरे O Hari, जय जय victory victory, इति thus, अलम् intensely, मुष्टिभिः with his fists, चिरम् for a long time, तव Your, समर्चनम् worship, व्यधित performed, भक्त-चूडामणिः the crest-jewel among devotees

Simple Meaning: Failing to recognize You in Your present Krishna form, the aged Jambavan—the crest-jewel among devotees—exclaimed, "Who can oppose me, who has taken sole refuge in Mukunda?" Shouting "O Omnipresent Lord! O Lord of the Raghus! O Hari! Victory, victory to You!", he engaged in a long duel using his fists, which was in truth his mode of worshipping You.

बुध्वाऽथ तेन दत्तां नवरमणीं वरमणिं च परिगृह्णन् ।
अनुगृह्णन्नमुमागाः सपदि च सत्राजिते मणिं प्रादाः ॥80.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: बुध्वा having realized Your divine identity, अथ then, तेन by him (Jambavan), दत्ताम् given, नव-रमणीम् his young daughter (Jambavati), वर-मणिम् the precious jewel, च and, परिगृह्णन् accepting, अनुगृह्णन् blessing, अमुम् him (Jambavan), आगाः You returned, सपदि immediately, च and, सत्राजिते to Satrajit, मणिम् the jewel, प्रादाः You handed over

Simple Meaning: Having finally recognized Your divine identity as Lord Rama, Jambavan bowed in devotion and offered You, his young daughter Jambavati along with the precious jewel. Accepting both and bestowing Your supreme blessings upon him, You returned immediately to Dwaraka and handed over the Syamantaka jewel to Satrajit.

तदनु स खलु ब्रीलालोलो विलोलविलोचनां
दुहितरमहो धीमान् भामां गिरैव परार्पिताम् ।
अदित मणिना तुभ्यं लभ्यं समेत्य भवानपि
प्रमुदितमनास्तस्यैवादान्मणिं गहनाशयः ॥80.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदनु thereafter, सः he (Satrajit), खलु indeed, व्रीडा-लोलः restless and overcome with shame, विलोल-विलोचनाम् with beautiful expressive eyes, दुहितरम् daughter, अहो wonder, धीमान् wise, भामाम् Satyabhama, गिरा एव by words alone, पर-अर्पिताम् previously promised to others, अदित gave, मणिना with the jewel, तुभ्यम् to You, लभ्यम् obtainable or proper to accept, समेत्य reaching/considering, भवान् You, अपि also, प्रमुदित-मनाः with a delighted heart, तस्य एव to him alone, अदात् returned, मणिम् the jewel, गहन-आशयः whose deep intentions are unfathomable

Simple Meaning: Overcome with shame for his false accusations, Satrajit offered You his beautiful-eyed daughter Satyabhama—who had previously been promised only verbally to other suitors—along with the Syamantaka jewel, considering it proper to give her to You. Accepting Satyabhama with a delighted heart, You, whose inner intentions are deep and unfathomable, returned the jewel right back to Satrajit.

व्रीडाकुलां रमयति त्वयि सत्यभामां
कौन्तेयदाहकथयाथ कुरून् प्रयाते ।
ही गान्दिनेयकृतवर्मगिरा निपात्य
सत्राजितं शतधनुर्मणिमाजहार ॥80.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: व्रीडा-कुलाम् overcome with shyness, रमयति making happy or delighting, त्वयि You, सत्यभामाम् Satyabhama, कौन्तेय-दाह-कथया by the news of the burning of the Pandavas in the lac house, अथ then, कुरून् to Hastinapura, प्रयाते having departed, ही alas, गान्दिनेय-कृतवर्म-गिरा at the instigation of Akrura (son of Gandini) and Kritavarma, निपात्य having killed, सत्राजितम् Satrajit, शतधनुः Shatadhanva, मणिम् the jewel, आजहार carried away

Simple Meaning: While You were bringing delight to Your new bride Satyabhama, who was shy and bashful, the news arrived that the Pandavas had been burned in the lac house at Varanavata. Thereupon You departed for Hastinapura to offer condolences. Alas, during Your absence, Satrajit was killed by Shatadhanva at the instigation of Akrura and Kritavarma, and Shatadhanva carried away the Syamantaka jewel.

शोकात् कुरूनुपगतामवलोक्य कान्तां
हत्वा द्रुतं शतधनुं समहर्षयस्ताम् ।
रत्ने सशङ्क इव मैथिलगेहमेत्य
रामो गदां समशिशिक्षत धार्तराष्ट्रम् ॥80.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: शोकात् due to grief, कुरून् to Hastinapura in the Kuru region, उपगताम् having come, अवलोक्य seeing, कान्ताम् Your beloved Satyabhama, हत्वा having slain, द्रुतम् quickly, शतधनुम् Shatadhanva, समहर्षयः You gladdened, ताम् her, रत्ने regarding the jewel, सशङ्कः suspicious, इव as if, मैथिल-गेहम् to the house of Janaka in Mithila, एत्य going, रामः Balarama, गदाम् mace-fighting techniques, समशिशिक्षत taught, धार्तराष्ट्रम् to the son of Dhritarashtra - Duryodhana

Simple Meaning: Seeing Your beloved Satyabhama arrive in Hastinapura overwhelmed with grief, You returned quickly, killed Shatadhanva, and brought solace to her. Since the jewel was not found on Shatadhanva, Balarama was as if suspicious of Your intentions and left Dwarka. He went to Mithila, and stayed at the house of King Janaka, where he taught mace-fighting techniques to Duryodhana.

अक्रूर एष भगवन् भवदिच्छयैव
सत्राजितः कुचरितस्य युयोज हिंसाम् ।
अक्रूरतो मणिमनाहृतवान् पुनस्त्वं
तस्यैव भूतिमुपधातुमिति ब्रुवन्ति ॥80.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: अक्रूरः Akrura, एषः this, भगवन् O Lord, भवत्-इच्छया एव by Your divine will alone, सत्राजितः of Satrajit, कु-चरितस्य who had wicked behavior and committed sins, युयोज engineered or brought about, हिंसाम् the destruction or killing, अक्रूरतः from Akrura, मणिम् the Syamantaka jewel, अनाहृतवान् did not retrieve, पुनः त्वम् You on the other hand, तस्य एव for his benefit, भूतिम् prosperity and glory, उपधातुम् to bestow, इति thus, ब्रुवन्ति wise men say

Simple Meaning: O Lord, wise men say that it was entirely by Your divine will that Akrura brought about the destruction of Satrajit to punish his wicked conduct. Furthermore, You refrained from reclaiming the divine jewel from Akrura only to ensure that the wealth and prosperity generated by it remained with him.

भक्तस्त्वयि स्थिरतरः स हि गान्दिनेयः
तस्यैव कापथमतिः कथमीश जाता ।
विज्ञानवान् प्रशमवानहमित्युदीर्णं
गर्वं ध्रुवं शमयितुं भवता कृतैव ॥80.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: भक्तः devotee, त्वयि in You, स्थिरतरः exceedingly firm and unwavering, सः he, हि indeed, गान्दिनेयः son of Gandini - Akrura, तस्य-एव of his indeed, कापथ-मतिः inclination toward a bad or evil path, कथम् how, ईश O Lord, जाता arose, विज्ञानवान् I am knowledgeable, प्रशमवान् I am calm and self-controlled, अहम् I, इति उदीर्णम् thus inflated, गर्वम् pride, ध्रुवम् surely, शमयितुम् to humble or remove, भवता by You, कृता एव was brought about indeed

Simple Meaning: O Lord, Akrura was an exceedingly firm and devoted soul unto You. How then could an inclination toward an evil path arise in his mind (of killing Satraajit and getting the jewel)? It was surely brought about by You to humble his subtle pride—the inflated feeling that "I am wise, knowledgeable, and completely self-controlled."

यातं भयेन कृतवर्मयुतं पुनः
तमाहूय तद्विनिहितं च मणिं प्रकाश्य ।
तत्रैव सुव्रतधरे विनिधाय तुष्यन्
भामाकुचान्तशयनः पवनेश पायाः ॥80.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: यातम् who had fled, भयेन out of fear, कृतवर्म-युतम् along with Kritavarma, पुनः again, तम् आहूय summoning him (Akrura), तत्-विनिहितम् kept with him, च and, मणिम् the Syamantaka jewel, प्रकाश्य revealing or exposing it before the assembly, तत्र एव with him alone, सुव्रत-धरे who was observing pious vows, विनिधाय entrusting or leaving it, तुष्यन् being pleased, भामा-कुच-अन्त-शयनः resting on the bosom of Satyabhama, पवनेश O Lord of Guruvayur, पायाः protect me

Simple Meaning: Summoning Akrura—who had fled Dwaraka out of fear along with Kritavarma—You had him produce the Syamantaka jewel in full assembly. Confirming that it was safely with him, You happily entrusted the sacred jewel to Akrura himself, as he was observing righteous vows for the public good. Thus pleased, You rejoiced in the loving company of Satyabhama. O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect me from all ailments!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
