Dashaka 2

Form of the Lord

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 2

Form of the Lord

(छन्दस् - शार्दूलविक्रिडितम्)

सूर्यस्पर्धिकिरीटमूर्ध्वतिलकप्रोद्भासिफालान्तरं
कारुण्याकुलनेत्रमार्द्रहसितोल्लासं सुनासापुटम् ।
गण्डोद्यन्मकराभकुण्डलयुगं कण्ठोज्वलत्कौस्तुभं
त्वद्रूपं वनमाल्यहारपटलश्रीवत्सदीप्रं भजे ॥2.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: सूर्य-स्पर्धि-किरीटम् a crown that rivals the sun, ऊर्ध्व-तिलक-प्रोद्भासि-फाल-अन्तरम् a forehead splendidly shining with a vertical tilaka, कारुण्य-आकुल-नेत्रम् eyes filled with compassion, आर्द्र-हसित-उल्लासम् radiant with a gentle smile, सु-नासा-पुटम् possessing beautiful nose, गण्ड-उद्यन्-मकर-आभ-कुण्डलयुगम् a pair of crocodile shaped earrings reflecting on the cheeks, कण्ठ-उज्वलत्-कौस्तुभम् the kaustubha jewel shining on the neck, त्वत्-रूपम् your form, वन-माल्य-हार-पटल-श्रीवत्स-दीप्रम् brilliant with a garland of first flowers, rows of necklaces and the Shrivatsa mark, भजे I worship

दीप्र  a. Shining, brilliant, radiant, resplendent; src. Kosha. Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: I worship Your divine form – with Your head adorned with a crown that rivals the brilliance of the sun; with forehead splendidly shining with a vertical tilaka; with Your eyes filled with deep compassion; with Your face radiating a gentle, warm smile; with a beautifully shaped nose; a pair of crocodile shaped earrings reflecting brilliantly against Your cheeks; with Your neck shining with the resplendent kaustubha jewel, and decorated with a garland of forest flowers, rows of necklaces, and the auspicious Shrivatsa mark.

केयूराङ्गदकङ्कणोत्तममहारत्नाङ्गुलीयाङ्कित-
श्रीमद्बाहुचतुष्कसङ्गतगदाशङ्खारिपङ्केरुहाम् ।
काञ्चित् काञ्चनकाञ्चिलाञ्च्छितलसत्पीताम्बरालम्बिनीम्
आलम्बे विमलाम्बुजद्युतिपदां मूर्तिं तवार्तिच्छिदम् ॥2.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: केयूर-अङ्गद-कङ्कण-उत्तम-महारत्न-अङ्गुलीय-अङ्कित-श्रीमत्-बाहु-चतुष्क-सङ्गत-गदा-शङ्ख-अरि-पङ्केरुहाम् adorned with Keyur (epaulets - ornamental shoulder pieces), Angada (armlets), Kankana (bracelets) and rings set with excellent precious gems and holding the mace, conch, discus and lotus in the four majestic arms, काञ्चित् an indescribable, काञ्चन-काञ्ची-लाञ्च्छित-लसत्-पीताम्बर-आलम्बिनीम् clad in a shining yellow silk garment adorned with a golden waist belt, आलम्बे I take refuge in, विमल-अम्बुज-द्युति-पदाम् whose feet have the luster of a pure lotus, मूर्तिम् divine form, तव your, आर्ति-छिदम् destroyer of all afflictions

Simple Meaning: I take refuge in Your indescribable divine form, which destroys all human afflictions. Your four majestic arms are beautifully adorned with Keyur (epaulets), Angada (armlets), Kankana (bracelets) and rings set with the finest precious gems, while holding the mace, conch, discus, and lotus. You are clad in a glowing yellow silk garment secured with a shining golden waist belt, and Your sacred feet possess the soft, pure radiance of a spotless lotus.

यत्त्त्रैलोक्यमहीयसोऽपि महितं सम्मोहनं मोहनात्
कान्तं कान्तिनिधानतोऽपि मधुरं माधुर्यधुर्यादपि ।
सौन्दर्योत्तरतोऽपि सुन्दरतरं त्वद्रूपमाश्चर्यतः
अप्याश्चर्यं भुवने न कस्य कुतुकं पुष्णाति विष्णो विभो ॥2.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: यत् which, त्रैलोक्य-महीयसः अपि even than the most glorious in the three worlds, महितम् more worshipful, सम्मोहनम् more enchanting, मोहनात् than the most charming, कान्तम् more radiant, कान्ति-निधानतः अपि even than the storehouse of brilliance, मधुरम् sweeter, माधुर्य-धुर्यात् अपि even than the ultimate embodiment of sweetness, सौन्दर्य-उत्तरतः अपि even than the highest peak of beauty, सुन्दरतरम् far more beautiful, त्वत्-रूपम् your form, आश्चर्यतः अपि even than the greatest wonder, आश्चर्यम् a wonder, भुवने in the world, न not, कस्य whose, कुतुकम् delight, पुष्णाति nourishes, विष्णो O Vishnu, विभो O all pervading Lord

Simple Meaning: O all pervading Lord Vishnu! Your divine form is more worshipful than the most glorious things in the three worlds, more enchanting than the most charming, more radiant than the very source of brilliance, sweeter than the ultimate sweetness itself, and far more beautiful than the very pinnacle of beauty. It is indeed a wonder of wonders. In this world, whose heart and mind would this form not fill with intense delight and curiosity?

तत्तादृङ्मधुरात्मकं तव वपुः सम्प्राप्य सम्पन्मयी
सा देवी परमोत्सुका चिरतरं नास्ते स्वभक्तेष्वपि ।
तेनास्या बत कष्टमच्युत विभो त्वद्रूपमानोज्ञक -
प्रेमस्थैर्यमयादचापलबलाच्चापल्यवार्तोदभूत् ॥2.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्-तादृक्-मधुर-आत्मकम् that body which is of such an incredibly sweet nature, तव your, वपुः divine form, सम्प्राप्य having obtained, सम्पद्-मयी the embodiment of prosperity and wealth, सा that, देवी Devi Lakshmi, परम-उत्सुका extremely eager to be with you, चिरतरम् for very long, न not, आस्ते stays, स्व-भक्तेषु अपि even with her own devotees, तेन because of that, अस्याः her, बत alas, कष्टम् what a pity, अच्युत O changeless Lord, विभो O all pervading Lord, त्वद्-रूप-मानोज्ञक-प्रेम-स्थैर्यमयात् due to the stability of her deep love for the beauty of your form, अचापल-बलात् due to the strength of her unwavering devotion, चापल्य-वार्ता the rumor of being fickle, उदभूत् arose

Simple Meaning: O all pervading and changeless Lord Achyuta! Having obtained Your divine form which is of such exquisite sweetness, the embodiment of prosperity and wealth Devi Lakshmi, is so deeply attached to You that she does not stay for long even with her own dedicated devotees. What a pity indeed! It is actually her absolute, unwavering devotion to Your beautiful form that keeps her constantly by Your side. Yet, because she never leaves You, it has ironically given rise to the popular rumor that she is fickle and never stays in one place!

लक्ष्मीस्तावकरामणीयकहृतैवेयं परेष्वस्थिरा
इत्यस्मिन्नन्यदपि प्रमाणमधुना वक्ष्यामि लक्ष्मीपते ।
ये त्वद्ध्यानगुणानुकीर्तनरसासक्ता हि भक्ता जनाः
तेष्वेषा वसति स्थिरैव दयितप्रस्तावदत्तादरा ॥2.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: लक्ष्मीः Lakshmi, तावक-रामणीयक-हृता एव इयम् completely captivated and stolen by Your beauty alone, परेषु in others (other deities or places), अस्थिरा unstable (fickle), इति अस्मिन् in this matter, अन्यत् another, अपि also, प्रमाणम् proof, अधुना now, वक्ष्यामि I shall tell, लक्ष्मीपते O Lord of Lakshmi, ये those who, त्वत्-ध्यान-गुण-अनुकीर्तन-रस-आसक्ताः immersed in the joy of meditating upon You and Your glories, हि indeed, भक्ताः devotees, जनाः people, तेषु in them, एषा She (Lakshmi), वसति dwells, स्थिरा एव permanently indeed, दयित-प्रस्ताव-दत्त-आदरा showing respect whenever her beloved Lord is mentioned

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Lakshmi! I shall now provide another proof to show that Devi Lakshmi's fickleness is only because her heart has been completely captivated by Your divine beauty. Those devoted souls who are joyfully immersed in meditating upon You and singing Your glories find that She dwells permanently in their homes. This is because, She dwells wherever her beloved Lord's glories are being discussed!

एवं भूतमनोज्ञतानवसुधानिष्यन्दसन्दोहनं
त्वद्रूपं परचिद्रसायनमयं चेतोहरं शृण्वताम् ।
सद्यः प्रेरयते मतिं मदयते रोमाञ्चयत्यङ्गकं
व्यासिञ्चत्यपि शीतबाष्पविसरैरानन्दमूर्छोद्भवैः ॥2.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् भूत thus, मनोज्ञता-नव-सुधा-निष्यन्द-सन्दोहनम् which is a torrential downpour of the fresh nectar of such exquisite beauty, त्वत्-रूपम् Your divine form, पर-चित्-रसायन-मयम् of the nature of the supreme elixir of pure consciousness, चेतः-हरम् captivating the heart, शृण्वताम् of those who hear (about it), सद्यः immediately, प्रेरयते stimulates (towards You), मतिम् the intellect, मदयते intoxicates with joy, रोमाञ्चयति causes goosebumps on, अङ्गकम् the body, व्यासिञ्चति अपि and also thoroughly drenches, शीत-बाष्प-विसरैः with a flood of cool tears, आनन्द-मूर्छा-उद्भवैः arising from the ecstatic swoon of supreme bliss

Simple Meaning: Thus, Your divine form is a torrential downpour of the fresh nectar of exquisite beauty, and the ultimate elixir of supreme consciousness that instantly captivates the heart. Even just hearing about Your glorious form immediately directs the mind towards You, fills the listener with ecstatic joy, sends a thrill of goosebumps through their body, and drenches them in cool tears of boundless bliss.

एवं भूततया हि भक्त्यभिहितो योगस्स योगद्वयात्
कर्मज्ञानमयात् भृशोत्तमतरो योगीश्वरैर्गीयते ।
सौन्दर्यैकरसात्मके त्वयि खलु प्रेमप्रकर्षात्मिका
भक्तिर्निश्रममेव विश्वपुरुषैर्लभ्या रमावल्लभ ॥2.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् भूततया due to being of such a nature, हि indeed, भक्ति-अभिहितः known by the name of bhakti, योगः path of yoga, सः that, योग-द्वयात् than the other two paths of yoga, कर्म-ज्ञान-मयात् consisting of karma (action) and gnana (knowledge), भृश-उत्तमतरः far superior, योग-ईश्वरैः by the greatest of yogis, गीयते is sung, सौन्दर्य-एक-रस-आत्मके in You who are the very essence of absolute beauty, त्वयि in You, खलु indeed, प्रेम-प्रकर्ष-आत्मिका of the nature of intense love, भक्तिः devotion, निश्रमम् एव with no effort at all, विश्व-पुरुषैः by all people in the world, लभ्या is attainable, रमा-वल्लभ O beloved Lord of Lakshmi

Simple Meaning: O beloved Lord of Lakshmi! Because of its sweet and joyful nature, this path of yoga known as Bhakti (devotion) is sung by the greatest of sages as being far superior to the other two paths of Karma (action) and Gnana (knowledge). In You, who are the ultimate embodiment of absolute beauty and love, this intense devotion can be attained effortlessly by any person in the world.

निष्कामं नियतस्वधर्मचरणं यत् कर्मयोगाभिधं
तद्दूरेत्यफलं यदौपनिषदज्ञानोपलभ्यं पुनः ।
तत्त्वव्यक्ततया सुदुर्गमतरं चित्तस्य तस्माद्विभो
त्वत्प्रेमात्मकभक्तिरेव सततं स्वादीयसी श्रेयसी ॥2.8॥

Word by word meaning: निष्कामम् selfless, नियत-स्व-धर्म-चरणम् practice of one's own prescribed duty, यत् which, कर्म-योग-अभिधम् known as karma yoga, तत् that, दूरेत्य-फलम् its fruits are attained in distant future, यत् which, औपनिषद-ज्ञान-उपलभ्यम् attainable through knowledge of the Upanishads, पुनः moreover, तत्त्व-अव्यक्ततया due to the unmanifest nature of the reality/abstract, सु-दुर्गमतरम् very difficult to attain, चित्तस्य for the mind, तस्मात् therefore, विभो O all pervading Lord, त्वत्-प्रेम-आत्मिक-भक्तिः एव bhakti which consists of love for You alone, सततम् always, स्वादीयसी sweeter, श्रेयसी better

Simple Meaning: Karma yoga, which consists of performing one's prescribed duties, gives fruits in the distant future. Gnana yoga, which consists of understanding the nature of Brahman through knowledge of Upanishads, is extremely difficult for the mind because of its abstract nature. Therefore, O all pervading Lord, Bhakti, which consists of love for You alone, is always sweeter and more beneficial.

अत्यायासकराणि कर्मपटलान्याचर्य निर्यन्मला
बोधे भक्तिपथेऽथवाऽप्युचिततामायान्ति किं तावता ।
क्लिष्ट्वा तर्कपथे परं तव वपुर्ब्रह्माख्यमन्ये पुनः
चित्तार्द्रत्वमृते विचिन्त्य बहुभिस्सिद्ध्यन्ति जन्मान्तरैः ॥2.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: अति-आयास-कराणि causing immense strain and effort, कर्म-पटलानि multitudes of ritual actions (Karma Yoga), आचर्य having performed, निर्यन्-मलाः having washed away their impurities, बोधे in the path of knowledge, भक्ति-पथे in the path of devotion, अथवा अपि or even, उचितताम् आयान्ति attain eligibility, किम् what (is the use), तावता by that much, क्लिष्ट्वा after exerting, तर्क-पथे on the path of logical reasoning (Gnana Yoga), परम् ultimate, तव Your, वपुः body, ब्रह्म-आख्यम् known as Brahman, अन्ये others, पुनः on the other hand, चित्त-आर्द्रत्वम् ऋते without the melting of the heart, विचिन्त्य contemplating, बहुभिः by many, सिद्ध्यन्ति achieve liberation, जन्म-अन्तरैः after several lifetimes

Simple Meaning: Having performed highly strenuous, ritualistic actions (Karma Yoga) over a long period, some people wash away their mental impurities and finally become eligible to enter the paths of knowledge (Gnana yoga) or devotion (Bhakti yoga). But what is the use of wasting so much time and effort in just preparing oneself? Others, on the other hand, exhaust themselves on the path of logical reasoning (Gnana Yoga), contemplating Your unmanifest aspect known as Brahman. Without the melting of the heart, they can only achieve liberation after struggling through many, many lifetimes.

त्वद्भक्तिस्तु कथारसामृतझरीनिर्मज्जनेन स्वयं
सिद्ध्यन्ती विमलप्रबोधपदवीमक्लेशतस्तन्वती ।
सद्यस्सिद्धिकरी जयत्ययि विभो सैवास्तु मे त्वत्पद-
प्रेमप्रौढिरसार्द्रता द्रुततरं वातालयाधीश्वर ॥2.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-भक्तिः तु but devotion to You, कथा-रस-अमृत-झरी-निर्मज्जनेन by immersing oneself in the nectarine torrent of Your stories, स्वयम् on its own, सिद्ध्यन्ती becoming accomplished, विमल-प्रबोध-पदवीम् the state of pure knowledge, अक्लेशतः without any effort, तन्वती bringing about, सद्यः सिद्धिकरी granting immediate liberation, जयति triumphs gloriously, अयि विभो O all-pervading Lord, सा एव अस्तु let that alone be, मे for me, त्वत्-पद-प्रेम-प्रौढि-रस-आर्द्रता the wetness of intense love for Your lotus feet, द्रुततरम् very quickly, वात-आलय-अधीश्वर O Lord of the home of the Wind - Guruvayoor

Simple Meaning: But devotion to You, O Lord, is altogether different. It is effortlessly accomplished on its own, simply by bathing in the flowing river of Your sweet, nectar-like stories. Without causing the slightest strain, it naturally brings about the state of pure knowledge, granting immediate liberation. Thus, the path of devotion stands gloriously supreme! O all-pervading Lord of Guruvayoor, please quickly bless me with that very devotion, drenching my heart in the intense love for Your lotus feet.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
