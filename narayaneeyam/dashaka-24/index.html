The story of Prahlada

दशकम् २४

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 24

The story of Prahlada

(छन्दस् - शिखरिणी)

हिरण्याक्षे पोत्रिप्रवरवपुषा देव भवता
हते शोकक्रोधग्लपितधृतिरेतस्य सहजः।
हिरण्यप्रारम्भः कशिपुरमरारातिसदसि
प्रतिज्ञमातेने तव किल वधार्थं मधुरिपो ॥24.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: हिरण्याक्षे when Hiranyaksha, पोत्रि-प्रवर-वपुषा by You in the form of the supreme boar, देव O Lord, भवता by You, हते when killed, शोक-क्रोध-ग्लपित-धृतिः his composure lost due to grief and anger, एतस्य his, सहजः brother, हिरण्य-प्रारम्भः the one whose name starts with Hiranya, कशिपुः Kashipu, अमर-अराति-सदसि in the assembly of the enemies of the immortals/devas, प्रतिज्ञाम् आतेने took a vow, तव Your, किल indeed, वध-अर्थम् for the purpose of killing, मधुरिपो O Slayer of Madhu

Simple Meaning: O Lord, Slayer of Madhu, when Hiranyaksha was slain by You incarnate in the form of the supreme boar, his brother Hiranyakashipu lost all mental composure owing to grief and anger. Standing in the assembly of the asuras, he took a solemn vow to bring about Your death.

विधातारं घोरं स खलु तपसित्वा न चिरतः
पुरः साक्षात्कुर्वन् सुरनरमृगाद्यैरनिधनम् ।
वरं लब्ध्वा दृप्तो जगदिह भवन्नायकमिदं
परिक्षुन्दन्निन्द्रादहरत दिवं त्वामगणयन् ॥24.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: विधातारम् Brahma (the creator), घोरम् intense, सः he, खलु indeed, तपसित्वा performing austerities, न चिरतः without delay/soon, पुरः before him, साक्षात् कुर्वन् causing to appear in person, सुर-नर-मृग-आद्यैः by devas, humans, beasts, and others, अनिधनम् unkillable/non-death, वरम् boon, लब्ध्वा obtaining, दृप्तः puffed up with pride/arrogant, जगत् इह this world, भवत्-नायकम् इदम् which has You as its Lord, परिक्षुन्दन् tormenting/oppressing, इन्द्रात् from Indra, अहरत snatched, दिवम् heaven, त्वाम् अगणयन् disregarding You

Simple Meaning: By performing intense austerities, Hiranyakashipu soon compelled Lord Brahma to appear before him and obtained the boon that he could not be killed by devas, humans, beasts, or any other creatures. Becoming highly arrogant upon receiving this boon, he began oppressing this entire world which has You as its Supreme Lord. Disregarding You, he usurped the heavens from Indra.

निहन्तुं त्वां भूयस्तव पदमवाप्तस्य च रिपोः
बहिर्दृष्टेरन्तर्दधिथ हृदये सूक्ष्मवपुषा ।
नदन्नुच्चैस्तत्राप्यखिलभुवनान्ते च मृगयन्
भिया यातं मत्वा स खलु जितकाशी निववृते ॥24.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: निहन्तुम् to kill, त्वाम् You, भूयः again, तव Your, पदम् अवाप्तस्य च who had reached the abode, रिपोः of the enemy, बहिः दृष्टेः from outer vision, अन्तर्दधिथ You vanished, हृदये in the heart, सूक्ष्म-वपुषा in a subtle form, नदन् उच्चैः roaring loudly, तत्र अपि even there, अखिल-भुवन-अन्ते च and across the boundaries of all the worlds, मृगयन् searching for You, भिया out of fear, यातम् having fled, मत्वा thinking, सः he, खलु indeed, जित-काशी boasting of victory/considering himself victorious, निववृते turned back

Simple Meaning: When that enemy Hiranyakashipu came searching for You to kill You, even having reached Your realm, You vanished from his outward sight and hid inside his very heart in a subtle form. Roaring loudly, he searched for You across the ends of all the worlds. Thinking that You had fled out of fear, he felt victorious and returned home.

ततोऽस्य प्रह्लादः समजनि सुतो गर्भवसतौ मुनेर्वीणापाणेरधिगतभवद्भक्तिमहिमा ।
स वै जात्या दैत्यः शिशुरपि समेत्य त्वयि रतिं
गतस्त्वद्भक्तानां वरद परमोदाहरणताम् ॥24.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः thereafter, अस्य to him, प्रह्लादः Prahlada, समजनि was born, सुतः son, गर्भ-वसतौ while residing in the womb, मुनेः वीणापाणेः from the sage holding the veena in hand - Narada, अधिगत-भवत्-भक्ति-महिमा who acquired the power of devotion unto You, सः he, वै indeed, जात्या by birth, दैत्यः a daitya, शिशुः अपि even as a child, समेत्य attaining, त्वयि in You, रतिम् intense love/attachment, गतः त्वत्-भक्तानाम् reached among Your devotees, वरद O Giver of Boons, परम-उदाहरणताम् the state of being the supreme example

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, a son named Prahlada was born to him. While still in his mother's womb, Prahlada had acquired knowledge about the power of devotion to You from the veena-playing sage Narada. Even though he was a daitya by birth and just a child, he attained deep love for You and became the supreme example among all Your devotees, O Giver of Boons!

सुरारीणां हास्यं तव चरणदास्यं निजसुते
स दृष्ट्वा दुष्टात्मा गुरुभिरशिशिक्षच्चिरममुम् ।
गुरुप्रोक्तं चासाविदमिदमभद्राय दृढमिति
अपाकुर्वन् सर्वं तव चरणभक्त्यैव ववृधे ॥24.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुर-अरीणाम् to the enemies of the devas, हास्यम् laughable/ridiculous, तव चरण-दास्यम् service to Your lotus feet, निज-सुते in his own son, सः he (Hiranyakashipu), दृष्ट्वा seeing, दुष्ट-आत्मा evil-minded, गुरुभिः by the gurus (Chanda and Amarka), अशिशिक्षत् चिरम् अमुम् got him instructed for a long time, गुरु-प्रोक्तम् च and what was taught by the gurus, असौ he (Prahlada), इदम् इदम् this and this, अभद्राय for ill-fortune/leading to harm, दृढम् surely, इति अपाकुर्वन् rejecting thus, सर्वम् everything, तव चरण-भक्त्या एव through devotion to Your feet only, ववृधे grew/flourished

Simple Meaning: Seeing his own son devoted to Your lotus feet—a practice considered ridiculous by the asuras—that evil-minded Hiranyakashipu had him instructed for a long time by his gurus. However, Prahlada rejected all their teachings, firmly realizing that worldly ways only lead to harm. Rejecting everything taught by them, he flourished solely through his steadfast devotion to Your lotus feet.

अधीतेषु श्रेष्ठं किमिति परिपृष्टेऽथ तनये
भवद्भक्तिं वर्यामभिगदति पर्याकुलधृतिः ।
गुरुभ्यो रोषित्वा सहजमतिरस्येत्यभिविदन्
वधोपायानस्मिन् व्यतनुत भवत्पादशरणे ॥24.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: अधीतेषु among the things learned, श्रेष्ठम् the best, किम् what, इति thus, परिपृष्टे when asked, अथ then, तनये his son, भवत्-भक्तिम् devotion to You, वर्याम् the supreme, अभिगदति declaring, पर्याकुल-धृतिः extremely agitated in mind, गुरुभ्यः at the teachers, रोषित्वा getting angry, सहज-मतिः अस्य natural intellect of his, इति अभिविदन् understanding thus, वध-उपायान् methods of killing, अस्मिन् against him, व्यतनुत employed/devised, भवत्-पाद-शरणे who took refuge at Your lotus feet

Simple Meaning: When Hiranyakashipu asked his son what was the best thing he had learned, Prahlada declared that devotion to You was the supreme pursuit. Deeply agitated and enraged at the teachers, Hiranyakashipu realized that this devotion was the child's natural mindset. He then began devising various methods to execute his own son, who took refuge at Your lotus feet.

स शूलैराविद्धः सुबहु मथितो दिग्गजगणैः महासर्पैर्दष्टोऽप्यनशनगराहारविधुतः ।
गिरीन्द्रवक्षिप्तोऽप्यहह! परमात्मन्नयि विभो
त्वयि न्यस्तात्मत्वात् किमपि न निपीडामभजत ॥24.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, शूलैः आविद्धः pierced with tridents, सुबहु-मथितः severely trampled, दिक्-गज-गणैः by the herd of mighty elephants, महा-सर्पैः दष्टः अपि even though bitten by huge serpents, अनशन-गर-आहार-विधुतः distressed by starvation and poison-laced food, गिरि-इन्द्र-अवक्षिप्तः अपि even though thrown from the king of mountains, अहह alas/O wonder, परमात्मन् अयि विभो O Supreme Soul, O All Pervading Lord, त्वयि in You, न्यस्त आत्मत्वात् because of dedicating his mind and soul, किमपि न not in the least, निपीडाम् pain/torment, अभजत suffered

Simple Meaning: Pierced with sharp tridents, trampled severely by herds of mighty elephants, bitten by deadly serpents, starved, fed poisoned food, and hurled down from high mountain peaks, he did not suffer even the slightest pain. What a wonder, O Supreme Spirit, O All Pervading Lord! This was because he had completely surrendered his mind and soul unto You.

ततः शङ्काविष्टः स पुनरतिदुष्टोऽस्य जनको
गुरूक्त्या तद्गेहे किल वरुणपाशैस्तमरुणत् ।
गुरोश्चासान्निध्ये स पुनरनुगान् दैत्यतनयान्
भवद्भक्तेस्तत्त्वं परममपि विज्ञानमशिषत् ॥24.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, शङ्का-आविष्टः seized with anxiety/suspicious, सः he, पुनः अति-दुष्टः again that extremely evil one, अस्य his, जनकः father, गुरु-उक्त्या on the advice of the teachers, तत्-गेहे in their home, किल indeed, वरुण-पाशैः with the bonds of Varuna, तम् him, अरुणत् bound/confined, गुरोः च असान्निध्ये and in the absence of the teachers, सः he (Prahlada), पुनः again, अनुगान् followers, दैत्य-तनयान् the sons of the daityas, भवत्-भक्तेः of devotion to You, तत्त्वम् the core truth, परमम् अपि as well as the supreme, विज्ञानम् direct spiritual realization, अशिषत् taught

Simple Meaning: Filled with anxiety and doubt, his extremely evil father, acting on the advice of the gurus, had him bound with the ropes of Varuna inside the teachers' home. However, during the absence of the teachers, Prahlada taught his fellow daitya classmates - the core truth of devotion to You along with the supreme knowledge.

पिता शृण्वन् बालप्रकरमखिलं त्वत्स्तुतिपरं
रुषान्धः प्राहैनं कुलहतक कस्ते बलमिति ।
बलं मे वैकुण्ठस्तव च जगतां चापि स बलं
स एव त्रैलोक्यं सकलमिति धीरोऽयमगदीत् ॥24.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: पिता father (Hiranyakashipu), शृण्वन् hearing, बाल-प्रकरम् all the group of children, अखिलम् completely, त्वत्-स्तुति-परम् engaged in praising You, रुषा-अन्धः blinded by anger, प्राह एनम् said to him (Prahlada), कुल-हतक O disgrace to the family, कः who, ते Your, बलम् strength, इति thus, बलम् strength, मे my, वैकुण्ठः Vaikuntha (Lord Vishnu), तव Your, च and, जगताम् of all the worlds, च अपि and also, सः He, बलम् strength, सः एव He alone, त्रैलोक्यम् the three worlds, सकलम् entire, इति thus, धीरः अयम् this courageous child, अगदीत् declared

Simple Meaning: Hearing that the entire group of children was devotedly singing Your praises, his father, blinded by fury, confronted Prahlada, and asked him, “O you who are a disgrace to the family! Who is your strength?” The courageous child replied, “Lord Vishnu is my strength, as well as your strength, and the strength of all the worlds, and He alone constitutes the entire universe.”

अरे क्वासौ क्वासौ सकलजगदात्मा हरिरिति
प्रभिन्ते स्म स्तंभं चलितकरवालो दितिसुतः ।
अतः पश्चाद्विष्णो न हि वदितुमीशोऽस्मि सहसा
कृपात्मन् विश्वात्मन् पवनपुरवासिन् मृडय माम् ॥24.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: अरे O, क्व असौ where is He, क्व असौ where is He, सकल-जगत्-आत्मा the soul of the entire universe, हरिः Hari, इति thus, प्रभिन्ते स्म smashed, स्तंभम् the pillar, चलित-करवालः brandishing a sword, दिति-सुतः the son of Diti (Hiranyakashipu), अतः पश्चात् after this, विष्णो O Vishnu, न हि indeed not, वदितुम् to describe, ईशः O Lord, अस्मि I am, सहसा suddenly/all at once, कृपात्मन् O Embodiment of Mercy, विश्वात्मन् O Soul of the Universe, पवन-पुर-वासिन् O Inhabitant of Guruvayur, मृडय माम् make me happy/bless me

Simple Meaning: Brandishing his sword in fury, that son of Diti shouted, “Where is He? Where is this Hari who is supposed to be the soul of the entire universe?”, and struck the pillar forcefully. O Lord Vishnu, O Embodiment of Mercy, O Soul of the Universe, O Inhabitant of Guruvayur, I am unable to describe what happened right after this moment. Please make me happy!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
