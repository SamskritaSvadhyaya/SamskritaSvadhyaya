Dashaka 82

Conquest over Bana and salvation of Nriga

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 82

Conquest over Bana and salvation of Nriga

प्रद्युम्नो रौक्मिणेयः स खलु तव कला शम्बरेणाहृतस्तं
हत्वा रत्या सहाप्तो निजपुरमहरद्रुक्मिकन्यां च धन्याम् ।
तत्पुत्रोऽथानिरुद्धो गुणनिधिरवहद्रोचनां रुक्मिपौत्रीं
तत्रोद्वाहे गतस्त्वं न्यवधि मुसलिना रुक्म्यपि द्यूतवैरात् ॥82.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रद्युम्नः Pradyumna, रौक्मिणेयः son of Rukmini, सः he, खलु indeed, तव Your, कला partial incarnation (aspect of Kamadeva), शम्बरेण by the asura Shambara, आहृतः was abducted, तम् him (Shambara), हत्वा having slain, रत्या सह along with Rati, आप्तः returning to, निज-पुरम् his own city Dwaraka, अहरत् abducted/married, रुक्मि-कन्याम् the daughter of Rukmi (Rukmavati), च and, धन्याम् blessed, तत्-पुत्रः his son, अथ then, अनिरुद्धः Aniruddha, गुण-निधिः a treasure house of virtues, अवहत् married, रोचनाम् Rochana, रुक्मि-पौत्रीम् the granddaughter of Rukmi, तत्र-उद्वाहे at that wedding, गतः went, त्वम् You, न्यवधि was slain, मुसलिना by Balarama (Musali), रुक्मी अपि Rukmi also, द्यूत-वैरात् on account of enmity/dispute over a game of dice

Simple Meaning: Your son Pradyumna, born of Rukmini and an aspect of Your divine power, was abducted as an infant by the asura Shambara. Having slain Shambara with the aid of Rati, Pradyumna returned to Dwaraka with her as his wife. He subsequently married Rukmi's virtuous daughter Rukmavati by carrying her away. Their son Aniruddha, a reservoir of noble virtues, married Rukmi's granddaughter Rochana. When You and Balarama attended that wedding in Bhojakata, a fierce dispute arose over a game of dice, and Balarama slew Rukmi.

बाणस्य सा बलिसुतस्य सहस्रबाहोः
माहेश्वरस्य महिता दुहिता किलोषा ।
त्वत्पौत्रमेनमनिरुद्धमदृष्टपूर्वं
स्वप्नेऽनुभूय भगवन् विरहातुराऽभूत् ॥82.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: बाणस्य of Banasura, सा she, बलिसुतस्य the son of King Bali, सहस्र-बाहोः having a thousand arms, माहेश्वरस्य a great devotee of Lord Shiva (Maheshvara), महिता honored and glorious, दुहिता daughter, किल indeed, उषा Usha, त्वत्-पौत्रम् एनम् अनिरुद्धम् this grandson of Yours Aniruddha, अदृष्ट-पूर्वम् never seen before, स्वप्ने in a dream, अनुभूय having experienced or met, भगवन् O Lord, विरह-आतुरा-अभूत् became overwhelmed with the agony of separation

Simple Meaning: O Lord, Banasura, the son of King Bali, was a thousand-armed king and an exalted devotee of Lord Shiva. His glorious daughter Usha, who had never seen Your grandson Aniruddha before, experienced a meeting with him in a dream and subsequently became deeply overwhelmed with the agony of separation from him.

योगिन्यतीव कुशला खलु चित्रलेखा
तस्याः सखी विलिखती तरुणानशेषान् ।
तत्रानिरुद्धमुषया विदितं निशायाम्
आनेष्ट योगबलतो भवतो निकेतात् ॥82.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: योगिनी a mystic yogini, अतीव extremely, कुशला skillful, खलु indeed, चित्रलेखा Chitralekha, तस्याः her, सखी companion, विलिखती drawing portraits, तरुणान् अशेषान् of all young princes without exception, तत्र among them, अनिरुद्धम् Aniruddha, उषया by Usha, विदितम् identified, निशायाम् at night, आनेष्ट brought, योगबलतः through her yogic powers, भवतः निकेतात् from Your palace in Dwaraka

Simple Meaning: Usha's companion Chitralekha was an exceptionally skilled mystic yogini. She drew portraits of all the young princes of the world, and as soon as Usha identified Aniruddha as the one from her dream, Chitralekha used her yogic powers under the cover of night to bring him straight from Your palace in Dwaraka to Usha's quarters.

कन्यापुरे दयितया सुखमारमन्तं
चैनं कथञ्चन बबन्धुषि शर्वबन्धौ ।
श्रीनारदोक्ततदुदन्तदुरन्तरोषैः
त्वं तस्य शोणितपुरं यदुभिर्न्यरुन्धाः ॥82.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: कन्या-पुरे in the maiden's inner palace chamber, दयितया with his beloved Usha, सुखम् happily, आरमन्तम् sporting and enjoying, च एनम् and him (Aniruddha), कथञ्चन with great difficulty, बबन्धुषि when bound or imprisoned, शर्व-बन्धौ by the friend and devotee of Lord Shiva (Sharva) - Banasura, श्री-नारद-उक्त-तत्-उदन्त-दुरन्त-रोषैः filled with intense wrath upon hearing those events reported by Sage Narada, त्वम् You, तस्य his, शोणितपुरम् Shonitapura, यदुभिः with the Yadava army, न्यरुन्धाः besieged or blockaded

Simple Meaning: While Aniruddha was happily enjoying the company of his beloved Usha in her inner palace, Banasura—an ardent devotee of Lord Shiva—discovered him and, with great difficulty, bound him with mystical serpent bonds. Hearing these terrible tidings from Sage Narada, You were filled with intense wrath and immediately marched with the Yadava army to besiege Banasura's capital city, Shonitapura.

पुरीपालश्शैलप्रियदुहितृनाथोऽस्य भगवान्
समं भूतव्रातैर्यदुबलमशङ्कं निरुरुधे ।
महाप्राणो बाणो झटिति युयुधानेनयुयुधे
गुहः प्रद्युम्नेन त्वमपि पुरहन्त्रा जघटिषे ॥82.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुरी-पालः guardian of the city, शैल-प्रिय-दुहितृ-नाथः the lord of Parvati (the beloved daughter of the mountain), अस्य his (Banasura's), भगवान् the divine Lord Shiva, समम् together with, भूत-व्रातैः the hosts of spirits and attendants (bhuta-ganas), यदु-बलम् the Yadava army, अशङ्कम् fearlessly, निरुरुधे resisted or confronted, महा-प्राणः mighty, बाणः Banasura, झटिति quickly, युयुधानेन with Yuyudhana (Satyaki), युयुधे fought, गुहः Guha (Kartikeya), प्रद्युम्नेन with Pradyumna, त्वम् अपि You too, पुर-हन्त्रा with Lord Shiva (the destroyer of the three cities), जघटिषे clashed or fought in battle

Simple Meaning: Lord Shiva, the lord of Parvati and protector of Shonitapura, fearlessly confronted the advancing Yadava army along with his fierce hosts of spirits and attendants. The mighty Banasura quickly engaged in battle with Satyaki, while Lord Kartikeya fought with Pradyumna. You Yourself clashed directly with Lord Shiva, the destroyer of the three cities.

निरुद्धाशेषास्त्रे मुमुहुषि तवास्त्रेण गिरिशे
द्रुता भूता भीताः प्रमथकुलवीराः प्रमथिताः ।
परास्कन्द्त् स्कन्दः कुसुमशरबाणैश्च सचिवः
स कुम्भाण्डो भाण्डं नवमिव बलेनाशु बिभिदे ॥82.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: निरुद्ध-अशेष-अस्त्रे whose all weapons were neutralized, मुमुहुषि who was rendered helpless or bewildered, तव-अस्त्रेण by Your divine weapon (Jrumbhanastra), गिरिशे when Lord Shiva (Girisha) was thus stunned, द्रुताः fled away, भूताः the spirits and ghouls, भीताः terrified, प्रमथ-कुल-वीराः the heroic warriors of Shiva's Pramatha clan, प्रमथिताः were thoroughly crushed, परास्कन्दत् retreated or fled, स्कन्दः Lord Kartikeya (Skanda), कुसुमशर-बाणैः by the arrows of Pradyumna (who was the partial incarnation of the Kamadeva - who has arrows made of flowers), च and, सचिवः the minister, सः he, कुम्भाण्डः Kumbhanda, भाण्डम् a pot, नवम् इव like a new clay pot, बलेन आशु by Balarama quickly, बिभिदे was shattered

Simple Meaning: When all of Lord Shiva's weapons were neutralized and he was rendered spellbound by Your divine weapon (Jrumbhanastra), the terrified spirits and ghouls fled the battlefield. The valiant warriors of the Pramatha clan were thoroughly crushed, and Lord Kartikeya retreated after being overwhelmed by the arrows of Pradyumna. Meanwhile, Banasura's minister Kumbhanda was swiftly shattered by Balarama like a new bhanda - clay pot.

चापानां पञ्चशत्या प्रसभमुपगते छिन्नचापेऽथ बाणे
व्यर्थे याते समेतो ज्वरपतिरशनैरज्वरि त्वज्ज्वरेण ।
ज्ञानी स्तुत्वाऽथ दत्वा तव चरितजुषां विज्वरं स ज्वरोऽगात्
प्रायोऽन्तर्ज्ञानवन्तोऽपि च बहुतमसा रौद्रचेष्टा हि रौद्राः ॥82.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: चापानाम् of bows, पञ्च-शत्या with five hundred, प्रसभम् उपगते forcefully advancing, छिन्न-चापे having his bows severed, अथ then, बाणे when Banasura, व्यर्थे याते was rendered powerless and fled, समेतः coming forward, ज्वर-पतिः the lord of fevers (Maheshvara Jvara/Shiva Jvara), अशनैः quickly, अज्वरि was overcome with burning torment, त्वत्-ज्वरेण by Your Vishnu Jvara (Vaishnava Jvara), ज्ञानी gaining true knowledge, स्तुत्वा having praised You, अथ then, दत्वा granting, तव-चरित-जुषाम् to those who recite or listen to Your divine deeds, विज्वरम् freedom from fever and disease, सः that, ज्वरः Jvara, अगात् departed, प्रायः mostly, अन्तः-ज्ञानवन्तः अपि even though possessing inner wisdom, च and, बहु-तमसा due to the influence of heavy tamo-guna, रौद्र-चेष्टाः having fierce and cruel conduct, हि indeed, रौद्राः the followers/aspects of Rudra

Simple Meaning: When Banasura advanced forcefully with five hundred bows, You severed all of them, rendering him powerless so that he fled. Thereupon, the terrible Shiva Jvara (three-headed, three-legged fever entity) approached, but was instantly tormented and overwhelmed by Your Vaishnava Jvara. Realizing Your supreme nature, Shiva Jvara praised You devotedly and departed after granting the boon that anyone who recites or hears this divine pastime will be freed from all fevers and diseases. Indeed, even the attendants of Rudra, who possess inner wisdom can exhibit fierce and destructive behavior when influenced by tamo-guna.

बाणं नानायुधोग्रं पुनरभिपतितं दर्पदोषाद्वितन्वन्
निर्लूनाशेषदोषं सपदि बुबुधुषा शङ्करेणोपगीतः ।
तद्वाचा शिष्टबाहुद्वितयमुभयतो निर्भयं तत्प्रियं तं
मुक्त्वा तद्दत्तमानो निजपुरमगम: सानिरुद्धः सहोषः ॥82.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: बाणम् Banasura, नाना-आयुध-उग्रम् fierce with various weapons, पुनः अभिपतितम् attacking again, दर्प-दोषात् due to the fault of pride, वितन्वन् making him, निर्लूण-अशेष-दोषम् with all his faults (and arms) severed, सपदि immediately, बुबुधुषा who realized the truth, शङ्करेण उपगीतः praised by Lord Shiva, तत्-वाचा at his (Shiva's) request, शिष्ट-बाहु-द्वितयम् leaving two arms intact, उभयतः निर्भयम् fearless on both sides (a devotee of both Shiva and Vishnu), तत्-प्रियम् dear to Shiva, तम् him (Banasura), मुक्त्वा sparing/releasing, तत्-दत्त-मानः honored with rich gifts by him, निज-पुरम् अगमः You returned to Your own city Dwaraka, स-अनिरुद्धः along with Aniruddha, सह-ऊषः together with Usha

Simple Meaning: Driven by blind pride, Banasura attacked again wielding various weapons with his thousand arms. With Your Sudarshana Chakra, You severed almost all his arms, purging him of his arrogance. Lord Shiva, recognizing Your supreme divinity, praised You and requested mercy for his devotee. Out of respect for Shiva's words, You spared Banasura, leaving him with two arms and bestowing upon him freedom from fear. Honored with magnificent gifts by Banasura, You returned to Dwaraka accompanied by Aniruddha and his bride Usha.

मुहुस्तावच्छक्रं वरुणमजयो नन्दहरणे
यमं बालानीतौ दवदहनपानेऽनिलसखम् ।
विधिं वत्सस्तेये गिरिशमिह बाणस्य समरे
विभो विश्वोत्कर्षी तदयमवतारो जयति ते ॥82.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: मुहुः repeatedly, तावत् indeed, शक्रम् Shakra - Indra, वरुणम् Varuna, अजयः You conquered, नन्द-हरणे during the abduction of Nanda, यमम् Yama, बाल-आनीतौ in bringing back the dead son of Your Guru Sandipani, दव-दहन-पाने in drinking the forest fire, अनिल-सखम् the friend of the Anila (Wind) - Agni, विधिम् Brahma, वत्स-स्तेये during the stealing of the calves, गिरिशम् Lord Shiva (Girisha), इह बाणस्य समरे here in the battle of Banasura, विभो O All-pervading Lord, विश्व-उत्कर्षी supreme over the entire universe, तत् अयम् therefore this, अवतारः incarnation, जयति is victorious, ते Your

Simple Meaning: Time and again You have demonstrated Your supremacy over the celestial deities—Indra - during the Parijata and Govardhana episodes, Varuna - when he abducted Nanda, Yama - when You brought back Your Guru Sandipani's dead son, Agni - when You swallowed the forest fire, Brahma - during the stealing of the calves and cowherd boys, and now Lord Shiva in this battle of Banasura. O All-pervading Lord, this Krishna incarnation of Yours, excelling over all the worlds, reigns supreme in all glory!

द्विजरुषा कृकलासवपुर्धरं नृगनृपं त्रिदिवालयमापयन् ।
निजजने द्विजभक्तिमनुत्तमामुपदिशन् पवनेश्वर् पाहि माम् ॥82.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्वि-जन-रुषा due to the curse/anger of brahmins, कृकलास-वपुः-धरम् who had assumed the form of a chameleon, नृग-नृपम् King Nriga, त्रिदिव-आलयम् to heaven (the celestial realm), आपयन् causing him to attain, निज-जने to Your own devotees and descendants, द्विज-भक्तिम् devotion and reverence toward learned brahmins, अनुत्तमाम् supreme, उपदिशन् instructing, पवनेश्वर O Lord of Guruvayur, पाहि protect, माम् me

Simple Meaning: Having delivered King Nriga—who was trapped in the body of a chameleon due to the curse of brahmins—You elevated him to the celestial realm. Through this deed, You taught Your own people and future generations the supreme importance of respecting and revering noble brahmins. O Lord of Guruvayur, protect me from all my afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
