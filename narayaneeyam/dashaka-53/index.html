Krishna slays Dhenukasura

दशकम् ५३ · Balarama slays Dhenukasura

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 53

Balarama slays Dhenukasura

अतीत्य बाल्यं जगतां पते त्वमुपेत्य पौगण्डवयो मनोज्ञं ।
उपेक्ष्य वत्सावनमुत्सवेन प्रावर्तथा गोगणपालनायाम् ॥53.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: अतीत्य passing beyond, बाल्यम् early childhood, जगताम् पते O Lord of the worlds, त्वम् You, उपेत्य reaching, पौगण्ड-वयः the age of boyhood from six to ten, मनोज्ञम् charming, उपेक्ष्य giving up, वत्स-अवनम् tending the calves, उत्सवेन with joy, प्रावर्तथा engaged Yourself, गो-गण-पालनायाम् in herding the cows

Simple Meaning: O Lord of the worlds, passing beyond early childhood and reaching the charming age of boyhood, You joyously gave up the duty of tending the calves and engaged Yourself in herding the cows.

उपक्रमस्यानुगुणैव सेयं मरुत्पुराधीश तव प्रवृत्तिः ।
गोत्रापरित्राणकृतेऽवतीर्णस्तदेव देवाऽऽरभथास्तदा यत् ॥53.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: उपक्रमस्य with Your incarnation's purpose, अनुगुणा एव appropriate only, सा इयम् that very, मरुत्पुराधीश O Lord of Guruvayur, तव Your, प्रवृत्तिः action, गोत्रा-परित्राण-कृते for the protection of the earth (and also the cows), अवतीर्णः incarnated, तत् एव that alone, देव O Lord, आरभथाः You began, तदा then, यत् which

गोत्रा (स्त्रीलिङ्गः)  भूमिः src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

गोत्रा (स्त्रीलिङ्गः)  गोसमूहः src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Guruvayur, this action of Yours was fully in line with the purpose of Your incarnation. Having descended to protect both the earth and the cows, You began doing precisely that right from then.

कदापि रामेण समं वनान्ते वनश्रियं वीक्ष्य चरन् सुखेन ।
श्रीदामनाम्नः स्वसखस्य वाचा मोदादगा धेनुककाननं त्वम् ॥53.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: कदापि once, रामेण समम् together with Balarama, वनान्ते in the forest, वन-श्रियम् the beauty of the forest, वीक्ष्य beholding, चरन् wandering, सुखेन happily, श्रीदाम-नाम्नः named Sridama, स्व-सखस्य of Your friend, वाचा by the request, मोदात् with joy, अगाः You went, धेनुक-काननम् to the forest of Dhenuka, त्वम् You

Simple Meaning: Once, as You were happily wandering through the forest with Balarama admiring its natural beauty, You joyfully went to the forest of Dhenuka at the request of Your dear friend named Sridama.

उत्तालतालीनिवहे त्वदुक्त्या बलेन धूतेऽथ बलेन दोर्भ्याम् ।
मृदुः खरश्चाभ्यपतत्पुरस्तात् फलोत्करो धेनुकदानवोऽपि ॥53.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: उत्ताल-ताली-निवहे when the cluster of tall palm trees, त्वत्-उक्त्या on Your instruction, बलेन by Balarama, धूते were shaken, अथ then, बलेन by force, दोर्भ्याम् with both arms, मृदुः soft, खरः च and hard, अभ्यपतत् पुरस्तात् fell down in front, फल-उत्करः the heap of fruits, धेनुक-दानवः अपि as well as the danava Dhenuka

Simple Meaning: When Balarama forcefully shook the cluster of tall palm trees with his arms upon Your instruction, a heap of soft and hard palm fruits fell down in front, along with the danava Dhenuka who was in the form of a donkey.

समुद्यतो धैनुकपालनेऽहं कथं वधं धैनुकमद्य कुर्वे ।
इतीव मत्वा ध्रुवमग्रजेन सुरौघयोद्धारमजीघनस्त्वम् ॥53.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: समुद्यतः engaged, धैनुक-पालने in protecting the herds of cows, अहम् I, कथम् how, वधम् the killing, धैनुकम् of Dhenuka, अद्य today, कुर्वे shall perform, इति-इव-मत्वा thinking thus as if, ध्रुवम् surely, अग्रजेन by Your elder brother Balarama, सुर-ओघ-योद्धारम् the one who fought against host of devas, अजीघनः You caused to be killed, त्वम् You

धैनुक (नपुंसकलिङ्गः)  धेनुसमूहः src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: Thinking to Yourself as if saying, "Having taken up the task of protecting the herds of cows (Dhenuka), how can I commit the killing of Dhenuka today?", You surely had Your elder brother Balarama kill that daitya Dhenuka, who was an enemy of the hosts of devas.

Note: Play on the word Dhenuka. Dhenuka means group of cows. It is also the name of the asura.

तदीयभृत्यानपि जम्बुकत्वेनोपागतानग्रजसंयुतस्त्वम् ।
जम्बूफलानीव तदा निरास्थस्तालेषु खेलन् भगवन् निरास्थः ॥53.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदीय-भृत्यन् अपि his followers as well, जम्बुकत्वेन in the form of jackals, उपागतान् who came, अग्रज-संयुतः त्वम् You together with Your elder brother Balarama, जम्बू-फलानि इव like jambu fruits, तदा then, निरास्थः hurled, तालेषु onto the palm trees, खेलन् playing, भगवन् O Lord, निरास्थः with ease

Simple Meaning: When Dhenuka's followers also arrived in the form of jackals, You, together with Your elder brother Balarama, effortlessly hurled them onto the palm trees like jambu fruits, O Lord.

विनिघ्नति त्वय्यथ जम्बुकौघं सनामकत्वाद्वरुणस्तदानीम् ।
भयाकुलो जम्बुकनामधेयं श्रुतिप्रसिद्धं व्यधितेति मन्ये ॥53.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: विनिघ्नति while destroying, त्वयि by You, अथ then, जम्बुक-ओघम् the host of jackals, सनामकत्वात् because of sharing the same name Jambuka, वरुणः Lord Varuna, तदानीम् at that time, भय-आकुलः overcome with fear, जम्बुक-नामधेयम् the title of Jambuka, श्रुति-प्रसिद्धम् famous in the Vedas, व्यधित hid, इति मन्ये I think so

जम्बुक (पुंलिङ्गः)  वरुणः src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: When You were destroying the host of jackals, Lord Varuna became overcome with fear simply because he shared the same name Jambuka. I think it was out of this fear that he hid his name Jambuka which is well-known in the Vedic texts. (That is why Varuna’s name as Jambuka is not very well-known - the poet thinks.)

तवावतारस्य फलं मुरारे सञ्जातमद्येति सुरैर्नुतस्त्वम् ।
सत्यं फलं जातमिहेति हासी बालैः समं तालफलान्यभुङ्क्थाः ॥53.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: तव Your, अवतारस्य of incarnation, फलम् fruit, मुरारे O Slayer of Mura, सञ्जातम् achieved, अद्य today, इति thus, सुरैः by the devas, नुतः praised, त्वम् You, सत्यम् truth, फलम् fruit, जातम् appeared, इह here, इति thus, हासी laughing, बालैः समम् together with the boys, ताल-फलानि the palm fruits, अभुङ्क्थाः You ate

Simple Meaning: O Slayer of Mura, when the devas praised You saying that the fruit of Your incarnation was realized today, You laughingly agreed saying that true fruit had indeed appeared here, and enjoyed eating the ripe palm fruits together with the cowherd boys.

मधुद्रवस्रुन्ति बृहन्ति तानि फलानि मेदोभरभृन्ति भुक्त्वा ।
तृप्तैश्च दृप्तैर्भवनं फलौघं वहद्भिरागाः खलु बालकैस्त्वम् ॥53.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: मधु-द्रव-स्रुन्ति dripping sweet juice, बृहन्ति large, तानि those, फलानि fruits, मेदः-भर-भृन्ति full of abundant pulp, भुक्त्वा having eaten, तृप्तैः च and completely satisfied, दृप्तैः joyous, भवनम् home, फल-ओघम् heaps of fruits, वहद्भिः carrying, आगाः You returned, खलु indeed, बालकैः त्वम् You along with the boys

Simple Meaning: Having eaten those large fruits dripping with sweet juice and packed with rich pulp, You returned home in high spirits together with the cowherd boys, who were completely satisfied and joyously carrying heaps of palm fruits with them.

हतो हतो धेनुक इत्युपेत्य फलान्यदद्भिर्मधुराणि लोकैः ।
जयेति जीवेति नुतो विभो त्वं मरुत्पुराधीश्वर पाहि रोगात् ॥53.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: हतः slain, हतः slain, धेनुकः Dhenuka, इति thus, उपेत्य approaching, फलानि fruits, अदद्भिः eating, मधुराणि sweet, लोकैः by the people, जय victory, इति thus, जीव live long, इति thus, नुतम् praised, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, त्वम् You, मरुत्पुराधीश्वर O Lord of Guruvayur, पाहि protect me, रोगात् from disease

Simple Meaning: Approaching to eat the sweet fruits while exclaiming that Dhenuka was slain, the people praised You with cries of Victory to You and May You live long. O All-Pervading Lord, O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect me from disease.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
