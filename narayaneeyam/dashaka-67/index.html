Krishna disappears and reappears

दशकम् ६७

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 67

Krishna disappears and reappears

स्फुरत्परानन्दरसात्मकेन त्वया समासादितभोगलीलाः ।
असीममानन्दभरं प्रपन्ना महान्तमापुर्मदमम्बुजाक्ष्यः ॥67.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्फुरत्-पर-आनन्द-रस-आत्मकेन who is the very embodiment of the overflowing bliss of the Supreme Self, त्वया with You, समासादित-भोग-लीलाः who had attained the intimate joy of love, असीमम् boundless, आनन्द-भरम् a flood of ecstasy, प्रपन्नाः having reached, महान्तम् great, आपुः attained, मदम् pride or arrogance, अम्बुज-अक्ष्यः those lotus-eyed gopis

Simple Meaning: Having attained the intimate joy of love with You, who are the very embodiment of the overflowing supreme bliss, those lotus-eyed gopis reached a state of boundless ecstasy and consequently succumbed to great pride.

निलीयतेऽसौ मयि मय्यमायं रमापतिर्विश्वमनोभिरामः ।
इति स्म सर्वाः कलिताभिमाना निरीक्ष्य गोविन्द् तिरोहितोऽभूः ॥67.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: निलीयते is engrossed, असौ this, मयि in me, मयि in me, अमायम् without guile or deceit, रमा-पतिः the Lord of Lakshmi, विश्व-मनः-अभिरामः the captivator of the minds of the universe, इति thus, स्म indeed, सर्वाः all of them, कलिता-भिमानाः having developed pride, निरीक्ष्य seeing, गोविन्द O Govinda, तिरोहितः hidden/disappeared, अभूः You became

Simple Meaning: "This Lord of Lakshmi, the captivator of the whole universe, is engrossed only in me, purely devoted to me alone!" Thinking thus, every single gopi became filled with self-importance. Seeing their pride, O Govinda, You suddenly vanished from their sight!

राधाभिधां तावदजातगर्वामतिप्रियां गोपवधूं मुरारे ।
भवानुपादाय गतो विदूरं तया सह स्वैरविहारकारी ॥67.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: राधा-अभिधाम् named Radha, तावत् however, अजात-गर्वाम् who was free from pride, अति-प्रियाम् exceedingly dear, गोप-वधूम् gopi maiden, मुरारे O Murari, भवान् You, उपादाय taking, गतः went away, वि-दूरम् to a distant place, तया सह with her, स्वैर-विहार-कारी sporting freely according to Your pleasure

Simple Meaning: O Lord Murari, taking with You that exceedingly dear gopi named Radha, who was completely free from pride, You went away to a far distant place and engaged in sweet, unrestrained sports of love with her.

तिरोहितेऽथ त्वयि जाततापाः समं समेताः कमलायताक्ष्यः ।
वने वने त्वां परिमार्गयन्त्यो विषादमापुर्भगवन्नपारम् ॥67.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तिरोहिते when You disappeared, अथ thereupon, त्वयि in You, जात-तापाः suffering the deep anguish of separation, समम् together, समेताः gathering, कमल-आयत्-अक्ष्यः those large lotus-eyed gopis, वने वने from forest to forest, त्वाम् You, परिमार्गयन्त्यः searching everywhere, विषादम् sorrow, आपुः attained, भगवन् O Blessed Lord, अपारम् boundless

Simple Meaning: O Blessed Lord, when You vanished from their sight, those large lotus-eyed gopis were struck with intense grief. Gathering together, they searched for You frantically from forest to forest, falling into the depths of boundless sorrow.

हा चूत हा चम्पक कर्णिकार हा मल्लिके मालति बालवल्यः ।
किं वीक्षितो नो हृदयैकचोरः इत्यादि तास्त्वत्प्रवणा विलेपुः ॥67.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: हा चूत O mango tree, हा चम्पक O champaka tree, कर्णिकार karnikara tree, हा मल्लिके O jasmine, मालति malati, बाल-वल्ल्यः young creepers, किम् whether, वीक्षितः seen, नः by us, हृदय-एक-चोरः the sole stealer of our hearts, इत्यादि in this manner, ताः they, त्वत्-प्रवणाः completely absorbed in You, विलेपुः lamented

Simple Meaning: "O Mango tree! O Champaka! O Karnikara! O Jasmine, Malati, and young tender creepers! Have you seen the sole stealer of our hearts?" Crying out in this manner, those gopis, completely absorbed in thoughts of You, wept and lamented bitterly.

निरीक्षितोऽयं सखि पङ्कजाक्षः पुरो ममेत्याकुलमालपन्ती ।
त्वां भावनाचक्षुषि वीक्ष्य काचित्तापं सखीनां द्विगुणीचकार ॥67.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: निरीक्षितः was seen, अयम् this, सखि O friend, पङ्कज-अक्षः the lotus-eyed Lord, पुरः in front, मम of me, इति thus, आकुलम् excitedly/agitatedly, आलपन्ती exclaiming, त्वाम् You, भावना-चक्षुषि through the eye of vivid imagination, वीक्ष्य having seen, काचित् a certain gopi, तापम् the grief, सखीनाम् of her friends, द्विगुणी-चकार doubled

Simple Meaning: Exclaiming excitedly, "O friend! I have just seen that lotus-eyed Lord right here before me!", a certain gopi, who had actually seen You only through the vivid eye of her intense meditation, doubled the sorrowful longing of her friends (because they thought, only that gopi could see You).

त्वदात्मिकास्ता यमुनातटान्ते तवानुचक्रुः किल चेष्टितानि ।
विचित्य भूयोऽपि तथैव मानात्त्वया विमुक्तां ददृशुश्च राधाम् ॥67.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-आत्मिकाः becoming one in spirit with You, ताः they, यमुना-तट-अन्ते on the banks of the Yamuna, तव Your, अनुचक्रुः imitated, किल indeed, चेष्टितानि playful activities, विचित्य searching, भूयः अपि once again, तथा एव in the exact same way, मानात् out of pride, त्वया by You, विमुक्ताम् left or abandoned, ददृशुः saw, च and, राधाम् Radha

Simple Meaning: Becoming completely absorbed in You and identifying themselves with You, those gopis began to imitate Your divine sports along the banks of the Yamuna. Continuing their search, they eventually found Radha, who had also been left behind by You due to a brief surge of pride in her.

ततः समं ता विपिने समन्तात्तमोवतारावधि मार्गयन्त्यः ।
पुनर्विमिश्रा यमुनातटान्ते भृशं विलेपुश्च जगुर्गुणांस्ते ॥67.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, समम् together, ताः those gopis, विपिने in the forest, समन्तात् everywhere, तमः-अवतार-अवधि until the advent of darkness, मार्गयन्त्यः searching, पुनः again, विमिश्राः reunited, यमुना-तट-अन्ते on the banks of the Yamuna, भृशम् bitterly or intensely, विलेपुः wept, च and, जगुः sang, गुणान् qualities, ते Your

Simple Meaning: Then, searching for You everywhere together in the forest until nightfall descended, those gopis reunited on the banks of the Yamuna. Intensely sorrowful in separation, they wept bitterly and began to sing aloud the glorious virtues and attributes of Your divine play.

तथा व्यथासङ्कुलमानसानां व्रजाङ्गनानां करुणैकसिन्धो ।
जगत्त्रयीमोहनमोहनात्मा त्वं प्रादुरासीरयि मन्दहासी ॥67.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: तथा thus, व्यथा-सङ्कुल-मानसानाम् whose minds were filled with intense anguish, व्रज-अङ्गनानाम् of the maidens of Vraja, करुणा-एक-सिन्धो O sole ocean of mercy, जगत्-त्रयी-मोहन-मोहन-आत्मा whose form captivates even the one who enchantingly captivates the three worlds, त्वम् You, प्रादुरासीः appeared, अयि O Lord, मन्द-हासी wearing a gentle smile

Simple Meaning: O sole ocean of mercy, as the maidens of Vraja were thus burning with intense anguish in their hearts, You, whose form captivates even Kamadeva who enchants all the three worlds, appeared right before them, sporting a gentle and sweet smile.

सन्दिग्धसन्दर्शनमात्मकान्तं त्वां वीक्ष्य तन्व्यः सहसा तदानीम् ।
किं किं न चक्रुः प्रमदातिभारात् स त्वं गदात् पालय मारुतेश ॥67.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: सन्दिग्ध-सन्दर्शनम् whose presence had become uncertain or doubted, आत्म-कान्तम् their own beloved, त्वाम् You, वीक्ष्य having seen, तन्व्यः those slender maidens, सहसा suddenly, तदानीम् at that moment, किम् किम् what and what not, न चक्रुः did they not do, प्रमद-अति-भारात् out of an overwhelming burden of joy, सः that, त्वम् You, गदात् from illness, पालय protect me, मारुत-ईश O Lord of Guruvayur

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Guruvayur, upon suddenly beholding You—their own beloved whose presence they had begun to doubt—those slender maidens were so overwhelmed by a flood of ecstatic joy that there was nothing they did not do to express their bliss! May You, who revealed Yourself thus, protect me from all my ailments!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
