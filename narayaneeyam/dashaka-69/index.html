Krishna’s Rasa-Dance with the gopis

दशकम् ६९

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 69

Krishna’s Rasa-Dance with the gopis

केशपाशधृतपिञ्छिकाविततिसञ्चलन्मकरकुण्डलं
हारजालवनमालिकाललितमङ्गरागघनसौरभम् ।
पीतचेलधृतकाञ्चिकाञ्चितमुदञ्चदंशुमणिनूपुरं
रासकेलिपरिभूषितं तव हि रूपमीश कलयामहे ॥69.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: केश-पाश-धृत-पिञ्छिका-वितति-सञ्चलत्-मकर-कुण्डलम् adorned with a mass of peacock feathers tucked in Your beautiful tresses, with fish-shaped earrings swaying gently, हार-जाल-वनमालिका-ललितम् embellished exquisitely with shimmering necklaces and a garlands of forest flowers, अङ्ग-राग-घन-सौरभम् diffusing a dense, enchanting fragrance of sandal paste applied to Your body, पीत-चेल-धृत-काञ्चि-काञ्चितम् radiant with a golden waist-band tied over Your yellow silk garment, उदञ्चत्-अंशुक-मणि-नूपुरम् wearing gem-studded anklets casting brilliant rays of light, रास-केलि-परिभूषितम् adorned specifically for the divine Rasaleela dance, तव Your, हि indeed, रूपम् form, ईश O Lord, कलयामहे we meditate upon

Simple Meaning: O Lord! We meditate upon Your divine form, exquisite in its adornments for the Rasaleela dance—Your beautiful hair decorated with peacock feathers, Your fish-shaped earrings gently swaying, Your chest adorned with shimmering necklaces and fresh garlands of forest flowers, diffusing the rich fragrance of sandal paste. Wearing a golden waistband over Your yellow silk dress, and with gem-studded anklets casting brilliant rays of light, Your captivating form shines in all its supreme glory.

तावदेव कृतमण्डने कलितकञ्चुलीककुचमण्डले
गण्डलोलमणिकुण्डले युवतिमण्डलेऽथ परिमण्डले ।
अन्तरा सकलसुन्दरीयुगलमिन्दिरारमण सञ्चरन्
मञ्जुलां तदनु रासकेलिमयि कञ्जनाभ समुपादधाः ॥69.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: तावत् एव at that very moment, कृत-मण्डने fully adorned with ornaments, कलित-कञ्चुलीक-कुच-मण्डले with their breasts covered by garments, गण्ड-लोल-मणि-कुण्डले with gem-studded earrings swaying against their cheeks, युवति-मण्डले as the assembly of young maidens, अथ then, परिमण्डले formed a circle, अन्तरा in between, सकल-सुन्दरी-युगलम् every pair of beautiful maidens, इन्दिरा-रमण O Lord of Lakshmi, सञ्चरन् manifesting Yourself, मञ्जुलाम् the enchanting, तदनु thereafter, रास-केलिम् divine Rasaleela dance, अयि O, कञ्ज-नाभ O Lotus-naveled Lord, समुपादधाः You commenced

Simple Meaning: O Lotus-naveled Lord! O beloved of Lakshmi! At that very moment, the young maidens—fully adorned with fine ornaments, wearing bodices, and with gem-studded earrings swaying gracefully against their cheeks—arranged themselves in a large circle. By miraculously manifesting Yourself between every single pair of those beautiful gopis, You then commenced the most enchanting and divine Rasaleela dance.

वासुदेव तव भासमानमिह रासकेलिरससौरभं
दूरतोऽपि खलु नारदागदितमाकलय्य कुतुकाकुला ।
वेषभूषणविलासपेशलविलासिनीशतसमावृता
नाकतो युगपदागता वियति वेगतोऽथ सुरमण्डली ॥69.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: वासुदेव O Lord Vasudeva, तव Your, भासमानम् shining resplendently, इह here, रास-केलि-रस-सौरभम् the fragrant sweetness of the Rasa dance, दूरतः अपि even from afar, खलु indeed, नारद-आगदितम् related by Sage Narada, आकलय्य hearing, कुतुक-आकुला overcome with curiosity, वेष-भूषण-विलास-पेशल-विलासिनी-शत-समावृता surrounded by hundreds of charming celestial damsels exquisite in their dress, ornaments, and graceful demeanor, नाकतः from the heavens, युगपत् simultaneously, आगता arrived, वियति in the sky, वेगतः with speed, अथ then, सुर-मण्डली the host of devas

Simple Meaning: O Lord Vasudeva, upon hearing Sage Narada describe even from afar the incomparable and fragrant bliss of Your divine Rasa dance, the host of devas was overcome with eagerness. Accompanied by hundreds of charming celestial women, exquisitely adorned in fine garments and jewels and displaying graceful movements, they swiftly arrived together from heaven and gathered in the sky to witness the grand spectacle.

वेणुनादकृततानदानकलगानरागगतियोजना-
लोभनीयमृदुपादपातकृततालमेलनमनोहरम् ।
पाणिसंक्वणितकङ्कणं च मुहुरंसलम्बितकराम्बुजं
श्रोणिबिम्बचलदम्बरं भजत रासकेलिरसडम्बरम् ॥69.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: वेणु-नाद-कृत-तान-दान-कल-गान-राग-गति-योजना-लोभनीय-मृदु-पाद-पात-कृत-ताल-मेलन-मनोहरम् captivating on account of the delightful harmony of rhythm created by soft footsteps that rhythmically matched the intricate modulations, melodic extensions, sweet singing, and ragas produced by the flute sound, पाणि-संक्वणित-कङ्कणम् with bangles jingling softly on wrists, च and, मुहुः repeatedly, अंस-लम्बित-कर-अम्बुजम् with lotus hands resting gently upon the shoulders of adjacent gopis, श्रोणि-बिम्ब-चलत्-अम्बरम् with garments fluttering around the hips, भजत worship/adore/meditate!, रास-केलि-रस-डम्बरम् the magnificent and blissful grandeur of the Rasaleela dance

Simple Meaning: Let us meditate on the blissful grandeur of the divine Rasaleela dance! It was made captivating by soft footsteps moving in perfect harmony with the intricate tunes, ragas, and sweet melodies flowing from His flute. With wrist bangles jingling rhythmically, lotus hands resting gracefully on the shoulders of the adjacent maidens, whose silk garments fluttered delicately around their waists, the divine spectacle unfolded in all its transcendent beauty.

स्पर्धया विरचितानुगानकृततारतारमधुरस्वरे
नर्तनेऽथ ललिताङ्गहारलुलिताङ्गहारमणिभूषणे ।
सम्मदेन कृतपुष्पवर्षमलमुन्मिषद्दिविषदां कुलं
चिन्मये त्वयि निलीयमानमिव सम्मुमोह सवधूकुलम् ॥69.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्पर्धया in competition, विरचित-अनुगान-कृत-तार-तार-मधुर-स्वरे with sweet notes raised to ever higher pitches in singing after You, नर्तने in the course of the dance, अथ then, ललित-अङ्ग-हार-लुलित-अङ्ग-हार-मणि-भूषणे with exquisite bodily movements causing the necklaces and gem-studded ornaments on their bodies to sway gracefully, सम्मदेन with intense joy, कृत-पुष्प-वर्षम् showering flowers in abundance, अलम् profusely, उन्मिषत्-दिविषाम् कुलम् the host of devas gazing with wide eyes in wonder, चिन्मये in You, who are pure Consciousness, त्वयि in You, निलीयमानम् इव as if dissolving or merging, सम्मुमोह became enraptured or lost in trance, स-वधू-कुलम् along with their wives

Simple Meaning: As the maidens sang in joyful response to Your flute with sweet notes rising to higher and higher pitches, their graceful bodily movements caused their gem-studded necklaces and ornaments to sway elegantly. Watching this divine spectacle with wide-eyed wonder, the hosts of devas along with their celestial wives showered flowers in abundance. Filled with supreme delight, they became so utterly enraptured that they felt as though they were dissolving into You, the embodiment of Pure Consciousness.

स्विन्नसन्नतनुवल्लरी तदनु कापि नाम पशुपाङ्गना
कान्तमंसमवलम्बते स्म तव तान्तिभारमुकुलेक्षणा ॥

काचिदाचलितकुन्तला नवपटीरसारघनसौरभं
वञ्चनेन तव सञ्चुचुम्ब भुजमञ्चितोरुपुलकाङ्कुरा ॥69.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्विन्न-सन्न-तनु-वल्लरी with her slender creeper-like body sweating and exhausted, तदनु thereafter, कापि नाम a certain, पशुप-अङ्गना a gopi maiden, कान्तम् attractive, अंसम् shoulder, अवलम्बते स्म supported herself on/leaned against, तव Your, तान्ति-भार-मुकुल-ईक्षणा with eyes drooped owing to fatigue, काचित् another maiden, आचलित-कुन्तला with disheveled tresses of hair, नव-पटीर-सार-घन-सौरभम् diffusing the rich fragrance of fresh sandalwood paste, वञ्चनेन under the pretext of resting, तव Your, सञ्चुचुम्ब kissed, भुजम् arm, अञ्चित-उरु-पुलक-अङ्कुरा with hair standing on end in deep ecstasy

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, a certain gopi maiden, her slender creeper-like body perspiring and exhausted from the dance, leaned for support against Your charming shoulder with her eyes half-closed from sheer fatigue. Another maiden, her tresses loosened and disheveled, under the pretext of resting, lovingly kissed Your arm—which was fragrant with fresh sandalwood paste—while her body thrilled with goosebumps of ecstatic delight.

कापि गण्डभुवि सन्निधाय निजगण्डमाकुलितकुण्डलं
पुण्यपूरनिधिरन्ववाप तव पूगचर्वितरसामृतम् ।
इन्दिराविहृतिमन्दिरं भुवनसुन्दरं हि नटनान्तरे
त्वामवाप्य दधुरङ्गनाः किमु न सम्मदोन्मददशान्तरम् ॥69.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: कापि a certain gopi, गण्ड-भुवि against Your cheek, सन्निधाय placing, निज-गण्डम् her own cheek, आकुलित-कुण्डलम् with her earrings swinging gently, पुण्य-पूर-निधिः a reservoir of immense merits, अन्ववाप received, तव Your, पूग-चर्वित-रस-अमृतम् the nectar-like juice of the chewed betel nut, इन्दिरा-विहृति-मन्दिरम् the abode of Lakshmi's sports, भुवन-सुन्दरम् the most beautiful being in all the worlds, हि indeed, नटन-अन्तरे in the midst of the dance, त्वाम् You, अवाप्य having attained, दधुः experienced, अङ्गनाः the maidens, किमु न did they not, सम्मद-उन्मद-दशान्तरम् a state of ecstatic intoxication

Simple Meaning: Placing her cheek against Yours so that her earrings gently brushed Your face, a certain gopi—a repository of boundless past merits—tasted the divine nectar of the chewed betel nut directly from Your lotus mouth. Having attained You in the midst of the dance—You who are the very abode of Lakshmi's delight and the most captivating beauty in all the worlds—what state of ecstatic intoxication would those blessed maidens not experience?

गानमीश विरतं क्रमेण किल वाद्यमेलनमुपारतं
ब्रह्मसम्मदरसाकुलाः सदसि केवलं ननृतुरङ्गनाः ।
नाविदन्नपि च नीविकां किमपि कुन्तलीमपि च कञ्चुलीं
ज्योतिषामपि कदम्बकं दिवि विलम्बितं किमपरं ब्रुवे ॥69.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: गानम् the singing, ईश O Lord, विरतम् ceased, क्रमेण gradually, किल indeed, वाद्य-मेलनम् the instrumental accompaniment, उपारतम् stopped, ब्रह्म-सम्मद-रस-आकुलाः overwhelmed with the bliss of Brahman, सदसि in the assembly, केवलम् purely, ननृतुः danced, अङ्गनाः the maidens, न अविदन् were completely unaware, अपि च and also, नीविकाम् of the knots of their garments, किमपि at all, कुन्तलीम् of their hair, अपि च and, कञ्चुलीम् of their bodices, ज्योतिषामपि even the host of heavenly bodies, कदम्बकम् the collection, दिवि in the sky, विलम्बितम् stood still, किम् अपरम् what more, ब्रुवे shall I say

Simple Meaning: O Lord, gradually the singing ceased and the playing of musical instruments came to a halt. Yet, completely overwhelmed with the supreme bliss of Brahman, the maidens continued to dance on. They became entirely oblivious of their hair, their garments, and their bodices. Even the host of stars and planets stood still in the sky, spellbound by the divine dance! What more can I say?

मोदसीम्नि भुवनं विलाप्य विहृतिं समाप्य च ततो विभो
केलिसम्मृदितनिर्मलाङ्गनवघर्मलेशसुभगात्मनाम् ।
मन्मथासहनचेतसां पशुपयोषितां सुकृतचोदितः
तावदाकलितमूर्तिरादधिथ मारवीरपरमोत्सवान् ॥69.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: मोद-सीम्नि in the absolute limit of supreme bliss, भुवनम् the entire world, विलाप्य immersing or dissolving, विहृतिम् the dance play, समाप्य bringing to a close, च and, ततः then, विभो O All-pervading Lord, केलि-सम्मृदित-निर्मल-अङ्ग-नव-घर्म-लेश-सुभग-आत्मनाम् whose pure bodies were rendered exquisitely beautiful by tiny fresh drops of perspiration caused by the vigorous dancing, मन्मथ-असहन-चेतसाम् whose minds were overcome by irresistible longing, पशुप-योषिताम् of the gopi maidens, सुकृत-चोदितः prompted by their boundless past merits, तावत् thereupon, आकलित-मूर्तिः assuming as many forms as there were maidens, आदधिथ You performed, मार-वीर-परम-उत्सवान् the grand supreme festivals of transcendent love

Simple Meaning: O All-pervading Lord, having immersed the entire universe in the supreme ocean of bliss, You brought the dance play to a close. Prompted by the immense merits of the gopi maidens—whose pure bodies were adorned with shimmering beads of perspiration from the intense dance, and whose hearts were filled with overwhelming spiritual longing—You multiplied Yourself into as many forms as there were maidens, bestowing upon each of them the ultimate festival of transcendental love.

केलिभेदपरिलोलिताभिरतिलालिताभिरबलालिभिः
स्वैरमीश ननु सूरजापयसि चारुनाम विहृतिं व्यधाः ।
काननेऽपि च विसारिशीतलकिशोरमारुतमनोहरे
सूनसौरभमये विलेसिथ विलासिनीशतविमोहनम् ॥69.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: केलि-भेद-परिलोलिताभिः who were thoroughly exhilarated and exhausted by the various sports of love, अति-लालिताभिः who were cherished with deep affection, अबला-आलिभिः with the host of young maidens, स्वैरम् freely and at will, ईश O Lord, ननु indeed, सूर-जा-पयसि in the waters of the daughter of the Sun - Yamuna, विहृतिम् water sports, व्यधाः You performed; कानने अपि च and also in the forest, विसारि-शीतल-किशोर-मारुत-मनोहरे charming with a soft, cool, spreading breeze, सून-सौरभ-मये filled with the fragrance of blossoms, विलेसिथ You sported resplendently, विलासिनी-शत-विमोहनम् captivating hundreds of charming maidens

Simple Meaning: O Lord, along with those young maidens who were cherished by You with deep affection and exhilarated by the diverse sports, You freely engaged in delightful water sports in the cool waters of the Yamuna. Afterwards, in the forest—rendered deeply enchanting by fragrant blossoms and gentle, cool breezes—You sported resplendently, completely captivating the hearts of hundreds of charming maidens.

कामिनीरिति हि यामिनीषु खलु कामनीयकनिधे भवान्
पूर्णसम्मदरसार्णवं कमपि योगिगम्यमनुभावयन् ।
ब्रह्मशङ्करमुखानपीह पशुपाङ्गनासु बहुमानयन्
भक्तलोकगमनीयरूप कमनीय कृष्ण परिपाहि माम् ॥69.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: कामिनीः the loving maidens, इति in this manner, हि indeed, यामिनीषु through the autumn nights, खलु truly, कामनीयक-निधे O treasure-house of all beauty, भवान् You, पूर्ण-सम्मद-रस-अर्णवम् an ocean of absolute bliss, कमपि indescribable, योगि-गम्यम् accessible only to great yogis, अनुभावयन् causing them to experience, ब्रह्म-शङ्कर-मुखान् even great devas headed by Brahma and Shiva, अपि इह also here, पशुप-अङ्गनासु towards these gopi maidens, बहुमानयन् making them hold in supreme reverence, भक्त-लोक-गमनीय-रूप O You whose form is attainable only by devotees, कमनीय O most charming one, कृष्ण Lord Krishna, परिपाहि protect, माम् me

Simple Meaning: O Lord Krishna, O treasure-house of all beauty and charm! Through those autumn nights, You granted those loving maidens the direct experience of an indescribable ocean of supreme bliss—a state usually reachable only by advanced yogis. In doing so, You caused even supreme devas like Brahma and Shiva to hold these simple gopi maidens in the highest reverence. O Lord, whose divine form is accessible through pure devotion, protect me from all my afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
