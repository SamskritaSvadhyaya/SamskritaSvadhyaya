Dashaka 60

Krishna steals the clothes of the gopis

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 60

Krishna steals the clothes of the gopis

मदनातुरचेतसोऽन्वहं भवदङ्घ्रिद्वयदास्यकाम्यया ।
यमुनातटसीम्नि सैकतीं तरलाक्ष्यो गिरिजां समार्चिचन् ॥60.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: मदन-आतुर-चेतसः with hearts overwhelmed by love, अन्वहम् day after day, भवत्-अङ्घ्रि-द्वय-दास्य-काम्यया with the desire to serve Your pair of lotus feet, यमुना-तट-सीम्नि on the sandy banks of the Yamuna, सैकतीम् made of sand, तरल-अक्ष्यः the young maidens with charming eyes, गिरिजाम् to Devi Girija (Parvati), समार्चिचन् worshipped devotely

Simple Meaning: Day after day, with hearts filled with longing and an intense desire to gain the privilege of serving Your lotus feet, the charming-eyed gopis crafted an image of Devi Girija out of sand on the banks of the Yamuna and worshipped Her with deep devotion.

तव नामकथारताः समं सुदृशः प्रातरुपागता नदीम् ।
उपहारशतैरपूजयन् दयितो नन्दसुतो भवेदिति ॥60.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: तव नाम-कथा-रताः absorbed in chanting Your names and Your divine stories, समम् together, सुदृशः the beautiful-eyed gopis, प्रातः उपागताः arrived early in the morning, नदीम् at the river, उपहार-शतैः अपूजयन् worshipped with hundreds of offerings, दयितः beloved husband, नन्द-सुतः the son of Nanda, भवेत् इति praying that He becomes

Simple Meaning: Absorbed in chanting Your names and recounting Your glorious tales, the beautiful-eyed gopis arrived together at the river early in the morning. They worshipped the Devi with hundreds of devout offerings, offering a single fervent prayer: "May the son of Nanda become my beloved husband!"

इति मासमुपाहितव्रतास्तरलाक्षीरभिवीक्ष्य ता भवान् ।
करुणामृदुलो नदीतटं समयासीत्तदनुग्रहेच्छया ॥60.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति in this manner, मासम् for a whole month, उपाहित-व्रताः having strictly observed the vow, तरलाक्षीः the tender-eyed maidens, अभिवीक्ष्य seeing, ताः them, भवान् You, करुणा-मृदुलः soft-hearted with compassion, नदी-तटम् to the riverbank, समयासीत् approached, तत्-अनुग्रह-इच्छया with the desire to bestow grace upon them

Simple Meaning: Seeing those tender-eyed maidens who had strictly observed their sacred vow for an entire month, Your heart melted with deep compassion. Desiring to bestow Your grace upon them, You proceeded to the banks of the Yamuna.

नियमावसितौ निजाम्बरं तटसीमन्यवमुच्य तास्तदा ।
यमुनाजलखेलनाकुलाः पुरतस्त्वामवलोक्य लज्जिताः ॥60.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: नियम-अवसितौ at the conclusion of their vow, निज-अम्बरम् their garments, तट-सीम्नि on the riverbank, अवमुच्य removing, ताः they, तदा then, यमुना-जल-खेलन-आकुलाः absorbed in playing in the waters of the Yamuna, पुरतः in front of them, त्वाम् अवलोक्य seeing You, लज्जिताः became embarrassed

Simple Meaning: At the conclusion of their month-long vow, the gopis removed their garments and left them on the riverbank to happily play in the waters of the Yamuna. But on suddenly spotting You standing right in front of them, they were instantly overcome with embarrassment.

त्रपया नमिताननास्वथो वनितास्वम्बरजालमन्तिके ।
निहितं परिगृह्य भूरुहो विटपं त्वं तरसाऽधिरूढवान् ॥60.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्रपया out of modesty, नमित-आननासु with downcast faces, अथः then, वनितासु while the maidens (stood), अम्बर-जालम् the heap of garments, अन्तिके nearby, निहितम् kept, परिगृह्य gathering up, भूरुहः of the tree, विटपम् onto the branch, त्वम् You, तरसा-अधिरूढवान् quickly climbed

Simple Meaning: While the maidens stood with their faces turned down out of modesty, You quickly gathered up all their garments left on the riverbank and climbed up onto the branch of a nearby tree.

इह तावदुपेत्य नीयतां वसनं वः सुदृशो यथायथम् ।
इति नर्ममृदुस्मिते त्वयि ब्रुवति व्यामुमुहे वधूजनैः ॥60.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: इह here, तावत्-उपेत्य coming forward, नीयताम् take back, वसनम् garments, वः your, सुदृशः O fair-eyed ones, यथायथम् according to each, इति thus, नर्म-मृदु-स्मिते with a playful gentle smile, त्वयि when You, ब्रुवति spoke, व्यामुमुहे were completely bewildered, वधू-जनैः by the maidens

Simple Meaning: "O fair-eyed maidens! Come up here and take back your garments as per each one's choice," You said, speaking with a gentle, playful smile. As You uttered these words, the young maidens were left thoroughly bewildered.

अयि जीव चिरं किशोर नस्तव दासीरवशीकरोषि किम् ।
प्रदिशाम्बरमम्बुजेक्षणेत्युदितस्त्वं स्मितमेव दत्तवान् ॥60.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: अयि O, जीव चिरम् may You live long, किशोर O youth, नः us, तव दासीः Your maids, अवशी-करोषि किम् why do You torment us so, प्रदिश अम्बरम् give back our garments, अम्बुज-ईक्षण O lotus-eyed one, इति उदितः thus addressed, त्वम् You, स्मितम् एव दत्तवान् offered only a smile

Simple Meaning: "O youth, may You live long! Why are You tormenting us, who are Your maids? O lotus-eyed one, please give us back our clothes!" When they pleaded with You in this manner, You simply responded with a smile.

अधिरुह्य तटं कृताञ्जलीः परिशुद्धाः स्वगतीर्निरीक्ष्य ताः ।
वसनान्यखिलान्यनुग्रहं पुनरेवं गिरमप्यदा मुदा ॥60.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: अधिरुह्य तटम् stepping up onto the bank, कृताञ्जलीः with folded hands, परिशुद्धाः completely purified, स्वगतीः totally surrendered to You, निरीक्ष्य seeing, ताः them, वसनानि अखिलानि all the garments, अनुग्रहम् blessing, पुनः एवम् and furthermore, गिरम् अपि words of promise as well, अदाः You granted, मुदा with great joy

Simple Meaning: Seeing them step up onto the riverbank with folded hands, completely purified of all ego and totally surrendered to You, You joyfully returned all their garments. Along with Your divine grace, You also granted them words of promise.

विदितं ननु वो मनीषितं वदितारस्त्विह योग्यमुत्तरम् ।
यमुनापुलिने सचन्द्रिकाः क्षणदा इत्यबलास्त्वमूचिवान् ॥60.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: विदितम् ननु is indeed well known, वः your, मनीषितम् innermost desire, वदितारः तु इह shall give however here, योग्यम् उत्तरम् proper response, यमुना-पुलिने on the sandy banks of the Yamuna, स-चन्द्रिकाः with moonlight, क्षणदाः nights, इति thus, अबलाः त्वम् ऊचिवान् You spoke to those maidens

Simple Meaning: "Your innermost desire is indeed well known to Me. I shall grant you a fitting response on the sandy banks of the Yamuna during the upcoming moonlight nights," You thus spoke to those young maidens.

उपकर्ण्य भवन्मुखच्युतं मधुनिष्यन्दि वचो मृगीदृशः ।
प्रणयादयि वीक्ष्य वीक्ष्य ते वदनाब्जं शनकैर्गृहं गताः ॥60.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: उपकर्ण्य having heard, भवत्-मुख-च्युतम् flowing from Your lotus mouth, मधु-निष्यन्दि dripping with honey, वचः words, मृगीदृशः the doe-eyed maidens, प्रणयात् out of deep love, अयि O Lord, वीक्ष्य वीक्ष्य gazing again and again, ते Your, वदन-अब्जम् lotus face, शनकैः गृहम् गताः slowly returned to their homes

Simple Meaning: Hearing those honeyed words flowing from Your lips, the doe-eyed maidens were filled with profound love. Gazing again and again at Your enchanting lotus face, they reluctantly and slowly made their way back to their homes.

इति नन्वनुगृह्य वल्लवीर्विपिनान्तेषु पुरेव सञ्चरन् ।
करुणाशिशिरो हरे हर त्वरया मे सकलामयावलिम् ॥60.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, ननु indeed, अनुगृह्य having blessed, वल्लवीः the gopis, विपिन-अन्तेषु in the depths of the forest, पुरा इव as before, सञ्चरन् wandering, करुणा-शिशिरः cool and soothing with compassion, हरे O Lord Hari, हर remove, त्वरया with speed, मे my, सकल-अमय-आवलिम् entire host of ailments

Simple Meaning: O Lord Hari! Having thus showered Your grace upon the gopis, You continued to wander through the forest as before, comforting all with Your cool and soothing compassion. O merciful Lord, please speed up and eradicate my entire host of afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
