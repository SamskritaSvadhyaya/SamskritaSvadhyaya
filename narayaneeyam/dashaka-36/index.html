दशकम् ३६

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 36

The incarnation as Parashurama

(Parashurama Avatara)

अत्रेः पुत्रतया पुरा त्वमनसूयायां हि दत्ताभिधो
जातः शिष्यनिबन्धतन्द्रितमनाः स्वस्थश्चरन् कान्तया ।
दृष्टो भक्ततमेन हेहयमहीपालेन तस्मै वरान्
अष्टैश्वर्यमुखान् प्रदाय ददिथ स्वेनैव चान्ते वधम् ॥36.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: अत्रेः of Sage Atri, पुत्रतया as the son, पुरा in ancient times, त्वम् You, अनसूयायाम् in Anasuya, हि indeed, दत्त-अभिधः named Datta (Dattatreya), जातः born, शिष्य-निबन्ध-तन्द्रित-मनाः whose mind was vexed by the continuous questions of Your disciples, स्वस्थः carefree/relaxed, चरन् wandering, कान्तया with Your consort, दृष्टः seen/met, भक्ततमेन by Your supreme devotee, हेहय-महीपालेन by the king of the Hehaya kingdom (Kartavirya Arjuna), तस्मै to him, वरान् boons, अष्ट-ऐश्वर्य-मुखान् boons such as the eight divine siddhis, प्रदाय bestowing, ददिथ You arranged/granted, स्वेन-एव by Yourself, च and, अन्ते in the end, वधम् his death/liberation

Simple Meaning: Long ago, You were born as Dattatreya to Sage Atri and Anasuya. Wanting to avoid the pestering of Your disciples, You wandered about freely and carefree in the company of Your consort. You were encountered by Your devout devotee, the Hehaya king Kartavirya Arjuna. Pleased with his devotion, You granted him grand boons, including the eight siddhis, while also promising that his eventual death would come only at Your own hands.

सत्यं कर्तुमथार्जुनस्य च वरं तच्छक्तिमात्रानतं
ब्रह्मद्वेषि तदाखिलं नृपकुलं हन्तुं च भूमेर्भरम् ।
सञ्जातो जमदग्नितो भृगुकुले त्वं रेणुकायां हरे
रामो नाम तदात्मजेष्ववरजः पित्रोरधाः सम्मदम् ॥36.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: सत्यम् true, कर्तुम् to make, अथ then, अर्जुनस्य of Kartavirya Arjuna, च and, वरम् the boon, तत्-शक्ति-मात्र-आनतम् which yielded only to Your divine power, ब्रह्म-द्वेषि hostile to the brahmanas, तदा at that time, अखिलम् entire, नृप-कुलम् the royal line/kshatriya race, हन्तुम् to destroy, च and, भूमेः of the earth, भरम् the burden, सञ्जातः born, जमदग्नितः from Sage Jamadagni, भृगु-कुले in the lineage of Bhrigu, त्वम् You, रेणुकायाम् in Renuka, हरे O Lord Hari, रामः नाम by the name of Rama, तद्-आत्मजेषु among their sons, अवरजः the youngest, पित्रोः of Your parents, अधाः You brought, सम्मदम् immense joy

Simple Meaning: To fulfill the boon given to Kartavirya Arjuna that he could only be slain by Your supreme power, to eliminate the oppressive royal dynasties that harbored hatred toward brahmanas, and to relieve the heavy burden of the earth, You incarnated as Parashurama. Born in the Bhrigu lineage to Sage Jamadagni and Renuka as the youngest of their sons, O Lord Hari, You brought boundless joy to Your parents.

लब्धाम्नायगणश्चतुर्दशवया गन्धर्वराजे मनाक्
आसक्तां किल मातरं प्रति पितुः क्रोधाकुलस्याज्ञया ।
ताताज्ञातिगसोदरैः सममिमां छित्वाऽथ शान्तात् पितुः
तेषां जीवनयोगमापिथ वरं माता च तेऽदाद्वरान् ॥36.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: लब्ध-आम्नाय-गणः having mastered the Vedas, चतुर्दश-वयाः at the age of fourteen, गन्धर्व-राजे towards the king of the Gandharvas, मनाक् slightly, आसक्ताम् attracted, किल indeed, मातरम् प्रति regarding the mother (Renuka), पितुः of the father (Jamadagni), क्रोध-आकुलस्य who was overcome by anger, आज्ञाया by the command, तात-आज्ञा-अतिग-सोदरैः along with the brothers who disobeyed the command of the father, समम् together, इमाम् her, छित्वा having beheaded, अथ then, शान्तात् from the pacified, पितुः father, तेषाम् of them (the mother and brothers), जीवन-योगम् the restoration of life, आपिथ You obtained, वरम् as a boon, माता the mother, च and, ते to You, अदात् granted, वरान् boons

Simple Meaning: By the age of fourteen, You had already mastered all the Vedas. Once, Your mother Renuka felt a fleeting mental attraction towards a Gandharva king. Overwhelmed by fierce anger, Your father Jamadagni ordered his sons to execute her. When Your older brothers refused, You strictly obeyed Your father's command and beheaded Your mother along with Your disobedient brothers. Pleased by Your absolute obedience, Your father's anger subsided. As a boon from Your pacified father, You cleverly asked for and obtained the restoration of life for all of them. Deeply moved upon being revived, Your mother also granted You numerous divine boons.

पित्रा मातृमुदे स्तवाहृतवियद्धेनोर्निजादाश्रमात्
प्रस्थायाथ भृगोर्गिरा हिमगिरावाराध्य गौरीपतिम् ।
लब्ध्वा तत्परशुं तदुक्तदनुजच्छेदी महास्त्रादिकं
प्राप्तो मित्रमथाकृतव्रणमुनिं प्राप्यागमः स्वाश्रमम् ॥36.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: पित्रा by Your father, मातृ-मुदे for the pleasure of Your mother, स्तव-आहृत-वियद्-धेनोः who brought the celestial cow Kamadhenu by his prayers, निजात् आश्रामत् from his own hermitage, प्रस्थाय setting out, अथ then, भृगोः of Sage Bhrigu, गिरा on the advice, हिमगिरौ on the Himalayas, आराध्य worshipping, गौरी-पतिम् Lord Shiva (the husband of Gauri), लब्ध्वा having obtained, तत्-परशुम् his axe, तत्-उक्त-दनुज-छेदी destroying the asura specified by him, महास्त्र-आदिकम् along with divine missiles, प्राप्तः having obtained, मित्रम् as a companion, अथ then, अकृतव्रण-मुनिम् the sage Akritavrana, प्राप्य meeting, अगमः You returned, स्व-आश्रमम् to Your own hermitage

Simple Meaning: Your father Jamadagni, to bring joy to Your mother, brought the celestial cow Kamadhenu to his hermitage through intense prayers. Later, on the advice of Sage Bhrigu, You journeyed to the Himalayas to worship Lord Shiva. Pleased with Your devotion, Lord Shiva granted You his divine axe (Parashu) and other formidable missiles, after You proved Your valor by destroying a powerful asura as directed by him. Gaining the sage Akritavrana as Your loyal companion along the way, You returned back to Your own hermitage.

आखेटोपगतोऽर्जुनः सुरगवीसम्प्राप्तसम्पद्गणैः
त्वत्पित्रा परिपूजितः पुरगतो दुर्मन्त्रिवाचा पुनः ।
गां क्रेतुं सचिवं न्ययुङ्क्त कुधिया तेनापि रुन्धन्मुनि-
प्राणक्षेपसरोषगोहतचमूचक्रेण वत्सो हृतः ॥36.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: आखेट-उपगतः who arrived while on a hunting expedition, अर्जुनः Kartavirya Arjuna, सुरगवी-सम्प्राप्त-सम्पद्-गणैः with the abundant wealth and hospitality obtained through the divine cow Kamadhenu, त्वत्-पित्रा by Your father, परिपूजितः grandly entertained/honored, पुर-गतः having returned to his capital, दुर्मन्त्रि-वाचा on the advice of his evil ministers, पुनः again, गाम् the divine cow, क्रेतुम् to buy, सचिवम् minister, न्ययुङ्क्त sent/commissioned, कु-धिया by that evil-minded minister, तेन by him, अपि even though, रुन्धन् prevented, मुनि-प्राण-क्षेप-सरोष-गो-हत-चमू-चक्रेण while the holy cow in extreme rage killed his army forces when they attacked the protecting sage, वत्सः the calf, हृतः was forcibly abducted

Simple Meaning: While out hunting, King Kartavirya Arjuna arrived at Your father's hermitage, where he was grandly entertained with lavish hospitality made possible by the celestial cow Kamadhenu. However, upon returning to his capital, he was misguided by evil counselors and dispatched a minister to purchase the cow. When the evil-minded minister tried to take her, Sage Jamadagni resisted; in the ensuing clash, the infuriated celestial cow wiped out the minister's army forces before the minister forcibly carried off her calf.

शुक्रोज्जीविततातवाक्यचलितक्रोधोऽथ सख्या समं
बिभ्रद्ध्यातमहोदरोपनिहितं चापं कुठारं शरान् ।
आरूढः सहवाहयन्तृकरथं माहिष्मतीमाविशन्
वाग्भिर्वत्समदाशुषि क्षितिपतौ सम्प्रास्तुथाः सङ्गरम् ॥36.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: शुक्र-उज्जीवित-तात-वाक्य-चलित-क्रोधः whose anger was aroused by the words of his father who was revived by Shukracharya, अथ then, सख्या समम् along with his companion (Akritavrana), बिभ्रत् carrying, ध्यात-महोदर-उपनिहितम् supplied by the giant Mahodara who was mediated upon, चापम् bow, कुठारम् axe, शरान् arrows, आरूढः mounted upon, सह-वाह-यन्तृक-रथम् a divine chariot complete with horses and a charioteer, माहिष्मतीम् Mahishmati (the capital city), आविशन् entering, वाग्भिः with words, वत्सम् the calf, अदाशुषि when he refused to return, क्षितिपतौ by the king (Kartavirya Arjuna), सम्प्रास्तुथाः You initiated/commenced, सङ्गरम् the battle

Simple Meaning: Informed of the outrage by Your father, who was revived by Sage Shukracharya, Your anger flared up. Armed with the bow, axe, and arrows brought to You instantly by Mahodara upon Your mere thought, You mounted a divine chariot complete with horses and a charioteer. Accompanied by Your friend Akritavrana, You entered the capital city of Mahishmati. When King Kartavirya Arjuna refused to return the stolen calf despite Your demands, You initiated a fierce battle against him.

पुत्राणामयुतेन सप्तदशभिश्चाक्षौहिणीभिर्महा-
सेनानीभिरनेकमित्रनिवहैर्व्याजृम्भितायोधनः ।
सद्यस्त्वत्ककुठारबाणविदलन्निश्शेषसैन्योत्करो
भीतिप्रद्रुतनष्टशिष्टतनयस्त्वामापतत् हेहयः ॥36.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुत्राणाम् अयुतेन with ten thousand sons, सप्त-दशभिः च अक्षौहिणीभिः and with seventeen Akshauhini divisions of troops, महा-सेनानीभिः with great generals, अनेक-मित्र-निवहैः with numerous allied kings, व्याजृम्भित-आयोधनः who launched a colossal battle, सद्यः instantly, त्वत्-क-कुठार-बाण-विदलन्-निश्शेष-सैन्य-उत्करः whose entire army forces were wiped out and severed by the axe and arrows in Your hand, भीति-प्रद्रुत-नष्ट-शिष्ट-तनयः whose few surviving sons fled in utter terror, त्वाम् आपतत् charged towards You, हेहयः Kartavirya Arjuna (the king of the Hehayas)

Simple Meaning: Kartavirya Arjuna launched a colossal battle, deploying ten thousand of his sons, seventeen Akshauhini army divisions, famous generals, and vast numbers of allied forces. However, in an instant, You wiped out his entire army using the formidable axe and arrows held in Your hands. With his surviving sons fleeing the field in terror, the king of the Hehayas himself charged furiously toward You.

लीलावारितनर्मदाजलवलल्लङ्केशगर्वापह-
श्रीमद्बाहुसहस्रमुक्तबहुशस्त्रास्त्रं निरुन्धन्नमुम् ।
चक्रे त्वय्यथ वैष्णवेऽपि विफले बुद्ध्वा हरिं त्वां मुदा
ध्यायन्तं छितसर्वदोषमवधीः सोऽगात् परं ते पदम् ॥36.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: लीला-वारित-नर्मदा-जल-वलल्-लङ्केश-गर्व-अपह-श्रीमद्-बाहु-सहस्र-मुक्त-बहु-शस्त्रास्त्रम् who released a barrage of numerous weapons and missiles from his glorious thousand arms that had once effortlessly dammed the waters of the Narmada in play and crushed the pride of the mighty Ravana (the king of Lanka), निरुन्धन् blocking/neutralizing, अमुम् him, चक्रे in the Sudarshana Chakra, त्वयि in You, अथ then, वैष्णवे अपि even when the Vaishnavastra missile, विफले was rendered ineffective, बुद्ध्वा realizing, हरिम् Lord Hari, त्वाम् You, मुदा joyfully, ध्यायन्तम् meditating, छित-सर्व-दोषम् freed from all his sins and impurities, अवधीः You killed/liberated, सः he, अगात् attained, परम् the supreme, ते Your, पदम् abode

Simple Meaning: Kartavirya Arjuna unleashed a barrage of weapons from his glorious thousand arms—the very arms that had playfully dammed the Narmada river and crushed the pride of Ravana. You neutralized all his attacks. Even when he hurled the powerful Vaishnavastra missile at You, it proved ineffective, as You are Lord Hari Himself. Realizing Your true divine identity, he joyfully meditated upon You. With all his sins and impurities washed away, You severed his heads and liberated him, enabling him to attain Your supreme abode.

भूयोऽमर्षितहेहयात्मजगणैस्ताते हते रेणुकाम्
आघ्नानां हृदयं निरीक्ष्य बहुशो घोरां प्रतिज्ञां वहन् ।
ध्यानानीतरथायुधस्त्वमकृथा विप्रद्रुहः क्षत्रियान्
दिक्चक्रेषु कुठारयन् विशिखयन् निःक्षत्रियां मेदिनीम् ॥36.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूयः again, अमर्षित-हेहय-आत्मज-गणैः by the group of enraged sons of Kartavirya Arjuna, ताते when Your father (Jamadagni), हते was killed, रेणुकाम् Renuka (Your mother), आघ्नानाम् beating, हृदयम् her chest, निरीक्ष्य seeing, बहुशः many times, घोराम् a terrible, प्रतिज्ञाम् vow, वहन् undertaking, ध्यान-आनीत-रथ-आयुधः whose chariot and weapons were summoned by mere thought, त्वम् You, अकृथाः You made, विप्र-द्रुहः who were hostile to brahmanas, क्षत्रियान् the kshatriyas, दिक्चक्रेषु across all directions of the earth, कुठारयन् striking down with Your axe, विशिखयन् piercing with Your arrows, निःक्षत्रियाम् devoid of kshatriyas, मेदिनीम् the earth

Simple Meaning: Later, when the enraged sons of Kartavirya Arjuna treacherously killed Your father Jamadagni, You saw Your mother Renuka weeping helplessly and beating her chest in anguish again and again. Filled with intense wrath, You took a fierce vow to rid the earth of kshatriyas. Summoning Your chariot and weapons by mere thought, You traversed all directions, striking down with Your axe and piercing with Your arrows all the oppressive, wicked kshatriyas who persecuted brahmanas, until the entire earth was completely purged of them.

तातोज्जीवनकृन्नृपालककुलं त्रिस्सप्तकृत्वो जयन्
सन्तर्प्याथ समन्तपञ्चकमहारक्तहृदौघे पितृन्
यज्ञे क्ष्मामपि काश्यपादिषु दिशन् साल्वेन युध्यन् पुनः
कृष्णोऽमुं निहनिष्यतीति शमितो युद्धात् कुमारैर्भवान् ॥36.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: तात-उज्जीवन-कृत् restoring Your father back to life, नृ-पालक-कुलम् the race of kings, त्रिस्सप्तकृत्वः twenty-one times, जयन् vanquishing/defeating, सन्तर्प्य having offered oblations, अथ then, समन्तपञ्चक-महारक्त-हृद-ओघे at a large lake of blood created at Samanta Panchaka, पितृन् to the ancestors, यज्ञे in a grand sacrifice, क्ष्माम् the earth, अपि also, काश्यप-आदिषु to Sage Kashyapa and others, दिशन् donating/bestowing, साल्वेन with King Salva, युध्यन् fighting, पुनः again, कृष्णः Krishna, अमुम् him, निहनिष्यति will kill/destroy, इति thus, शमितः persuaded to refrain, युद्धात् from battle, कुमारैः by the Sanatkumaras, भवान् You

Simple Meaning: After restoring Your father back to life, You vanquished the ruling kshatriya dynasties twenty-one times. At Samanta Panchaka, You offered oblations to Your ancestors at the large lake filled with the blood of the fallen kings. You then performed a majestic sacrifice and donated the entire earth to Sage Kashyapa and other sages. Later, while You were engaged in a fierce battle with King Salva, the Sanatkumaras intervened and dissuaded You from fighting, revealing that Krishna was destined to slay him.

न्यस्यास्त्राणि महेन्द्रभूभृति तपस्तन्वन् पुनर्मज्जितां
गोकर्णावधि सागरेण धरणीं दृष्ट्वार्थितस्तापसैः ।
ध्यातेष्वासधृतानलास्त्रचकितं सिन्धुं स्रुवक्षेपणात्
उत्सार्योद्धृतकेरलो भृगुपते वातेश संरक्ष माम् ॥36.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: न्यस्य laying down, अस्त्राणि Your weapons, महेन्द्रभूभृति on the Mahendra mountain, तपः penance, तन्वन् performing, पुनः again, मज्जिताम् submerged, गोकर्ण-अवधि up to Gokarna, सागरेण by the ocean, धरणीम् the land, दृष्ट्वा seeing, अर्थितः being implored/prayed to, तापसैः by the hermits/sages, ध्यात-इष्वास-धृत-अनल-अस्त्र-चकितम् terrified by the fire missile loaded onto the bow summoned by Your mere thought, सिन्धुम् the ocean, स्रुव-क्षेपणात् by hurling Your ladle/spoon, उत्सार्य driving back, उद्धृत-केरलः who reclaimed the land of Kerala, भृगुपते O Lord of the Bhrigu dynasty (Parashurama), वातेश O Lord of Guruvayur, संरक्ष protect, माम् me

Simple Meaning: Laying down Your weapons, You retired to the Mahendra mountain to perform severe penance. Seeing the coastal region up to Gokarna submerged under the ocean, and being implored by the resident hermits, You summoned Your bow by thought and loaded a fierce fire missile onto it. Terrified by Your divine wrath, the sea retreated when You hurled Your ladle, and brought back the holy land of Kerala from the sea. O Lord of the Bhrigu dynasty, O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect me!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
