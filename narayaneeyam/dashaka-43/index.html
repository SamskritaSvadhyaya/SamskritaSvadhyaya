Destruction of Trinavarta

दशकम् ४३

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 43

Destruction of Trinavarta

त्वामेकदा गुरुमरुत्पुरनाथ वोढुं गाढाधिरूढगरिमाणमपारयन्ती ।
माता निधाय शयने किमिदं बतेति ध्यायन्त्यचेष्टत गृहेषु निविष्टशङ्का ॥43.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वाम् You, एकदा once, गुरुमरुत्पुरनाथ O Lord of Guruvayur, वोढुम् to carry, गाढ-अधिरूढ-गरिमाणम् who had excessively increased in weight, अपारयन्ती unable, माता mother Yashoda, निधाय having placed, शयने on the bed, किम् इदम् बत what is this, oh, इति thus, ध्यायन्ती thinking, अचेष्टत was engrossed in, गृहेषु in the household, निविष्ट-शङ्का with filled apprehension

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Guruvayur! Once, unable to carry You due to Your sudden, immense increase in weight, Your mother placed You on the bed. Wondering with deep concern and apprehension, "Oh! What could this be?", she became absorbed in her daily household activities.

तावद्विदूरमुपकर्णितघोरघोषव्याजृम्भिपांसुपटलीपरिपूरिताशः ।
वात्यावपुस्स किल दैत्यवरस्तृणावर्ताख्यो जहार जनमानसहारिणं त्वाम् ॥43.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: तावत् then, विदूरम् from a distance, उपकर्णित-घोर-घोष-व्याजृम्भि-पांसुपटली-परिपूरित-आशः with a terrible sound heard from afar and filling all directions with an expanding mass of dust, वात्या-वपुः in the form of a whirlwind, सः he, किल indeed, दैत्य-वरः chief of daityas, तृणावर्त-आख्यः Trinavarta by name, जहार carried away, जन-मानस-हारिणम् who captivates the minds of the people, त्वाम् You

Simple Meaning: Then, a terrible roar was heard from a distance, and an expanding cloud of dust filled all directions. Indeed, the chief of daityas named Trinavarta took the form of a whirlwind and carried You away, You who captivate the minds of all people.

उद्दामपांसुतिमिराहतदृष्टिपाते द्रष्टुं किमप्यकुशले पशुपाललोके ।
हा बालकस्य किमिति त्वदुपान्तमाप्ता माता भवन्तमविलोक्य भृशं रुरोद ॥43.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: उद्दाम-पांसु-तिमिर-आहत-दृष्टि-पाते with the vision blinded by the intense darkness of dust, द्रष्टुम् to see, किम् अपि anything at all, अकुशले unable, पशुपाल-लोके when the cowherd people were, हा alas, बालकस्य किम् इति what happened to the child, त्वत्-उपान्तम् आप्ता having reached Your side, माता mother Yashoda, भवन्तम् You, अविलोक्य not seeing, भृशम् intensely, रुरोद wept

Simple Meaning: When the vision of the cowherd people was completely blinded by the intense darkness caused by the massive dust cloud, making them unable to see anything at all, mother Yashoda rushed to Your side. Cryingly asking, "Alas, what has happened to my child?", and not finding You there, she wept bitterly.

तावत् स दानववरोऽपि च दीनमूर्तिः भावत्कभारपरिधारणलूनवेगः ।
सङ्कोचमाप तदनु क्षतपांसुघोषे घोषे व्यतायत भवज्जननीनिनादः ॥43.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तावत् then, सः he, दानव-वरः chief of danavas, अपि also, च and, दीन-मूर्तिः appearing helpless, भावत्क-भार-परिधारण-लून-वेगः whose speed was slowed down by supporting Your weight, सङ्कोचम् आप became diminished, तदनु after that, क्षत-पांसु-घोषे when the dust and noise subsided, घोषे in Gokula, व्यतायत resounded, भवत्-जननी-निनादः the cry of Your mother

Simple Meaning: Then, that chief of danavas, appearing helpless and with his speed drastically reduced due to carrying Your immense weight, was forced to shrink in size. Following that, when the dust cloud and roaring noise subsided, the anguished cries of Your mother resounded throughout Gokula.

रोदोपकर्णनवशादुपगम्य गेहं क्रन्दत्सु नन्दमुखगोपकुलेषु दीनः ।
त्वां दानवस्त्वखिलमुक्तिकरं मुमुक्षुः त्वय्यप्रमुञ्चति पपात वियत्प्रदेशात् ॥43.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: रोद-उपकर्णन-वशात् on hearing the sound of weeping, उपगम्य coming to, गेहम् the house, क्रन्दत्सु while weeping, नन्द-मुख-गोप-कुलेषु the cowherds headed by Nanda, दीनः helpless, त्वाम् You, दानवः तु the danava on the other hand, अखिल-मुक्ति-करम् the bestower of liberation to all, मुमुक्षुः desiring to release, त्वयि अप्रमुञ्चति while You did not let go, पपात fell down, वियत्-प्रदेशात् from the sky

Simple Meaning: Hearing the sound of crying, the cowherds led by Nanda rushed to the house and wept in distress. Meanwhile, the helpless danava desired to release You, who grant liberation to all. But since You would not let go of him, he fell down from the high sky.

रोदाकुलास्तदनु गोपगणा बहिष्ठपाषाणपृष्ठभुवि देहमतिस्थविष्ठम् ।
प्रैक्षन्त हन्त निपतन्तममुष्य वक्षस्यक्षीणमेव च भवन्तमलं हसन्तम् ॥43.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: रोद-आकुलाः distraught with weeping, तदनु thereafter, गोप-गणाः the group of cowherds, बहिष्ठ-पाषाण-पृष्ठ-भुवि on the surface of a large rock outside, देहम् body, अति-स्थविष्ठम् extremely huge, प्रैक्षन्त saw, हन्त ah, निपतन्तम् falling, अमुष्य of that danava, वक्षसि on the chest, अक्षीणम् unharmed, एव indeed, च and, भवन्तम् You, अलम् slightly, हसन्तम् smiling

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, the group of cowherds, who were distraught with weeping, saw his extremely huge body falling onto the surface of a large rock outside. Ah, and there on his chest, they saw You, completely unharmed and slightly smiling!

ग्रावप्रपातपरिपिष्टगरिष्ठदेहभ्रष्टासुदुष्टदनुजोपरि धृष्टहासम् ।
आघ्नानमम्बुजकरेण भवन्तमेत्य गोपा दधुर्गिरिवरादिव नीलरत्नम् ॥43.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: ग्राव-प्रपात-परिपिष्ट-गरिष्ठ-देह-भ्रष्ट-असु-दुष्ट-दनुज-उपरि on top of the wicked danava whose huge body was crushed by the fall on the rock and whose life had departed, धृष्ट-हासम् laughingly, आघ्नानम् patting, अम्बुज-करेण with Your lotus hand, भवन्तम् You, एत्य approaching, गोपाः the cowherds, दधुः lifted, गिरि-वरात् इव as if from a great mountain, नील-रत्नम् a blue gem

Simple Meaning: Approaching You, who were laughingly patting with Your lotus hand on top of the wicked danava whose huge body was crushed by falling on the rock and whose life had departed, the cowherds lifted You up, just like picking up a precious blue gem from a great mountain.

एकैकमाशु परिगृह्य निकामनन्दन्नन्दादिगोपपरिरब्धविचुम्बिताङ्गम् ।
आदातुकामपरिशङ्कितगोपनारीहस्ताम्बुजप्रपतितं प्रणुमो भवन्तम् ॥43.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: एक-एकम् one by one, आशु quickly, परिगृह्य holding, निकाम-नन्दन्-नन्द-आदि-गोप-परिरब्ध-विचुम्बित-अङ्गम् whose limbs were embraced and kissed by Nanda and other cowherds who were immensely overjoyed, आदातु-काम-परिशङ्कित-गोप-नारी-हस्त-अम्बुज-प्रपतितम् who fell into the lotus hands of the cowherd women who were eager yet hesitant to take You, प्रणुमः we praise, भवन्तम् You

Simple Meaning: We praise You, whose limbs were joyfully embraced and kissed one by one by Nanda and the other cowherds who were ecstatic with happiness, and who then landed into the lotus-like hands of the cowherd women who were eagerly yet anxiously waiting to hold You.

भूयोऽपि किन्नु कृणुमः प्रणतार्तिहारी गोविन्द एव परिपालयतात् सुतं नः ।
इत्यादि मातरपितृप्रमुखैस्तदानीं सम्प्रार्थितस्त्वदवनाय विभो त्वमेव ॥43.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूयः अपि again, किम् नु what indeed, कृणुमः shall we do, प्रणत-आर्ति-हारी the remover of the distress of those who surrender, गोविन्दः Govinda, एव alone, परिपालयतात् may protect, सुतम् son, नः our, इति आदि thus and so on, मातृ-पितृ-प्रमुखैः by the parents and others, तदानीम् then, सम्प्रार्थितः prayed to, त्वत्-अवनाय for Your protection, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, त्वम् एव You alone

Simple Meaning: "What indeed shall we do again? May Govinda alone, who removes the distress of all those who surrender to Him, protect our son!" Thus and so on, O All-Pervading Lord, You alone were prayed to by Your parents and others for Your own protection!

वातात्मकं दनुजमेवमयि प्रधून्वन् वातोद्भवान् मम गदान् किमु नो धुनोषि ।
किं वा करोमि पुनरप्यनिलालयेश निश्शेषरोगशमनं मुहुरर्थये त्वाम् ॥43.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: वात-आत्मकम् in the form of wind, दनुजम् the danava, एवम् thus, अयि O Lord, प्रधून्वन् destroying, वात-उद्भवान् born of vata, मम my, गदान् ailments, किमु नो why not, धुनोषि (You) destroy, किम् वा what, करोमि shall I do, पुनः अपि once again, अनिल-आलय-ईश O Lord of Guruvayur, निश्शेष-रोग-शमनम् the complete eradication of all diseases, मुहुः repeatedly, अर्थये I pray, त्वाम् to You

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Thus destroying the danava who came in the form of a wind, why do You not destroy my ailments born of vata? What else can I do? O Lord of Guruvayur, I repeatedly pray to You for the complete eradication of all my diseases!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
