Krishna reveals the Vishwaroopa form to Yashoda

दशकम् ४६

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 46

Krishna reveals the Vishwaroopa form to Yashoda

अयि देव पुरा किल त्वयि स्वयमुत्तानशये स्तनन्धये ।
परिजृम्भणतो व्यपावृते वदने विश्वमचष्ट वल्लवी ॥46.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: अयि देव O Lord, पुरा in the past, किल indeed, त्वयि when You, स्वयम् उत्तानशये were lying on Your back, स्तनन्धये an infant drinking milk, परिजृम्भणतः due to yawning, व्यपावृते when opened, वदने in Your mouth, विश्वम् the entire universe, अचष्ट saw, वल्लवी Yashoda the gopi

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Long ago, while You were lying on Your back as a tiny infant drinking milk, You happened to yawn. As Your mouth opened wide, mother Yashoda saw the entire universe displayed within it!

पुनरप्यथ बालकैः समं त्वयि लीलानिरते जगत्पते ।
फलसञ्चयवञ्चनक्रुधा तव मृद्भोजनमूचुरर्भकाः ॥46.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुनः अपि अथ then once again, बालकैः समम् along with the other children, त्वयि when You, लीला-निरते were engaged in play, जगत्पते O Lord of the universe, फल-सञ्चय-वञ्चन-क्रुधा out of anger from being cheated of their share of fruits, तव Your, मृत्-भोजनम् eating of mud, ऊचुः अर्भकाः reported the children

Simple Meaning: Then once again, O Lord of the universe, while You were playing with the other children, You cheated them out of their share of fruits. Filled with anger over this, the children went and reported to Yashoda that You had eaten mud.

अयि ते प्रलयावधौ विभो क्षितितोयादिसमस्तभक्षिणः ।
मृदुपाशनतो रुजा भवेदिति भीता जननी चुकोप सा ॥46.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: अयि O, ते Your, प्रलय-अवधौ at the time of cosmic dissolution, विभो O all-pervading Lord, क्षिति-तोय-आदि-समस्त-भक्षिणः who devours everything including earth and water, मृत्-उपाशनतः from eating mud, रुजा disease, भवेत् might occur, इति भीता thus fearing, जननी (Your) mother, चुकोप सा she became angry

Simple Meaning: O all-pervading Lord! Though You swallow everything including earth and water at the time of cosmic dissolution, Your mother Yashoda feared that eating a little mud might make You sick, and so she became angry.

अयि दुर्विनयात्मक त्वया किमु मृत्सा बत वत्स भक्षिता ।
इति मातृगिरं चिरं विभो वितथां त्वं प्रतिजज्ञिषे हसन् ॥46.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अयि O, दुर्विनय-आत्मक O naughty child, त्वया by You, किमु is it that, मृत्सा mud, बत alas, वत्स O child, भक्षिता was eaten, इति thus, मातृ-गिरम् mother's words, चिरम् repeatedly, विभो O Lord, वितथाम् as untrue, त्वम् You, प्रतिजज्ञिषे denied, हसन् smilingly

Simple Meaning: "O naughty child! Did you really eat mud, my dear son?" Seeing your mother address you thus repeatedly, O Lord, you smilingly denied her words as completely false!

अयि ते सकलैर्विनिश्चिते विमतिश्चेद्वदनं विदार्यताम् ।
इति मातृविभर्त्सितो मुखं विकसत्पद्मनिभं व्यदारयः ॥46.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: अयि O my son, ते by You, सकलैः by all (the boys), विनिश्चिते when it has been confirmed, विमतिः चेत् if (You) disagree, वदनम् (Your) mouth, विदार्यताम् open wide, इति thus, मातृ-विभर्त्सितः reprimanded by (Your) mother, मुखम् (Your) mouth, विकसत्-पद्म-निभम् resembling a fully bloomed lotus, व्यदारयः (You) opened wide

Simple Meaning: "O child! When all the boys have confirmed that you ate mud, if you still disagree, then open your mouth!" Reprimanded thus by Your mother, You opened Your mouth, which resembled a radiant, fully bloomed lotus.

अपि मृल्लवदर्शनोत्सुकां जननीं तां बहु तर्पयन्निव ।
पृथिवीं निखिलां न केवलं भुवनान्यप्यखिलान्यदीदृशः ॥46.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: अपि even, मृत्-लव-दर्शन-उत्सुकाम् eager to see a small speck of mud, जननीम् mother, ताम् her, बहु generously, तर्पयन् इव as if satisfying her beyond expectation, पृथिवीम् the earth, निखिलाम् whole, न केवलम् not only, भुवनानि अपि अखिलानि but all the worlds as well, अदीदृशः You showed

Simple Meaning: As if generously satisfying Your mother, who was eager to see a tiny speck of mud, You showed her not only the whole earth, but all the worlds and universes within Your wide-open mouth!

कुहचिद्वनमम्बुधिः क्वचित् क्वचिदभ्रं कुहचिद्रसातलम् ।
मनुजा दनुजाः क्वचित् सुरा ददृशे किं न तदा त्वदानने ॥46.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: कुहचित् in some places, वनम् forests, अम्बुधिः ocean, क्वचित् in some places, क्वचित् in some places, अभ्रम् the sky, कुहचित् in some places, रसातलम् the netherworld, मनुजाः born of Manu (human beings), दनुजाः born of Danu (danavas), क्वचित् in some places, सुराः devas, ददृशे was seen, किम् न what indeed was not, तदा then, त्वत्-आनने in Your mouth

Simple Meaning: In some places forests, in some places oceans, in some places the sky, and in some places the netherworld; human beings, danavas, and in some places, the devas—what indeed was not seen in Your mouth then, O Lord!

कलशाम्बुधिशायिनं पुनः परवैकुण्ठपदाधिवासिनम् ।
स्वपुरश्च निजार्भकात्मकं कतिधा त्वां न ददर्श सा मुखे ॥46.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: कलश-अम्बुधि-शायिनम् as the one lying on the ocean of milk, पुनः also, पर-वैकुण्ठ-पद-अधिवासिनम् as the one dwelling in the supreme abode of Vaikuntha, स्व-पुरः right in front of herself, च and, निज-अर्भक-आत्मकम् in the form of her own little child, कतिधा in how many ways, त्वाम् You, न ददर्श did she not see, सा she (mother Yashoda), मुखे inside Your mouth

Simple Meaning: Inside Your mouth, she saw You lying on the ocean of milk, then again saw You dwelling in the supreme realm of Vaikuntha, and even standing right in front of her as her own little child! In how many wondrous and manifold ways did mother Yashoda not behold You there!

विकसद्भुवने मुखोदरे ननु भूयोऽपि तथाविधाननः ।
अनया स्फुटमीक्षितो भवाननवस्थां जगतां बतातनोत् ॥46.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: विकसत्-भुवने in the expanding universe, मुख-उदरे within the cavity of Your mouth, ननु indeed, भूयः अपि once again, तथा-विध-आननः having a mouth of that very same kind, अनया by her (Yashoda), स्फुटम् ईक्षितः clearly seen, भवान् You, अनवस्थाम् an infinite regress, जगताम् of the worlds, बत alas/indeed, अतनोत् displayed

Simple Meaning: Within the expanding universe visible inside Your mouth, Yashoda clearly saw You again, opening Your mouth just as before and revealing yet another universe within it! Indeed, O Lord, You thus displayed a wondrous infinite regress of worlds within worlds!

धृततत्त्वधियं तदा क्षणं जननीं तां प्रणयेन मोहयन् ।
स्तनमम्ब दिशेत्युपासजन् भगवन्नद्भुतबाल पाहि माम् ॥46.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: धृत-तत्त्व-धियम् having attained the knowledge of Supreme Reality, तदा then, क्षणम् for a moment, जननीम् mother, ताम् her, प्रणयेन with affection, मोहयन् deluding, स्तनम् milk, अम्ब O mother, दिश give me, इति (saying) thus, उपासजन् embracing her, भगवन् O Supreme Lord, अद्भुत-बाल O wondrous child, पाहि माम् protect me

Simple Meaning: Having given mother Yashoda a moment's realization of the Supreme Reality, You then deluded her once more with affection. Embracing her, You pleaded, "O Mother, give me milk!" O Supreme Lord, O wondrous child, please protect me!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
