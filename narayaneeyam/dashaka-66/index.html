Krishna plays Rasa-Lila with the gopis

दशकम् ६६

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 66

Krishna plays Rasa-Lila with the gopis

उपयातानां सुदृशां कुसुमायुधबाणपातविवशानाम् ।
अभिवाञ्छितं विधातुं कृतमतिरपि ता जगाथ वाममिव ॥66.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: उपयातानाम् of those who had arrived, सुदृशाम् of the fair-eyed gopis, कुसुम-आयुध-बाण-पात-विवशानाम् who were utterly helpless due to being struck by the arrows of Kamadeva, अभिवाञ्छितम् their heart's desire, विधातुम् to fulfill, कृत-मतिः having resolved or made up Your mind, अपि although, ताः to them, जगाथ You spoke, वामम् contrary or harsh words, इव as if

Simple Meaning: Although You were fully resolved in Your mind to fulfill the deepest desires of those fair-eyed gopis who had arrived before You, utterly overwhelmed as they were by the arrows of Kamadeva, You initially spoke to them in a seemingly contrary and harsh manner.

गगनगतं मुनिनिवहं श्रावयितुं जगिथ कुलवधूधर्मम् ।
धर्म्यं खलु ते वचनं कर्म तु नो निर्मलस्य विश्वास्यम् ॥66.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: गगन-गतम् situated in the sky, मुनि-निवहम् the assembly of sages, श्रावयितुम् to make them hear, जगिथ You spoke, कुल-वधू-धर्मम् the duties of chaste noble wives, धर्म्यम् righteous or aligned with dharma, खलु indeed, ते Your, वचनम् speech, कर्म Your action, तु however, नो not, निर्मलस्य of the pure-hearted one, विश्वास्यम् worthy of belief or imitation

Simple Meaning: To make the assembly of sages gathered in the sky listen, You spoke to the gopis on the sacred duties of chaste wives. Indeed, Your words were completely aligned with righteousness, but the actions of a supremely pure being like You are beyond the ordinary rules meant for worldlings and are not to be blindly imitated.

आकर्ण्य ते प्रतीपां वाणीमेणीदृशः परं दीनाः ।
मा मा करुणासिन्धो परित्यजेत्यतिचिरं विलेपुस्ताः॥66.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: आकर्ण्य having heard, ते Your, प्रतीपाम् contrary or discouraging, वाणीम् words, एणी-दृशः the doe-eyed gopis, परम् extremely, दीनाः distressed, मा मा do not, do not, करुणा-सिन्धो O Ocean of Compassion, परित्यज abandon us, इति thus, अति-चिरम् for a very long time, विलेपुः lamented, ताः they

Simple Meaning: Hearing Your discouraging words, those deer-eyed gopis became deeply distressed. Crying out, "O Ocean of Compassion, do not abandon us, please do not abandon us!", they lamented for a very long time in sorrow.

तासां रुदितैर्लपितैः करुणाकुलमानसो मुरारे त्वम् ।
ताभिस्समं प्रवृत्तो यमुनापुलिनेषु काममभिरन्तुम् ॥66.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तासाम् their, रुदितैः by the weepings, लपितैः by the lamentations, करुणा-आकुल-मानसः with a heart moved by compassion, मुरारे O Murari, त्वम् You, ताभिः समम् along with them, प्रवृत्तः proceeded, यमुना-पुलिनेषु on the sandy banks of Yamuna, कामम् to Your heart's content, अभिरन्तुम् to sport or engage in love play

Simple Meaning: O Lord Murari, Your heart was deeply moved by compassion upon hearing their tears and plaintive lamentations. Thus, You proceeded to sport to Your heart's content with them along the sandy banks of the Yamuna.

चन्द्रकरस्यन्दलसत्सुन्दरयमुनातटान्तवीथीषु ।
गोपीजनोत्तरीयैरापादितसंस्तरो न्यषीदस्त्वम् ॥66.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: चन्द्र-कर-स्यन्द-लसत्-सुन्दर-यमुना-तट-अन्त-वीथीषु on the beautiful stretches along the banks of the Yamuna shining with the flowing rays of the moon, गोपी-जन-उत्तरीयैः with the upper garments spread out by the gopis, आपादित-संस्तरः on the seat thus prepared, न्यषीदः sat down, त्वम् You

Simple Meaning: On the beautiful pathways along the banks of the Yamuna, glowing brightly under the flowing streams of moonlight, You sat down comfortably upon the soft seat prepared for You by the gopis using their own upper garments.

सुमधुरनर्मालपनैः करसंग्रहणैश्च चुम्बनोल्लासैः ।
गाढालिङ्गनसङ्गैस्त्वमङ्गनालोकमाकुलीचकृषे ॥66.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: सु-मधुर-नर्म-आलपनैः with exceedingly sweet playful pleasantries, कर-संग्रहणैः by holding their hands, च and, चुम्बन-उल्लासैः with joyous kisses, गाढ-आलिङ्गन-सङ्गैः through intimate tight embraces, त्वम् You, अङ्गना-लोकम् those maidens, आकुली-चकृषे filled with overwhelming love and ecstasy

Simple Meaning: Through exceedingly sweet and playful talk, by tenderly holding their hands, with joyous kisses, and in intimate, tight embraces, You filled those beautiful maidens with overwhelming love and ecstasy.

वासोहरणदिने यद्वासोहरणं प्रतिश्रुतं तासाम् ।
तदपि विभो रसविवशस्वान्तानां कान्त सुभ्रुवामदधाः ॥66.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: वासोहरण-दिने on the day of taking away their garments, यत् which, वासो-हरणम् removal of garments, प्रतिश्रुतम् promised, तासाम् to them, तत् that, अपि also, विभो O All-pervading Lord, रस-विवश-स्वान्तानाम् whose minds were overwhelmed by divine love, कान्त O Beloved, सुभ्रुवाम् of those lovely-browed maidens, अदधाः You fulfilled

Simple Meaning: O All-pervading Lord, O Beloved, You thus fulfilled that promise of removing their garments which You had made to those lovely-browed gopis on the day You took away their clothes during their vow, as their minds were now completely overwhelmed by the bliss of divine love.

कन्दलितघर्मलेशं कुन्दमृदुस्मेरवक्त्रपाथोजम् ।
नन्दसुत त्वां त्रिजगत्सुन्दरमुपगूह्य नन्दिता बालाः ॥66.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: कन्दलित-घर्म-लेशम् on whom tiny drops of perspiration had sprouted, कुन्द-मृदु-स्मेर-वक्त्र-पाथोजम् whose lotus face shone with a gentle smile as soft as a kunda flower, नन्द-सुत O Son of Nanda, त्वाम् You, त्रिजगत्-सुन्दरम् the most handsome one in all the three worlds, उपगूह्य embracing, नन्दिताः were overjoyed, बालाः those young maidens

Simple Meaning: O Son of Nanda, with tiny drops of perspiration sprouting on Your skin and Your lotus face glowing with a gentle smile as soft as a kunda flower, You stood as the most beautiful vision in all the three worlds. Embracing You tightly, those young maidens were filled with supreme joy.

विरहेष्वङ्गारमयः शृङ्गारमयश्च सङ्गमे हि त्वम् ।
नितरामङ्गारमयस्तत्र पुनस्सङ्गमेऽपि चित्रमिदम् ॥66.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: विरहेषु in separation, अङ्गार-मयः like burning charcoal or fire, शृङ्गार-मयः full of love or blissful passion, च and, सङ्गमे in union, हि indeed, त्वम् You, नितराम्र् intensely, अङ्गार-मयः Angara-like, तत्र in that state, पुनः moreover, सङ्गमे in union, अपि even, चित्रम् a wonder or marvel, इदम् this

Simple Meaning: In separation You are like burning embers to Your devotees, while in union You are the very essence of blissful love. Yet, what a marvel this is! Even in direct union, You were intensely Angaramaya to those gopis, inflaming their passion even while quenching their longing!

राधातुङ्गपयोधरसाधुपरीरम्भलोलुपात्मानम् ।
आराधये भवन्तं पवनपुराधीश शमय सकलगदान् ॥66.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: राधा-तुङ्ग-पयोधर-साधु-परीरम्भ-लोलुप-आत्मानम् whose mind is eagerly desirous of the tight embrace of the high breasts of Radha, आराधये I worship, भवन्तम् You, पवनपुर-अधीश O Lord of Guruvayur, शमय please cure, सकल-गदान् all diseases

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Guruvayur, I deeply worship You, whose mind is intensely eager for the close and loving embrace of Radha. I pray to You, please cure all my diseases and afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
