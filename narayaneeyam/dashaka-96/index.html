Bhakti as a self-sufficient path

दशकम् ९६

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 96

Bhakti as a self-sufficient path

त्वं हि ब्रह्मैव साक्षात् परमुरुमहिमन्नक्षराणामकारः
तारो मन्त्रेषु राज्ञां मनुरसि मुनिषु त्वं भृगुर्नारदोऽपि ।
प्रह्लादो दानवानां पशुषु च सुरभिः पक्षिणां वैनतेयो
नागानामस्यनन्तस्सुरसरिदपि च स्रोतसां विश्वमूर्ते ॥96.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वम् You, हि indeed, ब्रह्म एव Supreme Brahman itself, साक्षात् directly, परम-उरु-महिमन् O Lord of supreme and boundless glory, अक्षराणाम् among letters, अकारः the letter A, तारः the sacred syllable Om, मन्त्रेषु among mantras, राज्ञाम् among kings, मनुः Manu, असि You are, मुनिषु among sages, त्वम् You, भृगुः Bhrigu, नारदः अपि Narada as well, प्रह्लादः Prahlada, दानवानाम् among the danavas, पशुषु among animals, च and, सुरभिः Surabhi (Kamadhenu), पक्षिणाम् among birds, वैनतेयः Garuda - son of Vinata, नागानाम् among serpents, असि You are, अनन्तः Ananta (Adishesha), सुरसरित् अपि the divine river Ganga, च and, स्रोतसाम् among flowing streams, विश्व-मूर्ते O Lord of universal form

Simple Meaning: O Lord of supreme and boundless glory! O Lord of universal form! You are directly the Supreme Brahman itself! Among the letters of the alphabet, You are the foundational letter A; among mantras, You are the sacred syllable Om; among kings, You are Manu; and among sages, You are both Bhrigu and Narada. Among the danavas, You are Prahlada; among animals, You are the divine wish-fulfilling cow Surabhi; among birds, You are Garuda; among serpents, You are Ananta; and among flowing streams, You are the celestial river Ganga!

ब्रह्मण्यानां बलिस्त्वं क्रतुषु च जपयज्ञोऽसि वीरेषु पार्थो
भक्तानामुद्धवस्त्वं बलमसि बलिनां धाम तेजस्विनां त्वम् ।
नास्त्यन्तस्त्वद्विभूतेर्विकसदतिशयं वस्तु सर्वं त्वमेव
त्वं जीवस्त्वं प्रधानं यदिह भवदृते तन्न किञ्चित् प्रपञ्चे ॥96.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्रह्मण्यानाम् among holy or righteous men devoted to Brahman, बलिः King Bali, त्वम् You are, क्रतुषु च and among sacrifices, जप-यज्ञः असि You are Japa Yajna (chanting of sacred names), वीरेषु among heroes, पार्थः Arjuna (son of Pritha), भक्तानाम् among devotees, उद्धवः त्वम् You are Uddhava, बलम् असि You are strength, बलिनाम् of the strong, धाम glory or splendor, तेजस्विनाम् of the radiant ones, त्वम् You are, न अस्ति अन्तः there is no end, त्वत्-विभूतेः to Your divine manifestations, विकसत्-अतिशयम् possessing expanding excellence or greatness, वस्तु entity or object, सर्वम् all, त्वम् एव You alone, त्वम् जीवः You are the individual soul, त्वम् प्रधानम् You are Pradhana or primordial nature, यत् इह whatever, in this world, भवत्-ऋते except You, तत् that, न किञ्चित् is nothing, प्रपञ्चे in this universe

Simple Meaning: Among those devoted to the holy people, You are King Bali; among sacrifices, You are the chanting of sacred mantras (Japa Yajna); among heroes, You are Arjuna; and among devotees, You are Uddhava! You are the strength of the strong and the splendor of the radiant! There is no end to Your divine manifestations. Every entity that displays exceptional excellence or glory is indeed You alone! You are the individual soul, and You are of primordial nature. In this entire universe, there is nothing whatsoever that exists apart from You!

धर्मं वर्णाश्रमाणां श्रुतिपथविहितं त्वत्परत्वेन भक्त्या
कुर्वन्तोऽन्तर्विरागे विकसति शनकैः सन्त्यजन्तो लभन्ते ।
सत्तास्फूर्तिप्रियत्वात्मकमखिलपदार्थेषु भिन्नेष्वभिन्नं
निर्मूलं विश्वमूलं परममहमिति त्वद्विबोधं विशुद्धम् ॥96.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: धर्मम् duties or righteousness, वर्ण-आश्रमाणाम् of the four varnas and ashramas, श्रुति-पथ-विहितम् ordained by the path of the Vedas, त्वत्-परत्वेन with dedication unto You, भक्त्या through devotion, कुर्वन्तः performing, अन्तः-विरागे when inner dispassion or detachment, विकसति blossoms or awakens, शनकैः gradually, सन्त्यजन्तः relinquishing those actions, लभन्ते they attain, सत्ता-स्फूर्ति-प्रियत्व-आत्मकम् consisting of pure existence, consciousness, and bliss, अखिल-पदार्थेषु in all objects, भिन्नेषु which are varied and different, अभिन्नम् undivided/non-different, निर्मूलम् rootless/without any cause, विश्व-मूलम् the supreme root/source of the entire universe, परमम् ultimate, अहम् I, इति thus, त्वत्-विबोधम् realization of You, विशुद्धम् pure and immaculate

Simple Meaning: By performing the duties of their respective stations in life as ordained by the Vedas with devotion and complete dedication unto You, people gradually develop inner detachment. As this dispassion blossoms, they relinquish action and attain the pure, immaculate realization of You. They realize You as the ultimate, uncaused source of the entire universe, existing undivided as pure existence, consciousness, and bliss across all diverse objects, and they know that ultimate reality as their very own self!

ज्ञानं कर्मापि भक्तिस्त्रितयमिह भवत्प्रापकं तत्र तावत्
निर्विण्णानामशेषे विषय इह भवेत् ज्ञानयोगेऽधिकारः ।
सक्तानां कर्मयोगस्त्वयि हि विनिहितो ये तु नात्यन्तसक्ताः
नाप्यत्यन्तं विरक्तास्त्वयि च धृतरसा भक्तियोगो ह्यमीषाम् ॥96.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: ज्ञानम् knowledge, कर्म अपि and action, भक्तिः devotion, त्रितयम् the triad of these three paths, इह in this world, भवत्-प्रापकम् leading to the attainment of You, तत्र in that regard, तावत् indeed, निर्विण्णानाम् of those who have developed complete dispassion, अशेषे total, विषये in sensory objects, इह in this world, भवेत् is, ज्ञान-योगे in the path of knowledge (Jnana Yoga), अधिकारः eligibility/qualification, सक्तानाम् of those who are attached to worldly pleasures, कर्म-योगः the path of action (Karma Yoga), त्वयि in You, हि indeed, विनिहितः dedicated/offered, ये who, तु but, न अत्यन्त-सक्ताः are neither exceedingly attached, न अपि nor also, अत्यन्तम् exceedingly, विरक्ताः dispassionate, त्वयि in You, च and, धृत-रसाः possessing a deep taste/longing, भक्ति-योगः the path of devotion (Bhakti Yoga), हि indeed, अमीषाम् for them

Simple Meaning: The three paths of knowledge, action, and devotion are all means of attaining You in this world. Among them, those who have developed complete detachment from all worldly objects are qualified for the path of knowledge. Those who remain attached to material desires are eligible for the path of action, dedicated and offered unto You. But for those who are neither deeply attached to sensory pleasures nor completely dispassionate, yet possess an earnest taste and love for You, the path of devotion is indeed the rightful way!

ज्ञानं त्वद्भक्ततां वा लघु सुकृतवशान्मर्त्यलोके लभन्ते
तस्मात्तत्रैव जन्म स्पृहयति भगवन् नाकगो नारको वा ।
आविष्टं मां तु दैवाद्भवजलनिधिपोतायिते मर्त्यदेहे
त्वं कृत्वा कर्णधारं गुरुमनुगुणवातायितस्तारयेथाः ॥96.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: ज्ञानम् knowledge, त्वत्-भक्तताम् devotion unto You, वा or, लघु easily, सुकृत-वशात् by virtue of good deeds or merit, मर्त्य-लोके in the mortal world, लभन्ते they attain, तस्मात् therefore, तत्र एव in that mortal world alone, जन्म birth, स्पृहयति desires, भगवन् O Supreme Lord, नाकगः a dweller of heaven, वा or, नारकः a dweller of hell, आविष्टम् entered into/endowed with, माम् me, तु indeed, दैवात् by divine fortune, भव-जलनिधि-पोतायिते acting as a boat across the ocean of worldly existence, मर्त्य-देहे in this mortal human body, त्वम् You, कृत्वा making, कर्णधारम् the helmsman/captain, गुरुम् the spiritual master/guru, अनुगुण-वातायितः becoming the favorable wind, तारयेथाः may You cross me over

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Lord! People easily attain divine knowledge or devotion unto You in the mortal human world by virtue of past good merit. Therefore, even dwellers of heaven and hell desire to be born in this human realm alone. By divine fortune, I have been blessed with this human body, which serves as a sturdy boat to cross the vast ocean of worldly existence. May You, making the spiritual guru the helmsman and Yourself becoming the favorable wind, carry me safely across this ocean!

अव्यक्तं मार्गयन्तः श्रुतिभिरपि नयैः केवलज्ञानलुब्धाः
क्लिश्यन्तेऽतीव सिद्धिं बहुतरजनुषामन्त एवाप्नुवन्ति ।
दूरस्थः कर्मयोगोऽपि च परमफले नन्वयं भक्तियोगः
त्वामूलादेव हृद्यस्त्वरितमयि भवत्प्रापको वर्धतां मे ॥96.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: अव्यक्तम् the unmanifested supreme reality, मार्गयन्तः seeking or searching for, श्रुतिभिः through scriptural texts, अपि also, नयैः through logical reasoning and arguments, केवल-ज्ञान-लुब्धाः those who are greedy/desirous for abstract knowledge alone, क्लिश्यन्ते undergo great hardship or suffer, अतीव exceedingly, सिद्धिम् liberation/perfection, बहुतर-जनुषाम् of very many births, अन्ते at the end, एव alone, आप्नुवन्ति they attain, दूरस्थः far away or distant, कर्म-योगः the path of action, अपि also, च and, परम-फले in yielding the ultimate fruit of liberation, ननु indeed, अयम् this, भक्ति-योगः path of devotion, त्वा-मूलात् एव right from its very beginning, हृद्यः sweet and pleasing to the heart, त्वरितम् quickly, अयि O Lord, भवत्-प्रापकः bestowing the attainment of You, वर्धताम् may it grow, मे in me

Simple Meaning: Those who seek the unmanifested Supreme Being purely through scriptural study and intellectual logic, desiring abstract knowledge alone, undergo immense suffering and hardship. Even then, they attain liberation only at the end of countless births! The path of selfless action is also far removed from yielding the ultimate fruit of divine realization. But this path of devotion is sweet and enchanting right from its very beginning, and it leads quickly to the attainment of You. O Lord, may this path of devotion grow continuously within me!

ज्ञानायैवातियत्नं मुनिरपवदते ब्रह्मतत्त्वं तु शृण्वन्
गाढं त्वत्पादभक्तिं शरणमयति यस्तस्य मुक्तिः कराग्रे ।
त्वद्ध्यानेऽपीह तुल्या पुनरसुकरता चित्तचाञ्चल्यहेतोः
अभ्यासादाशु शक्यं तदपि वशयितुं त्वत्कृपाचारुताभ्याम् ॥96.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: ज्ञानाय एव अति-यत्नम् excess effort for knowledge alone, मुनिः the sage, अपवदते criticizes/deprecates, ब्रह्म-तत्त्वम् the ultimate truth of Brahman, तु but, शृण्वन् listening to, गाढम् firm or deep, त्वत्-पाद-भक्तिम् devotion to Your feet, शरणम् अयति takes refuge in, यः who, तस्य his, मुक्तिः liberation, कर-अग्रे at the tip of the hand or within easy reach, त्वत्-ध्याने in meditating upon You, अपि also, इह here, तुल्या equal, पुनः but, असुकरता difficulty, चित्त-चाञ्चल्य-हेतोः due to the restlessness of the mind, अभ्यासात् through practice, आशु quickly, शक्यम् possible, तत् अपि that also, वशयितुम् to control, त्वत्-कृपा-चारुताभ्याम् through Your grace and Your enchanting beauty

Simple Meaning: The sage criticizes the excessive effort expended purely for gaining abstract knowledge. But for one who, while listening to the ultimate truth of Brahman, takes firm refuge in deep devotion at Your feet, liberation is right at hand. It is true that meditating upon You also presents a similar difficulty due to the natural restlessness of the mind. However, through continuous practice, along with Your grace and the captivating charm of Your beautiful form, it is possible to control the mind very quickly.

निर्विण्णः कर्ममार्गे खलु विषमतमे त्वत्कथादौ च गाढं
जातश्रद्धोऽपि कामानयि भुवनपते नैव शक्नोमि हातुम् ।
तद्भूयो निश्चयेन त्वयि निहितमना दोषबुद्ध्या भजंस्तान्
पुष्णीयां भक्तिमेव त्वयि हृदयगते मङ्क्षु नङ्क्ष्यन्ति सङ्गाः ॥96.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: निर्विण्णः disgusted/indifferent, कर्म-मार्गे in the path of action, खलु indeed, विषमतमे which is extremely difficult/treacherous, त्वत्-कथा-आदौ in Your stories and legends, च and, गाढम् deep/firm, जात-श्रद्धः one in whom faith has arisen, अपि even though, कामान् desires for material pleasures, अयि O Lord, भुवन-पते O Lord of the universe, न एव not at all, शक्नोमि I am able, हातुम् to give up/abandon, तत् therefore, भूयः repeatedly, निश्चयेन with firm resolve, त्वयि in You, निहित-मनाः with mind fixed, दोष-बुद्ध्या seeing their defects/evils, भजन् using/experiencing, तान् them, पुष्णीयाम् I shall nourish, भक्तिम् devotion, एव alone, त्वयि in You, हृदय-गते who reside in my heart, मङ्क्षु quickly, नङ्क्ष्यन्ति will perish, सङ्गाः attachments

Simple Meaning: Having realized that the path of worldly actions is extremely difficult and troublesome, one develops deep faith in listening to Your divine stories. Yet, O Lord of the universe, I am unable to completely abandon my worldly desires. Therefore, with a firm mind, even while experiencing those worldly objects, I will view them as filled with defects and keep my mind fixed on You. In this way, I shall nourish my devotion towards You who reside in my heart, and all my material attachments will quickly perish.

कश्चित् क्लेशार्जितार्थक्षयविमलमतिर्नुद्यमानो जनौघैः
प्रागेवं प्राह विप्रो न खलु मम जनः कालकर्मग्रहा वा।
चेतो मे दुःखहेतुस्तदिह गुणगणं भावयत्सर्वकारी-
त्युक्त्वा शान्तो गतस्त्वां मम च कुरु विभो तादृशी चित्तशान्तिम् ॥96.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: कश्चित् a certain, क्लेश-अर्जित-अर्थ-क्षय-विमल-मतिः whose mind became purified by the loss of wealth earned through great hardship, नुद्यमानः being insulted or tormented, जन-ओघैः by crowds of people, प्राक् in the past, एवम् in this manner, प्राह said, विप्रः a brahmana, न not, खलु indeed, मम my, जनः people, काल-कर्म-ग्रहाः वा or time or past action or planets, चेतः the mind, मे my, दुःख-हेतुः cause of sorrow, तत् therefore, इह in this world, गुण-गणम् the group of gunas or modes of nature, भावयत् inducing, सर्व-कारी creator of everything, इति-उक्त्वा having said thus, शान्तः remaining peaceful, गतः त्वाम् attained You, मम for me, च and, कुरु grant or bestow, विभो O All-pervading Lord, तादृशीम् such, चित्त-शान्तिम् peace of mind

Simple Meaning: In the past, a certain brahmana whose mind had become purified after losing all his hard-earned wealth was severely harassed and mocked by crowds of people. He declared that neither people, nor time, nor karma, nor planets were the true cause of his suffering. Rather, his own mind, which projects the interaction of the gunas and drives all actions, was the root cause of his sorrow. Saying this, he attained complete inner peace and ultimately reached You. O All-pervading Lord, please grant me that same profound peace of mind!

ऐलः प्रागुर्वशीं प्रत्यतिविवशमनाः सेवमानश्चिरं तां
गाढं निर्विद्य भूयो युवतिसुखमिदं क्षुद्रमेवेति गायन् ।
त्वद्भक्तिं प्राप्य पूर्णः सुखतरमचरत्तद्वदुद्धूतसङ्गं
भक्तोत्तंसं क्रिया मां पवनपुरपते हन्त मे रुन्धि रोगान् ॥96.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऐलः son of Ilaa - Pururavas, प्राक् earlier, उर्वशीम् towards Urvashi, प्रति to, अति-विवश-मनाः with a mind completely overwhelmed by passion, सेवमानः serving or enjoying her company, चिरम् for a long time, ताम् her, गाढम् deeply, निर्विद्य becoming disgusted or detached, भूयः later, युवति-सुखम्-इदम् this pleasure derived from women, क्षुद्रम्-एव is trivial indeed, इति thus, गायन् singing or declaring, त्वत्-भक्तिम् devotion to You, प्राप्य having attained, पूर्णः completely fulfilled, सुखतरम् most happily, अचरत् moved about or lived, तत्-वत् like him, उद्धूत-सङ्गम् free from all attachments, भक्त-उत्तंसम् a jewel among devotees, क्रियाः make, माम् me, पवन-पुर-पते O Lord of Guruvayur, हन्त alas, मे my, रुन्धि destroy or remove, रोगान् afflictions or diseases

Simple Meaning: In the past, King Aila - Pururavas (son of Ilaa) was intensely attached to Urvashi and spent a long time enjoying her company with his mind totally overwhelmed by desire. Eventually, he developed deep detachment and proclaimed that the pleasure derived from earthly women is utterly trivial and fleeting. Turning to You, he attained supreme devotion, became fully fulfilled, and lived in perfect peace. O Lord of Guruvayur, please make me like him—a jewel among devotees, completely free from all material attachments! Alas, please destroy all my afflictions and diseases!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
