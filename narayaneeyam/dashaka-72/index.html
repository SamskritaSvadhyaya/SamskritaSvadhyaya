Akrura visits Vraja

दशकम् ७२

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 72

Akrura visits Vraja

कंसोऽथ नारदगिरा व्रजवासिनं त्वामाकर्ण्य दीर्णहृदयः स हि गान्दिनेयम् ।
आहूय कार्मुकमखच्छलतो भवन्तमानेतुमेनमहिनोदहिनाथशायिन् ॥72.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: कंसः Kamsa, अथ then, नारद-गिरा by the words of Sage Narada, व्रज-वासिनम् residing in Vraja, त्वाम् You, आकर्ण्य learning or hearing, दीर्ण-हृदयः with a terrified heart, सः he, हि indeed, गान्दिनेयम् the son of Gandini (Akrura’s mother) - Akrura, आहूय summoning, कार्मुक-मख-छलतः under the pretext of a bow sacrifice, भवन्तम् You, आनेतुम् to bring, एनम् him (Akrura), अहिनोत् sent forth, अहि-नाथ-शायिन् O Lord who reclines on the serpent king Shesha

Simple Meaning: O Lord who reclines serenely upon the serpent king Shesha! Upon hearing the words of Sage Narada confirming that You were alive and living in Vraja, Kamsa's heart was struck with terror. Desperate to destroy You, he summoned Akrura—the son of Gandini—and dispatched him to Vraja under the deceptive pretext of inviting You to a grand bow sacrifice in Mathura.

अक्रूर एष भवदंघ्रिपरश्चिराय त्वद्दर्शनाक्षममनाः क्षितिपालभीत्या ।
तस्याज्ञयैव पुनरीक्षितुमुद्यतस्त्वामानन्दभारमतिभूरितरं बभार ॥72.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: अक्रूरः Akrura, एषः this, भवत्-अंघ्रि-परः devoted to Your lotus feet, चिराय for a very long time, त्वत्-दर्शन-अक्षम-मनाः disappointed in mind because of being unable to see You, क्षितिपाल-भीत्या out of fear of the king Kamsa, तस्य आज्ञया एव by his order itself, पुनः again, ईक्षितुम् to see You, उद्यतः setting forth, त्वाम् You, आनन्द-भारम् an immense bliss, अति-भूरितरम् exceeding all measure, बभार experienced/carried

Simple Meaning: Akrura had long been intensely devoted to Your lotus feet, yet out of fear of the tyrannical king Kamsa, he had been disappointed that he was unable to visit You in Vraja. Now, dispatched by the king’s order itself to bring You to Mathura, he set forth with boundless joy, his heart overflowing with an immeasurable weight of divine ecstasy at the prospect of finally seeing You face-to-face.

सोऽयं रथेन सुकृती भवतो निवासं गच्छन् मनोरथगणांस्त्वयि धार्यमाणान् ।
आस्वादयन् मुहुरपायभयेन दैवं सम्प्रार्थयन् पथि न किञ्चिदपि व्यजानात् ॥72.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः अयम् that very person (Akrura), रथेन by his chariot, सुकृती the virtuous/fortunate one, भवतः Your, निवासम् abode/residence (in Vraja), गच्छन् going/traveling toward, मनोरथ-गणान् a multitude of devotional desires and thoughts, त्वयि upon You, धार्यमाणान् centered/focused, आस्वादयन् relishly experiencing, मुहुः repeatedly, अपाय-भयेन out of fear of any potential obstacle/interruption, दैवम् Providence/the Almighty, सम्प्रार्थयन् praying intensely to, पथि on the way, न किञ्चित् अपि nothing whatsoever, व्यजानात् was aware of

Simple Meaning: As that blessed and noble soul Akrura traveled by chariot toward Your residence in Vraja, his mind was utterly absorbed in a stream of sublime devotional thoughts about You. Relishing every imagined moment of meeting You, and constantly praying to Providence that no unforeseen obstacle might disrupt his journey, he became so overwhelmed with divine ecstasy that he lost all awareness of the road around him.

द्रक्ष्यामि वेदशतगीतगतिं पुमांसं स्प्रक्ष्यामि किंस्विदपि नाम परिष्वजेयम् ।
किं वक्ष्यते स खलु मां क्वनु वीक्षितः स्यादित्थं निनाय स भवन्मयमेव मार्गम् ॥72.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्रक्ष्यामि I shall behold, वेद-शत-गीत-गतिम् the Supreme Goal praised by hundreds of Vedic hymns, पुमांसम् the Supreme Being, स्प्रक्ष्यामि shall I touch, किम् स्वित् अपि नाम can it really be that, परिष्वजेयम् I may even embrace Him, किम् what, वक्ष्यते will say, सः He, खलु indeed, माम् to me, क्व नु where indeed, वीक्षितः स्यात् will He be seen, इत्थम् in this manner, निनाय spent or passed, सः he (Akrura), भवत्-मयम् एव completely absorbed in thoughts of You, मार्गम् the journey

Simple Meaning: "Will I truly behold the Supreme Being praised by hundreds of Vedic hymns? Will I be blessed to touch Him? Could it even be possible that He will embrace me? What words will He speak to me, and where in Vraja will I first catch sight of Him?" Immersed in these ecstatic reflections, Akrura spent his entire journey utterly absorbed in Your divine presence.

भूयः क्रमादभिविशन् भवदंघ्रिपूतं वृन्दावनं हरविरिञ्चसुराभिवन्द्यम् ।
आनन्दमग्न इव लग्न इव प्रमोहे किं किं दशान्तरमवाप न पङ्कजाक्ष ॥72.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूयः then, क्रमात् gradually, अभिविशन् entering, भवत्-अंघ्रि-पूतम् purified by Your lotus feet, वृन्दावनम् Vrindavan, हर-विरिञ्च-सुर-अभिवन्द्यम् worshiped by Shiva, Brahma, and all the devas, आनन्द-मग्नः इव as if drowned in bliss, लग्नः इव प्रमोहे as if lost in total fascination/ecstasy, किम् किम् what varied and marvelous, दशान्तरम् states of spiritual emotion, अवाप न did he not experience, पङ्कजाक्ष O Lotus-eyed Lord

Simple Meaning: O Lotus-eyed Lord, as Akrura gradually entered Vrindavan—the holy land sanctified by the touch of Your feet and revered even by Shiva, Brahma, and the devas—he was utterly overwhelmed. Drowning in an ocean of supreme bliss and spellbound by pure devotion, he experienced countless sublime and ecstatic states of transcendental emotion.

पश्यन्नवन्दत भवद्विहृतिस्थलानि पांसुष्ववेष्टत भवच्चरणाङ्कितेषु ।
किं ब्रूमहे बहुजना हि तदापि जाता एवं तु भक्तितरला विरलाः परात्मन् ॥72.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: पश्यन् beholding, अवन्दत he prostrated, भवत्-विहृति-स्थलानि the places of Your divine pastimes, पांसुषु in the dust, अवेष्टत he rolled around, भवत्-चरण-अङ्कितेषु imprinted with Your lotus feet, किम् what, ब्रूमहे shall we say, बहु-जनाः many people, हि indeed, तदा अपि even at that time, जाताः existed, एवम् like this, तु but, भक्ति-तरलाः trembling and overflowing with devotion, विरलाः rare, परात्मन् O Supreme Soul

Simple Meaning: Beholding the sacred sites where You performed Your divine pastimes, Akrura prostrated himself again and again. Upon seeing the dust imprinted with Your holy footmarks, he rolled in it with intense ecstatic love. What more can we say, O Supreme Soul? Even in those ancient times, though countless people lived upon the earth, devotees like Akrura—whose entire beings tremble and overflow with such ecstatic devotion—were rare indeed.

सायं स गोपभवनानि भवच्चरित्रगीतामृतप्रसृतकर्णरसायनानि ।
पश्यन् प्रमोदसरितेव किलोह्यमानो गच्छन् भवद्भवनसन्निधिमन्वयासीत् ॥72.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: सायम् in the evening, सः he Akrura, गोप-भवनानि the houses of the cowherds, भवत्-चरित्र-गीत-अमृत-प्रसृत-कर्ण-रसायनानि echoing with the nectar of songs glorifying Your divine pastimes which were elixir to the ears, पश्यन् beholding, प्रमोद-सरिता इव as if by a river of supreme joy, किल indeed, उह्यमानः being carried along, गच्छन् moving forward, भवत्-भवन-सन्निधिम् the vicinity of Your abode, अन्वयासीत् reached

Simple Meaning: Arriving in the evening, Akrura passed by the homes of the cowherds, which were echoing with sweeter-than-nectar songs celebrating Your glorious pastimes—a true elixir for the ears. Carried along as if swept by a flowing river of intense joy, he moved forward until he finally reached the vicinity of Your home.

तावद्ददर्श पशुदोहविलोकलोलं भक्तोत्तमागतिमिव प्रतिपालयन्तम् ।
भूमन् भवन्तमयमग्रजवन्तमन्तऋब्रह्मानुभूतिरससिन्धुमिवोद्वमन्तम् ॥72.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: तावत् just then, ददर्श he beheld, पशु-दोह-विलोक-लोलम् intently watching the milking of the cows, भक्त-उत्तम-आगतिम् इव as if awaiting the arrival of the greatest of devotees, प्रतिपालयन्तम् waiting expectantly, भूमन् O Infinite Supreme Lord, भवन्तम् You, अयम् this (Akrura), अग्रजवन्तम् accompanied by Your elder brother Balarama, अन्तः-ब्रह्म-अनुभूति-रस-सिन्धुम् इव as if pouring forth an inner ocean of the sweetness of Brahman-realization, उद्वमन्तम् radiating/effusing

Simple Meaning: Just then, O Infinite Lord, Akrura caught sight of You standing beside Your elder brother Balarama. You appeared to be intently watching the cows being milked, yet in truth You were expectantly awaiting the arrival of this noble devotee. As he gazed upon You, You seemed to be a personification of the an entire ocean of the sublime, blissful sweetness of Supreme Brahman-realization that Akrura was experiencing in his mind.

सायन्तनाप्लवविशेषविविक्तगात्रौ द्वौ पीतनीलरुचिराम्बरलोभनीयौ ।
नातिप्रपञ्चधृतभूषणचारुवेषौ मन्दस्मितार्द्रवदनौ स युवां ददर्श ॥72.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: सायन्तन-आप्लव-विशेष-विविक्त-गात्रौ both with bodies exceptionally cleansed and refreshed by an evening bath, द्वौ the two of You, पीत-नील-रुचिर-अम्बर-लोभनीयौ charming and captivating in radiant yellow and dark blue garments, न अति प्रपञ्च-धृत-भूषण-चारु-वेषौ beautifully adorned with modest yet exquisite ornaments, मन्द-स्मित-आर्द्र-वदनौ with faces softened by tender, gentle smiles, सः he (Akrura), युवाम् the two of You, ददर्श beheld

Simple Meaning: He beheld the two of You, refreshed and radiant after Your evening bath. Dressed in charming yellow and dark blue garments and beautifully adorned with simple, exquisite ornaments, Your radiant faces beamed with tender, gentle smiles as You welcomed His arrival.

दूराद्रथात्समवरुह्य नमन्तमेनमुत्थाप्य भक्तकुलमौलिमथोपगूहन् ।
हर्षान्मिताक्षरगिरा कुशलानुयोगी पाणिं प्रगृह्य सबलोऽथ गृहं निनेथ ॥72.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: दूरात् from a distance, रथात् from the chariot, समवरुह्य dismounting, नमन्तम् prostrating himself, एनम् him (Akrura), उत्थाप्य raising up, भक्त-कुल-मौलिम् the crown jewel among devotees, अथ then, उपगूहन् embracing, हर्षात् out of boundless joy, मित-अक्षर-गिरा with concise and sweet words, कुशल-अनुयोगी inquiring after his welfare, पाणिम् his hand, प्रगृह्य taking hold of, स-बलः along with Balarama, अथ then, गृहम् into the house, निनेथ You led him

Simple Meaning: Dismounting from his chariot while still at a distance, Akrura prostrated himself fully upon the ground. You immediately raised up this crown jewel among devotees and embraced him tightly to Your chest with deep joy. Inquiring warmly after his welfare in sweet, concise words, You and Balarama took him affectionately by the hand and led him into Your home.

नन्देन साकममितादरमर्चयित्वा तं यादवं तदुदितां निशमय्य वार्ताम् ।
गोपेषु भूपतिनिदेशकथां निवेद्य नानाकथाभिरिह तेन निशामनैषीः ॥72.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: नन्देन साकम् along with Nanda, अमित-आदरम् with boundless respect and hospitality, अर्चयित्वा having honored and worshipped, तम् यादवम् that Yadava (Akrura), तत्-उदिताम् reported by him, निशमय्य hearing, वार्ताम् the news, गोपेषु to the cowherds, भूपति-निदेश-कथाम् the news of King Kamsa's royal decree, निवेद्य announcing, नाना-कथाभिः with various conversations, इह here, तेन with him Akrura, निशाम् the night, अनैषीः You passed

Simple Meaning: Along with Nanda, You welcomed that Yadava (Akrura) with immense love and reverence, offering him rich hospitality. After hearing from him the real news and state of affairs in Mathura, You informed the cowherds about King Kamsa's command regarding the bow sacrifice. You then spent the night conversing about various matters with Akrura.

चन्द्रागृहे किमुत चन्द्रभगागृहे नु राधागृहे नु भवने किमु मैत्रविन्दे ।
धूर्तो विलम्बत इति प्रमदाभिरुच्चैराशङ्कितो निशि मरुत्पुरनाथ पायाः ॥72.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: चन्द्रा-गृहे in the house of Chandra, किमुत or perhaps, चन्द्रभगा-गृहे in the home of Chandrabhaga, नु indeed, राधा-गृहे in the abode of Radha, नु or else, भवने in the house, किमु whether, मैत्रविन्दे (in the house) of Mitravinda, धूर्तः the clever rogue, विलम्बते is delaying, इति thus, प्रमदाभिः by the beautiful damsels of Vraja, उच्चैः loudly and eagerly, आशङ्कितः conjectured/suspected, निशि at night, मरुत्पुर-नाथ O Lord of Guruvayur, पायाः protect me

Simple Meaning: During that night, as You were absorbed in conversation with Akrura, the beautiful maidens of Vraja began to speculate among themselves with annoyance: "Is that clever rogue delaying his return at Chandra's house, or is he at Candrabhaga's home? Could he be lingering at Radha's abode, or perhaps with Mitravinda?" O Lord of Guruvayur, protect me!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
