Gopis come to Krishna for rasa-lila

दशकम् ६५

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 65

Gopis come to Krishna for Rasa-Lila

गोपीजनाय कथितं नियमावसाने मारोत्सवं त्वमथ साधयितुं प्रवृत्तः ।
सान्द्रेण चान्द्रमहसा शिशिरीकृताशे प्रापूरयो मुरलिकां यमुनावनान्ते ॥65.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: गोपी-जनाय to the gopis, कथितम् promised, नियम-अवसाने at the conclusion of their vow, मार-उत्सवम् the festival of love or Rasa Leela, त्वम् You, अथ then, साधयितुम् to fulfill, प्रवृत्तः set out, सान्द्रेण by dense, चान्द्र-महसा moonbeams, शिशिरीकृत-आशे when all quarters were cooled, प्रापूरयः blew into, मुरलिकाम् flute, यमुना-वन-अन्ते in the woods along the banks of Yamuna

Simple Meaning: Then, intent on fulfilling the promise You had made to the gopis at the conclusion of their Katyayani vow to indulge in the grand festival of love, You played Your flute in the forest along the banks of the Yamuna, when all the directions were bathed and cooled by the dense radiance of the full moon.

सम्मूर्छनाभिरुदितस्वरमण्डलाभिः सम्मूर्छयन्तमखिलं भुवनान्तरालम् ।
त्वद्वेणुनादमुपकर्ण्य विभो तरुण्यः तत्तादृशं कमपि चित्तविमोहमापुः ॥65.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: सम्मूर्छनाभिः with rich melodic modulations, उदित-स्वर-मण्डलाभिः giving rise to the full cycle of musical notes, सम्मूर्छयन्तम् captivating/enchanting, अखिलम् the entire, भुवन-अन्तरालम् interior of the universe, त्वत्-वेणु-नादम् the divine sound of Your flute, उपकर्ण्य having heard, विभो O All-pervading Lord, तरुण्यः the young gopis, तत्-तादृशम् such an indescribable, कम् अपि an extraordinary, चित्त-विमोहम् state of mental bewitchment, आपुः attained

Simple Meaning: O All-pervading Lord, as the sound of Your flute resonated with rich musical modulations and the full harmony of notes, captivating the entire expanse of the universe, the young gopis heard it and instantly fell into an extraordinary, indescribable state of divine bewitchment.

ता गेहकृत्यनिरतास्तनयप्रसक्ताः कान्तोपसेवनपराश्च सरोरुहाक्ष्यः ।
सर्वं विसृज्य मुरलीरवमोहितास्ते कान्तारदेशमयि कान्ततनो समेताः ॥65.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताः those, गेह-कृत्य-निरताः who were deeply engaged in household chores, तनय-प्रसक्ताः who were devotedly attending to their children, कान्त-उपसेवन-पराः who were busily serving their husbands, च and, सरोरुहाक्ष्यः the lotus-eyed gopis, सर्वम् everything, विसृज्य leaving behind, मुरली-रव-मोहिताः captivated by the sound of the flute, ते to You, कान्तार-देशम् to the forest, अयि कान्त-तनो O Lord of enchanting form, समेताः reached

Simple Meaning: O Lord of most enchanting form, captivated by the divine notes of Your flute, those lotus-eyed gopis abandoned everything on the spot. Leaving behind their household chores, their children whom they were tending, and their husbands whom they were serving, they hurried into the deep forest to reach You.

काश्चिन्निजाङ्गपरिभूषणमादधाना वेणुप्रणादमुपकर्ण्य कृतार्धभूषाः ।
त्वामागता ननु तथैव विभूषिताभ्यः ता एव संरुरुचिरे तव लोचनाय ॥65.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: काश्चित् some gopis, निज-अङ्ग-परिभूषणम् ornamenting their own bodies, आदधानाः while in the process of putting on, वेणु-प्रणादम् the melodious sound of the flute, उपकर्ण्य hearing, कृत-अर्ध-भूषाः with their ornamentation only half-done, त्वाम् to You, आगताः came, ननु indeed, तथैव just as they were, विभूषिताभ्यः than those fully decorated, ताः they, एव alone, संरुरुचिरे appeared far more charming, तव Your, लोचनाय to the eyes

Simple Meaning: Some gopis were in the middle of decorating themselves when the sound of Your flute reached their ears. Stopping instantly, they ran to You with their adornments only half-complete. Indeed, in that half-adorned state, they appeared far more charming to Your eyes than those who had arrived fully decorated!

हारं नितम्बभुवि काचन धारयन्ती काञ्चीं च कण्ठभुवि देव समागता त्वाम् ।
हारित्वमात्मजघनस्य मुकुन्द तुभ्यं व्यक्तं बभाष इव मुग्धमुखी विशेषात् ॥65.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: हारम् necklace, नितम्ब-भुवि upon her hip region, काचन a certain gopi, धारयन्ती wearing, काञ्चीम् waist-band, च and, कण्ठ-भुवि around her neck region, देव O Lord, समागता came, त्वाम् to You, हारित्वम् the state of being attractive, आत्म-जघनस्य of her own hips, मुकुन्द O Mukunda, तुभ्यम् to You, व्यक्तम् clearly, बभाषे spoke, इव as if, मुग्ध-मुखी that innocent-faced gopi, विशेषात् especially

Simple Meaning: O Lord, in her utter confusion and haste upon hearing Your flute, one innocent-faced gopi arrived before You wearing her necklace around her hips and her girdle around her neck! O Mukunda, by putting her necklace (hara) around her waist, she seemed to be silently declaring to You the supreme, captivating beauty (haritva) of her hips.

काचित् कुचे पुनरसज्जितकञ्चुलीका व्यामोहतः परवधूभिरलक्ष्यमाणा ।
त्वामाययौ निरुपमप्रणयातिभारराज्याभिषेकविधये कलशीधरेव ॥65.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: काचित् a certain gopi, कुचे on her breasts, पुनः moreover, असज्जित-कञ्चुलीका with her bodice unfastened/improperly worn, व्यामोहतः due to intense distraction/bewitchment, पर-वधूभिः by the other women, अलक्ष्यमाणा unnoticed, त्वाम् to You, आययौ came, निरुपम-प्रणय-अतिभार-राज्य-अभिषेक-विधये carrying the weight of intense love for performing Your coronation ceremony, कलशी-धरा carrying ceremonial water jars, इव as if

Simple Meaning: Another gopi, in her state of intense divine absorption, rushed to You with her bodice unfastened and hanging loosely upon her chest. Unnoticed by the other women who were equally distracted, she appeared as though she were a maiden carrying two auspicious ceremonial water jars (kalasha) for Your coronation ceremony in the kingdom of Love!

काश्चित् गृहात् किल निरेतुमपारयन्त्यः त्वामेव देव हृदये सुदृढं विभाव्य ।
देहं विधूय परचित्सुखरूपमेकं त्वामाविशन् परमिमा ननु धन्यधन्याः ॥65.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: काश्चित् some gopis, गृहात् from their homes, किल indeed, निरेतुम् to go out, अपारयन्त्यः being unable, त्वाम् You, एव alone, देव O Lord, हृदये in their hearts, सुदृढम् deeply and intensely, विभाव्य meditating upon, देहम् physical body, विधूय shedding, पर-चित्-सुख-रूपम् of the nature of pure consciousness and supreme bliss, एकम् one and non-dual, त्वाम् You, आविशन् entered, परमम् supreme, इमाः these, ननु truly, धन्य-धन्याः the most blessed of all

Simple Meaning: O Lord, some gopis were prevented by their family members from leaving their homes. Meditating intensely upon You within their hearts with single-pointed devotion, they shed their mortal physical bodies on the spot and merged directly into You, who are of the nature of pure consciousness and supreme bliss. Truly, these gopis were the most blessed of all!

जारात्मना न परमात्मतया स्मरन्त्यो नार्यो गताः परमहंसगतिं क्षणेन ।
तं त्वां प्रकाशपरमात्मतनुं कथञ्चिच्चित्ते वहन्नमृतमश्रममश्नुवीय ॥65.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: जार-आत्मना as a paramour, न not, पर-आत्मतया as the Supreme Soul, स्मरन्त्यः thinking of, नार्यः those women, गताः attained, परमहंस-गतिम् the ultimate goal of the liberated sages, क्षणेन in a mere moment, तम् that, त्वाम् You, प्रकाश-परम-आत्म-तनुम् whose form is the self-luminous Supreme Self, कथञ्चित् somehow, चित्ते in the mind, वहन् bearing, अमृतम् immortality, अश्रमम् effortlessly, अश्नुवीय may I attain

Simple Meaning: Even though those women thought of You merely as their paramour and not as the Supreme Being, they attained in a single moment that supreme state of liberation reachable only by exalted sages (paramahamsas). Bearing You, whose divine form is the self-luminous Supreme Self, somehow within my mind, may I effortlessly attain the state of immortality!

अभ्यागताभिरभितो व्रजसुन्दरीभिः मुग्धस्मितार्द्रवदनः करुणावलोकी ।
निस्सीमकान्तिजलधिस्त्वमवेक्ष्यमाणो विश्वैकहृद्य हर मे पवनेश रोगान् ॥65.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: अभ्यागताभिः by those who had arrived, अभितः all around, व्रज-सुन्दरीभिः by the beautiful maidens of Vraja, मुग्ध-स्मित-आर्द्र-वदनः with a face softened by a sweet charming smile, करुणा-अवलोकी looking upon them with compassion, निस्सीम-कान्ति-जलधिः an boundless ocean of infinite beauty, त्वम् You, अवेक्ष्यमाणः being gazed upon, विश्व-एक-हृद्य O sole captivator of the universe, हर dispel, मे my, पवनेश O Lord of Guruvayur, रोगान् diseases and afflictions

Simple Meaning: Surrounded on all sides by the beautiful maidens of Vraja who had arrived, You stood as a boundless ocean of infinite beauty, gazing upon them with compassion and with a face rendered tender by a sweet, charming smile. O sole captivator of the entire universe, O Lord of Guruvayur who was thus gazed upon by them, please dispel all my diseases and afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
