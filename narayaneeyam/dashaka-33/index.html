॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 33

The story of Ambarisha

वैवस्वताख्यमनुपुत्रनभागजातनाभागनामकनरेन्द्रसुतोऽम्बरीषः ।
सप्तार्णवावृतमहीदयितोऽपि रेमे त्वत्सङ्गिषु त्वयि च मग्नमनास्सदैव ॥33.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: वैवस्वत-आख्य-मनु-पुत्र-नभाग-जात-नाभाग-नामक-नरेन्द्र-सुतः the son of the king named Naabhaaga who was born of Nabhaga, the son of Manu named Vaivasvata, अम्बरीषः Ambarisha, सप्त-अर्णव-आवृत-मही-दयितः अपि even though he was the beloved ruler of the entire earth surrounded by the seven oceans, रेमे delighted/found joy, त्वत्-सङ्गिषु in Your devotees, त्वयि च and in You, मग्न-मनाः with his mind completely absorbed, सदैव always

Vaivasvata -> Nabhaaga -> Naabhaaga -> Ambarisha

Simple Meaning: Ambarisha was the noble son of King Naabhaaga who was born to Nabhaaga, the son of Manu named Vaivasvata. Even though he was the supreme ruler of the entire earth bounded by the seven oceans, he possessed no attachment to the worldly kingdom. Instead, his mind was eternally submerged in deep devotion unto You and in the loving company of Your devotees.

त्वत्प्रीतये सकलमेव वितन्वतोऽस्य भक्त्यैव देव नचिरादभृथाः प्रसादम् ।
येनास्य याचनमृतेऽप्यभिरक्षणार्थं चक्रं भवान् प्रविततार सहस्रधारम् ॥33.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-प्रीतये for Your pleasure/delight, सकलम् एव every single action/everything, वितन्वतः who performed/conducted, अस्य of him (King Ambarisha), भक्त्या एव purely through his unswerving devotion, देव O Lord!, न चिरात् very quickly/without delay, अभृथाः You showered/extended, प्रसादम् Your divine grace, येन by which grace, अस्य his, याचनम् ऋते अपि even without him asking/begging for it, अभिरक्षणार्थम् for his complete protection, चक्रम् the divine Sudarshana Chakra, भवान् You, प्रविततार deployed/stationed, सहस्रधारम् possessing a thousand sharp edges/rays

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Lord! Because King Ambarisha performed every single action in his life solely for Your delight, You were so pleased with his pure devotion that You swiftly bestowed Your grace upon him. Even without him ever asking for it, You stationed Your invincible, thousand-spoked Sudarshana Chakra at his side for his perpetual protection.

स द्वादशीव्रतमथो भवदर्चनार्थं वर्षं दधौ मधुवने यमुनोपकण्ठे ।
पत्न्या समं सुमनसा महतीं वितन्वन् पूजां द्विजेषु विसृजन् पशुषष्टिकोटिम् ॥33.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (King Ambarisha), द्वादशी-व्रतम् the sacred Dwadashi Vrata (fasting vow on the eleventh day of the lunar fortnight and then breaking the fast on the twelfth day), अथ then, भवत्-अर्चनार्थम् for the worship and pleasure of You, वर्षम् for the duration of a year, दधौ observed/undertook, मधुवने in the holy forest of Madhu (Madhuvana), यमुना-उपकण्ठे on the banks of the river Yamuna, पत्न्या समम् along with his pious wife, सुमनसा with a noble/pure mind, महतीम् a grand, magnificent, वितन्वन् performing, पूजाम् worship, द्विजेषु unto holy Brahmins, विसृजन् giving away/donating, पशु-षष्टि-कोटिम् sixty crores of decorated cows (along with gold and fine attire)

Simple Meaning: To worship You and seek Your supreme grace, King Ambarisha, accompanied by his like-minded wife, observed the holy Dwadashi Vrata for a full year in Madhu-vana on the serene banks of the Yamuna. With pure devotion and a generous heart, he performed grand worship and donated sixty crore cows—richly adorned with gold-trimmed horns and fine garments—unto noble Brahmins.

तत्राथ पारणदिने भवदर्चनान्ते दुर्वाससाऽस्य मुनिना भवनं प्रपेदे ।
भोक्तुं वृतश्चस नृपेण परार्तिशीलो मन्दं जगाम यमुनां नियमान्विधास्यन् ॥33.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there (at Madhuvana), अथ then, पारण-दिने on the day of breaking the fast (Parana day), भवत्-अर्चनान्ते at the conclusion of Your worship, दुर्वाससा मुनिना by the sage Durvasa, अस्य his (King Ambarisha's), भवनम् palace/abode, प्रपेदे was reached/visited, भोक्तुम् to partake of the meal, वृतः च and being invited, सः he (Durvasa), नृपेण by the king, पर-आर्ति-शीलः by nature prone to causing distress to others, मन्दम् slowly/leisurely, जगाम went, यमुनाम् to the Yamuna river, नियमान्-विधास्यन् to perform his daily rituals/ablutions

Simple Meaning: Then, on the day of breaking the fast (Dvadashi), just as King Ambarisha concluded his elaborate worship of You, the hot-tempered sage Durvasa arrived at his palace. The king respectfully invited him to accept a meal. Durvasa—who was naturally prone to causing distress to others—accepted the invitation, but proceeded very leisurely to the river Yamuna to perform his midday ablutions, intentionally delaying his return.

राज्ञाऽथ पारणमुहूर्तसमाप्तिखेदात् वारैव पारणमकारि भवत्परेण ।
प्राप्तो मुनिस्तदथ दिव्यदृशा विजानन् क्षिप्यन् क्रुधोद्धृतजटो विततान कृत्याम् ॥33.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: राज्ञा by the king (Ambarisha), अथ then, पारण-मुहूर्त-समाप्ति-खेदात् out of distress over the impending expiration of the auspicious time for breaking the fast (Parana-muhurta), वारा एव with water only, पारणम् breaking the fast, अकारि was done, भवत्-परेण who was exclusively devoted to You, प्राप्तः arriving back, मुनिः the sage (Durvasa), तत्-अथ then that (act of sipping water), दिव्य-दृशा through his yogic vision, विजानन् knowing/realizing, क्षिप्यन् boiling with rage/rebuking, क्रुधा out of anger, उद्धृत-जटः pulling out a strand of his matted hair, विततान created/set loose, कृत्याम् a terrifying female spirit Kritya

Simple Meaning: As the auspicious moment for breaking the fast (Dvadashi Parana) was rapidly coming to an end with Durvasa still absent, the King—wholly devoted to You—was in great distress. He broke his fast by sipping a drop of water, which satisfied the vow without taking a full meal. When Sage Durvasa returned and discovered this through his yogic foresight, he flew into a raging fury. Rebuking the king, he plucked a strand of his matted hair and conjured a fierce, fiery spirit Kritya.

कृत्यां च तामसिधरां भुवनं दहन्तीम् अग्रेऽभिवीक्ष्य नृपतिर्न पदाच्चकम्पे ।
त्वद्भक्तबाधमभिवीक्ष्य सुदर्शनं ते कृत्यानलं शलभयन् मुनिमन्वधावीत् ॥33.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृत्याम् Kritya, च and, ताम् her, असि-धराम् brandishing a sword, भुवनम् the world/surrounding area, दहन्तीम् burning, अग्रे in front of him, अभिवीक्ष्य seeing, नृपतिः the king (Ambarisha), न not, पदात् from his spot/position, चकम्पे flinched/wavered, त्वत्-भक्त-बाधम् distress/danger to Your devotee, अभिवीक्ष्य seeing, सुदर्शनम् the Sudarshana Chakra, ते Your, कृत्या-अनलम् the fire of that spirit Kritya, शलभयन् turning into a moth (extinguishing her easily like a insect in flame), मुनिम् after the sage (Durvasa), अन्वधावीत् pursued/chased after

Simple Meaning: Even when that terrifying spirit Kritya emerged brandishing a sword and scorching everything in her path, King Ambarisha remained completely undisturbed, not flinching a single step. Seeing Your pure devotee in danger, Your divine Sudarshana Chakra instantly blazed forth, consuming the fierce spirit like a moth in a fire, and began chasing after the terrified sage Durvasa.

धावन्नशेषभुवनेषु भिया स पश्यन् विश्वत्र चक्रमपि ते गतवान् विरिञ्चम् ।
कः कालचक्रमतिलङ्घयतीत्यपास्तः शर्वं ययौ स च भवन्तमवन्दतैव ॥33.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: धावन् running, अशेष-भुवनेषु across all the worlds/realms, भिया out of sheer terror, सः he (Sage Durvasa), पश्यन् seeing, विश्वत्र everywhere, चक्रम् the divine Sudarshana Chakra, अपि even, ते Your, गतवान् went to/approached, विरिञ्चम् Lord Brahma (Virinchi), कः who?, काल-चक्रम् the wheel of Time/the cosmic discus, अतिलङ्घयति can cross over/escape, इति saying thus, अपास्तः turned away/rejected, शर्वम् Lord Shiva, ययौ he approached, सः च and he (Shiva), भवन्तम् अवन्दत एव only bowed down to/deferred to You

Simple Meaning: Running in sheer terror through every corner of the universe, Sage Durvasa saw Your blazing Sudarshana Chakra relentlessly pursuing him wherever he went. Desperate, he fled to Lord Brahma for protection, but Brahma turned him away, saying: "Who in the world can escape the invincible wheel of Time?" He then rushed to Lord Shiva, but Shiva too expressed his helplessness, deferring entirely to Your supreme will.

भूयो भवन्निलयमेत्य मुनिं नमन्तं प्रोचे भवानहमृषे ननु भक्तदासः ।
ज्ञानं तपश्च विनयान्वितमेव मान्यं याह्यम्बरीषपदमेव भजेति भूमन् ॥33.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूयः then/thereafter, भवत्-निलयम् एत्य arriving at Your supreme abode (Vaikuntha), मुनिम् unto the sage (Durvasa), नमन्तम् who bowed down in absolute surrender, प्रोचे spoke, भवान् You, अहम् I, ऋषे O Sage!, ननु indeed, भक्त-दासः the servant of My devotees, ज्ञानम् knowledge, तपः austerity, च and, विनय-अन्वितम् एव when accompanied by humility alone, मान्यम् is respectable/worthy of praise, याहि go!, अम्बरीष-पदम् एव to the feet of King Ambarisha alone, भज seek refuge in/bow to, इति thus, भूमन् O Infinite Supreme Being!

Simple Meaning: O Infinite Supreme Lord! When Sage Durvasa finally reached Your lotus abode of Vaikuntha and fell prostrate before You in total surrender, You addressed him: "O Great Sage! Know that I am entirely dependent upon My devotees—I am a servant of My devotees! Knowledge and austerities are truly worthy of respect only when adorned with humility. Therefore, go directly to King Ambarisha, seek refuge at his feet, and ask for his forgiveness."

तावत् स​मेत्य मुनिना स गृहीतपादो राजाऽपसृत्य भवदस्त्रमसावनौषीत् ।
चक्रे गते मुनिरदादखिलाशिषोऽस्मै त्वद्भक्तिमागसि कृतेऽपि कृपां च शंसन् ॥33.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: तावत् thereupon/immediately, समेत्य coming back, मुनिना by the sage (Durvasa), सः he (King Ambarisha), गृहीत-पादः whose feet were held/clasped in submission, राजा the king, अपसृत्य stepping back/moving away in modesty, भवत्-अस्त्रम् Your divine weapon (the Sudarshana Chakra), असौ he (Ambarisha), अनौषीत् praised/offered devout prayers to, चक्रे गते when the discus withdrew/departed, मुनिः the sage Durvasa, अदात् bestowed, अखिल-आशिषः all blessings, अस्मै unto him (Ambarisha), त्वत्-भक्तिम् devotion unto You, आगसि कृते अपि even when an offense was committed, कृपाम् च and Your grace/mercy, शंसन् extolling/praising

Simple Meaning: Returning immediately, Sage Durvasa fell prostrate and clasped the feet of King Ambarisha. Embarrassed by the great sage's gesture, the humble king stepped back and fervently prayed to Your divine weapon, the Sudarshana Chakra, to pacify its wrath. Once the discus withdrew, Sage Durvasa showered all blessings upon Ambarisha, deeply extolling the grandeur of devotion unto You and the incredible divine mercy that pardons even a grave offense.

राजा प्रतीक्ष्य मुनिमेकसमामनाश्वान् सम्भोज्य साधु तमृषिं विसृजन् प्रसन्नम् ।
भुक्त्वा स्वयं त्वयि ततोऽपि दृढं रतोऽभूत् सायुज्यमाप च स मां पवनेश पायाः ॥33.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजा the King (Ambarisha), प्रतीक्ष्य waiting for, मुनिम् for the sage (Durvasa), एक-समाम् for an entire year, अनाश्वान् without partaking of any food (maintaining his fast), सम्भोज्य offering a sumptuous meal, साधु properly/with great reverence, तम् ऋषिम् that Sage Durvasa, विसृजन् permitting him to depart, प्रसन्नम् completely pleased and satisfied, भुक्त्वा स्वयम् taking his own meal thereafter, त्वयि in You, ततः अपि even more than before, दृढम् firmly/intensely, रतः अभूत् became deeply absorbed/devoted, सायुज्यम् समाप attained divine absorption/liberation, च and, सः he (King Ambarisha), माम् me, पवनेश O Lord of Guruvayur!, पायाः please protect!

Simple Meaning: King Ambarisha had refrained from eating any food for a full year while awaiting Sage Durvasa's return! Upon the sage's arrival, the noble king served him a sumptuously prepared feast with deep reverence. Having thus pleased and honorably sent off the great sage, King Ambarisha finally partook of his own meal. From that day forth, his heart became even more deeply immersed in pure devotion unto You, and he ultimately attained supreme, eternal union with You. O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect me!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
