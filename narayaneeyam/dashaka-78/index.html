Dashaka 78

Rukmini’s message to Krishna

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 78

Rukmini’s message to Krishna

त्रिदिववर्धकिवर्धितकौशलं त्रिदशदत्तसमस्तविभूतिमत् ।
जलधिमध्यगतं त्वमभूषयो नवपुरं वपुरञ्चितरोचिषा ॥78.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्रिदिव-वर्धकि-वर्धित-कौशलम् built with the heightened skill of Vishwakarma the architect of the devas, त्रिदश-दत्त-समस्त-विभूतिमत् endowed with all divine wealth and glory gifted by the devas, जलधि-मध्य-गतम् situated in the middle of the ocean, त्वम् You, अभूषयः adorned, नव-पुरम् the new city, वपुः-अञ्चित-रोचिषा with the divine luster of Your splendid body

Simple Meaning: Situated in the middle of the ocean, that new city was constructed with the supreme craftsmanship of Vishvakarma, the celestial architect, and enriched with all divine splendors gifted by the devas. You adorned that new city of Dvaraka with the radiant luster of Your own divine form.

ददुषि रेवतभूभृति रेवतीं हलभृते तनयां विधिशासनात् ।
महितमुत्सवघोषमपूपुषः समुदितैर्मुदितैः सह यादवैः ॥78.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: ददुषि when giving, रेवत-भूभृति King Revata, रेवतीम् Revati, हलभृते to Balarama, तनयाम् his daughter, विधि-शासनात् by the command of Brahma, महितम् grand, उत्सव-घोषम् celebratory festivities, अपूपुषः You caused to thrive, समुदितैः who had assembled together, मुदितैः overjoyed, सह with, यादवैः the Yadavas

Simple Meaning: By the command of Brahma, King Revata gave his daughter Revati in marriage to Balarama. You conducted grand celebratory festivities in the company of the overjoyed Yadavas who had assembled together.

अथ विदर्भसुतां खलु रुक्मिणीं प्रणयिनीं त्वयि देव सहोदरः ।
स्वयमदित्सत चेदिमहीभुजे स्वतमसा तमसाधुमुपाश्रयन् ॥78.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथ then, विदर्भ-सुताम् the daughter of the king of Vidarbha, खलु indeed, रुक्मिणीम् Rukmini, प्रणयिनीम् who was deeply in love, त्वयि with You, देव O Lord, सहोदरः her brother (Rukmi), स्वयम् himself, अदित्सत desired to give, चेदि-महीभुजे to the king of Chedi - Shishupala, स्व-तमसा due to his own ignorance, तम् that, असाधुम् wicked man, उपाश्रयन् favoring

Simple Meaning: Then, O Lord, Rukmini, the princess of Vidarbha, was deeply in love with You. However, her brother Rukmi, influenced by his own ignorance and favoring the wicked Shishupala, desired on his own to give her in marriage to that king of Chedi.

चिरधृतप्रणया त्वयि बालिका सपदि काङ्क्षितभङ्गसमाकुला ।
तव निवेदयितुं द्विजमादिशत् स्वकदनं कदनङ्गविनिर्मितं ॥78.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: चिर-धृत-प्रणया who had long harbored deep love, त्वयि in You, बालिका the young maiden Rukmini, सपदि suddenly, काङ्क्षित-भङ्ग-समाकुला distressed by the disruption of her cherished desire, तव to You, निवेदयितुम् to inform, द्विजम् a trusted brahmana, आदिशत् dispatched, स्व-कदनम् her distress, कदनङ्ग-विनिर्मितम् caused by the agonizing distress of love

Simple Meaning: Having long cherished deep love for You, the young maiden Rukmini was suddenly overwhelmed with distress upon learning that her desires were about to be shattered. To convey to You her intense mental anguish caused by the pain of love, she dispatched a trusted brahmana as her messenger.

द्विजसुतोऽपि च तूर्णमुपाययौ तव पुरं हि दुराशदुरासदम् ।
मुदमवाप च सादरपूजितः स भवता भवतापहृता स्वयम् ॥78.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्विज-सुतः अपि च the brahmana youth also, तूर्णम् quickly, उपाययौ arrived at, तव Your, पुरम् city, हि indeed, दुराश-दुरासदम् unreachable for those with evil desires, मुदम् अवाप च and attained immense joy, सादर-पूजितः respectfully honored, सः he, भवता by You, भव-ताप-हृता by the remover of the miseries of worldly existence, स्वयम् Himself

Simple Meaning: The brahmana youth quickly arrived at Your city of Dwaraka, which is inaccessible to those harboring wicked desires. There, he experienced immense joy upon being respectfully welcomed and honored by You Himself, the remover of all worldly miseries.

स च भवन्तमवोचत कुण्डिने नृपसुता खलु राजति रुक्मिणी ।
त्वयि समुत्सुकया निजधीरतारहितया हि तया प्रहितोऽस्म्यहम् ॥78.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, च and, भवन्तम् अवोचत said to You, कुण्डिने in the city of Kundina, नृप-सुता the princess, खलु indeed, राजति resides, रुक्मिणी Rukmini, त्वयि for You, समुत्सुकया who is filled with intense longing, निज-धीरता her usual composure, रहितया having lost, हि indeed, तया by her, प्रहितः अस्मि अहम् I have been sent

Simple Meaning: And he said to You: In the city of Kundina resides princess Rukmini. Overwhelmed with intense longing for You and having lost all her composure, she has sent me to You.

तव हृताऽस्मि पुरैव गुणैरहं हरति मां किल चेदिनृपोऽधुना ।
अयि कृपालय पालय मामिति प्रजगदे जगदेकपते तया ॥78.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: तव Your, हृता अस्मि I have been captivated, पुरा एव long ago indeed, गुणैः by Your noble qualities, अहम्  I, हरति is carrying away, माम् me, किल indeed, चेदि-नृपः अधुना the king of Chedi now, अयि O, कृपा-आलय abode of mercy, पालय protect, माम् इति me thus, प्रजगदे was conveyed, जगत्-एक-पते O sole Lord of the universe, तया by her

Simple Meaning: Long ago, my heart was completely captivated by Your divine qualities. But now, the king of Chedi is about to take me away. O Abode of Mercy, protect me! O sole Lord of the universe, this message was thus conveyed to You by her.

अशरणां यदि मां त्वमुपेक्षसे सपदि जीवितमेव जहाम्यहम् ।
इति गिरा सुतनोरतनोत् भृशं सुहृदयं हृदयं तव कातरम् ॥78.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: अशरणाम् helpless, यदि if, माम् me, त्वम् You, उपेक्षासे ignore, सपदि immediately, जीवितम् एव life itself, जहामि अहम् I shall give up, इति by these, गिरा words, सुतनोः of the beautifully formed Rukmini, अतनोत् made, भृशम् exceedingly, सु-हृदयम् good-hearted, हृदयम् heart, तव Your, कातरम् anxious

Simple Meaning: If You ignore me in my helpless state, I shall immediately end my life! With these words from the exquisitely beautiful Rukmini, the good heart of Yours was filled with anxiety.

अकथयस्त्वमथैनमये सखे तदधिका मम मन्मथवेदना ।
नृपसमक्षमुपेत्य हराम्यहं तदयि तां दयितामसितेक्षणाम् ॥78.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: अकथयः त्वम् अथ एनम् You then said to him, अये सखे O friend, तत्-अधिका greater than hers, मम My, मन्मथ-वेदना torment of love, नृप-समक्षम् in the presence of the kings, उपेत्य having arrived, हरामि अहम् I shall carry away, तत् therefore, अयि O, ताम् her, दयिताम् my beloved, असित-ईक्षणाम् dark-eyed maiden

Simple Meaning: You then said to the brahmana: O friend, My anguish born of love for her is even greater than hers! Therefore, I shall arrive in the presence of all those kings and carry away my dark-eyed beloved.

प्रमुदितेन च तेन समं तदा रथगतो लघु कुण्डिनमेयिवान् ।
गुरुमरुत्पुरनायक मे भवान् वितनुतां तनुतां निखिलापदाम् ॥78.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रमुदितेन with the overjoyed, च and, तेन with him (the brahmana), समम् together, तदा then, रथ-गतः seated on Your chariot, लघु swiftly, कुण्डिनम् ऐयिवान् reached the city of Kundina, गुरुमरुत्पुर-नायक O Lord of Guruvayur, मे my, भवान् You, वितनुताम् cause, तनुताम् reduction or complete destruction, निखिल-आपदाम् of all afflictions and miseries

Simple Meaning: Seated on Your chariot alongside the overjoyed brahmana, You swiftly reached the city of Kundina. O Lord of Guruvayur, may You bring about the complete end of all my miseries and afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
