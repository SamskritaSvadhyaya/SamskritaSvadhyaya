The story of Vrikasura and the test of Brigu

दशकम् ८९

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 89

The story of Vrikasura and the test of Brigu

रमाजाने जाने यदिह तव भक्तेषु विभवो
न सद्यस्सम्पद्यस्तदिह मदकृत्त्वादशमिनाम् ।
प्रशान्तिं कृत्वैव प्रदिशसि ततः काममखिलं
प्रशान्तेषु क्षिप्रं न खलु भवदीये च्युतिकथा ॥89.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: रमा-जाने O Husband of Lakshmi, जाने I understand, यत् इह that in this world, तव भक्तेषु among Your devotees, विभवः worldly wealth/opulence, न सद्यः सम्पद्यः does not come instantly/immediately, तत् इह that wealth in this world, मद-कृत्त्वात् because it creates arrogance/pride, अ-शमिनाम् in those who lack mind control/self-restraints, प्रशान्तिम् mind control/tranquillity, कृत्वा एव only after bestowing, प्रदिशसि You grant, ततः thereafter, कामम् अखिलम् all desired objects, प्रशान्तेषु in those whose minds are tranquil, क्षिप्रम् quickly, न खलु never indeed, भवदीये regarding Your devotees, च्युति कथा any story/possibility of downfall

Simple Meaning: O Husband of Goddess Lakshmi, I understand why worldly wealth and material opulence are not granted immediately to Your devotees! In people who lack self-restraint and mental control, wealth creates vanity and pride. Therefore, You first grant them mental tranquillity and inner peace, and only then bestow upon them all their desired wealth. Once peace of mind is attained, Your devotees never suffer a moral or spiritual downfall!

सद्यः प्रसादरुषितान् विधिशङ्करादीन्
केचिद्विभो निजगुणानुगुणं भजन्तः ।
भ्रष्टा भवन्ति बत कष्टमदीर्घदृष्ट्या
स्पष्टं वृकासुर उदाहरणं किलास्मिन् ॥89.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: सद्यः instantly, प्रसाद-रुषितान् pleased or angered, विधि-शङ्कर-आदीन् devas like Brahma and Shiva, केचित् some people, विभो O Lord, निज-गुण-अनुगुणम् according to their own inherent qualities, भजन्तः worshipping, भ्रष्टाः ruined/fallen, भवन्ति become, बत alas, कष्टम् how sad, अदीर्घ-दृष्ट्या due to short-sightedness, स्पष्टम् clear, वृकासुरः Vrikasura, उदाहरणम् example, किल indeed, अस्मिन् in this matter

Simple Meaning: O Lord, deities like Brahma and Shiva are easily pleased and easily angered. Shortsighted people worship them according to their own inherent qualities (gunas) for quick gains, but alas, they ultimately face downfall and ruin! The story of Vrikasura stands as a clear and vivid example of this truth.

शकुनिजः स तु नारदमेकदा
त्वरिततोषमपृच्छदधीश्वरम् ।
स च दिदेश गिरीशमुपासितुं
न तु भवन्तमबन्धुमसाधुषु ॥89.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: शकुनि-जः the son of Shakuni, सः तु he indeed (Vrikasura), नारदम् एकदा once to Sage Narada, त्वरित-तोषम् who gets pleased quickly, अपृच्छत् अधीश्वरम् asked about such a supreme deity, सः च and he (Narada), दिदेश directed/advised him, गिरीशम् उपासितुम् to worship Lord Shiva (Girisha), न तु भवन्तम् but not You, अबन्धुम् असाधुषु who are no friend to the unrighteous/wicked

Simple Meaning: Vrikasura, the son of Shakuni, once asked Sage Narada which deity could be pleased most quickly. Narada advised him to worship Lord Shiva. He did not direct him towards You, as You never extend Your grace to the unrighteous and wicked.

तपस्तप्त्वा घोरं स खलु कुपितः सप्तमदिने
शिरः छित्वा सद्यः पुरहरमुपस्थाप्य पुरतः ।
अतिक्षुद्रं रौद्रं शिरसि करदानेन निधनं
जगन्नाथाद्वव्रे भवति विमुखानां क्व शुभधीः ॥89.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तपः तप्त्वा performing penance, घोरम् severe/terrible, सः खलु he indeed, कुपितः becoming angry, सप्तम-दिने on the seventh day, शिरः छित्वा intending to cut off his own head, सद्यः instantly, पुरहरम् Lord Shiva (destroyer of Tripura), उपस्थाप्य causing to appear, पुरतः in front of him, अति-क्षुद्रम् extremely vile/lowly, रौद्रम् dreadful, शिरसि कर-दानेन by placing his hand on anyone's head, निधनम् death/destruction, जगन्नाथात् from Lord Shiva, वव्रे chose/requested as a boon, भवति विमुखानाम् of those who are averse to You, क्व where is, शुभ-धीः noble thoughts/righteous intellect

Simple Meaning: Vrikasura performed severe penance for six days. On the seventh day, infuriated that Lord Shiva had not yet appeared, he prepared to sever his own head with a sword. Lord Shiva instantly manifested before him to stop him. Vrikasura then asked for an extremely vile and dreadful boon: that anyone on whose head he placed his hand should immediately die. Where, indeed, can there be any righteous intellect in those who are averse to You?

मोक्तारं बन्धमुक्तो हरिणपतिरिव प्राद्रवत्सोऽथ रुद्रं
दैत्यात् भीत्या स्म देवो दिशि दिशि वलते पृष्ठतो दत्तदृष्टिः ।
तूष्णीके सर्वलोके तव पदमधिरोक्ष्यन्तमुद्वीक्ष्य शर्वं
दूरादेवाग्रतस्त्वं पटुवटुवपुषा तस्थिषे दानवाय ॥89.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: मोक्तारम् the granter of the boon, बन्ध-मुक्तः released from a snare/trap, हरिण-पतिः इव like a lion, प्राद्रवत् सः अथ he then rushed after, रुद्रम् Lord Shiva, दैत्यात् out of fear of the daitya, भीत्या स्म with fright, देवः Lord Shiva, दिशि दिशि from direction to direction, वलते fled/ran, पृष्ठतः दत्त-दृष्टिः casting glances behind him, तूष्णीके सर्व-लोके while all the worlds remained silent/helpless, तव पदम् Thy abode (Vaikuntha), अधिरोक्ष्यन्तम् about to enter, उद्वीक्ष्य seeing, शर्वम् Lord Shiva, दूरात् एव from a distance itself, अग्रतः त्वम् in front, पटु-वटु-वपुषा in the guise of a clever young brahmachari, तस्थिषे You stood, दानवाय appearing before the danava

Simple Meaning: Just as a lion released from a trap pounces upon its deliverer, the wicked daitya rushed at Lord Shiva to test the boon on Him! Terrified of him, Lord Shiva fled in all directions, constantly looking back over His shoulder in fright, while all the worlds stood by in silent helplessness. As Lord Shiva approached Your supreme abode to seek refuge, You appeared before the daitya from afar, disguised as a clever young brahmachari.

भद्रं ते शाकुनेय भ्रमसि किमधुना त्वं पिशाचस्य वाचा
सन्देहश्चेन्मदुक्तौ तव किमु न करोष्यङ्गुलीमङ्गमौलौ ।
इत्थं त्वद्वाक्यमूढः शिरसि कृतकरः सोऽपतच्छिन्नपातं
भ्रंशो ह्येवं परोपासितुरपि च गतिः शूलिनोऽपि त्वमेव ॥89.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: भद्रम् ते may good fortune be unto you, शाकुनेय O son of Shakuni, भ्रमसि why do you wander/run, किम् अधुना now, त्वम् you, पिशाचस्य of a ghost (Lord Shiva), वाचा by the word, सन्देहः चेत् if there is a doubt, मत्-उक्तौ in my words, तव your, किमु न करोषि why do you not place, अङ्गुलीम् finger, अङ्ग O dear one, मौलौ on your own head, इत्थम् thus, त्वत्-वाक्य-मूढः bewildered/fooled by Your words, शिरसि-कृत-करः placing his hand on his own head, सः अपातत् he fell down, छिन्न-पातम् collapsing instantly like a severed tree, भ्रंशः हि एवम् such indeed is the downfall, पर-उपासितुः अपि even of one who worships other deities, च and, गतिः the sole refuge/protector, शूलिनः अपि even of Lord Shiva, त्वम् एव You alone

Simple Meaning: Greeting him courteously, You asked, "O son of Shakuni, why are you running around relying on the words of a ghost-like ascetic? If you doubt my words, why not test it by placing your own hand on your head?" Bewildered and thoroughly duped by Your persuasive speech, the daitya placed his hand on his own head and instantly collapsed dead like a felled tree. Such is the inevitable ruin of those who turn away from You to worship other deities, while You remain the ultimate refuge even for Lord Shiva Himself!

भृगुं किल सरस्वतीनिकटवासिनस्तापसाः
त्रिमूर्तिषु समादिशन्नधिकसत्त्वतां वेदितुम् ।
अयं पुनरनादरादुदितरुद्धरोषे विधौ
हरेऽपि च जिहिंसिषौ गिरिजया धृते त्वामगात् ॥89.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: भृगुम् Sage Bhrigu, किल indeed, सरस्वती-निकट-वासिनः living near the banks of the Saraswati river, तापसाः the ascetics/sages, त्रि-मूर्तिषु among the three deities (Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva), समादिशन् commissioned/directed him, अधिक-सत्त्वताम् the highest sattva quality, वेदितुम् to determine/ascertain, अयम् he (Sage Bhrigu), पुनः then, अनादरात् by showing deliberate disrespect, उदित-रुद्ध-रोषे whose anger arose but was suppressed, विधौ towards Brahma, हरे अपि च and even towards Lord Shiva, जिहिंसिषौ who wished to strike/kill him in anger, गिरिजया by Goddess Parvati, धृते being restrained/stopped, त्वाम् अगात् approached You

Simple Meaning: The ascetics residing on the banks of the Saraswati river once commissioned Sage Bhrigu to ascertain who among the cosmic trinity possessed the supreme abundance of sattva (purity and composure). To test them, Sage Bhrigu deliberately showed disrespect to Lord Brahma, who became angry but suppressed his wrath. Next, he disrespectfully ignored Lord Shiva, who became so enraged that he wished to strike him, only to be restrained by Goddess Parvati. Having tested both, Sage Bhrigu finally approached You.

सुप्तं रमाङ्कभुवि पङ्कजलोचनं त्वां
विप्रे विनिघ्नति पदेन मुदोत्थितस्त्वम् ।
सर्वं क्षमस्व मुनिवर्य भवेत् सदा मे
त्वत्पादचिन्हमिह भूषणमित्यवादीः ॥89.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुप्तम् sleeping/reclining, रमा-अङ्क-भुवि in the lap of Goddess Lakshmi, पङ्कज-लोचनम् O lotus-eyed Lord, त्वाम् You, विप्रे when the brahmana (Sage Bhrigu), विनिघ्नति kicked/struck, पदेन with his foot, मुदा-उत्थितः त्वम् You arose with joy, सर्वम् all/everything, क्षमस्व forgive, मुनि-वर्य O eminent sage, भवेत् सदा मे may it ever be for Me, त्वत्-पाद-चिन्हम् the mark of your foot, इह here (on My chest), भूषणम् an ornament, इति अवादीः thus You spoke

Simple Meaning: O lotus-eyed Lord! While You were reclining peacefully with Your head in Goddess Lakshmi's lap, Sage Bhrigu approached and struck Your chest with his foot. Rising up with joy and utmost humility, You welcomed him, saying, "O eminent sage, forgive My negligence! May the imprint of your foot remain forever on My chest as My ornament!"

निश्चित्य ते च सुदृढं त्वयि बद्धभावाः
सारस्वता मुनिवरा दधिरे विमोक्षम् ।
त्वामेवमच्युत पुनश्च्युतिदोषहीनं
सत्त्वोच्चयैकतनुमेव वयं भजामः ॥89.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: निश्चित्य having determined, ते च and they, सुदृढम् firmly, त्वयि in You, बद्ध-भावाः with deep devotion bound, सारस्वताः living on the banks of the Saraswati, मुनि-वराः the great sages, दधिरे attained, विमोक्षम् ultimate liberation, त्वाम् एवम् You alone in this manner, अच्युत O infallible Lord, पुनः च्युति-दोष-हीनम् free from the defect of falling back into samsara, सत्त्व-उच्चय-एक-तनुम् एव whose divine form is the absolute embodiment of pure sattva, वयम् we, भजामः worship

Simple Meaning: Having conclusively established Your supreme transcendence and unwavering purity, those eminent sages residing on the banks of the Saraswati bound their hearts to You with steadfast devotion and attained ultimate liberation. O Infallible Lord, whose divine form is the purest embodiment of absolute sattva—granting freedom from any possibility of spiritual downfall—we worship You alone!

जगत्सृष्ट्यादौ त्वां निगमनिवहैर्वन्दिभिरिव
स्तुतं विष्णो सच्चित्परमरसनिर्द्वैतवपुषम् ।
परात्मानं भूमन् पशुपवनिताभाग्यनिवहं
परितापश्रान्त्यै पवनपुरवासिन् परिभजे ॥89.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: जगत्-सृष्टि-आदौ at the beginning of the creation of the universe, त्वाम् You, निगम-निवहैः by the assemblies of the Vedas, वन्दिभिः इव like bard/court poets, स्तुतम् praised, विष्णो O All-Pervading Lord Vishnu, सत्-चित्-परम-रस-निर्द्वैत-वपुषम् whose divine form is non-dual, consisting of absolute existence, consciousness, and supreme bliss, परात्मानम् the Supreme Soul/Paramatman, भूमन् O Infinite One, पशुप-वनिता-भाग्य-निवहम् the accumulated fortune of the gopis (cowherd women), परिताप-श्रान्त्यै for the alleviation of all intense afflictions and exhaustion, पवन-पुर-वासिन् O Dweller of Guruvayur, परिभजे I worship wholeheartedly

Simple Meaning: O Lord Vishnu! At the very dawn of cosmic creation, You were praised by the personified Vedas just as kings are extolled by court bards. You are the Supreme Paramatman, the Infinite One, whose non-dual divine form is composed of absolute Existence, Consciousness, and supreme Bliss. You are the embodiment of the supreme good fortune earned by the gopis of Vrindavan. O Dweller of Guruvayur, I worship You wholeheartedly to obtain complete relief from all my afflictions and worldly exhaustion!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
