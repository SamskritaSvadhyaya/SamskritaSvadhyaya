Description of Krishna

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 100

Description of Krishna

अग्रे पश्यामि तेजो निबिडतरकलायावलीलोभनीयं
पीयूषाप्लावितोऽहं तदनु तदुदरे दिव्यकैशोरवेषम् ।
तारुण्यारम्भरम्यं परमसुखरसास्वादरोमाञ्चिताङ्गैः
आवीतं नारदाद्यैर्विलसदुपनिषत्सुन्दरीमण्डलैश्च ॥100.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: अग्रे in front, पश्यामि I see, तेजः a brilliant effulgence, निबिडतर-कलाय-वली-लोभनीयम् enchanting like a dense cluster of dark blue kalaya flowers, पीयूष-आप्लावितः drenched in nectar, अहम् I, तदनु then, तत्-उदरे in the midst of that light, दिव्य-कैशोर-वेषम् a divine youthful form, तारुण्य-आरम्भ-रम्यम् charming at the onset of youth, परम-सुख-रस-आस्वाद-रोमाञ्चित-अङ्गैः with bodies experiencing horripilation from tasting the essence of supreme bliss, आवीतम् surrounded, नारद-आद्यैः by Narada and other sages, विलसत्-उपनिषत्-सुन्दरी-मण्डलैः by the beautiful damsels representing the radiant Upanishads, च and

Simple Meaning: Before me, I behold a brilliant effulgence, as enchanting as a dense cluster of dark blue kalaya flowers! Drenched in nectar, I then see within that radiant light a divine youthful form, charming at the onset of youth. He is surrounded by Narada and other great sages whose bodies stand on end with joy from tasting the supreme bliss, as well as by the beautiful maidens representing the radiant Upanishads.

नीलाभं कुञ्चिताग्रं घनममलतरं संयतं चारुभङ्ग्या
रत्नोत्तंसाभिरामं वलयितमुदयच्चन्द्रकैः पिञ्छजालैः ।
मन्दारस्रङ्निवीतं तव पृथुकबरीभारमालोकयेऽहं
स्निग्धश्वेतोर्ध्वपुण्ड्रामपि च सुललितां फालबालेन्दुवीथीम् ॥100.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: नील-आभम् having a dark lustre, कुञ्चित-अग्रम् curly at the ends, घनम् thick, अमलतरम् immaculate, संयतम् tied back, चारु-भङ्ग्या in a beautiful style, रत्न-उत्तंस-अभिरामम् charming with gem-studded ornaments, वलयितम् encircled, उदयत्-चन्द्रकैः with glowing moon-like spots, पिञ्छ-जालैः with bunches of peacock feathers, मन्दार-स्रक्-निवीतम् entwined with garlands of mandara flowers, तव Your, पृथु-कबरी-भारम् abundant mass of locks, आलोकये I behold, अहम् I, स्निग्ध-श्वेत-ऊर्ध्व-पुण्ड्राम् marked with a glossy white tilaka, अपि also, च and, सुललिताम् exquisite, फाल-बाल-इन्दु-वीथीम् forehead resembling the young (waxing) moon line

Simple Meaning: I behold Your abundant, thick, and lustrous dark hair, with charming curly ends, neatly tied back and adorned with gem-studded ornaments, entwined with garlands of mandara flowers, and ringed by peacock feathers with glowing eye-spots. I also gaze upon Your exquisite forehead, shaped like a crescent moon and adorned with a glossy white tilaka!

हृद्यं पूर्णानुकम्पार्णवमृदुलहरीचञ्चलभ्रूविलासैः
आनीलस्निग्धपक्ष्मावलिपरिलसितं नेत्रयुग्मं विभो ते ।
सान्द्रच्छायं विशालारुणकमलदलाकारमामुग्धतारं
कारुण्यालोकलीलाशिशिरितभुवनं क्षिप्यतां मय्यनाथे ॥100.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: हृद्यम् captivating to the heart, पूर्ण-अनुकम्पा-अर्णव-मृदु-लहरी-चञ्चल-भ्रू-विलासैः with the playful movement of eyebrows like gentle waves on a full ocean of compassion, आनील-स्निग्ध-पक्ष्म-आवलि-परिलसितम् adorned with glossy dark eyelashes, नेत्र-युग्मम् pair of eyes, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, ते Your, सान्द्र-च्छायम् deep in hue, विशाल-अरुण-कमल-दल-आकारम् shaped like large red lotus petals, आमुग्ध-तारम् having charming pupils, कारुण्य-आलोक-लीला-शिशिरित-भुवनम् cooling the worlds with playful glances of mercy, क्षिप्यताम् may it be cast, मयि upon me, अनाथे who am without a protector

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Your two captivating eyes are shaped like large red lotus petals with deep hue, glossy dark eyelashes, and charming pupils. The playful movement of Your eyebrows resembles gentle ripples on a vast ocean of mercy, and Your compassionate glances cool and soothe the whole world. May those merciful eyes be cast upon me, who am without a protector!

उत्तुङ्गोल्लासिनासं हरिमणिमुकुरप्रोल्लसद्गण्डपाली-
व्यालोलत्कर्णपाशाञ्चितमकरमणीकुण्डलद्वन्द्वदीप्रम् ।
उन्मीलद्दन्तपङ्क्तिस्फुरदरुणतरच्छायबिम्बाधरान्तः
प्रीतिप्रस्यन्दिमन्दस्मितमधुरतरं वक्त्रमुद्भासतां मे ॥100.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: उत्तुङ्ग-उल्लासि-नासम् with a prominent and charming nose, हरि-मणि-मुकुर-प्रोल्लसत्-गण्डपाली-व्यालोलत्-कर्णपाश-अञ्चित-मकर-मणी-कुण्डल-द्वन्द्व-दीप्रम् glowing with a pair of gem-studded crocodile-shaped earrings dangling from the ears and reflecting against cheeks which are like sapphire mirrors, उन्मीलत्-दन्त-पङ्क्ति-स्फुरत्-अरुणतर-छाय-बिम्ब-अधर-अन्तः with gleaming rows of teeth showing through radiant deep-red bimba-fruit-like lips, प्रीति-प्रस्यन्दि-मन्द-स्मित-मधुरतरम् made sweet with a gentle smile overflowing with affection, वक्त्रम् face, उद्भासताम् may it shine, मे for me

Simple Meaning: May Your divine face shine before me—a face with a prominent and charming nose, glowing with a pair of gem-studded crocodile-shaped earrings that dangle against cheeks reflecting like polished sapphire mirrors. Through Your radiant, deep-red bimba-like lips, a gleaming row of teeth is faintly visible, rendered sweet by a gentle smile that overflows with deep affection!

बाहुद्वन्द्वेन रत्नोज्ज्वलवलयभृता शोणपाणिप्रवालेन​
उपात्तां वेणुनाली प्रसृतनखमयूखाङ्गुलीसङ्गशाराम् ।
कृत्वा वक्त्रारविन्दे सुमधुरविकसद्रागमुद्भाव्यमानैः
शब्दब्रह्मामृतैस्त्वं शिशिरितभुवनैः सिञ्च मे कर्णवीथीम् ॥100.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: बाहु-द्वन्द्वेन with two arms, रत्न-उज्ज्वल-वलय-भृता adorned with bright gem-encrusted bangles, शोण-पाणि-प्रवालेन with coral-red palms, उपात्ताम् held, वेणु-नालीम् flute, प्रसृत-नख-मयूख-अङ्गुली-सङ्ग-शाराम् variegated by the contact of fingers whose nails spread rays of light, कृत्वा placing, वक्त्र-अरविन्दे on the lotus-like face, सुमधुर-विकसत्-रागम् creating sweet and blooming musical notes, उद्भाव्यमानैः being produced, शब्द-ब्रह्म-अमृतैः with the divine nectar of Shabda Brahman, त्वम् You, शिशिरित-भुवनैः cooling all the worlds, सिञ्च drench, मे my, कर्ण-वीथीम् path of the ears

Simple Meaning: Holding the flute — variegated by the bright light beaming from Your nails, where Your fingers rest upon its stops—with Your two arms adorned with glowing gem-encrusted bangles and coral-red palms, You place it against Your lotus face. O Lord, please drench the orifices of my ears with the divine nectar of sound, the music of Shabda Brahman, whose sweetly unfolding notes soothe and cool the entire universe!

उत्सर्पत्कौस्तुभश्रीततिभिररुणितं कोमलं कण्ठदेशं
वक्षः श्रीवत्सरम्यं तरलतरसमुद्दीप्रहारप्रतानम् ।
नानावर्णप्रसूनावलिकिसलयिनीं वन्यमालां विलोल-
ल्लोलम्बां लम्बमानामुरसि तव तथा भावये रत्नमालाम् ॥100.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: उत्सर्पत्-कौस्तुभ-श्री-ततिभिः by the spreading rays of effulgence from the Kaustubha gem, अरुणितम् reddened, कोमलम् tender, कण्ठ-देशम् neck, वक्षः chest, श्रीवत्स-रम्यम् beautiful with the Shrivatsa mark, तरलतर-समुद्दीप्र-हार-प्रतानम् adorned with glistening strings of brightly shining necklaces, नाना-वर्ण-प्रसून-आवलि-किसलयिनीम् composed of colorful flowers and tender leaves, वन्य-मालाम् forest garland, विलोलत्-लोलम्बाम् surrounded by hovering honeybees, लम्बमानम् hanging, उरसि on Your chest, तव Your, तथा also, भावये I meditate upon, रत्न-मालाम् jewel necklace

Simple Meaning: I meditate upon Your tender neck, reddened by the radiance spreading from the Kaustubha gem, and Your broad chest, charmingly adorned with the Shrivatsa mark and gleaming strings of brilliant necklaces. I also contemplate the hanging forest garland (Vanamala), composed of vibrant blossoms and tender sprouts, surrounded by hovering bees, alongside Your glowing necklace of precious gems resting on Your chest!

अङ्गे पञ्चाङ्गरागैरतिशयविकसत्सौरभाकृष्टलोकं
लीनानेकत्रिलोकीविततिमपि कृशां बिभ्रतं मध्यवल्लीम् ।
शक्राश्मन्यस्ततप्तोज्ज्वलकनकनिभं पीतचेलं दधानं
ध्यायामो दीप्तरश्मिस्फुटमणिरशनाकिङ्किणीमण्डितं त्वां ॥100.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: अङ्गे on Your body, पञ्च-अङ्ग-रागैः with aromatic cosmetics composed of five fragrant unguents, अतिशय-विकसत्-सौरभ-आकृष्ट-लोकम् attracting all beings by its intensely blossoming fragrance, लीन-अनेक-त्रि-लोकी-विततिमपि although holding within itself the vast expanse of all three worlds, कृशाम् slender, बिभ्रतम् bearing, मध्य-वल्लीम् creeper-like waist, शक्र-अश्म-न्यस्त-तप्त-उज्ज्वल-कनक-निभम् shining like molten bright gold draped over a sapphire (शक्र) rock (अश्म), पीत-चेलम् yellow silk garment, दधानम् wearing, ध्यायामः we meditate, दीप्त-रश्मि-स्फुट-मणि-रसना-किङ्किणी-मण्डितम् adorned with a girdle of radiant gems and tinkling small bells, त्वाम् upon You

Simple Meaning: We meditate upon You, whose body is anointed with fivefold fragrant unguents that draw all beings near with their overwhelming fragrance. Though holding the vast expanse of all three worlds within Your form, You possess a slender, creeper-like waist. Draped in a brilliant yellow silk garment that shines like molten gold over a blue sapphire, You are beautifully adorned with a girdle of sparkling gems and softly tinkling tinkling bells!

ऊरू चारू तवोरू घनमसृणरुचौ चित्तचोरौ रमायाः
विश्वक्षोभं विशङ्क्य ध्रुवमनिशमुभौ पीतचेलावृताङ्गौ ।
आनम्राणां पुरस्तान्न्यसनधृतसमस्तार्थपालीसमुद्ग-
च्छायं जानुद्वयं च क्रमपृथुलमनोज्ञे च जङ्घे निषेवे ॥100.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऊरू stout, चारू beautiful, तव Your, ऊरू thighs, घन-मसृण-रुचौ shining with dense and smooth lustre, चित्त-चोरौ stealing the heart, रमायाः of Lakshmi, विश्व-क्षोभम् agitation/attraction of the entire world, विशङ्क्य fearing, ध्रुवम् surely, अनिशम् constantly, उभौ both, पीत-चेल-आवृत-अङ्गौ having their form covered by yellow silk garment, आनम्राणाम् of those who bow down, पुरस्तात् in front of, न्यसन-धृत-समस्त-अर्थ-पाली-समुद्ग-छायम् possessing the beauty of caskets filled with all wealth kept ready to be bestowed, जानु-द्वयम् pair of knees, च and, क्रम-पृथुल-मनोज्ञे gradually taper and are attractive, च and, जङ्घे forelegs, निषेवे I serve and meditate upon

Simple Meaning: I meditate upon Your two beautiful thighs, which shine with a smooth and dense radiance, stealing the heart of Goddess Lakshmi herself! Fearing that their intense beauty might agitate and bewitch the whole world, You surely keep both of them constantly covered in Your yellow silk garment. I also worship Your pair of knees, which resemble treasure caskets placed right before Your devotees, filled with all desired blessings to grant them, as well as Your lovely forelegs that gradually taper and attract the mind!

मञ्जीरं मञ्जुनादैरिव पदभजनं श्रेय इत्यालपन्तं
पादाग्रं भ्रान्तिमज्जत्प्रणतजनमनोमन्दरोद्धारकूर्मम् ।
उत्तुङ्गाताम्रराजन्नखरहिमकरज्योत्स्नया चाऽश्रितानां
सन्तापध्वान्तहन्त्रीं ततिमनुकलये मङ्गलामङ्गुलीनाम् ॥100.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: मञ्जीरम् anklet, मञ्जु-नादैः with sweet sounds, इव as if, पद-भजनम् worship of Your feet, श्रेयः the highest good, इति thus, आलपन्तम् declaring, पाद-अग्रम् forepart of Your feet, भ्रान्ति-मज्जत्-प्रणत-जन-मनः-मन्दर-उद्धार-कूर्मम् acting as the Kurma avatar lifting the Mandara mountain of the minds of bowing devotees sinking in delusion, उत्तुङ्ग-आताम्र-राजत्-नखर-हिमकर-ज्योत्स्नया by the moon-like light of the raised pinkish shining toe-nails, च and, आश्रितानाम् of those who take refuge, सन्ताप-ध्वान्त-हन्त्रीम् destroying the darkness of suffering, ततिम् collection (rows of toe-nails), अनुकलये I constantly meditate upon, मङ्गलाम् auspicious, अङ्गुलीनाम् of the toes

Simple Meaning: I constantly meditate upon Your anklet, which seems to proclaim through its sweet chiming sounds that worshipping Your lotus feet is the ultimate path to supreme good! The forepart of Your feet acts like the divine Kurma incarnation, lifting up the Mandara mountain of the minds of Your devotees whenever they sink into the ocean of delusion. I contemplate Your auspicious toes, whose raised and rosy shining nails cast a soothing, moonlike radiance that completely destroys the dark distress and grief of all who seek Your refuge!

योगीन्द्राणां त्वदङ्गेष्वधिकसुमधुरं मुक्तिभाजां निवासो
भक्तानां कामवर्षद्युतरुकिसलयं नाथ ते पादमूलम् ।
नित्यं चित्तस्थितं मे पवनपुरपते कृष्ण कारुण्यसिन्धो
हृत्वा निश्शेषतापान् प्रदिशतु परमानन्दसन्दोहलक्ष्मीम् ॥100.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: योगीन्द्राणाम् of the best among yogis, त्वत्-अङ्गेषु among all Your limbs, अधिक-सुमधुरम् most sweet and delightful, मुक्ति-भाजाम् of the liberated souls, निवासः abode, भक्तानाम् for the devotees, काम-वर्ष-द्यु-तरु-किसलयम् tender leaf of the wish-fulfilling celestial tree granting all desires, नाथ O Lord, ते Your, पाद-मूलम् base of the lotus feet, नित्यम् constantly, चित्त-स्थितम् residing in the mind, मे my, पवन-पुर-पते O Lord of Guruvayur, कृष्ण O Krishna, कारुण्य-सिन्धो O ocean of mercy, हृत्वा removing, निश्शेष-तापान् all sorrows and afflictions without remainder, प्रदिशतु may it bestow, परम-आनन्द-सन्दोह-लक्ष्मीम् the ultimate wealth of supreme bliss

Simple Meaning: The base of Your lotus feet is the sweetest and most delightful among all Your limbs to the greatest of yogis, the eternal abode for liberated souls, and a tender sprout of the celestial wish-fulfilling tree that showers every desire upon Your devotees. O Lord Krishna! O Lord of Guruvayur! O ocean of infinite mercy! May those lotus feet ever remain established in my mind, destroy all my suffering completely, and grant me the supreme wealth of everlasting bliss!

अज्ञात्वा ते महत्वं यदिह निगदितं विश्वनाथ क्षमेथाः
स्तोत्रं चैतत्सहस्रोत्तरमधिकतरं त्वत्प्रसादाय भूयात् ।
द्वेधा नारायणीयं श्रुतिषु च जनुषा स्तुत्यतावर्णनेन
स्फीतं लीलावतारैरिदमिह कुरुतामायुरारोग्यसौख्यम् ॥100.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: अज्ञात्वा without knowing, ते Your, महत्वम् greatness, यत् whatever, इह here, निगदितम् spoken or described, विश्वनाथ O Lord of the universe, क्षमेथाः please forgive, स्तोत्रम् hymn of praise, च and, एतत् this, सहस्र-उत्तरम् consisting of more than a thousand verses, अधिकतरम् abundantly, त्वत्-प्रसादाय for Your grace, भूयात् may it be, द्वेधा in two ways, नारायणीयम् Narayaneeyam, श्रुतिषु in the Vedas, च and, जनुषा by birth or origin, स्तुत्यता-वर्णनेन by the description of the praise of Lord Narayana, स्फीतम् enriched, लीला-अवतारैः by Your divine playful incarnations, इदम् this hymn, इह in this world, कुरुताम् may it bestow, आयुः-आरोग्य-सौख्यम् longevity, health, and happiness

Simple Meaning: O Lord of the universe! Please forgive whatever I have uttered here without fully knowing Your infinite greatness. May this hymn of praise, containing over a thousand slokas, abundantly secure Your divine grace! This work is named Narayaneeyam in two ways—because it originates from the Vedas which proclaim Narayana, and because it vividly describes the worship and divine incarnations of Lord Narayana Himself. May this sacred Narayaneeyam bestow longevity, radiant health, and eternal happiness upon all in this world!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
