Dashaka 90

Establishing the glory of Vishnu

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 90

Establishing the glory of Vishnu

वृकभृगुमुनिमोहिन्यम्बरीषादिवृत्तेषु
अयि तव हि महत्त्वं सर्वशर्वादिजैत्रम् ।
स्थितमिह परमात्मन् निष्कलार्वागभिन्नं
किमपि यदवभातं तद्धि रूपं तवैव ॥90.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: वृक-भृगु-मुनि-मोहिनी-अम्बरीष-आदि-वृत्तेषु in the episodes of Vrikasura, Sage Bhrigu, Mohini, Ambarisha and others, अयि O Lord, तव Your, हि indeed, महत्त्वम् supreme greatness, सर्व-शर्व-आदि-जैत्रम् conquering/surpassing all including Lord Shiva and other devas, स्थितम् established, इह here in this world, परमात्मन् O Supreme Soul, निष्कल-अर्वाक्-अभिन्नम् whole, part or non dual manifestations, किमपि indescribable/indefinable, यत्-अवभातम् that which shines forth, तत् हि that indeed, रूपम् divine form, तव एव Yours alone

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Soul! Through the stories of Vrikasura, Sage Bhrigu, Mohini, King Ambarisha, and others, Your supreme glory—surpassing all deities including Lord Shiva—stands undeniably established. Whatever indescribable ultimate Reality shines forth in this universe, be it whole, part or non dual manifestations, that supreme form is indeed Yours alone!

मूर्तित्रयेश्वरसदाशिवपञ्चकं यत्
प्राहुः परात्मवपुरेव सदाशिवोऽस्मिन् ।
तत्रेश्वरस्तु स विकुण्ठपदस्त्वमेव
त्रित्वं पुनर्भजसि सत्यपदे त्रिभागे ॥90.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: मूर्ति-त्रय-ईश्वर-सदाशिव-पञ्चकम् the fivefold manifest aspect comprising the three deities (Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva), Ishvara, and Sadashiva, यत् which, प्राहुः the scriptures declare, परात्म-वपुः एव as the form of the Supreme Spirit/Paramatman itself, सदाशिवः अस्मिन् Sadashiva among these, तत्र ईश्वरः तु and Ishvara in that hierarchy, सः He, विकुण्ठ-पदः residing in the abode of Vaikuntha, त्वम् एव is You alone, त्रित्वम् the trinity (Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva), पुनः further, भजसि You assume, सत्यपदे त्रिभागे in Satyaloka which is divided into three functional parts

Simple Meaning: The scriptures speak of a fivefold divine hierarchy comprising Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva, Ishvara, and Sadashiva, all of which are manifestations of the supreme Paramatman. Among these, Sadashiva represents the pure, transcendent aspect of the Supreme Spirit, while Ishvara—residing in Vaikuntha—is none other than You. Further, You assume the triple forms of Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva within Satyaloka to carry out the three cosmic functions of creation, sustenance, and dissolution.

तत्रापि सात्त्विकतनुं तव विष्णुमाहुः
धाता तु सत्त्वविरलो रजसैव पूर्णः ।
सत्त्वोत्कटत्वमपि चास्ति तमोविकार-
चेष्टादिकञ्च तव शङ्करनाम्नि मूर्तौ ॥90.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र अपि even among those forms, सात्त्विक-तनुम् as the pure sattvic form, तव Your, विष्णुम् आहुः they call Lord Vishnu, धाता Brahma (the creator), तु but, सत्त्व-विरलः devoid of/lacking in sattva, रजसा एव पूर्णः filled with rajas alone, सत्त्व-उत्कटत्वम् अपि च अस्ति and there is also an abundance of sattva, तमः-विकार-चेष्टादिकम् च as well as actions/modifications arising from tamas, तव Your, शङ्कर-नाम्नि मूर्तौ in the form named Shankara (Lord Shiva)

Simple Meaning: Among these forms, sages declare Lord Vishnu to be Your purely sattvic embodiment. Lord Brahma has traces of sattva and is filled predominantly with rajas. In Your form named Shankara (Lord Shiva), there is indeed a great abundance of sattva, yet it also exhibits activities and characteristics associated with tamas for specific cosmic roles.

तं च त्रिमूर्त्यतिगतं परपूरुषं त्वां
शर्वात्मनापि खलु सर्वमयत्वहेतोः ।
शंसन्त्युपासनविधौ तदपि स्वतस्तु
त्वद्रूपमित्यतिदृढं बहु नः प्रमाणम् ॥90.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् च Him, त्रि-मूर्ति-अतिगतम् transcending the three deities (Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva), पर-पूरुषम् the Supreme Purusha/Paramatman, त्वाम् You, शर्व-आत्मना अपि even in the form of Lord Shiva (Sharva), खलु indeed, सर्व-मयत्व-हेतोः because of Your all-pervading nature, शंसन्ति उपासन-विधौ scriptures praise Him in methods of worship, तत् अपि nevertheless, स्वतः तु inherently/by nature, त्वत्-रूपम् इति that supreme form is Yours alone, अति-दृढम् extremely firm/strong, बहु numerous, नः for us, प्रमाणम् evidence/scriptural proof

Simple Meaning: Because You pervade everything, scriptures sometimes describe that Supreme Purusha—who transcends the trinity—even under the name and form of Lord Shiva in certain modes of worship. Nevertheless, that transcendent form belongs inherently to You alone! We possess abundant and rock-solid scriptural evidence to firmly establish this truth.

श्रीशङ्करोऽपि भगवान् सकलेषु तावत्
त्वामेव मानयति यो न हि पक्षपाती ।
त्वन्निष्ठमेव स हि नामसहस्रकादि
व्याख्यात् भवत्स्तुतिपरश्च गतिं गतोऽन्ते ॥90.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री-शङ्करः अपि भगवान् even the revered Adi Shankara, सकलेषु among all the deities, तावत् indeed, त्वाम् एव You alone, मानयति holds as supreme, यः who, न हि indeed is not, पक्षपाती partial/biased, त्वत्-निष्ठम् एव centered entirely on You, सः he, हि indeed, नाम-सहस्रक-आदि the Vishnu Sahasranama and other works, व्याख्यात् commented upon, भवत्-स्तुति-परः च and deeply devoted to praising You, गतिम् गतः अन्ते attained the ultimate goal at the end

Simple Meaning: Even the revered Adi Shankara—who was completely impartial and unbiased toward any particular form—held You alone as the Supreme Being among all deities! He wrote profound commentaries on works centered entirely on You, such as the Vishnu Sahasranama, and spending his life engaged in singing Your praises, he ultimately attained the supreme goal of liberation.

मूर्तित्रयातिगमुवाच च मन्त्रशास्त्रः
स्यादौ कलायसुषमं सकलेश्वरं त्वाम् ।
ध्यानं च निष्कलमसौ प्रणवे खलूक्त्वा
त्वामेव तत्र सकलं निजगाद नान्यम् ॥90.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: मूर्ति-त्रय-अतिगम् transcending the trinity of deities, उवाच च declared, मन्त्र-शास्त्रः the ancient treatises on mantras (Prapanchasara), स्यादौ at the very beginning, कलाय-सुषमम् with a brilliant luster like the dark kalaya flower, सकल-ईश्वरम् the supreme Lord with all attributes, त्वाम् You, ध्यानम् च and meditation, निष्कलम् (nish-kala) on the formless/attributeless Brahman, असौ he (Adi Shankara), प्रणवे in the sacred syllable Om, खलु-उक्त्वा having explained, त्वाम् एव You alone, तत्र there, स-कलम् as the part manifested Lord, निजगाद declared, न अन्यम् not anyone else

Simple Meaning: Furthermore, in his work on mantras (the Prapanchasara), Adi Shankara declared You—who possesses a radiant complexion like the dark kalaya flower—to be the Supreme Lord of all, transcending the trinity. While explaining meditation on the formless Absolute (निष्कलम्) through the sacred syllable Om, he identifies Your manifest form (सकलम्) to be worshipped, and no other!

समस्तसारे च पुराणसङ्ग्रहे
विसंशयं त्वन्महिमैव वर्ण्यते ।
त्रिमूर्तियुक्सत्यपदत्रिभागतः
परं पदं ते कथितं न शूलिनः ॥90.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: समस्त-सारे in the essence of all scriptures, च and, पुराण-सङ्ग्रहे in the compendiums of the Puranas (such as the Bhagavata Purana), विसंशयम् without any doubt, त्वत्-महिमा एव Your glory alone, वर्ण्यते is described, त्रि-मूर्ति-युक्-सत्य-पद-त्रि-भागः-तः beyond the three functional divisions of Satyaloka associated with the trinity, परम् पदम् the supreme abode (Paramam Padam/Vaikuntha), ते Yours, कथितम् is declared, न शूलिनः not of Lord Shiva (the trident-bearer)

Simple Meaning: In all the essential scriptural compendiums and Puranas, it is unequivocally Your supreme glory alone that is extolled. The ultimate transcendent abode (Paramam Padam/Vaikuntha), which lies far beyond the three functional spheres of Satyaloka inhabited by the trinity, is explicitly declared by scriptures to be Yours alone, and not that of Lord Shiva!

यत् ब्राह्मकल्प इह भागवतद्वितीय-
स्कन्धोदितं वपुरनावृतमीश धात्रे ।
तस्यैव नाम हरिशर्वमुखं जगाद
श्रीमाधवः शिवपरोऽपि पुराणसारे ॥90.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: यत् which, ब्राह्म-कल्पे in the Brahma Kalpa, इह here, भागवत-द्वितीय-स्कन्ध-उदितम् described in the second canto of the Shrimad Bhagavata, वपुः divine form, अनावृतम् unvarnished/revealed in all its splendor, ईश O Lord, धात्रे to Lord Brahma (the creator), तस्य एव of that form alone, नाम the names, हरि-शर्व-मुखम् such as Hari, Sharva (Shiva), and others, जगाद proclaimed, श्री-माधवः Shri Madhavacharya, शिव-परः अपि though appearing devoted to Shiva, पुराण-सारे in his treatise Puranasara

Simple Meaning: O Lord! In the Brahma Kalpa, the transcendent form that You revealed directly to Lord Brahma as described in the Second Canto of Shrimad Bhagavata is the supreme Reality. Even Shri Madhavacharya, though writing from a Shaiva-leaning perspective in his treatise Puranasara, explicitly declared that all names—including Hari, Sharva (Shiva), and Brahma—ultimately denote that very divine form of Yours alone!

ये स्वप्रकृत्यनुगुणा गिरिशं भजन्ते
तेषां फलं हि दृढयैव तदीयभक्त्या।
व्यासो हि तेन कृतवानधिकारिहेतोः
स्कन्दादिकेषु तव हानिवचोऽर्थवादैः ॥90.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: ये those who, स्व-प्रकृति-अनुगुणाः according to their own inherent nature/gunas, गिरिशम् Lord Shiva (Girisha), भजन्ते worship, तेषाम् for them, फलम् fruit/reward, हि indeed, दृढया-एव only through firm, तदीय-भक्त्या through devotion to him, व्यासः Sage Vyasa, हि indeed, तेन for that reason, कृतवान् composed, अधिकारि-हेतोः for the sake of specific eligible seekers (adhikaris), स्कन्द-आदिकेषु in Puranas such as the Skanda Purana, तव Your, हानि-वचः words describing inferiority/subordination, अर्थवादैः by way of arthavada (exaggerated or eulogistic statements)

Simple Meaning: Those who worship Lord Shiva in accordance with their own inherent nature (gunas) attain rewards only through steadfast devotion to him. For this reason, Sage Vyasa composed Puranas such as the Skanda Purana—containing statements that seem to lower Your position or elevate others—purely as eulogies (arthavada) suited to the specific eligibility and mental temperament of those worshipers, so as to build firm faith in their chosen path.

भूतार्थकीर्तिरनुवादविरुद्धवादौ
त्रेधार्थवादगतयः खलु रोचनार्थाः ।
स्कान्दादिकेषु बहवोऽत्र विरुद्धवादाः
त्वत्तामसत्वपरिभूत्युपशिक्षणाद्याः ॥90.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूत-अर्थ-कीर्तिः statement of actual fact (bhutarthavada), अनुवादः extrapolating the truth (anuvadavada), विरुद्ध-वादौ stating what is opposite to the known facts (virodhavada), त्रेधा in three ways, अर्थवाद-गतयः the types/nature of arthavada (eulogistic passages), खलु indeed, रोचना-अर्थाः meant to create interest/inclination, स्कान्द-आदिकेषु in scriptures like the Skanda Purana, बहवः-अत्र many here, विरुद्ध-वादाः statements that contradict established facts, त्वत्-तामसत्व-परिभूति-उपशिक्षण-आद्याः such as ascribing tamas to You, Your subordination, or Your instruction by others

Simple Meaning: People who interpret texts classify eulogistic statements (arthavada) into three types: bhutarthavada (stating actual facts), anuvadavada (extrapolating known facts), and virodhavada (making statements that contradict established facts). All three serve purely to create interest in specific seekers. In Puranas like the Skanda, the numerous statements that contradict absolute truth—such as attributing tamas to You, depicting Your defeat, or showing You being instructed by others—are merely virodhavada meant to foster devotion in those specific worshipers.

यत् किञ्चिदप्यविदुषाऽपि विभो मयोक्तं
तन्मन्त्रशास्त्रवचनाद्यभिदृष्टमेव ।
व्यासोक्तिसारमयभागवतोपगीत
क्लेशान् विधूय कुरु भक्तिभरं परात्मन् ॥90.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: यत् किञ्चित् अपि whatever little, अविदुषा अपि though by an unlearned person like me, विभो O All-Pervading Lord, मया उक्तम् has been stated by me, तत्-मन्त्र-शास्त्र-वचन-आदि-अभिदृष्टम् एव all that is indeed directly verified from scriptures on mantras, sacred texts, and other authorities, व्यास-उक्ति-सार-मय-भागवत-उपगीत O Lord sung in the Bhagavata Purana, which is the quintessence of Sage Vyasa's words, क्लेशान् my afflictions/sufferings, विधूय dispelling, कुरु grant/bestow, भक्ति-भरम् an abundance of intense devotion, परात्मन् O Supreme Soul

Simple Meaning: O All-Pervading Lord! Whatever statements I have made here—though composed by an unlearned person like me—are fully corroborated by the statements of the Mantra Shastras, Puranas, and authoritative scriptures. O Supreme Soul, who is glorified in the Shrimad Bhagavata Purana, the absolute essence of Sage Vyasa's teachings! Dispel all my physical and mental afflictions, and grant me an abundance of supreme devotion unto You!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
