Dashaka 27

Churning the ocean for nectar

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 27

Churning the ocean for nectar

(छन्दस् - प्रहर्षिणी)

दुर्वासास्सुरवनिताप्तदिव्यमाल्यं
शक्राय स्वयमुपदाय तत्र भूयः ।
नागेन्द्रप्रतिमृदिते शशाप शक्रं
का क्षान्तिस्त्वदितरदेवतांशजानाम् ॥27.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुर्वासाः Sage Durvasa, सुर-वनिता-आप्त-दिव्य-माल्यम् a divine garland obtained from a celestial maiden, शक्राय to Shakra (Indra), स्वयम् himself, उपदाय presenting/giving, तत्र there, भूयः again, नाग-इन्द्र-प्रतिमृदिते trampled underfoot by the king of elephants, शशाप cursed, शक्रम् Shakra, का what, क्षान्तिः patience/forgiveness, त्वत्-इतर-देवता-अंशजानाम् of those born from the aspects of devas other than You

Simple Meaning: Sage Durvasa presented a divine garland, which he had received from a celestial maiden, to Indra. However, when Indra placed it on his elephant, the elephant threw it down and trampled it underfoot. Outraged, Durvasa cursed Indra. (The poet is asking Narayana rhetorically.) Indeed, where is the patience or tolerance in those born from aspects of devas other than You?

शापेन प्रथितजरेऽथ निर्जरेन्द्रे
देवेष्वप्यसुरजितेषु निष्प्रभेषु ।
शर्वाद्याः कमलजमेत्य सर्वदेवा
निर्वाणप्रभव समं भवन्तमापुः ॥27.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: शापेन by the curse, प्रथित-जरे experiencing advanced decay/old age, अथ then, निर्जर-इन्द्रे the lord of the immortals - Indra, देवेषु when the devas, अपि also, असुर-जितेषु were defeated by the asuras, निष्प्रभेषु rendered lusterless/powerless, शर्व-आद्याः led by Sharva (Shiva), कमलजम् the one born out of lotus - Brahma, एत्य approaching, सर्व-देवाः all the devas, निर्वाण-प्रभव O Source of Supreme Bliss, समम् together, भवन्तम् You, आपुः reached

Simple Meaning: When Indra, the lord of the devas, fell into a state of decay due to the curse, and all the devas were stripped of their luster after being defeated by the asuras, they along with Shiva approached Brahma. O Source of Supreme Bliss, all of them then together sought refuge in You!

ब्रह्माद्यैः स्तुतमहिमा चिरं तदानीं
प्रादुष्षन् वरद पुरः परेण धाम्ना ।
हे देवा दितिजकुलैर्विधाय सन्धिं
पीयूषं परिमथतेति पर्यशास्त्वम् ॥27.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्रह्मा-आद्यैः by Brahma and other devas, स्तुत-महिमा Your glory having been praised, चिरम् for a long time, तदानीम् then, प्रादुष्षन् appearing, वरद O Bestower of boons, पुरः in front of them, परेण धाम्ना with supreme resplendence, हे देवाः O devas, दितिज-कुलैः with the hosts of asuras, विधाय making, सन्धिम् a truce/alliance, पीयूषम् the nectar, परिमथत churn, इति thus, पर्यशाः instructed, त्वम् You

Simple Meaning: After Brahma and the other devas praised Your glory for a long time, You appeared before them in Your supreme, radiant form, O Bestower of boons! You instructed them, saying, “O devas, form an alliance with the hosts of asuras and churn the ocean to obtain the nectar.”

सन्धानं कृतवति दानवैः सुरौघे
मन्थानं नयति मदेन मन्दराद्रिम् ।
भ्रष्टेऽस्मिन् बदरमिवोद्वहन् खगेन्द्रे
सद्यस्त्वं विनिहितवान् पयःपयोधौ ॥27.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: सन्धानम् a truce, कृतवति having made, दानवैः with the danavas, सुर-ओघे the host of devas, मन्थानम् as the churning rod, नयति bringing, मदेन with pride, मन्दर-अद्रिम् Mount Mandara, भ्रष्टे slipped/dropped, अस्मिन् this mountain, बदरम् इव like a berry, उद्वहन् carrying, खग-इन्द्रे on the king of the birds - Garuda, सद्यः instantly, त्वम् You, विनिहितवान् placed, पयः-पयोधौ in the ocean of milk

Simple Meaning: After the host of devas formed a truce with the danavas, they proudly tried to carry Mount Mandara to use as a churning rod. When the mountain slipped and fell, You picked it up as easily as a berry, carried it on Garuda, and placed it in the ocean of milk.

आधाय द्रुतमथ वासुकिं वरत्रां
पाथोधौ विनिहितसर्वबीजजाले ।
प्रारब्धे मथनविधौ सुरासुरैस्तैः
व्याजात्त्वं भुजगमुखेऽकरोस्सुरारीन् ॥27.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: आधाय having tied/wrapped, द्रुतम् quickly, अथ then, वासुकिम् Vasuki the serpent, वरत्राम् as the rope, पाथोधौ in the ocean, विनिहित-सर्व-बीज-जाले wherein all seeds of life had been deposited, प्रारब्धे being commenced, मथन-विधौ the act of churning, सुर-असुरैः तैः by those devas and asuras, व्याजात् by a clever pretext, त्वम् You, भुजग-मुखे at the head of the serpent, अकरोः placed, सुर-अरीन् the enemies of the devas (asuras)

Simple Meaning: Then, wrapping the serpent Vasuki as the churning rope around the mountain in the ocean where all seeds of life had been deposited, the devas and asuras commenced the churning process. By a clever pretext, You cleverly positioned the asuras at the serpent's head.

क्षुब्धाद्रौ क्षुभितजलोदरे तदानीं
दुग्धाब्धौ गुरुतरभारतो निमग्ने ।
देवेषु व्यथिततमेषु तत्प्रियैषी
प्राणैषीः कमठतनुं कठोरपृष्ठाम् ॥27.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्षुब्ध-अद्रौ as the mountain was agitation-prone/unstable, क्षुभित-जल-उदरे as the waters were violently agitated, तदानीम् then, दुग्ध-अब्धौ in the ocean of milk, गुरुतर-भारतः due to its excessive weight, निमग्ने sinking, देवेषु when the devas, व्यथिततमेषु became extremely distressed, तत्-प्रिय-एषी seeking their welfare, प्राणैषीः You assumed, कमठ-तनुम् the body of a tortoise, कठोर-पृष्ठाम् with a rock-hard back

Simple Meaning: As the milk ocean was violently agitated and the giant mountain began to sink into its depths due to its immense weight, the devas became extremely distressed. Desiring their welfare, You assumed the form of a giant Kurma (tortoise) possessing a rock-hard back.

वज्रातिस्थिरतरकर्परेण विष्णो
विस्तारात्परिगतलक्षयोजनेन ।
अम्भोधेः कुहरगतेन वर्ष्मणा त्वं
निर्मग्नं क्षितिधरनाथमुन्निनेथ ॥27.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: वज्र-अति-स्थिर-तर-कर्परेण with a shell far harder and more solid than a diamond/thunderbolt, विष्णो O Vishnu, विस्तारात् in width/extent, परिगत-लक्ष-योजनेन measuring a hundred thousand yojanas, अम्भोधेः of the ocean, कुहर-गतेन located at the very bottom, वर्ष्मणा with Your form, त्वम् You, निर्मग्नम् submerged, क्षितिधर-नाथम् the king of mountains Mandara, उन्निनेथ lifted up

Simple Meaning: O Vishnu, assuming a form with a shell infinitely harder than a diamond and spanning a hundred thousand yojanas in width, You descended to the bottom of the ocean and lifted up the sinking Mount Mandara upon Your back.

उन्मग्ने झटिति तदा धराधरेन्द्रे
निर्मेथुर्दृढमिह सम्मदेन सर्वे ।
आविश्य द्वितयगणेऽपि सर्पराजे
वैवश्यं परिशमयन्नवीवृधस्तान् ॥27.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: उन्मग्ने having been raised/emerged, झटिति instantly, तदा then, धराधर-इन्द्रे the king of mountains, निर्मेथुः churned, दृढम् vigorously, इह here, सम्मदेन with great joy, सर्वे all of them, आविश्य entering into, द्वितय-गणे in both groups (devas and asuras), अपि and, सर्प-राजे in the serpent king Vasuki, वैवश्यम् exhaustion/weakness, परिशमयन् alleviating, नवीवृधः You invigorated/energized, तान् them

Simple Meaning: When the king of mountains was thus raised instantly, all the devas and asuras churned the ocean vigorously with immense joy. Entering into both groups as well as into the serpent king Vasuki, You infused them all with energy.

उद्दामभ्रमणजवोन्नमद्गिरीन्द्र-
न्यस्तैकस्थिरतरहस्तपङ्कजं त्वाम् ।
अभ्रान्ते विधिगिरिशादयः प्रमोदात्
उदद्भ्रान्ता नुनुवुरुपात्तपुष्पवर्षाः ॥27.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: उद्दाम-भ्रमण-जव-उन्नमत्-गिरि-इन्द्र-न्यस्त-एक-स्थिरतर-हस्त-पङ्कजम् who placed one steady lotus-hand atop the king of mountains which was rising up due to the speed of violent rotation, त्वाम् You, अभ्र-अन्ते in the sky, विधि-गिरिश-आदयः Brahma, Shiva, and other devas, प्रमोदात् out of immense joy, उदद्भ्रान्ताः dancing/hovering in excitement, नुनुवुः praised/worshipped, उपात्त-पुष्प-वर्षाः showering flowers gathered by them

Simple Meaning: As the mountain began to rise up due to the force of its rapid and violent rotation, You held it firm by placing one steady lotus hand on its peak. Beholding You in this wondrous act, Brahma, Shiva, and the other devas hovering in the sky rejoiced with delight, praising You and showering flowers upon You.

दैत्यौघे भुजगमुखानिलेन तप्ते
तेनैव त्रिदशकुलेऽपि किञ्चिदार्ते ।
कारुण्यात्तव किल देव वारिवाहाः
प्रावर्षन्नमरगणान्न दैत्यसङ्घान् ॥27.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: दैत्य-ओघे as the host of daityas (asuras), भुजग-मुख-अनिलेन by the hot breath/poisonous fumes from the serpent's mouth, तप्ते were scorched, तेन एव by that very breath, त्रिदश-कुले in the assembly of the devas, अपि also, किञ्चित् somewhat, आर्ते being afflicted, कारुण्यात् out of compassion, तव Your, किल indeed, देव O Lord, वारिवाहाः clouds, प्रावर्षन् showered rain upon, अमर-गणान् the hosts of immortals (devas), न not, दैत्य-सङ्घान् the groups of daityas

Simple Meaning: O Lord, while the asuras at the serpent's mouth were scorched by his burning, poisonous breath, the devas at the tail end were also slightly distressed by the heat. Out of Your divine compassion, clouds appeared and rained cooling water upon the assembly of devas, while deliberately sparing the hosts of asuras!

उद्भ्राम्यद्बहुतिमिनक्रचक्रवाले
तत्राब्धौ चिरमथितेऽपि निर्विकारे ।
एकस्त्वं करयुगकृष्टसर्पराजः
संराजन् पवनपुरेश पाहि रोगात् ॥27.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: उद्भ्राम्यत्-बहु-तिमि-नक्र-चक्रवाले where massive numbers of whales, sharks, and crocodiles were whirling around violently, तत्र in that, अब्धौ ocean, चिरम् for a long time, मथिते अपि even though churned, निर्विकारे remaining unchanged/yielding no nectar, एकः Alone, त्वम् You, कर-युग-कृष्ट-सर्पराजः pulling the serpent king Vasuki with both Your hands, संराजन् shining resplendently, पवन-पुर-ईश O Lord of the abode of the Wind - Guruvayoor, पाहि protect me, रोगात् from diseases

Simple Meaning: Even though the ocean—filled with swarms of agitated whales, sharks, and crocodiles—was churned for a very long time, it yielded nothing. Seeing this, You alone pulled the serpent king Vasuki with Your own two hands to accelerate the churning. O Lord of Guruvayoor, please protect me from all my afflictions and diseases!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
