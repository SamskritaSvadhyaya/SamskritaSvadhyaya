Dashaka 76

Krishna sends a message to the gopis through Uddhava

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 76

Krishna sends a message to the gopis through Uddhava

गत्वा सान्दीपनिमथ चतुष्षष्टिमात्रैरहोभिः
सर्वज्ञस्त्वं सह मुसलिना सर्वविद्या गृहीत्वा ।
पुत्रं नष्टं यमनिलयनादाहृतं दक्षिणार्थं
दत्वा तस्मै निजपुरमगा नादयन् पाञ्चजन्यम् ॥76.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: गत्वा having gone, सान्दीपनिम् to the sage Sandipani, अथ then, चतुष्षष्टि-मात्रैः अहोभिः in merely sixty-four days, सर्वज्ञः त्वम् You who are omniscient, सह with, मुसलिना Balarama (who wields the pestle weapon), सर्व-विद्याः all branches of knowledge and arts, गृहीत्वा having mastered, पुत्रम् नष्टम् his lost son, यम-निलयनात्-आहृतम् brought back from the abode of Yamaraja (the lord of death), दक्षिणार्थम् as the preceptor's fee (guru-dakshina), दत्वा having offered, तस्मै to him, निज-पुरम् अगाः You returned to Your own city of Mathura, नादयन् sounding loudly, पाञ्चजन्यम् the sacred Panchajanya conch

Simple Meaning: Accompanied by Balarama, You proceeded to the hermitage of Sage Sandipani in Avanti. Though You are inherently omniscient, You formally assumed the role of a student and mastered all sixty-four traditional arts and sciences in just sixty-four days. As the preceptor's fee (guru-dakshina), You retrieved Your teacher's long-lost son from the realm of Yamaraja and restored him alive to his parents. Then, blowing Your mighty conch Panchajanya, You made Your triumphant return to Mathura.

स्मृत्वा स्मृत्वा पशुपसुदृशः प्रेमभारप्रणुन्नाः
कारुण्येन त्वमपि विवशः प्राहिणोरुद्धवं तम् ।
किञ्चामुष्मै परमसुहृदे भक्तवर्याय तासां
भक्त्युद्रेकं सकलभुवने दुर्लभं दर्शयिष्यन् ॥76.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्मृत्वा स्मृत्वा remembering again and again, पशुप-सुदृशः the beautiful-eyed gopis of Vraja, प्रेम-भार-प्रणुन्नाः driven by the immense weight of their deep love, कारुण्येन with deep compassion, त्वम् You, अपि also, विवशः overcome by emotion, प्राहिणोः sent, उद्धवम् तम् that Uddhava, किञ्च moreover, अमुष्मै to this, परम-सुहृदे supreme, dear friend, भक्त-वर्याय to the crest-jewel among devotees, तासाम् their (the gopis'), भक्ति-उद्रेकम् the intense, overflowing flood of devotion, सकल-भुवने in all the worlds, दुर्लभम् rare and difficult to attain, दर्शयिष्यन् wishing to demonstrate and reveal

Simple Meaning: Remembering time and again the beautiful-eyed maidens of Vraja, who were overwhelmed by the intense burden of their boundless love, You too were overcome with deep compassion for them. You dispatched Your dear friend Uddhava—the greatest of devotees—to Vraja with a message. In doing so, You also desired to reveal to him the extraordinary, unparalleled height of devotional devotion possessed by the gopis, an intensity of love that is exceedingly rare anywhere in the universe.

त्वन्माहात्म्यप्रथिमपिशुनं गोकुलं प्राप्य सायं
त्वद्वार्ताभिर्बहु स रमयामास नन्दं यशोदाम् ।
प्रातर्द्दृष्ट्वा मणिमयरथं शङ्किताः पङ्कजाक्ष्यः
श्रुत्वा प्राप्तं भवदनुचरं त्यक्तकार्याः समीयुः ॥76.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-माहात्म्य-प्रथिम-पिशुनम् revealing the immense magnitude of Your divine glory, गोकुलम् the village of Gokula, प्राप्य having reached, सायम् in the evening, त्वत्-वार्ताभिः with news and stories about You, बहु abundantly, सः he (Uddhava), रमयामास gladdened, नन्दम् Nanda, यशोदाम् Yashoda, प्रातः in the morning, दृष्ट्वा seeing, मणिमय-रथम् the jewel-encrusted chariot, शङ्किताः apprehensive, पङ्कज-अक्ष्यः the lotus-eyed maidens (gopis), श्रुत्वा having heard, प्राप्तम् arrived, भवत्-अनुचरम् Your messenger and servant, त्यक्त-कार्याः abandoning all their household duties, समीयुः gathered around him

Simple Meaning: Arriving at Gokula in the evening—where every grove and path bore witness to Your immense divine glory—Uddhava spent the night bringing immense comfort to Nanda and Yashoda by recounting stories of Your activities and well-being. The next morning, seeing a magnificent jewel-adorned chariot parked outside, the lotus-eyed gopis became curious and apprehensive. Learning that Your personal messenger and servant had arrived, they immediately set aside all their household chores and rushed to meet him.

दृष्ट्वा चैनं त्वदुपमलसद्वेषभूषाभिरामं
स्मृत्वा स्मृत्वा तव विलसितान्युच्चकैस्तानि तानि ।
रुद्धालापाः कथमपि पुनर्गद्गदां वाचमूचुः
सौजन्यादीन् निजपरभिदामप्यलं विस्मरन्त्यः ॥76.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: दृष्ट्वा having seen, च and, एनम् him (Uddhava), त्वत्-उपम-लसत्-वेष-भूषा-अभिरामम् enchanting with attire and ornaments closely resembling Yours, स्मृत्वा स्मृत्वा remembering again and again, तव Your, विलसितानि playful sports and pastimes, उच्चकैः intensely, तानि तानि each of those distinct deeds, रुद्ध-आलापाः with voices choked with emotion, कथमपि somehow with great effort, पुनः again, गद्गदाम् वाचम् ऊचुः spoke in trembling, tearful words, सौजन्य-आदीन् courtesy, etiquette, and social norms, निज-पर-भिदाम् अपि and even the basic distinction between self and others, अलम् completely, विस्मरन्त्यः forgetting

Simple Meaning: Beholding Uddhava—who looked enchanting in attire and ornaments resembling Your very own—the gopis were instantly reminded of Your sweet playful sports. As memories of Your various divine pastimes flooded their minds, their voices choked with emotion. Completely oblivious to social etiquette, basic decorum, and even the distinction between themselves and others, they managed with great effort to speak to him in tearful, trembling words.

श्रीमान् किं त्वं पितृजनकृते प्रेषितो निर्दयेन
क्वासौ कान्तो नगरसुदृशां हा हरे नाथ पायाः ।
आश्लेषाणाममृतवपुषो हन्त ते चुम्बनानाम्
उन्मादानां कुहकवचसां विस्मरेत् कान्त का वा ॥76.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रीमान् O noble one, किम् is it that, त्वम् you, पितृजन-कृते for the sake of His parents, प्रेषितः have been sent, निर्दयेन by that heartless one, क्व असौ where is he, कान्तः the beloved, नगर-सुदृशाम् of the beautiful-eyed women of the city, हा Alas, हरे O Lord Hari, नाथ O Master, पायाः please protect us, आश्लेषाणाम् of the tight embraces, अमृत-वपुषः of Him whose form is divine nectar, हन्त Alas, ते of those, चुम्बनानाम् of the kisses, उन्मादानाम् of the intoxicating moments, कुहक-वचसाम् of the enchanting, deceptive words, विस्मरेत् could ever forget, कान्त O beloved, का वा which woman indeed

Simple Meaning: The gopis addressed Uddhava: "O noble one, have you been sent here by that heartless one merely to comfort His parents? Where is He now—that beloved lover of the charming city maidens? Alas! O Lord Hari! O Master, protect us! Who could ever forget His divine, nectar-like form, His sweet embraces, His intoxicating kisses, and His enchantingly deceptive words? O beloved, which woman could ever forget those moments?"

रासक्रीडालुलितललितं विश्लथत्केशपाशं
मन्दोद्भिन्नश्रमजलकणं लोभनीयं त्वदङ्गम् ।
कारुण्याब्धे सकृदपि समालिङ्गितुं दर्शयेति
प्रेमोन्मादाद्भुवनमदन त्वत्प्रियास्त्वां विलेपुः ॥76.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: रास-क्रीडा-लुलित-ललितम् charmingly ruffled and exquisite from the joy of the rasa dance, विश्लथत्-केश-पाशम् with loosened locks of dark hair, मन्द-उद्भिन्न-श्रम-जल-कणम् glistening with soft droplets of perspiration, लोभनीयम् supremely captivating, त्वत्-अङ्गम् Your divine form, कारुण्य-आब्धे O ocean of mercy, सकृत् अपि even for a single moment, समालिङ्गितुम् to embrace, दर्शय please show to us, इति thus, प्रेम-उन्मादात् out of the madness of divine love, भुवन-मदन O Enchanter of the whole universe, त्वत्-प्रियाः Your beloved gopis, त्वाम् to You, विलेपुः lamented and cried out

Simple Meaning: "O Ocean of Mercy! O Enchanter of the entire universe!" cried Your beloved gopis in the ecstasy and madness of divine love. "Please reveal to us just once more Your supremely captivating form—the way it shone during the rasa dance with loosened hair, charmingly disheveled grace, and soft drops of perspiration glistening upon Your skin—so that we may embrace You again!"

एवंप्रायैर्विवशवचनैराकुला गोपिकास्ताः
त्वत्सन्देशैः प्रकृतिमनयत् सोऽथ विज्ञानगर्भैः ।
भूयस्ताभिर्मुदितमतिभिस्त्वन्मयीभिर्वधूभिः
तत्तद्वार्तासरसमनयत् कानिचिद्वासराणि ॥76.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् प्रायैः of such nature, विवश-वचनैः with overwhelmed and helpless utterances, आकुलाः distressed and agitated, गोपिकाः-ताः those gopis, त्वत्-सन्देशैः with Your messages, प्रकृतिम् अनयत् restored to composure and natural state, सः he (Uddhava), अथ then, विज्ञान-गर्भैः filled with profound spiritual wisdom, भूयः again, ताभिः with them, मुदित-मतिभिः whose minds were gladdened, त्वत्-मयीभिः who were completely absorbed in You, वधूभिः with those gopi women, तत्-तत्-वार्ता-सरसम् in sweet and delightful conversations about Your pastimes, अनयत् spent, कानिचित् वासराणि a few days

Simple Meaning: As the gopis lamented, completely helpless and overwhelmed by such passionate outpourings of love, Uddhava gently brought them back to composure by delivering Your message. Blessed with peace of mind and finding solace in Your words, those gopis—whose very selves were completely absorbed in You—spent the next few days with Uddhava engaging in sweet and joyful conversations recounting Your endless divine pastimes.

त्वत्प्रोद्गानैः सहितमनिशं सर्वतो गेहकृत्यं
त्वद्वार्तैव प्रसरति मिथः सैव चोत्स्वापलापाः ।
चेष्टाः प्रायस्त्वदनुकृतयस्त्वन्मयं सर्वमेवं
दृष्ट्वा तत्र व्यमुहदधिकं विस्मयादुद्धवोऽयम् ॥76.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-प्रोद्गानैः सहितम् accompanied by singing of Your glories, अनिशम् day and night without stopping, सर्वतः everywhere, गेह-कृत्यम् all household chores, त्वत्-वार्ता-एव conversations about You alone, प्रसरति spreads and prevails, मिथः among one another, सा-एव that alone, च and, उत्स्वाप-लापाः babblings in sleep, चेष्टाः actions, प्रायः mostly, त्वत्-अनुकृतयः imitations of Your divine plays and pastimes, त्वत्-मयम् pervaded by You, सर्वम्-एव everything indeed, दृष्ट्वा having witnessed, तत्र there in Vraja, व्यमुहत् अधिकम् was completely stunned, विस्मयात् out of wonder, उद्धवः अयम् this Uddhava

Simple Meaning: All household chores were continuously accompanied by the singing of Your glories day and night. Among one another, conversations centered solely on Your pastimes, and even in sleep, their dream-talk was about You alone. Their physical actions were mostly imitations of Your divine sports. Seeing that their entire world and existence were thoroughly pervaded by You, Uddhava was overwhelmed with wonder and awe.

राधाया मे प्रियतममिदं मत्प्रियैवं ब्रवीति
त्वं किं मौनं कलयसि सखे मानिनीमत्प्रियेव।
इत्याद्येव प्रवदति सखि त्वत्प्रियो निर्जने माम्
इत्थंवादैररमदयं त्वत्प्रियामुत्पलाक्षीम् ॥76.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: राधायाः of Radha, मे to Me, प्रियतमम् इदम् this is very dear, मत्-प्रिया my beloved, एवम् thus, ब्रवीति speaks, त्वम् you, किम् why, मौनम् silence, कलयसि maintain, सखे O friend, मानिनी मत्-प्रिया इव like my proud and sensitive beloved, इति आदि एव such words alone, प्रवदति speaks, सखि O friend, त्वत्-प्रियः your beloved Krishna, निर्जने in private, माम् to me, इत्थम् वादैः with such words and messages, अरमयत् अयम् he (Uddhava) gladdened, त्वत्-प्रियाम् Your beloved, उत्पल-अक्षीम् lotus-eyed Radha

Simple Meaning: “This is my Radha's favourite.” “My dear one speaks like this.” “My dear friend! Why are you silent like my dear proud one (Radha)?” In this way, O dear friend (Radhaa) Your beloved (Krishna) speaks to me when we are alone.”  With these loving messages and assurances of Your constant remembrance, Uddhava comforted and gladdened her heart.

एष्यामि द्रागनुपगमनं केवलं कार्यभारात्
विश्लेषेऽपि स्मरणदृढतासम्भवान्मास्तु खेदः ।
ब्रह्मानन्दे मिलति नचिरात् सङ्गमो वा वियोगः
तुल्यो वः स्यादिति तव गिरा सोऽकरोन्निर्व्यथास्ताः ॥76.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: एष्यामि I shall return, द्राक् very soon, अनुपगमनम् my non-arrival, केवलम् is solely, कार्य-भारात् due to the heavy burden of duties, विश्लेषे अपि even in physical separation, स्मरण-दृढता-सम्भवात् because of the possibility of unwavering remembrance, मा अस्तु खेदः let there be no grief, ब्रह्म-आनन्दे when the supreme bliss of Para Brahman, मिलति is attained, नचिरात् before long, सङ्गमः वा union or, वियोगः separation, तुल्यः equal, वः to you all, स्यात् will become, इति thus, तव गिरा through Your divine words, सः he (Uddhava), अकरोत् निर्व्यथाः ताः rendered those gopis free from anguish

Simple Meaning: Uddhava conveyed Your comforting words to the gopis: "I shall return to Vraja very soon. My present delay is solely due to the burden of duties in Mathura. Even in physical separation, our bond deepens through constant remembrance, so do not grieve. Before long, when you fully merge into the supreme bliss of Para Brahman, both union and separation will feel equal to you." By delivering this divine wisdom from You, Uddhava relieved the gopis of their agonizing sorrow and restored peace to their hearts.

एवं भक्ति सकलभुवने नेक्षिता न श्रुता वा
किं शास्त्रौघैः किमिह तपसा गोपिकाभ्यो नमोऽस्तु ।
इत्यानन्दाकुलमुपगतं गोकुलादुद्धवं तं
दृष्ट्वा हृष्टो गुरुपुरपते पाहि मामामयौघात् ॥76.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् भक्तिः devotion of such extreme degree, सकल-भुवने in all the worlds, न ईक्षिता has never been seen, न श्रुता वा nor ever heard of, किम् शास्त्र-ओघैः of what use are floods of scriptural lore, किम् इह तपसा of what use is intense penance here, गोपिकाभ्यः नमः अस्तु salutations again and again unto these gopis, इति आनन्द आकुलम् thus overcome with bliss, उपगतम् returned, गोकुलात् from Gokula, उद्धवम् तम् that Uddhava, दृष्ट्वा having seen, हृष्टः pleased, गुरुपुरपते O Lord of Guruvayur, पाहि माम् protect me, आमय-ओघात् from the host of diseases and afflictions

Simple Meaning: Returning from Gokula completely overcome with spiritual ecstasy, Uddhava exclaimed: "Devotion like this has never been seen or heard of anywhere in the world! Of what avail is the mastery of vast scriptures or the practice of severe austerities? Prostrations again and again to these gopis!" Beholding Uddhava return with such joy, You were pleased with Your dear friend. O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect me from all my diseases and afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
