देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 81

Krishna’s marriage to Kalindi, Mitravinda, Satya (Nagnajiti), Bhadra and Lakshmanaa.

Salvation of Narakasura, taking away of the Parijata tree from Indra and Krishna’s marriage to sixteen thousand women who were held captive by Narakasura

स्निग्धां मुग्धां सततमपि तां लालयन् सत्यभामां
यातो भूयः सह खलु तया याज्ञसेनीविवाहम् ।
पार्थप्रीत्यै पुनरपि मनागास्थितो हस्तिपुर्यां
सशक्रप्रस्थं पुरमपि विभो संविधायागतोऽभूः ॥81.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्निग्धाम् affectionate, मुग्धाम् charming and innocent, सततम् अपि constantly, ताम् her, लालयन् cherishing, सत्यभामाम् Satyabhama, यातः You went, भूयः again, सह with, खलु indeed, तया her, याज्ञसेनी-विवाहम् the wedding of Yaagnaseni (Draupadi - daughter of Drupada who was also known as Yagnasena), पार्थ-प्रीत्यै for the pleasure of Partha (Arjuna), पुनः अपि once again, मनाक् for a short time, आस्थितः staying, हस्तिपुर्याम् in Hastinapura, स-शक्रप्रस्थम् along with Shakraprastha (Indraprastha), पुरम् the city, अपि also, विभो O Omnipresent Lord, संविधायागतः अभूः You returned after helping establish it

याज्ञसेनी -  स्त्री, यज्ञसेनस्य स्त्र्यपत्यम् ॥ (यज्ञसेन् + अण् । ङीप् ।) सा च द्रौपदी  src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

यज्ञसेन यज्ञसेनः पुं. यज्ञः-सेनः - राजा द्रुपद का विशेषण src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

सेन सेन वि. - सह इनेन - बहुव्रीहिः समासः - 'प्रभु वाला, जिसका कोई स्वामी हो, नेता हो' src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

इन - m. a lord, master src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

(Yagnasena - one who had “Yagna” as his master - Drupada. Yaagnaseni is Yagnasena’s (Drupada’s) daughter)

Simple Meaning: O Omnipresent Lord, while showering affection upon Your charming and devoted Satyabhama, You traveled with her to attend the wedding of Draupadi. To delight Your beloved friend Arjuna, You stayed for some time in Hastinapura and subsequently helped establish the magnificent city of Indraprastha before returning to Dwaraka.

भद्रां भद्रां भवदवरजां कौरवेणार्थ्यमानां
त्वद्वाचा तामहृत कुहनामस्करी शक्रसूनुः ।
तत्र क्रुद्धं बलमनुनयन् प्रत्यगास्तेन सार्धं
शक्रप्रस्थं प्रियसखमुदे सत्यभामासहायः ॥81.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: भद्राम् auspicious, भद्राम् Subhadra, भवत्-अवरजाम् Your younger sister, कौरवेण by Duryodhana (the Kuru prince), अर्थ्यमानम् being sought in marriage, त्वत्-वाचा by Your instruction, ताम् her, अहृत carried away or abducted, कुहना-मस्करी disguised as a fake ascetic, शक्र-सूनुः the son of Shakra (Indra) - Arjuna, तत्र thereupon, क्रुद्धम् who was enraged, बलम् Balarama, अनुनयन् pacifying, प्रत्यगात् You returned, तेन सार्धम् along with him (Balarama), शक्रप्रस्थम् to Shakraprastha (Indraprastha), प्रिय-सख-मुदे to delight Your dear friend Arjuna, सत्यभामा-सहायः accompanied by Satyabhama

Simple Meaning: Your fair and auspicious younger sister Subhadra was being sought in marriage by Duryodhana. Acting upon Your secret advice, Arjuna, disguised as a wandering ascetic, abducted her. When Balarama became furious upon learning of this, You pacified him and returned with him to Indraprastha in the company of Satyabhama to bring joy to Your dear friend Arjuna.

तत्र क्रीडन्नपि च यमुनाकूलदृष्टां गृहीत्वा
तां कालिन्दीं नगरमगमः खाण्डवप्रीणिताग्निः ।
भ्रातृत्रस्तां प्रणयविवशां देव पैतृष्वसेयीं
राज्ञां मध्ये सपदि जहृषे मित्रविन्दामवन्तीम् ॥81.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there at Indraprastha, क्रीडन् sportively spending time, अपि च and also, यमुना-कूल-दृष्टाम् seen on the banks of the Yamuna river, गृहीत्वा accepting or marrying, ताम् her, कालिन्दीम् Kalindi, नगरम्-अगमः You returned to Your city Dwaraka, खाण्डव-प्रीणित-अग्निः having pleased Agni through the burning of the Khandava forest, भ्रातृ-त्रस्ताम् who was afraid of her brothers, प्रणय-विवशाम् helpless with intense love, देव O Lord, पैतृष्वसेयीम् Your father's sister's daughter, राज्ञाम् मध्ये in the midst of assembled kings, सपदि at once, जहृषे You abducted, मित्रविन्दाम् Mitravinda, अवन्तीम् princess of Avanti

Simple Meaning: While spending time happily at Indraprastha, You met Kalindi on the banks of the Yamuna river and accepted her as Your bride. After pleasing Agni through the burning of the Khandava forest alongside Arjuna, You returned to Dwaraka. Later, O Lord, You abducted Your father’s sister’s daughter Mitravinda, the princess of Avanti, right from the midst of assembled kings, as she was deeply in love with You and lived in fear of her brothers who opposed her union with You.

सत्यां गत्वा पुनरुदवहो नग्नजिन्नन्दनां तां
बध्वा सप्तापि च वृषवरान् सप्तमूर्तिर्निमेषात् ।
भद्रां नाम प्रददुरथ ते देव सन्तर्दनाद्याः
स्तत्सोदर्या वरद भवतः साऽपि पैतृष्वसेयी ॥81.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: सत्याम् Satya, गत्वा having gone, पुनः again, उदवहः You married, नग्नजित्-नन्दनाम् the daughter of King Nagnajit, ताम् her, बध्वा having bound, सप्त-अपि all seven, च and, वृष-वरान् formidable bulls, सप्त-मूर्तिः assuming seven forms, निमेषात् in the twinkling of an eye, भद्राम् Bhadra, नाम by name, प्रददुः gave in marriage, अथ then, ते Your, देव O Lord, सन्तर्दन-आद्याः Santardana and others, तत्-सोदर्याः her brothers, वरद O Bestower of boons, भवतः to You, सा-अपि she also, पैतृष्वसेयी Your father's sister's daughter

Simple Meaning: O Lord, You then traveled to the kingdom of Kosala and married Satya, the daughter of King Nagnajit, after taming and binding seven fierce bulls in the twinkling of an eye by expanding Yourself into seven forms. Thereafter, Santardana and her other brothers gave Bhadra to You in marriage. O Bestower of boons, she too was the daughter of Your paternal aunt.

पार्थाद्यैरप्यकृतलवनं तोयमात्राभिलक्ष्यं
लक्षं छित्वा शफरमवृथा लक्ष्मणां मद्रकन्याम् ।
अष्टावेवं तव समभवन् वल्लभास्तत्र मध्ये
शुश्रोथ त्वं सुरपतिगिरा भौमदुश्चेष्टितानि ॥81.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: पार्थ-आद्यैः अपि even by Arjuna and other great warriors, अकृत-लवनम् uncut or unpierced, तोय-मात्र-अभिलक्ष्यम् visible only through reflection in water, लक्षम् the target, छित्वा having pierced, शफरम् a fish, अवृथाः You won, लक्ष्मणाम् Lakshmanaa, मद्र-कन्याम् the princess of Madra, अष्टौ eight, एवम् thus, तव Your, समभवन् were, वल्लभाः principal queens/beloveds, तत्र मध्ये in the midst of this, शुश्रोथ त्वम् You heard, सुर-पति-गिरा from the words of lord of the devas - Indra, भौम-दुश्चेष्टितानि the wicked misdeeds of Bhauma - Narakasura

Simple Meaning: Even Arjuna and other mighty warriors could not hit the target fish, which was visible only as a reflection in water. You pierced the target with absolute precision and won Lakshmanaa, the princess of Madra. Thus, You had eight principal queens. In the midst of these events, You heard from Indra about the wicked misdeeds and atrocities committed by Narakasura.

स्मृतायातं पक्षिप्रवरमधिरूढस्त्वमगमो
वहन्नङ्के भामामुपवनमिवारातिभवनम् ।
विभिन्दन् दुर्गाणि त्रुटितपृतनाशोणितरसैः
पुरं तावत् प्राग्ज्योतिषमकुरुथाः शोणितपुरम् ॥81.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्मृत-आयातम् who arrived as soon as remembered, पक्षि-प्रवरम् the king of birds - Garuda, अधिरूढः riding upon, त्वम् You, अगमः went, वहन् carrying, अङ्के on Your lap or side, भामाम् Satyabhama, उपवनम् इव as if going to a pleasure garden, अराति-भवनम् to the abode of the enemy Narakasura, विभिन्दन् breaking through, दुर्गाणि the formidable fortifications, त्रुटित-पृतना-शोणित-रसैः with the streams of blood from the annihilated enemy army, पुरम् the city, तावत् thereupon, प्राग्ज्योतिषम् Pragjyotishapura, अकुरुथाः You made, शोणितपुरम् a city of blood

Simple Meaning: Mounted upon Garuda, who appeared instantly the moment You remembered him, You set out toward the enemy city Pragjyotishapura accompanied by Satyabhama, as effortlessly as though going to a pleasure garden. Shattering its formidable defenses and destroying the enemy forces, You transformed Pragjyotishapura into “Shonitapura” - a city of blood.

मुरस्त्वां पञ्चास्यो जलधिवनमध्यादुदपतत्
स चक्रे चक्रेण प्रदलितशिरा मङ्क्षु भवता ।
चतुर्दन्तैर्दन्तावलपतिभिरिन्धानसमरं
रथाङ्गेन छित्वा नरकमकरोस्तीर्णनरकम् ॥81.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: मुरः Mura, त्वाम् towards You, पञ्च-आस्यः the five-headed one, जलधि-वन-मध्यात् from the midst of the vast ocean like forest, उदपतत् rushed forth, सः he, चक्रे was made, चक्रेण by Your Sudarshana Chakra, प्रदलित-शिराः with his heads severed, मङ्क्षु quickly, भवता by You, चतुः-दन्तैः with four-tusked, दन्तावल-पतिभिः majestic elephants, इन्धान-समरम् who was inflaming the fierce battle, रथ-अङ्गेन with Your Sudarshana Chakra, छित्वा having severed, नरकम् Narakasura, अकरोः You made, तीर्ण-नरकम् cross over the hell (of the worldly cycle of birth and death)

दन्तावल (पुंलिङ्गः) हस्तिः src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

Simple Meaning: The five-headed asura Mura rushed out at You from the depths of his watery fortification. You instantly severed all five of his heads with Your Sudarshana Chakra. Then Narakasura himself entered the battlefield mounted on four-tusked royal elephants, waging a fierce war. Severing his head with Your discus as well, You freed Narakasura from the naraka  - hell - of the cycle of birth and death.

स्तुतो भूम्या राज्यं सपदि भगदत्तेऽस्य तनये
गजञ्चैकं दत्वा प्रजिघयिथ नागान्निजपुरीम् ।
खलेनाबद्धानां स्वगतमनसां षोडश पुनः
सहस्राणि स्त्रीणामपि च धनराशिं च विपुलं ॥81.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्तुतः praised, भूम्या by Bhudevi (Mother Earth), राज्यम् the kingdom, सपदि immediately, भगदत्ते अस्य तनये to his son Bhagadatta, गजम् च एकम् and one elephant (Supratika), दत्वा having given, प्रजिघयिथ You dispatched, नागान् निजपुरीम् to Your own city Dvaraka, खलेन-आबद्धानाम् who had been imprisoned by the wicked Narakasura, स्वगत-मनसाम् whose minds were completely surrendered to You, षोडश sixteen, पुनः furthermore, सहस्राणि thousands, स्त्रीणाम् अपि च as well as women, धन-राशिम् च विपुलम् and immense treasures of wealth

Simple Meaning: Praised by Bhudevi, You bestowed the kingdom of Pragjyotishapura upon Narakasura's son Bhagadatta, along with the magnificent four-tusked elephant Supratika. You then dispatched to Dwaraka the vast treasure of wealth as well as the sixteen thousand princesses who had been held captive by the wicked Narakasura and who had mentally chosen You as their lord.

भौमापाहृतकुण्डलं तददितेर्दातुं प्रयातो दिवं
शक्राद्यैर्महितः समं दयितया द्युस्त्रीषु दत्तह्रिया ।
हृत्वा कल्पतरुं रुषाभिपतितं जित्वेन्द्रमभ्यागमः
तत्तु श्रीमददोष ईदृश इति व्याख्यातुमेवाकृथाः ॥81.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: भौम-अपाहृत-कुण्डलम् the earrings stolen by Bhauma - Narakasura, तत् that, अदितेः to Aditi, दातुम् to return, प्रयातः proceeding, दिवम् to heaven (Devaloka), शक्र-आद्यैः महितः worshiped by Indra and other devas, समम् together, दयितया with Your beloved Satyabhama, द्युस्त्रीषु दत्त-ह्रिया who put the heavenly celestial women to shame by her beauty, हृत्वा taking away, कल्पतरुम् the Kalpataru - Parijata tree, रुषा अभिपतितम् who attacked in anger, जित्वा इन्द्रम् having defeated Indra, अभ्यागमः You returned, तत् तु that action indeed, श्रीमद्-अदोषः the flaw of pride caused by wealth and power, ईदृशः is like this, इति thus, व्याख्यातुम् एव to demonstrate or explain to the world, अकृथाः You did

Simple Meaning: You traveled to Devaloka accompanied by Your beloved Satyabhama—whose exquisite beauty eclipsed that of all the celestial damsels—to return to Mother Aditi the divine earrings stolen by Narakasura. Though Indra and the devas received You with great honor, Indra later fought You in anger when You uprooted the Parijata tree to take it to Dwaraka. Defeating Indra, You returned home with the tree. You enacted this pastime to demonstrate to the world how wealth and power can induce pride even in the lord of the devas.

कल्पद्रुं सत्यभामाभवनभुवि सृजन् द्व्यष्टसाहस्रयोषाः
स्वीकृत्य प्रत्यगारं विहितबहुवपुर्लालयन् केलिभेदैः ।
आश्चर्यान्नारदालोकितविविधगतिस्तत्र तत्रापि गेहे
भूयः सर्वासु कुर्वन् दश दश तनयान् पाहि वातालयेश ॥81.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: कल्पद्रुमम् the Parijata tree (Kalpataru), सत्यभामा-भवन-भुवि in the courtyard of Satyabhama's mansion, सृजन् planting or establishing, द्वि-अष्ट-साहस्र-योषाः two times eight (sixteen) thousand ladies, स्वीकृत्य accepting in marriage, प्रति-अगारम् in each and every house, विहित-बहु-वपुः assuming multiple identical forms, लालयन् delighting them, केलि-भेदैः with various loving pastimes, आश्चर्यात् with wonder, नारद-आलोकित-विविध-गतिः whose diverse daily activities were observed by Sage Narada, तत्र तत्र अपि गेहे in each and every home, भूयः furthermore, सर्वासु in all of them, कुर्वन् begetting, दश दश तनयान् ten sons each, पाहि protect me, वातालयेश O Lord of Guruvayur

Simple Meaning: O Lord of Guruvayur, You planted the divine Parijata tree in the courtyard of Satyabhama's mansion and formally married the sixteen thousand princesses. Expanding Yourself into as many forms as there were houses, You resided in each home simultaneously, delighting Your wives with various loving pastimes—a divine mystery that left Sage Narada filled with wonder when he observed Your simultaneous activities across Dwaraka. Furthermore, You begot ten sons through each of Your wives. O Lord, protect me from all my afflictions!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
