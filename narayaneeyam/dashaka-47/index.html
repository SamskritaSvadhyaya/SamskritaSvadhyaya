Krishna bound to the mortar

दशकम् ४७

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥

॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Narayaneeyam

Dashaka 47

Krishna bound to the mortar

एकदा दधिविमाथकारिणीं मातरं समुपसेदिवान् भवान् ।
स्तन्यलोलुपतया निवारयन्नङ्कमेत्य पपिवान् पयोधरौ ॥47.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: एकदा once, दधि-विमाथ-कारिणीम् the one who was churning curd, मातरम् Your mother Yashoda, समुपसेदिवान् approached, भवान् You, स्तन्य-लोलुपतया out of eagerness for mother's milk, निवारयन् stopping her (from churning), अङ्कम्-एत्य climbing onto her lap, पपिवान् drank, पयोधरौ from her breasts

Simple Meaning: Once, as Your mother Yashoda was churning curd, You approached her, eager to drink milk. Stopping her work, You climbed onto her lap and began to drink from her breasts.

अर्धपीतकुचकुड्मले त्वयि स्निग्धहासमधुराननाम्बुजे ।
दुग्धमीश दहने परिस्रुतं धर्तुमाशु जननी जगाम ते ॥47.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: अर्ध-पीत-कुच-कुड्मले when You had only half-drunk from her lotus-bud like breast, त्वयि You, स्निग्ध-हास-मधुर-आनन-अम्बुजे with Your sweet lotus face with a tender smile, दुग्धम् milk, ईश O Lord, दहने on the fire, परिस्रुतम् boiling over, धर्तुम् to put it down, आशु quickly, जननी mother, जगाम went, ते Your

Simple Meaning: O Lord! While You were still drinking milk—having consumed only half—and looking up at her with a sweet, tender smile on Your lotus face, the milk boiling on the fire began to spill over. Seeing this, Your mother quickly set You down and ran to save it.

सामिपीतरसभङ्गसङ्गतक्रोधभारपरिभूतचेतसा।
मन्थदण्डमुपगृह्य पाटितं हन्त देव दधिभाजनं त्वया ॥47.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सामि-पीत-रस-भङ्ग-सङ्गत-क्रोध-भार-परिभूत-चेतसा with a mind completely overwhelmed by intense anger due to the interruption of Your half-drunk drink, मन्थ-दण्डम् the churning rod, उपगृह्य grabbing, पाटितम् broken/smashed, हन्त Oh, देव O Lord, दधि-भाजनम् the pot of curd, त्वया by You

Simple Meaning: O Lord! With Your mind completely overcome by anger at having Your drinking so abruptly interrupted, Oh! You grabbed the churning rod and smashed the pot of curd!

उच्चलद्ध्वनितमुच्चकैस्तदा सन्निशम्य जननी समाद्रुता ।
त्वद्यशोविसरवद्ददर्श सा सद्य एव दधि विस्तृतं क्षितौ ॥47.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: उच्चलत्-ध्वनितम् the loud breaking sound, उच्चकैः loudly, तदा then, सन्निशम्य hearing, जननी Your mother, समाद्रुता rushed back, त्वत्-यशः-विसर-वत् like the spreading expanse of Your fame, ददर्श saw, सा she, सद्यः एव instantly, दधि the curd, विस्तृतम् spilled/spread everywhere, क्षितौ on the floor

Simple Meaning: Hearing that loud crashing noise, Your mother rushed back immediately. There on the floor, she saw the curd spilled and spreading, just like the ever-expanding expanse of Your fame!

वेदमार्गपरिमार्गितं रुषा त्वामवीक्ष्य परिमार्गयन्त्यसौ ।
सन्ददर्श सुकृतिन्युलूखले दीयमाननवनीतमोतवे ॥47.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: वेद-मार्ग-परिमार्गितम् who is sought after through the paths of the Vedas, रुषा angrily, त्वाम् You, अवीक्ष्य not seeing, परिमार्गयन्ती searching around, असौ she (Yashoda), सन्ददर्श saw, सुकृतिनी that fortunate woman, उलूखले perched upon a wooden mortar, दीयमान-नवनीतम् giving away fresh butter, ओतवे to a cat

Simple Meaning: Not finding You nearby, that fortunate and virtuous mother Yashoda—angrily searching around for You, who are sought through the paths laid down by the Vedas —spotted You sitting atop a wooden mortar, feeding the fresh butter to a cat.

त्वां प्रगृह्य बत भीतिभावनाभासुराननसरोजमाशु सा ।
रोषरूषितमुखी सखीपुरो बन्धनाय रशनामुपाददे ॥47.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वाम् You, प्रगृह्य catching hold of, बत Oh!, भीति-भावना-भासुर-आनन-सरोजम् Your lotus-like face shining with expressions of fear, आशु quickly, सा she (Yashoda), रोष-रूषित-मुखी with a face flushed with anger, सखी-पुरः in the presence of her gopi friends, बन्धनाय for binding You, रशनाम् a rope, उपाददे took up

Simple Meaning: Oh! Catching hold of You—whose lotus face was radiant with pretend fear—mother Yashoda, her face flushed with anger, quickly took up a rope in front of her friends to bind You to the mortar!

बन्धुमिच्छति यमेव सज्जनस्तं भवन्तमयि बन्धुमिच्छती ।
सा नियुज्य रशनागुणान् बहून् द्व्यङ्गुलोनमखिलं किलैक्षत ॥47.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: बन्धुम् इच्छति desires as a relative/friend, यम् एव Whom alone, सज्जनः righteous/virtuous people, तम् You, भवन्तम् You (Lord), अयि O Lord, बन्धुम् इच्छती desiring to bind, सा she (Yashoda), नियुज्य tying together, रशना-गुणान् बहून् many strands of rope, द्व्यङ्गुल-ऊनम् short by two inches, अखिलम् all of them, किल ऐक्षत indeed found

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Whom the noble and virtuous souls desire to tie themselves to, that You, Yashoda desiring to bind, tied together many strands of rope. But no matter how much she tried, she found the rope falling short by two fingers.

विस्मितोत्स्मितसखीजनेक्षितां स्विन्नसन्नवपुषं निरीक्ष्य ताम् ।
नित्यमुक्तवपुरप्यहो हरे बन्धमेव कृपयाऽन्वमन्यथाः ॥47.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: विस्मित-उत्स्मित-सखी-जन-ईक्षिताम् looked at by her friends who were both amazed and smiling, स्विन्न-सन्न-वपुषम् her body covered with perspiration and exhausted from effort, निरीक्ष्य seeing, ताम् her, नित्य-मुक्त-वपुः अपि though Your true nature is eternally free, अहो O wonder, हरे O Lord Hari, बन्धम् एव to be bound itself, कृपया out of compassion, अन्वमन्यथाः You allowed

Simple Meaning: Seeing mother Yashoda exhausted, dripping with perspiration, and being watched by her friends who were smiling in wonder, You—though eternally free from all bondage—out of pure compassion allowed Yourself to be bound, O Lord Hari!

स्थीयतां चिरमुलूखले खलेत्यागता भवनमेव सा यदा।
प्रागुलूखलबिलान्तरे तदा सर्पिरर्पितमदन्नवास्थिथाः ॥47.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्थीयताम् stay put, चिरम् for a long time, उलूखले at the mortar, खल O mischievous one, इति thus, आगता returned, भवनम् एव to the house, सा she, यदा when, प्राक् earlier, उलूखल-बिल-अन्तरे inside the hollow cavity of the mortar, तदा then, सर्पिः the butter, अर्पितम् stored there, अदन् eating, अव-अस्थिथाः You remained present

Simple Meaning: Saying, "O mischievous child! Now stay tied to this mortar for a long time!", when Yashoda went back into the house, You simply remained there, contentedly eating the butter You had previously hidden inside the hollow cavity of that very mortar.

यद्यपाशसुगमो विभो भवान् संयतः किमु सपाशयाऽनया ।
एवमादि दिविजैरभिष्टुतो वातनाथ परिपाहि मां गदात् ॥47.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: यदि if, अ-पाश-सुगमः accessible only to those free from worldly ties/fetters, विभो O All-pervading Lord, भवान् You, संयतः bound, किमु how then, स-पाशया by her who possessed a rope, अनया by this (Yashoda), एवम् आदि in this manner and so on, दिविजैः by the celestials/devas, अभिष्टुतः praised, वात-नाथ O Lord of Guruvayur (Lord of Wind's abode), परिपाहि माम् protect me completely, गदात् from my afflictions/diseases

Simple Meaning: "If You, O All-pervading Lord, are accessible only to those who are completely free from the ropes of worldly attachment (pasha), how then were You bound by mother Yashoda, who was holding a rope (pasha)?" Praised thus by the celestials, O Lord of Guruvayur, please protect me from all my ailments!

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
