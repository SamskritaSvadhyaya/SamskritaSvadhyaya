Privacy policy

What we keep when you sign in, and what we never do

ॐ

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Samskrita Svadhyaya is a free library of Sanskrit texts. Every page can be read without an account. Creating an account is optional; it only lets you keep bookmarks, mark chapters as studied, continue where you left off, write private notes, and — if you choose — receive emails about new chapters.

What we collect

  • If you only read the site: nothing. We use no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Your text-size and light/dark settings are stored only in your own browser.
  • If you create an account: your email address and, if you give one, your name. With “Continue with Google” we receive your name and email address from Google; we never see your Google password. With email and password, your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us.
  • What you save: the pages you bookmark or mark as studied, the shlokas you mark as favourites or as where you stopped, the last page you were reading (and how far down), and the notes you write.
  • Announcements: whether you asked to be emailed about new chapters.

Who can see it

  • Your bookmarks, progress and notes are private to you. The site's security rules allow only your own signed-in account to read or change them.
  • If you opt in to announcements, the site owner can see your name and email address so that they can tell you about new chapters. If you don't opt in, your email address is not shared with anyone.
  • We never sell, rent or share your information with anyone, and we never use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Accounts and saved data are kept with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), part of Google Cloud, which protects them with encryption in transit and at rest. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs of visits (such as IP addresses) for security purposes, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Your choices

  • Turn announcement emails on or off at any time on your Account page; every announcement email will also explain how to stop them.
  • Delete your account at any time from the Account page. This permanently erases your account, bookmarks, progress, notes and announcement preference.

Children

The library is open to readers of all ages. Children under 13 should create an account only with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions or requests about your data can be raised on the site's GitHub page.
