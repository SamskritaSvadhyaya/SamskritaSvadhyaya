सर्गः ३१

Sundarakanda Sarga 31

॥ॐ॥

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।

वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

असाध्यसाधकस्वामिन् असाध्यं तव किं वद।

 रामदूत कृपासिन्धो मत्कार्यं साधय प्रभो॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 31

Hanuman sings the story of glory of Rama within Sita's hearing

(Benefit: Strengthening relations and friendships)

(19 Shlokas)

एवं बहुविधां चिन्तां चिन्तयित्वा महाकपिः।

संश्रवे मधुरं वाक्यं वैदेह्या व्याजहार ह॥5.31.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, बहु-विधाम् of many kinds/various, चिन्ताम् thoughts/considerations, चिन्तयित्वा having thought, महाकपिः the great monkey (Hanuman), संश्रवे in the hearing, मधुरम् sweet, वाक्यम् words, वैदेह्याः of Vaidehi, व्याजहार spoke/uttered, ह indeed

Anvay: महाकपिः (हनुमान्) एवं बहुविधां चिन्तां चिन्तयित्वा, वैदेह्याः संश्रवे मधुरं वाक्यं व्याजहार ह।

Simple meaning: Having reflected upon various thoughts in this way, the great monkey Hanuman then spoke sweet words, in the hearing of Vaidehi.

राजा दशरथो नाम रथकुञ्जरवाजिमान्।

पुण्यशीलो महाकीर्तिरिक्ष्वाकूणां महायशाः॥5.31.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजा king, दशरथः Dasharatha, नाम by name, रथ-कुञ्जर-वाजि-मान् possessor of chariots, elephants, and horses, पुण्य-शीलः virtuous by nature, महाकीर्तिः of great fame, इक्ष्वाकूणाम् of the Ikshvaku lineage, महायशाः highly renowned

Anvay: रथकुञ्जरवाजिमान्, पुण्यशीलः, महाकीर्तिः, इक्ष्वाकूणां महायशाः दशरथः नाम राजा (अभवत्)।

Simple meaning: “In the line of the Ikshvakus there was a highly illustrious and greatly renowned king named Dasharatha, who was given to meritorious deeds and owned excellent chariots, elephants and horses.

राजर्षीणां गुणश्रेष्ठस्तपसा चर्षिभिस्समः।

चक्रवर्तिकुले जातः पुरन्दरसमो बले॥5.31.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजर्षीणाम् among sage-like kings, गुण-श्रेष्ठः best in virtues, तपसा in austerity, च ऋषिभिः and to sages, समः equal, चक्रवर्ति-कुले in the family of emperors, जातः born, पुरन्दर-समः equal to Purandara (Indra), बले in strength

Anvay: राजर्षीणां गुणश्रेष्ठः, तपसा ऋषिभिः समः च, बले पुरन्दरसमः, चक्रवर्तिकुले जातः।

Simple meaning: “He was the best in virtues among sage-like kings, equal to sages in austerity, equal to Purandara (Indra) in strength, was born in a family of emperors.

अहिंसारतिरक्षुद्रो घृणी सत्यपराक्रमः।

मुख्यश्चेक्ष्वाकुवंशस्य लक्ष्मीवान् लक्ष्मिवर्धनः॥5.31.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अ-हिंसा-रतिः devoted to non-violence, अ-क्षुद्रः not petty/noble, घृणी compassionate, सत्य-पराक्रमः truly valiant/one whose valor is founded on truth, मुख्यः च and the chief, इक्ष्वाकु-वंशस्य of the Ikshvaku dynasty, लक्ष्मीवान् possessed of prosperity and grace, लक्ष्मि-वर्धनः enhancer of prosperity/dignity

Anvay: सः अहिंसारतिः, अक्षुद्रः, घृणी, सत्यपराक्रमः, इक्ष्वाकुवंशस्य मुख्यः च, लक्ष्मीवान्, लक्ष्मिवर्धनः (आसीत्)।

Simple meaning: “He (Dasharatha) was devoted to non-violence, noble, compassionate, truly valiant, the chief of the Ikshvaku dynasty, endowed with prosperity, and an enhancer of grace and dignity.

पार्थिवव्यञ्जनैर्युक्तः पृथुश्रीः पार्थिवर्षभः।

पृथिव्यां चतुरन्तायां विश्रुतस्सुखदस्सुखी॥5.31.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: पार्थिव-व्यञ्जनैः with royal qualities/characteristics of a king, युक्तः endowed with, पृथु-श्रीः of great splendor/glory, पार्थिव-ऋषभः best among kings (literally, the bull among kings), पृथिव्याम् on the earth, चतुर्-अन्तायाम् extending in all four directions (i.e., throughout the world), विश्रुतः renowned, सुखदः giver of happiness, सुखी happy himself

Anvay: सः पार्थिवव्यञ्जनैः युक्तः, पृथुश्रीः, पार्थिवर्षभः, पृथिव्यां चतुरन्तायां विश्रुतः, सुखदः, सुखी च (आसीत्)।

Simple meaning: “He (Dasharatha) was endowed with the distinguishing traits of a great ruler, possessed of immense glory, the best among kings, renowned throughout the four quarters of the world, a giver of happiness to his subjects, and himself content and happy.

तस्य पुत्रः प्रियो ज्येष्ठस्ताराधिपनिभाननः।

रामो नाम विशेषज्ञः श्रेष्ठस्सर्वधनुष्मताम्॥5.31.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य his (i.e., of Dasharatha), पुत्रः son, प्रियः beloved, ज्येष्ठः eldest, तारा-अधिप-निभ-आननः whose face resembles the lord of the stars i.e moon (lit. moon-like face), रामः Rama, नाम by name, विशेषज्ञः highly knowledgeable/discerning/accomplished, श्रेष्ठः the best, सर्व-धनुष्मताम् among all bowmen (archers)

Anvay: तस्य (दशरथस्य) प्रियः ज्येष्ठः पुत्रः रामः नाम, ताराधिपनिभाननः, विशेषज्ञः, सर्वधनुष्मतां श्रेष्ठः (आसीत्)।

Simple meaning: “His (Dasharatha's) beloved and eldest son was named Rama, whose face resembled the moon, who was highly accomplished, and who was the greatest among all bowmen.

रक्षिता स्वस्य धर्मस्य स्वजनस्य च रक्षिता।

रक्षिता जीवलोकस्य धर्मस्य च परन्तपः॥5.31.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: रक्षिता protector, स्वस्य of his own, धर्मस्य dharma (righteousness or duty), स्व-जनस्य of his own people, च and, रक्षिता protector, जीव-लोकस्य of all living beings, धर्मस्य of dharma, च and, परन्तपः subduer (or tormentor) of enemies

Anvay: परन्तपः (रामः) स्वस्य धर्मस्य रक्षिता, स्वजनस्य च, जीवलोकस्य च धर्मस्य च रक्षिता (आसीत्)।

Simple meaning: “He, the subduer of enemies (Rama), is the protector of his own dharma (righteousness), the protector of his own people, and the protector of the dharma of all living beings.

तस्य सत्याभिसन्धस्य वृद्धस्य वचनात् पितुः।

सभार्यस्सह च भ्रात्रा वीरः प्रव्राजितो वनम्॥5.31.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य of that (king), सत्य-अभिसन्धस्य one firmly resolved in truth (i.e., committed to truth), वृद्धस्य aged (here refers to the father), वचनात् by the command/due to the word, पितुः of the father, स-भार्यः he along with his wife, सह च भ्रात्रा and together with his brother, वीरः the valiant one (Rama), प्रव्राजितः was exiled/went into exile, वनम् to the forest

Anvay: तस्य सत्याभिसन्धस्य वृद्धस्य पितुः वचनात् सह भ्रात्रा च सभार्यः वीरः (रामः) वनं प्रव्राजितः।

Simple meaning: “Obeying the command of his aged father who was firmly resolved in truth, that valiant Rama went into exile to the forest along with his wife and brother.

तेन तत्र महारण्ये मृगयां परिधावता।

राक्षसा निहताश्शूरा बहवः कामरूपिणः॥5.31.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: तेन by him (i.e., by Rama), तत्र there (in the forest), महारण्ये in the great forest, मृगयाम् hunting, परिधावता while roaming about/chasing, राक्षसाः rakshasas, निहताः were slain/killed, शूराः valiant, बहवः many, काम-रूपिणः those who could assume any form at will

Anvay: तत्र महारण्ये मृगयां परिधावता तेन बहवः शूराः कामरूपिणः राक्षसाः निहताः।

Simple meaning: “In that great forest, while Rama was engaged in hunting, many valiant rakshasas with magical powers (who could assume various forms) were slain by him.

जनस्थानवधं श्रुत्वा हतौ च खरदूषणौ।

ततस्त्वमर्षापहृता जानकी रावणेन तु॥5.31.10॥

वञ्चयित्वा वने रामं मृगरूपेण मायया।

Word-by-word meaning: जनस्थान-वधम् the slaughter at Janasthana, श्रुत्वा having heard, हतौ killed, च and, खर-दूषणौ Khara and Dushana, ततः thereafter, तु indeed/then, आमर्ष-अपहृता carried away by anger/seized by rage, जानकी Janaki, रावणेन by Ravana, तु indeed/then, वञ्चयित्वा having deceived, वने in the forest, रामम् Rama, मृग-रूपेण in the form of a deer, मायया through illusion/deceit

Anvay: ततः तु जनस्थानवधं खरदूषणौ हतौ च श्रुत्वा, वने मृगरूपेण मायया रामं वञ्चयित्वा तु रावणेन जानकी आमर्षापहृता।

Simple meaning: “Then, having heard about the slaughter at Janasthana and the killing of Khara and Dushana, Ravana, overcome with rage, then deceitfully abducted Janaki in the forest, after deceiving Rama by taking the form of a deer through his illusion.

स मार्गमाणस्तां देवीं रामस्सीतामनिन्दिताम्॥5.31.11॥

आससाद वने मित्रं सुग्रीवं नाम वानरम्।

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Rama), मार्गमाणः searching for, ताम् that, देवीम् divine lady, रामः Rama, सीताम् Sita, अनिन्दिताम्  blameless/faultless, आससाद met/came upon, वने in the forest, मित्रम् friend, सुग्रीवम् Sugriva, नाम by name, वानरम् a monkey (chief)

Anvay: सः रामः अनिन्दितां देवीं तां सीतां मार्गमाणः वने सुग्रीवं नाम वानरं मित्रम् आससाद।

Simple meaning: “While searching for that blameless divine lady Sita, Rama met in the forest a friend, the monkey chief named Sugriva.

ततस्स वालिनं हत्वा रामः परपुरञ्जयः॥5.31.12॥

प्रायच्छत् कपिराज्यं तत् सुग्रीवाय महाबलः।

Word-by-word meaning: ततः thereafter, सः he (Rama), वालिनम् Vali (the monkey king), हत्वा having slain, रामः Rama, पर-पुरञ्जयः conqueror of enemy cities, प्रायच्छत् gave/bestowed, कपि-राज्यम् the kingdom of the monkeys, तत् that, सुग्रीवाय to Sugriva, महाबलः the mighty one

Anvay: ततः सः परपुरञ्जयः महाबलः रामः वालिनं हत्वा तत् कपिराज्यं सुग्रीवाय प्रायच्छत्।

Simple meaning: “Thereafter, the mighty Rama, the conqueror of enemy cities, having slain Vali, gave that monkey kingdom to Sugriva.

सुग्रीवेणापि सन्दिष्टा हरयः कामरूपिणः॥5.31.13॥

दिक्षु सर्वासु तां देवीं विचिन्वन्ति सहस्रशः।

Word-by-word meaning: सुग्रीवेण अपि by Sugriva also, सन्दिष्टाः commanded/instructed, हरयः monkeys, काम-रूपिणः those who can assume forms at will, दिक्षु सर्वासु in all directions, ताम् देवीम् that divine lady (Sita), विचिन्वन्ति search for, सहस्रशः in thousands

Anvay: सुग्रीवेण अपि सन्दिष्टाः कामरूपिणः सहस्रशः हरयः सर्वासु दिक्षु तां देवीं विचिन्वन्ति।

Simple meaning: “Commanded by Sugriva, thousands of monkeys—capable of assuming any form at will—searched for that divine lady (Sita) in all directions.

अहं सम्पातिवचनाच्छतयोजनमायतम्॥5.31.14॥

अस्या हेतोर्विशालाक्ष्याः सागरं वेगवान्प्लुतः।

Word-by-word meaning: अहम् I, सम्पाति-वचनात् by the words (advice) of Sampati, शत-योजनम्-आयतम् stretching a hundred yojanas (a unit of distance), अस्याः हेतोः for her sake, विशालाक्ष्याः of the large-eyed (Sita), सागरम् ocean, वेगवान् swift one (with great speed), प्लुतः leapt/crossed

Anvay: सम्पातिवचनात् अस्याः विशालाक्ष्याः हेतोः वेगवान् अहं शतयोजनमायतं सागरं प्लुतः।

Simple meaning: “By Sampati’s words, for the sake of this wide-eyed lady (Sita), I swiftly leapt across the ocean, which spans a hundred yojanas.

यथारूपां यथावर्णां यथालक्ष्मीवतीं च ताम्॥5.31.15॥

अश्रौषं राघवस्याहं सेयमासादिता मया।

Word-by-word meaning: यथा-रूपाम् as to her form, यथा-वर्णाम् as to her complexion, यथा-लक्ष्मीवतीम् as to her grace/beauty (endowed with fortune/lustre), च and, ताम् she, अश्रौषम् I heard, राघवस्य of Raghava (Rama), अहम् I, सा इयम् this very one, आसादिता has been found, मया by me

Anvay: अहं राघवस्य यथारूपां यथावर्णां यथालक्ष्मीवतीं च ताम् अश्रौषं, सा इयं मया आसादिता।

Simple meaning: “I heard the description of her form, complexion, and grace from Raghava (Rama). That very lady, I have now found.”

विररामैवमुक्त्वासौ वाचं वानरपुङ्गवः॥5.31.16॥

जानकी चापि तच्छ्रुत्वा परं विस्मयमागता।

Word-by-word meaning: विरराम paused/became silent, एवम् उक्त्वा having said thus, असौ that (he), वाचम् speech/words, वानर-पुङ्गवः the foremost of the vanaras (Hanuman), जानकी Sita, च अपि and also, तत् श्रुत्वा hearing that, परम् great/utmost, विस्मयम् astonishment, आगता came into/felt

Anvay: असौ वानरपुङ्गवः एवम् उक्त्वा वाचं विरराम। जानकी च अपि तत् श्रुत्वा परं विस्मयम् आगता।

Simple meaning: That foremost of the vanaras (Hanuman), having spoken thus, became silent. And Janaki, having heard that, was filled with great wonder.

ततस्सा वक्रकेशान्ता सुकेशी केशसंवृतम्॥5.31.17॥

उन्नम्य वदनं भीरुश्शिंशुपावृक्षमैक्षत।

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, सा she (Sita), वक्र-केश-अन्ता having curly hair at the ends, सुकेशी having beautiful hair, केश-संवृतम् covered with hair, उन्नम्य having raised, वदनम् her face, भीरुः the timid (woman)/delicate/fearful lady, शिंशुपा-वृक्षम् the Shimshupa tree (a tree under which she was sitting), ऐक्षत looked at

Anvay: ततः भीरुः वक्रकेशान्ता सुकेशी सा केशसंवृतं वदनम् उन्नम्य शिंशुपावृक्षम् ऐक्षत।

Simple meaning: Then she, the timid one, with beautiful hair curling at the ends, lifted her face covered with tresses, and looked at the Shimshupa tree.

निशम्य सीता वचनं कपेश्च दिशश्च सर्वाः प्रदिशश्च वीक्ष्य।

स्वयं प्रहर्षं परमं जगाम सर्वात्मना राममनुस्मरन्ती ॥5.31.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: निशम्य having heard, सीता Sita, वचनम् the words, कपेः च of the monkey (Hanuman) also, दिशः च directions also, सर्वाः all, प्रदिशः च intermediate directions also, वीक्ष्य having looked at, स्वयम् herself, प्रहर्षम् joy/elation, परमम् supreme/great, जगाम attained/experienced, सर्वात्मना with her whole being, रामम् अनुस्मरन्ती remembering Rama (again and again)

Anvay: सीता कपेः वचनं निशम्य, सर्वाः दिशः प्रदिशः च वीक्ष्य, सर्वात्मना रामम् अनुस्मरन्ती, स्वयं परमं प्रहर्षं जगाम।

Simple meaning: Hearing Hanuman’s words, and looking all around in every direction and intermediate direction, Sita, remembering Rama with all her heart, herself experienced supreme joy.

सा तिर्यगूर्ध्वं च तथाप्यधस्तान्निरीक्षमाणा तमचिन्त्यबुद्धिम्।

ददर्श पिङ्गाधिपतेरमात्यं वातात्मजं सूर्यमिवोदयस्थम्॥5.31.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Sita), तिर्यक् sideways, ऊर्ध्वम् च and upward, तथा अपि also likewise, अधस्तात् downward, निरीक्षमाणा gazing/looking carefully, तम् him (Hanuman), अचिन्त्य-बुद्धिम् of incomprehensible intellect, ददर्श saw, पिङ्ग-अधिपतेः of the lord of tawny ones (Sugriva), अमात्यम् minister, वात-आत्मजम् son of the Wing-God (Hanuman), सूर्यम् इव like the sun, उदय-स्थम् risen/stationed at dawn (on the horizon)

Anvay: सा तिर्यक् ऊर्ध्वं च तथा अपि अधस्तात् निरीक्षमाणा, तम् अचिन्त्यबुद्धिं पिङ्गाधिपतेः अमात्यं वातात्मजम् उदयस्थं सूर्यम् इव ददर्श।

Simple meaning: She (Sita), looking in all directions—sideways, upwards, and downward— saw Hanuman, of unfathomable intellect, the minister of Sugriva (lord of the tawny monkeys), the son of the Wind-God, radiant like the rising sun.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे एकत्रिंशः सर्गः ॥31॥

Thus ends the thirty-first sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
