Sarga 26

सर्गः २६

ॐ

Sundarakanda Sarga 26

॥ॐ॥

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।

वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 26

Sita decides to end her sad life

(51 Shlokas)

प्रसक्ताश्रुमुखीत्यवं ब्रुवन्ती जनकात्मजा।

अधोगतमुखी बाला विलप्तुमुपचक्रमे॥5.26.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रसक्त-अश्रु-मुखी face filled with tears, इति एवम् ब्रुवन्ती speaking in this way, जनकात्मजा the daughter of Janaka (Sita), अधोगत-मुखी with face lowered downward, बाला young woman, विलप्तुम् उपचक्रमे she began to lament

Anvay: प्रसक्ताश्रुमुखी अधोगतमुखी बाला जनकात्मजा इति एवं ब्रुवन्ती विलप्तुम् उपचक्रमे।

Simple meaning: The young Sita, daughter of King Janaka, with tears flowing down her face and her head lowered, began to lament in this way.

उन्मत्तेव प्रमत्तेव भ्रान्तचित्तेव शोचती।

उपावृत्ता किशोरीव विवेष्टन्ती महीतले॥5.26.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: उन्मत्ता इव like a mad woman, प्रमत्ता इव like a careless or bewildered woman, भ्रान्तचित्ता इव with a confused mind, शोचती mourning or grieving, उपावृत्ता fallen down, किशोरी इव like a young female horse, विवेष्टन्ती writhing in agony, मही-तले on the ground

Anvay: उन्मत्ता इव, प्रमत्ता इव, भ्रान्तचित्ता इव, किशोरी इव शोचती महीतले उपावृत्ता विवेष्टन्ती…

Simple meaning: Like a mad woman, a bewildered woman, a woman with a confused mind, like a young female horse Sita fell to the ground, writhing and grieving.

राघवस्य प्रमत्तस्य रक्षसा कामरूपिणा।

रावणेन प्रमथ्याहमानीता क्रोशती बलात्॥5.26.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: राघवस्य of Raghava (Rama), प्रमत्तस्य who was unwary or negligent (unprepared), रक्षसा by a rakshasa, काम-रूपिणा who can change form at will, रावणेन by Ravana, प्रमथ्य having forced, having overpowered, अहम् आनीता I was brought, क्रोशती crying aloud, बलात् by force

Anvay: प्रमत्तस्य राघवस्य कामरूपिणा राक्षसा रावणेन प्रमथ्य क्रोशती अहं बलात् आनीता।

Simple meaning: “While Raghava was unwary, a shape-shifting rakshasa, Ravana, overpowered me by force and carried me away as I cried aloud.

राक्षसीवशमापन्ना भर्त्स्यमाना सुदारुणम्।

चिन्तयन्ती सुदुःखार्ता नाहं जीवितुमुत्सहे॥5.26.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: राक्षसी-वशम् आपन्ना having fallen under the control of the rakshasis, भर्त्स्यमाना scolded, rebuked, सुदारुणम् very harsh, cruel, चिन्तयन्ती thinking, reflecting, सु-दुःख-आर्ता profoundly distressed, suffering greatly, न अहम् I (am) not, जीवितुम् to live, उत्सहे wish

Anvay: राक्षसीवशमापन्ना सुदारुणं भर्त्स्यमाना सुदुःखार्ता चिन्तयन्ती अहं जीवितुं न उत्सहे।

Simple meaning: “Having fallen under the control of the rakshasis, rebuked harshly and suffering greatly, thinking (about my fate), I do not wish to live.

न हि मे जीवितेनार्थो नैवार्थेर्न च भूषणैः।

वसन्त्या राक्षसीमध्ये विना रामं महारथम्॥5.26.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: न हि मे जीवितेन अर्थः surely there is no purpose for me to live, न एव अर्थैः not by wealth, material possessions, न च भूषणैः and nor by ornaments, jewels, वसन्त्याः राक्षसी-मध्ये while living in the midst of rakshasis, विना रामम् महारथम् without Rama the great charioteer, warrior

Anvay: महारथं रामं विना राक्षसीमध्ये वसन्त्याः मे जीवितेन अर्थः न, अर्थैः एव न, भूषणैः च न।

Simple meaning: “For me, there is no purpose in living, nor in wealth, nor in jewels, while staying amid rakshasis without the great warrior Rama.

अश्मसारमिदं नूनमथवाप्यजरामरम्।

हृदयं मम येनेदं न दुःखेनावशीर्यते॥5.26.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: अश्म-सारम् hard, firm as stone, इदम् this, नूनम् surely, certainly, अथवा अपि or perhaps, अजर-अमरम् without age and death, eternal, हृदयम् heart, मम mine, येन by which, इदम् this, न not, दुःखेन by suffering, grief, अवशीर्यते is breaking, shattering

Anvay: इदं मम हृदयं नूनम् अश्मसारम् अथवा अजरामरं; येन इदं दुःखेन अपि न अवशीर्यते।

Simple meaning: “Certainly, this heart of mine must be made of stone, or perhaps it is eternal and ageless, for it is not breaking under this suffering.

धिङ्मामनार्यामसतीं याहं तेना विना कृता।

मुहूर्तमपि रक्षामि जीवितं पापजीविता॥5.26.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: धिक् माम् fie upon me!/shame on me!, अनार्याम् ignoble, unworthy, disgraceful, अ-सतीम् unchaste, unfaithful (or morally fallen), या अहम् I who am, तेन विना कृता separated from him (Rama), made without him, मुहूर्तम् अपि even for a moment, रक्षामि preserve, protect, keep, जीवितम् life, पाप-जीविता sinful in living, one whose life is sinful

Anvay: अनार्याम् असतीं मां धिक्! या पापजीविता, तेन विना कृता अहं, मुहूर्तम् अपि जीवितं रक्षामि।

Simple meaning: “Shame on me — an unworthy, unchaste woman — I, whose life is sinful, who has been separated from him (Rama), and still protect this sinful life even for a moment.

का च मे जीविते श्रद्धा सुखे वा तं प्रियं विना।

भर्तारं सागरान्ताया वसुधायाः प्रियंवदम्॥5.26.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: का च what (indeed) is there/what value, मे for me, जीविते in life, श्रद्धा faith, interest, attachment, सुखे वा or in happiness/pleasure, तम् him, प्रियम् beloved, विना without, भर्तारम् husband, सागर-अन्तायाः bordered by the ocean, वसुधायाः of the earth, प्रियम् वदम् sweet-spoken one

Anvay: सागरान्तायाः वसुधायाः प्रियंवदं भर्तारं तं विना जीविते वा सुखे वा मे का श्रद्धा।

Simple meaning: “What interest do I have in life or in happiness, without him — my beloved, sweet-spoken husband, the lord of this earth bounded by the ocean?

भिद्यतां भक्ष्यतां वापि शरीरं विसृजाम्यहम्।

न चाप्यहं चिरं दुःखं सहेयं प्रियवर्जिता॥5.26.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: भिद्यताम् let it be torn apart, भक्ष्यताम् वा अपि or let it be eaten, शरीरम् the body, विसृजामि अहम् I abandon/give up/renounce, न च अपि अहम् and I certainly do not, चिरम् for long, दुःखम् sorrow, suffering, सहेयम् can endure, प्रियवर्जिता deprived of the beloved

Anvay: भिद्यतां भक्ष्यतां वा अपि। अहं शरीरं विसृजामि।  प्रियवर्जिता अहं चिरं दुःखं न सहेयं च।

Simple meaning: “Let this body be torn apart or eaten — I give it up. Being separated from my beloved, I cannot endure this sorrow for long.

चरणेनापि सव्येन न स्पृशेयं निशाचरम्।

रावणं किं पुनरहं कामयेयं विगर्हितम्॥5.26.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: चरणेन अपि even with the foot, सव्येन the left one (considered impure or disrespectful), न स्पृशेयम्  I would not touch, निशाचरम् the night-ranger, i.e., Ravana, रावणम् Ravana, किम् पुनः what to speak of/how much less/how then, अहम् I, कामयेयम् love (him), विगर्हितम् despicable, contemptible

Anvay: निशाचरं विगर्हितं रावणम् अहं सव्येन चरेणेन अपि न स्पृशेयम्। किं पुनः कामयेयम् ?

Simple meaning: "I will not even touch that despicable night ranger Ravana with my left foot. What to speak of loving him.

प्रत्याख्यातं न जानाति नात्मानं नात्मनः कुलम्।

यो नृशंसस्वभावेन मां प्रार्थयितुमिच्छति॥5.26.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रत्याख्यातम् being rejected, turned down, न जानाति  does not understand/recognize, न आत्मानम् nor his own self, न आत्मनः कुलम् nor his own lineage/family, यः he who, नृशंस-स्वभावेन by cruel nature, with a merciless temperament, माम् me, प्रार्थयितुम् इच्छति desires to ask for/wishes to woo

Anvay: यः नृशंसस्वभावेन मां प्रार्थयितुम् इच्छति, (सः) प्रत्याख्यातं न जानाति, न आत्मानं, न आत्मनः कुलम्।

Simple meaning: “He who, with a cruel nature, wishes to woo me does not recognize rejection, nor does he know himself or the dignity of his lineage.

छिन्ना भिन्ना विभक्ता वा दीप्तेवाग्नौ प्रदीपिता।

रावणं नोपतिष्ठेयं किं प्रलापेन वश्चिरम्॥5.26.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: छिन्ना cut off, भिन्ना split apart, विभक्ता वा or dismembered, दीप्ते वा अग्नौ प्रदीपिता set ablaze in blazing fire, रावणम् to Ravana, न उपतिष्ठेयम् I would not approach/submit/serve, किम् प्रलापेन वः what’s the use of your lamentation?, चिरम् for long/for a long time

Anvay: छिन्ना भिन्ना विभक्ता वा, दीप्ते अग्नौ प्रदीपिता वा, (अहम्) रावणं न उपतिष्ठेयम्। किं वः चिरं प्रलापेन?

Simple meaning: “Even if I were cut, torn apart, dismembered, or burned in blazing fire — I would never submit to Ravana. So what is the use of you continuing this lamentation for long?

ख्यातः प्राज्ञः कृतज्ञश्च सानुक्रोशश्च राघवः।

सद्वृत्तो निरनुक्रोशश्शङ्के मद्भाग्यसङ्क्षयात्॥5.26.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: ख्यातः well-known, famous, प्राज्ञः wise, intelligent, कृतज्ञः च and grateful (one who remembers kindness), सानुक्रोशः च and compassionate, राघवः Raghava (Rama, son of the Raghu dynasty), सद्वृत्तः of good conduct, निरनुक्रोशः lacking pity, शङ्के I fear/I suspect, मत्-भाग्य-सङ्क्षयात् due to the decline of my fortune

Anvay: राघवः ख्यातः, प्राज्ञः, कृतज्ञः च, सानुक्रोशः च, सद्वृत्तः च; मद्भाग्यसङ्क्षयात् निरनुक्रोशः शङ्के।

Simple meaning: “Raghava is renowned, wise, grateful, compassionate, and of noble conduct — yet I fear he has become indifferent (unfeeling) due to the decline of my fortune.

राक्षसानां सहस्राणि जनस्थाने चतुर्दश।

येनैकेन निरस्तानि स मां किं नाभिपद्यते॥5.26.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: राक्षसानाम् सहस्राणि thousands of rakshasas, जनस्थाने in Janasthana, चतुर्दश fourteen, येन by whom, एकेन alone/by one person, निरस्तानि were destroyed, defeated, सः he (Rama), माम् me, किम् न अभिपद्यते why does he not come to rescue me?

Anvay: येन एकेन जनस्थाने राक्षसानां चतुर्दश सहस्राणि निरस्तानि, सः मां किं न अभिपद्यते?

Simple meaning: “He who alone destroyed fourteen thousand rakshasas in Janasthana — why does he not come for me?

निरुद्धा रावणेनाहमल्पवीर्येण रक्षसा।

समर्थः खलु मे भर्ता रावणं हन्तुमाहवे॥5.26.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: निरुद्धा restrained, held captive, रावणेन by Ravana, अहम् I, अल्प-वीर्येण of little strength, of inferior valor, रक्षसा by the rakshasa, समर्थः capable, powerful, खलु indeed, surely, मे my, भर्ता husband, रावणम् Ravana, हन्तुम् to kill, आहवे in battle

Anvay: अहम् अल्पवीर्येण राक्षसा रावणेन निरुद्धा; मे भर्ता रावणं आहवे हन्तुं समर्थः खलु। 

Simple meaning: “I have been captured by Ravana, a rakshasa of little strength — my husband is surely capable of killing him in battle.

विराधो दण्डकारण्ये येन राक्षसपुङ्गवः।

रणे रामेण निहतस्स मां किं नाभिपद्यते॥5.26.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: विराधः Viradha, दण्डकारण्ये in the Dandakaranya forest, येन by whom, राक्षसपुङ्गवः the foremost among rakshasas, रणे in battle, रामेण by Rama, निहतः was slain, सः he (Rama), माम् me, किम् न अभिपद्यते why does he not come for me?

पदँ गतौ means to go away; अभि+पद्यते - to go towards somebody

Anvay: दण्डकारण्ये रणे येन रामेण राक्षसपुङ्गवः विराधः निहतः, सः मां किं न अभिपद्यते?

Simple meaning: “Viradha, the foremost among rakshasas, was slain in battle by Rama in the Dandakaranya forest — why then does he not come for me?

कामं मध्ये समुद्रस्य लङ्केयं दुष्प्रधर्षणा।

न तु राघवबाणानां गतिरोधो भविष्यति ॥5.26.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: कामम् truly/indeed/even though, मध्ये in the middle, समुद्रस्य of the ocean, लङ्का इयम् this Lanka, दुष्प्रधर्षणा difficult to attack or overpower, impregnable, न तु but not, राघव-बाणानाम् of Raghava’s (Rama’s) arrows, गतिरोधः any obstruction, impediment, भविष्यति will be/would be

Anvay: कामं समुद्रस्य मध्ये दुष्प्रधर्षणा इयं लङ्का, राघवबाणानां गतिरोधः न तु भविष्यति।

Simple meaning: “Indeed, this Lanka may lie in the middle of the ocean and be difficult to conquer — but it will never be an obstacle to Rama’s arrows.

किन्नु तत्कारणं येन रामो दृढपराक्रमः।

रक्षसापहृतां भार्यामिष्टां नाभ्यवपद्यते॥5.26.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: किम् नु what indeed/what possibly, तत् कारणम् is that reason/the cause, येन by which/due to which, रामः Rama, दृढ-पराक्रमः firm in valor, of unshakable prowess, रक्षसा by a rakshasa, अपहृताम् abducted, भार्याम् (his) wife, इष्टाम् beloved, न अभ्यवपद्यते does not come to (rescue her)/does not act upon

पदँ गतौ means to go ; अभि+अव+पद्यते - to go towards somebody

Anvay: किं नु तत् कारणं – येन दृढपराक्रमः रामः रक्षसा अपहृताम् इष्टां भार्यां न अभ्यवपद्यते?

Simple meaning: “What indeed is the reason because of which Rama, who is so firm in valor, has not acted to recover his beloved wife who was abducted by a rakshasa?

इहस्थां मां न जानीते शङ्के लक्ष्मणपूर्वजः।

जानन्नपि हि तेजस्वी धर्षणं मर्षयिष्यति॥5.26.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: इह स्थाम् staying here/present here (in Lanka), माम् me, न जानीते does not know, शङ्के I suspect/I fear, लक्ष्मणपूर्वजः the elder brother of Lakshmana (i.e., Rama), जानन् अपि even if he knows, हि surely/indeed, तेजस्वी the powerful one/the radiant one, धर्षणम् this insult, outrage (i.e., my abduction or captivity), मर्षयिष्यति will tolerate/forgive/endure

Anvay: शङ्के, लक्ष्मणपूर्वजः तेजस्वी रामः इहस्थां मां न जानीते; जानन् अपि हि धर्षणं मर्षयिष्यति?

Simple meaning: “I fear that Rama, the powerful elder brother of Lakshmana, does not know that I am here. After knowing, he would never tolerate this outrage.

हृतेति योऽधिगत्वा मां राघवाय निवेदयेत्।

गृध्रराजोऽपि स रणे रावणेन निपातितः॥5.26.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: हृता इति  “(Sita) has been abducted”/"that I was carried off", यः he who, अधिगत्वा after learning/understanding/finding out, माम् me, राघवाय to Raghava (Rama), निवेदयेत् would report/inform, गृध्रराजः अपि even the king of vultures (Jatayu), सः he, रणे in battle, रावणेन by Ravana, निपातितः was slain/struck down

Anvay: यः मां “हृता इति” अधिगत्वा राघवाय निवेदयेत्, सः गृध्रराजः अपि रणे रावणेन निपातितः।

Simple meaning: “The king of vultures, who realized I had been abducted and could inform Raghava, was also slain in battle by Ravana.

Simple meaning: “Surely, that hero, the elder brother of Lakshmana, has abandoned his body on this earth and gone to the world of the gods, consumed by sorrow on my account.

धन्या देवास्सगन्धर्वाः सिद्धाश्च परमर्षयः।

मम पश्यन्ति ये नाथं रामं राजीवलोचनम्॥5.26.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: धन्याः blessed, देवाः the gods, स-गन्धर्वाः along with the gandharvas, सिद्धाः the siddhas, च and, परम-ऋषयः the supreme sages, ये who, पश्यन्ति see, मम my, नाथम् lord (husband), रामम् Rama, राजीव-लोचनम् lotus-eyed one

Anvay: ये राजीवलोचनं मम नाथं रामं पश्यन्ति, ते देवाः सगन्धर्वाः सिद्धाः च परमर्षयः धन्याः।

Simple meaning: “Blessed are the gods, the gandharvas, the siddhas, and the great sages who behold my lord Rama, the lotus-eyed one.

अथवा न हि तस्यार्थो धर्मकामस्य धीमतः।

मया रामस्य राजर्षेर्भार्यया परमात्मनः॥5.26.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथवा or else, न हि truly not, तस्य for him, अर्थः purpose/desire, धर्म-कामस्य one who desires righteousness, धीमतः wise, मया with me, रामस्य of Rama, राजर्षेः king amongst sages, भार्यया as wife, परमात्मनः of the supreme soul

Anvay: अथवा धर्मकामस्य धीमतः राजर्षेः परमात्मनः रामस्य मया भार्यया तस्य अर्थः न हि।

Simple meaning: “Or perhaps, Rama, the king amongst the sages and supreme soul, who is devoted to righteousness and full of wisdom, no longer desires me as his wife.

दृश्यमाने भवेत्प्रीतिस्सौहृदं नास्त्यपश्यतः।

नाशयन्ति कृतघ्नास्तु न रामो नाशयिष्यति॥5.26.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: दृश्यमाने when seen, भवेत् there is, प्रीतिः affection/joy, सौहृदम् friendship/affection, न अस्ति does not exist, अपश्यतः for one who does not see, नाशयन्ति destroy, कृतघ्नाः तु the ungrateful indeed, न not, रामः Rama, नाशयिष्यति will destroy

Anvay: दृश्यमाने प्रीतिः भवेत्, अपश्यतः सौहृदं न अस्ति, कृतघ्नाः तु नाशयन्ति, रामः न नाशयिष्यति।

Simple meaning: “Affection arises when one is seen; there is no intimacy with one who is not seen. The ungrateful destroy such bonds—but Rama will not destroy them.

किं नु मे न गुणाः केचित्किं वा भाग्यक्षयो मम।

याहं सीदामि रामेण हीना मुख्येन भामिनी॥5.26.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: किम् नु is it that perhaps, मे in me, न not, गुणाः केचित् some virtues, किम् वा or is it, भाग्य-क्षयः loss of fortune, मम mine, या अहम् that I, सीदामि am sinking/suffering, रामेण from Rama, हीना separated, मुख्येन the noble one, भामिनी as a glorious woman

Anvay: किं नु मे केचित् गुणाः न, किं वा मम भाग्यक्षयः, या भामिनी अहं मुख्येन रामेण हीना सीदामि।

Simple meaning: “Is it that I have no virtues, or is it the loss of my fortune, that I, a noble and glorious woman, suffer being separated from the noble Rama?

श्रेयो मे जीवितान्मर्तुं विहीनाया महात्मनः।

रामादक्लिष्टचारित्राच्छूराच्छत्रुनिबर्हणात्॥5.26.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रेयः better, मे for me, जीवितात् than living, मर्तुम् to die, विहीनायाः for one who is separated, महात्मनः from the great-souled one, रामात् from Rama, अक्लिष्ट-चारित्रात् of blameless conduct, शूरात् the valiant one, शत्रु-निबर्हणात् destroyer of enemies

Anvay: अक्लिष्टचारित्रात् शूरात् शत्रुनिबर्हणात् महात्मनः रामात् विहीनायाः जीवितात् मे मर्तुं श्रेयः।

Simple meaning: “For one who has been separated from the great-souled Rama— of blameless conduct, valiant, and destroyer of enemies— death is better than life.

अथवा न्यस्तशत्रौ तौ वने मूलफलाशिनौ।

भ्रातरौ हि नरश्रेष्ठौ संवृत्तौ वनगोचरौ॥5.26.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथवा or perhaps, न्यस्त-शत्रौ having renounced enmity/weapons, तौ those two, वने in the forest, मूल-फल-आशिनौ eating roots and fruits, भ्रातरौ the two brothers, हि indeed, नर-श्रेष्ठौ the best among men, संवृत्तौ have become, वन-गोचरौ dwellers of the forest

Anvay: अथवा नरश्रेष्ठौ न्यस्तशत्रौ तौ भ्रातरौ वने मूलफलाशिनौ वनगोचरौ संवृत्तौ हि।

Simple meaning: “Or perhaps, those two brothers—the best among men—have renounced violence, are living in the forest, eating roots and fruits, and have become forest-dwellers.

अथवा राक्षसेन्द्रेण रावणेन दुरात्मना।

छद्मना घातितौ शूरौ भ्रातरौ रामलक्ष्मणौ॥5.26.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथवा or perhaps, राक्षसेन्द्रेण by the chief of rakshasas, रावणेन by Ravana, दुरात्मना wicked-minded, छद्मना by deceit, घातितौ have been killed, शूरौ the valiant ones, भ्रातरौ the two brothers, राम-लक्ष्मणौ Rama and Lakshmana

Anvay: अथवा राक्षसेन्द्रेण रावणेन दुरात्मना छद्मना भ्रातरौ रामलक्ष्मणौ शूरौ घातितौ।

Simple meaning: “Or perhaps, the two valiant brothers, Rama and Lakshmana, have been deceitfully killed by the wicked-minded rakshasa chief, Ravana.

साऽहमेवङ्गते काले मर्तुमिच्छामि सर्वथा।

न च मे विहितो मृत्युरस्मिन् दुःखेऽपि वर्तते॥5.26.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा अहम्  I, एवम् गते काले under such circumstances, मर्तुम् इच्छामि wish to die, सर्वथा in every way, न च and not, मे for me, विहितः appointed, मृत्युः death, अस्मिन् दुःखे अपि even in this sorrow, वर्तते exists/is present

Anvay: सा अहम् एवं गते काले सर्वथा मर्तुम् इच्छामि। अस्मिन् दुःखे अपि मे मृत्युः विहितः न वर्तते।

Simple meaning: “I wish to die, by all means,  under such circumstances. Even in this sorrow, death is not destined for me.

धन्याः खलु महात्मानो मुनयस्त्यक्तकिल्बिषाः।

जितात्मनो महाभागा येषां न स्तः प्रियाप्रिये॥5.26.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: धन्याः blessed, खलु indeed, महात्मानः great-souled, मुनयः sages, त्यक्त-किल्बिषाः freed from sin, जित-आत्मनः self-controlled, महाभागाः greatly fortunate, येषाम् for whom, न स्तः do not exist, प्रिय-अप्रिये pleasure and pain/likes and dislikes

Anvay: महात्मानः त्यक्तकिल्बिषाः, महाभागाः जितात्मनः मुनयः धन्याः खलु , येषां प्रियाप्रिये न स्तः।

Simple meaning: “Truly blessed are the great-souled sages, freed from all sin, greatly fortunate and self-controlled, for whom pleasure and pain do not exist.

प्रियान्न संभवेद्दुःखमप्रियादधिकं भयम्।

ताभ्यां हि ये वियुज्यन्ते नमस्तेषां महात्मनाम्॥5.26.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रियात् from the dear/beloved, न संभवेत् does not arise, दुःखम् sorrow, अप्रियात् from the unpleasant, अधिकम् greater, भयम् fear, ताभ्याम् हि from both indeed, ये वियुज्यन्ते who are detached/free, नमः salutations, तेषाम् to those, महात्मनाम् great-souled ones

Anvay: प्रियात् दुःखम्, अप्रियात् अधिकं भयं न संभवेत्। ताभ्यां हि ये वियुज्यन्ते, तेषां महात्मनां नमः।

Simple meaning: “(To them) Sorrow from what is dear, and even greater fear from what is unpleasant — does not arise. Salutations to those great-souled ones who are free from both.

साऽहं त्यक्ता प्रियार्हेण रामेण विदितात्मना।

प्राणांस्त्यक्ष्यामि पापस्य रावणस्य गता वशम्॥5.26.51॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा अहम् I, that very woman, त्यक्ता abandoned, प्रिय-अर्हेण by the one worthy of love, रामेण by Rama, विदित-आत्मना self-knowing (wise), प्राणान् त्यक्ष्यामि will give up my life-breaths, पापस्य of the sinful one, रावणस्य Ravana's, गता वशम् fallen under the control.

Anvay: प्रियार्हेण विदितात्मना रामेण त्यक्ता, पापस्य रावणस्य वशं गता, सा अहं, प्राणान् त्यक्ष्यामि।

Simple meaning: “I, who have been abandoned by Rama—the self-knowing and beloved one— and fallen under the control of the sinful Ravana, will now give up my life.”

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे षडविंशः सर्गः॥26॥

Thus ends the twenty-sixth sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
