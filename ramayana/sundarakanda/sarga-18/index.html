मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।

वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 18

Ravana comes to see Sita

(32 Shlokas)

तथा विप्रेक्ष्यमाणस्य वनं पुष्पितपादपम्।

विचिन्वतश्च वैदेहीं किंचिच्छेषा निशाऽभवत्॥5.18.1॥

Word-by-word meaning:  तथा in that manner/thus, विप्रेक्ष्यमाणस्य of him who was closely observing/scanning, वनम् the forest, पुष्पित-पादपम् filled with blossoming trees, विचिन्वतः च and of him searching, वैदेहीम् for Vaidehi (Sita), किंचित् शेषा a little remaining, निशा अभवत् the night remained/the night was nearly over

Anvay: तथा पुष्पितपादपं वनं विप्रेक्ष्यमाणस्य, वैदेहीं विचिन्वतः च, निशा किंचित् शेषा अभवत्।

Simple meaning: Thus, while he (Hanuman) was observing the forest filled with blooming trees and searching for Vaidehi (Sita), only a little of the night remained.

षडङ्गवेदविदुषां क्रतुप्रवरयाजिनाम्।

शुश्राव ब्रह्मघोषांश्च स विरात्रे ब्रह्मरक्षसाम्॥5.18.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: षड्-अङ्ग-वेद-विदुषाम् of those who are well-versed in the Vedas with the six Vedangas (limbs of the Vedas: shiksha, kalpa, vyakarana, nirukta, chandas, jyotisha), क्रतु-प्रवर-याजिनाम् of those who perform excellent sacrifices, शुश्राव (he) heard, ब्रह्म-घोषान् च and the chanting of Vedic mantras, सः he (Hanuman), वि-रात्रे at the end of the night, ब्रह्म-रक्षसाम् of the brahma-rakshasas (Brahmin Rakshasas)

Anvay: विरात्रे सः ब्रह्मरक्षसां षडङ्गवेदविदुषां क्रतुप्रवरयाजिनां ब्रह्मघोषान् च शुश्राव।

Simple meaning:  At the end of the night, Hanuman heard the chanting of Vedic hymns by Brahma-rakshasas— who were well-versed in the Vedas and the six Vedangas and performers of great sacrifices.

अथ मङ्गलवादित्रशब्दैः श्रुतिमनोहरैः।

प्राबुध्यत महाबाहुर्दशग्रीवो महाबलः॥5.18.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथ then/thereafter, मङ्गल-वादित्र-शब्दैः by the auspicious sounds of musical instruments, श्रुति-मनः-हरैः delightful to the ears and mind, प्राबुध्यत woke up, महाबाहुः the mighty-armed one, दश-ग्रीवः the ten-necked one (Ravana), महाबलः the one of great strength

Anvay: अथ श्रुतिमनोहरैः मङ्गलवादित्रशब्दैः महाबाहुः महाबलः दशग्रीवः प्राबुध्यत।

Simple meaning: Then, to the delightful and auspicious sounds of musical instruments, the mighty-armed and powerful ten-headed Ravana awoke.

विबुध्य तु यथाकालं राक्षसेन्द्रः प्रतापवान्।

स्रस्तमाल्याम्बरधरो वैदेहीमन्वचिन्तयत्॥5.18.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: विबुध्य having awakened, तु indeed/then, यथा-कालम् at the proper/appointed time, राक्षस-इन्द्रः the lord of the rakshasas, प्रतापवान् the glorious/powerful one, स्रस्त-माल्य-अम्बर-धरः wearing garlands and garments that had become disarranged/loosened, वैदेहीम् (about) Vaidehi, अन्वचिन्तयत् he thought upon/he contemplated

Anvay: प्रतापवान् स्रस्तमाल्याम्बरधरः राक्षसेन्द्रः यथाकालं विबुध्य वैदेहीम् अन्वचिन्तयत्।

Simple meaning: The glorious king of the rakshasas (Ravana), having awakened at the right time, with his garlands and garments loosened, began to think about Vaidehi.

भृशं नियुक्तस्तस्यां च मदनेन मदोत्कटः।

न स तं राक्षसः कामं शशाकात्मनि गूहितुम्॥5.18.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: भृशम् intensely/exceedingly, नियुक्तः engaged/possessed, तस्याम् at her (Sita), च and, मदनेन by the god of love (Kamadeva), मद-उत्कटः intensely intoxicated (with passion), न not, सः he (Ravana), तम् that (desire), राक्षसः rakshasa (Ravana), कामम् that desire (lustful longing), शशाक was able, आत्मनि in his heart/within himself, गूहितुम् to conceal/to keep hidden

*शशाक - शक् शकँ मर्षणे  (दिवादिः उभयपदी अकर्मकः सेट् पुषादिः ) कर्तरि लिट्लकारः परस्मैपदम् प्रथमपुरुष​: एकवचन​ (to be able, to be possible)

Anvay: सः राक्षसः मदनेन मदोत्कटः, तस्यां भृशं नियुक्तः, तं कामम् आत्मनि गूहितुं न शशाक।

Simple meaning: That rakshasa (Ravana), intensely overwhelmed by passion and deeply affected by love for her (Sita), was unable to conceal his desire within himself.

स सर्वाभरणैर्युक्तो बिभ्रच्छ्रियमनुत्तमाम्।

तां नगैर्बहुभिर्जुष्टां सर्वपुष्पफलोपगैः॥5.18.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Ravana), सर्व-आभरणैः युक्तः adorned with all kinds of ornaments, बिभ्रत् bearing/wearing/displaying, श्रियम् अनुत्तमाम् unsurpassed splendour/wealth/beauty, ताम् that (grove or garden), नगैः बहुभिः with many trees, जुष्टाम् graced/adorned/enriched, सर्व-पुष्प-फल-उपगैः bearing all kinds of flowers and fruits

Anvay: सः सर्वाभरणैः युक्तः, अनुत्तमां श्रियं बिभ्रत्, सर्वपुष्पफलोपगैः बहुभिः नगैः जुष्टां तां (वनिकां)...

Simple meaning: He (Ravana), adorned with all kinds of ornaments and bearing unsurpassed splendour, entered that grove which was adorned with many trees bearing all kinds of flowers and fruits.

वृतां पुष्करिणीभिश्च नानापुष्पोपशोभिताम्।

सदा मदैश्च विहगैर्विचित्रां परमाद्भुताम्॥5.18.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: वृताम् surrounded (by), पुष्करिणीभिः च by lotus-filled ponds (pools/lakes), नाना-पुष्प-उपशोभिताम् adorned with various kinds of flowers, सदा always, मदैः च विहगैः with intoxicated (joyful, cooing) birds, विचित्राम् variegated/beautifully adorned, परम-अद्भुताम् extremely wonderful/truly marvelous

Anvay: नानापुष्पोपशोभितां, पुष्करिणीभिः वृतां, सदा मदैः विहगैः विचित्रां परमाद्भुतां (वनिकाम्)...

Simple meaning: (That grove was) surrounded by lotus-filled ponds, adorned with a variety of flowers, always resonant with intoxicated (joyful) birds — variegated and truly marvelous.

ईहामृगैश्च विविधैर्जुष्टां दृष्टिमनोहरैः।

वीथीः संप्रेक्षमाणश्च मणिकाञ्चनतोरणाः॥5.18.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: ईहामृगैः with magical/mirage animals (or decorative/artificial animals), च विविधैः and of various kinds, जुष्टाम् adorned, filled with, दृष्टि-मनोहरैः pleasing to the eyes, delightful to look at, वीथीः streets/pathways, संप्रेक्षमाणः च (Ravana) observing/looking around, मणि-काञ्चन-तोरणाः (with) archways of jewels and gold

Anvay: मणिकाञ्चनतोरणाः वीथीः, दृष्टिमनोहरैः विविधैः ईहामृगैः जुष्टां (वनिकां) संप्रेक्षमाणः…

Simple meaning: (Ravana) beheld the streets adorned with archways of gold and jewels, filled with various kinds of mirage-like animals (decorative or mythical creatures), and delightful to the eye.

नानामृगगणाकीर्णां फलैः प्रपतितैर्वृताम्।

अशोकवनिकामेव प्राविशत्संततद्रुमाम्॥5.18.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: नाना-मृग-गण-आकीर्णाम् filled with groups of various animals, फलैः प्रपतितैः वृताम् covered with fallen fruits, अशोक-वनिकाम् एव the Ashoka grove itself, प्राविशत् (he) entered, संतत-द्रुमाम् dense with trees/thickly grown with trees

Anvay: (रावणः) नानामृगगणाकीर्णां, फलैः प्रपतितैः वृतां, संततद्रुमाम् अशोकवनिकाम् एव प्राविशत्।

Simple meaning: Ravana entered the Ashoka grove, which was filled with various kinds of animals, and strewn with fallen fruits and was dense with trees.

अङ्गनाशतमात्रं तु तं व्रजन्तमनुव्रजत्।

महेन्द्रमिव पौलस्त्यं देवगन्धर्वयोषितः॥5.18.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: अङ्गना-शत-मात्रम् about a hundred women (attendants), तु indeed/then, तम् व्रजन्तम् him who was going, अनुव्रजत् followed, महेन्द्रम् इव like Mahendra (Indra, the king of gods), पौलस्त्यम् the descendent of Pulastya (Ravana), देव-गन्धर्व-योषितः celestial nymphs of the gods and gandharvas

Anvay: पौलस्त्यं तं व्रजन्तम् अङ्गनाशतमात्रं – देवगन्धर्वयोषितः महेन्द्रम् इव – अनुव्रजत्।

Simple meaning: As Ravana (grandson of Pulastya) proceeded, a hundred women followed him — like celestial nymphs of the gods and Gandharvas would follow Mahendra (Indra).

दीपिकाः काञ्चनीः काश्चिज्जगृहुस्तत्र योषितः।

वालव्यजनहस्ताश्च तालवृन्तानि चापराः॥5.18.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: दीपिकाः lamps, small lights, काञ्चनीः made of gold, कश्चित् some, जगृहुः took, held, carried, तत्र there, योषितः women, ladies, वाल-व्यजन-हस्ताः च​ with hands adorned by fans made of yak tail, ताल-वृन्तानि branches or leaves of the palm tree (तालवृन्त = palm branches), च अपराः and others

Anvay: तत्र काश्चित् योषितः काञ्चनीः दीपिकाः, अपराः वालव्यजनहस्ता: तालवृन्तानि च जगृहुः।

Simple meaning: Some women carried golden lamps, some held yak tail fans in their hands while others bore palm leaf fans.

काञ्चनैरपि भृङ्गारैर्जह्रुः सलिलमग्रतः।

मण्डलाग्रान् बृसीश्चापि गृह्यान्याः पृष्ठतो ययुः॥5.18.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: काञ्चनैः अपि even with gold, भृङ्गारैः pitchers, जह्रुः collected, gathered, सलिलम् water, अग्रतः in the front, मण्डल-अग्रान्  bent or rounded ends, बृसीः a roll of twisted grass, pad, cushion, च अपि and also, गृह्य carrying, अन्याः others, पृष्ठतः ययुः from behind, went (carrying)

Anvay: अग्रतः अपि काञ्चनैः भृङ्गारैः सलिलं जह्रुः। अन्याः मण्डलाग्रान् बृसीः च अपि गृह्य पृष्ठतः ययुः।

Simple meaning: Some women went ahead, carrying golden pitchers with water, others, carrying rounded cushions, followed behind.

काचिद्रत्नमयीं स्थालीं पूर्णां पानस्य भामिनी।

दक्षिणा दक्षिणेनैव तदा जग्राह पाणिना॥5.18.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: काचित् one (of the women), रत्नमयीम् made of jewels, bejeweled, स्थालीम् a vessel or tray, पूर्णाम् filled (with), पानस्य drink (beverage), भामिनी the beautiful woman, दक्षिणा on the right/right-hand side (also "right-handed woman"), दक्षिणेन with the right (hand), एव indeed, just, तदा  at that time, जग्राह took, held, पाणिना with (her) hand

Anvay: काचित् दक्षिणा भामिनी रत्नमयीं पूर्णां पानस्य स्थालीं दक्षिणेन पाणिना तदा एव जग्राह।

Simple meaning: One beautiful woman, using her right hand, then held a jeweled tray filled with a beverage.

राजहंसप्रतीकाशं छत्रं पूर्णशशिप्रभम्।

सौवर्णदण्डमपरा गृहीत्वा पृष्ठतो ययौ॥5.18.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: राज-हंस-प्रतीकाशम् resembling a royal swan, छत्रम् a parasol, umbrella (used as a royal insignia), पूर्ण-शशि-प्रभम् radiant like the full moon, सौवर्ण-दण्डम् with a golden handle (staff), अपरा another (woman), गृहीत्वा having taken, holding, पृष्ठतः from behind, ययौ went

Anvay: अपरा राजहंसप्रतीकाशं पूर्णशशिप्रभं छत्रं, सौवर्णदण्डं गृहीत्वा पृष्ठतः ययौ।

Simple meaning: Another woman, holding a parasol that resembled a royal swan, radiant like the full moon and with a golden handle, walked behind (him).

निद्रामदपरीताक्ष्यो रावणस्योत्तमाः स्त्रियः।

अनुजग्मुः पतिं वीरं घनं विद्युल्लता इव॥5.18.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: निद्रा-मद-परीत-अक्ष्यः with eyes affected (reddened or dazed) by sleep and intoxication (or sensual pleasure), रावणस्य of Ravana, उत्तमाः स्त्रियः the finest/most distinguished women, अनुजग्मुः followed, पतिम् their lord (Ravana), वीरम् the mighty one, the hero, घनम्  a cloud, विद्युत्-लता इव like lightning streaks

Anvay: निद्रामदपरीताक्ष्यः रावणस्य उत्तमाः स्त्रियः वीरं पतिं – विद्युल्लताः घनम् इव – अनुजग्मुः।

Simple meaning: Ravana's foremost women, with eyes dazed by sleep and intoxication, followed their heroic husband like flashes of lightning following a cloud.

व्याविद्धहारकेयूराः समामृदितवर्णकाः।

समागळितकेशान्तास्सस्वेदवदनास्तथा॥5.18.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: व्याविद्ध-हार-केयूराः with necklaces (हार) and armlets (केयूर) displaced or disarranged (व्याविद्ध), समामृदित-वर्णकाः with body paint (cosmetics, makeup) rubbed or smeared off, समागळित-केशान्ताः with hair ends entangled or disheveled (coming undone), स-स्वेद-वदनाः तथा and with faces moist with sweat

Anvay: व्याविद्धहारकेयूराः समामृदितवर्णकाः समागळितकेशान्ताः तथा सस्वेदवदनाः (ताः स्त्रियः)।

Simple meaning: Their necklaces and armlets were displaced, their cosmetics rubbed off, their hair ends disheveled, and their faces moist with sweat.

घूर्णन्त्यो मदशेषेण निद्रया च शुभाननाः।

स्वेदक्लिष्टाङ्गकुसुमास्सुमाल्याकुलमूर्धजाः॥5.18.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: घूर्णन्त्यः staggering, swaying (while walking), मद-शेषेण due to the remnants of intoxication or passion, निद्रया च and from sleep, शुभ-आननाः with beautiful faces, स्वेद-क्लिष्ट-अङ्ग-कुसुमाः with flower-garlands (adorning the bodies) wilted or clung to the limbs due to sweat, सुमाल्य-आकुल-मूर्धजाः with their hair entangled or thickly covered with garlands

Anvay: मदशेषेण निद्रया च घूर्णन्त्यः शुभाननाः स्वेदक्लिष्टाङ्गकुसुमाः सुमाल्याकुलमूर्धजाः (स्त्रियः)।

Simple meaning: With beautiful faces, swaying from the remnants of passion and sleep, their flower-garlands clinging to their sweaty limbs, and their hair entangled with many garlands.

प्रयान्तं नैऋतपतिं नार्यो मदिरलोचनाः।

बहुमानाच्च कामाच्च प्रिया भार्यास्तमन्वयुः॥5.18.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रयान्तम् (as he was) going, नैऋत-पतिम् the lord of the rakshasas (i.e., Ravana), नार्यः women, मदिर-लोचनाः with eyes like wine (i.e., intoxicated-looking, beautiful eyes), बहुमानात् out of respect/reverence, च and, कामात् out of desire/love, च and, प्रियाः beloved, भार्याः wives, तम् him, अन्वयुः followed

Anvay: मदिरलोचनाः नार्यः, प्रियाः भार्याः बहुमानात् च कामात् च प्रयान्तं नैऋतपतिं तम् अन्वयुः।

Simple meaning: The women with intoxicating eyes, beloved wives, followed the lord of the rakshasas (Ravana) as he was going — out of reverence and love.

स च कामपराधीनः पतिस्तासां महाबलः।

सीतासक्तमना मन्दो मन्दाञ्चितगतिर्बभौ॥5.18.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Ravana), च and, काम-पराधीनः one who is subjugated/enslaved by desire (kama), पतिः the husband, तासाम् of those (women), महाबलः the mighty one, of great strength, सीता-आसक्त-मनाः with a mind attached to Sita, मन्दः dull, sluggish, confused, मन्द-अञ्चित-गतिः moving with slow and graceful steps, बभौ appeared/looked

Anvay: सः महाबलः तासां पतिः कामपराधीनः सीतासक्तमना: मन्दः मन्दाञ्चितगतिः बभौ।

Simple meaning: He, the mighty husband of those women, appeared enslaved by desire, with his mind attached to Sita, looking dull and slow-moving, walking with lethargic, hesitant steps.

ततः काञ्चीनिनादं च नूपुराणां च निस्वनम्।

शुश्राव परमस्त्रीणां स कपिर्मारुतात्मजः॥5.18.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, thereafter, काञ्ची-निनादम् च the sound of girdles (waist ornaments), नूपुराणाम् च निस्वनम् and the jingling sound of anklets, शुश्राव heard, परम-स्त्रीणाम् of the most excellent/royal women, सः कपिः that monkey (Hanuman), मारुत-आत्मजः the son of Wind-God

Anvay: ततः सः कपिः मारुतात्मजः परमस्त्रीणां काञ्चीनिनादं च नूपुराणां च निस्वनं शुश्राव।

Simple meaning: Then that monkey, Hanuman, the son of Wind-God, heard the tinkling sound of the girdles and the jingling of the anklets of the most exquisite women.

तं चाप्रतिमकर्माणमचिन्त्यबलपौरुषम्।

द्वारदेशमनुप्राप्तं ददर्श हनुमान् कपिः॥5.18.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् च and that (Ravana), अप्रतिम-कर्माणम् one whose deeds are unparalleled, matchless, अचिन्त्य-बल-पौरुषम् possessing inconceivable strength and valor, द्वार-देशम् अनुप्राप्तम् having reached the gateway area (entrance of Ashok Vanika or inner palace grounds), ददर्श saw, beheld, हनुमान् कपिः Hanuman, the monkey

Anvay: कपिः हनुमान् अप्रतिमकर्माणम् अचिन्त्यबलपौरुषं द्वारदेशम् अनुप्राप्तं तं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: The monkey, Hanuman saw Ravana, whose deeds were unparalleled and whose strength and valor were unimaginable, as he approached the entrance area.

दीपिकाभिरनेकाभिः समन्तादवभासितम्।

गन्धतैलावसिक्ताभिर्ध्रियमाणाभिरग्रतः॥5.18.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: दीपिकाभिः by lamps, अनेकाभिः many, समन्तात् on all sides, all around, अवभासितम् illuminated, lit up, गन्ध-तैल-अवसिक्ताभिः smeared with fragrant oil, ध्रियमाणाभिः being held or carried, अग्रतः in front

Anvay: (हनुमान्) अग्रतः ध्रियमाणाभिः गन्धतैलावसिक्ताभिः अनेकाभिः दीपिकाभिः समन्तात् अवभासितम् (तं ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw Ravana —Illuminated on all sides by many lamps soaked in fragrant oil, which were being carried in front.

कामदर्पमदैर्युक्तं जिह्मताम्रायतेक्षणम्।

समक्षमिव कन्दर्पमपविद्धशरासनम्॥5.18.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: काम-दर्प-मदैः युक्तम् filled with lust, arrogance and intoxication, जिह्म-ताम्र-आयत-ईक्षणम् with crooked, reddish, long eyes/gaze, समक्षम् इव as if present before the eyes/directly visible, कन्दर्पम् (like) Cupid (god of love/Kama), अपविद्ध-शर-आसनम् with his arrows and bow cast away

Anvay: कामदर्पमदैः युक्तं, जिह्मताम्रायतेक्षणं – समक्षं अपविद्धशरासनं कन्दर्पम् इव – (तं ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Filled with lust, pride, and intoxication, with long, reddish, sidelong glances, he (Ravana) appeared just like Cupid himself, standing directly in front, but with his bow and arrows cast aside.

मथितामृतफेनाभमरजोवस्त्रमुत्तमम्।

सलीलमनुकर्षन्तं विमुक्तं सक्तमङ्गदे॥5.18.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: मथित-अमृत-फेन-आभम् like churned nectar foam (i.e., dazzling white or radiant), अ-रजः-वस्त्रम् उत्तमम् excellent silken garment without dust, सलीलम् gracefully, with fluid movement, अनुकर्षन्तम् dragging or trailing along, विमुक्तम् loosened, let loose, सक्तम् clinging, attached, अङ्गदे to the armlet, i.e., ornament worn on the upper arm

Anvay: अङ्गदे सक्तं मथितामृतफेनाभम् उत्तमम् अरजोवस्त्रं विमुक्तं सलीलम् अनुकर्षन्तम् (तं ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw Ravana who wore an exquisite, delicate garment, resembling the foam of churned nectar, which was loosely hanging, gracefully trailing, and clinging to his armlet.

तं पत्रविटपे लीनः पत्रपुष्पघनावृतः।

समीपमिव संक्रान्तं निध्यातुमुपचक्रमे॥5.18.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम्  him (Ravana), पत्र-विटपे in a leafy branch, लीनः hidden, concealed, पत्र-पुष्प-घन-आवृतः covered densely with leaves and flowers, समीपम् इव as if nearby, seemingly close, संक्रान्तम् having come or approached, निध्यातुम् to observe closely, to scrutinize, to reflect upon, उपचक्रमे began, started

Anvay: (हनुमान्) पत्रविटपे लीनः, पत्रपुष्पघनावृतः, समीपम् इव संक्रान्तं तं (रावणम्) निध्यातुम् उपचक्रमे।

Simple meaning:  Hidden in the leafy branch, densely covered with leaves and flowers, Hanuman began to closely observe Ravana, who had seemingly come near.

अवेक्षमाणस्तु ततो ददर्श कपिकुञ्जरः।

रूपयौवनसम्पन्ना रावणस्य वरस्त्रियः॥5.18.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: अवेक्षमाणः तु looking attentively/observing carefully, ततः then, thereafter, ददर्श he saw, कपिकुञ्जरः the mighty monkey (i.e., Hanuman); रूप-यौवन-सम्पन्नाः endowed with beauty and youth, रावणस्य of Ravana, वर-स्त्रियः best/noble women; Ravana’s principal or most distinguished wives and consorts

Anvay: कपिकुञ्जरः तु ततः अवेक्षमाणः रावणस्य रूपयौवनसम्पन्नाः वरस्त्रियः ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Then the mighty monkey, while closely observing, saw Ravana’s noble women, endowed with beauty and youth.

ताभिः परिवृतो राजा सुरूपाभिर्महायशाः।

तन्मृगद्विजसङ्घुष्टं प्रविष्टः प्रमदावनम्॥5.18.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताभिः by those (women), परिवृतः surrounded, राजा the king (here, Ravana), सु-रूपाभिः by beautiful (women), महायशाः of great fame, renowned, तत् that, मृग-द्विज-सङ्घुष्टम् resounding with the cries/sounds of animals (मृग) and birds (द्विज), प्रविष्टः entered, प्रमदा-वनम् the pleasure garden (literally, "women's grove" or “garden of women”)

Anvay: राजा महायशाः सुरूपाभिः ताभिः परिवृतः तत् मृगद्विजसङ्घुष्टं प्रमदावनं प्रविष्टः। 

Simple meaning: The renowned king, surrounded by those beautiful women, entered the pleasure garden, resounding with the calls of animals and birds.

क्षीबो विचित्राभरणः शङ्कुकर्णो महाबलः।

तेन विश्रवसः पुत्रः स दृष्टो राक्षसाधिपः॥5.18.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्षीबः intoxicated, drunk, विचित्र-आभरणः adorned with various ornaments, शङ्कु-कर्णः having conical ears (or "Shankukarna" – a descriptive name for Ravana due to his ear shape), महाबलः very powerful, तेन by him (i.e., by Hanuman), विश्रवसः पुत्रः the son of Vishravas (i.e., Ravana), सः he, दृष्टः was seen, राक्षस-अधिपः the lord of the rakshasas

Anvay: क्षीबः विचित्राभरणः शङ्कुकर्णः महाबलः विश्रवसः पुत्रः सः राक्षसाधिपः तेन दृष्टः।

Simple meaning: Intoxicated, adorned with splendid ornaments, and with conical ears,  the powerful son of Vishravas, that lord of the rakshasas was seen by Hanuman.

वृतः परमनारीभिस्ताराभिरिव चन्द्रमाः।

तं ददर्श महातेजास्तेजोवन्तं महाकपिः॥5.18.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: वृतः surrounded, परम-नारीभिः by the most excellent women, ताराभिः by stars, इव like, as if, चन्द्रमाः the moon, तम् him (Ravana), ददर्श saw, महातेजाः possessing great brilliance, great energy (referring to Hanuman), तेजोवन्तम् radiant, resplendent, full of power, महाकपिः the great monkey (Hanuman)

Anvay: महातेजाः महाकपिः तेजोवन्तं तं (रावणं) परमनारीभिः –  चन्द्रमाः ताराभिः इव – वृतं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: The great and radiant Hanuman saw that powerful and resplendent Ravana, surrounded by the most beautiful women, just like the moon is surrounded by stars.

रावणोऽयं महाबाहुरिति सञ्चिन्त्य वानरः।

अवप्लुतो महातेजा हनुमान्मारुतात्मजः॥5.18.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: रावणः अयम् This is Ravana, महाबाहुः the mighty-armed one, इति thus, सञ्चिन्त्य having thought, reflecting, considering, वानरः the monkey, अवप्लुतः leapt down, jumped away, महातेजाः of great energy, radiant one, हनुमान् Hanuman, मारुतात्मजः son of the Wind-God

Anvay: वानरः महातेजाः हनुमान् मारुतात्मजः अयं महाबाहुः रावणः इति सञ्चिन्त्य अवप्लुतः।

Simple meaning: The radiant vanara, Hanuman, the son of the Wind-God, having thought, “This mighty-armed one is Ravana,” leapt away (from where he was hiding or observing).

स तथाप्युग्रतेजाः सन्निर्धूतस्तस्य तेजसा।

पत्रगुह्यान्तरे सक्तो हनुमान् संवृतोऽभवत्॥5.18.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (referring to Hanuman), तथा अपि even so, despite that, उग्र-तेजाः of fierce energy, with immense brilliance, सन्निर्धूतः shaken, overpowered, staggered, तस्य तेजसा by his (Ravana’s) radiance/energy, पत्र-गुह्य-अन्तरे in the inner space of leafy branches, within the leafy cover, सक्तः attached, clung to, hidden within, हनुमान् Hanuman, संवृतः अभवत् became concealed, was covered, remained hidden

Anvay: सः हनुमान् तथा अपि उग्रतेजाः सन् तस्य (रावणस्य) तेजसा सन्निर्धूतः, पत्रगुह्यान्तरे सक्तः संवृतः अभवत्।

Simple meaning: Even though Hanuman was of fierce brilliance, he was shaken by the radiance of Ravana. Clinging to the inner cover of leafy branches, he remained hidden and concealed.

स तामसितकेशान्तां सुश्रोणीं संहतस्त्नीम्।

दिदृक्षुरसितापाङ्गामुपावर्तत रावणः॥5.18.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः He (referring to Ravana), ताम् her (referring to Sita), असित-केश- अन्ताम् one whose dark hair framed her face, with black hair at the ends, सुश्रोणीम् of lovely hips, gracefully-hipped woman, संहत-स्त्नीम् with firm and full breasts, दिदृक्षुः desiring to see, eager to behold, असित-अपाङ्गाम् with dark (black) eyes, having dark corners of the eyes, उपावर्तत turned towards, approached, moved toward, रावणः Ravana

Anvay: सः रावणः असितकेशान्तां सुश्रोणीं संहतस्त्नीम् असितापाङ्गां तां दिदृक्षुः उपावर्तत।

Simple meaning: Ravana, desiring to behold her, she of dark hair, graceful hips, firm breasts, and dark, beautiful eyes, turned towards her.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे अष्टादशः सर्गः॥18॥

Thus ends the eighteenth sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
