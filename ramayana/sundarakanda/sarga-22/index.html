Sundarakanda Sarga 22

॥ॐ॥

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।

वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 22

Ravana appeals to Sita, asks the rakshasi guards to change her mind, gives ultimatum to Sita

(46 Shlokas)

सीताया वचनं श्रुत्वा परुषं राक्षसाधिपः।

प्रत्युवाच ततः सीतां विप्रियं प्रियदर्शनाम्॥5.22.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: सीतायाः of Sita, वचनम् words/speech, श्रुत्वा having heard, परुषम्  harsh/bitter, राक्षस-अधिपः the lord of rakshasas (i.e., Ravana), प्रत्युवाच replied/said in return, ततः then/thereafter, सीताम् to Sita, विप्रियम् unpleasant/harsh words, प्रिय-दर्शनाम् one who is pleasant to behold/lovely in appearance

Anvay: राक्षसाधिपः सीतायाः परुषं वचनं श्रुत्वा ततः प्रियदर्शनां सीतां विप्रियं प्रत्युवाच।

Simple meaning: Hearing Sita's harsh words, Ravana, the lord of the rakshasas, then replied to her — who was lovely in appearance — with unpleasant (words).

यथा यथा सान्त्वयिता वश्यः स्त्रीणां तथा तथा।

यथा यथा प्रियं वक्ता परिभूतस्तथा तथा॥5.22.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: यथा यथा in whatever manner/the more and more, सान्त्वयिता one who pacifies/flatters, वश्यः becomes submissive/is controlled, स्त्रीणाम् by women, तथा तथा in the same proportion/accordingly, यथा यथा however, in whatever manner, प्रियम् वक्ता one who speaks sweetly/says pleasant things, परिभूतः gets insulted/is disrespected, तथा तथा likewise/accordingly

Anvay: यथा यथा सान्त्वयिता तथा तथा स्त्रीणां वश्यः। यथा यथा प्रियं वक्ता तथा तथा परिभूतः।

Simple meaning: “The courteous a man is to women, the more agreeable he becomes to them. In your case, however, I find that the more kind words I speak the more I am snubbed by you.

सन्नियच्छति मे क्रोधं त्वयि कामः समुत्थितः।

द्रवतोऽमार्गमासाद्य हयानिव सुसारथिः॥5.22.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सन्नियच्छति restrains/holds back, मे my, क्रोधम् anger, त्वयि towards you, कामः desire/lust, समुत्थितः which has arisen/awakened, द्रवतः running/galloping, अमार्गम् the wrong path, आसाद्य having reached/upon encountering, हयान् horses, इव like, सुसारथिः a skilled charioteer

Anvay: त्वयि समुत्थितः कामः – सुसारथिः अमार्गम् आसाद्य द्रवतः हयान् इव – मे क्रोधं सन्नियच्छति।

Simple meaning: “My desire for you, which has arisen, is restraining my anger toward you—just like a skilled charioteer controls galloping horses that have strayed onto the wrong path.

वामः कामो मनुष्याणां यस्मिन् किल निबध्यते।

जने तस्मिंस्त्वनुक्रोशः स्नेहश्च किल जायते॥5.22.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: वामः fickle/inconstant/unpredictable, कामः desire/love/lust, मनुष्याणाम् of human beings, यस्मिन् in whom/upon whom, किल indeed/surely, निबध्यते gets bound/attaches, जने to a person/in a person, तस्मिन् in that (person), तु (emphatic particle, here refers to “certainly”), अनुक्रोशः compassion/pity, स्नेहः affection/love, च and, किल indeed/truly, जायते arises/is born

Anvay: मनुष्याणां वामः कामः यस्मिन् जने निबध्यते, तस्मिन् (जने) तु अनुक्रोशः च स्नेहः च किल जायते।

Simple meaning: “The fickle desire of human beings, once it gets attached to someone, certainly gives rise to both compassion and affection toward that person.

एतस्मात्कारणान्न त्वां घातयामि वरानने।

वधार्हामवमानार्हां मिथ्याप्रव्रजिते रताम्॥5.22.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतस्मात् कारणात् for this reason/due to this cause, न not, त्वाम् you, घातयामि I cause (you) to be killed/I slay, वरानने O beautiful-faced one!, वध-अर्हाम् deserving of death, अवमान-अर्हाम् deserving of insult/contempt, मिथ्या-प्रव्रजिते (in Rama) who is in false exile, रताम् one devoted to/attached to

Anvay: वरानने! एतस्मात् कारणात्, वधार्हाम् अवमानार्हां मिथ्याप्रव्रजिते रतां त्वां न घातयामि।

Simple meaning: "O beautiful-faced one! For this reason, I do not kill you, who you are deserving of death and insult and are devoted to one who is in a false exile (i.e., Rama).

परुषाणीह वाक्यानि यानि यानि ब्रवीषि माम्।

तेषु तेषु वधो युक्तस्तव मैथिलि दारुणः॥5.22.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: परुषाणि harsh/bitter, इह here/in this situation, वाक्यानि words/statements, यानि यानि whichever (plural emphatic), ब्रवीषि you speak/say, माम् to me, तेषु तेषु for each of those/in all those, वधः killing/punishment by death, युक्तः appropriate/justified, तव your, मैथिलि O daughter of Mithila/Sita, दारुणः severe/terrible/harsh

Anvay: मैथिलि! यानि यानि परुषाणि वाक्यानि इह मां ब्रवीषि, तेषु तेषु तव दारुणः वधः युक्तः।

Simple meaning: "O Maithili (Sita)! For every harsh word that you speak to me here, a terrible death would be a justified punishment for you.

एवमुक्त्वा तु वैदेहीं रावणो राक्षसाधिपः।

क्रोधसंरम्भसंयुक्तः सीतामुत्तरमब्रवीत्॥5.22.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus/in this manner, उक्त्वा having said/after speaking, तु but/then (used for emphasis), वैदेहीम् Vaidehi, रावणः Ravana, राक्षसाधिपः king of the rakshasas, क्रोध-संरम्भ-संयुक्तः filled with rage and agitation, सीताम् to Sita, उत्तरम् a reply/further speech, अब्रवीत् said/spoke

Anvay: राक्षसाधिपः रावणः एवं वैदेहीम् उक्त्वा, क्रोधसंरम्भसंयुक्तः सीताम् उत्तरम् अब्रवीत्।

Simple meaning: “The lord of the rakshasas, Ravana, filled with anger and rage, after speaking thus to Vaidehi (Sita), addressed her again with a further response.

द्वौ मासौ रक्षितव्यौ मे योऽवधिस्ते मया कृतः।

ततः शयनमारोह मम त्वं वरवर्णिनि॥5.22.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्वौ मासौ two months, रक्षितव्यौ to be protected/kept safe, मे by me/for me, यः अवधिः that time limit/deadline, ते to you/for you, मया कृतः has been fixed by me, ततः after that, शयनम् bed, आरोह ascend/come to, मम my, त्वम् you, वरवर्णिनि O beautiful-complexioned lady/O one of excellent complexion!

Anvay: ते मया यः अवधिः कृतः, द्वौ मासौ मे रक्षितव्यौ। वरवर्णिनि! ततः त्वं मम शयनम् आरोह।

Simple meaning: "The time limit that I have set for you is two months, during which you shall be protected by me. After that, O lady of lovely complexion, you must ascend to my bed.

ऊर्ध्वं द्वाभ्यां तु मासाभ्यां भर्तारं मामनिच्छतीम्।

मम त्वां प्रातराशार्थमालभन्ते महानसे॥5.22.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऊर्ध्वम् द्वाभ्याम् तु मासाभ्याम् then after two months भर्तारम् as husband, माम् me, अनिच्छतीम् not accepting/unwilling, मम my, त्वाम् you, प्रातः अश​-अर्थम् for my morning meal, आलभन्ते they will prepare (for food)/handle (as meat), महानसे in the kitchen

Anvay: द्वाभ्यां मासाभ्याम् ऊर्ध्वं तु, मां भर्तारम् अनिच्छतीं त्वां, मम प्रातराशार्थं, महानसे आलभन्ते।

Simple meaning: "If, after two months, you still refuse to accept me as your husband, then you will be prepared for my breakfast — in the kitchen.

तां तर्ज्यमानां सम्प्रेक्ष्य राक्षसेन्द्रेण जानकीम्।

देवगन्धर्वकन्यास्ता विषेदुर्विकृतेक्षणाः॥5.22.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her (Sita), तर्ज्यमानाम् being threatened, सम्प्रेक्ष्य seeing, राक्षसेन्द्रेण by the king of Rakshasas (Ravana), जानकीम् Janaki (Sita), देव-गन्धर्व-कन्याः celestial maidens (daughters of gods and gandharvas), ताः they, विषेदुः felt dejected/were distressed, विकृतेक्षणाः with distressed eyes (due to grief or shock)

Anvay: राक्षसेन्द्रेण तर्ज्यमानां तां जानकीं सम्प्रेक्ष्य, ताः विकृतेक्षणाः देवगन्धर्वकन्याः विषेदुः।

Simple meaning: Seeing Janaki (Sita) being threatened by Ravana, the king of the Rakshasas, those celestial maidens — the daughters of the gods and gandharvas —felt dejected, their eyes distressed with sorrow.

ओष्ठप्रकारैरपरा वक्त्रनेत्रैस्तथापराः।

सीतामाश्वासयामासुस्तर्जितां तेन रक्षसा॥5.22.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: ओष्ठ-प्रकारैः with movements of the lips, अपराः some others (among the celestial women), वक्त्र-नेत्रैः with expressions of the face and eyes, तथा अपराः similarly, some others, सीताम् (to) Sita, आश्वासयामासुः consoled/reassured, तर्जिताम् (being) threatened, तेन रक्षसा by that rakshasa (Ravana)

Anvay: अपराः ओष्ठप्रकारैः, तथा अपराः वक्त्रनेत्रैः, तेन रक्षसा तर्जितां सीताम् आश्वासयामासुः।

Simple meaning: Some (celestial women) consoled Sita — who was being threatened by that Rakshasa (Ravana) — with gestures of their lips, while others did so with expressions of their faces and eyes.

ताभिराश्वासिता सीता रावणं राक्षसाधिपम्।

उवाचात्महितं वाक्यं वृत्तशौण्डीर्यगर्वितम्॥5.22.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताभिः by those (celestial women), आश्वासिता consoled, reassured, सीता Sita, रावणम् to Ravana, राक्षसाधिपम् the king/lord of rakshasas, उवाच spoke, आत्म-हितम् beneficial to herself (and also to Ravana if he had taken it rightly), वाक्यम् words, speech, वृत्त-शौण्डीर्य-गर्वितम् filled with the pride of noble conduct and moral strength (वृत्त – conduct, character, शौण्डीर्य – strength, valor, nobility, गर्वितम् – filled with pride, dignified)

Anvay: ताभिः आश्वासिता सीता राक्षसाधिपं रावणं वृत्तशौण्डीर्यगर्वितम् आत्महितं वाक्यम् उवाच।

Simple meaning: Consoled by those celestial women, Sita spoke to Ravana, the king of the rakshasas, words that were beneficial to herself and filled with the pride of noble conduct and strength.

नूनं न ते जनः कश्चिदस्ति निःश्रेयसे स्थितः।

निवारयति यो न त्वां कर्मणोऽस्माद्विगर्हितात्॥5.22.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: नूनम् surely, indeed, न not, ते for you, जनः कश्चित् any person (anyone at all), अस्ति exists, निःश्रेयसे स्थितः established in righteousness/focused on your true welfare, निवारयति restrains, stops, यः who, न त्वाम् does not restrain you, कर्मणः अस्मात् from this action/deed, विगर्हितात् despicable, reprehensible, condemnable

Anvay: नूनं ते कश्चित् निःश्रेयसे स्थितः जनः न अस्ति, यः त्वाम् अस्मात् विगर्हितात् कर्मणः निवारयति।

Simple meaning: “Surely, there is no one who truly cares for your ultimate welfare, who would restrain you from this reprehensible act.

मां हि धर्मात्मनः पत्नीं शचीमिव शचीपतेः।

त्वदन्यस्त्रिषु लोकेषु प्रार्थयेन्मनसापि कः॥5.22.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: माम् me, हि indeed/surely, धर्मात्मनः of the righteous soul (referring to Rama), पत्नीम् wife, शचीम् इव like Shachi (wife of Indra), शचीपतेः of the Lord of Shachi, i.e., Indra, त्वत् अन्यः anyone other than you, त्रिषु लोकेषु in all the three worlds (heaven, earth, and the underworld), प्रार्थयेत् would desire/seek, मनसा अपि कः even in the mind, who?

Anvay: धर्मात्मनः पत्नीं – शचीपतेः शचीम् इव – मां त्रिषु लोकेषु त्वत् अन्यः कः अपि मनसा अपि प्रार्थयेत् हि?

Simple meaning: “I am the wife of a righteous man, just like Shachi is to Indra. Who else but you, in all the three worlds, would even dare to desire me—even in thought?

राक्षसाधम रामस्य भार्याममिततेजसः।

उक्तवानसि यत्पापं क्व गतस्तस्य मोक्ष्यसे॥5.22.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: राक्षस-अधम O lowest among the rakshasas! रामस्य of Rama, भार्याम् (his) wife, अमित-तेजसः of boundless energy, immeasurable brilliance, उक्तवान् असि you have spoken, यत् पापम् that sinful (speech/act), क्व गतः where will you go (to escape), तस्य मोक्ष्यसे will you be freed from that (sin)?

Anvay: राक्षसाधम! अमिततेजसः रामस्य भार्यां यत् पापं उक्तवान् असि, तस्य क्व गतः मोक्ष्यसे?

Simple meaning: “O lowest of the Rakshasas! You have uttered sinful words to the wife of Rama, whose splendor is immeasurable. Where can you possibly go to escape the consequences of that sin?

यथा दृप्तश्च मातङ्गः शशश्च सदृशो युधि।

तथा मातङ्गवद्रामस्त्वं नीचः शशवत् स्मृतः॥5.22.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: यथा just as/like, दृप्तः च मातङ्गः a proud/strong elephant, शशः च and a hare, सदृशः similarly, युधि तथा in battle, in the same way, मातङ्गवत् रामः Rama is like the elephant, त्वम् you, lowly, नीचः शशवत् स्मृतः are like the lowly hare, and should be considered as such

Anvay: युधि यथा दृप्तः मातङ्गः शशः च सदृशः, तथा रामः मातङ्गवत्, त्वं नीचः शशवत् स्मृतः।

Simple meaning: “In a battle the way a proud elephant and a hare are, like-wise Rama is like the elephant, and you are like the lowly hare, and should be considered as such.

स त्वमिक्ष्वाकुनाथं वै क्षिपन्निह न लज्जसे।

चक्षुषोर्विषयं तस्य न तावदुपगच्छसि॥5.22.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः त्वम् the one such as you, इक्ष्वाकु-नाथम् the lord of the Ikshvaku dynasty (i.e., Rama), वै indeed (emphasizer), क्षिपन् insulting, reviling, speaking ill of, इह here, in this situation, न not, लज्जसे you feel ashamed, चक्षुषोः of the eyes (dual case), विषयम् range, object, reach, तस्य his (i.e., Rama’s), न not, तावत् even to that extent, उपगच्छसि you approach, you go near

Anvay: सः त्वम् (नीचः शशवत्) इह इक्ष्वाकुनाथं वै क्षिपन् न लज्जसे, तस्य चक्षुषोः विषयं तावत् न उपगच्छसि।

Simple meaning: "That you (who are like a hare - from previous shloka) do not feel ashamed to insult Rama, the Lord of the Ikshvaku dynasty, here without any shame, as long as you do not come within the range of his sight.

इमे ते नयने क्रूरे विरूपे कृष्णपिङ्गले।

क्षितौ न पतिते कस्मान्मामनार्य निरीक्षितः॥5.22.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: इमे these, ते your, नयने eyes (dual form of "nayanam"), क्रूरे cruel, विरूपे deformed, ugly, monstrous, कृष्ण-पिङ्गले black and tawny/red-brown, क्षितौ on the ground, न not, पतिते fallen, कस्मात् why, माम् me, अनार्य O wicked one!, निरीक्षितः you are looking at, gazing at

Anvay: अनार्य! ते क्रूरे विरूपे कृष्णपिङ्गले इमे नयने मां निरीक्षितः कस्मात् क्षितौ न पतिते?

Simple meaning: "O wicked one! Why have these cruel, deformed, black-and-tawny eyes of yours not fallen to the ground while gazing at me?

तस्य धर्मात्मनः पत्नीं स्नुषां दशरथस्य च।

कथं व्याहरतो मां ते न जिह्वा व्यवशीर्यते॥5.22.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य of that (Rama), धर्मात्मनः righteous-souled, virtuous, पत्नीम् wife, स्नुषाम् daughter-in-law, दशरथस्य of Dasharatha, च and, कथम् how, व्याहरतः speaking, माम् me, ते your, जिह्वा tongue, न not, व्यवशीर्यते does not fall off/split apart/perish

Simple meaning: "How is it that your tongue does not fall off while you speak to me — the wife of that righteous man (Rama) and the daughter-in-law of Dasharatha?

*कुर्मि –  Arsha Prayoga for lat lakara, uttama purusha, ekvachana. Arsha Prayoga means an irregular usage - not as per standard rules, but since it is used by the great sages, it is just accepted. Src. - Dr. Sowmya Krishnapur

Simple meaning: "O Ravana, who deserves to be reduced to ashes! I do not reduce you to ashes with my power only because I have not received a command from Rama and out of respect for my power of spiritual discipline.

Simple meaning: "I, who belong to the wise Rama, cannot be abducted by you. Destiny has ordained this for the purpose of your death—there is no doubt about that.

Simple meaning: "You, a valiant one and the brother of Kubera, endowed with armies— luring Rama away, why indeed did you resort to stealing another's wife (me)?”

Simple meaning: He was like the wish-fulfilling tree, like spring embodied; yet, though adorned, he was terrifying—like a shrine in a cremation ground.

Simple meaning: Looking at Vaidehi with eyes reddened by anger, hissing like a serpent, spoke to her.

अनयेनाभिसम्पन्नमर्थहीनमनुव्रते।

Word-by-word meaning: अनयेन with wickedness, unrighteousness, अभिसम्पन्नम् endowed, possessed, अर्थ-हीनम् devoid of purpose or value or wealth, अनुव्रते O you, devoted to her husband! नाशयामि I will destroy, अहम् I, अद्य today, त्वाम् you, सूर्यः the sun, सन्ध्याम् इव like twilight, औजसा with brilliance, with energy

Anvay: अनयेन अभिसम्पन्नम् अर्थहीनम् अनुव्रते! अद्य अहम् – औजसा सूर्यः सन्ध्याम् इव – त्वां नाशयामि।

Simple meaning: O you, devoted to her husband who is filled with wickedness and is devoid of purpose! Today, I will destroy you like the sun destroys twilight with its brilliance.

इत्युक्त्वा मैथिलीं राजा रावणः शत्रुरावणः।

Simple meaning: Having spoken thus to Maithili, King Ravana—the subduer of enemies—then ordered all the rakshasis who were terrifying in appearance.

Simple meaning: “Attempt to subdue (or win over) Vaidehi using both gentle and harsh methods, through persuasion strategies like conciliation, gifts, and sowing dissension — and also by threatening punishment.”

इति प्रतिसमादिश्य राक्षसेन्द्रः पुनः पुनः।

काममन्युपरीतात्मा जानकीं पर्यतर्जयत्॥5.22.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, प्रतिसमादिश्य having repeatedly instructed, राक्षसेन्द्रः the king of the rakshasas (Ravana), पुनः पुनः again and again, काम-मन्यु-परीत-आत्मा whose mind was overpowered by desire and anger, जानकीम् Janaki, पर्यतर्जयत् threatened, intimidated, or terrorized by pointing his finger at her

Anvay: राक्षसेन्द्रः पुनः पुनः इति प्रतिसमादिश्य, काममन्युपरीतात्मा जानकीं पर्यतर्जयत्।

Simple meaning: Having repeatedly given such orders (to the rakshasis), the king of the rakshasas, whose mind was overpowered by desire and anger, continued to threaten and intimidate Janaki.

उपगम्य ततः शीघ्रं राक्षसी धान्यमालिनी।

परिष्वज्य दशग्रीवमिदं वचनमब्रवीत्॥5.22.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: उपगम्य having approached, ततः then, शीघ्रम् quickly, राक्षसी the rakshasi, धान्यमालिनी Dhanyamalini (name of a rakshasi), परिष्वज्य having embraced, दशग्रीवम् Dashagriva (ten-headed one, i.e., Ravana), इदम् these, वचनम् words, अब्रवीत् said/spoke

Anvay: ततः शीघ्रं राक्षसी धान्यमालिनी उपगम्य, दशग्रीवं परिष्वज्य, इदं वचनम् अब्रवीत्।

Simple meaning: Then, the rakshasi Dhanyamalini quickly approached, embraced the ten-headed Ravana, and spoke these words.

मया क्रीड महाराज सीतया किं तवानया।

विवर्णया कृपणया मानुष्या राक्षसेश्वर॥5.22.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: मया by me, क्रीड sport, play, enjoy, महाराज O great king, सीतया with Sita, किम् what (is the use), तव​ to you/for you, अनया with this one, विवर्णया pale, discolored, कृपणया miserable, pitiable, मानुष्या human woman, राक्षसेश्वर O lord of the rakshasas!

Anvay: राक्षसेश्वर! महाराज! मया क्रीड। विवर्णया कृपणया मानुष्या अनया सीतया तव​ किम्?

Simple meaning: “O Lord of the Rakshasas, O Great King! Enjoy yourself with me. What is the use for you of this pale, pitiable human woman, Sita?

नूनमस्या महाराज न दिव्यान् भोगसत्तमान्।

विदधात्यमरश्रेष्ठस्तव बाहुबलार्जितान्॥5.22.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: नूनम् surely, certainly, अस्याः by her, महाराज O great king, न not, दिव्यान् divine, heavenly, भोग-सत्तमान् supreme enjoyments, विदधाति grants, bestows, अमर-श्रेष्ठः the foremost among the immortals (i.e., Brahma), तव your, बाहु-बल-अर्जितान् earned by the strength of your arms

Anvay: महाराज! नूनम् अमरश्रेष्ठः तव बाहुबलार्जितान् दिव्यान् भोगसत्तमान् अस्याः न विदधाति।

Simple meaning: “O Great King! Surely, the foremost among the immortals (Brahma) has not bestowed upon this woman (Sita) the supreme heavenly enjoyments earned by the strength of your arms.

अकामां कामयानस्य शरीरमुपतप्यते।

इच्छन्तीं कामयानस्य प्रीतिर्भवति शोभना॥5.22.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: अकामाम् one who is not willing/not desiring, कामयानस्य of one who desires (i.e., a man desiring someone), शरीरम् the body, उपतप्यते becomes tormented, suffers, इच्छन्तीम् one who desires (i.e., a willing woman), कामयानस्य of one who desires, प्रीतिः affection, pleasure, भवति becomes, शोभना delightful, beautiful, pleasing

Anvay: अकामां कामयानस्य शरीरम् उपतप्यते। कामयानस्य इच्छन्तीं प्रीतिः शोभना भवति।

Simple meaning: "When a man desires a woman who does not reciprocate, his body suffers (withers in torment). But when he desires one who returns his affection, the resulting joy is beautiful and pleasing.

एवमुक्तस्तु राक्षस्या समुत्क्षिप्तस्ततो बली।

प्रहसन्मेघसङ्काशो राक्षसः स न्यवर्तत॥5.22.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, उक्तः having been spoken to/addressed, तु but/then (emphatic particle), राक्षस्या by the rakshasi, समुत्क्षिप्तः turned away, ततः then/from there, बली powerful, प्रहसन् laughing, मेघ-सङ्काशः dark/cloud-like in appearance (i.e., massive and dark like a cloud), राक्षसः the rakshasa (i.e., Ravana), सः he, न्यवर्तत went back/withdrew

Anvay: राक्षस्या एवम् उक्तः बली सः मेघसङ्काशः राक्षसः, ततः समुत्क्षिप्तः प्रहसन् न्यवर्तत।

Simple meaning: Thus addressed by the rakshasi, that powerful rakshasa, resembling a dark rain-cloud, turned away and laughing withdrew from there.

प्रस्थितः स दशग्रीवः कम्पयन्निव मेदिनीम्।

ज्वलद्भास्करवर्णाभं प्रविवेश निवेशनम्॥5.22.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रस्थितः having set out/departed, सः he (referring to Rāvaṇa), दशग्रीवः the ten-headed one, कम्पयन् इव as if shaking/causing to tremble, मेदिनीम् the earth, ज्वलत् blazing, भास्कर-वर्ण-आभम् having the color or appearance of the sun (radiant like the sun), प्रविवेश entered, निवेशनम् the palace/residence

Anvay: सः दशग्रीवः मेदिनीं कम्पयन् इव प्रस्थितः, ज्वलत् भास्करवर्णाभं निवेशनं प्रविवेश।

Simple meaning: The ten-headed Ravana, having departed (from there), as it were shaking the earth, entered his radiant palace which resembled the blazing sun.

देवगन्धर्वकन्याश्च नागकन्याश्च सर्वतः।

परिवार्य दशग्रीवं विविशुस्तं गृहोत्तमम्॥5.22.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: देव-गन्धर्व-कन्याः च celestial and Gandharva maidens (daughters of gods and Gandharvas), नाग-कन्याः च and Naga maidens (serpent maidens), सर्वतः from all directions/on all sides, परिवार्य surrounding/having surrounded, दशग्रीवम् the ten-headed one (i.e., Ravana), विविशुः entered, तम् गृहोत्तमम् that excellent mansion/palace

Anvay: देवगन्धर्वकन्याः च नागकन्याः च सर्वतः दशग्रीवं परिवार्य तं गृहोत्तमं विविशुः।

Simple meaning: Celestial maidens, Gandharva maidens, and Naga maidens, having surrounded the ten-headed Ravana from all sides, entered that splendid palace with him.

स मैथिलीं धर्मपरामवस्थितां प्रवेपमानां परिभर्त्स्य रावणः।

विहाय सीतां मदनेन मोहितः स्वमेव वेश्म प्रविवेश भास्वरम्॥5.22.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, मैथिलीम् (to) Maithili, धर्मपराम् devoted to righteousness, अवस्थिताम् who was firmly established (in), प्रवेपमानाम् who was trembling, परिभर्त्स्य having scolded/rebuked/insulted, रावणः Ravana, विहाय leaving/abandoning, सीताम् Sita, मदनेन मोहितः deluded/confused by lust, स्वम् एव वेश्म his own palace, प्रविवेश entered, भास्वरम् radiant/splendid

Anvay: मदनेन मोहितः सः रावणः धर्मपराम् अवस्थितां प्रवेपमानां मैथिलीं परिभर्त्स्य, सीतां विहाय, स्वम् एव भास्वरं वेश्म प्रविवेश।

Simple meaning: Deluded by lust, that Ravana, having rebuked Maithili, who was trembling and firmly devoted to righteousness, abandoned her and entered his own splendid palace.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे द्वाविंशः सर्गः॥22॥

Thus ends the twenty-second sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
