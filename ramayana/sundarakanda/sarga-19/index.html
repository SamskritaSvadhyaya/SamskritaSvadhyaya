Sarga 19

सर्गः १९

ॐ

॥ॐ॥

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।

वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 19

Description of Sita who was overcome with grief

(Benefit: Attaining powerful speech)

(23 Shlokas)

तस्मिन्नेव ततः काले राजपुत्री त्वनिन्दिता।

रूपयौवनसम्पन्नं भूषणोत्तमभूषितम्॥5.19.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मिन् एव ततः काले at that very moment/at that exact time, राजपुत्री the princess (referring to Sita), तु अनिन्दिता (Sita) who was blameless/beyond reproach, रूप-यौवन-सम्पन्नम् endowed with beauty and youth, भूषण-उत्तम-भूषितम् adorned with excellent ornaments

Anvay: तस्मिन् एव ततः काले अनिन्दिता राजपुत्री तु रूपयौवनसम्पन्नं भूषणोत्तमभूषितम्… 

Simple meaning: At that very moment, the faultless princess (Sita) saw (Ravana), endowed with beauty and youth, and adorned with the finest ornaments.

ततो दृष्ट्वैव वैदेही रावणं राक्षसाधिपम्।

प्रावेपत वरारोहा प्रवाते कदली यथा॥5.19.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then/after that, दृष्ट्वा एव upon seeing, वैदेही Vaidehi, रावणम् Ravana, राक्षस-अधिपम् the lord of the rakshasas, प्रावेपत trembled/shivered, वरारोहा of beautiful waist, प्रवाते in the wind, कदली a banana plant, यथा just as/like

Anvay: ततः एव वरारोहा वैदेही राक्षसाधिपं रावणं दृष्ट्वा – प्रवाते कदली यथा – प्रावेपत।

Simple meaning: Then, as soon as Vaidehi of a beautiful waist saw Ravana, the king of the rakshasas, she trembled like a banana plant in the wind.

आच्छाद्योदरमूरुभ्यां बाहुभ्यां च पयोधरौ।

उपविष्टा विशालाक्षी रुदन्ती वरवर्णिनी॥5.19.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: आच्छाद्य covering/concealing, उदरम् her abdomen/stomach, ऊरुभ्याम् with (her) thighs, बाहुभ्याम् च and with (her) arms, पयोधरौ (her) breasts, उपविष्टा seated/sitting, विशालाक्षी large-eyed, रुदन्ती weeping/crying, वर-वर्णिनी of excellent complexion/a radiant woman

Anvay: विशालाक्षी वरवर्णिनी ऊरुभ्याम् उदरम् आच्छाद्य, बाहुभ्यां च पयोधरौ आच्छाद्य रुदन्ती उपविष्टा (आसीत्)।

Simple meaning: The wide-eyed, radiant woman (Sita) sat weeping, covering her stomach with her thighs and her breasts with her arms.

दशग्रीवस्तु वैदेहीं रक्षितां राक्षसीगणैः।

ददर्श सीतां दुःखार्तां नावं सन्नामिवार्णवे॥5.19.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: दशग्रीवः ten-headed one, तु but/indeed/then, वैदेहीम् Vaidehi, रक्षिताम् protected/guarded, राक्षसी-गणैः by groups of rakshasis, ददर्श saw/beheld, सीताम् Sita, दुःख-आर्ताम् afflicted with sorrow, नावम् a boat, सन्नाम् damaged/sunken/broken, इव like/as if, अर्णवे in the ocean/sea

Anvay: दशग्रीवः तु राक्षसीगणैः रक्षितां दुःखार्तां सीतां वैदेहीम् – अर्णवे सन्नां नावम् इव – ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Then the ten-headed Ravana saw Sita, the daughter of Videha, who was afflicted with sorrow and guarded by groups of rakshasis— like a sinking boat in the ocean.

असंवृतायामासीनां धरण्यां संशितव्रताम्।

छिन्नां प्रपतितां भूमौ शाखामिव वनस्पतेः॥5.19.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: असंवृतायाम् uncovered/exposed/bare, आसीनाम् sitting, धरण्याम् on the ground/earth, संशित-व्रताम् one of firm vows/strict austerity, छिन्नाम् broken/cut, प्रपतिताम् fallen, भूमौ on the ground, शाखाम् branch, इव like/as if, वनस्पतेः of a tree/plant

Anvay: (रावणः) असंवृतायां धरण्यां आसीनां संशितव्रतां – भूमौ प्रपतितां वनस्पतेः छिन्नां शाखाम् इव –  (सीतां ददर्श )।

Simple meaning: He (Ravana) saw Sita –  of firm vows, sitting on the bare ground, like a broken branch fallen from a tree.

मलमण्डनदिग्धाङ्गीं मण्डनार्हाममण्डिताम्।

मृणाली पङ्कदिग्धेव विभाति न विभाति च॥5.19.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: मल-मण्डन-दिग्ध-अङ्गीम् whose body appears “decorated” with dirt, मण्डन-अर्हाम् worthy of adornment, अ-मण्डिताम् yet unadorned/not decorated, मृणाली lotus stem/lotus stalk, पङ्क-दिग्धा smeared with mud, इव like/as if, विभाति shines/appears radiant, न विभाति च and yet does not shine

Anvay: (रावणः) मलमण्डनदिग्धाङ्गीं मण्डनार्हाम् अमण्डितां – पङ्कदिग्धा मृणाली इव – विभाति न विभाति च (सीतां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He (Ravana) saw Sita – whose body was as if adorned with dirt, though worthy of ornamentation and yet unadorned — appeared like a lotus stalk smeared with mud: radiant, and yet not radiant.

समीपं राजसिंहस्य रामस्य विदितात्मनः।

सङ्कल्पहयसंयुक्सैर्यान्तीमिव मनोरथैः॥5.19.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: समीपम् near/close to, राज-सिंहस्य of the lion among kings (i.e., Rama), रामस्य of Rama, विदितात्मनः one who knows his true Self; self-realized, सङ्कल्प-हय-संयुक्तैः yoked to the horses of resolve/thoughts, यान्तीम् proceeding/going, इव like/as if, मनोरथैः by thoughts/desires/chariots of imagination or wish

Anvay: (रावणः) राजसिंहस्य विदितात्मनः रामस्य समीपं – सङ्कल्पहयसंयुक्तैः मनोरथैः यान्तीम् इव – (सीतां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He (Ravana) saw Sita – who appeared as if being carried by the chariot of her thoughts, yoked to the horses of resolve, toward the lion-like, self-realized king — Rama.

शुष्यन्तीं रुदतीमेकां ध्यानशोकपरायणाम्।

दुःखस्यान्तमपश्यन्तीं रामां राममनुव्रताम्॥5.19.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: शुष्यन्तीम् withering/drying up (due to grief or sorrow), रुदतीम् weeping/crying, एकाम् alone, ध्यान-शोक-परायणाम् wholly given over to meditation and sorrow, दुःखस्य अन्तम् अपश्यन्तीम् seeing no end to her suffering, रामाम् beautiful woman, रामम् अनुव्रताम्  one who is wholly devoted to Rama; ever faithful to Rama

Anvay: (रावणः) शुष्यन्तीं, रुदतीम्, एकां, ध्यानशोकपरायणां, दुःखस्य अन्तम् अपश्यन्तीं, रामम् अनुव्रताम् रामाम् (सीतां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He (Ravana) saw the beautiful Sita – withering away, weeping alone, wholly immersed in meditation and sorrow, seeing no end to her suffering, ever faithful to Rama.

वेष्टमानां तथाऽविष्टां पन्नगेन्द्रवधूमिव।

धूप्यमानां ग्रहेणेव रोहिणीं धूमकेतुना॥5.19.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: वेष्टमानाम् writhing/twisting (in sorrow or despair), तथा thus/in that manner, आविष्टाम् overwhelmed/possessed/seized (by sorrow), पन्नग-इन्द्र-वधूम् इव like the wife of the king of serpents (i.e., a serpent queen), धूप्यमानाम् being scorched/tormented, ग्रहेण by a planet (here: an eclipsing or malevolent planet), इव like, रोहिणीम् the Rohini (a star associated with prosperity, beloved of the moon god), धूमकेतुना by a comet

Anvay: (रावणः) – वेष्टमानां तथा आविष्टां पन्नगेन्द्रवधूम् इव –, – धूमकेतुना ग्रहेण धूप्यमानां रोहिणीम् इव –  (सीतां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He (Ravana) saw Sita–who was writhing and overwhelmed, like the queen of serpents seized (by danger); who appeared like the star Rohini being scorched by the comet.

वृत्तशीलकुले जातामाचारवति धार्मिके।

पुनःसंस्कारमापन्नां जातामिव च दुष्कुले॥5.19.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: वृत्त-शील-कुले जाताम् born in a family of noble conduct and character, आचारवति full of right conduct/well-mannered, धार्मिके virtuous/righteous (family or person), पुनः संस्कारम् आपन्नाम् one who has (undeservedly) undergone a second “refining” or purification process, जाताम् इव च दुष्कुले as if born in a wicked or lowly family  

Anvay: (रावणः) धार्मिके आचारवति वृत्तशीलकुले जातां – दुष्कुले जातां पुनः संस्कारम् आपन्नाम् इव – च (सीतां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He saw Sita – who, born in a virtuous and disciplined family, with noble conduct and righteous ways, appeared as though she had been born in an ignoble family, having been (unjustly) subjected to purification again.

अभूतेनापवादेन कीर्तिं निपतितामिव।

आम्नायानामयोगेन विद्यां प्रशिथिलामिव॥5.19.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: अभूतेन false/baseless, अपवादेन by slander/accusation/defamation, कीर्तिम् reputation/fame, निपतिताम् इव as if fallen/brought down, आम्नायानाम् of sacred teachings (Vedas or scriptures), अयोगेन due to improper association/lack of correct application, विद्याम् knowledge/learning, प्रशिथिलाम् इव as if loosened, weakened, made ineffective

Anvay: (रावणः) – अभूतेन अपवादेन निपतितां कीर्तिम् इव –, – आम्नायानाम् अयोगेन प्रशिथिलां विद्याम् इव – (सीतां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He saw Sita–who appeared like a reputation ruined by false slander, like knowledge weakened by the misapplication of the Vedas..

सन्नामिव महाकीर्तिं श्रद्धामिव विमानिताम्।

पूजामिव परिक्षीणामाशां प्रतिहतामिव॥5.19.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: सन्नाम् इव like faded/dulled, महा-कीर्तिम् great fame/glorious reputation, श्रद्धाम् इव like faith/devotion, वि-मानिताम् insulted/dishonored, पूजाम् इव like worship/veneration, परिक्षीणाम् diminished/worn out/exhausted, आशाम् hope, प्रति-हताम् इव like obstructed/thwarted

Anvay: (रावणः) – सन्नां महाकीर्तिम् इव –, – विमानितां श्रद्धाम् इव –, – परिक्षीणां पूजाम् इव –, – प्रतिहतां आशाम् इव – (सीतां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He saw Sita–who appeared like great fame that had faded, like faith that had been insulted, like veneration that had dwindled, like hope that had been shattered.

आयतीमिव विध्वस्तामाज्ञां प्रतिहतामिव।

दीप्तामिव दिशं काले पूजामपहृतामिव॥5.19.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: आयतीम् इव like a future (hope/fortune/prosperity), विध्वस्ताम् destroyed/shattered, आज्ञाम् command/authority, प्रतिहताम् इव obstructed/defied, दीप्ताम् इव like something bright or radiant, दिशम् direction/region, काले at an inauspicious time/due to time, पूजाम् अपहृताम् इव like worship that has been taken away/withdrawn

Anvay: (रावणः) – विध्वस्ताम् आयतीम् इव –, – प्रतिहताम् आज्ञाम् इव –, – काले दिशं दीप्ताम् इव –, – अपहृतां पूजाम् इव – (सीतां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He saw Sita–who appeared like a shattered future, like a royal command that had been defied, like a quarter appearing in flame at the time of some portentous phenomenon, like worship that had been withdrawn.

पद्मिनीमिव विध्वस्तां हतशूरां चमूमिव।

प्रभामिव तमोध्वस्तामुपक्षीणामिवापगाम्॥5.19.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: पद्मिनीम् इव like a lotus pond, विध्वस्ताम् ravaged/destroyed, हत-शूराम् चमूम् इव like an army whose heroes have been slain, प्रभाम् इव like radiance/light, तमः-ध्वस्ताम् destroyed by darkness, उपक्षीणाम् इव like one that is diminished/depleted, आपगाम् a river

Anvay: (रावणः) – विध्वस्तां पद्मिनीम् इव –, – हतशूरां चमूम् इव –, – तमोध्वस्तां प्रभाम् इव –, – उपक्षीणाम् आपगाम् इव – (सीतां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He saw Sita–who appeared like a lotus pond ravaged, like an army whose brave warriors were slain, like radiance destroyed by darkness, like a river that had dried up.

वेदीमिव परामृष्टां शान्तामग्निशिखामिव।

पौर्णमासीमिव निशां राहुग्रस्तेन्दुमण्डलाम्॥5.19.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: वेदीम् इव like a sacrificial altar, परामृष्टाम् defiled/desecrated/violated, शान्ताम् अग्नि-शिखाम् इव like an extinguished flame, पौर्णमासीम् इव निशाम् like the full moon night, राहु-ग्रस्त-इन्दु-मण्डलाम् whose moon-disc is eclipsed by Rahu

Anvay: (रावणः) – परामृष्टां वेदीम् इव –, – शान्ताम् अग्निशिखाम् इव –, – राहुग्रस्तइन्दुमण्डलां पौर्णमासीं निशाम् इव – (सीतां ददर्श)।  

Simple meaning: He saw Sita–who appeared like a sacrificial altar that had been defiled, like a flame of fire that had been extinguished, like a full moon night whose moon had been swallowed by Rahu.

उत्कृष्टपर्णकमलां वित्रासितविहङ्गमाम्।

हस्तिहस्तपरामृष्टामाकुलां पद्मिनीमिव॥5.19.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: उत्कृष्ट-पर्ण-कमलाम् filled with beautiful (well-spread) leaves and lotuses, वित्रासित-विहङ्गमाम् whose birds have been frightened away, हस्ति-हस्त-परामृष्टाम् trampled by the trunk of an elephant, आकुलाम् agitated/distressed/disturbed, पद्मिनीम् इव like a lotus pond

Anvay: (रावणः) उत्कृष्टपर्णकमलां वित्रासितविहङ्गमां हस्तिहस्तपरामृष्टाम् आकुलां पद्मिनीम् इव (सीतां ददर्श)।  

Simple meaning: He saw Sita–who appeared like a lotus pond filled with fine leaves and lotuses, whose birds had been frightened away, trampled by the trunk of an elephant, disturbed and agitated.

पतिशोकातुरां शुष्कां नदीं विस्रावितामिव।

परया मृजया हीनां कृष्णपक्षनिशामिव॥5.19.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: पति-शोक-आतुराम् afflicted with grief for her husband, शुष्काम् dried up, नदीम् river, विस्राविताम् इव as if drained (of water), परया मृजया हीनाम् deprived of great beauty or adornment, कृष्ण-पक्ष-निशाम् इव  like a night in the dark fortnight (i.e., without the moon, gloomy)

Anvay: (रावणः) पतिशोकातुरां – शुष्कां विस्रावितां नदीम् इव –, परया मृजया हीनां, – कृष्णपक्षनिशाम् इव – (सीतां ददर्श)।  

Simple meaning: He saw Sita–tormented with grief for her husband, who appeared like a river, dried up and drained, like a night of the dark fortnight, devoid of all beauty and luster.

सुकुमारीं सुजाताङ्गीं रत्नगर्भगृहोचिताम्।

तप्यमानामिवोष्णेन मृणालीमचिरोद्धृताम्॥5.19.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुकुमारीम् a very delicate young woman, सुजात-अङ्गीम् one with well-formed, graceful limbs, रत्न-गर्भ-गृह-उचिताम् one who is suited for palaces rich with jewels, तप्यमानाम् scorched, burning, इव like, उष्णेन by heat, मृणालीम् lotus stem, अचिर-उद्धृताम् recently uprooted

Anvay: (रावणः) सुकुमारीं सुजाताङ्गीं रत्नगर्भगृहोचिताम् – उष्णेन तप्यमानाम् अचिरोद्धृतां मृणालीम् इव – (सीतां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Ravana saw Sita - a delicate, finely-limbed maiden, suited to live in jewel-filled palaces, but now like a freshly uprooted lotus stalk, scorched by the heat.

गृहीतामालितां स्तम्भे यूथपेन विनाकृताम्।

निःश्वसन्तीं सुदुःखार्तां गजराजवधूमिव॥5.19.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: गृहीताम् seized, held, आलिताम् tied, bound, स्तम्भे to a pillar, यूथपेन by the herd leader (here, symbolically referring to her husband, Rama), विना कृताम् separated from, निःश्वसन्तीम् sighing deeply, सुदुःखार्ताम् greatly afflicted by sorrow, गज-राज-वधूम् इव like the queen of the king of elephants

Anvay: (रावणः) गृहीतां, स्तम्भे आलितां, यूथपेन विनाकृतां, निःश्वसन्तीं सुदुःखार्तां, गजराजवधूम् इव (सीतां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He saw Sita–who appeared like the consort of a king elephant, bound and tied to a pillar, separated from her lord, sighing deeply, and consumed by immense sorrow.

एकया दीर्घया वेण्या शोभमानामयत्नतः।

नीलया नीरदापाये वनराज्या महीमिव॥5.19.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: एकया with one, दीर्घया long, वेण्या braid (of hair), शोभमानाम् appearing/looking beautiful, अयत्नतः untended, without care (effortlessly, not dressed up), नीलया dark (in color), नीरद-अपाये after the departure of the clouds (i.e., post-monsoon), वनराज्या covered with forest or wild vegetation, महीम् इव like the Earth

Anvay: (रावणः) दीर्घया एकया वेण्या अयत्नतः शोभमानां, नीरदापाये नीलया वनराज्या महीम् इव (सीतां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He saw Sita–With a single long braid, appearing beautiful even without effort, dark in color, she looked like the earth covered with a single row of dark trees after the clouds had departed.

उपवासेन शोकेन ध्यानेन च भयेन च।

परिक्षीणां कृशां दीनामल्पाहारां तपोधनाम्॥5.19.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: उपवासेन due to fasting, शोकेन due to grief/sorrow, ध्यानेन due to deep meditation or contemplation, च and, भयेन च and due to fear, परिक्षीणाम् emaciated, weakened, कृशाम् thin, frail, दीनाम् miserable, distressed, अल्प-आहाराम् taking little food, तपोधनाम् rich in austerity (one who possesses the wealth of penance)

Anvay: (रावणः) उपवासेन, शोकेन, ध्यानेन च, भयेन च, परिक्षीणां, कृशां, दीनाम्, अल्पाहारां, तपोधनां  (सीतां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He saw Sita–who due to fasting, sorrow, deep contemplation, and fear, appeared emaciated, thin, miserable, taking very little food, but was rich in austerity.

आयाचमानां दुःखार्तां प्राञ्जलिं देवतामिव।

भावेन रघुमुख्यस्य दशग्रीवपराभवम्॥5.19.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: आयाचमानाम् one who is supplicating/begging/pleading, दुःख-आर्ताम् afflicted by sorrow, प्राञ्जलिम् with folded hands, देवताम् इव like a goddess, भावेन by her demeanor/inner disposition/devotion, रघु-मुख्यस्य of Rama, the foremost of the Raghu lineage, दशग्रीव-पराभवम् the cause of Ravana’s defeat (or downfall of Ravana)

Anvay: (रावणः) दुःखार्तां प्राञ्जलिं रघुमुख्यस्य भावेन दशग्रीवपराभवम् आयाचमानां देवताम् इव (सीतां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He saw Sita–who tormented with grief, with folded hands, looked as if a goddess pleading to Rama, the foremost of the Raghu lineage, to bring about the downfall of the ten-necked Ravana.

समीक्षमाणां रुदतीमनिन्दितां सुपक्ष्मताम्रायतशुक्ललोचनाम्।

अनुव्रतां राममतीव मैथिलीं प्रलोभयामास वधाय रावणः॥5.19.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: समीक्षमाणाम् looking around/observing (attentively), रुदतीम् weeping, अनिन्दिताम् blameless/virtuous, सुपक्ष्म-ताम्र-आयत-शुक्ल-लोचनाम् with beautiful eyelashes, reddish, large/elongated, white/bright eyes (This compound describes her eyes: having long reddish-white eyes with beautiful lashes), अनुव्रताम् devoted (faithfully), रामम् अतीव exceedingly to Rama, मैथिलीम् the princess of Mithila (Sita), प्रलोभयामास tried to tempt/entice, वधाय for her destruction, रावणः Ravana

Anvay: रावणः समीक्षमाणां रुदतीम् अनिन्दितां सुपक्ष्मताम्रायतशुक्ललोचनां रामम् अतीव अनुव्रतां मैथिलीं वधाय प्रलोभयामास।

Simple meaning: Ravana tried to tempt Maithili (Sita), who was blameless, weeping, looking around, and had large, bright eyes with reddish corners and long lashes. She was deeply devoted to Rama. Seeing her unmoved by his advances, Ravana attempted to allure her and threatened to kill her.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे एकोनविंशः सर्गः॥19॥

Thus ends the nineteenth sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
