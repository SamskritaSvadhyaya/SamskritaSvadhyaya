Sundarakanda Sarga 10

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।

वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 10

Hanuman chances upon Mandodari, wife of Ravana, mistakes her for Sita on account of her beauty and charm

(Benefit: Overcoming Cheating and Deceit)

(54 Shlokas)

तत्र दिव्योपमं मुख्यं स्फाटिकं रत्नभूषितम्।

अवेक्षमाणो हनुमान् ददर्श शयनासनम्॥5.10.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र  there, दिव्य-उपमम् resembling the divine, मुख्यम् excellent/chief/foremost, स्फाटिकम् made of crystal, रत्न-भूषितम् adorned with gems, अवेक्षमाणः looking around/observing, हनुमान् Hanuman, ददर्श saw, शयन-आसनम् bed or couch (used for reclining or sleeping)

Anvay: अवेक्षमाणः हनुमान् तत्र दिव्योपमं मुख्यं स्फाटिकं रत्नभूषितं शयनासनं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: There, as he observed carefully, Hanuman saw a divine and magnificent bed, made of excellent crystal and adorned with gems.

दान्तकाञ्चनचित्राङ्गैर्वैडूर्यैश्च वरासनैः।

महार्हास्तरणोपेतैरुपपन्नं महाधनैः॥5.10.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: दान्त-काञ्चन-चित्र-अङ्गैः with wonderful ornamented parts made of ivory and gold, वैडूर्यैः with cat’s eye gems (beryls), च and, वर-आसनैः with excellent seats, महा-अर्ह-आस्तरण-उपेतैः furnished with/endowed with very costly/valuable, coverings, spreads, उपपन्नम् equipped/provided, महाधनैः with great wealth or very precious things

Anvay: दान्तकाञ्चनचित्राङ्गैः वैडूर्यैः महार्हास्तरणोपेतैः महाधनैः वरासनैः उपपन्नं (शयनासनं ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: The couch (or bed) was fitted with parts decorated with ivory and gold, adorned with cat’s eye gems and excellent seats, furnished with very costly coverings, and provided with extremely valuable ornaments or riches.

तस्य चैकतमे देशे सोऽग्य्रमालाविभूषितम्।

ददर्श पाण्डुरं छत्रं ताराधिपतिसन्निभम्॥5.10.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य of that (couch/seat/place), च and, एकतमे  in one particular/in a certain, देशे place/part/region, सः he (Hanuman), अग्र-माला-विभूषितम् adorned with a beautiful garland, ददर्श saw, पाण्डुरम् white, छत्रम् parasol/royal umbrella, तारा-अधिपति-सन्निभम् resembling the lord of the stars-the moon

Anvay: सः तस्य एकतमे देशे अग्य्रमालाविभूषितं ताराधिपतिसन्निभं पाण्डुरं छत्रं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: In one particular part of that place, he (Hanuman) saw a white royal umbrella, adorned with a beautiful garland, resembling the moon, the lord of the stars.

जातरूपपरिक्षिप्तं चित्रभानुसमप्रभम्।

अशोकमालाविततं ददर्श परमासनम्॥5.10.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: जातरूप-परिक्षिप्तम् covered with gold, चित्रभानु-सम-प्रभम् (literally "bright-rayed"), equal to the sun in brilliance/radiance, अशोक-माला-विततम् spread over with the garland of Ashoka flowers, ददर्श saw, परम-आसनम् the excellent seat

Anvay: सः जातरूपपरिक्षिप्तं चित्रभानुसमप्रभं अशोकमालाविततं परमासनं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman) saw the excellent seat, covered with gold, radiant like the sun, and adorned with garlands of Ashoka flowers.

वालव्यजनहस्ताभिर्वीज्यमानं समन्ततः।

गन्धैश्च विविधैर्जुष्टं वरधूपेन धूपितम्॥5.10.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: वाल-व्यजन-हस्ताभिः with hands holding the yak-tail fans, वीज्यमानम् being fanned, समन्ततः all around/from all sides, गन्धैः with fragrances, च and, विविधैः various/of many kinds, जुष्टम् endowed/filled/pervaded, वर-धूपेन with excellent incense, धूपितम् perfumed/scented/fumigated

Anvay: वालव्यजनहस्ताभिः समन्ततः वीज्यमानं, विविधैः गन्धैः च जुष्टं, वरधूपेन धूपितम् (परमासनम्) ददर्श।

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman) saw the supreme seat being fanned from all sides by hands (of maid servants) holding yak-tail fans, pervaded with various fragrances, and perfumed with excellent incense.

परमास्तरणास्तीर्णमाविकाजिनसंवृतम्।

दामभिर्वरमाल्यानां समन्तादुपशोभितम्॥5.10.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: परम-आस्तरण-आस्तीर्णम् spread with excellent bedding/covering, आविक-अजिन-संवृतम् covered in sheepskin, दामभिः with garlands/strings/festoons, वर-माल्यानाम् of excellent garlands, समन्तात् on all sides/all around, उपशोभितम् adorned/beautified

Anvay: परमास्तरणास्तीर्णम्, आविकाजिनसंवृतं, वरमाल्यानां दामभिः समन्तात् उपशोभितं (परमासनम्)।

Simple meaning: (The excellent seat was) spread with excellent bedding, covered with sheepskin, and adorned all around with festoons of exquisite garlands.

तस्मिन् जीमूतसंकाशं प्रदीप्तोत्तमकुण्डलम्।

लोहिताक्षं महाबाहुं महारजतवाससम्॥5.10.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मिन् on that (seat), जीमूत-संकाशम् resembling a rain-bearing cloud, प्रदीप्त-उत्तम-कुण्डलम् adorned with blazing, excellent earrings, लोहित-अक्षम् having reddish eyes, महा-बाहुम् with mighty arms, महा-रजत-वाससम् wearing splendid silver-colored clothes

Anvay: तस्मिन् (आसने) जीमूतसंकाशं, प्रदीप्तोत्तमकुण्डलं, लोहिताक्षं, महाबाहुं, महारजतवाससम् (राक्षसं ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: On that (seat), ( Hanuman saw a rakshasa ) resembling a dark rain cloud, wearing blazing, excellent earrings, with red eyes, mighty arms, and clad in splendid silver-colored garments.

लोहितेनानुलिप्ताङ्गं चन्दनेन सुगन्धिना।

सन्ध्यारक्तमिवाकाशे तोयदं सतटिद्गणम्॥5.10.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: लोहितेन with red, अनुलिप्त-अङ्गम्  body smeared/anointed, चन्दनेन with sandalwood, सुगन्धिना fragrant, सन्ध्या-रक्तम् red like the twilight sky, इव like/as if, आकाशे in the sky, तोयदम् a cloud, स-तटिद्गणम् accompanied by flashes of lightning

Anvay: लोहितेन सुगन्धिना चन्दनेन अनुलिप्ताङ्गम्, आकाशे सतटिद्गणं सन्ध्यारक्तं तोयदं इव (स: राक्षसः)।

Simple meaning: His body was anointed with red, fragrant sandal paste, and he resembled a twilight-red cloud in the sky, accompanied by flashes of lightning (because he was wearing silver coloured garments).

वृतमाभरणैर्दिव्यैः सुरूपं कामरूपिणम्।

सवृक्षवनगुल्माढ्यं प्रसुप्तमिव मन्दरम्॥5.10.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: वृतम् covered/adorned, आभरणैः with ornaments, दिव्यैः divine/celestial, सु-रूपम् handsome/well-formed, काम-रूपिणम् one who can assume any form at will, स-वृक्ष-वन-गुल्म-आढ्यम् rich with trees, forests, and shrubs (आढ्यम्  rich/full of), प्रसुप्तम् asleep, इव like, मन्दरम्  the Mandara mountain

Anvay: दिव्यैः आभरणैः वृतं, सुरूपं, कामरूपिणं – सवृक्षवनगुल्माढ्यं प्रसुप्तं मन्दरम् इव – (तं राक्षसं)...

Simple meaning: (That rakshasa was) adorned with divine ornaments, handsome, capable of assuming any form at will, and rich with (like) forests, trees, and shrubs — like the Mandara mountain lying asleep.

क्रीडित्वोपरतं रात्रौ वराभरणभूषितम्।

प्रियं राक्षसकन्यानां राक्षसानां सुखावहम्॥5.10.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्रीडित्वा उपरतम् one who had just ceased playing/having finished amusement, रात्रौ at night, वर-आभरण-भूषितम् adorned with excellent ornaments, प्रियम् dear/beloved, राक्षस-कन्यानाम् of the rakshasa maidens, राक्षसानाम् of the rakshasas, सुख-आवहम् source of pleasure/one who brings joy

Anvay: रात्रौ क्रीडित्वा उपरतं, वराभरणभूषितं, राक्षसकन्यानां प्रियं, राक्षसानां सुखावहं (तं रावणं ददर्श हनुमान्)।

Simple meaning: (Hanuman saw Ravana) who had just ceased his nightly revels, adorned with exquisite ornaments, beloved of the rakshasa maidens, and a source of delight to the rakshasas.

पीत्वाऽप्युपरतं चापि ददर्श स महाकपिः।

भास्वरे शयने वीरं प्रसुप्तं राक्षसाधिपम्॥5.10.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: पीत्वा having drunk, अपि also, उपरतम् ceased/stopped, च अपि and also, ददर्श (he) saw, सः that (he, i.e., Hanuman), महा-कपिः the great monkey (Hanuman), भास्वरे shining/resplendent, शयने on the bed, वीरम् the hero/brave one, प्रसुप्तम् sleeping, राक्षस-अधिपम् the lord of the rakshasas (Ravana)

Anvay: सः महाकपिः, पीत्वा अपि उपरतं च अपि, भास्वरे शयने प्रसुप्तं वीरं राक्षसाधिपं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: That great monkey (Hanuman) saw the lord of the rakshasas (Ravana), the hero, sleeping on a resplendent bed, who had ceased (his revels) even after drinking.

निःश्वसन्तं यथा नागं रावणं वानरर्षभः।

आसाद्य परमोद्विग्नस्सोपासर्पत्सुभीतवत्॥5.10.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: निःश्वसन्तम् breathing heavily/exhaling deeply (hissing), यथा like, नागम् a serpent, रावणम् Ravana, वानर-ऋषभः the foremost among monkeys (Hanuman), आसाद्य having approached, परम-उद्विग्नः extremely anxious/deeply disturbed, सः he, अपासर्पत्  drew back/retreated, सु-भीतवत् like one who is very frightened

Anvay: वानरर्षभः सः, निःश्वसन्तं नागं यथा रावणम् आसाद्य, परमोद्विग्न: सुभीतवत् अपासर्पत्।

Simple meaning: The foremost among the monkeys (Hanuman), having approached Ravana who was breathing heavily like a serpent, became extremely anxious and withdrew like one who is very frightened.

अथाऽऽरोहणमासाद्य वेदिकाऽन्तरमाश्रितः।

सुप्तं राक्षसशार्दूलं प्रेक्षते स्म महाकपिः॥5.10.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथ then, आरोहणम् a raised platform/elevation, आसाद्य having reached, वेदिका-अन्तरम् the inner part of the platform or terrace, आश्रितः taking refuge on/resting upon, सुप्तम् sleeping, राक्षस-शार्दूलम् tiger among rakshasas (i.e., Ravana), प्रेक्षते स्म kept gazing/was observing, महा-कपिः the great monkey (Hanuman)

Anvay: महाकपिः अथ आरोहणम् आसाद्य वेदिकान्तरम् आश्रितः सुप्तं राक्षसशार्दूलं प्रेक्षते स्म।

Simple meaning: Then, the great monkey (Hanuman), having reached a raised platform and taking position in the inner part of the terrace, kept observing the sleeping tiger among rakshasas (Ravana).

शुशुभे राक्षसेन्द्रस्य स्वपतः शयनोत्तमम्।

गन्धहस्तिनि संविष्टे यथा प्रस्रवणं महत्॥5.10.14॥

Word-by-word meaning:  शुशुभे shone/appeared beautiful, राक्षस-इन्द्रस्य of the king of rakshasas (Ravana), स्वपतः sleeping, शयन-उत्तमम् excellent bed/luxurious couch, गन्ध-हस्तिनि on a rutting elephant, संविष्टे when occupied, यथा like, प्रस्रवणम् Prasravana mountain महत् great/vast

Anvay: स्वपतः राक्षसेन्द्रस्य शयनोत्तमं – गन्धहस्तिनि संविष्टे महत् प्रस्रवणं यथा – शुशुभे।

Simple meaning: As Ravana, the king of rakshasas, lay sleeping on the magnificent couch, he looked like a scent-elephant (an elephant of the best kind, whose scent alone drives away hostile elephants) sleeping on the lofty Prasravana mountain.

काञ्चनाङ्गदसन्नद्धौ च ददर्श स महात्मनः।

विक्षिप्तौ राक्षसेन्द्रस्य भुजाविन्द्रध्वजोपमौ॥5.10.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: काञ्चन-अङ्गद-सन्नद्धौ with golden armlets worn on the upper arm, च and, ददर्श (he) saw, सः he (Hanuman), महा-आत्मनः of the great one (Ravana), विक्षिप्तौ spread out/thrown apart, राक्षस-इन्द्रस्य of the king of rakshasas, भुजौ both arms, इन्द्र-ध्वज-उपमौ like Indra's banner/pole (majestic and tall)

Anvay: सः हनुमान् महात्मनः राक्षसेन्द्रस्य काञ्चनाङ्गदसन्नद्धौ इन्द्रध्वजोपमौ भुजौ विक्षिप्तौ च ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw the two arms of the great king of the rakshasas (Ravana), adorned with golden armlets, spread out, resembling Indra’s banner.

ऐरावतविषाणाग्रैरापीडनकृतव्रणौ।

वज्रोल्लिखितपीनांसौ विष्णुचक्रपरिक्षतौ॥5.10.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऐरावत-विषाण-अग्रैः by the tips of Airavata’s tusks, आपीडन-कृत-व्रणौ bearing wounds caused by pressing (or striking), वज्र-उल्लिखित-पीन-अंसौ broad shoulders scratched or marked by the vajra (thunderbolt), विष्णु-चक्र-परिक्षतौ bearing cuts or scars from Vishnu’s discus (chakra)

Anvay: (राक्षसेन्द्रस्य भुजौ) ऐरावतविषाणाग्रैः आपीडनकृतव्रणौ, वज्रोल्लिखितपीनांसौ, विष्णुचक्रपरिक्षतौ।

Simple meaning: (Hanuman saw that) Ravana’s arms bore wounds caused by the tips of Airavata’s tusks, had broad shoulders marked by the thunderbolt (vajra), and were scarred by Vishnu’s discus (chakra).

पीनौ समसुजातांसौ संगतौ बलसंयुतौ।

सुलक्षणनखाङ्गुष्ठा स्वङ्गुलीतललक्षितौ॥5.10.17॥

Word-by-word meaning:  पीनौ broad, सम-सु-जात-अंसौ with evenly formed shoulders, संगतौ well-joined/closely aligned, बल-संयुतौ endowed with strength, सु-लक्षण-नख-अङ्गुष्ठौ with auspiciously marked nails and thumbs, स्व-अङ्गुली-तल-लक्षितौ distinguished by the undersides of (his own) fingers

Anvay: पीनौ समसुजातांसौ संगतौ बलसंयुतौ सुलक्षणनखाङ्गुष्ठौ स्वङ्गुलीतललक्षितौ (भुजौ बभूवतुः)।

Simple meaning: (Hanuman saw that Ravana’s) arms were broad, with evenly shaped shoulders, well-joined, powerful, with auspicious fingernails and thumbs, and marked with distinct lines on the undersides of the fingers.

संहतौ परिघाकारौ वृत्तौ करिकरोपमौ।

विक्षिप्तौ शयने शुभ्रे पञ्चशीर्षाविवोरगौ॥5.10.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: संहतौ compact/close together, परिघ-आकारौ shaped like iron clubs, वृत्तौ round, करि-कर-उपमौ resembling the trunks of elephants, विक्षिप्तौ  spread out/thrown apart, शयने on the couch, शुभ्रे white/pure, पञ्च-शीर्षौ इव like five-hooded, उरगौ serpents

Anvay: संहतौ परिघाकारौ वृत्तौ करिकरोपमौ विक्षिप्तौ शुभ्रे शयने – पञ्चशीर्षौ उरगौ इव – (भुजौ बभूवतुः)।

Simple meaning: (Hanuman saw Ravana’s) two arms, compact and shaped like iron clubs, round and resembling elephant trunks, thrown apart on the white couch, like two five-hooded serpents.

शशक्षतजकल्पेन सुशीतेन सुगन्धिना।

चन्दनेन परार्ध्येन स्वनुलिप्तौ स्वलङ्कृतौ॥5.10.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: शश-क्षतज-कल्पेन like the blood oozing from the wound of a hare (a poetic metaphor for red-colored sandal paste), सु-शीतेन very cool, सु-गन्धिना fragrant, चन्दनेन with sandal paste, परार्ध्येन most excellent/highly valuable, स्व-अनुलिप्तौ self-anointed/smeared by oneself, स्व-अलङ्कृतौ self-adorned/well-decorated

Anvay: शशक्षतजकल्पेन सुशीतेन सुगन्धिना परार्ध्येन चन्दनेन स्वनुलिप्तौ स्वलङ्कृतौ (भुजौ बभूवतुः)।

Simple meaning: (Hanuman saw Ravana's arms) self-anointed and well-adorned, with excellent, cool, and fragrant sandal paste, resembling the blood from the wound of a hare (i.e., reddish in color, poetic metaphor).

चिरस्य रमणं लब्ध्वा परिष्वज्येव भामिनी॥5.10.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: पटहम् a drum (specifically a kind of flat drum, like a kettle-drum), चारु-सर्व-अङ्गी  beautiful in all limbs, पीड्य pressing (against herself), holding tightly, शेते lies (sleeps), शुभ-स्तनी having beautiful breasts, चिरस्य after a long time, रमणम् her beloved (husband or lover), लब्ध्वा having obtained, परिष्वज्य embracing, इव  as if, भामिनी a beautiful woman

Anvay: चारुसर्वाङ्गी शुभस्तनी भामिनी पटहं पीड्य – चिरस्य रमणं लब्ध्वा परिष्वज्य इव – शेते।

Simple meaning: A beautiful woman, lovely in all her limbs and with graceful breasts, lay pressing a drum to herself, as if she were embracing her beloved after a long time.

काचिद्वंशं परिष्वज्य सुप्ता कमललोचना।

रहः प्रियतमं गृह्य सकामेव च कामिनी॥5.10.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: काचित् a certain (woman), वंशम् a flute (bamboo flute), परिष्वज्य embracing, सुप्ता lying asleep, कमल-लोचना lotus-eyed (woman with eyes like lotus petals), रहः in privacy, प्रियतमम् her dearest beloved, गृह्य having taken (held), सकामा इव as though with desire, च  and, कामिनी a passionate woman/a desirous lady

Anvay: काचित् कमललोचना कामिनी वंशं परिष्वज्य – रहः प्रियतमं गृह्य सकामा इव – सुप्ता।

Simple meaning: A certain lotus-eyed passionate woman lay asleep, embracing a flute, as though she were holding her beloved in private with desire.

विपञ्चीं परिगृह्यान्या नियता नृत्तशालिनी।

निद्रावशमनुप्राप्ता सह कान्तेव भामिनी॥5.10.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: विपञ्चीम् a stringed musical instrument (similar to a lute or lyre), परिगृह्य having embraced or held, अन्या another (woman), नियता disciplined, composed, नृत्त-शालिनी skilled in dance, निद्रा-वशम् under the influence of sleep, अनुप्राप्ता had come (fallen), सह-कान्ता इव as if with her beloved, भामिनी the graceful woman

Anvay: अन्या नियता नृत्तशालिनी भामिनी विपञ्चीं परिगृह्य – सहकान्ता इव – निद्रावशम् अनुप्राप्ता।

Simple meaning: Another graceful woman, disciplined and skilled in dance, had fallen asleep while holding a vipanchi (stringed instrument), as if she were with her beloved.

अन्या कनकसङ्काशैर्मृदुपीनैर्मनोरमैः।

मृदङ्गं परिपीड्याङ्गैः प्रसुप्ता मत्तलोचना॥5.10.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्या another (woman), कनक-सङ्काशैः golden-hued (shining like gold), मृदु-पीनैः soft and full (plump), मनोरमैः charming, delightful, मृदङ्गम् a drum, परिपीड्य tightly pressed (against her body), अङ्गैः with limbs, प्रसुप्ता asleep, मत्त-लोचना with intoxicated eyes (or dreamy eyes)

Anvay: अन्या मत्तलोचना कनकसङ्काशैः मृदुपीनैः मनोरमैः अङ्गैः मृदङ्गं परिपीड्य प्रसुप्ता।

Simple meaning: Another woman with intoxicated (dreamy) eyes lay asleep, pressing a mridangam to her body with her soft, full, golden-hued, and delightful limbs.

भुजपार्श्वान्तरस्थेन कक्षगेन कृशोदरी।

पणवेन सहानिन्द्या सुप्ता मदकृतश्रमा॥5.10.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: भुज-पार्श्व-अन्तर-स्थेन placed between the side and the arms, कक्षगेन by the one kept at the side (in the armpit/side embrace), कृश-उदरी the slender-waisted woman, पणवेन with a panava (a small drum), सह together with, अनिन्द्या irreproachable (blameless, of flawless beauty), सुप्ता slept, मद-कृत-श्रमा tired from intoxication (or passion)

Anvay: अनिन्द्या कृशोदरी मदकृतश्रमा भुजपर्श्वान्तरस्थेन कक्षगेन पणवेन सह सुप्ता।

Simple meaning: A slender-waisted and blameless (beautiful) woman, weary from intoxication (or passion), slept holding a small drum (panava) at her side, nestled between her arms—just as one would a beloved.

डिण्डिमं परिगृह्यान्या तथैवासक्तडिण्डिमा।

प्रसुप्ता तरुणं वत्समुपगूह्येव भामिनी॥5.10.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: डिण्डिमम् a type of drum (similar to a kettledrum or war drum), परिगृह्य having embraced/held closely, अन्या another (woman), तथा एव likewise/in the same manner, आसक्त-डिण्डिमा attached to the drum (lit. clinging to the dindima), प्रसुप्ता lay asleep, तरुणम्  a young one (youth or calf), वत्सम् calf (often used symbolically for a child or beloved), उपगुह्य इव as though embracing closely, भामिनी a beautiful woman

Anvay: आसक्तडिण्डिमा अन्या भामिनी डिण्डिमं परिगृह्य तथा एव – तरुणं वत्सम् उपगुह्य इव – प्रसुप्ता।

Simple meaning: Another beautiful woman, closely embracing a dindima drum and clinging to it, lay asleep as if she were holding a young calf (or a young beloved) in a tender embrace.

काचिदाडम्बरं नारी भुजसंयोगपीडितम्।

कृत्वा कमलपत्राक्षी प्रसुप्ता मदमोहिता॥5.10.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: काचित् one (a certain woman), आडम्बरम् adambara (a type of musical instrument, possibly a drum), नारी woman, भुज-संयोग-पीडितम् pressed by the embrace of her arms, कृत्वा having placed/made, कमल-पत्र-आक्षी one with eyes like lotus petals, प्रसुप्ता was asleep, मद-मोहिता overcome with intoxication or passion

Anvay: काचित् कमलपत्राक्षी नारी मदमोहिता भुजसंयोगपीडितम् आडम्बरं कृत्वा प्रसुप्ता।

Simple meaning: A certain lotus-eyed woman, overcome with intoxication, had made a drum (adambara) her pillow, pressing it in the embrace of her arms, and lay fast asleep.

कलशीमपविध्यान्या प्रसुप्ता भाति भामिनी।

वसन्ते पुष्पशबला मालेव परिमार्जिता॥5.10.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: कलशीम् water pot or pitcher, अपविध्य having thrown aside/set aside, अन्या another (woman), प्रसुप्ता lay asleep, भाति appears/shines, भामिनी the beautiful woman, वसन्ते in spring, पुष्प-शबला adorned with many flowers (lit. flower-speckled), माला garland, इव like, परिमार्जिता scattered, disheveled (or loosely strewn)

Anvay: अन्या भामिनी कलशीम् अपविध्य प्रसुप्ता – वसन्ते पुष्पशबला परिमार्जिता माला इव – भाति।

Simple meaning: Another beautiful woman, having cast aside her water pot, lay asleep—appearing like flower-speckled garland loosely strewn during the spring season.

पाणिभ्यां च कुचौ काचित्सुवर्णकलशोपमौ।

उपगूह्याबला सुप्ता निद्राबलपराजिता॥5.10.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: पाणिभ्याम् with (both) hands, च  and, कुचौ (her) breasts, काचित् a certain (lady)/one woman, सुवर्ण-कलश-उपमौ like golden pitchers, उपगूह्य embracing/holding close, अबला the delicate woman, सुप्ता was asleep, निद्रा-बल-पराजिता overpowered by the strength of sleep

Anvay: काचित् अबला सुवर्णकलशोपमौ कुचौ पाणिभ्याम् उपगूह्य निद्राबलपराजिता सुप्ता।

Simple meaning: A certain delicate woman, embracing her breasts (which were) like golden pitchers with both hands, was asleep—overpowered by the strength of sleep.

अन्या कमलपत्राक्षी पूर्णेन्दुसदृशानना।

अन्यामालिङ्ग्य सुश्रोणीं प्रसुप्ता मदविह्वला॥5.10.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्या another (woman), कमल-पत्र-अक्षी with eyes like lotus petals, पूर्ण-इन्दु-सदृश-आनना whose face resembled the full moon, अन्याम् another (woman), आलिङ्ग्य  embracing, सुश्रोणीम् one with beautiful hips, प्रसुप्ता was sleeping soundly, मद-विह्वला overcome with intoxication (drunk, or lost in passion or pleasure)

Anvay: अन्या कमलपत्राक्षी पूर्णेन्दुसदृशानना मदविह्वला अन्यां सुश्रोणीम् आलिङ्ग्य प्रसुप्ता।

Simple meaning: Another woman, with lotus-petal-like eyes and a face like the full moon, lay fast asleep—intoxicated, embracing yet another woman with beautiful hips.

आतोद्यानि विचित्राणि परिष्वज्य वरस्त्रियः।

निपीड्य च कुचैस्सुप्ता कामिन्यः कामुकानिव॥5.10.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: आतोद्यानि musical instruments, विचित्राणि various/of many kinds/decorative, परिष्वज्य embracing/holding close, वर-स्त्रियः beautiful women, निपीड्य pressing, च and, कुचैः with (their) breasts, सुप्ता were sleeping, कामिन्यः passionate women/loving women, कामुकान् इव  as if with lovers/like with passionate men

आतोद्य (नपुंसकलिङ्गः)  समानार्थकाः — वाद्य वादित्र आतोद्य A musical instrument; src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

Anvay: वरस्त्रियः विचित्राणि आतोद्यानि परिष्वज्य कुचैः निपीड्य च – कामिन्यः कामुकान् इव – सुप्ता।

Simple meaning: The beautiful women, holding various kinds of musical instruments close and pressing them with their breasts, were asleep—like loving women with their lovers.

तासामेकान्तविन्यस्ते शयानां शयने शुभे।

ददर्श रूपसम्पन्नामपरां स कपिः स्त्रियम्॥5.10.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: तासाम् among them/of those (women), एकान्त-विन्यस्ते placed in seclusion/set apart, शयानाम् who were lying down/sleeping, शयने on a bed, शुभे auspicious/beautiful, ददर्श (he) saw, रूप-सम्पन्नाम् endowed with beauty/full of grace, अपराम्  another (woman), सः कपिः that monkey (Hanuman), स्त्रियम् woman

Anvay: सः कपिः तासां शयानाम् एकान्तविन्यस्ते शुभे शयने रूपसम्पन्नाम् अपरां स्त्रियं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Among the sleeping women, Hanuman saw another beautiful woman, placed apart on a splendid bed.

मुक्तामणिसमायुक्तैर्भूषणैः सुविभूषिताम्।

विभूषयन्तीमिव तत्स्वश्रिया भवनोत्तमम्॥5.10.51॥

Word-by-word meaning: मुक्ता-मणि-समायुक्तैः adorned with pearls and precious gems, भूषणैः with ornaments, सुविभूषिताम् well-ornamented/beautifully adorned, विभूषयन्तीम् इव as if decorating/seeming to adorn, तत् that, स्व-श्रिया with her own radiance/natural beauty, भवन-उत्तमम्  the excellent palace/grand mansion

Anvay: मुक्तामणिसमायुक्तैः भूषणैः सुविभूषितां, स्वश्रिया तत् भवनोत्तमं विभूषयन्तीम् इव।

Simple meaning: Adorned beautifully with ornaments set with pearls and precious gems, she appeared as if she were enhancing that grand palace with the radiance of her own beauty.

गौरीं कनकवर्णाभामिष्टामन्तःपुरेश्वरीम्।

कपिर्मन्दोदरीं तत्र शयानां चारुरूपिणीम्॥5.10.52॥

Word-by-word meaning: गौरीम् fair-complexioned woman, कनक-वर्ण-आभाम् shining with a golden complexion, इष्टाम् beloved/dear, अन्तःपुरेश्वरीम् queen of the inner palace/chief among the harem women, कपिः the monkey (Hanuman), मन्दोदरीम् Mandodari (name of Ravana’s wife), तत्र there, शयनाम् lying down/sleeping, चारु-रूपिणीम् of beautiful form

Anvay: कपिः तत्र शयनां चारुरूपिणीं गौरीं कनकवर्णाभाम् इष्टाम् अन्तःपुरेश्वरीं मन्दोदरीम् (इति ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: There, the monkey Hanuman saw a fair and golden-complexioned woman of charming appearance, beloved and queen of the inner chambers—Mandodari—lying asleep.

स तां दृष्ट्वा महाबाहुर्भूषितां मारुतात्मजः।

तर्कयामास सीतेति रूपयौवनसम्पदा।

हर्षेण महता युक्तो ननन्द हरियूथपः॥5.10.53॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, ताम् her, दृष्ट्वा having seen, महा-बाहुः strong-armed, भूषिताम् adorned/decorated, मारुतात्मजः son of the Wind-God (Hanuman), तर्कयामास inferred, सीता इति “this is Sita”, रूप-यौवन-सम्पदा due to the wealth of beauty and youth, हर्षेण महता युक्तः filled with great joy, ननन्द rejoiced, हरि-यूथपः the leader of the monkey troops

Anvay: महाबाहुः हरियूथपः मारुतात्मजः सः रूपयौवनसम्पदा भूषितां तां दृष्ट्वा, सीता इति तर्कयामास। हर्षेण महता युक्तः ननन्द।

Simple meaning: Seeing that beautifully adorned woman, the strong-armed son of the Wind-God, Hanuman—the leader of the monkey army—thought, “This must be Sita,” judging by her beauty and youthful grace. Filled with great joy, he rejoiced.

आस्फोटयामास चुचुम्ब पुच्छं ननन्द चिक्रीड जगौ जगाम।

स्तम्भानरोहन्निपपात भूमौ निदर्शयन् स्वां प्रकृतिं कपीनाम्॥5.10.54॥

Word-by-word meaning: आस्फोटयामास clapped his hands/made a loud sound (with joy), चुचुम्ब kissed, पुच्छम् (his own) tail, ननन्द rejoiced, चिक्रीड frolicked/played about, जगौ sang, जगाम moved about/walked around, स्तम्भान्-अरोहत् climbed pillars, निपपात भूमौ leapt down to the ground, निदर्शयन् showing/displaying, स्वाम् प्रकृतिम् his natural behavior/inherent traits, कपीनाम् of monkeys

Anvay: सः (हनुमान्) आस्फोटयामास, पुच्छं चुचुम्ब, ननन्द, चिक्रीड, जगौ, जगाम, स्तम्भान् अरोहत्, भूमौ निपपात, कपीनां स्वां प्रकृतिं निदर्शयन्।

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman) clapped his hands, kissed his tail, rejoiced, frolicked, sang, wandered about, climbed pillars and leapt to the ground—thus displaying the natural playful behavior of monkeys.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे दशमः सर्गः॥10॥

Thus ends the tenth sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
