Sarga 11

सर्गः ११

ॐ

Sundarakanda Sarga 11                                                                                                                                                            

॥ॐ॥

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।

वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 11

Hanuman mistakes Mandodari for Sita, soon dismisses his wrong inference, sees the women of harem lying in disorder, cannot find Sita

(Benefit: Overcoming Cheating and Deceit)

(47 Shlokas)

अवधूय च तां बुद्धिं बभूवावस्थितस्तदा।

जगाम चापरां चिन्तां सीतां प्रति महाकपिः॥5.11.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: अवधूय having discarded/shaken off, च and, ताम् बुद्धिम् that thought/idea (i.e., that the woman was Sita), बभूव became, अवस्थितः तदा composed/steady at that time, जगाम went to/turned to, च and, अपराम् चिन्ताम् another thought/reflection, सीताम् प्रति about Sita, महाकपिः the great monkey (Hanuman)

Anvay: महाकपिः तदा तां बुद्धिम् अवधूय, आवस्थितः बभूव, सीतां प्रति अपरां चिन्तां जगाम।

Simple meaning: At that moment, the great monkey (Hanuman), having discarded that earlier thought (that the sleeping woman was Sita), composed himself and turned his mind to another line of reflection about Sita.

न रामेण वियुक्ता सा स्वप्तुमर्हति भामिनी।

न भोक्तुं वाप्यलङ्कर्तुं न पानमुपसेवितुम्॥5.11.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, रामेण वियुक्ता separated from Rama, सा she (Sita), स्वप्तुम् to sleep, अर्हति is capable of, भामिनी that noble and virtuous woman, न भोक्तुम् not to eat, वा अपि or even, अलङ्कर्तुम् to adorn herself, न पानम् उपसेवितुम् nor to drink (any beverage or liquor)

Anvay: सा भामिनी रामेण वियुक्ता — न स्वप्तुं, न भोक्तुं वा अलङ्कर्तुं, न पानम् उपसेवितुम् अर्हति।

Simple meaning: That noble and virtuous lady, separated from Rama, would neither sleep, nor eat, nor adorn herself, nor partake of any drink.

नान्यं नरमुपस्थातुं सुराणामपि चेश्वरम्।  

न हि रामसमः कश्चिद्विद्यते त्रिदशेष्वपि॥5.11.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, अन्यम् नरम् any other man, उपस्थातुम् to approach/to seek union with, सुराणाम् अपि च ईश्वरम् even the lord of the gods, न हि for indeed not, रामसमः equal to Rama, कश्चित् anyone, विद्यते exists, त्रिदशेषु अपि even among the gods

Gods are called त्रिदश because they  have only three stages in life, viz., childhood, adolescence and youth, the fourth stage, viz., old age, being absent in them.

Anvay: सुराणाम् ईश्वरम् अपि अन्यं नरं न उपस्थातुम् (अर्हति)।  त्रिदशेषु अपि रामसमः कश्चित् न विद्यते।

Simple meaning: Nor would she even approach another man—not even the lord of the gods—for no one, not even among the gods, equals Rama.

अन्येयमिति निश्चित्य पानभूमौ चचार सः।

Word-by-word meaning: अन्या इयम् this is another woman, इति निश्चित्य having determined thus, पान-भूमौ in the drinking hall/place of enjoyment, चचार moved about/wandered, सः he (Hanuman)

Anvay: सः (हनुमान्) ‘इयम् अन्या’ इति निश्चित्य, पानभूमौ चचार। 

Simple meaning: Having concluded, “This is another woman,” Hanuman wandered further in the hall of drinking and enjoyment.

क्रीडितेनापराः क्लान्ता गीतेन च तथाऽपराः॥5.11.4॥

नृत्तेन चापराः क्लान्ताः पानविप्रहतास्तथा।

Word-by-word meaning: क्रीडितेन by playing (or sport), अपराः other (women), क्लान्ताः tired/exhausted, गीतेन by singing, च and, तथा likewise, अपराः others, नृत्तेन by dancing, च and, अपराः other women, क्लान्ताः tired/worn out, पान-विप्रहताः overcome/intoxicated by drink, तथा likewise

Anvay: अपराः क्रीडितेन तथा अपराः गीतेन च क्लान्ताः, अपराः नृत्तेन तथा पानविप्रहताः च क्लान्ताः।

Simple meaning: Some women were tired from playing, others from singing, some were exhausted from dancing, and others were overcome with intoxication from drinking.

मुरजेषु मृदङ्गेषु पीठिकासु च संस्थिताः॥5.11.5॥

तथाऽऽस्तरणमुख्येषु संविष्टाश्चापराः स्त्रियः।

Word-by-word meaning: मुरजेषु on the muraja drums, मृदङ्गेषु on the mridanga drums, पीठिकासु on platforms or small seats, च and, संस्थिताः lying/resting/placed, तथा likewise, आस्तरण-मुख्येषु  on luxurious bedding or rich carpets, संविष्टाः lying down/sleeping, च and, अपराः other, स्त्रियः women

Anvay: स्त्रियः मुरजेषु मृदङ्गेषु पीठिकासु च संस्थिताः, तथा अपराः आस्तरणमुख्येषु संविष्टाः च।

Simple meaning: Some women were lying on muraja and mridanga drums and on small platforms; others were reclining on luxurious bedding and rich carpets.

अङ्गनानां सहस्रेण भूषितेन विभूषणैः॥5.11.6॥

रूपसँल्लापशीलेन युक्तगीतार्थभाषिणा।

Word-by-word meaning: अङ्गनानाम् of women, सहस्रेण by a thousand, भूषितेन adorned/decorated, विभूषणैः with ornaments, रूप-सँल्लाप-शीलेन endowed with qualities of beauty and charming conversation, युक्त-गीत-अर्थ-भाषिणा skilled in interpreting or expressing the essence of songs

Anvay: अङ्गनानां सहस्रेण विभूषणैः भूषितेन, रूपसँल्लापशीलेन, युक्तगीतार्थभाषिणा…

Simple meaning: Thousands of women adorned with ornaments, possessing the qualities of beauty, charming conversation, and the ability to speak meaningfully about the essence of songs…

देशकालाभियुक्तेन युक्तवाक्याभिधायिना॥5.11.7॥

रताभिरतसंसुप्तं ददर्श हरियूथपः।

Word-by-word meaning: देश-काल-अभियुक्तेन one who is appropriate to the time and place (i.e., tactful, situationally aware), युक्त-वाक्य-अभिधायिना one who speaks appropriate and fitting words, रताभिः with women engaged in pleasure, रत-संसुप्तम् exhausted and asleep after enjoyment, ददर्श saw, हरि-यूथपः the monkey-chief (Hanuman)

Anvay: हरियूथपः देशकालाभियुक्तेन युक्तवाक्याभिधायिना, रताभिः रतसंसुप्तं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the monkey-chief, saw thousands of women skilled in pleasing speech appropriate to time and place, women engaged in pleasure — lying asleep after indulgence.

तासां मध्ये महाबाहुः शुशुभे राक्षसेश्वरः॥5.11.8॥

गोष्ठे महति मुख्यानां गवां मध्ये यथा वृषः।

Word-by-word meaning: तासाम् मध्ये among those women, महाबाहुः the long-armed one (Ravana), शुशुभे shone/appeared splendid, राक्षसेश्वरः the lord of the rakshasas (Ravana), गोष्ठे in a cowshed, महति large/great, मुख्यानाम् गवाम् of the chief cows, मध्ये among, यथा like, वृषः a bull

Anvay: महाबाहुः राक्षसेश्वरः तासां मध्ये – महति गोष्ठे मुख्यानां गवां मध्ये वृषः यथा – शुशुभे।

Simple meaning: Among those women, Ravana—the mighty-armed lord of the rakshasas—shone brilliantly, like a strong bull among leading cows in a large cowshed.

स राक्षसेन्द्रः शुशुभे ताभिः परिवृतस्स्वयम्॥5.11.9॥

करेणुभिर्यथाऽरण्ये परिकीर्णो महाद्विपः।

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, राक्षसेन्द्रः the king of the rakshasas (Ravana), शुशुभे shone/looked resplendent, ताभिः by those women, परिवृतः surrounded, स्वयम् himself, करेणुभिः by female elephants, यथा just as/like, अरण्ये in a forest, परिकीर्णः encircled/scattered around, महा-द्विपः a great elephant

Anvay: सः राक्षसेन्द्रः स्वयं ताभिः परिवृतः – अरण्ये करेणुभिः परिकीर्णः महाद्विपः यथा – शुशुभे।

Simple meaning: The king of the rakshasas, Ravana, surrounded by those women, shone like a mighty elephant encircled by female elephants in the forest.

सर्वकामैरुपेतां च पानभूमिं महात्मनः॥5.11.10॥

ददर्श हरिशार्दूलस्तस्य रक्षःपतेर्गृहे।

Word-by-word meaning: सर्व-कामैः with all enjoyments/pleasures, उपेताम् endowed/filled, च and, पान-भूमिम् the hall of drinking/pleasure-hall, महात्मनः of the great-souled (Ravana), ददर्श saw, हरि-शार्दूलः the tiger among monkeys (Hanuman), तस्य of him (Ravana), रक्षःपतेः the lord of the rakshasas, गृहे in the palace/house

Anvay: हरिशार्दूलः सः महात्मनः तस्य रक्षःपतेः गृहे सर्वकामैः उपेतां पानभूमिं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the tiger among monkeys, saw the drinking hall of the great Ravana within his palace — a hall endowed with all kinds of pleasures.

मृगाणां महिषाणां च वराहाणां च भागशः॥5.11.11॥

तत्र न्यस्तानि मांसानि पानभूमौ ददर्श सः।

Word-by-word meaning: मृगाणाम् of deer, महिषाणाम् of buffaloes, च and, वराहाणाम् of wild boars, च and, भागशः in portions/pieces, तत्र there, न्यस्तानि placed/laid out, मांसानि meats, पान-भूमौ in the drinking hall, ददर्श saw, सः he (Hanuman)

Anvay: तत्र पानभूमौ सः मृगाणां महिषाणां वराहाणां च भागशः न्यस्तानि मांसानि ददर्श।

Simple meaning: There in the drinking hall, Hanuman saw pieces of meat from deer, buffaloes, and wild boars laid out in portions.

रौक्मेषु च विशालेषु भाजनेष्वर्धभक्षितान्॥5.11.12॥

ददर्श हरिशार्दूलो मयूरान् कुक्कुटांस्तथा।

Word-by-word meaning: रौक्मेषु made of gold, च and, विशालेषु large, भाजनेषु vessels/containers, अर्ध-भक्षितान् half-eaten, ददर्श saw, हरि-शार्दूलः the tiger among monkeys (i.e., Hanuman), मयूरान् peacocks, कुक्कुटान् roosters/fowls, तथा as well

Anvay: हरिशार्दूलः रौक्मेषु विशालेषु च भाजनेषु अर्धभक्षितान् मयूरान् तथा कुक्कुटान् ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the best among monkeys, saw half-eaten peacocks and fowls in large golden vessels.

वराहवार्ध्राणसकान् दधिसौवर्चलायुतान्॥5.11.13॥

शल्यान् मृगमयूरांश्च हनुमानन्ववैक्षत।

Word-by-word meaning: वराह-वार्ध्राणसकान् wild boar, rhinoceros, दधि-सौवर्चल-आयुतान् mixed/seasoned with curd, a type of spice (often identified with black salt or long pepper), शल्यान् skewered meat (usually on spits or skewers), मृग-मयूरान् च deer and peacocks, हनुमान् Hanuman, अन्ववैक्षत observed/saw attentively

Anvay: हनुमान् दधिसौवर्चलायुतान् वराहवार्ध्राणसकान् शल्यान् मृगमयूरान् च अन्ववैक्षत।

Simple meaning: Hanuman attentively observed skewered meats of wild boars and rhinoceroses, seasoned with curd and spices, as well as deer and peacocks.

क्रकरान्विविधान् सिद्धांश्चकोरानर्धभक्षितान्॥5.11.14॥

महिषानेकशल्यांश्च छागांश्च कृतनिष्ठितान्।

Word-by-word meaning: क्रकरान् krakaras (a kind of bird, possibly wild fowl or vulture-like birds), विविधान् of various kinds, सिद्धान् च cooked, चकोरान् chakora birds (a partridge-like bird), अर्ध-भक्षितान् half-eaten, महिषान् buffaloes, एक-शल्यान् च having single skewers (spits run through them), छागान् च goats, कृतनिष्ठितान् properly roasted/fixed on skewers

Anvay: (हनुमान्) विविधान् सिद्धान् क्रकरान्, अर्धभक्षितान् चकोरान्, एकशल्यान् महिषान्, कृतनिष्ठितान् छागान् च (ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw various types of cooked birds (karakaras), half-eaten chakora birds, buffaloes skewered on single spits, and well-roasted goats.

लेह्यानुच्चावचान्पेयान् भोज्यानि विविधानि च॥5.11.15॥

तथाऽऽम्ललवणोत्तंसैर्विविधै रागखाण्डवैः।

Word-by-word meaning: लेह्यान् items to be licked (lickable foods like sauces, chutneys, syrups), उच्चावचान् of various types, पेयान् drinkables (beverages), भोज्यानि eatables (solid foods), विविधानि of various kinds, च and, तथा likewise, आम्ल-लवण-उत्तंसैः garnished/flavored with sour (acidic) and salty items, विविधैः of various types, राग-खाण्डवैः the juice of grapes and pomegranates sweetened with sugar candy, honey etc., is known by the name of Raga in the fluid state and is called Khandava when thickened

उच्चावच (विशेषणम्)  अनेकप्रकारकम् src. Kosha, Ashtadhyayi

Anvay: उच्चावचान् लेह्यान्, पेयान्, विविधानि भोज्यानि च, तथा विविधैः आम्ललवणोत्तंसैः रागखाण्डवैः युक्तानि (ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw various kinds of lickable foods (sauces, pastes), drinkables, and solid eatables of many types, all garnished with sour and salty seasonings and juices and syrups.

हारनूपुरकेयूरैरपविद्धैर्महाधनैः॥5.11.16॥

पानभाजनविक्षिप्तैः फलैश्च विविधैरपि।

कृतपुष्पोपहारा भूरधिकं पुष्यति श्रियम्॥5.11.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: हार-नूपुर-केयूरैः with necklaces, anklets and armlets, अपविद्धैः thrown about, scattered, महाधनैः great treasures (ornaments of immense value), पान-भाजन-विक्षिप्तैः drinking vessels tossed or lying around, फलैः fruits, च and, विविधैः of various types, अपि also, कृत-पुष्प-उपहारा decorated with flower offerings, भूः the floor/area/space (i.e., the hall or room), अधिकम् greatly, पुष्यति bore/possessed/radiated, श्रियम् splendour/beauty/wealth

Anvay: महाधनैः हारनूपुरकेयूरैः अपविद्धैः, पानभाजनविक्षिप्तैः विविधैः फलैः च अपि, कृतपुष्पोपहारा भूः अधिकं श्रियं पुष्यति।

Simple meaning: The floor (or space) was adorned with great treasures—necklaces, anklets, and armlets scattered around—along with various kinds of fruits and overturned drinking vessels. Decorated with offerings of flowers, it radiated immense splendor.

तत्र तत्र च विन्यस्तै: सुश्लिष्टैश्शयनासनैः।

पानभूमिर्विना वह्निं प्रदीप्तेवोपलक्ष्यते॥5.11.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र तत्र  here and there, च and, विन्यस्तैः arranged, placed, सुश्लिष्टैः well-joined, closely set, well-arranged, शयन-आसनैः couches and seats (places to recline or lie upon), पान-भूमिः the drinking hall, विना वह्निम् without fire, प्रदीप्ता इव as if blazing, as though shining brightly, उपलक्ष्यते appears, is seen

Anvay: तत्र तत्र विन्यस्तैः सुश्लिष्टैः शयनासनैः च पानभूमिः – वह्निं विना प्रदीप्ता इव – उपलक्ष्यते।

Simple meaning: The drinking hall, with well-arranged and closely set couches and seats placed here and there, appeared as if it were ablaze with brilliance—even though there was no fire.

बहुप्रकारैर्विविधैर्वरसंस्कारसंस्कृतैः।

मांसैः कुशलसम्पृक्तैः पानभूमिगतैः पृथक्॥5.11.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: बहु-प्रकारैः of many kinds, in various forms, विविधैः various, diverse, वर-संस्कार-संस्कृतैः best, excellent, seasoning, preparation, made ready, prepared, मांसैः with meat dishes, कुशल-सम्पृक्तैः blended or associated with skill/expertise, पान-भूमि-गतैः placed or located in the drinking hall, पृथक् separately, distinctly

परदारावरोधस्य प्रसुप्तस्य निरीक्षणम्॥5.11.37॥

इदं खलु ममात्यर्थं धर्मलोपं करिष्यति।

Word-by-word meaning: पर-दारा-आवरोधस्य of the private quarters of another man's wife, प्रसुप्तस्य of (those who are) asleep, निरीक्षणम् the act of looking (observing), इदम् this, खलु indeed/truly, मम to me, अत्यर्थम् exceedingly, greatly, धर्म-लोपम् a transgression of dharma, moral lapse, करिष्यति will cause/will result in

Anvay: परदारावरोधस्य प्रसुप्तस्य इदं निरीक्षणं खलु मम अत्यर्थं धर्मलोपं करिष्यति।

Simple meaning: Indeed, looking at the private chambers of another man's wives while they are asleep will greatly lead to a lapse in my dharma.

न हि मे परदाराणां दृष्टिर्विषयवर्तिनी॥5.11.38॥

अयं चात्र मया दृष्टः परदारापरिग्रहः।

Word-by-word meaning: न हि indeed not, मे my, परदाराणाम् of other men’s wives, दृष्टिः sight/gaze, विषयवर्तिनी  turned toward sensual objects/sensual indulgence, अयम् च and this (fact), अत्र here/in this case, मया दृष्टः has been seen by me/I have seen, परदारा-परिग्रहः abstention from another man’s wife (i.e., moral restraint or vow of celibacy regarding others' wives)

Anvay: मे परदाराणां दृष्टिः विषयवर्तिनी न हि। च अत्र मया परदारापरिग्रहः अयं दृष्टः।

Simple meaning: Indeed, my gaze upon other men's wives is not driven by sensual desire. Moreover, I have observed here the vow (of restraint) of not coveting another’s wife.

तस्य प्रादुरभूच्चिन्ता पुनरन्या मनस्विनः॥5.11.39॥

निश्चितैकान्तचित्तस्य कार्यनिश्चयदर्शिनी।

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य his (i.e., Hanuman's), प्रादुरभूत् arose/appeared, चिन्ता  thought/anxiety, पुनः again, अन्या another/a different one, मनस्विनः of the thoughtful/intelligent one, निश्चित-एकान्त-चित्तस्य of one whose mind is firmly determined and focused on the goal, कार्य-निश्चय-दर्शिनी directed toward the accomplishment of his mission/task-focused

Anvay: निश्चितैकान्तचित्तस्य मनस्विनः तस्य पुनः अन्या कार्यनिश्चयदर्शिनी चिन्ता प्रादुरभूत्।

Simple meaning: For that wise and resolute Hanuman, whose mind was firmly fixed on his mission, another thought arose—one that was focused on the successful accomplishment of his task.

कामं दृष्टा मया सर्वा विश्वस्ता रावणस्त्रियः॥5.11.40॥

न हि मे मनसः किञ्चिद्वैकृत्यमुपजायते।

Word-by-word meaning: कामम् indeed/truly, दृष्टाः (have been) seen, मया by me, सर्वाः all, विश्वस्ताः unguarded/uninhibited/relaxed, रावण-स्त्रियः the women belonging to Ravana, न हि not indeed, मे to me/in me, मनसः of the mind, किञ्चित् even a little, वैकृत्यम् agitation/distraction/perversion, उपजायते arises/is born

Anvay: कामं मया सर्वाः विश्वस्ताः रावणस्त्रियः दृष्टाः। मे मनसः किञ्चित् वैकृत्यं न हि उपजायते।

Simple meaning: Indeed, I have seen all the women of Ravana, relaxed and unguarded. Yet, not even the slightest agitation or disturbance arises in my mind.

मनो हि हेतुः सर्वेषामिन्द्रियाणां प्रवर्तने॥5.11.41॥

शुभाशुभास्ववस्थासु तच्च मे सुव्यवस्थितम्।

Word-by-word meaning: मनः the mind, हि indeed, हेतुः is the cause, सर्वेषाम् of all, इन्द्रियाणाम् the senses, प्रवर्तने in their activity, engagement, शुभ-अशुभासु in good and bad (i.e., auspicious and inauspicious), अवस्थासु states/situations, तत् च and that (mind), मे my/in me, सु-व्यवस्थितम् is well-controlled/well-established/stable

Anvay: हि मनः सर्वेषाम् इन्द्रियाणां शुभाशुभासु अवस्थासु प्रवर्तने हेतुः। तत् च मे सुव्यवस्थितम्।

Simple meaning: Indeed, the mind is the cause behind the activity of all the senses in both auspicious and inauspicious situations, and my mind is well-disciplined and stable.

नान्यत्र हि मया शक्या वैदेही परिमार्गितुम्॥5.11.42॥

स्त्रियो हि स्त्रीषु दृश्यन्ते सदा सम्परिमार्गणे।

Word-by-word meaning: न not, अन्यत्र elsewhere/in any other way, हि indeed, मया by me, शक्या possible, वैदेही Sita (daughter of Videha, i.e., Janaka’s daughter), परिमार्गितुम् to search for/to be searched, स्त्रियः women, हि indeed, स्त्रीषु among women, दृश्यन्ते are seen/are found, सदा always, सम्परिमार्गणे in searching thoroughly

Anvay: वैदेही अन्यत्र परिमार्गितुं मया शक्या न हि। सदा सम्परिमार्गणे स्त्रियः स्त्रीषु दृश्यन्ते।

Simple meaning: Surely Sita, a princess of the Videha territory, could not be searched for by me anywhere else; for when search has to be carried out thoroughly, women are always looked for in the midst of women.

यस्य सत्त्वस्य या योनिस्तस्यां तत्परिमार्ग्यते।

न शक्या प्रमदा नष्टा मृगीषु परिमार्गितुम्॥5.11.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: यस्य of which, सत्त्वस्य being/creature/entity, या which, योनिः source/species/kind/class/category, तस्याम् in that, तत् that (i.e., the subject being searched), परिमार्ग्यते is searched/can be searched, न  not, शक्या possible, प्रमदा a woman/lady, नष्टा lost, मृगीषु among deer, परिमार्गितुम् to be searched for

Anvay: यस्य सत्त्वस्य या योनिः तत् तस्यां परिमार्ग्यते। नष्टा प्रमदा मृगीषु परिमार्गितुं न शक्या।

Simple meaning: An entity must be searched for within its own kind or class; a lost woman cannot be searched for among deer.

तदिदं मार्गितं तावच्छुद्धेन मनसा मया॥5.11.44॥

रावणान्तःपुरं सर्वं दृश्यते न तु जानकी।

Word-by-word meaning: तत् thus/therefore, इदम् this, मार्गितम् has been searched, तावत् so far/up to this extent, शुद्धेन pure, मनसा with the mind, मया by me, रावण-अन्तःपुरम् Ravana's inner palace/harem, सर्वम् entirely/completely, दृश्यते is seen, न तु but not, जानकी Janaki

Anvay: तावत् तत् इदं रावणान्तःपुरं सर्वं मया शुद्धेन मनसा मार्गितम्। जानकी तु न दृश्यते।

Simple meaning: So far, I have searched Ravana’s entire inner palace with a pure and disciplined mind. But Janaki is not to be seen.

देवगन्धर्वकन्याश्च नागकन्याश्च वीर्यवान्॥5.11.45॥

अवेक्षमाणो हनुमान्नैवापश्यत जानकीम्।

Word-by-word meaning: देव-गन्धर्व-कन्याः च daughters of Devas and Gandharvas also, नाग-कन्याः च and daughters of Nagas, वीर्यवान्  the mighty (Hanuman), अवेक्षमाणः observing/looking over, हनुमान् Hanuman, न एव not at all, अपश्यत् saw, जानकीम् Janaki

Anvay: वीर्यवान् हनुमान् देवगन्धर्वकन्याः च नागकन्याः च अवेक्षमाणः, जानकीं न एव अपश्यत्।

Simple meaning: The mighty Hanuman, while looking at the daughters of Devas, Gandharvas, and Nagas, did not see Janaki among them.

तामपश्यन्कपिस्तत्र पश्यञ्श्चान्या परस्त्रियः॥5.11.46॥

अपक्रम्य तदा वीरः प्रध्यातुमुपचक्रमे।

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her (Sita), अपश्यन् not seeing, कपिः the monkey (Hanuman), तत्र there (in that place), पश्यन् while seeing, च and, अन्याः other, पर-स्त्रियः women belonging to others (wives of other men), अपक्रम्य having withdrawn (from there), तदा then, वीरः the valiant one, प्रध्यातुम् to reflect deeply/to contemplate, उपचक्रमे began/undertook

Anvay: वीरः कपिः तदा तत्र ताम् (सीताम्) अपश्यन्, अन्याः परस्त्रियः पश्यन् च, अपक्रम्य प्रध्यातुम् उपचक्रमे।

Simple meaning: Not finding Sita there and having seen the other women (who were wives of others), the valiant Hanuman withdrew from that place and began to contemplate deeply.

स भूयस्तु परं श्रीमान् मारुतिर्यत्नमास्थितः।

आपानभूमिमुत्सृज्य तद्विचेतुं प्रचक्रमे॥5.11.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Hanuman), भूयः again, तु but/then, परम् greater/further, श्रीमान् glorious/noble, मारुतिः son of the Wind-God, यत्नम् effort, आस्थितः undertook/adopted, आपान-भूमिम् the drinking hall/place where drinking was done, उत्सृज्य leaving behind, तत् that (other place/rest of the palace), विचेतुम् to search, प्रचक्रमे began/commenced

Anvay: श्रीमान् सः मारुतिः भूयः तु परं यत्नम् आस्थितः, आपानभूमिम् उत्सृज्य तत् विचेतुं प्रचक्रमे।

Simple meaning: The glorious Hanuman, again taking up greater effort, left the drinking hall and began to search the rest (of the palace).

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे एकादशः सर्गः॥11॥

Thus ends the eleventh sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
