मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।

वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 14

Hanuman ascends the shimshupa tree in the Ashoka grove and watches

(52 Shlokas)

स मुहूर्तमिव ध्यात्वा मनसा चाधिगम्य ताम्।

अवप्लुतो महातेजाः प्राकारं तस्य वेश्मनः॥5.14.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, मुहूर्तम् इव for a moment, ध्यात्वा having meditated (reflected), मनसा च and with his mind, अधिगम्य ताम् having mentally reached her (Sita), अवप्लुतः jumped/leapt, महातेजाः of great brilliance/power, प्राकारम् (to) the outer wall or rampart, तस्य वेश्मनः of that palace (of Ravana)

Anvay: सः महातेजाः (हनुमान्) मुहूर्तम् इव ध्यात्वा, मनसा च ताम् (सीताम्) अधिगम्य, तस्य वेश्मनः प्राकारम् अवप्लुतः।

Simple meaning: He, the mighty and radiant Hanuman, having meditated for a moment and reached Sita in his mind, leapt over the rampart of that palace.

स तु संहृष्टसर्वाङ्गः प्राकारस्थो महाकपिः।

पुष्पिताग्रान्वसन्तादौ ददर्श विविधान् द्रुमान्॥5.14.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः तु he indeed, संहृष्ट-सर्वाङ्गः with all his limbs thrilled with joy, प्राकार-स्थः standing on the rampart (outer wall), महाकपिः the great monkey (Hanuman), पुष्पित-अग्रान् having blossomed tops (i.e., flowering at the tips), वसन्त-आदौ at the beginning of spring, ददर्श saw, विविधान् various, द्रुमान् trees

Anvay: प्राकारस्थः संहृष्टसर्वाङ्गः सः महाकपिः वसन्तादौ पुष्पिताग्रान् विविधान् द्रुमान् ददर्श।

Simple meaning: That great monkey, standing on the rampart, his whole body thrilled with joy, saw various trees blooming at the beginning of spring.

सालानशोकान् भव्यांश्च चम्पकांश्च सुपुष्पितान्।

उद्दालकान्नागवृक्षांश्चूतान्कपिमुखानपि॥5.14.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सालान् sal trees, अशोकान् ashoka trees, भव्यान् च and splendid ones, चम्पकान् च and champaka trees, सुपुष्पितान् well-bloomed, fully flowered, उद्दालकान् uddalaka trees (a flowering tree), नाग-वृक्षान् naga trees (another large flowering tree), चूतान् कपिमुखान् अपि also mangoes in the colour of the snout of a monkey

Anvay: सः सालान् भव्यान् अशोकान् च सुपुष्पितान् चम्पकान् च उद्दालकान् नागवृक्षान् कपिमुखान् चूतान् अपि (ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman) saw sal trees, splendid ashoka trees, champaka trees in full bloom, uddalaka trees, naga trees, and even mangoes in the colour of the snout of a monkey.

अथाम्रवणसञ्छन्नां लताशतसमावृताम्।

ज्यामुक्त इव नाराचः पुप्लुवे वृक्षवाटिकाम्॥5.14.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथ then/thereafter, आम्र-वण-सञ्छन्नाम् covered with a grove of mango trees, लता-शत-समावृताम् enveloped with hundreds of creepers, ज्या-मुक्तः released from the bowstring, इव like, नाराचः a sharp arrow (a kind of long, straight arrow), पुप्लुवे leapt or sprang, वृक्ष-वाटिकाम् into the grove of trees (garden full of trees)

Anvay: अथ सः आम्रवणसञ्छन्नां लताशतसमावृतां वृक्षवाटिकां – ज्यामुक्तः नाराचः इव – पुप्लुवे।

Simple meaning: Then he (Hanuman) sprang into the tree-garden, which was covered with a mango grove and thick with hundreds of creepers, like a sharp arrow released from a bowstring.

स प्रविश्य विचित्रां तां विहगैरभिनादिताम्।

राजतैः काञ्चनैश्चैव पादपैः सर्वतो वृताम्॥5.14.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Hanuman), प्रविश्य having entered, विचित्राम् wondrous/beautiful/variously adorned, ताम् that (garden), विहगैः by birds, अभिनादिताम् filled with sounds (resounded), राजतैः with silver, काञ्चनैः च एव and also with gold, पादपैः with trees, सर्वतः on all sides, वृताम् covered or surrounded

Anvay: सः तां विचित्रां विहगैः अभिनादितां, सर्वतः राजतैः काञ्चनैः च एव पादपैः वृतां प्रविश्य…

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman), having entered that wondrous grove, which was resounding with the cries of birds and surrounded on all sides by trees shining with silver and gold—

विहगैर्मृगसङ्घैश्च विचित्रां चित्रकाननाम्।

उदितादित्यसङ्काशां ददर्श हनुमान् कपिः॥5.14.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: विहगैः with birds, मृग-सङ्घैः च and with groups of deer, विचित्राम् wondrous, variegated, चित्र-काननाम् lovely forest/garden, उदित-आदित्य-सङ्काशाम् shining like the rising sun, ददर्श saw, हनुमान् Hanuman, कपिः the vanara

Anvay: कपिः हनुमान् विहगैः मृगसङ्घैः च उदितादित्यसङ्काशां विचित्रां चित्रकाननां ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the vanara, beheld that beautiful and wondrous grove, adorned with birds and herds of deer, and shining like the rising sun.

वृतां नानाविधैर्वृक्षैः पुष्पोपगफलोपगैः।

कोकिलैर्भृङ्गराजैश्च मत्तैर्नित्यनिषेविताम्॥5.14.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: वृताम् surrounded, covered, नाना-विधैः of various kinds, वृक्षैः with trees, पुष्प-उपग-फल-उपगैः bearing flowers and fruits, कोकिलैः by cuckoos, भृङ्ग-राजैः च and by king bees, मत्तैः intoxicated (with honey or joy), नित्य-निषेविताम् constantly frequented (or inhabited)

उपग  furnished with, src.Kosha Ashtadhyayi

Anvay: नानाविधैः पुष्पोपगफलोपगैः वृक्षैः वृतां, मत्तैः कोकिलैः भृङ्गराजैः च नित्यनिषेविताम् (वाटिकां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: (He saw) the grove surrounded by trees of various kinds, bearing flowers and fruits, and constantly frequented by intoxicated cuckoos and king bees.

प्रहृष्टमनुजे काले मृगपक्षिसमाकुले।

मत्तबर्हिणसङ्घुष्टां नानाद्विजगणायुताम्॥5.14.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रहृष्ट-मनुजे in a time (or season) when humans are joyful, काले at the time/season (contextually: spring or pleasant season), मृग-पक्षि-समाकुले filled with beasts and birds, मत्त-बर्हिण-सङ्घुष्टाम् resounding with the cries of intoxicated peacocks, नाना-द्विज-गण-आयुताम् filled with flocks of various birds (dvija = birds, also means ‘twice-born’)

Anvay: प्रहृष्टमनुजे काले, मृगपक्षिसमाकुले, मत्तबर्हिणसङ्घुष्टां नानाद्विजगणायुताम् (वाटिकां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: (He saw) the grove during a joyful season for humans, filled with animals and birds, resounding with the cries of intoxicated peacocks, and populated with flocks of various birds.

मार्गमाणो वरारोहां राजपुत्रीमनिन्दिताम्।

सुखप्रसुप्तान्विहगान् बोधयामास वानरः॥5.14.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: मार्गमाणः searching for, वरारोहाम् of excellent figure, best in form (referring to Sita), राज-पुत्रीम् the princess (daughter of a king), अनिन्दिताम् blameless, faultless, सुख-प्रसुप्तान् comfortably sleeping, विहगान् birds, बोधयामास woke up, वानरः the vanara (Hanuman)

Anvay: वानरः वरारोहाम् अनिन्दितां राजपुत्रीं मार्गमाणः, सुखप्रसुप्तान् विहगान् बोधयामास।

Simple meaning: While searching for the blameless princess of excellent form, the vanara (Hanuman) woke up the birds that were sleeping comfortably.

उत्पतद्भिर्द्विजगणैः पक्षैः सालास्समाहताः।

अनेकवर्णा विविधा मुमुचुः पुष्पवृष्टयः॥5.14.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: उत्पतद्भिः flying up, द्विज-गणैः by flocks of birds, पक्षैः by their wings, सालाः sala trees, समाहताः struck, shaken, अनेक-वर्णाः of many colors, विविधाः various kinds, मुमुचुः released, showered, पुष्प-वृष्टयः showers of flowers

Anvay: उत्पतद्भिः द्विजगणैः पक्षैः समाहताः सालाः अनेकवर्णाः विविधाः पुष्पवृष्टयः मुमुचुः।

Simple meaning: As flocks of birds flew up and struck the sala trees with their wings, the trees showered flowers of various kinds and colours.

पुष्पावकीर्णश्शुशुभे हनुमान् मारुतात्मजः।

अशोकवनिकामध्ये यथा पुष्पमयो गिरिः॥5.14.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुष्प-अवकीर्णः covered with flowers, शुशुभे shone, looked splendid, हनुमान् Hanuman, मारुत-आत्मजः the son of the Wind-God, अशोकवनिका मध्ये in the middle of the Ashoka grove, यथा like, पुष्पमयः made of flowers, गिरिः mountain

Anvay: पुष्पावकीर्णः मारुतात्मजः हनुमान् अशोकवनिका मध्ये पुष्पमयः गिरिः यथा शुशुभे।

Simple meaning: Covered with flowers, Hanuman, the son of the Wind-God, shone in the middle of the Ashoka grove like a mountain made of flowers.

﻿दिशस्सर्वाः प्रधावन्तं वृक्षषण्डगतं कपिम्।

दृष्ट्वा सर्वाणि भूतानि वसन्त इति मेनिरे॥5.14.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: दिशः सर्वाः all directions, प्रधावन्तम् rushing about, darting around, वृक्ष-षण्ड-गतम् gone into the grove of trees, कपिम् the monkey (Hanuman), दृष्ट्वा seeing, सर्वाणि भूतानि all creatures, वसन्तः इति (that) it was Spring, मेनिरे thought, assumed

Anvay: सर्वाणि भूतानि सर्वाः दिशः प्रधावन्तं वृक्षषण्डगतं कपिं दृष्ट्वा वसन्तः इति मेनिरे।

Simple meaning: Seeing the monkey (Hanuman) darting in all directions in the grove of trees, all the creatures thought that spring had arrived.

﻿वृक्षेभ्यः पतितैः पुष्पैरवकीर्णा पृथग्विदैः।

रराज वसुधा तत्र प्रमदेव विभूषिता॥5.14.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: वृक्षेभ्यः from the trees, पतितैः पुष्पैः with fallen flowers, अवकीर्णा scattered over, covered, पृथक्-विदैः of various kinds, रराज shone, looked splendid, वसुधा तत्र there, the earth, प्रमदा इव like a woman, विभूषिता adorned

Anvay: वृक्षेभ्यः पतितैः पृथग्विदैः पुष्पैः अवकीर्णा तत्र वसुधा – विभूषिता प्रमदा इव – रराज।

Simple meaning: There, the earth, covered with various kinds of flowers fallen from the trees, shone like a woman beautifully adorned with ornaments.

तरस्विना ते तरवस्तरसाभिप्रकम्पिताः।

कुसुमानि विचित्राणि ससृजुः कपिना तदा॥5.14.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: तरस्विना by the swift one (i.e., by the fast-moving one, Hanuman), ते तरवः those trees, तरसा अभिप्रकम्पिताः shaken strongly/thoroughly by speed (by his force), कुसुमानि विचित्राणि various colorful flowers, ससृजुः released/dropped/shed, कपिना तदा by the monkey then (i.e., at that time by Hanuman)

Anvay: ते तरवः तरस्विना कपिना तरसा अभिप्रकम्पिताः तदा विचित्राणि कुसुमानि ससृजुः।

Simple meaning: Those trees, shaken strongly by the swift-moving Hanuman, then shed various beautiful and colorful flowers.

निर्धूतपत्रशिखराः शीर्णपुष्पफला द्रुमाः।

निक्षिप्तवस्त्राभरणा धूर्ता इव पराजिताः॥5.14.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: निर्धूत-पत्र-शिखराः with their leaves and tops shaken, शीर्ण-पुष्प-फलाः with flowers and fruits fallen, द्रुमाः the trees, निक्षिप्त-वस्त्र-आभरणाः with their garments and ornaments cast off (metaphorically), धूर्ताः इव like rogues/tricksters, पराजिताः defeated

Anvay: निर्धूतपत्रशिखराः शीर्णपुष्पफलाः द्रुमाः – निक्षिप्तवस्त्राभरणाः पराजिताः धूर्ताः इव – (अभवन्)।

Simple meaning: The trees, with their leaves and tops shaken, their flowers and fruits fallen, looked like defeated tricksters—stripped of their clothes and ornaments.

हनूमता वेगवता कम्पितास्ते नगोत्तमाः।

पुष्पपर्णफलान्याशु मुमुचुः पुष्पशालिनः॥5.14.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: हनूमता by Hanuman, वेगवता swift, powerful (lit. "possessed of speed/force"), कम्पिताः shaken, ते those, नग​-उत्तमाः excellent trees, पुष्प-पर्ण-फलानि flowers, leaves, and fruits, आशु swiftly, immediately, मुमुचुः shed, dropped, पुष्प-शालिनः full of flowers (i.e., flowering trees)

Anvay: ते पुष्पशालिनः नगोत्तमाः हनूमता वेगवता कम्पिताः आशु पुष्पपर्णफलानि मुमुचुः।

Simple meaning: Shaken by the swift and mighty Hanuman, those excellent, flower-laden trees swiftly shed their flowers, leaves, and fruits.

विहङ्गसङ्घैर्हीनास्ते स्कन्धमात्राश्रया द्रुमाः।

बभूवुरगमाः सर्वे मारुतेनेव निर्धुताः।5.14.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: विहङ्ग-सङ्घैः by flocks of birds, हीनाः deprived, ते those, स्कन्ध-मात्र-आश्रयाः being resort to branches only, द्रुमाः trees, बभूवुः became, अगमाः unapproachable (because of being without beauty, devoid of charm), सर्वे all, मारुतेन इव as if by a wind, निर्धुताः shaken

Anvay: ते सर्वे द्रुमाः विहङ्गसङ्घैः हीनाः, स्कन्धमात्राश्रयाः – मारुतेन निर्धुताः इव – अगमाः बभूवुः।

Simple meaning: All those trees, deprived of their flocks of birds and being bare branched only, became unapproachable (due to being devoid of beauty), shaken as if by a gust of wind.

निर्धूतकेशी युवतिर्यथा मृदितवर्णका।

निष्पीतशुभदन्तोष्ठी नखैर्दन्तैश्च विक्षता॥5.14.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: निर्धूत-केशी with disheveled hair, युवतिः young woman, यथा like/as, मृदित-वर्णका with bruised color/complexion, निष्पीत-शुभ-दन्त-ओष्ठी with beautiful teeth and lips sucked or bitten, नखैः by nails, दन्तैः च and by teeth, विक्षता scratched, wounded

Anvay: निर्धूतकेशी मृदितवर्णका निष्पीतशुभदन्तोष्ठी नखैः दन्तैः च विक्षता युवतिः यथा..

Simple meaning: Like a young woman with disheveled hair, her complexion bruised, her lovely lips and teeth ravaged, scratched by nails and teeth…

तथा लाङ्गूलहस्तैश्च चरणाभ्यां च मर्दिता।

बभूवाशोकवनिका प्रभग्नवरपादपा॥5.14.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: तथा likewise/in the same way, लाङ्गूल-हस्तैः च by the tail and the hands, चरणाभ्याम् च and by the feet, मर्दिता crushed, trampled, pressed, बभूव became, अशोकवनिका Ashoka grove, प्रभग्न-वर-पादपा with excellent trees violently broken

Anvay: …तथा अशोकवनिका लाङ्गूलहस्तैः च चरणाभ्यां च मर्दिता प्रभग्नवरपादपा बभूव।

Simple meaning: …likewise, trampled by his tail, hands, and feet, the Ashoka grove was shattered, with its finest trees broken.

महालतानां दामानि व्यथमत्तरसा कपिः।

यथा प्रावृषि विन्ध्यस्य मेघजालानि मारुतः॥5.14.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: महा-लतानाम् of the great creepers/vines, दामानि clusters/garlands (i.e., entwinings, festoons), व्यधमत् shook, disturbed, agitated, तरसा forcefully, with speed or strength, कपिः the monkey (Hanuman), यथा just as, प्रावृषि in the rainy season, विन्ध्यस्य of the Vindhya mountains, मेघ-जालानि masses of clouds, मारुतः the wind

Simple meaning: The monkey (Hanuman), with great force, shook the festoons of huge creepers, just as the wind shakes the cloud formations over the Vindhya mountains during the rainy season.

स तत्र मणिभूमीश्च राजतीश्च मनोरमाः।

भूमीः, राजतीः मनोरमाः - all are द्वितीयाबहुवचनम्

Anvay: सः कपिः तत्र विचरन् मणिभूमीः च राजतीः च तथा मनोरमाः काञ्चनभूमीः च ददर्श।

Simple meaning: While roaming there, the monkey (Hanuman) saw delightful floors made of jewels, silver, and gold.

﻿वापीश्च विविधाकाराः पूर्णाः परमवारिणा।

Word-by-word meaning: वापीः tanks, reservoirs, च and, विविध-आकाराः of various shapes/forms, पूर्णाः filled, परम-वारिणा with excellent water, महार्हैः very precious, of great value, मणि-सोपानैः with steps/stairways made of gems, उपपन्नाः furnished, equipped, ततः ततः here and there, in various places

Anvay: विविधाकाराः परमवारिणा पूर्णाः वापीः च ततः ततः महार्हैः मणिसोपानैः उपपन्नाः (हनुमान् ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw reservoirs of various shapes, filled with excellent water, and adorned with splendid jeweled stairways, located here and there.

मुक्ताप्रवालसिकताः स्फाटिकान्तरकुट्टिमाः।

काञ्चनैस्तरुभिश्चित्रैस्तीरजैरुपशोभिताः॥5.14.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: मुक्ता-प्रवाल-सिकताः having sands made of pearls and coral (मुक्ता = pearl, प्रवाल = coral, सिकताः = sands), स्फाटिक-अन्तर-कुट्टिमाः with inner pavements made of crystal (स्फाटिक = crystal, अन्तर = inner, कुट्टिमाः = floors or pavements), काञ्चनैः golden, तरुभिः with trees, चित्रैः variegated, beautiful, तीरजैः growing on the banks (तीर = bank, ज = born/grown), उपशोभिताः adorned, beautified

Anvay: मुक्ताप्रवालसिकताः स्फाटिकान्तरकुट्टिमाः काञ्चनैः चित्रैः तीरजैः तरुभिः उपशोभिताः (वाप्यः आसीन्)।

Simple meaning: The reservoirs had sands of pearls and coral, pavements made of crystal, and were beautified with golden, variegated trees growing along their banks.

फुल्लपद्मोत्पलवनाश्चक्रवाकोपकूजिताः।

नत्यूहरुतसंघुष्टा हंससारसनादिताः॥5.14.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: फुल्ल-पद्म-उत्पल-वनाः having groves of blooming lotuses (padma = lotus) and blue lilies (utpala = blue lotus), चक्रवाक-उपकूजिताः softly cooed by chakravaka birds (a type of waterfowl, often symbolic of love and separation), नत्यूह-रुत-संघुष्टाः resounding with the cries of natyuha birds (possibly curlews or herons), हंस-सारस-नादिताः filled with the sounds of hamsas (swans) and sarasa birds (cranes)

Anvay: फुल्लपद्मोत्पलवनाः, चक्रवाकोपकूजिताः, नत्यूहरुतसंघुष्टाः, हंससारसनादिताः (वाप्यः) आसन्।

Simple meaning: The ponds were filled with blooming lotus and lily groves, softly cooed by chakravaka birds, resounding with the cries of natyuha birds, and filled with the sounds of swans and cranes.

दीर्घाभिर्द्रुमयुक्ताभिः सरद्भिश्च समन्ततः।

अमृतोपमतोयाभिश्शिवाभिरुपसंस्कृताः॥5.14.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: दीर्घाभिः long, द्रुम-युक्ताभिः furnished with trees, सरद्भिः with lakes (सर = lake or pond), च and, समन्ततः on all sides, all around, अमृत-उपम-तोयाभिः with waters like nectar (अमृत = nectar, उपम = like, तोय = water), शिवाभिः auspicious, beautiful, उपसंस्कृताः adorned, embellished

Anvay: ते (वाप्यः) दीर्घाभिः द्रुमयुक्ताभिः सरद्भिः च, समन्ततः अमृतोपमतोयाभिः शिवाभिः उपसंस्कृताः (आसन्)।

Simple meaning: Those ponds were adorned on all sides with long lakes lined with trees and filled with beautiful, nectar-like waters.

लताशतैरवततास्सन्तानकुसुमावृताः।

नानागुल्मावृतघनाः करवीरकृतान्तराः॥5.14.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: लता-शतैः with hundreds of creepers, अवतताः covered, spread over, सन्तान-कुसुम-आवृताः covered with Santana flowers (a fragrant, auspicious blossom), नाना-गुल्म-आवृत-घनाः thick with various bushes, करवीर-कृत-अन्तराः having oleander trees (karavīra) planted in between

Anvay: (ते उद्यानभागाः) लताशतैः अवतताः, सन्तानकुसुमैः आवृताः, नानागुल्मैः आवृतघनाः, करवीरकृतान्तराः (आसन्)।

Simple meaning: The garden areas were covered with hundreds of creepers, overlaid with Santana blossoms, thick with various shrubs, and interspersed with oleander trees.

ततोऽम्बुधरसङ्काशं प्रवृद्धशिखरं गिरिम्।

विचित्रकूटं कूटैश्च सर्वतः परिवारितम्॥5.14.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः thereafter/then, अम्बु-धर-सङ्काशम् resembling a raincloud (अम्बुधर = rain cloud, lit. one who holds water, सङ्काशम् = similar to), प्रवृद्ध-शिखरम् having lofty (greatly grown) peaks, गिरिम् a mountain, विचित्र-कूटम् with wondrous/craggy peaks, कूटैः च with peaks also, सर्वतः all around, परिवारितम् surrounded

Anvay: ततः (हनुमान्) अंबुधरसङ्काशं प्रवृद्धशिखरं विचित्रकूटं कूटैः च सर्वतः परिवारितं गिरिं (ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Then Hanuman saw a mountain resembling a raincloud, with lofty peaks, adorned with wonderful crags, and surrounded on all sides by other peaks.

शिलागृहैरवततं नानावृक्षैः समावृतम्।

ददर्श हरिशार्दूलो रम्यं जगति पर्वतम्॥5.14.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: शिला-गृहैः with stone caverns (rocky caves), अवततम् covered, filled, नाना-वृक्षैः with various kinds of trees, समावृतम् surrounded, ददर्श (he) saw, हरि-शार्दूलः the best among monkeys (Hanuman), रम्यम् beautiful, delightful, जगति in the world, पर्वतम् a mountain

Anvay: हरिशार्दूलः हनुमान् शिलागृहैः अवततं नानावृक्षैः समावृतं जगति रम्यं पर्वतं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the best among monkeys, saw the most beautiful mountain in the world, covered with rocky caves and surrounded by a variety of trees.

ददर्श च नगात्तस्मान्नदीं निपतितां कपिः।

अङ्कादिव समुत्पत्य प्रियस्य पतितां प्रियाम्॥5.14.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: ददर्श (he) saw, च and, नगात् from the mountain, तस्मात् from that (same) mountain, नदीम् a river, निपतिताम् falling down, descending, कपिः the monkey (Hanuman), अङ्कात् from the lap, इव as if, like, समुत्पत्य having risen suddenly, प्रियस्य of the beloved, पतिताम् fallen, प्रियाम् the beloved (female)

Anvay: कपिः तस्मात् नगात् निपतितां – प्रियस्य अङ्कात् समुत्पत्य पतितां प्रियाम् इव – नदीं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw a river cascading down from that mountain, like a beloved woman who has suddenly leapt up from her dear one's lap and fallen down.

जले निपतिताग्रैश्च पादपैरुपशोभिताम्।

Word-by-word meaning: जले in the water, निपतित-अग्रैः च​ with tips (of trees) touching  (the water), पादपैः with trees, उपशोभिताम् adorned, made beautiful, वार्यमाणाम् being restrained, held back, इव as if, like, क्रुद्धाम् angry, enraged, प्रमदाम् a young woman, प्रिय-बन्धुभिः by dear relatives, close companions

Anvay: जले निपतिताग्रैः पादपैः च​ उपशोभितां — प्रियबन्धुभिः वार्यमाणां क्रुद्धां प्रमदाम् इव।

Simple meaning: That river, adorned with the tree-tops touching its waters, appeared like an angry young woman being restrained by her dear ones.

पुनरावृत्ततोयां च ददर्श स महाकपिः।

प्रसन्नामिव कान्तस्य कान्तां पुनरुपस्थिताम्॥5.14.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुनः-आवृत्त-तोयाम् with waters that had returned again (i.e., replenished waters), च and, ददर्श saw, beheld, सः महाकपिः that great Vanara (Hanuman), प्रसन्नाम् calm, serene, joyful, इव as if, like, कान्तस्य of a beloved man, कान्ताम् the beloved woman, पुनः-उपस्थिताम्  again come back, returned

Anvay: सः महाकपिः पुनरावृत्ततोयां – कान्तस्य प्रसन्नां कान्तां पुनरुपस्थिताम् इव – ददर्श च।

Simple meaning: That great vanara (Hanuman) saw the river with its waters replenished, appearing as calm and joyful as a beloved woman who has returned to her beloved.

तस्या दूरात्सपद्मिन्यो नानाद्विजगणायुताः।

ददर्श हरिशार्दूलो हनुमान् मारुतात्मजः॥5.14.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्याः of that (river), दूरात् from a distance, स-पद्मिन्यः with lotuses (i.e., full of lotus plants), नाना-द्विज-गण-आयुताः filled with groups of various kinds of birds, ददर्श saw, हरि-शार्दूलः the tiger among vanaras (Hanuman), हनुमान् Hanuman, मारुत-आत्मजः son of the Wind-God

Anvay: मारुतात्मजः हरिशार्दूलः हनुमान् तस्याः (नद्याः) दूरात्, सपद्मिन्यः नानाद्विजगणायुताः (जलाशयाः) ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the tiger among vanaras and son of the Wind-God, saw from a distance lotus-filled waters teeming with flocks of various birds.

कृत्रिमां दीर्घिकां चापि पूर्णां शीतेन वारिणा।

Word-by-word meaning: कृत्रिमाम् artificial, man-made, दीर्घिकाम् pond, long reservoir, च अपि and also, पूर्णाम् filled, शीतेन वारिणा with cool water, मणि-प्रवर-सोपानाम् having excellent gem-studded stairways, मुक्ता-सिकत-शोभिताम् adorned with pearl-like sands (gleaming with pearls or white sand)

Anvay: सः (हनुमान्) शीतेन वारिणा पूर्णां मणिप्रवरसोपानां मुक्तासिकतशोभितां च कृत्रिमां दीर्घिकाम् अपि (ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman) also saw an artificial pond filled with cool water, adorned with excellent gem-studded stairways and gleaming with pearl-like sands.

विविधैर्मृगसङ्घैश्च विचित्रां चित्रकाननाम्।

प्रासादैस्सुमहद्भिश्च निर्मितैर्विश्वकर्मणा॥5.14.34॥

काननैः कृत्रिमैश्चापि सर्वतः समलङ्कृताम्।

Word-by-word meaning: विविधैः of various kinds, मृग-सङ्घैः च with herds of deer, विचित्राम् wonderful, varied, चित्र-काननाम् beautiful forested area, प्रासादैः with palaces, सुमहद्भिः च very grand, निर्मितैः built, विश्वकर्मणा by Vishvakarma (the celestial architect), काननैः with groves, कृत्रिमैः च अपि also artificial, सर्वतः all around, समलङ्कृताम् well adorned, beautifully decorated

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) विविधैः मृगसङ्घैः च विचित्रां चित्रकाननां, विश्वकर्मणा निर्मितैः सुमहद्भिः प्रासादैः, कृत्रिमैः काननैः अपि च सर्वतः समलङ्कृताम् (ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw a splendid and beautifully varied grove, full of herds of diverse deer, adorned on all sides with magnificent palaces built by Vishvakarma and with artificial gardens all around.

ये केचित्पादपास्तत्र पुष्पोपगफलोपगाः॥5.14.35॥

सच्छत्रास्सवितर्दीकास्सर्वे सौवर्णवैदिकाः।

Word-by-word meaning: ये केचित् पादपाः तत्र whatever trees were there, पुष्प-उपग-फल-उपगाः bearing flowers and fruits, सच्छत्राः with parasol-like (or umbrella-like) canopies, सवितर्दीकाः with proper seats or platforms (like small thrones or benches), सर्वे  all, सौवर्ण-वैदिकाः with golden altars/platforms (or golden railing-like enclosures)

Anvay: तत्र ये केचित् पादपाः पुष्पोपगफलोपगाः, सर्वे सच्छत्राः सवितर्दीकाः सौवर्णवैदिकाः (आसन्)।

Simple meaning: All the trees there, which bore flowers and fruits, seemed like canopies, and had proper seating platforms, and golden altars or railings.

लताप्रतानैर्बहुभिःपर्णैश्च बहुभिर्वृताम्॥5.14.36॥

काञ्चनीं शिंशुपामेकां ददर्श हरियूथपः।

वृतां हेममयीभिस्तु वेदिकाभिस्समन्ततः॥5.14.37॥

Word-by-word meaning: लता-प्रतानैः with creepers hanging down or spread around, बहुभिः many, पर्णैः च and with leaves, बहुभिः many, वृताम् covered or surrounded, काञ्चनीम् golden, शिंशुपाम् a Shimsupa tree (also known as Indian Rosewood or a symbolic sacred tree), एकाम् one, ददर्श saw, हरियूथपः the leader of the monkeys (Hanuman), वृताम् surrounded, हेममयीभिः made of gold,  तु indeed/but (used for emphasis), वेदिकाभिः by altars/platforms/railings, समन्ततः all around/on all sides,

Anvay: हरियूथपः बहुभिः लताप्रतानैः बहुभिः पर्णैः च वृतां, समन्ततः हेममयीभिः वेदिकाभिः वृताम्, एकां काञ्चनीं शिंशुपां तु ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the leader of the monkeys, saw a single golden Shimsupa tree, covered with many creepers and leaves, and surrounded on all sides by golden platforms or altars.

सोऽपश्यद्भूमिभागांश्च गर्तप्रस्रवणानि च।

सुवर्णवृक्षानपरान् ददर्श शिखिसन्निभान्॥5.14.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Hanuman), अपश्यत् saw, भूमि-भागान् च pieces/portions of land also, गर्त-प्रस्रवणानि च and (natural) hollows and springs, सुवर्ण-वृक्षान् golden trees,अपरान् other, additional, ददर्श saw, शिखि-सन्निभान् resembling a peacock

Anvay: सः (हनुमान्) भूमिभागान् च गर्तप्रस्रवणानि च अपश्यत्। अपरान् सुवर्णवृक्षान् शिखिसन्निभान् ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw various patches of land and natural springs and hollows. He also beheld other golden trees that resembled peacocks in their brilliance.

तेषां द्रुमाणां प्रभया मेरोरिव दिवाकरः।

अमन्यत तदा वीरः काञ्चनोऽस्मीति वानरः॥5.14.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: तेषाम् of those, द्रुमाणाम् trees, प्रभया by the radiance/brightness, मेरोः of Meru mountain, इव like, दिवाकरः the sun, अमन्यत thought/assumed, तदा then, वीरः the heroic one, काञ्चनः golden, अस्मि I am, इति thus, वानरः the vanara

Anvay: तदा तेषां द्रुमाणां प्रभया वीरः वानरः “मेरोः दिवाकरः इव काञ्चनः अस्मि” इति अमन्यत।

Simple meaning: At that moment, due to the radiance of the golden trees around him, the heroic vanara thought, “I am golden as Mount Meru due to the brilliance of the Sun.

तां काञ्चनैस्तरुगणैर्मारुतेन च वीजिताम्।

किङ्किणीशतनिर्घोषां दृष्ट्वा विस्मयमागमत्॥5.14.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् that (Ashoka grove/grove), काञ्चनैः golden, तरु-गणैः with groups of trees, मारुतेन by the wind, च and, वीजिताम् being fanned/gently swayed, किङ्किणी-शत-निर्घोषाम् echoing with the sound of hundreds of small bells (kinkinis), दृष्ट्वा having seen, विस्मयम् amazement/wonder, आगमत् he attained/was filled with

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) काञ्चनैः तरुगणैः मारुतेन च वीजितां, किङ्किणीशतनिर्घोषां तां (अशोकवाटिकां) दृष्ट्वा, विस्मयम् आगमत्।

Simple meaning: Upon seeing that Ashoka grove filled with golden trees, swaying gently in the wind, and resonating with the sound of hundreds of small bells, Hanuman was filled with wonder.

स पुष्पिताग्रां रुचिरां तरुणाङ्कुरपल्लवाम्।

तामारुह्य महाबाहुश्शिंशुपां पर्णसंवृताम्॥5.14.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Hanuman), पुष्पित-अग्राम् with blossomed tips (i.e., flowers blooming at the top), रुचिराम् beautiful/charming, तरुण-अङ्कुर-पल्लवाम् with tender sprouts and young leaves, ताम् that, आरुह्य having ascended/climbed, महाबाहुः the mighty-armed one (Hanuman), शिंशुपाम् the Shimshupa tree (a tree found in the Ashoka grove, possibly a rosewood or Indian kino tree), पर्ण-संवृताम् covered with leaves

Anvay: सः महाबाहुः तां पुष्पिताग्रां, रुचिरां, तरुणाङ्कुरपल्लवां, पर्णसंवृतां शिंशुपाम् आरुह्य…

Simple meaning: That mighty-armed Hanuman climbed the beautiful Shimshupa tree, which had blossoming tips, tender new shoots and leaves, and was thickly covered with foliage.

इतो द्रक्ष्यामि वैदेहीं रामदर्शनलालसाम्।

इतश्चेतश्च दुःखार्तां सम्पतन्तीं यदृच्छया॥5.14.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: इतः from here, द्रक्ष्यामि I shall see, वैदेहीम् Vaidehi, राम-दर्शन-लालसाम् longing to see Rama, इतः च​ from this direction also, इतः च​ and from that direction (i.e., in every direction), दुःख-आर्ताम् tormented by sorrow, सम्पतन्तीम् moving about/wandering, यदृच्छया by chance/as fate would have it

Anvay: इतः च इतः च यदृच्छया सम्पतन्तीं दुःखार्तां रामदर्शनलालसां वैदेहीम् इतः द्रक्ष्यामि।

Simple meaning: "From here, by chance, I may see Vaidehi (Sita), who is tormented by sorrow and longs to see Rama and must be wandering about here and there.

अशोकवनिका चेयं दृढं रम्या दुरात्मनः।

चम्पकैश्चन्दनैश्चापि बकुलैश्च विभूषिता॥5.14.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: अशोकवनिका the Ashoka grove, च and/indeed, इयम् this, दृढम् certainly/surely, रम्या delightful/beautiful, दुरात्मनः of the evil-minded one (i.e., Ravana), चम्पकैः with champaka trees (fragrant yellow flowers), चन्दनैः with sandalwood trees, च अपि and also, बकुलैः च with bakula trees, विभूषिता adorned/beautified

Anvay: चम्पकैः च चन्दनैः च अपि बकुलैः च विभूषिता रम्या इयम् अशोकवनिका दृढं दुरात्मनः (अस्ति)।

Simple meaning: "Surely this beautiful Ashoka grove adorned with delightful Champak, Chandan and Bakula trees belongs to the wicked Ravana.

इयं च नलिनी रम्या द्विजसङ्घनिषेविता।

इमां सा राममहिषी नूनमेष्यति जानकी॥5.14.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: इयम् this, च and, नलिनी lotus-pond/lotus-garden, रम्या beautiful/delightful, द्विज-सङ्घ-निषेविता frequented/inhabited by groups of birds, इमाम् to this (place), सा she (i.e., Sita), राम-महिषी Rama’s queen/consort, नूनम् surely/certainly, एष्यति will come, जानकी Janaka's daughter

Anvay: इयं रम्या नलिनी द्विजसङ्घनिषेविता च। इमां नूनम् सा राममहिषी जानकी एष्यति।

Simple meaning: "This beautiful lotus garden is frequented by flocks of birds — surely, Sita, the queen of Rama and daughter of Janaka, will come here.

सा रामा राममहिषी राघवस्य प्रिया सती।

वनसञ्चारकुशला नूनमेष्यति जानकी॥5.14.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (i.e., Sita), रामा pleasing one/delightful lady (a feminine form of Rama; also used as an epithet of Sita), राम-महिषी the queen/consort of Rama, राघवस्य of Raghava (another name for Rama), प्रिया beloved, सती chaste/virtuous woman, वन-सञ्चार-कुशला accustomed to moving about in the forest, नूनम् surely/certainly, एष्यति will come, जानकी daughter of Janaka

Anvay: सा सती रामा, राममहिषी, राघवस्य प्रिया, वनसञ्चारकुशला जानकी नूनम् एष्यति।

Simple meaning: "That chaste lady, Sita — the beloved of Raghava, Rama's queen, accustomed to roaming in the forest — will surely come here.

अथवा मृगशावाक्षी वनस्यास्य विचक्षणा।

वनमेष्यति साऽर्येह रामचिन्तानुकर्शिता॥5.14.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथवा or perhaps, मृग-शाव-अक्षी doe-eyed (having eyes like a मृग-शाव baby deer- fawn), वनस्य अस्य of this forest, विचक्षणा observant/intelligent/familiar, वनम् (to the) forest/grove, एष्यति will come, सा she (Sita), आर्या the noble lady, इह here, राम-चिन्ता-अनुकर्शिता pulled/drawn by thoughts of Rama, distressed due to constant contemplation of Rama

Anvay: अथवा मृगशावाक्षी सा आर्या अस्य वनस्य विचक्षणा रामचिन्तानुकर्शिता इह वनम् एष्यति।

Simple meaning: "Or perhaps, the noble doe-eyed lady, well acquainted with this forest, tormented by her constant thoughts of Rama, will come here.

रामशोकाभिसन्तप्ता सा देवी वामलोचना।

वनवासे रता नित्यमेष्यते वनचारिणी॥5.14.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: राम-शोक-अभिसन्तप्ता scorched/tormented by grief for Rama, सा she (i.e., Sita), देवी the noble lady/goddess-like woman, वाम-लोचना with beautiful (graceful/charming) eyes, वन-वासे in forest-dwelling/forest life, रता engaged/fond of, नित्यम् always/constantly, एष्यते comes/will come, वन-चारिणी she who wanders in the forest (forest-dweller)

Anvay: सा वामलोचना देवी रामशोकाभिसन्तप्ता नित्यं वनवासे रता वनचारिणी एष्यते।

Simple meaning: "That graceful-eyed lady, scorched by the sorrow of separation from Rama, always fond of forest life and who wanders in the forest, surely comes here.

वने चराणां सततं नूनं स्पृहयते पुरा।

रामस्य दयिता भार्या जनकस्यसुता सती॥5.14.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: वने चराणाम् for forest-dwellers/those who roam the forest, सततम् constantly, नूनम् surely/certainly, स्पृहयते desires/longs for, पुरा formerly/earlier, रामस्य of Rama, दयिता beloved, भार्या wife, जनकस्य of Janaka, सुता daughter, सती chaste/virtuous

Anvay: रामस्य दयिता भार्या, जनकस्य सुता सती, पुरा सततं वने चराणां नूनं स्पृहयते।

Simple meaning: "Surely, earlier, the chaste daughter of Janaka, the beloved wife of Rama, always longed for the company of forest-dwellers.

सन्ध्याकालमनाः श्यामा ध्रुवमेष्यति जानकी।

नदीं चेमां शुभजलां सन्ध्यार्थे वरवर्णिनी॥5.14.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: सन्ध्या-काल-मनाः with her mind set on twilight worship/focused on the evening twilight, श्यामा beautiful, ध्रुवम् surely/certainly, एष्यति will come, जानकी Janaki, नदीम् च इमाम् and this river, शुभ-जलाम् having auspicious/pure waters, सन्ध्या-अर्थे for the purpose of twilight rituals (i.e., evening prayers), वर-वर्णिनी the one with the best complexion/excellent beauty

Anvay: श्यामा वरवर्णिनी जानकी सन्ध्याकालमनाः सन्ध्यार्थे इमां शुभजलां नदीं ध्रुवम् एष्यति।

Simple meaning: "The beautiful, noble lady Janaki, intent on evening worship, will surely come to this river with its auspicious waters, for her twilight rituals.

तस्याश्चाप्यनुरूपेयमशोकवनिका शुभा।

शुभायाः पार्थिवेन्द्रस्य पत्नी रामस्य सम्मता॥5.14.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्याः च अपि and also for her (Sita), अनुरूपा इयम् suitable/appropriate is this, अशोक-वनिका शुभा beautiful Ashoka grove, शुभायाः of her who is auspicious, पार्थिव-इन्द्रस्य of the king of the Earth, पत्नी the wife, रामस्य of Rama, सम्मता respected/honored/suitable

Anvay: रामस्य पार्थिवेन्द्रस्य सम्मता पत्नी शुभायाः तस्याः इयं शुभा अशोकवनिका अनुरूपा च अपि।

Simple meaning: "This beautiful Ashoka grove is indeed suitable for her — the virtuous and auspicious wife of Rama, the supreme among kings.

यदि जीवति सा देवी ताराधिपनिभानना।

आगमिष्यति साऽवश्यमिमां शिवजलां नदीम्॥5.14.51॥

Word-by-word meaning: यदि जीवति if she is alive, सा देवी that divine lady, तारा-अधिप-निभ-आनना she whose face resembles the moon (lit. "with a face like the lord of stars," i.e., the moon), आगमिष्यति will certainly come, सा she, अवश्यम् surely/without fail, इमाम्  this, शिव-जलाम् auspicious/pure-watered, नदीम् river

Anvay: यदि सा देवी जीवति, ताराधिपनिभानना, सा अवश्यम् इमां शिवजलां नदीम् आगमिष्यति।

Simple meaning: "If that divine lady, whose face resembles the moon, is still alive, then she will surely come to this auspicious river.”

एवं तु मत्वा हनुमान्महात्मा प्रतीक्षमाणो मनुजेन्द्रपत्नीम्।

अवेक्षमाणश्च ददर्श सर्वं सुपुष्पिते पर्णघने निलीनः॥5.14.52॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् तु मत्वा having thought thus/having reasoned this way, हनुमान् Hanuman, महात्मा the great soul, प्रतीक्षमाणः waiting/expecting, मनुज-इन्द्र-पत्नीम् the wife of the lord of men (i.e., Rama's wife, Sita), अवेक्षमाणः च and observing/watching attentively, ददर्श he saw/observed, सर्वम् everything, सुपुष्पिते full of blossoms, पर्ण-घने thick with leaves/dense foliage, निलीनः hidden/concealed

Anvay: एवं तु मत्वा महात्मा हनुमान् मनुजेन्द्रपत्नीं प्रतीक्षमाणः, सुपुष्पिते पर्णघने निलीनः, अवेक्षमाणः च सर्वं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Having thought thus, the great Hanuman waited for the wife of the lord of men (Sita). Concealed within the dense foliage full of blossoms, he attentively observed everything around.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे चतुर्दशः सर्गः॥14॥

Thus ends the fourteenth sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
