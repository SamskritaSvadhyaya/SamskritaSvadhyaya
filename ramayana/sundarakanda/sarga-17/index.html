Sundarakanda Sarga 17                                                                                                                                                                        

॥ॐ॥

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।

वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 17

Hanuman hiding on the Shimshupa tree, experiences the presence of rakshasis

(32 Shlokas)

ततः कुमुदषण्डाभो निर्मलं निर्मलोदयः।

प्रजगाम नभश्चन्द्रो हंसो नीलमिवोदकम्॥5.17.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, कुमुद-षण्ड-आभः having the appearance of a cluster of kumuda flowers (white water-lilies), निर्मलम् clear/pure, निर्मल-उदयः having a spotless rise (free from blemish), or with a pure appearance at rise, प्रजगाम proceeded, moved forward, नभः in the sky, चन्द्रः the moon, हंसः like a swan, नीलम् blue, इव like, उदकम् water

Anvay: ततः कुमुदषण्डाभः निर्मलोदयः चन्द्रः – हंसः नीलम् उदकम् इव – निर्मलं नभः प्रजगाम।

Simple meaning: Then the moon, rising in spotless clarity and resembling a cluster of white water lilies, moved through the clear sky like a swan gliding over blue waters.

साचिव्यमिव कुर्वन् स प्रभया निर्मलप्रभः।

चन्द्रमा रश्मिभिश्शीतैस्सिषेवे पवनात्मजम्॥5.17.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: साचिव्यम् इव as though offering counsel or support (like a minister assisting a king), कुर्वन् doing, performing, सः he (the moon), प्रभया with his light, निर्मल-प्रभः one with pure, spotless radiance, चन्द्रमाः the moon, रश्मिभिः with rays, शीतैः cool, soothing, सिषेवे served, honored, attended to, पवनात्मजम् the son of the Wind-God (Hanuman)

Anvay: सः निर्मलप्रभः चन्द्रमाः शीतैः रश्मिभिः प्रभया – साचिव्यं कुर्वन् इव – पवनात्मजं सिषेवे।

Simple meaning: The moon, with his pure and spotless light, as though offering his support like a trusted minister, served Hanuman (the son of the Wind-God) with his cool and soothing rays.

स ददर्श ततः सीतां पूर्णचन्द्रनिभाननाम्।

शोकभारैरिव न्यस्तां भारैर्नावमिवाम्भसि॥5.17.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Hanuman), ददर्श saw, ततः then, सीताम् Sita, पूर्ण-चन्द्र-निभ-आननाम् having a face resembling the full moon, शोक-भारैः इव as if with the burdens of sorrow, न्यस्ताम् sunk, weighed down, भारैः by burdens, नावम् इव like a boat, अम्भसि in water

Anvay: सः ततः पूर्णचन्द्रनिभाननां सीतां शोकभारैः इव – अम्भसि भारैः नावम् इव – न्यस्तां ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Then Hanuman saw Sita, whose face resembled the full moon, looking weighed down by the burdens of sorrow, like a boat sunk in water under a heavy load.

दिदृक्षमाणो वैदेहीं हनुमान्मारुतात्मजः।

स ददर्शाविदूरस्था राक्षसीर्घोरदर्शनाः॥5.17.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: दिदृक्षमाणः desiring to see, वैदेहीम् Vaidehi (Sita, daughter of the King of Videha), हनुमान् Hanuman, मारुतात्मजः the son of the Wind-God, सः he, ददर्श saw, अविदूरस्थाः stationed not far away, nearby, राक्षसीः rakshasis, घोर-दर्शनाः terrifying in appearance

राक्षसी - ईकारान्तः, स्त्रीलिङ्गः, नदीसंज्ञकः, राक्षसीः द्वितियबहुवचनम्

Anvay: सः मारुतात्मजः हनुमान् वैदेहीं दिदृक्षमाणः अविदूरस्थाः घोरदर्शनाः राक्षसीः ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the son of the Wind-God, desiring to see Sita, saw rakshasis nearby who were terrifying in appearance.

एकाक्षीमेककर्णां च कर्णप्रावरणां तथा।

अकर्णां शङ्कुकर्णां च मस्तकोच्छ्वासनासिकाम्॥5.17.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: एक-अक्षीम् having one eye, एक-कर्णाम् having one ear, च and, कर्ण-प्रावरणाम् with ears covered (or having ears like flaps), तथा likewise, अ-कर्णाम् earless, शङ्कु-कर्णाम् having ears shaped like cones or spears, च and, मस्तक-उच्छ्वास-नासिकाम् one whose nose was raised high up on the forehead (literally: a nose puffing out from the head)

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) एकाक्षीम्, एककर्णां, कर्णप्रावरणां, तथा अकर्णां, शङ्कुकर्णां, मस्तकोच्छ्वासनासिकां राक्षसीम् (अपि ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman) saw rakshasis with monstrous forms — one with a single eye, one with a single ear, one whose ears were like flaps, another with no ears at all, one with pointed (conical) ears, and one whose nose puffed out from her forehead.

अतिकायोत्तमाङ्गीं च तनुदीर्घशिरोधराम्।

ध्वस्तकेशीं तथाऽकेशीं केशकम्बलधारिणीम्॥5.17.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: अतिकाय-उत्तम-अङ्गीम् very huge-bodied with a prominent or strange head (or upper body), च and, तनु-दीर्घ-शिरोधराम् with a thin and long neck, ध्वस्त-केशीम् with disheveled hair, तथा also, अकेशीम् without hair, केश-कम्बल-धारिणीम् wearing a blanket of hair (covered in matted or thick hair)

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) अतिकायोत्तमाङ्गीं, तनुदीर्घशिरोधरां, ध्वस्तकेशीं, तथा अकेशीं, केशकम्बलधारिणीं (राक्षसीम् अपि ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman also saw rakshasis with strange and frightening forms — one with an enormous body and strange head, one with a thin and long head and neck, one with disheveled hair, one who was bald, and one who seemed to be wrapped in a blanket of matted hair.

लम्बकर्णललाटां च लम्बोदरपयोधराम्।

लम्बोष्ठीं चुबुकोष्ठीं च लम्बास्यां लम्बजानुकाम्॥5.17.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: लम्ब-कर्ण-ललाटाम् one with long ears and forehead, च and, लम्ब-उदर-पयोधराम् one with a hanging belly and drooping breasts, लम्ब-ओष्ठीम् with hanging lips, चुबुक-ओष्ठीम् with lips reaching down to the chin, लम्ब-आस्याम् with a long/hanging mouth, लम्ब-जानुकाम् with long/hanging knees

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) लम्बकर्णललाटां च  लम्बोदरपयोधरां, लम्बोष्ठीं, चुबुकोष्ठीं, लम्बास्यां, लम्बजानुकां (राक्षसीम् अपि ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman also saw rakshasis with grotesque appearances — one with long ears and a protruding forehead, one with a sagging belly and drooping breasts, one with hanging lips, another whose lips touched her chin, one with a gaping mouth, and one with long drooping knees.

ह्रस्वां दीर्घां तथा कुब्जां विकटां वामनां तथा।

करालां भुग्नवक्त्रां च पिङ्गाक्षीं विकृताननाम्॥5.17.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: ह्रस्वाम् short, दीर्घाम् tall, तथा also, कुब्जाम् hunchbacked, विकटाम् monstrous, hideous, वामनाम् dwarf-like, very short-statured, करालाम् terrifying-faced, भुग्न-वक्त्राम् with a twisted or crooked mouth, च and, पिङ्ग-अक्षीम् tawny- or reddish-eyed, विकृत-आननाम्  deformed-faced, with misshapen faces

भुग्न (विशेषणम्)  वक्रम्, crooked

भग्न   भग्न p. p. [भंज्-क्त] 1 Broken, fractured, shattered, torn. Src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

* Note the difference between भुग्न and भग्न

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) ह्रस्वां, दीर्घां, तथा कुब्जां, विकटां, वामनां, करालां, भुग्नवक्त्रां च  पिङ्गाक्षीं, विकृताननां (राक्षसीम् अपि ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw rakshasis of all terrifying shapes — short ones, tall ones, hunchbacked ones, monstrous ones, dwarf-like ones, those with fearsome faces, those with twisted mouths, reddish eyes, and horribly deformed faces.

विकृताः पिङ्गलाः कालीः क्रोधनाः कलहप्रियाः।

कालायसमहाशूलकूटमुद्गरधारिणीः॥5.17.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: विकृताः deformed, monstrous in appearance, पिङ्गलाः reddish- or yellowish-brown, कालीः dark-complexioned, क्रोधनाः angry by nature, कलह-प्रियाः fond of quarrels, argumentative, काल-अयस-महाशूल-कूटमुद्गर-धारिणीः bearing/holding great spears, maces and clubs (used to crush) made of black iron

अयस् (नपुंसकलिङ्गः)  लोहः src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) विकृताः, पिङ्गलाः, कालीः, क्रोधनाः, कलहप्रियाः, कालायसमहाशूलकूटमुद्गरधारिणीः (राक्षसीः अपि ददर्श)। 

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw hideous and deformed rakshasis— tawny and dark in complexion, quick to anger, fond of quarrels, and wielding massive black iron spears, crushing maces, and clubs.

वराहमृगशार्दूलमहिषाजशिवामुखीः।

गजोष्ट्रहयपादीश्च निखातशिरसोऽपराः॥5.17.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: वराह-मृग-शार्दूल-महिष-अज-शिवा-मुखीः having faces of boar, deer,  tiger, buffalo, goat, jackal, गज-उष्ट्र-हय-पादीः with feet/legs of elephant, camel,horse, च and, निखात-शिरसः with buried (sunken) heads, अपराः others

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) वराहमृगशार्दूलमहिषाजशिवामुखीः, गजोष्ट्रहयपादीः च, निखातशिरसः अपराः (राक्षसीः अपि ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw rakshasis with faces of boars, deer, tigers, buffaloes, goats, and jackals; some had the feet of elephants, camels, or horses, and others had their heads sunken into their bodies.

एकहस्तैकपादाश्च खरकर्ण्यश्वकर्णिकाः।

गोकर्णीर्हस्तिकर्णीश्च हरिकर्णीस्तथापराः॥5.17.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: एक-हस्ताः with one hand, एक-पादाः with one leg, च and, खर-कर्ण्यः with ears like a donkey, अश्व-कर्णिकाः with ears like a horse, गो-कर्णीः with ears like a cow, हस्ति-कर्णीः with ears like an elephant, च and, हरि-कर्णीः with ears like a monkey, तथा likewise, अपराः others

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) एकहस्ताः, एकपादाः च, खरकर्ण्यः, अश्वकर्णिकाः, गोकर्णीः, हस्तिकर्णीः, हरिकर्णीः तथा अपराः (राक्षसीः अपि ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw rakshasis with just one hand, or one leg; some had ears like donkeys, others like horses, cows, elephants, or monkeys.

अनासा अतिनासाश्च तिर्यङ्नासा विनासिकाः।

गजसन्निभनासाश्च ललाटोच्छवासनासिकाः॥5.17.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: अ-नासाः without noses, अति-नासाः च with excessively large noses, तिर्यङ्-नासाः with crooked or sideways noses, वि-नासिकाः noseless (entirely without a nose structure), गज-सन्निभ-नासाः having noses like an elephant's trunk, ललाट-उच्छवास-नासिकाः having noses turned upwards or protruding from the forehead

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) अनासाः, अतिनासाः च, तिर्यङ्नासाः, विनासिकाः, गजसन्निभनासाः, ललाटोच्छवासनासिकाः (राक्षसीः अपि ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw rakshasis with no noses, some with extremely large noses, some with crooked or sideways noses, some entirely noseless, some with noses like elephant trunks, and some with noses sticking out from their foreheads.

हस्तिपादा महापादा गोपादाः पादचूलिकाः।

अतिमात्रशिरोग्रीवा अतिमात्रकुचोदरीः॥5.17.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: हस्ति-पादाः with elephant-like feet, महा-पादाः with very large feet, गो-पादाः  with cow-like feet, पाद-चूलिकाः with tufts of hair on their feet/or small hairy feet, अतिमात्र-शिरः-ग्रीवाः excessively large heads and necks, अतिमात्र-कुच-उदरीः with excessively large breasts and bellies

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) हस्तिपादाः, महापादाः, गोपादाः, पादचूलिकाः, अतिमात्रशिरोग्रीवाः, अतिमात्रकुचोदरीः (राक्षसीः अपि ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw rakshasis with feet like elephants, some with huge feet, some with cow-like feet, and some with hairy or tufted feet; others had excessively large heads and necks, and yet others had disproportionately large breasts and bellies.

अतिमात्रास्यनेत्राश्च दीर्घजिह्वानखास्तथा।

अजामुखीर्हस्तिमुखीर्गोमुखीस्सूकरीमुखीः॥5.17.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: अतिमात्र-आस्य-नेत्राः च with excessively large mouths and eyes, दीर्घ-जिह्वा-नखाः तथा with long tongues and nails, अजा-मुखीः with goat faces, हस्ति-मुखीः with elephant faces, गो-मुखीः with cow faces, सूकरी-मुखीः with pig (boar) faces

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) अतिमात्रास्यनेत्राः, दीर्घजिह्वानखाः तथा, अजामुखीः, हस्तिमुखीः, गोमुखीः, सूकरीमुखीः (राक्षसीः अपि ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw rakshasis with excessively large mouths and eyes, long tongues and nails, some with faces like goats, elephants, cows, and pigs.

हयोष्ट्रखरवक्त्राश्च राक्षसीर्घोरदर्शनाः।

शूलमुद्गरहस्ताश्च क्रोधनाः कलहप्रियाः॥5.17.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: हय-उष्ट्र-खर-वक्त्राः च with horse, camel and donkey faces, राक्षसीः rakshasis, घोर-दर्शनाः terrifying in appearance, शूल-मुद्गर-हस्ताः च holding spears and maces in their hands, क्रोधनाः angry-natured, कलह-प्रियाः fond of quarrels/conflict-loving

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) हयोष्ट्रखरवक्त्राः च, घोरदर्शनाः, शूलमुद्गरहस्ताः, क्रोधनाः, कलहप्रियाः राक्षसीः (अपि ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw terrifying-looking rakshasis with faces of horses, camels, and donkeys. Some held spears and maces in their hands; they were angry by nature and fond of quarrels.

कराला धूम्रकेशीश्च राक्षसीर्विकृताननाः।

पिबन्ती: सततं पानं सदा मांससुराप्रियाः॥5.17.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: करालाः fierce-looking, धूम्र-केशीः च with smoky, disheveled hair, राक्षसीः rakshasis, विकृताननाः with deformed or grotesque faces, पिबन्तीः drinking, सततम् constantly, पानम् wine or liquor, सदा always, मांस-सुरा-प्रियाः fond of meat and alcohol

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) करालाः, धूम्रकेशीः, विकृताननाः, सततं पानं पिबन्तीः, सदा मांससुराप्रियाः च राक्षसीः (ददर्श)। 

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw fierce-looking rakshasis with smoky, disheveled hair and deformed faces, constantly drinking liquor and always fond of meat and wine.

मांसशोणितदिग्धाङ्गीर्मांसशोणितभोजनाः।

ता ददर्श कपिश्रेष्ठो रोमहर्षणदर्शनाः॥5.17.17॥

स्कन्धवन्तमुपासीनाः परिवार्य वनस्पतिम्।

Word-by-word meaning: मांस-शोणित-दिग्ध-अङ्गीः whose bodies were smeared with flesh and blood, मांस-शोणित-भोजनाः who fed on flesh and blood, ताः those (rakshasis), ददर्श (Hanuman) saw, कपि-श्रेठः the best among monkeys (Hanuman), रोम-हर्षण-दर्शनाः horrifying to look at; appearance that made one’s hair stand on end, स्कन्धवन्तम् to the ones which have branches (trees), उपासीनाः seated near, परिवार्य surrounding, वनस्पतिम् a tree (plant/tree deity, in context, the ashoka tree under which Sita sat)

Anvay: कपिश्रेष्ठः मांसशोणितदिग्धाङ्गीः, मांसशोणितभोजनाः, रोमहर्षणदर्शनाः ताः राक्षसीः, स्कन्धवन्तं वनस्पतिं परिवार्य उपासीनाः ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the best among monkeys, saw those rakshasis whose bodies were smeared with flesh and blood, who fed on flesh and blood, and whose terrifying appearance made one’s hair stand on end. They were seated around a tree with a lot of branches.

तस्याधस्ताच्च तां देवीं राजपुत्रीमनिन्दिताम्॥5.17.18॥

लक्षयामास लक्ष्मीवान् हनुमान् जनकात्मजाम्।

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य अधस्तात् beneath that (tree), च and, ताम् देवीम् that divine lady, राजपुत्रीम् the princess (daughter of a king), अनिन्दिताम् blameless, faultless, लक्षयामास he noticed, saw, observed, लक्ष्मीवान् the auspicious/glorious one (Hanuman), हनुमान् Hanuman, जनकात्मजाम् the daughter of Janaka (Sita)

Anvay: लक्ष्मीवान् हनुमान् तस्य (वनस्पतेः) अधस्तात्, ताम् अनिन्दितां देवीं राजपुत्रीं जनकात्मजां लक्षयामास।

Simple meaning: The glorious Hanuman saw beneath that tree the faultless divine princess—Sita, the daughter of Janaka.

निष्प्रभां शोकसन्तप्तां मलसङ्कुलमूर्धजाम्॥5.17.19॥

क्षीणपुण्यां च्युतां भूमौ तारां निपतितामिव।

Word-by-word meaning: निष्प्रभाम् without radiance, lustreless, शोक-सन्तप्ताम् scorched/burnt by sorrow; deeply pained by grief, मल-सङ्कुल-मूर्धजाम् with hair entangled and dirty, क्षीण-पुण्याम् whose merit (punya) was exhausted, च्युताम् fallen, भूमौ on the ground, ताराम् a star, निपतिताम् इव as if fallen

Anvay: (हनुमान्) निष्प्रभां, शोकसन्तप्तां, मलसङ्कुलमूर्धजां – क्षीणपुण्यां भूमौ च्युतां ताराम् इव – (तां सीतां लक्षयामास)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw her (Sita), devoid of radiance, scorched by grief, with hair matted and dirty —like a star whose merits have been spent and fallen on the ground.

चारित्रव्यपदेशाढ्यां भर्तृदर्शनदुर्गताम्॥5.17.20॥

भूषणैरुत्तमैर्हीनां भर्तृवात्सल्यभूषणाम्।

Word-by-word meaning: चारित्र-व्यपदेश-आढ्याम् rich in the reputation of good character, भर्तृ-दर्शन-दुर्गताम् miserable due to the absence of her husband’s sight, भूषणैः उत्तमैः हीनाम् deprived of fine ornaments, भर्तृ-वात्सल्य-भूषणाम् adorned by her love for her husband (whose ornament is her love for her husband)

Anvay: हनुमान् चारित्रव्यपदेशाढ्यां, भर्तृदर्शनदुर्गताम्, उत्तमैः भूषणैः हीनां, भर्तृवात्सल्यभूषणाम् (तां सीतां लक्षयामास)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw Sita—rich in the glory of virtuous character, suffering from the absence of her husband’s sight, bereft of splendid ornaments, yet adorned by her deep love for her husband.

राक्षसाधिपसंरुद्धां बन्धुभिश्च विना कृताम्॥5.17.21॥

वियूथां सिंहसंरुद्धां बद्धां गजवधूमिव।

Word-by-word meaning: राक्षस-आधिप-संरुद्धाम् imprisoned/guarded by the king of the rakshasas (i.e., Ravana), बन्धुभिः च विना कृताम् made to be without her relatives, वियूथाम् separated from her herd/group, सिंह-संरुद्धाम् surrounded (or seized) by a lion, बद्धाम् bound, tied, गज-वधूम् इव like a female elephant

Anvay: (हनुमान्) राक्षसाधिपसंरुद्धां, बन्धुभिः च विना कृतां – वियूथां, सिंहसंरुद्धां, बद्धां गजवधूम् इव – (तां सीताम् लक्षयामास)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw Sita, imprisoned by the king of rakshasas, bereft of her kin, like a female elephant separated from her herd, seized and bound by a lion.

चन्द्ररेखां पयोदान्ते शारदाभ्रैरिवावृताम्॥5.17.22॥

क्लिष्टरूपामसंस्पर्शादयुक्तामिव वल्लकीम्।

Word-by-word meaning: चन्द्र-रेखाम् the crescent moon, पयोद-अन्ते  at the close of the rainy season, शारद-अभ्रैः इव like autumn clouds, आवृताम् covered, obscured, क्लिष्ट-रूपाम् worn out in appearance, distressed, असंस्पर्शात् from lack of touch (i.e., lack of use or care), अयुक्ताम् इव as if unplayed or untuned, वल्लकीम् a veena (a stringed musical instrument)

Anvay: (हनुमान्) पयोदान्ते शारदाभ्रैः आवृतां चन्द्ररेखाम् इव, क्लिष्ट रूपां, असंस्पर्शात् अयुक्तां वल्लकीम् इव (तां सीतां लक्षयामास)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw Sita like the crescent moon veiled by autumn clouds at the close of the rainy season—her appearance distressed, like a veena that had lost its tune from long neglect and lack of touch.

सीतां भर्तृवशे युक्तामयुक्तां राक्षसीवशे॥5.17.23॥

अशोकवनिकामध्ये शोकसागरमाप्लुताम्।

Word-by-word meaning: सीताम् Sita, भर्तृ-वशे युक्ताम् devoted to her husband (Rama), under her husband’s influence (in loyalty), अयुक्ताम् yet made to be under (i.e., controlled by), राक्षसी-वशे the power/influence of rakshasis, अशोक-वनिका-मध्ये in the middle of the Ashoka grove, शोक-सागरम् आप्लुताम् submerged in an ocean of sorrow

Anvay: हनुमान् भर्तृवशे युक्तां, राक्षसीवशे अयुक्तां, अशोकवनिकामध्ये शोकसागरम् आप्लुतां (तां सीतां ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw Sita, devoted to her husband’s will but forcibly subjected to the control of the rakshasis, submerged in an ocean of sorrow in the midst of the Ashoka grove.

ताभिः परिवृतां तत्र सग्रहामिव रोहिणीम्॥5.17.24॥

ददर्श हनुमान् देवीं लतामकुसुमामिव।

Word-by-word meaning: ताभिः by those (rakshasis), परिवृताम् surrounded, तत्र there, स-ग्रहाम् इव like being afflicted by planets (i.e., under planetary influence, here indicating adversity), रोहिणीम् the Rohini star (a delicate and auspicious nakshatra), ददर्श saw, हनुमान् Hanuman, देवीम् the divine lady (Sita), लताम् a creeper, अकुसुमाम् इव like one without flowers (i.e., wilted or in distress)

Anvay: हनुमान् तत्र ताभिः राक्षसीभिः परिवृतां – सग्रहां रोहिणीम् इव –, – अकुसुमां लताम् इव – देवीं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: There, Hanuman saw the divine lady (Sita), surrounded by rakshasis like the star Rohini afflicted by malefic planets, like a flowerless creeper—wilted and joyless.

सा मलेन च दिग्धाङ्गी वपुषा चाप्यलङ्कृता॥5.17.25॥

मृणाली पङ्कदिग्धेव विभाति न विभाति च।

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Sita), मलेन with dirt, च and, दिग्ध-अङ्गी her limbs smeared (with it), वपुषा च अपि अलङ्कृता and her body with grace, मृणाली a lotus stalk, पङ्क-दिग्धा इव as if smeared with mud, विभाति appears radiant, न विभाति च and yet does not appear radiant (both radiant and not radiant)

Anvay: सा सीता मलेन दिग्धाङ्गी, वपुषा च अपि अलङ्कृता – पङ्कदिग्धा मृणाली इव विभाति – च न विभाति च।

Simple meaning: That Sita, her graceful body soiled by dirt, looked like a lotus stalk smeared with mud—appearing radiant, and yet not truly radiant.

मलिनेन तु वस्त्रेण परिक्लिष्टेन भामिनीम्॥5.17.26॥

संवृतां मृगशावाक्षीं ददर्श हनुमान् कपिः।

Word-by-word meaning: मलिनेन dirty, तु indeed/but, वस्त्रेण with a garment/cloth, परिक्लिष्टेन soiled, worn out, in distress, भामिनीम् the radiant (or beautiful) woman, संवृताम् covered (with that garment), मृग-शाव-अक्षीम् doe-eyed (having eyes like a young deer), ददर्श saw, हनुमान् Hanuman, कपिः the monkey (Hanuman)

Anvay: हनुमान् कपिः मलिनेन परिक्लिष्टेन वस्त्रेण संवृतां मृगशावाक्षीं भामिनीं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the monkey, saw the beautiful and doe-eyed lady (Sita), draped in a dirty and worn-out garment.

तां देवीं दीनवदनामदीनां भर्तृतेजसा॥5.17.27॥

रक्षितां स्वेन शीलेन सीतामसितलोचनाम्।

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her, देवीम् the divine lady (Sita), दीन-वदनाम् having a face (expression) like that of a distressed one, अ-दीनाम् but not actually distressed (courageous at heart), भर्तृ-तेजसा by her husband’s glory or power, रक्षिताम् protected, स्वेन शीलेन by her own character/virtue, सीताम् Sita, असित-लोचनाम् black-eyed (dark-eyed)

Anvay: हनुमान् दीनवदनाम् अदीनां भर्तृतेजसा स्वेन शीलेन रक्षिताम् असितलोचनां तां देवीं सीतां  ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw that divine lady Sita of dark eyes—whose face appeared distressed, though she was not truly weak-hearted—protected by her husband’s glory and by her own virtue.

तां दृष्ट्वा हनुमान् सीतां मृगशावनिभेक्षणाम्।

मृगकन्यामिव त्रस्तां वीक्षमाणां समन्ततः॥5.17.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her (Sita), दृष्ट्वा having seen, हनुमान् Hanuman, सीताम् Sita, मृग-शाव-निभ-ईक्षणाम्  one whose eyes resembled those of a young deer (doe-eyed), मृग-कन्याम् इव like a young female deer, त्रस्ताम् frightened, वीक्षमाणाम् glancing/looking around, समन्ततः all around/in all directions

Anvay: हनुमान् मृगशावनिभेक्षणां त्रस्तां समन्ततः वीक्षमाणां – मृगकन्याम् इव – तां सीतां दृष्ट्वा…

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw Sita, whose eyes resembled those of a young deer—frightened, glancing all around—like a terrified doe.

दहन्तीमिव निःश्वासैर्वृक्षान् पल्लवधारिणः।

संघातमिव शोकानां दुःखस्योर्मिमिवोत्थिताम्॥5.17.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: दहन्तीम् इव as if burning, निःश्वासैः with her sighs, वृक्षान् trees, पल्लव-धारिणः bearing young leaves (tender foliage), संघातम् इव like a heap/cluster, शोकानाम् of griefs/sorrows, दुःखस्य of sorrow/suffering, ऊर्मिम् इव like a wave, उत्थिताम् arisen/surging

Anvay: निःश्वासैः पल्लवधारिणः वृक्षान् दहन्तीम् इव –, – शोकानां संघातम् इव –, – दुःखस्य उत्थिताम् ऊर्मिम् इव – (तां सीतां दृष्ट्वा… )

Simple meaning: (He saw her) as if she were burning the tender-leaved trees with her sighs, like a heap of sorrows, like a surging wave of grief.

तां क्षमां सुविभक्ताङ्गीं विनाभरणशोभिनीम्।

प्रहर्षमतुलं लेभे मारुतिः प्रेक्ष्य मैथिलीम्॥5.17.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her (Sita), क्षमाम् patient/forbearing (also: resembling the Earth in tolerance), सु-विभक्त-अङ्गीम् with well-proportioned limbs, विना आभरण-शोभिनीम् shining even without ornaments, प्रेक्ष्य upon seeing, मैथिलीम् the princess of Mithila (Sita), प्रहर्षम् delight/joy, अतुलम् incomparable, लेभे obtained/felt, मारुतिः the son of the Wind-God (Hanuman)

Anvay: मारुतिः सुविभक्ताङ्गीं, विना आभरणशोभिनीं, क्षमां तां मैथिलीं प्रेक्ष्य अतुलं प्रहर्षं लेभे।

Simple meaning: Upon seeing Sita — patient like the Earth, with well-proportioned limbs, and radiant even without ornaments — Hanuman experienced incomparable joy.

हर्षजानि च सोऽश्रूणि तां दृष्ट्वा मदिरेक्षणाम्।

मुमुचे हनुमांस्तत्र नमश्चक्रे च राघवम्॥5.17.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: हर्षजानि born of joy, च and, सः he (Hanuman), अश्रूणि tears, ताम् her (Sita), दृष्ट्वा having seen, मदिर-ईक्षणाम् with eyes like intoxicating wine (large, beautiful eyes), मुमुचे released/shed, हनुमान् Hanuman, तत्र there, नमः चक्रे offered salutations, च and, राघवम् to Rama (descendant of Raghu)

Anvay: सः हनुमान् तत्र मदिरेक्षर्णां तां दृष्ट्वा हर्षजानि अश्रूणि मुमुचे, राघवं च नमः चक्रे।

Simple meaning: There, upon seeing her whose eyes were intoxicating, Hanuman shed tears of joy and offered salutations to Rama.

नमस्कृत्वा च रामाय लक्ष्मणाय च वीर्यवान्।

सीतादर्शनसंहृष्टो हनुमान् संवृतोऽभवत्॥5.17.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: नमस्कृत्वा having saluted/offered obeisance, च and, रामाय to Rama, लक्ष्मणाय to Lakshmana, च and, वीर्यवान् the mighty one (Hanuman), सीता-दर्शन-संहृष्टः overjoyed by the sight of Sita, हनुमान् Hanuman, संवृतः अभवत् became concealed/stayed hidden

Anvay: वीर्यवान् हनुमान् रामाय लक्ष्मणाय च नमस्कृत्वा, सीतादर्शनसंहृष्टः संवृतः अभवत्।

Simple meaning: The mighty Hanuman, having offered salutations to Rama and Lakshmana, was overjoyed at the sight of Sita and remained hidden.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे सप्तदशः सर्गः ॥17॥

Thus ends the seventeenth sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.

॥ जय श्री हनुमान् ॥
