Sundarakanda Sarga 33

॥ॐ॥

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।

वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 33

Sita reveals her story to Hanuman

(Benefit: Strengthening relations and friendships)

(31 Shlokas)

सोऽवतीर्य द्रुमात्तस्माद्विद्रुमप्रतिमाननः।

विनीतवेषः कृपणः प्रणिपत्योपसृत्य च॥5.33.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः अवतीर्य he (Hanuman), descending, द्रुमात् तस्मात् from that tree, विद्रुम-प्रतिम-आननः with a face like red coral (vidruma = coral, pratima = like, anana = face), विनीत-वेषः with a humble appearance, कृपणः looking pitiful/sorrowful, प्रणिपत्य bowing down, उपसृत्य च and approaching (her)

Anvay: सः विद्रुमप्रतिमाननः विनीतवेषः कृपणः तस्मात् द्रुमात् अवतीर्य, प्रणिपत्य च उपसृत्य…

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman), whose face resembled red coral, with a humble demeanor and a pitiful appearance, descended from that tree, and having bowed down, approached (Sita).

तामब्रवीन्महातेजा हनूमान्मारुतात्मजः।

शिरस्यञ्जलिमाधाय सीतां मधुरया गिरा॥5.33.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् अब्रवीत् to her (Sita), he spoke, महातेजाः the one with great brilliance, हनूमान् Hanuman, मारुतात्मजः the son of the Wind-God, शिरसि अञ्जलिम् आधाय placing folded hands (anjali) upon his head, सीताम् to Sita, मधुरया गिरा with sweet speech/gentle words

Anvay: महातेजाः मारुतात्मजः हनूमान्, शिरसि अञ्जलिम् आधाय, तां सीतां मधुरया गिरा अब्रवीत्।

Simple meaning: The one possessing great brilliance, Hanuman, the son of the Wind-God, placed his folded hands upon his head (as a mark of deep respect) and spoke gently to Sita with sweet and respectful words.

का नु पद्मपलाशाक्षि क्लिष्टकौशेयवासिनि।

द्रुमस्य शाखामालम्ब्य तिष्ठसि त्वमनिन्दिते॥5.33.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: का नु who indeed, पद्म-पलाश-अक्षि O lotus-petal-eyed one!, क्लिष्ट-कौशेय-वासिनि wearing crumpled silk garments, द्रुमस्य शाखाम् the branch of a tree, आलम्ब्य holding onto/leaning on, तिष्ठसि are standing, त्वम् you, अनिन्दिते O blameless one/virtuous lady!

Anvay: पद्मपलाशाक्षि! क्लिष्टकौशेयवासिनि! अनिन्दिते! द्रुमस्य शाखाम् आलम्ब्य तिष्ठसि। का नु त्वम्?

Simple meaning: "O lotus-eyed one, clad in wrinkled silk garments, O blameless lady! Who are you that stands here, holding onto a branch of the tree?

किमर्थं तव नेत्राभ्यां वारि स्रवति शोकजम्।

पुण्डरीकपलाशाभ्यां विप्रकीर्णमिवोदकम्॥5.33.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: किमर्थम् why/for what reason, तव from your, नेत्राभ्याम् eyes (dual form), वारि water (i.e., tears), स्रवति flows, शोकजम् born of sorrow/grief, पुण्डरीक-पलाशाभ्याम् like (from) white lotus petals, विप्रकीर्णम् trickling, इव like/as if, उदकम् water

Anvay: किमर्थं तव नेत्राभ्यां शोकजं वारि – पुण्डरीकपलाशाभ्यां विप्रकीर्णम् उदकम् इव – स्रवति?

Simple meaning: "Why do tears born of sorrow flow from your eyes, as if water were trickling from white lotus petals?

सुराणामसुराणां वा नागगन्धर्वरक्षसाम्।

यक्षाणां किन्नराणां वा का त्वं भवसि शोभने॥5.33.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुराणाम् of the gods (devas), असुराणाम् of the asuras, वा or, नाग-गन्धर्व-रक्षसाम् of nagas (serpent-beings), gandharvas (celestial musicians), and rakshasas, यक्षाणाम् of yakshas (nature-spirits or demi-gods), किन्नराणाम् of kinnaras (celestial beings with human-animal features), वा or, का who (feminine), त्वम् you, भवसि are, शोभने O beautiful one/graceful lady

Anvay: शोभने! सुराणाम्, असुराणां, नागगन्धर्वरक्षसां, यक्षाणां किन्नराणां वा का त्वं भवसि?

Simple meaning: "O graceful lady! Are you a celestial being from among the gods, asuras, nagas, gandharvas, rakshasas, yakshas, or kinnaras? Who are you, truly?

Note: Asuras: Nature: Asuras are not inherently evil; some are described as powerful, even possessing divine qualities. They are often associated with the pursuit of power and knowledge, and their conflict with the Devas is often a struggle for dominance in the cosmos. Origin: Asuras are often described as the children of Sage Kashyapa and his wives, Diti and Danu. Habitat: While some Asuras are associated with the nether regions, they are not exclusively tied to one location. Examples: Some notable Asuras include Hiranyakashipu, Mahabali, and Prahlada. Relationship with Devas: Asuras are often in conflict with the Devas, but some Asuras have been known to be devotees of Devas.

Rakshasas: Nature: Rakshasas are generally depicted as malevolent beings, often associated with darkness, chaos, and evil deeds. They are known for their monstrous appearances, shapeshifting abilities, and fondness for human flesh.  Origin: Rakshasas are said to have been created by Brahma, the creator god. Some are also said to be descendants of Sage Pulastya. Habitat: Rakshasas are often associated with forests and dark places, and some are also associated with Lanka.  Examples: Notable Rakshasas include Ravana, Kumbhakarna, and Vibhishana.  Relationship with Humans: Rakshasas are often in conflict with humans, sometimes preying on them or causing them harm.

का त्वं भवसि रुद्राणां मरुतां वा वरानने।

वसूनां हि वरारोहे देवता प्रतिभासि मे॥5.33.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: का who (feminine), त्वम् you, भवसि are, रुद्राणाम् among the Rudras (a class of deities, often associated with aspects of Shiva), मरुताम् among the Maruts (storm gods, sons of Rudra), वा or, वरानने O beautiful-faced one!, वसूनाम् among the Vasus (another class of deities), हि indeed/truly, वरारोहे O lovely-hipped one!/one with best form, देवता a divine being, प्रतिभासि you appear, मे to me

Anvay: वरानने! त्वं रुद्राणां वा मरुतां वा वसूनां हि का भवसि? वरारोहे! मे देवता प्रतिभासि।

Simple meaning: "O beautiful-faced one! Are you a divine being from among the Rudras, Maruts, or Vasus? O graceful one! You truly appear to me as a celestial goddess.

किं नु चन्द्रमसा हीना पतिता विबुधालयात्।

रोहिणी ज्योतिषां श्रेष्ठा श्रेष्ठसर्वगुणान्विता॥5.33.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: किम् नु is it that/could it be, चन्द्रमसा from the Moon, हीना separated/deprived, पतिता fallen, विबुध-आलयात् from the abode of the gods, रोहिणी Rohini (a prominent and beloved star/wife of the Moon), ज्योतिषाम् among the stars (luminaries), श्रेष्ठा the best, श्रेष्ठ-सर्व-गुण-अन्विता endowed with all the best qualities

विबुध  —2 A god - src. Kosha. Ashtadhyayi

Anvay: किं नु ज्योतिषां श्रेष्ठा श्रेष्ठसर्वगुणान्विता चन्द्रमसा हीना विबुधालयात् पतिता रोहिणी (इति)?

Simple meaning: "Could it be that Rohini, the best among stars, endowed with all excellent qualities, has fallen from the abode of the gods, having been separated from the Moon?

का त्वं भवसि कल्याणि त्वमनिन्दितलोचने।

कोपाद्वा यदि वा मोहाद्भर्तारमसितेक्षणे।

वसिष्ठं कोपयित्वा त्वं नासि कल्याण्यरुन्धती॥5.33.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: का त्वम् भवसि Who are you, (you) seem to be, कल्याणि O auspicious one/blessed lady!, त्वम् you, अनिन्दित-लोचने O one with blameless eyes (pure and faultless)!, कोपात् वा either out of anger, यदि वा मोहात् or due to delusion/confusion, भर्तारम् husband, असितेक्षणे O dark-eyed lady!, वसिष्ठम् Vasishtha (the sage), कोपयित्वा having angered, त्वम् न असि you are not, कल्याणी अरुन्धती (the) auspicious Arundhati

Anvay: कल्याणि! अनिन्दितलोचने! त्वं का भवसि? असितेक्षणे! कोपात् वा यदि वा मोहात् भर्तारं वसिष्ठं कोपयित्वा त्वं कल्याणी अरुन्धती न असि।

Simple meaning: "O auspicious one, O you with flawless eyes, who are you? O dark-eyed lady, you cannot be Arundhati, the virtuous wife, who either out of anger or confusion, has angered her husband Sage Vasishtha.

को नु पुत्रः पिता भ्राता भर्ता वा ते सुमध्यमे।

अस्माल्लोकादमुं लोकं गतं त्वमनुशोचसि॥5.33.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: कः नु who indeed, पुत्रः son, पिता father, भ्राता brother, भर्ता वा or husband, ते your, सुमध्यमे O slender-waisted lady!, अस्मात् लोकात् from this world, अमुम् लोकम् to that (other) world (i.e., has died), गतम् has gone, त्वम् you, अनुशोचसि are mourning/lamenting

Anvay: सुमध्यमे! कः नु ते पुत्रः, पिता, भ्राता, भर्ता वा, अस्मात् लोकात् अमुं लोकं गतं, त्वम् अनुशोचसि?

Simple meaning: "O slender-waisted lady! Who is it of yours that has passed away to the other world—your son, father, brother, or husband—that you are mourning so deeply?

रोदनादतिनिश्श्वासाद्भूमिसंस्पर्शनादपि।

न त्वां देवीमहं मन्ये राज्ञस्संज्ञावधारणात्॥5.33.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: रोदनात् from weeping, अतिनिःश्वासात् from heavy sighing, भूमि-संस्पर्शनात् अपि and from touching the ground (sitting on the earth), न not, त्वाम् you, देवीम् goddess, अहम् I, मन्ये consider, राज्ञः संज्ञा signs of royalty, अवधारणात् due to recognition/understanding

Anvay: रोदनात्, अतिनिःश्वासात्, भूमिसंस्पर्शनात् अपि, राज्ञः संज्ञा अवधारणात्, अहं त्वां देवीं न मन्ये।

Simple meaning: "Because of your weeping, deep sighing, and sitting on the ground (goddesses don’t touch the ground) and because of the signs that indicate royalty—I do not think you are a goddess.

व्यञ्जनानि च ते यानि लक्षणानि च लक्षये।

महिषी भूमिपालस्य राजकन्या च मे मता॥5.33.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: व्यञ्जनानि distinguishing marks (external features), च and, ते your, यानि which, लक्षणानि characteristics, auspicious signs (often bodily or behavioral traits), च and, लक्षये I perceive/observe, महिषी queen/chief consort, भूमि-पालस्य of a king (lit. protector of the land), राज-कन्या princess (daughter of a king), च and, मे to me, मता you appear (I think/believe)

Anvay: ते यानि व्यञ्जनानि लक्षणानि च लक्षये, त्वं भूमिपालस्य महिषी राजकन्या च मे मता।

Simple meaning: "The marks and signs that I perceive in you make me believe that you are the queen of a king, and also a princess by birth.

रावणेन जनस्थानाद्बलादपहृता यदि।

सीता त्वमसि भद्रं ते तन्ममाचक्ष्व पृच्छतः॥5.33.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: रावणेन by Ravana, जनस्थानात् from Janasthana, बलात् by force, अपहृता taken away, यदि if, सीता Sita, त्वम् you, असि are, भद्रम् ते may auspiciousness be with you/blessings to you, तत् then, मम to me, आचक्ष्व tell, पृच्छतः one who is asking

Anvay: यदि त्वं जनस्थानात् बलात् रावणेन अपहृता सीता असि, भद्रं ते, तत् पृच्छतः मम आचक्ष्व।

Simple meaning: "If you are Sita, who was forcibly taken from Janasthana by Ravana, then, blessings to you, please tell me, the one who is asking.

यथा हि तव वै दैन्यं रूपं चाप्यतिमानुषम्।

तपसा चान्वितो वेषस्त्वं राममहिषी ध्रुवम्॥5.33.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: यथा हि just as/indeed how, तव your, वै indeed/truly, दैन्यम् misery/sorrowful state, रूपम् च अपि and even (your) appearance, अति-मानुषम् superhuman/extraordinary, तपसा च अन्वितः accompanied by austerity (tapas), वेषः attire/appearance, त्वम् you, राम-महिषी Rama’s queen (mahishi = chief queen), ध्रुवम् surely/certainly

Anvay: वै यथा हि तव दैन्यम् अतिमानुषं रूपं च अपि, तपसा च अन्वितः वेषः — त्वं राममहिषी ध्रुवम्।

Simple meaning: "Indeed, seeing your sorrowful condition, your extraordinary (divine) appearance, and your ascetic-like attire imbued with austerity, I am certain that you are Rama’s queen.”

सा तस्य वचनं श्रुत्वा रामकीर्तनहर्षिता।

उवाच वाक्यं वैदेही हनुमन्तं द्रुमाश्रितम्॥5.33.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Sita), तस्य वचनम् his words (Hanuman’s speech), श्रुत्वा having heard, राम-कीर्तन-हर्षिता delighted by the glorification (mention) of Rama, उवाच spoke, वाक्यम् a sentence/words, वैदेही Vaidehi, हनुमन्तम् to Hanuman, द्रुम-आश्रितम् who was perched on a tree

Anvay: सा वैदेही तस्य वचनं श्रुत्वा रामकीर्तनहर्षिता द्रुमाश्रितं हनुमन्तं वाक्यम् उवाच।

Simple meaning: Vaidehi, having heard his (Hanuman’s) words, delighted by the mention of Rama, spoke to Hanuman, who was perched on a tree.

पृथिव्यां राजसिंहानां मुख्यस्य विदितात्मनः।

स्नुषा दशरथस्याहं शत्रुसैन्यप्रतापिनः॥5.33.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: पृथिव्याम् on the earth, राज-सिंहानाम् among lion-like kings (great rulers), मुख्यस्य chief/foremost, विदितात्मनः of one who knows his own self/a wise one, स्नुषा daughter-in-law, दशरथस्य of King Dasharatha, अहम् I am, शत्रु-सैन्य-प्रतापिनः of him who is powerful (burns) against enemy armies

Anvay: अहं पृथिव्यां राजसिंहानां मुख्यस्य विदितात्मनः शत्रुसैन्यप्रतापिनः दशरथस्य स्नुषा।

Simple meaning: "I am the daughter-in-law of King Dasharatha — the chief among lion-like kings on this earth, a knower of the self, and a scourge to enemy armies.

दुहिता जनकस्याहं वैदेहस्य महात्मनः।

सीतेति नाम नाम्नाऽहं भार्या रामस्य धीमतः॥5.33.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुहिता daughter, जनकस्य of Janaka, अहम् I am, वैदेहस्य of the Videha king (another name for Janaka), महात्मनः the great-souled one, सीता इति नाम by the name ‘Sita’, नाम्ना by name, अहम् I am, भार्या the wife, रामस्य of Rama, धीमतः the wise one/intelligent one

Anvay: अहं वैदेहस्य महात्मनः जनकस्य दुहिता। अहं नाम्ना सीता इति नाम रामस्य धीमतः भार्या।

Simple meaning: "I am the daughter of Janaka, the great-souled king of Videha. By name, I am called Sita, the wife of Rama, the wise one.

समा द्वादश तत्राहं राघवस्य निवेशने।

भुञ्जाना मानुषान्भोगान्सर्वकामसमृद्धिनी॥5.33.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: समाः द्वादश for twelve years, तत्र there (in Ayodhya/in Rama’s household), अहम् I, राघवस्य of Raghava (Rama), निवेशने in the residence/house, भुञ्जाना enjoying, मानुषान् human/earthly, भोगान् enjoyments/pleasures, सर्व-काम-समृद्धिनी endowed with all desires fulfilled/enjoying every comfort

Anvay: अहं तत्र राघवस्य निवेशने द्वादश समाः मानुषान् भोगान् सर्वकामसमृद्धिनी भुञ्जाना।

Simple meaning: "For twelve years, I lived in Rama’s house, enjoying human pleasures, endowed with the fulfillment of all desires.

तत्र त्रयोदशे वर्षे राज्येनेक्ष्वाकुनन्दनम्।

अभिषेचयितुं राजा सोपाध्यायः प्रचक्रमे॥5.33.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र then/at that time, त्रयोदशे वर्षे in the thirteenth year, राज्येन for the kingdom/to the kingship, इक्ष्वाकु-नन्दनम् the joy of the Ikshvaku dynasty (Rama), अभिषेचयितुम् to consecrate/to anoint (as king), राजा the king (Dasharatha), स-उपाध्यायः with the priests/accompanied by preceptors, प्रचक्रमे began/initiated

Anvay: तत्र त्रयोदशे वर्षे सोपाध्यायः राजा इक्ष्वाकुनन्दनं राज्येन अभिषेचयितुं प्रचक्रमे।

Simple meaning: "In the thirteenth year, the king, along with the royal priests, began preparations to anoint the joy of the Ikshvaku dynasty (Rama) as the king.

तस्मिन्सम्भ्रियमाणे तु राघवस्याभिषेचने।

कैकयी नाम भर्तारं देवी वचनमब्रवीत्॥5.33.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मिन् at that time/during that (event), सम्भ्रियमाणे being prepared/in the midst of preparations, तु but/however, राघवस्य of Raghava (Rama), अभिषेचने in the anointing/coronation, कैकयी नाम (a queen) named Kaikeyi, भर्तारम् her husband, देवी the queen/lady, वचनम् अब्रवीत् spoke these words/said to

Anvay: तस्मिन् राघवस्य अभिषेचने सम्भ्रियमाणे तु देवी कैकयी नाम भर्तारं वचनम् अब्रवीत्।

Simple meaning: "But as the preparations for Raghava’s (Rama’s) coronation were underway, the queen named Kaikeyi spoke to her husband (King Dasharatha).

न पिबेयं न खादेयं प्रत्यहं मम भोजनम्।

एष मे जीवितस्यान्तो रामो यद्यभिषिच्यते॥5.33.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: न पिबेयम् I shall not drink, न खादेयम् I shall not eat, प्रति-अहम् daily/every day, मम भोजनम् my food, एषः this, मे for me/my, जीवितस्य अन्तः end of life, रामः यदि अभिषिच्यते if Rama is anointed

Anvay: प्रत्यहं मम भोजनं न पिबेयं, न खादेयम्। यदि रामः अभिषिच्यते (तर्हि) एषः मे जीवितस्य अन्तः।

Simple meaning: “‘I will neither eat nor drink my food daily. If Rama is crowned (as king), that will be the end of my life.’

यत्तदुक्तं त्वया वाक्यं प्रीत्या नृपतिसत्तम।

तच्छेन्न वितथं कार्यं वनं गच्छतु राघवः॥5.33.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: यत् तत् उक्तम् that which was said, त्वया by you, वाक्यम् the promise/statement, प्रीत्या with affection, नृपति-सत्तम O best among kings!, तत् that, चेत् if, न वितथम् कार्यम् should not be made false, वनम् गच्छतु let him go to the forest, राघवः Raghava (Rama)

Anvay: नृपतिसत्तम! यत् तत् वाक्यं त्वया प्रीत्या उक्तं, तत् न वितथं कार्यं चेत्, राघवः वनं गच्छतु।

Simple meaning: "’O best among kings! That promise you made out of affection—if it is not to be made false—then let Raghava (Rama) go to the forest.’

स राजा सत्यवाग्देव्या वरदानमनुस्मरन्।

मुमोह वचनं श्रुत्वा कैकेय्याः क्रूरमप्रियम्॥5.33.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः राजा that king, सत्य-वाक् one who speaks the truth/truth-speaking, देव्याः of the queen (Kaikeyi), वर-दानम् the boon given, अनुस्मरन् remembering/recalling, मुमोह fainted/was stunned/fell unconscious, वचनम् the words/statement, श्रुत्वा having heard, कैकेय्याः of Kaikeyi, क्रूरम् cruel, अप्रियम् unpleasant/disagreeable

Anvay: सः सत्यवाक् राजा देव्याः कैकेय्याः क्रूरम् अप्रियं वचनं श्रुत्वा, वरदानम् अनुस्मरन्, मुमोह।

Simple meaning: “That truthful king, hearing the cruel and unpleasant words of queen Kaikeyi and recalling the boon he had granted, fell unconscious (or was overwhelmed and fainted).

ततस्तु स्थविरो राजा सत्ये धर्मे व्यवस्थितः।

ज्येष्ठं यशस्विनं पुत्रं रुदन्राज्यमयाचत॥5.33.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः तु then, indeed, स्थविरः the aged one/the elderly (king), राजा king (Dasharatha), सत्ये in truth, धर्मे in righteousness, व्यवस्थितः established/firmly rooted, ज्येष्ठम् the eldest, यशस्विनम् glorious/illustrious, पुत्रम् son (Rama), रुदन् weeping/crying, राज्यम् the kingdom, अयाचत begged/requested/asked for

Anvay: ततः स्थविरः सत्ये धर्मे च व्यवस्थितः राजा, रुदन् ज्येष्ठं यशस्विनं पुत्रं राज्यम् अयाचत।

Simple meaning: “Then, the aged king, who was firmly rooted in truth and righteousness, weeping, begged his eldest and illustrious son (Rama) to return the kingdom.

स पितुर्वचनं श्रीमानभिषेकात्परं प्रियम्।

मनसा पूर्वमासाद्य वाचा प्रतिगृहीतवान्॥5.33.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Rama), पितुः वचनम् father's words, श्रीमान् the glorious one, अभिषेकात् परम् प्रियम् dearer than the coronation, मनसा in his mind, पूर्वम् first/beforehand, आसाद्य having grasped/accepted, वाचा with words/verbally, प्रतिगृहीतवान् accepted/received/agreed to

Anvay: सः श्रीमान् रामः पितुः अभिषेकात् परं प्रियं वचनं मनसा पूर्वम् आसाद्य, वाचा प्रतिगृहीतवान्।

Simple meaning: “That glorious Rama, having wholeheartedly accepted his father's words—which were dearer to him than the coronation— also conveyed the acceptance verbally.

दद्यान्न प्रतिगृह्णीयान्न ब्रूयात्किञ्चिदप्रियम्।

अपि जीवितहेतोर्वा रामस्सत्यपराक्रमः॥5.33.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: दद्यात् he may give, न प्रतिगृह्णीयात् but would not accept (in return), न ब्रूयात् would not speak, किञ्चित् अप्रियम् even a little unpleasant thing/anything disagreeable, अपि even, जीवित-हेतोः वा for the sake of his own life, रामः Rama, सत्य-पराक्रमः one whose valor is rooted in truth; truly valiant

Anvay: सत्यपराक्रमः रामः जीवितहेतोः अपि किञ्चित् अप्रियं न ब्रूयात्, दद्यात्, न च प्रतिगृह्णीयात्।

Simple meaning: “Even for the sake of his life, the truly valiant Rama would never say anything unpleasant, would give generously, but would not accept anything in return.

स विहायोत्तरीयाणि महार्हाणि महायशाः।

विसृज्य मनसा राज्यं जनन्यै मां समादिशत्॥5.33.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Rama), विहाय having given up, उत्तरीयाणि upper garments, महार्हाणि very costly, excellent, महायशाः of great fame, विसृज्य renouncing, मनसा in his mind, राज्यम् the kingdom, जनन्यै to (his) mother, माम् me (Sita), समादिशत् entrusted me

Anvay: सः महायशाः रामः महार्हाणि उत्तरीयाणि विहाय, मनसा राज्यं विसृज्य, जनन्यै मां समादिशत्।

Simple meaning: “That Rama, of great fame, gave up his costly upper garments, mentally renounced the kingdom, and entrusted me (Sita) to his mother.

साहं तस्याग्रतस्तूर्णं प्रस्थिता वनचारिणी।

न हि मे तेन हीनाया वासस्स्वर्गेऽपि रोचते॥5.33.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा अहम् that me, तस्य अग्रतः ahead of him (Rama), तूर्णम् प्रस्थिता immediately started on the journey, proceeded, वन-चारिणी forest wanderer (feminine) – Sita referring to herself as one who wanders in the forest, न हि मे not indeed, to me, तेन हीनायाः deprived of Rama, वासः residence, dwelling, स्वर्गे in heaven, अपि even, रोचते is agreeable, pleasing

Anvay: सा अहं तूर्णं तस्य अग्रतः प्रस्थिता वनचारिणी। तेन हीनायाः मे स्वर्गे अपि वासः न हि रोचते।

Simple meaning: “I, (deciding) to go to the forest, immediately proceeded ahead of him. Indeed, even in heaven, a residence deprived of him (Rama) is not pleasing to me.

प्रागेव तु महाभागस्सौमित्रिर्मित्रनन्दनः।

पूर्वजस्यानुयात्रार्थे द्रुमचीरैरलङ्कृतः॥5.33.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्राक् एव तु even before (us/earlier) indeed, महाभागः highly fortunate, noble one, सौमित्रिः son of Sumitra, i.e., Lakshmana, मित्र-नन्दनः delight of his friends, पूर्वजस्य of the elder brother (i.e., Rama), अनुयात्रा-अर्थे for the purpose of accompanying (on journey), द्रुम-चीरैः with bark garments (bark of trees), अलङ्कृतः adorned, dressed

Anvay: प्राक् एव तु महाभागः मित्रनन्दनः सौमित्रिः पूर्वजस्य अनुयात्रार्थे द्रुमचीरैः अलङ्कृतः (बभूव)।

Simple meaning: “But even before (me), the highly blessed Lakshmana — the delight of his friends — had already adorned himself with garments of tree bark, ready to accompany his elder brother.

ते वयं भर्तुरादेशं बहुमान्य दृढव्रताः।

प्रविष्टास्स्म पुरादृष्टं वनं गम्भीरदर्शनम्॥5.33.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: ते वयम् we (all three of us), भर्तुः आदेशम् the command of the King, बहु-मान्य highly respected, दृढ-व्रताः firm in our vows/resolute in our commitment, प्रविष्टाः स्म had entered, पुरा before, अदृष्टम् (that was) not seen, वनम् the forest, गम्भीर-दर्शनम् of deep/serious appearance; solemn-looking

Anvay: दृढव्रताः ते वयं भर्तुः आदेशं बहुमान्य, पुरा गम्भीरदर्शनम् अदृष्टं वनं प्रविष्टाः स्म।

Simple meaning: “We, firm in our vows and deeply respectful of our King’s command, entered that solemn and profound forest which had never been seen before (by us).

वसतो दण्डकारण्ये तस्याहममितौजसः।

रक्षसाऽपहृता भार्या रावणेन दुरात्मना॥5.33.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: वसतः while (he was) dwelling, दण्डकारण्ये in the Dandakaranya forest, तस्य of him (Rama), अहम् I (Sita), अमित-ओजसः of immeasurable strength (referring to Rama), रक्षसा by rakshasa, अपहृता abducted/taken away, भार्या wife, रावणेन by Ravana, दुरात्मना evil-souled/wicked-hearted

Anvay: दण्डकारण्ये वसतः तस्य अमितौजसः (रामस्य) भार्या अहं दुरात्मना रक्षसा रावणेन अपहृता।

Simple meaning: “While that mighty Rama was residing in the Danḍakarayya forest, I — his wife — was abducted by the evil-souled rakshasa Ravana.

द्वौ मासौ तेन मे कालो जीवितानुग्रहः कृतः।

ऊर्ध्वं द्वाभ्यां तु मासाभ्यां ततस्तक्ष्यामि जीवितम्॥5.33.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्वौ मासौ two months, तेन by him (Ravana), मे for me, कालः time, जीवित-अनुग्रहः (for the) grace/favor of life (a reprieve from death), कृतः was given/was done, ऊर्ध्वम् after, द्वाभ्याम् तु मासाभ्याम् two months, however/but, ततः then, तक्ष्यामि I will give up/cut off/end, जीवितम् life

Anvay: तेन मे द्वौ मासौ कालः जीवितानुग्रहः कृतः। द्वाभ्यां मासाभ्याम् ऊर्ध्वं तु ततः जीवितं तक्ष्यामि।

Simple meaning: “A period of two months as a reprieve to live has been granted to me by Ravana. But after those two months, I shall end my life.”

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे त्रयस्त्रिंशः सर्गः॥33॥

Thus ends the thirty-third sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
