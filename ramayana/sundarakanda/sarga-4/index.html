मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।

वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 4

Hanuman witnesses Ravana's strength at Lanka

(29 Shlokas)

स निर्जित्य पुरीं श्रेष्ठां लङ्कां तां कामरूपिणीम्।

विक्रमेण महातेजा हनुमान् कपिसत्तमः॥5.4.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः निर्जित्य he having won, पुरीम् श्रेष्ठाम् the excellent city, लङ्काम् ताम् that (the guardian of) Lanka, काम-रूपिणीम् who can assume any form, विक्रमेण by his prowess, महा-तेजाः mighty, हनुमान् that Hanuman, कपि-सत्तमः monkey chief

Anvay: सः महातेजाः कपिसत्तमः हनुमान् श्रेष्ठां पुरीं कामरूपिणीं तां लङ्कां विक्रमेण निर्जित्य …

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the one of great brilliance and the best among monkeys, by his valor, having conquered that excellent city of Lanka, which was guarded by the one capable of assuming any form at will…

अद्वारेण च महाबाहुः प्राकारमभिपुप्लुवे।

प्रविश्य नगरीं लङ्कां कपिराजहितङ्करः ॥5.4.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: अ-द्वारेण च by the rear entrance, महा-बाहुः of strong shoulders, प्राकारम् boundary, अभिपुप्लुवे leapt over, प्रविश्य entered, नगरीम् लङ्काम् Lanka city, कपि-राज-हितम्-करः the one who sought the welfare of the monkey king (Sugriva)

Anvay: महाबाहुः कपिराजहितङ्करः सः अद्वारेण प्राकारम् अभिपुप्लुवे च लङ्कां नगरीं प्रविश्य …

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the strong-armed one who sought the welfare of the monkey king Sugriva, leapt over the city wall through the back entrance and entered the city of Lanka.

चक्रेऽथ पादं सव्यं च शत्रूणां स तु मूर्धनि।

प्रविष्टः सत्त्वसम्पन्नो निशायां मारुतात्मजः॥5.4.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: चक्रे placed, अथ then, पादम् सव्यम् च left foot, शत्रूणाम् of enemy, सः तु he, मूर्धनि on the forehead, प्रविष्टः entered, सत्त्व-सम्पन्नः endowed with great strength, निशायाम् at night, मारुत-आत्मजः son of the Wind-God

Anvay: अथ सत्त्वसम्पन्नः मारुतात्मजः सः शत्रूणां मूर्धनि सव्यं पादं तु चक्रे, निशायां प्रविष्टः च।

Simple meaning: Then Hanuman, the son of the Wind-God, full of strength and courage, entered (Lanka) at night, placing his left foot forward, as if on the heads of the enemies.

स महापथमास्थाय मुक्तापुष्पविराजितम्।

ततस्तु तां पुरीं लङ्कां रम्यामभिययौ कपिः॥5.4.4॥

Word-by-word meaning:  सः he, महा-पथम् highway, आस्थाय having made way, मुक्ता-पुष्प-विराजितम् set with flowers of pearls, ततः तु then, ताम् that, पुरीम् लङ्काम् Lanka city, रम्याम् enchanting, अभिययौ went, कपिः monkey

Anvay: मुक्तापुष्पविराजितं महापथम् आस्थाय ततः सः कपिः तां रम्यां लङ्कां पुरीम् अभिययौ।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the monkey, took the grand road adorned with pearl-like flowers and proceeded toward the enchanting city of Lanka.

हसितोत्कृष्टनिनदैस्तूर्यघोषपुरस्सरैः।

वज्रांकुशनिकाशैश्च वज्रजालविभूषितैः॥5.4.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: हसित-उत्कृष्ट-निनदैः with loud and elevated sounds of laughter, तूर्य-घोष-पुरःसरैः preceded by the sound of musical instruments, वज्र-अङ्कुश-निकाशैः च​​ resembling vajra (thunderbolt) and goads (ankusha), वज्र-जाल-विभूषितैः adorned with networks of diamond-like brilliance

Anvay: हसितोत्कृष्टनिनदैः तूर्यघोषपुरस्सरैः वज्रांकुशनिकाशैः च वज्रजालविभूषितैः (राक्षसैः) …

Simple meaning: Echoing with loud bursts of laughter, preceded by the sound of musical instruments, and guarded by beings resembling thunderbolts and goads, adorned with networks of diamond-like weaponry…

गृहमेघैः पुरी रम्या बभासे द्यौरिवाम्बुदैः।

प्रजज्वाल ततो लङ्का रक्षोगणगृहैः शुभैः॥5.4.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: गृह-मेघैः the houses like clouds, पुरी city, रम्या beautiful, बभासे was shining, द्यौः इव like the sky, अम्बुदैः with clouds, प्रजज्वाल glittering, ततः then, लङ्का Lanka, रक्षः-गण-गृहैः by the houses of rakshasas, शुभैः by auspicious ones

Anvay: गृहमेघैः रम्या पुरी – अम्बुदैः द्यौः इव – बभासे। ततः शुभैः रक्षोगणगृहैः लङ्का प्रजज्वाल।

Simple meaning: The beautiful city, with houses like clouds, shone like the sky filled with clouds. Then Lanka glittered brilliantly with the splendid homes of the rakshasas.

सिताभ्रसदृशैश्चित्रैः पद्मस्वस्तिकसंस्थितैः।

वर्धमानगृहैश्चापि सर्वतः सुविभूषिता॥5.4.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: सित-अभ्र-सदृशैः resembling white clouds, चित्रैः beautifully decorated, पद्म-स्वस्तिक-संस्थितैः  adorned with lotus and swastika patterns, वर्धमान-गृहैः with mansions rising upward/ever-expanding houses, च अपि and also सर्वतः on all sides, सुविभूषिता very well decorated

Anvay: (सा पुरी) सिताभ्रसदृशैः चित्रैः पद्मस्वस्तिकसंस्थितैः वर्धमानगृहैः च अपि सर्वतः सुविभूषिता (आसीत्)।

Simple meaning: The city (of Lanka) was adorned on all sides with splendid mansions that looked like white clouds, decorated with lotus and swastika patterns, and magnificent rising structures.

तां चित्रमाल्याभरणां कपिराजहितङ्करः।

राघवार्थं चरन् श्रीमान् ददर्श च ननन्द च॥5.4.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् that city, चित्र-माल्य-आभरणाम् adorned with varied garlands and ornaments, कपि-राज-हितङ्करः one who sought the welfare of the monkey-king (Sugriva), राघव-अर्थम् for the sake of Rama, चरन् looked around, श्रीमान् illustrious, ददर्श च saw and, ननन्द च rejoiced

Anvay: कपिराजहितङ्करः श्रीमान् सः राघवार्थं चरन् चित्रमाल्याभरणां तां (लङ्कां) ददर्श च ननन्द च।

Simple meaning: The glorious Hanuman, who sought the welfare of the monkey-king and was searching on behalf of Rama, beheld the beautifully adorned Lanka and rejoiced.

भवनाद्भवनं गच्छन् ददर्श पवनात्मजः।

विविधाकृतिरूपाणि भवनानि ततस्ततः॥5.4.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: भवनात् from one mansion, भवनम् to another mansion, गच्छन् going, ददर्श saw, पवन-आत्मजः the son of Wind-God, विविध-आकृति-रूपाणि of various shapes and forms, भवनानि mansions, ततः ततः here and there

Anvay: पवनात्मजः भवनात् भवनं गच्छन् ततः ततः विविधाकृतिरूपाणि भवनानि ददर्श।

Simple meaning: The son of the Wind-God, Hanuman, going from mansion to mansion, saw buildings of various shapes and designs scattered all around.

शुश्राव मधुरं गीतं त्रिस्थानस्वरभूषितम्।

स्त्रीणां मदसमृद्धानां दिवि चाप्सरसामिव॥5.4.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: शुश्राव he heard, मधुरम् melodious, गीतम् song, त्रिस्थान-स्वर-भूषितम् embellished with notes from the three registers (Mandra, Madhya and Tara), स्त्रीणाम् of women, मद-समृद्धानाम्  brimming with intoxicated joy or passion, दिवि in heaven, च अप्सरसाम् इव like that of the celestial nymphs (apsaras)

Anvay: मदसमृद्धानां स्त्रीणां – दिवि अप्सरसाम् इव – त्रिस्थानस्वरभूषितं मधुरं गीतं शुश्राव च।

Simple meaning: Hanuman heard sweet songs, adorned with notes from all three registers, sung by women intoxicated with joy — like those of celestial nymphs in heaven.

शुश्राव काञ्चीनिनदं नूपुराणां च निःस्वनम्।

सोपाननिनदांश्चैव भवनेषु महात्मनाम्॥5.4.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: शुश्राव heard, काञ्ची-निनदम् the tinkling of girdles, नूपुराणाम् च निःस्वनम् and the jingling of anklets, सोपान-निनदान् च एव also the echoes of stairways (footsteps or sounds made on stairs), भवनेषु in the mansions, महा-आत्मानाम् of the great souls (i.e., mighty rakshasas or nobles)

Anvay: महात्मनां भवनेषु काञ्चीनिनदं, नूपुराणां निःस्वनं, सोपाननिनदान् च एव शुश्राव।

Simple meaning: Hanuman heard the tinkling of girdles, the jingling of anklets, and the echoes of footsteps on stairways in the mansions of the noble rakshasas.

आस्फोटितनिनादांश्च क्ष्वेलितांश्च ततस्ततः।

शुश्राव जपतां तत्र मन्त्रान् रक्षोगृहेषु वै॥5.4.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: आस्फोटित-निनादान् च the resounding claps of arms (a traditional gesture of challenge or readiness before battle or wrestling), क्ष्वेलितान् च heroic roars or battle cries made by warriors, either in combat or as a display of pride/power, ततः ततः here and there/from all sides, शुश्राव he heard, जपताम् मन्त्रान्  of those chanting mantras, रक्षः-गृहेषु वै indeed in the houses of the rakshasas

Anvay: रक्षोगृहेषु तत्र ततः अस्फोटितनिनादान् क्ष्वेलितान् च जपतां मन्त्रान् शुश्राव वै।

Simple meaning: Hanuman heard the clapping of arms in challenge, the fierce roars of warriors, and the chanting of mantras from the houses of the rakshasas.

स्वाध्यायनिरतांश्चैव यातुधानान् ददर्श सः।

रावणस्तवसंयुक्तान् गर्जतो राक्षसानपि॥5.4.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्वाध्याय-निरतान् च एव  and those engaged in study of vedas, यातुधानान्  rakshasas, ददर्श सः he (Hanuman) saw, रावण-स्तव-संयुक्तान् those who were singing the praises of Ravana, गर्जतः राक्षसान् अपि also rakshasas who were roaring loudly

Anvay: सः हनुमान् स्वाध्यायनिरतान् रावणस्तवसंयुक्तान् यातुधानान् गर्जतः राक्षसान् च अपि ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw rakshasas engaged in the recitation of sacred texts, some extolling Ravana, and others roaring loudly.

राजमार्गं समावृत्य स्थितं रक्षोबलं महत्।

ददर्श मध्यमे गुल्मे रावणस्य चरान् बहून्॥5.4.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: राज-मार्गम् the royal highway, समावृत्य occupying, covering, स्थितम् stationed, रक्षः-बलम् the army of rakshasas, महत् great, massive, ददर्श he saw, मध्यमे in the midst, गुल्मे in a battalion or group, रावणस्य of Ravana, चरान् spies, बहून् many

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) राजमार्गं समावृत्य स्थितं महत् रक्षोबलं, गुल्मे मध्यमे रावणस्य बहून् चरान् ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw a large army of rakshasas stationed along the royal highway, and in the middle of the battalion, he saw many of Ravana’s spies.

दीक्षितान् जटिलान् मुण्डान् गोजिनाम्बरवाससः।

दर्भमुष्टिप्रहरणानग्निकुण्डायुधांस्तथा॥5.4.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: दीक्षितान् initiated (into sacred vows/rites), जटिलान् matted-haired, मुण्डान्  shaven-headed, गो-जिन-अम्बर-वाससः wearing garments made of cowhide (deer or cow skin), दर्भ-मुष्टि-प्रहरणान् wielding darbha grass as weapons, अग्नि-कुण्ड-आयुधान् having fire pits as their armaments or engaged in ritual fires, तथा likewise/also

Anvay: (सः हनुमान्) दीक्षितान् जटिलान् मुण्डान् गोजिनाम्बरवाससः दर्भमुष्टिप्रहरणान् अग्निकुण्डायुधान् तथा (राक्षसान्) ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw rakshasas who appeared as ascetics — some with matted hair, some with shaved heads, wearing cowhide garments, wielding bunches of darbha grass as weapons, and others engaged in ritual fires with fire pits as their armaments.

कूटमुद्गरपाणींश्च दण्डायुधधरानपि।

एकाक्षानेककर्णांश्च लम्बोदरपयोधरान्॥5.4.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: कूट-मुद्गर-पाणीन् च holding mallets and clubs in their hands, दण्ड-आयुध-धरान् अपि also bearing staff-weapons, एक-अक्षान् one-eyed beings, एक-कर्णान् च  and one-eared beings, लम्ब-उदर-पयोधरान् those with large bellies and pendulous breasts

Anvay: कूटमुद्गरपाणीन्, दण्डायुधधरान् अपि, एकाक्षान् एककर्णान् च, लम्बोदरपयोधरान् अपि (ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman) saw rakshasas holding mallets and clubs in their hands, some bearing staff-weapons, some one-eyed, others one-eared, and many with large bellies and hanging breasts.

करालान् भुग्नवक्त्रांश्च विकटान् वामनांस्तथा।

धन्विनः खङ्गिनश्चैव शतघ्नीमुसलायुधान्॥5.4.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: करालान् fierce-looking, terrifying ones, भुग्न-वक्त्रान् च and those with crooked/distorted faces, विकटान् monstrous, misshapen ones, वामनान् तथा and also dwarfish ones, धन्विनः archers (those holding bows), खङ्गिनः च एव and also those wielding swords, शतघ्नी-मुसल-आयुधान् those wielding shataghnis (multi-barbed weapons) and musala (pestle-like weapons)

Anvay: (सः) करालान्, भुग्नवक्त्रान्, विकटान्, वामनान्, धन्विनः, खङ्गिनः च, शतघ्नीमुसलायुधान् च (ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman) saw terrifying beings with crooked faces, monstrous forms, and dwarfish stature; archers, sword-bearers, and those wielding shataghnis and pestle-like weapons.

परिघोत्तमहस्तांश्च विचित्रकवचोज्ज्वलान्।

नातिस्थूलान्नातिकृशान्नातिदीर्घातिह्रस्वकान्॥5.4.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: परिघ-उत्तम-हस्तान् च and those with excellent maces (parigha) in hand, विचित्र-कवच-उज्ज्वलान् shining with various kinds of armor, न अति-स्थूलान् not excessively stout, न अति-कृशान् nor excessively thin, न अति-दीर्घ-अति-ह्रस्वकान् not extremely tall or extremely short

Anvay: (सः) परिघोत्तमहस्तान् विचित्रकवचोज्ज्वलान् न अतिस्थूलान्, न अतिकृशान्, न अतिदीर्घातिह्रस्वकान् च (ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman) saw warriors holding excellent maces, shining in varied armor—not too stout, nor too thin, neither very tall nor very short.

नातिगौरान्नातिकृष्णान्नातिकुब्जान्नवामनान्।

विरूपान् बहुरूपांश्च सुरूपांश्च सुवर्चसः॥5.4.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: न अति-गौरान् not very fair-complexioned, न अति-कृष्णान् not very dark, न अति-कुब्जान् not very hunchbacked, न वामनान् not dwarfish, विरूपान् deformed, misshapen, बहु-रूपान् च and those with many forms (or various appearances), सु-रूपान् च and those with beautiful forms, सु-वर्चसः having great radiance or luster

Anvay: सः न अतिगौरान्, न अतिकृष्णान्, न अतिकुब्जान्, न वामनान्, विरूपान्, बहुरूपान् च, सुरूपान् च सुवर्चसः (राक्षसान्) ददर्श।

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman) saw radiant rakshasas who were not too fair, not too dark, not excessively hunchbacked or dwarfish—some were deformed, some of many forms, and others handsome and resplendent.

ध्वजीन् पताकिनश्चैव ददर्श विविधायुधान्।

शक्तिवृक्षायुधांश्चैव पट्टिसाशनिधारिणः॥5.4.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: ध्वजीन् those bearing banners, पताकिनः च एव and those bearing flags, ददर्श (he) saw, विविध-आयुधान् carrying various kinds of weapons, शक्ति-वृक्ष-आयुधान् च एव  and those wielding weapons like spears and trees, पट्टिस-अशनि-धारिणः wielders of pattisa (a kind of long sword or lance) and thunderbolt-like weapons

Anvay: सः ध्वजीन्, पताकिनः च, विविधायुधान्, शक्तिवृक्षायुधान् च, पट्टिसाशनिधारिणः च ददर्श।

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman) saw warriors bearing banners and flags, armed with various weapons—some wielding spears and even trees, others bearing swords and thunderbolt-like weapons.

क्षेपणीपाशहस्तांश्च ददर्श स महाकपिः।

स्रग्विणस्त्वनुलिप्तांश्च वराभरणभूषितान्॥5.4.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्षेपणी-पाश-हस्तान् च  having in their hands slings and nooses (or throwing weapons and lassos), ददर्श (he) saw, सः महाकपिः that great monkey (Hanuman), स्रग्विणः adorned with garlands, अनुलिप्तान् च also smeared (with unguents or sandal paste), वर-आभरण-भूषितान् decorated with excellent ornaments

Anvay: सः महाकपिः क्षेपणीपाशहस्तान्, स्रग्विणः, अनुलिप्तान्, वराभरणभूषितान् च ददर्श।

Simple meaning: The great monkey (Hanuman) saw warriors holding slings and nooses in their hands, adorned with garlands, smeared with sandal paste, and decked with splendid ornaments.

नानावेषसमायुक्तान् यथास्स्वैरगतान् बहून्।

तीक्ष्णशूलधरांश्चैव वज्रिणश्च महाबलान्॥5.4.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: नाना-वेष-समायुक्तान् adorned in various kinds of attire, यथा स्वैरगतान्  moving about freely as they pleased, बहून् many, तीक्ष्ण-शूल-धरान् च एव also those carrying sharp spears, वज्रिणः च and those bearing thunderbolts (vajra), महा-बलान् of great strength

Anvay: सः (महाकपिः) नानावेषसमायुक्तान्, यथा स्वैरगतान्, तीक्ष्णशूलधरान् च वज्रिणः च बहून् महाबलान् ददर्श।

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman) saw many powerful beings, dressed in various kinds of attire, moving about freely as they pleased—some bearing sharp spears, and others wielding thunderbolts.

शतसाहस्रमव्यग्रमारक्षं मध्यमं कपिः।

रक्षोधिपतिनिर्दिष्टं ददर्शान्तःपुराग्रतः॥5.4.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: शत-साहस्रम् one hundred thousand, अव्यग्रम् vigilant, आरक्षम् guards, मध्यमम् of middle rank, कपिः vanara, रक्षः-अधिपति-निर्दिष्टम् ordered (stationed) by the lord of rakshasas, ददर्श saw, अन्तःपुर-अग्रतः in front of the harem

Anvay: कपिः अन्तःपुराग्रतः रक्षोधिपतिनिर्दिष्टं शतसाहस्रम् अव्यग्रं मध्यमम् आरक्षं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw, in front of the harem, a hundred thousand vigilant guards of middle rank, appointed by the king of rakshasas.

स तदा तद्गृहं दृष्ट्वा महाहाटकतोरणम्।

राक्षसेन्द्रस्य विख्यातमद्रिमूर्ध्नि प्रतिष्ठितम्॥5.4.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Hanuman), तदा then, तत् गृहम्  that house (palace), दृष्ट्वा having seen, महा-हाटक-तोरणम् having grand golden arch ways (हाटक = gold तोरण = arch), राक्षस-इन्द्रस्य of the king of rakshasas, विख्यातम् famous, well-known, अद्रि-मूर्ध्नि on the mountain top (अद्रि = mountain, मूर्धन् = top/head), प्रतिष्ठितम् situated, established

Anvay: तदा सः अद्रिमूर्ध्नि प्रतिष्ठितं महाहाटकतोरणं विख्यातं राक्षसेन्द्रस्य तत् गृहं दृष्ट्वा…

Simple meaning: Then Hanuman, seeing that well-known palace of the king of rakshasas, adorned with grand golden arch ways, situated on the top of a mountain…

पुण्डरीकावतंसाभिः परिखाभिरलङ्कृतम्।

प्राकारावृतमत्यन्तं ददर्श स महाकपिः॥5.4.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुण्डरीक-अवतंसाभिः with white lotus flowers appearing like ear ornaments, परिखाभिः with moats, अलङ्कृतम् decorated, प्राकार-आवृतम् surrounded by a compound wall, अत्यन्तम् all over, ददर्श saw, सः that, महा-कपिः great monkey

Anvay: सः महाकपिः पुण्डरीकावतंसाभिः परिखाभिः अलङ्कृतम् अत्यन्तं प्राकारावृतं (तत् गृहं) ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman, the great monkey, saw the palace adorned with moats decorated with lotus-like ornaments resembling ear pendants, and entirely surrounded by massive ramparts.

त्रिविष्टपनिभं दिव्यं दिव्यनादनिनादितम्।

वाजिहेषितसङ्घुष्टं नादितं भूषणैस्तथा॥5.4.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्रिविष्टप-निभम् resembling heaven (Trivishtapa = heaven, realm of the gods), दिव्यम् divine, दिव्य-नाद-निनादितम् resounding with celestial sounds, वाजि-हेषित-सङ्घुष्टम् reverberating with the neighing of horses, नादितम् resonating, भूषणैः तथा also with (the sound of) ornaments

Anvay: सः महाकपिः त्रिविष्टपनिभं दिव्यं दिव्यनादनिनादितं वाजिहेषितसङ्घुष्टं भूषणैः नादितं (तत्) ददर्श।

Simple meaning: Hanuman saw that divine palace, resembling the celestial realm (Trivishtapa), resounding with heavenly music, the neighing of horses, and the jingling of ornaments.

रथैर्यानैर्विमानैश्च तथा हयगजैः शुभैः।

वारणैश्च चतुर्दन्तैः श्वेताभ्रनिचयोपमैः॥5.4.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: रथैः by chariots, यानैः by vehicles (carriages), विमानैः च and by aerial cars, तथा as well, हय-गजैः शुभैः by auspicious horses and elephants, वारणैः च and by elephants, चतुर्दन्तैः having four tusks, श्वेत-अभ्र-निचय-उपमैः resembling clusters of white clouds

गजैः refers to regular elephants, possibly those used in transportation or ceremonial roles.
वारणैः चतुर्दन्तैः refers to extraordinary elephants with four tusks, likely mythical or celestial in nature, symbolic of great power and prestige—used perhaps by Ravana’s elite guard.

Anvay: रथैः यानैः विमानैः च तथा शुभैः हयगजैः चतुर्दन्तैः वारणैः च श्वेताभ्रनिचयोपमैः (सः महाकपिः ददर्श)।

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman) saw chariots, vehicles, aerial cars, and auspicious horses and elephants—especially those four-tusked elephants resembling masses of white clouds.

भूषितं रुचिरद्वारं मत्तैश्च मृगपक्षिभिः।

रक्षितं सुमहावीर्यैर्यातुधानैः सहस्रशः।

राक्षसाधिपतेर्गुप्तमाविवेश महाकपिः॥5.4.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूषितम् adorned, decorated, रुचिर-द्वारम् beautiful gates, मत्तैः‌ intoxicated (with joy or natural fervor), च and, मृग-पक्षिभिः with animals and birds, रक्षितम्  guarded, सु-महा-वीर्यैः by those of great strength, यातुधानैः by rakshasas, सहस्रशः by the thousands, राक्षस-अधिपतेः of the king of rakshasas, गुप्तम् well-protected, आविवेश entered, महा-कपिः the great monkey (Hanuman)

Anvay: सः महाकपिः मत्तैः मृगपक्षिभिः च सुमहावीर्यैः सहस्रशः यातुधानैः रक्षितं राक्षसाधिपतेः गुप्तं भूषितं रुचिरद्वारम् आविवेश।

Simple meaning: The great monkey Hanuman entered the beautifully adorned and charmingly gated palace of the king of rakshasas, which was teeming with joyous animals and birds, and guarded by thousands of powerful rakshasas.

सहेमजाम्बूनदचक्रवालं महार्हमुक्तामणिभूषितान्तम्।

परार्थ्यकालागुरुचन्दनाक्तं स रावणान्तःपुरमाविवेश॥5.4.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: स-हेम-जाम्बूनद-चक्रवालम् having golden and Jambunada (gold from Jambu river) circular domes, महा-अर्ह-मुक्ता-मणि-भूषित-अन्तम् interiors adorned with highly valuable pearl and gems, परार्थ्य meant for royalty, काल-अगुरु-चन्दन-आक्तम् anointed with black aloeswood (a fragrant substance), sandalwood, सः he, रावण-अन्तःपुरम् inner palace of Ravana (harem), आविवेश entered

Anvay: सः सहेमजाम्बूनदचक्रवालं महार्हमुक्तामणिभूषितान्तं परार्थ्यकालागुरुचन्दनाक्तं रावणान्तःपुरम् आविवेश।

Simple meaning: He (Hanuman) entered Ravana’s inner palace, which had circular domes of gold and Jambunada gold, interiors adorned with costly pearls and gems, and was perfumed with black aloeswood and sandal paste fit for royalty.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे चतुर्थः सर्गः॥4॥

Thus ends the fourth sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
