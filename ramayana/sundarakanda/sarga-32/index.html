Sarga 32

सर्गः ३२

ॐ

Sundarakanda Sarga 32

॥ॐ॥

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।

वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 32

Sita's conjecture

(Benefit: Strengthening relations and friendships)

(14 Shlokas)

ततश्शाखान्तरे लीनं दृष्ट्वा चलितमानसा।

वेष्टितार्जुनवस्त्रं तं विद्युत्सङ्घातपिङ्गलम्॥5.32.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, शाखा-अन्तरे amidst the branches, लीनम् hidden, nestled, दृष्ट्वा having seen, चलित-मानसा with a disturbed (moved or shaken) mind, वेष्टित-अर्जुन-वस्त्रम् wrapped in white cloth (like that of Arjuna tree bark/or cloth resembling Arjuna tree bark), तम् him (Hanuman), विद्युत्-सङ्घात-पिङ्गलम् tawny or reddish like a cluster of lightning

Anvay: ततः सा शाखान्तरे लीनं वेष्टितार्जुनवस्त्रं विद्युत्सङ्घातपिङ्गलं तं दृष्ट्वा चलितमानसा (अभवत्)।

Simple meaning: Then, seeing him (Hanuman), hidden among the branches, clothed in cloth resembling the bark of the Arjuna tree, and tawny like a cluster of lightning, her mind (Sita’s) was shaken (with surprise, curiosity, or wonder).

सा ददर्श कपिं तत्र प्रश्रितं प्रियवादिनम्।

फुल्लाशोकोत्कराभासं तप्तचामीकरेक्षणम्॥5.32.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Sita), ददर्श saw, कपिम् the monkey (Hanuman), तत्र there, प्रश्रितम् humble, respectful, प्रिय-वादिनम् one who speaks sweetly or lovingly, फुल्ल-अशोक-उत्कर-आभासम् shining like a blooming cluster of Ashoka flowers, तप्त-चामीकर-ईक्षणम् with eyes like molten gold (glowing golden eyes)

Anvay: सा तत्र प्रश्रितं प्रियवादिनं फुल्लाशोकोत्कराभासं तप्तचामीकरेक्षणं कपिं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: She (Sita) saw there the monkey (Hanuman), humble and sweet-spoken, shining like a blooming cluster of Ashoka flowers, with eyes resembling molten gold.

मैथिली चिन्तयामास विस्मयं परमं गता।

अहो भीममिदं रूपं वानरस्य दुरासदम्॥5.32.3॥

दुर्निरीक्षमिति ज्ञात्वा पुनरेव मुमोह सा।

Word-by-word meaning: मैथिली Maithili, चिन्तयामास began to think/pondered, विस्मयम् amazement, परम् great/supreme, गता (she) reached/was filled with, अहो Oh!/Alas! (expression of surprise), भीमम् terrifying/formidable, इदम् this, रूपम् form, वानरस्य of the monkey (Hanuman), दुरासदम् unapproachable/hard to face, दुर्निरीक्ष्यम् hard to look at/unbearable to behold, इति thus, ज्ञात्वा having known/realized, पुनः एव once again, मुमोह fainted/swooned, सा she

Anvay: मैथिली परं विस्मयं गता चिन्तयामास — अहो! वानरस्य इदं भीमं दुरासदं रूपम्। दुर्निरीक्ष्यम् इति ज्ञात्वा सा पुनः एव मुमोह।

Simple meaning: Maithili, filled with great amazement, began to ponder: "Oh! This terrifying and unapproachable form of the monkey!" Realizing that it was difficult even to look at, she once again fainted.

विललाप भृशं सीता करुणं भयमोहिता॥5.32.4॥

रामरामेति दुःखार्ता लक्ष्मणेति च भामिनी।

रुरोद बहुधा सीता मन्दं मन्दस्वरा सती॥5.32.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: विललाप lamented/wailed, भृशम् intensely, सीता Sita, करुणम् piteously/sorrowfully, भय-मोहिता deluded/confused by fear, राम राम इति "Rama! Rama!" thus, दुःखार्ता afflicted with sorrow, लक्ष्मण इति च and (cried out) "Lakshmana!", भामिनी the noble lady/beautiful woman, रुरोद she cried/wept, बहुधा in many ways/repeatedly, सीता Sita, मन्दम् मन्द-स्वरा in a soft and low voice, सती the virtuous lady

Anvay: भामिनी सीता भयमोहिता दुःखार्ता "राम राम" इति, "लक्ष्मण" इति च करुणं भृशं विललाप। सती सीता मन्दं मन्दस्वरा बहुधा रुरोद।

Simple meaning: Sita, overwhelmed by fear and delusion, wailed intensely and sorrowfully. Afflicted with grief, the noble lady cried out, "Rama! Rama!" and "Lakshmana!" The virtuous Sita wept in many ways, her voice soft and faint.

सा तं दृष्ट्वा हरिश्रेष्ठं विनीतवदुपस्थितम्।

मैथिली चिन्तयामास स्वप्नोऽयमिति भामिनी॥5.32.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she, तम् him, दृष्ट्वा having seen, हरि-श्रेष्ठम् the best among the vanaras (Hanuman), विनीतवत् with humility/as if humble, उपस्थितम् standing nearby/present, मैथिली Maithili, चिन्तयामास began to think/pondered, स्वप्नः अयम् इति "this is a dream", thus, भामिनी the noble lady/beautiful woman

Anvay: सा भामिनी मैथिली विनीतवत् उपस्थितं तं हरिश्रेष्ठं दृष्ट्वा "अयं स्वप्नः" इति चिन्तयामास।

Simple meaning: Seeing that best among the vanaras (Hanuman), who stood before her humbly, the noble lady Maithili thought to herself, "This must be a dream."

सा वीक्षमाणा पृथुभुग्नवक्त्रं शाखामृगेन्द्रस्य यथोक्तकारम्।

ददर्श पिङ्गाधिपतेरमात्यं वातात्मजं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्॥5.32.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Sita), वीक्षमाणा looking at/observing, पृथु-भुग्न-वक्त्रम् broad and slightly bent face, शाखा-मृग-इन्द्रस्य of the king of the monkeys (Hanuman), यथा-उक्त-कारम् one who acted as told/did exactly as instructed, ददर्श saw, पिङ्ग-अधिपतेः of Sugriva, the lord of the tawny-colored vanaras, अमात्यम् minister, वात-आत्मजम् son of the Wind-God (Hanuman) बुद्धिमताम् वरिष्ठम् foremost among the wise

Anvay: सा वीक्षमाणा शाखामृगेन्द्रस्य पृथुभुग्नवक्त्रं यथोक्तकारं पिङ्गाधिपतेः अमात्यं वातात्मजं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठं ददर्श।

Simple meaning: She observing, saw him— with a broad and slightly bent face, who was the king among tree-dwellers (monkeys), who acted just as instructed— the minister of Sugriva, the son of the Wind-God, and the foremost among the wise.

सा तं समीक्ष्यैव भृशं विसंज्ञा गतासुकल्पेव बभूव सीता।

चिरेण संज्ञां प्रतिलभ्य भूयो विचिन्तयामास विशालनेत्रा॥5.32.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Sita), तम् him (Hanuman), समीक्ष्य एव upon just seeing/closely observing, भृशम् intensely/extremely, विसंज्ञा unconscious/losing awareness, गत-आसु-कल्पा इव appearing as if life had departed, बभूव became, सीता Sita, चिरेण after a long time, संज्ञाम् consciousness, प्रतिलभ्य regaining, भूयः again, विचिन्तयामास began to ponder/think, विशालनेत्रा large-eyed (epithet for Sita)

Anvay: सा तं समीक्ष्य एव भृशं विसंज्ञा गतासुकल्पा इव बभूव । विशालनेत्रा सीता चिरेण संज्ञां प्रतिलभ्य भूयः विचिन्तयामास।

Simple meaning: Upon seeing him (Hanuman), Sita became extremely overwhelmed and lost consciousness, appearing as though life had left her body. After a long while, the large-eyed Sita regained awareness and began to think once again.

स्वप्ने मयाऽयं विकृतोऽद्य दृष्टश्शाखामृगश्शास्त्रगणैर्निषिद्धः।

स्वस्त्यस्तु रामाय सलक्ष्मणाय तथा पितुर्मे जनकस्य राज्ञः॥5.32.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्वप्ने in a dream, मया by me, अयम् this (being), विकृतः strange/unnatural/frightening, अद्य today, दृष्टः seen, शाखा-मृगः monkey (literally: branch-animal), शास्त्र-गणैः by collections of scriptures, निषिद्धः prohibited/considered inauspicious, स्वस्ति अस्तु may there be well-being/auspiciousness, रामाय to Rama, सलक्ष्मणाय with Lakshmana, तथा and also, पितुः मे of my father, जनकस्य राज्ञः King Janaka

Anvay: अद्य मया स्वप्ने शास्त्रगणैः निषिद्धः अयं विकृतः शाखामृगः दृष्टः। सलक्ष्मणाय रामाय तथा मे पितुः जनकस्य राज्ञः स्वस्ति अस्तु।

Simple meaning: “Today, in a dream, I saw this strange monkey (a being appearing in odd form), which is considered inauspicious by groups of scriptures. May there be well-being for Rama, with Lakshmana, and also for my father, King Janaka.

स्वप्नोऽपि नायं नहि मेऽस्ति निद्रा शोकेन दुःखेन च पीडितायाः।

सुखं हि मे नास्ति यतोऽस्मि हीना तेनेन्दुपूर्णप्रतिमाननेन॥5.32.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्वप्नः अपि न अयम् This is not even a dream, न हि मे अस्ति निद्रा for I truly have no sleep, शोकेन due to sorrow, दुःखेन च and due to suffering, पीडितायाः (being) tormented, सुखम् हि मे न अस्ति indeed I have no happiness, यतः because, अस्मि I am, हीना separated, तेन from that one (Rama), इन्दु-पूर्ण-प्रतिमा-आननेन whose face is like the full moon

Anvay: अयं स्वप्नः अपि न (अस्ति), शोकेन दुःखेन च पीडितायाः मे निद्रा न हि अस्ति। मे सुखं न हि अस्ति, यतः (अहम्) तेन इन्दुपूर्णप्रतिमाननेन (रामेण) हीना अस्मि।

Simple meaning: “This is not even a dream, for I truly get no sleep, being tormented by sorrow and suffering. Indeed, I have no happiness, because I am separated from him— the one whose face resembles the full moon.

रामेति रामेति सदैव बुद्ध्या विचिन्त्य वाचा ब्रुवती तमेव।

तस्यानुरूपां च कथां तदर्थमेवं प्रपश्यामि तथा शृणोमि॥5.32.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: राम इति राम इति only “Rama, Rama”, सदा एव always, बुद्ध्या in thought/with my intellect, विचिन्त्य constantly thinking, वाचा with speech, ब्रुवती speaking, तम् एव of him alone, तस्य अनुरूपाम् befitting of him, च कथाम् and stories (narrative), तदर्थम् for his sake/concerning him, एवम् thus, प्रपश्यामि I see, तथा शृणोमि and I hear likewise

Anvay: सदैव बुद्ध्या “राम इति राम इति” विचिन्त्य, वाचा तम् एव ब्रुवती, तस्य अनुरूपां च कथां तदर्थम् एवं प्रपश्यामि, तथा शृणोमि।

Simple meaning: “Always thinking “Rama, Rama” in my mind, and speaking only of him with my words, I see everything in connection to him, and likewise, I hear everything as relating to him.

अहं हि तस्याद्य मनोभवेन सम्पीडिता तद्गतसर्वभावा।

विचिन्तयन्ती सततं तमेव तथैव पश्यामि तथा शृणोमि॥5.32.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: अहम् हि I indeed, तस्य of him (Rama), अद्य now/today/at present, मनोभवेन by love/desire (born of the mind, सम्पीडिता tormented/afflicted, तत्-गत-सर्व-भावा whose entire being has gone toward him (absorbed in him), विचिन्तयन्ती constantly thinking, सततम् always, तम् एव him alone, तथा एव in the same way/thus, पश्यामि I see, तथा शृणोमि and likewise I hear

Anvay: अद्य अहं तस्य मनोभवेन सम्पीडिता हि, तद्गतसर्वभावा, सततं तम् एव विचिन्तयन्ती, तथा एव पश्यामि, तथा शृणोमि।

Simple meaning: “Indeed, I am now tormented by love for him (Rama), my entire being absorbed in him; constantly thinking only of him, I see everything in that way, and likewise I hear everything that way.

मनोरथस्स्यादिति चिन्तयामि तथापि बुद्ध्या च वितर्कयामि।

किं कारणं तस्य हि नास्ति रूपं सुव्यक्तरूपश्च वदत्ययं माम्॥5.32.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: मनोरथः imagination/desire of the mind/fancy, स्यात् may be/could be, इति thus, चिन्तयामि I think, तथा अपि even then/nevertheless, बुद्ध्या च with intellect also, वितर्कयामि I reason/deliberate, किम् कारणम् what is the reason, तस्य हि of that (mental imagination) indeed, न अस्ति रूपम् there is no form (visible appearance), सुव्यक्त-रूपः च yet this one has a clearly defined form, वदति अयम् माम् this one speaks to me

Anvay: मनोरथः स्यात् इति चिन्तयामि। तथा अपि बुद्ध्या च वितर्कयामि— किं कारणं? तस्य (मनोरथस्य​) रूपं न हि अस्ति, अयं सुव्यक्तरूपः च मां वदति।

Simple meaning: “I think, "This might be just a mental fancy (imagination),"  but even so, I reason with my intellect: What is the cause? For a mental fantasy does not have a form, and yet this being has a clearly visible form and is speaking to me.

नमोऽस्तु वाचस्पतये सवज्रिणे स्वयंभुवे चैव हुताशनाय च।

अनेन चोक्तं यदिदं ममाग्रतो वनौकसा तच्छ तथास्तु नान्यथा॥5.32.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: नमः अस्तु salutation be/let there be obeisance, वाचस्पतये to the lord of speech (Brihaspati), सवज्रिणे to the one who wields the thunderbolt (Indra), स्वयंभुवे to the self-born (Brahma), च एव and also, हुताशनाय च and to the fire god (Agni), अनेन च उक्तम् and this which has been said by him, यत् इदम् मम अग्रतः this that has been said in front of me, वनौकसा by this forest-dweller (Hanuman), तत् च may that, तथा अस्तु be so, न अन्यथा not otherwise

Anvay: वाचस्पतये सवज्रिणे स्वयंभुवे च हुताशनाय च नमः अस्तु। अनेन वनौकसा मम अग्रतः यत् इदम् उक्तं, तत् तथा अस्तु, न अन्यथा।

Simple meaning: “Salutations be to Brihaspati (lord of speech), to Indra (wielder of the thunderbolt), to Brahma (the self-born), and to Agni (the fire god). May what this forest-dweller (Hanuman) has just said before me come true exactly as he said — and not otherwise.”

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे द्वात्रिंशः सर्गः॥32॥

Thus ends the thirty-second sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
