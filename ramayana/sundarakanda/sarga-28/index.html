सर्गः २८

Sundarakanda Sarga 28

॥ॐ॥

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।

वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

Sundarakanda Sarga 28

Sita's wailings, finally finds some auspicious signs

(20 Shlokas)

सा राक्षसेन्द्रस्य वचो निशम्य तद्रावणस्याप्रियमप्रियार्ता।

सीता वितत्रास यथा वनान्ते सिंहाभिपन्ना गजराजकन्या॥5.28.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Sita), राक्षस-इन्द्रस्य of the king of rakshasas (i.e., Ravana), वचः words, निशम्य having heard, तत् that, रावणस्य of Ravana, अप्रियम् unpleasant (i.e., harsh, threatening words), प्रिय-आर्ता distressed for her beloved (Rama), सीता Sita, वितत्रास was terrified/trembled in fear, यथा just as, वन-अन्ते in the forest, सिंह-अभिपन्ना attacked by a lion, गज-राज-कन्या the daughter of the king of elephants (i.e., a young female elephant)

Anvay: सा सीता, राक्षसेन्द्रस्य रावणस्य तत् अप्रियं वचः निशम्य, प्रियार्ता, वनान्ते सिंहाभिपन्ना गजराजकन्या यथा, वितत्रास।

Simple meaning: Sita, who was distressed for her beloved Rama, on hearing those harsh and unpleasant words of Ravana, the king of the rakshasas, trembled in fear — just like the daughter of the king of elephants when attacked by a lion in the depths of the forest.

सा राक्षसीमध्यगता च भीरुर्वाग्भिर्भृशं रावणतर्जिता च।

कान्तारमध्ये विजने विसृष्टा बालेव कन्या विललाप सीता॥5.28.2॥

 

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Sita), राक्षसी-मध्य-गता च and surrounded by rakshasis, भीरुः timid/frightened, वाग्भिः with words, भृशम् excessively/severely, रावण-तर्जिता threatened by Ravana, च and, कान्तार-मध्ये in the midst of a forest, विजने in a lonely place, विसृष्टा abandoned/left alone, बाला इव like a young girl, कन्या maiden/daughter, विललाप lamented/cried piteously, सीता Sita

Anvay: राक्षसीमध्यगता च, भृशं वाग्भिः रावणतर्जिता च – विजने कान्तारमध्ये विसृष्टा, बाला कन्या इव – सा भीरुः सीता विललाप।

Simple meaning: That timid Sita, surrounded by rakshasis, severely threatened by Ravana's harsh words, lamented like a young maiden abandoned in the wilderness.

सत्यं बतेदं प्रवदन्ति लोके नाकालमृत्युर्भवतीति सन्तः।

यत्राहमेवं परिभर्त्स्यमाना जीवामि दीना क्षणमप्यपुण्या॥5.28.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सत्यम् truly, ब‌त indeed/surely (emphatic particle), इदम् this, प्रवदन्ति they say, लोके in the world, न अकाल-मृत्युः भवति untimely death does not occur, इति like this, सन्तः wise people/the good, यत्र where, अहम् I, एवम् in this way, परिभर्त्स्यमाना being insulted/rebuked harshly, जीवामि I live/continue to live, दीना miserable, क्षणम् अपि even for a moment, अपुण्या unworthy/devoid of merit

Anvay: लोके ‘अकालमृत्युः न भवति’ इति सन्तः प्रवदन्ति, इदं सत्यं ब‌त। यत्र अपुण्या, दीना, एवं परिभर्त्स्यमाना अहं क्षणम् अपि जीवामि।

Simple meaning: “Indeed, what the wise say in this world is true — "There is no such thing as an untimely death." For even I, miserable and unworthy, am still alive even for a moment, despite being rebuked and humiliated in this manner.

सुखाद्विहीनं बहुदुःखपूर्णमिदं तु नूनं हृदयं स्थिरं मे।

विशीर्यते यन्न सहस्रधाऽद्य वज्राहतं शृङ्गमिवाचलस्य॥5.28.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुखात् विहीनम् deprived of happiness, बहु-दुःख-पूर्णम् filled with many sorrows, इदम् this, तु indeed/but, नूनम् surely, हृदयम् heart, स्थिरम् firm/strong, मे my, विशीर्यते does not shatter, यत् which, न not, सहस्रधा into a thousand pieces, अद्य today, वज्र-आहतम् struck by a thunderbolt, शृङ्गम् peak, इव like, अचलस्य of a mountain

Anvay: सुखात् विहीनं बहुदुःखपूर्णं च मे इदं हृदयं, नूनं स्थिरं, यत् अद्य – वज्राहतम् अचलस्य शृङ्गम् इव – सहस्रधा न विशीर्यते।

Simple meaning: “This heart of mine, deprived of joy and filled with immense sorrow, must surely be very strong — for it does not shatter into a thousand pieces today, like the peak of a mountain struck by a thunderbolt.

नैवास्ति दोषो मम नूनमत्र वध्याहमस्याप्रियदर्शनस्य।

भावं न चास्याहमनुप्रदातुमलं द्विजो मन्त्रमिवाद्विजाय॥5.28.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: न एव अस्ति there is indeed no, दोषः fault, मम of mine, नूनम् surely/indeed, अत्र in this (matter), वध्या अहम् I deserve to be killed, अस्य by him/of this one (Ravana), अ-प्रियदर्शनस्य unpleasant to look at, भावम् feeling/affection/love, न च and not, अस्य to him (Ravana), अहम् I, अनुप्रदातुम् to give/respond with, अलम् able to, द्विजः a Brahmana, मन्त्रम् mantra/sacred chant, इव like, अ-द्विजाय to a non-Brahmana

Anvay: नूनम् अत्र मम दोषः न एव अस्ति। अहम् अस्य अप्रियदर्शनस्य वध्या। अहम् अस्य भावं – द्विजः अद्विजाय मन्त्रम् इव – अनुप्रदातुं न अलम्।

Simple meaning: “Surely, there is no fault of mine in this matter. I deserve to be killed by this unpleasant one (Ravana). I cannot offer him my love — just as a Brahmana cannot offer a sacred mantra to a non-Brahmana.

नूनं ममाङ्गान्यचिरादनार्यः शस्त्रैश्शितैश्छेत्स्यति राक्षसेन्द्रः।

तस्मिननागच्छति लोकनाथे गर्भस्थजन्तोरिव शल्यकृन्तः॥5.28.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: नूनम् surely/indeed, मम अङ्गानि my limbs, अचिरात् soon, अनार्यः the ignoble one/wicked person (i.e., Ravana), शस्त्रैः with weapons, शितैः sharp, छेत्स्यति will cut/sever, राक्षसेन्द्रः king of the rakshasas (i.e., Ravana), तस्मिन् when he (Rama), अनागच्छति does not come/arrive, लोक-नाथे the lord of the world (i.e., Rama), गर्भस्थ-जन्तोः इव like a fetus in the womb, शल्यकृन्तः the surgeon cutting with a knife/sharp instrument

Anvay: तस्मिन् लोकनाथे अनागच्छति, नूनम् अचिरात् अनार्यः राक्षसेन्द्रः – शल्यकृन्तः गर्भस्थजन्तोः इव – मम अङ्गानि शितैः शस्त्रैः छेत्स्यति।

Simple meaning: “If the lord of the world (Rama) does not come here, surely, the ignoble king of the Rakshasas (Ravana) will soon cut my limbs with sharp weapons. It will be like a surgeon cutting into a fetus still in the womb.

दुःखं बतेदं मम दुःखिताया मासौ चिरायाधिगमिष्यतो द्वौ।

बद्धस्य वध्यस्य तथा निशान्ते राजापराधादिव तस्करस्य॥5.28.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुःखम् sorrow/suffering, बत alas, इदम् this, मम for me, दुःखितायाः who is already sorrowful, मासौ two months, चिराय long (a long time), अधिगमिष्यतः will be reached/will be completed, द्वौ two, बद्धस्य for one who is imprisoned, वध्यस्य condemned to die, तथा likewise, निशान्ते at the end of the night, राज-अपराधात् इव like due to offense against the king, तस्करस्य of a thief

Anvay: बत! दुःखितायाः मम इदं दुःखं – राजापराधात् बद्धस्य तस्करस्य निशान्ते वध्यस्य इव – द्वौ मासौ चिराय अधिगमिष्यतः।

Simple meaning: “Alas! For me, who is already suffering, this sorrow will stretch on for two long months.  It is like the agony of a thief, condemned to death for an offense against the king, waiting through the night in chains for his execution at dawn.

हा राम हा लक्ष्मण हा सुमित्रे हा राममातः सह मे जनन्या।

एषा विपद्याम्यहमल्पभाग्या महार्णवे नौरिव मूढवाता॥5.28.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: हा राम! O Rama!, हा लक्ष्मण! O Lakshmana!, हा सुमित्रे! O Sumitra! , हा राममातः! O mother of Rama, सह मे जनन्या along with my own mother, एषा this person (Sita), विपद्यामि am perishing/am getting destroyed, अहम् I, अल्प-भाग्या unfortunate/ill-fated, महार्णवे in the great ocean, नौः इव like a boat, मूढ-वाता caught in a storm/driven by wild winds

Anvay: हा राम! हा लक्ष्मण! हा सुमित्रे! हा राममातः! मे जनन्या सह। अल्पभाग्या एषा अहं – महार्णवे मूढवाता नौः इव – विपद्यामि।

Simple meaning: “O Rama! O Lakshmana! O Sumitra! O mother of Rama, along with my own mother! I, this unfortunate woman, am perishing— like a boat in the vast ocean, tossed by a violent storm.

तरस्विनौ धारयता मृगस्य सत्त्वेन रूपं मनुजेन्द्रपुत्रौ।

नूनं विशस्तौ मम कारणात्तौ सिंहर्षभौ द्वाविव वैद्युतेन॥5.28.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: तरस्विनौ swift/valiant ones (full of energy and courage), धारयता having assumed, मृगस्य of a deer, सत्त्वेन by a being, रूपम् the form, मनुजेन्द्र-पुत्रौ the sons of the king of men (Rama and Lakshmana), नूनम् surely, विशस्तौ have been slain, मम कारणात् because of me/for my sake, तौ those two (Rama and Lakshmana), सिंहर्षभौ two lion-like bulls (metaphor for powerful warriors), द्वौ इव like two, वैद्युतेन by lightning (i.e., like being struck by lightning)

सत्त्व - A living or sentient being, animal, beast; src. Kosha. Ashtadhyayi

Anvay: मम कारणात् तरस्विनौ तौ मनुजेन्द्रपुत्रौ, मृगस्य रूपं धारयता सत्त्वेन – द्वौ सिंहर्षभौ वैद्युतेन इव – नूनं विशस्तौ।

Simple meaning: “Because of me, the two valiant sons of the king of men, must surely have been slain by the being who took on the form of a deer — like two lion-like bulls struck down by lightning.

नूनं स कालो मृगरूपधारी मामल्पभाग्यां लुलुभे तदानीम्।

यत्रार्यपुत्रं विससर्ज मूढा रामानुजं लक्ष्मणपूर्वजं च॥5.28.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: नूनम् surely/indeed, सः कालः that Death/Time (as a destructive force), मृग-रूप-धारी having taken the form of a deer, माम् me, अल्प-भाग्याम् unfortunate one/of little fortune, लुलुभे tempted/deluded/enticed, तदानीम् at that moment/then, यत्र due to which/whereupon, आर्यपुत्रम् the noble prince (Rama), विससर्ज I sent away/let go, मूढा foolish (I)/deluded, राम-अनुजम् the younger brother of Rama (Lakshmana), लक्ष्मण-पूर्वजम् च and the elder brother of Lakshmana (Rama)

Anvay: नूनं मृगरूपधारी सः कालः तदानीं माम् अल्पभाग्यां लुलुभे, यत्र मूढा अहम् आर्यपुत्रं लक्ष्मणपूर्वजं रामानुजं च विससर्ज।

Simple meaning: “Surely it was Death itself, disguised in the form of a deer, that enticed me, the unfortunate one, at that moment. Deluded, I sent away both the noble prince Rama— Lakshmana's elder brother—and also Lakshmana, Rama’s younger brother.

हा राम सत्यव्रत दीर्घबाहो हा पूर्णचन्द्रप्रतिमानवक्त्र।

हा जीवलोकस्य हितः प्रियश्च वध्यां न मां वेत्सि हि राक्षसानाम्॥5.28.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: हा राम O Rama!, सत्यव्रत O you who are devoted to truth/one firm in vows of truth, दीर्घ-बाहो O long-armed one, हा पूर्ण-चन्द्र-प्रतिमान-वक्त्र O one whose face resembles the full moon, हा O!, जीव-लोकस्य of the world of living beings, हितः the benefactor/the one who does good, प्रियः च and beloved, वध्याम् one who is to be killed/condemned, न not, माम् me, वेत्सि you know/are aware, हि surely/indeed, राक्षसानाम् of the rakshasas

Anvay: हा राम! सत्यव्रत! दीर्घबाहो! पूर्णचन्द्रप्रतिमानवक्त्र! हा! जीवलोकस्य हितः, प्रियः च, राक्षसानां मां वध्यां न हि वेत्सि।

Simple meaning: “O Rama! O you who are steadfast in truth, O long-armed one! O you whose face resembles the full moon! O you who are the well-wisher and beloved of all living beings— you do not know that I am doomed to die at the hands of the rakshasas.

अनन्यदैवत्वमियं क्षमा च भूमौ च शय्या नियमश्च धर्मे।

पतिव्रतात्वं विफलं ममेदं कृतं कृतघ्नेष्विव मानुषाणाम्॥5.28.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: अनन्य-दैवत्वम् devotion to no other deity (exclusive devotion to one’s husband as god), इयम्  this (i.e., my), क्षमा च and forbearance/patience, भूमौ च शय्या sleeping on the ground, नियमः च धर्मे and discipline in righteousness (following strict religious conduct), पतिव्रतात्वम् chastity/vow of devotion to husband, विफलम् fruitless/in vain, मम my, इदम् this, कृतम् done/practiced, कृतघ्नेषु towards the ungrateful, इव like/as though, मानुषाणाम् of men/people

Anvay: मम अनन्यदैवत्वम्, इयं क्षमा च, भूमौ शय्या, धर्मे नियमः च, इदं पतिव्रतात्वं च – मानुषाणां कृतघ्नेषु कृतम् इव – विफलम्।

Simple meaning: “This exclusive devotion to my husband as my only deity, my patience, sleeping on the bare ground, my strict adherence to righteousness, and my vow of chastity— all this has become fruitless, like kindness shown by human beings to ungrateful people.

मोघो हि धर्मश्चरितो मयायं तथैकपत्नीत्वमिदं निरर्थम्।

या त्वां न पश्यामि कृशा विवर्णा हीना त्वया सङ्गमने निराशा॥5.28.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: मोघः fruitless, in vain, हि indeed, धर्मः righteousness/religious conduct, चरितः practiced, मया by me, अयम् this, तथा likewise, एक-पत्नीत्वम् vow of monogamy (i.e., being devoted to only one husband), इदम् this, निरर्थम् meaningless, या who (I), त्वाम् you (Rama), न पश्यामि do not see, कृशा emaciated, विवर्णा discolored/pale, हीना bereft (separated from), त्वया by you, सङ्गमने in meeting (union), निराशा hopeless/despairing

Anvay: हि मया चरितः अयं धर्मः मोघः। तथा इदं एकपत्नीत्वं निरर्थम्। कृशा, विवर्णा, त्वया हीना, सङ्गमने निराशा, या (अहम्) त्वां न पश्यामि।

Simple meaning: “Indeed, the righteous conduct I have followed has been in vain, and likewise, this vow of monogamy is meaningless. For I, who am emaciated, pale, and separated from you, have lost all hope of ever being united with you, and I do not see you.

पितुर्निदेशं नियमेन कृत्वा वनान्निवृत्तश्चरितव्रतश्च।

स्त्रीभिस्तु मन्ये विपुलेक्षणाभिस्त्वं रंस्यसे वीतभयः कृतार्थः॥5.28.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: पितुः of the father, निदेशम् command, order, नियमेन with discipline/strictly, कृत्वा having carried out, वनात् from the forest, निवृत्तः returned, चरित-व्रतः च having completed your vow, स्त्रीभिः with women, तु but/however, मन्ये I think/I believe, विपुल-इक्षणाभिः large-eyed (beautiful-eyed) women, त्वम् you, रंस्यसे will enjoy/take delight, वीत-भयः free from fear, कृत-अर्थः having achieved your purpose

Anvay: त्वं नियमेन पितुः निदेशं कृत्वा चरितव्रतः, वनात् निवृत्तः च, वीतभयः कृतार्थः च, विपुलेक्षणाभिः स्त्रीभिः रंस्यसे, (इति) मन्ये।

Simple meaning: “Having strictly followed your father's command, and completed your vows, you will return from the forest. Now, being free from fear and having fulfilled your mission, I believe, you will take pleasure in the company of large-eyed women.

अहं तु राम त्वयि जातकामा चिरं विनाशाय निबद्धभावा।

मोघं चरित्वाथ तपो व्रतञ्च त्यक्ष्यामि धिग्जीवितमल्पभाग्याम्॥5.28.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: अहम् तु but I, राम O Rama, त्वयि in you, जात-कामा one whose desire/love was born (for you), चिरम् for a long time, विनाशाय for destruction/in vain/to no avail, निबद्ध-भावा firmly attached in heart/emotionally bound, मोघम् in vain/fruitless, चरित्वा having practiced, अथ and, तपः penance, व्रतम् च and vows/religious observance, त्यक्ष्यामि I will abandon/give up, धिक्-जीवितम् fie upon life/curse this life, अल्प-भाग्याम् (me) of little fortune/ill-fated

Anvay: राम! अहं तु त्वयि जातकामा, चिरं विनाशाय निबद्धभावा। अथ तपः व्रतं च मोघं चरित्वा, जीवितं त्यक्ष्यामि, अल्पभाग्यां धिक्।

Simple meaning: “O Rama! I, who have developed love for you, and have remained emotionally bound to you for so long — but all for ruin — having practiced penance and vows in vain, shall now abandon this wretched life. Fie upon (this) life of mine, so ill-fated!

सा जीवितं क्षिप्रमहं त्यजेयं विषेण शस्त्रेण शितेन वापि।

विषस्य दाता न हि मेऽस्ति कश्चिच्छस्त्रस्य वा वेश्मनि राक्षसस्य॥5.28.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा thus/in this state (referring to herself), जीवितम् life, क्षिप्रम् quickly/at once, अहम् I, त्यजेयम् would give up/abandon, विषेण by poison, शस्त्रेण by weapon, शितेन sharp/keen-edged, वा अपि or even, विषस्य of poison, दाता giver, न हि indeed not/there is none, मे for me, अस्ति there is, कश्चित् anyone, शस्त्रस्य of a weapon, वा or, वेश्मनि in the house/abode, राक्षसस्य of the rakshasa (Ravana)

Anvay: सा अहं जीवितं क्षिप्रं विषेण शितेन शस्त्रेण वा अपि त्यजेयम्। विषस्य शस्त्रस्य वा दाता मे राक्षसस्य वेश्मनि कश्चित् न हि अस्ति।

Simple meaning: “Thus, I would quickly give up this life — either by poison or by a sharp weapon. But there is no one in the house of this Rakshasa (Ravana) who would give me poison or a weapon.”

इतीव देवी बहुधा विलप्य सर्वात्मना राममनुस्मरन्ती।

प्रवेपमाना परिशुष्कवक्त्रा नगोत्तमं पुष्पितमाससाद॥5.28.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति इव thus indeed/in this manner, देवी the noble lady (Sita), बहुधा in many ways/repeatedly, विलप्य lamenting/weeping aloud, सर्व-आत्मना with her whole being/wholeheartedly, रामम् अनुस्मरन्ती constantly thinking of Rama, प्रवेपमाना trembling, परिशुष्क-वक्त्रा with a dried-up mouth/parched lips, नगोत्तमम् a great tree, पुष्पितम् in full bloom/flowering, आससाद she approached/came near

Anvay: इति इव देवी बहुधा विलप्य, सर्वात्मना रामम् अनुस्मरन्ती, प्रवेपमाना, परिशुष्कवक्त्रा पुष्पितं नगोत्तमम् आससाद।

Simple meaning: Thus lamenting in many ways, the noble lady, constantly thinking of Rama with all her heart, trembling and with a parched mouth, approached a large flowering tree.

सा शोकतप्ता बहुधा विचिन्त्य सीताऽथ वेण्युद्ग्रथनं गृहीत्वा।

उद्बुध्य वेण्युद्ग्रथनेन शीघ्रमहं गमिष्यामि यमस्य मूलम्॥5.28.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Sita), शोक-तप्ता scorched by grief/tormented by sorrow, बहुधा विचिन्त्य having considered many things/deliberated deeply, सीता अथ then Sita, वेणि-उद्ग्रथनम् the fastening of her braid/the knot of her braid, गृहीत्वा having taken hold of/seizing, उद्बुध्य rising up/lifting herself, वेणि-उद्ग्रथनेन with the knot of her braid/using her hair-knot, शीघ्रम् quickly, अहम् I, गमिष्यामि shall go, यमस्य मूलम् to the root of Yama (i.e., to the abode of Yama, the god of death)

Anvay: शोकतप्ता सा सीता बहुधा विचिन्त्य, अथ वेण्युद्ग्रथनं गृहीत्वा उद्बुध्य, वेण्युद्ग्रथनेन शीघ्रम् अहं यमस्य मूलं गमिष्यामि।

Simple meaning: Tormented by sorrow and after considering many things, Sita then took hold of the knot of her braid. Rising up, she resolved: "Quickly, I shall go to the abode of Yama (i.e., end my life) using this hair-knot."

उपस्थिता सा मृदुसर्वगात्री शाखां गृहत्वाऽध नगस्य तस्य।

तस्यास्तु रामं प्रविचिन्तयन्त्या रामानुजं स्वं च कुलं शुभाङ्ग्याः॥5.28.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: उपस्थिता approached/stood near, सा she (Sita), मृदु-सर्व-गात्री tender in all her limbs (having a soft, delicate body), शाखाम् a branch, गृहीत्वा having taken hold of, अध नगस्य तस्य at the base of that tree/below that tree, तस्याः तु but of that (Sita), रामम् Rama, प्रविचिन्तयन्त्याः while deeply contemplating/thinking over and over, रामानुजम् Lakshmana (Rama’s younger brother), स्वम् च कुलम् and her own family, शुभ-अङ्ग्याः of the auspicious-limbed one (Sita, whose body is considered auspicious or graceful)

Anvay: मृदुसर्वगात्री सा तस्य नगस्य अध शाखां गृहीत्वा उपस्थिता। रामं रामानुजं स्वं च कुलं प्रविचिन्तयन्त्याः शुभाङ्ग्याः तस्याः (एवम् आस्थितायाः)...

Simple meaning: That delicate-limbed lady (Sita), having approached the base of that tree, took hold of a branch and stood there. But even as she did so, she kept deeply contemplating upon Rama, Lakshmana, and her own noble family.

तस्या विशोकानि तथा बहूनि धैर्यार्जितानि प्रवराणि लोके।

प्रादुर्निमित्तानि तदा बभूवुः पुरापि सिद्धान्युपलक्षितानि॥5.28.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्याः for her (i.e., for Sita), विशोकानि indicative of relief from sorrow/sorrow-dispelling, तथा likewise/also, बहूनि many, धैर्य-अर्जितानि restoring her courage, प्रवराणि excellent/auspicious, लोके in the world, प्रादुः manifested/appeared, निमित्तानि omens/signs, तदा then (at that moment), बभूवुः came into being/occurred, पुरा अपि even in the past, सिद्धानि well-known/proven/validated, उपलक्षितानि perceived/noticed/observed

Anvay: तस्याः (सीतायाः) विशोकानि, धैर्यार्जितानि, प्रवराणि बहूनि निमित्तानि तदा लोके प्रादुः बभूवुः, (यानि) निमित्तानि पुरा अपि सिद्धानि उपलक्षितानि।

Simple meaning: Then, for Sita, many auspicious and sorrow-dispelling omens appeared which gave her courage — omens that had in the past proven true and been recognized as such.

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे अष्टाविंशः सर्गः॥28॥

Thus ends the twenty-eighth sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
