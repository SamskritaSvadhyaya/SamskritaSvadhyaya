श्री शुक उवाच

अथ विज्ञाय भगवान् सर्वात्मा सर्वदर्शनः ।

सैरन्ध्र्याः कामतप्तायाः प्रियमिच्छन् गृहं ययौ ॥1.48.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अथ then, विज्ञाय knowing, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, सर्व-आत्मा the soul of all existence, सर्व-दर्शनः the all-seeing witness, सैरन्ध्र्याः of the maidservant Trivakra (the hunchbacked maidservant), काम-तप्तायाः who was tormented by desire, प्रियम् satisfaction/pleasure, इच्छन् desiring, गृहम् to the house, ययौ went

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच- अथ भगवान् सर्वात्मा सर्वदर्शनः कामतप्तायाः सैरन्ध्र्याः प्रियम् इच्छन् (तस्याः) गृहं ययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अथ Then, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, सर्वात्मा the soul of all existence, सर्वदर्शनः the all-seeing witness, कामतप्तायाः who was tormented by desire, सैरन्ध्र्याः of the maidservant Trivakra, प्रियम् the pleasure, इच्छन् desiring, तस्याः her, गृहं house, ययौ went.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Then, the Supreme Lord Krishna, who is the all-seeing witness and the soul of all existence, desired to satisfy the longing of Trivakra (the hunchbacked maidservant) who was tormented by desire for Him, and thus proceeded to her house.

महार्होपस्करैराढ्यं कामोपायोपबृहितम् ।

मुक्तादामपताकाभिर्वितानशयनासनैः।

धूपैः सुरभिभिर्दीपैः स्रग्गन्धैरपि मण्डितम् ॥10.48.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: महार्ह-उपस्करैः with costly furnishings/appliances, आढ्यम् opulent/richly decorated, काम-उपाय-उपबृंहितम् enhanced with various means of sensual enjoyment, मुक्ता-दाम-पताकाभिः with strings of pearls and banners, वितान-शयन-आसनैः with canopies, comfortable beds, and sitting couches, धूपैः with incense, सुरभिभिः fragrant, दीपैः with lamps, स्रग-गन्धैः with flower garlands and aromatic pastes, अपि also, मण्डितम् adorned

Anvaya: (तत् गृहं) महार्होपस्करैः आढ्यं कामोपायोपबृंहितं मुक्तादामपताकाभिः वितानशयनासनैः सुरभिभिः धूपैः दीपैः स्रग्गन्धैः अपि मण्डितम् (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (तत् गृहं) That house, महार्होपस्करैः with costly furnishings, आढ्यं opulent, कामोपायोपबृंहितं enhanced with means of enjoyment, मुक्तादामपताकाभिः with strings of pearls and banners, वितानशयनासनैः with canopies, beds, and seats, सुरभिभिः with fragrant, धूपैः incense, दीपैः lamps, स्रग्गन्धैः with flower garlands and aromatic pastes, अपि also, मण्डितम् adorned, (आसीत्) was.

Simple Meaning: That house was highly opulent, richly filled with costly furnishings and enhanced with every means of sensual comfort. It was beautifully adorned with strings of pearls, decorative banners, elegant canopies, luxurious beds and couches, as well as fragrant incense, glowing lamps, fresh flower garlands, and aromatic pastes.

गृहं तमायान्तमवेक्ष्य साऽऽसनात् सद्यः समुत्थाय हि जातसम्भ्रमा ।

यथोपसङ्गम्य सखीभिरच्युतं सभाजयामास सदासनादिभिः ॥10.48.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: गृहम् to the house, तम् Him (Krishna), आयान्तम् arriving, अवेक्ष्य seeing, सा she (Trivakra), आसनात् from her seat, सद्यः immediately, समुत्थाय rising up, हि indeed, जात-सम्भ्रमा filled with respectful flurry/excitement, यथा-उपसङ्गम्य respectfully approaching, सखीभिः along with her female friends, अच्युतम् the infallible Lord, सभाजयामास honored, सत्-आसन-आदिभिः with an excellent seat and other presentations

Anvaya: तं गृहम् आयान्तम् अवेक्ष्य जातसम्भ्रमा सा सद्यः आसनात् समुत्थाय हि सखीभिः (सह) यथोपसङ्गम्य अच्युतं सदासनादिभिः सभाजयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तं Him, गृहम् to the house, आयान्तम् arriving, अवेक्ष्य seeing, जातसम्भ्रमा filled with excitement, सा she, सद्यः immediately, आसनात् from her seat, समुत्थाय rising up, हि indeed, सखीभिः (सह) along with her friends, यथोपसङ्गम्य respectfully approaching, अच्युतं the infallible Lord, सदासनादिभिः with an excellent seat and other items, सभाजयामास honored.

Simple Meaning: Seeing the infallible Lord Krishna arriving at her home, Trivakra was overcome with respectful excitement. She immediately rose from her seat, approached Him respectfully along with her female friends, and honored Him by offering a luxurious seat and other traditional presentations.

तथोद्धवः साधु तथाभिपूजितो न्यषीददुर्व्यामभिमृश्य चासनम् ।

कृष्णोऽपि तूर्णं शयनं महाधनं विवेश लोकाचरितान्यनुव्रतः ॥10.48.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तथा similarly, उद्धवः Uddhava, साधु properly/respectfully, तथा in that manner, अभिपूजितः being honored, न्यषीदत् sat down, उर्व्याम् on the floor, अभिमृश्य touching, च and, आसनम् the seat, कृष्णः Lord Krishna, अपि also, तूर्णम् quickly, शयनम् the bed, महा-धनम् highly valuable/luxurious, विवेश entered/sat upon, लोक-चरितानि ordinary human behavior/social customs, अनुव्रतः following

Anvaya: तथा साधु अभिपूजितः उद्धवः आसनम् अभिमृश्य च उर्व्यां न्यषीदत्। लोकाचरितानि अनुव्रतः कृष्णः अपि तूर्णं महाधनं शयनं विवेश।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तथा Similarly, साधु properly, अभिपूजितः being honored, उद्धवः Uddhava, आसनम् the offered seat, अभिमृश्य touching, च and, उर्व्यां on the floor, न्यषीदत् sat down. लोकाचरितानि Ordinary human behavior, अनुव्रतः following, कृष्णः Krishna, अपि also, तूर्णं quickly, महाधनं the luxurious, शयनं bed, विवेश entered.

Simple Meaning: Having been respectfully honored in the same manner, Uddhava simply touched the offered seat out of respect and sat down on the floor. Meanwhile, Lord Krishna, following standard human etiquette, quickly went and sat upon the highly luxurious bed.

सा मज्जनालेपदुकूलभूषणस्रग्गन्धताम्बूलसुधासवादिभिः ।

प्रसाधितात्मोपससार माधवं सव्रीडलीलोत्स्मितविभ्रमेक्षितैः ॥10.48.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Trivakra), मज्जन-आलेप-दुकूल-भूषण-स्रग्-गन्ध-ताम्बूल-सुधा-आसव-आदिभिः by bathing, anointing with fragrant pastes, putting on fine silk garments, wearing ornaments, flower garlands, perfumes, chewing betel nut, drinking sweet liquor and so on, प्रसाधित-आत्मा having decorated herself, उपससार approached, माधवम् Lord Madhava, स-व्रीड-लीला-उत्स्मित-विभ्रम-ईक्षितैः with glances filled with modesty, playful gestures, gentle smiles, and feminine charm

Anvaya: मज्जनालेपदुकूलभूषणस्रग्गन्धताम्बूलसुधासवादिभिः प्रसाधितात्मा सा सव्रीडलीलोत्स्मितविभ्रमेक्षितैः माधवम् उपससार।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मज्जनालेपदुकूलभूषणस्रग्गन्धताम्बूलसुधासवादिभिः By bathing, anointing with pastes, fine silk garments, ornaments, garlands, perfumes, betel nut, and sweet liquor, प्रसाधितात्मा having decorated herself, सा she, सव्रीडलीलोत्स्मितविभ्रमेक्षितैः with glances of modesty, playful gestures, smiles, and charm, माधवम् Lord Madhava, उपससार approached.

Simple Meaning: Having beautifully decorated herself by bathing, anointing her body with fragrant pastes, dressing in fine silk garments, and putting on exquisite ornaments, flower garlands, and perfumes—and after chewing betel nut and drinking sweet liquor—Trivakra approached Lord Madhava, casting glances full of modesty, playful charm, gentle smiles, and amorous allure.

आहूय कान्तां नवसङ्गमह्रिया विशङ्कितां कङ्कणभूषिते करे ।

प्रगृह्य शय्यामधिवेश्य रामया रेमेऽनुलेपार्पणपुण्यलेशया ॥10.48.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: आहूय calling, कान्ताम् his beloved (Trivakra), नव-सङ्गम-ह्रिया with the shyness of a new union, विशङ्किताम् hesitant/fearful, कङ्कण-भूषिते decorated with bangles, करे by the hand, प्रगृह्य taking hold of, शय्याम् onto the bed, अधिवेश्य making her sit, रामया with that beautiful woman, रेमे enjoyed/sported, अनुलेप-अर्पण-पुण्य-लेशया who possessed a small particle of piety earned by offering scented paste to the Lord

Anvaya: नवसङ्गमह्रिया विशङ्कितां कान्ताम् आहूय, कङ्कणभूषिते करे प्रगृह्य शय्याम् अधिवेश्य (च) अनुलेपार्पणपुण्यलेशया रामया रेमे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नवसङ्गमह्रिया With the shyness of a new union, विशङ्कितां hesitant, कान्ताम् his beloved, आहूय calling, कङ्कणभूषिते decorated with bangles, करे by the hand, प्रगृह्य taking hold of, शय्याम् onto the bed, अधिवेश्य making her sit, च and, अनुलेपार्पणपुण्यलेशया who possessed a small particle of piety from offering scented paste, रामया with that beautiful woman, रेमे He enjoyed.

Simple Meaning: Calling forward his beloved Trivakra, who was hesitant due to the natural shyness of a first intimate meeting, Krishna took her by her bangle-adorned hand, placed her beside Him on the bed, and sportively enjoyed with that beautiful woman. All of this was the result of her small fraction of piety accrued from once offering fragrant paste to the Lord.

सानङ्गतप्तकुचयोरुरसस्तथाक्ष्णोर्जिघ्रन्त्यनन्तचरणेन रुजो मृजन्ती ।

दोर्भ्यां स्तनान्तरगतं परिरभ्य कान्तमानन्दमूर्तिमजहादतिदीर्घतापम् ॥10.48.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Trivakra), अनङ्ग-तप्त-कुचयोः of her breasts burning with Cupid's desire, उरसः of her chest, तथा similarly, अक्ष्णोः of her eyes, जिघ्रन्ती smelling/inhaling (the fragrance), अनन्त-चरणेन by the feet of the infinite Lord Krishna, रुजः the tormenting pain, मृजन्ती wiping away/dispelling, दोर्भ्याम् with both her arms, स्तन-अन्तर-गतम् placed between her breasts, परिरभ्य embracing, कान्तम् her beloved, आनन्द-मूर्तिम् the embodiment of pure bliss, अजहात् gave up, अति-दीर्घ-तापम् her long-standing distress

Anvaya: सा अनन्तचरणेन अनङ्गतप्तकुचयोः उरसः तथा अक्ष्णोः रुजः मृजन्ती, (तं) जिघ्रन्ती, स्तनन्तरगतं आनन्दमूर्तिं कान्तं दोर्भ्यां परिरभ्य अतिदीर्घतापम् अजहात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सा She, अनन्तचरणेन by the feet of the infinite Lord, अनङ्गतप्तकुचयोः of her breasts burning with desire, उरसः of her chest, तथा similarly, अक्ष्णोः of her eyes, रुजः the pain, मृजन्ती wiping away, (तं) Him, जिघ्रन्ती inhaling His fragrance, स्तनन्तरगतं placed between her breasts, आनन्दमूर्तिं the embodiment of bliss, कान्तं her beloved, दोर्भ्यां with both arms, परिरभ्य embracing, अतिदीर्घतापम् her long-standing distress, अजहात् gave up.

Simple Meaning: By placing the lotus feet of the infinite Lord Krishna upon herself, Trivakra wiped away the agonizing heat of desire burning in her breasts, chest, and eyes. Intoxicated by His divine fragrance, she locked her arms around her beloved—the very embodiment of supreme bliss—and held Him tightly against her chest. In that ecstatic embrace, she completely cast away the heavy, long-standing distress of her desires.

सैवं कैवल्यनाथं तं प्राप्य दुष्प्रापमीश्वरम् ।

अङ्गरागार्पणेनाहो दुर्भगेदमयाचत ॥10.48.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Trivakra), एवम् in this manner, कैवल्य-नाथम् the Lord of liberation, तम् Him, प्राप्य having attained, दुष्प्रापम् very difficult to achieve, ईश्वरम् the Supreme Lord, अङ्ग-राग-अर्पणेन simply by offering scented body paste, अहो oh, दुर्भगा unfortunate, इदम् this, अयाचत begged for

Anvaya: अहो! अङ्गरागार्पणेन दुष्प्रापं तं कैवल्यनाथम् ईश्वरं प्राप्य सा दुर्भगा एवम् इदम् अयाचत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अहो Oh! अङ्गरागार्पणेन by offering body paste, दुष्प्रापं difficult to attain, तं Him, कैवल्यनाथम् the Lord of liberation, ईश्वरं the Supreme Lord, प्राप्य having attained, सा she, दुर्भगा unfortunate, एवम् in this manner, इदम् this, अयाचत begged for.

Simple Meaning: Oh, what an irony! By simply offering scented body paste to the Supreme Lord, Trivakra attained the presence of the ultimate Controller and Lord of liberation, who is otherwise almost impossible to reach. Yet, she used this rare opportunity to beg only for temporary, sensual pleasure. In doing so, despite her immense fortune, she acted like someone deeply unfortunate.

आहोष्यतामिह प्रेष्ठ दिनानि कतिचिन्मया ।

रमस्व नोत्सहे त्यक्तुं सङ्गं तेऽम्बुरुहेक्षण ॥10.48.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: आह (she) said, उष्यताम् please stay, इह here, प्रेष्ठ O dearest one, दिनानि days, कतिचित् a few, मया with me, रमस्व enjoy/sport, न उत्सहे I cannot tolerate/do not have the strength, त्यक्तुम् to give up, सङ्गम् the association, ते your, अम्बुरुह-ईक्षण O lotus-eyed one

Anvaya: प्रेष्ठ! अम्बुरुहेक्षण! मया (सह) इह कतिचित् दिनानि उष्यतां, रमस्व (च)। ते सङ्गं त्यक्तुं न उत्सहे — (इति) आह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रेष्ठ O dearest, अम्बुरुहेक्षण O lotus-eyed one, मया सह with me, इह here, कतिचित् a few, दिनानि days, उष्यतां please stay, च and, रमस्व enjoy. ते Your, सङ्गं company, त्यक्तुं to give up, न उत्सहे I am not able, (इति) आह thus, she said.

Simple Meaning: She said: "O dearest one! O lotus-eyed Lord! Please stay here with me for a few more days and continue to sport and enjoy with me. I am not able to give up your wonderful company."

तस्यै कामवरं दत्त्वा मानयित्वा च मानदः ।

सहोद्धवेन सर्वेशः स्वधामागमदर्चितम् ॥10.48.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्यै unto her (Trivakra), काम-वरम् the boon of fulfilling her material desires, दत्त्वा having granted, मानयित्वा honoring her, च and, मान-दः He who gives honor to others, सह उद्धवेन along with Uddhava, सर्व-ईशः the Lord of all lords, स्व-धाम to His own residence, आगमत् returned, अर्चितम् being worshiped/honored by her

Anvaya: मानदः सर्वेशः तस्यै कामवरं दत्त्वा मानयित्वा च अर्चितं (सन्) उद्धवेन सह स्वधाम आगमत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मानदः He who bestows honor on others, सर्वेशः the Lord of all, तस्यै unto her, कामवरं the boon of her desires, दत्त्वा having granted, मानयित्वा honoring her, च and, अर्चितं being worshiped by her, (सन्) being, उद्धवेन सह along with Uddhava, स्वधाम to His own residence, आगमत् returned.

Simple Meaning: Krishna, the Supreme Controller of all and the bestower of honor upon others, granted Trivakra the boon of fulfilling her desired pleasure and showed her due respect. Then, after being worshiped by her, He returned to His own residence accompanied by Uddhava.

दुराराध्यं समाराध्य विष्णुं सर्वेश्वरेश्वरम् ।

यो वृणीते मनोग्राह्यमसत्त्वात् कुमनीष्यसौ ॥10.48.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुराराध्यम् who is very difficult to worship/please (by those who are not self-controlled), समाराध्य completely worshiping/pleasing, विष्णुम् Lord Vishnu, सर्व-ईश्वर-ईश्वरम् the Lord of all lords, यः who, वृणीते chooses/begs for, मनः-ग्राह्यम् that which is perceived by the mind (sensual pleasure), असत्त्वात् due to being temporary/unreal, कु-मनीषी a person of poor intelligence, असौ such a person

Anvaya: सर्वेश्वरेश्वरं दुराराध्यं विष्णुं समाराध्य यः असत्त्वात् मनोग्राह्यं वृणीते, असौ कुमनीषी (अस्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सर्वेश्वरेश्वरं The Lord of all lords, दुराराध्यं who is difficult to please, विष्णुं Lord Vishnu, समाराध्य after fully worshiping, यः whoever, असत्त्वात् due to its temporary nature, मनोग्राह्यं that which is grasped by the mind (material pleasure), वृणीते begs for, असौ that person, कुमनीषी of poor intelligence, (अस्ति) is.

Simple Meaning: Lord Vishnu, the Supreme Lord of all lords, is exceedingly difficult to satisfy (for those lacking spiritual discipline). After achieving the rare fortune of fully worshiping and pleasing Him, anyone who begs only for temporary, mundane pleasures of the mind and senses is surely a person of poor intelligence.

Simple Meaning: After worshiping them, Akrura bowed his head low in profound reverence. Taking Their feet onto his lap, he gently massaged them, and, bent down with deep humility, he began to speak to Krishna and Balarama.

दिष्ट्या पापो हतः कंसः सानुगो वामिदं कुलम् ।

युवां प्रधानपुरुषौ जगद्धेतू जगन्मयौ ।

Anvaya: युवां प्रधानपुरुषौ जगद्धेतू जगन्मयौ (च स्थः)। भवद्भ्यां विना परम् अपरं च किञ्चित् न अस्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: युवां You two, प्रधानपुरुषौ are material nature and the conscious soul, जगद्धेतू the causes of the universe, जगन्मयौ and embodying the universe. भवद्भ्यां विना Apart from You two, परम् a superior cause, अपरं च or an inferior effect, किञ्चित् anything, न अस्ति does not exist.

Simple Meaning: "You two are the primordial ingredients of creation—both material nature (Pradhana) and the Supreme conscious Spirit (Purusha). You are the ultimate cause of this cosmos, and indeed, the entire universe is pervaded by You. Apart from You, nothing else exists, whether it be a subtle cause (परम्) or a gross, visible effect (अपरम्).

आत्मसृष्टमिदं विश्वमन्वाविश्य स्वशक्तिभिः ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्रह्मन् O Supreme Brahman! स्वशक्तिभिः Through Your own potencies, आत्मसृष्टम् created by Yourself, इदं this, विश्वम् universe, अन्वाविश्य entering within, श्रुतप्रत्यक्षगोचरं which is accessible through hearing and direct perception, (तत्) that, बहुधा in multiple ways, ईयते is perceived.

Simple Meaning: "O Supreme Spirit! After creating this universe through Your own distinct energies, You enter deep within it. Consequently, whatever is perceived in this world—whether through hearing or experienced directly —is seen as manifesting in countless, diverse ways as a reflection of You.

यथा हि भूतेषु चराचरेषु मह्यादयो योनिषु भान्ति नाना ।

एवं भवान् केवल आत्मयोनिष्वात्माऽऽत्मतन्त्रो बहुधा विभाति ॥10.48.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: यथा just as, हि indeed, भूतेषु in living entities, चर-अचरेषु both moving and non-moving, मही-आदयः the elements, beginning with earth, योनिषु in their various sources/forms, भान्ति appear, नाना in diverse ways, एवम् in the same way, भवान् You, केवलः the one non-dual Lord, आत्म-योनिषु within the bodies created by Your own energy, आत्मा the Supreme Soul, आत्म-तन्त्रः self-dependent/independent, बहुधा in many forms, विभाति manifest/appear

Anvaya: यथा हि चराचरेषु भूतेषु योनिषु मह्यादयः नाना भान्ति, एवं केवलः आत्मतन्त्रः भवान् आत्मा आत्मयोनिषु बहुधा विभाति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यथा हि Just as indeed, चराचरेषु in moving and non-moving, भूतेषु living beings, योनिषु who act as physical forms, मह्यादयः the material elements like earth and others, नाना in various ways, भान्ति appear, एवं in the same way, केवलः the singular, आत्मतन्त्रः independent, भवान् You, आत्मा the Supreme Soul, आत्मयोनिषु within the bodies generated by Your own power, बहुधा in manifold ways, विभाति shine forth.

Simple Meaning: "Just as the primary material elements like earth, water, and fire enter into all moving and non-moving living beings and appear in countless different shapes and forms, similarly, You—the one, singular, completely independent Supreme Soul—enter into the various bodies created by Your own divine energy, appearing to manifest in manifold, diverse ways.

सृजस्यथो लुम्पसि पासि विश्वं रजस्तमः सत्त्वगुणैः स्वशक्तिभिः ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (भवान्) You, स्वशक्तिभिः by Your own potencies, रजस्तमःसत्त्वगुणैः through the modes of passion, ignorance, and goodness, विश्वं the universe, सृजसि create, अथ and further, पासि maintain, (च) and, लुम्पसि destroy, (किन्तु) but, तत्गुणकर्मभिः by the actions and qualities of those modes, न बध्यसे are not bound. ज्ञानात्मनः For the embodiment of pure knowledge, ते for You, बन्धहेतुः a cause of bondage, क्व च वा where possibly, (अस्ति) is there?

Simple Meaning: "Utilizing Your own material energies—manifested as the modes of passion, ignorance, and goodness—You create, maintain, and eventually annihilate this entire universe. Yet, You are never bound by the qualities or actions of these modes. For You, who are the absolute embodiment of pure, eternal knowledge, how could any cause of bondage ever exist?

देहाद्युपाधेरनिरूपितत्वाद् भवो न साक्षान्न भिदाऽऽत्मनः स्यात् ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: देहाद्युपाधेः Because material designations like the body and mind, अनिरूपितत्वात् are ultimately unprovable and unreal, आत्मनः for the soul, साक्षात् directly, भवः birth and death, न does not exist, भिदा च nor division, स्यात् can be. अतः Therefore, तव for You, न बन्धः there is no bondage, न एव मोक्षः and certainly no liberation. बन्धमोक्षौ Conceptions of bondage and liberation, त्वयि regarding You, नः our, निकामः complete, अविवेकः lack of discrimination, स्याताम् would be.

Simple Meaning: "Since material designations like the physical body and subtle mind prove to be illusory and unreal upon ultimate inquiry, the soul itself never truly undergoes birth and death, nor does it suffer any real division. Consequently, for You, there is absolutely no such thing as bondage, and naturally, no such thing as liberation. Any ideas of bondage and liberation applied to You are merely the result of our own profound lack of discrimination.

आनीताः स्वपुरं राज्ञा वसन्त इति शुश्रुम ॥10.48.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: पितरि when their father (Pandu), उपरते passed away, बालाः the young boys (the Pandavas), सह along with, मात्रा their mother (Kunti), सु-दुःखिताः deeply distressed, आनीताः were brought, स्व-पुरम् to his own city (Hastinapura), राज्ञा by the King (Dhritarashtra), वसन्ति are living, इति thus, शुश्रुम we have heard

Anvaya: पितरि उपरते सुदुःखिताः बालाः मात्रा सह राज्ञा स्वपुरम् आनीताः। (तत्र एव) वसन्ति इति (वयं) शुश्रुम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पितरि When their father, उपरते passed away, सुदुःखिताः deeply grieved, बालाः the young children, मात्रा सह along with their mother, राज्ञा by the King, स्वपुरम् to his own capital, आनीताः were brought. (तत्र एव) There indeed, वसन्ति they are residing, इति thus, (वयं) we, शुश्रुम have heard.

Simple Meaning: "We have heard that after their father, King Pandu, passed away, the young Pandavas and their deeply grieved mother, Kunti, were brought to Hastinapura by King Dhritarashtra, and they are currently living there under his care.

तेषु राजाम्बिकापुत्रो भ्रातृपुत्रेषु दीनधीः ।

समो न वर्तते नूनं दुष्पुत्रवशगोऽन्धदृक् ॥10.48.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: तेषु toward them, राजा the King, अम्बिका-पुत्रः the son of Ambika (Dhritarashtra), भ्रातृ-पुत्रेषु toward his brother's sons (the Pandavas), दीन-धीः weak-minded/of small intelligence, समः impartial/equally, न not, वर्तते behaves/acts, नूनम् surely, दुष्ट-पुत्र-वश-गः completely under the control of his wicked sons (like Duryodhana), अन्ध-दृक् whose eyes are blind (physically and spiritually)

Anvaya: दीनधीः अन्धदृक् राजा अम्बिकापुत्रः दुष्पुत्रवशगः (सन्) तेषु भ्रातृपुत्रेषु नूनं समः न वर्तते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दीनधीः The weak-minded, अन्धदृक् physically and spiritually blind, राजा King, अम्बिकापुत्रः Dhritarashtra (son of Ambika), दुष्पुत्रवशगः being under the control of his wicked sons, तेषु toward those, भ्रातृपुत्रेषु sons of his brother, नूनं surely, समः impartially, न वर्तते does not act.

Simple Meaning: "That weak-minded King Dhritarashtra, the son of Ambika, is blind both physically and in his spiritual judgment. Because he is completely under the sway of his wicked sons led by Duryodhana, he surely does not treat his orphaned nephews with fairness or impartiality.

गच्छ जानीहि तद्वृत्तमधुना साध्वसाधु वा ।

विज्ञाय तद् विधास्यामो यथा शं सुहृदां भवेत् ॥10.48.35॥

Word-by-word meaning: गच्छ please go, जानीहि find out/ascertain, तत्-वृत्तम् their situation/condition, अधुना now, साधु good, असाधु bad, वा or, विज्ञाय having understood, तत् that, विधास्यामः we shall arrange/take necessary action, यथा so that, शम् happiness/welfare, सुहृदाम् of our dear friends, भवेत् may be

Anvaya: (त्वं) गच्छ। अधुना तत् साधु असाधु वा वृत्तं जानीहि। तत् विज्ञाय (वयं तथा) विधास्यामः यथा सुहृदां शं भवेत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (त्वं) गच्छ You go. अधुना Now, तत् that, साधु good, असाधु bad, वा or, वृत्तं state of affairs, जानीहि learn. तत् That, विज्ञाय having thoroughly understood, (वयं) we, (तथा) in such a way, विधास्यामः shall act, यथा so that, सुहृदां for our friends, शं happiness, भवेत् may be.

Simple Meaning: "Go there now and find out exactly what their situation is—whether they are being treated well or poorly. Once we clearly understand the true state of affairs, we will take whatever action is necessary to ensure the complete safety and welfare of our dear friends.”

इत्यक्रूरं समादिश्य भगवान् हरिरीश्वरः ।

सङ्कर्षणोद्धवाभ्यां वै ततः स्वभवनं ययौ ॥10.48.36॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, अक्रूरम् to Akrura, समादिश्य fully instructing, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, हरिः Hari, ईश्वरः the ultimate controller, सङ्कर्षण-उद्धवाभ्याम् with Sankarshana (Balarama) and Uddhava, वै indeed, ततः then, स्व-भवनम् to His own palace, ययौ went

Anvaya: इति अक्रूरं समादिश्य ईश्वरः भगवान् हरिः सङ्कर्षणोद्धवाभ्यां (सह) वै ततः स्वभवनं ययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति Thus, अक्रूरं Akrura, समादिश्य having fully instructed, ईश्वरः the controller, भगवान् हरिः the Supreme Lord Hari, सङ्कर्षणोद्धवाभ्यां along with Sankarshana and Uddhava, वै indeed, ततः then, स्वभवनं to His own residence, ययौ went.

Simple Meaning: Having thus fully instructed Akrura, the Supreme Lord Hari, the ultimate ruler of all, then returned to His own palace accompanied by Lord Balarama and Uddhava.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे पूर्वार्धे “कृष्णस्य कुब्जाक्रूरगृहगमनं नाम​” अष्टचत्वारिंशः अध्यायः ॥48॥

Thus ends the forty-eighth discourse, named “Krishna pays a visit to the homes of Kubja and Akrura” in the first half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
