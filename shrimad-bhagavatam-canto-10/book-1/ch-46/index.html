Shrimad Bhagavatam - Book 1

Canto 10

Chapter 46

Krishna relieves the agony of Nanda (by sending Uddhava to Vraja with a message of endearment)

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

नन्दशोकापनयनं नाम षट्चत्वारिंशः अध्यायः

(49 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच

वृष्णीनां प्रवरो मन्त्री कृष्णस्य दयितः सखा ।

शिष्यो बृहस्पतेः साक्षादुद्धवो बुद्धिसत्तमः ॥10.46.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, वृष्णीनाम् of the Vrishni dynasty, प्रवरः the chief, मन्त्री counselor, कृष्णस्य of Lord Krishna, दयितः beloved, सखा friend, शिष्यः disciple, बृहस्पतेः of Brihaspati, साक्षात् directly, उद्धवः Uddhava, बुद्धि-सत्तमः the most intelligent, extraordinary counselor

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच  वृष्णीनां प्रवरः मन्त्री, कृष्णस्य दयितः सखा, साक्षात् बृहस्पतेः शिष्यः बुद्धिसत्तमः उद्धवः (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, वृष्णीनां प्रवरः मन्त्री Of the Vrishni clan, the most prominent counselor, कृष्णस्य दयितः सखा of Lord Krishna, a beloved friend, साक्षात् बृहस्पतेः शिष्यः directly the disciple of Brihaspati, बुद्धिसत्तमः the topmost possessor of intelligence, उद्धवः Uddhava (was present).

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King, the Vrishni dynasty had a most prominent counselor named Uddhava. He was a beloved friend of Lord Krishna, a direct disciple of Brihaspati, and possessed the topmost spiritual and intellectual acumen.

तमाह भगवान् प्रेष्ठं भक्तमेकान्तिनं क्वचित् ।

गृहीत्वा पाणिना पाणिं प्रपन्नार्तिहरो हरिः ॥10.46.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् to him (Uddhava), आह said, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, प्रेष्ठम् most dear, भक्तम् devotee, एकान्तिनम् unalloyed/completely pure, क्वचित् at a certain time, गृहीत्वा taking, पाणिना with His hand, पाणिम् the hand, प्रपन्न-आर्ति-हरः who removes the distress of those who surrender, हरिः Lord Hari

Anvaya: प्रपन्नार्तिहरः भगवान् हरिः क्वचित् एकान्तिनं प्रेष्ठं भक्तं तं पाणिना पाणिं गृहीत्वा आह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रपन्नार्तिहरः Who removes the suffering of those who surrender, भगवान् हरिः the Supreme Lord Hari, क्वचित् at one time, एकान्तिनं completely pure, प्रेष्ठं भक्तं most dear devotee, तं him (Uddhava), पाणिना with His own hand, पाणिं the hand (of Uddhava), गृहीत्वा holding, आह spoke.

Simple Meaning: On one occasion, the Supreme Lord Hari, who dispels the distress of all surrendered souls, took his most dear, unalloyed devotee Uddhava by the hand and spoke to him.

गच्छोद्धव व्रजं सौम्य पित्रोर्नौ प्रीतिमावह ।

गोपीनां मद्वियोगाधिं मत्सन्देशैर्विमोचय ॥10.46.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: गच्छ go, उद्धव O Uddhava, व्रजम् to Vraja, सौम्य O gentle one, पित्रोः to the parents, नौ of both of us (Krishna and Balarama), प्रीतिम् pleasure, आवह bring, गोपीनाम् of the gopis, मत्-वियोग-आधिम् the mental agony of separation from me, मत्-सन्देशैः by my messages, विमोचय please relieve

आधि   आधिः [आधीयते स्थाप्यते प्रतीकाराय मनोऽनेन, आ-धाकि P. III. 3. 92 , connected with आध्यै in some senses] 1 Mental pain or anguish, agony, anxiety, (opp. व्याधि which is bodily pain) ,  src. Kosha. Ashtadhyayi

Anvaya: सौम्य उद्धव! व्रजं गच्छ। नौ पित्रोः प्रीतिम् आवह। मत्सन्देशैः गोपीनां मद्वियोगाधिं विमोचय।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सौम्य उद्धव O gentle Uddhava, व्रजं गच्छ go to Vraja. नौ पित्रोः To our parents, प्रीतिम् pleasure, आवह bring. मत्सन्देशैः By my messages, गोपीनां of the gopis, मद्वियोगाधिं the mental pain due to separation from me, विमोचय relieve.

Simple Meaning: "O gentle Uddhava, please go to Vraja. Bring joy to our parents, Nanda and Yashoda, and relieve the gopis of their intense mental agony born of separation from me by delivering my messages to them.

ता मन्मनस्का मत्प्राणा मदर्थे त्यक्तदैहिकाः ।

मामेव दयितं प्रेष्ठमात्मानं मनसा गताः ।

ये त्यक्तलोकधर्माश्च मदर्थे तान् बिभर्म्यहम् ॥10.46.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताः they (the gopis), मत्-मनस्काः whose minds are fully absorbed in me, मत्-प्राणाः whose life-airs are dedicated to me, मत्-अर्थे for my sake, त्यक्त-दैहिकाः who have abandoned all bodily and material attachments, माम् me, एव certainly, दयितम् lover, प्रेष्ठम् most dear, आत्मानम् the Supreme Soul, मनसा with their minds, गताः have attained, ये those who, त्यक्त-लोक-धर्माः च and who have abandoned worldly duties and conventional religion, मत्-अर्थे for my sake, तान् them, बिभर्मि maintain, अहम् I

Anvaya: ताः मन्मनस्काः मत्प्राणाः मदर्थे त्यक्तदैहिकाः (सन्त्यः) माम् एव दयितं प्रेष्ठम् आत्मानं मनसा गताः। ये मदर्थे त्यक्तलोकधर्माः च (भवन्ति) तान् अहं बिभर्मि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ताः They (the gopis), मन्मनस्काः with minds fully fixed on me, मत्प्राणाः with their very lives dedicated to me, मदर्थे for my sake, त्यक्तदैहिकाः having given up care for their physical bodies, माम् एव me alone, दयितं as their beloved, प्रेष्ठम् most dear, आत्मानं Supreme Soul, मनसा with their minds, गताः have surrendered to and attained. ये Those who, मदर्थे for my sake, त्यक्तलोकधर्माः च and have abandoned worldly conventions and social duties, तान् them, अहं I, बिभर्मि completely maintain and carry.

Simple Meaning: "The gopis have fully absorbed their minds in me, dedicated their very lives to me, and abandoned all concern for their physical bodies for my sake. In their minds, they have completely attained me as their ultimate beloved, dearest friend, and Supreme Soul. I personally maintain, carry, and protect all those who renounce conventional social duties and worldly religion solely for my sake.

मयि ताः प्रेयसां प्रेष्ठे दूरस्थे गोकुलस्त्रियः ।

स्मरन्त्योऽङ्ग विमुह्यन्ति विरहौत्कण्ठ्यविह्वलाः ॥10.46.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: मयि in me, ताः they, प्रेयसाम् of all dear objects, प्रेष्ठे the most dear, दूर-स्थे being far away, गोकुल-स्त्रियः the women of Gokula, स्मरन्त्यः remembering, अङ्ग O dear friend (Uddhava), विमुह्यन्ति become bewildered, विरह-औत्कण्ठ्य-विह्वलाः overwhelmed by the anxiety of separation

Anvaya: अङ्ग! प्रेयसां प्रेष्ठे मयि दूरस्थे (सति) ताः गोकुलस्त्रियः (मां) स्मरन्त्यः विरहौत्कण्ठ्यविह्वलाः विमुह्यन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अङ्ग O dear friend Uddhava, प्रेयसां प्रेष्ठे of all dear things the most dear, मयि in me, दूरस्थे being situated far away, ताः गोकुलस्त्रियः those women of Gokula, स्मरन्त्यः remembering me, विरहौत्कण्ठ्यविह्वलाः overwhelmed by the intense longing of separation, विमुह्यन्ति become completely stunned and bewildered.

Simple Meaning: "O dear Uddhava, because I the absolute dearest object of their love am staying so far away, those women of Gokula constantly remember me. Overwhelmed by the agonizing longing of separation, they fall into complete mental bewilderment.

धारन्त्यतिकृच्छ्रेण प्रायः प्राणान् कथञ्चन ।

प्रत्यागमनसन्देशैर्बल्लव्यो मे मदात्मिकाः ॥10.46.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: धारन्ति they maintain, अति-कृच्छ्रेण with great difficulty, प्रायः practically, प्राणान् their life-airs, कथञ्चन somehow or other, प्रत्यागमन-सन्देशैः by the promises of my return, बल्लव्यः the cowherd girls (gopis), मे my, मत्-आत्मिकाः whose minds and hearts are completely merged in me

Anvaya: मदात्मिकाः मे बल्लव्यः प्रत्यागमनसन्देशैः प्रायः कथञ्चन अतिकृच्छ्रेण प्राणान् धारन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मदात्मिकाः Whose minds are fully merged in me, मे बल्लव्यः my gopis, प्रत्यागमनसन्देशैः by the promises of my return, प्रायः practically, कथञ्चन somehow or other, अतिकृच्छ्रेण with supreme difficulty, प्राणान् their life-airs, धारन्ति are maintaining.

Simple Meaning: "My gopis have completely merged their minds and hearts in me. They are practically sustaining their lives with the greatest difficulty, somehow or other, relying solely on my promises that I will eventually return to them.”

श्री शुक उवाच

इत्युक्त उद्धवो राजन् संदेशं भर्तुरादृतः।

आदाय रथमारुह्य प्रययौ नन्दगोकुलम् ॥10.46.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति thus, उक्तः addressed, उद्धवः Uddhava, राजन् O King (Parikshit), संदेशम् the message, भर्तुः of his master, आदृतः respectfully, आदाय taking, रथम् the chariot, आरुह्य mounting, प्रययौ departed for, नन्द-गोकुलम् the cowherd pasture of Nanda (Vrindavan)

Anvaya: राजन्! इति उक्तः उद्धवः भर्तुः संदेशं आदृतः आदाय रथम् आरुह्य नन्दगोकुलं प्रययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, राजन् O King, इति उक्तः thus addressed, उद्धवः Uddhava, भर्तुः संदेशं the message of his master, आदृतः respectfully, आदाय taking, रथम् आरुह्य mounting the chariot, नन्दगोकुलं for the domain of Nanda, प्रययौ departed.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said, O King Parikshit, having been instructed in this manner, Uddhava respectfully accepted his master's message, mounted his chariot, and departed for the cowherd village of Nanda.

प्राप्तो नन्दव्रजं श्रीमान् निम्लोचति विभावसौ ।

छन्नयानः प्रविशतां पशूनां खुररेणुभिः ॥10.46.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्राप्तः arrived, नन्द-व्रजम् at the cowherd pasture of Nanda, श्रीमान् the glorious/fortunate one (Uddhava), निम्लोचति was setting, विभावसौ when the sun, छन्न-यानः whose chariot was hidden, प्रविशताम् of those entering, पशूनाम् of the domestic animals, खुर-रेणुभिः by the dust raised by the hooves

Anvaya: विभावसौ निम्लोचति (सति) श्रीमान् (उद्धवः) प्रविशतां पशूनां खुररेणुभिः छन्नयानः (सन्) नन्दव्रजं प्राप्तः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विभावसौ निम्लोचति When the sun was setting, श्रीमान् the glorious Uddhava, प्रविशतां of the entering, पशूनां of the cows and animals, खुररेणुभिः by the dust from the hooves, छन्नयानः with his chariot concealed, नन्दव्रजं at the pasture of Nanda, प्राप्तः arrived.

Simple Meaning: The glorious Uddhava arrived at Nanda’s pasture just as the sun was setting. His chariot was completely enveloped and hidden from view by the thick cloud of dust raised by the hooves of the cows returning home to the village.

वासितार्थेऽभियुध्यद्भिर्नादितं शुष्मिभिर्वृषैः ।

धावन्तीभिश्च वस्राभिरूधोभारैः स्ववत्सकान् ॥10.46.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: वासिता-अर्थे for the sake of a fertile cow, अभियुक्तभिः fighting fiercely, नादितम् resounded, शुष्मिभिः by passionate, powerful, वृषैः by the bulls, धावन्तीभिः च and by those running swiftly, वस्राभिः by the milch cows, ऊधः-भारैः carrying the heavy burden of their full udders, स्व-वत्सकान् toward their own calves

Anvaya: वासितार्थे अभियुक्तभिः शुष्मिभिः वृषैः नादितम्, ऊधोभारैः वस्राभिः च स्ववत्सकान् (प्रति) धावन्तीभिः (युक्तं नन्दव्रजं प्राप्तः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वासितार्थे For the sake of a cow in heat, अभियुक्तभिः fighting aggressively, शुष्मिभिः वृषैः by powerful bulls, नादितम् filled with roaring sounds, ऊधोभारैः carrying the heavy weight of full udders, वस्राभिः च and by the milch cows, स्ववत्सकान् toward their own calves, धावन्तीभिः running eagerly (the village of Nanda was filled with these).

Simple Meaning: The village was filled with the loud roaring of powerful, passionate bulls fighting one another for the attention of fertile cows, and with the hurried movement of milch cows who, despite being weighed down by their heavy, milk-filled udders, were running eagerly to feed their calves.

इतस्ततो विलङ्घद्भिर्गोवत्सैर्मण्डितं सितैः ।

गोदोहशब्दाभिरवं वेणूनां निःस्वनेन च ॥10.46.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: इतस्ततः here and there, विलङ्घद्भिः leaping dynamically, गो-वत्सैः by the calves, मण्डितम् decorated, सितैः white, गो-दोह-शब्द-अभिरवम् resounding with the sounds of milking the cows, वेणूनाम् of the flutes, निःस्वनेन च and with the vibrations

Anvaya: इतस्ततः विलङ्घद्भिः सितैः गोवत्सैः (च) मण्डितं, गोदोहशब्दाभिरवं वेणूनां निःस्वनेन च (युक्तं नन्दव्रजं प्राप्तः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इतस्ततः Here and there, विलङ्घद्भिः leaping, सितैः गोवत्सैः with white calves, मण्डितं adorned, गोदोहशब्दाभिरवं resounding with the noises of cow-milking, वेणूनां निःस्वनेन च and by the acoustic vibrations of flutes (the village of Nanda was filled with these).

Simple Meaning: The village pasture was beautifully adorned with white calves leaping playfully in all directions. It was completely filled with the vibrant, overlapping sounds of cows being milked and the melodious notes of flutes echoing throughout the area.

गायन्तीभिश्च कर्माणि शुभानि बलकृष्णयोः ।

स्वलङ्कृताभिर्गोपीभिर्गोपैश्च सुविराजितम् ॥10.46.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: गायन्तीभिः च and by those singing, कर्माणि the activities, शुभानि auspicious, बल-कृष्णयोः of Balarama and Krishna, सु-अलङ्कृताभिः by those beautifully adorned, गोपीभिः by the gopis, गोपैः च and by the cowherd men, सु-विराजितम् beautifully illuminated and splendid

Anvaya: बलकृष्णयोः शुभानि कर्माणि गायन्तीभिः स्वलङ्कृताभिः गोपीभिः गोपैः च (नन्दव्रजं) सुविराजितम् (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बलकृष्णयोः Of Balarama and Krishna, शुभानि कर्माणि the auspicious activities, गायन्तीभिः by those singing, स्वलङ्कृताभिः गोपीभिः by the beautifully adorned gopis, गोपैः च and by the cowherd men, सुविराजितम् was splendidly illuminated.

Simple Meaning: The pasture of Nanda was rendered exquisitely splendid by the presence of beautifully adorned gopis and cowherds, with the gopis loudly singing the auspicious pastimes of Balarama and Krishna.

अग्न्यर्कातिथिगोविप्रपितृदेवार्चनान्वितैः ॥

धूपदीपैश्च माल्यैश्च गोपावासैर्मनोरमम् ॥10.46.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: अग्नि-अर्क-अतिथि-गो-विप्र-पितृ-देव-अर्चना-अन्वितैः engaged in the worship of the sacrificial fire, the sun, guests, the cows, the brahmanas, the forefathers, and the demigods, धूप-दीपैः च with incense and lamps, माल्यैः च and with flower garlands, गोप-आवासैः by the residences of the cowherd men, मनोरमम् enchanting and beautiful to the mind

Anvaya: अग्न्यर्कातिथिगोविप्रपितृदेवार्चनान्वितैः धूपदीपैः च माल्यैः च (युक्तैः) गोपावासैः (तत् नन्दव्रजं) मनोरमम् (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अग्न्यर्कातिथिगोविप्रपितृदेवार्चनान्वितैः Engaged in the worship of fire, the sun, guests, cows, brahmanas, ancestors, and deities, धूपदीपैः च and with incense and lamps, माल्यैः च and with flower garlands (equipped with these), गोपावासैः by the homes of the cowherds, मनोरमम् enchanting to the mind (was the village of Nanda).

Simple Meaning: The cowherd residences of the village were rendered thoroughly enchanting by the residents who were diligently engaged in their traditional worship of the sacred fire, the sun, unexpected guests, cows, brahmanas, forefathers, and deities, and who had beautifully decorated their homes with fragrant incense, shining lamps, and floral garlands.

इति संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य नन्दः कृष्णानुरक्तधीः ।

अत्युत्कण्ठोऽभवत्तूष्णीं प्रेमप्रसरविह्वलः ॥10.46.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति thus, संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य repeatedly remembering, नन्दः Nanda, कृष्ण-अनुरक्त-धीः whose mind was deeply attached to Krishna, अति-उत्कण्ठः highly overwhelmed with longing, अभवत् became, तूष्णीम् silent, प्रेम-प्रसर-विह्वलः overwhelmed by the overflowing current of love

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच—इति संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य कृष्णानुरक्तधीः नन्दः अत्युत्कण्ठः प्रेमप्रसरविह्वलः (च) तूष्णीम् अभवत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति thus, संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य repeatedly remembering, कृष्णानुरक्तधीः whose mind was deeply attached to Krishna, नन्दः Nanda, अत्युत्कण्ठः highly overwhelmed with longing, प्रेमप्रसरविह्वलः (च) and overwhelmed by the overflowing current of love, तूष्णीम् silent, अभवत् became.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Thus repeatedly remembering the activities of Krishna, King Nanda, whose mind was deeply attached to Him, became highly overwhelmed with intense longing. Swept away by an overflowing current of ecstatic love, he fell completely silent.

यशोदा वर्ण्यमानानि पुत्रस्य चरितानि च ।

शृण्वन्त्यश्रूण्यवास्राक्षीत् स्नेहस्नुतपयोधरा ॥10.46.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: यशोदा Yashoda, वर्ण्यमानानि being described, पुत्रस्य of her son, चरितानि the activities, च and, शृण्वन्ती hearing, अश्रूणि tears, अवास्राक्षीत् shed, स्नेह-स्नुत-पयोधरा her breasts overflowing out of affection

Anvaya: पुत्रस्य वर्ण्यमानानि चरितानि शृण्वन्ती च यशोदा स्नेहस्नुतपयोधरा (सती) अश्रूणि अवास्राक्षीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पुत्रस्य Of her son, वर्ण्यमानानि being described, चरितानि the activities, शृण्वन्ती hearing, च and, यशोदा Yashoda, स्नेहस्नुतपयोधरा her breasts overflowing out of affection, (सती) (being), अश्रूणि tears, अवास्राक्षीत् shed.

Simple Meaning: As Yashoda listened to the descriptions of her son's activities, tears poured continuously from her eyes, and her breasts overflowing out of affection.

तयोरित्थं भगवति कृष्णे नन्दयशोदयोः ।

वीक्ष्यानुरागं परमं नन्दमाहोद्धवो मुदा ॥10.46.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: तयोः of both of them, इत्थम् in this manner, भगवति toward the Supreme Lord, कृष्णे Krishna, नन्द-यशोदयोः of Nanda and Yashoda, वीक्ष्य observing, अनुरागम् the attachment, परमम् supreme, नन्दम् to Nanda, आह spoke, उद्धवः Uddhava, मुदा happily

Anvaya: इत्थं तयोः नन्दयशोदयोः भगवति कृष्णे परमम् अनुरागं वीक्ष्य उद्धवः मुदा नन्दम् आह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इत्थं In this manner, तयोः नन्दयशोदयोः of both Nanda and Yashoda, भगवति कृष्णे toward the Supreme Lord Krishna, परमम् अनुरागं supreme attachment, वीक्ष्य observing, उद्धवः Uddhava, मुदा happily, नन्दम् to Nanda, आह spoke.

Simple Meaning: Observing this manifestation of supreme, ecstatic love for the Supreme Lord Krishna by both Nanda and Yashoda, Uddhava felt great joy and spoke to Nanda as follows.

उद्धव उवाच

युवां श्लाघ्यतमौ नूनं देहिनामिह मानद ।

नारायणेऽखिलगुरौ यत्कृता मतिरीदृशी ॥10.46.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: उद्धवः उवाच Uddhava said, युवाम् both of you, श्लाघ्य-तमौ most praiseworthy, नूनम् surely, देहिनाम् among all embodied beings, इह in this world, मान-द O giver of honor (Nanda), नारायणे toward Narayana, अखिल-गुरौ the master of all universes, यत् by which, कृता has been developed, मतिः mental absorption, ईदृशी such as this

Anvaya: उद्धवः उवाच—मानद! इह देहिनां युवां नूनं श्लाघ्यतमौ, यत् अखिलगुरौ नारायणे (वां) ईदृशी मतिः कृता।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: उद्धवः उवाच Uddhava said, मानद O giver of honor, इह in this world, देहिनां among all embodied beings, युवां both of you, नूनं surely, श्लाघ्यतमौ are most praiseworthy, यत् because, अखिलगुरौ the master of all universes, नारायणे toward Narayana, (वां) by both of you, ईदृशी such as this, मतिः mental absorption, कृता has been developed.

Simple Meaning: Uddhava said: O master of honor, both of you are surely the most praiseworthy of all embodied beings in this world, because you have developed such deep, unprecedented mental absorption in Narayana, the universal teacher and master of all.

एतौ हि विश्वस्य च बीजयोनी रामो मुकुन्दः पुरुषः प्रधानम् ।

अन्वीय भूतेषु विलक्षणस्य ज्ञानस्य चेशात इमौ पुराणौ ॥10.46.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतौ these two, हि indeed, विश्वस्य of the universe, च and, बीज-योनी the seed and the source, रामः Balarama, मुकुन्दः Krishna, पुरुषः the supreme soul, प्रधानम् the material nature, अन्वीय entering, भूतेषु into the living beings, विलक्षणस्य of the distinct, ज्ञानस्य of knowledge, च and, ईशाते are the controllers, इमौ these two, पुराणौ the primeval ones

Anvaya: एतौ रामः मुकुन्दः (च) हि विश्वस्य बीजयोनी, पुरुषः प्रधानम् (च) (स्तः)। इमौ पुराणौ भूतेषु अन्वीय विलक्षणस्य ज्ञानस्य च ईशाते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एतौ These two, रामः मुकुन्दः (च) Balarama and Krishna, हि indeed, विश्वस्य of the universe, बीजयोनी are the seed and the source, पुरुषः प्रधानम् (च) and are the supreme spirit and material nature, (स्तः) (they are). इमौ These two, पुराणौ primeval ones, भूतेषु into the living beings, अन्वीय entering, विलक्षणस्य of the distinct, ज्ञानस्य of knowledge, च and, ईशाते are the controllers.

Simple Meaning: These two, Balarama and Krishna, are indeed the ultimate seed and the source of the entire universe. They are the supreme spirit (Purusha) and the material nature (Pradhana). Entering into all living beings, these two primeval lords control the distinct individual consciousness and knowledge.

यस्मिञ्जनः प्राणवियोगकाले क्षणं समावेश्य मनो विशुद्धम् ।

निर्हृत्य कर्माशयमाशु याति परां गतिं ब्रह्ममयोऽर्कवर्णः ॥10.46.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: यस्मिन् in whom, जनः a person, प्राण-वियोग-काले at the time of leaving the life-air, क्षणम् for a single moment, समावेश्य completely concentrating, मनः the mind, विशुद्धम् purified, निर्हृत्य destroying, कर्म-आशयम् the repository of karmic impressions, आशु swiftly, याति attains, पराम् the supreme, गतिम् destination, ब्रह्म-मयः spiritualized, अर्क-वर्णः resplendent like the sun

Anvaya: प्राणवियोगकाले यस्मिन् क्षणं विशुद्धं मनः समावेश्य, जनः कर्माशयं निर्हृत्य, ब्रह्ममयः अर्कवर्णः (सन्) आशु परां गतिं याति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्राणवियोगकाले At the time of leaving the life-air, यस्मिन् in whom, क्षणं for a single moment, विशुद्धं मनः purified mind, समावेश्य completely concentrating, जनः a person, कर्माशयं the repository of karmic impressions, निर्हृत्य destroying, ब्रह्ममयः spiritualized, अर्कवर्णः resplendent like the sun, (सन्) (becoming), आशु swiftly, परां गतिं supreme destination, याति attains.

Simple Meaning: If a person, at the time of death, concentrates their purified mind on Him for even a single moment, they completely destroy the vast repository of past karmic impressions. Thus thoroughly spiritualized and becoming resplendent like the sun, they swiftly attain the supreme destination.

तस्मिन् भवन्तावखिलात्महेतौ नारायणे कारणमर्त्यमूर्तौ ।

भावं विधत्तं नितरां महात्मन् किं वावशिष्टं युवयोः सुकृत्यम् ॥10.46.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मिन् toward Him, भवन्तौ both of you, अखिल-आत्म-हेतौ the cause of all souls and the universe, नारायणे toward Narayana, कारण-मर्त्य-मूर्तौ who has assumed a humanlike form for a specific reason, भावम् supreme love and devotion, विधत्तम् are maintaining, नितराम् continuously, महा-आत्मन् O great soul Nanda, किम् what, वा or, अवशिष्टम् remaining, युवयोः for both of you, सु-कृत्यम् pious duty to be done

Anvaya: महात्मन्! अखिलात्महेतौ कारणमर्त्यमूर्तौ तस्मिन् नारायणे भवन्तौ नितरां भावं विधत्तम्, युवयोः किं वा सुकृत्यम् अवशिष्टम् (अस्ति)?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महात्मन् O great soul Nanda, अखिलात्महेतौ the cause of all souls and the universe, कारणमर्त्यमूर्तौ who has assumed a humanlike form for a specific reason, तस्मिन् toward Him, नारायणे Narayana, भवन्तौ both of you, नितरां continuously, भावं supreme love, विधत्तम् are maintaining. युवयोः For both of you, किं वा what, सुकृत्यम् pious duty, अवशिष्टम् remaining, (अस्ति) (is there)?

Simple Meaning: O great soul Nanda, since both you and Yasoda continuously maintain such deep, supreme love for Lord Narayana—who is the ultimate cause of all souls and universes, and who has now assumed a humanlike form for His own cosmic purpose—what further pious duty could possibly remain for you to perform?

आगमिष्यत्यदीर्घेण कालेन व्रजमच्युतः ।

प्रियं विधास्यते पित्रोर्भगवान् सात्वतां पतिः ॥10.46.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: आगमिष्यति will come, अदीर्घेण by a not long, कालेन time, व्रजम् to Vraja, अच्युतः the infallible Lord, प्रियम् pleasure, विधास्यते He will fulfill, पित्रोः of His parents, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, सात्वताम् of the Yadus, पतिः the master

Anvaya: अच्युतः भगवान् सात्वतां पतिः अदीर्घेण कालेन व्रजम् आगमिष्यति। पित्रोः प्रियं च विधास्यते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अच्युतः The infallible Lord, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, सात्वतां पतिः the master of the Yadus, अदीर्घेण कालेन in a short time, व्रजम् to Vraja, आगमिष्यति will come. पित्रोः Of His parents, प्रियं pleasure, च and, विधास्यते will fulfill.

Simple Meaning: The infallible Lord, the Supreme Lord and master of the Yadus, will return to Vraja in a very short time. He will surely fulfill the desires and bring joy to His parents.

हत्वा कंसं रङ्गमध्ये प्रतीपं सर्वसात्वताम् ।

यदाह वः समागत्य कृष्णः सत्यं करोति तत् ॥10.46.35॥

Word-by-word meaning: हत्वा having killed, कंसम् Kamsa, रङ्ग-मध्ये in the middle of the wrestling arena, प्रतीपम् the enemy, सर्व-सात्वताम् of all the Yadus, यत् what, आह He said, वः to you, समागत्य having approached, कृष्णः Krishna, सत्यम् true, करोति He makes, तत् that

Anvaya: रङ्गमध्ये सर्वसात्वतां प्रतीपं कंसं हत्वा वः समागत्य कृष्णः यत् आह, तत् (सः) सत्यं करोति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रङ्गमध्ये In the middle of the wrestling arena, सर्वसात्वतां of all the Yadus, प्रतीपं the enemy, कंसं Kamsa, हत्वा having killed, वः you, समागत्य having approached, कृष्णः Krishna, यत् what, आह said, तत् that, (सः) He, सत्यं true, करोति makes.

Simple Meaning: Having killed Kamsa, the enemy of all the Yadus, in the middle of the wrestling arena, Krishna approached you and made a promise. He will surely make that promise come true.

मा खिद्यतं महाभागौ द्रक्ष्यथः कृष्णमन्तिके ।

अन्तर्हृदि स भूतानामास्ते ज्योतिरिवैधसि ॥10.46.36॥

Word-by-word meaning: मा do not, खिद्यतम् lament, महा-भागौ O most fortunate ones, द्रक्ष्यथः you two will see, कृष्णम् Krishna, अन्तिके nearby, अन्तः-हृदि within the core of the heart, सः He, भूतानाम् of all living beings, आस्ते resides, ज्योतिः fire, इव like, एधसि in firewood

Anvaya: महाभागौ! (युवां) मा खिद्यतम्। अन्तिके कृष्णं द्रक्ष्यथः। सः भूतानाम् अन्तर्हृदि एधसि ज्योतिः इव आस्ते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महाभागौ O most fortunate ones, (युवां) you two, मा खिद्यतम् do not lament. अन्तिके Nearby, कृष्णं Krishna, द्रक्ष्यथः will see. सः He, भूतानाम् of all living beings, अन्तर्हृदि within the core of the heart, एधसि in firewood, ज्योतिः fire, इव like, आस्ते resides.

Simple Meaning: O most fortunate ones, do not lament. You will soon see Krishna beside you. He resides within the hearts of all living beings, just as unmanifest fire is inherently present within firewood.

न ह्यस्यास्ति प्रियः कश्चिन्नाप्रियो वास्त्यमानिनः ।

Word-by-word meaning: न not, हि indeed, अस्य for Him, अस्ति there is, प्रियः dear, कश्चित् anyone, न not, अप्रियः not dear (despicable), वा or, अस्ति there is, अमानिनः who is free from material pride (or desire for respect), न not, उत्तमः superior, न not, अधमः inferior, न अपि nor also, समानस्य for Him who is equal to all, असमः unequal (anyone who is not equal to Him / no one is peerless or a stranger), अपि वा or even

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अमानिनः Of Him who is free from egoistic pride, समानस्य and who is equal to all, अस्य for Him, कश्चित् anyone, प्रियः dear, न हि अस्ति is indeed not, अप्रियः वा or an enemy (not dear), न अस्ति there is not. उत्तमः Superior, न not, अधमः inferior, न not, असमः unequal (separate/stranger), अपि वा or even, न (अस्ति) there is not.

Simple Meaning: For Him, who is completely free from material pride and is equally disposed toward all, no one is uniquely dear, nor is anyone an object of dislike. There is no one who is superior, nor inferior, and no one is an outsider or unequal for Him.

न माता न पिता तस्य न भार्या न सुतादयः ।

नात्मीयो न परश्चापि न देहो न जन्म एव च ॥10.46.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, माता mother, न not, पिता father, तस्य His, न not, भार्या wife, न not, सुत-आदयः sons and other relatives, न not, आत्मीयः one's own kinsman, न not, परः stranger (or enemy), च also, अपि even, न not, देहः material body, न not, जन्म birth, एव certainly, च and

Anvaya: तस्य माता न, पिता न, भार्या न, सुतादयः न, आत्मीयः न, परः च अपि न, देहः न, जन्म एव च न (अस्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तस्य For Him, माता mother, न there is none, पिता father, न there is none, भार्या wife, न there is none, सुतादयः sons and others, न there are none, आत्मीयः kinsman, न there is none, परः stranger, च अपि and even, न there is none, देहः a material body, न there is none, जन्म birth, एव certainly, च and, न (अस्ति) there is not.

Simple Meaning: He has no mother, no father, no wife, no children, and no relatives. For Him, there is no one who is a relative and no one who is a stranger. He possesses no material body, and He certainly has no material birth.

न चास्य कर्म वा लोके सदसन्मिश्रयोनिषु ।

Word-by-word meaning: न not, च and, अस्य for Him, कर्म material work, वा or, लोके in this world, सत्-असत्-मिश्र-योनिषु among the high (pious), low (impious), and mixed species of life, क्रीडा-अर्थः for the sake of amusement, सः that, अपि even, साधूनाम् of the saintly devotees, परित्राणाय for the protection, कल्पते is meant

Anvaya: लोके सदसन्मिश्रयोनिषु अस्य कर्म च न (अस्ति)। सः अपि क्रीडार्थः (भवति, यः) साधूनां परित्राणाय कल्पते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: लोके In this world, सदसन्मिश्रयोनिषु among the high, low, and mixed species of birth, अस्य for Him, कर्म material work, च and, न (अस्ति) there is none. सः That (action), अपि even, क्रीडार्थः for the sake of play or pastime, (भवति occupies, यः which), साधूनां of the saintly devotees, परित्राणाय for the protection, कल्पते is meant.

Simple Meaning: He has no material duty or work to perform in this world among the higher, lower, or mixed species of life. His appearances and actions are purely for the sake of pastimes, which ultimately serve to protect His saintly devotees.

सत्त्वं रजस्तम इति भजते निर्गुणो गुणान् ।

कीडन्नतीतोऽत्र गुणैः सृजत्यवति हन्त्यजः ॥10.46.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: सत्त्वम् goodness, रजः passion, तमः ignorance, इति thus, भजते accepts, निर्गुणः who is free from material qualities, गुणान् the modes of nature, क्रीडन् playing, अतीतः transcendental (beyond), अत्र in this world, गुणैः by the modes, सृजति He creates, अवति maintains, हन्ति destroys, अजः the unborn

Anvaya: निर्गुणः अजः अत्र गुणैः क्रीडन् सत्त्वं रजः तमः इति गुणान् भजते, (तैः च) सृजत्यवति हन्ति (च)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: निर्गुणः He who is beyond material qualities, अजः the unborn, अत्र in this world, गुणैः with the modes of nature, क्रीडन् playing, सत्त्वं goodness, रजः passion, तमः ignorance, इति thus, गुणान् the modes, भजते accepts, (तैः च and by them), सृजति He creates, अवति maintains, हन्ति (च) and destroys.

Simple Meaning: Although He is unborn and entirely free from material qualities, He assumes the modes of goodness, passion, and ignorance to play in this world. Through these modes, He executes the creation, maintenance, and destruction of the universe while remaining completely transcendental to them.

यथा भ्रमरिकादृष्ट्या भ्राम्यतीव महीयते ।

चित्ते कर्तरि तत्रात्मा कर्तेवाहंधिया स्मृतः ॥10.46.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: यथा just as, भ्रमरिका-दृष्ट्या by the vision of a person who is spinning around (or through a spinning object), भ्राम्यति is spinning, इव as if, मही the earth, ईयते is perceived, चित्ते when the mind, कर्तरि is the actual performer of action, तत्र in that situation, आत्मा the soul, कर्ता the doer, इव as if, अहम्-धिया by the false identification of ego, स्मृतः is considered

Anvaya: यथा भ्रमरिकादृष्ट्या मही भ्राम्यति इव ईयते, तत्र चित्ते कर्तरि (सति) अहंधिया आत्मा कर्ता इव स्मृतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यथा Just as, भ्रमरिकादृष्ट्या by the vision of one who is whirling around, मही the earth, भ्राम्यति spins, इव as if, ईयते is perceived, तत्र in that same way, चित्ते when the mind, कर्तरि is the doer, (सति being so), अहंधिया by false ego, आत्मा the soul, कर्ता doer, इव as if, स्मृतः is thought.

Simple Meaning: Just as a person who is spinning around perceives the earth itself as spinning, similarly, when the mind and senses are the actual performers of material activities, the soul falsely identifies with them through ego and considers itself to be the doer.

युवयोरेव नैवायमात्मजो भगवान् हरिः ।

सर्वेषामात्मजो ह्यात्मा पिता माता स ईश्वरः ॥10.46.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: युवयोः of you two, एव alone, न not, एव certainly, अयम् this, आत्मजः son, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, हरिः Hari, सर्वेषाम् of all living beings, आत्मजः the son, हि indeed, आत्मा the soul, पिता father, माता mother, सः He, ईश्वरः the Supreme Controller

Anvaya: अयं भगवान् हरिः युवयोः एव आत्मजः न एव (अस्ति)। सः ईश्वरः हि सर्वेषाम् आत्मजः आत्मा पिता माता (च अस्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अयं This, भगवान् Supreme Lord, हरिः Hari, युवयोः of you two, एव alone, आत्मजः son, न एव is certainly not. सः He, ईश्वरः the Supreme Controller, हि indeed, सर्वेषाम् of everyone, आत्मजः son, आत्मा soul, पिता father, माता mother, (च अस्ति) and is.

Simple Meaning: This Supreme Lord Hari is not your son alone. He is the ultimate Supreme Controller who is indeed the son, the soul, the father, and the mother of all living beings.

दृष्टं श्रुतं भूतभवद्भविष्यत् स्थास्नुश्चरिष्णुर्महदल्पकं च ।

विनाच्युताद् वस्तु तरां न वाच्यं स एव सर्वं परमार्थभूतः ॥10.46.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: दृष्टम् seen, श्रुतम् heard, भूत-भवत्-भविष्यत् past, present, and future, स्थास्नुः stationary, चरिष्णुः moving, महत् large, अल्पकम् small, च and, विना without (apart from), अच्युतात् the infallible Lord Achyuta, वस्तु a thing, तराम् at all, न not, वाच्यम् can be said to exist, सः He, एव certainly, सर्वम् everything, परमार्थ-भूतः the ultimate reality

Anvaya: दृष्टं श्रुतं भूतभवद्भविष्यत् स्थास्नुः चरिष्णुः महत् अल्पकं च (यत् किञ्चित् अस्ति), अच्युतात् विना वस्तु तरां न वाच्यम्। सः एव सर्वं परमार्थभूतः (अस्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दृष्टं Seen, श्रुतं heard, भूतभवद्भविष्यत् past, present, and future, स्थास्नुः stationary, चरिष्णुः moving, महत् large, अल्पकं च and small (whatever exists), अच्युतात् apart from Lord Achyuta, वस्तु a thing, तरां at all, न वाच्यम् cannot be said to exist. सः He, एव certainly, सर्वं everything, परमार्थभूतः (अस्ति) is the ultimate reality.

Simple Meaning: Whatever is seen or heard; whatever belongs to the past, present, or future; whether it is stationary or moving, large or small—none of these things can be said to exist apart from the infallible Lord Achyuta. He alone is everything, constituting the sole ultimate reality.

एवं निशा सा ब्रुवतोर्व्यतीता नन्दस्य कृष्णानुचरस्य राजन् ।

गोप्यः समुत्थाय निरूप्य दीपान् वास्तून् समभ्यर्च्य दधीन्यमन्थन् ॥10.46.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, निशा night, सा that, ब्रुवतोः while they were conversing, व्यतीता passed, नन्दस्य of Nanda, कृष्ण-अनुचरस्य of the servant of Krishna (Uddhava), राजन् O King (Parikshit), गोप्यः the gopis, समुत्थाय rising up, निरूप्य lighting, दीपान् lamps, वास्तून् their households (or thresholds), समभ्यर्च्य worshiping properly, दधीनि yogurt, अमन्थन् churned

Anvaya: राजन्! एवं ब्रुवतोः नन्दस्य कृष्णानुचरस्य (च) सा निशा व्यतीता। गोप्यः समुत्थाय दीपान् निरूप्य वास्तून् समभ्यर्च्य दधीनि अमन्थन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, एवं thus, ब्रुवतोः while speaking, नन्दस्य of Nanda, कृष्णानुचरस्य (च) and of the servant of Krishna, सा that, निशा night, व्यतीता passed away. गोप्यः The gopis, समुत्थाय rising up, दीपान् lamps, निरूप्य lighting, वास्तून् their households, समभ्यर्च्य worshiping, दधीनि yogurt, अमन्थन् churned.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, while Nanda and Krishna's servant Uddhava were thus conversing, the night passed away. The gopis of Vraja then arose from bed, lit their lamps, offered worship to their household deities and thresholds, and began churning the yogurt.

ता दीपदीप्तैर्मणिभिर्विरेजू रज्जूर्विकर्षद्भुजकङ्कणस्रजः ।

चलन्नितम्बस्तनहारकुण्डलत्विषत्कपोलारुणकुङ्कुमाननाः ॥10.46.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताः they (the gopis), दीप-दीप्तैः illuminated by the lamps, मणिभिः with jewels, विरेजुः shone brilliantly, रज्जूः the ropes, विकर्षत् pulling, भुज-कङ्कण-स्रजः the clusters of bangles on their arms, चलत्-नितम्ब-स्तन-हार-कुण्डल-त्विषत्-कपोल-अरुण-कुङ्कुम्-आननाः with the beauty of their moving hips, breasts, necklaces, and earrings, their faces with cheeks reddened by the glow of kunkuma powder

Anvaya: रज्जूः विकर्षत् भुजकङ्कणस्रजः चलन्नितम्बस्तनहारकुण्डलत्विषत्कपोलारुणकुङ्कुमाननाः ताः दीपदीप्तैः मणिभिः विरेजुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रज्जूः The ropes, विकर्षत् pulling, भुजकङ्कणस्रजः having clusters of bangles on their arms, चलन्नितम्बस्तनहारकुण्डलत्विषत्कपोलारुणकुङ्कुमाननाः whose faces had cheeks glowing red with kunkuma powder, enhanced by the splendor of their moving hips, breasts, necklaces, and earrings, ताः they (the gopis), दीपदीप्तैः illuminated by the lamps, मणिभिः with jewels, विरेजुः shone brilliantly.

Simple Meaning: As the gopis pulled the churning ropes, the bangles on their arms jangled, and their jewels shone brilliantly in the lamplight. Their faces, adorned with cheeks reddened by kunkuma powder, radiant earrings, and moving necklaces, glowed with beauty alongside the rhythmic motion of their hips and breasts.

उद्गायतीनामरविन्दलोचनं व्रजाङ्गनानां दिवमस्पृशद्ध्वनिः ।

दध्नश्च निर्मन्थनशब्दमिश्रितो निरस्यते येन दिशाममङ्गलम् ॥10.46.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: उद्गायतीनाम् of those who were loudly singing, अरविन्द-लोचनम् about the lotus-eyed Lord Krishna, व्रज-अङ्गनानाम् of the women of Vraja (the gopis), दिवम् the sky (heaven), अस्पृशत् touched, ध्वनिः the sound, दध्नः of the yogurt, च and, निर्मन्थन-शब्द-मिश्रितः blended with the sound of churning, निरस्यते is driven away, येन by which, दिशाम् of all directions, अ-मङ्गलम् the inauspiciousness

Anvaya: अरविन्दलोचनम् उद्गायतीनां व्रजाङ्गनानां दध्नः निर्मन्थनशब्दमिश्रितः ध्वनिः दिवम् अस्पृशत्, येन दिशाम् अमङ्गलं निरस्यते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अरविन्दलोचनम् About the lotus-eyed Lord, उद्गायतीनां of those who were loudly singing, व्रजाङ्गनानां of the women of Vraja, दध्नः of the yogurt, निर्मन्थनशब्दमिश्रितः blended with the sound of churning, ध्वनिः the sound, दिवम् the sky, अस्पृशत् touched, येन by which, दिशाम् of all directions, अमङ्गलं the inauspiciousness, निरस्यते is driven away.

Simple Meaning: The sound of the ladies of Vraja loudly singing the glories of the lotus-eyed Krishna blended with the rhythmic sound of churning yogurt. This combined resonance rose up and touched the sky, completely driving away all inauspiciousness from every direction.

भगवत्युदिते सूर्ये नन्दद्वारि व्रजौकसः।

दृष्ट्वा रथं शातकौम्भं कस्यायमिति चाब्रुवन् ॥10.46.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: भगवति on the divine presence (of), उदिते having risen, सूर्ये the sun, नन्द-द्वारि at the doorway of Nanda, व्रज-ओकसः the inhabitants of Vraja, दृष्ट्वा seeing, रथम् the chariot, शातकौम्भम् made of gold, कस्य whose, अयम् this, इति thus, च and, अब्रुवन् asked among themselves

Anvaya: भगवति सूर्ये उदिते (सति) नन्दद्वारि शातकौम्भं रथं दृष्ट्वा व्रजौकसः "अयं कस्य?" इति च अब्रुवन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवति सूर्ये When the divine sun, उदिते (सति) had risen, नन्दद्वारि at Nanda's doorway, शातकौम्भं golden, रथं chariot, दृष्ट्वा seeing, व्रजौकसः the residents of Vraja, "अयं this (is), कस्य whose?", इति च thus also, अब्रुवन् spoke.

Simple Meaning: When the sun rose, the residents of Vraja noticed the golden chariot parked at Nanda’s doorway. They began asking one another, "Whose chariot is this?

अक्रूर आगतः किं वा यः कंसस्यार्थसाधकः ।

येन नीतो मधुपुरीं कृष्णः कमललोचनः ॥10.46.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: अक्रूरः Akrura, आगतः has come, किम् वा whether, यः who, कंसस्य of Kamsa, अर्थ-साधकः the achiever of the purpose (servant), येन by whom, नीतः was taken away, मधुपुरीम् to the city of Mathura, कृष्णः Krishna, कमल-लोचनः the lotus-eyed one

Anvaya: यः कंसस्य अर्थसाधकः (अस्ति), येन कमललोचनः कृष्णः मधुपुरीं नीतः, किं वा (सः) अक्रूरः आगतः?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः Who, कंसस्य of Kamsa, अर्थसाधकः the executor of purpose, (अस्ति) is, येन by whom, कमललोचनः the lotus-eyed, कृष्णः Krishna, मधुपुरीं to Mathura, नीतः was taken away, किं वा whether, (सः) that, अक्रूरः Akrura, आगतः has arrived?

Simple Meaning: “Has Akrura arrived here again? He is the one who serves the interests of Kamsa, and who previously took our lotus-eyed Krishna away to the city of Mathura.

किं साधयिष्यत्यस्माभिर्भर्तुः प्रेतस्य निष्कृतिम् ।

इति स्त्रीणां वदन्तीनामुद्धवोऽगात् कृताह्निकः ॥10.46.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: किम् whether, साधयिष्यति he will execute, अस्माभिः by using us, भर्तुः of his master (Kamsa), प्रेतस्य who is deceased, निष्कृतिम् the funeral rites (or offering satisfaction), इति thus, स्त्रीणाम् while the women, वदन्तीनाम् were speaking, उद्धवः Uddhava, अगात् arrived, कृत-आह्निकः having finished his morning rituals

Anvaya: "किं (सः) अस्माभिः प्रेतस्य भर्तुः निष्कृतिं साधयिष्यति?" इति स्त्रीणां वदन्तीनां (सतीनां), कृताह्निकः उद्धवः अगात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: किं Whether, (सः) he, अस्माभिः through us, प्रेतस्य of the deceased, भर्तुः master, निष्कृतिं the funeral obligations, साधयिष्यति will perform? इति thus, स्त्रीणां while the women, वदन्तीनां were speaking, (सतीनां being so), कृताह्निकः having performed his morning duties, उद्धवः Uddhava, अगात् came out.

Simple Meaning: "Is he going to use us to offer funeral oblations for his deceased master Kamsa?" While the women were speaking thus, Uddhava appeared, having completed his morning rituals.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे पूर्वार्धे नन्दशोकापनयनं नाम षट्चत्वारिंशः अध्यायः ॥46॥

Thus ends the forty-sixth discourse named “Krishna relieves the agony of Nanda (by sending Uddhava to Vraja with a message of endearment)”  in the first half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
