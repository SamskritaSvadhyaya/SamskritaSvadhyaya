Shrimad Bhagavatam - Book 1

Canto 10

Chapter 49

Akrura’s visit to Hastinapura

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

अक्रूरस्य हस्तिनापुरगमनं नाम​ एकोनवपञ्चाशः अध्यायः

(31 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच

स गत्वा हास्तिनपुरं पौरवेन्द्रयशोऽङ्कितम् ।

ददर्श तत्राम्बिकेयं सभीष्मं विदुरं पृथाम् ॥10.49.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, सः he (Akrura), गत्वा having gone, हास्तिनपुरम् to Hastinapura, पौरव-इन्द्र-यशः-अङ्कितम् marked by the glory of the best of the Puru kings, ददर्श saw, तत्र there, आम्बिकेयम् the son of Ambika (Dhritarashtra), स-भीष्मम् along with Bhishma, विदुरम् Vidura, पृथाम् Pritha (Kunti)

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— सः पौरवेन्द्रयशोऽङ्कितं हास्तिनपुरं गत्वा तत्र सभीष्मम् आम्बिकेयं विदुरं पृथां च ददर्श।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said— सः He, पौरवेन्द्रयशोऽङ्कितं  which was marked by the glory of the great Puru kings, हास्तिनपुरं to Hastinapura, गत्वा having gone, तत्र there, सभीष्मम् along with Bhishma, आम्बिकेयं Dhritarashtra, विदुरं Vidura, पृथां च and Pritha, ददर्श saw.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Traveling to Hastinapura—a capital deeply defined by the historic glory of the great rulers of the Puru dynasty—Akrura arrived and met with King Dhritarashtra, Bhishma, Vidura, and Kunti.

सहपुत्रं च बाह्लीकं भारद्वाजं सगौतमम् ।

कर्णं सुयोधनं द्रौणिं पाण्डवान् सुहृदोऽपरान् ॥10.49.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: सह पुत्रम् along with his son (Somadatta), च and, बाह्लीकम् Bahlika, भारद्वाजम् the son of Bharadwaja (Dronacharya), स-गौतमम् along with Kripacharya (the descendant of Gotama), कर्णम् Karna, सुयोधनम् Suyodhana (Duryodhana), द्रौणिम् the son of Drona (Ashvatthama), पाण्डवान् the Pandavas, सुहृदः well-wishing friends, अपरान् and others

Anvaya: (सः तत्र) सह पुत्रं बाह्लीकं च, भारद्वाजं, सगौतमं, कर्णं, सुयोधनं, द्रौणिं, पाण्डवान्, अपरान् सुहृदः च (ददर्श)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He, तत्र there, सह पुत्रं along with his son, बाह्लीकं च and Bahlika, भारद्वाजं the son of Bharadwaja - Dronacharya, सगौतमं along with Kripacharya, कर्णं Karna, सुयोधनं Duryodhana, द्रौणिं Ashvatthama, पाण्डवान् the Pandavas, अपरान् other, सुहृदः च and friends, (ददर्श) saw.

Simple Meaning: There he also met with Bahlika and his son Somadatta, Dronacharya, Kripacharya, Karna, Duryodhana, Ashvatthama, the Pandavas, and his other relatives and well-wishing friends.

यथावदुपसङ्गम्य बन्धुभिर्गान्दिनीसुतः ।

सम्पृष्टस्तैः सुहृद्वार्तां स्वयं चापृच्छदव्ययम् ॥10.49.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: यथावत् appropriately/according to custom, उपसङ्गम्य approaching, बन्धुभिः with his relatives, गान्दिनी-सुतः the son of Gandini (Akrura - Gandini is Akrura’s mother’s name), सम्पृष्टः was questioned, तैः by them, सुहृद-वार्ताम् about the welfare of his friends (in Mathura), स्वयम् himself, च and, आपृच्छत् inquired, अव्ययम् seamlessly/perfectly (or about their unimpaired welfare)

Anvaya: बन्धुभिः यथावत्  उपसङ्गम्य गान्दिनीसुतः तैः सुहृद्वार्तां सम्पृष्टः, स्वयं च (तेषाम्) अव्ययम् आपृच्छत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बन्धुभिः With his relatives, यथावत्  उपसङ्गम्य properly meeting, गान्दिनीसुतः Akrura (the son of Gandini), तैः by them, सुहृद्वार्तां about the news of his friends, सम्पृष्टः was questioned, स्वयं च and he himself, (तेषाम्) regarding their, अव्ययम् flawless welfare/well-being, आपृच्छत् asked.

Simple Meaning: Greeting his relatives and peers with all the customary respect due to each of them, Akrura (the son of Gandini) was warmly questioned by them about the well-being of their shared friends back in Mathura. He, too, in return, thoroughly inquired after their health, safety, and prosperity.

उवास कतिचिन्मासान् राज्ञो वृत्तविवित्सया ।

दुष्प्रजस्याल्पसारस्य खलच्छन्दानुवर्तिनः॥10.49.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: उवास he resided/stayed, कतिचित् मासान् for a few months, राज्ञः of the King (Dhritarashtra), वृत्त-विवित्सया with a desire to closely observe the behavior and conduct, दुष्प्रजस्य who had wicked children, अल्प-सारस्य who possessed little strength of character/weak-willed, खलः-छन्द-अनुवर्तिनः who followed the whims and desires of wicked people (like Duryodhana)

Anvaya: (सः) खलच्छन्दानुवर्तिनः दुष्प्रजस्य अल्पसारस्य राज्ञः वृत्तविवित्सया कतिचित् मासान् (तत्र) उवास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सः) He (Akrura), खलच्छन्दानुवर्तिनः who followed the whims of wicked advisors, दुष्प्रजस्य evil children, अल्पसारस्य who was weak-willed, राज्ञः of the King, वृत्तविवित्सया with a desire to fully understand his conduct, कतिचित् मासान् for a few months, (तत्र) there, उवास lived.

Simple Meaning: Akrura stayed there in Hastinapura for several months to closely observe the conduct of King Dhritarashtra. He wanted to understand the mind of this weak-willed monarch, who possessed troublesome, wicked children and constantly yielded to the whims of evil-minded counselors.

तेज ओजो बलं वीर्यं प्रश्रयादींश्च सद्गुणान् ।

प्रजानुरागं पार्थेषु न संहद्भिश्चिकीर्षितम् ॥10.49.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: तेजः influence/splendor, ओजः bodily/mental strength, बलम् physical power, वीर्यम् heroism/prowess, प्रश्रय-आदीन् च and qualities like humility, सद्गुणान् noble virtues, प्रजा-अनुरागम् the deep affection of the citizens, पार्थेषु in the sons of Pritha (the Pandavas), न not, संहद्भिः by those who could not tolerate/envious ones, चिकीर्षितम् the plots/actions intended (against them)

Anvaya: पार्थेषु तेजः ओजः बलं वीर्यं प्रश्रयादीन् सद्गुणान् च प्रजानुरागं न संहद्भिः चिकीर्षितम्…

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पार्थेषु In the Pandavas, तेजः brilliance, ओजः vitality, बलं power, वीर्यं prowess, प्रश्रयादीन् humility and so forth, सद्गुणान् च and noble attributes, प्रजानुरागं and the love of the citizens, न संहद्भिः by those who could not tolerate, चिकीर्षितम् what was plotted/intended…

Simple Meaning: Akrura clearly witnessed the incredible brilliance, vitality, physical power, heroism, intrinsic humility, and other sublime virtues of the Pandavas, as well as the immense devotion they commanded from the citizens. Consequently, he also observed the treacherous plots hatched against them by Dhritarashtra's envious sons, who simply could not tolerate the Pandavas' rising glory.

कृतं च धार्तराष्ट्रैर्यद् गरदानाद्यपेशलम् ।

आचख्यौ सर्वमेवास्मै पृथा विदुर एव च ॥10.49.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृतम् done, च and, धार्तराष्ट्रैः by the sons of Dhritarashtra, यत् which, गर-दान-आदि administration of poison and so forth, अपेशलम् wicked/vile deeds, आचख्यौ narrated, सर्वम् all, एव indeed, अस्मै to him (Akrura), पृथा Pritha (Kunti), विदुरः Vidura, एव च as well as

Anvaya: धार्तराष्ट्रैः यत् गरदानादि अपेशलं कृतं, पृथा विदुरः एव च अस्मै सर्वम् एव आचख्यौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: धार्तराष्ट्रैः By the sons of Dhritarashtra, यत् which, गरदानादि administration of poison and other atrocities, अपेशलं wicked deeds, कृतं were done, पृथा Pritha, विदुरः एव च as well as Vidura, अस्मै to him (Akrura), सर्वम् एव all of it indeed, आचख्यौ narrated.

Simple Meaning: Queen Kunti and Vidura then fully narrated to Akrura all the vile and wicked atrocities plotted and executed by Dhritarashtra's sons against the Pandavas, including their heinous attempts to administer poison to them.

पृथा तु भ्रातरं प्राप्तमक्रूरमुपसृत्य तम् ।

उवाच जन्मनिलयं स्मरन्त्यश्रुकलेक्षणा ॥10.49.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: पृथा Pritha (Kunti), तु indeed, भ्रातरम् brother, प्राप्तम् arrived, अक्रूरम् Akrura, उपसृत्य approaching, तम् him, उवाच spoke, जन्म-निलयम् her birthplace/paternal home, स्मरन्ती remembering, अश्रु-कल-ईक्षणा her eyes filled with tears

Anvaya: पृथा तु प्राप्तं भ्रातरं तम् अक्रूरम् उपसृत्य, जन्मनिलयं स्मरन्ती अश्रुकलेक्षणा उवाच।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पृथा तु Pritha indeed, प्राप्तं arrived, भ्रातरं brother, तम् अक्रूरम् that Akrura, उपसृत्य approaching, जन्मनिलयं her paternal home, स्मरन्ती remembering, अश्रुकलेक्षणा with eyes filled with tears, उवाच spoke.

Simple Meaning: Approaching her brother Akrura who had just arrived, Queen Kunti remembered her paternal home. Filled with deep emotion, she spoke to him with her eyes overflowing with tears.

अपि स्मरन्ति नः सौम्य पितरौ भ्रातरश्च मे ।

भगिन्यो भ्रातृपुत्राश्च जामयः सख्य एव च ॥10.49.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: अपि whether, स्मरन्ति remember, नः us, सौम्य O gentle one, पितरौ father and mother, भ्रातरः brothers, च and, मे my, भगिन्यः sisters, भ्रातृपुत्राः nephews, च and, जामयः sisters-in-law, सख्यः friends, एव certainly, च and

Anvaya: सौम्य! मे पितरौ भ्रातरः च, भगिन्यः भ्रातृपुत्राः च, जामयः सख्यः एव च, नः अपि स्मरन्ति?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सौम्य O gentle one, मे my, पितरौ father and mother, भ्रातरः brothers, च and, भगिन्यः sisters, भ्रातृपुत्राः nephews, च and, जामयः sisters-in-law, सख्यः friends, एव certainly, च and, नः us, अपि whether, स्मरन्ति remember.

Simple Meaning: O gentle Akrura, do my father and mother, my brothers, sisters, nephews, sisters-in-law, and friends still remember us?

भ्रात्रेयो भगवान् कृष्णः शरण्यो भक्तवत्सलः ।

पैतृष्वसेयान् स्मरति रामश्चाम्बुरुहेक्षणः ॥10.49.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: भ्रात्रेयः brother’s son, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, कृष्णः Krishna, शरण्यः the giver of shelter, भक्त-वत्सलः affectionate to His devotees, पैतृष्वसेयान् the sons of His father's sister (aunt), स्मरति remembers, रामः Balarama, च and, अम्बुरुह-ईक्षणः lotus-eyed one

Anvaya: शरण्यः भक्तवत्सलः भ्रात्रेयः भगवान् कृष्णः, अम्बुरुहेक्षणः रामः च, पैतृष्वसेयान् स्मरति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शरण्यः Giver of shelter, भक्तवत्सलः affectionate to His devotees, भ्रात्रेयः sister's son, भगवान् कृष्णः Lord Krishna, अम्बुरुहेक्षणः रामः lotus-eyed, Balarama, च and, पैतृष्वसेयान् the sons of His paternal aunt, स्मरति remembers.

Simple Meaning: Does Lord Krishna, the giver of shelter and the affectionate protector of His devotees, remember the sons of His paternal aunt? And does the lotus-eyed Balarama also remember us?

सापत्नमध्ये शोचन्तीं वृकाणां हरिणीमिव ।

सान्त्वयिष्यति मां वाक्यैः पितृहीनांश्च बालकान् ॥10.49.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: सपत्न-मध्ये among enemies, शोचन्तीम् lamenting, वृकाणाम् of wolves, हरिणीम् a doe, इव like, सान्त्वयिष्यति will console, माम् me, वाक्यैः by words, पितृ-हीनान् deprived of their father, च and, बालकान् children

Anvaya: वृकाणां मध्ये हरिणीम् इव सपत्नमध्ये शोचन्तीं मां, पितृहीनान् च बालकान्, वाक्यैः सान्त्वयिष्यति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वृकाणां Of wolves, मध्ये in the midst, हरिणीम् a doe, इव like, सपत्नमध्ये among enemies, शोचन्तीं lamenting, मां me, पितृहीनान् deprived of their father, च and, बालकान् children, वाक्यैः by words, सान्त्वयिष्यति will console.

Simple Meaning: Like a helpless doe lamenting in the midst of wolves, I grieve among my enemies Will Krishna come and console me and my children, who have been deprived of their father, with His comforting words?

कृष्ण कृष्ण महायोगिन् विश्वात्मन् विश्वभावन ।

प्रपन्नां पाहि गोविन्द शिशुभिश्चावसीदतीम् ॥10.49.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृष्ण Krishna, कृष्ण Krishna, महायोगिन् O great master of mystic power, विश्वात्मन् O soul of the universe, विश्वभावन O creator and sustainer of the universe, प्रपन्नाम् surrendered, पाहि protect, गोविन्द O Govinda, शिशुभिः with my children, च and, अवसीदतीम् distressed and sinking in sorrow

Anvaya: कृष्ण! कृष्ण! महायोगिन्! विश्वात्मन्! विश्वभावन! गोविन्द!, शिशुभिः च अवसीदतीं प्रपन्नां मां पाहि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्ण कृष्ण O Krishna, O Krishna, महायोगिन् O great master of mystic power, विश्वात्मन् O soul of the universe, विश्वभावन O creator and sustainer of the universe, गोविन्द O Govinda, शिशुभिः with my children, च and, अवसीदतीं distressed and sinking in sorrow, प्रपन्नां surrendered, मां me, पाहि protect.

Simple Meaning: O Krishna, O Krishna, O great master of mystic power! O Soul of the universe and sustainer of all beings! O Govinda, please protect me, for I have surrendered unto You. Together with my children, I am overwhelmed by sorrow and sinking in distress.

नान्यत्तव पदाम्भोजात् पश्यामि शरणं नृणाम् ।

बिभ्यतां मृत्युसंसारादीश्वरस्यापवर्गिकात् ॥10.49.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, अन्यत् other, तव Your, पद-अम्भोजात् than the lotus feet, पश्यामि I see, शरणम् shelter, नृणाम् for human beings, बिभ्यताम् who are fearful, मृत्यु-संसारात् from death and material existence, ईश्वरस्य of the Supreme Lord, अपवर्गिकात् which bestows liberation

Anvaya: मृत्युसंसारात् बिभ्यतां नृणाम्, ईश्वरस्य अपवर्गिकात् तव पदाम्भोजात् अन्यत् शरणं न पश्यामि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मृत्युसंसारात् From death and material existence, बिभ्यतां for those who are fearful, नृणाम् for human beings, ईश्वरस्य of the Supreme Lord, अपवर्गिकात् which bestows liberation, तव Your, पदाम्भोजात् than the lotus feet, अन्यत् other, शरणं shelter, न not, पश्यामि I see.

Simple Meaning: For human beings who are afraid of death and the cycle of material existence, I see no shelter other than Your lotus feet, O Supreme Lord, whose feet grant liberation.

नमः कृष्णाय शुद्धाय ब्रह्मणे परमात्मने ।

योगेश्वराय योगाय त्वामहं शरणं गता ॥10.49.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: नमः obeisances, कृष्णाय unto Krishna, शुद्धाय unto the pure one, ब्रह्मणे unto the Absolute Truth, परमात्मने unto the Supreme Soul, योगेश्वराय unto the lord of yoga, योगाय unto yoga itself, त्वाम् unto You, अहम् I, शरणम् shelter, गता have taken

Anvaya: शुद्धाय ब्रह्मणे परमात्मने योगेश्वराय योगाय कृष्णाय नमः। अहं त्वां शरणं गता।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शुद्धाय Unto the pure one, ब्रह्मणे unto the Absolute Truth, परमात्मने unto the Supreme Soul, योगेश्वराय unto the lord of yoga, योगाय unto yoga itself, कृष्णाय unto Krishna, नमः obeisances, अहं I, त्वां unto You, शरणं shelter, गता have taken.

Simple Meaning: Obeisances to Krishna, the pure one, the Absolute Truth, and the Supreme Soul. Obeisances to the Lord of yoga and to yoga itself. I have taken shelter of You alone.

श्री शुक उवाच

इत्यनुस्मृत्य स्वजनं कृष्णं च जगदीश्वरम् ।

प्रारुदद् दुःखिता राजन् भवतां प्रपितामही ॥10.49.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति thus, अनुस्मृत्य remembering, स्वजनम् her relatives, कृष्णम् Krishna, च and, जगत्-ईश्वरम् the Lord of the universe, प्रारुदत् began to weep, दुःखिता distressed, राजन् O King (Parikshit), भवताम् your, प्रपितामही great-grandmother

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच – राजन्, भवतां प्रपितामही दुःखिता, स्वजनं कृष्णं च जगदीश्वरम् अनुस्मृत्य, इति प्रारुदत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, राजन् O King (Parikshit), भवतां your, प्रपितामही great-grandmother, दुःखिता distressed, स्वजनं her relatives, कृष्णं Krishna, च and, जगदीश्वरम् the Lord of the universe, अनुस्मृत्य remembering, इति thus, प्रारुदत् began to weep.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King, your great-grandmother Kunti, overwhelmed with sorrow, remembered her relatives and Krishna, the Lord of the universe. Speaking in this way, she began to weep.

समदुःखसुखोऽक्रूरो विदुरश्च महायशाः ।

सान्त्वयामासतुः कुन्तीं तत्पुत्रोत्पत्तिहेतुभिः ॥10.48.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: सम-दुःख-सुखः equal in distress and happiness, अक्रूरः Akrura, विदुरः Vidura, च and, महायशाः greatly renowned, सान्त्वयामासतुः consoled, कुन्तीम् Kunti, तत्-पुत्र-उत्पत्ति-हेतुभिः with accounts of the causes of the birth of her sons

Anvaya: समदुःखसुखः अक्रूरः, महायशाः विदुरः च, तत्पुत्रोत्पत्तिहेतुभिः कुन्तीं सान्त्वयामासतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: समदुःखसुखः Equal in distress and happiness, अक्रूरः Akrura, महायशाः greatly renowned, विदुरः Vidura, च and, तत्पुत्रोत्पत्तिहेतुभिः with accounts of the causes of the birth of her sons, कुन्तीं Kunti, सान्त्वयामासतुः consoled.

Simple Meaning: Akrura, who remained equipoised in both happiness and distress, and the greatly renowned Vidura consoled Kunti by reminding her of the divine circumstances that had led to the birth of her sons.

यास्यन् राजानमभ्येत्य विषमं पुत्रलालसम् ।

अवदत् सुहृदां मध्ये बन्धुभिः सौहृदोदितम् ॥10.49.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: यास्यन् about to depart, राजानम् the king (Dhritarashtra), अभ्येत्य approaching, विषमम् biased/unfair, पुत्र-लालसम् fondly attached to his sons, अवदत् spoke, सुहृदाम् of friends, मध्ये in the midst, बन्धुभिः by well-wishing relatives, सौहृद-उदितम् spoken out of friendship/goodwill

Anvaya: यास्यन् पुत्रलालसं विषमं राजानम् अभ्येत्य सुहृदां मध्ये बन्धुभिः सौहृदोदितम् अवदत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यास्यन् About to depart, पुत्रलालसं intensely attached to his sons, विषमं biased, राजानम् the king, अभ्येत्य approaching, सुहृदां मध्ये in the midst of friends, बन्धुभिः by relatives, सौहृदोदितम् words spoken out of goodwill, अवदत् spoke.

Simple Meaning: Before departing, Akrura approached King Dhritarashtra, who was completely blinded by his biased affection for his own sons. In the assembly of friends and well-wishers, Akrura spoke words of profound wisdom and goodwill, as a caring relative naturally would.

अक्रूर उवाच

भो भो वैचित्रवीर्य त्वं कुरूणां कीर्तिवर्धन ।

भ्रातर्युपरते पाण्डावधुनाऽऽसनमास्थितः ॥10.49.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: अक्रूरः उवाच Akrura said, भो भो O O, वैचित्रवीर्य son of Vichitravirya (Dhritarashtra), त्वम् you, कुरूणाम् of the Kurus, कीर्ति-वर्धन augmenter of fame, भ्रातरि brother, उपरते having departed, पाण्डौ Pandu, अधुना now, आसनम् the throne, आस्थितः seated

Anvaya: अक्रूरः उवाच— भो भो वैचित्रवीर्य! कुरूणां कीर्तिवर्धन! भ्रातरि पाण्डौ उपरते, त्वम् अधुना आसनम् आस्थितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अक्रूरः उवाच Akrura said, भो भो O O, वैचित्रवीर्य Son of Vichitravirya, कुरूणां कीर्तिवर्धन Augmenter of fame of the Kurus!, भ्रातरि पाण्डौ Brother Pandu, उपरते having departed, त्वम् you, अधुना now, आसनम् आस्थितः are seated on the throne.

Simple Meaning: Akrura said: O son of Vichitravirya, O augmenter of the fame of the Kurus! Since your brother Pandu has passed away, you have now ascended the throne.

धर्मेण पालयन्नुर्वीं प्रजाः शीलेन रञ्जयन् ।

वर्तमानः समः स्वेषु श्रेयः कीर्तिमवाप्स्यसि ॥10.49.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: धर्मेण righteously/in accordance with duty, पालयन् ruling/protecting, उर्वीम् the earth, प्रजाः the citizens/subjects, शीलेन by good character/amiable disposition, रञ्जयन् pleasing/delighting, वर्तमानः behaving, समः equally/impartially, स्वेषु among your own kinsmen, श्रेयः spiritual welfare/ultimate good, कीर्तिम् fame/glory, अवाप्स्यसि you will achieve

Anvaya: धर्मेण उर्वीं पालयन्, शीलेन प्रजाः रञ्जयन्, स्वेषु समः वर्तमानः श्रेयः कीर्तिं च अवाप्स्यसि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: धर्मेण Righteously, उर्वीं the earth, पालयन् ruling, शीलेन by noble character, प्रजाः the citizens, रञ्जयन् pleasing, स्वेषु among your own kinsmen, समः impartially, वर्तमानः behaving, श्रेयः ultimate welfare, कीर्तिं च and fame, अवाप्स्यसि you will achieve.

Simple Meaning: By ruling the earth righteously, delighting your subjects with your noble character, and maintaining total impartiality toward all your kinsmen (both your own sons and the Pandavas), you will attain ultimate spiritual welfare in this life and everlasting glory in the next.

अन्यथा त्वाचरँल्लोके गर्हितो यास्यसे तमः ।

तस्मात् समत्वे वर्तस्व पाण्डवेष्वात्मजेषु च ॥10.49.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्यथा otherwise, तु indeed, आचरन् acting/behaving, लोके in this world, गर्हितः condemned/censured, यास्यसे you will go, तमः to darkness/hell, तस्मात् therefore, समत्वे in equality/impartiality, वर्तस्व abide/act, पाण्डवेषु toward the sons of Pandu, आत्मजेषु toward your own sons, च and

Anvaya: अन्यथा तु आचरन्, लोके गर्हितः तमः यास्यसे। तस्मात् पाण्डवेषु आत्मजेषु च समत्वे वर्तस्व।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अन्यथा Otherwise, तु indeed, आचरन् acting, लोके in this world, गर्हितः condemned, तमः to darkness, यास्यसे you will go. तस्मात् Therefore, पाण्डवेषु toward the Pandavas, आत्मजेषु toward your own sons, च and, समत्वे in impartiality, वर्तस्व abide.

Simple Meaning: If you act otherwise, you will be heavily condemned in this world and will descend into the darkness of hellish regions after death. Therefore, you must maintain absolute impartiality and treat both the Pandavas and your own sons with equal love and justice.

नेह चात्यन्तसंवासः कर्हिचित् केनचित् सह ।

राजन् स्वेनापि देहेन किमु जायात्मजादिभिः ॥10.49.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, इह in this world, च and, अत्यन्त-संवासः eternal association/perpetual living together, कर्हिचित् at any time, केनचित् सह with anyone, राजन् O King (Dhritarashtra), स्वेन own, अपि even, देहेन with the body, किमु what to speak of, जाया-आत्मज-आदिभिः with wife, children, and others

Anvaya: राजन्! इह केनचित् सह कर्हिचित् अत्यन्तसंवासः न (अस्ति), स्वेन देहेन अपि (संवासः न अस्ति), जायात्मजादिभिः किमु।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, इह in this world, केनचित् सह with anyone, कर्हिचित् at any time, अत्यन्तसंवासः eternal association, न does not exist. स्वेन देहेन अपि Even with one's own body. जायात्मजादिभिः With wife, children, and others, किमु what to speak of.

Simple Meaning: O King, in this material world, no one can enjoy permanent association with anyone else. When we cannot even remain permanently united with our own physical body, what to speak of our wives, children, wealth, and relatives?

एकः प्रसूयते जन्तुरेक एव प्रलीयते ।

एकोऽनुभुङ्क्ते सुकृतमेक एव च दुष्कृतम् ॥10.49.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: एकः alone, प्रसूयते is born, जन्तुः a living being, एकः alone, एव indeed, प्रलीयते dies/perishes, एकः alone, अनुभुङ्क्ते experiences, सुकृतम् the results of pious deeds, एकः alone, एव indeed, च and, दुष्कृतम् the results of sinful deeds

Anvaya: जन्तुः एकः प्रसूयते, एकः एव प्रलीयते, एकः सुकृतं, एकः एव च दुष्कृतम् अनुभुङ्क्ते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जन्तुः A living being, एकः alone, प्रसूयते is born. एकः Alone, एव indeed, प्रलीयते dies. एकः Alone, सुकृतं the fruits of good deeds (reaps). एकः Alone, एव च and indeed, दुष्कृतम् the fruits of bad deeds, अनुभुङ्क्ते experiences.

Simple Meaning: Every living being enters this world entirely alone and departs from it entirely alone. Furthermore, it is completely by oneself that a soul must experience the consequences of its own pious acts and suffer the reactions of its own sinful deeds.

अधर्मोपचितं वित्तं हरन्त्यन्येऽल्पमेधसः ।

सम्भोजनीयापदेशैर्जलानीव जलौकसः ॥10.49.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: अधर्म-उपचितम् accumulated through unrighteous means, वित्तम् wealth, हरन्ति take away, अन्ये others, अल्प-मेधसः of a person with meager intelligence/foolish, सम्भोजनीय-अपदेशैः under the pretext of being dependents/shared consumers, जलानि waters, इव just like, जल-ओकसः aquatic creatures

Anvaya: अल्पमेधसः अधर्मोपचितं वित्तं सम्भोजनीयापदेशैः अन्ये हरन्ति, जलौकसः जलानि इव।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अल्पमेधसः Of a foolish person, अधर्मोपचितं accumulated unrighteously, वित्तं the wealth, सम्भोजनीयापदेशैः under the pretext of being dependents who maintain him, अन्ये others, हरन्ति take away, जलौकसः aquatic creatures, जलानि the waters, इव just like.

Simple Meaning: The unrighteous wealth accumulated by a foolish man is systematically drained by his so-called dependents under the pretext of loving companionship and shared survival. This is exactly how aquatic creatures unconsciously drink up and dry out the very water that forms their dwelling.

पुष्णाति यानधर्मेण स्वबुद्ध्या तमपण्डितम् ।

तेऽकृतार्थं प्रहिण्वन्ति प्राणा रायः सुतादयः ॥10.49.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुष्णाति maintains/nourishes, यान् whom, अधर्मेण by unrighteous means, स्व-बुद्ध्या by his own understanding/thinking, तम् him, अपण्डितम् the foolish/unintelligent person, ते they, अकृत-अर्थम् without achieving his purpose/unfulfilled, प्रहिण्वन्ति abandon/leave behind, प्राणाः the life airs, रायः wealth, सुत-आदयः sons and others

Anvaya: यान् अधर्मेण स्वबुद्ध्या पुष्णाति, ते प्राणाः रायः सुतादयः तम् अपण्डितम् अकृतार्थं प्रहिण्वन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यान् Whom, अधर्मेण through unrighteous means, स्वबुद्ध्या by his own thinking, पुष्णाति he maintains, ते they, प्राणाः the life-airs, रायः wealth, सुतादयः sons and others, तम् that, अपण्डितम् foolish person, अकृतार्थं unfulfilled/unsuccessful, प्रहिण्वन्ति abandon.

Simple Meaning: A foolish person uses unrighteous means to passionately nurture his family, wealth, and senses, thinking them to be his ultimate shelter. Yet, in the end, his very life-airs, hard-earned wealth, and beloved family members completely abandon him, leaving him spiritually unfulfilled and utterly ruined.

स्वयं किल्बिषमादाय तैस्त्यक्तो नार्थकोविदः ।

असिद्धार्थो विशत्यन्धं स्वधर्मविमुखस्तमः ॥10.49.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्वयम् himself, किल्बिषम् sins/sinful reactions, आदाय taking/carrying, तैः by them (family, wealth, etc.), त्यक्तः abandoned, न not, अर्थ-कोविदः knowing what is beneficial/wise, असिद्ध-अर्थः without achieving his true purpose, विशति he enters, अन्धम् blinding/pitch-black, स्वधर्म-विमुखः turned away from his own duty, तमः darkness

Anvaya: स्वयं किल्बिषम् आदाय, तैः त्यक्तः, न अर्थकोविदः असिद्धार्थः स्वधर्मविमुखः (सः) अन्धं तमः विशति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: स्वयं Himself, किल्बिषम् the sinful reactions, आदाय carrying, तैः by them, त्यक्तः abandoned, न अर्थकोविदः not knowing his true self-interest, असिद्धार्थः without achieving his life's goal, स्वधर्मविमुखः turned away from his religious duties, अन्धं तमः blinding darkness, विशति enters.

Simple Meaning: Abandoned in the end by the very family and wealth he sinfully maintained, the foolish person must carry the heavy burden of his sinful reactions all by himself. Unaware of his true self-interest and having turned his back on his prescribed duties, he fails to achieve the real purpose of human life and plunges into the deepest, blinding darkness of hellish existence.

तस्माल्लोकमिमं राजन् स्वप्नमायामनोरथम् ।

वीक्ष्यायम्यात्मनाऽऽत्मानं समः शान्तो भव प्रभो ॥10.49.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मात् therefore, लोकम् world, इमम् this, राजन् O King, स्वप्न-माया-मनोरथम् like a dream, an illusion, or a mental fabrication, वीक्ष्य seeing/realizing, आयम्य restraining/controlling, आत्मना by the mind/intelligence, आत्मानम् the self, समः equanimous/impartial, शान्तः peaceful, भव become, प्रभो O Lord/O Master

Anvaya: राजन्! प्रभो! तस्मात् इमं लोकं स्वप्नमायामनोरथं वीक्ष्य, आत्मना आत्मानम् आयम्य समः शान्तः भव।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, प्रभो O master, तस्मात् therefore, इमं लोकं this world, स्वप्नमायामनोरथं as a dream, illusion, and mental fantasy, वीक्ष्य seeing, आत्मना by your intelligence, आत्मानम् your mind, आयम्य restraining, समः impartial, शान्तः peaceful, भव become.

Simple Meaning: Therefore, O King, recognize that this material world is temporary and insubstantial—nothing more than a passing dream, a magical illusion, or a figment of the imagination. Controlling your mind with steady intelligence, bring yourself to a state of complete impartiality and find true, inner peace.

धृतराष्ट्र उवाच

यथा वदति कल्याणीं वाचं दानपते भवान् ।

तथानया न तृप्यामि मर्त्यः प्राप्य यथामृतम् ॥10.49.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: धृतराष्ट्रः उवाच Dhritarashtra said, यथा just as, वदति you speak, कल्याणीम् auspicious/beneficial, वाचम् words, दानपते O master of charity (Akrura), भवान् you, तथा so, अनया with this (speech), न not, तृप्यामि I am satiated/satisfied, मर्त्यः a mortal being, प्राप्य having obtained, यथा just as, अमृतम् nectar/the drink of immortality

Anvaya: धृतराष्ट्रः उवाच—दानपते! भवान् यथा कल्याणीं वाचं वदति, अनया (वाचा) अहं तथा न तृप्यामि, यथा अमृतं प्राप्य मर्त्यः (न तृप्यति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: धृतराष्ट्रः उवाच Dhritarashtra said, दानपते O master of charity, भवान् you, यथा just as, कल्याणीं वाचं auspicious words, वदति you speak, अनया with this speech, तथा न तृप्यामि I am not satiated in that way, यथा just as, अमृतं nectar, प्राप्य having obtained, मर्त्यः a mortal man.

Simple Meaning: King Dhritarashtra replied: O master of charity, your words are immensely auspicious and full of profound wisdom. Just as a mortal being can never feel fully satiated no matter how much nectar he drinks, I too can never hear enough of your beautiful, enlightening instructions.

तथापि सूनृता सौम्य हृदि न स्थीयते चले ।

पुत्रानुरागविषमे विद्युत् सौदामनी यथा ॥10.49.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: तथापि still/nevertheless, सूनृता pleasing and truthful, सौम्य O gentle one (Akrura), हृदि in the heart, न not, स्थीयते remains/stays, चले in the unsteady/fickle, पुत्र-अनुराग-विषमे biased by fond attachment to my sons, विद्युत् lightning, सौदामनी flashing from the cloud, यथा just like

Anvaya: सौम्य! तथापि पुत्रानुरागविषमे चले (मम) हृदि (इयं) सूनृता (वाक्) न स्थीयते, सौदामनी विद्युत् यथा।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सौम्य O gentle one, तथापि nevertheless, पुत्रानुरागविषमे biased by fond attachment to my sons, चले in my fickle, हृदि heart, इयं सूनृता this pleasing and truthful speech, न स्थीयते does not remain, सौदामनी विद्युत् a flash of lightning, यथा just like.

Simple Meaning: Nevertheless, O gentle Akrura, because my unsteady heart is so deeply biased by blind attachment to my sons, your pleasing and truthful advice cannot find a permanent place within me. It simply flashes through my mind and disappears, just like a sudden bolt of lightning in the sky.

ईश्वरस्य विधिं को नु विधुनोत्यन्यथा पुमान् ।

भूमेर्भारावताराय योऽवतीर्णो यदोः कुले ॥10.49.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: ईश्वरस्य of the Supreme Lord, विधिम् the decree/will, कः who, नु indeed, विधुनोति can shake off/unravel, अन्यथा otherwise, पुमान् a human being/mortal, भूमेः of the earth, भार-अवताराय for diminishing the heavy burden, यः who, अवतीर्णः has descended, यदोः of Yadu, कुले in the dynasty

Anvaya: यः भूमेः भारावताराय यदोः कुले अवतीर्णः, (तस्य) ईश्वरस्य विधिं कः नु पुमान् अन्यथा विधुनोति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः Who, भूमेः of the earth, भारावताराय for relieving the burden, यदोः कुले in the Yadu dynasty, अवतीर्णः has descended, ईश्वरस्य of that Supreme Lord's, विधिं will, कः नु पुमान् what mortal indeed, अन्यथा alter/undo, विधुनोति can shake off.

Simple Meaning: That Supreme Lord has now personally descended into the Yadu dynasty to relieve the heavy burden of the earth. Knowing this, what mortal man could possibly alter, undo, or resist His supreme will and cosmic plan?

यो दुर्विमर्शपथया निजमाययेदं सृष्ट्वा गुणान् विभजते तदनुप्रविष्टः।

तस्मै नमो दुरवबोधविहारतन्त्रसंसारचक्रगतये परमेश्वराय ॥10.49.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः who, दुर्विमर्श-पथया whose path of action is inconceivable/unfathomable, निज-मायया by His own illusory energy, इदम् this universe, सृष्ट्वा having created, गुणान् the modes of nature/material qualities, विभजते interacts with/distributes, तत्-अनुप्रविष्टः having entered within it thereafter, तस्मै unto Him, नमः obeisances, दुर्-अवबोध-विहार-तन्त्र-संसार-चक्र-गतये whose pastimes are incomprehensible and who controls the movement of the wheel of material existence, परम-ईश्वराय unto the Supreme Lord

Anvaya: यः दुर्विमर्शपथया निजमायया इदं सृष्ट्वा, तदनुप्रविष्टः गुणान् विभजते, दुरवबोधविहारतन्त्रसंसारचक्रगतये तस्मै परमेश्वराय नमः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः Who, दुर्विमर्शपथया through an unfathomable path, निजमायया by His own illusory energy, इदं this universe, सृष्ट्वा having created, तदनुप्रविष्टः having entered within it thereafter, गुणान् the modes of nature, विभजते interacts with, दुरवबोधविहारतन्त्रसंसारचक्रगतये whose pastimes are incomprehensible and who directs the movement of the wheel of material existence, तस्मै unto Him, परमेश्वराय unto the Supreme Lord, नमः obeisances.

Simple Meaning: I offer my respectful obeisances unto the Supreme Lord. Utilizing His own unfathomable illusory energy, He creates this vast universe, enters within it as the indwelling soul, and directs the interactions of the material modes of nature while driving the continuous rotation of the wheel of worldly existence which are completely beyond human comprehension.

श्री शुक उवाच

इत्यभिप्रेत्य नृपतेरभिप्रायं स यादवः ।

सुहृद्भिः समनुज्ञातः पुनर्यदुपुरीमगात् ॥10.49.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति thus, अभिप्रेत्य understanding, नृपतेः of the king (Dhritarashtra), अभिप्रायम् the mindset/intention, सः that, यादवः descendant of Yadu (Akrura), सुहृद्भिः by his well-wishing friends, समनुज्ञातः taking permission/bidding farewell, पुनः back, यदु-पुरीम् to the city of the Yadus (Mathura), अगात् went

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच—सः यादवः नृपतेः इति अभिप्रायम् अभिप्रेत्य, सुहृद्भिः समनुज्ञातः (सन्) पुनः यदुपुरीम् अगात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, सः यादवः that descendant of Yadu, नृपतेः of the king, इति thus, अभिप्रायम् the mindset, अभिप्रेत्य understanding, सुहृद्भिः by his well-wishing friends, समनुज्ञातः taking permission, पुनः back, यदुपुरीम् to the city of the Yadus, अगात् went.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King, Akrura thus clearly understood Dhritarashtra's mindset. He then affectionately took leave of his friends and relatives, and returned to the Yadu capital of Mathura.

शशंस रामकृष्णाभ्यां धृतराष्ट्रविचेष्टितम् ।

पाण्डवान् प्रति कौरव्य यदर्थं प्रेषितः स्वयम् ॥10.49.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: शशंस related/reported, राम-कृष्णाभ्याम् to Balarama and Krishna, धृतराष्ट्र-विचेष्टितम् the behavior/attitude of Dhritarashtra, पाण्डवान् प्रति toward the Pandavas, कौरव्य O descendant of Kuru (King Parikshit), यत्-अर्थम् for which purpose, प्रेषितः he had been sent, स्वयम् himself

Anvaya: कौरव्य! यदर्थं स्वयं प्रेषितः, (सः) पाण्डवान् प्रति धृतराष्ट्रविचेष्टितं रामकृष्णाभ्यां शशंस।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कौरव्य O descendant of Kuru, यदर्थं for which purpose, स्वयं himself, प्रेषितः he had been sent, पाण्डवान् प्रति toward the Pandavas, धृतराष्ट्रविचेष्टितं the conduct of Dhritarashtra, रामकृष्णाभ्यां to Balarama and Krishna, शशंस reported.

Simple Meaning: O descendant of Kuru (King Parikshit), Akrura then approached Balarama and Krishna and reported everything regarding Dhritarashtra's biased attitude and behavior toward the Pandavas. He thus fully described the ground reality of the situation, fulfilling the exact mission for which he had been sent to Hastinapura.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे वैयासिक्यामष्टादशसाहस्र्यां पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे पूर्वार्धे “अक्रूरस्य हस्तिनापुरगमनं नाम”​ एकोनवपञ्चाशः अध्यायः ॥49॥

Thus ends the forty-ninth discourse, named “Akrura’s visit to Hastinapura” in the first half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
