Dialogue between Uddhava and the Gopis and ode to a black bee

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

उद्धवगोपीसंवादः तथा भ्रमरगीतं नाम​ सप्तचत्वारिंशः अध्यायः

(69 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच

तं वीक्ष्य कृष्णानुचरं व्रजस्त्रियः प्रलम्बबाहुं नवकञ्जलोचनम् ।

पीताम्बरं पुष्करमालिनं लसन्मुखारविन्दं मणिमृष्टकुण्डलम् ॥10.47.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, तम् him, वीक्ष्य seeing, कृष्ण-अनुचरम् the follower of Krishna, व्रज-स्त्रियः the women of Vraja, प्रलम्ब-बाहुम् possessing long arms, नव-कञ्ज-लोचनम् whose eyes were like a newly blossomed lotus, पीत-अम्बरम् wearing yellow garments, पुष्कर-मालिनम् wearing a lotus garland, लसत्-मुख-अरविन्दम् whose lotus face was radiant, मणि-मृष्ट-कुण्डलम् wearing earrings polished with jewels

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— प्रलम्बबाहुं नवकञ्जलोचनं पीताम्बरं पुष्करमालिनं लसतमुखारविन्दं मणिमृष्टकुण्डलं तं कृष्णानुचरं वीक्ष्य व्रजस्त्रियः …

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, प्रलम्बबाहुं Possessing long arms, नवकञ्जलोचनं whose eyes were like fresh lotuses, पीताम्बरं wearing yellow silken garments, पुष्करमालिनं adorned with a lotus garland, लसतमुखारविन्दं whose lotus face was shining, मणिमृष्टकुण्डलं possessing earrings polished with brilliant jewels, तं that, कृष्णानुचरं follower of Krishna, वीक्ष्य seeing, व्रजस्त्रियः the women of Vraja…

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Seeing that friend of Krishna, who possessed long arms, eyes like newly blossomed lotuses, and a radiant lotus face adorned with gleaming jeweled earrings, and who wore yellow garments and a fresh lotus garland, the women of Vraja …

शुचिस्मिताः कोऽयमपीच्यदर्शनः कुतश्च कस्याच्युतवेषभूषणः ।

इति स्म सर्वाः परिवव्रुरुत्सुकास्तमुत्तमश्लोकपदाम्बुजाश्रयम् ॥10.47.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: शुचि-स्मिताः possessing pure smiles, कः who, अयम् this, अपीच्य-दर्शनः of most beautiful appearance, कुतः from where, च and, कस्य whose (follower/friend), अच्युत-वेष-भूषणः wearing the dress and ornaments of Achyuta (Krishna), इति thus, स्म indeed, सर्वाः all of them, परिवव्रुः surrounded, उत्सुकाः being very eager, तम् him, उत्तमश्लोक-पद-अम्बुज-आश्रयम् who has taken shelter of the lotus feet of the Supreme Lord of glorious renown

Anvaya: शुचिस्मिताः (ताः) सर्वाः "अपीच्यदर्शनः अच्युतवेषभूषणः अयं कः? कुतः च (आगतः)? कस्य (च दूतः)?" इति उत्सुकाः (सत्यः) उत्तमश्लोकपदाम्बुजाश्रयं तं परिवव्रुः स्म।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शुचिस्मिताः Possessing pure smiles, (ताः) सर्वाः all of them, "अपीच्यदर्शनः Of most beautiful appearance, अच्युतवेषभूषणः wearing the dress and ornaments of Achyuta, अयं कः who (is) this?, कुतः च and from where (has he come)?, कस्य whose (friend is he)?," इति thus, उत्सुकाः eager, उत्तमश्लोकपदाम्बुजाश्रयं who has taken shelter of the lotus feet of the gloriously praised Lord, तं him, परिवव्रुः स्म indeed surrounded.

Simple Meaning: Bearing pure smiles, all those eager gopis began asking, "Who is this beautiful man? Where has he come from, and whose friend is he, wearing the very clothes and ornaments of Lord Achyuta?" Whispering thus, they gathered around Uddhava, who had fully taken shelter of the lotus feet of the Supreme Lord.

तं प्रश्रयेणावनताः सुसत्कृतं सव्रीडहासेक्षणसूनृतादिभिः ।

रहस्यपृच्छन्नुपविष्टमासने विज्ञाय सन्देशहरम् रमापतेः ॥10.47.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him, प्रश्रयेण with humility, अवनताः bowing down, सु-सत्कृतम् properly honored, स-व्रीड-हास-ईक्षण-सूनृत-आदिभिः with bashful smiles, glances, pleasing words, and so on, रहसि in a private place, अपृच्छन् they questioned, उपविष्टम् seated, आसने on a seat, विज्ञाय understanding him to be, संदेश-हरम् the bearer of message, रमा-पतेः of the husband of the goddess of fortune (Krishna)

Anvaya: प्रश्रयेण अवनताः (ताः) सव्रीडहासेक्षणसूनृतादिभिः सुसत्कृतम् आसने उपविष्टं तं रमापतेः सन्देशहरं विज्ञाय रहसि अपृच्छन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रश्रयेण With humility, अवनताः bowing down, (ताः they), सव्रीडहासेक्षणसूनृतादिभिः with bashful smiles, glances, pleasing words, and other expressions, सुसत्कृतम् well-received, आसने on a seat, उपविष्टं seated, तं him, रमापतेः of the Lord of Goddess Rama, सन्देशहरं the bearer of message, विज्ञाय understanding, रहसि in private, अपृच्छन् questioned.

Simple Meaning: Bowing down with humility, the gopis honored Uddhava with bashful smiles, respectful glances, and pleasing words. Once he was comfortably seated on his asana, understanding him to be the confidential messenger of Krishna, the Lord of Rama (Lakshmi), they questioned him in a secluded place.

जानीमस्त्वां यदुपतेः पार्षदं समुपागतम् ।

भर्त्रेह प्रेषितः पित्रोर्भवान् प्रियचिकीर्षया ॥10.47.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: जानीमः we know, त्वाम् you, यदुपतेः of the Chief of the Yadus (Krishna), पार्षदम् a personal associate, समुपागतम् arrived here, भर्त्रा by your master, इह to this place, प्रेषितः sent, पित्रोः of His parents, भवान् you, प्रिय-चिकीर्षया with the desire to do what is pleasing

Anvaya: (वयं) त्वां यदुपतेः पार्षदं समुपागतं जानीमः। भवान् भर्त्रा पित्रोः प्रियचिकीर्षया इह प्रेषितः (अस्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (वयं) We, त्वां you, यदुपतेः of the Lord of the Yadus, पार्षदं personal associate, समुपागतं arrived here, जानीमः know. भवान् You, भर्त्रा by your master, पित्रोः of His parents, प्रियचिकीर्षया out of a desire to bring happiness, इह here, प्रेषितः have been sent.

Simple Meaning: We know you to be a confidential associate of the Lord of the Yadus, who has arrived here. You have certainly been sent here by your master out of His desire to bring happiness and satisfaction to His parents.

अन्यथा गोव्रजे तस्य स्मरणीयं न चक्ष्महे ।

स्नेहानुबन्धो बन्धूनां मुनेरपि सुदुस्त्यजः ॥10.47.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्यथा otherwise, गो-व्रजे in the cowherd pasture (Vraja), तस्य for Him, स्मरणीयम् anything worth remembering, न not, चक्ष्महे we see, स्नेह-अनुबन्धः the bond of affection, बन्धूनाम् towards one's own relatives, मुनेः even for a sage, अपि also, सु-दुस्त्यजः very difficult to renounce

Anvaya: अन्यथा गोव्रजे तस्य स्मरणीयं (किञ्चित्) न चक्ष्महे। बन्धूनां स्नेहानुबन्धः मुनेः अपि सुदुस्त्यजः (भवति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अन्यथा Otherwise, गोव्रजे in the cowherd village of Vraja, तस्य for Him, स्मरणीयं something to be remembered, (किञ्चित् anything), न चक्ष्महे we do not see. बन्धूनां Towards one's own kinsmen, स्नेहानुबन्धः the bond of affection, मुनेः of a detached sage, अपि even, सुदुस्त्यजः is very difficult to give up.

Simple Meaning: Otherwise, we do not see anything else in this cowherd village of Vraja that He would care to remember. Of course, the bond of affection toward one's own family members is very difficult to sever, even for a detached sage.

अन्येष्वर्थकृता मैत्री यावदर्थविडम्बनम् ।

पुम्भिः स्त्रीषु कृता यद्वत् सुमनस्स्विव षट्पदैः ॥10.47.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्येषु toward others, अर्थ-कृता motivated by self-interest, मैत्री friendship, यावत्-अर्थ-विडम्बनम् lasts only as long as the purpose is served (a deceptive imitation), पुम्भिः by men, स्त्रीषु toward women, कृता made, यद्वत् just as, सुमनस्सु toward flowers, इव like, षट्पदैः by bumblebees

Anvaya: अन्येषु अर्थकृता मैत्री यावदर्थविडम्बनम् (भवति), यद्वत् षट्पदैः सुमनस्सु इव पुम्भिः स्त्रीषु कृता (मैत्री भवति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अन्येषु Toward others, अर्थकृता friendship based on utility, यावदर्थविडम्बनम् is a deceptive imitation lasting only until the purpose is fulfilled. यद्वत् Just as, षट्पदैः by bumblebees, सुमनस्सु toward flowers, इव like, पुम्भिः by men, स्त्रीषु toward women, कृता made (friendship is).

Simple Meaning: Friendship with others that is based on self-interest is just a deceptive imitation, lasting only as long as one's purpose is being served. It is exactly like the affection shown by bumblebees toward flowers, or by men toward women, which disappears once their desire is satisfied.

निस्स्वं त्यजन्ति गणिका अकल्पं नृपतिं प्रजाः।

अधीतविद्या आचार्यमृत्विजो दत्तदक्षिणम् ॥10.47.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: निस्स्वम् a penniless man, त्यजन्ति they abandon, गणिकाः prostitutes, अकल्पम् incompetent (or unable to protect), नृपतिम् a king, प्रजाः the subjects, अधीत-विद्याः those who have completed their education, आचार्यम् the teacher, ऋत्विजः the priests, दत्त-दक्षिणम् a patron who has paid his sacrificial fees

Anvaya: गणिकाः निस्स्वं (त्यजन्ति), प्रजाः अकल्पं नृपतिं (त्यजन्ति), अधीतविद्याः आचार्यं (त्यजन्ति), ऋत्विजः दत्तदक्षिणं (यजमानं त्यजन्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गणिकाः Prostitutes, निस्स्वं a man devoid of wealth (त्यजन्ति abandon); प्रजाः the citizens, अकल्पं an incapable, नृपतिं king (त्यजन्ति abandon); अधीतविद्याः students who have completed their learning, आचार्यं the teacher (त्यजन्ति abandon); ऋत्विजः the priests, दत्तदक्षिणं one who has paid the sacrificial fees, (यजमानं त्यजन्ति abandon the patron).

Simple Meaning: Prostitutes abandon a man when he loses his wealth, and citizens desert a king who is no longer capable of protecting them. Students leave their teacher once they have completed their education, and priests depart from a patron as soon as he has paid them their sacrificial fees.

खगा वीतफलं वृक्षं भुक्त्वा चातिथयो गृहम् ।

दग्धं मृगास्तथारण्यं जारो भुक्त्वा रतां स्त्रियम् ॥10.47.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: खगाः birds, वीत-फलम् devoid of fruit, वृक्षम् a tree, भुक्त्वा having enjoyed (the food), च and, अतिथयः guests, गृहम् a house, दग्धम् burnt down, मृगाः animals, तथा similarly, अरण्यम् a forest, जारः a paramour, भुक्त्वा having enjoyed, रताम् lustful (or deeply attached), स्त्रियम् a woman

Anvaya: (यथा) खगाः वीतफलं वृक्षम्, अतिथयः (भोजनं) भुक्त्वा गृहं, मृगाः दग्धम् अरण्यं, तथा जारः (तां) भुक्त्वा रतां स्त्रियम् (त्यजन्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (यथा) Just as, खगाः birds, वीतफलं stripped of fruit, वृक्षम् a tree, अतिथयः guests, (भोजनं) भुक्त्वा having enjoyed their meal, गृहं the house, मृगाः wild animals, दग्धम् burnt, अरण्यं forest, तथा similarly, जारः a paramour, (तां) भुक्त्वा having enjoyed her, रतां स्त्रियम् an attached woman (त्यजन्ति abandon).

Simple Meaning: Birds abandon a tree once it is cleared of its fruits, and guests depart from a house after enjoying a meal. Animals leave a forest that has been ravaged by fire, and in the very same way, a paramour deserts a woman after he has finished enjoying her.

इति गोप्यो हि गोविन्दे गतवाक्कायमानसाः ।

कृष्णदूते व्रजं याते उद्धवे त्यक्तलौकिकाः ॥10.47.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, गोप्यः the gopis, हि indeed, गोविन्दे in Lord Govinda, गत-वाक-काय-मानसाः whose words, bodies, and minds were fully absorbed, कृष्ण-दूते when the messenger of Krishna, व्रजम् to Vraja, याते had come, उद्धवे Uddhava, त्यक्त-लौकिकाः who had abandoned all worldly considerations

Anvaya: कृष्णदूते उद्धवे व्रजं याते (सति), गोविन्दे गतवाक्कायमानसाः त्यक्तलौकिकाः गोप्यः हि इति (अब्रुवन्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्णदूते When the messenger of Krishna, उद्धवे Uddhava, व्रजं to Vraja, याते (सति) had come, गोविन्दे in Govinda, गतवाक्कायमानसाः whose speech, bodies, and minds were entirely directed, त्यक्तलौकिकाः who had abandoned all worldly conventions, गोप्यः the gopis, हि indeed, इति thus (spoke).

Simple Meaning: When Krishna’s messenger Uddhava arrived in Vraja, the gopis—whose speech, bodies, and minds were completely absorbed in Govinda, and who had entirely abandoned all worldly attachments—began to speak in this manner.

गायन्त्यः प्रियकर्माणि रुदत्यश्च गतह्रियः ।

तस्य संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य यानि कैशोरबाल्ययोः ॥10.47.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: गायन्त्यः loudly singing, प्रिय-कर्माणि the dear activities (of their beloved Krishna), रुदत्यः weeping, च and, गत-ह्रियः completely devoid of shyness, तस्य of Him, संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य repeatedly and deeply remembering, यानि which (activities), कैशोर-बाल्ययोः during His youth and childhood.

Anvaya: (ताः गोप्यः) कैशोरबाल्ययोः तस्य यानि प्रियकर्माणि (आसन्), तानि संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य गतह्रियः (सत्यः) गायन्त्यः रुदत्यः च (अभवन्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (ताः गोप्यः) Those gopis, कैशोरबाल्ययोः of His youth and childhood, तस्य His, यानि which, प्रियकर्माणि dear activities (were), तानि them, संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य repeatedly remembering, गतह्रियः (सत्यः) being completely stripped of shyness, गायन्त्यः singing, रुदत्यः च and weeping (became).

Simple Meaning: Intensely and repeatedly remembering all the endearing pastimes Krishna had performed in Vraja during His childhood and youth, the gopis cast aside all worldly shyness, weeping loudly and singing together about their beloved.

काचिन्मधुकरं दृष्ट्वा ध्यायन्ती कृष्णसङ्गमम् ।

प्रियप्रस्थापितं दूतं कल्पयित्वेदमब्रवीत् ॥10.47.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: काचित् a certain gopi, मधुकरम् a bumblebee, दृष्ट्वा seeing, ध्यायन्ती meditating deep within, कृष्ण-सङ्गमम् on her intimate association with Krishna, प्रिय-प्रस्थापितम् sent by her beloved, दूतम् a messenger, कल्पयित्वा imagining it to be, इदम् this, अब्रवीत् spoke

Anvaya: कृष्णसङ्गमं ध्यायन्ती काचित् (गोपी) मधुकरं दृष्ट्वा (तं) प्रियप्रस्थापितं दूतं कल्पयित्वा इदम् अब्रवीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्णसङ्गमं On union with Krishna, ध्यायन्ती deeply meditating, काचित् a certain (gopi), मधुकरं a bumblebee, दृष्ट्वा seeing, (तं him), प्रियप्रस्थापितं sent by her beloved, दूतं a messenger, कल्पयित्वा imagining, इदम् this, अब्रवीत् spoke.

Simple Meaning: Absorbed in deep meditation on her past intimacy with Krishna, a certain gopi noticed a bumblebee flying nearby. Imagining it to be a confidential messenger sent directly by her beloved, she began to address it as follows.

गोपी उवाच

मधुप कितवबन्धो मा स्पृशाङ्घ्रिं सपत्न्याः कुचविलुलितमालाकुङ्कुमश्मश्रुभिर्नः ।

वहतु मधुपतिस्तन्मानिनीनां प्रसादं यदुसदसि विडम्ब्यं यस्य दूतस्त्वमीदृक् ॥10.47.12॥

Simple Meaning: The gopi said: O bumblebee! O friend of a cheater! Do not dare touch my feet with your whiskers, which are stained red with the kumkuma powder gathered from the flower garland crushed against the breasts of my rival! Let your master, the Lord of the Madhus, keep the favors of those proud city ladies. He is already a laughingstock in the royal assembly of the Yadus for employing a deceitful messenger like you.

Simple Meaning: After making us drink the enchanting nectar of His lips just once, He suddenly abandoned us, just as a bumblebee like you deserts flowers after tasting their honey. Alas! I wonder how Goddess Lakshmi can continue to serve His lotus feet. She must certainly be entirely captivated and deceived by the clever, flattering words of that gloriously renowned Lord.

Word-by-word meaning: दिवि in heaven, भुवि on earth, च and, रसायाम् in the subterranean world, काः which, स्त्रियः women, तत्-दुरापाः difficult for Him to attain, कपट-रुचिर-हास-भ्रू-विजृम्भस्य of His deceptive, charming smiles and the arching of His eyebrows, याः which (women), स्युः would become (captivated), चरण-रजः the dust of His feet, उपास्ते worships, यस्य whose, भूतिः Goddess Lakshmi, वयम् we, काः who are we (by comparison)?, अपि च furthermore, कृपण-पक्षे on the side of the wretched / fallen, ही indeed, उत्तमश्लोक-शब्दः the title "Uttamashloka" (He who is praised by choice verses)

Simple Meaning: In heaven, on earth, or in the lower worlds, what woman is difficult for Him to win? They are all easily captivated by the playful movement of His eyebrows and His deceptively charming smiles. Even Goddess Lakshmi constantly worships the dust of His feet—so who are we compared to her? Nevertheless, His title "Uttamasloka" (the Savior of the fallen) truly becomes meaningful only when He shows mercy to wretched and miserable souls like us.

Simple Meaning: O bumblebee! You can stop bowing your head to my feet and trying to placate me. I perfectly understand your purpose in coming here as a messenger with flattering words from Mukunda, who is an expert in false conciliation. Simple Meaning: Like innocent deer who foolishly trust the sweet melody of a hunter's pipe only to be trapped, we naively believed the deceptive words of Krishna to be the absolute truth. Because of that trust, we now repeatedly suffer the agonizing torments of love ignited by the memory of His fingernails' touch. Therefore, O messenger, please stop talking about Him and speak of something else.

Simple Meaning: O dear messenger! O sweet friend! Have you returned here because you were sent once again by my beloved? You are completely respectable to me, so please ask for whatever boon you desire. O gentle bee, how can you expect to take us back to that Lord who is so difficult to abandon, when His consort, Goddess Lakshmi, is already staying constantly with Him, resting upon His chest?

अपि बत मधुपुर्यामार्य पुत्रोऽधुनाऽऽस्ते स्मरति स पितृगेहान् सौम्य बन्धूंश्च गोपान् ।

Simple Meaning: O gentle bee! Is our noble Prince still residing happily in the city of Mathura? Does He ever remember His father's home, His family, and His old cowherd friends? Does He ever even mention the names of us, His poor maidservants? Oh, when will He return and place His comforting arm, sweet with the fragrance of aguru, upon our heads once again?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अथ then, उद्धवः Uddhava, एवं thus (the words of the gopis), निशम्य having heard, कृष्णदर्शनलालसाः who were deeply longing to see Krishna, गोपीः the gopis, प्रियसन्देशैः with the messages of their beloved, सान्त्वयन् comforting, इदम् this, अभाषत spoke.

उद्धव उवाच

दानव्रततपोहोमजपस्वाध्यायसंयमैः ।

श्रेयोभिर्विविधैश्चान्यैः कृष्णे भक्तिर्हि साध्यते ॥10.47.24॥

Anvaya: दानव्रततपोहोमजपस्वाध्यायसंयमैः अन्यैः च विविधैः श्रेयोभिः कृष्णे भक्तिः हि साध्यते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दानव्रततपोहोमजपस्वाध्यायसंयमैः Through charity, vows, penance, fire sacrifices, japa, Vedic study, and sense control, अन्यैः other, च and, विविधैः various types of, श्रेयोभिः auspicious spiritual practices, कृष्णे in Krishna, भक्तिः pure loving devotion, हि indeed, साध्यते is attained.

Simple Meaning: By practicing charity, sacred vows, austerities, fire sacrifices, mantra meditation, scriptural study, self-control, and various other auspicious spiritual disciplines, pure, unalloyed devotion for Lord Krishna is attained.

भगवत्युत्तमश्लोके भवतीभिरनुत्तमा ।

Anvaya: भगवति उत्तमश्लोके भवतीभिः अनुत्तमा भक्तिः दिष्ट्या प्रवर्तिता, (या) मुनीनाम् अपि दुर्लभा (अस्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवति In the Supreme Lord, उत्तमश्लोके Uttamashloka (who is praised by choice verses), भवतीभिः by you all, अनुत्तमा unsurpassed, भक्तिः loving devotion, दिष्ट्या by great fortune, प्रवर्तिता has been exhibited/manifested, (या which), मुनीनाम् even for great sages, अपि also, दुर्लभा difficult to attain (अस्ति is).

Simple Meaning: By your supreme good fortune, you all have developed and manifested an unsurpassed, ultimate stage of pure loving devotion for the Supreme Lord Uttamashloka—a level of devotion that is extremely rare and difficult to attain even for the greatest of self-realized sages.

दिष्ट्या पुत्रान् पतीन् देहान् स्वजनान् भवनानि च ।

श्रूयतां प्रियसन्देशो भवतीनां सुखावहः ।

श्री भगवान् उवाच

भवतीनां वियोगो मे न हि सर्वात्मना क्वचित् ।

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, भवतीनाम् of you all, वियोगः separation, मे from Me, न not, हि indeed, सर्व-आत्मना in any real sense/completely, क्वचित् at any time or place, यथा just as, भूतानि the physical elements, भूतेषु within all living beings, खम् ether/space, वायुः air, अग्निः fire, जलम् water, मही earth, तथा in the same way, अहम् I, च also, मनः-प्राण-भूत-इन्द्रिय-गुण-आश्रयः the ultimate basis and resting place of the mind, life-airs, gross elements, senses, and the modes of nature

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच— भवतीनां मे वियोगः क्वचित् सर्वात्मना न हि (अस्ति)। यथा भूतेषु भूतानि— खं वायुः अग्निः जलं मही (च अन्वितानि सन्ति), तथा अहं च मनःप्राणभूतेन्द्रियगुणाश्रयः (अस्मि)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, भवतीनां Of you all, मे from Me, वियोगः separation, क्वचित् anywhere, सर्वात्मना completely, न हि is absolutely not. यथा Just as, भूतेषु within material bodies, भूतानि the elements— खं वायुः अग्निः जलं मही ether, air, fire, water, earth, (are ever-present), तथा so also, अहं I, च indeed, मनःप्राणभूतेन्द्रियगुणाश्रयः the absolute foundation of the mind, vital breath, elements, senses, and material qualities, (अस्मि am present within you).

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord Krishna said: My dear gopis, there is actually no real separation between us at any time or place. Just as the primary elements—ether, air, fire, water, and earth—are inherently present within all physical bodies, I am naturally and fully present within you as the absolute source and soul of your minds, life-airs, senses, and hearts.

आत्मन्येवात्मनाऽऽत्मानं सृजे हन्म्यनुपालये ।

आत्ममायानुभावेन भूतेन्द्रियगुणात्मना ॥10.47.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: आत्मनि एव within My own self alone, आत्मना by My own self, आत्मानम् Myself (in the form of the universe), सृजे I create, हन्मि I destroy, अनुपालये I sustain/protect, आत्म-माया-अनुभावेन by the influence of My own mystic power (Maya), भूत-इन्द्रिय-गुण-आत्मना which manifests in the form of the material elements, senses, and the modes of nature

Anvaya: (अहं) भूतेन्द्रियगुणात्मना आत्ममायानुभावेन आत्मनि एव आत्मना आत्मानं सृजे हन्मि अनुपालये (च)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (अहं) I, भूतेन्द्रियगुणात्मना which consists of the gross elements, senses, and material qualities, आत्ममायानुभावेन by the influence of My own internal potencies, आत्मनि एव within My own self alone, आत्मना by My own agency, आत्मानं Myself (as the cosmic manifestation), सृजे create, हन्मि destroy, अनुपालये and sustain.

Simple Meaning: By the influence of My own divine energy—which manifests as the material elements, senses, and the modes of nature—I alone create, sustain, and eventually dissolve this entire universe within Myself, using nothing other than My own potencies.

आत्मा ज्ञानमयः शुद्धो व्यतिरिक्तोऽगुणान्वयः ।

सुषुप्तिस्वप्नजाग्रद्भिर्मायावृत्तिभिरीयते ॥10.47.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: आत्मा the soul (and the Supreme Soul), ज्ञान-मयः full of pure knowledge/consciousness, शुद्धः completely pure/untainted, व्यतिरिक्तः distinct (from the material body and mind), अगुण-अन्वयः devoid of any direct connection with the modes of nature (Sattva, Rajas, Tamas), सुषुप्ति-स्वप्न-जाग्रद्भिः by the states of deep sleep, dreaming, and waking, माया-वृत्तिभिः which are functions/operations of the illusory energy, ईयते is perceived/appears to be affected

Anvaya: ज्ञानमयः शुद्धः व्यतिरिक्तः अगुणान्वयः आत्मा मायावृत्तिभिः सुषुप्तिस्वप्नजाग्रद्भिः ईयते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ज्ञानमयः Full of pure awareness, शुद्धः untainted, व्यतिरिक्तः completely distinct, अगुणान्वयः without direct contact with the material modes, आत्मा the individual soul (and the Supersoul), मायावृत्तिभिः through the operations of the material energy, सुषुप्तिस्वप्नजाग्रद्भिः namely the states of deep sleep, dream, and waking, ईयते appears to be involved or perceived.

Simple Meaning: The soul is pure consciousness, entirely untainted, distinct from matter, and completely free from the influence of the three modes of nature. It is only because of the functions of Maya (the illusory energy) that the soul appears to experience and pass through the material states of deep sleep, dreaming, and waking.

येनेन्द्रियार्थान् ध्यायेत मृषा स्वप्नवदुत्थितः ।

तन्निरुन्ध्यादिन्द्रियाणि विनिद्रः प्रत्यपद्यत ॥10.47.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: येन by which (mind), इन्द्रिय-अर्थान् the objects of the senses, ध्यायेत one contemplates, मृषा false/illusory, स्वप्न-वत् like a dream, उत्थितः a person who has awoken, तत् that (mind), निरुन्ध्यात् one should restrain/control, इन्द्रियाणि the senses (also), विनिद्रः being fully awake (free from the sleep of ignorance), प्रत्यपद्यत attains/regains his true spiritual position

Anvaya: उत्थितः (पुरुषः) स्वप्नवत् मृषा येन इन्द्रियार्थान् ध्यायेत, तत् (मनः) इन्द्रियाणि च निरुन्ध्यात्, (ततः) विनिद्रः (सन् आत्मानं) प्रत्यपद्यत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: उत्थितः An awoken person, स्वप्नवत् like a dream, मृषा which are false, येन by which (mind), इन्द्रियार्थान् objects of the senses, ध्यायेत thinks about; तत् that (mind), इन्द्रियाणि च and the senses, निरुन्ध्यात् one should restrain; (ततः then), विनिद्रः fully awake (free from illusion), (सन् being), (आत्मानं oneself/the soul), प्रत्यपद्यत regains/realizes.

Simple Meaning: Just as a person who has awoken realizes that their dream objects were completely illusory, one should understand that the mind's contemplation of material sense objects is equally false. Therefore, controlling the mind and senses, one should cast off the sleep of ignorance and regain their true spiritual consciousness.

एतदन्तः समाम्नायो योगः सांख्यं मनीषिणाम् ।

त्यागस्तपो दमः सत्यं समुद्रान्ता इवापगाः ॥10.47.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतत्-अन्तः ending in this (attaining Me/controlling the mind), समाम्नायः the study of the Vedic scriptures, योगः the practice of mystic yoga, सांख्यम् the analytical study of spirit and matter, मनीषिणाम् of the wise sages, त्यागः renunciation, तपः austerities, दमः control of the senses, सत्यम् truthfulness, समुद्र-अन्ताः ending in the ocean, इव just like, आपगाः the rivers

Anvaya: मनीषिणां समाम्नायः योगः सांख्यं त्यागः तपः दमः सत्यं (च) समुद्रान्ताः आपगाः इव एतदन्तः (भवन्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मनीषिणां Of wise sages, समाम्नायः the study of the Vedas, योगः the path of yoga, सांख्यं the philosophy of Sankhya, त्यागः renunciation, तपः austerity, दमः sense control, सत्यं and truthfulness, समुद्रान्ताः flowing into the ocean, आपगाः rivers, इव like, एतदन्तः end in this alone (attaining Me through mind control).

Simple Meaning: For wise sages, the ultimate goal of studying the Vedas, practicing yoga, analyzing matter and spirit, practicing renunciation, performing austerities, controlling the senses, and strictly adhering to truthfulness is simply to control the mind and attain Me—just as all flowing rivers naturally find their final destination in the ocean.

यत्त्वहं भवतीनां वै दूरे वर्ते प्रियो दृशाम् ।

मनसः सन्निकर्षार्थं मदनुध्यानकाम्यया ॥10.47.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: यत् तु but that, अहम् I, भवतीनाम् of you all, वै indeed, दूरे far away, वर्ते exist, प्रियः the beloved, दृशाम् of the eyes, मनसः of the mind, सन्निकर्ष-अर्थम् for the sake of bringing about closer proximity, मत्-अनुध्यान-काम्यया with the desire to facilitate constant meditation on Me

Anvaya: वै यत् प्रियः (अहं) भवतीनां दृशां तु दूरे वर्ते, मदनुध्यानकाम्यया मनसः सन्निकर्षार्थम् (एव अस्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वै यत् Indeed because, प्रियः beloved, (अहं I), तु although, भवतीनां of you all, दृशां from the eyes, दूरे far away, वर्ते stay, मदनुध्यानकाम्यया with the intent of keeping you absorbed in meditation on Me, मनसः सन्निकर्षार्थम् for the purpose of absolute closeness of the mind.

Simple Meaning: Although I am your beloved, the only reason I am staying physically far away from your sight is to bring you much closer to Me in mind. My absolute purpose is to intensify your constant, unalloyed meditation upon Me.

यथा दूरचरे प्रेष्ठे मन आविश्य वर्तते ।

स्त्रीणां च न तथा चेतः सन्निकृष्टेऽक्षिगोचरे ॥10.47.35॥

Word-by-word meaning: यथा just as, दूर-चरे walking/living far away, प्रेष्ठे upon the most beloved, मनः the mind, आविश्य entering into/fully absorbed, वर्तते remains, स्त्रीणाम् of women, च and, न not, तथा in that manner, चेतः the consciousness, सन्निकृष्टे when nearby, अक्षि-गोचरे within the range of the eyes

Anvaya: यथा दूरचरे प्रेष्ठे स्त्रीणां मनः आविश्य वर्तते, सन्निकृष्टे अक्षिगोचरे (प्रेष्ठे) चेतः तथा न (वर्तते)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यथा Just as, दूरचरे living far away, प्रेष्ठे upon the most beloved, स्त्रीणां of women, मनः the mind, आविश्य entering deeply, वर्तते remains absorbed, सन्निकृष्टे when staying close by, अक्षिगोचरे within the sight of the eyes, (प्रेष्ठे upon the beloved), चेतः the consciousness, तथा in that manner, न does not (remain).

Simple Meaning: When a woman's beloved is far away, her mind becomes completely absorbed in him, constantly dwelling on his qualities and actions. Her consciousness is never as intensely fixed or deeply meditative when he is right in front of her eyes or staying nearby.

मय्यावेश्य मनः कृत्स्नं विमुक्ताशेषवृत्ति यत् ।

अनुस्मरन्त्यो मां नित्यमचिरान्मामुपैष्यथ ॥10.47.36॥

Word-by-word meaning: मयि in Me, आवेश्य fixing/absorbing, मनः the mind, कृत्स्नम् completely, विमुक्त-अशेष-वृत्ति completely freed from all other mental activities (material desires/tendencies), यत् because, अनुस्मरन्त्यः constantly remembering, माम् Me, नित्यम् always, अचिरात् very soon, माम् to Me, उपैष्यथ you will attain

Anvaya: यत् मयि कृत्स्नं मनः आवेश्य, विमुक्ताशेषवृत्ति (कृत्वा), मां नित्यम् अनुस्मरन्त्यः (सन्त्यः), अचिरात् माम् उपैष्यथ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यत् Because, मयि in Me, कृत्स्नं मनः (your) entire mind, आवेश्य having deeply absorbed, विमुक्ताशेषवृत्ति completely freed from all other thoughts or functions, (कृत्वा having made it), मां Me, नित्यम् अनुस्मरन्त्यः constantly remembering, (सन्त्यः being), अचिरात् very soon, माम् Me, उपैष्यथ will achieve/attain.

Simple Meaning: By completely absorbing your entire mind in Me, freeing it entirely from all other mundane thoughts and material tendencies, and by constantly remembering Me at every moment, you will very soon be fully reunited with Me and attain Me.

या मया क्रीडता रात्र्यां वनेऽस्मिन् व्रज आस्थिताः ।

अलब्धरासाः कल्याण्यो माऽऽपुर्मद्वीर्यचिन्तया ॥10.47.37॥

Word-by-word meaning: याः who, मया with Me, क्रीडता while sporting, रात्र्याम् at night, वने अस्मिन् in this forest, व्रजे in Vraja, आस्थिताः stayed back/remained, अलब्ध-रासाः who were unable to join the Rasa dance, कल्याण्यः O auspicious ladies!, माम् Me, आपुः attained, मत्-वीर्य-चिन्तया by meditating deeply on My divine pastimes and glories

Anvaya: हे कल्याण्यः! अस्मिन् वने रात्र्यां क्रीडता मया (सह) याः व्रजे आस्थिताः अलब्धरासाः (ताः) मद्वीर्यचिन्तया माम् आपुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: हे कल्याण्यः O auspicious ladies!, अस्मिन् वने in this forest, रात्र्यां at night, क्रीडता while sporting, मया with Me, (सह along with), याः those who, व्रजे in Vraja, आस्थिताः remained behind, अलब्धरासाः and were unable to join the Rasa dance, (ताः they also), मद्वीर्यचिन्तया through deep meditation on My divine power and glories, माम् Me, आपुः attained.

Simple Meaning: O auspicious gopis! Those who had to stay behind in Vraja and were unable to physically join Me in the forest during the Rasa dance at night attained Me completely. Simply by absorbing their consciousness in deep, unalloyed meditation upon My divine nature and transcendental pastimes, they crossed all boundaries and were reunited with Me.

श्री शुक उवाच

एवं प्रियतमादिष्टमाकर्ण्य व्रजयोषितः ।

ता ऊचुरुद्धवं प्रीतास्तत्सन्देशागतस्मृतीः ॥10.47.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, एवम् thus, प्रियतम-आदिष्टम् the instructions/messages ordered by their most beloved, आकर्ण्य having heard, व्रज-योषितः the women of Vraja (the gopis), ताः they, ऊचुः spoke, उद्धवम् to Uddhava, प्रीताः being highly pleased/satisfied, तत्-सन्देश-आगत-स्मृतीः whose memories of Him were vividly awakened by that message

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— व्रजयोषितः एवं प्रियतमादिष्टम् आकर्ण्य प्रीताः (अभवन्), तत्सन्देशागतस्मृतीः ताः उद्धवम् ऊचुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, व्रजयोषितः the ladies of Vraja, एवं thus, प्रियतमादिष्टम् the message directed by their most beloved, आकर्ण्य having heard, प्रीताः were deeply pleased, तत्सन्देशागतस्मृतीः they whose vivid memories of pastimes were fully revived by that message, ताः they, उद्धवम् to Uddhava, ऊचुः spoke.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Hearing this intimate message sent by their most beloved Krishna, the gopis of Vraja felt deeply comforted and pleased. The message vividly revived the memories of their loving pastimes with Him, and with hearts full of affection, they spoke to Uddhava as follows.

गोप्य ऊचुः

दिष्ट्याहितो हतः कंसो यदूनां सानुगोऽघकृत् ।

दिष्ट्याऽऽप्तैर्लब्धसर्वार्थैः कुशल्यास्तेऽच्युतोऽधुना ॥10.47.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: गोप्यः ऊचुः the gopis said, दिष्ट्या by good fortune, आहितः the enemy/the source of trouble, हतः has been killed, कंसः Kamsa, यदूनाम् of the Yadus, स-अनुगः along with his followers, अघ-कृत् the sinful evildoer, दिष्ट्या by good fortune, आप्तैः by his well-wishing relatives, लब्ध-सर्व-अर्थैः who have obtained all their desires/objectives, कुशली well/happily, आस्ते resides, अच्युतः the infallible Lord (Krishna), अधुना now

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गोप्यः ऊचुः The gopis said, यदूनाम् Of the Yadus, आहितः the great enemy, अघकृत् the sinful worker, कंसः Kamsa, सानुगः along with his followers, दिष्ट्या fortunately, हतः has been slain. लब्धसर्वार्थैः Who have achieved all their goals, आप्तैः with his beloved relatives, अच्युतः the infallible Lord, अधुना now, कुशली comfortably, आस्ते resides.

Simple Meaning: The gopis said: What wonderful, great fortune it is that the sinful Kamsa—the tormentor of the Yadus—has been slain along with all his followers! Krishna is now safe and sound, living happily in the company of His beloved family and friends, who have finally regained everything they had lost.

कच्चिद् गदाग्रजः सौम्य करोति पुरयोषिताम् ।

Word-by-word meaning: कच्चित् whether, गद-अग्रजः Krishna (the elder brother of Gada), सौम्य O gentle Uddhava!, करोति performs/wins, पुर-योषिताम् of the women of the city (Mathura), प्रीतिम् the affection/love, नः our (or like ours), स्निग्ध-सव्रीड-हास्य-उदार-ईक्षण-अर्चितः worshiped or honored by His affectionate, bashful smiles and generous, longing glances

Anvaya: हे सौम्य! नः स्निग्धसव्रीडहासोदारेक्षणार्चितः गदाग्रजः कच्चित् पुरयोषितां प्रीतिं करोति?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: हे सौम्य O gentle one!, नः स्निग्धसव्रीडहासोदारेक्षणार्चितः He who is worshiped/honored by affectionate, bashful smiles and generous, loving glances by us, गदाग्रजः Krishna, कच्चित् whether, पुरयोषितां of the women of the city, प्रीतिं affection, करोति inspires/reciprocates?

Simple Meaning: O gentle Uddhava! Does Krishna, the elder brother of Gada, who is honored and adored by us with affectionate, bashful smiles and deep, loving glances, now win the hearts of the city women of Mathura?

कथं रतिविशेषज्ञः प्रियश्च वरयोषिताम् ।

नानुबध्येत तद्वाक्यैर्विभ्रमैश्चानुभाजितः ॥10.47.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: कथम् how, रति-विशेष-ज्ञः He who is an expert in the nuances of loving pastimes, प्रियः beloved, च and, वर-योषिताम् of the most beautiful, noble women, न not, अनुबध्येत would be bound/captivated, तत्-वाक्यैः by their charming words, विभ्रमैः by their enchanting gestures/movements, च and, अनुभाजितः being deeply influenced/reciprocated

Anvaya: रतिविशेषज्ञः वरयोषितां प्रियः च (सः) तत्वाक्यैः विभ्रमैः च अनुभाजितः (सन्) कथं न अनुबध्येत?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रतिविशेषज्ञः An expert in the arts of love, वरयोषितां of the exquisite city women, प्रियः the beloved, च and, (सः He), तत्वाक्यैः by their sweet words, विभ्रमैः by their alluring gestures, च and, अनुभाजितः being profoundly moved, कथं how, न does He not, अनुबध्येत become captivated/bound?

Simple Meaning: Being an absolute expert in the intricate expressions of love, and now the darling of those sophisticated city women, how could Krishna not be completely charmed and bound by their elegant speech, alluring glances, and graceful, feminine gestures?

अपि स्मरति नः साधो गोविन्दः प्रस्तुते क्वचित् ।

गोष्ठीमध्ये पुरस्त्रीणां ग्राम्याः स्वैरकथान्तरे ॥10.47.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: अपि whether, स्मरति remembers, नः us, साधो O pious/saintly Uddhava!, गोविन्दः Lord Govinda, प्रस्तुते when brought up/occasionally, क्वचित् at any time, गोष्ठी-मध्ये in the assembly/midst, पुर-स्त्रीणाम् of the women of the city, ग्राम्याः village girls/rustic women, स्वैर-कथा-अन्तरे during the course of casual, free conversations

Anvaya: हे साधो! गोविन्दः पुरस्त्रीणां गोष्ठीमध्ये स्वैरकथान्तरे प्रस्तुते क्वचित् ग्राम्याः नः अपि स्मरति?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: हे साधो O saintly one!, गोविन्दः Lord Govinda, पुरस्त्रीणां of the city women, गोष्ठीमध्ये in the gatherings, स्वैरकथान्तरे during the course of informal conversations, प्रस्तुते when topics arise, क्वचित् sometimes, ग्राम्याः rustic village girls, नः us, अपि whether, स्मरति remembers?

Simple Meaning: O saintly Uddhava! Does Govinda ever happen to remember us, the simple, rustic village girls during his casual, free-flowing conversations while sitting in the gatherings of those sophisticated city women?

ताः किं निशाः स्मरति यासु तदा प्रियाभिर्वृन्दावने कुमुदकुन्दशशाङ्करम्ये ।

रेमे क्वणच्चरणनूपुररासगोष्ठ्यामस्माभिरीडितमनोज्ञकथः कदाचित् ॥10.47.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताः those, किम् whether, निशाः nights, स्मरति remembers, यासु in which, तदा then, प्रियाभिः with His beloveds, वृन्दावने in the forest of Vrindavana, कुमुद-कुन्द-शशाङ्क-रम्ये beautiful with night-blooming water lilies, jasmine flowers, and the bright moon, रेमे He rejoiced/sported, क्वणत्-चरण-नूपुर-रास-गोष्ठ्याम् in the gathering of the Rasa dance filled with the tinkling sound of ankle bells, अस्माभिः by us, ईडित-मनोज्ञ-कथः He whose charming glories and pastimes were sung/praised, कदाचित् ever/at all

Anvaya: कुमुदकुन्दशशाङ्करम्ये वृन्दावने यासु (निशासु) अस्माभिः ईडितमनोज्ञकथः (सः) तदा प्रियाभिः (सह) क्वणच्चरणनूपुररासगोष्ठ्यां रेमे, ताः निशाः किं कदाचित् स्मरति?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कुमुदकुन्दशशाङ्करम्ये Beautiful with lilies, jasmines, and the bright moon, वृन्दावने in Vrindavana, यासु on which (nights), अस्माभिः by us, ईडितमनोज्ञकथः He whose enchanting glories were sung, (सः He), तदा then, प्रियाभिः (सह) with His beloved ones, क्वणच्चरणनूपुररासगोष्ठ्यां in the assembly of the Rasa dance resonant with jingling ankle bells, रेमे sported, ताः निशाः those nights, किं whether, कदाचित् ever, स्मरति He remembers?

Simple Meaning: Does He ever remember those enchanting autumn nights in the Vrindavana forest—made breathtakingly beautiful by the blossoming water lilies, sweet jasmines, and the brilliant rays of the full moon? Does He remember when He danced with us, His beloveds, in the joyous circle of the Rasa dance, where our ankle bells jingled in rhythm, and we blissfully sang songs of His charming pastimes?

अप्येष्यतीह दाशार्हस्तप्ताः स्वकृतया शुचा ।

सञ्जीवयन् नु नो गात्रैर्यथेन्द्रो वनमम्बुदैः ॥10.47.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: अपि also, एष्यति will come, इह here (to Vraja), दाशार्हः the descendant of the Dasharha dynasty (Krishna), तप्ताः burnt / suffering intensely, स्व-कृतया caused by Himself (due to separation from Him), शुचा by grief, सञ्जीवयन् reviving, नु indeed, नः us, गात्रैः with the touch of His transcendental limbs, यथा just as, इन्द्रः Indra, वनम् the forest, अम्बुदैः by water-bearing clouds

Anvaya: स्वकृतया शुचा तप्ताः नः, यथा इन्द्रः अम्बुदैः वनं (सञ्जीवयति), तथा (सः) दाशार्हः गात्रैः सञ्जीवयन् इह एष्यति अपि नु?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: स्वकृतया शुचा Tormented by the grief caused by Him, तप्ताः suffering, नः us, यथा Just as, इन्द्रः Lord Indra, अम्बुदैः by rain clouds, वनं the forest, (सञ्जीवयति revives), तथा similarly, (सः He), दाशार्हः the descendant of Dasharha, गात्रैः with His transcendental body (touch), सञ्जीवयन् reviving (us), इह here, एष्यति अपि will come also, नु is it possible?

Simple Meaning: Will He, that descendant of the Dasharha dynasty, ever return here to Vraja? We are burning in the fire of grief caused by our separation from Him—will He come and revive us with the touch of His transcendental limbs, just as Lord Indra revives the parched forest with the rain from his clouds?

कस्मात् कृष्ण इहायाति प्राप्तराज्यो हताहितः ।

नरेन्द्रकन्या उद्वाह्य प्रीतः सर्वसुहृद्वृतः ॥10.47.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: कस्मात् for what reason, कृष्णः Krishna, इह here (to Vraja), आयाति come, प्राप्त-राज्यः who has obtained a kingdom, हत-अहितः who has killed His enemies, नरेन्द्र-कन्याः the daughters of kings (princesses), उद्वाह्य marrying, प्रीतः completely satisfied, सर्व-सुहृत्-वृतः surrounded by all His friends and well-wishers

Anvaya: प्राप्तराज्यः हताहितः नरेन्द्रकन्याः उद्वाह्य प्रीतः सर्वसुहृद्वृतः (च सः) कृष्णः कस्मात् इह आयाति?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्राप्तराज्यः Having obtained a kingdom, हताहितः having destroyed His enemies, नरेन्द्रकन्याः the daughters of kings, उद्वाह्य marrying, प्रीतः well-pleased, सर्वसुहृद्वृतः and surrounded by all His friends, (सः He) कृष्णः Krishna, कस्मात् for what reason, इह here (to Vraja), आयाति would come?

Simple Meaning: But why will Krishna ever return to a simple village like Vraja? He has conquered His enemies, secured a royal kingdom, married beautiful princesses, and is now perfectly happy and content, surrounded by powerful friends and well-wishers.

किमस्माभिर्वनौकोभिरन्याभिर्वा महात्मनः ।

श्रीपतेराप्तकामस्य क्रियेतार्थः कृतात्मनः ॥10.47.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: किम् what, अस्माभिः by us, वन-ओकोभिः by the forest-dwellers, अन्याभिः by other women, वा or, महा-आत्मनः of the great soul, श्री-पतेः of the Lord of the goddess of fortune (Laxmi), आप्त-कामस्य whose every desire is already fulfilled, क्रियेत can be accomplished, अर्थः any purpose/benefit, कृत-आत्मनः who is entirely self-satisfied/perfect

Anvaya: आप्तकामस्य कृतात्मनः श्रीपतेः महात्मनः वनौकोभिः अस्माभिः अन्याभिः वा किम् अर्थः क्रियेत?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: आप्तकामस्य Whose desires are fully satisfied, कृतात्मनः who is perfectly self-sufficient, श्रीपतेः the Lord of the goddess of fortune, महात्मनः of that Supreme Soul, वनौकोभिः by the forest-dwellers, अस्माभिः by us, अन्याभिः वा or by any other women, किम् what, अर्थः purpose, क्रियेत can be fulfilled?

Simple Meaning: After all, He is the Supreme Lord, the master of Laxmi (the goddess of fortune). He is entirely self-sufficient, and every single one of His desires is inherently fulfilled. What possible purpose or happiness could be offered to such a magnificent soul by simple forest-dwellers like us, or by any other women for that matter?

परं सौख्यं हि नैराश्यं स्वैरिण्यप्याह पिङ्गला ।

तज्जानतीनां नः कृष्णे तथाप्याशा दुरत्यया ॥10.47.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: परम् the highest, सौख्यम् happiness, हि indeed, नैराश्यम् having no expectations, स्वैरिणी अपि even the unchaste woman/prostitute, आह said, पिङ्गला Pingala, तत् that, जानतीनाम् of those who know, नः of us, कृष्णे for Krishna, तथापि still/nonetheless, आशा hope/expectation, दुरत्यया impossible to overcome/hard to give up

Anvaya: 'नैराश्यं हि परं सौख्यं' (इति) स्वैरिणी पिङ्गला अपि आह। तत् जानतीनां नः कृष्णे (या) आशा (अस्ति), (सा) तथापि दुरत्यया (अस्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: 'नैराश्यं हि परं सौख्यं' "Freedom from expectation is indeed the highest happiness," (इति thus), स्वैरिणी पिङ्गला अपि even the courtesan Pingala, आह said. तत् That, जानतीनां knowing, नः of us, कृष्णे for Krishna, (या which), आशा hope, (सा that), तथापि still, दुरत्यया is impossible to abandon.

Simple Meaning: Even the famous courtesan Pingala once acknowledged that giving up all expectations is the secret to ultimate happiness. We know this truth well, yet we find it absolutely impossible to abandon our deep, unyielding hope of being reunited with Krishna.

Note: The story of Pingala is narrated in the Eleventh Canto, Eighth Chapter, of Shrimad Bhagavatam.

क उत्सहेत सन्त्यक्तुमुत्तमश्लोकसंविदम् ।

अनिच्छतोऽपि यस्य श्रीरङ्गान्न च्यवते क्वचित् ॥10.47.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: कः who, उत्सहेत can tolerate/would be capable, सन्त्यक्तुम् to completely give up, उत्तम-श्लोक-संविदम् connection with/remembrance of the Lord of glorious renown (Krishna), अनिच्छतः अपि even though (Krishna is) unwilling, यस्य from whose, श्रीः the goddess of fortune (Laxmi), अङ्गात् from the chest/body, न does not, च्यवते stray/leave, क्वचित् at any time

Anvaya: यस्य अनिच्छतः अपि श्रीः अङ्गात् क्वचित् न च्यवते, (तस्य) उत्तमश्लोकसंविदं सन्त्यक्तुं कः उत्सहेत?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यस्य From whom, अनिच्छतः अपि even though He does not desire her, श्रीः the goddess of fortune, अङ्गात् from His chest/body, क्वचित् ever, न च्यवते does not move away, (तस्य His), उत्तमश्लोकसंविदं the loving remembrance of that Lord of glorious renown, सन्त्यक्तुं to abandon, कः who, उत्सहेत could possibly have the heart or power?

Simple Meaning: Who could ever have the power or willingness to give up their loving attachment to Krishna, the Lord of glorious renown? Even though He does not seek her out, the goddess of fortune herself refuses to leave His chest even for a single moment.

सरिच्छैलवनोद्देशा गावो वेणुरवा इमे ।

सङ्कर्षणसहायेन कृष्णेनाचरिताः प्रभो ॥10.47.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: सरित्-शैल-वन-उद्देशाः the rivers, mountains, and forest areas, गावः the cows; वेणु-रवाः the sweet vibrations of His flute, इमे all these, सङ्कर्षण-सहायेन along with His companion Sankarshana (Balarama), कृष्णेन by Krishna, आचरिताः frequented/blessed by His activities, प्रभो O master/O Uddhava!

Anvaya: प्रभो! इमे सरिच्छैलवनोद्देशाः गावः वेणुरवाः (च) सङ्कर्षणसहायेन कृष्णेन आचरिताः (सन्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रभो O master!, इमे these, सरिच्छैलवनोद्देशाः regions of rivers, mountains, and forests, गावः the cows, वेणुरवाः the sounds of the flute, (च and), सङ्कर्षणसहायेन accompanied by Balarama, कृष्णेन by Krishna, आचरिताः have been frequented and played upon.

Simple Meaning: O master Uddhava! All these rivers, mountains, forest glades, the herds of cows, and the lingering echoes of the flute were once beautifully frequented and filled with life by Krishna, alongside His elder brother Balarama.

पुनः पुनः स्मारयन्ति नन्दगोपसुतं बत ।

श्रीनिकेतैस्तत्पदकैर्विस्मर्तुं नैव शक्नुमः ॥10.47.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुनः पुनः again and again, स्मारयन्ति remind (us), नन्द-गोप-सुतम् of Krishna, the son of Nanda, बत alas!, श्री-निकेतैः which are the abodes of all beauty and opulence, तत्-पदकैः by His footprints, विस्मर्तुम् to forget Him, न एव not at all, शक्नुमः we are able

Anvaya: (इमे) श्रीनिकेतैः तत्पदकैः नन्दगोपसुतं पुनः पुनः स्मारयन्ति, बत! (वयं) विस्मर्तुं न एव शक्नुमः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (इमे These places), श्रीनिकेतैः which are the abodes of beauty, तत्पदकैः by His footprints, नन्दगोपसुतं the son of Nanda Gopa, पुनः पुनः again and again, स्मारयन्ति make us remember; बत Alas!, (वयं we), विस्मर्तुं to forget Him, न एव शक्नुमः are not at all able.

Simple Meaning: Alas! All these places are marked with His beautiful footprints, which are the very home of all opulence. These footprints constantly force us to remember the son of Nanda at every single moment, making it absolutely impossible for us to ever forget Him.

गत्या ललितयोदारहासलीलावलोकनैः ।

माध्व्या गिरा हृतधियः कथं तं विस्मरामहे ॥10.47.51॥

Word-by-word meaning: गत्या by His gait/walking style, ललितया beautiful/charming, उदार-हास-लीला-अवलोकनैः by His generous smiles and playful, loving glances, माध्व्या sweet as honey, गिरा by His speech, हृत-धियः we whose minds/intelligence have been completely stolen away, कथम् how, तम् Him, विस्मरामहे can we forget

Anvaya: ललितया गत्या उदारहासलीलावलोकनैः माध्व्या गिरा च हृतधियः (वयं) तं कथं विस्मरामहे?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ललितया By His charming, गत्या gait, उदारहासलीलावलोकनैः by His generous smiles and playful glances, माध्व्या गिरा च and by His honey-sweet speech, हृतधियः we whose hearts and minds have been completely stolen, (वयं we), तं Him, कथं how, विस्मरामहे can forget?

Simple Meaning: He has completely stolen our hearts and minds away with His graceful, charming gait, His generous smiles, His playful glances, and His words that flow like sweet honey. How is it even possible for us to ever forget Him?

हे नाथ हे रमानाथ व्रजनाथार्तिनाशन ।

मग्नमुद्धर गोविन्द गोकुलं वृजिनार्णवात् ॥10.47.52॥

Word-by-word meaning: हे नाथ O Lord/master!, हे रमा-नाथ O master of the goddess of fortune!, व्रज-नाथ O master of Vraja!, आर्ति-नाशन O destroyer of all suffering!, मग्नम् which is drowning, उद्धर please lift up/deliver, गोविन्द O Govinda!, गोकुलम् the entire community of Gokula, वृजिन-अर्णवात् from this vast ocean of misery and despair

Anvaya: हे नाथ! हे रमानाथ! व्रजनाथ! आर्तिनाशन! गोविन्द! वृजिनार्णवात् मग्नं गोकुलम् उद्धर।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: हे नाथ O Lord!, हे रमानाथ O Lord of Rama (Lakshmi)!, व्रजनाथ O Lord of Vraja!, आर्तिनाशन O destroyer of affliction!, गोविन्द O Govinda!, वृजिनार्णवात् in the ocean of sorrow, मग्नं drowning, गोकुलम् Gokula, उद्धर please deliver.

Simple Meaning: O Lord! O master of Lakshmi! O master of Vraja! O destroyer of all distress! O Govinda, please look upon us! The entire community of Gokula is drowning in a vast, overwhelming ocean of grief caused by Your absence—please come and lift us up from this misery!

श्री शुक उवाच

ततस्ताः कृष्णसन्देशैर्व्यपेतविरहज्वराः ।

उद्धवं पूजयाञ्चक्रुर्ज्ञात्वाऽऽत्मानमधोक्षजम् ॥10.47.53॥

वन्दे नन्दव्रजस्त्रीणां पादरेणुमभीक्ष्णशः ।

यासां हरिकथोद्गीतं पुनाति भुवनत्रयम् ॥10.47.63॥

Word-by-word meaning: वन्दे I offer my respectful obeisances, नन्द-व्रज-स्त्रीणाम् of the women of the cowherd pasture of Nanda (the gopis), पाद-रेणुम् the dust of the feet, अभीक्ष्णशः constantly/repeatedly, यासाम् whose, हरि-कथा-उद्गीतम् loud singing of the glories of Lord Hari, पुनाति purifies, भुवन-त्रयम् the three worlds

Anvaya: (अहं) नन्दव्रजस्त्रीणां पादरेणुम् अभीक्ष्णशः वन्दे, यासां हरिकथोद्गीतं भुवनत्रयं पुनाति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (अहं) I, नन्दव्रजस्त्रीणां of the women of Nanda's Vraja, पादरेणुम् the foot-dust, अभीक्ष्णशः repeatedly, वन्दे bow down to, यासां whose, हरिकथोद्गीतं loud singing of the topics of Hari, भुवनत्रयं the three worlds, पुनाति purifies.

Simple Meaning: I repeatedly offer my most respectful obeisances unto the dust of the feet of the women of Nanda's Vraja. Their loud, ecstatic singing of the glories of Lord Hari purifies all the three worlds.

श्री शुक उवाच

अथ गोपीरनुज्ञाप्य यशोदां नन्दमेव च ।

गोपानामन्त्र्य दाशार्हो यास्यन्नारुरुहे रथम् ॥10.47.64॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अथ then, गोपीः the gopis, अनुज्ञाप्य taking permission from, यशोदाम् Yashoda, नन्दम् Nanda, एव also, च and, गोपान् the cowherd men, आमन्त्र्य bidding farewell to, दाशार्हः the descendant of Dasharha (Uddhava), यास्यन् being about to leave, आरुरुहे ascended, रथम् the chariot

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच — अथ दाशार्हः गोपीः यशोदां नन्दम् एव च अनुज्ञाप्य गोपान् आमन्त्र्य यास्यन् रथम् आरुरुहे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अथ then, दाशार्हः the descendant of Dasharha, गोपीः the gopis, यशोदां Yashoda, नन्दम् Nanda, एव aslo, च and, अनुज्ञाप्य having taken permission from, गोपान् the cowherd men, आमन्त्र्य bidding farewell to, यास्यन् being about to depart, रथम् the chariot, आरुरुहे ascended.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Then, Uddhava, the descendant of Dasarha, prepared for his departure. Having taken permission from the gopis, Yashoda, and Nanda, and having bid farewell to all the cowherd men, he ascended his chariot to return.

तं निर्गतं समासाद्य नानोपायनपाणयः ।

नन्दादयोऽनुरागेण प्रावोचन्नश्रुलोचनाः ॥10.47.65॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् Him (Uddhava), निर्गतम् departing, समासाद्य approaching, नाना-उपायन-पाणयः with various kinds of gifts in their hands, नन्द-आदयः Nanda and the other cowherd men, अनुरागेण out of deep affection, प्रावोचन् spoke, अश्रु-लोचनाः their eyes filled with tears

Anvaya: निर्गतं तं समासाद्य नानोपायनपाणयः अश्रुलोचनाः नन्दादयः अनुरागेण प्रावोचन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: निर्गतं Departing, तं him, समासाद्य approaching, नानोपायनपाणयः with various gifts in their hands, अश्रुलोचनाः their eyes filled with tears, नन्दादयः Nanda and others, अनुरागेण out of deep affection, प्रावोचन् spoke.

Simple Meaning: As Uddhava was departing, Nanda and the other cowherd men approached him with various gifts in their hands. Driven by affection, and with their eyes overflowing with tears, they spoke to him.

मनसो वृत्तयो नः स्युः कृष्णपादाम्बुजाश्रयाः ।

वाचोऽभिधायिनीर्नाम्नां कायस्तत्प्रह्वणादिषु ॥10.47.66॥

Word-by-word meaning: मनसः of the mind, वृत्तयः the functions/activities, नः our, स्युः may be, कृष्ण-पाद-अम्बुज-आश्रयाः taking shelter of the lotus feet of Krishna, वाचः words/speech, अभिधायिनीः expressing/uttering, नाम्नाम् His holy names, कायः the body, तत्-प्रह्वण-आदिषु in bowing down to Him and other such services

Anvaya: नः मनसः वृत्तयः कृष्णपादाम्बुजाश्रयाः स्युः, वाचः नाम्नाम् अभिधायिनीः (स्युः), कायः तत्प्रह्वणादिषु (स्यात्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नः Our, मनसः of the mind, वृत्तयः activities, कृष्णपादाम्बुजाश्रयाः taking shelter of the lotus feet of Krishna, स्युः may be, वाचः speech, नाम्नाम् His names, अभिधायिनीः uttering, (स्युः) may be, कायः the body, तत्प्रह्वणादिषु in bowing down to Him and other such acts, (स्यात्) may be occupied.

Simple Meaning: May the thoughts and activities of our minds always take shelter of the lotus feet of Krishna, may our speech be constantly engaged in uttering His holy names, and may our bodies remain completely occupied in offering obeisances to Him and performing His service.

कर्मभिर्भ्राम्यमाणानां यत्र क्वापीश्वरेच्छया ।

मङ्गलाचरितैर्दानै रतिर्नः कृष्ण ईश्वरे ॥10.47.67॥

Word-by-word meaning: कर्मभिः by our own past actions/fruitive activities, भ्राम्यमाणानाम् of us who are wandering, यत्र क्वापि wherever it may be, ईश्वर-इच्छया by the will of the Supreme Lord, मङ्गल-आचरितैः by auspicious deeds/pious activities, दानैः by acts of charity, रतिः deep attachment/love, नः our, कृष्णे in Krishna, ईश्वरे the Supreme Controller

Anvaya: ईश्वरेच्छया कर्मभिः यत्र क्वापि भ्राम्यमाणानां नः मङ्गलाचरितैः दानैः (च) ईश्वरे कृष्णे रतिः (स्यात्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ईश्वरेच्छया By the will of the Supreme Lord, कर्मभिः due to our past actions, यत्र क्वापि wherever it may be, भ्राम्यमाणानां of us who are wandering, नः our, मङ्गलाचरितैः by pious deeds, दानैः by charity, (च) and, ईश्वरे in the Supreme Lord, कृष्णे in Krishna, रतिः attachment, (स्यात्) may there be.

Simple Meaning: By the supreme will of the Lord, wherever we may have to wander in this world according to the reactions of our past actions, may our pious deeds and acts of charity always bear the fruit of granting us deep, unswerving attachment to the Supreme Controller, Lord Krishna.

एवं सभाजितो गोपैः कृष्णभक्त्या नराधिप ।

उद्धवः पुनरागच्छन्मथुरां कृष्णपालिताम् ॥10.47.68॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this manner, सभाजितः honored, गोपैः by the cowherd men, कृष्ण-भक्त्या with devotion to Krishna, नराधिप O King (Pariksit), उद्धवः Uddhava, पुनः back, आगच्छत् came, मथुराम् to Mathura, कृष्ण-पालिताम् protected by Lord Krishna

Anvaya: नराधिप! एवं कृष्णभक्त्या गोपैः सभाजितः उद्धवः कृष्णपालितां मथुरां पुनरागच्छत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नराधिप O King, एवं in this manner, कृष्णभक्त्या with devotion to Krishna, गोपैः by the cowherd men, सभाजितः honored, उद्धवः Uddhava, कृष्णपालितां protected by Lord Krishna, मथुरां to Mathura, पुनरागच्छत् returned.

Simple Meaning: O King, after being thus deeply honored by the cowherd men out of their profound devotion for Krishna, Uddhava returned to Mathura, which was being safely protected by Lord Krishna.

कृष्णाय प्रणिपत्याह भक्त्युद्रेकं व्रजौकसाम् ।

वसुदेवाय रामाय राज्ञे चोपायनान्यदात् ॥10.47.69॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृष्णाय unto Lord Krishna, प्रणिपत्य offering prostrated obeisances, आह he described, भक्ति-उद्रेकम् the intense overflow of devotion, व्रज-ओकसाम् of the inhabitants of Vraja, वसुदेवाय unto Vasudeva, रामाय unto Balarama, राज्ञे unto King Ugrasena, च and, उपायनानि the gifts, अदात् he delivered

Anvaya: (उद्धवः) कृष्णाय प्रणिपत्य व्रजौकसां भक्त्युद्रेकम् आह। वसुदेवाय रामाय राज्ञे च उपायनानि अदात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (उद्धवः) Uddhava, कृष्णाय unto Krishna, प्रणिपत्य offering prostrated obeisances, व्रजौकसां of the inhabitants of Vraja, भक्त्युद्रेकम् the intense overflow of devotion, आह described. वसुदेवाय Unto Vasudeva, रामाय unto Balarama, राज्ञे unto the King, च and, उपायनानि the gifts, अदात् delivered.

Simple Meaning: After offering his prostrated obeisances unto Krishna, Uddhava described the extraordinary, intense overflow of pure devotion possessed by the residents of Vraja. He then delivered the various gifts brought from Vraja to Vasudeva, Balarama, and King Ugrasena.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे पूर्वार्धे “उद्धवगोपीसंवादः तथा भ्रमरगीतं नाम​” सप्तचत्वारिंशः अध्यायः ॥47॥

Thus ends the forty-seventh discourse, named “Dialogue between Uddhava and the Gopis and ode to a black bee” in the first half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
