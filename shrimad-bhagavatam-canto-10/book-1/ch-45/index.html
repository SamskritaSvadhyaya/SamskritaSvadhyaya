Krishna brings back His preceptor's son from the abode of Death and obtains Panchajanya conch

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

गुरुपुत्रानयनं पञ्चजन्यप्राप्तिं च​ नाम पञ्चचत्वारिंशः अध्यायः

(50 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच

पितरावुपलब्धार्थौ विदित्वा पुरुषोत्तमः।

मा भूदिति निजां मायां ततान जनमोहिनीम् ॥10.45.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, पितरौ His parents, उपलब्ध-अर्थौ having understood the truth (of His divinity), विदित्वा knowing, पुरुषोत्तमः the Supreme Lord, मा भूत् let this not be, इति thus (thinking), निजाम् His own, मायाम् illusory energy, ततान expanded or spread, जन-मोहिनीम् which deludes ordinary people

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच - पुरुषोत्तमः पितरौ उपलब्धार्थौ विदित्वा, 'मा भूत्' इति (विचिन्त्य) निजां जनमोहिनीं मायां ततान।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, पुरुषोत्तमः the Supreme Lord, पितरौ His parents, उपलब्धार्थौ having understood the truth, विदित्वा knowing, मा भूत् let this not be, इति thus, निजां His own, जनमोहिनीं which deludes ordinary people, मायां illusory energy, ततान spread.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Knowing that His parents had become aware of His divine nature, the Supreme Lord Krishna, thought, "Let this awareness not get in the way of our parental relationship." He then expanded His own internal illusory energy, which naturally captivates and deludes human minds.

उवाच पितरावेत्य साग्रजः सात्वतर्षभः ।

प्रश्रयावनतः प्रीणन्नम्ब तातेति सादरम् ॥10.45.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: उवाच spoke, पितरौ to His parents, एत्य approaching, स-अग्रजः along with His elder brother (Balarama), सात्वत-ऋषभः the chief of the Satvatas (Krishna), प्रश्रय-अवनतः bowing low with humility, प्रीणन् pleasing them, अम्ब O mother, तात O father, इति thus, स-आदरम् with respect

Anvaya: सात्वतर्षभः साग्रजः पितरौ एत्य, प्रश्रयावनतः (सन्) “अम्ब तात” इति सादरं प्रीणन् उवाच।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सात्वतर्षभः The chief of the Satvatas, साग्रजः along with His elder brother, पितरौ His parents, एत्य approaching, प्रश्रयावनतः bowing low with humility, अम्ब O mother, तात O father, इति thus, सादरं respectfully, प्रीणन् pleasing them, उवाच spoke.

Simple Meaning: Approaching His parents along with His elder brother Balarama, Lord Krishna, the chief of the Satvatas, bowed low with deep humility. Pleasing them by affectionately addressing them as "dear mother" and "dear father," He spoke to them with great respect.

नास्मत्तो युवयोस्तात नित्योत्कण्ठितयोरपि ।

बाल्यपौगण्डकैशोराः पुत्राभ्यामभवन् क्वचित् ॥10.45.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, अस्मत्तः from us, युवयोः of both of you, तात O dear father, नित्य-उत्कण्ठितयोः who were constantly anxiously yearning, अपि even though, बाल्य-पौगण्ड-कैशोराः the stages of childhood, boyhood, and youth, पुत्राभ्याम् from your two sons, अभवन् were experienced, क्वचित् at any time

Anvaya: तात ! नित्योत्कण्ठितयोः अपि युवयोः अस्मत्तः पुत्राभ्यां बाल्यपौगण्डकैशोराः क्वचित् न अभवन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तात O dear father, नित्योत्कण्ठितयोः constantly anxiously yearning, अपि even though, युवयोः of both of you, अस्मत्तः from us, पुत्राभ्यां from your two sons, बाल्यपौगण्डकैशोराः the stages of childhood, boyhood, and youth, क्वचित् at any time, न अभवन् were not experienced.

Simple Meaning: O dear father, even though both of you were constantly and anxiously yearning for us, you could never enjoy the pleasures of our childhood, boyhood, or youth as your sons (since we were away from you all this time).

न लब्धो दैवहतयोर्वासो नौ भवदन्तिके ।

यां बालाः पितृगेहस्था विन्दन्ते लालिता मुदम् ॥10.45.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, लब्धः obtained, दैव-हतयोः of us whose fate is struck down, वासः residence, नौ of the two of us, भवत्-अन्तिके in your presence, याम् which, बालाः children, पितृ-गेह-स्थाः staying in the home of their parents, विन्दन्ते enjoy, लालिताः fondled or pampered, मुदम् happiness

Anvaya: दैवहतयोः नौ भवदन्तिके वासः न लब्धः, यां पितृगेहस्थाः बालाः लालिताः (सन्तः) मुदं विन्दन्ते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दैवहतयोः Of us whose fate is struck down, नौ of the two of us, भवदन्तिके in your presence, वासः residence, न लब्धः was not obtained, यां which, पितृगेहस्थाः staying in the home of their parents, बालाः children, लालिताः fondled, मुदं happiness, विन्दन्ते enjoy.

Simple Meaning: Ill-fated as we are, the two of us could not live in your presence to experience that special happiness which children staying at their parents' home enjoy while being lovingly pampered.

सर्वार्थसम्भवो देहो जनितः पोषितो यतः ।

न तयोर्यानि निर्वेशं पित्रोर्मर्त्यः शतायुषा ॥10.45.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: सर्व-अर्थ-सम्भवः the source of achieving all goals of life, देहः this body, जनितः is produced, पोषितः is nourished, यतः by whom, न not, तयोः of those two, याति goes or achieves, निर्वेशम् repayment of debt, पित्रोः of the parents, मर्त्यः a mortal man, शत-आयुषा even with a lifespan of a hundred years

Anvaya: यतः सर्वार्थसम्भवः देहः जनितः पोषितः (च), तयोः पित्रोः मर्त्यः शतायुषा (अपि) निर्वेशं न याति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यतः By whom, सर्वार्थसम्भवः the source of achieving all goals of life, देहः this body, जनितः is produced, पोषितः and nourished, तयोः पित्रोः to those two parents, मर्त्यः a mortal man, शतायुषा even with a lifespan of a hundred years, निर्वेशं repayment of debt, न याति does not achieve.

Simple Meaning: This mortal body is the very instrument through which a person can achieve all goals of life, including economic prosperity, religious merit, pleasure, and liberation. Since this body is produced and nourished by one's parents, a mortal man cannot repay his debt to them even if he serves them for a full lifespan of a hundred years.

यस्तयोरात्मजः कल्प आत्मना च धनेन च ।

वृत्तिं न दद्यात्तं प्रेत्य स्वमांसं खादयन्ति हि ॥10.45.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः who, तयोः of those two (parents), आत्मजः a son, कल्पः capable, आत्मना with his own physical body, च and, धनेन with wealth, च and, वृत्तिम् maintenance or livelihood, न does not, दद्यात् give, तम् him, प्रेत्य after death, स्व-मांसम् his own flesh, खादयन्ति they make him eat, हि indeed

Anvaya: यः कल्पः आत्मजः आत्मना च धनेन च तयोः वृत्तिं न दद्यात्, प्रेत्य (यमदूताः) तं स्वमांसं खादयन्ति हि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः Who, कल्पः capable, आत्मजः a son, आत्मना by his physical efforts, च and, धनेन by wealth, च and, तयोः of those two parents, वृत्तिं maintenance, न दद्यात् does not give, प्रेत्य after death, तं him, स्वमांसं his own flesh, खादयन्ति they force to eat, हि indeed.

Simple Meaning: A son who is fully capable, yet fails to provide for his parents using his own physical efforts and wealth, is severely punished after death. In the next life, the agents of death force him to eat his own flesh.

मातरं पितरं वृद्धं भार्यां साध्वीं सुतं शिशुम् ।

गुरुं विप्रं प्रपन्नं च कल्पोऽबिभ्रच्छ्वसन् मृतः ॥10.45.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: मातरम् mother, पितरम् father, वृद्धम् old, भार्याम् wife, साध्वीम् chaste or devoted, सुतम् son, शिशुम् an infant, गुरुम् spiritual master, विप्रम् a brahmana, प्रपन्नम् a dependent or one who takes shelter, च and, कल्पः capable, अविभ्रत् failing to maintain, श्वसन् breathing, मृतः is dead

Anvaya: वृद्धं मातरं पितरं साध्वीं भार्यां शिशुं सुतं गुरुं विप्रं प्रपन्नं च कल्पः (सन्) अविभ्रत् श्वसन् (अपि) मृतः (एव)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वृद्धं Old, मातरं mother, पितरं father, साध्वीं भार्यां chaste wife, शिशुं सुतं infant son, गुरुं spiritual master, विप्रं brahmana, प्रपन्नं च and a dependent, कल्पः capable, अविभ्रत् failing to maintain, श्वसन् breathing, मृतः is dead.

Simple Meaning: A person who is capable but fails to maintain his aged parents, his devoted wife, his infant child, his spiritual master, a qualified brahmana, or anyone who has taken shelter of him, is considered dead even though he is still breathing.

तन्नावकल्पयोः कंसान्नित्यमुद्विग्नचेतसोः ।

मोघमेते व्यतिक्रान्ता दिवसा वामनर्चतोः ॥10.45.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् therefore, नौ of us, अकल्पयोः who were unable, कंसात् because of Kamsa, नित्यम् constantly, उद्विग्न-चेतसोः whose minds were anxious, मोघम् in vain, एते these, व्यतिक्रान्ताः have passed, दिवसाः days, वाम् you two, अनर्चतोः without serving or worshipping

Anvaya: तत् कंसात् नित्यम् उद्विग्नचेतसोः अकल्पयोः नौ वाम् अनर्चतोः एते दिवसाः मोघं व्यतिक्रान्ताः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत् Therefore, कंसात् because of Kamsa, नित्यम् constantly, उद्विग्नचेतसोः whose minds were anxious, अकल्पयोः who were unable, नौ of us, वाम् you two, अनर्चतोः without serving, एते these, दिवसाः days, मोघं in vain, व्यतिक्रान्ताः have passed.

Simple Meaning: Therefore, because we were constantly anxious out of fear of Kamsa, we were rendered incapable of acting. All these days have passed in vain without us being able to offer you two our proper service and worship.

तत् क्षन्तुमर्हथस्तात मातर्नौ परतन्त्रयोः ।

अकुर्वतोर्वां शुश्रुषां क्लिष्टयोर्दुर्हृदा भृशम् ॥10.45.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् therefore, क्षन्तुम् to forgive, अर्हथः you two ought, तात O father, मातः O mother, नौ us, पर-तन्त्रयोः dependent on others, अकुर्वतोः not performing, वाम् your, शुश्रूषाम् service, क्लिष्टयोः who were tormented, दुर्हृदा by the cruel-hearted (Kamsa), भृशम् greatly

Anvaya: तात मातः! दुर्हृदा भृशं क्लिष्टयोः परतन्त्रयोः (च) नौ वां शुश्रूषाम् अकुर्वतोः तत् क्षन्तुम् अर्हथः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तात O father, मातः O mother, दुर्हृदा by the cruel-hearted Kamsa, भृशं greatly, क्लिष्टयोः who were tormented, परतन्त्रयोः who were dependent, नौ us, वां your, शुश्रूषाम् service, अकुर्वतोः not performing, तत् therefore, क्षन्तुम् to forgive, अर्हथः you two ought.

Simple Meaning: O mother, O father, because we were completely dependent and constantly tormented by the cruel Kamsa, we were unable to serve you. Therefore, please forgive us for this failure.

श्री शुक उवाच

इति मायामनुष्यस्य हरेर्विश्वात्मनो गिरा ।

मोहितावङ्कमारोप्य परिष्वज्यापतुर्मुदम् ॥10.45.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति thus, माया-मनुष्यस्य of Him who appeared as a human being by His internal potency, हरेः of Lord Hari, विश्व-आत्मनः of the soul of the universe, गिरा by the words, मोहितौ bewildered or captivated by affection, अङ्कम् onto their laps, आरोप्य lifting, परिष्वज्य embracing, आपतुः they achieved, मुदम् great joy

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच—विश्वात्मनः मायामनुष्यस्य हरेः इति गिरा मोहितौ (पितरौ) (तौ) अङ्कम् आरोप्य परिष्वज्य मुदम् आपतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, विश्वात्मनः of the soul of the universe, मायामनुष्यस्य of Lord Hari who played the role of a human child by His internal energy, हरेः of Lord Hari, इति गिरा by these words, मोहितौ captivated by parental affection, तौ parents, अङ्कम् आरोप्य lifting Him onto their laps, परिष्वज्य embracing Him, मुदम् आपतुः achieved great joy.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Thus captivated by the words of Lord Hari, the Supreme Soul of the universe who was playing the role of a human child through His internal potency, His parents, Vasudeva and Devaki, lifted Him onto their laps, embraced Him, and experienced boundless joy.

सिञ्चन्तावश्रुधाराभिः स्नेहपाशेन चावृतौ ।

न किञ्चिदूचतू राजन् बाष्पकण्ठौ विमोहितौ ॥10.45.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: सिञ्चन्तौ moistening or bathing, अश्रु-धाराभिः with streams of tears, स्नेह-पाशेन by the ropes of affection, च and, आवृतौ bound, न not, किञ्चित् anything, ऊचतुः they two spoke, राजन् O King (Parikshit), बाष्प-कण्ठौ whose throats were choked with tears, विमोहितौ bewildered by love

Anvaya: राजन्! स्नेहपाशेन आवृतौ अश्रुधाराभिः (तौ) सिञ्चन्तौ बाष्पकण्ठौ विमोहितौ (च) किञ्चित् न ऊचतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, स्नेहपाशेन by the ropes of affection, आवृतौ bound, अश्रुधाराभिः with streams of tears, सिञ्चन्तौ bathing Him, बाष्पकण्ठौ whose throats were choked with tears, विमोहितौ bewildered by love, किञ्चित् anything, न ऊचतुः they two did not speak.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, bound tightly by the ropes of parental affection, Vasudeva and Devaki bathed Lord Krishna with their streaming tears. Their throats were so choked with emotion and their minds so overwhelmed by love that they could not utter a single word.

एवमाश्वास्य पितरौ भगवान् देवकीसुतः।

मातामहं तूग्रसेनं यदूनामकरोन्नृपम् ॥10.45.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, आश्वास्य comforting, पितरौ His parents, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, देवकी-सुतः the son of Devaki, मातामहम् maternal grandfather, तु indeed, उग्रसेनम् Ugrasena, यदूनामः of the Yadus, अकरोत् made, नृपम् the king

Anvaya: एवं पितरौ आश्वास्य भगवान् देवकीसुतः मातामहं उग्रसेनं तु यदूनां नृपम् अकरोत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवं Thus, पितरौ His parents, आश्वास्य comforting, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, देवकीसुतः the son of Devaki, मातामहं maternal grandfather, उग्रसेनं Ugrasena, तु indeed, यदूनां of the Yadus, नृपम् king, अकरोत् made.

Simple Meaning: After thus comforting His parents, the Supreme Lord, Krishna, the son of Devaki, installed His maternal grandfather, Ugrasena, as the king of the Yadu dynasty.

आह चास्मान् महाराज प्रजाश्चाज्ञप्तुमर्हसि ।

ययाति शापात् यदुभिर्नासितव्यं नृपासने ॥10.45.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: आह said, च and, अस्मान् to us, महाराज O Maharaja, प्रजाः the subjects, च and, आज्ञप्तुम् to command, अर्हसि you ought, ययाति-शापात् because of the curse of King Yayati, यदुभिः by those born in the Yadu dynasty, न आसितव्यम् should not sit, नृप-आसने on the royal throne

Anvaya: (भगवान्) आह च—महाराज! अस्मान् प्रजाः च आज्ञप्तुम् अर्हसि, ययातिशापात् यदुभिः नृपासने न आसितव्यम् (इति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: आह च And He said, महाराज O Maharaja, अस्मान् us, प्रजाः the subjects, च and, आज्ञप्तुम् to command, अर्हसि you ought, ययातिशापात् because of the curse of Yayati, यदुभिः by the Yadus, नृपासने on the royal throne, न आसितव्यम् should not sit.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna said: O Maharaja, you ought to rule over us, your subjects, and issue your commands. Because of the curse of King Yayati, those born in the Yadu dynasty are forbidden from sitting on the royal throne.

मयि भृत्य उपासीने भवतो विबुधादयः ।

बलिं हरन्त्यवनताः किमुतान्ये नराधिपाः ॥10.45.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: मयि when I, भृत्ये as your servant, उपासीने am sitting nearby to attend you, भवतः to you, विबुध-आदयः the demigods and other celestial beings, बलिम् tribute or offerings, हरन्ति will bring, अवनताः bowing down, किम् उत what then (to speak of), अन्ये other, नर-अधिपाः kings of men

Anvaya: मयि भृत्ये उपासीने (सति) अवनताः विबुधादयः भवतः बलिं हरन्ति, अन्ये नराधिपाः किमुत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मयि When I, भृत्ये as a servant, उपासीने am sitting near to attend, अवनताः bowing down, विबुधादयः the demigods and others, भवतः to you, बलिं tribute, हरन्ति will bring, अन्ये other, नराधिपाः kings of men, किमुत what to speak of.

Simple Meaning: When I stand before you as your personal servant to attend to your needs, even the demigods and celestial rulers will bow down and offer you tribute. What to speak, then, of the ordinary kings of this earth.

सर्वान् स्वाञ्ज्ञातिसंबन्धान् दिग्भ्यः कंसभयाकुलान् ।

यदुवृष्ण्यन्धकमधुदाशार्हकुकुरादिकान् ॥10.45.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: सर्वान् all, स्वान् His own, ज्ञाति-संबन्धान् relatives and family members, दिग्भ्यः from various directions, कंस-भय-आकुलान् distressed due to fear of Kamsa, यदु-वृष्णि-अन्धक-मधु-दाशार्ह-कुकुर-आदिकान् belonging to the Yadu, Vrishni, Andhaka, Madhu, Dasharha, Kukura and other clans

Anvaya: कंसभयाकुलान् दिग्भ्यः (पलायितान्) सर्वान् स्वान् ज्ञातिसंबन्धान् यदुवृष्ण्यन्धकमधुदाशार्हकुकुरादिकान् (च)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कंसभयाकुलान् Distressed due to fear of Kamsa, दिग्भ्यः to various directions fleeing, सर्वान् all, स्वान् His own, ज्ञातिसंबन्धान् relatives and family members, यदुवृष्ण्यन्धकमधुदाशार्हकुकुरादिकान् च belonging to the Yadu, Vrishni, Andhaka, Madhu, Dasharha, Kukura and other clans.

Simple Meaning: All of His own relatives and family members—belonging to the Yadu, Vrishni, Andhaka, Madhu, Dasharha, Kukura, and other clans—had fled in all directions, deeply distressed out of fear of Kamsa.

सभाजितान् समाश्वास्य विदेशावासकर्शितान् ।

न्यवासयत् स्वगेहेषु वित्तैः संतर्प्य विश्वकृत् ॥10.45.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: सभाजितान् honoured, समाश्वास्य comforting, विदेश-आवास-कर्शितान् worn out by living in foreign lands, न्यवासयत् He settled them, स्व-गेहेषु in their own homes, वित्तैः with wealth, संतर्प्य fully satisfying, विश्व-कृत् the creator of the universe (Lord Krishna)

Anvaya: विश्वकृत् विदेशावासकर्शितान् (तान्) सभाजितान् समाश्वास्य वित्तैः संतर्प्य स्वगेहेषु न्यवासयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विश्वकृत् The creator of the universe, विदेशावासकर्शितान् worn out by living in foreign lands, (तान्) them, सभाजितान् honouring, समाश्वास्य comforting, वित्तैः with wealth, संतर्प्य fully satisfying, स्वगेहेषु in their own homes, न्यवासयत् settled them.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna, the creator of the universe, honoured and comforted His relatives, who were exhausted from living in exile in foreign lands. Satisfying them thoroughly by giving them abundant wealth, He resettled them comfortably in their own homes.

कृष्णसंकर्षणभुजैर्गुप्ता लब्धमनोरथाः ।

गृहेषु रेमिरे सिद्धाः कृष्णरामगतज्वरा ॥10.45.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृष्ण-संकर्षण-भुजैः by the arms of Krishna and Sankarshana, गुप्ताः protected, लब्ध-मनोरथाः having attained their desires, गृहेषु in their homes, रेमिरे they enjoyed, सिद्धाः completely fulfilled, कृष्ण-राम-गत-ज्वराः whose burning fever of separation was removed by Krishna and Balarama

Anvaya: कृष्णसंकर्षणभुजैः गुप्ताः लब्धमनोरथाः सिद्धाः कृष्णरामगतज्वराः (ते) गृहेषु रेमिरे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्णसंकर्षणभुजैः By the arms of Krishna and Sankarshana, गुप्ताः protected, लब्धमनोरथाः having attained their desires, सिद्धाः completely fulfilled, कृष्णरामगतज्वराः whose burning fever of separation was removed by Krishna and Balarama, ते they, गृहेषु in their homes, रेमिरे enjoyed.

Simple Meaning: Protected by the powerful arms of Krishna and Balarama, the relatives found all their desires fulfilled and felt completely satisfied. Released from the burning fever of long separation from Krishna and Balarama, they lived happily and peacefully in their own homes.

वीक्षन्तोऽहरहः प्रीता मुकुन्दवदनाम्बुजम् ।

नित्यं प्रमुदितं श्रीमत् सदयस्मितवीक्षणम् ॥10.45.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: वीक्षन्तः beholding, अहः अहः day after day, प्रीताः full of love, मुकुन्द-वदन-अम्बुजम् the lotus face of Mukunda, नित्यम् always, प्रमुदितम् cheerful, श्रीमत् beautiful or radiant, स-दय-स्मित-वीक्षणम् with merciful smiling glances

Anvaya: अहः अहः प्रीताः (ते) नित्यं प्रमुदितं श्रीमत् सदयस्मितवीक्षणं मुकुन्दवदनाम्बुजं वीक्षन्तः (रेमिरे)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अहः अहः Day after day, प्रीताः full of love, ते they, नित्यं always, प्रमुदितं cheerful, श्रीमत् radiant, सदयस्मितवीक्षणं filled with merciful smiling glances, मुकुन्दवदनाम्बुजं the lotus face of Mukunda, वीक्षन्तः beholding, (रेमिरे) rejoiced.

Simple Meaning: Filled with deep love, the residents rejoiced day after day as they gazed upon the lotus face of Mukunda, which was always cheerful, radiantly beautiful, and full of merciful, smiling glances.

तत्र प्रवयसोऽप्यासन् युवानोऽतिबलौजसः ।

पिबन्तोऽक्षैर्मुकुन्दस्य मुखाम्बुजसुधां मुहुः ॥10.45.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there (in Mathura), प्रवयसः elderly men, अपि even, आसन् became, युवानः young men, अति-बल-ओजसः possessing extraordinary physical strength and vitality, पिबन्तः drinking, अक्षैः with their eyes, मुकुन्दस्य of Mukunda, मुख-अम्बुज-सुधाम् the nectar of the lotus face, मुहुः repeatedly

Anvaya: तत्र प्रवयसः अपि मुकुन्दस्य मुखाम्बुजसुधां मुहुः अक्षैः पिबन्तः अतिबलौजसः युवानः आसन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत्र There, प्रवयसः elderly men, अपि even, मुकुन्दस्य of Mukunda, मुखाम्बुजसुधां the nectar of the lotus face, मुहुः repeatedly, अक्षैः with their eyes, पिबन्तः drinking, अतिबलौजसः possessing extraordinary strength and vitality, युवानः young men, आसन् became.

Simple Meaning: Even the elderly men in that place regained the vigor of youth, acquiring extraordinary physical strength and vitality, as they drank the nectar of Mukunda's lotus face repeatedly with their eyes.

अथ नन्दं समासाद्य भगवान् देवकीसुतः ।

संकर्षणश्च राजेन्द्र परिष्वज्येदमूचतुः ॥10.45.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथ then, नन्दम् Nanda Maharaja, समासाद्य approaching, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, देवकी-सुतः the son of Devaki, संकर्षणः Sankarshana, च and, राजेन्द्र O King of kings (Parikshit), परिष्वज्य embracing him, इदम् this, ऊचतुः they two said

Anvaya: राजेन्द्र! अथ भगवान् देवकीसुतः संकर्षणः च नन्दं समासाद्य परिष्वज्य इदम् ऊचतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजेन्द्र O King of kings, अथ then, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, देवकीसुतः the son of Devaki, संकर्षणः च and Sankarshana, नन्दं Nanda, समासाद्य approaching, परिष्वज्य embracing him, इदम् this, ऊचतुः they two said.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, after this, the Supreme Lord Krishna, the son of Devaki, along with Balarama, approached Nanda. Embracing him affectionately, they spoke as follows.

पितर्युवाभ्यां स्निग्धाभ्यां पोषितौ लालितौ भृशम् ।

पित्रोरभ्यधिका प्रीतिरात्मजेष्वात्मनोऽपि हि ॥10.45.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: पितः O father, युवाभ्याम् by both of you, स्निग्धाभ्याम् who are full of affection, पोषितौ nourished, लालितौ fondly sustained or cherished, भृशम् greatly, पित्रोः of parents, अभ्यधिका greater, प्रीतिः affection, आत्मजेषु toward their own children, आत्मनः than toward themselves, अपि even, हि indeed

Anvaya: पितः! स्निग्धाभ्यां युवाभ्याम् (आवां) भृशं पोषितौ लालितौ (च), पित्रोः आत्मनः अपि आत्मजेषु अभ्यधिका प्रीतिः (भवति) हि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पितः O father, स्निग्धाभ्यां affectionate, युवाभ्याम् by both of you, (आवां) we two, भृशं greatly, पोषितौ लालितौ nourished, and cherished. पित्रोः Of parents, आत्मनः अपि even than toward themselves, आत्मजेषु toward their own children, अभ्यधिका प्रीतिः greater affection, (भवति) हि indeed is.

Simple Meaning: O father, both of you have nurtured and cherished us with unbounded affection. Indeed, parents love their children far more than their own selves.

स पिता सा च जननी यौ पुष्णीतां स्वपुत्रवत् ।

शिशून् बन्धुभिरुत्सृष्टानकल्पैः पोषरक्षणे ॥10.45.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, पिता father, सा she, च and, जननी mother, यौ who, पुष्णीताम् maintain or nourish, स्व-पुत्र-वत् like their own sons, शिशून् children, बन्धुभिः by relatives, उत्सृष्टान् abandoned, अकल्पैः who were incapable, पोष-क्षणे of maintaining and protecting

Anvaya: यौ पोषरक्षणे अकल्पैः बन्धुभिः उत्सृष्टान् शिशून् स्वपुत्रवत् पुष्णीतां, सः पिता सा च जननी (भवति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यौ Who, पोषरक्षणे in maintaining and protecting, अकल्पैः incapable, बन्धुभिः by relatives, उत्सृष्टान् शिशून् abandoned children, स्वपुत्रवत् like their own sons, पुष्णीतां nourish, सः पिता he is the father, सा च जननी and she is the mother.

Simple Meaning: They are the real father and mother who cherish and protect like their own children those helpless infants abandoned by relatives who were incapable of maintaining them.

यात यूयं व्रजं तात वयं च स्नेहदुःखितान् ।

ज्ञातीन् वो द्रष्टुमेष्यामो विधाय सुहृदां सुखम् ॥10.45.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: यात please go, यूयम् you all, व्रजम् to Vraja, तात O father, वयम् we, च and, स्नेह-दुःखितान् aggrieved due to affection, ज्ञातीन् relatives, वः your, द्रष्टुम् to see, एष्यामः will come, विधाय after securing, सुहृदाम् of our well-wishers, सुखम् the happiness

Anvaya: तात! यूयं व्रजं यात। वयं च सुहृदां सुखं विधाय स्नेहदुःखितान् वः ज्ञातीन् द्रष्टुम् एष्यामः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तात O father, यूयं you all, व्रजं to Vraja, यात please go. वयं च And we, सुहृदां of our well-wishers, सुखं happiness, विधाय after securing, स्नेहदुःखितान् aggrieved due to affection, वः your, ज्ञातीन् relatives, द्रष्टुम् to see, एष्यामः will come.

Simple Meaning: O father, please return to Vraja along with your companions. After we have secured the safety and happiness of our well-wishers here in Mathura, we will surely come to visit you and all our relatives who are deeply grieving out of affection for us.

एवं सान्त्वय्य भगवान् नन्दं सव्रजमच्युतः ।

वासोऽलङ्कारकुप्याद्यैरर्हयामास सादरम् ॥10.45.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this manner, सान्त्वय्य having pacified, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, नन्दम् Nanda, स-व्रजम् along with the people of Vraja, अच्युतः the infallible Lord, वासः-अलङ्कार-कुप्य-आद्यैः with garments, ornaments, items made of base metals like copper and brass and other gifts, अर्हयामास honored, सादरम् respectfully

Anvaya: एवं भगवान् अच्युतः नन्दं सव्रजं सान्त्वय्य वासोऽलङ्कारकुप्याद्यैः सादरम् अर्हयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवं In this manner, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, अच्युतः the infallible Lord, नन्दं Nanda, सव्रजं along with the people of Vraja, सान्त्वय्य having pacified, वासोऽलङ्कारकुप्याद्यैः with garments, ornaments, items made of base metals, and other gifts, सादरम् respectfully, अर्हयामास honored.

Simple Meaning: In this manner, the infallible Supreme Lord Krishna, pacified Nanda and the accompanying residents of Vraja. He then respectfully honored them by presenting gifts of garments, ornaments, valuable utensils, and other offerings.

इत्युक्तस्तौ परिष्वज्य नन्दः प्रणयविह्वलः ।

पूरयन्नश्रुभिर्नेत्रे सह गोपैर्व्रजं ययौ ॥10.45.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, उक्तः addressed, तौ those two (Krishna and Balarama), परिष्वज्य embracing, नन्दः Maharaja Nanda, प्रणय-विह्वलः overwhelmed by love, पूरयन् filling, अश्रुभिः with tears, नेत्रे his eyes, सह along with, गोपैः the cowherd men, व्रजम् to Vraja, ययौ went

Anvaya: इति उक्तः नन्दः प्रणयविह्वलः तौ परिष्वज्य अश्रुभिः नेत्रे पूरयन् गोपैः सह व्रजं ययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति उक्तः Thus addressed, नन्दः Nanda, प्रणयविह्वलः overwhelmed by love, तौ those two, परिष्वज्य embracing, अश्रुभिः with tears, नेत्रे his eyes, पूरयन् filling, गोपैः सह along with the cowherd men, व्रजं to Vraja, ययौ went.

Simple Meaning: Thus addressed by Lord Krishna, Nanda was overwhelmed with deep affectionate love. Embracing both Krishna and Balarama, his eyes filled with tears as he departed for Vraja accompanied by the cowherd men.

अथ शूरसुतो राजन् पुत्रयोः समकारयत् ।

पुरोधसा ब्राह्मणैश्च यथावद् द्विजसंस्कृतिम् ॥10.45.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथ then, शूर-सुतः the son of Shurasena (Vasudeva), राजन् O King (Parikshit), पुत्रयोः of his two sons, समकारयत् caused to be performed, पुरोधसा by the family priest (Garga Muni), ब्राह्मणैः by the Brahmanas, च and, यथावत् properly, द्विज-संस्कृतिम् the purificatory rituals of the twice-born (specifically the sacred thread ceremony)

Anvaya: राजन्! अथ शूरसुतः पुरोधसा ब्राह्मणैः च पुत्रयोः यथावत् द्विजसंस्कृतिं समकारयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, अथ then, शूरसुतः the son of Shurasena, पुरोधसा by the family priest, ब्राह्मणैः च and by the Brahmanas, पुत्रयोः of his two sons, यथावत् properly, द्विजसंस्कृतिं the purificatory rituals of the twice-born, समकारयत् caused to be performed.

Simple Meaning: O King, thereafter Vasudeva, the son of Shurasena, arranged for his family priest Garga Muni and other learned Brahmanas to properly perform the sacred thread ceremony and initiation rituals for his two sons, Krishna and Balarama.

तेभ्योऽदाद् दक्षिणा गावो रुक्ममालाः स्वलंकृताः ।

स्वलंकृतेभ्यः संपूज्य सवत्साः क्षौममालिनीः ॥10.45.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: तेभ्यः to them (the Brahmanas), अदात् gave, दक्षिणाः as sacrificial fees, गावः cows, रुक्म-मालाः with gold necklaces, सु-अलङ्कृताः beautifully decorated, सु-अलङ्कृतेभ्यः to those who were beautifully decorated (the Brahmanas), सम्पूज्य having worshiped, स-वत्साः along with their calves, क्षौम-मालिनीः wearing silken garments and garlands

Anvaya: सः स्वलङ्कृतेभ्यः तेभ्यः सम्पूज्य रुक्ममालाः स्वलङ्कृताः सवत्साः क्षौममालिनीः गावः दक्षिणाः अदात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He (Vasudeva), स्वलङ्कृतेभ्यः to those who were beautifully decorated, तेभ्यः to them, सम्पूज्य having worshiped, रुक्ममालाः with gold necklaces, स्वलङ्कृताः beautifully decorated, सवत्साः along with their calves, क्षौममालिनीः wearing silken garments and garlands, गावः cows, दक्षिणाः as sacrificial fees, अदात् gave.

Simple Meaning: Vasudeva first worshiped the beautifully adorned Brahmanas. He then presented them as sacrificial fees with beautifully decorated cows, who were adorned with gold necklaces, garlands, and silken garments, and were accompanied by their calves.

याः कृष्णरामजन्मर्क्षे मनोदत्ता महामतिः ।

ताश्चाददादनुस्मृत्य कंसेनाधर्मतो हृताः ॥10.45.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: याः which, कृष्ण-राम-जन्म-ऋक्षे on the occasion of the birth stars of Krishna and Balarama, मनः-दत्ताः given within his mind (mentally), महामतिः the broad-minded Vasudeva, ताः those (cows), च and, आददात् gave, अनुस्मृत्य remembering, कंसेन by Kamsa, अधर्मतः unrighteously, हृताः stolen

Anvaya: महामतिः कृष्णरामजन्मर्क्षे याः मनोदत्ताः (गावः) कंसेन अधर्मतः हृताः अनुस्मृत्य ताः च आददात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महामतिः The broad-minded Vasudeva, कृष्णरामजन्मर्क्षे on the occasion of the birth stars of Krishna and Balarama, याः which, मनोदत्ताः given within his mind, कंसेन by Kamsa, अधर्मतः unrighteously, हृताः stolen, अनुस्मृत्य remembering, ताः those (cows), च and, आददात् gave.

Simple Meaning: The broad-minded Vasudeva remembered the cows he had given away mentally on the occasion of Krishna and Balarama's births, which Kamsa had unrighteously stolen from him. Now that those cows were recovered, he formally gave them away in charity as well.

ततश्च लब्धसंस्कारौ द्विजत्वं प्राप्य सुव्रतौ ।

गर्गाद् यदुकुलाचार्याद् गायत्रं व्रतमास्थितौ ॥10.45.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, च and, लब्ध-संस्कारौ having received the purificatory rituals, द्विजत्वम् twice-born status, प्राप्य attaining, सु-व्रतौ fixed in auspicious vows, गर्गात् from Garga Muni, यदु-कुल-आचार्यात् from the spiritual master of the Yadu dynasty, गायत्रम् the Gayatri mantra, व्रतम् the vow of celibacy, आस्थितौ accepted

Anvaya: ततः च सुव्रतौ लब्धसंस्कारौ द्विजत्वं प्राप्य यदुकुलाचार्यात् गर्गात् गायत्रं व्रतम् आस्थितौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः च And then, सुव्रतौ fixed in auspicious vows, लब्धसंस्कारौ having received the purificatory rituals, द्विजत्वं twice-born status, प्राप्य attaining, यदुकुलाचार्यात् from the spiritual master of the Yadu dynasty, गर्गात् from Garga Muni, गायत्रं the Gayatri mantra, व्रतम् the vow of celibacy, आस्थितौ accepted.

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, having properly received the purificatory samskaras and attained twice-born status, the two brothers, who were strictly fixed in their auspicious vows, accepted the sacred Gayatri mantra and the vow of celibacy from Garga Muni, the acharya of the Yadu dynasty.

प्रभवौ सर्वविद्यानां सर्वज्ञौ जगदीश्वरौ ।

नान्यसिद्धामलज्ञानं गूहमानौ नरेहितैः ॥10.45.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रभवौ the source, सर्व-विद्यानाम् of all branches of knowledge, सर्व-ज्ञौ omniscient, जगत्-ईश्वरौ the lords of the universe, न अन्य-सिद्ध-अमल-ज्ञानम् whose flawless knowledge is not perfected by anyone else, गूहमानौ concealing, नर-ईहितैः with human activities

Anvaya: सर्वविद्यानां प्रभवौ सर्वज्ञौ जगदीश्वरौ (तौ) नरेहितैः न अन्यसिद्धामलज्ञानं गूहमानौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सर्वविद्यानां Of all branches of knowledge, प्रभवौ the source, सर्वज्ञौ omniscient, जगदीश्वरौ the lords of the universe, तौ those two, नरेहितैः with human activities, न अन्यसिद्धामलज्ञानं their flawless knowledge which is not perfected by anyone else, गूहमानौ concealing.

Simple Meaning: Although Lord Krishna and Balarama are the original source of all branches of knowledge, are completely omniscient, and are the sovereign lords of the universe, they chose to conceal their inherently perfect, flawless knowledge by acting just like ordinary human children.

अथो गुरुकुले वासमिच्छन्तावुपजग्मतुः।

काश्यं सान्दीपनिं नाम ह्यवन्तीपुरवासिनम् ॥10.45.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथः then, गुरुकुले in the school of the spiritual master, वासम् residence, इच्छन्तौ desiring, उपजग्मतुः they approached, काश्यम् the descendant of Kashi, सान्दीपनिम् Sandipani, नाम named, हि indeed, अवन्ती-पुर-वासिनम् a resident of the city of Avanti

Anvaya: अथः गुरुकुले वासम् इच्छन्तौ (तौ) अवन्तीपुरवासिनं काश्यं सान्दीपनिं नाम हि उपजग्मतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथः Then, गुरुकुले in the school of the spiritual master, वासम् residence, इच्छन्तौ desiring, तौ those two, अवन्तीपुरवासिनं a resident of the city of Avanti, काश्यं the descendant of Kashi, सान्दीपनिं नाम Sandipani named, हि indeed, उपजग्मतुः approached.

Simple Meaning: Then, desiring to reside at the school of the spiritual master, Krishna and Balarama approached the sage Sandipani, a descendant of Kashi who lived in the city of Avanti.

यथोपसाद्य तौ दान्तौ गुरौ वृत्तिमनिन्दिताम् ।

ग्राहयन्तावुपेतौ स्म भक्त्या देवमिवाहतौ ॥10.45.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: यथा properly, उपसाद्य approaching, तौ those two, दान्तौ self-controlled, गुरौ towards the guru, वृत्तिम् conduct, अनिन्दिताम् blameless, ग्राहयन्तौ demonstrating, उपेतौ submissive, स्म indeed, भक्त्या with devotion, देवम् a deity, इव like, आहतौ serving

Anvaya: दान्तौ तौ यथा उपसाद्य अनिन्दितां वृत्तिं ग्राहयन्तौ उपेतौ (सन्तौ) भक्त्या देवम् इव गुरौ आहतौ स्म।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दान्तौ Self-controlled, तौ those two, यथा properly, उपसाद्य approaching, अनिन्दितां blameless, वृत्तिं conduct, ग्राहयन्तौ demonstrating, उपेतौ submissive, (सन्तौ) being, भक्त्या with devotion, देवम् a deity, इव like, गुरौ towards the guru, आहतौ serving, स्म indeed.

Simple Meaning: Approaching their spiritual master properly, those two perfectly self-controlled brothers submissively lived in his ashram. Demonstrating exemplary and blameless conduct, they served their guru with intense devotion just as one would worship a supreme deity.

तयोर्द्वजवरस्तुष्टः शुद्धभावानुवृत्तिभिः ।

प्रोवाच वेदानखिलान् साङ्गोपनिषदो गुरुः ॥10.45.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: तयोः of those two, द्विज-वरः the best of the twice-born (Sandipani Muni), तुष्टः satisfied, शुद्ध-भाव-अनुवृत्तिभिः by their pure motivation and submissive service, प्रोवाच taught, वेदान् the Vedas, अखिलान् all, स-अङ्ग-उपनिषदः along with the ancillary branches and Upanishads, गुरुः the spiritual master

Anvaya: द्विजवरः गुरुः तयोः शुद्धभावानुवृत्तिभिः तुष्टः (सन्) साङ्गोपनिषदः अखिलान् वेदान् प्रोवाच।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: द्विजवरः The best of the twice-born, गुरुः the spiritual master, तयोः of those two, शुद्धभावानुवृत्तिभिः by their pure motivation and submissive service, तुष्टः (सन्) being satisfied, साङ्गोपनिषदः along with the ancillary branches and Upanishads, अखिलान् वेदान् all the Vedas, प्रोवाच taught.

Simple Meaning: That excellent Brahmana and spiritual master, Sandipani Muni, was completely satisfied by the pure motivation and submissive, flawless service of the two brothers. He then thoroughly taught them the entirety of the Vedas, along with all their ancillary branches and the philosophical Upanishads.

सरहस्यं धनुर्वेदं धर्मान् न्यायपथांस्तथा ।

तथा चान्वीक्षिकां विद्यां राजनीतिं च षड्विधाम् ॥10.45.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: स-रहस्यम् along with its confidential secrets, धनुर्वेदम् the science of archery and warfare, धर्मान् the principles of religious duty, न्याय-पथान् the paths of logic and jurisprudence, तथा as well as, तथा च and also, आन्वीक्षिकीम् philosophical inquiry, विद्याम् knowledge, राजनीतिम् statecraft, च and, षड्विधाम् consisting of six political strategies

Anvaya: (तौ) सरहस्यं धनुर्वेदं धर्मान् तथा न्यायपथान् तथा च आन्वीक्षिकां विद्यां षड्विधं राजनीतिं च (अधीतवन्तौ)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (तौ) Those two, सरहस्यं along with its confidential secrets, धनुर्वेदं the science of archery, धर्मान् the principles of religious duty, तथा as well as, न्यायपथान् the paths of logic, तथा च and also, आन्वीक्षिकां विद्यां the knowledge of philosophical inquiry, षड्विधं consisting of six strategies, राजनीतिं च and statecraft, (अधीतवन्तौ) studied.

Simple Meaning: They studied the military science of archery along with its confidential secrets, the principles of religious duty, the paths of logic and law, as well as philosophical inquiry and the sixfold strategies of statecraft.

सर्वं नरवरश्रेष्ठौ सर्वविद्याप्रवर्तकौ ।

सकृन्निगदमात्रेण तौ संजगृहतुर्नृप ॥10.45.35॥

Word-by-word meaning: सर्वम् everything, नर-वर-श्रेष्ठौ the best among the foremost of men, सर्व-विद्या-प्रवर्तकौ the originators of all branches of knowledge, सकृत्-निगद-मात्रेण simply by hearing a single recitation, तौ those two, संजगृहतुः mastered, नृप O King (Parikshit)

Anvaya: नृप! सर्वविद्याप्रवर्तकौ नरवरश्रेष्ठौ तौ सकृन्निगदमात्रेण सर्वं संजगृहतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King, सर्वविद्याप्रवर्तकौ the originators of all branches of knowledge, नरवरश्रेष्ठौ the best among the foremost of men, तौ those two, सकृन्निगदमात्रेण simply by hearing a single recitation, सर्वं everything, संजगृहतुः mastered.

Simple Meaning: O King, Krishna and Balarama are the original authors and promulgators of all branches of knowledge, and They stand supreme among all human beings. Consequently, They completely mastered every subject taught to Them simply by hearing it recited a single time.

अहोरात्रैश्चतुःषष्ट्या संयत्तौ तावतीः कलाः ।

गुरुदक्षिणयाऽऽचार्यं छन्दयामासतुर्नृप ॥10.45.36॥

Word-by-word meaning: अहः-रात्रैः by days and nights, चतुः-षष्ट्या sixty-four, संयत्तौ fully intent and disciplined, तावतीः that many (sixty-four), कलाः arts, गुरु-दक्षिणया with the traditional offering to the teacher, आचार्यम् their spiritual master, छन्दयामासतुः requested him to choose what he desired, नृप O King (Parikshit)

Anvaya: नृप! संयत्तौ तौ चतुःषष्ट्या अहोरात्रैः तावतीः कलाः (अधीत्य) गुरुदक्षिणया आचार्यं छन्दयामासतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King, संयत्तौ fully intent and disciplined, तौ those two, चतुःषष्ट्या sixty-four, अहोरात्रैः by days and nights, तावतीः that many, कलाः arts, (अधीत्य) having learned, गुरुदक्षिणया with the traditional offering to the teacher, आचार्यं their spiritual master, छन्दयामासतुः requested him to choose what he desired.

Simple Meaning: O King, applying themselves with absolute discipline, Krishna and Balarama mastered all sixty-four traditional arts and sciences in just sixty-four days and nights. They then requested their preceptor to name whatever he desired as his guru-dakshina.

द्विजस्तयोस्तं महिमानमद्भुतं संलक्ष्य राजन्नतिमानुषीं मतिम् ।

सम्मन्त्र्य पत्न्या स महार्णवे मृतं बालं प्रभासे वरयाम्बभूव ह ॥10.45.37॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्विजः the Brahmana (Sandipani), तयोः of those two, तम् that, महिमानम् greatness, अद्भुतम् wonderful, संलक्ष्य perceiving, राजन् O King (Parikshit), अति-मानुषीम् superhuman, मतिम् intelligence, सम्मन्त्र्य consulting, पत्न्या with his wife, सः he, महा-अर्णवे in the great ocean, मृतम् dead, बालम् child, प्रभासे at the holy place Prabhasa, वरयाम्बभूव ह requested as his reward indeed

Anvaya: राजन्! सः द्विजः तयोः तम् अद्भुतं महिमानम् अतिमानुषीं मतिं च संलक्ष्य पत्न्या सम्मन्त्र्य प्रभासे महार्णवे मृतं बालं वरयाम्बभूव ह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, सः he, द्विजः the Brahmana, तयोः of those two, तम् that, अद्भुतं wonderful, महिमानम् greatness, अतिमानुषीं superhuman, मतिं intelligence, च and, संलक्ष्य perceiving, पत्न्या with his wife, सम्मन्त्र्य consulting, प्रभासे at Prabhasa, महार्णवे in the great ocean, मृतं dead, बालं child, वरयाम्बभूव ह requested as his reward indeed.

Simple Meaning: O King, perceiving the wonderful greatness and superhuman intelligence of the two brothers, the Brahmana Sandipani consulted with his wife. He then requested as his guru-dakshina the restoration of his young son, who had drowned in the great ocean at the holy place of Prabhasa.

तथेत्यथारुह्य महारथौ रथं प्रभासमासाद्य दुरन्तविक्रमौ ।

वेलामुपव्रज्य निषीदतुः क्षणं सिन्धुर्विदित्वार्हणमाहरत्तयोः ॥10.45.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: तथा इति "so be it", अथ then, आरुह्य mounting, महारथौ the great chariot warriors, रथम् their chariot, प्रभासम् Prabhasa, आसाद्य reaching, दुरन्त-विक्रमौ of limitless prowess, वेलाम् the seashore, उपव्रज्य approaching, निषीदतुः sat, क्षणम् for a moment, सिन्धुः the ocean deity, विदित्वा recognizing (them), अर्हणम् offerings of worship, आहरत् brought, तयोः to them

Anvaya: अथ दुरन्तविक्रमौ महारथौ तौ 'तथा' इति रथम् आरुह्य, प्रभासम् आसाद्य, वेलाम् उपव्रज्य, क्षणं निषीदतुः, सिन्धुः च तयोः विदित्वा अर्हणम् आहरत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ Then, दुरन्तविक्रमौ of limitless prowess, महारथौ the great chariot warriors, तौ those two, 'तथा' इति saying "so be it", रथम् their chariot, आरुह्य mounting, प्रभासम् Prabhasa, आसाद्य reaching, वेलाम् the seashore, उपव्रज्य approaching, क्षणं for a moment, निषीदतुः sat, सिन्धुः the ocean deity, च and, तयोः of them, विदित्वा recognizing, अर्हणम् offerings of worship, आहरत् brought.

Simple Meaning: Replying "so be it," the two master warriors of limitless prowess mounted their chariot and drove to Prabhasa. Approaching the edge of the ocean, they sat on the shore for a moment. Recognizing their supreme identity, the deity of the ocean soon arrived carrying grand offerings of worship for them.

तमाह भगवानाशु गुरुपुत्रः प्रदीयताम् ।

योऽसाविह त्वया ग्रस्तो बालको महतोर्मिणा ॥10.45.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् to him (the ocean deity), आह said, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, आशु immediately, गुरु-पुत्रः the son of our spiritual master, प्रदीयताम् let him be given back, यः असौ he who, इह here, त्वया by you, ग्रस्तः swallowed, बालकः the young boy, महता ऊर्मिणा by a massive wave

Anvaya: भगवान् तम् आशु आह— यः असौ बालकः इह त्वया महता ऊर्मिणा ग्रस्तः, (सः) गुरुपुत्रः प्रदीयताम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवान् The Supreme Lord, तम् to him, आशु आह immediately said, यः असौ he who, बालकः the young boy, इह here, त्वया by you, महता ऊर्मिणा by a massive wave, ग्रस्तः swallowed, (सः) गुरुपुत्रः he, the son of our spiritual master, प्रदीयताम् let him be given back.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord Krishna, immediately addressed the ocean deity, saying: Return the son of our spiritual master, that young boy whom you swallowed up here when he was carried away by a massive wave.

नैवाहार्षमहं देव दैत्य पञ्चजनो महान् ।

अन्तर्जलचरः कृष्ण शङ्खरूपधरोऽसुरः॥10.45.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: समुद्रः उवाच the ocean deity said, न एव not at all, अहार्षम् abducted, अहम् I, देव O Lord, दैत्यः a daitya, पञ्चजनः Panchajana, महान् powerful, अन्तः-जल-चरः moving within the water, कृष्ण O Krishna, शङ्ख-रूप-धरः possessing the form of a conch shell, असुरः an asura

Anvaya: समुद्रः उवाच— देव कृष्ण! अहं (तं बालकं) न एव अहार्षम्। महान् दैत्यः अन्तर्जलचरः शङ्खरूपधरः पञ्चजनः नाम असुरः (अस्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: समुद्रः उवाच The ocean deity said, देव O Lord, कृष्ण O Krishna, अहं I, (तं बालकं) that boy, न एव not at all, अहार्षम् abducted, महान् a powerful, दैत्यः daitya, अन्तर्जलचरः moving within the water, शङ्खरूपधरः possessing the form of a conch shell, पञ्चजनः नाम असुरः an asura named Panchajana is there.

Simple Meaning: The ocean deity said: O Lord Krishna, it was not I who abducted the boy. There is a powerful asura named Panchajana who moves within the depths of my waters in the form of a conchshell.

आस्ते तेनाहृतो नूनं तच्छ्रुत्वा सत्वरं प्रभुः ।

जलमाविश्य तं हत्वा नापश्यदुदरेऽर्भकम् ॥10.45.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: आस्ते he remains, तेन by him (Panchajana), आहृतः taken away, नूनम् surely, तत् that, श्रुत्वा hearing, सत्वरम् quickly, प्रभुः the Lord, जलम् the water, आविश्य entering, तम् him, हत्वा killing, नापश्यत् did not see, उदरे in his belly, अर्भकम् the boy

Anvaya: नूनं तेन आहृतः (बालकः तत्र) आस्ते। तत् श्रुत्वा प्रभुः सत्वरं जलम् आविश्य तं हत्वा उदरे अर्भकं न अपश्यत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नूनं Surely, तेन by him, आहृतः taken away, (बालकः) the boy, (तत्र) आस्ते remains there. तत् That, श्रुत्वा hearing, प्रभुः the Lord, सत्वरं quickly, जलम् the water, आविश्य entering, तं him, हत्वा killing, उदरे in his belly, अर्भकं the boy, न अपश्यत् did not see.

Simple Meaning: The ocean deity continued, "Surely the boy remains captured by him." Hearing this, Lord Krishna quickly entered the water, found the asura, and killed him. However, upon tearing open his belly, the Lord did not find the boy there.

तदङ्गप्रभवं शङ्खमादाय रथमागमत् ।

ततः संयमनीं नाम यमस्य दयितां पुरीम् ॥10.45.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्-अङ्ग-प्रभवम् grown as part of that asura's body, शङ्खम् the conch shell, आदाय taking, रथम् to his chariot, आगमत् returned, ततः then, संयमनीम् Samyamani, नाम named, यमस्य of Yamaraja, दयिताम् the beloved, पुरीम् capital city

Anvaya: (प्रभुः) तदङ्गप्रभवं शङ्खम् आदाय रथम् आगमत्, ततः यमस्य दयितां संयमनीं नाम पुरीं (जगाम)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (प्रभुः) The Lord, तदङ्गप्रभवं grown as part of his body, शङ्खम् the conch shell, आदाय taking, रथम् to his chariot, आगमत् returned. ततः Then, यमस्य of Yamaraja, दयितां the beloved, संयमनीं नाम पुरीं capital city named Samyamani, जगाम went.

Simple Meaning: Taking the conch shell (Panchajanya) that had grown as part of the asura’s body, Lord Krishna returned to His chariot. From there, He and Balarama traveled directly to Samyamani, the beloved capital city of Yamaraja, the lord of death.

गत्वा जनार्दनः शङ्खं प्रदध्मौ सहलायुधः ।

शङ्खनिर्हादमाकर्ण्य प्रजासंयमनो यमः ॥10.45.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: गत्वा arriving there, जनार्दनः Lord Krishna (the maintainer of all living entities), शङ्खम् His conch shell, प्रदध्मौ blew loudly, स-हलायुधः along with Balarama (He who wields a plow weapon), शङ्ख-निर्हादम् the resounding sound of the conch shell, आकर्ण्य hearing, प्रजा-संयमनः the corrector/restrainer of the living beings, यमः Yamaraja

Anvaya: सहलायुधः जनार्दनः (तत्र) गत्वा शङ्खं प्रदध्मौ। प्रजासंयमनः यमः शङ्खनिर्हादम् आकर्ण्य (सहसा समाजगाम)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सहलायुधः Along with Balarama, जनार्दनः Janardana (Krishna), (तत्र) गत्वा arriving there, शङ्खं the conch shell, प्रदध्मौ blew loudly. प्रजासंयमनः The restrainer of the citizens, यमः Yamaraja, शङ्खनिर्हादम् the resounding sound of the conch, आकर्ण्य hearing, (सहसा समाजगाम) quickly arrived.

Simple Meaning: Arriving at Yamaraja's capital alongside Balarama, Lord Janardana blew His conchshell. Hearing that resounding vibration, Yamaraja, the judge and restrainer of all living beings, immediately understood the arrival of a supreme authority.

तयोः सपर्यां महतीं चक्रे भक्त्युपबृंहिताम् ।

उवाचावनतः कृष्णं सर्वभूताशयालयम् ।

लीलामनुष्य हे विष्णो युवयोः करवाम किम् ॥10.45.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: तयोः of those two (Krishna and Balarama), सपर्याम् worship, महतीम् grand, चक्रे performed, भक्ति-उपबृंहिताम् amplified by deep devotion, उवाच spoke, अवनतः bowing down, कृष्णम् to Lord Krishna, सर्व-भूत-आशय-आलयम् who resides as the Supersoul in the hearts of all living beings, लीला-मनुष्य playing the role of ordinary humans for cosmic pastimes, हे विष्णो O Lord Vishnu, युवयोः for the two of you, करवाम किम् what can I do

Anvaya: (सः यमः) तयोः भक्त्युपबृंहितां महतीं सपर्यां चक्रे। अवनतः (सन्) सर्वभूताशयालयं कृष्णम् उवाच— लीलामनुष्य हे विष्णो! युवयोः किं करवाम?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सः यमः) That Yamaraja, तयोः of those two, भक्त्युपबृंहितां amplified by deep devotion, महतीं सपर्यां grand worship, चक्रे performed. अवनतः (सन्) Bowing down, सर्वभूताशयालयं who resides in the hearts of all living beings, कृष्णम् to Lord Krishna, उवाच spoke, लीलामनुष्य हे विष्णो O Lord Vishnu who are playing the role of humans, युवयोः for the two of you, किं करवाम what can I do?

Simple Meaning: Yamaraja executed a grand and reverent worship of the two divine brothers, fueled by deep devotion. Bowing down before Lord Krishna—who is residing in the hearts of all beings, —Yamaraja asked, "O Lord Vishnu, You who are acting as a human child, what service may I perform for the two of you?"

श्री भगवान् उवाच

गुरुपुत्रमिहानीतं निजकर्मनिबन्धनम् ।

आनयस्व महाराज मच्छासनपुरस्कृतः ॥10.45.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, गुरु-पुत्रम् the son of the spiritual master, इह here, आनीतम् brought, निज-कर्म-निबन्धनम् bound by the reactions of his own past deeds, आनयस्व please bring back, महाराज O great king (Yamaraja), मत्-शासन-पुरस्कृतः honoring my command

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच— महाराज! निजकर्मनिबन्धनम् इह आनीतं गुरुपुत्रं मच्छासनपुरस्कृतः आनयस्व।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, महाराज O great king, निजकर्मनिबन्धनम् bound by the conditioning of his own past actions, इह आनीतं brought here to this place, गुरुपुत्रं the son of my guru, मच्छासनपुरस्कृतः honoring my supreme command, आनयस्व please bring back.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord Krishna said, "O great king Yamaraja, my guru's son was brought here to your abode because he was bound by the reactions of his own past karma. Now, honoring my supreme command, please bring him back to me."

तथेति तेनोपानीतं गुरुपुत्रं यदूत्तमौ ।

दत्त्वा स्वगुरवे भूयो वृणीष्वेति तमूचतुः ॥10.45.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: तथा इति saying yes, तेन by him (Yamaraja), उपनीतम् brought near, गुरुपुत्रम् the son of the spiritual master, यदूत्तमौ the two best of the Yadu dynasty (Krishna and Balarama), दत्त्वा having given, स्व-गुरवे to their own spiritual master, भूयो again, वृणीष्व please ask for a boon, इति thus, तम् to him (the guru), ऊचतुः the two of them said

Anvaya: तेन ‘तथा’ इति (उक्त्वा) उपनीतं गुरुपुत्रं यदूत्तमौ स्वगुरवे दत्त्वा ‘भूयः वृणीष्व’ इति तम् ऊचतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तेन By him (Yamaraja), तथा इति saying "let it be so", उपनीतं brought forward, गुरुपुत्रं the son of the guru, यदूत्तमौ the two best of the Yadus, स्वगुरवे to their own guru, दत्त्वा having presented, भूयः again, वृणीष्व please ask for another blessing, इति thus, तम् to him (the guru), ऊचतुः the two of them said.

Simple Meaning: Replying "let it be so," Yamaraja brought the guru's son forward. The two heroes of the Yadu dynasty, Krishna and Balarama, took the boy and presented him to their spiritual master. They then told the guru, "Please ask for another blessing from us."

गुरुः उवाच

सम्यक् संपादितो वत्स गुरुनिष्क्रयः ।

को नु युष्मद्विधगुरोः कामानामवशिष्यते ॥10.45.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: गुरुः उवाच the spiritual master (Sandipani Muni) said, सम्यक् perfectly, संपादितः accomplished, वत्स O dear boys, गुरु-निष्क्रयः the dakshina or payment to the guru, कः what, नु indeed, युष्मत्-विध-गुरोः for a guru who has disciples like the two of you, कामानाम् of desires, अवशिष्यते remains unfulfilled

Anvaya: गुरुः उवाच— वत्स! गुरुनिष्क्रयः सम्यक् संपादितः। युष्मद्विधगुरोः कामां कः नु अवशिष्यते?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गुरुः उवाच The spiritual master said, वत्स O dear boys, गुरुनिष्क्रयः the return payment to the teacher, सम्यक् संपादितः has been completely and perfectly fulfilled. युष्मद्विधगुरोः For a preceptor who has disciples like you, कामां of desires, कः नु अवशिष्यते what indeed remains left over?

Simple Meaning: The guru Sandipani Muni said, "O dear boys, you have completely and perfectly fulfilled the traditional dakshina due to the teacher. For a teacher who is blessed with disciples like you, what desire could possibly remain unfulfilled in this world?"

गच्छतं स्वगृहं वीरौ कीर्तिर्वामस्तु पावनी ।

छन्दांस्ययातयामानि भवन्त्विह परत्र च ॥10.45.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: गच्छतम् please go, स्व-गृहम् to your own homes, वीरौ O heroes, कीर्तिः the fame, वाम् of the two of you, अस्तु let it be, पावनी purifying, छन्दांसि the Vedic hymns, अयात-यामानि ever fresh and potent, भवन्तु let them remain, इह in this life, परत्र च and in the next life

Anvaya: वीरौ! स्वगृहं गच्छतम्। वां पावनी कीर्तिः अस्तु। इह परत्र च छन्दांसि अयातयामानि भवन्तु।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वीरौ O heroes, स्वगृहं to your own homes, गच्छतम् please return. वां Of the two of you, पावनी कीर्तिः purifying fame, अस्तु let there be. इह In this world, परत्र च and in the next world, छन्दांसि the Vedic chants, अयातयामानि ever fresh and effective, भवन्तु let them remain.

Simple Meaning: Sandipani Muni said, "O heroic boys, please return to your homes. May your purifying fame spread everywhere. May the Vedic mantras you have learned remain ever fresh, never losing their power and efficacy for you, both in this lifetime and the next."

गुरुणैवमनुज्ञातौ रथेनानिलरंहसा ।

आयातौ स्वपुरं तात पर्जन्यनिनदेन वै ॥10.45.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: गुरुणा by the spiritual master, एवम् thus, अनुज्ञातौ permitted, रथेन by the chariot, अनिल-रंहसा with the speed of the wind, आयातौ arrived, स्व-पुरम् at their own city (Mathura), तात O dear one (Parikshit), पर्जन्य-निनदेन with a sound like a rumbling rain cloud, वै indeed

Anvaya: तात! गुरुणा एवम् अनुज्ञातौ (तौ) अनिलरंहसा पर्जन्यनिनदेन वै रथेन स्वपुरम् आयातौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तात O dear one, गुरुणा by their spiritual master, एवम् thus, अनुज्ञातौ being permitted, अनिलरंहसा with the speed of the wind, पर्जन्यनिनदेन making a sound like a rain cloud, वै indeed, रथेन by a chariot, स्वपुरम् to their own city, आयातौ they returned.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said, "O dear King Parikshit, having received permission from their spiritual master in this way, the two brothers returned to their own city of Mathura on a chariot that traveled with the speed of the wind and rumbled deeply like a rain cloud."

समनन्दन् प्रजाः सर्वा दृष्ट्वा रामजनार्दनौ ।

अपश्यन्त्यो बह्वहानि नष्टलब्धधना इव ॥10.45.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: समनन्दन् rejoiced, प्रजाः the citizens, सर्वाः all, दृष्ट्वा seeing, राम-जनार्दनौ Balarama and Krishna, अपश्यन्त्यः not seeing, बहु-अहानि for many days, नष्ट-लब्ध-धनाः individuals who had lost and then recovered their wealth, इव like

Anvaya: बह्वहानि (तौ) अपश्यन्त्यः सर्वाः प्रजाः रामजनार्दनौ दृष्ट्वा नष्टलब्धधनाः इव समनन्दन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बह्वहानि For many days, अपश्यन्त्यः not seeing them, सर्वाः प्रजाः all the citizens, रामजनार्दनौ Balarama and Krishna, दृष्ट्वा upon seeing, नष्टलब्धधनाः like those who have regained lost wealth, इव as if, समनन्दन् rejoiced.

Simple Meaning: Having not seen Balarama and Krishna for many days, all the citizens of Mathura rejoiced greatly upon their return. They felt exactly like people who had lost all their valuable wealth and then suddenly recovered it.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे पूर्वार्धे “गुरुपुत्रानयनं पञ्चजन्यप्राप्तिं च​ नाम” पञ्चचत्वारिंशः अध्यायः ॥45॥

Thus ends the forty-fifth discourse named “Krishna brings back His preceptor's son from the abode of Death and obtains Panchajanya conch”, in the first half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
