Chapter 82

The Meeting of Krishna and Balarama with the Gopas and Gopis of Vraja

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

कृष्णबलरामगोपसङ्गमः​ नाम​ द्वावशीतितमः अध्यायः

(48 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
अथैकदा द्वारवत्यां वसतो रामकृष्णयोः ।
सूर्योपरागः सुमहानासीत् कल्पक्षये यथा ॥10.82.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अथ then, एकदा once, द्वारवत्याम् in Dwaraka, वसतोः while residing, राम-कृष्णयोः of Balarama and Krishna, सूर्य-उपरागः solar eclipse, सुमहान् a very great, आसीत् occurred, कल्प-क्षये at the end of a kalpa, यथा just as

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच- अथ एकदा द्वारवत्यां रामकृष्णयोः वसतोः कल्पक्षये यथा (तथा) सुमहान् सूर्योपरागः आसीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अथ एकदा द्वारवत्यां रामकृष्णयोः वसतोः then once while Balarama and Krishna were residing in Dwaraka, कल्पक्षये यथा तथा सुमहान् सूर्योपरागः आसीत् a very great solar eclipse occurred just like at the end of a kalpa.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Once, while Lord Balarama and Lord Krishna were residing in Dwaraka, a total solar eclipse occurred, resembling the darkness at the time of the dissolution of the universe.

तं ज्ञात्वा मनुजा राजन् पुरस्तादेव सर्वतः।
समन्तपञ्चकं क्षेत्रं ययु: श्रेयोविधित्सया ॥10.82.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् that eclipse, ज्ञात्वा knowing, मनुजाः people, राजन् O King Parikshit, पुरस्तात् beforehand, एव indeed, सर्वतः from all directions, समन्त-पञ्चकम् Samantapanchaka, क्षेत्रम् holy place, ययुः went, श्रेयः-विधित्सया with the desire to earn pious merits

Anvaya: राजन्! पुरस्तात् एव तं ज्ञात्वा सर्वतः मनुजाः श्रेयोविधित्सया समन्तपञ्चकं क्षेत्रं ययुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, पुरस्तात् एव तं ज्ञात्वा knowing about that beforehand, सर्वतः मनुजाः people from all directions, श्रेयोविधित्सया with the desire to earn spiritual merit, समन्तपञ्चकं क्षेत्रं ययुः went to the holy pilgrimage place of Samantapanchaka.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, knowing about the upcoming eclipse beforehand, people gathered from all directions and traveled to the holy place of Samantapanchaka with the desire to acquire spiritual merit.

निःक्षत्रियां महीं कुर्वन् रामः शस्त्रभृतां वरः ।
नृपाणां रुधिरौघेण यत्र चक्रे महाह्रदान् ॥10.82.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: निःक्षत्रियाम् devoid of kshatriyas, महीम् the earth, कुर्वन् making, रामः Parashurama, शस्त्र-भृताम् of weapon wielders, वरः the foremost, नृपाणाम् of kings, रुधिर-ओघेण with torrents of blood, यत्र where, चक्रे created, महा-ह्रदान् great lakes

Anvaya: यत्र शस्त्रभृतां वरः रामः महीं निःक्षत्रियां कुर्वन् नृपाणां रुधिरौघेण महाह्रदान् चक्रे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यत्र Where, शस्त्रभृतां वरः रामः the foremost of weapon wielders - Parashurama, महीं निःक्षत्रियां कुर्वन् making the earth devoid of kshatriyas, नृपाणां रुधिरौघेण महाह्रदान् चक्रे created great lakes with torrents of blood of kings.

Simple Meaning: That holy place Samantapanchaka was where Parashurama, the greatest among weapon wielders, rid the earth of kshatriyas and created vast lakes filled with the blood of kings.

ईजे च भगवान् रामो यत्रास्पृष्टोऽपि कर्मणा ।
लोकस्य ग्राहयन्नीशो यथान्योऽघापनुत्तये ॥10.82.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: ईजे worshipped, च and, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, रामः Parashurama, यत्र where, अस्पृष्टः untainted, अपि although, कर्मणा by karmic action, लोकस्य of the world, ग्राहयन् setting an example to be followed, ईशः the Supreme Controller, यथा like, अन्यः an ordinary person, अघ-अपनुत्तये for the eradication of sins

Anvaya: यत्र कर्मणा अस्पृष्टः अपि ईशः भगवान् रामः लोकस्य ग्राहयन् अघापनुत्तये अन्यः यथा (यज्ञैः) ईजे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यत्र Where (at Samantapanchaka), कर्मणा अस्पृष्टः अपि ईशः भगवान् रामः although the Supreme Lord Parashurama is untouched by karmic reactions, लोकस्य ग्राहयन् setting an example for the world to follow, अघापनुत्तये अन्यः यथा ईजे like an ordinary person seeking to cleanse sins, he performed sacrifices.

Simple Meaning: Although Lord Parashurama remains untouched by any karmic actions, he performed sacrifices at Samantapanchaka like an ordinary person to cleanse himself of sins, thereby setting an example for the world to follow.

महत्यां तीर्थयात्रायां तत्रागन् भारतीः प्रजाः ।
वृष्णयश्च तथाक्रूरवसुदेवाहुकादयः ॥10.82.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: महत्याम् on a great, तीर्थ-यात्रायाम् pilgrimage, तत्र there, आगन् came, भारतीः of Bharata Varsha, प्रजाः the people, वृष्णयः the Vrishnis, च and, तथा also, अक्रूर-वसुदेव-आहुक-आदयः Akrura, Vasudeva, Ugrasena and others

आहुक आहुकः N. of a prince, grandfather of Krishna. Src. Kosha. Ashtadhyayi.

Anvaya: तत्र महत्यां तीर्थयात्रायां भारतीः प्रजाः तथा अक्रूरवसुदेवाहुकादयः वृष्णयः च आगन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत्र महत्यां तीर्थयात्रायां There on that great pilgrimage, भारतीः प्रजाः the people of Bharata Varsha, तथा अक्रूरवसुदेवाहुकादयः वृष्णयः च and also the Vrishnis such as Akrura, Vasudeva, Ugrasena and others, आगन् came.

Simple Meaning: On that occasion of a massive pilgrimage, people gathered at Samantapanchaka from all parts of Bharata Varsha, alongside Akrura, Vasudeva, Ugrasena, and other prominent leaders of the Vrishni clan.

ययुर्भारत तत् क्षेत्रं स्वमघं क्षपयिष्णवः ।
गदप्रद्युम्नसाम्बाद्याः सुचन्द्रशुकसारणैः ॥10.82.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: ययुः went, भारत O Scion of Bharata, तत् to that, क्षेत्रम् holy place, स्वम् अघम् their own sins, क्षपयिष्णवः desiring to erase, गद-प्रद्युम्न-साम्ब-आद्याः Gada, Pradyumna, Samba and others, सुचन्द्र-शुक-सारणैः with Suchandra, Shuka and Sarana

Anvaya: भारत! स्वम् अघं क्षपयिष्णवः गदप्रद्युम्नसाम्बाद्याः सुचन्द्रशुकसारणैः (सह) तत् क्षेत्रं ययुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भारत O Scion of Bharata, स्वम् अघं क्षपयिष्णवः desiring to erase their own sins, गदप्रद्युम्नसाम्बाद्याः Gada, Pradyumna, Samba and others, सुचन्द्रशुकसारणैः सह along with Suchandra, Shuka and Sarana, तत् क्षेत्रं ययुः went to that holy place.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, desiring to set an ideal example of eradicating sins, prominent heroes of the Vrishni clan such as Gada, Pradyumna, and Samba, along with Suchandra, Shuka, and Sarana, journeyed to that sacred place.

आस्तेऽनिरुद्धो रक्षायां कृतवर्मा च यूथपः ।
ते रथैर्देवधिष्ण्याभैर्हयैश्च तरलप्लवैः ॥82.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: आस्ते remained, अनिरुद्धः Aniruddha, रक्षायाम् for the protection of Dwaraka, कृतवर्मा Kritavarma, च and, यूथ-पः commander of the army, ते they (the Vrishnis), रथैः on chariots, देव-धिष्ण्याभैः shining like divine celestial dwellings, हयैः with horses, च and, तरल-प्लवैः with speed like waves

Anvaya: अनिरुद्धः यूथपः कृतवर्मा च रक्षायाम् आस्ते। ते देवधिष्ण्याभैः रथैः तरलप्लवैः हयैः च (ययुः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यूथपः कृतवर्मा अनिरुद्धः च Aniruddha and the army commander Kritavarma, रक्षायाम् आस्ते stayed back for protection. ते They, देवधिष्ण्याभैः रथैः on chariots splendid as celestial palaces, तरलप्लवैः हयैः च and with swift galloping horses (departed).

Simple Meaning: Aniruddha  and the commander of the army Kritavarma remained behind to safeguard Dwaraka, while the rest of the Vrishni heroes set out on brilliant chariots resembling divine abodes, pulled by swift and powerful horses.

गजैर्नदद्भिरभ्राभैर्नृभिर्विद्याधरद्युभिः ।
व्यरोचन्त महातेजाः पथि काञ्चनमालिनः ॥10.82.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: गजैः with elephants, नदद्भिः roaring, अभ्र-आभैः resembling dark storm clouds, नृभिः with men, विद्याधर-द्युभिः effulgent like Vidyadharas, व्यरोचन्त shone brilliantly, महा-तेजाः possessing great luster, पथि on the road, काञ्चन-मालिनः wearing golden garlands

Anvaya: अभ्राभैः नदद्भिः गजैः विद्याधरद्युभिः नृभिः (उपेताः) काञ्चनमालिनः महातेजाः (ते) पथि व्यरोचन्त।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अभ्राभैः नदद्भिः गजैः Accompanied by trumpeting elephants resembling dark clouds, विद्याधरद्युभिः नृभिः and by men possessing effulgence like Vidyadharas, काञ्चनमालिनः महातेजाः ते those lustrous heroes adorned with golden garlands, पथि व्यरोचन्त shone resplendently along the road.

Simple Meaning: Accompanied by trumpeting elephants that resembled dark rain clouds and men splendid as Vidyadharas, those highly lustrous Vrishni heroes adorned with golden garlands shone brilliantly as they traveled along the road.

दिव्यस्रग्वस्त्रसन्नाहाः कलत्रैः खेचरा इव ।
तत्र स्नात्वा महाभागा उपोष्य सुसमाहिताः ॥10.82.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: दिव्य-स्रग्-वस्त्र-सन्नाहाः adorned with heavenly garlands, garments and armor, कलत्रैः with their wives, खेचराः celestial beings moving in the sky, इव like, तत्र there at Samantapanchaka, स्नात्वा having bathed, महा-भागाः the highly fortunate ones, उपोष्य fasting, सु-समाहिताः with minds fully concentrated

Anvaya: कलत्रैः (सह) खेचराः इव दिव्यस्रग्वस्त्रसन्नाहाः महाभागाः तत्र स्नात्वा उपोष्य सुसमाहिताः (अभवन्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कलत्रैः सह With their wives, खेचराः इव like the beings moving in the heavens, दिव्यस्रग्वस्त्रसन्नाहाः dressed in celestial garlands fine garments and armor, महाभागाः the highly fortunate Vrishnis, तत्र स्नात्वा उपोष्य there having taken holy bath and observed fast, सुसमाहिताः (अभवन्) became fully self-controlled and attentive.

Simple Meaning: Accompanied by their wives and resplendent like celestial beings in divine garments, garlands, and armor, the fortunate Vrishni heroes took holy baths at Samantapanchaka, observed fasts during the eclipse, and engaged in vows with focused and quiet minds.

ब्राह्मणेभ्यो ददुर्धेनूर्वासः स्रग्रुक्ममालिनीः ।
रामह्रदेषु विधिवत् पुनराप्लुत्य वृष्णयः ॥10.82.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्राह्मणेभ्यः unto the brahmanas, ददुः gave in charity, धेनूः cows, वासः garments, स्रग्-रुक्म-मालिनीः adorned with flower garlands and golden necklaces, राम-ह्रदेषु in the sacred lakes of Lord Parashurama, विधिवत् according to prescribed rituals, पुनः again, आप्लुत्य taking bath, वृष्णयः the Vrishnis

Anvaya: वृष्णयः रामह्रदेषु विधिवत् पुनः आप्लुत्य ब्राह्मणेभ्यः स्रग्रुक्ममालिनीः धेनूः वासः (च) ददुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वृष्णयः The Vrishnis, रामह्रदेषु विधिवत् पुनः आप्लुत्य taking bath again according to scriptural ordinances in the sacred lakes of Parashurama, ब्राह्मणेभ्यः unto the brahmanas, स्रग्रुक्ममालिनीः धेनूः वासः च ददुः gave cows decorated with flower garlands and golden necklaces, as well as fine clothing.

Simple Meaning: The Vrishni heroes took holy baths again in the sacred lakes of Lord Parashurama according to Vedic rituals. They then gifted the brahmanas fine garments and cows beautifully decorated with floral garlands and golden ornaments.

ददुः स्वन्नं द्विजाग्र्येभ्यः कृष्णे नो भक्तिरस्त्विति ।
स्वयं च तदनुज्ञाता वृष्णयः कृष्णदेवताः ॥10.82.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: ददुः they gave, सु-अन्नम् excellent cooked food, द्विज-अग्र्येभ्यः unto the best of the brahmanas, कृष्णे unto Krishna, नः our, भक्तिः devotion, अस्तु may there be, इति praying thus, स्वयम् themselves, च and, तत्-अनुज्ञाताः permitted by those brahmanas, वृष्णयः the Vrishnis, कृष्ण-देवताः who have Krishna as their Lord

Anvaya: कृष्णदेवताः वृष्णयः नः कृष्णे भक्तिः अस्तु — इति द्विजाग्र्येभ्यः स्वन्नं ददुः, तदनुज्ञाताः स्वयं च (भुञ्जते)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्णदेवताः वृष्णयः The Vrishnis who have Krishna as their Lord, नः कृष्णे भक्तिः अस्तु इति praying "may our devotion unto Krishna remain unshakeable", द्विजाग्र्येभ्यः स्वन्नं ददुः offered sumptuous food unto the foremost of brahmanas, तदनुज्ञाताः स्वयं च (भुञ्जते) and with their permission they ate themselves.

Simple Meaning: The Vrishni heroes, who have Lord Krishna as their Lord, offered rich and sumptuous food to the exalted brahmanas, praying, "May we always possess unshakeable devotion unto Lord Krishna." Afterward, with the permission of the brahmanas, they themselves ate.

भुक्त्वोपविविशुः कामं स्निग्धच्छायाङ्घ्रिपाङ्घ्रिषु ।
तत्रागतांस्ते ददृशुः सुहृत्सम्बन्धिनो नृपान् ॥10.82.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: भुक्त्वा having eaten, उपविविशुः sat down, कामम् comfortably, स्निग्ध-छाया-अङ्घ्रिप-अङ्घ्रिषु at the foot of trees yielding dense cool shade, तत्र there, आगतान् who had arrived, ते they, ददृशुः saw, सुहृत्-सम्बन्धिनः friends and relatives, नृपान् kings

Anvaya: (ते) भुक्त्वा स्निग्धच्छायाङ्घ्रिपाङ्घ्रिषु कामम् उपविविशुः। ते तत्र आगतान् सुहृत्सम्बन्धिनः नृपान् ददृशुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (ते) भुक्त्वा They having eaten, स्निग्धच्छायाङ्घ्रिपाङ्घ्रिषु कामम् उपविविशुः sat down comfortably at the foot of trees offering dense cooling shade. ते तत्र आगतान् सुहृत्सम्बन्धिनः नृपान् ददृशुः They saw there kings who were their dear friends and relatives who had also arrived.

Simple Meaning: Having honored their meal, the Vrishnis sat comfortably in the cool, lush shade of spreading trees. There, they met many kings, dear friends, and relatives who had also journeyed from various kingdoms to assemble at the holy site.

मत्स्योशीनरकौसल्यविदर्भकुरुसृञ्जयान् ।
काम्बोजकैकयान् मद्रान् कुन्तीनानर्तकेरलान् ॥10.82.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: मत्स्य-उशीनर-कौसल्य-विदर्भ-कुरु-सृञ्जयान् the rulers and inhabitants of Matsya, Ushinara, Kausalya, Vidarbha, Kuru and Srinjaya, काम्बोज-कैकयान् मद्रान् the rulers and inhabitants of Kamboja, Kaikeya and Madra, कुन्तीन् आनर्त-केरलान् the rulers and inhabitants of Kunti, Anarta and Kerala

Anvaya: (ते) मत्स्योशीनरकौसल्यविदर्भकुरुसृञ्जयान् काम्बोजकैकयान् मद्रान् कुन्तीन् आनर्तकेरलान् (च ददृशुः)।

Word-by-word meaning: रोहिणी Rohini, देवकी Devaki, च and, अथ then, परिष्वज्य having embraced, व्रज-ईश्वरीम् the Queen of Vraja - Yashoda, स्मरन्त्यौ remembering, तत्-कृताम् shown by her Yashoda, मैत्रीम् friendship, बाष्प-कण्ठ्यौ with throats choked with tears, समूचतुः spoke together

Anvaya: अथ रोहिणी देवकी च व्रजेश्वरीं परिष्वज्य तत्कृतां मैत्रीं स्मरन्त्यौ बाष्पकण्ठ्यौ (भूत्वा) समूचतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ Then, रोहिणी देवकी च Rohini and Devaki, व्रजेश्वरीं परिष्वज्य having embraced Yashoda - the Queen of Vraja, तत्कृतां मैत्रीं स्मरन्त्यौ remembering the loving friendship shown by her, बाष्पकण्ठ्यौ with tears choking their throats, समूचतुः spoke together.

Simple Meaning: Then Rohini and Devaki warmly embraced Yashoda, the Queen of Vraja. Recalling the love, care, and friendship she had shown, their voices trembled and their throats grew choked with emotional tears as they addressed her.

का विस्मरेत वां मैत्रीमनिवृत्तां व्रजेश्वरि ।
अवाप्याप्यैन्द्रमैश्वर्यं यस्या नेह प्रतिक्रिया ॥10.82.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: का which woman, विस्मरेत could ever forget, वाम् your, मैत्रीम् loving friendship, अनिवृत्ताम् unceasing/unconditional, व्रज-ईश्वरि O Queen of Vraja Yashoda, अवाप्य having attained, अपि even, ऐन्द्रम् equal to that of King Indra, ऐश्वर्यम् royal opulence, यस्याः for which friendship, न not, इह in this world, प्रतिक्रिया any adequate return/repayment

Anvaya: व्रजेश्वरि! ऐन्द्रम् ऐश्वर्यम् अवाप्य अपि वाम् अनिवृत्ताम् मैत्रीं का विस्मरेत यस्याः इह प्रतिक्रिया न (भवति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: व्रजेश्वरि O Queen of Vraja, ऐन्द्रम् ऐश्वर्यम् अवाप्य अपि even after attaining opulence equal to Lord Indra, वाम् अनिवृत्ताम् मैत्रीं your (Nanda and Yashoda’s) unceasing friendship, का विस्मरेत who could forget, यस्याः for which friendship, इह in this world, प्रतिक्रिया न exists no repayment.

Simple Meaning: Rohini and Devaki said: O Queen of Vraja Yashoda! Even if one were to gain the grand kingdom and royal opulence of Lord Indra himself, who could ever forget the unconditional love and friendship you showered upon us? Nothing in this world could ever serve as an adequate repayment for what you have done.

एतावदृष्टपितरौ युवयोः स्म पित्रोः सम्प्रीणनाभ्युदयपोषणपालनानि ।
प्राप्योषतुर्भवति पक्ष्म ह यद्वदक्ष्णोर्न्यस्तावकुत्र च भयौ न सतां परः स्वः ॥10.82.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतौ these two, अदृष्ट-पितरौ not having seen their own birth parents Devaki and Vasudeva, युवयोः of both of you, स्म indeed, पित्रोः as their parents, सम्प्रीणना-अभ्युदय-पोषण-पालनानि affection, protection, rearing and loving care, प्राप्य having received, ऊषतुः lived, भवति O noble lady Yashoda, पक्ष्म eyelids, ह indeed, यद्वत् just as, अक्ष्णोः of the eyes, न्यस्तौ placed under your shelter, अकुत्र च अभयौ completely free from fear anywhere, न not, सताम् for saintly noble souls, परः an outsider, स्वः one's own

Anvaya: (हे) भवति! अदृष्टपितरौ एतौ पित्रोः युवयोः सम्प्रीणनाभ्युदयपोषणपालनानि प्राप्य अक्ष्णोः पक्ष्म यद्वत् अकुत्र च अभयौ न्यस्तौ ऊषतुः, (यतः) सतां परः स्वः न (अस्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (हे) भवति O noble lady Yashoda, अदृष्टपितरौ unable to see their own parents, एतौ these two boys, पित्रोः युवयोः from both of you as their parents, सम्प्रीणनाभ्युदयपोषणपालनानि receiving affection, prosperity, nourishment and protection, अक्ष्णोः पक्ष्म यद्वत् just as eyelids protect the eyes, अकुत्र च अभयौ completely fearless anywhere, न्यस्तौ sheltered under your care, ऊषतुः lived all this time, यतः because, सतां for saintly souls, परः स्वः न (अस्ति) there is no division between stranger and one's own.

Simple Meaning: Rohini and Devaki continued: O noble Yashoda! Before these two boys had ever known or seen their birth parents, they lived under the loving care of you and Nanda. You gave them love, nourishment, protection, and upbringing. Just as eyelids protect the eyes, you sheltered Them so that They never experienced fear anywhere. Indeed, for saintly souls like you, there is no distinction between one's own child and another's.

श्री शुक उवाच
गोप्यश्च कृष्णमुपलभ्य चिरादभीष्टं यत्प्रेक्षणे दृशिषु पक्ष्मकृतं शपन्ति ।
दृग्भिर्हृदीकृतमलं परिरभ्य सर्वास्तद्भावमापुरपि नित्ययुजां दुरापम् ॥10.82.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, गोप्यः the gopis, च and, कृष्णम् Krishna, उपलभ्य having obtained, चिरात् after a long time, अभीष्टम् their most cherished desire, यत्-प्रेक्षणे while looking at whom, दृशिषु on their eyes, पक्ष्म-कृतम् the creator of eyelids Lord Brahma, शपन्ति used to curse, दृग्भिः through their eyes, हृदीकृतम् brought into their hearts, अलम् fully, परिरभ्य embracing Him, सर्वाः all of them, तत्-भावम् that state of supreme ecstatic union, आपुः attained, अपि even though, नित्य-युजाम् for great yogis continuously merged in meditation, दुरापम् difficult to attain

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच — यत्प्रेक्षणे दृशिषु पक्ष्मकृतं शपन्ति (ताः) सर्वाः गोप्यः चिरात् अभीष्टं कृष्णम् उपलभ्य दृग्भिः हृदीकृतम् अलं परिरभ्य नित्ययुजाम् अपि दुरापं तद्भावम् आपुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, यत्प्रेक्षणे while gazing upon whom, दृशिषु पक्ष्मकृतं शपन्ति they used to curse the creator of eyelids, ताः सर्वाः गोप्यः all those gopis, चिरात् after a long time, अभीष्टं कृष्णम् उपलभ्य having regained Krishna their heart's desire, दृग्भिः through their eyes, हृदीकृतम् having brought Him inside their hearts, अलं परिरभ्य fully embracing Him, नित्ययुजाम् अपि दुरापं difficult to reach even for steadfast yogis, तद्भावम् आपुः attained that sublime state of absorption.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: The gopis—who previously cursed Lord Brahma for creating eyelids that blinked and interrupted their viewing of Krishna—finally met their most beloved Lord after a long separation. Taking Him inside their hearts through the doorway of their eyes, they embraced Him within. In doing so, they attained a transcendental state of absorption so profound that it remains virtually unreachable even for great yogis constantly absorbed in meditation.

भगवांस्तास्तथाभूता विविक्त उपसङ्गतः ।
आश्लिष्यानामयं पृष्ट्वा प्रहसन्निदमब्रवीत् ॥10.82.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: भगवान् the Supreme Lord Krishna, ताः them (the gopis), तथा-भूताः absorbed in that ecstatic state of love, विविक्ते in a secluded private place, उपसङ्गतः approaching, आश्लिष्य having embraced, अनामयम् wellbeing and welfare, पृष्ट्वा having asked about, प्रहसन् smiling gently, इदम् this, अब्रवीत् spoke

Anvaya: भगवान् तथाभूताः ताः विविक्ते उपसङ्गतः (ताः) आश्लिष्य अनामयं पृष्ट्वा प्रहसन् इदम् अब्रवीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवान् The Supreme Lord Krishna, तथाभूताः ताः those gopis who were so deeply absorbed in devotion, विविक्ते in a quiet place, उपसङ्गतः approaching, ताः आश्लिष्य embracing them, अनामयं पृष्ट्वा inquiring about their health and welfare, प्रहसन् smiling affectionately, इदम् अब्रवीत् said this.

Simple Meaning: Seeing the gopis completely absorbed in pure ecstatic love for Him, the Supreme Lord Krishna approached them in a quiet, private area. He embraced them warmly, inquired about their welfare, and with a gentle smile spoke to them as follows.

अपि स्मरथ नः सख्यः स्वानामर्थचिकीर्षया ।
गतांश्चिरायिताञ्छत्रुपक्षक्षपणचेतसः ॥10.82.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: अपि whether, स्मरथ do you remember, नः us, सख्यः O dear friends gopis, स्वानाम् of Our relatives, अर्थ-चिकीर्षया with the desire to accomplish the benefit and welfare, गतान् having gone away, चिरायितान् having stayed away for a long time, शत्रु-पक्ष-क्षपण-चेतसः Our mind intently focused on destroying the enemy faction Kamsa and his allies

Anvaya: सख्यः! स्वानाम् अर्थचिकीर्षया गतान् शत्रुपक्षक्षपणचेतसः चिरायितान् नः अपि स्मरथ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सख्यः O dear friends, स्वानाम् अर्थचिकीर्षया with a desire to secure the welfare of Our family members, गतान् having departed, शत्रुपक्षक्षपणचेतसः Our mind occupied with vanquishing the enemy forces, चिरायितान् having been delayed for so long, नः अपि स्मरथ do you still remember us.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna said: O Friends! Do you even remember us, who had gone away for a long time, to serve the purpose of their own people and were engaged in destroying the enemy side.

अप्यवध्यायथास्मान् स्विदकृतज्ञाविशङ्कया ।
नूनं भूतानि भगवान् युनक्ति वियुनक्ति च ॥10.82.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: अपि whether, अवध्यायथ did you think ill of, अस्मान् Us, स्वित् perhaps, अकृत-ज्ञ-विशङ्कया suspecting Us to be ungrateful, नूनम् surely, भूतानि all living beings, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, युनक्ति joins together, वियुनक्ति separates, च and

Anvaya: अकृतज्ञाविशङ्कया अस्मान् अपि अवध्यायथ स्वित्, नूनं भगवान् भूतानि युनक्ति वियुनक्ति च।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अकृतज्ञाविशङ्कया Suspecting Us of being ungrateful, अस्मान् अपि अवध्यायथ स्वित् did you perhaps harbor ill thoughts toward Us, नूनं surely, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, भूतानि living beings, युनक्ति joins together, वियुनक्ति च and separates.

Simple Meaning: Did you perhaps think ill of Us, suspecting Us of being ungrateful for your love? In truth, it is the Supreme Lord who brings living beings together and subsequently separates them.

वायुर्यथा घनानीकं तृणं तूलं रजांसि च ।
संयोज्याक्षिपते भूयस्तथा भूतानि भूतकृत् ॥10.82.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: वायुः the wind, यथा just as, घन-अनीकम् a mass of clouds, तृणम् grass, तूलम् cotton, रजांसि dust, च and, संयोज्य having brought together, आक्षिपते scatters away, भूयः again, तथा in the same way, भूतानि living beings, भूत-कृत् the creator of all beings Supreme Lord

Anvaya: यथा वायुः घनानीकं तृणं तूलं रजांसि च संयोज्य भूयः आक्षिपते, तथा भूतकृत् भूतानि (संयोज्य भूयः वियुनक्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यथा वायुः Just as the wind, घनानीकं तृणं तूलं रजांसि च masses of clouds, grass, cotton and particles of dust, संयोज्य having brought together, भूयः आक्षिपते scatters them apart once more, तथा in the same manner, भूतकृत् the Creator of all beings, भूतानि (संयोज्य भूयः वियुनक्ति) brings living beings together and then separates them.

Simple Meaning: Just as the wind gathers together masses of clouds, blades of grass, strands of cotton, and particles of dust, only to scatter them far apart once again, so does the Supreme Creator bring living entities together in temporary relationships and then separate them according to the movement of time.

मयि भक्तिर्हि भूतानाममृतत्वाय कल्पते ।
दिष्ट्या यदासीन्मत्स्नेहो भवतीनां मदापनः ॥10.82.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: मयि unto Me, भक्तिः loving devotional service, हि indeed, भूतानाम् for all living entities, अमृतत्वाय for eternal life or liberation, कल्पते leads to, दिष्ट्या by good fortune, यत् that, आसीत् there is, मत्-स्नेहः affection toward Me, भवतीनाम् of all of you gopis, मत्-आपनः that which enables you to attain Me directly

Anvaya: मयि भक्तिः हि भूतानाम् अमृतत्वाय कल्पते, दिष्ट्या भवतीनां यः मत्स्नेहः (अस्ति, सः) मदापनः आसीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मयि भक्तिः Loving devotion unto Me, हि indeed, भूतानाम् for living beings, अमृतत्वाय कल्पते leads to immortality, दिष्ट्या by good fortune, भवतीनां of all of you, यः मत्स्नेहः that affection for Me, मदापनः आसीत् brings about direct attainment of Me.

Simple Meaning: Devotional service rendered unto Me leads all living beings to eternal liberation and immortality. Fortunately for you, your pure love for Me has enabled you to attain Me directly.

अहं हि सर्वभूतानामादिरन्तोऽन्तरं बहिः ।
भौतिकानां यथा खं वार्भूर्वायुर्ज्योतिरङ्गनाः ॥10.82.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: अहम् I, हि indeed, सर्व-भूतानाम् of all created living entities, आदिः the origin, अन्तः the end, अन्तरम् inside, बहिः outside, भौतिकानाम् of all material objects made of elements, यथा just as, खम् ether, वाः water, भूः earth, वायुः air, ज्योतिः light or fire, अङ्गनाः O beautiful ladies

Anvaya: अङ्गनाः! यथा खं वाः भूः वायुः ज्योतिः भौतिकानां (आदिः अन्तः अन्तरं बहिः भवति), तथा अहं हि सर्वभूतानाम् आदिः अन्तः अन्तरं बहिः (अस्मि)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अङ्गनाः O beautiful women, यथा just as, खं वाः भूः वायुः ज्योतिः sky, water, earth, air and light, भौतिकानां of all material physical creations, (आदिः अन्तः अन्तरं बहिः भवति) are the source, destruction, interior and exterior, तथा in the same manner, अहं हि I indeed, सर्वभूतानाम् of all living beings, आदिः the beginning, अन्तः the end, अन्तरं inside, बहिः outside (अस्मि am).

Simple Meaning: O beautiful ladies, just as the five basic elements—ether, water, earth, air, and light—are the origin, dissolution, interior, and exterior of all physical objects created from them, so am I the ultimate cause, destruction, inner soul, and outer reality of all living beings.

एवं ह्येतानि भूतानि भूतेष्वात्माऽऽत्मना ततः ।
उभयं मय्यथ परे पश्यताभातमक्षरे ॥10.82.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this manner, हि indeed, एतानि all these, भूतानि created elements and bodies, भूतेषु within the physical elements, आत्मा the Supreme Soul, आत्मना through His own divine potency, ततः therefore, उभयम् both the material manifestations and the individual souls, मयि in Me, अथ and, परे the supreme, पश्यत see, आभातम् as illuminated/manifested, अक्षरे in the imperishable

Anvaya: एवम् एतानि भूतानि भूतेषु आत्मना (प्रविष्टानि), ततः परे अक्षरे मयि उभयम् आभातम् (इति) पश्यत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवम् Thus, एतानि भूतानि these created beings and material elements, भूतेषु within the elemental forms, आत्मना by His own spiritual power, ततः therefore, परे अक्षरे मयि in Me, who am the supreme and imperishable, उभयम् both matter and spirit, आभातम् (इति) as manifested, पश्यत see.

Simple Meaning: In this way, all created beings and material elements are pervaded by the Supreme Self through His own divine power. Therefore, perceive both the material creation and the individual living entities as existing within Me, the supreme, imperishable, and ultimate truth.

श्री शुक उवाच
अध्यात्मशिक्षया गोप्य एवं कृष्णेन शिक्षिताः ।
तदनुस्मरणध्वस्तजीवकोशास्तमध्यगन् ॥10.82.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अध्यात्म-शिक्षया by this spiritual instruction on absolute truth, गोप्यः the gopis, एवम् thus, कृष्णेन by Krishna, शिक्षिताः instructed, तत्-अनुस्मरण-ध्वस्त-जीव-कोशाः having their subtle body made of material desires destroyed by constant remembrance of Him, तम् Him Krishna, अध्यगन् attained

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच — कृष्णेन एवम् अध्यात्मशिक्षया शिक्षिताः गोप्यः तदनुस्मरणध्वस्तजीवकोशाः तम् अध्यगन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, कृष्णेन by Krishna, एवम् in this manner, अध्यात्मशिक्षया शिक्षिताः taught through spiritual philosophy, गोप्यः the gopis, तदनुस्मरणध्वस्तजीवकोशाः having the subtle sheath of their conditioned existence dissolved by constant meditation on Him, तम् अध्यगन् attained Him.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Having been instructed by Krishna in this supreme spiritual truth, the gopis constantly meditated upon His words. Through uninterrupted remembrance of Him, the subtle sheath of material illusion covering their souls was completely dissolved, and they fully attained Him.

आहुश्च ते नलिननाभ पदारविन्दं योगेश्वरैर्हृदि विचिन्त्यमगाधबोधैः ।
संसारकूपपतितोत्तरणावलम्बं गेहञ्जुषामपि मनस्युदियात् सदा नः ॥10.82.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: आहुः they said, च and, ते Your, नलिन-नाभ O Lord from whose navel sprung the lotus (where Brahma was born), पद-अरविन्दम् lotus feet, योगेश्वरैः by the masters of yoga, हृदि in the heart, विचिन्त्यम् meditated upon, अगाध-बोधैः possessed of deep unfathomable wisdom, संसार-कूप-पतित-उत्तूरण-अवलम्बम् the sole support for lifting up those fallen into the dark well of material existence, गेहम्-जुषाम् of us who are attached to domestic household duties, अपि even, मनसि in the mind, उदियात् may it manifest, सदा always, नः our

Anvaya: (गोप्यः) आहुः च — नलिननाभ! अगाधबोधैः योगेश्वरैः हृदि विचिन्त्यं संसारकूपपतितोत्तरणावलम्बं ते पदारविन्दं गेहञ्जुषाम् अपि नः मनसि सदा उदियात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (गोप्यः) आहुः च The gopis said, नलिननाभ O lotus-naveled Lord, अगाधबोधैः by sages of deep realization, योगेश्वरैः by great masters of yoga, हृदि विचिन्त्यं meditated upon within the heart, संसारकूपपतितोत्तरणावलम्बं the only refuge for raising those fallen into the deep well of worldly bondage, ते पदारविन्दं Your lotus feet, गेहञ्जुषाम् अपि even though attached to householder life, नः मनसि in our minds, सदा always, उदियात् may ever arise.

Simple Meaning: The gopis said: O lotus-naveled Lord! Great masters of yoga and sages of spiritual realization constantly meditate upon Your lotus feet within their hearts, for Your feet are the only refuge for raising the souls who have fallen into the deep well of material existence. Even though we are ordinary women engaged in household duties, may those very same lotus feet always remain vividly present within our minds!

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “कृष्णबलरामगोपसङ्गमः​ नाम​” द्वावशीतितमः अध्यायः ॥82॥

Thus ends the eighty-second discourse named “The Meeting of Krishna and Balarama with the Gopas and Gopis of Vraja”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
