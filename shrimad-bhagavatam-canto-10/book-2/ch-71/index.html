श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

कृष्णस्य इन्द्रप्रस्थगमनं नाम एकसप्ततितमः अध्यायः

(46 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
इत्युदीरितमाकर्ण्य देवर्षेरुद्धवोऽब्रवीत् ।
सभ्यानां मतमाज्ञाय कृष्णस्य च महामतिः ॥10.71.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति thus, उदीरितम् what was spoken (by Narada), आकर्ण्य hearing, देव-ऋषेः of the divine sage Narada, उद्धवः Uddhava, अब्रवीत् spoke, सभ्यानाम् of the members of the assembly, मतम् opinion/desire, आज्ञाय understanding/knowing, कृष्णस्य of Krishna, च and, महा-मतिः the greatly intelligent one

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— महामतिः उद्धवः देवर्षेः इति उदीरितम् आकर्ण्य, सभ्यानां कृष्णस्य च मतम् आज्ञाय अब्रवीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said— महामतिः उद्धवः the highly intelligent Uddhava, देवर्षेः of the celestial sage Narada, इति उदीरितम् these words spoken, आकर्ण्य after listening to, सभ्यानां of the Yadu members present in the assembly, कृष्णस्य च and of Krishna Himself, मतम् आज्ञाय having comprehended their respective minds/opinions, अब्रवीत् spoke.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Having listened carefully to the proposal of the divine sage Narada, the brilliant Uddhava gauged the inclinations of both the assembly members—who desired to defeat Jarasandha—and Krishna, who wished to fulfill both duties. Uddhava then began to speak.

उद्धव उवाच
यदुक्तमृषिणा देव साचिव्यं यक्ष्यतस्त्वया ।
कार्यं पैतृष्वसेयस्य रक्षा च शरणैषिणाम् ॥10.71.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: उद्धवः उवाच Uddhava said, यत् which, उक्तम् was stated, ऋषिणा by the sage (Narada), देव O Lord, साचिव्यम् assistance/help, यक्ष्यतः of the one intending to perform the sacrifice (Yudhishthira), त्वया by You, कार्यम् should be done, पैतृष्वसेयस्य of the son of Your father's sister (Yudhishthira), रक्षा protection, च and, शरण-एषिणाम् of those seeking refuge/surrender (the captive kings)

Anvaya: उद्धव उवाच— देव! यक्ष्यतः पैतृष्वसेयस्य त्वया साचिव्यं कार्यं, शरणैषिणां रक्षा च ऋषिणा यत् उक्तं (तत् उभयम् एव युक्तम्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: उद्धव उवाच Uddhava said— देव O Lord, यक्ष्यतः पैतृष्वसेयस्य for the son of Your aunt (King Yudhishthira) who intends to perform the sacrifice, त्वया साचिव्यं कार्यं assistance ought to be rendered by You, शरणैषिणां रक्षा च and protection for those who seek refuge (the kings imprisoned by Jarasandha), ऋषिणा यत् उक्तं what was spoken by Sage Narada, तत् उभयम् both of these actions (are proper to be performed).

Simple Meaning: Uddhava said: "O Lord, Sage Narada has rightly pointed out that You ought to assist Your aunt's son, King Yudhishthira, in performing the Rajasuya sacrifice. At the same time, it is Your duty to protect the kings who have surrendered and sought Your refuge from Jarasandha's captivity.

यष्टव्यं राजसूयेन दिक्चक्रजयिना विभो ।
अतो जरासुतजय उभयार्थो मतो मम ॥10.71.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: यष्टव्यम् should be performed, राजसूयेन with the Rajasuya sacrifice, दिक्-चक्र-जयिना by one who has conquered all directions/rulers of the world, विभो O Almighty Lord, अतः therefore, जरासुत-जयः victory over Jarasandha (the son of Jara), उभय-अर्थः serving both purposes, मतः opinion, मम my

Anvaya: विभो! दिग्चक्रजयिना (राज्ञा एव) राजसूयेन यष्टव्यम्। अतः जरासुतजयः उभयार्थः (इति) मम मतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विभो O Almighty Lord, दिग्चक्रजयिना only by a ruler who has subdued the entire circle of directions and defeated all surrounding kings, राजसूयेन यष्टव्यम् can the Rajasuya sacrifice be performed. अतः Therefore, जरासुतजयः defeating Jarasandha, उभयार्थः accomplishes both objectives at once, (इति) मम मतः this is my opinion.

Simple Meaning: "O Almighty Lord, only a monarch who has conquered all directions and brought all earthly kings under his sovereignty is qualified to perform the Rajasuya sacrifice. Therefore, defeating Jarasandha serves both purposes—it liberates the captive kings and clears the path for King Yudhishthira's imperial sacrifice.

अस्माकं च महानर्थो ह्येतेनैव भविष्यति ।
यशश्च तव गोविन्द राज्ञो बद्धान् विमुच्यतः ॥10.71.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अस्माकम् for us, च and, महान् great, अर्थः benefit/purpose, हि indeed, एतेन by this (defeating Jarasandha), एव alone, भविष्यति will happen, यशः fame/glory, च and, तव Your, गोविन्द O Govinda, राज्ञः the kings, बद्धान् who are imprisoned, विमुच्यतः who releases/frees

Anvaya: गोविन्द! एतेन एव अस्माकं महान् अर्थः भविष्यति, बद्धान् राज्ञः विमुच्यतः तव यशः च (भविष्यति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गोविन्द O Govinda, एतेन एव by this action alone, अस्माकं for all of us (the Pandavas and Yadus), महान् अर्थः भविष्यति great purpose will be accomplished; बद्धान् राज्ञः विमुच्यतः liberating the captive kings, तव यशः च Your immense glory as well.

Simple Meaning: "O Govinda! By defeating Jarasandha, our purpose will be completely fulfilled— immense glory will be upon You for freeing the suffering, imprisoned kings."

स वै दुर्विषहो राजा नागायुतसमो बले ।
बलिनामपि चान्येषां भीमं समबलं विना ॥10.71.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Jarasandha), वै indeed, दुर्विषहः invincible/unbearable, राजा king, नाग-अयुत-समः equal to ten thousand elephants, बले in physical strength, बलिनाम् among the strong, अपि even, च and, अन्येषाम् of others, भीमम् Bhima, सम-बलम् equal in prowess/strength, विना except for

Anvaya: बले नागायुतसमः वै सः राजा (जरासन्धः), समबलं भीमं विना अन्येषां बलिनाम् अपि दुर्विषहः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बले In physical strength, नागायुतसमः equal to a force of ten thousand elephants, वै सः राजा indeed that king Jarasandha, अन्येषां बलिनाम् अपि even among other mighty warriors, समबलं भीमं विना except for Bhima who is his equal in strength, दुर्विषहः is irresistible.

Simple Meaning: "King Jarasandha possesses physical strength equal to ten thousand elephants. Among all the mighty warriors in the world, except Bhima, who alone possesses equal strength, none can match him in power."

द्वैरथे स तु जेतव्यो मा शताक्षौहिणीयुतः ।
ब्रह्मण्योऽभ्यर्थितो विप्रैर्न प्रत्याख्याति कर्हिचित् ॥10.71.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्वैरथे in a dual fight, सः he (Jarasandha), तु but, जेतव्यः should be conquered/defeated, मा not, शत-अक्षौहिणी-युतः accompanied by one hundred akshauhini divisions, ब्रह्मण्यः devoted to the brahmanas, अभ्यर्थितः requested/begged, विप्रैः by brahmanas, न never, प्रत्याख्याति refuses/denies, कर्हिचित् at any time

Anvaya: सः तु द्वैरथे जेतव्यः, शताक्षौहिणीयुतः मा (जेतव्यः)। ब्रह्मण्यः (सः) विप्रैः अभ्यर्थितः (सन्) कर्हिचित् न प्रत्याख्याति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः तु He, however, द्वैरथे in a duel between two warriors, जेतव्यः must be conquered; मा शताक्षौहिणीयुतः not by an army of one hundred akshauhini divisions. ब्रह्मण्यः Because he is devoted to the welfare of brahmanas, विप्रैः अभ्यर्थितः when requested by brahmanas, कर्हिचित् न प्रत्याख्याति he never refuses their demand at any time.

Simple Meaning: "Jarasandha should be defeated in face to face dual combat, not by a vast army of one hundred akshauhini divisions. He is deeply devoted to the culture of brahmanas and never turns down a request made by them.

ब्रह्मवेषधरो गत्वा तं भिक्षेत वृकोदरः ।
हनिष्यति न सन्देहो द्वैरथे तव सन्निधौ ॥10.71.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्रह्म-वेष-धरः wearing the garb of a brahmana, गत्वा having gone, तम् from him (Jarasandha), भिक्षेत should beg, वृकोदरः Bhima (Vrikodara), हनिष्यति he will kill, न no, सन्देहः doubt, द्वैरथे in a duel, तव Your, सन्निधौ in the presence of

Anvaya: वृकोदरः ब्रह्मवेषधरः गत्वा तं भिक्षेत। (सः) तव सन्निधौ द्वैरथे (जरासन्धं) हनिष्यति, सन्देहः न।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वृकोदरः Bhima, ब्रह्मवेषधरः disguised in the dress of a brahmana, गत्वा going to him, तं भिक्षेत should beg a favor from him. सः He, तव सन्निधौ in Your very presence, द्वैरथे in a duel, (जरासन्धं) हनिष्यति will slay Jarasandha; सन्देहः न of this there is no doubt.

Simple Meaning: "Let Bhima disguise himself in the attire of a brahmana, approach Jarasandha, and request him for a duel. Standing in Your presence, Bhima will undoubtedly slay Jarasandha in the duel combat; of this there is no doubt.

निमित्तं परमीशस्य विश्वसर्गनिरोधयोः ।
हिरण्यगर्भः शर्वश्च कालस्यारूपिणस्तव ॥10.71.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: निमित्तम् instrumental causes/instruments, परम् Supreme, ईशस्य of the Supreme Lord, विश्व-सर्ग-निरोधयोः for the creation and destruction of the universe, हिरण्यगर्भः Lord Brahma, शर्वः Lord Shiva, च and, कालस्य of time, अ-रूपिणः the one without a form, तव Your

Anvaya: अरूपिणः कालस्य परम् ईशस्य तव हिरण्यगर्भः शर्वः च विश्वसर्गनिरोधयोः निमित्तम् (स्तः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अरूपिणः कालस्य Of the one who exists as formless Time, परम् ईशस्य तव of You, the Supreme Master, हिरण्यगर्भः Lord Brahma, शर्वः च and Lord Shiva, विश्वसर्गनिरोधयोः in the processes of cosmic creation and dissolution, निमित्तम् are instruments.

Simple Meaning: "O Lord, You are the Supreme Master who drives all events in the form of unmanifest Time. Lord Brahma and Lord Shiva are Your instruments in carrying out the creation and destruction of the universe.

गायन्ति ते विशदकर्म गृहेषु देव्यो राज्ञां स्वशत्रुवधमात्मविमोक्षणं च ।
गोप्यश्च कुञ्जरपतेर्जनकात्मजायाः पित्रोश्च लब्धशरणा मुनयो वयं च ॥10.71.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: गायन्ति sing, ते Your, विशद-कर्म pure/spotless deeds, गृहेषु in their homes, देव्यः the queens, राज्ञाम् of the kings, स्व-शत्रु-वधम् the slaying of their enemy (Jarasandha), आत्म-विमोक्षणम् their own liberation/release, च and, गोप्यः the gopis, च and, कुञ्जर-पतेः of the king of elephants (Gajendra), जनक-आत्मजायाः of the daughter of Janaka (Sita), पित्रोः of Your parents (Vasudeva and Devaki), च and, लब्ध-शरणाः who have attained Your shelter, मुनयो sages, वयम् we, च and

Anvaya: राज्ञां देव्यः गृहेषु स्वशत्रुवधम् आत्मविमोक्षणं च (गायन्ति), कुञ्जरपतेः जनकात्मजायाः पित्रोः च (विमोक्षणं) गोप्यः च (तव लीलाः), लब्धशरणाः मुनयः वयं च ते विशदकर्म गायन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राज्ञां देव्यः The royal consorts of the captive kings, गृहेषु in their palaces, स्वशत्रुवधम् the slaying of their enemy Jarasandha, आत्मविमोक्षणं च and the liberation of their husbands (will sing); कुञ्जरपतेः of Gajendra the elephant king, जनकात्मजायाः of Sita, the daughter of Janaka, पित्रोः च and of Your parents, (विमोक्षणं) the deliverance (as well as), गोप्यः च the gopis (singing Your exploits), लब्धशरणाः मुनयः the sages who have taken shelter of You, वयं च and all of us, ते विशदकर्म Your spotless and glorious deeds, गायन्ति continuously sing.

Simple Meaning: "O Lord Krishna, in their palaces, the queens of the imprisoned kings will joyfully sing of how You destroyed their enemy Jarasandha and released their husbands. They will chant Your glorification just as the gopis sing of Your divine pastimes, just as sages who have taken shelter in You celebrate Your acts of delivering Gajendra, Sita, and Your own parents. Indeed, we all shall forever sing of Your spotless deeds."

जरासन्धवधः कृष्ण भूर्यर्थायोपकल्पते ।
प्रायः पाकविपाकेन तव चाभिमतः क्रतुः ॥10.71.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: जरासन्ध-वधः the killing of Jarasandha, कृष्ण O Krishna, भूरि-अर्थाय for immense benefit, उपकल्पते leads to/serves, प्रायः practically, पाक-विपाकेन by the culmination of past pious deeds/destiny, तव Your, च and, अभिमतः desired, क्रतुः sacrifice (the Rajasuya)

Anvaya: जरासन्धवधः भूर्यर्थाय उपकल्पते। तव अभिमतः क्रतुः च प्रायः (पाण्डवानां) पाकविपाकेन (सिध्यति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जरासन्धवधः the destruction of Jarasandha, भूर्यर्थाय उपकल्पते serves a great multitude of purposes. तव अभिमतः Desired by You, क्रतुः च and the Rajasuya sacrifice, प्रायः virtually, (पाण्डवानां) पाकविपाकेन through the fruition of the Pandavas' past spiritual merits, (सिध्यति) is brought to fulfillment.

Simple Meaning: "O Krishna, the slaying of Jarasandha will yield immense benefits on all fronts. Furthermore, the Rajasuya sacrifice desired by You is now ripe for fulfillment, coming about as the direct fruit of the Pandavas' great merit.

श्री शुक उवाच
इत्युद्ध्ववचो राजन् सर्वतोभद्रमच्युतम् ।
देवर्षिर्यदुवृद्धाश्च कृष्णश्च प्रत्यपूजयन् ॥10.71.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति thus, उद्धव-वचः the words of Uddhava, राजन् O King, सर्वतः भद्रम् completely auspicious from all sides, अच्युतम् infallible and flawless, देव-ऋषिः the divine sage Narada, यदु-वृद्धाः the elders of the Yadu dynasty, च and, कृष्णः Lord Krishna, च and, प्रत्यपूजयन् praised and accepted with honor

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— राजन्! देवर्षिः यदुवृद्धाः कृष्णः च सर्वतोभद्रम् अच्युतम् इति (तद्) उद्धववचः प्रत्यपूजयन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said— राजन् O King Parikshit, देवर्षिः the divine sage Narada, यदुवृद्धाः the elders of the Yadu dynasty, कृष्णः च and Krishna, सर्वतोभद्रम् entirely beneficial in every way, अच्युतम् flawless, इति thus, उद्धववचः the advice of Uddhava, प्रत्यपूजयन् highly praised and accepted.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King Parikshit, the divine sage Narada, the elders of the Yadu dynasty, and Krishna Himself completely agreed with the words of Uddhava. They greatly praised his advice, recognizing it as perfectly flawless and completely auspicious from every possible perspective.

अथादिशत् प्रयाणाय भगवान् देवकीसुतः ।
भृत्यान् दारुकजैत्रादीननुज्ञाप्य गुरून् विभुः ॥10.71.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथ then, आदिशत् gave orders, प्रयाणाय for departure, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, देवकी-सुतः the son of Devaki, भृत्यान् unto His servants, दारुक-जैत्र-आदीन् led by Daruka and Jaitra, अनुज्ञाप्य taking permission/blessings from, गुरून् His elders, विभुः the Almighty Supreme Master

Anvaya: अथ विभुः भगवान् देवकीसुतः गुरून् अनुज्ञाप्य दारुकजैत्रादीन् भृत्यान् प्रयाणाय आदिशत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ Thereupon, विभुः the almighty, भगवान् Supreme Lord, देवकीसुतः the son of Devaki, गुरून् अनुज्ञाप्य having taken formal permission and blessings from His elders (such as Vasudeva and Ugrasena), दारुकजैत्रादीन् भृत्यान् His attendants and drivers headed by Daruka and Jaitra, प्रयाणाय आदिशत् instructed to prepare for departure.

Simple Meaning: Thereupon, the almighty Lord Krishna, the son of Devaki, sought permission and blessings from His elders. He then instructed His servants, headed by His chariot drivers Daruka and Jaitra, to prepare everything for immediate departure to Indraprastha.

अर्जुनेन परिष्वक्तो यमाभ्यामभिवादितः ।

Simple Meaning: Having been warmly embraced by Arjuna and reverently greeted by Nakula and Sahadeva, Lord Krishna then offered His respectful obeisances to the learned brahmanas and elder members of the assembly, honoring each person according to custom and honored the members of the Kuru, Srinjaya, and Kekaya clans in return.

उवास कतिचिन्मासान् राज्ञः प्रियचिकीर्षया ।
विहरन् रथमारुह्य फाल्गुनेन भटैर्वृतः ॥10.71.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: उवास He resided/stayed, कतिचित् मासान् for several months, राज्ञः of King Yudhishthira, प्रिय-चिकीर्षया desiring to do what was pleasing, विहरन् sporting/enjoying outings, रथम् chariot, आरुह्य mounting/riding, फाल्गुनेन with Phalguna (Arjuna), भटैः by soldiers/guards, वृतः surrounded/accompanied

Anvaya: (कृष्णः) राज्ञः प्रियचिकीर्षया कतिचित् मासान् उवास, फाल्गुनेन भटैः च वृतः रथम् आरुह्य विहरन् (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (कृष्णः) Krishna, राज्ञः प्रियचिकीर्षया out of a desire to bring joy to King Yudhishthira, कतिचित् मासान् उवास stayed there for several months; फाल्गुनेन भटैः च वृतः accompanied by Arjuna and protected by soldiers, रथम् आरुह्य विहरन् (आसीत्) He spent His time riding on chariots and enjoying pleasant outings.

Simple Meaning: Desiring to please King Yudhishthira, Krishna resided at Indraprastha for several months. During His stay, He frequently went on pleasure rides in His chariot alongside Arjuna, accompanied by an escort of soldiers.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “कृष्णस्य इन्द्रप्रस्थगमनं नाम” एकसप्ततितमः अध्यायः ॥71॥

Thus ends the seventy-first discourse named “Krishna’s visit to Indraprastha”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
