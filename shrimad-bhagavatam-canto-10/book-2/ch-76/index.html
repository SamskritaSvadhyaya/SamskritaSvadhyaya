Shrimad Bhagavatam - Book 2

Canto 10

Chapter 76

Battle between Shalva and Yadavas

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

शाल्वयादवयुध्दः नाम षट्सप्ततितमः अध्यायः

(33 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
अथान्यदपि कृष्णस्य शृणु कर्माद्भुतं नृप ।
क्रीडानरशरीरस्य यथा सौभपतिर्हतः ॥10.76.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अथ now, अन्यत् another, अपि also, कृष्णस्य of Lord Krishna, शृणु please hear, कर्म work/deed, अद्भुतम् wonderful/astounding, नृप O King Parikshit, क्रीडा-नर-शरीरस्य of Him who assumes a human-like body for His divine play, यथा how, सौभ-पतिः the master of the flying Saubha airship - Shalva, हतः was slain

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच—नृप अथ क्रीडानरशरीरस्य कृष्णस्य अन्यत् अपि अद्भुतं कर्म शृणु, यथा सौभपतिः हतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, नृप O King Parikshit, अथ now, क्रीडानरशरीरस्य of Lord Krishna who assumes a human form to enact His divine play, अन्यत् अपि अद्भुतं कर्म another extraordinary and miraculous deed, शृणु listen, यथा how, सौभपतिः the lord of the airship Saubha - Shalva, हतः was killed.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King Parikshit, now hear about another wonderful deed of Krishna, who assumes a human form for His divine pastimes—how He defeated and killed Shalva, the master of the airship Saubha.

शिशुपालसखः शाल्वो रुक्मिण्युद्वाह आगतः ।
यदुभिर्निर्जितः संख्ये जरासन्धादयस्तथा ॥10.76.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: शिशुपाल-सखः friend of Shishupala, शाल्वः Shalva, रुक्मिणी-उद्वाहे to the marriage of Rukmini, आगतः had come, यदुभिः by the Yadus, निर्जितः was defeated, संख्ये in battle, जरासन्ध-आदयः Jarasandha and others, तथा also

Anvaya: शिशुपालसखः शाल्वः जरासन्धादयः तथा रुक्मिण्युद्वाहे आगतः संख्ये यदुभिः निर्जितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शिशुपालसखः शाल्वः The friend of Shishupala - Shalva, जरासन्धादयः तथा as well as Jarasandha and others, रुक्मिण्युद्वाहे आगतः who had come to the wedding of Rukmini, संख्ये in battle, यदुभिः by the Yadus, निर्जितः were defeated.

Simple Meaning: Shalva, a friend of Shishupala, had come to the wedding of Rukmini along with Jarasandha and other kings, where they were all defeated in battle by the Yadus.

शाल्वः प्रतिज्ञामकरोत् शृण्वतां सर्वभूभुजाम् ।
अयादवीं क्ष्मां करिष्ये पौरुषं मम पश्यत ॥10.76.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: शाल्वः Shalva, प्रतिज्ञाम् a vow, अकरोत् made, शृण्वताम् while listening, सर्व-भूभुजाम् all the kings, अ-यादवीम् devoid of the Yadus, क्ष्माम् the earth, करिष्ये I shall make, पौरुषम् prowess/valour, मम my, पश्यत see

Anvaya: शाल्वः शृण्वतां सर्वभूभुजां  - क्ष्माम् अयादवीं करिष्ये, मम पौरुषं पश्यत - इति प्रतिज्ञाम् अकरोत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शाल्वः Shalva, शृण्वतां सर्वभूभुजां in the hearing of all the kings, क्ष्माम् अयादवीं करिष्ये I will render the earth free of Yadus, मम पौरुषं पश्यत behold my prowess, इति प्रतिज्ञाम् अकरोत् made such a vow.

Simple Meaning: Shalva made a vow in the hearing of all the assembled kings: I will render the earth completely free of the Yadus. Behold my prowess!

इति मूढः प्रतिज्ञाय देवं पशुपतिं प्रभुम् ।
आराधयामास नृप पांसुमुष्टिं सकृद् ग्रसन् ॥10.76.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, मूढः fool, प्रतिज्ञाय having vowed, देवम् lord, पशुपतिम् Pashupati/Lord Shiva, प्रभुम् the master, आराधयामास worshiped, नृप O King Parikshit, पांसु-मुष्टिम् a handful of dust, सकृत् once daily, ग्रसन् eating

Anvaya: नृप! इति प्रतिज्ञाय मूढः सकृत् पांसुमुष्टिं ग्रसन् प्रभुं देवं पशुपतिम् आराधयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King Parikshit, इति प्रतिज्ञाय having made this vow, मूढः that fool, सकृत् पांसुमुष्टिं ग्रसन् eating a handful of dust once every day, प्रभुं देवं पशुपतिम् worshiped the master, Lord Pashupati.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, having made such a vow, that foolish Shalva began worshipping Lord Pashupati - Shiva as his master by eating just a handful of dust once every day.

संवत्सरान्ते भगवानाशुतोष उमापतिः ।
वरेणच्छन्दयामास शाल्वं शरणमागतम् ॥10.76.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: संवत्सर-अन्ते at the end of a year, भगवान् the opulent Lord, आशु-तोषः one who is easily pleased, उमा-पतिः husband of Uma - Shiva, वरेण with a boon, छन्दयामास offered, शाल्वम् to Shalva, शरणम् आगतम् who had taken refuge

Anvaya: संवत्सरान्ते आशुतोषः भगवान् उमापतिः शरणम् आगतं शाल्वं वरेण छन्दयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: संवत्सरान्ते At the end of a year, आशुतोषः भगवान् उमापतिः the easily pleased lord Uma husband Shiva, शरणम् आगतं शाल्वं to Shalva who had taken shelter, वरेण छन्दयामास offered a boon.

Simple Meaning: At the end of a year, the easily pleased Lord Shiva, husband of Uma, offered a boon to Shalva, who had taken shelter of him.

देवासुरमनुष्याणां गन्धर्वोरगरक्षसाम् ।
अभेद्यं कामगं वव्रे स यानं वृष्णिभीषणम् ॥10.76.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: देवा-असुर-मनुष्याणाम् of the devas, asuras, and humans, गन्धर्व-उरग-रक्षसाम् of the gandharvas, uragas (nagas), and rakshasas, अभेद्यम् impenetrable, काम-गम् moving at will, वव्रे chose/requested, सः he, यानम् a vehicle, वृष्णि-भीषणम् terrifying to the Vrishnis

Anvaya: सः देवासुरमनुष्याणां गन्धर्वोरगरक्षसाम् अभेद्यं कामगं वृष्णिभीषणं यानं वव्रे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He, देवासुरमनुष्याणां गन्धर्वोरगरक्षसाम् अभेद्यं impenetrable by devas, asuras, humans, gandharvas, uragas (nagas), and rakshasas, कामगं moving according to one's will, वृष्णिभीषणं terrifying to the Vrishnis, यानं वव्रे requested a vehicle.

Simple Meaning: He chose a vehicle that was impenetrable to devas, asuras, humans, gandharvas, uragas (nagas), and rakshasas, could move at his will, and would strike terror into the Vrishnis.

तथेति गिरिशादिष्टो मयः परपुरञ्जयः ।
पुरं निर्माय शाल्वाय प्रादात्सौभमयस्मयम् ॥10.76.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: तथा so be it, इति thus, गिरिश-आदिष्टः commanded by Lord Shiva, मयः Maya Danava, पर-पुरम्-जयः conqueror of enemy cities, पुरम् a city/vehicle, निर्माय having constructed, शाल्वाय unto Shalva, प्रादात् gave, सौभम् named Saubha, अयः-मयम् made of iron

Anvaya: तथा इति गिरिशादिष्टः परपुरञ्जयः मयः सौभम् अयस्मयं पुरं निर्माय शाल्वाय प्रादात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तथा इति गिरिशादिष्टः Commanded thus by Lord Shiva, परपुरञ्जयः मयः Maya the conqueror of enemy cities, सौभम् अयस्मयं पुरं निर्माय having built an iron aerial vehicle named Saubha, शाल्वाय प्रादात् gave it to Shalva.

Simple Meaning: Lord Shiva saying “So be it”. On his command Maya Danava, the conqueror of enemy cities, constructed an iron aerial vehicle named Saubha and gave it to Shalva.

स ल्ब्ध्वा कामगं यानं तमोधाम दुरासदम् ।
ययौ द्वारवतीं शाल्वो वैरं वृष्णिकृतं स्मरन् ॥10.76.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, लब्ध्वा having obtained, काम-गम् moving at will, यानम् vehicle, तमः-धाम abode of darkness, दुरासदम् difficult to approach, ययौ went, द्वारवतीम् to Dwaraka, शाल्वः Shalva, वैरम् enmity, वृष्णि-कृतम् done by the Vrishnis, स्मरन् remembering

Anvaya: सः शाल्वः वृष्णिकृतं वैरं स्मरन् दुरासदं तमोधाम कामगं यानं लब्ध्वा द्वारवतीं ययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः शाल्वः He Shalva, वृष्णिकृतं वैरं स्मरन् remembering the enmity caused by the Vrishnis, दुरासदं तमोधाम कामगं यानं लब्ध्वा having obtained the unapproachable vehicle moving at will which was an abode of darkness, द्वारवतीं ययौ went to Dwaraka.

Simple Meaning: Remembering his deep grudge against the Vrishnis, Shalva took command of that unapproachable airship, which could move at will and was a very abode of darkness, and traveled to Dwaraka.

निरुद्ध्य सेनया शाल्वो महत्या भरतर्षभ ।
पुरीं बभञ्जोपवनान्युद्यानानि च सर्वशः ॥10.76.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: निरुद्ध्य having besieged, सेनया with an army, शाल्वः Shalva, महत्या great/huge, भरतर्षभ O best of the Bharatas - King Parikshit, पुरीम् the city, बभञ्ज destroyed, उपवनानि small gardens/groves, उद्यानानि parks/gardens, च and, सर्वशः everywhere/all around

Anvaya: भरतर्षभ! शाल्वः महत्या सेनया पुरीं निरुद्ध्य उपवनानि उद्यानानि च सर्वशः बभञ्ज।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भरतर्षभ O best of the Bharatas, शाल्वः Shalva, महत्या सेनया पुरीं निरुद्ध्य having surrounded the city with a vast army, उपवनानि उद्यानानि च सर्वशः बभञ्ज destroyed the groves and parks all around.

Simple Meaning: O best of the Bharatas, Shalva besieged Dwaraka with a vast army, destroying all the surrounding parks and gardens.

सगोपुराणि द्वाराणि प्रासादाट्टालतोलिकाः ।
विहारान् स विमानाग्र्यान्निपेतुः शस्त्रवृष्टयः ॥10.76.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: स-गोपुराणि with city gates, द्वाराणि main entrances, प्रासाद-अट्टाल-तोलिकाः palaces, watchtowers, and archways, विहारान् pleasure houses/recreation parks, सः he, विमान-अग्र्यात् from the supreme airship, निपेतुः rained down, शस्त्र-वृष्टयः showers of weapons

Anvaya: सः विमानाग्र्यात् सगोपुराणि द्वाराणि प्रासादाट्टालतोलिकाः विहारान् शस्त्रवृष्टयः निपेतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He, विमानाग्र्यात् from the magnificent airship Saubha, सगोपुराणि द्वाराणि प्रासादाट्टालतोलिकाः विहारान् upon the city gates, main entrances, palaces, watchtowers, archways, and pleasure grounds, शस्त्रवृष्टयः निपेतुः rained down weapons.

Simple Meaning: From his magnificent airship, Shalva rained down weapons upon the city gates, entrances, palaces, watchtowers, archways, and recreational parks of Dwaraka.

शिला द्रुमाश्चाशनयः सर्पा आसारशर्कराः ।
प्रचण्डश्चक्रवातोऽभूद् रजसाऽऽच्छादिता दिशः ॥10.76.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: शिलाः stones, द्रुमाः trees, च and, अशनयः thunderbolts, सर्पाः snakes, आसार-शर्कराः hailstones, प्रचण्डः fierce, चक्रवातः whirlwind, अभूत् arose, रजसा by dust, आच्छादिताः covered, दिशः all directions

Anvaya: शिलाः द्रुमाः अशनयः सर्पाः आसारशर्कराः च निपेतुः प्रचण्डः चक्रवातः अभूत्, रजसा दिशः आच्छादिताः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शिलाः द्रुमाः अशनयः सर्पाः आसारशर्कराः च Stones, trees, thunderbolts, serpents, and hailstones rained down, प्रचण्डः चक्रवातः अभूत् a fierce cyclone arose, रजसा दिशः आच्छादिताः all directions were covered by dust.

Simple Meaning: Stones, trees, thunderbolts, serpents, and torrents of hailstones rained down from the sky. A fierce cyclone arose, completely obscuring all directions in thick clouds of dust.

इत्यर्द्यमाना सौभेन कृष्णस्य नगरी भृशम् ।
नाभ्यपद्यत शं राजंस्त्रिपुरेण यथा मही ॥10.76.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, अर्द्यमाना being distressed, सौभेन by the Saubha airship, कृष्णस्य of Lord Krishna, नगरी the city, भृशम् severely, न अभ्यपद्यत could not find, शम् peace/relief, राजन् O King Parikshit, त्रिपुरेण by Tripura, यथा just as, मही the earth

Anvaya: राजन्! इत्थं सौभेन भृशम् अर्द्यमाना कृष्णस्य नगरी त्रिपुरेण मही यथा शं न अभ्यपद्यत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, इत्थं सौभेन भृशम् अर्द्यमाना thus being severely tormented by the Saubha airship, कृष्णस्य नगरी the city of Lord Krishna, त्रिपुरेण मही यथा just like the earth tormented by the asura Tripura, शं न अभ्यपद्यत could not find any peace or relief.

Simple Meaning: O King, tormented severely in this manner by the Saubha airship, Krishna's city of Dwaraka could find no peace, just as the earth once found no peace when tormented by the asura Tripura.

प्रद्युम्नो भगवान् वीक्ष्य बाध्यमाना निजाः प्रजाः ।
मा भैष्टेत्यभ्यधाद् वीरो रथारूढो महायशाः ॥10.76.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रद्युम्नः Pradyumna, भगवान् the lordly, वीक्ष्य seeing, बाध्यमानाः being harassed, निजाः his own, प्रजाः citizens, मा fear, भैष्ट do not, इति thus, अभ्यधात् spoke, वीरः hero, रथ-आरूढः mounted on a chariot, महा-यशाः of great fame

Anvaya: महायशाः वीरः भगवान् प्रद्युम्नः निजाः प्रजाः बाध्यमानाः वीक्ष्य रथारूढः (सन्) मा भैष्ट इति अभ्यधात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महायशाः वीरः भगवान् प्रद्युम्नः The illustrious heroic Lord Pradyumna, निजाः प्रजाः बाध्यमानाः वीक्ष्य seeing his citizens being tormented, रथारूढः (सन्) mounting on his chariot, मा भैष्ट इति अभ्यधात् spoke to them saying do not fear.

Simple Meaning: Seeing his citizens being tormented, the illustrious and heroic Lord Pradyumna mounting his chariot reassured them, saying: Do not fear!

सात्यकिश्चारुदेष्णश्च साम्बोऽक्रूरः सहानुजः ।
हार्दिक्यो भानुविन्दश्च गदश्च शुकसारणौ ॥10.76.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: सात्यकिः Satyaki, चारुदेष्णः Charudeshna, च and, साम्बः Samba, अक्रूरः Akrura, सह-अनुजः along with his younger brother, हार्दिक्यः the son of Hridika - Kritavarma, भानुविन्दः Bhanuvinda, च and, गदः Gada, च and, शुक-सारणौ Shuka and Sarana

Anvaya: सात्यकिः चारुदेष्णः साम्बः सहानुजः अक्रूरः हार्दिक्यः भानुविन्दः गदः शुकसारणौ च (निर्ययुः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सात्यकिः चारुदेष्णः साम्बः Satyaki, Charudeshna, Samba, सहानुजः अक्रूरः Akrura along with his younger brother, हार्दिक्यः भानुविन्दः गदः शुकसारणौ च as well as Kritavarma, Bhanuvinda, Gada, Shuka and Sarana marched out to fight.

Simple Meaning: Satyaki, Charudeshna, Samba, Akrura with his younger brother, Kritavarma, Bhanuvinda, Gada, Shuka, and Sarana all marched out to defend the city.

विना मत् क्लीवचित्तेन सूतेन प्राप्तकिल्बिषात् ॥10.76.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, यदूनाम् of the Yadus, कुले in the dynasty, जातः born, श्रूयते is heard of, रण-विच्युतः one who retreats from the battlefield, विना except, मत् me, क्लीव-चित्तेन with a faint heart, सूतेन by the charioteer, प्राप्त-किल्बिषात् through whom who has attained taint

Anvaya: क्लीवचित्तेन सूतेन प्राप्तकिल्बिषात् मत् विना यदूनां कुले रणविच्युतः जातः न श्रूयते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: क्लीवचित्तेन सूतेन By a charioteer with a faint heart, प्राप्तकिल्बिषात् मत् विना except for me who has attained taint, यदूनां कुले in the line of the Yadus, रणविच्युतः जातः any person born fleeing from combat, न श्रूयते is never heard of.

Simple Meaning: No one born in the dynasty of the Yadus has ever been known to flee from battle, except for me, who has attained taint due to a charioteer who was faint of heart.

किं नु वक्ष्येऽभिसङ्गम्य पितरौ रामकेशवौ ।
युद्धात् सम्यगपक्रान्तः पृष्टस्तत्रात्मनः क्षमम् ॥10.76.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: किम् what, नु indeed, वक्ष्ये shall I say, अभिसङ्गम्य approaching, पितरौ the two elders/fathers, राम-केशवौ Balarama and Krishna, युद्धात् from the battle, सम्यक् completely, अपक्रान्तः retreated, पृष्टः being questioned, तत्र there, आत्मनः for myself, क्षमम् suitable/proper explanation

Anvaya: युद्धात् सम्यग् अपक्रान्तः तत्र आत्मनः क्षमं पृष्टः रामकेशवौ पितरौ अभिसङ्गम्य किं नु वक्ष्ये।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: युद्धात् सम्यग् अपक्रान्तः Having completely retreated from the fight, तत्र आत्मनः क्षमं पृष्टः when questioned there for an explanation suitable to my character, रामकेशवौ पितरौ अभिसङ्गम्य upon meeting my elders Balarama and Krishna, किं नु वक्ष्ये what indeed shall I say.

Simple Meaning: Having completely fled from battle, what proper explanation can I possibly give when I meet my elders, Balarama and Krishna, and they question me about my conduct?

व्यक्तं मे कथयिष्यन्ति हसन्त्यो भ्रातृजामयः ।
क्लैब्यं कथं कथं वीर तवान्यैः कथ्यतां मृधे ॥10.76.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: व्यक्तम् clearly, मे to me, कथयिष्यन्ति will say, हसन्त्यः laughing, भ्रातृ-जामयः the wives of my brothers/sisters-in-law, क्लैब्यम् cowardice, कथम् कथम् how indeed, वीर O hero, तव your, अन्यैः by others, कथ्यताम् can be described, मृधे in battle

Anvaya: भ्रातृजामयः हसन्त्यः मे व्यक्तं कथयिष्यन्ति – वीर! मृधे अन्यैः तव क्लैब्यं कथं कथं कथ्यताम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भ्रातृजामयः My sisters-in-law, हसन्त्यः laughing, मे व्यक्तं कथयिष्यन्ति will surely say to me, वीर O hero, मृधे in combat, अन्यैः by others, तव क्लैब्यं your faint-heartedness, कथं कथं how could it possible, कथ्यताम् please tell.

Simple Meaning: My sisters-in-law will mockingly laugh and ask me: O great hero, how could the enemies turn you into a faint-heart in the battle?

सारथिः उवाच
धर्मं विजानताऽऽयुष्मन् कृतमेतन्मया विभो ।
सूतः कृच्छ्रगतं रक्षेद् रथिनं सारथिं रथी ॥10.76.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: सारथिः उवाच the charioteer said, धर्मम् duty/chivalric law, विजानता knowing, आयुष्मन् O long-lived one, कृतम् done, एतत् this, मया by me, विभो O master; सूतः the charioteer, कृच्छ्र-गतम् placed in distress, रक्षेत् should protect, रथिनम् the chariot warrior, सारथिम् the charioteer, रथी the warrior

Anvaya: सारथिः उवाच— आयुष्मन् विभो! धर्मं विजानता मया एतत् कृतम्। सूतः कृच्छ्रगतं रथिनं रक्षेत्, रथी सारथिं (रक्षेत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सारथिः उवाच The charioteer said, आयुष्मन् विभो O long-lived master, धर्मं विजानता मया by me who knows the codes of righteousness, एतत् कृतम् this was done. सूतः The charioteer, कृच्छ्रगतं रथिनं रक्षेत् should protect the warrior who is in distress, रथी (च) and the chariot warrior, सारथिं (रक्षेत्) should protect the charioteer.

Simple Meaning: The charioteer replied: O long-lived lord, I acted in full knowledge of military righteousness. The eternal rule of combat dictates that a charioteer must protect his warrior when he is in distress, just as a chariot warrior must protect his charioteer.

एतत् विदित्वा तु भवान् मयापोवाहितो रणात् ।
उपसृष्टः परेणेति मूर्च्छितो गदया हतः ॥10.76.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतत् this, विदित्वा knowing, तु indeed, भवान् you, मया by me, अपोवाहितः were carried away, रणात् from the battle, उपसृष्टः overwhelmed/in danger, परेण by the enemy, इति thinking thus, मूर्च्छितः unconscious, गदया by the mace, हतः struck

Anvaya: भवान् परेण गदया हतः मूर्च्छितः उपसृष्टः इति एतत् विदित्वा मया तु रणात् अपोवाहितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भवान् You, परेण by the enemy, गदया हतः struck by a mace, मूर्च्छितः unconscious, उपसृष्टः इति surrounded by peril, एतत् विदित्वा knowing this, मया तु by me indeed, रणात् अपोवाहितः was carried away from the battlefield.

Simple Meaning: The charioteer continued: Knowing this sacred rule of combat, and seeing that you had been knocked unconscious by the blow of the enemy's mace and were in grave danger, I carried you away from the battlefield.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “शाल्वयादवयुध्दः नाम” षट्सप्ततितमः अध्यायः ॥76॥

Thus ends the seventy-sixth discourse named “Battle between Shalva and the Yadavas”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
