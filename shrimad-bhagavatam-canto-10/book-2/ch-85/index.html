Chapter 85

Krishna’s teachings to Vasudeva and bringing back of His six elder brothers (who were killed by Kamsa) to life

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

कृष्णोपदेश​मृताग्रजानयनं नाम पञ्चाशीतितमः अध्यायः

(59 Shlokas)

श्री बादरायणिः उवाच
अथैकदाऽऽत्मजौ प्राप्तौ कृतपादाभिवन्दनौ ।
वसुदेवोऽभिनन्द्याह प्रीत्या सङ्कर्षणाच्युतौ ॥10.85.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री बादरायणिः उवाच Shri Shuka (son of Badarayana - Vyasa) said, अथ then, एकदा once, आत्मजौ his two sons, प्राप्तौ approached/came, कृत-पाद-अभिवन्दनौ who had paid respects at his feet, वसुदेवः Vasudeva, अभिनन्द्य welcoming/greetings, आह spoke, प्रीत्या with affection/love, सङ्कर्षण-अच्युतौ to Sankarshana (Balarama) and Achyuta (Krishna)

Anvaya: श्री बादरायणिः उवाच — अथ एकदा प्राप्तौ कृतपादाभिवन्दनौ आत्मजौ सङ्कर्षणाच्युतौ वसुदेवः अभिनन्द्य प्रीत्या आह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री बादरायणिः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अथ एकदा then on one occasion, प्राप्तौ coming before him, कृतपादाभिवन्दनौ who had reverently bowed down and touched his feet, आत्मजौ his two sons, सङ्कर्षणाच्युतौ Sankarshana and Achyuta, वसुदेवः Vasudeva, अभिनन्द्य warmly receiving them, प्रीत्या आह spoke with affection.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: On one occasion, when his two sons, Balarama and Krishna, came to him and respectfully bowed down at his feet, Vasudeva welcomed them warmly and spoke to them with affection.

मुनीनां स वचः श्रुत्वा पुत्रयोर्धामसूचकम् ।
तद्वीर्यैर्जातविश्रम्भः परिभाष्याभ्यभाषत ॥10.85.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: मुनीनाम् of the sages, सः he (Vasudeva), वचः words, श्रुत्वा having heard, पुत्रयोः of his two sons, धाम-सूचकम् revealing the divine glory/nature, तत्-वीर्यैः by their heroic deeds, जात-विश्रम्भः having firm faith/conviction, परिभाष्य addressing them, अभ्यभाषत spoke

Anvaya: सः मुनीनां पुत्रयोः धामसूचकं वचः श्रुत्वा तत्वीर्यैः जातविश्रम्भः परिभाष्य अभ्यभाषत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः Vasudeva, मुनीनां of the great sages at Kurukshetra, पुत्रयोः धामसूचकं वचः words declaring the divine transcendental status of his two sons, श्रुत्वा having heard, तत्वीर्यैः and due to their extraordinary deeds, जातविश्रम्भः having acquired full conviction in their divinity, परिभाष्य addressing them, अभ्यभाषत spoke to them.

Simple Meaning: Having heard the words of the sages that revealed the true divine nature of his two sons, and having firm conviction in their divinity through their heroic deeds, Vasudeva addressed Krishna and Balarama.

कृष्ण कृष्ण महायोगिन् सङ्कर्षण सनातन ।
जाने वामस्य यत् साक्षात् प्रधानपुरुषौ परौ ॥10.85.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृष्ण O Krishna, कृष्ण O Krishna, महा-योगिन् O supreme master of yoga, सङ्कर्षण O Sankarshana (Balarama), सनातन O eternal one, जाने I know, वाम् of both of you, अस्य of this universe, यत् that, साक्षात् directly, प्रधान-पुरुषौ the primal cause (prakriti) and the supreme person (purusha), परौ transcendental

Anvaya: कृष्ण! कृष्ण! महायोगिन्! सनातन! सङ्कर्षण! अस्य साक्षात् प्रधानपुरुषौ परौ वाम् (अहं) जाने।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्ण कृष्ण महायोगिन् O Krishna, O supreme master of yoga, सनातन सङ्कर्षण O eternal Sankarshana, अस्य साक्षात् of this manifest universe directly, प्रधानपुरुषौ परौ the origin of material nature and the spirit, वाम् of both of you, (अहं) जाने I know as transcendental supreme entities.

Simple Meaning: O Krishna! O supreme master of yoga! O eternal Balarama! I now know that You both are directly the supreme transcendental person and the primal source of this universe.

यत्र येन यतो यस्य यस्मै यद् यद् यथा यदा ।
स्यादिदं भगवान् साक्षात् प्रधानपुरुषेश्वरः ॥10.85.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: यत्र wherein/by which place, येन by which instrument/cause, यतः from which source, यस्य of which owner/controller, यस्मै for whose sake, यत् which object, यत् which effect, यथा in which manner, यदा at which time, स्यात् comes into existence, इदम् this universe, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, साक्षात् directly, प्रधान-पुरुष-ईश्वरः the Lord of primary nature and the spirit

Anvaya: यत्र येन यतः यस्य यस्मै यत् यत् यथा यदा इदं स्यात् साक्षात् प्रधानपुरुषेश्वरः भगवान्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यत्र Wherein, येन by which means, यतः from which origin, यस्य to whom belonging, यस्मै for whose satisfaction, यत् यत् whatever cause and effect, यथा in whichever way, यदा at whichever time, इदं स्यात् this entire creation comes to be, साक्षात् directly that is the Supreme Lord, प्रधानपुरुषेश्वरः भगवान् the ultimate ruler of material nature and the individual soul.

Simple Meaning: In whatever place, by whatever instrument, from whatever cause, for whose sake, whatever effect comes to be, in whatever manner, and at whatever time this universe manifests, that is directly the Supreme Lord Himself, who is the controller of both material nature and the individual soul.

एतन्नानाविधं विश्वमात्मसृष्टमधोक्षज ।
आत्मनानुप्रविश्यात्मन् प्राणो जीवो बिभर्ष्यज ॥10.85.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतत् this, नाना-विधम् variegated/of diverse forms, विश्वम् universe, आत्म-सृष्टम् created by Yourself, अधोक्षज O Lord beyond the perception of senses, आत्मना by Your own energy/expansion, अनुप्रविश्य entering into, आत्मन् O Soul of all souls, प्राणः the vital life-air, जीवः the living entity, बिभर्षि You sustain/support, अज O unborn Lord

Anvaya: अधोक्षज! आत्मन्! अज! एतत् आत्मसृष्टं नानाविधं विश्वं आत्मना अनुप्रविश्य प्राणः जीवः बिभर्षि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अधोक्षज O Lord beyond the perception of senses, आत्मन् O supreme inner soul, अज O unborn Lord, एतत् आत्मसृष्टं नानाविधं विश्वं this variegated world created by Yourself, आत्मना अनुप्रविश्य having entered into it by Your own personal expansions, प्राणः as the life-force, जीवः and living entity, बिभर्षि You sustain.

Simple Meaning: O Lord beyond material senses, O unborn soul of all beings, after creating this variegated universe by Your own power, You enter into it through Your own expansions and sustain it as the life-force and the living entity.

प्राणादीनां विश्वसृजां शक्तयो याः परस्य ताः ।
पारतन्त्र्याद् वै सादृश्याद् द्वयोश्चेष्टैव चेष्टताम् ॥10.85.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्राण-आदीनाम् of the vital life-airs and senses, विश्व-सृजाम् of the creators of the universe (such as Brahma and devas), शक्तयः powers/energies, याः which, परस्य of the Supreme Lord, ताः those, पारतन्त्र्यात् due to dependence, वै indeed, सादृश्यात् due to similarity/reflection, द्वयोः of both, चेष्टा activity/movement, एव indeed, चेष्टताम् of those who act

Anvaya: विश्वसृजां प्राणादीनां याः शक्तयः ताः परस्य (एव)। पारतन्त्र्यात् वै सादृश्यात् द्वयोः चेष्टतां चेष्टा (एव)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विश्वसृजां Of the universal creators like Brahma, प्राणादीनां of the life-airs and sense organs, याः शक्तयः whatever powers exist, ताः परस्य (एव) those belong exclusively to You - the Supreme Lord. पारतन्त्र्यात् Due to complete dependence on You, वै सादृश्यात् and due to reflecting Your energy, द्वयोः of both, चेष्टतां of all active agents, चेष्टा (एव) the activity itself is inspired and driven solely by You.

Simple Meaning: Whatever powers are possessed by the creators of the universe, or by the senses and vital airs of living beings, belong entirely to the You - Supreme Lord. Because all active entities are completely dependent on You and reflect Your divine power, their actions and abilities exist only through Your energy.

कान्तिस्तेजः प्रभा सत्ता चन्द्राग्न्यर्कर्क्षविद्युताम् ।
यत् स्थैर्यं भूभृतां भूमेर्वृत्तिर्गन्धोऽर्थतो भवान् ॥10.85.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: कान्तिः glow/radiance, तेजः heat/brilliance, प्रभा luminosity, सत्ता existence/power, चन्द्र-अग्नि-अर्क-ऋक्ष-विद्युताम् of the moon, fire, sun, stars, and lightning, यत् which, स्थैर्यम् stability/firmness, भूभृताम् of mountains, भूमेः of the earth, वृत्तिः support/sustenance, गन्धः fragrance, अर्थतः in reality, भवान् You

Anvaya: चन्द्राग्न्यर्कर्क्षविद्युतां कान्तिः तेजः प्रभा सत्ता भूभृतां यत् स्थैर्यं भूमेः वृत्तिः गन्धः अर्थतः भवान् (एव)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: चन्द्राग्न्यर्कर्क्षविद्युतां Of the moon, fire, sun, stars, and lightning, कान्तिः तेजः प्रभा सत्ता the beauty, heat, brilliance, and existence, भूभृतां यत् स्थैर्यं the firmness of the mountains, भूमेः वृत्तिः गन्धः the sustaining capacity and primary quality of scent of the earth, अर्थतः in truth, भवान् (एव) You are.

Simple Meaning: The glow of the moon, the heat of fire, the brilliance of the sun, and the stars, the flash of lightning, the firmness of mountains, and the sustaining quality and fragrance of the earth are all in reality You alone.

तर्पणं प्राणनमपां देवत्वं ताश्च तद्रसः ।
ओजः सहो बलं चेष्टा गतिर्वायोस्तवेश्वर ॥10.85.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: तर्पणम् satisfying quality, प्राणनम् life-sustaining power, अपाम् of water, देवत्वम् divine potency/nature, ताः that water, च and, तत्-रसः its subtle element of taste, ओजः sensory power, सहः mental power, बलम् physical strength, चेष्टा physical effort, गतिः movement, वायोः of the wind, तव Yours, ईश्वर O Supreme Lord

Anvaya: अपां तर्पणं प्राणनं ताः च तद्रसः वायोः ओजः सहः बलं चेष्टा गतिः ईश्वर तव (एव)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अपां तर्पणं Of water, the ability to satisfy, प्राणनं the power to refresh and sustain life, ताः च that water itself, तद्रसः and its essential attribute taste, वायोः of the wind, ओजः the strength of senses, सहः mental tenacity, बलं physical power, चेष्टा activity, गतिः and motion, ईश्वर O Supreme Lord, तव (एव) are all Your manifestations.

Simple Meaning: O Lord, the refreshing and life-sustaining capacity of water, water itself, and its essential quality of taste are all You. Likewise, the sensory vigor, mental energy, physical strength, movement, and activity of the wind are Your powers alone.

दिशां त्वमवकाशोऽसि दिशः खं स्फोट आश्रयः ।
नादो वर्णस्त्वमोङ्कार आकृतीनां पृथक्कृतिः ॥10.85.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: दिशाम् of the directions, त्वम् You, अवकाशः the space/capacity, असि are, दिशः the cardinal directions, खम् sky/ether, स्फोटः the subtle elemental sound, आश्रयः the substratum/abode, नादः sound vibration, वर्णः articulated letters/sounds, त्वम् You, ओङ्कारः the sacred syllable Om, आकृतीनाम् of manifest forms, पृथक्-कृतिः the differentiating cause

Anvaya: त्वं दिशाम् अवकाशः असि, दिशः खं स्फोटः आश्रयः नादः वर्णः ओङ्कारः त्वम् आकृतीनां पृथक्कृतिः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: त्वं You, दिशाम् अवकाशः असि are the spatial room of the directions, दिशः directions, खं the sky, स्फोटः sound origin, आश्रयः ultimate abode, नादः unmanifest sound, वर्णः manifest sound, ओङ्कारः the holy sound Pranava, त्वम् You are, आकृतीनां पृथक्कृतिः the mechanism that differentiates distinct physical forms.

Simple Meaning: You are the space that allows the existence of directions, as well as the directions themselves, the ether, the subtle elemental sound, its ultimate foundation, the primary sound vibration, articulated letters, the sacred syllable Om, and the power that differentiates various physical shapes and forms.

इन्द्रियं त्विन्द्रियाणां त्वं देवाश्च तदनुग्रहः ।
अवबोधो भवान् बुद्धेर्जीवस्यानुस्मृतिः सती ॥10.85.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: इन्द्रियम् the perceptive power, तु indeed, इन्द्रियाणाम् of the senses, त्वम् You, देवाः the devas presiding over the senses, च and, तत्-अनुग्रहः their grace/illumination, अवबोधः intelligence/power of comprehension, भवान् You, बुद्धेः of the intellect, जीवस्य of the living entity, अनुस्मृतिः memory/recollection, सती correct/faithful

Anvaya: इन्द्रियाणां इन्द्रियं तु त्वम्। देवाः तदनुग्रहः च, बुद्धेः अवबोधः भवान्, जीवस्य सती अनुस्मृतिः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इन्द्रियाणां Of all the senses, इन्द्रियं the essential power of perception, तु त्वम् You indeed are. देवाः The presiding devas, तदनुग्रहः च and their capacity to illuminate the senses, बुद्धेः of the intellect, अवबोधः the power of determination and comprehension, भवान् You are, जीवस्य of the individual soul, सती अनुस्मृतिः memory and remembrance.

Simple Meaning: You are the essential perceptive capacity of the senses, the presiding devas, and their grace that allows the senses to function. You are the intelligence of the intellect and the living entity's true faculty of memory and remembrance.

भूतानामसि भूतादिरिन्द्रियाणां च तैजसः।
वैकारिको विकल्पानां प्रधानमनुशायिनाम् ॥10.85.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूतानाम् of the elements, असि You are, भूत-आदिः tamasa-ahankara (false ego in ignorance, the cause of gross elements), इन्द्रियाणाम् of the senses, च and, तैजसः rajasa-ahankara (false ego in passion, the cause of senses), वैकारिकः sattvika-ahankara (false ego in goodness), विकल्पानाम् of the devas, प्रधानम् unmanifest material nature (prakriti), अनुशायिनाम् of the embodied soul

Anvaya: भूतानां भूतादिः इन्द्रियाणां च तैजसः विकल्पानां वैकारिकः अनुशायिनां प्रधानम् असि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भूतानां Of the elements, भूतादिः the origin of elements known as false ego in ignorance, इन्द्रियाणां च and of the sensory organs, तैजसः the false ego in passion, विकल्पानां of the presiding devas of the senses, वैकारिकः the false ego in goodness, अनुशायिनां for all embodied souls, प्रधानम् the unmanifest origin of nature, असि You are.

Simple Meaning: You are the origin of the gross material elements through ego in ignorance, the origin of the senses through ego in passion, and the origin of presiding devas through ego in goodness. For all embodied souls, You are the unmanifest source of material nature.

नश्वरेष्विह भावेषु तदसि त्वमनश्वरम् ।
यथा द्रव्यविकारेषु द्रव्यमात्रं निरूपितम् ॥10.85.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: नश्वरेषु among perishable, इह in this world, भावेषु existing objects/manifestations, तत् that, असि You are, त्वम् You, अनश्वरम् imperishable, यथा just as, द्रव्य-विकारेषु in the transformations of a substance, द्रव्य-मात्रम् the fundamental substance alone, निरूपितम् is determined/ascertained

Anvaya: इह नश्वरेषु भावेषु तत् अनश्वरं त्वम् असि यथा द्रव्यविकारेषु द्रव्यमात्रं निरूपितम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इह In this world, नश्वरेषु भावेषु among all temporary perishable manifestations, तत् अनश्वरं that eternal imperishable essence, त्वम् असि You are, यथा just as, द्रव्यविकारेषु in all the various transformations of a basic substance, द्रव्यमात्रं the underlying substance itself, निरूपितम् is ascertained to be the sole constant reality.

Simple Meaning: Just as in all transformations of a material substance like clay or gold, the substance itself is ascertained to be the sole underlying reality, so among all the temporary and perishable objects of this universe, You alone are the eternal and imperishable essence.

सत्त्वं रजस्तम इति गुणास्तद्वृत्तयश्च याः ।
त्वय्यद्धा ब्रह्मणि परे कल्पिता योगमायया ॥10.85.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: सत्त्वम् goodness, रजः passion, तमः ignorance, इति thus, गुणाः the modes of nature, तत्-वृत्तयः their functions/activities, च and, याः which, त्वयि in You, अद्धा directly, ब्रह्मणि in the Supreme Spirit, परे transcendental, कल्पिताः projected, योग-मायया by Your cosmic energy (yogamaya)

Anvaya: सत्त्वं रजः तमः इति गुणाः याः च तद्वृत्तयः परे ब्रह्मणि त्वयि योगमायया अद्धा कल्पिताः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सत्त्वं रजः तमः इति गुणाः The three modes of material nature known as Sattva - goodness, Rajas - passion, and Tamas - ignorance, याः च तद्वृत्तयः and whatever functions arise from them, परे ब्रह्मणि in the transcendental Supreme Spirit, त्वयि in You, योगमायया by Your divine potency, अद्धा directly, कल्पिताः are projected.

Simple Meaning: The three modes of material nature—goodness, passion, and ignorance—and all the functions that arise from them are projections manifested within You, the transcendental Supreme Spirit, through Your Yogmaya.

तस्मान्न सन्त्यमी भावा यर्हि त्वयि विकल्पिताः ।
त्वं चामीषु विकारेषु ह्यन्यदाव्यावहारिकः ॥10.84.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मात् therefore, न do not, सन्ति exist, अमी these, भावाः material states/entities, यर्हि because, त्वयि in You, विकल्पिताः imaginatively projected, त्वम् You, च and, अमीषु in these, विकारेषु transformations, हि indeed, अन्यदा otherwise, अव्यावहारिकः beyond empirical interaction/unaffected

Anvaya: तस्मात् यर्हि त्वयि विकल्पिताः अमी भावाः न सन्ति, त्वं च अमीषु विकारेषु अन्यदा हि अव्यावहारिकः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तस्मात् Therefore, यर्हि because, त्वयि in You, विकल्पिताः imaginatively projected by illusion, अमी भावाः these material objects and states, न सन्ति do not possess independent reality, त्वं च and You, अमीषु विकारेषु in these worldly transformations, अन्यदा हि otherwise, अव्यावहारिकः remain entirely unattached and untouched.

Simple Meaning: Therefore, because these material entities are merely imaginative projections upon You through illusion, they have no independent existence. Furthermore, while present within all these worldly transformations, You remain eternally distinct and untouched by them.

गुणप्रवाह एतस्मिन्नबुधास्त्वखिलात्मनः ।
गतिं सूक्ष्मामवबोधेन संसरन्तीह कर्मभिः ॥10.85.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: गुण-प्रवाहे in the continuous flow/stream of the material modes, एतस्मिन् in this, अबुधाः ignorant persons, तु indeed, अखिल-आत्मनः of the Soul of all beings, गतिम् the transcendental movement/nature, सूक्ष्माम् subtle, अवबोधेन through lack of true knowledge, संसरन्ति undergo continuous material rebirth/transmigration, इह in this world, कर्मभिः by their fruitive actions

Anvaya: एतस्मिन् गुणप्रवाहे अबुधाः तु कर्मभिः अखिलात्मनः सूक्ष्मां गतिम् अवबोधेन इह संसरन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एतस्मिन् गुणप्रवाहे Within this endless stream of the modes of material nature, अबुधाः तु foolish and ignorant individuals, कर्मभिः bound by their fruitive activities, अखिलात्मनः of You the Supreme Lord who are the inner Soul of all, सूक्ष्मां गतिम् Your subtle transcendental nature, अवबोधेन due to lack of spiritual comprehension, इह in this material world, संसरन्ति continuously undergo the cycle of birth and death.

Simple Meaning: Entangled in the continuous flow of the material modes, ignorant people fail to understand Your subtle divine nature as the Soul of all beings. Bound by the reactions of their fruitive actions, they continuously wander through the cycle of birth and death in this world.

यदृच्छया नृतां प्राप्य सुकल्पामिह दुर्लभाम् ।
स्वार्थे प्रमत्तस्य वयो गतं त्वन्माययेश्वर ॥10.85.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: यदृच्छया by rare good fortune/providence, नृताम् human birth, प्राप्य having obtained, सुकल्पाम् fully equipped/suitable, इह in this life, दुर्लभाम् very rare, स्वार्थे in one's true self-interest/spiritual benefit, प्रमत्तस्य of one who is bewildered/negligent, वयः lifespan/age, गतम् has passed away, त्वत्-मायया by Your illusory potency, ईश्वर O Supreme Lord

Anvaya: ईश्वर! इह यदृच्छया दुर्लभां सुकल्पां नृतां प्राप्य त्वन्मायया स्वार्थे प्रमत्तस्य वयः गतम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ईश्वर O Supreme Sovereign Lord, इह in this worldly existence, यदृच्छया by providence, दुर्लभां very difficult to attain, सुकल्पां fully capable of bestowing liberation, नृतां human form of life, प्राप्य having attained, त्वन्मायया deluded by Your illusory energy, स्वार्थे regarding my own spiritual well-being, प्रमत्तस्य of me who remained completely negligent, वयः my entire lifetime, गतम् has slipped away uselessly.

Simple Meaning: O Lord, by rare good fortune I obtained this human body, which is so difficult to attain and perfectly suited for attaining liberation. Yet, deluded by Your Maya, I remained negligent of my true spiritual self-interest, and my whole life has passed away in vain.

असावहं ममैवैते देहे चास्यान्वयादिषु ।
स्नेहपाशैर्निबध्नाति भवान् सर्वमिदं जगत् ॥10.85.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: असौ this, अहम् I, मम my, एव indeed, एते these, देहे in the body, च and, अस्य of this, अन्वय-आदिषु in children and relatives, स्नेह-पाशैः by the ropes of affection/attachment, निबध्नाति binds, भवान् You, सर्वम् all, इदम् this, जगत् universe

Anvaya: असौ अहम्, एते मम एव – इति अस्य देहे अन्वयादिषु च भवान् स्नेहपाशैः इदं सर्वं जगत् निबध्नाति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: असौ अहम् I am this body, एते मम एव these family members are mine alone, इति thus, अस्य देहे in relation to this physical body, अन्वयादिषु च and toward one's descendants and kinsmen, भवान् You, स्नेहपाशैः by the strong ropes of worldly affection, इदं सर्वं जगत् this entire universe, निबध्नाति keep bound in illusion.

Simple Meaning: Thinking "I am this body, and these relatives belong to me," the living entities remain bound in this world because You tie the whole universe with the strong ropes of affection toward the body, children, and kinsmen.

युवां न नः सुतौ साक्षात् प्रधानपुरुषेश्वरौ ।
भूभारक्षत्रक्षपण अवतीर्णौ तथाऽऽत्थ ह ॥10.85.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: युवाम् You two, न not, नः our, सुतौ sons, साक्षात् directly, प्रधान-पुरुष-ईश्वरौ the Supreme Lords of material nature and the individual soul, भू-भार-क्षत्र-क्षपणे to destroy the warrior kings who burden the earth, अवतीर्णौ descended/incarnated, तथा so, आत्थ You have stated, ह indeed

Anvaya: युवां नः सुतौ न, साक्षात् प्रधानपुरुषेश्वरौ भूभारक्षत्रक्षपणे अवतीर्णौ, तथा आत्थ ह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: युवां You two, नः सुतौ न are not merely our sons, साक्षात् directly, प्रधानपुरुषेश्वरौ the Supreme Controllers, भूभारक्षत्रक्षपणे for the destruction of the burden of oppressive royal dynasties on earth, अवतीर्णौ have descended, तथा so, आत्थ ह You Yourself have indeed declared.

Simple Meaning: You two are not merely our sons; You are directly the Supreme Lords of both material nature and the individual soul. You have descended to earth to remove the heavy burden of tyrannical rulers, as You Yourself have declared to us.

तत्ते गतोऽस्म्यरणमद्य पदारविन्दमापन्नसंसृतिभयापहमार्तबन्धो ।
एतावतालमलमिन्द्रियलालसेन मर्त्यात्मदृक् त्वयि परे यदपत्यबुद्धिः ॥10.85.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् therefore, ते Your, गतः अस्मि I have approached/attained, शरणम् refuge/surrender, अद्य today, पद-अरविन्दम् lotus feet, आपन्न-संसृति-भय-अपहम् destroying the fear of material birth and death for those who surrender, आर्त-बन्धो O friend of the distressed, एतावता by this much duration, अलम् अलम् enough indeed, इन्द्रिय-लालसेन with thirst for sensory gratification, मर्त्य-आत्म-दृक् viewing the mortal physical body as the self, त्वयि in You, परे the Supreme Lord, यत् that, अपत्य-बुद्धिः the conception of being my child

Anvaya: तत् आर्तबन्धो! आपन्नसंसृतिभयापहं ते पदारविन्दम् अद्य शरणं गतः अस्मि। इन्द्रियलालसेन मर्त्यात्मदृक् परे त्वयि यत् अपत्यबुद्धिः एतावता अलम् अलम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत् Therefore, आर्तबन्धो O compassionate friend of the afflicted, आपन्नसंसृतिभयापहं which removes the terrible fear of worldly existence for those who seek shelter, ते पदारविन्दम् Your lotus feet, अद्य today, शरणं गतः अस्मि I have taken ultimate refuge in. इन्द्रियलालसेन Driven by sensory attachment, मर्त्यात्मदृक् identifying the perishable physical body with the self, परे त्वयि in You the Supreme Being, यत् अपत्यबुद्धिः that I held the mistaken conception of You as my son, एतावता with that ignorance, अलम् अलम् enough is enough.

Simple Meaning: Therefore, O friend of the distressed, today I take ultimate refuge in Your lotus feet, which destroy the fear of material rebirth for all who seek shelter. Driven by sensory desire and identifying with this perishable body, I mistakenly considered You, the Supreme Lord, to be my son. I have had enough of this illusion.

सूतीगृहे ननु जगाद भवानजो नौ संजज्ञ इत्यनुयुगं निजधर्मगुप्त्यै ।
नानातनूर्गगनवद् विदधज्जहासि को वेद भूम्न उरुगाय विभूतिमायाम् ॥10.85.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: सूतीगृहे in the maternity room, ननु indeed, जगाद spoke, भवान् You, अजः the unborn one, नौ to both of us, संजज्ञे I have appeared/born, इति thus, अनुयुगम् in every millennium, निज-धर्म-गुप्त्यै for the protection of Your own righteousness, नाना-तनुः assuming various forms, गगन-वत् like the sky, विदधत् manifesting, जहासि You abandon/withdraw, कः who, वेद knows, भूम्नः of the infinite, उरुगाय of the Supreme Lord of glorious songs, विभूति-मायाम् the mystic power of opulences

Anvaya: अजः भवान् सूतीगृहे नौ जगाद ननु 'निजधर्मगुप्त्यै अनुयुगं संजज्ञे' इति। गगनवत् नानातनुः विदधत् जहासि। भूम्न उरुगाय विभूतिमायां कः वेद?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अजः भवान् You, the unborn one, सूतीगृहे in the maternity room, नौ to both of us, जगाद you spoke, ननु indeed, 'निजधर्मगुप्त्यै for the protection of righteousness, अनुयुगं in every age, संजज्ञे I take birth' इति thus. गगनवत् Like the sky which remains unattached, नानातनुः with diverse forms, विदधत् manifesting, जहासि and later withdrawing them. भूम्नः Of the all-pervading Supreme, उरुगाय of Him of glorious renown, विभूतिमायां the inconceivable mystic potency of opulences, कः वेद who can fully know?

Simple Meaning: You, the unborn one, right here in the maternity room, declared to us both, “I take birth in every Yuga to protect my own laws of righteousness.” Like the sky, You assume diverse forms and withdraw them without being tainted. Who can ever fully understand the inconceivable mystic opulences of You, the all-pervading Supreme Lord of glorious renown?

श्री शुक उवाच
आकर्ण्येत्थं पितुर्वाक्यं भगवान् सात्वतर्षभः ।

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच — सात्वतर्षभः भगवान् पितुः वाक्यम् आकर्ण्य प्रश्रयानम्रः प्रहसन् श्लक्ष्णया गिरा प्रत्याह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, सात्वतर्षभः the best of the descendants of Satvata, भगवान् the Supreme Lord Krishna, पितुः of His father Vasudeva, वाक्यम् the speech, आकर्ण्य hearing, प्रश्रयानम्रः bowing humbly, प्रहसन् smiling, श्लक्ष्णया गिरा with gentle words, प्रत्याह replied.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
वचो वः समवेतार्थं तातैतदुपमन्महे ।
यन्नः पुत्रान् समुद्दिश्य तत्त्वग्राम उदाहृतः ॥10.85.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, वचः words, वः Your, समवेत-अर्थम् full of profound meaning, तात O father, एतत् this, उपमन्महे I consider, यत् which, नः us, पुत्रान् as Your sons, समुद्दिश्य addressing, तत्त्व-ग्रामः the entire assembly of cosmic truths, उदाहृतः has been expounded

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच — तात! नः पुत्रान् समुद्दिश्य यत् एतत् तत्त्वग्रामः उदाहृतः वः वचः समवेतार्थम् उपमन्महे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, तात O father, नः पुत्रान् us, as Your sons, समुद्दिश्य addressing, यत् that, एतत् this, तत्त्वग्रामः assembly of ultimate truths, उदाहृतः has been spoken, वः वचः Your speech, समवेतार्थम् profoundly meaningful, उपमन्महे I consider.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord said: O father, I consider these words of Yours to be profoundly meaningful. Addressing us as Your sons, You have illuminated the entire collection of ultimate truths.

अहं यूयमसावार्य इमे च द्वारकौकसः ।
सर्वेऽप्येवं यदुश्रेष्ठ विमृश्याः सचराचरम् ॥10.85.23॥

Anvaya: यदुश्रेष्ठ! अहं यूयम् असौ आर्यः इमे द्वारकौकसः च सचराचरं सर्वे अपि एवं विमृश्याः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यदुश्रेष्ठ O best of the Yadus, अहं I, यूयम् you, असौ आर्यः this noble Balarama, इमे द्वारकौकसः च and these citizens of Dwaraka, सचराचरं including all moving and stationary creation, सर्वे अपि all indeed, एवं विमृश्याः in this way, ought to be understood (as Brahman).

Simple Meaning: O best of the Yadus, I, you, My noble brother Balarama, these citizens of Dwaraka, and indeed all moving and non-moving beings in existence should be analyzed and understood as Brahman.

आत्मा ह्येकः स्वयंज्योतिर्नित्योऽन्यो निर्गुणो गुणैः ।
आत्मसृष्टैस्तत्कृतेषु भूतेषु बहुधेयते ॥10.85.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: आत्मा the Self/Supreme Soul, हि indeed, एकः one, स्वयम्-ज्योतिः self-luminous, नित्यः eternal, अन्यः distinct/beyond, निर्गुणः transcendental to material modes, गुणैः by the modes, आत्म-सृष्टैः created by His own power/potency, तत्-कृतेषु in their products/creations, भूतेषु among physical bodies, बहुधा as manifold, ईयते is perceived/appears

Anvaya: आत्मा हि एकः स्वयंज्योतिः नित्यः अन्यः गुणैः निर्गुणः, आत्मसृष्टैः गुणैः तत्कृतेषु भूतेषु बहुधा ईयते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: आत्मा The Self, हि indeed, एकः is one, स्वयंज्योतिः self-luminous, नित्यः eternal, अन्यः distinct from matter, गुणैः निर्गुणः completely free from the material modes, आत्मसृष्टैः गुणैः by the modes of nature created by His own potency, तत्कृतेषु भूतेषु within the physical bodies produced from those modes, बहुधा as manifold, ईयते appears to be divided.

Simple Meaning: The Soul is one, self-luminous, eternal, distinct from material nature, and untouched by the modes. Yet, through the modes created by His own energy, He appears in manifold forms within all the physical bodies produced by those modes.

Anvaya: राजन् (ते) देवक्याः उदरे जाताः कंसविहिंसिताः। सा स्वान् आत्मजान् तान् शोचति, ते इमे अन्तिके अध्यासिते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King Bali, ते those six sons, देवक्याः उदरे in the womb of Devaki, जाताः were born, कंसविहिंसिताः and were slain by Kamsa. सा She (Devaki), स्वान् आत्मजान् तान् for those sons, शोचति is grieving. ते इमे These very sons, अन्तिके near you, अध्यासिते are currently residing.

Simple Meaning: O King Bali, those six sons were subsequently born from the womb of Devaki and were slain by Kamsa. She continues to grieve for her children. Those very same sons are presently residing right here in your proximity.

इत एतान् प्रणेष्यामो मातृशोकापनुत्तये ।
ततः शापाद् विनिर्मुक्ता लोकं यास्यन्ति विज्वराः ॥10.85.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: इतः from here, एतान् these six children, प्रणेष्यामः We shall take away, मातृ-शोक-अपनुत्तये for removing the grief of Our mother, ततः thereafter, शापात् from the curse, विनिर्मुक्ताः completely freed, लोकम् to their divine abode, यास्यन्ति they will go, विज्वराः free from all torment and suffering

Anvaya: मातृशोकापनुत्तये एतान् इतः प्रणेष्यामः। ततः शापात् विनिर्मुक्ताः विज्वराः (सन्तः) लोकं यास्यन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मातृशोकापनुत्तये In order to dispel the sorrow of Our mother, एतान् these (six sons), इतः from this place of Sutala, प्रणेष्यामः We shall take with Us. ततः After that, शापात् from the curse (of Brahma), विनिर्मुक्ताः completely liberated, विज्वराः free from all pain and torment, लोकं to their respective celestial realm, यास्यन्ति they will return.

Simple Meaning: We shall take these six children from here to dispel the sorrow of Our mother. After that, they will be released from their curse and, freed from all torment, will return to their own celestial abode.

स्मरोद्गीथः परिष्वङ्गः पतङ्गः क्षुद्रभृद् घृणी ।
षडिमे मत्प्रसादेन पुनर्यान्ति सद्गतिम् ॥10.85.51॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्मरः Smara, उद्गीथः Udgitha, परिष्वङ्गः Parishvanga, पतङ्गः Patanga, क्षुद्रभृत् Kshudrabhrit, घृणी Ghrini, षट् six, इमे these, मत्-प्रसादेन by My mercy, पुनः again, यान्ति will reach, सत्-गतिम् the ultimate auspicious destination/liberation

Anvaya: इमे षट् स्मरः उद्गीथः परिष्वङ्गः पतङ्गः क्षुद्रभृत् घृणी (च) मत्प्रसादेन पुनः सद्गतिं यान्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इमे षट् These six sons, स्मरः Smara, उद्गीथः Udgitha, परिष्वङ्गः Parishvanga, पतङ्गः Patanga, क्षुद्रभृत् Kshudrabhrit, घृणी and Ghrini, मत्प्रसादेन by My divine grace, पुनः once again, सद्गतिं the supreme liberation, यान्ति shall attain.

Simple Meaning: The six of them—Smara, Udgitha, Parishvanga, Patanga, Kshudrabhrit, and Ghrini—by My divine mercy, will attain the supreme liberation.

इत्युक्त्वा तान् समादाय इन्द्रसेनेन पूजितौ ।
पुनर्द्वारवतीमेत्य मातु: पुत्रानयच्छताम् ॥10.85.52॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति उक्त्वा having spoken thus, तान् those six children, समादाय taking along with Them, इन्द्रसेनेन by Indrasena (Bali), पूजितौ worshipped, पुनः again, द्वारवतीम् to Dwaraka, एत्य having returned, मातुः to Their mother (Devaki), पुत्रान् her sons, अयच्छताम् They handed over

Anvaya: इति उक्त्वा तान् समादाय इन्द्रसेनेन पूजितौ (कृष्णाबलदेवौ) पुनः द्वारवतीम् एत्य मातुः पुत्रान् अयच्छताम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति उक्त्वा Having said this, तान् those (six sons), समादाय taking with Them, इन्द्रसेनेन by King Bali, पूजितौ being duly worshipped, (Krishna and Balarama) पुनः back, द्वारवतीम् to the city of Dwaraka, एत्य returning, मातुः to Their mother Devaki, पुत्रान् her sons, अयच्छताम् presented.

Simple Meaning: Having spoken thus, Krishna and Balarama took the six children with Them. After receiving worship from Bali, They returned to Dwaraka and handed the children over to Their mother, Devaki.

तान् दृष्ट्वा बालकान् देवी पुत्रस्नेहस्नुतस्तनी ।
परिष्वज्याङ्कमारोप्य मूर्ध्न्यजिघ्रदभीक्ष्णशः ॥10.85.53॥

Word-by-word meaning: तान् those, दृष्ट्वा seeing, बालकान् children, देवी lady Devaki, पुत्र-स्नेह-स्नुत-स्तनी whose breasts flowed with milk due to maternal affection, परिष्वज्य embracing them, अङ्कम् upon her lap, आरोप्य placing, मूर्ध्नि on their heads, अजिघ्रत् smelled, अभीक्ष्णशः repeatedly

Anvaya: देवी तान् बालकान् दृष्ट्वा पुत्रस्नेहस्नुतस्तनी परिष्वज्य अङ्कम् आरोप्य अभीक्ष्णशः मूर्ध्नि अजिघ्रत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: देवी Lady Devaki, तान् बालकान् those children, दृष्ट्वा upon seeing, पुत्रस्नेहस्नुतस्तनी her breasts overflowing with milk out of overwhelming motherly love, परिष्वज्य embracing them, अङ्कम् आरोप्य placing them on her lap, अभीक्ष्णशः repeatedly, मूर्ध्नि on their heads, अजिघ्रत् smelled them.

Simple Meaning: Upon seeing those children, Lady Devaki was filled with such intense maternal love that milk began to flow from her breasts. Embracing them affectionately, she placed them on her lap and repeatedly kissed and smelled their heads.

अपाययत् स्तनं प्रीता सुतस्पर्शपरिप्लुता ।
मोहिता मायया विष्णोर्यया सृष्टिः प्रवर्तते ॥10.85.54॥

Word-by-word meaning: अपाययत् she fed/nursed, स्तनम् milk from her breast, प्रीता joyful, सुत-स्पर्श-परिप्लुता overwhelmed by the touch of her sons, मोहिता bewildered, मायया by the illusion, विष्णोः of Lord Vishnu, यया by which, सृष्टिः the creation of the universe, प्रवर्तते proceeds/continues

Anvaya: सुतस्पर्शपरिप्लुता प्रीता (सा) स्तनम् अपाययत्, विष्णोः मायया मोहिता यया सृष्टिः प्रवर्तते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सुतस्पर्शपरिप्लुता Overwhelmed with emotion by the physical touch of her sons, प्रीता filled with joy, (she) स्तनम् her breast milk, अपाययत् fed them, विष्णोः मायया by the illusory energy of Lord Vishnu, मोहिता bewildered, यया by which Maya, सृष्टिः the material universe, प्रवर्तते continues to function.

Simple Meaning: Overwhelmed with joyful emotion at the touch of her long-lost sons, she nursed them at her breast. She was bewildered by the illusory potency of Lord Vishnu, the very Maya by which the entire cosmic creation is sustained and kept in motion.

पीत्वामृतं पयस्तस्याः पीतशेषं गदाभृतः ।
नारायणाङ्गसंस्पर्शप्रतिलब्धात्मदर्शनाः ॥10.85.55॥

Word-by-word meaning: पीत्वा having drunk, अमृतम् nectar-like, पयः milk, तस्याः of her (Devaki), पीत-शेषम् the remnant of what was drunk, गदाभृतः by the wielder of the mace (Lord Krishna), नारायण-अङ्ग-संस्पर्श-प्रतिलब्ध-आत्म-दर्शनाः who regained self-realization through direct bodily contact with Narayana

Anvaya: तस्याः गदाभृतः पीतशेषं अमृतं पयः पीत्वा नारायणाङ्गसंस्पर्शप्रतिलब्धात्मदर्शनाः (ते अभवन्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तस्याः Of Devaki, गदाभृतः by Lord Krishna, पीतशेषं which was the remnant previously tasted by Him, अमृतं पयः the nectar-like breast milk, पीत्वा having drunk, नारायणाङ्गसंस्पर्शप्रतिलब्धात्मदर्शनाः (they) gained their self-realization through physical touch with the limbs of Lord Narayana.

Simple Meaning: Having drunk Devaki's nectar-like milk—which was the remnant of the previously tasted milk by Lord Krishna Himself—and having come into direct physical contact with the transcendental body of Lord Narayana, the six sons gained self-realization.

ते नमस्कृत्य गोविन्दं देवकीं पितरं बलम् ।
मिषतां सर्वभूतानां ययुर्धाम दिवौकसाम् ॥10.85.56॥

Word-by-word meaning: ते they (the six sons), नमस्कृत्य offering obeisances, गोविन्दम् unto Lord Govinda (Krishna), देवकीम् unto mother Devaki, पितरम् unto their father (Vasudeva), बलम् unto Lord Balarama, मिषताम् while watching, सर्व-भूतानाम् all living beings, ययुः they went, धाम to the abode, दिव-ओकसाम् of the inhabitants of heaven

Anvaya: ते गोविन्दं देवकीं पितरं बलं नमस्कृत्य सर्वभूतानां मिषतां दिवौकसां धाम ययुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ते Those (six sons), गोविन्दं unto Lord Govinda, देवकीं unto Devaki, पितरं unto father Vasudeva, बलं and unto Lord Balarama, नमस्कृत्य offering respectful bows, सर्वभूतानां of all the living entities present, मिषतां while they were gazing on, दिवौकसां धाम to the heavenly abode of the celestials, ययुः departed.

Simple Meaning: Offering their humble obeisances unto Lord Govinda, Devaki, Vasudeva, and Balarama, those six sons ascended to the celestial realm of the devas as everyone looked on.

तं दृष्ट्वा देवकी देवी मृतागमननिर्गमम् ।
मेने सुविस्मिता मायां कृष्णस्य रचितां नृप ॥10.85.57॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् that, दृष्ट्वा seeing, देवकी Devaki, देवी the lady, मृत-आगमन-निर्गमम् the arrival and departure of her deceased sons, मेने considered, सुविस्मिता highly amazed, मायाम् the illusory potency, कृष्णस्य of Lord Krishna, रचिताम् arranged/created, नृप O King

Anvaya: नृप! देवी देवकी तं मृतागमननिर्गमं दृष्ट्वा सुविस्मिता (सती) (एतत्) कृष्णस्य रचितां मायां मेने।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King Parikshit, देवी देवकी Lady Devaki, तं मृतागमननिर्गमं that coming back to life and subsequent departure of her dead sons, दृष्ट्वा upon beholding, सुविस्मिता utterly astonished, कृष्णस्य of Krishna, रचितां arranged, मायां to be the work of His divine yogamaya potency, मेने understood it to be.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, seeing her deceased sons return to life and then ascend to the heavens, Lady Devaki was filled with wonder. She understood that this entire astounding event was arranged by the divine mystic potency of Lord Krishna.

एवं विधान्यद्भुतानि कृष्णस्य परमात्मनः ।
वीर्याण्यनन्तवीर्यस्य सन्त्यनन्तानि भारत ॥10.85.58॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् विधानि of such a kind, अद्भुतानि wonderful deeds, कृष्णस्य of Lord Krishna, परमात्मनः of the Supreme Soul, वीर्याणि heroic acts/powers, अनन्त-वीर्यस्य of Him whose prowess is infinite, सन्ति there are, अनन्तानि unlimited, भारत O descendant of Bharata (King Parikshit)

Anvaya: भारत! अनन्तवीर्यस्य परमात्मनः कृष्णस्य एवं विधानि अद्भुतानि वीर्याणि अनन्तानि सन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भारत O Parikshit descendant of Bharata, अनन्तवीर्यस्य of the Lord possessing limitless power, परमात्मनः कृष्णस्य of the Supreme Soul, Krishna, एवं विधानि such types, अद्भुतानि miraculous, वीर्याणि heroic pastimes and potencies, अनन्तानि boundless, सन्ति exist.

Simple Meaning: O Parikshit, descendant of Bharata! Such miraculous and wondrous acts performed by Lord Krishna, the Supreme Soul who possesses infinite prowess, are truly unlimited.

सूत उवाच
य इदमनुशृणोति श्रावयेद् वा मुरारेश्चरितममृतकीर्तेर्वर्णितं व्यासपुत्रैः ।
जगदघभिदलं तद्भक्तसत्कर्णपूरं भगवति कृतचित्तो याति तत्क्षेमधाम ॥10.86.59॥

Word-by-word meaning: सूतः उवाच Suta said, यः whoever, इदम् this, अनुशृणोति regularly hears, श्रावयेत् causes others to hear, वा or, मुरारेः of Lord Murari (Krishna), चरितम् the divine activities and pastimes, अमृत-कीर्तेः whose fame is immortal/nectar-like, वर्णितम् described, व्यास-पुत्रैः by the son of Vyasa - Shuka, जगत-अघ-भित् destroying the sins of the entire world, अलम् completely, तत्-भक्त-सत्-कर्ण-पूरम् the sublime ear ornament for His devotees, भगवति upon the Supreme Lord, कृत-चित्तः fixing one's mind, याति goes/attains, तत्-क्षेम-धाम that eternal abode of ultimate peace and security

Anvaya: सूतः उवाच — यः व्यासपुत्रैः वर्णितम् अमृतकीर्तेः मुरारेः जगदघभित् अलं तद्भक्तसत्कर्णपूरं इदं चरितम् अनुशृणोति वा श्रावयेत्, (सः) भगवति कृतचित्तः (सन्) तत्क्षेमधाम याति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सूतः उवाच Suta said, यः whoever, व्यासपुत्रैः by the son of Vyasa - Shuka, वर्णितम् described, अमृतकीर्तेः मुरारेः whose glory is eternal nectar, of Lord Murari, जगदघभित् which destroys all sins of the world, अलं तद्भक्तसत्कर्णपूरं which completely acts as the most auspicious ear ornament for His devotees, इदं चरितम् this divine narrator of pastimes, अनुशृणोति वा श्रावयेत् faithfully hears, or recites to others, (सः) that person, भगवति upon the Supreme Lord, कृतचित्तः fixing their mind, तत्क्षेमधाम that eternal abode of supreme well-being, याति reaches.

Simple Meaning: Suta Gosvami said: Anyone who regularly hears or narrates these glorious pastimes of Lord Murari—whose eternal fame is like nectar, as described by Shuka—will find all worldly sins destroyed. These pastimes serve as the most precious ornament for the ears of pure devotees. By absorbing one's mind in the Supreme Lord through this narration, one surely attains His supreme and eternal abode of complete peace and security.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “कृष्णोपदेश​मृताग्रजानयनं” नाम पञ्चाशीतितमः अध्यायः ॥85॥

Thus ends the eighty-fifth discourse named “Krishna’s teachings to Vasudeva and bringing back of His six elder brothers (who were killed by Kamsa) to life”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
