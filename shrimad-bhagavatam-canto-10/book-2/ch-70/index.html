दशमः स्कन्धः

कृष्णस्य दिनचर्या जरासन्धबन्दीकृतनृपाणां दूतप्रेषणं च नाम सप्ततितमः अध्यायः

श्री शुक उवाच
अथोषस्युपवृत्तायां कुक्कुटान् कूजतोऽशपन् ।
गृहीतकण्ठ्यः पतिभिर्माधव्यो विरहातुराः ॥10.70.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अथ then, उषसि upondawn, उपवृत्तायाम् approaching/arriving, कुक्कुटान् the roosters, कूजतः crowing, अशपन् cursed, गृहीत-कण्ठ्यः whose necks were embraced, पतिभिः by their husbands (Lord Krishna), माधव्यः the wives of Madhava (Krishna), विरह-आतुराः distressed by the thought of impending separation

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच - अथ उषसि उपवृत्तायां, पतिभिः गृहीतकण्ठ्यः विरहातुराः माधव्यः कूजतः कुक्कुटान् अशपन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said - अथ then, उषसि when dawn, उपवृत्तायां was fast approaching, पतिभिः by their beloved husband Krishna, गृहीतकण्ठ्यः embraced around their necks, विरहातुराः deeply distressed at the thought of upcoming separation, माधव्यः the wives of Madhava, कूजतः crowing, कुक्कुटान् the roosters, अशपन् cursed.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: As dawn began to break, the roosters started to crow, signaling the end of the night. Each of Krishna's queens, who were held closely in the embrace of their beloved husband Krishna (in their own separate palace), grew anxious at the thought of separation from Him as He would soon leave for His morning duties. In their distress, they cursed the crowing roosters for disturbing their precious moments together.

वयांस्यरूरुवन् कृष्णं बोधयन्तीव वन्दिनः ।
गायत्स्वलिष्वनिद्राणि मन्दारवनवायुभिः ॥10.70.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: वयांसि the birds, आरूरुवन् chirped loudly/made sweet sounds, कृष्णम् Krishna, बोधयन्ति इव as if awakening Him, वन्दिनः like court bards/praisers, गायत्सु while singing, अलिषु the honeybees, अ-निद्राणि unable to sleep/kept awake, मन्दार-वन-वायुभिः by the gentle breezes carrying fragrance from the Mandara flower groves

Anvaya: मन्दारवनवायुभिः अनिद्राणि वयांसि, गायत्सु अलिषु, वन्दिनः इव कृष्णं बोधयन्ति (सन्ति) आरूरुवन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मन्दारवनवायुभिः By the fragrant breezes coming from the Mandara flower groves, अनिद्राणि kept awake and aroused from slumber, वयांसि the birds, गायत्सु अलिषु while the bees were humming sweet songs, वन्दिनः इव like royal panegyrists or bards, कृष्णं Krishna, बोधयन्ति to wake Him up, आरूरुवन् chirped melodiously.

Simple Meaning: Awoken by the cool, fragrant breezes blowing from the Mandara flower gardens, the birds began to chirp melodiously amid the sweet humming of bees. Like royal bards chanting morning praises, they sang softly to awaken Krishna from His sleep.

मुहुर्तं तं तु वैदर्भी नामृष्यदतिशोभनम् ।
परिरम्भणविश्लेषात् प्रियबाह्वन्तरं गता ॥10.70.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: मुहूर्तम् the hour of dawn, तम् that, तु indeed, वैदर्भी Rukmini (the princess of Vidarbha), न अमृष्यत् did not tolerate/relish, अति-शोभनम् most auspicious/beautiful, परिरम्भण-विश्लेषात् because of the impending separation from His embrace, प्रिय-बाहु-अन्तरम् within the arms/chest of her beloved Lord Krishna, गता resting/enfolded

Anvaya: प्रियबाह्वन्तरं गता वैदर्भी परिरम्भणविश्लेषात् अतिशोभनं तु तं मुहूर्तं न अमृष्यत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रियबाह्वन्तरं Within the warm embrace of her beloved Lord's chest and arms, गता resting, वैदर्भी Vaidarbhi (Rukmini), परिरम्भणविश्लेषात् because it signaled the end of His loving embrace, अतिशोभनं extraordinarily auspicious and beautiful though it was, तु indeed, तं मुहूर्तं  that hour of dawn, न अमृष्यत् could not welcome or tolerate.

Simple Meaning: Resting within the comforting embrace of her beloved Krishna, Rukmini could not welcome even that most auspicious hour of dawn. Though the time was inherently holy and beautiful, to her it only brought sorrow, as it signaled the painful end of their intimate embrace when Krishna would arise for His morning duties.

ब्राह्मे मुहुर्त उत्थाय वार्युपस्पृश्य माधवः ।
दध्यौ प्रसन्नकरण आत्मानं तमसः परम् ॥10.70.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्राह्मे मुहुर्ते during the holy hour of brahma-muhurta (before dawn), उत्थाय rising/getting up, वारी उपस्पृश्य touching/sipping water (performing achamana for purification), माधवः Madhava (Krishna), दध्यौ meditated upon, प्रसन्न-करणः with serene and tranquil senses, आत्मानम् the Supreme Self, तमसः परम् beyond all material darkness/ignorance

Anvaya: माधवः ब्राह्मे मुहुर्ते उत्थाय वार्युपस्पृश्य प्रसन्नकरणः तमसः परम् आत्मानं दध्यौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: माधवः Krishna, ब्राह्मे मुहुर्ते during the sacred brahma-muhurta, उत्थाय rising from bed, वार्युपस्पृश्य sipping clean water for ritual purification (achamana), प्रसन्नकरणः with perfectly calm, tranquil, and purified mind and senses, तमसः परम् existing beyond all cosmic darkness and material illusion, आत्मानं upon His own Supreme Self, दध्यौ meditated.

Simple Meaning: Rising from bed during the auspicious brahma-muhurta period, Krishna performed ritual ablutions by sipping water. Then, with a completely serene mind and controlled senses, He absorbed Himself in meditation upon His own transcendental, supreme self—the ultimate reality that lies beyond all material darkness and delusion.

एकं स्वयंज्योतिरनन्यमव्ययं स्वसंस्थया नित्यनिरस्तकल्मषम् ।
ब्रह्माख्यमस्योद्भवनाशहेतुभिः स्वशक्तिभिर्लक्षितभाव निर्वृतिम् ॥10.70.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: एकम् one without a second (non-dual), स्वयम्-ज्योतिः self-luminous, अनन्यम् unsurpassed/without an equal, अव्ययम् imperishable/infallible, स्व-संस्थया by His own divine nature/existence, नित्य-निरस्त-कल्मषम् eternally free from all material contamination and defects, ब्रह्म-आख्यम् known by the name Brahman, अस्य of this cosmos, उद्भव-नाश-हेतुभिः as the cause of creation, maintenance, and destruction, स्व-शक्तिभिः through His own potencies, लक्षित-भाव-निर्वृतिम् whose blissful existence is realized and indicated

Anvaya: (माधवः) अस्य उद्भवनाशहेतुभिः स्वशक्तिभिः लक्षितभावनिर्वृतिं, स्वसंस्थया नित्यनिरस्तकल्मषम्, एकं स्वयंज्योतिः अनन्यम् अव्ययं ब्रह्माख्यं (स्वात्मानं दध्यौ)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (माधवः Krishna), अस्य (विश्वस्य) of this material world, उद्भवनाशहेतुभिः through the causes of creation, maintenance, and dissolution, स्वशक्तिभिः by His own divine energies (Maya/Yogamaya), लक्षितभावनिर्वृतिं whose transcendental existence and supreme bliss are clearly indicated, स्वसंस्थया by His own inherent potency, नित्यनिरस्तकल्मषम् eternally untainted by material impurities, एकं one undivided non-dual reality, स्वयंज्योतिः self-effulgent, अनन्यम् without an equal, अव्ययं indestructible, ब्रह्माख्यं designated as the Absolute Truth (Brahman), (स्वात्मानं दध्यौ meditated upon His own Self).

Simple Meaning: Krishna meditated upon His own eternal, transcendental Self—known as the Absolute Supreme Truth (Brahman). He is non-dual, self-luminous, matchless, and imperishable. By His own inherent potency, He remains eternally free from all material flaws, and His infinite bliss and supreme existence are demonstrated throughout the universe via His energies that govern creation, maintenance, and dissolution.

अथाप्लुतोऽम्भस्यमले यथाविधि क्रियाकलापं परिधाय वाससी ।
चकार सन्ध्योपगमादि सत्तमो हुतानलो ब्रह्म जजाप वाग्यतः ॥10.70.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथ then, आप्लुतः having bathed, अम्भसि in water, अमले pure/clear, यथा-विधि according to scriptural injunctions, क्रिया-कलापम् the whole series of daily duties, परिधाय having donned, वाससी two clean garments (upper and lower garments), चकार performed, सन्ध्या-उपगम-आदि beginning with the morning sandhyavandanam, सत्-तमः the most virtuous among the righteous, हुत-अनलः offering oblations into the sacred fire, ब्रह्म the sacred Vedic mantra (the Gayatri mantra), जजाप chanted/recited, वाक्-यतः maintaining silence (controlling speech)

Anvaya: अथ सत्तमः (कृष्णः) अमले अम्भसि यथाविधि आप्लुतः वाससी परिधाय क्रियाकलापं (तथा) सन्ध्योपगमादि चकार, वाग्यतः (सन्) हुतानलः ब्रह्म जजाप।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ Then, सत्तमः the supreme among the virtuous ( Krishna), अमले अम्भसि in pure water, यथाविधि in accordance with scriptural rites, आप्लुतः having bathed, वाससी two fresh garments, परिधाय having worn, क्रियाकलापं the complete sequence of morning rituals, (तथा and), सन्ध्योपगमादि performance of the morning twilight worship (Sandhyavandanam) and allied rites, चकार executed, वाग्यतः maintaining strict silence, हुतानलः having offered oblations into the sacred sacrificial fires, ब्रह्म the holy Gayatri mantra, जजाप chanted in quiet contemplation.

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, Krishna—the supreme exemplar of righteous virtue—bathed in pure water according to prescribed scriptural injunctions and dressed in two fresh garments. He executed the full sequence of morning rituals, beginning with the Sandhya worship. Then, maintaining solemn silence, He offered oblations into the sacred sacrificial fire and chanted the holy Gayatri mantra with mind focused in devotion.

उपस्थायार्कमुद्यन्तं तर्पयित्वाऽऽत्मनः कलाः।
देवानृषीन् पितृन् वृद्धान् विप्रानभ्यर्च्य चात्मवान् ॥10.70.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: उपस्थाय worshiping/offering prayers to, अर्कम् the rising sun, उद्यन्तम् as it ascends, तर्पयित्वा offering oblations/water, आत्मनः His own, कलाः expansions/plenary portions, देवान् the devas, ऋषीन् the sages, पितॄन् the ancestors, वृद्धान् the elders, विप्रान् the holy brahmanas, अभ्यर्च्य having reverently worshiped, च and, आत्मवान् the self-possessed/self-mastered Lord

Anvaya: (अथ) आत्मवान् (कृष्णः) उद्यन्तम् अर्कम् उपस्थाय, आत्मनः कलाः देवान् ऋषीन् पितॄन् तर्पयित्वा, वृद्धान् विप्रान् च अभ्यर्च्य।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ Then, आत्मवान् the perfectly self-controlled and supreme Lord Krishna, उद्यन्तम् अर्कम् unto the rising sun, उपस्थाय offering formal prayers and reverence, आत्मनः कलाः who are expansions and divine manifestations of His own Self, देवान् the devas, ऋषीन् the great sages, पितॄन् the forefathers/ancestors, तर्पयित्वा having satisfied with water offerings, वृद्धान् the venerated elders, विप्रान् च and the learned brahmanas, अभ्यर्च्य reverently worshiped.

Simple Meaning: Krishna, the supreme master of all self-control, then paid His respects to the rising sun. He offered water oblations to satisfy the devas, sages, and ancestors—who are all expansions of His own universal being. Thereafter, He reverently worshiped the elders and holy brahmanas with utmost devotion.

धेनूनां रुक्मशृङ्गीणां साध्वीनां मौक्तिकस्रजाम् ।
पयस्विनीनां गृष्टीनां सवत्सानां सुवाससाम् ॥10.70.8॥

ददौ रूप्यखुराग्राणां क्षौमाजिनतिलैः सह ।
अलङ्कृतेभ्यो वेप्रेभ्यो बद्वं बद्वं दिने दिने ॥10.70.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: धेनूनाम् of cows, रुक्म-शृङ्गीणाम् having gold plated horns, साध्वीनाम् gentle/docile, मौक्तिक-स्रजाम् wearing pearl necklaces, पयस्विनीनाम् yielding abundant milk, गृष्टीनाम् having calved only once, स-वत्सानाम् along with their calves, सु-वाससाम् draped in fine garments, ददौ gave in charity, रूप्य-खुर-अग्राणाम् having silver plated hooves, क्षौम-अजिन-तिलैः सह together with silk cloth, deer skins, and sesame seeds, अलङ्कृतेभ्यः adorned/decorated, विप्रेभ्यः unto the learned brahmanas, बद्वम् बद्वम् thirteen thousand eight hundred and four, दिने दिने day after day

Anvaya: (कृष्णः) दिने दिने रुक्मशृङ्गीणां मौक्तिकस्रजां पयस्विनीनां साध्वीनां गृष्टीनां सवत्सानां सुवाससां रूप्यखुराग्राणां धेनूनां बद्वं बद्वम् अलङ्कृतेभ्यः विप्रेभ्यः क्षौमाजिनतिलैः सह ददौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दिने दिने Every day, रुक्मशृङ्गीणां  whose horns were covered in gold, मौक्तिकस्रजां decorated with pearl garlands, पयस्विनीनां rich with milk, साध्वीनां naturally gentle, गृष्टीनां having calved for the first time, सवत्सानां accompanied by their calves, सुवाससां covered in fine cloth, रूप्यखुराग्राणां with silver plated hooves, धेनूनां of such cows, बद्वं बद्वम् thirteen thousand eight hundred and four, अलङ्कृतेभ्यः विप्रेभ्यः unto nicely ornamented learned brahmanas, क्षौमाजिनतिलैः सह along with silk garments, deer skins, and sesame seeds, ददौ (Krishna) gave in charity.

Simple Meaning: Every single day, Krishna gave away thirteen thousand eight hundred and four of gentle cows in charity to well adorned, learned brahmanas. These cows had calved only once, yielded abundant milk, and were accompanied by their calves. They were beautifully decorated with gold plated horns, silver hooves, fine clothing, and pearl necklaces. Along with these cows, He also presented the brahmanas with silk garments, deer skins, and sesame seeds.

गोविप्रदेवतावृद्धगुरून् भूतानि सर्वशः ।
नमस्कृत्यात्मसम्भूतीर्मङ्गलानि समस्पृशत् ॥10.70.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: गो-विप्र-देवता-वृद्ध-गुरून् unto the cows, the brahmanas, the devas, the elders, and the spiritual masters, भूतानि all living beings, सर्वशः in all respects, नमस्कृत्य having offered bowings/prostrations, आत्म-सम्भूतीः who are manifestations of Himself, मङ्गलानि auspicious items, समस्पृशत् touched thoughtfully

Anvaya: (कृष्णः) आत्मसम्भूतीः गोविप्रदेवतावृद्धगुरून् सर्वशः भूतानि च नमस्कृत्य मङ्गलानि समस्पृशत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: आत्मसम्भूतीः Who are all manifestations of His own supreme Self, गोविप्रदेवतावृद्धगुरून् unto the cows, learned brahmanas, devas, venerated elders, and spiritual masters, सर्वशः भूतानि च and unto all living entities in every way, नमस्कृत्य after offering His respectful prostrations, मङ्गलानि highly auspicious objects, समस्पृशत् (Krishna) touched.

Simple Meaning: Krishna bowed down respectfully to the cows, learned brahmanas, devas, elders, spiritual masters, and all living entities, recognizing them all as manifestations of His own supreme being. After offering these respectful bows, He touched various auspicious items to start His day.

आत्मानं भूषयामास नरलोकविभूषणम् ।
वासोभिर्भूषणैः स्वीयैर्दिव्यस्रगनुलेपनैः ॥10.70.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: आत्मानम् Himself, भूषयामास adorned/decorated, नर-लोक-विभूषणम् the ornament of human society, वासोभिः with garments, भूषणैः with ornaments, स्वीयैः His own, दिव्य-स्रग्-अनुलेपनैः with divine flower garlands and fragrant pastes

Anvaya: स्वीयैः वासोभिः भूषणैः दिव्यस्रगनुलेपनैः नरलोकविभूषणम् आत्मानं भूषयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: स्वीयैः वासोभिः With His own garments, भूषणैः gorgeous ornaments, दिव्यस्रगनुलेपनैः and heavenly flower garlands along with fragrant sandal pastes, नरलोकविभूषणम् He who is the supreme ornament of all human society, आत्मानं Himself, भूषयामास adorned.

Simple Meaning: Krishna, who is the ultimate ornament of all human society, then adorned Himself with exquisite garments, precious ornaments, heavenly flower garlands, and fragrant pastes.

अवेक्ष्याज्यं तथाऽऽदर्शं गोवृषद्विजदेवताः ।
कामांश्च सर्ववर्णानां पौरान्तःपुरचारिणाम् ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्ण कृष्ण O Krishna O Krishna, अप्रमेयात्मन् O Lord of immeasurable nature, प्रपन्नभयभञ्जन O dispeller of the fear of those who surrender unto You, पृथग्धियः possessing divided and bewildered minds, भवभीताः terrified of material existence, वयं त्वां शरणं यामः we take shelter in You.

Simple Meaning: “O Krishna! O Krishna! O immeasurable Supreme Lord! O dispeller of fear for all who surrender unto You! We have been bewildered by material desires and are terrified of conditional existence. In our distress, we come to You and take full shelter in You.

लोको विकर्मनिरतः कुशले प्रमत्तः कर्मण्ययं त्वदुदिते भवदर्चने स्वे ।

Word-by-word meaning: लोकः the general public/people, विकर्म-निरतः engaged in sinful activities, कुशले in auspicious/beneficial activity, प्रमत्तः careless/negligent, कर्मणि in duty, अयम् this, त्वत्-उदिते prescribed by You, भवत्-अर्चने in Your worship, स्वे which is naturally beneficial for oneself, यः who, तावत् indeed, अस्य of this person, बलवान् mighty, इह in this world, जीवित-आशाम् hopes for living long, सद्यः instantly, छिनत्ति cuts down/destroys, अनिमिषाय unto Time which never blinks, नमः अस्तु let there be bowings, तस्मै unto Him

Anvaya: अयं लोकः विकर्मनिरतः। त्वदुदिते स्वे भवदर्चने कुशले कर्मणि प्रमत्तः। यः तावत् इह अस्य बलवान् जीविताशां सद्यः छिनत्ति, तस्मै अनिमिषाय नमः अस्तु।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अयं लोकः These people of the world, विकर्मनिरतः are fully engaged in sinful acts. त्वदुदिते स्वे भवदर्चने Prescribed by You for their own benefit in Your worship, कुशले कर्मणि in auspicious spiritual duties, प्रमत्तः are completely negligent. यः तावत् But that which, इह अस्य in this world of theirs, बलवान् as the powerful force of Time, जीविताशां their strong hopes of long life, सद्यः छिनत्ति instantly cuts down, तस्मै अनिमिषाय unto that unblinking Eternal Time, नमः अस्तु let our respectful obeisances be offered.

Simple Meaning: “People in this world are generally devoted to sinful activities and completely neglectful of the auspicious duties You prescribe for their benefit—namely, Your devotional worship. Yet the powerful force of unblinking Time suddenly arrives and cuts down all their hopes for a long life. We offer our respectful obeisances unto You, who operate as that relentless Time.

लोके भवाञ्जगदिनः कलयावतीर्णः सद्रक्षणाय खलनिग्रहणाय चान्यः ।
कश्चित् त्वदीयमतियातिनिदेशमीश किं वा जनः स्वकृतमृच्छति तन्न विद्मः ॥10.70.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: लोके in this world, भवान् You, जगत-इनः the master/ruler of the universe, कलया with Your plenary portion, अवतीर्णः incarnated, सत्-रक्षणाय for the protection of the saintly, खल-निग्रहणाय for the chastisement of the wicked, च and, अन्यः other, कश्चित् anyone, त्वदीयम् Your, अतियाति transgresses, निर्देशम् order/will, ईश O Supreme Lord, किम् वा or else, जनः a person, स्व-कृतम् the result of his own past deeds, ऋच्छति suffers/reaps, तत् that, न do not, विद्मः we know

Anvaya: ईश! जगदिनः भवान् सद्रक्षणाय खलनिग्रहणाय च कलया लोके अवतीर्णः। अन्यः कश्चित् त्वदीयं निर्देशम् अतियाति किं वा जनः स्वकृतम् ऋच्छति, तत् न विद्मः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ईश O Lord, जगदिनः भवान् You who are the master of the universe, सद्रक्षणाय खलनिग्रहणाय च for protecting the righteous and chastising the wicked, कलया with Your plenary portion, लोके अवतीर्णः have descended into this world. अन्यः कश्चित् Other person, त्वदीयं निर्देशम् अतियाति is disobeying Your supreme divine order, किं वा or whether, जनः स्वकृतम् ऋच्छति a person simply reaps the fruits of his own karma, तत् न विद्मः that we do not understand.

Simple Meaning: “O Lord, You are the master of the whole universe who has descended into this world with Your plenary portion to protect the virtuous and punish the wicked. We cannot comprehend how anyone (like Jarasandha) can defy Your divine will, or whether people are simply suffering the fruits of their own past deeds.

स्वप्नायितं नृपसुखं परतन्त्रमीश शश्वद्भयेन मृतकेन धुरं वहामः ।
हित्वा तदात्मनि सुखं त्वदनीहलभ्यं क्लिश्यामहेऽतिकृपणास्तव माययेह ॥10.70.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्वप्नायितम् illusory/like a dream, नृप-सुखम् royal happiness, पर-तन्त्रम् dependent on others, ईश O Lord, शश्वत् constantly, भयेन with fear, मृतकेन by this perishable body, धुरम् the heavy burden, वहामः we carry, हित्वा neglecting/giving up, तत् that, आत्मनि within the self, सुखम् true happiness, त्वत्-अनीह-लभ्यम् attainable by selfless devotion unto You, क्लिश्यामहे we suffer, अति-कृपणाः most wretched, तव Your, मायया by the illusion, इह in this material world

Anvaya: ईश! परतन्त्रं शश्वत् भयेन (युक्तं) नृपसुखं स्वप्नायितम्। (वयं) मृतकेन धुरं वहामः। अतिकृपणाः तव मायया इह आत्मनि त्वदनीहलभ्यं तत् सुखं हित्वा क्लिश्यामहे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ईश O Supreme Lord, परतन्त्रं dependent on external circumstances, शश्वत् भयेन and filled with constant fear, नृपसुखं the (so-called) happiness of being a king, स्वप्नायितम् is entirely illusory like a dream. (वयं) We, मृतकेन with this perishable, corpse-like body, धुरं वहामः carry the heavy burden of worldly responsibilities. अतिकृपणाः Being most unfortunate and wretched, तव मायया by the influence of Your illusory potency, इह in this world, आत्मनि resting within the self, त्वदनीहलभ्यं attainable through desireless devotion unto You, तत् सुखं that spiritual bliss, हित्वा having abandoned, क्लिश्यामहे we suffer continuously.

Simple Meaning: O Lord, the worldly happiness of a king is completely illusory—just like a dream—dependent on others and haunted by constant fear. Using these perishable, corpse-like bodies, we carry a heavy burden of responsibilities. Driven by Your illusory potency, we most unfortunate souls have neglected the true spiritual bliss hidden within ourselves—which is attained effortlessly by selfless devotion to You—and instead choose to suffer in this material world.

तन्नो भवान् प्रणतशोकहराङ्घ्रियुग्मो बद्धान् वियुङ्क्ष्व मगधाह्वयकर्मपाशात् ।
यो भूभुजोऽयुतमतङ्गजवीर्यमेको बिभ्रद् रुरोध भवने मृगराडिवावीः ॥10.70.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् therefore, नः us, भवान् Your Lordship, प्रणत-शोक-हर-अङ्घरि-युग्मः whose pair of lotus feet removes the sorrow of those who surrender, बद्धान् who are bound/imprisoned, वियुङ्क्ष्व please release/untie, मगध-आह्वय-कर्म-पाशात् from the bondage of karma in the form of the King of Magadha (Jarasandha), यः who, भूभुजः the kings, अयुत-मातङ्ग-वीर्यम् the prowess of ten thousand elephants, एकः alone, बिभ्रत् possessing, रुरोध imprisoned, भवने in his fortress, मृग-राट् a lion, इव like, अवीः sheep

Anvaya: तत् प्रणतशोकहराङ्घ्रियुग्मः भवान् बद्धान् नः मगधाह्वयकर्मपाशात् वियुङ्क्ष्व, यः एकः अयुतमातङ्गवीर्यं बिभ्रत् मृगराट् अवीः इव भूभुजः भवने रुरोध।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत् Therefore, प्रणतशोकहराङ्घ्रियुग्मः You whose pair of feet removes the grief of those who bow down, भवान् Your Lordship, बद्धान् नः us who are captive, मगधाह्वयकर्मपाशात् from the shackles of past karma manifesting as Jarasandha, the King of Magadha, वियुङ्क्ष्व please deliver. यः He who, एकः alone, अयुतमातङ्गवीर्यं the mighty strength of ten thousand elephants, बिभ्रत् possessing, मृगराट् a lion, अवीः इव just like sheep, भूभुजः the kings, भवने रुरोध has locked up inside his prison stronghold.

Simple Meaning: “Therefore, O Lord, whose lotus feet destroy the suffering of all who surrender unto You, please free us from these bonds born of our past karma in the form of Jarasandha. Possessing the strength of ten thousand elephants single-handedly, he has trapped us kings in his fortress just as a lion traps a flock of helpless sheep.

यो वै त्वया द्विनवकृत्व उदात्तचक्र भग्नो मृधे खलु भवन्तमनन्तवीर्यम् ।
जित्वा नृलोकनिरतं सकृदूढदर्पो युष्मत्प्रजा रुजति नोऽजित तद् विधेहि ॥10.70.30॥

Anvaya: उदात्तचक्र! यः वै द्विनवकृत्वः त्वया मृधे भग्नः, अनन्तवीर्यं नृलोकनिरतं भवन्तं सकृत् जित्वा (सः) ऊढदर्पः (सन्) युष्मत्प्रजाः नः रुजति। अजित! तत् (उचितं) विधेहि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: उदात्तचक्र O Lord who holds the radiant Sudarshana disc, यः वै indeed he who (Jarasandha), द्विनवकृत्वः eighteen times, त्वया by You, मृधे भग्नः was utterly defeated in battle, अनन्तवीर्यं नृलोकनिरतं भवन्तं You who possess boundless strength and engaging in mortal-like play (retreated to Dvaraka), सकृत् जित्वा defeating just once, सः he, ऊढदर्पः  (सन्) becoming puffed up with arrogance, युष्मत्प्रजाः Your subjects, नः us, रुजति torments. अजित O Unconquerable Lord, तत् therefore, विधेहि please tell us.

Simple Meaning: “O Lord wielder of the Sudarshana disc! Eighteen times You defeated Jarasandha in battle. Yet, when You, who possess boundless strength, behaved as a human, he defeated You just once and became puffed up with supreme arrogance. Now, filled with pride, he torments us—Your own subjects. O Unconquerable One, please take whatever action You deem fit!”

तथात्र ब्रूह्यनुष्ठेयं श्रद्दधमः करवाम तत् ॥10.70.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, त्वम् you, हि indeed, नः our, परमम् supreme/best, चक्षुः eye/vision, सुहृत् dear friend/well-wisher, मन्त्र-अर्थ-तत्त्व-वित् who knows the essential truth of political counsel and duty, तथा therefore, अत्र in this situation, ब्रूहि tell/speak, अनुष्ठेयम् what ought to be done, श्रद्दधमः having full faith, करवाम we shall execute, तत् that

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच— त्वं हि नः परमं चक्षुः मन्त्रार्थतत्त्ववित् सुहृत् (असि)। तथा अत्र अनुष्ठेयं ब्रूहि, (वयं) श्रद्दधमः तत् करवाम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said— त्वं हि you indeed, नः our, परमं चक्षुः highest vision/guidance, मन्त्रार्थतत्त्ववित् who understands the deepest core of counsel, सुहृत् and our true well-wisher (are). तथा Therefore, अत्र in this dilemma, अनुष्ठेयं the proper course of action, ब्रूहि speak; (वयं) we, श्रद्दधमः placing complete trust in your words, तत् करवाम shall carry that out.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord Krishna said: "My dear Uddhava, you are truly our highest vision and best guide, deeply versed in the art of sound counsel and dedicated to our ultimate welfare. Therefore, please tell us what ought to be done in this present dilemma. Having full faith in your wisdom, we shall act according to your guidance."

इत्युपामन्त्रितो भर्त्रा सर्वज्ञेनापि मुग्धवत् ।
निदेशं शिरसाऽऽधाय उद्धवः प्रत्यभाषत ॥10.70.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, उपामन्त्रितः consulted/addressed, भर्त्रा by his master (Lord Krishna), सर्व-ज्ञेन who is all-knowing, अपि even though, मुग्ध-वत् as if bewildered/innocent, निदेशम् the instruction/order, शिरसा with his head, आधाय placing/accepting respectfully, उद्धवः Uddhava, प्रत्यभाषत replied

Anvaya: सर्वज्ञेन अपि मुग्धवत् भर्त्रा इति उपामन्त्रितः उद्धवः (तस्य) निदेशं शिरसा आधाय प्रत्यभाषत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सर्वज्ञेन अपि Even though He is fully omniscient, मुग्धवत् like an innocent person, भर्त्रा by his Master, इति उपामन्त्रितः having been thus consulted, उद्धवः Uddhava, तस्य निदेशं His order, शिरसा आधाय placing upon his head with deep humility, प्रत्यभाषत replied.

Simple Meaning: Even though Krishna is completely all-knowing, He consulted Uddhava as if He were uncertain what to do. Bowing his head in deep humility and accepting his Master's command with reverence, Uddhava began to speak in reply.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “कृष्णस्य दिनचर्या जरासन्धबन्दीकृतनृपाणां दूतप्रेषणं च नाम” सप्ततितमः अध्यायः ॥70॥

Thus ends the seventieth discourse named “Krishna's Daily Routine and

the sending of the messenger by the kings imprisoned by Jarasandha”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
