Chapter 57

Syamantaka stolen; Shatadhanva killed and Akrura recalled to Dwaraka

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

स्यमन्तकमण्यपहरणं शतधन्वनः वधः च अक्रूरस्य द्वारकायामाह्वानं च नाम​​ सप्तप​ञ्चाशत्तमः अध्यायः

(42 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
विज्ञातार्थोऽपि गोविन्दो दग्धानाकर्ण्य पाण्डवान् ।
कुन्तीं च कुल्यकरणे सहरामो ययौ कुरून् ॥10.57.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, विज्ञात-अर्थः knowing the actual facts, अपि although, गोविन्दः Govinda, दग्धान् burned, आकर्ण्य hearing, पाण्डवान् the Pandavas, कुन्तीम् Kunti, च and, कुल्य-करणे to perform the funeral rites, सह-रामः along with Balarama, ययौ went, कुरून् to the Kurus

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच – सहरामः गोविन्दः विज्ञातार्थः अपि पाण्डवान् कुन्तीं च दग्धान् आकर्ण्य कुल्यकरणे कुरून् ययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, सहरामः Along with Balarama, गोविन्दः विज्ञातार्थः अपि Govinda although knowing the actual facts, पाण्डवान् कुन्तीं च दग्धान् आकर्ण्य hearing that the Pandavas and Kunti had been burned, कुल्यकरणे to perform the funeral rites, कुरून् ययौ went to the land of the Kurus.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Although Govinda already knew the truth of the situation, upon hearing the report that the Pandavas and Kunti had been burned to death, He went to Hastinapura along with Balarama to perform the customary funeral rites.

भीष्मं कृपं सविदुरं गान्धारीं द्रोणमेव च ।
तुल्यदुःखौ च सङ्गम्य हा कष्टमिति होचतुः ॥10.57.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: भीष्मम् Bhishma, कृपम् Kripa, स-विदुरम् along with Vidura, गान्धारीम् Gandhari, द्रोणम् Drona, एव indeed, च and, तुल्य-दुःखौ sharing equal grief, च and, सङ्गम्य meeting, हा Alas, कष्टम् what a tragedy, इति thus, ह indeed, ऊचतुः they both said

Anvaya: तुल्यदुःखौ भीष्मं कृपं सविदुरं गान्धारीं द्रोणम् एव च सङ्गम्य – हा कष्टम् – इति ह ऊचतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तुल्यदुःखौ Sharing equal grief, भीष्मं कृपं सविदुरं गान्धारीं द्रोणम् एव च सङ्गम्य meeting Bhishma, Kripa, along with Vidura, Gandhari, and Drona, हा कष्टम् Alas, what a tragedy, इति ह ऊचतुः they both said.

Simple Meaning: Sharing in the sorrow of the family, Krishna and Balarama met with Bhishma, Kripa, Vidura, Gandhari, and Drona, and lamented along with them, saying, "Alas, what a terrible tragedy!"

लब्ध्वैतदन्तरं राजन् शतधन्वानमूचतुः ।
अकूरकृतवर्माणौ मणिः कस्मान्न गृह्यते ॥10.57.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: लब्ध्वा having obtained, एतत् अन्तरम् this opportunity, राजन् O King, शतधन्वानम् to Shatadhanva, ऊचतुः they both said, अक्रूर-कृतवर्माणौ Akrura and Kritavarma, मणिः the jewel, कस्मात् why, न not, गृह्यते is being taken

शतधन्वा नकारन्तः "शतधन्वन्" पुल्लिंग

कृतवर्मा नकारन्तः "कृतवर्मन्" पुल्लिंग

Anvaya: राजन्! एतत् अन्तरं लब्ध्वा अक्रूरकृतवर्माणौ शतधन्वानम् ऊचतुः – मणिः कस्मात् न गृह्यते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King (Parikshit), एतत् अन्तरं लब्ध्वा having obtained this opportunity, अक्रूरकृतवर्माणौ Akrura and Kritavarma, शतधन्वानम् ऊचतुः said to Shatadhanva, मणिः कस्मात् न गृह्यते why is the jewel not being taken?

Simple Meaning: O King, taking advantage of this opportunity of Krishna’s absence in Dwaraka, Akrura and Kritavarma approached Shatadhanva and said, "Why do you not take the Syamantaka jewel for yourself?"

योऽस्मभ्यं संप्रतिश्रुत्य कन्यारत्नं विगर्ह्य नः।
कृष्णायादान्न सत्राजित् कस्मात् भ्रातरमन्वियात् ॥10.57.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः who, अस्मभ्यम् to us, संप्रतिश्रुत्य having promised, कन्या-रत्नम् the jewel of a daughter, विगर्ह्य insulting, नः us, कृष्णाय to Krishna, अदात् gave, न not, सत्राजित् Satrajit, कस्मात् why, भ्रातरम् his brother, अन्वियात् should follow

Anvaya: यः अस्मभ्यं कन्यारत्नं संप्रतिश्रुत्य, नः विगर्ह्य, कृष्णाय अदात्, (सः) सत्राजित् कस्मात् भ्रातरं न अन्वियात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः Who, अस्मभ्यं कन्यारत्नं संप्रतिश्रुत्य having promised the jewel of a daughter to us, नः विगर्ह्य insulting us, कृष्णाय अदात् gave her to Krishna, (सः) सत्राजित् कस्मात् भ्रातरं न अन्वियात् why should that Satrajit not follow his (deceased) brother.

Simple Meaning: Satrajit promised to give us his wonderful daughter but then insulted us by giving her to Krishna instead. Why then should he not be sent to follow his deceased brother Prasena?

एवं भिन्नमतिस्ताभ्यां सत्राजितमसत्तमः ।
शयानमवधील्लोभात् स पापः क्षीणजीवितः ॥10.57.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this way, भिन्न-मतिः having his mind influenced, ताभ्याम् by those two, सत्राजितम् Satrajit, असत्-तमः the most sinful one, शयानम् while sleeping, अवधीत् killed, लोभात् out of greed, सः he, पापः sinful person, क्षीण-जीवितः whose life span was exhausted

Anvaya: एवं ताभ्यां भिन्नमतिः असत्तमः पापः क्षीणजीवितः सः लोभात् शयानं सत्राजितम् अवधीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवं ताभ्यां भिन्नमतिः in this way having his mind influenced by those two, असत्तमः पापः क्षीणजीवितः सः that most sinful person whose life span was exhausted, लोभात् out of greed, शयानं सत्राजितम् अवधीत् killed Satrajit while he was sleeping.

Simple Meaning: His mind thus influenced by Akrura and Kritavarma, the highly sinful Shatadhanva, whose life span was now exhausted, killed Satrajit out of greed while he was fast asleep.

स्त्रीणां विक्रोशमानानां क्रन्दन्तीनामनाथवत् ।
हत्वा पशून् सौनिकवन्मणिमादाय जग्मिवान् ॥10.57.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्त्रीणाम् of the women, विक्रोशमानानाम् who were screaming, क्रन्दन्तीनाम् crying, अनाथ-वत् like helpless orphans, हत्वा having killed, पशून् animals, सौनिक-वत् like a butcher, मणिम् the jewel, आदाय taking, जग्मिवान् went away

Anvaya: अनाथवत् क्रन्दन्तीनां विक्रोशमानानां स्त्रीणां सौनिकवत् पशून् हत्वा मणिम् आदाय जग्मिवान्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अनाथवत् क्रन्दन्तीनां विक्रोशमानानां स्त्रीणां While the women were crying and screaming like helpless orphans, सौनिकवत् पशून् हत्वा just like a butcher slaughtering animals, मणिम् आदाय जग्मिवान् he took the jewel and went away.

Simple Meaning: While the women of the household screamed and wept helplessly like orphans, Shatadhanva murdered Satrajit like a butcher slaughtering animals, took the Syamantaka jewel, and fled.

सत्यभामा च पितरं हतं वीक्ष्य शुचार्पिता ।
व्यलपत्तात तातेति हा हतास्मीति मुह्यती ॥10.57.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: सत्यभामा Satyabhama, च and, पितरम् her father, हतम् killed, वीक्ष्य seeing, शुचा-अर्पिता overwhelmed with grief, व्यलपत् lamented, तात O father, तात O father, इति thus, हा alas, हता अस्मि I am ruined, इति thus, मुह्यती losing consciousness

Anvaya: सत्यभामा च पितरं हतं वीक्ष्य शुचार्पिता “तात तात” इति “हा हता अस्मि” इति व्यलपत् मुह्यती…

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सत्यभामा च पितरं हतं वीक्ष्य And Satyabhama seeing her killed father, शुचार्पिता overwhelmed with grief, तात तात इति हा हता अस्मि इति व्यलपत् lost in lamentation saying O father O father alas I am ruined, मुह्यती च and losing consciousness…

Simple Meaning: When Satyabhama saw that her father had been murdered, she was overwhelmed with intense grief. She lamented loudly, crying out, "O father! My dear father! Alas, I am ruined!" and repeatedly lost consciousness.

तैलद्रोण्यां मृतं प्रास्य जगाम गजसाह्वयम् ।
कृष्णाय विदितार्थाय तप्ताऽऽचख्यौ पितुर्वधम् ॥10.57.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: तैल-द्रोण्याम् in a boat-like vessel filled with oil, मृतम् the dead body, प्रास्य placing, जगाम went, गज-साह्वयम् to the city named after the elephant Hastinapura, कृष्णाय to Krishna, विदित-अर्थाय who already knew the facts, तप्ता distressed, आचख्यौ she reported, पितुः of her father, वधम् the murder

Anvaya: तैलद्रोण्यां मृतं प्रास्य गजसाह्वयं जगाम। तप्ता विदितार्थाय कृष्णाय पितुर्वधम् आचख्यौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तैलद्रोण्यां मृतं प्रास्य Placing the dead body in a vessel of oil, गजसाह्वयं जगाम she went to Hastinapura. तप्ता She who was distressed, विदितार्थाय कृष्णाय to Krishna who already knew the facts, पितुर्वधम् आचख्यौ reported the murder of her father.

Simple Meaning: Satyabhama placed her father's corpse in a large vessel of oil to preserve it and traveled to Hastinapura. There, she, who was deeply distressed, reported the murder of her father to Lord Krishna, who was already aware of the situation.

तदाकर्ण्येश्वरौ राजन्ननुसृत्य नृलोकताम् ।
अहो नः परमं कष्टमित्यस्राक्षौ विलेपतुः ॥10.57.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that, आकर्ण्य hearing, ईश्वरौ the two Lords Krishna and Balarama, राजन् O King, अनुसृत्य imitating, नृ-लोकताम् the ways of human society, अहो Oh, नः for us, परमम् greatest, कष्टम् tragedy, इति thus, अस्राक्षौ shedding tears, विलेपतुः they both lamented

Anvaya: राजन्! ईश्वरौ तत् आकर्ण्य नृलोकताम् अनुसृत्य – अहो नः परमं कष्टम् – इति अस्राक्षौ विलेपतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, ईश्वरौ तत् आकर्ण्य the two Lords hearing that, नृलोकताम् अनुसृत्य imitating the ways of human society, अहो नः परमं कष्टम् इति – अस्राक्षौ विलेपतुः lamented while shedding tears saying, “Oh for us this is the greatest tragedy.”

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, upon hearing this news, the two Supreme Lords, Krishna and Balarama, played the role of ordinary human beings. They shed tears and lamented, crying out, "Oh, what a terrible tragedy has befallen us!"

आगत्य भगवांस्तस्मात् सभार्यः साग्रजः पुरम् ।
शतधन्वानमारेभे हन्तुं हर्तुं मणिं ततः ॥10.57.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: आगत्य having returned, भगवान् the Supreme Lord Krishna, तस्मात् from that place Hastinapura, स-भार्यः along with His wife Satyabhama, स-अग्रजः along with His elder brother Balarama, पुरम् to His city Dwarka, शतधन्वानम् Shatadhanva, आरेभे prepared, हन्तुम् to kill, हर्तुम् to take back, मणिम् the jewel, ततः then

Anvaya: तस्मात् सभार्यः साग्रजः भगवान् पुरम् आगत्य ततः शतधन्वानं हन्तुं मणिं हर्तुं च आरेभे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तस्मात् सभार्यः साग्रजः भगवान् पुरम् आगत्य Having returned from that place to His own city along with His wife and His elder brother, the Supreme Lord, ततः शतधन्वानं हन्तुं मणिं हर्तुं च आरेभे then prepared to kill Shatadhanva and take back the jewel.

Simple Meaning: Returning from Hastinapura to His own city of Dwarka along with His wife Satyabhama and His elder brother Balarama, the Supreme Lord Krishna made preparations to kill Shatadhanva and retrieve the Syamantaka jewel.

सोऽपि कृष्णोद्यमं ज्ञात्वा प्राणपरीप्सया ।
साहाय्ये कृतवर्माणमयाचत स चाब्रवीत् ॥10.57.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Shatadhanva), अपि also, कृष्ण-उद्यमम् the preparations of Krishna, ज्ञात्वा knowing, प्राण-परीप्सया out of a desire to save his own life, साहाय्ये for help, कृतवर्माणम् Kritavarma, अयाचत begged, सः he Kritavarma, च and, अब्रवीत् spoke

Anvaya: सः अपि कृष्णोद्यमं ज्ञात्वा प्राणपरीप्सया कृतवर्माणं साहाय्ये अयाचत। सः च अब्रवीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः अपि कृष्णोद्यमं ज्ञात्वा He also knowing the preparations of Krishna, प्राणपरीप्सया कृतवर्माणं साहाय्ये अयाचत out of a desire to save his own life begged Kritavarma for help. सः च अब्रवीत् And he (Kritavarma) spoke.

Simple Meaning: Learning of Krishna's preparations against him, Shatadhanva became terrified for his life and begged Kritavarma for assistance. However, Kritavarma replied to him as follows.

नाहमीश्वरयोः कुर्यां हेलनं रामकृष्णयोः ।
को नु क्षेमाय कल्पेत तयोर्वृजिनमाचरन् ॥10.57.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, अहम् I, ईश्वरयोः of the two Lords, कुर्याम् would commit, हेलनम् offense, राम-कृष्णयोः against Balarama and Krishna, कः who, नु indeed, क्षेमाय for his own well-being, कल्पेत can hope, तयोः against them, वृजिनम् offense, आचरन् committing

Anvaya: अहम् ईश्वरयोः रामकृष्णयोः हेलनं न कुर्याम्। तयोः वृजिनम् आचरन् कः नु क्षेमाय कल्पेत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अहम् ईश्वरयोः रामकृष्णयोः हेलनं न कुर्याम् I would not commit an offense against the two Lords Balarama and Krishna. तयोः वृजिनम् आचरन् कः नु क्षेमाय कल्पेत Committing an offense against them indeed who can hope for his own well-being.

Simple Meaning: Kritavarma said: I cannot commit an offense against the two Lords, Balarama and Krishna. Indeed, who can hope to achieve any well-being or safety in this world if they act inimically toward Them?

कंसः सहानुगोऽपीतो यद्द्वेषात्त्याजितः श्रिया ।
जरासन्धः सप्तदश संयुगान् विरथो गतः ॥10.57.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: कंसः Kamsa, सह-अनुगः along with his followers, अपीतः was destroyed, यत्-द्वेषात् out of hatred for whom, त्याजितः was deprived, श्रिया of his royal fortune, जरासन्धः Jarasandha, सप्तदश seventeen, संयुगान् battles, विरथः deprived of his chariot, गतः went away

Anvaya: यद्द्वेषात् कंसः सहानुगः अपीतः श्रिया त्याजितः च, जरासन्धः सप्तदश संयुगान् विरथः गतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यद्द्वेषात् कंसः सहानुगः अपीतः श्रिया त्याजितः च Out of hatred for Whom, Kamsa along with his followers was destroyed and deprived of his royal fortune, जरासन्धः सप्तदश संयुगान् विरथः गतः and Jarasandha went away deprived of his chariot in seventeen battles.

Simple Meaning: Out of hatred for Krishna, Kamsa and his followers were utterly destroyed and stripped of all their royal fortune, while Jarasandha was defeated in seventeen battles and forced to retreat each time, completely deprived of his chariot.

प्रत्याख्यातः स चाक्रूरं पार्ष्णिग्राहमयाचत ।
सोऽप्याह को विरुध्येत विद्वानीश्वरयोर्बलम् ॥10.57.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रत्याख्यातः rejected, सः he Shatadhanva, च and, अक्रूरम् Akrura, पार्ष्णि-ग्राहम् for protection and backing, अयाचत begged, सः he (Akrura), अपि also, आह said, कः who, विरुध्येत would oppose, विद्वान् a wise person knowing the truth, ईश्वरयोः of the two Lords, बलम् the power

Anvaya: प्रत्याख्यातः सः च अक्रूरं पार्ष्णिग्राहम् अयाचत। सः अपि आह – कः विद्वान् ईश्वरयोः बलं विरुध्येत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रत्याख्यातः सः च अक्रूरं पार्ष्णिग्राहम् अयाचत And rejected he begged Akrura for protection and backing. सः अपि आह – कः विद्वान् ईश्वरयोः बलं विरुध्येत He also said, “What wise person would oppose the power of the two Lords.”

Simple Meaning: Having been rejected by Kritavarma, Shatadhanva turned to Akrura and begged him for protection and backing. However, Akrura also replied: What wise person who understands the truth would ever try to oppose the supreme power of the two Lords, Krishna and Balarama?

य इदं लीलया विश्वं सृजत्यवति हन्ति च ।
चेष्टां विश्वसृजो यस्य न विदुर्मोहिताजया ॥10.57.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः who, इदम् this, लीलया as a pastime, विश्वम् universe, सृजति creates, अवति maintains, हन्ति destroys, च and, चेष्टाम् the activities, विश्व-सृजः the creators of the universe (Brahma and others), यस्य whose, न do not, विदुः understand, मोहिताः bewildered, अजया by His illusory energy

Anvaya: यः इदं विश्वं लीलया सृजति अवति हन्ति च, यस्य चेष्टां अजया मोहिताः विश्वसृजः न विदुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः इदं विश्वं लीलया सृजति अवति हन्ति च Who creates maintains and destroys this universe as a pastime, यस्य चेष्टां अजया मोहिताः विश्वसृजः न विदुः whose activities, the creators of the universe, bewildered by His illusory energy do not understand.

Simple Meaning: He creates, maintains, and destroys this entire universe simply as a pastime. Even the great demigods who control the creation of the universe are bewildered by His illusory energy and cannot comprehend His mysterious activities.

यः सप्तहायनः शैलमुत्पट्यैकेन पाणिना ।
दधार लीलया बाल उच्छिलीन्ध्रमिवार्भकः ॥10.57.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः who, सप्त-हायनः seven years old, शैलम् the mountain (Govardhana), उत्पाट्य lifting up, एकेन with one, पाणिना hand, दधार held, लीलया effortlessly, बालः a child, उच्छिलीन्ध्रम् a mushroom, इव like, अर्भकः a young boy

Anvaya: यः सप्तहायनः बालः – अर्भकः उच्छिलीन्ध्रम् इव – शैलम् एकेन पाणिना उत्पाट्य लीलया दधार।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः सप्तहायनः बालः Who as a seven years old child, अर्भकः उच्छिलीन्ध्रम् इव like a young boy (lifting) a mushroom, शैलम् एकेन पाणिना उत्पाट्य lifted up the mountain with one hand, लीलया दधार held it effortlessly.

Simple Meaning: When He was only a seven year old child, He lifted the massive Govardhana mountain with just one hand and held it up effortlessly, just like a young boy lifting a tiny mushroom.

नमस्तस्मै भगवते कृष्णायाद्भुतकर्मणे ।
अनन्तायादिभूताय कूटस्थायात्मने नमः ॥10.57.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: नमः obeisances, तस्मै to Him, भगवते the Supreme Lord, कृष्णाय to Krishna, अद्भुत-कर्मणे whose activities are wonderful, अनन्ताय to the limitless, आदि-भूताय the origin of all creation, कूट-स्थाय the unchanging one, आत्मने to the Supreme Soul, नमः obeisances

Anvaya: अद्भुतकर्मणे तस्मै भगवते कृष्णाय नमः, अनन्ताय आदिभूताय कूटस्थाय आत्मने नमः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अद्भुतकर्मणे तस्मै भगवते कृष्णाय नमः Obeisances to that Supreme Lord Krishna whose activities are wonderful, अनन्ताय आदिभूताय कूटस्थाय आत्मने नमः obeisances to the limitless origin of all creation, the unchanging Supreme Soul.

Simple Meaning: I offer my respectful obeisances unto Him, the Supreme Lord Krishna, whose activities are so wonderful. Obeisances again and again to that limitless, unchanging Supreme Soul, who is the ultimate source of all creation.

प्रत्याख्यातः स तेनापि शतधन्वा महामणिम् ।
तस्मिन् न्यस्याश्वमारुह्य शतयोजनगं ययौ ॥10.57.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रत्याख्यातः rejected, सः he, तेन by him Akrura, अपि also, शतधन्वा Shatadhanva, महा-मणिम् the great jewel, तस्मिन् in him, न्यस्य placing, अश्वम् horse, आरुह्य mounting, शत-योजन-गम् capable of traveling one hundred yojanas, ययौ fled

Anvaya: तेन अपि प्रत्याख्यातः सः शतधन्वा महामणिम् तस्मिन् न्यस्य शतयोजनगं अश्वम् आरुह्य ययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तेन अपि प्रत्याख्यातः सः शतधन्वा rejected by him also that Shatadhanva, महामणिम् तस्मिन् न्यस्य placing the great jewel in his custody, शतयोजनगं अश्वम् आरुह्य ययौ mounting a horse capable of traveling one hundred yojanas fled.

Simple Meaning: Rejected by Akrura as well, Shatadhanva left the magnificent Syamantaka jewel in Akrura's care, mounted a swift horse capable of traveling one hundred yojanas, and fled.

गरुडध्वजमारुह्य रथं रामजनार्दनौ ।
अन्वयातां महावेगैरश्वै राजन् गुरुद्रुहम् ॥10.57.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: गरुड-ध्वजम् bearing the flag marked with Garuda, आरुह्य mounting, रथम् the chariot, राम-जनार्दनौ Balarama and Krishna, अन्वयाताम् pursued, महा-वेगैः of great speed, अश्वैः with horses, राजन् O King, गुरु-द्रुहम् the murderer of their father-in-law

Anvaya: राजन्! रामजनार्दनौ गरुडध्वजं रथम् आरुह्य महावेगैः अश्वैः गुरुद्रुहम् अन्वयाताम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, रामजनार्दनौ गरुडध्वजं रथम् आरुह्य Balarama and Krishna mounting the chariot bearing the flag marked with Garuda, महावेगैः अश्वैः गुरुद्रुहम् अन्वयाताम् pursued the murderer of their father-in-law with horses of great speed.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, mounting Their chariot marked with the flag of Garuda, Balarama and Krishna pursued the murderer of Their father-in-law with exceptionally swift horses.

मिथिलायामुपवने विसृज्य पतितं हयम्
पद्भ्यामधावत् सन्त्रस्तः कृष्णोऽप्यन्वद्रवद् रुषा ॥10.57.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: मिथिलायाम् in Mithila, उपवने in a garden, विसृज्य leaving behind, पतितम् fallen, हयम् the horse, पद्भ्याम् on foot, अधावत् fled, सन्त्रस्तः terrified, कृष्णः Krishna, अपि also, अन्वद्रवत् pursued, रुषा in anger

Anvaya: मिथिलायाम् उपवने पतितं हयं विसृज्य सन्त्रस्तः पद्भ्याम् अधावत्, कृष्णः अपि रुषा अन्वद्रवत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मिथिलायाम् उपवने पतितं हयं विसृज्य Leaving behind the fallen horse in a garden of Mithila, सन्त्रस्तः पद्भ्याम् अधावत् terrified he fled on foot, कृष्णः अपि रुषा अन्वद्रवत् Krishna also pursued him in anger.

Simple Meaning: Leaving behind his fallen horse in a garden outside Mithila, the terrified Shatadhanva fled on foot, and Krishna pursued him on foot with great anger.

पदातेर्भगवांस्तस्य पदातिस्तिग्मनेमिना ।
चक्रेण शिर उत्कृत्य वाससो व्यचिनोन्मणिम् ॥10.57.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: पदातेः of him who was traveling on foot, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, तस्य his, पदातिः traveling on foot, तिग्म-नेमिना with the sharp-edged, चक्रेण Sudarshana disc, शिरः the head, उत्कृत्य severing, वाससः from his garments, व्यचिनोत् searched for, मणिम् the jewel

Anvaya: पदातेः तस्य भगवान् पदातिः तिग्मनेमिना चक्रेण शिरः उत्कृत्य वाससः मणिं व्यचिनोत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पदातेः तस्य Of him who was traveling on foot, भगवान् पदातिः the Supreme Lord traveling on foot, तिग्मनेमिना चक्रेण शिरः उत्कृत्य severing his head with His sharp edged disc, वाससः मणिं व्यचिनोत् searched his garments for the jewel.

Simple Meaning: Following him on foot, the Supreme Lord decapitated the fleeing Shatadhanva with His sharp edged Sudarshana disc and then searched his garments thoroughly for the Syamantaka jewel.

अलब्धमणिरागत्य कृष्ण आहाग्रजान्तिकम् ।
वृथा हतः शतधनुर्मणिस्तत्र न विद्यते ॥10.57.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: अलब्ध-मणिः not having obtained the jewel, आगत्य returning, कृष्णः Krishna, आह spoke, अग्रज-अन्तिकम् near His elder brother, वृथा in vain, हतः killed, शतधनुः Shatadhanva, मणिः the jewel, तत्र there, न not, विद्यते is present

Anvaya: अलब्धमणिः कृष्णः आगत्य अग्रजान्तिकम् आह – वृथा शतधनुः हतः तत्र मणिः न विद्यते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अलब्धमणिः कृष्णः आगत्य Not having obtained the jewel, Krishna returning, अग्रजान्तिकम् आह spoke near His elder brother Balarama, वृथा शतधनुः हतः Shatadhanva was killed in vain, तत्र मणिः न विद्यते the jewel is not present there with him.

Simple Meaning: Not finding the jewel on Shatadhanva, Krishna returned to His elder brother Balarama and said that they had killed Shatadhanva in vain because the jewel was not with him.

तत आह बलो नूनं स मणिः शतधन्वना ।
कस्मिंश्चित् पुरुषे न्यस्तस्तमन्वेष पुरं व्रज ॥10.57.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, आह said, बलः Balarama, नूनम् certainly, सः that, मणिः jewel, शतधन्वना by Shatadhanva, कस्मिंश्चित् with some, पुरुषे person, न्यस्तः has been deposited, तम् him, अन्वेष search for, पुरम् to the city, व्रज go

Anvaya: ततः बलः आह — नूनं सः मणिः शतधन्वना कस्मिंश्चित् पुरुषे न्यस्तः, तम् अन्वेष, पुरं व्रज।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः बलः आह Then Balarama said, नूनं सः मणिः शतधन्वना कस्मिंश्चित् पुरुषे न्यस्तः certainly that jewel has been deposited by Shatadhanva with some person, तम् अन्वेष search for him, पुरं व्रज go to the city.

Simple Meaning: Then Balarama said, "Shatadhanva must have certainly deposited the jewel with someone. Go back to the city of Dwaraka and search for that person."

अहं विदेहमिच्छामि द्रष्टुं प्रियतमं मम ।
इत्युक्त्वा मिथिलां राजन् विवेश यदुनन्दनः ॥10.57.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: अहम् I, विदेहम् the King of Videha, इच्छामि wish, द्रष्टुम् to see, प्रिय-तमम् who is very dear, मम to Me, इति thus, उक्त्वा saying, मिथिलाम् Mithila, राजन् O King, विवेश entered, यदु-नन्दनः Balarama the beloved descendant of Yadu

Anvaya: राजन्! अहं मम प्रियतमं विदेहं द्रष्टुम् इच्छामि, इति उक्त्वा यदुनन्दनः मिथिलां विवेश।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, अहं मम प्रियतमं विदेहं द्रष्टुम् इच्छामि I wish to see the King of Videha who is very dear to Me, इति उक्त्वा saying this, यदुनन्दनः मिथिलां विवेश Balarama, the beloved descendant of Yadu, entered Mithila.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, after saying, "I wish to visit the King of Videha who is very dear to Me," Balarama, the beloved descendant of Yadu, entered the city of Mithila.

तं दृष्ट्वा सहसोत्थाय मैथिलः प्रीतमानसः ।
अर्हयामास विधिवदर्हणीयं समर्हणैः ॥10.57.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् Him, दृष्ट्वा seeing, सहसा immediately, उत्थाय standing up, मैथिलः the King of Mithila, प्रीत-मानसः with a joyful mind, अर्हयामास worshiped, विधिवत् according to Vedic injunctions, अर्हणीयम् Him who is worthy of worship, समर्हणैः with respectful presentations

Anvaya: मैथिलः तं दृष्ट्वा प्रीतमानसः सहसा उत्थाय अर्हणीयं विधिवत् समर्हणैः अर्हयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मैथिलः तं दृष्ट्वा प्रीतमानसः The King of Mithila seeing Him with a joyful mind, सहसा उत्थाय standing up immediately, अर्हणीयं विधिवत् समर्हणैः अर्हयामास worshiped Him who is worthy of worship according to Vedic injunctions with respectful presentations.

Simple Meaning: Upon seeing Lord Balarama, the King of Mithila joyfully stood up immediately and worshiped Him, who is most worthy of worship, in accordance with Vedic scriptures by offering respectful presentations.

उवास तस्यां कतिचिन्मिथिलायां समा विभुः ।
मानितः प्रीतियुक्तेन जनकेन महात्मना ।
ततोऽशिक्षद् गदां काले धार्तराष्ट्रः सुयोधनः ॥10.57.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: उवास resided, तस्याम् in that, कतिचित् several, मिथिलायाम् in Mithila, समाः years, विभुः the almighty Lord Balarama, मानितः honored, प्रीति-युक्तेन with love and devotion, जनकेन by Janaka, महात्मना the great soul, ततः from Him, अशिक्षत् learned, गदाम् the art of club fighting, काले during that time, धार्तराष्ट्रः the son of Dhritarashtra, सुयोधनः Duryodhana

Anvaya: विभुः प्रीतियुक्तेन महात्मना जनकेन मानितः तस्यां मिथिलायां कतिचित् समाः उवास, काले धार्तराष्ट्रः सुयोधनः ततः गदाम् अशिक्षत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विभुः प्रीतियुक्तेन महात्मना जनकेन मानितः The almighty Lord Balarama, honored with love and devotion by the great soul King Janaka, तस्यां मिथिलायां कतिचित् समाः उवास resided in that city of Mithila for several years. काले धार्तराष्ट्रः सुयोधनः ततः गदाम् अशिक्षत् During that time Duryodhana, the son of Dhritarashtra, learned the art of club fighting from Him.

Simple Meaning: The almighty Lord Balarama lived in Mithila for several years, highly honored with great love and devotion by the noble King Janaka. During this time, Dhritarashtra's son Duryodhana came to Mithila and learned the art of club fighting from Him.

केशवो द्वारकामेत्य निधनं शतधन्वनः ।
अप्राप्तिं च मणेः प्राह प्रियायाः प्रियकृद् विभुः ॥10.57.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: केशवः Krishna, द्वारकाम् to Dwaraka, एत्य returning, निधनम् the death, शतधन्वनः of Shatadhanva, अप्राप्तिम् the non-acquisition, च and, मणेः of the jewel, प्राह told, प्रियायाः to His beloved (Satyabhama), प्रिय-कृत् doing what was pleasing, विभुः the almighty Lord

Anvaya: प्रियायाः प्रियकृत् विभुः केशवः द्वारकाम् एत्य शतधन्वनः निधनं मणेः अप्राप्तिं च प्राह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रियायाः प्रियकृत् Doing what was pleasing to His beloved Satyabhama, विभुः केशवः the almighty Lord Krishna, द्वारकाम् एत्य returning to Dwarka, शतधन्वनः निधनं of the death of Shatadhanva, मणेः अप्राप्तिं च and the failure to obtain the jewel, प्राह told her.

Simple Meaning: The almighty Lord Krishna, always acting to please His beloved wife Satyabhama, returned to Dwaraka and informed her of Shatadhanva's death and their failure to recover the Syamantaka jewel.

ततः स कारयामास क्रिया बन्धोर्हतस्य वै ।
साकं सुहृद्भिर्भगवान् या याः स्युः साम्परायिकाः ॥10.57.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, सः He, कारयामास caused to be performed, क्रियाः the funeral rites, बन्धोः of the relative, हतस्य of the slain one, वै indeed, साकम् together, सुहृद्भिः with well-wishers, भगवान् the Supreme Lord Krishna, याः याः whatever, स्युः are, साम्परायिकाः necessary for the next world

Anvaya: ततः भगवान् हतस्य बन्धोः साम्परायिकाः याः याः क्रियाः स्युः ताः वै सुहृद्भिः साकं कारयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः भगवान् Then the Supreme Lord, हतस्य बन्धोः of the slain relative (father-in-law Satrajit), साम्परायिकाः याः याः क्रियाः स्युः whatever funeral rites are necessary for the next world, ताः वै सुहृद्भिः साकं कारयामास caused them indeed to be performed together with well-wishers.

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, the Supreme Lord Krishna, along with their close relatives and well-wishers, arranged for the performance of all the necessary funeral rites for His deceased  father-in-law Satrajit, ensuring his peaceful transition to the next world.

अक्रूरः कृतवर्मा च श्रुत्वा शतधनोर्वधम् ।
व्यूषतुर्भयवित्रस्तौ द्वारकायाः प्रयोजकौ ॥10.57.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: अक्रूरः Akrura, कृतवर्मा Kritavarma, च and, श्रुत्वा hearing, शतधनोः of Shatadhanva, वधम् the killing, व्यूषतुः went into exile, भय-वित्रस्तौ fear-stricken, द्वारकायाः from Dwaraka, प्रयोजकौ the instigators

Anvaya: शतधनोः वधं श्रुत्वा भयवित्रस्तौ प्रयोजकौ अक्रूरः कृतवर्मा च द्वारकायाः व्यूषतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शतधनोः वधं श्रुत्वा Hearing of the killing of Shatadhanva, भयवित्रस्तौ प्रयोजकौ the terrified instigators, अक्रूरः कृतवर्मा च Akrura and Kritavarma, द्वारकायाः व्यूषतुः fled from Dwaraka.

Simple Meaning: Upon hearing of the killing of Shatadhanva, the two instigators of the conspiracy, Akrura and Kritavarma, became utterly terrified and fled from Dwaraka.

अक्रूरे प्रोषितेऽरिष्टान्यासन् वै द्वारकौकसाम् ।
शारीरा मानसास्तापा मुहुर्दैविकभौतिकाः ॥10.57.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: अक्रूरे when Akrura, प्रोषिते had gone away, अरिष्टानि calamities, आसन् arose, वै indeed, द्वारक-ओकसाम् for the residents of Dwaraka, शारीराः physical, मानसाः mental, तापाः sufferings, मुहुः repeatedly, दैविक-भौतिकाः caused by supernatural forces and physical elements

Anvaya: अक्रूरे प्रोषिते द्वारकौकसां शारीराः मानसाः दैविकभौतिकाः तापाः अरिष्टानि च वै मुहुः आसन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अक्रूरे प्रोषिते When Akrura had gone away, द्वारकौकसां शारीराः मानसाः दैविकभौतिकाः तापाः physical, mental, supernatural, and environmental sufferings for the inhabitants of Dwaraka, अरिष्टानि च वै मुहुः आसन् and calamities indeed repeatedly arose.

Simple Meaning: When Akrura went away, the inhabitants of Dwaraka repeatedly experienced natural calamities along with constant physical, mental, supernatural, and environmental sufferings.

इत्यङ्गोपदिशन्त्येके विस्मृत्य प्रागुदाहृतम् ।
मुनिवासनिवासे किं घटेतारिष्टदर्शनम् ॥10.57.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, अङ्ग O dear one, उपदिशन्ति declare, एके some, विस्मृत्य forgetting, प्राक् previously, उदाहृतम् what was described, मुनि-वास-निवासे in the abode of the protector of all sages, किम् how, घटेत could happen, अरिष्ट-दर्शनम् the appearance of calamities

Anvaya: अङ्ग! एके प्राक् उदाहृतं विस्मृत्य इति उपदिशन्ति, मुनिवासनिवासे अरिष्टदर्शनं किं घटेत?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अङ्ग O dear one, एके प्राक् उदाहृतं विस्मृत्य इति उपदिशन्ति some declare thus, forgetting what was previously described. मुनिवासनिवासे अरिष्टदर्शनं किं घटेत How could the appearance of calamities happen in the abode of the protector of all sages.

Simple Meaning: My dear King, some people declare Akrura’s absence to be the reason for the calamities, forgetting what was previously described. For how could calamities ever appear in Dwaraka, the ultimate abode of Lord Krishna, who is the protector and shelter of all sages?

देवेऽवर्षति काशीशः श्वफल्कायागताय वै ।
स्वसुतां गान्दिनीं प्रादात् ततोऽवर्षत् स्म काशिषु ॥10.57.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: देवे when Indra - the god of rain, अवर्षति did not pour rain, काशी-ईशः the King of Kashi, श्वफल्काय unto Shvaphalka, आगताय who had arrived, वै indeed, स्व-सुताम् his own daughter, गान्दिनीम् Gandini, प्रादात् gave, ततः then, अवर्षत् rained, स्म indeed, काशिषु in the kingdom of Kashi

Anvaya: देवे अवर्षति काशीशः आगताय श्वफल्काय वै स्वसुतां गान्दिनीं प्रादात्, ततः काशिषु अवर्षत् स्म।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: देवे अवर्षति When the rain-god did not pour rain, काशीशः आगताय श्वफल्काय वै स्वसुतां गान्दिनीं प्रादात् the King of Kashi indeed gave his own daughter Gandini to Shvaphalka who had arrived there, ततः काशिषु अवर्षत् स्म then it, indeed, rained in the kingdom of Kashi.

Simple Meaning: Once, when there was a severe drought in Kashi due to lack of rain, the King of Kashi gave his daughter Gandini in marriage to Shvaphalka, who had just arrived in his kingdom. Immediately after this, it rained abundantly throughout Kashi.

तत्सुतस्तत्प्रभावोऽसावक्रूरो यत्र यत्र ह ।
देवोऽभिवर्षते तत्र नोपतापा न मारिकाः ॥10.57.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्-सुतः his son, तत्-प्रभावः possessing the same power as his father, असौ this, अक्रूरः Akrura, यत्र यत्र wherever, ह indeed, देवः the rain-god, अभिवर्षते pours rain, तत्र there, न no, उपतापाः calamities, न no, मारिकाः plagues

Anvaya: तत्सुतः तत्प्रभावः असौ अक्रूरः यत्र यत्र ह (वर्तते), तत्र देवः अभिवर्षते, उपतापाः न, मारिकाः न।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत्सुतः तत्प्रभावः असौ अक्रूरः His son this Akrura possessing the same power as his father, यत्र यत्र ह (वर्तते) wherever indeed he resides, तत्र देवः अभिवर्षते there the rain-god pours rain, उपतापाः न मारिकाः न there are no calamities and no plagues.

Simple Meaning: His son Akrura possesses the same extraordinary power as his father. Wherever Akrura resides, the rain-god pours sufficient rain, and that place remains free from natural calamities and deadly plagues.

इति वृद्धवचः श्रुत्वा नैतावदिह कारणम् ।
इति मत्वा समानाय्य प्राहाक्रूरं जनार्दनः ॥10.57.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, वृद्ध-वचः the words of the elders, श्रुत्वा hearing, न not, एतावत् only this, इह in this matter, कारणम् the cause, इति thus, मत्वा thinking, समानाय्य calling back, प्राह spoke to, अक्रूरम् Akrura, जनार्दनः Lord Krishna

Anvaya: जनार्दनः इति वृद्धवचः श्रुत्वा, इह एतावत् कारणं न इति मत्वा, अक्रूरं समानाय्य प्राह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जनार्दनः इति वृद्धवचः श्रुत्वा Janardana, hearing these words of the elders, इह एतावत् कारणं न इति मत्वा thinking that this was not the only cause in this matter, अक्रूरं समानाय्य प्राह called Akrura back and spoke to him.

Simple Meaning: Hearing these suggestions from the elder citizens, Lord Janardana, knowing well that Akrura's absence was not the sole cause of the disturbances, had Akrura summoned back to Dwarka and spoke to him.

पूजयित्वाभिभाष्यैनं कथयित्वा प्रियाः कथाः ।
विज्ञाताखिलचित्तज्ञः स्मयमान उवाच ह ॥10.57.35॥

Word-by-word meaning: पूजयित्वा having worshiped, अभिभाष्य addressing, एनम् him, कथयित्वा telling, प्रियाः pleasing, कथाः topics, विज्ञात-अखिल-चित्त-ज्ञः He who perfectly knows the minds of all, स्मयमानः smiling, उवाच spoke, ह indeed

Anvaya: विज्ञाताखिलचित्तज्ञः एनं पूजयित्वा अभिभाष्य प्रियाः कथाः कथयित्वा स्मयमानः उवाच ह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विज्ञाताखिलचित्तज्ञः He who perfectly knows the minds of all, एनं पूजयित्वा having worshiped, अभिभाष्य and addressed him, प्रियाः कथाः कथयित्वा and having told him pleasing stories, स्मयमानः उवाच ह smilingly spoke.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna, who knows the hearts of all living beings, first worshiped Akrura and welcomed him respectfully. After conversing with him on pleasant topics, the Lord smiled and began to speak to him.

ननु दानपते न्यस्तस्त्वय्यास्ते शतधन्वना ।
स्यमन्तको मणिः श्रीमान् विदितः पूर्वमेव नः ॥10.57.36॥

Word-by-word meaning: ननु surely, दान-पते O master of charity, न्यस्तः deposited, त्वयि in you, आस्ते remains, शतधन्वना by Shatadhanva, स्यमन्तकः Syamantaka, मणिः the jewel, श्रीमान् glorious, विदितः known, पूर्वम् before, एव indeed, नः to Us

Anvaya: ननु दानपते! शतधन्वना त्वयि न्यस्तः श्रीमान् स्यमन्तकः मणिः आस्ते, एतत् नः पूर्वम् एव विदितम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ननु दानपते O master of charity, शतधन्वना त्वयि न्यस्तः श्रीमान् स्यमन्तकः मणिः आस्ते surely the glorious Syamantaka jewel deposited by Shatadhanva remains with you, एतत् नः पूर्वम् एव विदितम् this was indeed known to Us beforehand.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna said: O master of charity, the glorious Syamantaka jewel deposited with you by Shatadhanva is surely in your possession. Indeed, We have known this all along.

सत्राजितोऽनपत्यत्वाद् गृह्णीयुर्दुहितुः सुताः ।
दायं निनीयापः पिण्डान् विमुच्यर्णं शेषितम् ॥10.57.37॥

Word-by-word meaning: सत्राजितः of Satrajit, अनपत्यत्वात् because of having no sons, गृह्णीयुः should inherit, दुहितुः of the daughter, सुताः the sons, दायम् the inheritance, निनीय after offering, अपः water, पिण्डान् funeral cakes, विमुच्य and clearing, ऋणम् the debts, शेषितम् remaining

Anvaya: सत्राजितः अनपत्यत्वात् दुहितुः सुताः अपः पिण्डान् च निनीय, शेषितम् ऋणं विमुच्य दायं गृह्णीयुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सत्राजितः अनपत्यत्वात् Because Satrajit had no sons, दुहितुः सुताः the sons of his daughter, अपः पिण्डान् च निनीय after offering water and funeral cakes to him, शेषितम् ऋणं विमुच्य and clearing his remaining debts, दायं गृह्णीयुः should inherit his estate.

Simple Meaning: Since Satrajit had no sons, his daughter's sons should inherit his property, clear his remaining debts, and perform his funeral rites by offering water and funeral cakes.

तथापि दुर्धरस्त्वन्यैस्त्वय्यास्तां सुव्रते मणिः ।
किन्तु मामग्रजः सम्यङ् न प्रत्येति मणिं प्रति ॥10.57.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: तथापि nevertheless, दुर्धरः difficult to be held, तु indeed, अन्यैः by others, त्वयि in you, आस्ताम् let it remain, सु-व्रते O keeper of sacred vows, मणिः the jewel, किन्तु but, माम् Me, अग्रजः My elder brother Balarama, सम्यक् fully, न not, प्रत्येति believes, मणिम् प्रति concerning the jewel

Anvaya: तथापि सुव्रते! अन्यैः दुर्धरः मणिः त्वयि आस्ताम्, किन्तु अग्रजः मणिं प्रति मां सम्यङ् न प्रत्येति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तथापि सुव्रते Nevertheless O keeper of sacred vows, अन्यैः दुर्धरः मणिः त्वयि आस्ताम् let the jewel, which is difficult for others to keep, remain with you. किन्तु अग्रजः मणिं प्रति मां सम्यङ् न प्रत्येति However, my elder brother does not fully believe Me concerning this jewel.

Simple Meaning: Nevertheless, O keeper of sacred vows, let the jewel remain in your custody, as it is extremely difficult for others to handle safely. However, My elder brother Balarama does not fully believe Me regarding this jewel.

दर्शयस्व महाभाग बन्धूनां शान्तिमावह ।
अव्युच्छिन्ना मखास्तेऽद्य वर्तन्ते रुक्मवेदयः॥10.57.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: दर्शयस्व please show it, महा-भाग O greatly fortunate one, बन्धूनाम् to the relatives, शान्तिम् peace, आवह bring about, अव्युच्छिन्नाः uninterruptedly, मखाः sacrifices, ते your, अद्य today, वर्तन्ते are going on, रुक्म-वेदयः with golden altars

Anvaya: महाभाग! (मणिं) दर्शयस्व। बन्धूनां शान्तिम् आवह। अद्य ते रुक्मवेदयः मखाः अव्युच्छिन्नाः वर्तन्ते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महाभाग O greatly fortunate one, (मणिं) दर्शयस्व please show the jewel. बन्धूनां शान्तिम् आवह Bring peace to our relatives. अद्य ते रुक्मवेदयः मखाः अव्युच्छिन्नाः वर्तन्ते Today your sacrifices performed on golden altars are going on uninterruptedly.

Simple Meaning: Therefore, O greatly fortunate one, please show the jewel to us to bring peace of mind to our relatives. We know it is with you, because your sacrificial performances on altars of gold are going on uninterruptedly to this day.

एवं सामभिरालब्धः श्वफल्कतनयो मणिम् ।
आदाय वाससाच्छन्नं ददौ सूर्यसमप्रभम् ॥10.57.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, सामभिः with gentle words, आलब्धः persuaded, श्वफल्क-तनयः the son of Shvaphalka (Akrura), मणिम् the jewel, आदाय taking, वाससा with a cloth, आच्छन्नम् covered, ददौ gave, सूर्य-सम-प्रभम् brilliant like the sun

Anvaya: एवं सामभिः आलब्धः श्वफल्कतनयः वाससा आच्छन्नं सूर्यसमप्रभं मणिम् आदाय ददौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवं सामभिः आलब्धः Thus persuaded by gentle words, श्वफल्कतनयः the son of Shvaphalka, Akrura, वाससा आच्छन्नं सूर्यसमप्रभं मणिम् आदाय ददौ taking the jewel, which was covered with a cloth and brilliant like the sun, gave it.

Simple Meaning: Thus persuaded by Krishna's sweet and gentle words, Akrura, the son of Shvaphalka, took out the Syamantaka jewel—which was wrapped in a cloth and shone as brilliantly as the sun—and handed it over.

स्यमन्तकं दर्शयित्वा ज्ञातिभ्यो रज आत्मनः ।
विमृज्य मणिना भूयस्तस्मै प्रत्यर्पयत् प्रभुः ॥10.57.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्यमन्तकम् the Syamantaka jewel, दर्शयित्वा having shown, ज्ञातिभ्यः to His relatives, रजः the dirt of suspicion, आत्मनः of Himself, विमृज्य erasing, मणिना by means of the jewel, भूयः again, तस्मै to him, प्रत्यर्पयत् returned, प्रभुः the Almighty Lord Krishna

Anvaya: प्रभुः मणिना आत्मनः रजः विमृज्य, ज्ञातिभ्यः स्यमन्तकं दर्शयित्वा, भूयः तस्मै (अक्रूराय) प्रत्यर्पयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रभुः The Almighty Lord, मणिना आत्मनः रजः विमृज्य by means of the jewel erasing the dirt of suspicion cast upon Himself, ज्ञातिभ्यः स्यमन्तकं दर्शयित्वा and showing the Syamantaka jewel to His relatives, भूयः तस्मै प्रत्यर्पयत् returned it again to him.

Simple Meaning: The Almighty Lord Krishna showed the Syamantaka jewel to His relatives, thereby completely erasing the dirt of false suspicion that had been cast upon Himself. Afterwards, He returned the jewel back to Akrura.

यस्त्वेतद् भगवत ईश्वरस्य विष्णोर्वीर्याढ्यं वृजिनहरं सुमङ्गलं च ।
आख्यानं पठति शृणोत्यनुस्मरेद् वा दुष्कीर्तिं दुरितमपोह्य याति शान्तिम् ॥10.57.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः who, तु indeed, एतत् this, भगवतः of the Supreme Lord, ईश्वरस्य of the controller, विष्णोः of Lord Vishnu, वीर्य-आढ्यम् rich in heroic deeds, वृजिन-हरम् destroying sins, सु-मङ्गलम् highly auspicious, च and, आख्यानम् narration, पठति recites, शृणोति hears, अनुस्मरेत् remembers, वा or, दुष्कीर्तिम् bad reputation, दुरितम् sins, अपोह्य driving away, याति attains, शान्तिम् peace

Anvaya: यः तु भगवतः ईश्वरस्य विष्णोः एतत् वीर्याढ्यं वृजिनहरं सुमङ्गलं च आख्यानं पठति शृणोति अनुस्मरेत् वा, (सः) दुष्कीर्तिं दुरितं च अपोह्य शान्तिं याति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः तु He who indeed, भगवतः ईश्वरस्य विष्णोः of the Supreme Lord and controller Lord Vishnu, एतत् वीर्याढ्यं वृजिनहरं सुमङ्गलं च आख्यानं this narration which is rich in heroic deeds, destroying sins, and highly auspicious, पठति recites, शृणोति hears, अनुस्मरेत् वा or remembers, (सः) he, दुष्कीर्तिं दुरितं च अपोह्य driving away bad reputation and sins, शान्तिं याति attains peace.

Simple Meaning: Anyone who recites, hears, or remembers this highly auspicious narration of the glorious deeds of the Supreme Lord and Controller, Lord Vishnu, which destroys all sins, will be freed from all infamy and sinful reactions, and will attain supreme peace.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “स्यमन्तकमण्यपहरणं शतधन्वनः वधः च अक्रूरस्य द्वारकायामाह्वानं च नाम” सप्तपञ्चाशत्तमः अध्यायः ॥57॥

Thus ends the fifty-seventh discourse named “Syamantaka stolen; Shatadhanva killed and Akrura recalled to Dwaraka” in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
