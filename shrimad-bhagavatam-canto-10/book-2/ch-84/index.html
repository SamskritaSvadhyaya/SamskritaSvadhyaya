श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

ऋषिणाम् उपदेशः वसुदेवस्य यज्ञः च​ नाम चतुरशीतितमः अध्यायः

(71 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
श्रुत्वा पृथा सुबलपुत्र्यथ याज्ञसेनी माधव्यथ क्षितिपपत्न्य उत स्वगोप्यः ।
कृष्णेऽखिलात्मनि हरौ प्रणयानुबन्धं सर्वा विसिस्म्युरलमश्रुकलाकुलाक्ष्यः ॥10.84.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, श्रुत्वा having heard, पृथा Pritha - Kunti, सुबल-पुत्री daughter of Subala - Gandhari, अथ and then, याज्ञसेनी Draupadi (daughter of Yagnasena - Drupada), माधवी Subhadra, अथ also, क्षितिप-पत्न्यः the wives of other kings, उत as well as, स्व-गोप्यः His own gopis of Vraja, कृष्णे in Krishna, अखिल-आत्मनि the Supreme Soul of all beings, हरौ in Hari, प्रणय-अनुबन्धम् the profound bond of pure loving attachment, सर्वाः all of them, विसिस्म्युः were struck with amazement, अलम् completely, अश्रु-कला-आकुल-अक्ष्यः their eyes filled with tears of love

माधवी a woman of the race of Madhu or Yadu  src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

Anvaya: श्री शुक उवाच— (एवं) श्रुत्वा पृथा सुबलपुत्री अथ याज्ञसेनी माधवी अथ क्षितिपपत्न्यः उत स्वगोप्यः सर्वाः अखिलात्मनि हरौ कृष्णे प्रणयानुबन्धं (दृष्ट्वा/श्रुत्वा) अश्रुकलाकुलाक्ष्यः अलं विसिस्म्युः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुक उवाच Shri Shuka said, (एवं) श्रुत्वा hearing all these narrations, पृथा Kunti, सुबलपुत्री Gandhari - the daughter of King Subala, अथ and, याज्ञसेनी Draupadi - the daughter of Yajnasena, माधवी Madhavi - Subhadra, अथ and, क्षितिपपत्न्यः the wives of various earthly kings, उत as well as, स्वगोप्यः His own gopis of Vraja, सर्वाः all these women, अखिलात्मनि in the Supreme Soul of all living entities, हरौ in Lord Hari, कृष्णे in Krishna, प्रणयानुबन्धम् the boundlessness of deep devotional love, अश्रुकलाकुलाक्ष्यः their eyes overflowing with tears, अलं completely, विसिस्म्युः were filled with wonder.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: After hearing these extraordinary accounts of the queens' pure love for Krishna, Kunti, Gandhari, Draupadi, Subhadra, the wives of the other kings, and His own gopis of Vraja were all deeply moved. Observing such boundless, unalloyed devotion toward Lord Hari—the Supreme Soul of all—their eyes welled with tears, and they were filled with wonder.

इति सम्भाषमाणासु स्त्रीभिः स्त्रीषु नृभिर्नृषु ।
आययुर्मुनयस्तत्र कृष्णरामदिदृक्षया ॥10.84.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति in this manner, सम्भाषमाणासु while conversing with one another, स्त्रीभिः with the women, स्त्रीषु among the women, नृभिः with the men, नृषु among the men, आययुः arrived, मुनयः the great sages, तत्र there (at Samantapanchaka), कृष्ण-राम-दिदृक्षया out of a desire to see Krishna and Balarama

Anvaya: इति स्त्रीषु स्त्रीभिः नृषु नृभिः सम्भाषमाणासु (सतीषु), तत्र कृष्णरामदिदृक्षया मुनयः आययुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति In this way, स्त्रीषु among the womenfolk, स्त्रीभिः with other women, नृषु among the men, नृभिः with other men, सम्भाषमाणासु while engaged in loving conversations, तत्र at that pilgrimage assembly of Samantapanchaka, कृष्णरामदिदृक्षया desiring to obtain the darshana of Sri Krishna and Lord Balarama, मुनयः the great exalted sages, आययुः arrived on the scene.

Simple Meaning: While the women were thus talking among themselves, and the men were similarly conversing with one another, a multitude of sages arrived at Samantapanchaka, desiring to see Krishna and Balarama.

द्वैपायनो नारदश्च च्यवनो देवलोऽसितः ।
विश्वामित्रः शतानन्दो भरद्वजोऽथ गौतमः ॥10.84.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्वैपायनः Dwaipayana (Veda Vyasa), नारदः Narada, च and, च्यवनः Chyavana, देवलः Devala, असितः Asita, विश्वामित्रः Vishwamitra, शतानन्दः Shatananda, भरद्वाजः Bharadwaja, अथ and also, गौतमः Gautama

Anvaya: द्वैपायनः नारदः च्यवनः देवलः असितः विश्वामित्रः शतानन्दः भरद्वाजः अथ गौतमः (आययुः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: द्वैपायनः Dwaipayana Vyasa, नारदः Narada, च्यवनः Chyavana, देवलः Devala, असितः Asita, विश्वामित्रः Vishwamitra, शतानन्दः Shatananda, भरद्वाजः Bharadvaja, अथ and, गौतमः Gautama, (आययुः) arrived at the gathering.

Simple Meaning: Among the great sages who arrived to see Krishna and Balarama were Dwaipayana Vyasa, Narada, Chyavana, Devala, Asita, Vishwamitra, Shatananda, Bharadwaja, and Gautama.

रामः सशिष्यो भगवान् वशिष्ठो गालवो भृगुः ।
पुलस्त्यः कश्यपोऽत्रिश्च मार्कण्डेयो बृहस्पतिः ॥10.84.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: रामः Lord Parasurama, स-शिष्यः accompanied by His disciples, भगवान् the venerable, वशिष्ठः Vasishtha, गालवः Gaalava, भृगुः Bhrigu, पुलस्त्यः Pulastya, कश्यपः Kashyapa, अत्रिः Atri, च and, मार्कण्डेयः Markandeya, बृहस्पतिः Brihaspati

Anvaya: (तथा) सशिष्यः भगवान् रामः, वशिष्ठः, गालवः, भृगुः, पुलस्त्यः, कश्यपः, अत्रिः, मार्कण्डेयः, बृहस्पतिः च (आययुः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (तथा) Likewise, सशिष्यः along with His disciples, भगवान् रामः the glorious Lord Parasurama, वशिष्ठः Vasishtha, गालवः Gaalava, भृगुः Bhrigu, पुलस्त्यः Pulastya, कश्यपः Kashyapa, अत्रिः Atri, मार्कण्डेयः Markandeya, बृहस्पतिः च and Brihaspati, (आययुः) came there.

Simple Meaning: Also arriving were the venerable Lord Parasurama accompanied by His disciples, Vasishtha, Gaalava, Bhrigu, Pulastya, Kashyapa, Atri, Markandeya, and Brihaspati.

द्वितस्त्रितश्चैकतश्च ब्रह्मपुत्रास्तथाङ्गिराः ।
अगस्त्यो याज्ञवल्क्यश्च वामदेवादयोऽपरे ॥10.84.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्वितः Dvita, त्रितः Trita, च and, एकतः Ekata, च and, ब्रह्म-पुत्राः the mind-born sons of Brahma (Sanaka, Sanatana, Sanandana, Sanatkumara), तथा as well as, अङ्गिराः Angiraa, अगस्त्यः Agastya, याज्ञवल्क्यः Yaagnavalkya, च and, वामदेव-आदयः Vamadeva and others, अपरे other great sages

Anvaya: द्वितः त्रितः एकतः च ब्रह्मपुत्राः तथा अङ्गिराः अगस्त्यः याज्ञवल्क्यः वामदेवाद्यः अपरे च (तत्र समागताः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: द्वितः Dvita, त्रितः Trita, एकतः Ekata, च and, ब्रह्मपुत्राः the mind-born sons of Lord Brahma, तथा as well as, अङ्गिराः Sage Angiraa, अगस्त्यः Sage Agastya, याज्ञवल्क्यः Sage Yaagnavalkya, वामदेवाद्यः Vamadeva and other rishis, अपरे other sages, च and, (तत्र समागताः) assembled at that sacred venue.

Simple Meaning: Dvita, Trita, Ekata, the mind-born sons of Brahma (Sanaka, Sanatana, Sanandana, Sanatkumara), Angiraa, Agastya, Yaagnavalkya, Vamadeva, and many other exalted sages and seers gathered there at Samantapanchaka.

तान् दृष्ट्वा सहसोत्थाय प्रागासीना नृपादयः ।
पाण्डवाः कृष्णरामौ च प्रणेमुर्विश्ववन्दितान् ॥10.84.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: तान् them (the arriving sages), दृष्ट्वा seeing, सहसा instantly, उत्थाय standing up, प्राक्-आसीनाः those who were previously seated, नृप-आदयः the kings and others, पाण्डवाः the Pandavas, कृष्ण-रामौ Krishna and Balarama, च and, प्रणेमुः offered bows and obeisances, विश्व-वन्दितान् to those sages who are revered by the entire universe

Anvaya: प्रागासीनाः नृपादयः पाण्डवाः कृष्णरामौ च विश्ववन्दितान् तान् दृष्ट्वा सहसा उत्थाय प्रणेमुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रागासीनाः Those who were seated beforehand, नृपादयः the assembled kings and dignitaries, पाण्डवाः the Pandava brothers, कृष्णरामौ Krishna and Balarama, च and, विश्ववन्दितान् तान् दृष्ट्वा upon beholding those great sages who are revered throughout the entire creation, सहसा उत्थाय instantly rising up from their seats, प्रणेमुः offered their prostrations.

Simple Meaning: Seeing the arrival of those exalted sages—who are worshiped across the universe— Krishna, Balarama, the Pandavas, and all the kings who had been seated stood up immediately and offered their prostrations to them.

तानानर्चुर्यथा सर्वे सहरामोऽच्युतोऽर्चयत् ।
स्वागतासनपाद्यार्घ्यमाल्यधूपानुलेपनैः ॥10.84.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: तान् them (the sages), आनर्चुः worshipped, यथा in the same manner as, सर्वे all the kings and assembly, सह-रामः along with Balarama, अच्युतः the infallible Krishna, अर्चयत् was worshipping, स्वागत-आसन-पाद्य-अर्घ्य-माल्य-धूप-अनुलेपनैः with words of welcome, comfortable seating, water for washing the feet, respectful oblations, flower garlands, fragrant incense, and sandalwood paste

Anvaya: सहरामः अच्युतः यथा अर्चयत्, (तथा) सर्वे स्वागतासनपाद्यार्घ्यमाल्यधूपानुलेपनैः तान् आनर्चुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सहरामः अच्युतः Along with Balarama, the infallible Krishna, यथा अर्चयत् in whichever respectful manner, was performing worship, (तथा) in that exact manner, सर्वे all the assembled kings and leaders, स्वागतासनपाद्यार्घ्यमाल्यधूपानुलेपनैः with welcome greetings, seats, foot-washing water, arghya, garlands, incense, and aromatic paste, तान् those revered sages, आनर्चुः reverently honored.

Simple Meaning: Following the example set by the infallible Krishna and Balarama, all the assembled kings and dignitaries reverently worshipped the great sages by offering them warm words of welcome, comfortable seats, water to wash their feet, respectful oblations, flower garlands, aromatic incense, and fine sandalwood paste.

उवाच सुखमासीनान् भगवान् धर्मगुप्तनुः ।
सदसस्तस्य महतो यतवाचोऽनुशृण्वतः ॥10.84.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: उवाच spoke, सुखम्-आसीनान् to those sages comfortably seated, भगवान् the Supreme Lord Krishna, धर्म-गुप्-तनूः He whose body protects dharma, सदसः तस्य of that assembly, महतः great, यत-वाचः keeping silent with controlled speech, अनुशृण्वतः while listening attentively

Anvaya: धर्मगुप्तनूः भगवान् सुखमासीनान् (ऋषीन्) तस्य महतः सदसः यतवाचः अनुशृण्वतः उवाच।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: धर्मगुप्तनूः भगवान् The Supreme Lord Krishna whose form protects dharma, सुखमासीनान् to those sages sitting comfortably, तस्य महतः सदसः as the grand and august assembly, यतवाचः keeping their speech restrained, अनुशृण्वतः listened with rapt attention, उवाच addressed.

Simple Meaning: When those great sages were comfortably seated, the Supreme Lord Krishna—who incarnates to protect dharma—began to speak to them, while everyone in that vast assembly controlled their speech and listened in silence.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
अहो वयं जन्मभृतो लब्धं कार्त्स्न्येन तत्फलम् ।
देवानामपि दुष्प्रापं यद् योगेश्वरदर्शनम् ॥10.84.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, अहो oh, वयम् we, जन्म-भृतः who possess a human birth, लब्धम् obtained, कार्त्स्न्येन in its entirety, तत्-फलम् that true fruit of human existence, देवानाम् for the devas, अपि even, दुष्प्रापम् difficult to attain, यत् which, योग-ईश्वर-दर्शनम् the visual audience (darshana) of the lords of yoga

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच— अहो! जन्मभृतः वयं तत्फलं कार्त्स्न्येन लब्धं, देवानाम् अपि दुष्प्रापं यत् योगेश्वरदर्शनम् (अस्माभिः प्राप्तम्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, अहो Oh, जन्मभृतः वयं we who hold human embodiment, तत्फलं that supreme goal of existence, कार्त्स्न्येन in all its fullness, लब्धं have attained, देवानाम् अपि even for the devas, दुष्प्रापं exceedingly difficult to obtain, यत् that, योगेश्वरदर्शनम् the direct audience of such master yogis and sages.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord Krishna said: Oh, how fortunate we are! Among all who hold human birth, we have today achieved the ultimate fruit of life in its complete fullness, that we have the direct audience of the supreme lords of yoga, whose vision and presence are difficult to attain even for the devas.

किं स्वल्पतपसां नृणामर्चायां देवचक्षुषाम् ।
दर्शनस्पर्शनप्रश्नप्रह्वपादार्चनादिकम् ॥10.84.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: किम् how is it possible, स्वल्प-तपसाम् for persons of meager austerities, नृणाम् among human beings, अर्चायाम् in the worshiped deity, देव-चक्षुषाम् who see the Divine only in installed idols, दर्शन-स्पर्शन-प्रश्न-प्रह्व-पाद-अर्चन-आदिकम् seeing, touching, inquiring, bowing low, and worshiping the feet of such exalted saints

Anvaya: देवचक्षुषाम् अर्चायां स्वल्पतपसां नृणां (भवतां) दर्शनस्पर्शनप्रश्नप्रह्वपादार्चनादिकं किं (सम्भवति)?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: देवचक्षुषाम् For those whose vision of God is confined, अर्चायां solely to the temple deity, स्वल्पतपसां and who possess only meager spiritual austerities, नृणां among men, (भवतां) of such exalted sages as you, दर्शनस्पर्शनप्रश्नप्रह्वपादार्चनादिकं beholding, touching, inquiring after welfare, bowing down, and worshiping the lotus feet, किं (सम्भवति) how could it ever be achievable?

Simple Meaning: How can ordinary human beings—whose austerities are meager and who recognize Divinity only within worshiped idols in temples—ever obtain the rare privilege of directly seeing, touching, conversing with, bowing before, and serving the lotus feet of self-realized saints like you?

न ह्यम्मयानि तीर्थानि न देवा मृच्छिलामयाः ।
ते पुनन्त्युरुकालेन दर्शनादेव साधवः ॥10.84.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, हि indeed, अम्-मयानि consisting of water, तीर्थानि holy pilgrimage sites, न nor, देवाः deities, मृत्-शिला-मयाः made of clay or stone, ते they (such waters and idols), पुनन्ति purify, उरु-कालेन after a long period of time, दर्शनात् by the mere sight, एव alone, साधवः saintly persons

Anvaya: अम्‍मयानि तीर्थानि न हि, मृच्छिलामयाः देवाः न (च) हि; ते उरुकालेन पुनन्ति, साधवः (तु) दर्शनात् एव (पुनन्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अम्‍मयानि तीर्थानि Holy places near water bodies, न हि do not (purify immediately), मृच्छिलामयाः देवाः and deities made of clay or stone, न हि do not (purify immediately); ते those pilgrimage waters and images, उरुकालेन over a long period of time, पुनन्ति purify a person, साधवः तु saintly devotees however, दर्शनात् एव by their mere audience alone, पुनन्ति purify.

Simple Meaning: Sacred places near water bodies and deities fashioned from clay or stone, purify a seeker only after a long time of dedicated worship. However the sight of saintly people purifies a person instantly.

नाग्निर्न सूर्यो न च चन्द्रतारका न भूर्जलं खं श्वसनोऽथ वाङ्मनः ।
उपासिता भेदकृतो हरन्त्यघं विपश्चितो घ्नन्ति मुहुर्तसेवया ॥10.84.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, अग्निः fire, न nor, सूर्यः the sun, न nor, च and, चन्द्र-तारकाः the moon and stars, न not, भूः earth, जलम् water, खम् ether, श्वसनः air, अथ and, वाक्-मनः mind and speech, उपासिताः being worshipped, भेद-कृतः which maintain the sense of dualistic difference, हरन्ति remove, अघम् sins, विपश्चितः the wise sages, घ्नन्ति destroy, मुहूर्त-सेवया by being served even for a single moment

Anvaya: अग्निः सूर्यः चन्द्रतारकाः भूः जलं खं श्वसनः वाङ्मनः च उपासिताः (अपि) भेदकृतोऽघं हरन्ति, (किन्तु) विपश्चितः मुहूर्तसेवया (अघं) घ्नन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अग्निः सूर्यः चन्द्रतारकाः भूः जलं खं श्वसनः वाङ्मनः च Fire, the sun, the moon and stars, earth, water, ether, air, and speech, उपासिताः when worshipped, भेदकृतः (still) maintain the dualistic material concept of difference, अघं हरन्ति and remove sins (gradually), (किन्तु) विपश्चितः of the self-realized sages, मुहूर्तसेवया by the service or being associated with them for even a mere moment, घ्नन्ति utterly destroy all sins and material bondage.

Simple Meaning: Fire, the sun, the moon and stars, earth, water, ether, air, and speech—even when duly worshipped—do not immediately dispel the dualistic illusion of material existence, whereas self-realized sages eradicate all sins and ignorance the moment one associates with them, even for a single fraction of a second.

यस्यात्मबुद्धिः कुणपे त्रिधातुके स्वधीः कलत्रादिषु भौम इज्यधीः ।
तत्तीर्थबुद्धिः सलिले न कर्हिचिज्जनेष्वभिज्ञेषु स एव गोखरः ॥10.84.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: यस्य of whom, आत्म-बुद्धिः the concept of self, कुणपे in a corpse-like body, त्रि-धातुके composed of three humors (vata, pitta, and kapha), स्व-धीः the sense of "mine", कलत्र-आदिषु in wife, children, and relatives, भौमे in objects made of earth (such as idols or native land), इज्य-धीः one who thinks that it is worthy of worship, तत्-तीर्थ-बुद्धिः the view of sacredness, सलिले in water, न never, कर्हिचित् at any time, जनेषु अभिज्ञेषु in saintly people who possess spiritual knowledge, सः he, एव indeed, गो-खरः an ass among cows (a foolish beast)

Anvaya: यस्य त्रिधातुके कुणपे आत्मबुद्धिः, कलत्रादिषु स्वधीः, भौमे इज्यधीः, सलिले तीर्थबुद्धिः (भवति), कर्हिचित् अभिज्ञेषु जनेषु (तीर्थबुद्धिः) न (भवति), सः एव गोखरः (भवति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यस्य One for whom, त्रिधातुके कुणपे आत्मबुद्धिः in this bodily bag composed of three humors (kapha, pitta, and vata), which is no better than a corpse, the identification of the true self lies, कलत्रादिषु स्वधीः in wife, family, and relations, the sense of ownership and affection exists, भौमे इज्यधीः in earthly land or stone images, one who thinks that it is worshipable, सलिले तीर्थबुद्धिः in water, the belief of pilgrimage purification is held, कर्हिचित् but never at any time, अभिज्ञेषु जनेषु in self-realized, wise, and saintly persons, (तीर्थबुद्धिः) न spiritual pilgrimage not seen, सः एव गोखरः he indeed, is a donkey among cows/a mere beast.

Simple Meaning: One who identifies the self with this corpse-like body made of three humors, who considers wife and kin to be his own, who views earth or stone as objects of worship, and who regards water as a holy place of pilgrimage—yet never sees sanctity in wise, spiritually realized saints—is no better than a donkey among cows.

श्री शुक उवाच
निशम्येत्थं भगवतः कृष्णस्याकुणठमेधसः ।
वचो दुरन्वयं विप्रास्तूष्णीमासन् भ्रमद्धियः ॥10.84.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, निशम्य having heard, इत्थम् thus, भगवतः of the Supreme Lord, कृष्णस्य of Krishna, अकुण्ठ-मेधसः of infallible and unlimited intelligence, वचः the speech/words, दुरन्वयम् difficult to comprehend, विप्राः the learned sages and brahmanas, तूष्णीम् आसन् remained silent, भ्रमत्-धियः their minds bewildered in wonder

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— अकुण्ठमेधसः भगवतः कृष्णस्य इत्थं दुरन्वयं वचः निशम्य विप्राः भ्रमद्धियः (सन्तः) तूष्णीम् आसन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच  Shri Shuka said, अकुण्ठमेधसः of the Supreme Lord whose intelligence is never obstructed or diminished, भगवतः कृष्णस्य of Lord Krishna, इत्थं in this manner, दुरन्वयं वचः incomprehensible words (acting as an ordinary seeker worshiping saints), निशम्य having heard, विप्राः all those exalted brahmanas and sages, भ्रमद्धियः their intellects bewildered, तूष्णीम् आसन् were silent.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Hearing these incomprehensible words from Krishna—whose omniscience and wisdom are infinite and unimpeded—the assembled sages were astonished. Bewildered by how the Supreme Lord was speaking like a humble, ordinary seeker glorifying them, they remained speechless.

चिरं विमृश्य मुनय ईश्वरस्येशितव्यताम् ।
जनसङ्ग्रह इत्युचुः स्मयन्तस्तं जगद्गुरुम् ॥10.84.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: चिरम् for a long time, विमृश्य having reflected, मुनयः the sages, ईश्वरस्य of the Supreme Controller (Lord Krishna), ईशितव्यताम् playing the role of a subject or subordinate, जन-सङ्ग्रहः guiding and setting an example for the general populace, इति thus, ऊचुः spoke, स्मयन्तः smiling gently, तम् to Him, जगत-गुरुम् the universal teacher

Anvaya: मुनयः चिरं (विचार्य) ईश्वरस्य ईशितव्यतां जनसङ्ग्रहः (इति) विमृश्य, स्मयन्तः जगद्गुरुं तम् ऊचुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मुनयः The great sages, चिरं (विचार्य) for a long time, pondering, ईश्वरस्य of the Supreme Sovereign Lord, ईशितव्यतां assumption of the role of a humble, subordinate seeker, जनसङ्ग्रहः is meant to instruct and guide humanity, (इति) विमृश्य understanding this internal purpose, स्मयन्तः smiling with deep admiration, जगद्गुरुं to that supreme teacher of the universe - Krishna, तम् ऊचुः addressed Him as follows.

Simple Meaning: After reflecting deeply for a long time on why the Supreme Lord was acting as if He were a humble, subordinate seeker, the sages understood that He was doing so to set an ideal example for humanity (Loka-sangraha). Smiling at the Supreme Teacher of the Universe, they addressed Him.

मुनयः ऊचुः
यन्मायया तत्त्वविदुत्तमा वयं विमोहिता विश्वसृजामधीश्वराः ।
यदीशितव्यायति गूढ ईहया अहो विचित्रं भगवद्विचेष्टितम् ॥10.84.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: मुनयः ऊचुः the sages said, यत्-मायया by whose illusionary potency (Maya), तत्त्व-विद्-उत्तमाः the foremost knowers of the Absolute Truth, वयम् we, विमोहिताः are utterly bewildered, विश्व-सृजाम् of the creators of the universe (such as Brahma and Prajapatis), अधीश्वराः the supreme masters, यत् which Supreme Lord, ईशितव्यायति acts as if subordinate or ruled, गूढः hidden, ईहया by His transcendental activities, अहो Oh, विचित्रम् wonderful and incomprehensible, भगवत्-विचेष्टितम् the pastimes/deeds of the Supreme Lord

Anvaya: मुनयः ऊचुः— विश्वसृजाम् अधीश्वराः तत्त्वविदुत्तमाः वयं यन्मायया विमोहिताः (स्मः), यत् ईहया गूढः (सन्) ईशितव्यायति (इति), अहो भगवद्विचेष्टितं विचित्रम्!

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मुनयः ऊचुः The great sages said, विश्वसृजाम् अधीश्वराः even the lords over the creators of the universe, तत्त्वविदुत्तमाः along with us who are considered foremost among knowers of Truth, वयं we all, यन्मायया by whose divine Maya, विमोहिताः are rendered completely bewildered, यत् who, ईहया through His playful activities, गूढः remaining hidden, ईशितव्यायति acts as if He were subordinate and under our instruction, अहो Oh, भगवद्विचेष्टितं the mysterious pastimes of the Supreme Sovereign, विचित्रम् are astonishing indeed!

Simple Meaning: The sages said: O Supreme Lord, even the great creators of the universe and we who are considered the foremost knowers of the Absolute Truth are bewildered by Your Maya. Remaining concealed beneath Your human-like playfulness, You act as if You were an ordinary subject subordinate to us and in need of our instruction. Ah, how wonderfully bewildering are Your divine pastimes!

अनीह एतद् बहुधैक आत्मना सृजत्यवत्यत्ति न बध्यते यथा ।
भौमैर्हि भूमिर्बहुनामरूपिणी अहो विभूम्नश्चरितं विडम्बनम् ॥10.84.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: अनीहः without effort, एतत् this universe, बहुधा in manifold forms, एकः the one Supreme Lord, आत्मना by His own potency, सृजति creates, अवति sustains, अत्ति annihilates, न is not, बध्यते bound, यथा just as, भौमैः by its earthy transformations/products, भूमिः the earth, बहु-नाम-रूपिणी assuming many names and forms, अहो oh, विभूम्नः of the Unlimited Supreme Lord, चरितम् the actions/activities, विडम्बनम् a playful imitation/acting

Anvaya: अनीहः एकः (अपि भगवान्) आत्मना एतत् बहुधा सृजति अवति अत्ति, (तथापि) न बध्यते; यथा बहुनामरूपिणी भूमिः भौमैः (न बध्यते)। अहो! विभूम्नः चरितं विडम्बनम्!

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अनीहः Without any effort, एकः one without a second, आत्मना by His own spiritual power, एतत् this entire cosmos, बहुधा in diverse forms, सृजति creates, अवति preserves, अत्ति and dissolves, (तथापि) yet, न बध्यते is never bound by these cosmic operations; यथा just as, बहुनामरूपिणी the earth element assuming manifold names and forms, भौमैः by its own transformed clay products, (न बध्यते) is not entangled; अहो Oh, विभूम्नः of the all-pervading Supreme Almighty, चरितं the transcendental behavior, विडम्बनम् is indeed a marvelous.

Simple Meaning: Though You are without material desire and remain one without a second, You effortlessly create, maintain, and dissolve this vast universe through Your own potency. Yet, You are never entangled by these activities, just as the earth remains untransformed by the various earthen objects formed from it. Ah, how marvelous is the supreme sport of Your transcendental pastimes!

अथापि काले स्वजनाभिगुप्तये बिभर्षि सत्त्वं खलनिग्रहाय च ।
स्वलीलया वेदपथं सनातनं वर्णाश्रमात्मा पुरुषः परो भवान् ॥10.84.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथ अपि yet, काले at the appropriate time, स्वजन-अभिगुप्तये for the protection of Your own devotees, बिभर्षि You assume/manifest, सत्त्वम् pure goodness (sattva-guna/divine form), खल-निग्रहाय for the chastisement of the wicked, च and, स्व-लीलया through Your divine pastimes, वेद-पथम् the path of the Vedas, सनातनम् eternal, वर्णाश्रम-आत्मा the soul/foundation of the social and spiritual orders (varnasrama), पुरुषः परः the Supreme Person, भवान् You

Anvaya: अथ अपि स्वजनाभिगुप्तये खलनिग्रहाय च काले सत्त्वं बिभर्षि, परः पुरुषः भवान् वर्णाश्रमात्मा (सन्) स्वलीलया सनातनं वेदपथं (च पालयसि)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ अपि Yet despite Your transcendence, स्वजनाभिगुप्तये for the protection of Your devotees, खलनिग्रहाय च and for the suppression of the wicked, काले at the proper time, सत्त्वं the pure transcendental form of goodness, बिभर्षि assume. परः पुरुषः भवान् The Supreme Person beyond material nature - You, स्वलीलया through Your own sportive pastimes, सनातनं the eternal, वेदपथं path of Vedic wisdom, वर्णाश्रमात्मा (सन्) as the soul of the social and spiritual order, (पालयसि protect and establish).

Simple Meaning: Yet, for the protection of Your devotees and the chastisement of the wicked, You assume this pure transcendental form at the appropriate time. Through Your divine pastimes, the Supreme Person You uphold the eternal path of the Vedas and serve as the very soul and protector of the social and spiritual orders (varnasrama).

ब्रह्म ते हृदयं शुक्लं तपः स्वाध्यायसंयमैः ।
यत्रोपलब्धं सद् व्यक्तमव्यक्तं च ततः परम् ॥10.84.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्रह्म the Veda, ते Your, हृदयम् heart, शुक्लम् pure, तपः-स्वाध्याय-संयमैः through penance, study of the Vedas, and self-control, यत्र wherein, उपलब्धम् realized, सत् manifest creation, व्यक्तम् the differentiated cosmos, अव्यक्तम् the unmanifest primal cause (prakriti), च and, ततः परम् that Absolute Transcendence beyond both

Anvaya: ब्रह्म ते शुक्लं हृदयं यत्र तपःस्वाध्यायसंयमैः व्यक्तं सत् अव्यक्तं च ततः परम् उपलब्धम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्रह्म ते शुक्लं हृदयं The Vedas are Your pure heart, यत्र where, तपःस्वाध्यायसंयमैः Through austerities, study of scriptures, and sense control, व्यक्तं सत् the manifest creation, अव्यक्तं च and the unmanifest cause, ततः परम् and that Supreme Truth beyond both, उपलब्धम् is directly experienced and realized.

Simple Meaning: Your pure heart is the very embodiment of the Veda. Within that heart, through austere penance, sacred study, and self-control, is realized the manifest universe, the unmanifest material cause, and the Supreme Truth that transcends them both.

तस्माद् ब्रह्मकुलं ब्रह्मन् शास्त्रयोनेस्त्वमात्मनः ।
सभाजयसि सद्धाम तद् ब्रह्मण्याग्रणीर्भवान् ॥10.84.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मात् therefore, ब्रह्म-कुलम् the order of brahmanas, ब्रह्मन् O Supreme Knower of Brahman (addressing Krishna), शास्त्र-योनेः of whom the Vedas are the source/who is the source of the scriptures, त्वम् You, आत्मनः of Yourself, सभाजयसि honor and worship, सत्-धाम the abode of spiritual truth, तत् that order of brahmanas, ब्रह्मणि in spiritual realization, अग्रणीः foremost, भवान् You

Anvaya: ब्रह्मन्! शास्त्रयोनेः आत्मनः ब्रह्मकुलं सत्धाम, तस्मात् त्वं तत् सभाजयसि, (यतः) भवान् ब्रह्मणि अग्रणीः (असि)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्रह्मन् O Supreme Lord who are Brahman personified, शास्त्रयोनेः of the Vedas which are the womb of knowledge (or of whom the Veda is the source), आत्मनः of Yourself, ब्रह्मकुलं the order of brahmanas, सत्धाम is the supreme abode of spiritual truth; तस्मात् therefore, त्वं You, तत् सभाजयसि honor them with prostrations and worship, (यतः) because भवान् You Yourself, ब्रह्मणि in spiritual wisdom and among knowers of Brahman, अग्रणीः are the foremost leader.

Simple Meaning: O Lord, you are the source and master of all sacred scriptures, yet you bow down to and honor the community of brahmanas as Your very own self, recognizing them as the abode of spiritual truth. This is entirely fitting, for You Yourself are the supreme leader among all knowers of Brahman.

अद्य नो जन्मसाफल्यं विद्यायास्तपसो दृशः ।
त्वया सङ्गम्य सद्गत्या यदन्तः श्रेयसां परः ॥10.84.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: अद्य today, नः our, जन्म-साफल्यम् fulfillment of birth, विद्यायाः of education, तपसः of austerities, दृशः and of vision/sight, त्वया with You, सङ्गम्य having met, सद्गत्या who are the ultimate destination of the righteous, यत् since, अन्तः within/at the end, श्रेयसाम् of all auspiciousness and spiritual benefit, परः the supreme embodiment

Anvaya: सद्गत्या त्वया सङ्गम्य अद्य नः जन्मसाफल्यं विद्यायाः तपसः दृशः (साफल्यम् अभवत्), यत् (त्वं) श्रेयसां परः (असि)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सद्गत्या त्वया With You, who are the ultimate refuge of the pious, सङ्गम्य having attained association and meeting, अद्य today, नः our, जन्मसाफल्यं complete fulfillment of birth, विद्यायाः of education, तपसः of austerities, दृशः and of vision/sight, (साफल्यम् अभवत्) fulfillment has been achieved, यत् since, (त्वं) You are, श्रेयसां परः the supreme limit and culmination of all ultimate good.

Simple Meaning: Today, our birth, our education, our austerities, and our very power of vision have all attained fulfillment by meeting You—the supreme destination of the righteous—for You are the ultimate embodiment of all supreme blessings and liberation.

नमस्तस्मै भगवते कृष्णायाकुण्ठमेधसे ।
स्वयोगमाययाच्छन्नमहिम्ने परमात्मने ॥10.84.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: नमः obeisances, तस्मै unto that, भगवते Supreme Lord, कृष्णाय Krishna, अकुण्ठ-मेधसे whose intelligence is never obstructed or diminished, स्व-योग-मायाया by His own internal potency (yogamaya), आच्छन्न-महिम्ने whose supreme glory and majesty remain concealed, परम-आत्माने unto the Supreme Soul

Anvaya: स्वयोगमायया आच्छन्नमहिम्ने अकुण्ठमेधसे तस्मै भगवते कृष्णाय परमात्मने नमः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: स्वयोगमायया By His own divine spiritual potency (yogamaya), आच्छन्नमहिम्ने whose supreme transcendental majesty and opulence remain veiled, अकुण्ठमेधसे whose unimpeded intelligence and wisdom are infinite, तस्मै unto that, भगवते Supreme Lord, कृष्णाय Krishna, परमात्मने the Supreme Soul residing within all, नमः our humble prostrations and obeisances.

Simple Meaning: Obeisances unto that Supreme Lord Krishna, whose transcendental glory is veiled by His own internal potency of yogamaya, whose intelligence is ever unimpeded, and who is the Supreme Soul of all existence.

न यं विदन्त्यमी भूपा एकारामाश्च वृष्णयः ।
मायाजवनिकाच्छन्नमात्मानं कालमीश्वरम् ॥10.84.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, यम् whom, विदन्ति know/understand, अमी these, भूपाः kings, एक-आरामाः enjoy with You, च and, वृष्णयः the Vrishni heroes, माया-जवनिका-छन्नम् covered by the curtain of illusion (maya), आत्मानम् the Supreme Self, कालम् time personified, ईश्वरम् the Supreme Controller

Anvaya: यम् अमी एकारामाः भूपाः वृष्णयः च मायाजवनिकाच्छन्नं आत्मानं कालम् ईश्वरं न विदन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यम् Whom, अमी एकारामाः भूपाः these kings, who enjoy with You, वृष्णयः च and the Vrishni leaders, मायाजवनिकाच्छन्नं who remains covered by the screen of His mysterious potency, आत्मानं the indwelling Soul of all, कालम् the destructive power of Time, ईश्वरं and the Supreme Sovereign Lord, न विदन्ति do not truly comprehend.

Simple Meaning: Neither these assembled kings nor the Vrishni heroes—who live with You and enjoy with You—truly recognize You as the Supreme Controller, the ultimate form of Time, and the Soul of all beings, for You remain concealed behind the divine curtain of Your own illusionary potency.

यथा शयानः पुरुष आत्मानं गुणतत्त्वदृक् ।
नाममात्रेन्द्रियाभातं न वेद रहितं परम् ॥10.84.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: यथा just as, शयानः sleeping, पुरुषः a person, आत्मानम् himself, गुण-तत्त्व-दृक् who perceives only the objects and concepts created by the dream state, नाम-मात्र-इन्द्रिय-आभातम् consisting merely of names and mental images perceived through the dream-senses, न does not, वेद know, रहितम् distinct/free from that dream world, परम् the true waking self

Anvaya: यथा गुणतत्त्वदृक् शयानः पुरुषः नाममात्रेन्द्रियाभातं (स्वप्नं पश्यन्) रहितं परम् आत्मानं न वेद (तथा इमे न विदन्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यथा Just as, गुणतत्त्वदृक् a person perceiving only the subtle mental transformations during sleep, शयानः पुरुषः a sleeping individual, नाममात्रेन्द्रियाभातं that which appears through dream-senses as mere illusory names and forms, (स्वप्नं पश्यन् while witnessing the dream), रहितं as completely distinct from the dream state, परम् higher/transcendental, आत्मानम् his true awake self, न वेद does not perceive; (तथा in the same way, इमे these kings and Vrishnis do not perceive Krishna's true identity).

Simple Meaning: Just as a sleeping man, absorbed in the dream images created by his subtle mind, fails to realize his true waking self that exists entirely apart from the dream world, so too these kings and Vrishnis, bound by the illusion of material identity, fail to recognize Krishna as the transcendental Supreme Reality existing beyond the universe.

एवं त्वा नाममात्रेषु विषयेष्विन्द्रियेहया ।
मायया विभ्रमच्चित्तो न वेद स्मृत्युपप्लवात् ॥10.84.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this way, त्वाम् You (O Lord), नाम-मात्रेषु existing merely in name, विषयेषु in material sense objects, इन्द्रिय-इहया by the activities of the senses, मायया by illusory energy, विभ्रमत्-चित्तः whose consciousness is bewildered, न वेद does not know/fail to recognize, स्मृति-उपप्लवात् due to the disturbance/loss of spiritual memory

Anvaya: एवं मायाया इन्द्रियेहया नाममात्रेषु विषयेषु विभ्रमच्चित्तः (नृणाम् अयम् लोकः) स्मृत्युपप्लवात् त्वां न वेद।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवं In this very manner, मायाया by the divine illusory energy, इन्द्रियेहया and by the frantic urges of the material senses, नाममात्रेषु existing merely as transient names and forms, विषयेषु in external sense objects, विभ्रमच्चित्तः whose mind is thoroughly agitated and bewildered, (अयं लोकः this worldly populace), स्मृत्युपप्लवात् due to the complete loss of spiritual memory and true identity, त्वां You (the Supreme Self), न वेद fail to recognize.

Simple Meaning:  In this manner, bewildered by Maya and driven by the frantic urges of the senses toward ephemeral material objects of mere name and form, the people lose their spiritual memory and thus fail to recognize You as their true Supreme Self.

तस्याद्य ते ददृशिमाङ्घ्रिमघौघमर्षतीर्थास्पदं हृदि कृतं सुविपक्वयोगैः ।
उत्सिक्तभक्त्युपहताशयजीवकोशा आपुर्भवद्गतिमथोऽनुगृहाण भक्तान् ॥10.84.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य of You, अद्य today, ते Your, ददृशिम we have seen, अङ्घ्रियम् lotus feet, अघ-ओघ-मर्ष-तीर्थ-आस्पदम् which are the holy place of pilgrimage capable of washing away heaps of sins, हृदि in the heart, कृतम् established, सुविपक्व-योगैः by those whose yoga has fully matured, उत्सिक्त-भक्ति-उपहत-आशय-जीव-कोशाः whose coverings of the soul have been melted and washed away by intense devotion, आपुः have attained, भवत्-गतिम् Your transcendental destination/refuge, अथ and now, अनुगृहाण show grace, भक्तान् unto Your devotees

Anvaya: अद्य सुविपक्वयोगैः हृदि कृतम् अघौघमर्षतीर्थास्पदं ते तस्य अङ्घ्रिं वयं ददृशिम; उत्सिक्तभक्त्युपहताशयजीवकोशाः भवद्गतिम् आपुः, अथ भक्तान् अनुगृहाण।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अद्य Today, सुविपक्वयोगैः By great sages whose practice of yoga has reached absolute maturity, हृदि in the core of their hearts, कृतम् and established, अघौघमर्षतीर्थास्पदं which serve as the ultimate holy place that destroys torrents of sins, ते Your, तस्य अङ्घ्रिं those very lotus feet of Yours, वयं ददृशिम we have directly beheld; उत्सिक्तभक्त्युपहताशयजीवकोशाः (those) whose subtle coverings of the soul have been dissolved by overflowing devotion, भवद्गतिम् आपुः have attained Your supreme state, अथ therefore भक्तान् अनुगृहाण shower Your grace upon us, Your devoted servants.

Simple Meaning: Today we have beheld those lotus feet of Yours, which are established in the hearts of those whose yoga has fully matured, and which act as the ultimate holy place capable of washing away all sins. Those whose inner hearts have been melted by intense, overflowing devotion attain Your supreme destination. Therefore, shower Your grace upon us, Your devoted servants.

श्री शुक उवाच
इत्यनुज्ञाप्य दाशार्हं धृतराष्ट्रं युधिष्ठिरम् ।
राजर्षे स्वाश्रमान् गन्तुं मुनयो दधिरे मनः ॥10.84.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति thus, अनुज्ञाप्य having taken permission from, दाशार्हम् descendant of Dasharha - Krishna, धृतराष्ट्रम् King Dhritarashtra, युधिष्ठिरम् and King Yudhishthira, राजर्षेः O King Parikshit, स्व-आश्रमान् to their respective hermitages, गन्तुम् to return, मुनयः the sages, दधिरे fixed, मनः their minds

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— राजर्षे! मुनयः दाशार्हं धृतराष्ट्रं युधिष्ठिरं च इति अनुज्ञाप्य स्वाश्रमान् गन्तुं मनः दधिरे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, राजर्षे O King Parikshit, मुनयः the great sages, दाशार्हं Lord Krishna, धृतराष्ट्रं King Dhritarashtra, युधिष्ठिरं च and King Yudhishthira, इति in this manner, अनुज्ञाप्य having taken leave of, स्वाश्रमान् to their respective hermitages, गन्तुं to return, मनः the mind, दधिरे resolved.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King Parikshit, after taking leave of Lord Krishna, King Dhritarashtra, and Emperor Yudhishthira, the great sages resolved in their minds to return to their respective hermitages.

तद् वीक्ष्य तानुपव्रज्य वसुदेवो महायशाः ।
प्रणम्य चोपसंगृह्य बभाषेदं सुयन्त्रितः ॥10.84.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that, वीक्ष्य seeing, तान् them (the departing sages), उपव्रज्य approaching, वसुदेवः Vasudeva, महा-यशाः the greatly renowned, प्रणम्य bowing down, च and, उपसंगृह्य touching their feet in reverence, बभाषे spoke, इदम् these words, सु-यन्त्रितः with humility and self-control

Anvaya: तत् वीक्ष्य महायशाः वसुदेवः तान् उपव्रज्य प्रणम्य (पादान्) उपसंगृह्य च सुयन्त्रितः इदं बभाषे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत् Seeing that (the sages were preparing to depart), महायशाः the greatly famous, वसुदेवः Vasudeva, तान् them, उपव्रज्य going near, प्रणम्य bowing low, (पादान्) उपसंगृह्य च and taking hold of their feet in devotion, सुयन्त्रितः with humility and self-restraint, इदं words, बभाषे addressed them.

Simple Meaning: Seeing the great sages preparing to leave, the highly renowned Vasudeva approached them, bowed low, and reverently touched their feet. With humility and self-control, he addressed them as follows.

वसुदेव उवाच
नमो वः सर्वदेवेभ्य ऋषयः श्रोतुमर्हथ ।
कर्मणा कर्मनिर्हारो यथा स्यन्नस्तदुच्यताम् ॥10.84.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: वसुदेवः उवाच Vasudeva said, नमः obeisances, वः unto you, सर्व-देवेभ्यः who are the embodiments of gods, ऋषयः O great sages, श्रोतुम् to hear (me), अर्हथ you ought, कर्मणा by karma/prescribed actions, कर्म-निर्हारः the eradication and release from the bondage of karma, यथा in such a way that, स्यात् may be, नः us, तत् that, उच्यताम् please explain

Anvaya: वसुदेव उवाच— वः सर्वदेवेभ्यः नमः; ऋषयः! श्रोतुम् अर्हथ। (यथा) कर्मणा कर्मनिर्हारः स्यात्, नः (तत्) उच्यताम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वसुदेव उवाच Vasudeva said, वः सर्वदेवेभ्यः unto you who are the embodiments of all the gods, नमः our humble prostrations; ऋषयः O exalted sages, श्रोतुम् अर्हथ please listen to me. (यथा) In such a manner that, कर्मणा through prescribed actions, कर्मनिर्हारः the wiping away of the bondage of past karma, स्यात् can take place, नः to us, तत् that path, उच्यताम् please explain.

Simple Meaning: Vasudeva said: O great sages, who are the embodiments of all the gods, I offer my respectful obeisances unto you. Please be gracious enough to listen to my query and explain how ordinary human beings can completely eradicate the bondage of karma through prescribed actions.

नारद उवाच
नातिचित्रमिदं विप्रा वसुदेवो बुभुत्सया ।
कृष्णं मत्वार्भकं यन्नः पृच्छति श्रेय आत्मनः ॥10.84.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: नारदः उवाच Narada Muni said, न not, अति-चित्रम् very surprising, इदम् this, विप्राः O learned brahmanas, वसुदेवः Vasudeva, बुभुत्सया with a desire to know, कृष्णम् Krishna, मत्वा considering, अर्भकम् an ordinary child/son, यत् that, नः of us, पृच्छति asks, श्रेयः ultimate welfare/good, आत्मनः for himself

Anvaya: नारद उवाच— विप्राः! वसुदेवः बुभुत्सया कृष्णम् अर्भकं मत्वा यत् आत्मनः श्रेयः नः पृच्छति, इदं न अतिचित्रम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नारद उवाच Narada Muni said, हे विप्राः O learned brahmanas, वसुदेवः Vasudeva, बुभुत्सया eager for knowledge and liberation, कृष्णम् Lord Krishna, अर्भकं मत्वा mistaking for a mere child or his own young son, यत् because, आत्मनः श्रेयः the ultimate spiritual good for himself, नः us, पृच्छति asks, इदं this behavior of his, न अतिचित्रम् is not at all surprising.

Simple Meaning: Narada Muni said: O learned brahmanas, it is not at all surprising that Vasudeva, driven by a desire for spiritual progress, asks us about his ultimate welfare—for he considers Lord Krishna to be merely his own young son rather than the Supreme Lord Himself.

सन्निकर्षो हि मर्त्यानामनादरकारणम् ।
गाङ्गं हित्वा यथान्याम्भस्तत्रत्यो याति शुद्धये ॥10.84.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: सन्निकर्षः constant proximity/familiarity, हि indeed, मर्त्यानाम् for mortal men, अनादर-कारणम् a cause of disrespect/negligence, गाङ्गम् the sacred water of the Ganges, हित्वा leaving behind, यथा just as, अन्य-अम्भः other (ordinary) water, तत्रत्यः a person living on the banks of the Ganges, याति goes (to seek), शुद्धये for purification

Anvaya: मर्त्यानां सन्निकर्षः अनादरकारणं हि; यथा तत्रत्यः गाङ्गं हित्वा शुद्धये अन्याम्भः याति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मर्त्यानां For ordinary mortal human beings, सन्निकर्षः close physical familiarity, अनादरकारणं हि becomes verily a cause of oversight and disregard; यथा just as, तत्रत्यः a resident living right on the banks of the Holy Ganges, गाङ्गं हित्वा leaving aside the holy Ganges water flowing beside him, शुद्धये for his purification, अन्याम्भः याति goes far away in search of other water sources.

Simple Meaning: Indeed, for mortal human beings, constant proximity and familiarity often breeds disregard. Just as a person living right on the banks of the holy Ganges might ignore its sacred, purifying waters and travel elsewhere in search of purification, Vasudeva, having the Supreme Lord constantly present as his son, asks us about the path to spiritual liberation.

यस्यानुभूतिः कालेन लयोत्पत्त्यादिनास्य वै ।
स्वतोऽन्यस्माच्च गुणतो न कुतश्चन रिष्यति ॥10.84.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: यस्य whose, अनुभूतिः pure self-luminous consciousness/perception, कालेन by time, लय-उत्पत्ति-आदिना through creation, dissolution, and other cycles, अस्य of this material universe, वै indeed, स्वतः from Himself, अन्यस्मात् from another, च and, गुणतः from the material modes (gunas), न not, कुतश्चन in any way/from any cause, रिष्यति undergoes diminution/destruction

Anvaya: अस्य (जगतः) लयोत्पत्त्यादिना कालेन स्वतः अन्यस्मात् गुणतः च यस्य अनुभूतिः कुतश्चन न रिष्यति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अस्य (जगतः) Of this cosmos, लयोत्पत्त्यादिना through creation, dissolution, and intermediate stages, कालेन brought about by Time, स्वतः through internal causes, अन्यस्मात् from external agents, गुणतः च or from the influence of the material modes, यस्य Whose, अनुभूतिः absolute eternal consciousness, कुतश्चन in any manner whatsoever, न रिष्यति undergoes no diminution.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna's eternal self-luminous consciousness never suffers any diminution or destruction—neither by time, nor by the creation and dissolution of the universe, nor by internal changes, external factors, or the influence of the material modes (gunas).

तं क्लेशकर्मपरिपाकगुणप्रवाहैरव्याहतानुभवमीश्वरमद्वितीयम् ।
प्राणादिभिः स्वविभवैरुपगूढमन्यो मन्येत सूर्यमिव मेघहिमोपरागैः ॥10.84.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् Him, क्लेश-कर्म-परिपाक-गुण-प्रवाहैः by the afflictions, actions, their fruition, and the ongoing flow of the material modes, अव्याहत-अनुभवम् whose eternal consciousness is completely unobstructed, ईश्वरम् the Supreme Controller, अद्वितीयम् non-dual/one without a second, प्राण-आदिभिः by the life-airs, senses, mind, and intellect, स्व-विभवैः which are His own powers/manifestations, उपगूढम् veiled/covered, अन्यः an ignorant/unrealized person, मन्येत considers/perceives, सूर्यम् इव like the sun, मेघ-हिम-उपरागैः by clouds, mist, or an eclipse

Anvaya: क्लेशकर्मपरिपाकगुणप्रवाहैः अव्याहतानुभवम् अद्वितीयम् ईश्वरं तं (स्वैः) स्वविभवैः प्राणादिभिः उपगूढं (सन्तं) मेघहिमोपरागैः सूर्यम् इव अन्यः मन्येत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: क्लेशकर्मपरिपाकगुणप्रवाहैः By the chain of afflictions (klesha), fruitive activities (karma), their fruits (paripaka), and the continuous flow of the material modes (guna-pravaha), अव्याहतानुभवम् whose pristine, absolute consciousness is ever unobstructed; अद्वितीयम् ईश्वरं the one non-dual Supreme Controller; तं Him, स्वविभवैः which are manifestations of His own potency, प्राणादिभिः by the life-forces, senses, and mind, उपगूढं covered/concealed, (सन्तं) being so, मेघहिमोपरागैः by clouds, frost, or eclipses, सूर्यम् इव like the sun, अन्यः an ignorant soul, मन्येत considers Him to be limited.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Controller is one without a second, and His eternal consciousness is never obstructed by afflictions, karmic reactions, or the flow of the material modes. Yet, just as an ignorant person thinks the sun is covered or dimmed when obscured by clouds, mist, or an eclipse—even though those clouds are formed by the sun's own energy—so an unillumined soul considers the Supreme Lord to be an ordinary human conditioned by the very senses and life-airs that originate from Him.

अथोचुर्मुनयो राजन्नाभाष्यानकदुन्दुभिम् ।
सर्वेषां शृण्वतां राज्ञां तथैवाच्युतरामयोः ॥10.84.34॥

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King Parikshit, अथ thereupon, सर्वेषां राज्ञां of all the gathered kings, तथैव as well as, अच्युतरामयोः Krishna and Balarama, शृण्वतां while listening, मुनयः the great assembly of sages, आनकदुन्दुभिम् Vasudeva, आभाष्य addressing directly, (इदम्) ऊचुः spoke as follows.

Simple Meaning: (Shri Shuka said:) O King, thereupon, while all the assembled kings as well as Krishna and Balarama listened attentively, the great sages addressed Vasudeva and spoke as follows.

कर्मणा कर्मनिर्हार एष साधु निरूपितः ।
यच्छ्रद्धया यजेद्विष्णुं सर्वयज्ञेश्वरं मखैः ॥10.84.35॥

Anvaya: कर्मणा कर्मनिर्हारः एषः साधु निरूपितः— यत् श्रद्धया सर्वयज्ञेश्वरं विष्णुं मखैः यजेत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कर्मणा Through prescribed duties, कर्मनिर्हारः the total wiping out and liberation from karmic bondage, एषः this truth, साधु perfectly, निरूपितः has been declared and established; यत् that, श्रद्धया with faith, सर्वयज्ञेश्वरं विष्णुं the Supreme Lord and master of all sacrifices, Lord Vishnu, मखैः through sacred Vedic sacrifices, यजेत् one should worship.

Simple Meaning: The true method of neutralizing karma through karma has been thoroughly established by the scriptures—it is to worship Lord Vishnu, the Supreme Master and Enjoyer of all sacrifices, with devout faith through the performance of Vedic sacrifices.

चित्तस्योपशमोऽयं वै कविभिः शास्त्रचक्षुषा ।

Anvaya: कविभिः शास्त्रचक्षुषा चित्तस्य अयम् उपशमः आत्ममुदावहः च सुगमः योगः धर्मः वै दर्शितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कविभिः By the wise seers and sages, शास्त्रचक्षुषा through the enlightened vision of the scriptures, चित्तस्य of the agitated mind, अयम् उपशमः this tranquility and cessation of desire, आत्ममुदावहः च and bringing delight to the self, सुगमः योगः an easy path, धर्मः and duty, वै indeed, दर्शितः has been shown.

Simple Meaning: Wise seers, viewing through the lens of scriptural authority, have declared that dedicated sacrifice unto the Supreme Lord is the most effective means to bring peace to the mind and joy to the soul. This is an easy path and a sacred duty.

अयं स्वस्त्ययनः पन्था द्विजातेर्गृहमेधिनः ।
यच्छ्रद्धयाऽऽप्तवित्तेन शुक्लेनेज्येत पूरुषः ॥10.84.37॥

Word-by-word meaning: अयम् this, स्वस्ति-अयनः leading to true auspiciousness/well-being, पन्थाः path, द्विजातेः for a twice-born person (brahmana), गृह-मेधिनः for a householder, यत् wherein, श्रद्धया with genuine faith, आप्त-वित्तेन with wealth acquired, शुक्लेन through honest/righteous means, इज्येत is worshiped, पूरुषः the Supreme Lord (Purusha)

Anvaya: द्विजातेः गृहमेधिनः अयं स्वस्त्ययनः पन्थाः (अस्ति), यत् आप्तवित्तेन शुक्लेन श्रद्धया पूरुषः इज्येत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: द्विजातेः गृहमेधिनः For a brahmana, who lives the life of a householder, अयं स्वस्त्ययनः पन्थाः this is the path leading to ultimate welfare and auspiciousness; यत् wherein, शुक्लेन आप्तवित्तेन with wealth earned through righteous means, श्रद्धया with unshakeable faith, पूरुषः the Supreme Lord, इज्येत is devoutly worshiped.

Simple Meaning: For a brahmana living as a householder, this is the most auspicious path of well-being: to worship the Supreme Lord with deep faith using righteously earned wealth.

वित्तैषणां यज्ञदानैर्गृहैर्दारसुतैषणाम् ।
आत्मलोकैषणां देव कालेन विसृजेद् बुधः ।
ग्रामे त्यक्तैषणाः सर्वे ययुर्धीरास्तपोवनम् ॥10.84.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: वित्त-एषणाम् the desire for wealth, यज्ञ-दानैः through performance of sacrifices and charity, गृहैः by renouncing home/household life, दार-सुत-एषणाम् the desire for wife and progeny, आत्म-लोक-एषणाम् the desire for heavenly planets or one's own elevated status, देव O noble soul, कालेन through the course of time, विसृजेत् should abandon, बुधः an intelligent person, ग्रामे in the village/worldly community, त्यक्त-एषणणाः having given up all desires, सर्वे all, ययुः went, धीराः the sober and wise sages, तपोवनम् to the forest of penance

Anvaya: देव! बुधः यज्ञदानैः वित्तैषणां, गृहैः दारसुतैषणां, कालेन (च) आत्मलोकैषणां विसृजेत्; (एवं) ग्रामे त्यक्तैषणाः सर्वे धीराः तपोवनं ययुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: देव O noble Vasudeva, बुधः an intelligent/wise seeker, यज्ञदानैः by performing sacrifices and giving charity, वित्तैषणां the deep-rooted desire for wealth, गृहैः by relinquishing household attachment, दारसुतैषणां the longing for wife and children, कालेन च and through the relentless passage of time, आत्मलोकैषणां the desire for heavenly planets and high spiritual realms, विसृजेत् should cast off; (एवं) in this manner, ग्रामे while in the worldly community, त्यक्तैषणाः having relinquished all three material desires, सर्वे all, धीराः sober and self-controlled sages, तपोवनं to the sacred forest of austerity, ययुः proceeded.

Simple Meaning: O noble Vasudeva, a wise person should overcome the desire for wealth by performing sacrifices and giving charity; the desire for family and home life by renouncing household attachments; and the desire for heavenly elevation through the realization of the relentless passage of time. Having relinquished these three desires while still residing in the world, sober sages retire to the sacred forests of austerity to perform penance.

ऋणैस्त्रिभिर्द्विजो जातो देवर्षिपितृणां प्रभो ।
यज्ञाध्ययनपुत्रैस्तान्यनिस्तीर्य त्यजन् पतेत् ॥10.84.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऋणैः with debts, त्रिभिः three, द्विजः a twice-born person, जातः is born, देव-ऋषि-पितॄणाम् owed to the devas, the sages, and the ancestors, प्रभो O lord, यज्ञ-अध्ययन-पुत्रैः through sacrifices, study of scriptures, and procreating sons, ताानि those debts, अनिस्तीर्य without clearing/paying back, त्यजन् giving up (household life), पतेत् falls down

Anvaya: प्रभो! द्विजः देवर्षिपितॄणां त्रिभिः ऋणैः जातः (भवति)। यज्ञाध्ययनपुत्रैः ताानि अनिस्तीर्य (गृहं) त्यजन् पतेत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रभो O Vasudeva, द्विजः A brahmana, देवर्षिपितॄणां to the devas, the sages, and the forefathers, त्रिभिः ऋणैः with three inherent debts, जातः is born into this world. यज्ञाध्ययनपुत्रैः Through performance of sacrifices (for the devas), study of the Vedas (for the sages), and begetting offspring (for the ancestors), ताानि those three debts, अनिस्तीर्य without fully satisfying, (गृहं) त्यजन् giving up household life, पतेत् falls down spiritually.

Simple Meaning: O Vasudeva, every brahmana is born into this world bound by three inherent debts—those owed to the devas, to the sages, and to the ancestors. If one attempts to renounce household life without clearing these debts through the performance of sacrifices, the study of the Vedas, and the procreation of righteous offspring, one suffers a spiritual downfall.

त्वं त्वद्य मुक्तो द्वाभ्यां वै ऋषिपित्रोर्महामते ।
यज्ञैर्देवर्णमुन्मुच्य निर्-ऋणोऽशरणो भव ॥10.84.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वम् you, तु indeed, अद्य today, मुक्तः freed, द्वाभ्याम् from both, वै indeed, ऋषि-पित्रोः from the debts to the sages and the ancestors, महा-मते O noble-minded one, यज्ञैः through sacrifices, देव-ऋणम् the debt to the devas, उन्मुच्य having discharged, निः-ऋणः free from all debts, अशरणः free from worldly shelter/surrendered entirely to the Supreme, भव become/remain

Anvaya: महामते! त्वं तु अद्य ऋषिपित्रोः द्वाभ्यां वै मुक्तः (असि); यज्ञैः देवऋणम् उन्मुच्य निःऋणः (सन्) अशरणः भव।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महामते O noble-minded Vasudeva, त्वं तु indeed you, अद्य today, ऋषिपित्रोः द्वाभ्यां from the two debts owed to the sages and the ancestors, वै certainly मुक्तः are already freed; (therefore) यज्ञैः through your sacred sacrifices, देवऋणम् the debt owed to the devas, उन्मुच्य having fully discharged, निःऋणः free from all material debts, अशरणः detached from all worldly shelter and completely surrendered unto the Lord, भव remain.

Simple Meaning: O noble-minded Vasudeva, you are already freed from the debts owed to the sages and the ancestors. Simply discharge your debt to the devas through proper sacrifices, and thus becoming entirely free from all debts and worldly dependencies, remain completely surrendered unto the Supreme Lord.

वसुदेव भवान् नूनं भक्त्या परमया हरिम् ।
जगतामीश्वरं प्रार्चः स यद् वां पुत्रतां गतः ॥10.84.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: वसुदेव O Vasudeva, भवान् you, नूनम् certainly, भक्त्या with devotion, परमया supreme, हरिम् Lord Hari, जगताम् of the entire universe, ईश्वरम् the Supreme Controller, प्रार्चः worshipped, सः He, यत् because, वाम् of both of you (Vasudeva and Devaki), पुत्रताम् the state of being a son, गतः has attained

Anvaya: वसुदेव! भवान् नूनं परमया भक्त्या जगताम् ईश्वरं हरिं प्रार्चः, यत् सः वां पुत्रतां गतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वसुदेव O Vasudeva, भवान् you, नूनं certainly, परमया भक्त्या with transcendental and supreme devotion, जगताम् ईश्वरं हरिं the Supreme Master of all worlds - Lord Hari, प्रार्चः have devoutly worshiped, यत् because, सः He (that Supreme Lord), वां of you two (yourself and Devaki), पुत्रतां the status of a son, गतः has assumed/attained.

Simple Meaning: O Vasudeva, you must have surely worshiped Lord Hari, the Supreme Controller of the entire universe, with extraordinary and transcendental devotion in past lives—for He Himself has now appeared as your very son.

वसुदेव उवाच

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वसुदेव उवाच Vasudeva said, भ्रातः O brother, नृणां among human beings, यः ईशकृतः which is created by the supreme controller, स्नेहसंज्ञितः पाशः the bond known as affection, तं शूराणां योगिनाम् अपि दुस्त्यजम् that bond even for great heroes and yogis hard to break, (इति) अहं मन्ये I consider.

Simple Meaning: Vasudeva said: My dear brother, I consider this bond of affection created by the Supreme Lord among human beings to be extremely difficult to break, even for great heroes and elevated yogis.

अस्मास्वप्रतिकल्पेयं यत् कृताज्ञेषु सत्तमैः ।

Anvaya: सत्तमैः कृताज्ञेषु अस्मासु इयं यत् अप्रतिकल्पा मैत्री अर्पिता अफला वा अपि कर्हिचित् न निवर्तेत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सत्तमैः By the most saintly personalities, अस्मासु कृताज्ञेषु towards us who are ungrateful, इयं अप्रतिकल्पा मैत्री यत् अर्पिता this matchless and incomparable friendship that is offered, अफला वा अपि even if it appears to be without fruit, कर्हिचित् न निवर्तेत never comes to an end at any time.

Simple Meaning: The matchless friendship that saintly souls like you have extended to ungrateful persons like us never diminishes or comes to an end at any time, even if it appears to bring no returned benefit.

प्रागकल्पाच्च कुशलं भ्रातर्वो नाचराम हि ।

Word-by-word meaning: प्राक् before, अकल्पात् being incapable/unable, च and, कुशलम् good service/welfare, भ्रातः O brother, वः to you, न not, आचराम performed/did, हि indeed, अधुना now, श्री-मद-अन्ध-अक्षाः eyes blinded by the intoxication of wealth/opulence, न not, पश्यामः we see, पुरः standing in front of us, सतः saintly persons like you

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भ्रातः O brother Nanda, प्राक् अकल्पात् previously due to being helpless and incapable, वः कुशलं न हि आचराम we could not perform any good service or welfare for you. अधुना श्रीमदान्धाक्षाः Now our eyes being blinded by the intoxication of opulence and high status, पुरः सतः even when saintly personalities like you are present right in front of us, न पश्यामः we fail to truly see or honor you.

Simple Meaning: O dear brother, previously due to our helplessness we were unable to do anything good for you. And now, blinded by the pride of opulence and high status, we fail to honor noble saintly souls like you even when you stand directly before us.

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तीर्थयात्रायां During the sacred pilgrimage to Kurukshetra, सुहृत्सन्दर्शनादिकं the joyful reunions with dear friends and relatives, यत् आसीत् which occurred, यदुदेवमहोत्सवं that magnificent grand festival of Vasudeva, जनेभ्यः कथयाञ्चक्रुः they narrated to all the citizens.

Simple Meaning: Upon returning to Dwaraka, they narrated to the citizens, who had stayed behind, all the events of the grand festival of Vasudeva that took place during the pilgrimage, including the joyful reunions with their friends and relatives.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “ऋषिणाम् उपदेशः वसुदेवस्य यज्ञः च​” नाम चतुरशीतितमः अध्यायः ॥84॥

Thus ends the eighty-fourth discourse named “The teachings of the sages; Vasudeva conducts a grand sacrifice”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
