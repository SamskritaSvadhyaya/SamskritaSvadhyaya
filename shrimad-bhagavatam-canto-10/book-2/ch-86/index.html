Chapter 86

Arjuna carries away Subhadra. Krishna’s grace upon Shrutadeva and Bahulashva

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

अर्जुनेन सुभद्राहरणं, श्रुतदेवबहुलाश्वयोः भगवतः कृपा नाम षडशीतितमः अध्यायः

(59 Shlokas)

राजा उवाच
ब्रह्मन् वेदितुमिच्छामः स्वसारं रामकृष्णयोः ।
यथोपयेमे विजयो या ममासीत् पितामही ॥10.86.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजा उवाच King Parikshit said, ब्रह्मन् O brahmana (Shri Shuka), वेदितुम् इच्छामः we wish to know, स्वसारम् the sister, रामकृष्णयोः of Balarama and Krishna, यथा how, उपयेमे married, विजयः Vijaya (Arjuna), या who, मम my, आसीत् was, पितामही paternal grandmother

Anvaya: राजा उवाच – ब्रह्मन्! रामकृष्णयोः स्वसारं या मम पितामही आसीत्, (तां) विजयः यथा उपयेमे (तत्) वेदितुम् इच्छामः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजा उवाच King Parikshit said, ब्रह्मन् O holy sage, रामकृष्णयोः Of Balarama and Krishna, स्वसारं sister (Subhadra), या मम पितामही आसीत् who was my paternal grandmother, (तां) her, विजयः Arjuna, यथा in what manner, उपयेमे wedded, (तत्) that, वेदितुम् इच्छामः we desire to learn.

Simple Meaning: King Parikshit said: O holy brahmana, I wish to know how Arjuna married Subhadra, the sister of Krishna and Balarama, who was my paternal grandmother.

श्री शुक उवाच
अर्जुनस्तीर्थयात्रायां पर्यटन्नवनीं प्रभुः ।
गतः प्रभासमशृणोन्मातुलेयीं स आत्मनः ॥10.86.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अर्जुनः Arjuna, तीर्थ-यात्रायाम् on a pilgrimage to holy places, पर्यटन् wandering/traveling, अवनीम् over the earth, प्रभुः the powerful lord, गतः arrived at, प्रभासम् Prabhasa, अशृणोत् heard about, मातुलेयीम् his maternal uncle's daughter (Subhadra), सः he, आत्मनः his own

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच — प्रभुः अर्जुनः अवनीं पर्यटन् तीर्थयात्रायां प्रभासं गतः। सः आत्मनः मातुलेयीम् अशृणोत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, प्रभुः the mighty hero, अर्जुनः that Arjuna, अवनीं over the earth, पर्यटन् traveling, तीर्थयात्रायां during his pilgrimage, प्रभासं to Prabhasa, गतः had gone. सः He, आत्मनः मातुलेयीम् his maternal uncle's daughter Subhadra, अशृणोत् heard about.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: While traveling across the earth on a holy pilgrimage, the mighty hero Arjuna arrived at Prabhasa-kshetra. There he heard about Subhadra, his maternal uncle Vasudeva's daughter.

दुर्योधनाय रामस्तां दास्यतीति न चापरे ।
तल्लिप्सुः स यतिर्भूत्वा त्रिदण्डी द्वारकामगात् ॥10.86.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुर्योधनाय unto Duryodhana, रामः Balarama, ताम् her (Subhadra), दास्यति intends to give, इति thus, न not, च and, अपरे others, तत्-लिप्सुः desiring to obtain her, सः he (Arjuna), यतिः a sannyasi/ascetic, भूत्वा having become, त्रिदण्डी carrying a triple staff, द्वारकाम् to Dwaraka, अगात् went

Anvaya: रामः तां दुर्योधनाय दास्यति अपरे च न। सः तल्लिप्सुः त्रिदण्डी यतिः भूत्वा द्वारकाम् अगात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रामः Balarama, तां her (Subhadra), दुर्योधनाय unto Duryodhana, दास्यति wished to give in marriage, अपरे च न and not any other. सः He (Arjuna), तल्लिप्सुः desiring to win her hand, त्रिदण्डी यतिः an ascetic, carrying a triple staff, भूत्वा having disguised himself as, द्वारकाम् to the city of Dwaraka, अगात् went.

Simple Meaning: Arjuna heard that Balarama intended to give Subhadra in marriage to Duryodhana, and not to anyone else. Desiring to obtain her as his wife, Arjuna disguised himself as a sannyasi carrying a triple staff and traveled to Dwaraka.

तत्र वै वार्षिकान् मासानवात्सीत् स्वार्थसाधकः ।
पौरैः सभाजितोऽभीक्ष्णं रामेणाजानता च सः ॥10.86.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there (in Dwaraka), वै indeed, वार्षिकान् during the rainy season, मासान् months, अवात्सीत् he resided, स्व-अर्थ-साधकः accomplishing his self-interest, पौरैः by the citizens of Dwaraka, सभाजितः honored/worshipped, अभीक्ष्णम् constantly, रामेण by Balarama, अजानता who did not know (his true identity), च and, सः he (Arjuna)

Anvaya: स्वार्थसाधकः सः तत्र वै वार्षिकान् मासान् अवात्सीत्। अजानता रामेण पौरैः च अभीक्ष्णं सभाजितः (अभवत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: स्वार्थसाधकः Focused on securing his desire, सः he (Arjuna), तत्र in Dwaraka, वै indeed, वार्षिकान् मासान् through the four rainy months, अवात्सीत् resided. पौरैः By the citizens, अजानता रामेण च and by Balarama who did not know his real identity, अभीक्ष्णं सभाजितः very well, was honored.

Simple Meaning: Intent on achieving his goal, Arjuna stayed in Dvaraka throughout the four months of the rainy season. Disguised as an ascetic, he was honored by the citizens as well as by Balarama, who did not recognize his true identity.

एकदा गृहमानीय आतिथ्येन निमन्त्र्य तम् ।
श्रद्धयोपहृतं भैक्ष्यं बलेन बुभुजे किल ॥10.86.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: एकदा once, गृहम् to his home, आनीय having brought, आतिथ्येन with hospitality, निमन्त्र्य inviting, तम् him (Arjuna in disguise), श्रद्धया with great reverence, उपहृतम् offered, भैक्ष्यम् alms/food, बलेन by Balarama, बुभुजे he ate, किल indeed

Anvaya: एकदा बलेन आतिथ्येन तं निमन्त्र्य गृहम् आनीय श्रद्धया उपहृतं भैक्ष्यं बुभुजे किल।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एकदा On one occasion, बलेन by Balarama, आतिथ्येन with hospitable honors, तं निमन्त्र्य inviting him, गृहम् आनीय to his palace, having brought, श्रद्धया with devotion, उपहृतं served, भैक्ष्यं the sanctified food, बुभुजे was partaken (by Arjuna), किल indeed.

Simple Meaning: One day, Lord Balarama invited the disguised ascetic to His palace, received him with full hospitality, and served him alms with great reverence. Arjuna ate the food offered to him by Balarama.

सोऽपश्यत्तत्र महतीं कन्यां वीरमनोहराम् ।
प्रीत्युत्फुल्लेक्षणस्तस्यां भावक्षुब्धं मनो दधे ॥10.86.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Arjuna), अपश्यत् saw, तत्र there (in the palace of Balarama), महतीम् charming/splendid, कन्याम् maiden (Subhadra), वीर-मनो-हराम् captivating the hearts of heroes, प्रीति-उत्फुल्ल-ईक्षणः his eyes wide open with love and delight, तस्याम् upon her, भाव-क्षुब्धम् agitated with emotion, मनः his mind, दधे fixed

Anvaya: सः तत्र महतीं वीरमनोहरां कन्याम् अपश्यत्, (ततः) प्रीत्युत्फुल्लेक्षणः तस्यां भावक्षुब्धं मनः दधे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He (Arjuna), तत्र there, महतीं very beautiful, वीरमनोहरां capable of stealing the minds of heroes, कन्याम् maiden Subhadra, अपश्यत् beheld. (ततः Then) प्रीत्युत्फुल्लेक्षणः his eyes wide with affection, तस्यां upon her, भावक्षुब्धं stirred up with emotion, मनः his mind, दधे he centered.

Simple Meaning: While dining in the palace, Arjuna saw the enchanting young princess Subhadra, who was so charming that she could captivate the mind of any great hero. His eyes expanded with delight upon seeing her, and his heart became deeply agitated with love for her.

सापि तं चकमे वीक्ष्य नारीणां हृदयङ्गमम् ।
हसन्ती व्रीडितापाङ्गी तन्न्यस्तहृदयेक्षणा ॥10.86.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Subhadra), अपि also, तम् him (Arjuna), चकमे desired/fell in love with, वीक्ष्य seeing, नारीणाम् of women, हृदयम्-गमम् one who enters the hearts, हसन्ती smiling, व्रीडित-अपाङ्गी with shy sidelong glances, तत्-न्यस्त-हृदय-ईक्षणा her heart and eyes fixed upon him

Anvaya: नारीणां हृदयङ्गमं तं वीक्ष्य सा अपि (तं) चकमे, हसन्ती व्रीडितापाङ्गी तन्न्यस्तहृदयेक्षणा (अभवत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नारीणां हृदयङ्गमं Capable of enchanting the hearts of all women, तं him (Arjuna), वीक्ष्य having seen, सा अपि she also, (तं) him, चकमे desired. हसन्ती Smiling gently, व्रीडितापाङ्गी casting shy sidelong glances, तन्न्यस्तहृदयेक्षणा her mind and gaze entirely anchored upon him, (अभवत्) she became).

Simple Meaning: Seeing Arjuna, who naturally captivates the heart of any woman, Subhadra also fell deeply in love with him. Smiling gently, she cast shy sidelong glances toward him, her heart and eyes entirely fixed upon him.

तां परं समनुध्यायन्नन्तरं प्रेप्सुरर्जुनः ।
न लेभे शं भ्रमच्चित्तः कामेनातिबलीयसा ॥10.86.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her (Subhadra), परम् constantly/intensely, समनुध्यायन् thinking, अन्तरम् an opportunity, प्रेप्सुः seeking to obtain, अर्जुनः Arjuna, न not, लेभे obtained, शम् peace of mind, भ्रमत्-चित्तः whose mind was wandering/restless, कामेन by love/desire, अति-बलीयसा extremely powerful

Anvaya: तां परं समनुध्यायन् अन्तरं प्रेप्सुः अर्जुनः अतिबलीयसा कामेन भ्रमच्चित्तः (सन्) शं न लेभे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तां Upon her, परं समनुध्यायन् deeply thinking, अन्तरं an opportunity, प्रेप्सुः seeking, अर्जुनः Arjuna, अतिबलीयसा कामेन by extremely strong desire, भ्रमच्चित्तः (सन्) his mind becoming agitated, शं न लेभे peace, did not obtain.

Simple Meaning: Constantly meditating upon Subhadra and eagerly looking for an opportunity to secure her, Arjuna could find no peace of mind, as his heart was intensely agitated by powerful love for her.

महत्यां देवयात्रायां रथस्थां दुर्गनिर्गताम् ।
जहारानुमतः पित्रोः कृष्णस्य च महारथः ॥10.86.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: महत्याम् during a grand, देव-यात्रायाम् festival procession of the deity, रथ-स्थाम् seated on a chariot, दुर्ग-निर्गताम् coming out of the fortress, जहार seized/carried away, अनुमतः permitted/approved, पित्रोः by her parents (Vasudeva and Devaki), कृष्णस्य of Lord Krishna, च and, महा-रथः the great chariot warrior (Arjuna)

Anvaya: महारथः (अर्जुनः) पित्रोः कृष्णस्य च अनुमतः महत्यां देवयात्रायां दुर्गनिर्गतां रथस्थां (तां) जहार।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महारथः The great chariot warrior Arjuna, पित्रोः of her parents (Vasudeva and Devaki), कृष्णस्य of Krishna, च and, अनुमतः having received the consent, महत्यां देवयात्रायां in the great festival of the deity, दुर्गनिर्गतां coming out from the fort, रथस्थां standing upon her chariot, (तां) her (Subhadra), जहार carried off.

Simple Meaning: During a grand festival procession, as Subhadra emerged from the fortress riding on a chariot, the great warrior Arjuna carried her away with prior approval of her parents and Krishna.

Note:

अथ काल उपावृत्ते देवकी सर्वदेवता ।

पुत्रान् प्रसुषुवे चाष्टौ कन्यां चैवानुवत्सरम् ॥10.1.56॥

Devaki gave birth to eight sons and a daughter.

रथस्थो धनुरादाय शूरांश्चारुन्धतो भटान् ।
विद्राव्य क्रोशतां स्वानां स्वभागं मृगराडिव ॥10.86.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: रथ-स्थः standing on the chariot, धनुः his bow, आदाय taking up, शूरान् brave, च and, आरुन्धतः who tried to stop him, भटान् guards/warriors, विद्राव्य driving away/chasing off, क्रोशम् कुर्वताम् while crying out/protesting, स्वानाम् of her relatives, स्व-भागम् his own share, मृग-राट् इव like a lion

Anvaya: (सः) रथस्थः धनुः आदाय आरुन्धतः शूरान् भटान् विद्राव्य स्वानां क्रोशतां (सतां) मृगराट् स्वभागम् इव (तां जहार)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सः He), रथस्थः standing upon his chariot, धनुः bow, आदाय taking, आरुन्धतः who attempted to block his way, शूरान् भटान् valiant guards, विद्राव्य routing, स्वानां of her kinsmen, क्रोशतां while they cried out in protest, मृगराट् a lion, स्वभागम् his rightful prey, इव just as, (तां जहार carried her away).

Simple Meaning: Standing boldly on his chariot, Arjuna picked up his bow and routed the valiant guards who tried to block his way. Just as a mighty lion seizes his prey right before other animals, Arjuna took Subhadra away while her kinsmen cried out in protest.

तच्छ्रुत्वा क्षुभितो रामः पर्वणीव महार्णवः ।
गृहीतपादः कृष्णेन सुहृद्भिश्चान्वशाम्यत ॥10.86.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that (event), श्रुत्वा hearing, क्षुभितः agitated/enraged, रामः Balarama, पर्वणि on the full-moon/new-moon day, इव like, महा-अर्णवः the great ocean, गृहीत-पादः whose feet were held, कृष्णेन by Krishna, सुहृद्भिः by His relatives/well-wishers, च and, अन्वशाम्यत was pacified

Anvaya: तत् श्रुत्वा पर्वणि महार्णवः इव क्षुभितः रामः कृष्णेन सुहृद्भिः च गृहीतपादः (सन्) अन्वशाम्यत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत् That abduction, श्रुत्वा having heard, पर्वणि on the full moon or new moon day, महार्णवः the great ocean, इव just like, क्षुभितः agitated, रामः Balarama, कृष्णेन by Krishna, सुहृद्भिः by other relatives, च and, गृहीतपादः (सन्) His feet being grasped in entreaty, अन्वशाम्यत was gradually pacified.

Simple Meaning: Upon hearing of the abduction, Balarama became as furious and agitated as the ocean churning on a full-moon day. However, when Krishna and other family well-wishers clasped His feet and reasoned with Him, He was pacified.

प्राहिणोत् पारिबर्हाणि वरवध्वोर्मुदा बलः ।
महाधनोपस्करेभरथाश्वनरयोषितः ॥10.86.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्राहिणोत् sent, पारिबर्हाणि wedding gifts/bridal presents, वर-वध्वोः for the bridegroom and bride (Arjuna and Subhadra), मुदा with joy, बलः Balarama, महा-धन-उपस्कर-इभ-रथ-अश्व-नर-योषितः consisting of immense wealth, costly furniture, elephants, chariots, horses, male servants, and maidservants

Anvaya: बलः मुदा वरवध्वोः महाधनोपस्करेभरथाश्वनरयोषितः पारिबर्हाणि प्राहिणोत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बलः Balarama, मुदा joyfully, वरवध्वोः for the newly married couple, महाधनोपस्करेभरथाश्वनरयोषितः containing immense riches, household furnishings, elephants, chariots, horses, manservants, and maidservants, पारिबर्हाणि sumptuous wedding gifts, प्राहिणोत् dispatched.

Simple Meaning: Having been fully pacified, Balarama joyfully sent lavish wedding gifts to the newly married couple, Arjuna and Subhadra. The present included vast treasures, valuable furnishings, elephants, chariots, horses, along with male and female servants.

श्री शुक उवाच
कृष्णस्यासीद् द्विजश्रेष्ठः श्रुतदेव इति श्रुतः ।
कृष्णैकभक्त्या पूर्णार्थः शान्तः कविरलम्पटः ॥10.86.13॥

एतदन्तो नृणां क्लेशो यद् भवानक्षिगोचरः ॥10.86.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः He/that same Lord, त्वम् You, शाधि please instruct/command, स्व-भृत्यान् Your own servants, नः us, किम् what, देव O Lord, करवामहे shall we do, एतत्-अन्तः ending with this, नृणाम् of human beings, क्लेशः material distress/suffering, यत् that, भवान् You, अक्षि-गोचरः visible before the eyes

Anvaya: देव! सः त्वं नः स्वभृत्यान् शाधि, किं करवामहे। यत् भवान् अक्षिगोचरः (जातः), नृणां क्लेशः एतदन्तः (भवति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: देव O Lord, सः that very person, त्वं You, नः us, स्वभृत्यान् Your own servants, शाधि please instruct, किं करवामहे what service we may perform. यत् Because, भवान् You, अक्षिगोचरः visible before our eyes, (जातः) has become, नृणां of mankind, क्लेशः suffering and material distress, एतदन्तः comes to an end right here, (भवति) it becomes.

Simple Meaning: O Lord, You are that very Supreme Person. Please command us, Your humble servants—what service may we render unto You? Indeed, when You become directly visible before one's eyes, all the material miseries of human life come to an end.

श्री शुक उवाच
तदुक्तमित्युपकर्ण्य भगवान् प्रणतार्तिहा ।
गृहीत्वा पाणिना पाणिं प्रहसंस्तमुवाच ह ॥10.86.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, तत्-उक्तम् these words spoken (by Shrutadeva), इति thus, उपकर्ण्य hearing, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, प्रणत-आर्ति-हा the destroyer of the distress of those who surrender to Him, गृहीत्वा taking, पाणिना with His hand, पाणिम् his hand, प्रहसन् smiling gently, तम् unto him (Shrutadeva), उवाच spoke, ह indeed

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच—प्रणतार्तिहा भगवान् इति तदुक्तम् उपकर्ण्य प्रहसन् पाणिना (तस्य) पाणिं गृहीत्वा तम् उवाच ह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said: प्रणतार्तिहा the remover of the suffering of surrendered souls, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, इति thus, तदुक्तम् those words of prayer, उपकर्ण्य having heard, प्रहसन् smiling, पाणिना with His hand, (तस्य) Shrutadeva's, पाणिं hand, गृहीत्वा taking hold of, तम् unto him, उवाच spoke, ह indeed.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Hearing these heartfelt prayers, Lord Krishna, who removes all distress from those who surrender unto Him, smiled gently. Taking Shrutadeva’s hand in His own, the Lord addressed him.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
ब्रह्मंस्तेऽनुग्रहार्थाय सम्प्राप्तान् विद्धि अमून् मुनीन् ।
सञ्चरन्ति मया लोकान् पुनन्तः पादरेणुभिः ॥10.86.51॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, ब्रह्मन् O learned brahmana, ते for your, अनुग्रह-अर्थाय for the purpose of bestowing grace/blessing, सम्प्राप्तान् arrived, विद्धि know, अमून् these, मुनीन् sages, सञ्चरन्ति they travel, मया with Me, लोकान् the worlds, पुनन्तः purifying, पाद-रेणुभिः by the dust of their feet

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच—ब्रह्मन्! ते अनुग्रहार्थाय सम्प्राप्तान् अमून् मुनीन् विद्धि, (एते) पादरेणुभिः लोकान् पुनन्तः मया (सह) सञ्चरन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said: ब्रह्मन् O brahmana, ते for your, अनुग्रहार्थाय bestowing of grace, सम्प्राप्तान् arrived here, अमून् मुनीन् these sages, विद्धि know, (एते) these sages, पादरेणुभिः by the dust of their feet, लोकान् all the worlds, पुनन्तः purifying, मया (सह) along with Me, सञ्चरन्ति travel.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord said: O holy brahmana, know that these great sages have arrived here to bestow their grace upon you. They travel throughout all the worlds together with Me, purifying all places by the dust of their feet.

देवाः क्षेत्राणि तीर्थानि दर्शनस्पर्शनार्चनैः ।
शनैः पुनन्ति कालेन तदप्यर्हत्तमेक्षया ॥10.86.52॥

Word-by-word meaning: देवाः the devas, क्षेत्राणि holy places/pilgrimage sites, तीर्थानि sacred bathing places, दर्शन-स्पर्शन-अर्चनैः by sight, touch, and worship, शनैः gradually, पुनन्ति purify, कालेन in course of time, तत्-अपि the same (purification), अर्हत्तम-ईक्षया by the glance/sight of the greatest of saints

Anvaya: देवाः क्षेत्राणि तीर्थानि दर्शनस्पर्शनार्चनैः कालेन शनैः पुनन्ति, तदपि अर्हत्तमेक्षया (पुनन्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: देवाः The celestial beings, क्षेत्राणि holy abodes, तीर्थानि and sacred rivers or pilgrimage sites, दर्शनस्पर्शनार्चनैः through being seen, touched, and worshipped, कालेन over a long period of time, शनैः gradually, पुनन्ति purify (those who visit them), तदपि yet the same purification, अर्हत्तमेक्षया by the mere glance of the exalted sages, (is obtained).

Simple Meaning: Celestial beings, holy places, and sacred rivers gradually purify those who see, touch, or worship them over a long period of time. However, the purification is obtained simply by the glance of the exalted sages.

ब्राह्मणो जन्मना श्रेयान् सर्वेषां प्राणिनामिह ।
तपसा विद्यया तुष्ट्या किमु मत्कलया युतः ॥10.86.53॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्राह्मणः a brahmana, जन्मना by birth, श्रेयान् superior/best, सर्वेषाम् of all, प्राणिनाम् living beings, इह in this world, तपसा by austerity, विद्यया by knowledge, तुष्ट्या by contentment, किम् उत् what to speak of when, मत्-कलया with My divine potency/devotion to Me, युतः endowed

Anvaya: इह जन्मना ब्राह्मणः सर्वेषां प्राणिनां श्रेयान् (भवति)। तपसा विद्यया तुष्ट्या मत्कलया युतः (ब्राह्मणः) किमु।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इह In this world, जन्मना ब्राह्मणः by birth, a brahmana, सर्वेषां प्राणिनां of all living entities, श्रेयान् (भवति) is superior. तपसा With austerity, विद्यया with Vedic learning, तुष्ट्या with contentment, मत्कलया and with devotion to Me, युतः endowed, (ब्राह्मणः) a brahmana, किमु what more to speak of then.

Simple Meaning: In this world, a brahmana is superior by birth among all living entities. What then to speak of a brahmana who is further endowed with austerity, spiritual knowledge, inner contentment, and devotion to Me?

न ब्राह्मणान्मे दयितं रूपमेतच्चतुर्भुजम् ।
सर्ववेदमयो विप्रः सर्वदेवमयो ह्यहम् ॥10.86.54॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, ब्राह्मणात् than a brahmana, मे by Me, दयितम् dearer, रूपम् form, एतत् this, चतुर्भुजम् four-armed, सर्व-वेद-मयः consisting of all Vedas, विप्रः a brahmana, सर्व-देव-मयः consisting of all the deities, हि indeed, अहम् I

Anvaya: चतुर्भुजम् एतत् मे रूपं ब्राह्मणात् न दयितम्। विप्रः सर्ववेदमयः, अहं सर्वदेवमयः हि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: चतुर्भुजम् एतत् This four-armed, मे रूपं My form, ब्राह्मणात् than a brahmana, न दयितम् not dearer. विप्रः सर्ववेदमयः A brahmana embodies all the Vedas, अहं सर्वदेवमयः I embody all the devas, हि indeed.

Simple Meaning: Even My four-armed form is not dearer to Me than a brahmana. A brahmana embodies all the Vedas, and I Myself embody all the devas.

दुष्प्रज्ञाः अविदित्वैवमवजानन्त्यसूयवः ।
गुरुं मां विप्रमात्मानमर्चादाविज्यदृष्टयः ॥10.86.55॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुः-प्रज्ञाः foolish/destitute of true wisdom, अविदित्वा not knowing, एवम् thus, अवजानन्ति they disrespect/despise (Me), असूयवः envious, गुरुम् the spiritual master, माम् Me, विप्रम् as an ordinary brahmana, आत्मानम् the Supreme Soul, अर्चा-आदौ in idol and other, इज्य-दृष्टयः whose vision is restricted to the worship

असूयवः - असूयु पुल्लिंग​ 1.3

अर्चा (स्त्रीलिङ्गः) प्रतिमा src. Kosha. Ashtadhyayi

Anvaya: दुष्प्रज्ञाः असूयवः (जनाः) एवम् अविदित्वा अर्चादौ इज्यदृष्टयः (सन्तः) गुरुं विप्रम् आत्मानं माम् अवजानन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दुष्प्रज्ञाः Lacking true spiritual intelligence, असूयवः envious by nature, (people), एवम् in this manner, अविदित्वा without understanding My real identity, अर्चादौ इज्यदृष्टयः whose perception is bound entirely to material worship and external rituals, गुरुं the universal preceptor, विप्रम् as a mere brahmana, आत्मानं the Supreme Soul and indwelling Controller of all, माम् Me, अवजानन्ति foolishly disrespect.

Simple Meaning: Foolish and envious people, whose vision is restricted only to external rituals and worship, fail to recognize My true nature. Not knowing that I am the Supreme Soul, the universal preceptor, and the ultimate Lord, they disrespect Me.

चराचरमिदं विश्वं भावा ये चास्य हेतवः ।
मद्रूपाणीति चेतस्याधत्ते विप्रो मदीक्षया ॥10.86.56॥

Word-by-word meaning: चर-अचरम् moving and non-moving, इदम् this, विश्वम् universe, भावाः objects/states of being, ये which, च and, अस्य of this (universe), हेतवः the causes, मत्-रूपाणि My forms, इति thus, चेतसि in the mind, आधत्ते fixes/contemplates, विप्रः the brahmana (Shrutadeva), मत्-ईक्षया through seeing Me (or by My grace)

Anvaya: चराचरम् इदं विश्वं ये च अस्य हेतवः भावाः, (तत् सर्वे) मद्रूपाणि इति विप्रः मदीक्षया चेतसि आधत्ते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: चराचरम् With all moving and non-moving entities, इदं विश्वं this universe, ये च and whichever, अस्य of this creation, हेतवः भावाः causes and manifestations, (तत् सर्वे) all of those, मद्रूपाणि are My personal forms, इति thus, विप्रः the brahmana Shrutadeva, मदीक्षया by direct realization of Me (or through My grace), चेतसि आधत्ते within his mind firmly holds/contemplates.

Simple Meaning: By the grace of beholding Me, the brahmana Shrutadeva firmly established in his mind that this entire universe—consisting of all moving and non-moving entities, as well as all its underlying causes and manifestations—is nothing other than My own divine form.

तस्माद् ब्रह्मऋषीनेतान् ब्रह्मन् मच्छ्रद्धयार्चय ।
एवं चेदर्चितोऽस्म्यद्धा नान्यथा भूरिभूतिभिः ॥10.86.57॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मात् therefore, ब्रह्मन् O brahmana, ब्रह्म-ऋषीन् these brahmana sages, एतान् these, मत्-श्रद्धया with faith in Me, अर्चय worship/honor, एवम् in this way, च and, अर्चितः worshipped, अस्मि I am, अद्धा truly/directly, न अन्यथा not otherwise, भूरि-भूतिभिः by opulent offerings/lavish wealth

Anvaya: तस्मात् ब्रह्मन्! एतान् ब्रह्मऋषीन् मच्छ्रद्धया अर्चय। एवम् अद्धा अर्चितः अस्मि, भूरिभूतिभिः (तु) न (तथा अर्चितः अस्मि)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तस्मात् Therefore, ब्रह्मन् O brahmana, एतान् these, ब्रह्मऋषीन् exalted brahmana sages, मच्छ्रद्धया with the same faith and devotion you would offer unto Me, अर्चय worship. एवम् In this exact manner, अद्धा truly, अर्चितः worshipped, अस्मि I am, भूरिभूतिभिः with lavish material opulences and grand presentations, (तु) न but not (so).

Simple Meaning: Therefore, O brahmana, worship these exalted sages with the exact same faith and devotion you would offer unto Me. When you honor them in this way, I consider Myself truly worshipped—far more than if someone were to worship Me with lavish material opulences alone.

श्री शुक उवाच
स इत्थं प्रभुणाऽऽदिष्टः सहकृष्णान् द्विजोत्तमान् ।
आराध्यैकात्मभावेन मैथिलश्चाप सद्गतिम् ॥10.86.58॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, सः he (Shrutadeva), इत्थम् in this manner, प्रभुणा by the Lord, आदिष्टः instructed, सह-कृष्णान् along with Lord Krishna, द्विज-उत्तमान् the best of the twice-born (the brahmana sages), आराध्य having worshipped, एक-आत्म-भावेन with single-minded devotion, मैथिलः the King of Mithila (Bahulashva), च and, आप attained, सत्-गतिम् the supreme destination

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच—प्रभुणा इत्थम् आदिष्टः सः (श्रुतदेवः) सहकृष्णान् द्विजोत्तमान् एकात्मभावेन आराध्य, मैथिलः च सद्गतिम् आप।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said:, प्रभुणा by the Supreme Lord, इत्थम् आदिष्टः thus instructed, सः he (Shrutadeva), सहकृष्णान् along with Krishna, द्विजोत्तमान् those best among brahmanas, एकात्मभावेन with single-minded absolute devotion, आराध्य having fully worshipped, मैथिलः King Bahulashva of Mithila, च and, सद्गतिम् the ultimate spiritual destination, आप attained.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Having been thus instructed by the Lord, Shrutadeva worshipped the exalted brahmana sages together with Lord Krishna with single-minded devotion. Likewise, King Bahulashva of Mithila rendered the same pure devotion, and both of them ultimately attained the supreme spiritual destination.

एवं स्वभक्तयोः राजन् भगवान् भक्त भक्तिमान् ।
उषित्वाऽऽदिष्य सन्मार्गं पुनर्द्वारवतीमगात् ॥10.86.59॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, स्व-भक्तयोः of His two devotees (Shrutadeva and Bahulasva), राजन् O King (Parikshit), भगवान् the Supreme Lord, भक्तः devoted to His devotees, भक्तिमान् reciprocates devotion/is won over by devotion, उषित्वा having stayed, आदिष्य having instructed, सत्-मार्गम् the righteous path, पुनः back, द्वारवतीम् to Dwaraka, अगात् went

Anvaya: राजन्! भगवान् भक्तभक्तिमान् स्वभक्तयोः एवम् उषित्वा सत्मार्गम् आदिष्य पुनः द्वारवतीम् अगात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King Parikshit, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, भक्तभक्तिमान् who is deeply affectionate toward His devotees and easily bound by their devotion, स्वभक्तयोः toward His two devoted servants, एवम् in this manner, उषित्वा having resided for some time, सत्मार्गं the path of absolute truth and righteousness, आदिष्य having instructed them, पुनः back, द्वारवतीम् to the city of Dwaraka, अगात् returned.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, the Supreme Lord, who is completely conquered by the love of His devotees, stayed with His two faithful servants for some time. After guiding them in the path of pure devotion and righteousness, He returned to Dwaraka.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “अर्जुनेन सुभद्राहरणं, श्रुतदेवबहुलाश्वयोः भगवतः कृपा” नाम षडशीतितमः अध्यायः ॥86॥

Thus ends the eighty-sixth discourse named “Arjuna carries away Subhadra. Krishna’s grace upon Shrutadeva and Bahulashva”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
