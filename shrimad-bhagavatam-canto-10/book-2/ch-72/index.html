श्री शुक उवाच
एकदा तु सभामध्ये आस्थितो मुनिभिर्वृतः ।
ब्राह्मणैः क्षत्रियैर्वैश्यैर्भ्रातृभिश्च युधिष्ठिरः ॥10.72.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, एकदा once, तु indeed, सभा-मध्ये in the middle of the assembly hall, आस्थितः seated, मुनिभिः by sages, वृतः surrounded, ब्राह्मणैः by brahmanas, क्षत्रियैः by kshatriyas, वैश्यैः by vaishyas, भ्रातृभिः by his brothers, च and, युधिष्ठिरः King Yudhishthira

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— एकदा तु युधिष्ठिरः सभामध्ये मुनिभिः ब्राह्मणैः क्षत्रियैः वैश्यैः भ्रातृभिः च वृतः आस्थितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said: एकदा तु On one occasion, युधिष्ठिरः King Yudhishthira, सभामध्ये in the royal assembly hall, मुनिभिः ब्राह्मणैः क्षत्रियैः वैश्यैः भ्रातृभिः च वृतः  आस्थितः sat surrounded by prominent sages, learned brahmanas, noble kshatriyas, vaishyas, and his own brothers.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Once, King Yudhishthira was seated in the royal assembly hall, surrounded by great sages, learned brahmanas, noble warriors, merchants, and his brothers.

आचार्यै कुलवृद्धैश्च ज्ञातिसम्बन्धिबान्धवैः ।
शृण्वतामेव चैतेषामाभाष्येदमुवाच ह ॥10.72.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: आचार्यैः by preceptors/teachers, कुल-वृद्धैः by elders of the family/dynasty, च and, ज्ञाति-सम्बन्धि-बान्धवैः by kinsmen, relatives and friends, शृण्वताम् listening, एव indeed, च and, एतेषाम् while all these were, आभाष्य addressing (Krishna), इदम् these words, उवाच ह (King Yudhishthira) spoke

Anvaya: आचार्यैः कुलवृद्धैः ज्ञातिसम्बन्धिबान्धवैः च एतेषां शृण्वतां च एव (युधिष्ठिरः कृष्णम्) आभाष्य इदम् उवाच ह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: आचार्यैः कुलवृद्धैः ज्ञातिसम्बन्धिबान्धवैः च While spiritual preceptors, family elders, kinsmen, relatives, and friends, एतेषां शृण्वतां च एव as well as all these members were listening attentive, युधिष्ठिरः Yudhishthira, कृष्णम् आभाष्य addressing Krishna, इदम् उवाच ह spoke these words.

Simple Meaning: In the presence of spiritual preceptors, family elders, kinsmen, friends, and close relatives—all of whom were listening attentively— Yudhishthira addressed Krishna and spoke as follows.

युधिष्ठिर उवाच
क्रतुराजेन गोविन्द राजसूयेन पावनीः ।
यक्ष्ये विभूतीर्भवतस्तत् सम्पादय नः प्रभो ॥10.72.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: युधिष्ठिरः उवाच Yudhishthira said, क्रतु-राजेन with the king among sacrifices (the Rajasuya), गोविन्द O Govinda, राजसूयेन with the Rajasuya sacrifice, पावनीः purifying/sanctifying, यक्ष्ये I wish to worship, विभूतीः the expansions/demigods who represent Your opulences, भवतः Your, तत् therefore, सम्पादय please make possible/fulfill, नः our (desire), प्रभो O Lord

Anvaya: युधिष्ठिरः उवाच— गोविन्द! प्रभो! क्रतुराजेन राजसूयेन भवतः पावनीः विभूतीः यक्ष्ये; तत् नः सम्पादय।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: युधिष्ठिरः उवाच Yudhishthira said: गोविन्द O Govinda! प्रभो O Almighty Lord! क्रतुराजेन राजसूयेन Through the Rajasuya, which is the king of all sacrifices, भवतः पावनीः विभूतीः यक्ष्ये I desire to worship Your pure divine expansions; तत् नः सम्पादय therefore, please fulfill this desire of ours.

Simple Meaning: Yudhishthira said: O Govinda! O Almighty Master! I desire to perform the grand Rajasuya sacrifice—the king of all rituals—to worship Your pure divine expansions. O Lord, please fulfill this desire of ours.

त्वत्पादुके अविरतं परि ये चरन्ति ध्यायन्त्यभद्रनशने शुचयो गृणन्ति ।
विन्दन्ति ते कमलनाभ भवापवर्गमाशासते यदि त आशिष ईश नान्ये ॥10.72.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वत्-पादुके Your foot-sandals, अविरतम् unceasingly/constantly, परि fully, ये those who, चरन्ति carry out service, ध्यायन्ति meditate upon, अभद्र-नशने which destroy all inauspiciousness, शुचयः purified souls, गृणन्ति praise/chant, विन्दन्ति obtain, ते they, कमल-नाभ O Lord whose navel is like a lotus, भव-अपवर्गम् liberation from the cycle of birth and death (samsara), आशासते they desire/ask for, यदि if, ते they, आशिषः material blessings/benedictions, ईश O Supreme Master, न not, अन्ये others (who lack devotion)

Anvaya: कमलनाभ! ईश! अभद्रनशने त्वत्पादुके ये शुचयः अविरतं परि चरन्ति, ध्यायन्ति, गृणन्ति च, ते भवापवर्गं विन्दन्ति। यदि ते आशिषः आशासते (तर्हि ताः अपि विन्दन्ति), अन्ये न।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कमलनाभ O Lotus-naveled Supreme Lord!,  ईश O Supreme Controller!, अभद्रनशने Which destroy every trace of evil and inauspiciousness, त्वत्पादुके Your divine foot-sandals, ये शुचयः those purified souls who, अविरतं परि चरन्ति constantly serve, ध्यायन्ति meditate upon, गृणन्ति च and glorify through praise— ते भवापवर्गं विन्दन्ति they surely attain complete liberation from worldly existence. यदि ते आशिषः आशासते If they (such pure devotees) ever happen to desire any material blessings, (ताः अपि विन्दन्ति) they attain those as well; अन्ये न but not others (who do not serve Your feet can never achieve true liberation or lasting success).

Simple Meaning: O Lord Kamalanabha! O Supreme Controller! Those purified souls who constantly serve, meditate upon, and chant the glories of Your lotus sandals—which destroy all inauspiciousness—easily attain liberation from the cycle of birth and death. Furthermore, if such devotees ever desire any material blessings, they naturally attain them as well, whereas non-devotees can never achieve true fulfillment.

तद् देव देव भवतश्चरणारविन्दसेवानुभावमिह पश्यतु लोक एषः ।
ये त्वां भजन्ति न भजन्त्युत वोभयेषां निष्ठां प्रदर्शय विभो कुरुसृञ्जयानाम् ॥10.72.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् therefore, देव देव O God of gods, भवतः Your, चरण-अरविन्द-सेवा-अनुभावम् the supreme glory and influence of serving Your lotus feet, इह in this world, पश्यतु may see, लोकः world/people, एषः this, ये those who, त्वाम् You, भजन्ति worship, न भजन्ति do not worship, उत and, वा or, उभयेषाम् of both, निष्ठाम् the destination/ultimate state, प्रदर्शय please show/demonstrate, विभो O All-pervading Master, कुरु-सृञ्जयानाम् of the Kurus and the Srinjayas

Anvaya: तत् देव देव! इह एषः लोकः भवतः चरणारविन्दसेवानुभावं पश्यतु। विभो! ये त्वां भजन्ति, ये उत न भजन्ति, कुरुसृञ्जयानां उभयेषां निष्ठां प्रदर्शय।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत् देव देव Therefore, O Lord of all divine beings! इह एषः लोकः Here, may all the people in this world, भवतः चरणारविन्दसेवानुभावं पश्यतु witness the extraordinary power and glory that comes from serving Your lotus feet. विभो O Almighty Master! ये त्वां भजन्ति Those who worship You, ये उत न भजन्ति and those who do not worship You, कुरुसृञ्जयानां उभयेषां of both the Kurus and the Srinjayas, निष्ठां प्रदर्शय please reveal clearly the contrasting ultimate ends reached by both.

Simple Meaning: Therefore, O God of gods, let the world witness firsthand the immense glory and power of devotional service unto Your lotus feet! O Almighty Lord, please demonstrate to everyone the distinct outcomes achieved by those Kurus and Srinjayas who worship You versus those who do not, so that the ultimate truth may be manifest to all.

न ब्रह्मणः स्वपरभेदमतिस्तव स्यात् सर्वात्मनः समदृशः स्वसुखानुभूतेः ।
संसेवतां सुरतरोरिव ते प्रसादः सेवानुरूपमुदयो न विपर्ययोऽत्र ॥10.72.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, ब्रह्मणः of the Supreme Absolute Truth, स्व-पर-भेद-मतिः discrimination between "mine" and "other", तव Your, स्यात् can be, सर्व-आत्मनः of the Soul of all beings, सम-दृशः who views everyone with equal vision, स्व-सुख-अनुभूतेः who is self-satisfied in His own inherent bliss, संसेवताम् of those who render service unto You, सुर-तरोः इव like the heavenly desire-fulfilling tree (Kalpavriksha), ते Your, प्रसादः grace/mercy, सेवा-अनुरूपम् proportional to the degree of service rendered, उदयः reward/prosperity, न not, विपर्ययः partiality/contrary result, अत्र in this matter

समदृशः - समदृश् शकारान्तः, पुंलिङ्गः 6.1

Anvaya: सर्वात्मनः समदृशः स्वसुखानुभूतेः ब्रह्मणः तव स्वपरभेदमतिः न स्यात्। संसेवतां सुरतरोः इव ते प्रसादः सेवानुरूपम् उदयः (भवति), अत्र विपर्ययः न (भवति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सर्वात्मनः Being the indwelling Supersoul of all living entities, समदृशः possessing equal vision toward all, स्वसुखानुभूतेः ब्रह्मणः तव for You, who eternally experience Your own innate spiritual bliss and who are the Supreme Absolute Truth, स्वपरभेदमतिः न स्यात् there can be no conception of "mine" vs. "another" (no partiality or bias). संसेवतां For those who serve You, सुरतरोः इव just like a heavenly desire fulfilling tree, ते प्रसादः Your mercy सेवानुरूपम् उदयः (भवति) yields results strictly in proportion to the service offered; अत्र विपर्ययः न (भवति) in this divine reciprocity, there is no arbitrary prejudice or injustice.

Simple Meaning: O Lord, You are the Supreme Absolute, the indwelling Soul of all beings, equal to all, and forever absorbed in Your own divine bliss. Therefore, You possess no discrimination between friend and foe. Like the heavenly desire fulfilling tree (Kalpavriksha), Your grace manifests precisely according to the degree of service rendered by each person. You reward everyone proportionately, without any trace of bias or arbitrary favor.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
सम्यग् व्यवसितं राजन् भवता शत्रुकर्शन ।
कल्याणी येन ते कीर्तिर्लोकाननु भविष्यति ॥10.72.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, सम्यक् perfectly/properly, व्यवसितम् resolved/determined, राजन् O King, भवता by you, शत्रु-कर्शन O chastiser of enemies, कल्याणी auspicious/pure, येन by which (decision), ते your, कीर्तिः glorious fame, लोकान् अनु throughout all the worlds, भविष्यति will spread/endure

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच— राजन्! शत्रुकर्शन! भवता सम्यक् व्यवसितं, येन ते कल्याणी कीर्तिः लोकान् अनु भविष्यति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said: राजन् O King!, शत्रुकर्शन O chastiser of foes! भवता सम्यक् व्यवसितं A most excellent and proper determination has been made by you, येन as a result of which, ते कल्याणी कीर्तिः your auspicious, purifying fame लोकान् अनु भविष्यति will spread continuously throughout all the worlds.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord Krishna said: O King, subduer of enemies! You have made a truly wonderful and righteous decision. By undertaking this great Rajasuya sacrifice, your sacred and auspicious fame will spread throughout the worlds.

ऋषीणां पितृदेवानां सुहृदामपि नः प्रभो ।
सर्वेषामपि भूतानामीप्सितः क्रतुराडयम् ॥10.72.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऋषीणाम् of the great sages, पितृ-देवानाम् of the forefathers and the devas, सुहृदाम् of well-wishing friends, अपि also, नः of ours, प्रभो O King, सर्वेषाम् of all, अपि indeed, भूतानाम् living beings, ईप्सितः desired / cherished, क्रतु-राट् the king of sacrifices (Rajasuya), अयम् this

Anvaya: प्रभो! अयं क्रतुराट् ऋषीणां पितृदेवानां सुहृदां नः च सर्वेषाम् अपि भूतानाम् ईप्सितः (अस्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रभो O King! अयं क्रतुराट् This king among sacrifices (the Rajasuya) ऋषीणां of the great sages, पितृदेवानां of the forefathers and devas, सुहृदां of all affectionate friends, नः च and of Us as well, सर्वेषाम् अपि भूतानाम् indeed, of every living entity, ईप्सितः (अस्ति) is deeply desired and cherished.

Simple Meaning: O King Yudhishthira, this supreme Rajasuya sacrifice is welcomed and desired by everyone—the great sages, the forefathers, the devas, our well-wishing friends, all living beings, and indeed by Me as well.

विजित्य नृपतीन् सर्वान् कृत्वा च जगतीं वशे ।
सम्भृत्य सर्वसम्भारानाहरस्व महाक्रतुम् ॥10.72.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: विजित्य having conquered, नृपतीन् the kings, सर्वान् all, कृत्वा having brought/made, च and, जगतीम् the earth/world, वशे under control/subjugation, सम्भृत्य having gathered/collected, सर्व-सम्भारान् all the required materials and ingredients, आहरस्व perform/execute, महा-क्रतुम् this grand sacrifice (the Rajasuya)

Anvaya: सर्वान् नृपतीन् विजित्य, जगतीं वशे कृत्वा च, सर्वसम्भारान् सम्भृत्य महाक्रतुम् आहरस्व।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सर्वान् नृपतीन् विजित्य Having conquered all rival kings, जगतीं वशे कृत्वा च and having brought the entire earth under your allegiance, सर्वसम्भारान् सम्भृत्य and having gathered all necessary paraphernalia and supplies, महाक्रतुम् आहरस्व execute this grand sacrifice (the Rajasuya).

Simple Meaning: O King, first conquer all the rulers and bring the entire world under your rightful sway. Then, after gathering all the required ritual ingredients and paraphernalia, perform this grand Rajasuya sacrifice.

एते ते भ्रातरो राजन् लोकपालांशसम्भवाः ।
जितोऽस्म्यात्मवता तेऽहं दुर्जयो योऽकृतात्मभिः ॥10.72.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: एते these, ते your, भ्रातरः brothers, राजन् O King, लोकपाल-अंश-सम्भवाः born from partial expansions/portions of the universal guardians, जितः conquered/won over, अस्मि I am, आत्मवता by one who is self-controlled/master of the self, ते by you, अहम् I, दुर्जयः unconquerable, यः who, अकृत-आत्मभिः by those who are uncontrolled in mind and senses

Anvaya: राजन्! एते ते भ्रातरः लोकपालांशसम्भवाः (सन्ति)। अकृतात्मभिः यः अहं दुर्जयः, (सः अहम्) आत्मवता ते जितः अस्मि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King! एते ते भ्रातरः These brothers of yours लोकपालांशसम्भवाः (सन्ति) are born from the divine portions of the rulers/guardians of the worlds. अकृतात्मभिः यः अहं दुर्जयः I, who am impossible to be conquered by those who lack self-control, (सः अहम्) आत्मवता ते जितः अस्मि have been completely conquered by you, who are perfectly self-controlled and devoted.

Simple Meaning: O King Yudhishthira, your noble brothers are born from the partial expansions of the great universal guardians (the Lokapalas). I, who am unconquerable by those who cannot control their mind and senses, have been conquered by you, who are self-controlled and devoted.

न कश्चिन्मत्परं लोके तेजसा यशसा श्रिया ।
विभूतिभिर्वाभिभवेद् देवोऽपि किमु पार्थिवः ॥10.72.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, कश्चित् anyone, मत्-परम् my devotee/one who has Me as his supreme goal, लोके in this world, तेजसा in influence/prowess, यशसा in glory/fame, श्रिया in beauty/opulence, विभूतिभिः in divine powers/possessions, वा or, अभिभवेत् can surpass/overpower, देवः a deva, अपि even, किमु what to speak of, पार्थिवः an earthly king

Literal meaning of the anvaya: लोके In all the worlds, मत्परं (जनं) a person who considers Me to be his supreme refuge, तेजसा यशसा श्रिया विभूतिभिः वा in terms of influence, fame, beauty, opulence, or extraordinary powers, कश्चित् देवः अपि न अभिभवेत् no one—not even a celestial demigod—can ever surpass or defeat; किमु पार्थिवः what to speak then of an earthly king?

Word-by-word meaning: मण्डलानि circular movements/footwork, विचित्राणि intricate/varied, सव्यम् to the left, दक्षिणम् to the right, एव च and also, चरतोः of the two moving about, शुशुभे shone/looked splendid, युद्धम् the combat/fight, नटयोः इव like two dancers/actors, रङ्गिणोः on a stage/in a performance arena

Anvaya: सव्यं दक्षिणम् एव च विचित्राणि मण्डलानि चरतोः (तयोः) युद्धं रङ्गिणोः नटयोः इव शुशुभे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सव्यं दक्षिणम् एव च Moving to the left and to the right, विचित्राणि मण्डलानि चरतोः as they executed intricate circular footwork movements, (तयोः) युद्धं the duel between those two, रङ्गिणोः नटयोः इव like two expert dancers on a stage, शुशुभे looked splendid.

Simple Meaning: As Bhima and Jarasandha maneuvered swiftly across the arena, circling each other with intricate footwork to the left and right, their ferocious duel looked as magnificent and captivating as a dynamic performance by two master dancers on stage.

ततश्चटचटाशब्दो वज्रनिष्पेषसन्निभः ।
गदयोः क्षिप्तयो राजन् दन्तयोरिव दन्तिनोः ॥10.72.36॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, चट-चटा-शब्दः a sharp, cracking sound (chata-chata), वज्र-निष्पेष-सन्निभः like the clash of thunderbolts, गदयोः of the two maces, क्षिप्तयोः being struck against each other, राजन् O King (Parikshit), दन्तयोः इव like the tusks, दन्तिनोः of two mighty elephants

Anvaya: राजन्! ततः क्षिप्तयोः गदयोः दन्तिनोः दन्तयोः इव वज्रनिष्पेषसन्निभः चटचटाशब्दः (अभवत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King Parikshit! ततः Then, क्षिप्तयोः गदयोः as their maces were violently hurled against one another, दन्तिनोः दन्तयोः इव just like the tusks of two colliding elephants, वज्रनिष्पेषसन्निभः a terrible sound resembling the shattering impact of thunderbolts, चटचटाशब्दः a loud chata-chata cracking sound resounded.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit! As the two warriors fiercely smashed their maces together, the violent impact produced a deafening chata-chata cracking sound. Resembling the sudden crash of thunderbolts or the thunderous clash of two giant elephants locking tusks in battle, the noise reverberated across the arena.

ते वै गदे भुजजवेन निपात्यमाने अन्योन्यतोंऽसकटिपादकरोरुजत्रून् ।
चूर्णीबभूवतुरुपेत्य यथार्कशाखे संयुध्यतोर्द्विरदयोरिव दीप्तमन्य्वोः॥10.72.37॥

Word-by-word meaning: ते those two, वै indeed, गदे maces, भुज-जवेन by the force/velocity of their arms, निपात्यमाने being brought down/struck, अन्योन्यतः upon each other, अंस-कटि-पाद-कर-ऊरु-जत्रून् the shoulders, waist, feet, hands, thighs, and collarbones, चूर्णी-बभूवतुः became pulverized/shattered to pieces, उपेत्य colliding, यथा just as, अर्क-शाखे branches of a sugarcane plant, संयुध्यतोः of two fighting, द्विरदयोः इव like two bull elephants, दीप्त-मन्य्वोः burning with intense fury

Anvaya: दीप्तमन्य्वोः संयुध्यतोः द्विरदयोः (इव) भुजजवेन अन्योन्यतः अंसकटिपादकरोरुजत्रून् निपात्यमाने ते गदे उपेत्य अर्कशाखे यथा चूर्णीबभूवतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दीप्तमन्य्वोः संयुध्यतोः द्विरदयोः इव Like two enraged elephants battling fiercely, भुजजवेन by the immense force of their arms, अन्योन्यतः against each other's अंसकटिपादकरोरुजत्रून् shoulders, waist, feet, hands, thighs, and collarbones, निपात्यमाने brought down repeatedly, ते गदे those two heavy maces, उपेत्य upon striking each other, अर्कशाखे यथा like brittle branches of a sugarcane plant, चूर्णीबभूवतुः were shattered into fine pieces.

Simple Meaning: Driven by furious rage, the two warriors fought like two wild bull elephants in rut. They struck at each other's shoulders, waist, feet, hands, thighs, and collarbones with extraordinary force. Under the sheer violence of these blows, their heavy iron maces shattered into splinters upon impact, breaking as easily as the fragile stems of a sugarcane plant.

इत्थं तयोः प्रहतयोर्गदयोर्नृवीरौ क्रुद्धौ स्वमुष्टिभिरयः स्पर्शैरपिष्टाम् ।
शब्दस्तयोः प्रहरतोरिभयोरिवासीन्निर्घातवज्रपरुषस्तलताडनोत्थः ॥10.72.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: इत्थम् in this manner, तयोः of those two, प्रहतयोः being shattered/broken, गदयोः weapons/maces, नृ-वीरौ the two heroes among men (Bhima and Jarasandha), क्रुद्धौ enraged, स्व-मुष्टिभिः with their own bare fists, अयः-स्पर्शैः hard like iron to the touch, अप्पिष्टाम् battered / pounded each other, शब्दः the roaring sound, तयोः of the two, प्रहरतोः striking each other, इभयोः इव like two giant elephants, आसीत् was produced, निर्घात-वज्र-परुषः harsh like a thunderbolt accompanied by a sudden gust, तल-ताडन-उत्थः arising from the slapping/striking of their palms and fists

Anvaya: इत्थं तयोः गदयोः प्रहतयोः (सत्योः) क्रुद्धौ नृवीरौ अयःस्पर्शैः स्वमुष्टिभिः (अन्योन्यं) अपिष्टाम्। इभयोः इव प्रहरतोः तयोः तलताडनोत्थः शब्दः निर्घातवज्रपरुषः आसीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इत्थं तयोः गदयोः प्रहतयोः In this way, when both their maces were shattered, क्रुद्धौ नृवीरौ those two furious hero-kings, अयःस्पर्शैः स्वमुष्टिभिः using their own fists that were as hard to the touch as solid iron, अपिष्टाम् began pounding and pulverizing each other. इभयोः इव प्रहरतोः As they struck each other like two clashing elephants, तयोः तलताडनोत्थः शब्दः the crackling sound generated by their palm-slaps and fist-strikes, निर्घातवज्रपरुषः आसीत् was as harsh and deafening as a violent thunderbolt crashing during a tempest.

Simple Meaning: With their maces broken to pieces, the two enraged heroes turned to bare-handed combat. They began violently pounding each other with fists as hard as iron. The sounds generated by their striking palms and colliding fists echoed across the battlefield like two warring bull elephants, making a harsh, thunderous crackling noise like lightning striking in a storm.

तयोरेवं प्रहरतोः समशिक्षाबलौजसोः ।
निर्विशेषमभूद् युद्धमक्षीणजवयोर्नृप ॥10.72.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: तयोः of the two of them, एवम् in this manner, प्रहरतोः continuously striking each other, सम-शिक्षा-बल-ओजसोः who were equal in training, physical strength, and stamina/vitality, निर्विशेषम् without distinction (evenly matched/yielding no victor), अभूत् became, युद्धम् the duel, अक्षीण-जवयोः whose speed and vigor remained unflagging, नृप O King (Parikshit)

Anvaya:  नृप! समशिक्षाबलौजसोः अक्षीणजवयोः तयोः एवं प्रहरतोः युद्धं निर्विशेषम् अभूत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King Parikshit! समशिक्षाबलौजसोः Of those two who possessed identical training, strength, and inner vitality, अक्षीणजवयोः and whose swift movements and speed never slackened, तयोः एवं प्रहरतोः as they continued striking each other in this manner, युद्धं निर्विशेषम् अभूत् their duel remained completely undecided.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit! Because Bhima and Jarasandha were perfectly matched in martial training, physical power, and inner vitality, their speed and vigor never flagged. As they exchanged relentless blows, the duel remained deadlocked, with neither warrior gaining the upper hand.

एवं तयोर्महाराज युध्यतोः सप्तविंशतिः ।
दिनानि निरगंस्तत्र सुहृद्वन्निशि तिष्ठतोः ॥10.72.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, तयोः of those two (Bhima and Jarasandha), महाराज O Great King (Parikshit), युध्यतोः while fighting, सप्त-विंशतिः twenty-seven, दिनानि days, निरगन् passed away, तत्र there, सुहृद्-वत् like friendly companions, निशि at night, तिष्ठतोः abiding / resting together

Anvaya: महाराज! एवं युध्यतोः निशि सुहृद्वत् तिष्ठतोः तयोः तत्र सप्तविंशतिः दिनानि निरगन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महाराज O Great King! एवं युध्यतोः While they were fighting in this manner by day, निशि सुहृद्वत् तिष्ठतोः and residing together like warm friends at night, तयोः तत्र for the two of them there, सप्तविंशतिः दिनानि निरगन् twenty-seven days passed away continuously.

Simple Meaning: O Great King Parikshit! In this manner, they fought fiercely during the day and rested together like cordial friends at night. A total of twenty-seven days passed while the duel remained completely unresolved.

एकदा मातुलेयं वै प्राह राजन् वृकोदरः ।
न शक्तोऽहं जरासन्धं निर्जेतुं युधि माधव ॥10.72.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: एकदा one day (on the twenty-eighth day), मातुलेयम् unto his maternal cousin (Krishna), वै indeed, प्राह spoke, राजन् O King (Parikshit), वृकोदरः Vrikodara (Bhima), न not, शक्तः capable/able, अहम् I am, जरासन्धम् Jarasandha, निर्जेतुम् to conquer/defeat, युधि in battle, माधव O Madhava (Krishna)

Anvaya: राजन्! एकदा वै वृकोदरः मातुलेयं (कृष्णं) प्राह— माधव! अहं युधि जरासन्धं निर्जेतुं न शक्तः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King Parikshit! एकदा वै On one such day, वृकोदरः Bhimasena, मातुलेयं प्राह spoke to his maternal cousin Krishna: माधव "O Madhava! अहं युधि जरासन्धं निर्जेतुं न शक्तः I do not seem capable of vanquishing Jarasandha in combat."

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit! On the twenty-eighth day, Bhima approached his maternal cousin, Lord Krishna, and confessed: "O Madhava! I find myself unable to defeat Jarasandha in battle."

शत्रोर्जन्ममृती विद्वान् जीवितं च जराकृतम् ।
पार्थमाप्याययन् स्वेन तेजसाचिन्तयत् हरिः ॥10.72.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: शत्रोः of the enemy (Jarasandha), जन्म-मृती birth and secret of death, विद्वान् knowing/aware of, जीवितम् life/survival, च and, जरा-कृतम् brought about/put together by the rakshasi Jara, पार्थम् the son of Pritha (Bhima), आप्याययन् invigorating/infusing with strength, स्वेन with His own, तेजसा divine potency/power, अचिन्तयत् thought/deliberated, हरिः Lord Hari (Krishna)

Anvaya: शत्रोः जन्ममृती जराकृतं जीवितं च विद्वान् हरिः स्वेन तेजसा पार्थम् आप्याययन् अचिन्तयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शत्रोः जन्ममृती Knowing the enemy Jarasandha's birth and the secret of his death, जराकृतं जीवितं च and how his life was brought together by the rakshasi Jara, विद्वान् हरिः the omniscient Lord Hari, स्वेन तेजसा पार्थम् आप्याययन् invigorating Bhima with His own divine strength, अचिन्तयत् reflected deeply on how to proceed.

Simple Meaning: Lord Hari knew the secret of Jarasandha’s birth and death—how his two split halves were joined together by the rakshasi Jara to give him life. Infusing Bhima with His own divine power and energy, Krishna reflected on how to guide him to victory.

संचिन्त्यारिवधोपायं भीमस्यामोघदर्शनः ।
दर्शयामास विटपं पाटयन्निव संज्ञया ॥10.72.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: संचिन्त्य contemplating, अरि-वध-उपायम् the means to slay the enemy (Jarasandha), भीमस्य unto Bhima, अमोघ-दर्शनः whose vision is never failing, दर्शयामास He demonstrated, विटपम् a tree twig, पाटयन् इव as if splitting apart, संज्ञया by a gesture

Anvaya: अमोघदर्शनः (कृष्णः) अरिवधोपायं संचिन्त्य, विटपं पाटयन् इव संज्ञया भीमस्य दर्शयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अमोघदर्शनः Krishna, whose vision is infallible, अरिवधोपायं संचिन्त्य having pondered the exact means to destroy the enemy, विटपं पाटयन् इव as if splitting a tree branch, संज्ञया through a subtle gesture, भीमस्य दर्शयामास demonstrated the strategy to Bhima.

Simple Meaning: Having carefully considered the means to eliminate Jarasandha, Lord Krishna—whose vision is always infallible—picked up a small tree branch. Through a subtle gesture, He split the branch in two down the middle, silently indicating to Bhima how to tear the king's body apart to slay him.

तद् विज्ञाय महासत्त्वो भीमः प्रहरतां वरः ।
गृहीत्वा पादयोः शत्रुं पातयामास भूतले ॥10.72.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that (hint/gesture), विज्ञाय understanding, महा-सत्त्वः possessed of immense strength, भीमः Bhima, प्रहरताम् among all strikers/warriors, वरः the foremost, गृहीत्वा grabbing, पादयोः by the two feet, शत्रुम् the enemy (Jarasandha), पातयामास slammed down, भूतले onto the ground

Anvaya: महासत्त्वः प्रहरतां वरः भीमः तत् विज्ञाय, शत्रुं पादयोः गृहीत्वा भूतले पातयामास।

Simple Meaning: Grasping Krishna’s subtle hint, the immensely powerful Bhima—the greatest of warriors—seized Jarasandha by both feet and slammed him violently to the ground.

एकं पादं पदाऽऽक्रम्य दोर्भ्यामन्यं प्रगृह्य सः ।

Word-by-word meaning: एकम् one, पादम् foot/leg, पदा with his own foot, आक्रम्य stepping on/pinning down, दोर्भ्याम् with both his arms, अन्यम् the other (foot/leg), प्रगृह्य seizing firmly, सः he (Bhima), गुदतः starting from the anus/split-line of the perineum, पाटयामास tore apart, शाखाम् इव like a tree branch, महा-गजः a giant elephant

Anvaya: सः (भीमः) एकं पादं पदा आक्रम्य, दोर्भ्याम् अन्यं (पादं) प्रगृह्य, महागजः शाखाम् इव (तं) गुदतः पाटयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He (Bhima), एकं पादं पदा आक्रम्य pinning down one of Jarasandha's legs with his own foot, दोर्भ्याम् अन्यं प्रगृह्य and grasping the other leg firmly with both his arms, महागजः शाखाम् इव just as a giant elephant splits a branch, गुदतः पाटयामास tore him completely in two starting from the trunk!

Simple Meaning: Pinning one of Jarasandha's legs down under his own heavy foot, Bhima firmly grabbed the other leg with both hands. Just as a mighty elephant casually rips a tree branch in two, Bhima tore Jarasandha’s body straight up the middle, splitting him completely in half.

एकपादोरुवृषणकटिपृष्ठस्तनांसके ।

Anvaya: प्रजाः एकपादोरुवृषणकटिपृष्ठस्तनांसके एकबाह्वक्षिभ्रूकर्णे (द्वे) शकले ददृषुः।

हाहाकारो महानासीन्निहते मगधेश्वरे ।

Anvaya: मगधेश्वरे निहते महान् हाहाकारः आसीत्। जयाच्युतौ भीमं परिरभ्य पूजयामासतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मगधेश्वरे निहते When the king of Magadha was slain, महान् हाहाकारः आसीत् an immense wail of shock and sorrow arose. जयाच्युतौ Arjuna and Krishna, भीमं परिरभ्य embracing Bhima, पूजयामासतुः honored him.

Simple Meaning: When Jarasandha was killed, a great cry of shock and anguish erupted throughout the kingdom of Magadha. Arjuna and Krishna embraced Bhima with great joy, and congratulated him on his victory.

सहदेवं तत्तनयं भगवान् भूतभावनः ।
अभ्यषिञ्चदमेयात्मा मगधानां पतिं प्रभुः ।
मोचयामास राजन्यान् संरुद्धा मागधेन ये ॥10.72.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: सहदेवम् Sahadeva (the son of Jarasandha), तत्-तनयम् his son, भगवान् the Supreme Lord Krishna, भूत-भावनः the creator and nurturer of all living beings, अभ्यषिञ्चत् crowned/anointed, अमेय-आत्मा whose nature is unfathomable/boundless, मगधानाम् of the Magadha kingdom, पतिम् as the ruler, प्रभुः the almighty Lord, मोचयामास released/freed, राजन्यान् the kings, संरुद्धाः who were imprisoned/held captive, मागधेन by Jarasandha (the ruler of Magadha), ये those who

Anvaya: भूतभावनः अमेयात्मा प्रभुः भगवान् तत्तनयं सहदेवं मगधानां पतिम् अभ्यषिञ्चत्, मागधेन ये संरुद्धाः (तान्) राजन्यान् मोचयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भूतभावनः अमेयात्मा प्रभुः भगवान् The almighty, unfathomable Lord Krishna, creator and protector of all beings, तत्तनयं सहदेवं anointed Jarasandha's son, Sahadeva, मगधानां पतिम् अभ्यषिञ्चत् as the new ruler of Magadha. मागधेन ये संरुद्धाः And those who were held captive by Jarasandha, तान् राजन्यान् मोचयामास He released those kings from imprisonment.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna, the supreme protector and creator of all beings, crowned Jarasandha's son, Sahadeva, as the new king of Magadha. He then arranged for the release of all the captive kings whom Jarasandha had imprisoned in his fortress.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “जरासन्धवधो नाम” द्विसप्ततितमः अध्यायः ॥72॥

Thus ends the seventy-second discourse named “Jarasandha killed”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
