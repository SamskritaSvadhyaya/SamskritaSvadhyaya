श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

नारदनारायणसंवादे वेदस्तुतिः नाम सप्ताशीतितमः अध्यायः

(50 Shlokas)

परीक्षित् उवाच
ब्रह्मन् ब्रह्मण्यनिर्देश्ये निर्गुणे गुणवृत्तयः।
कथं चरन्ति श्रुतयः साक्षात् सदसतः परे ॥10.87.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: परीक्षित् उवाच King Parikshit said, ब्रह्मन् O learned brahmana (Shri Shuka), ब्रह्मणि unto the Supreme Brahman, निर्देश्ये indescribable/beyond words, निर्गुणे devoid of material qualities, गुण-वृत्तयः the functions, designations, or descriptions of attributes, कथम् how, चरन्ति operate/apply, श्रुतयः Vedic mantras/scriptures, साक्षात् directly, सत्-असतः परे who is beyond both the manifest and unmanifest (cause and effect)

Anvaya: परीक्षित् उवाच—ब्रह्मन्! निर्देश्ये निर्गुणे सदसतः परे ब्रह्मणि गुणवृत्तयः श्रुतयः कथं साक्षात् चरन्ति?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: परीक्षित् उवाच King Parikshit said, ब्रह्मन् O master of spiritual wisdom, निर्देश्ये unto that Supreme Reality which is beyond mental and verbal description, निर्गुणे devoid of material gunas, सदसतः परे and transcendent to both cause and effect (the manifest and unmanifest universe), ब्रह्मणि in the Supreme Brahman, गुणवृत्तयः descriptions involving attributes and qualities, श्रुतयः how can the Vedic scriptures, कथं in what manner, साक्षात् directly, चरन्ति operate or apply.

Simple Meaning: King Parikshit said: O master, how can the Vedas directly describe the Supreme Brahman, seeing that He is beyond all verbal designation, devoid of material qualities, and completely transcendent to both cause and effect (the manifest and unmanifest universe)?

श्री शुक उवाच
बुद्धीन्द्रियमनः प्राणान् जनानामसृजत् प्रभुः ।
मात्रार्थं च भवार्थं च आत्मनेऽकल्पनाय च ॥10.87.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, बुद्धि-इन्द्रिय-मनः-प्राणान् intellect, senses, mind, and vital life-airs, जनानाम् of the living entities, असृजत् created, प्रभुः the Supreme Lord, मात्रा-अर्थम् for sensory enjoyment, च and, भव-अर्थम् for repeated birth/future lives, च and, आत्मने for the Soul (Supreme Lord), अकल्पनाय for liberation/attainment of transcendental form

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच—प्रभुः जनानां बुद्धीन्द्रियमनःप्राणान् मात्रार्थं भवार्थम् आत्मने अकल्पनाय च असृजत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, प्रभुः the almighty Master, जनानां for the living beings, बुद्धीन्द्रियमनःप्राणान् the intellect, the senses, the mind, and the vital life-forces, मात्रार्थं for enjoying the material objects of the senses, भवार्थम् for undergoing repeated births in material existence, आत्मने for serving the Supreme Controller, अकल्पनाय and for attaining liberation beyond all mental designations, असृजत् created.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: The Lord created the intellect, senses, mind, and vital forces for the living entities so that they may experience material objects, transmigrate through various births, engage in devotional service to the Supreme Soul, and ultimately attain final liberation.

सैषा ह्युपनिषद् ब्राह्मी पूर्वेषां पूर्वजैर्धृता ।
श्रद्धया धारयेद् यस्तां क्षेमं गच्छेदकिञ्चनः ॥10.87.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा this, एषा same, हि indeed, उपनिषत् sacred secret teaching/Upanishad, ब्राह्मी regarding the Supreme Brahman, पूर्वेषाम् of the ancient ones, पूर्वजैः by the ancestors (the early sages like Sanaka and others), धृता held/contemplated, श्रद्धया with faith, धारयेत् should hold/meditate upon, यः whoever, ताम् that (Upanishad), क्षेमम् ultimate spiritual well-being/liberation, गच्छेत् goes to/attains, अकिञ्चनः one who is free from material possessions/attachments

Anvaya: पूर्वेषां पूर्वजैः धृता सा एषा ब्राह्मी उपनिषत् हि, यः तां श्रद्धया धारयेत्, अकिञ्चनः (सः) क्षेमं गच्छेत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पूर्वेषां Of the earlier sages, पूर्वजैः by their ancestors (the mind-born sons of Brahma like Sanandana), धृता meditated upon and preserved, सा this, एषा very, ब्राह्मी relating to the Supreme Lord, उपनिषत् Upanishad, हि indeed. यः whoever, तां that sacred truth, श्रद्धया with steady devotion and faith, धारयेत् holds in his heart, अकिञ्चनः free from material attachments, (सः he), क्षेमं ultimate spiritual perfection and shelter, गच्छेत् attains.

Simple Meaning: This very sacred Upanishadic truth about the Supreme Brahman was meditated upon and preserved by the most ancient ancestors of the early sages. Whoever contemplates this confidential teaching with firm faith and becomes free from material attachments will surely attain ultimate spiritual well-being and liberation.

Note: Vedas, Shrutis and the Upanishads are the truth about the Supreme Brahman. The terms are used interchangeably and mean the same.

अत्र ते वर्णयिष्यामि गाथां नारायणान्विताम् ।
नारदस्य च संवादमृषेर्नारायणस्य च ॥10.87.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अत्र in this regard, ते unto you, वर्णयिष्यामि I shall relate, गाथाम् an ancient narrative/history, नारायण-अन्विताम् connected with Lord Narayana, नारदस्य of Sage Narada, च and, संवादम् the dialogue, ऋषेः of the sage, नारायणस्य of Narayana, च and

Anvaya: अत्र ते नारायणान्वितां गाथां नारदस्य ऋषेः नारायणस्य च संवादं वर्णयिष्यामि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अत्र In this context, ते unto you, नारायणान्वितां connected with Lord Narayana, गाथां story, नारदस्य of Narada, ऋषेः नारायणस्य च and of the sage Narayana, संवादं the dialogue, वर्णयिष्यामि I shall describe.

Simple Meaning: In this regard, I shall relate to you a narrative connected with Lord Narayana—a dialogue between the sage Narada and Sage Narayana.

एकदा नारदो लोकान् पर्यटन् भगवत्प्रियः ।
सनातनमृषिं द्रष्टुं ययौ नारायणाश्रमम् ॥10.87.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: एकदा once upon a time, नारदः Sage Narada, लोकान् the worlds, पर्यटन् wandering, भगवत्-प्रियः dear to the Supreme Lord, सनातनम् eternal, ऋषिम् sage, द्रष्टुम् to see, ययौ went, नारायण-आश्रमम् to Narayana-ashrama (also known as Badarikashrama - the abode of Sage Narayana)

Anvaya: एकदा लोकान् पर्यटन् भगवत्प्रियः नारदः सनातनम् ऋषिं द्रष्टुं नारायणाश्रमं ययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एकदा Once, लोकान् पर्यटन् through the various planets and worlds, traveling around, भगवत्प्रियः नारदः who is most dear to the Supreme Lord, Sage Narada, सनातनम् ऋषिं eternal sage (Narayana), द्रष्टुं to behold, नारायणाश्रमं to Narayana-ashrama, ययौ traveled.

Simple Meaning: Once, while traveling throughout the various worlds, Narada—the great sage dear to the Supreme Lord—went to Narayana-ashrama to visit the eternal sage Narayana.

यो वै भारतवर्षेऽस्मिन् क्षेमाय स्वस्तये नृणाम् ।
धर्मज्ञानशमोपेतमाकल्पादास्थितस्तपः ॥10.87.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः who, वै indeed, भारत-वर्षे in the land of Bharata, अस्मिन् in this, क्षेमाय for spiritual well-being, स्वस्तये for auspiciousness, नृणाम् of men, धर्म-ज्ञान-शम-उपेतम् endowed with righteousness, wisdom, and inner peace, आ-कल्पात् until the end of the cosmic age, आस्थितः engaged in, तपः severe austerity

Anvaya: यः वै अस्मिन् भारतवर्षे नृणां क्षेमाय स्वस्तये आकल्पात् धर्मज्ञानशमोपेतं तपः आस्थितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः Who, वै indeed, अस्मिन् भारतवर्षे in this land of Bharata, नृणां क्षेमाय स्वस्तये for welfare and auspiciousness of human beings, आकल्पात् from the beginning until the end of a kalpa, धर्मज्ञानशमोपेतं तपः penance with dharma, spiritual knowledge, and tranquil mind, आस्थितः remains steadfastly performing.

Simple Meaning: Indeed, it is He—Lord Narayana—who, for the welfare and salvation of humanity in this land of Bharatavarsha, remains steadfastly engaged in penance with dharma, spiritual knowledge, and tranquil mind from the beginning until the end of a kalpa.

तत्रोपविष्टमृषिभिः कलापग्रामवासिभिः ।
परीतं प्रणतोऽपृच्छदिदमेव कुरूद्वह ॥10.87.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there (at Narayana-ashrama), उपविष्टम् sitting, ऋषिभिः by sages, कलाप-ग्राम-वासिभिः residing in the village of Kalapa, परीतम् surrounded, प्रणतः bowing down, अपृच्छत् asked, इदम् this, एव indeed, कुरूद्वह O best of the Kurus (King Parikshit)

Kalapa Grama is another name of Badarikashrama. It is present day Badarinath.

Anvaya: कुरूद्वह! तत्र कलापग्रामवासिभिः ऋषिभिः परीतम् उपविष्टं (नारायणं) प्रणतः (नारदः) इदम् एव अपृच्छत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कुरूद्वह O best of the Kurus, तत्र at Narayana-ashrama, कलापग्रामवासिभिः who dwell in the exalted village of Kalapa, ऋषिभिः परीतम् surrounded by saintly sages, उपविष्टं seated, (नारायणं) Narayana, प्रणतः offering humble obeisances, (नारदः) Sage Narada, इदम् एव this very question, अपृच्छत् asked.

Simple Meaning: O best of the Kurus, upon arriving at Narayana-ashrama, Narada bowed down before Sage Narayana, who was sitting surrounded by the saintly sages residing in the village of Kalapa, and asked Him this very same question (which King Parikshit had asked Shri Shuka in shloka 10.87.1).

तस्मै ह्यवोचद् भगवानृषीणां शृण्वतामिदम् ।
यो ब्रह्मवादः पूर्वेषां जनलोकनिवासिनाम् ॥10.87.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मै unto him (Narada), हि indeed, अवोचत् spoke, भगवान् the Supreme Lord (Narayana), ऋषीणाम् while the sages, शृण्वताम् were listening, इदम् this, यः which, ब्रह्म-वादः discourse on the Absolute Truth, पूर्वेषाम् of the ancient ones, जनलोक-निवासिनाम् who reside in the Janaloka planetary system

Anvaya: भगवान् (नारायणः) ऋषीणां शृण्वतां जनलोकनिवासिनां पूर्वेषां यः ब्रह्मवादः (आसीत्), तस्मै इदं हि अवोचत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवान् The Supreme Lord Narayana, ऋषीणां शृण्वतां while the sages were listening, जनलोकनिवासिनां residing in the Janaloka planetary system, पूर्वेषां of the ancient sages, यः which, ब्रह्मवादः discourse on the Absolute Truth, (आसीत्) was, तस्मै unto Narada, इदं this, हि indeed, अवोचत् spoke.

Simple Meaning: While all the assembled sages were listening, Lord Narayana spoke to Narada about the discourse on the Absolute Truth which had formerly been discussed by the ancient sages residing in the Janaloka.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
स्वायम्भुव ब्रह्मसत्रं जनलोकेऽभवत् पुरा ।
तत्रस्थानां मानसानां मुनीनामूर्ध्वरेतसाम् ॥10.87.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, स्वायम्भुव O son of Brahma (Narada), ब्रह्म-सत्रम् a sacrifice/discourse on the Supreme Brahman, जनलोके in the Janaloka realm, अभवत् took place, पुरा in ancient times, तत्र-स्थानाम् residing there, मानसानाम् mind-born (sons of Brahma), मुनीनाम् of the sages, ऊर्ध्व-रेतसाम् celibate/holding unbroken vow of chastity

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच—स्वायम्भुव! पुरा जनलोके तत्रस्थानां मानसानाम् ऊर्ध्वरेतसां मुनीनां ब्रह्मसत्रम् अभवत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, स्वायम्भुव O Narada born from Brahma, पुरा in former times, जनलोके in the Janaloka, तत्रस्थानां residing in that abode, मानसानाम् mind-born sons of Brahma, ऊर्ध्वरेतसां who are lifelong celibates, मुनीनां of those saintly sages, ब्रह्मसत्रम् a great sacrificial assembly discussing the Absolute Truth, अभवत् occurred.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord Narayana said: O Narada, long ago on the Janaloka, a great sacrificial assembly focused on the Supreme Brahman took place among the mind-born, lifelong celibate sages residing there.

श्वेतद्वीपं गतवति त्वयि द्रष्टुं तदीश्वरम् ।
ब्रह्मवादः सुसंवृत्तः श्रुतयो यत्र शेरते ।
तत्र हायमभूत् प्रश्नस्त्वं मां यमनुपृच्छसि ॥10.87.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्वेत-द्वीपम् to Shweta-dwipa, गतवति having gone, त्वयि when you had, द्रष्टुम् to see, तद्-ईश्वरम् its Lord Aniruddha (form of Vishnu), ब्रह्म-वादः a discussion on the Absolute Brahman, सु-संवृत्तः took place/arose, श्रुतयः the Shrutis/Vedas/Upanishads, यत्र where, शेरते lie down/rest (during cosmic dissolution), तत्र there, ह indeed, अयम् this, अभूत् arose, प्रश्नः question, त्वम् you, माम् from Me, यम् which, अनुपृच्छसि are now inquiring

Anvaya: त्वयि तदीश्वरं द्रष्टुं श्वेतद्वीपं गतवति (सति), यत्र श्रुतयः शेरते, (तत्र) ब्रह्मवादः सुसंवृत्तः, यं त्वं माम् अनुपृच्छसि, तत्र अयं प्रश्नः ह अभूत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: त्वयि When you, तदीश्वरं द्रष्टुं to see the Lord of that realm, श्वेतद्वीपं गतवति had gone to Shweta-dwipa, यत्र श्रुतयः शेरते where the Shrutis rest, (तत्र) there in Janaloka, ब्रह्मवादः a discourse on the Absolute Reality, सुसंवृत्तः took place, यं which, त्वं you, माम् from Me, अनुपृच्छसि are now asking, तत्र in that assembly, अयं प्रश्नः this very question, ह अभूत् indeed, arose.

Simple Meaning: When you had gone to Shweta-dwipa to see the Lord Aniruddha of that realm - where the Shrutis rest, a profound discussion on the Supreme Brahman took place among the sages on Janaloka. In that assembly they discussed this very same question that you are now asking Me.

तुल्यश्रुततपः शीलास्तुल्यस्वीयारिमध्यमाः ।
अपि चक्रुः प्रवचनमेकं सुश्रुषवोऽपरे ॥10.87.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: तुल्य-श्रुत-तपः-शीलाः equal in learning, austerity, and noble character, तुल्य-स्वीय-अरि-मध्यमाः holding equal vision toward friends, enemies, and neutral parties, अपि even though, चक्रुः made, प्रवचनम् speaker/spokesman, एकम् one, सुश्रुषवः eager to listen, अपरे the others

Anvaya: तुल्यश्रुततपःशीलाः तुल्यस्वीयारिमध्यमाः (मुनयः) अपि एकं प्रवचनं चक्रुः, अपरे (तु) शुश्रूषवः (अभवन्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तुल्यश्रुततपःशीलाः Possessing equal Vedic knowledge, severe asceticism, and flawless character, तुल्यस्वीयारिमध्यमाः viewing friends, foes, and neutral observers with identical equanimity, (मुनयः) all those assembled sages, अपि even though, एकं प्रवचनं a single individual as the primary speaker/spokesman, चक्रुः designated, अपरे but all the other sages, शुश्रूषवः as eager and respectful listeners, (अभवन्) remained.

Simple Meaning: Even though all the sages present were entirely equal in Vedic learning, austerity, and flawless character, and held equal vision toward friends, enemies, and neutral parties, they appointed a single sage as their speaker, while all the others listened attentively.

सनन्दन उवाच
स्वसृष्टमिदमापीय शयानं सह शक्तिभिः ।
तदन्ते बोधयाञ्चक्रुस्तल्लिङ्गैः श्रुतयः परम् ॥10.87.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: सनन्दनः उवाच Sanandana said, स्व-सृष्टम् its own creation (the cosmic manifestation), आपीय having withdrawn/swallowed back (into Himself), शयानम् lying down (in cosmic sleep at dissolution), सह with, शक्तिभिः His potencies (material energies/time), तत्-अन्ते at the end of that night of dissolution, बोधयाञ्चक्रुः awakened/aroused, तत्-लिङ्गैः with indications/characteristic attributes indicative of His supreme nature, श्रुतयः the Shrutis/Vedas/Upanishads/scriptures, परम् unto the Supreme Lord

Anvaya: सनन्दन उवाच—श्रुतयः स्वसृष्टम् इदम् आपीय शक्तिभिः सह शयानं परं (भगवान्) तत्-अन्ते तत्लिङ्गैः बोधयाञ्चक्रुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सनन्दन उवाच Sage Sanandana spoke, श्रुतयः The personified Vedas/Shrutis, स्वसृष्टम् इदम् आपीय which He had created, this entire universe, having drawn back within Himself at the time of dissolution, शक्तिभिः सह along with His inherent potencies, शयानं resting in the causal waters, परं unto the Supreme Lord, तत्-अन्ते at the end of that period of dissolution, तत्लिङ्गैः by hymns and direct indications descriptive of His transcendental glories, बोधयाञ्चक्रुः awakened (as if singing songs of praise to rouse Him to create anew).

Simple Meaning: Sage Sanandana said: When the Supreme Lord withdraws the entire cosmic manifestation into Himself at the time of dissolution and lies resting in the waters with His potencies, the personified Vedas/Shrutis approach Him at the end of the cosmic night and awaken Him with hymns and descriptions characteristic of His supreme, absolute nature.

You are forever complete in all divine opulences by Your own inherent spiritual potency. O Awakener of all powers within living beings inhabiting both moving and non-moving forms! Sometimes when You act through Your maya during transcendent pastimes, the Vedas can follow You and describe Your divine activities and nature.

बृहदुपलब्धमेतदवयन्त्यवशेषतया यत उदयास्तमयौ विकृतेर्मृदि वाविकृतात् ।

Simple Meaning: Because this entire transformed universe originates from You and dissolves back into You while You remain eternally unchanged—just as products made of clay arise from and dissolve back into clay—the wise realize that You alone are the ultimate, underlying Reality (Brahman). Therefore, ancient sages direct all their thoughts, words, and actions unto You. Just as every step a person takes on earth (whether it is on a plank, stone or anywhere else), rests inevitably upon the ground itself, every Vedic description and mental effort ultimately finds its rest in You.

इति तव सूरयस्त्र्यधिपतेऽखिललोकमलक्षपणकथामृताब्धिमवगाह्य तपांसि जहुः ।

Simple Meaning: O Master of the three worlds! By immersing themselves in the nectar-like ocean of Your divine topics—which cleanses the impurities of all the universes—wise devotees abandon penances. O Supreme Lord, what then can be said of those liberated souls who, having entered Your transcendent abode, are completely freed from material desires, time, and the modes of nature, and perpetually serve Your lotus feet in uninterrupted eternal bliss?

दृतय इव श्वसन्त्यसुभृतो यदि तेऽनुविधा महदहमादयोऽण्डमसृजन् यदनुग्रहतः ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: असुभृतः यदि द्रुतयः इव श्वसन्ति Living entities, if, like blacksmith's bellows, breathe (and act mechanically), (तत् तत्सर्वं) ते अनुविधाः are guided by You, यत्-अनुग्रहतः by whose supreme grace, महदहमादयः the cosmic principles of Mahat, ego, and the subtle elements, अण्डम् the universal egg (creation), असृजन् is generated. अत्र In this creation, पुरुषविधः अन्वयः Your immanent presence, in the form of the indwelling Purusha, चरमः is the ultimate culmination. अन्नमयादिषु Within the physical and higher sheaths, यः who resides, सत्-असतः परं beyond the manifested and unmanifested, त्वम् You are. अथ Furthermore, एषु in all these transient states, यत् अवशेषम् what remains as the unchanging core, ऋतम् is the absolute Truth.

Simple Meaning: Living creatures breathe and function like a blacksmith's bellows only because they are animated by Your divine energy; by Your grace alone did the cosmic principles (Mahat and ego) combine to create the universal egg. You are present everywhere as the indwelling Purusha, the ultimate essence underlying the physical sheaths and all layers of existence. You transcend both the manifested and unmanifested reality, and when everything else dissolves, You alone remain as the absolute, unchanging Truth.

Consequently, enlightened sages whose intellects are completely purified, look past these transient, illusory forms and—like astute merchants tracking true wealth—intelligently trace out Your unchanging, uniform, and absolute essence within them all.

Word-by-word meaning: स्वजन-सुत-आत्म-दार-धन-धाम-धरा-सुरथैः with relatives, children, own body, wives, wealth, houses, land, life and excellent vehicles, त्वयि in You, सति who are present, किम् what (use is there?), नृणाम् for human beings, श्रयतः who take shelter, आत्मनि in the Supreme Self, सर्व-रसे who is the embodiment of all sweetness/joy, इति thus, सत्-अजानताम् of them who do not know this truth, मिथुनतः in sexual pairing/conjugal pleasure, रतये for the sake of enjoyment, चरताम् who wander/engage, सुखयति gives true happiness, कः who, नु indeed, इह in this world, स्व-विहते destroyed by their own actions/ignorance, स्व-निरस्त-भगे who have cast away true spiritual fortune/bliss

Anvaya: सर्वरसे आत्मनि त्वयि सति, स्वजनसुतात्मदारधनधामधरासुरथैः श्रयतः नृणां किम्? इति सत् अजानतातां मिथुनतो रतये चरतां स्वविहते स्वनिरस्तभगे इह कः नु सुखयति?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सर्वरसे आत्मनि त्वयि सति When You, who is the supreme reservoir of all emotional and spiritual sweetness, who is the very Self of all, exist, स्वजनसुतात्मदारधनधामधरासुरथैः श्रयतः नृणां किम् what real purpose do relatives, children, bodies, wives, wealth, homes, land, and chariots serve for human beings, who take complete refuge in You? इति Thus, सत्-अजानताम् of them who do not know this truth, मिथुनतः in the mutual attraction of male and female for the sake of sensual pleasure, रतये absorbed, चरतां wandering through worldly life, स्वविहते ruined by their own self-defeating actions, स्वनिरस्तभगे and having rejected their own supreme spiritual fortune—इह कः नु सुखयति who among them can ever experience true happiness in this world?

Simple Meaning: When You—the Supreme Self and the infinite reservoir of all joy—are present, what true necessity do human beings have for relatives, children, bodies, spouses, wealth, homes, land, and vehicles? Those who ignore this supreme truth and remain absorbed in worldly sexual pleasure and ephemeral attachments are ruined by their own self-defeating choices and have cast away their true spiritual fortune. In this material world, who among such deluded souls can ever attain true happiness?

भुवि पुरुपुण्यतीर्थसदनान्यृषयो विमदास्त उत भवत्पदाम्बुजहृदोऽघभिदङ्घ्रिजलाः ।
दधति सकृन्मनस्त्वयि य आत्मनि नित्यसुखे न पुनरुपासते पुरुषसारहरावसथान् ॥10.87.35॥

Word-by-word meaning: भुवि on the earth, पुरु-पुण्य-तीर्थ-सदानानि the abodes of extremely holy places of pilgrimage, ऋषयः great sages/saints, विमदाः free from pride and illusion, ते those, उत indeed, भवत्-पद-अम्बुज-हृदः whose hearts are anchored in Your lotus feet, अघ-भिद्-अङ्घ्रि-जलाः whose waters of the holy feet destroy all sins, दधति hold/fix, सकृत् even once, मनः the mind, त्वयि in You, यः who art, आत्मनि the Supreme Self, नित्य-सुखे the embodiment of eternal bliss, न not, पुनः again, उपासते worship/seek, पुरुष-सार-हर-अवस्थान् places and homes that steal away the essence of human life (household life centered on bodily attachment)

Anvaya: भुवि पुरुपुण्यतीर्थसदनानि ऋषयः विमदाः (सन्ति), ते उत भवत्पदाम्बुजहृदोऽघभिदङ्घ्रिजलाः (भवन्ति)। ये सकृत् आत्मनि नित्यसुखे त्वयि मनः दधति, (ते) पुरुषसारहरावसथान् न पुनः उपासते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भुवि On this earth, पुरुपुण्यतीर्थसदनानि places that are renowned as great holy pilgrimage spots, विमदाः ऋषयः are actually the great sages who are free from pride, vanity, and delusion. ते उत They indeed, भवत्पदाम्बुजहृदः whose hearts are immersed in Your lotus feet, अघभिदङ्घ्रिजलाः possess the water washing Your lotus feet which destroys all sins. ये Those who, सकृत् even once, आत्मनि नित्यसुखे त्वयि in You who is the Supreme Self and Who is the source of eternal happiness, मनः दधति their minds, fix with devotion, (ते) they, पुरुषसारहरावसथान् worldly homes and places that steal away the true essence and spiritual wealth of human life, न पुनः उपासते do not ever return to worship or inhabit again.

Simple Meaning: Great sages free from all pride are themselves the holiest places of pilgrimage on earth, for their hearts are anchored in Your lotus feet, and the water washing their feet washes away all sins. Those fortunate souls who fix their minds even once upon You—the Supreme Self and the source of eternal bliss—never again turn back to ordinary worldly households, which only drain the true essence and spiritual wealth of human life.

सत इदमुत्थितं सदिति चेन्ननु तर्कहतं व्यभिचरति क्व च क्व च मृषा न तथोभययुक् ।
व्यवहृतये विकल्प इषितोऽन्धपरम्परया भ्रमयति भारती त उरुवृत्तिभिरुक्थजडान् ॥10.87.36॥

Word-by-word meaning: सतः from existing reality, इदम् this universe, उत्थितम् has arisen/manifested, सत् as reality, इति thus, चेत् if, ननु indeed/objection, तर्क-हतम् refuted by logic/reasoning, व्यभिचरति deviates/contradicts experience, क्व च and where, क्व च where (or in some cases), मृषा false/illusory, न not, तथा similarly, उभय-युक् possessing both (neither purely real nor purely false, or indescribable), व्यवहार्यये for practical worldly transactions (vyavahara), विकल्पः mental imagination/conceptual construction, इषितः is intended/accepted, अन्ध-परम्परया by blind tradition/succession of the blind, भ्रमयति bewilders, भारती Your verbal/illusory energy (Vedic word-power), ते Your, उरु-वृत्तिभिः with manifold modifications/functions, उक्थ-जडान् who are stupefied by ritualistic recitations (uktha) or dry scriptural dogmas

Anvaya: (चेत् वदेत्) सतः इदम् उत्थितं, ननु (तत्) तर्कहतं (यतः) क्व च व्यभिचरति, क्व च मृषा (भवति), तथा (च इदम्) उभययुक् न (भवति)। (तथापि) व्यवहृतये विकल्पः अन्धपरम्परया इषितः। (किन्तु) ते भारती उरुवृत्तिभिः उक्थजडान् भ्रमयति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya:  (चेत् वदेत्) (If someone argues), सतः इदम् उत्थितं "This universe has arisen directly from the ultimate real Brahman (and therefore the world itself is entirely real)," ननु (तत्) तर्कहतं (we reply) no, this argument is refuted by logic, (यतः) क्व च व्यभिचरति because in some instances it contradicts direct observation, क्व च मृषा (भवति) and in others it is proven false; तथा (च इदम्) उभययुक् न (भवति) nor can it be described as possessing both absolute reality and unreality simultaneously. (तथापि) व्यवहृतये Still, for the purpose of practical worldly dealings (vyavahara), विकल्पः अन्धपरम्परया इषितः mental imagination and relative categories (vikalpa), handed down through blind traditional succession, are accepted. (किन्तु) ते भारती उरुवृत्तिभिः उक्थजडान् भ्रमयति But Your verbal energy (Bharati) - the Vedas, manifesting through manifold mental functions, bewilder the dull-witted.

Simple Meaning: If someone argues that because the universe comes from the Supreme Reality it must also be completely real, that claim is refuted by logic and experience, as worldly objects are seen to be temporary and ultimately illusory. Mental constructs and dualities are accepted merely for the sake of practical worldly interaction through blind tradition. Your wondrous verbal energy (Bharati), operating through manifold modifications, bewilder the dull-witted.

न यदिदमग्र आस न भविष्यदतो निधनादनुमितमन्तरा त्वयि विभाति मृषैकरसे ।
अत उपमीयते द्रविणजातिविकल्पपथैर्वितथमनोविलासमृतमित्यवयन्त्यबुधाः ॥10.87.37॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, यत् which universe, इदम् this, अग्रे in the beginning (before creation), आस existed, न not, भविष्यत् will exist, अतः therefore, निधनात् from destruction/dissolution, अनुमितम् inferred, अन्तरा in the interim period (between creation and destruction), त्वयि in You (the underlying reality), विभाति shines/appears, मृषा illusory/false, एक-रसे in You who are the one unchanging essence, अतः therefore, उपमीयते is compared, द्रविण-जाति-विकल्प-पथैः by the various imagined forms and transformations of wealth (or gold/substance), वितथम् false/unreal, मनः-विलासम् mental play/imagination, ऋतम् reality, इति thus, अवयन्ति consider/conclude, अबुधाः foolish/ignorant persons (who mistake the dream for truth)

Anvaya: यत् इदम् अग्रे न आस, निधनात् (च) न भविष्यत्, अतः अनुमितम् (तत्) अन्तरा एकरसे त्वयि मृषा विभाति। (यथा स्वप्ने) द्रविणजातिविकल्पपथैः उपमीयते, अबुधाः (तत्) वितथमनोविलासमृतम् इति अवयन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यत् इदम् Which universe, अग्रे in the beginning (before cosmic manifestation), न आस did not exist, निधनात् and after final dissolution, न भविष्यत् will not exist, अतः therefore, अनुमितम् inferred to be non-existent in its essence. अन्तरा During the interim state of manifestation, एकरसे त्वयि within You, who are the one absolute, unchanging essence, मृषा as an illusion, विभाति shines/appears. (Just as things seen in a dream are experienced), द्रविणजातिविकल्पपथैः through various imagined modifications of material-substances or dream-objects, उपमीयते it is illustrated; However, अबुधाः ignorant persons, वितथम् ऋतम् this unreal mental play, as absolute truth, इति अवयन्ति thus, miscomprehend.

Simple Meaning: This universe did not exist prior to creation, nor will it exist after dissolution; hence it is known to be non-existent in its true essence. During the interim period, it appears as an illusion. We compare this universe as subject to various imagined modifications of material-substances or dream-objects. Only the ignorant mistake this temporary mental play for absolute truth.

स यदजया त्वजामनुशयीत गुणांश्च जुषन् भजति सरूपतां तदनु मृत्युमपेतभगः ।
त्वमुत जहासि तामहिरिव त्वचमात्तभगो महसि महीयसेऽष्टगुणितेऽपरिमेयभगः ॥10.87.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः that individual living entity (jiva), यत् because, अजया with material nature (Prakriti/Maya, the unborn energy), अनुशयीत identifies and lies down (gets entangled in material existence), गुणान् च and the modes of nature (sattva, rajas, tamas), जुषन् enjoying/serving, भजति assumes, सरूपताम् identification with material forms/bodies, तत्-अनु subsequently, मृत्युम् death/rebirth, अपेत-भगः bereft of spiritual fortune and glory, त्वम् You, उत indeed, जहासि give up/abandon, ताम् material nature, अहिः a snake, इव like, त्वचम् its sloughed skin, आत्त-भगः possessing supreme spiritual opulence/glory, महसि in Your own supreme glory/splendor, महीयसे You remain exalted and glorified, अष्ट-गुणिते possessing the eightfold spiritual perfections or infinite supreme opulence, अपरिमेय-भगः whose transcendental opulences are limitless and immeasurable

Anvaya: यत् सः (जीवः) अजया अनुशयीत, गुणान् च जुषन् सरूपतां भजति, ततः अपेतभगः मृत्युम् (अनुप्राप्नोति)। त्वम् उत अहिः त्वचम् इव तां जहासि। आत्तभगः अपरिमेयभगः (त्वम्) अष्टगुणिते महसि महीयसे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यत् सः Because the individual soul (who thinks that the world is real), अजया with the illusory material energy (Prakriti), अनुशयीत intimately associates and identifies, गुणान् च जुषन् and enjoying material modes, सरूपतां भजति a conditioned bodily form corresponding to those modes, assumes, तदनु then, अपेतभगः losing all spiritual awareness and divine fortune, मृत्युम् (अनुप्राप्नोति) undergoes repeated birth and death. त्वम् उत You, however, अहिः त्वचम् इव like a snake shedding its old sloughed skin, तां जहासि that material nature, abandon and remain untouched by. आत्तभगः Possessing supreme, all-inclusive opulences, अपरिमेयभगः whose transcendental glory is boundless, (त्वम्) You, अष्टगुणिते possessing the eightfold spiritual perfections or infinite supreme opulence, महसि in Your own eternal, self-luminous splendor, महीयसे shine in supreme exaltation.

Simple Meaning: When the individual soul identifies with material nature and indulges in the modes of passion and ignorance, it assumes conditioned bodily forms and is subjected to repeated birth and death, losing its divine awareness. You, however, effortlessly cast off material nature just as a snake sheds its dead skin. Possessing limitless and supreme opulences, You remain forever established in Your own self-luminous, transcendent glory!

यदि न समुद्धरन्ति यतयो हृदि कामजटा दुरधिगमोऽसतां हृदि गतोऽस्मृतकण्ठमणिः ।

Word-by-word meaning: यदि if, न not, समुद्धरन्ति completely uproot/extract, यतयः ascetics/sages/practitioners, हृदि within the heart, काम-जटाः the tangled roots of material desires/lust, दुरधिगमः difficult to attain, असताम् for the impure/materialistic, हृदि गतः situated in the heart, अस्मृत-कण्ठ-मणिः like the forgotten jewel around one's neck, असुतृप-योगिनाम् for dry yogis or those who seek material sustenance/siddhis through yoga, उभयतः अपि on both sides (here and hereafter), असुखम् misery/unhappiness, भगवन् O Lord, अनपगत-अन्तकात् from death which never departs, अनधिरूढ-पदात् and from the unachieved supreme footing, भवतः of You

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवन् O Lord! यदि if, यतयः spiritual practitioners or ascetics, हृदि within the core of their hearts, कामजटाः the deep, tangled roots of material desires and lust, न समुद्धरन्ति do not completely uproot and destroy, (तर्हि) then, असतां for the impure or insincere, हृदि गतः though ever present within the heart, अस्मृतकण्ठमणिः like the necklace jewel whose presence is forgotten around one's own neck, (त्वं) You, दुरधिगमः remain entirely unattainable and hidden. असुतृपयोगिनाम् For those yogis who are attached to sense-gratification or the pursuit of material mystic perfections (siddhis), अनपगतान्तकात् from death which never departs, अनधिरूढपदात् and from failing to attain Your supreme abode, भवतः in relation to You, उभयतः अपि on both counts (in this life and the next), असुखम् utter misery and failure alone remain.

Simple Meaning: O Lord! If practitioners do not completely uproot the tangled roots of material desire from their hearts, You remain hidden from them—just like a person who forgets the precious jewel worn around their own neck, even though it is always close. For those who practice yoga merely for superficial attainments or sense control without surrendering to You, both this world and the next bring only misery, for they remain trapped by unrelenting death and fail to reach Your supreme lotus feet.

त्वदवगमी न वेत्ति भवदुत्थशुभाशुभयोर्गुणविगुणान्वयांस्तर्हि देहभृतां च गिरः।

Simple Meaning: One who has attained direct realization of You is no longer bound by the auspicious or inauspicious reactions of material existence. Yet, in every age, embodied beings continually purify their ears and hearts through the continuous tradition of chanting Your divine glories and transcendental attributes, for You are ultimate path to liberation for men.

ख इव रजांसि वान्ति वयसा सह यच्छ्रुतयस्त्वयि हि फलन्त्यतन्निरसनेन भवन्निधनाः ॥10.87.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्यु-पतयः the lords of the heavenly realms (like Brahma and Indra), एव indeed, ते Your, न not, ययुः attained, अन्तम् the limit, अनन्ततया due to Your infiniteness, त्वम् अपि You also, यत् because, अन्तः-अण्ड-निचयाः the clusters of multi-layered universes, ननु indeed, स-आवरणाः along with their coverings, खे in the sky, इव like, रजांसि specks of dust, वान्ति blow/drift, वयसा with time, सह along with, यत् which, श्रुतयः the Vedic scriptures, त्वयि in You, हि indeed, फलन्ति find their culmination and ultimate purpose, अतत्-निरसनेन by the negation of all that is not You, भवत्-निधनाः finding their final refuge and establishment in You

Anvaya: द्युपतयः एव त्वम् अपि अनन्ततया ते अन्तं न ययुः। सावरणाः अन्तराण्डनिचयाः त्वयि वयसा सह खे रजांसि इव वान्ति। हि श्रुतयः अतत्निरसनेन भवत्निधनाः त्वयि फलन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: द्युपतयः एव The lords of the heavenly realms also, त्वम् अपि even You, अनन्ततया due to Your boundless nature, ते अन्तं Your limit, न ययुः could never reach or measure. सावरणाः Along with their sevenfold material coverings, अन्तराण्डनिचयाः the countless clusters of universes, त्वयि within You, वयसा सह along with the relentless march of time, खे within the sky, रजांसि इव like floating particles of dust, वान्ति drift and rotate. हि Indeed, श्रुतयः all Vedic revelations and scriptural utterances, अतत्निरसनेन by systematically refuting every illusory, finite concept, भवत्निधनाः finding their ultimate resting place and realization in You, त्वयि within You alone, फलन्ति fulfill their supreme purpose.

Simple Meaning: The highest lords of the heavenly realms and even You yourself have never reached the limit of Your infinity. Countless universes, complete with their material coverings, drift within You like specks of dust in the vast sky, carried along by the flow of time. All the sacred Vedas find their ultimate fulfillment and realization in You alone, having systematically discarded everything that is not You through the path of transcendental negation.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
इत्येतद् ब्रह्मणः पुत्रा आश्रुत्यात्मानुशासनम् ।
सनन्दमथानर्चुः सिद्धा ज्ञात्वाऽऽत्मनो गतिम् ॥10.87.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord Narayana said, इति thus, एतत् this, ब्रह्मणः of Lord Brahma, पुत्राः sons (the Kumaras), आश्रुत्य hearing, आत्म-अनुशासनम् the supreme instruction regarding the Self, सनन्दम् unto Sanandana, अथ then, आनर्चुः worshipped/revered, सिद्धाः self-realized/perfection-attained sages, ज्ञात्वा understanding, आत्मनः of the Self, गतिम् the ultimate destination/nature

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच— ब्रह्मणः सिद्धाः पुत्राः इति एतत् आत्मानुशासनम् आश्रुत्य आत्मनः गतिं ज्ञात्वा अथ सनन्दम् आनर्चुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord Narayana said— ब्रह्मणः सिद्धाः पुत्राः the self-realized sons of Brahma (Sanaka, Sanatana, and Sanat-kumara), इति एतत् this, आत्मानुशासनम् supreme instruction concerning the Absolute Truth, आश्रुत्य having heard, आत्मनः गतिं ज्ञात्वा the true nature and destination of the Self, realizing, अथ then, सनन्दम् Sanandana (who spoke these prayers), आनर्चुः worshipped.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord Narayana said: Having heard this supreme exposition of the Absolute Truth from Sanandana, his brothers—the self-realized, mind-born sons of Brahma—fully understood the ultimate nature and goal of the Self, and with deep reverence, worshipped Sanandana for his immortal discourse.

इत्यशेषसमाम्नायपुराणोपनिषद्रसः ।
समुद्धृतः पूर्वजातैर्व्योमयानैर्महात्मभिः ॥10.87.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, अशेष-समाम्नाय-पुराण-उपनिषद्-रसः the essence of all the Vedas, Puranas, and Upanishads, समुद्धृतः extracted/summarized, पूर्व-जातैः born in ancient times, व्योम-यानैः by those who travel freely in the heavenly/skyways (the cosmic sages), महा-आत्मभिः by those great souls

Anvaya: पूर्वजातैः व्योमयानैः महात्मभिः इति अशेषसमाम्नायपुराणोपनिषद्रसः समुद्धृतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पूर्वजातैः By the ones born in the ancient times, व्योमयानैः महात्मभिः by great spiritual masters (such as the Four Kumaras and Narada) traveling in the sky, इति thus, अशेषसमाम्नायपुराणोपनिषद्रसः the pure nectar and ultimate essence of all Vedic texts, Puranas, and Upanishads, समुद्धृतः has been extracted (for the liberation of all beings).

Simple Meaning: Thus, the ancient and elevated sages who travel freely throughout the heavens have extracted and distilled this nectar—the quintessential truth of all the Vedas, Puranas, and Upanishads (for the ultimate spiritual benefit of all).

त्वं चैतद् ब्रह्मदायाद श्रद्धयाऽऽत्मानुशासनम् ।
धारयंश्चरं गां कामं कामानां भर्जनं नृणाम् ॥10.87.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वम् You, च and, एतत् this, ब्रह्म-दायाद O son of Brahma - Narada, श्रद्धया with deep faith, आत्म-अनुशासनम् instruction on the Supreme Self, धारयन् carrying/holding within the heart, चर wandering, गाम् the earth, कामम् as you desire/at will, कामानाम् of material desires, भर्जनम् the destroyer/scorcher, नृणाम् for human beings

Anvaya: ब्रह्मदायाद! त्वं च नृणां कामानां भर्जनम् एतत् आत्मानुशासनं श्रद्धया धारयन् कामं गां चर।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्रह्मदायाद O son of Brahma - Narada! त्वं च And You, नृणां for all human beings, कामानां of all material worldly desires, भर्जनम् that which burns up and destroys, एतत् आत्मानुशासनं this supreme instruction regarding the Absolute Self, श्रद्धया with profound faith, धारयन् retaining in Your heart, कामं freely/as You wish, गां across the earth, चर wander.

Simple Meaning: “O son of Brahma! With deep faith, hold fast in your heart this supreme teaching of the Self—which completely burns away all material desires for humanity—and wander freely across the earth according to your will.”

श्री शुक उवाच
एवं स ऋषिणाऽऽदिष्टं गृहीत्वा श्रद्धयाऽऽत्मवान् ।
पूर्णः श्रुतधरो राजन्नाह वीरव्रतो मुनिः ॥10.87.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, एवम् thus, सः he (Narada Muni), ऋषिणा by the divine sage (Lord Narayana Rishi), आदिष्टम् instructed, गृहीत्वा having accepted/received, श्रद्धया with deep faith, आत्मवान् self-realized/absorbed in the Self, पूर्णः completely fulfilled, श्रुतधरः retainer of sacred hearing/Vedic wisdom, राजन् O King (Parikshit), आह spoke, वीर-व्रतः dedicated to vows of heroic spiritual determination, मुनिः the silent sage (Narada)

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— राजन्! एवम् ऋषिणा आदिष्टं श्रद्धया गृहीत्वा आत्मवान् पूर्णः श्रुतधरः वीरव्रतः सः मुनिः (नारायणं नमस्कृत्य) आह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said— राजन् O King Parikshit! एवम् in this manner, ऋषिणा आदिष्टं the supreme instruction by the divine sage Narayana, श्रद्धया गृहीत्वा with devotion, having accepted, आत्मवान् the self-realized, पूर्णः eternally fulfilled, श्रुतधरः perfectly retaining every word of scriptural truth, वीरव्रतः steadfast in his vows of uncompromising devotion, सः मुनिः that great sage Narada, (offering his obeisances), आह spoke.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King Parikshit! Having thus received these sacred instructions from the divine sage Narayana with deep faith, the great self-realized sage Narada—eternally fulfilled, perfectly retaining all scriptural truth, and unwavering in his spiritual vows—offered his obeisances and spoke.

नारद उवाच
नमस्तस्मै भगवते कृष्णायामलकीर्त्तये ।
यः धत्ते सर्वभूतानामभवायोशतीः कलाः ॥10.87.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: नारदः उवाच Narada said, नमः obeisances/salutations, तस्मै unto Him, भगवते the Supreme Lord, कृष्णाय unto Lord Krishna, अमल-कीर्तये of spotless/pure fame, यः who, धत्ते assumes/manifests, सर्व-भूतानाम् of all living beings, अभवाय for the liberation/cessation of material birth, उशतीः all-attractive/splendid, कलाः plenary expansions and avatars

Anvaya: नारदः उवाच— यः सर्वभूतानाम् अभवाय उशतीः कलाः धत्ते, तस्मै अमलकीर्तये भगवते कृष्णाय नमः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नारदः उवाच Narada said— यः who, सर्वभूतानाम् अभवाय for all living entities, to bring an end to material birth and bestow ultimate liberation, उशतीः कलाः धत्ते assumes attractive, divine avatars, तस्मै unto Him, अमलकीर्तये whose transcendental fame is entirely spotless, भगवते कृष्णाय नमः unto the Supreme Lord, Krishna, respectful obeisances.

Simple Meaning: Narada said: To grant complete liberation from the cycle of birth and death to all living beings, He who descends into this world through His all-attractive, transcendental forms and avatars, unto that Supreme Lord Krishna, whose fame is eternally pure and spotless, I offer my most respectful obeisances.

इत्याद्यमृषिमानम्य तच्छिष्यांश्च महात्मनः ।
ततोऽगादाश्रमं साक्षात् पितुर्द्वैपायनस्य मे ॥10.87.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, आदिम् the primal, ऋषिम् the sage Narayana, आनम्य offering respectful obeisances, तत्-शिष्यान् His disciples, च and, महात्मनः the great souls, ततः then, अगात् went, आश्रमम् the hermitage, साक्षात् directly, पितुः of father, द्वैपायनस्य of Dwaipayan (Vyasa), मे of mine (Shri Shuka is speaking)

Anvaya: इति आदिम् ऋषिं तच्छिष्यान् च महात्मनः आनम्य, ततः मे पितुः द्वैपायनस्य आश्रमं साक्षात् अगात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति Thus, आदिम् ऋषिं the primal Sage Narayana, तत्शिष्यान् च आनम्य and His disciples, offering obeisances, ततः then, मे पितुः my father, द्वैपायनस्य आश्रमं Dwaipayana Vyasa's, hermitage, साक्षात् अगात् (Narada) directly proceeded.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Having thus offered his respectful obeisances to the primal Sage Narayana, and all His great disciples, Narada proceeded directly to the hermitage of my father, Dwaipayana Vyasa.

सभाजितो भगवता कृतासनपरिग्रह: ।
तस्मै तद् वर्णयामास नारायणमुखाच्छ्रुतम् ॥10.87.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: सभाजितः being honored, भगवता by the divine sage Vyasa, तस्मै unto him (sage Vyasa), तत् that (teaching of the Shruti-stuti), वर्णयामास described/recounted, नारायण-मुखात् from the mouth of Lord Narayana, श्रुतम् which was heard

Anvaya: भगवता सभाजितः कृतासनपरिग्रहः (नारदः) नारायणमुखात् श्रुतं तत् तस्मै वर्णयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवता सभाजितः By the divine sage Vyasa, being welcomed, कृतासनपरिग्रहः and having accepted the seat offered to him, (नारदः) Narada, नारायणमुखात् directly from the lotus mouth of Lord Narayana, श्रुतं that which was heard, तत् that sacred narrative, तस्मै वर्णयामास to Vyasa, fully narrated.

Simple Meaning: Having been duly honored by Vyasa and having accepted the seat offered to him, Narada recounted to Vyasa, that very transcendental knowledge of the Shruti-stuti which he had received Sage Narayana.

इत्येतद् वर्णितं राजन् यन्नः प्रश्नः कृतस्त्वया ।
यथा ब्रह्मण्यनिर्देश्ये निर्गुणेऽपि मनश्चरेत् ॥10.87.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, एतत् this, वर्णितम् has been described/recounted, राजन् O King Parikshit, यत् which, नः our (or your), प्रश्नः question, कृतः was asked, त्वया by you, यथा how, ब्रह्मणि in the Supreme Brahman, अनिर्देश्ये which is beyond words and definitions, निर्गुणे अपि even though devoid of material attributes, मनः the mind, चरेत् can move/become absorbed/find rest

Anvaya: राजन्! त्वया यः प्रश्नः कृतः—'अनिर्देश्ये निर्गुणे अपि ब्रह्मणि मनः कथं चरेत्' इति—एतत् मया वर्णितम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King Parikshit! त्वया by you, यः प्रश्नः कृतः the question which was raised— “अनिर्देश्ये Which is beyond verbal description, निर्गुणे अपि and devoid of material attributes, ब्रह्मणि in the Supreme Brahman, मनः the mind, कथं चरेत् how can become fixed?"—एतत् मया वर्णितम् all of this has been described by me.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka concluded: O King Parikshit! This completely answers the profound question you asked at the beginning: How can the human mind become fixed and absorbed in the Supreme Brahman, which is beyond all material definitions and transcendental to the modes of nature?

योऽस्योत्प्रेक्षक आदिमध्यनिधने योऽव्यक्तजीवेश्वरो यः सृष्ट्वेदमनुप्रविश्य ऋषिणा चक्रे पुरः शास्ति ताः ।
यं संपद्य जहात्यजामनुशयी सुप्तः कुलायं यथा तं कैवल्यनिरस्तयोनिमभयं ध्यायेदजस्रं हरिम् ॥10.87.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः who, अस्य of this universe, उत्प्रेक्षकः the supreme director/witness, आदि-मध्य-निधने in its creation, maintenance, and dissolution, यः who, अव्यक्त-जीव-ईश्वरः the controller/Lord of unmanifest nature (Prakriti) and the individual living entities (Jivas), यः who, सृष्ट्वा having created, इदम् this material world, अनुप्रविश्य having entered within it, ऋषिणा with the living entity (the inner conscious seer), चक्रे fashioned, पुरः the physical bodies/dwellings, शास्ति rules/controls, ताः them, यम् whom, संपद्य attaining, जहाति casts off/abandons, अजाम् the unborn illusory nature (Maya), अनुशयी the conditioned soul who was sleeping in ignorance, सुप्तः a sleeping person, कुलायम् the body/nest (dream-body upon awakening), यथा just as, तम् Him, कैवल्य-निरस्त-योनिम् who eliminates all material birth through oneness with Him, अभयम् the embodiment of fearlessness, ध्यायेत् one should meditate, अजस्रम् incessantly/continuously, हरिम् upon Lord Hari

Anvaya: यः अस्य आदिमध्यनिधने उत्प्रेक्षकः, यः अव्यक्तजीवेश्वरः, यः इदं सृष्ट्वा अनुप्रविश्य ऋषिणा (जीवेन सह) पुरः चक्रे ताः (च) शास्ति, यं संपद्य अनुशयी (जीवः) सुप्तः कुलायं यथा अजां जहाति, कैवल्यनिरस्तयोनिम् अभयं तं हरिम् (जनः) अजस्रं ध्यायेत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः अस्य आदिमध्यनिधने उत्प्रेक्षकः He who, of this entire universe, during its creation, sustenance, and ultimate dissolution, is the supreme supervisor and impartial witness; यः अव्यक्तजीवेश्वरः who is the master and sovereign ruler of unmanifest nature (Prakriti) and all individual souls (Jivas); यः who, इदं सृष्ट्वा this universe, having created, अनुप्रविश्य having entered within as the Paramatma, ऋषिणा पुरः चक्रे along with the conscious individual soul, physical bodies and realms of experience, fashioned, ताः (च) शास्ति and controls/directs them from within; यं संपद्य whom, attaining through spiritual realization, अनुशयी the soul who had been sleeping in material illusion, सुप्तः कुलायं यथा a person waking from sleep, just as he discards his dream body or nest upon awakening, अजां जहाति completely casts off the unborn illusory energy (Maya); (तं) कैवल्यनिरस्तयोनिम् Him who terminates all material birth through oneness with Him, अभयं तम् who is the supreme source of fearlessness, Hari, अजस्रं ध्यायेत् continuously without interruption, one should meditate upon.

Simple Meaning: He who is the supreme director and witness of the universe's creation, maintenance, and dissolution; who is the Lord of both unmanifest nature and all living beings; who, after creating this world, enters within it alongside the individual soul to fashion bodily forms and rule them from within—He is the ultimate goal. Attaining Him, the sleeping soul awakens and discards the illusory material energy, just as a person waking up forgets the body seen in a dream. One should continuously meditate upon that Lord Hari, the supreme source of fearlessness, who forever eradicates the cycle of material birth and death.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “नारदनारायणसंवादे वेदस्तुतिः” नाम सप्ताशीतितमः अध्यायः ॥87॥

Thus ends the eighty-seventh discourse named “The song of the praises by the Vedas personified in the dialogue between Narada and Narayana”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥

In Hindu scriptures, the seven heavens (or higher realms) are called the Sapta Urdhva Lokas (सप्त ऊर्ध्व लोकाः), which exist above the earthly plane (Bhuloka). These realms are described in various texts, including the Puranas, Upanishads, and Smritis.

The Seven Heavens (Upper Realms)

Beyond the Seven Heavens:

Vaikuntha (वैकुण्ठ) – The eternal abode of Lord Vishnu
Goloka (गोलोक) – The highest spiritual realm, Krishna’s divine abode.

These seven realms represent ascending levels of spiritual progress, and the ultimate goal is Moksha (liberation), which is beyond all these lokas.
