Conclusion of the Rajasuya sacrifice and Duryodhana’s humiliation

दशमः स्कन्धः

राजसूययज्ञस्य समाप्तिः दुर्योधनस्य मानभङ्गः च​​ नाम पञ्चसप्ततितमः अध्यायः

राजा उवाच
अजातशत्रोस्तं दृष्ट्वा राजसूयमहोदयम् ।
सर्वे मुमुदिरे ब्रह्मन् नृदेवा ये समागताः ॥10.75.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजा उवाच King Parikshit said, अजातशत्रोः of King Yudhisthira (one whose enemy is never born), तम् that, दृष्ट्वा having seen, राजसूय-महोदयम् the grand success/glory of the Rajasuya sacrifice, सर्वे all, मुमुदिरे rejoiced, ब्रह्मन् O sage (Shri Shuka), नृ-देवाः the kings/rulers of men, ये who, समागताः assembled

Anvaya: राजा उवाच—ब्रह्मन्! समागताः ये सर्वे नृदेवाः, (ते) अजातशत्रोः तं राजसूयमहोदयं दृष्ट्वा मुमुदिरे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजा उवाच King Parikshit said, ब्रह्मन् O sage, समागताः ये सर्वे नृदेवाः all those rulers of men who had assembled there, अजातशत्रोः तं राजसूयमहोदयं दृष्ट्वा seeing that magnificent success of the Rajasuya sacrifice performed by King Yudhisthira, मुमुदिरे rejoiced.

Simple Meaning: King Parikshit said: O sage, all the kings and noble rulers who had assembled there rejoiced upon witnessing the grand success and splendor of King Yudhisthira's Rajasuya sacrifice.

दुर्योधनं वर्जयित्वा राजानः सर्षयः सुराः ।
इति श्रुतं नो भगवंस्तत्र कारणमुच्यताम् ॥10.75.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुर्योधनम् Duryodhana, वर्जयित्वा except for, राजानः the kings, स-ऋषयः along with the sages, सुराः the devas, इति thus, श्रुतम् heard, नः by us, भगवन् O venerable one, तत्र in that matter, कारणम् the reason, उच्यताम् please tell/explain

Anvaya: भगवन्! दुर्योधनं वर्जयित्वा राजानः सर्षयः सुराः (मुमुदिरे) इति नः श्रुतं, तत्र कारणम् उच्यताम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवन् O venerable sage Sukadeva, दुर्योधनं वर्जयित्वा except for Duryodhana, राजानः सर्षयः सुराः (मुमुदिरे) all the kings, sages, and devas rejoiced, इति नः श्रुतं thus it has been heard by us, तत्र कारणम् उच्यताम् please state the reason behind this.

Simple Meaning: King Parikshit asked: O venerable sage, we have heard that everyone—sages, devas, and kings alike—rejoiced, except for Duryodhana. Please explain the reason behind his discontent.

ऋषिः उवाच
पितामहस्य ते यज्ञे राजसूये महात्मनः ।
बान्धवाः परिचर्यायां तस्यासन् प्रेमबन्धनाः ॥10.75.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऋषिः उवाच Shri Shuka said, पितामहस्य of grandfather, ते your, यज्ञे in the sacrifice, राजसूये at the Rajasuya, महात्मनः of the great soul (Yudhishthira), बान्धवाः relatives/kinsmen, परिचर्यायाम् in the service/management, तस्य his, आसन् were engaged, प्रेम-बन्धनाः bound by love and affection

Anvaya: ऋषिः उवाच—ते महात्मनः पितामहस्य राजसूये यज्ञे बान्धवाः प्रेमबन्धनाः (सन्तः) तस्य परिचर्यायाम् आसन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ऋषिः उवाच Shri Shuka said, ते महात्मनः पितामहस्य at the Rajasuya sacrifice of your great-souled grandfather Yudhishthira, बान्धवाः all his kinsmen and relatives, प्रेमबन्धनाः (सन्तः) being bound by deep bonds of love, तस्य परिचर्यायाम् आसन् were joyfully engaged in serving him and managing the affairs.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King, at the great Rajasuya sacrifice of your noble grandfather Yudhisthira, all his relatives were bound to him by bonds of deep love, and they eagerly took up various duties to serve him in conducting the grand event.

भीमो महानसाध्यक्षो धनाध्यक्षः सुयोधनः ।
सहदेवस्तु पूजायां नकुलो द्र्व्यसाधने ॥10.75.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: भीमः Bhima, महानस-अध्यक्षः superintendent/in charge of the kitchen and food, धन-अध्यक्षः in charge of the treasury/finances, सुयोधनः Duryodhana, सहदेवः तु Sahadeva, पूजायाम् in charge of receiving and honoring guests, नकुलः Nakula, द्रव्य-साधने in procuring supplies and materials

Anvaya: भीमः महानसाध्यक्षः, सुयोधनः धनाध्यक्षः, सहदेवः तु पूजायां (नियुक्तः), नकुलः द्रव्यसाधने (नियुक्तः आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भीमः महानसाध्यक्षः Bhima was the superintendent of the kitchen, सुयोधनः धनाध्यक्षः Duryodhana was the officer in charge of the treasury and gold, सहदेवः तु पूजायां (नियुक्तः) Sahadeva was appointed to honor and welcome the distinguished guests, नकुलः द्रव्यसाधने (नियुक्तः आसीत्) and Nakula was assigned to procure all necessary provisions and supplies.

Simple Meaning: Bhima took charge of the kitchen, while Duryodhana managed the treasury and distributions. Sahadeva was entrusted with properly receiving and honoring guests, and Nakula was responsible for gathering all required supplies.

गुरुशुश्रुषणे जिष्णुः कृष्णः पादावनेजने ।
परिवेषणे द्रुपदजा कर्णो दाने महामनाः ॥10.75.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: गुरु-शुश्रूषणे in the service of the elders/gurus, जिष्णुः Jishnu (Arjuna), कृष्णः Krishna, पाद-अवनेजने in washing the feet of guests, परिवेषणे in serving meals, द्रुपद-जा Draupadi (the daughter of Drupada), कर्णः Karna, दाने in bestowing gifts/charity, महा-मनाः the magnanimous one

Anvaya: जिष्णुः गुरुशुश्रूषणे, कृष्णः पादावनेजने, द्रुपदजा परिवेषणे, (तथा) महामनाः कर्णः दाने (नियुक्ताः आसन्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जिष्णुः गुरुशुश्रूषणे Arjuna was devoted to serving the elders, कृष्णः पादावनेजने Lord Krishna took the humble duty of washing the feet of all guests, द्रुपदजा परिवेषणे Draupadi was in charge of serving food, (तथा) महामनाः कर्णः दाने (नियुक्ताः आसन्) and the magnanimous Karna was assigned to distribute gifts and charity.

Simple Meaning: Arjuna personally attended to the spiritual masters and elders, while Krishna took upon Himself the humble service of washing the feet of arriving guests. Draupadi supervised the serving of meals, and the magnanimous Karna oversaw the distribution of charity.

युयुधानो विकर्णश्च हार्दिक्यो विदुरादयः ।
बाह्लीकपुत्राभूर्याद्या ये च सन्तर्दनादयः ॥10.75.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: युयुधानः Yuyudhana (Satyaki), विकर्णः Vikarna, च and, हार्दिक्यः Hardikya (Kritavarma), विदुर-आदयः Vidura and others, बाह्लीक-पुत्राः the sons of Bahlika, भूरि-आद्याः led by Bhurishravas, ये who, च and, सन्तर्दन-आदयः Santardana and others

Anvaya: युयुधानः, विकर्णः च, हार्दिक्यः, विदुरादयः, भूर्याद्याः बाह्लीकपुत्राः, ये च सन्तर्दनादयः (ते सर्वे नाना कार्येषु नियुक्ताः आसन्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: युयुधानः Yuyudhana, विकर्णः च and Vikarna, हार्दिक्यः Kritavarma, विदुरादयः Vidura and other elders, भूर्याद्याः बाह्लीकपुत्राः the sons of Bahlika starting with Bhurishravas, ये च सन्तर्दनादयः and those led by Santardana, (ते सर्वे नाना कार्येषु नियुक्ताः आसन्) all of them were actively appointed to oversee various important duties.

Simple Meaning: Yuyudhana, Vikarna, Kritavarma, Vidura, the sons of Bahlika led by Bhurishravas, along with Santardana and many other heroes, were all assigned specific responsibilities to ensure the sacrifice ran smoothly.

निरूपिताः महायज्ञे नाना कर्मसु ते तदा ।
प्रवर्तन्ते स्म राजेन्द्र राज्ञः प्रियचिकीर्षवः ॥10.75.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: निरूपिताः appointed/assigned, महा-यज्ञे in that great sacrifice, नाना-कर्मसु in various duties and tasks, ते they, तदा then, प्रवर्तन्ते स्म engaged/executed, राजेन्द्र O king among kings (Parikshit), राज्ञः of King Yudhisthira, प्रिय-चिकीर्षवः desiring to do what was pleasing

Anvaya: राजेन्द्र! तदा महायज्ञे नाना कर्मसु निरूपिताः ते राज्ञः प्रियचिकीर्षवः (सन्तः) प्रवर्तन्ते स्म।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजेन्द्र O king among kings Parikshit, तदा at that time, महायज्ञे in that grand sacrifice, नाना कर्मसु निरूपिताः being assigned to various tasks, ते they all, राज्ञः प्रियचिकीर्षवः (सन्तः) desiring to act for the pleasure and satisfaction of King Yudhisthira, प्रवर्तन्ते स्म diligently carried out their respective duties.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, assigned to their respective tasks in that grand sacrifice, all these noble personalities performed their duties with great joy and dedication, eager to please and honor King Yudhisthira.

ऋत्विक्सदस्यबहुवित्सु सुहृत्तमेषु स्विष्टेषु सूनृतसमर्हणदक्षिणाभिः ।
चैद्ये च सात्वतपतेश्चरणं प्रविष्टे चक्रुस्ततस्त्ववभृथस्नपनं द्युनद्याम् ॥10.75.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऋत्विक्-सदस्य-बहु-विद्-सु among the priests the assembly members and the vastly learned scholars, सुहृत्-तमेषु among the most dear friends and well-wishers, स्विष्टेषु when all were thoroughly worshiped and satisfied, सूनृत-समर्हण-दक्षिणाभिः with sweet truthful words respectful honors and generous gifts, चैद्ये when the ruler of Chedi Shishupala, च and, सात्वत-पतेः of the protector of the Satvatas - Krishna, चरणम् the lotus feet, प्रविष्टे had merged into, चक्रुः they performed, ततः thereafter, तु indeed, अवभृथ-स्नपनम् the final ceremonial purification bath, द्युनद्याम् in the sacred heavenly river Ganges

Anvaya: ततः तु ऋत्विक्सदस्यबहुवित्सु सुहृत्तमेषु सूनृतसमर्हणदक्षिणाभिः स्विष्टेषु, चैद्ये च सात्वतपतेः चरणं प्रविष्टे, द्युनद्याम् अवभृथस्नपनं चक्रुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः तु Thereafter indeed, ऋत्विक्सदस्यबहुवित्सु सुहृत्तमेषु when the officiating priests members of the assembly vastly learned scholars and most dear friends, सूनृतसमर्हणदक्षिणाभिः स्विष्टेषु were thoroughly honored and pleased through pleasing truthful speech respectful worship and suitable monetary offerings, चैद्ये च and when Shishupala, सात्वतपतेः चरणं प्रविष्टे had merged into the lotus feet of Lord Krishna the master of the Satvatas, द्युनद्याम् अवभृथस्नपनं चक्रुः they performed the auspicious final cleansing bath in the holy Ganges river.

Simple Meaning: After the priests assembly members learned scholars and close friends were fully honored and satisfied with kind truthful words respectful worship and rich gifts and after Shishupala merged into the lotus feet of Lord Krishna they all performed the sacred concluding purification bath in the holy river Ganges.

मृदङ्गशङ्खपणवधुन्धुर्यानकगोमुखाः।
वादित्राणि विचित्राणि नेदुरावभृथोत्सवे ॥10.75.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: मृदङ्ग-शङ्ख-पणव-धुन्धुर्य-आनक-गोमुखाः drums, conchshells, kettledrums, large drums, mridanga-like instruments, and horns, वादित्राणि musical instruments, विचित्राणि various types, नेदुः resounded/echoed, अवभृथ-उत्सवे during the festival of the final bath

Anvaya: अवभृथोत्सवे मृदङ्गशङ्खपणवधुन्धुर्यानकगोमुखाः विचित्राणि वादित्राणि नेदुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अवभृथोत्सवे During the grand celebration of the final ritual bath, मृदङ्गशङ्खपणवधुन्धुर्यानकगोमुखाः various drums, conch shells, kettle drums and horn instruments, विचित्राणि वादित्राणि diverse musical instruments, नेदुः loudly resounded together.

Simple Meaning: During the grand festival of the final ceremonial bath, a wide variety of musical instruments including mridangas conch shells, kettle drums, large drums and horns vibrated with resounding music.

नर्तक्यो ननृतुर्हृष्टा गायका यूथशो जगुः ।
वीणावेणुतलोन्नादस्तेषां स दिवमस्पृशत् ॥10.75.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: नर्तक्यः female dancers, ननृतुः danced, हृष्टाः joyful, गायकाः singers, यूथशः in groups, जगुः sang, वीणा-वेणु-तल-उन्नादः the booming blend of stringed lutes, flutes and hand cymbals, तेषाम् of them, सः that sound, दिवम् heaven, अस्पृशत् reached/touched

Anvaya: हृष्टाः नर्तक्यः ननृतुः, गायकाः यूथशः जगुः, तेषां सः वीणावेणुतलोन्नादः दिवम् अस्पृशत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: हृष्टाः नर्तक्यः ननृतुः The joyful female dancers danced, गायकाः यूथशः जगुः the singers sang together in groups, तेषां सः वीणावेणुतलोन्नादः that harmonious loud sound produced by their lutes, flutes and hand cymbals, दिवम् अस्पृशत् reached all the way up to heaven.

Simple Meaning: Exuberant female dancers danced with delight and singers performed in groups while the resounding music created by lutes, flutes and hand cymbals echoed all the way up to heaven.

चित्रध्वजपताकाग्रैरिभेन्द्रस्यन्दनार्वभिः ।
स्वलङ्कृतैर्भटैर्भूपा निर्ययू रुक्ममालिनः ॥10.75.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: चित्र-ध्वज-पताका-अग्रैः adorned with colorful banners flags and decorative tops, इभ-इन्द्र-स्यन्दन-अर्वभिः riding on great royal elephants chariots and horses, सु-अलङ्कृतैः well decorated, भटैः with soldiers, भूपाः the rulers, निर्ययूः marched forth, रुक्म-मालिनः wearing golden necklaces

Anvaya: चित्रध्वजपताकाग्रैः इभेन्द्रस्यन्दनार्वभिः स्वलङ्कृतैः भटैः रुक्ममालिनः भूपाः निर्ययूः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: चित्रध्वजपताकाग्रैः इभेन्द्रस्यन्दनार्वभिः Accompanied by magnificent elephants, chariots and swift horses decorated with colorful flags and banners, स्वलङ्कृतैः भटैः along with splendidly arrayed guards, रुक्ममालिनः भूपाः the royal kings wearing golden garlands, निर्ययूः marched out in grand procession.

Simple Meaning: Wearing brilliant golden necklaces and accompanied by nicely adorned soldiers riding on majestic royal elephants chariots and fine horses decorated with colorful flags and banners the rulers marched out in grand procession.

यदुसृञ्जयकाम्बोजकुरुकेकयकोसलाः ।
कम्पयन्तो भुवं सैन्यैर्यजमानपुरःसराः ॥10.75.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: यदु-सृञ्जय-काम्बोज-कुरु-केकय-कोसलाः the warriors and rulers of the Yadu, Srinjaya, Kamboja, Kuru, Kekaya and Kosala dynasties, कम्पयन्तः causing to tremble, भुवम् the earth, सैन्यैः with their vast armies, यजमान-पुरः-सराः keeping the performer of the sacrifice King Yudhisthira in the lead

Anvaya: यदुसृञ्जयकाम्बोजकुरुकेकयकोसलाः सैन्यैः भुवं कम्पयन्तः यजमानपुरःसराः (निर्ययूः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यदुसृञ्जयकाम्बोजकुरुकेकयकोसलाः The Yadu, Srinjaya, Kamboja, Kuru, Kekaya and Kosala clans, सैन्यैः भुवं कम्पयन्तः making the entire earth shake under the weight of their armies, यजमानपुरःसराः following right behind King Yudhisthira the master of the sacrifice, (निर्ययूः) proceeded forward in procession.

Simple Meaning: The great rulers of the Yadu, Srinjaya, Kamboja, Kuru, Kekaya and Kosala dynasties marched forth behind King Yudhisthira the performer of the sacrifice while their massive military forces caused the earth to shake.

Word-by-word meaning: अथ then, ऋत्विजः the priests, महा-शीलाः of exalted character, सदस्याः the assembly members, ब्रह्म-वादिनः learned speakers on the Vedas, ब्रह्म-क्षत्रिय-विट्-शूद्राः brahmanas, kshatriyas, vaishyas and shudras, राजानः the kings, ये who, समागताः had gathered

Anvaya: अथ ये महाशीलाः ऋत्विजः, ब्रह्मवादिनः सदस्याः, ब्रह्मक्षत्रियविट्शूद्राः, राजानः समागताः (ते सर्वे)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ Then, ये महाशीलाः ऋत्विजः those highly virtuous officiating priests, ब्रह्मवादिनः सदस्याः learned Vedantic assembly members, ब्रह्मक्षत्रियविट्शूद्राः members of all four classes - brahmanas, kshatriyas, vaishyas and shudras, राजानः समागताः and all visiting kings who had assembled, (ते सर्वे) all of them.

Simple Meaning: Then all those virtuous priests, learned Vedic assembly members, visiting kings, and people belonging to all four classes brahmanas, kshatriyas, vaishyas and shudras who had gathered there.

देवर्षिपितृभूतानि लोकपालाः सहानुगाः ।
पूजितास्तमनुज्ञाप्य स्वधामानि ययुर्नृप ॥10.75.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: देव-ऋषि-पितृ-भूतानि the devas, sages, ancestors and living beings, लोक-पालाः the guardians of the planets/realms, सह-अनुगाः along with their followers, पूजिताः having been properly honored, तम् King Yudhisthira, अनुज्ञाप्य taking permission from, स्व-धामानि to their respective abodes, ययुः departed, नृप O King Parikshit

Anvaya: नृप! देवर्षिपितृभूतानि सहानुगाः लोकपालाः पूजिताः तम् अनुज्ञाप्य स्वधामानि ययुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King Parikshit, देवर्षिपितृभूतानि the devas, celestial sages, ancestors and various living entities, सहानुगाः लोकपालाः the cosmic protectors along with their retinues, पूजिताः having been lavishly honored by Yudhisthira, तम् अनुज्ञाप्य asking his permission to leave, स्वधामानि ययुः departed for their own respective spiritual and heavenly abodes.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, having been properly honored by King Yudhisthira, the devas sages ancestors living beings and planetary guardians along with their followers took his permission and departed for their respective abodes.

हरिदासस्य राजर्षे राजसूयमहोदयम् ।
नैवातृप्यन् प्रशंसन्तः पिबन् मर्त्यऽमृतं यथा ॥10.75.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: हरि-दासस्य of the servant of Lord Hari, राज-ऋषेः of the saintly king Yudhisthira, राजसूय-महोदयम् the great success and magnificence of the Rajasuya sacrifice, न एव अतृप्यन् were never satiated, प्रशंसन्तः glorifying, पिबन् drinking, मर्त्यः a mortal, अमृतम् nectar, यथा just as

Anvaya: मर्त्यः अमृतं पिबन् यथा, (तथा ते) हरिदासस्य राजर्षेः राजसूयमहोदयं प्रशंसन्तः न एव अतृप्यन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मर्त्यः अमृतं पिबन् यथा Just as a mortal person drinking nectar never feels satiated, (तथा ते) in the same way they, हरिदासस्य राजर्षेः of the saintly king Yudhisthira the devotee-servant of Lord Hari, राजसूयमहोदयं प्रशंसन्तः repeatedly praising the magnificent grandeur of the Rajasuya sacrifice, न एव अतृप्यन् never felt satisfied or tired of praising it.

Simple Meaning: Just as a mortal drinking immortal nectar never feels fully satiated, those who departed never grew tired of praising the magnificent success of the Rajasuya sacrifice performed by the saintly King Yudhisthira, the great servant of Lord Hari.

ततो युधिष्ठिरो राजा सुहृत्सम्बन्धिबान्धवान् ।
प्रेम्णा निवासयामास कृष्णं च त्यागकातरः ॥10.75.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, युधिष्ठिरः राजा King Yudhisthira, सुहृत्-सम्बन्धि-बान्धवान् his well-wishers, relatives and dear friends, प्रेम्णा out of deep love, निवासयामास requested to stay back, कृष्णम् च and Krishna, त्याग-कातरः distressed at the thought of separation

Anvaya: ततः त्यागकातरः राजा युधिष्ठिरः प्रेम्णा सुहृत्सम्बन्धिबान्धवान् कृष्णं च निवासयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः Then, त्यागकातरः राजा युधिष्ठिरः King Yudhisthira feeling deeply distressed at the thought of separation from his loved ones, प्रेम्णा out of pure affection, सुहृत्सम्बन्धिबान्धवान् his friends family members and kinsmen, कृष्णं च and especially Krishna, निवासयामास requested them to prolong their stay.

Simple Meaning: Then King Yudhisthira feeling deeply distressed at the thought of parting ways with his beloved ones affectionately requested his friends relatives and Krishna to stay back longer with him.

भगवानपि तत्राङ्ग न्यवात्सीत्तत्प्रियङ्करः ।
प्रस्थाप्य यदुवीरांश्च साम्बादींश्च कुशस्थलीम् ॥10.75.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: भगवान् the Supreme Lord, अपि also, तत्र there in Indraprastha, अङ्ग O King Parikshit, न्यवात्सीत् resided, तत्-प्रियम्-करः doing what was pleasing to King Yudhisthira, प्रस्थाप्य having sent back, यदु-वीरान् the Yadu heroes, च and, साम्ब-आदीन् Sambha and others, च and, कुशस्थलीम् to the city of Dwaraka

Anvaya: अङ्ग भगवान् अपि यदुवीरान् साम्बादीन् च कुशस्थलीं  प्रस्थाप्य तत्प्रियङ्करः (सन्) तत्र न्यवात्सीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अङ्ग O King Parikshit, भगवान् अपि Lord Krishna also, यदुवीरान् साम्बादीन् च कुशस्थलीं प्रस्थाप्य sending back the heroic Yadu warriors led by Samba and others to Dvaraka, तत्प्रियङ्करः doing everything to please his beloved devotee Yudhisthira, तत्र न्यवात्सीत् remained and resided there in Indraprastha for some time.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, Krishna sent Samba and the other Yadu heroes back to Dwaraka, and out of deep affection for King Yudhisthira, He stayed back in Indraprastha to please him.

इत्थं राजा धर्मसुतो मनोरथमहार्णवम् ।
सुदुस्तरं समुत्तीर्य कृष्णेनासीद् गतज्वरः ॥10.75.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: इत्थम् in this manner, राजा King Yudhisthira, धर्म-सुतः the son of Dharmaraja, मनोरथ-महा-अर्णवम् the great ocean of his desires, सु-दुस्तरम् extremely difficult to cross over, समुत्तीर्य having fully crossed over, कृष्णेन with the help of Krishna, आसीत् became, गत-ज्वरः free from all anxiety/feverish worries

Anvaya: इत्थं धर्मसुतः राजा कृष्णेन सुदुस्तरं मनोरथमहार्णवं समुत्तीर्य गतज्वरः आसीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इत्थं In this way, धर्मसुतः राजा King Yudhisthira the son of Dharma, कृष्णेन through the divine grace of Krishna, सुदुस्तरं मनोरथमहार्णवं समुत्तीर्य having successfully navigated across the vast ocean of his long cherished desire (of performing Rajasuya sacrifice) that was otherwise impossible to accomplish, गतज्वरः आसीत् became free from all anxieties.

Simple Meaning: In this manner King Yudhisthira, the son of Dharma, successfully crossed the vast and formidable ocean of his desire of performing Rajasuya sacrifice through the grace of Lord Krishna, and became free from all anxieties.

एकदान्तःपुरे तस्य वीक्ष्य दुर्योधनः श्रियम् ।
अतप्यद् राजसूयस्य महित्वं चाच्युतात्मनः ॥10.75.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: एकदा once, अन्तःपुरे in the inner palace, तस्य of King Yudhisthira, वीक्ष्य having observed, दुर्योधनः Duryodhana, श्रियम् the opulence/resplendence, अतप्यत् burned with envy/suffered, राजसूयस्य of the Rajasuya sacrifice, महित्वम् the grand glory/magnificence, च and, अच्युत-आत्मनः of King Yudhisthira whose soul is dedicated to Achyuta - Krishna

Anvaya: एकदा दुर्योधनः अच्युतात्मनः तस्य अन्तःपुरे श्रियम् राजसूयस्य महित्वं च वीक्ष्य अतप्यत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एकदा Once, दुर्योधनः Duryodhana, अच्युतात्मनः तस्य अन्तःपुरे श्रियम् the vast wealth and royal opulence within the palace of the King Yudhishthira whose heart belonged to Lord Achyuta, राजसूयस्य महित्वं च and the great glory of the Rajasuya sacrifice, वीक्ष्य अतप्यत् seeing all this burned inwardly with intense jealousy.

Simple Meaning: Once Duryodhana observed the vast opulence within King Yudhisthira palace and the magnificent glory of the Rajasuya sacrifice performed by the King who was fully surrendered to Lord Krishna, and he burned with intense envy.

यस्मिन् नरेन्द्रदितिजेन्द्रसुरेन्द्रलक्ष्मीर्नाना विभान्ति किल विश्वसृजोपक्लृप्ताः ।
ताभिः पतीन् द्रुपदराजसुतोपतस्थे यस्यां विषक्तहृदयः कुरुराडतप्यत् ॥10.75.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: यस्मिन् in which palace, नरेन्द्र-दितिज-इन्द्र-सुर-इन्द्र-लक्ष्मीः the opulences of mortal kings, daitya kings and the king of devas - Indra, नाना in various ways, विभान्ति shone, किल indeed, विश्व-सृज-उपक्लृप्ताः crafted by Mayadanava the architect of the universe, ताभिः with those opulences, पतीन् her husbands, द्रुपद-राज-सुता Draupadi - the daughter of King Drupada, उपतस्थे served, यस्याम् for whom, विषक्त-हृदयः attached at heart, कुरु-राट् the king of the Kurus Duryodhana, अतप्यत् burned with anguish

Anvaya: यस्मिन् विश्वसृजोपक्लृप्ताः नरेन्द्रदितिजेन्द्रसुरेन्द्रलक्ष्मीः नाना विभान्ति स्म, द्रुपदराजसुता ताभिः पतीन् उपतस्थे, यस्यां विषक्तहृदयः कुरुराट् अतप्यत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यस्मिन् In which palace, विश्वसृजोपक्लृप्ताः fashioned by the great architect Mayadanava, नरेन्द्रदितिजेन्द्रसुरेन्द्रलक्ष्मीः the sum total of opulences belonging to rulers of the earth, daitya lords and Lord Indra, नाना विभान्ति स्म shone brilliantly in multiple forms, द्रुपदराजसुता Draupadi - the princess of Drupada, ताभिः with those royal riches, पतीन् उपतस्थे served her Pandava husbands, यस्यां for whose beauty and majesty, विषक्तहृदयः possessing an enamored and envious heart, कुरुराट् अतप्यत् Duryodhana the Kuru king burned with torment.

Simple Meaning: In that palace, which was crafted by Mayadanava and shone with the combined opulences of human kings, asuras, and Indra himself, Draupadi served her husbands. Seeing that incomparable majesty, Duryodhana who was attracted to Draupadi, burned with torment and envy.

यस्मिंस्तदा मधुपतेर्महिषीसहस्रं श्रोणीभरेण शनकैः क्वणदङ्घ्रिशोभम् ।
मध्ये सुचारुकुचकुङ्कुमशोणहारं श्रीमन्मुखं प्रचलकुण्डलकुन्तलाढ्यम् ॥10.75.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: यस्मिन् in which palace, तदा then, मधुपतेः of Lord Krishna - the Lord of the Madhus, महिषी-सहस्रम् the thousands of queens, श्रोणी-भरेण due to the heavy weight of their hips, शनकैः slowly, क्वणत्-अङ्घ्रि-शोभम् beautiful with jingling ankle-bells, मध्ये with slender waists, सु-चारु-कुच-कुङ्कुम-शोण-हारम् whose necklaces were stained red with kumkuma powder from their shapely breasts, श्रीमत्-मुखम् with glorious radiant faces, प्रचल-कुण्डल-कुन्तलाढ्यम् adorned with swinging earrings and dangling locks of hair

Anvaya: यस्मिन् तदा मधुपतेः महिषीसहस्रं श्रोणीभरेण शनकैः (चलत्) क्वणदङ्घ्रिशोभं मध्ये (कृशम्) सुचारुकुचकुङ्कुमशोणहारं प्रचलकुण्डलकुन्तलाढ्यं श्रीमन्मुखं (बिभ्रत् विरेजौ)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यस्मिन् In which palace, तदा at that time, मधुपतेः महिषीसहस्रं thousands of royal queens belonging to Lord Krishna, श्रोणीभरेण शनकैः walking slowly owing to the weight of their hips, क्वणदङ्घ्रिशोभं made alluring by the soft tinkling of their anklets, मध्ये slender at the waist, सुचारुकुचकुङ्कुमशोणहारं carrying pearl necklaces reddened by kumkuma paste from their exquisite breasts, प्रचलकुण्डलकुन्तलाढ्यं श्रीमन्मुखं displaying radiant countenances adorned with swinging earrings and charming locks of hair.

Simple Meaning: In that palace, thousands of Lord Krishna's queens walked gracefully with slow steps due to the weight of their hips, their anklets tinkling melodiously. Slender-waisted, their necklaces were tinted red by the kumkuma from their breasts, and their beautiful faces were adorned with swinging earrings and dangling locks of hair.

जले च स्थलवद् भ्रान्त्या मयमायाविमोहितः ॥10.75.37॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्थले on dry land, अभ्यगृह्णात् lifted up, वस्त्र-अन्तम् the end of his garment, जलम् water, मत्वा thinking, स्थले on dry land, अपतत् fell down, जले into water, च and, स्थल-वत् like dry land, भ्रान्त्या due to illusion, मय-माया-विमोहितः completely bewildered by the magical craft of Maya Danava

Anvaya: मयमायाविमोहितः (दुर्योधनः) स्थले जलं मत्वा वस्त्रान्तम् अभ्यगृह्णात्, भ्रान्त्या जले च स्थलवत् (मत्वा) स्थले अपतत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मयमायाविमोहितः Duryodhana being utterly confused by the optical illusions crafted by Maya Danava, स्थले जलं मत्वा mistaking solid dry floor for a body of water, वस्त्रान्तम् अभ्यगृह्णात् lifted the hem of his lower garment, भ्रान्त्या जले च स्थलवत् and owing to delusion mistaking actual water for dry ground, स्थले अपतत् on that place fell.

Simple Meaning: Completely bewildered by the magical illusions crafted by Mayadanava in the assembly hall, Duryodhana lifted the hem of his dress on dry floor thinking it was water, and later, mistaking actual water for dry land, he stepped right into it and fell down.

जहास भीमस्तं दृष्ट्वा स्त्रियो नृपतयोऽपरे ।
निवार्यमाणा अप्यङ्ग राज्ञा कृष्णानुमोदिताः ॥10.75.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: जहास laughed, भीमः Bhima, तम् him (Duryodhana), दृष्ट्वा seeing, स्त्रियः the women, नृपतयः अपरे and other kings, निवार्यमाणाः although being restrained, अपि even, अङ्ग O King Parikshit, राज्ञा by King Yudhisthira, कृष्ण-अनुमोदिताः quietly encouraged by Krishna

Anvaya: अङ्ग! तं दृष्ट्वा राज्ञा निवार्यमाणाः अपि भीमः स्त्रियः अपरे नृपतयः कृष्णानुमोदिताः (सन्तः) जहास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अङ्ग O King Parikshit, तं दृष्ट्वा seeing him slip and fall, राज्ञा निवार्यमाणाः अपि although being checked and restrained by King Yudhisthira, भीमः Bhima, स्त्रियः the royal women, अपरे नृपतयः and other visiting kings, कृष्णानुमोदिताः feeling silently encouraged by the unspoken hint of Lord Krishna, जहास burst into laughter.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, seeing Duryodhana fall, Bhima, the royal women, and other kings burst into laughter. Although King Yudhisthira tried to stop them, they were encouraged by Krishna's quiet approval.

स व्रीडितोऽवाग्वदनो रुषा ज्वलन् निष्क्रम्य तूष्णीं प्रययौ गजाह्वयम् ।
हाहेति शब्दः सुमहानभूत् सतामजातशत्रुर्विमना इवाभवत् ।
बभूव तूष्णीं भगवान् भुवो भरं समुज्जिहीर्षुर्भ्रमति स्म यद्दृशा ॥10.75.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Duryodhana), व्रीडितः humiliated/embarrassed, अवाक्-वदनः with face cast downward, रुषा with rage, ज्वलन् burning, निष्क्रम्य departing, तूष्णीम् silently, प्रययौ left for, गज-आह्वयम् Hastinapura, हा हा इति "Alas, alas!", शब्दः exclamation, सु-महान् very great, अभूत् arose, सताम् among the virtuous, अजात-शत्रुः King Yudhisthira (whose enemies are never born), विमनाः इव disheartened/distressed, अभूत् became, बभूव remained, तूष्णीम् silent, भगवान् the Supreme Lord Krishna, भुवः of the earth, भरम् the heavy burden, समुज्जिहीर्षुः desiring to relieve, भ्रमति स्म was working/manipulating, यत् because, दृशा by His glance/will

Anvaya: सः व्रीडितः अवाग्वदनः रुषा ज्वलन् तूष्णीं निष्क्रम्य गजाह्वयं प्रययौ। सतां सुमहान् 'हा हा' इति शब्दः अभूत्, अजातशत्रुः विमनाः इव अभूत्, भगवान् भुवः भरं समुज्जिहीर्षुः तूष्णीं बभूव यत् दृशा भ्रमति स्म।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He Duryodhana, व्रीडितः deeply embarrassed, अवाग्वदनः keeping his face down in shame, रुषा ज्वलन् burning inwardly with intense anger, तूष्णीं निष्क्रम्य exiting without speaking a word, गजाह्वयं प्रययौ headed straight to Hastinapura. सतां सुमहान् 'हा हा' इति शब्दः अभूत् Among the noble gentlemen present a great cry of "Alas! Alas!" arose, अजातशत्रुः विमनाः इव अभूत् King Yudhisthira became profoundly distressed at heart, भगवान् भुवः भरं समुज्जिहीर्षुः तूष्णीं बभूव while the Supreme Lord Krishna, who desired to relieve the heavy burden of the earth, remained silent, यत् दृशा भ्रमति स्म because it was His glance that had bewildered Duryodhana.

Simple Meaning: Humiliated and burning with anger, Duryodhana cast his eyes down and left silently for Hastinapura. A great cry of lamentation ("Alas, alas!") arose among the noble assembly, and King Yudhisthira was deeply distressed. Lord Krishna, who desired to relieve the heavy burden of the earth, remained silent, because it was His glance that had bewildered Duryodhana.

एतत्तेऽभिहितं राजन् यत् पृष्टोऽहमिह त्वया ।
सुयोधनस्य दौरात्म्यं राजसूये महाक्रतौ ॥10.75.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतत् this, ते to you, अभिहितम् described/narrated, राजन् O King Parikshit, यत् which, पृष्टः asked, अहम् I, इह here, त्वया by you, सुयोधनस्य of Suyodhana (Duryodhana), दौरात्म्यम् the wickedness/evil intent, राजसूये at the Rajasuya, महा-क्रतौ during the great sacrifice

Anvaya: राजन्! इह त्वया अहं यत् पृष्टः, (तत्) महाक्रतौ राजसूये सुयोधनस्य एतत् दौरात्म्यं ते अभिहितम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King Parikshit, इह in this context, त्वया by you, अहं यत् पृष्टः what was asked of me, (तत्) that, महाक्रतौ राजसूये during the great Rajasuya sacrifice, सुयोधनस्य एतत् दौरात्म्यं this evil behavior and malicious envy of Duryodhana, ते अभिहितम् has now been fully explained and narrated to you.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, I have now narrated to you everything concerning the malicious envy and evil intention of Duryodhana during the great Rajasuya sacrifice, as you had inquired of me.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “राजसूययज्ञस्य समाप्तिः दुर्योधनस्य मानभङ्गः च​​ नाम” पञ्चसप्ततितमः अध्यायः ॥75॥

Thus ends the seventy-fifth discourse named “Conclusion of the Rajasuya sacrifice and Duryodhana’s humiliation”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
