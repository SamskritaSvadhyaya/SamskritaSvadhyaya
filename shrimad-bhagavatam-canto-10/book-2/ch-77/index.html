Chapter 77

The deliverance of Shalva

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

शाल्वोद्धारः नाम सप्तसप्ततितमः अध्यायः

(37 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
स तूपस्पृश्य सलिलं दंशितो धृतकार्मुकः ।
नय मां द्युमतः पार्श्वं वीरस्येत्याह सारथिम् ॥10.77.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, सः he (Pradyumna), तु indeed, उपस्पृश्य touching/sipping, सलिलम् water, दंशितः clad in armor, धृत-कार्मुकः holding his bow, नय take, माम् me, द्युमतः of Dyuman, पार्श्वम् to the presence/side, वीरस्य of the hero, इति thus, आह said, सारथिम् to his charioteer

Anvaya: श्री शुक उवाच— सः तु सलिलम् उपस्पृश्य दंशितः धृतकार्मुकः (सन्) सारथिम् इति आह— मां वीरस्य द्युमतः पार्श्वं नय।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुक उवाच Shri Shuka said: सः तु he indeed, सलिलम् उपस्पृश्य having touched water for purification, दंशितः armored, धृतकार्मुकः holding his bow, सारथिम् इति आह said thus to his charioteer, मां me, वीरस्य द्युमतः पार्श्वम् to the side of the warrior Dyuman, नय take.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Having purified himself by touching water, donning his armor, and taking up his bow, Pradyumna instructed his charioteer, "Take me to that warrior Dyuman."

विधमन्तं स्वसैन्यानि द्युमन्तं रुक्मिणीसुतः ।
प्रतिहत्य प्रत्यविध्यन्नाराचैरष्टभिः स्मयन् ॥10.77.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: विधमन्तम् destroying/scattering, स्व-सैन्यानि his own forces, द्युमन्तम् Dyuman, रुक्मिणी-सुतः Pradyumna (the son of Rukmini), प्रतिहत्य repelling/stopping, प्रत्यविध्यत् struck back, नाराचैः with iron arrows, अष्टभिः eight, स्मयन् smiling

Anvaya: रुक्मिणीसुतः स्वसैन्यानि विधमन्तं द्युमन्तं प्रतिहत्य स्मयन् अष्टभिः नाराचैः प्रत्यविध्यत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रुक्मिणीसुतः The son of Rukmini - Pradyumna, स्वसैन्यानि विधमन्तं who was routing his army, द्युमन्तं Dyuman, प्रतिहत्य having intercepted, स्मयन् smiling, अष्टभिः नाराचैः with eight heavy iron shafts, प्रत्यविध्यत् pierced him in return.

Simple Meaning: Intercepting Dyuman as he was laying waste to the Yadu forces, Rukmini's son Pradyumna smiled and struck him back with eight iron arrows.

चतुर्भिश्चतुरो वाहान् सूतमेकेन चाहनत् ।
द्वाभ्यां धनुश्च केतुं च शरेणान्येन वै शिरः ॥10.77.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: चतुर्भिः with four (arrows), चतुरः four, वाहान् horses, सूतम् the charioteer, एकेन with one, च and, आहनत् struck, द्वाभ्याम् with two, धनुः bow, च and, केतुम् banner, च and, शरेण with an arrow, अन्येन another, वै indeed, शिरः head

Anvaya: (रुक्मिणीसुतः) चतुर्भिः चतुरः वाहान्, एकेन सूतं च आहनत्, द्वाभ्यां धनुः केतुं च, अन्येन शरेण वै (द्युमतः) शिरः (आहनत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (Pradyumna) चतुर्भिः With four arrows, चतुरः वाहान् four horses, एकेन सूतं च and with one arrow the charioteer, आहनत् struck down, द्वाभ्याम् with two arrows, धनुः केतुं च the bow and flag, अन्येन शरेण वै and with another arrow indeed, (द्युमतः) शिरः Dyuman's head (he severed).

Simple Meaning: With four arrows Pradyumna struck down Dyuman's four horses, with one arrow his charioteer, with two arrows his bow and victory flag, and with yet another arrow he severed Dyuman's head.

गदसात्यकिसाम्बाद्या जघ्नुः सौभपतेर्बलम् ।
पेतुः समुद्रे सौभेयाः सर्वे संछिन्नकन्धराः ॥10.77.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: गद-सात्यकि-साम्ब-आद्याः Gada, Satyaki, Samba, and others, जघ्नुः struck down/slaughtered, सौभ-पतेः of the master of Saubha - Shalva, बलम् the army, पेतुः fell, समुद्रे into the ocean, सौभेयाः the inhabitants/soldiers of the Saubha ship, सर्वे all, संछिन्न-कन्धराः with severed necks/heads

Anvaya: गदसात्यकिसाम्बाद्याः सौभपतेः बलं जघ्नुः, सर्वे सौभेयाः संछिन्नकन्धराः समुद्रे पेतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गदसात्यकिसाम्बाद्याः Gada, Satyaki, Samba and others, सौभपतेः बलं the army of the lord of Saubha - Shalva, जघ्नुः slaughtered, सर्वे सौभेयाः all the soldiers aboard the Saubha airship, संछिन्नकन्धराः having their necks severed, समुद्रे पेतुः fell into the ocean.

Simple Meaning: Gada, Satyaki, Samba, and the other Yadu commanders slaughtered Shalva's army. All the combatants aboard the Saubha airship fell into the ocean with their heads severed.

एवं यदूनां शाल्वानां निघ्नतामितरेतरम् ।
युद्धं त्रिणवरात्रं तदभूत्तुमुलमुल्बणम् ॥10.77.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this manner, यदूनाम् of the Yadus, शाल्वानाम् of Shalva's forces, निघ्नताम् striking/slaying, इतरेतरम् one another, युद्धम् battle, त्रि-नव-रात्रम् twenty-seven nights, तत् that, अभूत् was, तुमुलम् tumultuous, उल्बणम् fierce/terrible

Anvaya: एवम् इतरेतरं निघ्नतां यदूनां शाल्वानां तत् तुमुलम् उल्बणं युद्धं त्रिणवरात्रम् अभूत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवम् In this way, इतरेतरं निघ्नतां as they were striking each other down, यदूनां शाल्वानां between the Yadus and Shalva's forces, तत् तुमुलम् उल्बणं युद्धं that tumultuous, terrible battle, त्रिणवरात्रम् for twenty-seven nights (3 × 9 = 27 nights), अभूत् continued.

Simple Meaning: In this manner, as the Yadus and Shalva's forces struck each other down relentlessly, that fierce and tumultuous battle raged on continuously for twenty-seven nights.

इन्द्रप्रस्थं गतः कृष्ण आहूतो धर्मसूनुना ।
राजसूयेऽथ निर्वृत्ते शिशुपाले च संस्थिते ॥10.77.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: इन्द्रप्रस्थम् to Indraprastha, गतः gone, कृष्णः Lord Krishna, आहूतः invited, धर्म-सूनुना by King Yudhishthira (the son of Dharma), राजसूये in the Rajasuya sacrifice, अथ then, निर्वृत्ते having been concluded, शिशुपाले Shishupala, च and, संस्थिते having been slain/attained his end

Anvaya: धर्मसूनुना आहूतः कृष्णः इन्द्रप्रस्थं गतः, अथ राजसूये निर्वृत्ते शिशुपाले च संस्थिते (सति)...

Literal meaning of the anvaya: धर्मसूनुना आहूतः Invited by King Yudhishthira, कृष्णः Lord Krishna, इन्द्रप्रस्थं गतः had gone to Indraprastha; अथ then, राजसूये निर्वृत्ते the Rajasuya sacrifice having been successfully completed, शिशुपाले च संस्थिते and Shishupala having been slain…

Simple Meaning: Meanwhile, Lord Krishna had gone to Indraprastha upon the invitation of King Yudhishthira. The Rajasuya sacrifice was now fully completed, and Shishupala had been slain.

कुरुवृद्धाननुज्ञाप्य मुनींश्च ससुतां पृथाम् ।
निमित्तान्यतिघोराणि पश्यन् द्वारवतीं ययौ ॥10.77.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: कुरु-वृद्धान् the elders of the Kuru clan, अनुज्ञाप्य taking leave of, मुनीन् the sages, च and, स-सुताम् with her sons, पृथाम् Kunti, निमित्तानि inauspicious omens, अति-घोराणि extremely dreadful, पश्यन् observing, द्वारवतीम् to Dwaraka, ययौ departed

Anvaya: …अतिघोराणि निमित्तानि पश्यन् कुरुवृद्धान् मुनीन् ससुतां पृथां च अनुज्ञाप्य द्वारवतीं ययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: …अतिघोराणि निमित्तानि पश्यन् observing extremely terrifying omens, कुरुवृद्धान् the Kuru elders, मुनीन् the sages, ससुतां पृथां च and Kunti along with her sons, अनुज्ञाप्य taking formal leave of, द्वारवतीं ययौ Krishna proceeded toward Dwaraka.

Simple Meaning: Krishna observed extremely terrifying omens and so taking leave of the Kuru elders, the sages, and Kunti with her sons, and immediately departed for Dwaraka.

आह चाहमिहायात आर्यमिश्राभिसङ्गतः ।
राजन्याश्चैद्यपक्षीया नूनं हन्युः पुरीं मम ॥10.77.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: आह spoke, च and, अहम् I, इह here, आयातः came, आर्य-मिश्र-अभिसङ्गतः along with My elder brother, राजन्याः kings, चैद्य-पक्षीयाः allied with Shishupala (the lord of Chedi), नूनम् surely, हन्युः are attacking/destroying, पुरीम् city, मम my

Anvaya: आह च— अहम् आर्यमिश्राभिसङ्गतः इह आयातः, चैद्यपक्षीयाः राजन्याः नूनं मम पुरीं हन्युः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: आह च And He said (to Himself), आर्यमिश्राभिसङ्गतः along with My elder brother, अहम् I, इह आयातः came here, (तस्मिन् काले) in the meantime, चैद्यपक्षीयाः राजन्याः the kings allied with Shishupala, नूनं surely, मम पुरीं हन्युः must be attacking my city.

Simple Meaning: He said (to Himself), "I came here along with My elder brother, but in My absence, the royal allies of Shishupala are surely laying siege to My city."

वीक्ष्य तत् कदनं स्वानां निरूप्य पुररक्षणम् ।
सौभं च शाल्वराजं च दारुकं प्राह केशवः ॥10.77.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: वीक्ष्य seeing/observing, तत् that, कदनम् destruction/havoc, स्वानाम् of His own people, निरूप्य arranging for/setting up, पुर-रक्षणम् protection of the city, सौभम् the Saubha airship, च and, शाल्व-राजम् King Shalva, च and, दारुकम् to Daruka, प्राह spoke, केशवः Krishna

Anvaya: स्वानां तत् कदनं वीक्ष्य, पुररक्षणं निरूप्य, सौभं शाल्वराजं च (दृष्ट्वा) केशवः दारुकं प्राह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: स्वानां तत् कदनं वीक्ष्य Seeing that heavy destruction wrought upon His own people, पुररक्षणं निरूप्य having secured the defense of the city, सौभं शाल्वराजं च (दृष्ट्वा) seeing the Saubha vessel and King Shalva, केशवः Krishna, दारुकं प्राह spoke to His charioteer Daruka.

Simple Meaning: Upon arriving and seeing the terrible havoc inflicted upon His people, Lord Krishna first arranged for the thorough defense of Dvaraka. Then, seeing the airship Saubha and King Shalva, Keshava spoke to His charioteer, Daruka.

रथं प्रापय मे सूत शाल्वस्यान्तिकमाशु वै ।
सम्भ्रमस्ते न कर्तव्यो मायावी सौभराडयम् ॥10.77.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: रथम् the chariot, प्रापय lead/drive, मे my, सूत O charioteer, शाल्वस्य of Shalva, अन्तिकम् to the presence/proximity, आशु quickly, वै indeed, सम्भ्रमः bewilderment/fear, ते by you, न not, कर्तव्यः should be made, मायावी a master of illusion, सौभ-राट् the lord of Saubha, अयम् this one

Anvaya: सूत! मे रथम् आशु शाल्वस्य अन्तिकं प्रापय वै। अयंसौभराट् मायावी (अस्ति), ते सम्भ्रमः न कर्तव्यः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सूत O charioteer, मे रथम् my chariot, आशु quickly, शाल्वस्य अन्तिकं to the vicinity of Shalva, प्रापय वै drive indeed. अयं सौभराट् This king of Saubha, मायावी is a practitioner of mystic illusions, (तस्मात्) therefore, ते सम्भ्रमः न कर्तव्यः no bewilderment or panic should be felt by you.

Simple Meaning: "O charioteer, drive my chariot swiftly to where Shalva is stationed. This lord of Saubha is a master of illusionary tricks, so you must remain calm and not let yourself be bewildered by his magic."

इत्युक्तश्चोदयामास रथमास्थाय दारुकः ।
विशन्तं ददृशुः सर्वे स्वे परे चारुणानुजम् ॥10.77.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, उक्तः requested/instructed, चोदयामास drove/guided, रथम् the chariot, आस्थाय taking his seat, दारुकः Daruka, विशन्तम् entering (the battlefield), ददृशुः saw, सर्वे all, स्वे one's own (allies), परे enemies, च and, अरुण-अनुजम् Garuda (the younger brother of Aruna, used here as an epithet for Lord Krishna's emblem/presence)

Anvaya: इति उक्तः दारुकः रथम् आस्थाय चोदयामास। स्वे परे च सर्वे विशन्तम् अरुणानुजं ददृशुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति उक्तः Having been thus instructed, दारुकः Daruka, रथम् आस्थाय taking his position on the chariot, चोदयामास drove forward. स्वे परे च सर्वे Both allies and enemies alike, विशन्तम् entering the arena of combat, अरुणानुजं the chariot of the Lord whose flag bears Garuda emblem, ददृशुः saw.

Simple Meaning: Thus instructed by the Lord, Daruka mounted the chariot and drove it forward into combat. Everyone present—allies and enemies alike—beheld Lord Krishna entering the battlefield.

शाल्वश्च कृष्णमालोक्य हतप्रायबलेश्वरः ।
प्राहरत् कृष्णसूताय शक्तिं भीमरवां मृधे ॥10.77.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: शाल्वः Shalva, च and, कृष्णम् Krishna, आलोक्य seeing, हत-प्राय-बल-ईश्वरः whose chief commanders and forces were mostly destroyed, प्राहरत् hurled, कृष्ण-सूताय at Krishna's charioteer (Daruka), शक्तिम् a spear/missile, भीम-रवाम् making a terrifying sound, मृधे in the battle

Anvaya: मृधे हतप्रायबलेश्वरः शाल्वः च कृष्णम् आलोक्य कृष्णसूताय भीमरवां शक्तिं प्राहरत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मृधे In battle, हतप्रायबलेश्वरः whose generals and troops were nearly annihilated, शाल्वः च and Shalva, कृष्णम् आलोक्य upon seeing Krishna, कृष्णसूताय at Krishna's driver, भीमरवां शक्तिं a spear producing a dreadful roar, प्राहरत् hurled.

Simple Meaning: Seeing Krishna arrive on the battlefield, Shalva—whose generals and army had already been almost entirely wiped out—hurled a terrifying, roaring spear directly at Krishna's charioteer, Daruka.

तामापतन्तीं नभसि महोल्कामिव रंहसा ।
भासयन्तीं दिशः शौरिः सायकैः शतधाच्छिनत् ॥10.77.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् that (spear), आपतन्तीम् hurtling down, नभसि in the sky, महा-उल्काम् इव like a great meteor, रंहसा with tremendous speed, भासयन्तीम् illuminating, दिशः the directions, शौरिः Lord Krishna (descendant of Shura), सायकैः with His arrows, शतधा into a hundred pieces, अच्छिनत् shattered/cut

Anvaya: शौरिः नभसि रंहसा आपतन्तीं महोल्काम् इव दिशः भासयन्तीं तां सायकैः शतधा अच्छिनत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शौरिः Krishna, नभसि in the sky, रंहसा with great speed, आपतन्तीं hurtling down, महोल्काम् इव like a brilliant meteor, दिशः all directions, भासयन्तीं illuminating, तां that weapon, सायकैः with arrows, शतधा into a hundred fragments, अच्छिनत् split.

Simple Meaning: Krishna shot His arrows and shattered the spear into a hundred pieces while it was still hurtling through the sky—blazing with speed and illuminating all directions like a giant meteor.

तं च षोडशभिर्विद्ध्वा बाणैः सौभं च खे भ्रमत् ।
अविध्यच्छरसन्दोहैः खं सूर्य इव रश्मिभिः ॥10.77.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him (Shalva), च and, षोडशभिः with sixteen, विद्ध्वा having pierced, बाणैः arrows, सौभम् the Saubha airship, च and, खे in the sky, भ्रमत् moving/flying around, अविध्यत् pierced/struck, शर-सन्दोहैः with torrents/masses of arrows, खम् the sky, सूर्यः the sun, इव like, रश्मिभिः with its rays

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तं च And him (Shalva), षोडशभिः बाणैः with sixteen arrows, विद्ध्वा having struck, खे भ्रमत् सौभं च and the Saubha airship flying about in the sky, शरसन्दोहैः with dense showers of arrows, सूर्यः the sun, रश्मिभिः with its rays, खम् इव as if (covering) the sky, अविध्यत् He pierced.

Simple Meaning: Having pierced Shalva with sixteen arrows, Krishna pierced the flying Saubha airship with a torrent of shafts, just as the sun pierces the entire sky with its rays.

शाल्वः शौरेस्तु दोः सव्यं सशार्ङ्गं शार्ङ्गधन्वनः ।

Anvaya: शाल्वः तु शार्ङ्गधन्वनः शौरेः सशार्ङ्गं सव्यं दोः बिभेद, (तेन) हस्तात् शार्ङ्गं न्यपतत्, तत् अद्भुतम् आसीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शाल्वः तु Shalva indeed, शार्ङ्गधन्वनः शौरेः of Krishna, the wielder of the Sharnga, सशार्ङ्गं holding the bow, सव्यं दोः left arm, बिभेद pierced; (तेन) thereby, हस्तात् from His hand, शार्ङ्गं the Sharnga bow, न्यपतत् fell; तत् that, अद्भुतम् आसीत् was truly astonishing.

Simple Meaning: Shalva then struck Krishna's left arm, which was holding His famous Sharnga bow. As a result, the bow slipped and fell from Krishna's hand—an occurrence that astonished everyone present.

हाहाकारो महानासीद् भूतानां तत्र पश्यताम् ।

Word-by-word meaning: हाहाकारः a loud cry of distress ("alas, alas!"), महान् great, आसीत् arose, भूतानाम् of the living beings, तत्र there, पश्यताम् who were witnessing it, विनद्य roaring/bellowing, सौभ-राट् the lord of Saubha - Shalva, उच्चैः loudly, इदम् these words, आह spoke to, जनार्दनम् Krishna

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत्र पश्यतां भूतानां Among the beings witnessing the event there, महान् हाहाकारः आसीत् a great cry of dismay arose; (तदा) then, सौभराट् Shalva, the lord of Saubha, उच्चैः विनद्य roaring loudly, जनार्दनम् इदम् आह spoke these words to Janardana (Krishna).

Simple Meaning: A great cry of dismay arose from all the living beings who witnessed the bow slip from Krishna's hand. Seeing this, Shalva, the king of Saubha, let out a loud, triumphant roar and addressed Krishna with these words.

यत्त्वया मूढ नः सख्युर्भ्रातुर्भार्या हृतेक्षताम् ।

Anvaya: मूढ! यत् ईक्षतां नः भ्रातुः सख्युः भार्या त्वया हृता, सः सखा प्रमत्तः सभामध्ये त्वया व्यापादितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मूढ O fool! यत् because, ईक्षतां नः while we were watching, भ्रातुः सख्युः of our brother and friend (Shishupala), भार्या the betrothed wife (Rukmini), त्वया हृता was abducted by you, (तथा) and, सः सखा that friend, प्रमत्तः being off guard, सभामध्ये in the middle of the assembly, त्वया व्यापादितः was slain by you.

Simple Meaning: "O fool! You abducted the betrothed bride of our friend and cousin, Shishupala, right before our eyes. And then, taking him off guard in the middle of the royal assembly, you murdered our friend!

तं त्वाद्य निशितैर्बाणैरपराजितमानिनम् ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अपराजितमानिनं Considering yourself unvanquished, तं त्वा that very you, अद्य today, निशितैः बाणैः with sharp arrows, अपुनरावृत्तिं नयामि I shall send to the region of no return (death), यदि if, मम अग्रतः in front of me, तिष्ठेः you continue to stand.

Simple Meaning: "Today I shall send You, who consider yourself invincible, to the realm of death with my sharp arrows—if you dare to remain standing in front of me!"

श्री भगवान् उवाच
वृथा त्वं कत्थसे मन्द न पश्यस्यन्तिकेऽन्तकम् ।

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, वृथा in vain, त्वम् you, कत्थसे brag/boast, मन्द O fool, न not, पश्यसि you see, अन्तिके nearby, अन्तकम् Death, पौरुषम् heroism/prowess, दर्शयन्ति show, स्म indeed, शूराः true heroes, न not, बहु-भाषिणः talkative/verbose people

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, मन्द O dull-witted fool! त्वं you, वृथा कत्थसे brag uselessly, अन्तिके near you, अन्तकं Death, न पश्यसि you do not perceive? शूराः Warriors, पौरुषं prowess, दर्शयन्ति स्म demonstrate, बहुभाषिणः न not talkative men.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord said: "O fool! You brag uselessly in vain, unable to see Death standing right beside you. True heroes demonstrate their valor through deeds, not empty chatter!"

इत्युक्त्वा भगवाञ्छाल्वं गदया भीमवेगया ।
तताड जत्रौ संरब्धः स चकम्पे वमन्नसृक् ॥10.77.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, उक्त्वा having spoken, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, शाल्वम् Shalva, गदया with His club, भीम-वेगया of terrible speed/force, तताड struck, जत्रौ on the collarbone, संरब्धः infuriated, सः he (Shalva), चकम्पे trembled/reeled, वमन् vomiting, असृक् blood

Anvaya: इति उक्त्वा संरब्धः भगवान् भीमवेगया गदया शाल्वं जत्रौ तताड, सः असृक् वमन् चकम्पे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति उक्त्वा Having spoken thus, संरब्धः भगवान् Lord Krishna, aroused in anger, भीमवेगया with a great speed, गदया with a mace, शाल्वं जत्रौ on Shalva's collarbone, तताड struck; सः he (Shalva), असृक् वमन् vomiting blood, चकम्पे reeled.

Simple Meaning: Having spoken these words, the infuriated Supreme Lord struck Shalva with great force on the collarbone with His mace. Shalva reeled as he vomited blood.

गदायां सन्निवृत्तायां शाल्वस्त्वन्तरधीयत ।
ततो मूहुर्त आगत्य पुरुषः शिरसाच्युतम् ।
देवक्या प्रहितोऽस्मीति नत्वा प्राह वचो रुदन् ॥10.77.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: गदायाम् the mace, सन्निवृत्तायाम् when it returned (to Krishna's hand), शाल्वः Shalva, तु indeed, अन्तरधीयत vanished/became invisible, ततः then, मुहूर्तः a short time (a muhurta) later, आगत्य approaching, पुरुषः a man, शिरसा with his head bowed, अच्युतम् to Lord Krishna (Achyuta), देवक्या by Devaki, प्रहितः sent, अस्मि I am, इति thus saying, नत्वा bowing down, प्राह spoke, वचः words, रुदन् weeping

Anvaya: गदायां सन्निवृत्तायां तु शाल्वः अन्तरधीयत। ततः मुहूर्तः (पश्चात्) पुरुषः आगत्य शिरसा अच्युतं नत्वा रुदन् इति वचः प्राह – "देवक्या प्रहितः अस्मि"।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गदायां सन्निवृत्तायां तु When the mace had returned, शाल्वः Shalva, अन्तरधीयत vanished from sight; ततः then, मुहूर्तः a short while later, पुरुषः a man, आगत्य coming forward, शिरसा by bowing his head, अच्युतं नत्वा prostrating before Achyuta, रुदन् weeping, इति वचः प्राह uttered these words, "देवक्या प्रहितः अस्मि" “I have been sent by Devaki.”

Simple Meaning: When Krishna's mace returned to Him, Shalva suddenly vanished. A moment later, a messenger arrived weeping, bowed his head before Achyuta, and declared that he had been sent by Devaki.

बद्ध्वापनीतः शाल्वेन सौनिकेन यथा पशुः ॥10.77.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृष्ण O Krishna, कृष्ण O Krishna, महा-बाहो O mighty-armed one, पिता father, ते your, पितृ-वत्सल O You who are devoted to parents, बद्ध्वा having bound, अपनीतः carried away, शाल्वेन by Shalva, सौनिकेन by a butcher, यथा as, पशुः an animal

Anvaya:  कृष्ण! कृष्ण! महाबाहो! पितृवत्सल! ते पिता शाल्वेन बद्ध्वा सौनिकेन पशुः यथा अपनीतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्ण कृष्ण O Krishna, O Krishna! महाबाहो O mighty-armed Lord! पितृवत्सल O You who are devoted to parents! ते पिता Your father Vasudeva, शाल्वेन by Shalva, बद्ध्वा having been bound, सौनिकेन पशुः यथा like an animal by a butcher, अपनीतः has been carried away.

Simple Meaning: "O Krishna! O Krishna! O mighty-armed Lord who loves His father! Shalva has bound your father, Vasudeva, and carried him away just like a butcher dragging off a helpless animal!"

Word-by-word meaning: निशम्य hearing, विप्रियम् this unpleasant news, कृष्णः Lord Krishna, मानुषीम् human, प्रकृतिम् nature/disposition, गतः assuming, विमनस्कः distressed/despondent, घृणी compassionate, स्नेहात् out of affection, बभाषे spoke, प्राकृतः an ordinary man, यथा like

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत् विप्रियं निशम्य Hearing that distressing news, कृष्णः Krishna, मानुषीं प्रकृतिं गतः assuming a human disposition, स्नेहात् out of parental affection, घृणी compassionate, विमनस्कः (सन्) becoming sorrowful, प्राकृतः यथा like an ordinary mortal, बभाषे spoke.

एवं निर्भर्त्स्य मायावी खड्गेनानकदुन्दुभेः।

Anvaya: राजन्! तस्मिन् पापे निपतिते गदया सौभे च हते (सति), दिवि देवगणेरिताः दुन्दुभयः नेदुः। (ततः) सखीनाम् अपचितिं कुर्वन् दन्तवक्त्रः रुषा अभ्यगात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King Parikshit! तस्मिन् पापे निपतिते when that sinful Shalva had fallen dead, गदया सौभे च हते and the Saubha airship was shattered by the mace, दिवि in the heavens, देवगणेरिताः sounded by hosts of devas, दुन्दुभयः kettle-drums, नेदुः resounded. (ततः) Then, सखीनाम् of his slain friends, अपचितिं कुर्वन् seeking to avenge/pay the debt, दन्तवक्त्रः Dantavakra, रुषा in extreme wrath, अभ्यगात् rushed forward into battle.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit! When that sinful villain Shalva fell dead and his Saubha airship was completely destroyed by Krishna's mace, celestial kettle-drums resounded throughout the heavens, beaten joyfully by the devas. Then, seeking to avenge his fallen friends, Dantavakra rushed forward in a blazing fury to confront the Lord.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “शाल्वोद्धारः नाम” सप्तसप्ततितमः अध्यायः ॥77॥

Thus ends the seventy-seventh discourse named “The deliverance of Shalva”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
