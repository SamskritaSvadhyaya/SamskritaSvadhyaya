Chapter 63

Fight between Krishna and Banasura. Aniruddha’s rescue.

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

कृष्णबाणासुरयुद्धम् अनिरुद्धमोचनं च नाम त्रिषष्टितमः अध्यायः

(53 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
अपश्यतां अनिरुद्धं तद्बन्धूनां च भारत ।
चत्वारो वार्षिका मासा व्यतीयुरनुशोचताम् ॥10.63.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अपश्यताम् of those who could not see, अनिरुद्धम् Aniruddha, तत्-बन्धूनाम् of his relatives, च and, भारत O descendant of Bharata, चत्वारः four, वार्षिकाः of the rainy season, मासाः months, व्यतीयुः passed away, अनुशोचताम् who were deeply lamenting

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच — भारत! अनिरुद्धम् अपश्यताम् अनुशोचतां तद्बन्धूनां च चत्वारः वार्षिका मासा व्यतीयुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, भारत O descendant of Bharata, अनिरुद्धम् अपश्यताम् अनुशोचतां तद्बन्धूनां च for his relatives who were unable to see Aniruddha and were grieving continuously, चत्वारः वार्षिका मासा व्यतीयुः four months of the rainy season passed away.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King Parikshit, descendant of Bharata, four months of the rainy season passed while Aniruddha family members and relatives, unable to trace him, grieved continuously in deep sorrow over his sudden disappearance.

नारदात्तदुपाकर्ण्य वार्तां बद्धस्य कर्म च ।
प्रययुः शोणितपुरं वृष्णयः कृष्णदेवताः ॥10.63.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: नारदात् from Narada, तत् that, उपाकर्ण्य hearing, वार्ताम् the news, बद्धस्य of the capture or binding, कर्म च and the events or activities, प्रययुः departed, शोणित-पुरम् to the city of Shonitapura, वृष्णयः the members of the Vrishni dynasty, कृष्ण-देवताः who made Krishna their worshipable deity

Anvaya: नारदात् बद्धस्य वार्तां कर्म च उपाकर्ण्य कृष्णदेवताः वृष्णयः शोणितपुरं प्रययुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नारदात् बद्धस्य वार्तां कर्म च उपाकर्ण्य Hearing from Narada the news of his binding and the events that transpired, कृष्णदेवताः वृष्णयः शोणितपुरं प्रययुः the Vrishnis who held Krishna as their Lord departed for the city of Shonitapura.

Simple Meaning: Upon hearing from the sage Narada about Aniruddha capture and the fierce battle that had taken place, the heroes of the Vrishni clan—who worshipped Krishna as their ultimate Lord—immediately set out for the city of Shonitapura.

प्रद्युम्नो युयुधानश्च गदः साम्बोऽथ सारणः ।
नन्दोपनन्दभद्राद्या रामकृष्णानुवर्तिनः ॥10.63.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रद्युम्नः Pradyumna, युयुधानः Yuyudhana (Satyaki), च and, गदः Gada, साम्बः Samba, अथ as well as, सारणः Sarana, नन्द-उपनन्द-भद्र-आद्याः Nanda, Upananda, Bhadra and others, राम-कृष्ण-अनुवर्तिनः followers of Balarama and Krishna

Anvaya: प्रद्युम्नः युयुधानः च गदः साम्बः अथ सारणः नन्दोपनन्दभद्राद्याः रामकृष्णानुवर्तिनः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रद्युम्नः युयुधानः च गदः साम्बः अथ सारणः Pradyumna, Yuyudhana (Satyaki), Gada, Samba as well as Sarana, नन्दोपनन्दभद्राद्याः Nanda, Upananda, Bhadra and other heroes, रामकृष्णानुवर्तिनः followers who accompanied Balarama and Krishna.

Simple Meaning: Led by Balarama and Krishna, the great Vrishni warriors—including Pradyumna, Satyaki, Gada, Samba, Sarana, Nanda, Upananda, Bhadra, and many other brave leaders—marched out together for the battle.

अक्षौहिणीभिर्द्वादशभिः समेताः सर्वतोदिशम् ।
रुरुधुर्बाणनगरं समन्तात् सात्वतर्षभाः ॥10.63.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अक्षौहिणीभिः with military divisions, द्वादशभिः twelve, समेताः equipped or gathered, सर्वतः दिशम् in all directions, रुरुधुः encircled or besieged, बाण-नगरम् the city of Bana, समन्तात् completely, सात्वत-ऋषभाः the chief leaders of the Satvata clan

Anvaya: द्वादशभिः अक्षौहिणीभिः समेताः सात्वतर्षभाः सर्वतोदिशं समन्तात् बाणनगरं रुरुधुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: द्वादशभिः अक्षौहिणीभिः समेताः Equipped with twelve military divisions, सात्वतर्षभाः the prominent leaders of the Satvata clan, सर्वतोदिशं समन्तात् from all directions on every side, बाणनगरं रुरुधुः completely surrounded and besieged the city of Bana.

Simple Meaning: Accompanied by twelve full military divisions, the exalted leaders of the Satvata clan completely surrounded and besieged Bana’s city from every direction.

भज्यमानपुरोद्यानप्राकाराट्टालगोपुरम् ।
प्रेक्षमाणो रुषाविष्टस्तुल्यसैन्योऽभिनिर्ययौ ॥10.63.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: भज्यमान-पुर-उद्यान-प्राकार-अट्टाल-गोपुरम् where the city gardens, walls, watchtowers and gateways were being destroyed, प्रेक्षमाणः seeing, रुषा-विष्टः overcome with anger, तुल्य-सैन्यः accompanied by an army equal in strength, अभिनिर्ययौ came out to fight

Anvaya: भज्यमानपुरोद्यानप्राकाराट्टालगोपुरं प्रेक्षमाणः रुषाविष्टः बाणः तुल्यसैन्यः अभिनिर्ययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भज्यमानपुरोद्यानप्राकाराट्टालगोपुरं प्रेक्षमाणः Seeing the city gardens perimeter walls watchtowers and gates being laid waste, रुषाविष्टः बाणः overcome with intense rage, Bana, तुल्यसैन्यः अभिनिर्ययौ marched out to battle flanked by an equally powerful army.

Simple Meaning: Observing his city gardens, defense walls, watchtowers, and grand entrance gates being destroyed by the invading Vrishni forces, Bana was filled with fury. He marched out of the city to confront them, leading an army equal in strength.

बाणार्थे भगवान् रुद्रः ससुतैः प्रमथैर्वृतः ।
आरुह्य नन्दिवृषभं युयुधे रामकृष्णयोः ॥10.63.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: बाण-अर्थे for the sake of Bana, भगवान् the divine, रुद्रः Lord Shiva, स-सुतैः along with his sons, प्रमथैः by his ghostly attendants, वृतः surrounded, आरुह्य riding, नन्दि-वृषभम् his bull Nandi, युयुधे fought, राम-कृष्णयोः against Balarama and Krishna

Anvaya: भगवान् रुद्रः बाणार्थे ससुतैः प्रमथैः वृतः नन्दिवृषभम् आरुह्य रामकृष्णयोः युयुधे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवान् रुद्रः Lord Shiva, बाणार्थे  for the sake of Bana, ससुतैः प्रमथैः वृतः surrounded by his sons and ghostly attendants, नन्दिवृषभम् आरुह्य mounted upon his bull Nandi, रामकृष्णयोः युयुधे fought against Balarama and Krishna.

Simple Meaning: To defend his devotee Bana, the glorious Lord Shiva mounted his bull Nandi and came out to fight against Krishna and Balarama, surrounded by his sons as well as his multitude of ghostly attendants.

आसीत् सुतुमुलं युद्धमद्भुतं रोमहर्षणम् ।
कृष्णशङ्करयो राजन् प्रद्युम्नगुहयोरपि ॥10.63.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: आसीत् there was, सु-तुमुलम् extremely tumultuous, युद्धम् battle, अद्भुतम् amazing/wondrous, रोम-हर्षणम् causing hair to stand on end (thrilling), कृष्ण-शङ्करयोः between Krishna and Shankara (Shiva), राजन् O King Parikshit, प्रद्युम्न-गुहयोः between Pradyumna and Guha (Kartikeya), अपि also

Anvaya: राजन्! कृष्णशङ्करयोः प्रद्युम्नगुहयोः अपि अद्भुतं रोमहर्षणं सुतुमुलं युद्धम् आसीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, कृष्णशङ्करयोः प्रद्युम्नगुहयोः अपि between Krishna and Shankara as well as between Pradyumna and Guha, अद्भुतं रोमहर्षणं सुतुमुलं युद्धम् आसीत् there took place a wondrous, hair-raising, and extremely fierce battle.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, a thrilling, fierce, and extraordinary battle broke out between Krishna and Shankara, as well as between Pradyumna and Kartikeya (Guha).

कुम्भाण्डकूपकर्णाभ्यां बलेन सह संयुगः ।
साम्बस्य बाणपुत्रेण बाणेन सह सात्यकेः ॥10.63.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: कुम्भाण्ड-कूपकर्णाभ्याम् with Kumbhanda and Kupakarna, बलेन सह with Balarama, संयुगः a duel/combat, साम्बस्य of Samba, बाण-पुत्रेण with the son of Bana, बाणेन सह with Bana, सात्यकेः of Satyaki

Anvaya: बलेन सह कुम्भाण्डकूपकर्णाभ्यां, साम्बस्य बाणपुत्रेण सह, सात्यकेः बाणेन सह संयुगः (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बलेन सह कुम्भाण्डकूपकर्णाभ्यां There was combat of Balarama with Kumbhanda and Kupakarna, साम्बस्य बाणपुत्रेण सह of Samba with the son of Bana, सात्यकेः बाणेन सह संयुगः and of Satyaki with Bana himself.

Simple Meaning: Simultaneous duels broke out across the battlefield: Balarama fought against Bana's ministers Kumbhanda and Kupakarna, Samba battled the son of Bana, and Satyaki engaged directly in combat with Bana.

ब्रह्मादयः सुराधीशा मुनयः सिद्धचारणाः ।
गन्धर्वाप्सरसो यक्षा विमानैर्द्रष्टुमागमन् ॥10.63.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्रह्मा-दयः Brahma and others, सुर-अधीशाः the chief demigods, मुनयः great sages, सिद्ध-चारणाः the Siddhas and Charanas, गन्धर्व-अप्सरसः the Gandharvas and Apsaras, यक्षाः the Yakshas, विमानैः in their celestial airplanes/chariots, द्रष्टुम् to witness (the battle), आगमन् came

Anvaya: ब्रह्मादयः सुराधीशाः मुनयः सिद्धचारणाः गन्धर्वाप्सरसः यक्षाः (च) द्रष्टुं विमानैः आगमन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्रह्मादयः सुराधीशाः Brahma and other ruler demigods, मुनयः सिद्धचारणाः sages, Siddhas, and Charanas, गन्धर्वाप्सरसः यक्षाः Gandharvas, Apsaras, and Yakshas, द्रष्टुं विमानैः आगमन् came in their flying celestial vehicles to watch the battle.

Simple Meaning: Lord Brahma and the demigods, along with great sages, Siddhas, Charanas, Gandharvas, Apsaras, and Yakshas, all arrived in their airborne celestial vehicles to witness this extraordinary duel.

शङ्करानुचराञ्छौरिर्भूतप्रमथगुह्यकान् ।
डाकिनीर्यातुधानांश्च वेतालान् सविनायकान् ॥10.63.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: शौरिः Krishna, शङ्कर-अनुचरान् the followers of Lord Shiva, भूत-प्रमथ-गुह्यकान् the Bhutas, Pramathas, and Guhyakas, डाकिनीः the Dakinis, यातुधानान् the Yatudhanas, च and, वेतालान् the Vetalas, स-विनायकान् along with the Vinayakas

Anvaya: शौरिः शङ्करानुचरान् भूतप्रमथगुह्यकान् डाकिनीः यातुधानान् वेतालान् सविनायकान् च (अजयत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शौरिः Krishna, शङ्करानुचरान् the followers of Lord Shiva, भूतप्रमथगुह्यकान् the Bhutas, Pramathas, and Guhyakas, डाकिनीः यातुधानान् the Dakinis and Yatudhanas, वेतालान् सविनायकान् च and the Betalas along with the Vinayakas (vanquished them).

Simple Meaning: Krishna defeated all the followers of Lord Shiva, including the Bhutas, Pramathas, Guhyakas, Dakinis, Yatudhanas, Betalas, and Vinayakas.

प्रेतमातृपिशाचांश्च कूष्माण्डान् ब्रह्मराक्षसान् ।
द्रावयामास तीक्ष्णाग्रैः शरैः शार्ङ्गधनुश्च्युतैः ॥10.63.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रेत-मातृ-पिशाचान् the Pretas, Matris, and Pisachas, च and, कूष्माण्डान् the Kushmandas, ब्रह्म-राक्षसान् the Brahma-rakshasas, द्रावयामास caused to flee, तीक्ष्ण-अग्रैः with sharp-pointed, शरैः with arrows, शार्ङ्ग-धनुः-च्युतैः released from the Sharnga bow

Anvaya: (शौरिः) शार्ङ्गधनुश्च्युतैः तीक्ष्णाग्रैः शरैः प्रेतमातृपिशाचान् कूष्माण्डान् ब्रह्मराक्षसान् च द्रावयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शार्ङ्गधनुश्च्युतैः तीक्ष्णाग्रैः शरैः With sharp pointed arrows released from the Sharnga bow, प्रेतमातृपिशाचान् the Pretas, Matris, and Pisachas, कूष्माण्डान् ब्रह्मराक्षसान् च and the Kushmandas and Brahma-rakshasas, द्रावयामास caused them to flee.

Simple Meaning: Krishna made the Pretas, Matris, Pisachas, Kushmandas, and Brahma-rakshasas scatter and flee using sharp-pointed arrows released from His Sharnga bow.

पृथग्विधानि प्रायुङ्क्त पिनाक्यस्त्राणि शार्ङ्गिणे ।
प्रत्यस्त्रैः शमयामास शार्ङ्गपाणिरविस्मितः ॥10.63.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: पृथक्-विधानि various kinds of, प्रायुङ्क्त hurled/released, पिनाकी Shiva who holds the Pinaka bow, अस्त्राणि weapons, शार्ङ्गिणे against Krishna who holds the Sharnga bow, प्रति-अस्त्रैः with counter-weapons, शमयामास neutralized, शार्ङ्ग-पाणिः Krishna holding the Sharnga bow in His hand, अविस्मितः unperturbed/without surprise

Anvaya: पिनाकी शार्ङ्गिणे पृथग्विधानि अस्त्राणि प्रायुङ्क्त, (तथा) अविस्मितः शार्ङ्गपाणिः प्रत्यस्त्रैः (तानि) शमयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पिनाकी शार्ङ्गिणे Shiva who holds the Pinaka bow against Krishna who holds the Sharnga bow, पृथग्विधानि अस्त्राणि प्रायुङ्क्त released various kinds of supernatural weapons. अविस्मितः शार्ङ्गपाणिः Unperturbed Krishna holding the Sharnga bow, प्रत्यस्त्रैः शमयामास neutralized them with counter weapons.

Simple Meaning: Shiva launched various powerful weapons against Krishna, but Krishna, remaining completely unperturbed, neutralized every single one of them with His counter-weapons.

ब्रह्मास्त्रस्य च ब्रह्मास्त्रं वायव्यस्य च पार्वतम् ।
आग्नेयस्य च पार्जन्यं नैजं पाशुपतस्य च ॥10.63.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्रह्म-अस्त्रस्य against the Brahma Astra weapon, च and, ब्रह्म-अस्त्रम् another Brahma Astra weapon, वायव्यस्य against the Vayavya Astra, च and, पार्वतम् the Parvata Astra, आग्नेयस्य against the Agneya Astra, च and, पार्जन्यम् the Parjanya Astra, नैजम् His own weapon (Narayana Astra), पाशुपतस्य against the Pashupata Astra weapon, च and

Anvaya: (शौरिः) ब्रह्मास्त्रस्य च ब्रह्मास्त्रं, वायव्यस्य च पार्वतं, आग्नेयस्य च पार्जन्यं, पाशुपतस्य च नैजं (प्रत्यस्त्रम् असृजत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्रह्मास्त्रस्य च ब्रह्मास्त्रं a Brahmastra against the Brahmastra, वायव्यस्य च पार्वतं and a Parvatastra against the Vayavya Astra, आग्नेयस्य च पार्जन्यं and a Parjanya Astra against the Agneya Astra, पाशुपतस्य च नैजं and His own Narayana Astra against the Pashupata Astra.

Simple Meaning: Krishna countered each of Shiva's weapons with its appropriate counter-weapon: He discharged a Brahma Astra against Shiva's Brahma Astra, a Parvata Astra against the Vayavya Astra, a Parjanya Astra against the Agneya Astra fire weapon, and His own personal weapon Narayana Astra against Shiva's Pasupata Astra.

प्रतीयसेऽथापि यथाविकारं स्वमायया सर्वगुणप्रसिद्ध्यै ॥10.63.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वम् You, एकः one, आद्यः original, पुरुषः Supreme Person, अद्वितीयः without a second, तुर्यः transcendental beyond three states of consciousness, स्व-दृक् self-effulgent, हेतुः cause of all causes, अहेतुः uncaused, ईशः Supreme Controller, प्रतीयसे You appear, अथ अपि and yet, यथा-विकारम् according to the transformations, स्व-मायया by Your own Maya, सर्व-गुण-प्रसिद्ध्यै for the manifestation of all cosmic qualities

Anvaya: त्वम् एकः आद्यः अद्वितीयः तुर्यः स्वदृक् हेतुः अहेतुः ईशः पुरुषः (असि)। अथ अपि स्वमायया सर्वगुणप्रसिद्ध्यै यथाविकारं प्रतीयसे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: त्वम् एकः You are the one, आद्यः अद्वितीयः original and secondless, तुर्यः स्वदृक् transcendental and self effulgent, हेतुः अहेतुः cause of all yet uncaused, ईशः पुरुषः Supreme Controlling Person, अथ अपि स्वमायया and yet by Your own Maya, सर्वगुणप्रसिद्ध्यै for the manifestation of all the three Gunas, यथाविकारं प्रतीयसे You appear according to various transformations.

Simple Meaning: You are the one and only original Supreme Person, secondless, transcendental, and self effulgent. You are the ultimate cause of everything while remaining uncaused Yourself, the supreme controller of all. Yet, by Your own divine energy known as Maya, You manifest Yourself in diverse form according to the material transformations (such as those of gods, men, birds, beasts and various other beings) for the manifestation of all the three Gunas.

यथैव सूर्यः पिहितश्छायया स्वया छायां च रूपाणि च सञ्चकास्ति ।
एवं गुणेनापिहितो गुणांस्त्वमात्मप्रदीपो गुणिनश्च भूमन् ॥10.63.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: यथा as, एव indeed, सूर्यः the sun, पिहितः covered, छायया by shadow, स्वया by its own, छायाम् shadow, च and, रूपाणि forms, च and, सञ्चकास्ति illuminates, एवम् in the same way, गुणेन by the gunas, अपिहितः covered, गुणान् the gunas, त्वम् You, आत्म-प्रदीपः self-luminous, गुणिनः the possessors of the gunas, च and, भूमन् O Infinite One

Anvaya: भूमन्! यथा स्वया छायया पिहितः एव सूर्यः छायां रूपाणि च सञ्चकास्ति, एवम् आत्मप्रदीपः त्वं गुणेन अपिहितः (अपि) गुणान् गुणिनः च (सञ्चकासि)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भूमन् O Infinite One, यथा स्वया छायया पिहितः एव सूर्यः just as the sun though covered by its own shadow, छायां रूपाणि च सञ्चकास्ति illuminates both the shadow and other forms, एवम् आत्मप्रदीपः त्वं in the same way You who are self luminous, गुणेन अपिहितः though appearing covered by material gunas, गुणान् गुणिनः च illuminates the gunas and their possessors.

Simple Meaning: O Infinite Lord, just as the sun appears covered by a cloud, which is merely a shadow created by its own heat, and yet continues to illuminate both that shadow and all other visible forms, similarly, You are self luminous. Even though You appear covered by Your material gunas, You remain the illuminator and witness of the gunas and all living entities who possess them.

यन्मायामोहितधियः पुत्रदारगृहादिषु ।
उन्मज्जन्ति निमज्जन्ति प्रसक्ता वृजिनार्णवे ॥10.63.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: यत्-माया-मोहित-धियः whose minds are bewildered by Your Maya, पुत्र-दार-गृह-आदिषु in sons, wife, home and other such attachments, उन्मज्जन्ति they emerge, निमज्जन्ति they sink, प्रसक्ताः attached, वृजिन-अर्णवे in the ocean of suffering

Anvaya: यन्मायामोहितधियः पुत्रदारगृहादिषु प्रसक्ताः वृजिनार्णवे उन्मज्जन्ति निमज्जन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यन्मायामोहितधियः Persons whose minds are bewildered by Your Maya, पुत्रदारगृहादिषु प्रसक्ताः attached to sons, wife, home and family, वृजिनार्णवे उन्मज्जन्ति निमज्जन्ति repeatedly sink and float in the ocean of miseries.

Simple Meaning: Those living beings whose minds are bewildered by Your divine illusion (Maya) become deeply attached to worldly possessions like children, spouse, house, and wealth. Entangled in these temporary attachments, they repeatedly float and sink in the vast ocean of material suffering.

देवदत्तमिमं लब्ध्वा नृलोकमजितेन्द्रियः ।
यो नाद्रियेत त्वत्पादौ स शोच्यो ह्यात्मवञ्चकः ॥10.63.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: देव-दत्तम् given by Lord (You), इमम् this, लब्ध्वा having attained, नृ-लोकम् human form of life, अजित-इन्द्रियः one who has not controlled the senses, यः who, न not, आद्रियेत worships, त्वत्-पादौ Your feet, सः he, शोच्यः pitiable, हि indeed, आत्म-वञ्चकः cheat of one's own self

Anvaya: देवदत्तम् इमं नृलोकं लब्ध्वा यः अजितेन्द्रियः त्वत्पादौ न आद्रियेत, सः आत्मवञ्चकः हि शोच्यः (अस्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: देवदत्तम् इमं नृलोकं लब्ध्वा Having attained this human body given by God, यः अजितेन्द्रियः who uncontrolled in senses, त्वत्पादौ न आद्रियेत does not worship Your feet, सः आत्मवञ्चकः he who cheats himself, हि शोच्यः is indeed pitiable.

Simple Meaning: Anyone who obtains this rare human body bestowed by You, yet fails to control the senses and does not worship Your lotus feet, is a cheat of his own self. Such a person is truly pitiable and wastes the valuable opportunity of human life.

यस्त्वां विसृजते मर्त्य आत्मानं प्रियमीश्वरम् ।
विपर्ययेन्द्रियार्थार्थं विषमत्त्यमृतं त्यजन् ॥10.63.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः who, त्वाम् You, विसृजते rejects, मर्त्यः a mortal, आत्मानम् the True Self, प्रियम् the dear one, ईश्वरम् the Supreme Controller, विपर्यय-इन्द्रिय-अर्थ-अर्थम् for the sake of perishable material sense objects, विषम् poison, अत्ति eats, अमृतम् nectar, त्यजन् abandoning

Anvaya: यः मर्त्यः आत्मानं प्रियम् ईश्वरं त्वां विसृजते, (सः) विपर्ययेन्द्रियार्थार्थं अमृतं त्यजन् विषम् अत्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः मर्त्यः That mortal who, आत्मानं प्रियम् ईश्वरं त्वां विसृजते rejects You who are the True Self dear Lord and Supreme Controller, विपर्ययेन्द्रियार्थार्थं for the sake of illusory material sense objects, अमृतं त्यजन् विषम् अत्ति abandoning nectar eats poison.

Simple Meaning: A mortal who rejects You—who are the true Soul, the most dear friend, and the Supreme Controller of all—for the sake of illusory and perishable sense objects is abandoning divine nectar to consume deadly poison.

अहं ब्रह्माथ विबुधा मुनयश्चामलाशयाः ।
सर्वात्मना प्रपन्नास्त्वामात्मानं प्रेष्ठमीश्वरम् ॥10.63.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: अहम् I, ब्रह्मा Lord Brahma, अथ and, विबुधाः the demigods, मुनयः sages, च and, अमल-आशयाः pure in heart, सर्व-आत्मना with all our heart and soul, प्रपन्नाः surrendered, त्वाम् unto You, आत्मानम् the True Self, प्रेष्ठम् the most beloved, ईश्वरम् the Supreme Controller

Anvaya: अहम् अथ ब्रह्मा विबुधाः अमलाशयाः मुनयः च आत्मानं प्रेष्ठम् ईश्वरं त्वां सर्वात्मना प्रपन्नाः (स्मः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अहम् अथ ब्रह्मा I and Lord Brahma, विबुधाः अमलाशयाः मुनयः च the demigods and pure hearted sages, आत्मानं प्रेष्ठम् ईश्वरं त्वां unto You who are the True Self most beloved and Supreme Controller, सर्वात्मना प्रपन्नाः have surrendered with our entire heart and soul.

Simple Meaning: I, Lord Brahma, all the demigods, and the sages of pure heart have surrendered completely with our whole heart and soul unto You, who are the true Self, the most beloved Lord, and the Supreme Controller of all.

तं त्वा जगत्स्थित्युदयान्तहेतुं समं प्रशान्तं सुहृदात्मदैवम् ।
अनन्यमेकं जगदात्मकेतं भवापवर्गाय भजाम देवम् ॥10.63.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् Him, त्वाम् You, जगत्-स्थिति-उदय-अन्त-हेतुम् the cause of the maintenance creation and destruction of the universe, समम् equal to all, प्रशान्तम् perfectly peaceful, सुहृत्-आत्म-दैवम् the well wisher the True Self and the Supreme God, अनन्यम् non-different, एकम् one without a second, जगत्-आत्म-केतम् the refuge of the universe and the soul, भव-अपवर्गाय for liberation from material existence, भजामः we worship, देवम् the Supreme Lord

Anvaya: जगत्स्थित्युदयान्तहेतुं समं प्रशान्तं सुहृदात्मदैवम् अनन्यम् एकं जगदात्मकेतं तं देवं त्वां भवापवर्गाय (वयं) भजाम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जगत्स्थित्युदयान्तहेतुं The cause of creation maintenance and destruction of the universe, समं प्रशान्तं equal to all and supreme peace, सुहृदात्मदैवम् well wisher True Self and God of all, अनन्यम् एकं one without a second and the only one, जगदात्मकेतं refuge of the universe and soul, तं देवं त्वां You that Supreme Lord, भवापवर्गाय भजाम we worship for liberation from material existence.

Simple Meaning: You are the ultimate cause of the creation, maintenance, and dissolution of this universe. You are equal to all beings, perfectly peaceful, the true well wisher, the innermost Self, and the supreme deity of everyone. You are one without a second and the sole refuge for all living beings and the cosmos. To obtain complete liberation from the cycle of material birth and death, we worship You, the Supreme Lord.

अयं ममेष्टो दयितोऽनुवर्ती मयाभयं दत्तममुष्य देव ।
सम्पाद्यतां तद् भवतः प्रसादो यथा हि ते दैत्यपतौ प्रसादः ॥10.63.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: अयम् this one, मम my, इष्टः worshipper, दयितः dear, अनुवर्ती follower, मया by me, अभयम् fearlessness, दत्तम् given, अमुष्य to him, देव O Lord, सम्पाद्यताम् let it be fulfilled, तत् that, भवतः Your, प्रसादः mercy, यथा as, हि indeed, ते Your, दैत्य-पतौ unto Prahlada the king of daityas, प्रसादः mercy

Anvaya: देव! अयं (बाणः) मम इष्टः दयितः अनुवर्ती (अस्ति)। अमुष्य मया अभयं  दत्तम्। यथा हि ते दैत्यपतौ प्रसादः (आसीत्), तत् भवतः प्रसादः सम्पाद्यताम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: देव O Supreme Lord, अयं (बाणः) मम इष्टः दयितः अनुवर्ती this Banasura is my worshipper dear and obedient servant, अमुष्य मया अभयं दत्तम् to him protection from fear was granted by me. यथा हि ते दैत्यपतौ प्रसादः Just as Your mercy was shown to Prahlada the king of daityas, तत् भवतः प्रसादः सम्पाद्यताम् let that mercy of Yours be fulfilled here as well.

Simple Meaning: O Lord, Banasura is my dear devotee, worshipper, and faithful follower, and I have granted him assurance of protection. Just as You previously showed Your special grace to his ancestor Prahlada, the king of daityas, please extend Your mercy to him now and fulfill my promise of protection.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
यदात्थ भगवंस्त्वन्नः करवाम प्रियं तव ।
भवतो यद् व्यवसितं तन्मे साध्वनुमोदितम् ॥10.63.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, यत् what, आत्थ You have spoken, भगवन् O venerable Lord, त्वत् by You, नः by Us, करवाम let it be done, प्रियम् pleasing, तव Your, भवतः by You, यत् whatever, व्यवसितम् decided, तत् that, मे by Me, साधु fully, अनुमोदितम् approved

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच—हे भगवन्! त्वत् यत् आत्थ, (तत्) तव प्रियं नः करवाम। भवतः यत् व्यवसितं, तत् मे साधु अनुमोदितम् (अस्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, भगवन् O venerable Lord, त्वत् यत् आत्थ what You have spoken, तव प्रियं नः करवाम what is pleasing to You, will be done by Us. भवतः यत् व्यवसितं Whatever is decided by You, तत् मे साधु अनुमोदितम् that is fully approved by Me.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord said: O venerable Lord Shiva, whatever You have requested, We shall certainly fulfill to please You. Whatever decision You have made in this matter is entirely approved by Me.

अवध्योऽयं ममाप्येष वैरोचनिसुतोऽसुरः ।
प्रह्रादाय वरो दत्तो न वध्यो मे तवान्वयः ॥10.63.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: अवध्यः not to be killed, अयम् this, मम by Me, अपि also, एषः this, वैरोचनि-सुतः son of Bali, असुरः asura, प्रह्रादाय unto Prahlada, वरः boon, दत्तः given, न not, वध्यः to be killed, मे by Me, तव your, अन्वयः descendant

Hiranyakashipu -> Prahlada -> Virochana -> Bali(Vairochani) -> Banasura

Anvaya: एषः वैरोचिनिसुतः असुरः मम अपि अवध्यः (अस्ति)। प्रह्रादाय वरः दत्तः (यत्) मे तव अन्वयः न वध्यः (इति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एषः वैरोचिनिसुतः असुरः This asura son of Bali, मम अपि अवध्यः is not to be killed by Me as well, प्रह्रादाय वरः दत्तः a boon was given unto Prahlada, मे तव अन्वयः न वध्यः that your lineage shall not be killed by Me.

Simple Meaning: This asura, the son of Bali, is also not to be killed by Me. I previously granted a boon to Prahlada promising that I would never slay any descendant born in his lineage.

दर्पोपशमनायास्य प्रवृक्णा बाहवो मया ।
सूदितं च बलं भूरि यच्च भारायितं भुवः ॥10.63.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: दर्प-उपशमनाय for curbing the pride, अस्य of him, प्रवृक्णाः severed, बाहवः arms, मया by Me, सूदितम् destroyed, च and, बलम् army, भूरि huge, यत् which, भारायितम् became a burden, भुवः of the earth

Anvaya: अस्य दर्पोपशमनाय मया बाहवः प्रवृक्णाः, यत् च भूरि बलं भुवः भारायितं (तत्) सूदितम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अस्य दर्पोपशमनाय For curbing his pride, मया बाहवः प्रवृक्णाः his arms were severed by Me, यत् च भूरि बलं and that huge army which, भुवः भारायितं became a burden to the earth, सूदितम् has been destroyed.

Simple Meaning: I have severed his extra arms simply to curb his false pride, and I have destroyed his vast army because it had become a heavy burden upon the earth.

चत्वारोऽस्य भुजाः शिष्टा भविष्यन्त्यजरामराः ।
पार्षदमुख्यो भवतो नकुतस्चिद्भयोऽसुरः ॥10.63.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: चत्वारः four, अस्य his, भुजाः arms, शिष्टाः remaining, भविष्यन्ति will be, अजराः free from old age, अमराः immortal, पार्षद-मुख्यः chief attendant, भवतः Your, न not, कुतश्चित् from anywhere, भयः fear, असुरः asura

Anvaya: अस्य चत्वारः भुजाः शिष्टाः अजरामराः भविष्यन्ति। (एषः) असुरः भवतः पार्षदमुख्यः (भविष्यति), (अस्य) कुतश्चित् भयः न (भविष्यति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अस्य चत्वारः भुजाः His four arms, शिष्टाः अजरामराः भविष्यन्ति remaining shall become free from aging and death. एषः असुरः This asura, भवतः पार्षदमुख्यः (भविष्यति) will be a principal attendant of Yours, अस्य कुतश्चित् भयः न and from nowhere shall he have any fear.

Simple Meaning: His four remaining arms shall remain intact, free from decay and death. This asura will serve as a chief attendant among Your followers, and he shall have nothing to fear from any quarter ever again.

इति लब्ध्वाभयं कृष्णं प्रणम्य शिरसासुरः ।
प्राद्युम्निं रथमारोप्य सवध्वा समुपानयत् ॥10.63.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, लब्ध्वा having obtained, अभयम् freedom from fear, कृष्णम् unto Krishna, प्रणम्य bowing down, शिरसा with his head, असुरः the asura Banasura, प्राद्युम्निम् the son of Pradyumna - Aniruddha, रथम् on the chariot, आरोप्य seating, स-वध्वा along with his bride Usha, समुपानयत् brought forward

Anvaya: इति अभयं लब्ध्वा असुरः शिरसा कृष्णं प्रणम्य, प्राद्युम्निं सवध्वा रथम् आरोप्य समुपानयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति अभयं लब्ध्वा Thus having obtained freedom from fear, असुरः शिरसा कृष्णं प्रणम्य the asura Banasura offered obeisances to Lord Krishna with his head, प्राद्युम्निं सवध्वा रथम् आरोप्य seating Aniruddha along with his bride Usha upon the chariot, समुपानयत् brought them forward.

Simple Meaning: Having thus received assurance of protection, the asura Banasura bowed his head down reverently to Lord Krishna. He then seated Aniruddha and his bride Usha together on a chariot and brought them forward.

अक्षौहिण्या परिवृतं सुवासःसमलङ्कृतम् ।
सपत्नीकं पुरस्कृत्य ययौ रुद्रानुमोदितः ॥10.63.51॥

Word-by-word meaning: अक्षौहिण्या by a full military division, परिवृतम् surrounded, सुवासः-समलङ्कृतम् nicely dressed and decorated, स-पत्नीकम् along with his wife, पुरस्कृत्य placing in front, ययौ departed, रुद्र-अनुमोदितः permitted by Lord Shiva

Anvaya: रुद्रानुमोदितः (कृष्णः) अक्षौहिण्या परिवृतं सुवासःसमलङ्कृतं सपत्नीकम् (अनिरुद्धं) पुरस्कृत्य ययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रुद्रानुमोदितः (कृष्णः) Krishna permitted by Shiva, अक्षौहिण्या परिवृतं surrounded by an akshauhini division, सुवासःसमलङ्कृतं finely dressed and ornamented, सपत्नीकम्  (अनिरुद्धं) Aniruddha along with his wife Usha, पुरस्कृत्य ययौ placing in front departed.

Simple Meaning: Taking leave with the permission of Lord Shiva, Lord Krishna placed His grandson Aniruddha and his wife Usha—who were beautifully dressed and adorned with ornaments—at the head of the procession. Surrounded by a full military division, Lord Krishna then departed for Dwaraka.

स्वराजधानीं समलङ्कृतां ध्वजैः सतोरणैरुक्षितमार्गचत्वराम् ।
विवेश शङ्खानकदुन्दुभिस्वनैरभ्युद्यतः पौरसुहृद्द्विजातिभिः ॥10.63.52॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्व-राजधानीम् His own capital city Dwaraka, समलङ्कृताम् beautifully decorated, ध्वजैः with flags, स-तोरणैः with archways, उक्षित-मार्ग-चत्वराम् with sprinkled roads and crossroads, विवेश He entered, शङ्ख-आनक-दुन्दुभि-स्वनैः amidst the sounds of conch shells and drums and kettle drums, अभ्युद्यतः welcomed, पौर-सुहृद्-द्विजातिभिः by the citizens friends and brahmanas

Anvaya: (कृष्णः) ध्वजैः सतोरणैः समलङ्कृताम् उक्षितमार्गचत्वरां स्वराजधानीं पौरसुहृद्द्विजातिभिः अभ्युद्यतः शङ्खानकदुन्दुभिस्वनैः विवेश।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (कृष्णः) Krishna, ध्वजैः सतोरणैः समलङ्कृताम् beautifully decorated with flags and arches, उक्षितमार्गचत्वरां with roads and crossroads sprinkled with scented water, स्वराजधानीं His own capital city Dwaraka, पौरसुहृद्द्विजातिभिः अभ्युद्यतः welcomed by citizens friends and brahmanas, शङ्खानकदुन्दुभिस्वनैः विवेश entered amidst the resounding of conch shells and drums.

Simple Meaning: Krishna entered His own capital city of Dwaraka, which was grandly decorated with flags and archways, and had its main streets and crossroads freshly sprinkled with water. He was warmly welcomed by the joyful citizens, friends, and brahmanas to the triumphant sounds of conchshells, kettledrums, and ceremonial drums.

य एवं कृष्णविजयं शङ्करेण च संयुगम् ।
संस्मरेत् प्रातरुत्थाय न तस्य स्यात् पराजयः ॥10.63.53॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः who, एवम् thus, कृष्ण-विजयम् the victory of Krishna, शङ्करेण with Lord Shiva, च and, संयुगम् the battle, संस्मरेत् remembers, प्रातः early morning, उत्थाय having awakened, न not, तस्य his, स्यात् there shall be, पराजयः defeat

Anvaya: यः प्रातः उत्थाय एवं कृष्णविजयं शङ्करेण संयुगं च संस्मरेत्, तस्य पराजयः न स्यात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः प्रातः उत्थाय Whoever getting up early in the morning, एवं कृष्णविजयं शङ्करेण संयुगं च संस्मरेत् thus remembers the victory of Lord Krishna and His battle with Lord Shiva, तस्य पराजयः न स्यात् there shall never be any defeat for him.

Simple Meaning: Anyone who wakes up early in the morning and regularly remembers Lord Krishna's victory and His grand battle with Lord Shiva will never suffer defeat anywhere in life.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे  “कृष्णबाणासुरयुद्धम् अनिरुद्धमोचनं च

 नाम” त्रिषष्टितमः अध्यायः ॥63॥

Thus ends the sixty-third discourse named “Fight between Krishna and Banasura. Aniruddha’s rescue.”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
