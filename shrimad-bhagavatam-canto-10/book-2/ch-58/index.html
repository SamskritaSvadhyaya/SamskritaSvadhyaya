श्री शुक उवाच
एकदा पाण्डवान् द्रष्टुं प्रतीतान् पुरुषोत्तमः ।
इन्द्रप्रस्थं गतः श्रीमान् युयुधानादिभिर्वृतः ॥1.58.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, एकदा once, पाण्डवान् the Pandavas, द्रष्टुम् to see, प्रतीतान् who had been revealed to be alive, पुरुष-उत्तमः the Supreme Person, इन्द्रप्रस्थम् to Indraprastha, गतः went, श्रीमान् the glorious Lord, युयुधान-आदिभिः by Yuyudhana and others, वृतः accompanied

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच - एकदा श्रीमान् पुरुषोत्तमः प्रतीतान् पाण्डवान् द्रष्टुं युयुधानादिभिः वृतः इन्द्रप्रस्थं गतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, एकदा श्रीमान् पुरुषोत्तमः Once the glorious Supreme Person, प्रतीतान् पाण्डवान् द्रष्टुं to see the Pandavas who had been revealed to be alive, युयुधानादिभिः वृतः accompanied by Yuyudhana and others, इन्द्रप्रस्थं गतः went to Indraprastha.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Once, upon learning that the Pandavas had been revealed to be alive, the glorious Supreme Lord Krishna, accompanied by Satyaki and others, went to Indraprastha to visit them.

दृष्ट्वा तमागतं पार्था मुकुन्दमखिलेश्वरम् ।
उत्तस्थुर्युगपद् वीराः प्राणा मुख्यमिवागतम् ॥10.58.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: दृष्ट्वा having seen, तम् Him, आगतम् arrived, पार्थाः the sons of Pritha the Pandavas, मुकुन्दम् Mukunda, अखिल-ईश्वरम् the Lord of all, उत्तस्थुः stood up, युगपत् simultaneously, वीराः the heroic brothers, प्राणाः the senses, मुख्यम् the life-air, इव like, आगतम् returned

Anvaya: वीराः पार्थाः आगतं तम् अखिलेश्वरं मुकुन्दं दृष्ट्वा, मुख्यं प्राणम् आगतम् (दृष्ट्वा) प्राणाः इव, युगपत् उत्तस्थुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वीराः पार्थाः The heroic Pandavas, आगतं तम् अखिलेश्वरं मुकुन्दं दृष्ट्वा having seen Him, Mukunda, the Lord of all, who had arrived, मुख्यं प्राणम् आगतम् (दृष्ट्वा) प्राणाः इव like the senses when the main life-air returns, युगपत् उत्तस्थुः simultaneously stood up.

Simple Meaning: Upon seeing Mukunda, the Master of the universe, arrive, the heroic Pandavas immediately stood up all at once, just as the physical senses and limbs of a body instantly awaken and rise when the life-air returns.

परिष्वज्याच्युतं वीरा अङ्गसङ्गहतैनसः ।
सानुरागस्मितं वक्त्रं वीक्ष्य तस्य मुदं ययुः ॥10.58.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: परिष्वज्य having embraced, अच्युतम् Lord Achyuta, वीराः the heroes, अङ्ग-सङ्ग-हत-एनसः whose sins were cleansed by the contact of His body, स-अनुराग-स्मितम् smiling with affection, वक्त्रम् the face, वीक्ष्य seeing, तस्य His, मुदम् joy, ययुः they attained

Anvaya: वीराः अच्युतं परिष्वज्य अङ्गसङ्गहतैनसः (भूत्वा), तस्य सानुरागस्मितं वक्त्रं वीक्ष्य मुदं ययुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वीराः The heroic brothers, अच्युतं परिष्वज्य having embraced Lord Achyuta, अङ्गसङ्गहतैनसः (भूत्वा) and having their sins destroyed by the contact of His body, तस्य सानुरागस्मितं वक्त्रं वीक्ष्य seeing His face smiling with affection, मुदं ययुः attained great joy.

Simple Meaning: By embracing Lord Achyuta, the heroic Pandavas were completely cleansed of all sins through the touch of His divine body. Looking upon His face, which beamed with an affectionate smile, they experienced deep joy.

युधिष्ठिरस्य भीमस्य कृत्वा पादाभिवन्दनम् ।
फाल्गुनं परिरभ्याथ यमाभ्यां चाभिवन्दितः ॥10.58.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: युधिष्ठिरस्य of Yudhishthira, भीमस्य of Bhima, कृत्वा having done, पाद-अभिवन्दनम् bowing down to the feet, फाल्गुनम् Arjuna, परिरभ्य embracing, अथ then, यमाभ्याम् by the twins Nakula and Sahadeva, च and, अभिवन्दितः was offered respects

Anvaya: युधिष्ठिरस्य भीमस्य च पादाभिवन्दनं कृत्वा, अथ फाल्गुनं परिरभ्य, यमाभ्यां च (सः) अभिवन्दितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: युधिष्ठिरस्य भीमस्य च पादाभिवन्दनं कृत्वा Having offered bows at the feet of Yudhishthira and Bhima, अथ फाल्गुनं परिरभ्य then embracing Arjuna, यमाभ्यां च (सः) अभिवन्दितः and He was offered respects by the twins Nakula and Sahadeva.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna offered His respects at the feet of Yudhishthira and Bhima, and then affectionately embraced Arjuna. Afterwards, the twins, Nakula and Sahadeva, bowed down and offered their respects to the Lord.

परमासन आसीनं कृष्णा कृष्णमनिन्दिता ।
नवोढा व्रीडिता किञ्चिच्छनैरेत्याभ्यवन्दत ॥10.58.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: परम-आसने on an exalted seat, आसीनम् seated, कृष्णा Draupadi, कृष्णम् Lord Krishna, अनिन्दिता faultless, नव-ऊढा newly married, व्रीडिता shy, किञ्चित् somewhat, शनैः slowly, एत्य approaching, अभ्यवन्दत offered her obeisances

Anvaya: अनिन्दिता नवोढा कृष्णा परमासने आसीनं कृष्णम् एत्य, किञ्चित् व्रीडिता शनैः अभ्यवन्दत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अनिन्दिता नवोढा कृष्णा The faultless and newly married Draupadi, परमासने आसीनं कृष्णम् एत्य approaching Lord Krishna who was seated on an exalted seat, किञ्चित् व्रीडिता शनैः अभ्यवन्दत offered her obeisances slowly, being somewhat shy.

Simple Meaning: The faultless Draupadi, who was then a newlywed bride, approached Lord Krishna as He sat upon an exalted seat. Feeling somewhat shy, she stepped forward slowly and offered her respectful obeisances to Him.

तथैव सात्यकिः पार्थैः पूजितश्चाभिवन्दितः ।
निषसादासनेऽन्ये च पूजिताः पर्युपासत ॥10.58.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: तथा एव in the same way, सात्यकिः Satyaki, पार्थैः by the sons of Pritha the Pandavas, पूजितः honored, च and, अभिवन्दितः greeted with respect, निषसाद sat down, आसने on a seat, अन्ये others, च and, पूजिताः honored, पर्युपासत sat around in attendance

Anvaya: तथा एव पार्थैः पूजितः च अभिवन्दितः सात्यकिः आसने निषसाद, अन्ये च पूजिताः पर्युपासत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तथा एव In the same way, पार्थैः पूजितः च अभिवन्दितः सात्यकिः Satyaki, being honored and respectfully greeted by the Pandavas, आसने निषसाद sat down on a seat, अन्ये च पूजिताः and the others, also being honored, पर्युपासत sat around them.

Simple Meaning: In the same manner, Satyaki was warmly welcomed, honored, and respectfully greeted by the Pandavas, after which he took his seat. The other companions who accompanied Lord Krishna were also honored and sat down around them.

पृथां समागत्य कृताभिवादनस्तयातिहार्दार्द्रदृशाभिरम्भितः ।
आपृष्टवांस्तां कुशलं सहस्नुषां पितृश्वसारं परिपृष्टबान्धवः ॥10.58.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: पृथाम् Kunti, समागत्य approaching, कृत-अभिवादनः having offered his obeisances, तया by her, अति-हार्द-आर्द्र-दृशा whose eyes were moist with deep affection, अभिरम्भितः embraced, आपृष्टवान् asked, ताम् her, कुशलम् about her welfare, सह-स्नुषाम् along with her daughter-in-law Draupadi, पितृ-श्वसारम् his father's sister, परिपृष्ट-बान्धवः having inquired about their relatives

Anvaya: पृथां समागत्य कृताभिवादनः, अतिहार्दार्द्रदृशा तया अभिरम्भितः परिपृष्टबान्धवः (सः), सहस्नुषां तां पितृश्वसारं कुशलम् आपृष्टवान्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पृथां समागत्य कृताभिवादनः Approaching Kunti and offering his obeisances, अतिहार्दार्द्रदृशा तया अभिरम्भितः embraced by her whose eyes were moist with deep affection, परिपृष्टबान्धवः and was inquired about their other relatives, सहस्नुषां तां पितृश्वसारं कुशलम् आपृष्टवान् asked about the welfare of his father's sister along with her daughter-in-law,

Simple Meaning: Approaching his aunt Kunti, Lord Krishna offered her His respectful obeisances. With her eyes moist with deep affection, Kunti warmly embraced Him and was asked about the well-being of all their other relatives. The Lord then inquired about the welfare of His father's sister and her daughter-in-law Draupadi.

तमाह प्रेमवैक्लव्यरुद्धकण्ठाश्रुलोचना ।
स्मरन्ती तान् बहून क्लेशान् क्लेशापायात्मदर्शनम् ॥10.58.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् to Him, आह said, प्रेम-वैक्लव्य-रुद्ध-कण्ठा-अश्रु-लोचना she whose throat was choked and eyes filled with tears due to the overwhelming emotion of love, स्मरन्ती remembering, तान् those, बहून् many, क्लेशान् sufferings, क्लेश-अपाय-आत्म-दर्शनम् whose very sight brings about the end of all miseries

Anvaya: तान् बहून् क्लेशान् स्मरन्ती, प्रेमवैक्लव्यरुद्धकण्ठाश्रुलोचना (पृथा) क्लेशापायात्मदर्शनं तम् आह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तान् बहून् क्लेशान् स्मरन्ती Remembering those many sufferings, प्रेमवैक्लव्यरुद्धकण्ठाश्रुलोचना she whose throat was choked and eyes filled with tears due to the overwhelming emotion of love, क्लेशापायात्मदर्शनं तम् to Him, whose very sight brings about the end of all miseries, आह spoke.

Simple Meaning: Remembering the many sufferings they had undergone, Kunti, her throat choked with intense love and her eyes filled with tears, spoke to Lord Krishna, whose very presence and sight bring about the end of all miseries.

तदैव कुशलं नोऽभूत् सनाथास्ते कृता वयम् ।
ज्ञातीन् नः स्मरता कृष्ण भ्राता मे प्रेषितस्त्वया ॥10.58.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदा एव at that very moment, कुशलम् welfare, नः our, अभूत् became, स-नाथाः having a protector, ते by You, कृताः made, वयम् we, ज्ञातीन् relatives, नः us, स्मरता remembering, कृष्ण O Krishna, भ्राता brother, मे my, प्रेषितः was sent, त्वया by You

Anvaya: कृष्ण! त्वया नः ज्ञातीन् स्मरता मे भ्राता प्रेषितः, तदैव नः कुशलम् अभूत्। ते वयं सनाथाः कृताः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्ण O Krishna, त्वया नः ज्ञातीन् स्मरता मे भ्राता प्रेषितः by You – who remembered us as Your relatives – my brother Akrura was sent, तदैव नः कुशलम् अभूत् at that very moment our welfare was assured. ते वयं सनाथाः कृताः By You, we were made to have a true protector.

Simple Meaning: O Krishna, at that very moment when You remembered us as Your relatives and sent my brother Akrura to visit us, our welfare was fully assured. By Your grace, we have truly been blessed with a protector.

न तेऽस्ति स्वपरभ्रान्तिर्विश्वस्य सुहृदात्मनः ।
तथापि स्मरतां शश्वत् क्लेशान् हंसि हृदि स्थितः ॥10.58.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, ते of You, अस्ति there is, स्व-पर-भ्रान्तिः the illusion of "mine" and "stranger", विश्वस्य of the universe, सुहृदः of the well-wishing friend, आत्मनः of the Indwelling Soul, तथापि nevertheless, स्मरताम् of those who remember You, शश्वत् constantly, क्लेशान् sufferings, हंसि You destroy, हृदि in the heart, स्थितः seated

Anvaya: विश्वस्य सुहृदः आत्मनः ते स्वपरभ्रान्तिः न अस्ति, तथापि हृदि स्थितः (त्वम्) शश्वत् स्मरतां क्लेशान् हंसि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विश्वस्य सुहृदः आत्मनः ते Of You, who are the well-wishing friend and Indwelling Soul of the entire universe, स्वपरभ्रान्तिः न अस्ति the illusion of making distinctions between "mine" and "others" does not exist. तथापि Nevertheless, हृदि स्थितः being seated within the heart, शश्वत् स्मरतां क्लेशान् हंसि You completely destroy the sufferings of those who constantly remember You.

Simple Meaning: Since You are the ultimate well-wishing friend and the Indwelling Soul of the entire universe, You harbor no illusory distinctions of "friend" or "stranger." Nevertheless, residing within the hearts of all, You completely destroy the sufferings of those who constantly remember You.

युधिष्ठिर उवाच
किं नु आचरितं श्रेयो न वेदाहमधीश्वर ।
योगेश्वराणां दुर्दर्शो यन्नो दृष्टः कुमेधसाम् ॥10.58.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: युधिष्ठिरः उवाच Yudhishthira said, किम् what, नु indeed, आचरितम् performed, श्रेयः pious activity, न not, वेद know, अहम् I, अधि-ईश्वर O supreme controller, योग-ईश्वराणाम् of the masters of yoga, दुर्दर्शः difficult to be seen, यत् because, नः by us, दृष्टः seen, कु-मेधसाम् who are of poor intelligence

Anvaya: युधिष्ठिरः उवाच - अधीश्वर! किं नु श्रेयः आचरितम्, अहं न वेद। योगेश्वराणां दुर्दर्शः, यत् कुमेधसां नः (त्वं) दृष्टः ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: युधिष्ठिरः उवाच Yudhishthira said, अधीश्वर O supreme controller, किं नु श्रेयः आचरितम् what pious activity indeed has been performed, अहम् न वेद I do not know. योगेश्वराणां दुर्दर्शः (असि) You are difficult to be seen even for the masters of yoga. कुमेधसां नः by us who are of poor intelligence, यत् (त्वं) दृष्टः that You are seen.

Simple Meaning: King Yudhishthira said: O Supreme Controller, I do not know what pious activities we have performed to deserve this, that You—who are rarely seen even by the greatest masters of yoga—are now visible to us, who possess such poor intelligence.

इति वै वार्षिकान् मासान् राज्ञा सोऽभ्यर्थितः सुखम् ।
जनयन् नयनानन्दमिन्द्रप्रस्थौकसां विभुः ॥10.58.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, वै indeed, वार्षिकान् of the rainy season, मासान् the months, राज्ञा by the King Yudhishthira, सः He Lord Krishna, अभ्यर्थीतः requested, सुखम् happily, जनयन् generating, नयन-आनन्दम् pleasure for the eyes, इन्द्रप्रस्थ-ओकसाम् of the residents of Indraprastha, विभुः the Almighty Lord

Anvaya: इति वै राज्ञा अभ्यर्थीतः सः विभुः इन्द्रप्रस्थौकसां नयनानन्दं जनयन् वार्षिकान् मासान् सुखम् (उवास)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति वै राज्ञा अभ्यर्थीतः Thus requested by the King, सः विभुः He, the Almighty Lord, इन्द्रप्रस्थौकसां नयनानन्दं जनयन् generating pleasure for the eyes of the residents of Indraprastha, वार्षिकान् मासान् सुखम् (उवास) happily resided there during the months of the rainy season.

Simple Meaning: Thus requested by King Yudhishthira, the Almighty Lord Krishna happily remained in Indraprastha during the months of the rainy season, bringing immense joy to the eyes of all its residents by His divine presence.

एकदा रथमारुह्य विजयो वानरध्वजम् ।
गाण्डीवं धनुरादाय तूणौ चाक्षयसायकौ ॥10.58.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: एकदा once, रथम् chariot, आरुह्य having ascended, विजयः Vijaya (Arjuna), वानर-ध्वजम् bearing the flag with the emblem of Hanuman, गाण्डीवम् the Gandiva, धनुः bow, आदाय taking, तूणौ two quivers, च and, अक्षय-सायकौ containing an inexhaustible supply of arrows

Anvaya: एकदा विजयः वानरध्वजं रथम् आरुह्य, गाण्डीवं धनुः अक्षयसायकौ तूणौ च आदाय (वनं गतः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एकदा विजयः Once Arjuna, वानरध्वजं रथम् आरुह्य having ascended his chariot which bore the emblem of Hanuman on its flag, गाण्डीवं धनुः his Gandiva bow, अक्षयसायकौ तूणौ च and two quivers containing an inexhaustible supply of arrows, आदाय taking, (वनं गतः) went to the forest.

Simple Meaning: Once, Arjuna ascended his chariot, which flew the flag bearing the emblem of Hanuman. Armed with his famous Gandiva bow and two quivers containing an inexhaustible supply of arrows, he set forth.

साकं कृष्णेन सन्नद्धो विहर्तुं विपिनं वनम् ।

Anvaya: कृष्णेन साकं सन्नद्धः परवीरहा (विजयः) विहर्तुं बहुव्यालमृगाकीर्णं विपिनं वनं प्राविशत्।

Simple Meaning: Fully equipped and accompanied by Lord Krishna, Arjuna, the destroyer of enemy heroes, entered a dense forest filled with numerous wild beasts and deer to sport and hunt.

तत्राविध्यच्छरैर्व्याघ्रान् सूकरान् महिषान् रुरून् ।
शरभान् गवयान् खड्गान् हरिणाच्छशशल्लकान् ॥10.58.15॥

Anvaya: तत्र (सः) शरैः व्याघ्रान् सूकरान् महिषान् रुरून् शरभान् गवयान् खड्गान् हरिणाच्छशशल्लकान् (च) अविध्यत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत्र (सः) There he (Arjuna), शरैः with arrows, व्याघ्रान् सूकरान् महिषान् रुरून् शरभान् गवयान् खड्गान् हरिणाच्छशशल्लकान् (च) अविध्यत् pierced tigers, boars, wild buffaloes, ruru deer, sharabhas, wild oxen, rhinoceroses, black deer, bears, rabbits, and porcupines.

Simple Meaning: There in the forest, Arjuna pierced various animals with his arrows, including tigers, boars, wild buffaloes, ruru deer, sharabhas, wild oxen, rhinoceroses, black deer, bears, rabbits, and porcupines.

तान् निन्युः किङ्करा राज्ञे मेध्यान् पर्वण्युपागते ।
तृट्परीतः परिश्रान्तो बीभत्सुर्यमुनामगात् ॥10.58.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: तान् them, निन्युः brought, किङ्कराः servants, राज्ञे to the king, मेध्यान् suitable for sacrifice, पर्वणि-उपागते when the festival arrived, तृट्-परीतः overcome by thirst, परिश्रान्तः exhausted, बीभत्सुः Arjuna, यमुनाम् to the Yamuna river, अगात् went

Anvaya: पर्वणि उपागते (सति) किङ्कराः तान् मेध्यान् राज्ञे निन्युः। तृट्परीतः परिश्रान्तः बीभत्सुः यमुनाम् अगात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पर्वणि उपागते (सति) When the festival arrived, किङ्कराः servants, तान् मेध्यान् राज्ञे निन्युः brought those sacrificial animals to the king. तृट्परीतः परिश्रान्तः बीभत्सुः Overcome by thirst and exhausted, Arjuna, यमुनाम् अगात् went to the Yamuna river.

Simple Meaning: When the time for the festival arrived, the servants brought those animals suitable for sacrifice to the king. Meanwhile, exhausted and overcome by thirst, Arjuna went to the banks of the Yamuna river.

तत्रोपस्पृश्य विशदं पीत्वा वारि महारथौ ।
कृष्णौ ददृशतुः कन्यां चरन्तीं चारुदर्शनाम् ॥10.58.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there, उपस्पृश्य having bathed, विशदम् pure, पीत्वा having drunk, वारि water, महा-रथौ the two great chariot warriors, कृष्णौ the two Krishnas (Krishna and Arjuna), ददृशतुः saw, कन्याम् a maiden, चरन्तीम् walking, चारु-दर्शनाम् beautiful to look at

Anvaya: तत्र उपस्पृश्य विशदं वारि पीत्वा (च) महारथौ कृष्णौ चारुदर्शनां चरन्तीं कन्यां ददृशतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत्र उपस्पृश्य विशदं वारि पीत्वा (च) There having bathed and drunk the pure water, महारथौ कृष्णौ the two great chariot warriors Krishna and Arjuna, चारुदर्शनां चरन्तीं कन्यां ददृशतुः saw a beautiful maiden walking.

Simple Meaning: After bathing and drinking the pure water there, the two great warriors Krishna and Arjuna saw an extraordinarily beautiful maiden walking along the river bank.

Note: Both of whom had a dark complexion and therefore were called as Krishna.

तामासाद्य वरारोहां सुद्विजां रुचिराननाम् ।
पप्रच्छ प्रेषितः सख्या फाल्गुनः प्रमदोत्तमाम् ॥10.58.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her, आसाद्य having approached, वर-आरोहाम् of beautiful hips, सु-द्विजाम् having beautiful teeth, रुचिर-आननाम् possessing a lovely face, पप्रच्छ asked, प्रेषितः prompted, सख्या by his friend (Krishna), फाल्गुनः Arjuna, प्रमदा-उत्तमाम् the best among women

Anvaya: सख्या प्रेषितः फाल्गुनः तां वरारोहां सुद्विजां रुचिराननां प्रमदोत्तमाम् आसाद्य पप्रच्छ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सख्या प्रेषितः फाल्गुनः Prompted by his friend Krishna, Arjuna, तां वरारोहां सुद्विजां रुचिराननां प्रमदोत्तमाम् आसाद्य पप्रच्छ approached and asked that excellent woman who possessed beautiful hips, beautiful teeth, and a lovely face.

Simple Meaning: Prompted by his friend Krishna, Arjuna approached that exceptionally beautiful woman, who possessed a lovely face, beautiful teeth, and an attractive form, and questioned her.

का त्वं कस्यासि सुश्रोणि कुतोऽसि किं चिकीर्षसि ।
मन्ये त्वां पतिमिच्छन्तीं सर्वं कथय शोभने ॥10.58.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: का who, त्वम् you, कस्य whose, असि are you, सु-श्रोणि O woman with beautiful hips, कुतः from where, असि are you, किम् what, चिकीर्षसि do you wish to do, मन्ये I think, त्वाम् you, पतिम् a husband, इच्छन्तीम् desiring, सर्वम् everything, कथय please tell, शोभने O beautiful one

Anvaya: सुश्रोणि! त्वं का? कस्य असि? कुतः असि? किं चिकीर्षसि? शोभने! त्वां पतिम् इच्छन्तीं मन्ये। सर्वं कथय।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सुश्रोणि! त्वं का O woman with beautiful hips, who are you? कस्य असि कुतः असि Whose daughter or relative are you, and where are you from? किं चिकीर्षसि What do you wish to do? शोभने! त्वां पतिम् इच्छन्तीं मन्ये O beautiful one, I think you are desiring a husband. सर्वं कथय Please tell me everything.

Simple Meaning: O beautiful lady with charming hips, who are you? Whose daughter are you, and where do you come from? What is your purpose here? O beautiful one, I assume you are looking for a husband. Please tell me everything.

कालिन्दी उवाच
अहं देवस्य सवितुर्दुहिता पतिमिच्छती ।
विष्णुं वरेण्यं वरदं तपः परममास्थिता ॥10.58.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: कालिन्दी Kalindi, उवाच said, अहम् I, देवस्य of the god, सवितुः of the sun, दुहिता the daughter, पतिम् a husband, इच्छती desiring, विष्णुम् Vishnu, वरेण्यम् the most desirable, वर-दम् the giver of boons, तपः austerity, परमम् supreme, आस्थिता have undertaken

Anvaya: कालिन्दी उवाच - अहं देवस्य सवितुः दुहिता वरेण्यं वरदं विष्णुं पतिम् इच्छती परमं तपः आस्थिता।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कालिन्दी उवाच Kalindi said, अहं देवस्य सवितुः दुहिता I am the daughter of the sun god, वरेण्यं वरदं विष्णुं पतिम् इच्छती desiring the most excellent giver of boons Lord Vishnu as my husband, परमं तपः आस्थिता have undertaken supreme austerity.

Simple Meaning: Kalindi said, I am the daughter of the sun god. Desiring to attain the most excellent giver of boons, Lord Vishnu, as my husband, I have undertaken supreme austerities.

नान्यं पतिं वृणे वीर तमृते श्रीनिकेतनम् ।
तुष्यतां मे स भगवान् मुकुन्दोऽनाथसंश्रयः॥10.58.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, अन्यम् other, पतिम् husband, वृणे I choose, वीर O heroic one, तम् him, ऋते except, श्री-निकेतनम् the abode of Goddess Lakshmi, तुष्यताम् may he be pleased, मे with me, सः that, भगवान् Supreme Lord, मुकुन्दः Mukunda, अनाथ-संश्रयः the shelter of the helpless

Anvaya: वीर! अहं श्रीनिकेतनं तम् ऋते अन्यं पतिं न वृणे। अनाथसंश्रयः सः भगवान् मुकुन्दः मे तुष्यताम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वीर! अहं श्रीनिकेतनं तम् ऋते अन्यं पतिं न वृणे O heroic one, I do not choose any other husband except Him, the abode of Goddess Lakshmi. अनाथसंश्रयः सः भगवान् मुकुन्दः मे तुष्यताम् May that Supreme Lord Mukunda, who is the shelter of the helpless, be pleased with me.

Simple Meaning: O heroic prince, I will not choose any husband other than Him, who is the absolute abode of Goddess Lakshmi. May that Supreme Lord Mukunda, the ultimate shelter of the helpless, be pleased with my devotion.

कालिन्दीति समाख्याता वसामि यमुनाजले ।
निर्मिते भवने पित्रा यावदच्युतदर्शनम् ॥10.58.22॥

Anvaya: कालिन्दी इति समाख्याता (अहं) पित्रा निर्मिते भवने यमुनाजले अच्युतदर्शनं यावत् वसामि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कालिन्दी इति समाख्याता (अहं) I, who am well-known as Kalindi, पित्रा निर्मिते भवने यमुनाजले live in the waters of the Yamuna in a mansion constructed by my father, अच्युतदर्शनं यावत् वसामि until I obtain the sight of Lord Achyuta.

Simple Meaning: Known by the name Kalindi, I live within the waters of the Yamuna inside a mansion built by my father, and I shall remain here until I get the vision of the infallible Lord Achyuta.

तथावदत् गुडाकेशो वासुदेवाय सोऽपि ताम् ।
रथमारोप्य तद् विद्वान् धर्मराजमुपागमत् ॥10.58.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: तथा as it was, अवदत् spoke, गुडाकेशः Arjuna, वासुदेवाय to Krishna, सः He (Krishna), अपि also, ताम् her, रथम्-आरोप्य having placed on the chariot, तत् that, विद्वान् knowing, धर्म-राजम् to King Yudhishthira, उपागमत् approached

Anvaya: गुडाकेशः तथा वासुदेवाय अवदत्। तत् विद्वान् सः अपि तां रथम् आरोप्य धर्मराजम् उपागमत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गुडाकेशः तथा वासुदेवाय अवदत् Arjuna spoke to Krishna exactly as it was told. तत् विद्वान् सः अपि Knowing that, He (Krishna) also, तां रथम् आरोप्य धर्मराजम् उपागमत् having placed her on the chariot, approached King Yudhishthira.

Simple Meaning: Arjuna repeated everything she had said to Krishna. Fully understanding her heart, Krishna placed the maiden upon His chariot, and then they both went back to meet King Yudhishthira.

Simple Meaning: Wishing to please His dear relatives the Pandavas, the Supreme Lord resided there. He acted as Arjuna's chariot driver so that Arjuna could offer the Khandava forest to the fire god Agni.

सोऽग्निस्तुष्टो धनुरदाद् हयाञ्छ्वेतान् रथं नृप ।

Anvaya: नृप! सः अग्निः तुष्टः (सन्) अर्जुनाय धनुः, श्वेतान् हयान्, रथम्, अक्षयौ तूणौ, अस्त्रिभिः अभेद्यम् वर्म च अदात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King, सः अग्निः तुष्टः (सन्) that fire god being satisfied, अर्जुनाय धनुः श्वेतान् हयान् रथम् अक्षयौ तूणौ अस्त्रिभिः अभेद्यम् वर्म च अदात् gave to Arjuna a bow, white horses, a chariot, two inexhaustible quivers, and armor impenetrable by weapons.

Simple Meaning: O King, being highly satisfied, the fire god Agni presented Arjuna with a mighty bow, white horses, a celestial chariot, two inexhaustible quivers, and protective armor that could not be pierced by any weapon.

मयश्च मोचितो वह्नेः सभां सख्य उपाहरत् ।

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच— नरेन्द्र! निजधर्मवर्तिनः राजन्यबन्धोः याच्ञा कविभिः विगर्हिता। तथापि तव सौहृदेच्छया त्वदीयां कन्यां याचे, वयं शुल्कदाः न हि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said— नरेन्द्र O ruler of men, निजधर्मवर्तिनः राजन्यबन्धोः याच्ञा कविभिः विगर्हिता begging by a member of the royal order who stays fixed in his own duty is condemned by learned authorities. तथापि तव सौहृदेच्छया त्वदीयां कन्यां याचे Nevertheless, out of a desire for your friendship, I ask for your daughter, वयं शुल्कदाः न हि though we are certainly not givers of a bride-price.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord said: O King, authorities condemn begging for a kshatriya who remains fixed in his prescribed duties. Nevertheless, desiring an alliance of friendship with you, I am asking for your daughter, though we certainly do not pay any bride-price or dowry for marriage.

राजा उवाच
कोऽन्यस्तेऽभ्यधिको नाथ कन्यावर इहेप्सितः।
गुणैकधाम्नो यस्याङ्गे श्रीर्वसत्यनपायिनी ॥10.58.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजा उवाच the King said, कः who, अन्यः other, ते than You, अभ्यधिकः superior, नाथ O Lord, कन्या-वरः a groom for the daughter, इह in this world, ईप्सितः desired, गुण-एक-धाम्नः of Him who is the sole abode of all transcendental qualities, यस्य on whose, अङ्गे chest or body, श्रीः the goddess of fortune, वसति resides, अनपायिनी eternally without ever leaving

Anvaya: राजा उवाच— नाथ! गुणैकधाम्नः यस्य अङ्गे अनपायिनी श्रीः वसति, इह ते अभ्यधिकः अन्यः कः ईप्सितः कन्यावरः?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजा उवाच The King said— नाथ O Lord, गुणैकधाम्नः यस्य अङ्गे अनपायिनी श्रीः वसति for You who are the sole abode of excellent qualities on whose chest the goddess of fortune eternally resides, इह ते अभ्यधिकः अन्यः कः ईप्सितः कन्यावरः what other groom superior to You could possibly be desired in this world for my daughter?

Simple Meaning: The King said: O Lord, You are the ultimate abode of all divine qualities, and the goddess of fortune herself permanently resides upon Your chest. What other groom superior to You could anyone ever desire for their daughter in this world?

किन्तु अस्माभिः कृतः पूर्वं समयः सात्वतर्षभ ।
पुंसां वीर्यपरीक्षार्थं कन्यावरपरीप्सया ॥10.58.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: किन्तु but, अस्माभिः by us, कृतः was made, पूर्वम् previously, समयः a condition, सात्वत-ऋषभ O chief of the Satvatas, पुंसाम् of men, वीर्य-परीक्षा-अर्थम् for the sake of testing the prowess, कन्या-वर-परीप्सया with a desire to evaluate the groom for the daughter

Anvaya: सात्वतर्षभ! किन्तु अस्माभिः पूर्वं कन्यावरपरीप्सया पुंसां वीर्यपरीक्षार्थं समयः कृतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सात्वतर्षभ O chief of the Satvatas, किन्तु अस्माभिः पूर्वं but a condition was made by us previously, कन्यावरपरीप्सया पुंसां वीर्यपरीक्षार्थं समयः कृतः with a desire to evaluate the groom for my daughter and for the sake of testing the prowess of the suitors.

Simple Meaning: O chief of the Satvatas! However, we have previously set a strict condition to test the prowess of any suitor, out of a desire to properly evaluate the prospective groom for my daughter.

सप्तैते गोवृषा वीर दुर्दान्ता दुरवग्रहाः ।
एतैर्भग्नाः सुबहवो भिन्नगात्रा नृपात्मजाः ॥10.58.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: सप्त seven, एते these, गो-वृषाः bulls, वीर O hero, दुर्दान्ताः untamable, दुरवग्रहाः uncontrollable, एतैः by them, भग्नाः broken, सु-बहवः very many, भिन्न-गात्राः having their limbs shattered, नृप-आत्मजाः sons of the kings

Anvaya: वीर! एते सप्त गोवृषाः दुर्दान्ताः दुरवग्रहाः (सन्ति)। एतैः सुबहवः नृपात्मजाः भग्नाः भिन्नगात्राः (च जाताः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वीर O hero, एते सप्त गोवृषाः दुर्दान्ताः दुरवग्रहाः these seven bulls are untamable and uncontrollable. एतैः सुबहवः नृपात्मजाः भग्नाः भिन्नगात्राः By them very many princes have been broken and have had their limbs shattered.

Simple Meaning: O heroic one, these seven bulls are entirely untamable and uncontrollable. They have crushed many prospective princes who tried to subdue them, leaving them with shattered limbs.

यदिमे निगृहीताः स्युस्त्वयैव यदुनन्दन ।
वरो भवानभिमतो दुहितुर्मे श्रियः पते ॥10.58.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: यदि if, इमे these, निगृहीताः subdued, स्युः should be, त्वया एव by you alone, यदु-नन्दन O joy of the Yadus, वरः groom, भवान् you, अभिमतः desired, दुहितुः of daughter, मे my, श्रियः पते O Lord of Shri

Anvaya: यदुनन्दन! श्रियः पते! यदि इमे त्वया एव निगृहीताः स्युः, (तर्हि) भवान् मे दुहितुः अभिमतः वरः (भविष्यति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यदुनन्दन O joy of the Yadus, श्रियः पते O Lord of Shri, यदि इमे त्वया एव निगृहीताः स्युः if these are subdued by you alone, भवान् मे दुहितुः अभिमतः वरः you will be the desired groom of my daughter.

Simple Meaning: O joy of the Yadus, O Lord of Shri, if these bulls are subdued by you alone, then you will be the most desirable groom for my daughter.

एवं समयमाकर्ण्य बद्ध्वा परिकरं प्रभुः ।
आत्मानं सप्तधा कृत्वा न्यगृह्णाल्लीलयैव तान् ॥10.58.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, समयम् condition, आकर्ण्य having heard, बद्ध्वा girding, परिकरम् waistcloth, प्रभुः the Lord, आत्मानम् Himself, सप्त-धा into seven forms, कृत्वा having divided, न्यगृह्णात् subdued, लीलया एव effortlessly indeed, तान् them

Anvaya: प्रभुः एवं समयम् आकर्ण्य परिकरं बद्ध्वा आत्मानं सप्तधा कृत्वा तान् लीलया एव न्यगृह्णात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रभुः एवं समयम् आकर्ण्य The Lord having heard thus the condition, परिकरं बद्ध्वा girding His waistcloth, आत्मानं सप्तधा कृत्वा having divided Himself into seven forms, तान् लीलया एव न्यगृह्णात् effortlessly indeed subdued them.

Simple Meaning: Having heard this condition, the Lord girded His waistcloth, expanded Himself into seven forms, and effortlessly subdued the bulls.

बद्ध्वा तान् दामभिः शौरिर्भग्नदर्पान् हतौजसः ।
व्यकर्षल्लीलया बद्धान् बालो दारुमयान् यथा ॥10.58.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: बद्ध्वा having bound, तान् them, दामभिः with ropes, शौरिः Krishna, भग्न-दर्पान् whose pride was broken, हत-ओजसः whose strength was destroyed, व्यकर्षत् dragged, लीलया effortlessly, बद्धान् bound, बालः a child, दारु-मयान् made of wood, यथा just like

Anvaya: शौरिः भग्नदर्पान् हतौजसः तान् दामभिः बद्ध्वा, बद्धान् दारुमयान् (मृगान्) बालः यथा, लीलया व्यकर्षत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शौरिः भग्नदर्पान् हतौजसः तान् दामभिः बद्ध्वा Krishna having bound with ropes those bulls whose pride was broken and whose strength was destroyed, बद्धान् दारुमयान् बालः यथा लीलया व्यकर्षत् effortlessly dragged the bound bulls just like a child drags toy animals made of wood.

Simple Meaning: Having bound the bulls with ropes after breaking their pride and destroying their strength, Krishna effortlessly dragged them along just like a young child drags toy animals made of wood.

ततः प्रीतः सुतां राजा ददौ कृष्णाय विस्मितः ।
तां प्रत्यगृह्णाद् भगवान् विधिवत् सदृशीं प्रभुः ॥10.58.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, प्रीतः pleased, सुताम् daughter, राजा the king, ददौ gave, कृष्णाय to Krishna, विस्मितः astonished, ताम् her, प्रत्यगृह्णात् accepted, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, विधिवत् according to Vedic rituals, सदृशीम् suitable, प्रभुः the master

Anvaya: ततः विस्मितः राजा प्रीतः (सन्) कृष्णाय सुतां ददौ। भगवान् प्रभुः सदृशीं तां विधिवत् प्रत्यगृह्णात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः विस्मितः राजा Then the astonished king, प्रीतः कृष्णाय सुतां ददौ being pleased gave his daughter to Krishna. भगवान् प्रभुः The Supreme Lord and master, सदृशीं तां her who was suitable for Him, विधिवत् प्रत्यगृह्णात् accepted according to Vedic rituals.

Simple Meaning: Then, filled with astonishment and pleasure, the king presented his daughter to Krishna. The Supreme Lord and master accepted the suitable bride according to proper Vedic rituals.

राजपत्न्यश्च दुहितुः कृष्णं लब्ध्वा प्रियं पतिम् ।
लेभिरे परमानन्दं जातश्च परमोत्सवः ॥10.58.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: राज-पत्न्यः the queens, च and, दुहितुः of their daughter, कृष्णम् Krishna, लब्ध्वा having obtained, प्रियम् dear, पतिम् husband, लेभिरे obtained, परम-आनन्दम् supreme happiness, जातः arose, च and, परम-उत्सवः a great celebration

Anvaya: राजपत्न्यः च दुहितुः प्रियं पतिं कृष्णं लब्ध्वा परमानन्दं लेभिरे, परमोत्सवः च जातः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजपत्न्यः च दुहितुः प्रियं पतिं कृष्णं लब्ध्वा The queens also having obtained Krishna as the dear husband of their daughter, परमानन्दं लेभिरे obtained supreme happiness, परमोत्सवः च जातः and a great celebration arose.

Simple Meaning: The queens were also filled with supreme happiness upon obtaining Krishna as the beloved husband of their daughter, and a grand celebration took place.

शङ्खभेर्यानका नेदुर्गीतवाद्यद्विजाशिषः ।
नरा नार्यः प्रमुदिताः सुवासः स्रगलङ्कृताः ॥10.58.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: शङ्ख-भेरि-आनकाः conch shells, kettledrums, and tabors, नेदुः sounded, गीत-वाद्य-द्विज-आशिषः with songs, instrumental music, and the blessings of the brahmanas, नराः men, नार्यः women, प्रमुदिताः overjoyed, सु-वासः-स्रक्-अलङ्कृताः adorned with fine clothes and garlands

Anvaya: शङ्खभेर्यानकाः नेदुः (तथा) गीतवाद्यद्विजाशिषः (अभवन्)। सुवासःस्रगलङ्कृताः नराः नार्यः च प्रमुदिताः (अभवन्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शङ्खभेर्यानकाः नेदुः गीतवाद्यद्विजाशिषः Conch shells, kettledrums, and tabors sounded, accompanied by songs, instrumental music, and the blessings of the brahmanas. सुवासःस्रगलङ्कृताः नराः नार्यः च प्रमुदिताः Adorned with fine clothes and garlands, the men and women were overjoyed.

Simple Meaning: Conch shells, kettledrums, and tabors resounded alongside songs, instrumental music, and the blessings of the brahmanas. Dressed in fine clothes and beautiful garlands, all the men and women of the city were overjoyed.

दशधेनुसहस्राणि पारिबर्हमदाद् विभुः ।
युवतीनां त्रिसाहस्रं निष्कग्रीवसुवाससाम् ॥10.58.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: दश-धेनु-सहस्राणि ten thousand cows, पारिबर्हम् as a dowry, अदात् gave, विभुः the powerful king, युवतीनाम् of young maid servants, त्रि-साहस्रम् three thousand, निष्क-ग्रीव-सु-वाससाम् whose necks were adorned with gold lockets and who were dressed in fine garments

Anvaya: विभुः दशधेनुसहस्राणि, निष्कग्रीवसुवाससाम् युवतीनां त्रिसाहस्रं (च) पारिबर्हम् अदात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विभुः The powerful king, दशधेनुसहस्राणि ten thousand cows, निष्कग्रीवसुवाससाम् युवतीनां त्रिसाहस्रं (च) and three thousand young maid servants whose necks were adorned with gold lockets and who were dressed in fine garments,  पारिबर्हम् अदात् gave as dowry.

Simple Meaning: The powerful king presented a grand dowry consisting of ten thousand cows and three thousand young maid servants adorned with fine garments and golden necklaces.

नवनागसहस्राणि नागाच्छतगुणान् रथान् ।
रथाच्छतगुणानश्वानश्वाच्छतगुणान् नरान् ॥10.58.51॥

Word-by-word meaning: नव-नाग-सहस्राणि nine thousand elephants, नागात् than the elephants, शत-गुणान् a hundred times more, रथान् chariots, रथात् than the chariots, शत-गुणान् a hundred times more, अश्वान् horses, अश्वात् than the horses, शत-गुणान् a hundred times more, नरान् infantrymen

Anvaya: (सः) नवनागसहस्राणि, नागात् शतगुणान् रथान्, रथात् शतगुणान् अश्वान्, अश्वात् शतगुणान् नरान् (च पारिबर्हम् अदात्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नवनागसहस्राणि नागात् शतगुणान् रथान् Nine thousand elephants, and a hundred times more chariots than the elephants, रथात् शतगुणान् अश्वान् अश्वात् शतगुणान् नरान् a hundred times more horses than the chariots, and a hundred times more infantrymen than the horses he gave as dowry.

Simple Meaning: The king also included in the dowry nine thousand elephants, a hundred times as many chariots as elephants, a hundred times as many horses as chariots, and a hundred times as many infantrymen as horses.

दम्पती रथमारोप्य महत्या सेनया वृतौ ।
स्नेहप्रक्लिन्नहृदयो यापयामास कोसलः॥10.58.52॥

Word-by-word meaning: दम्पती the newlywed couple, रथम् onto the chariot, आरोप्य having placed, महत्या by a large, सेनया army, वृतौ surrounded, स्नेह-प्रक्लिन्न-हृदयः whose heart was melting with affection, यापयामास sent off, कोसलः the king of Kosala

Anvaya: कोसलः स्नेहप्रक्लिन्नहृदयः (सन्) दम्पती रथम् आरोप्य महत्या सेनया वृतौ (कृत्वा) यापयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कोसलः स्नेहप्रक्लिन्नहृदयः दम्पती रथम् आरोप्य The king of Kosala whose heart was melting with affection having placed the newlywed couple onto the chariot, महत्या सेनया वृतौ यापयामास sent them off surrounded by a large army.

Simple Meaning: His heart melting with deep affection, the king of Kosala placed the newlywed couple onto their chariot and sent them off accompanied by a massive escorting army.

श्रुत्वैतद् रुरुधुर्भूपा नयन्तं पथि कन्यकाम् ।
भग्नवीर्यः सुदुर्मर्षा यदुभिर्गोवृषैः पुरा ॥10.58.53॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रुत्वा having heard, एतत् this, रुरुधुः blocked, भूपाः the kings, नयन्तम् who was taking away, पथि on the road, कन्यकाम् the bride, भग्न-वीर्याः whose prowess was broken, सु-दुर्मर्षाः very intolerant, यदुभिः by the Yadus, गो-वृषैः by the bulls, पुरा previously

Anvaya: एतत् श्रुत्वा, पुरा गोवृषैः यदुभिः च भग्नवीर्याः सुदुर्मर्षाः भूपाः पथि कन्यकां नयन्तं (कृष्णं) रुरुधुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एतत् श्रुत्वा Having heard this, पुरा गोवृषैः यदुभिः च भग्नवीर्याः सुदुर्मर्षाः भूपाः the very intolerant kings whose prowess was previously broken by the bulls and by the Yadus, पथि कन्यकां नयन्तं रुरुधुः blocked Krishna on the road as He was taking away the bride.

Simple Meaning: Hearing this news, the highly intolerant rival kings, whose pride and prowess had previously been crushed by both the bulls and the Yadus, gathered on the road to block Krishna as He was taking His bride home.

तानस्यतः शरव्रातान् बन्धुप्रियकृदर्जुनः ।
गाण्डीवी कालयामास सिंहः क्षुद्रमृगानिव ॥10.58.54॥

Word-by-word meaning: तान् them, अस्यतः shooting, शर-व्रातान् showers of arrows, बन्धु-प्रिय-कृत् who acts for the pleasure of His friend, अर्जुनः Arjuna, गाण्डीवी the wielder of the Gandiva bow, कालयामास drove away, सिंहः a lion, क्षुद्र-मृगान small animals, इव just like

Anvaya: बन्धुप्रियकृत् गाण्डीवी अर्जुनः शरव्रातान् अस्यतः तान्, सिंहः क्षुद्रमृगान् इव, कालयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बन्धुप्रियकृत् गाण्डीवी अर्जुनः Arjuna the wielder of the Gandiva bow who acts for the pleasure of His friend, शरव्रातान् अस्यतः तान् those kings who were shooting showers of arrows, सिंहः क्षुद्रमृगान् इव कालयामास just like a lion (drives away) small animals, drove away.

Simple Meaning: Acting for the pleasure of His dear friend Krishna, Arjuna, wielding his famous Gandiva bow, easily drove away those attacking kings and their showers of arrows, just as a powerful lion scatters helpless small animals.

पारिबर्हमुपागृह्य द्वारकामेत्य सत्यया ।
रेमे यदूनामृषभो भगवान् देवकीसुतः ॥10.58.55॥

Word-by-word meaning: पारिबर्हम् the dowry, उपागृह्य having accepted, द्वारकाम् to Dwaraka, एत्य having returned, सत्यया with Satya, रेमे enjoyed, यदूनाम् of the Yadus, ऋषभः the chief, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, देवकी-सुतः the son of Devaki

Anvaya: यदूनाम् ऋषभः भगवान् देवकीसुतः पारिबर्हम् उपागृह्य सत्यया (सह) द्वारकाम् एत्य रेमे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यदूनाम् ऋषभः भगवान् देवकीसुतः पारिबर्हम् उपागृह्य The chief of the Yadus the Supreme Lord the son of Devaki having accepted the dowry, सत्यया सह द्वारकाम् एत्य रेमे having returned to Dwaraka with Satya enjoyed.

Simple Meaning: Having accepted the grand dowry, the Supreme Lord, the son of Devaki and chief of the Yadus, returned to Dwaraka and lived happily there with His new bride, Satya.

श्रुतकीर्तेः सुतां भद्रामुपयेमे पितृष्वसुः ।
कैकेयीं भ्रातृभिर्दत्तां कृष्णः सन्तर्दनादिभिः ॥10.58.56॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रुतकीर्तेः of Shrutakirti, सुताम् the daughter, भद्राम् Bhadra, उपयेमे married, पितृ-ष्वसुः of His paternal aunt, कैकेयीम् the princess of Kaikeya, भ्रातृभिः by the brothers, दत्ताम् given, कृष्णः Krishna, सन्तर्दन-आदिभिः headed by Santardana

Anvaya: कृष्णः पितृष्वसुः श्रुतकीर्तेः सुतां कैकेयीं, भ्रातृभिः सन्तर्दनादिभिः दत्तां, भद्राम् उपयेमे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्णः पितृष्वसुः श्रुतकीर्तेः सुतां कैकेयीं Krishna married the princess of Kaikeya who was the daughter of His paternal aunt Shrutakirti, भ्रातृभिः सन्तर्दनादिभिः दत्तां भद्राम् उपयेमे who was given in marriage by her brothers headed by Santardana, who was named Bhadra.

Simple Meaning: Krishna then married Bhadra, the princess of Kaikeya and the daughter of His paternal aunt Shrutakirti. She was given to Him in marriage by her brothers, who were led by Santardana.

सुतां च मद्राधिपतेर्लक्ष्मणां लक्षणैर्युताम् ।
स्वयंवरे जहारैकः स सुपर्णः सुधामिव ॥10.58.57॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुताम् daughter, च and, मद्र-अधिपतेः of the king of Madra, लक्ष्मणाम् Lakshmanaa, लक्षणैः with auspicious qualities, युताम् endowed, स्वयंवरे in the swayamvara assembly, जहार carried away, एकः alone, सः he, सुपर्णः Garuda, सुधाम् the nectar, इव just like

Anvaya: च (कृष्णः) स्वयंवरे लक्षणैः युतां मद्राधिपतेः सुतां लक्ष्मणां, सः सुपर्णः सुधाम् इव, एकः जहार।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: च स्वयंवरे लक्षणैः युतां मद्राधिपतेः सुतां लक्ष्मणां And in the swayamvara assembly the daughter of the king of Madra named Lakshmanaa who was endowed with auspicious qualities, सः सुपर्णः सुधाम् इव एकः जहार he alone carried away just like Garuda carried away the nectar.

Simple Meaning: Krishna also single-handedly carried away Lakshmanaa, the exceptionally qualified daughter of the king of Madra, from her own svayamvara assembly, just as Garuda once carried away the celestial nectar.

Note: The story of how Krishna married Lakshmanaa is narrated by Lakshmanaa herself in Chapter 83, when Draupadi asks each of Krishna’s wives to narrate the story of how Krishna marries each of them. 

अन्याश्चैवंविधा भार्याः कृष्णस्यासन् सहस्रशः ।
भौमं हत्वा तन्निरोधादाहृताश्चारुदर्शनाः ॥10.58.58॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्याः other, च and, एवम् विधाः of this kind, भार्याः wives, कृष्णस्य of Krishna, आसन् there were, सहस्रशः by the thousands, भौमम् Bhauma, हत्वा having killed, तत्-निरोधात् from his confinement, आहृताः brought away, चारु-दर्शनाः beautiful in appearance

Anvaya: एवं विधाः कृष्णस्य सहस्रशः अन्याः च चारुदर्शनाः भार्याः आसन्, (याः) भौमं हत्वा तन्निरोधात् आहृताः (आसन्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवं विधाः कृष्णस्य सहस्रशः अन्याः च चारुदर्शनाः भार्याः आसन् In this way, Krishna had thousands of other such beautiful wives, (याः) भौमं हत्वा तन्निरोधात् आहृताः who were brought away from confinement after He killed Bhauma.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna had thousands of other such beautiful wives. He wedded them after killing the asura Bhauma (also known as Narakasura) and releasing them from the captivity in which they were being held.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “कृष्णस्य शेषपञ्चमहिषीपरिणयः नाम”​​ अष्टप​ञ्चाशत्तमः अध्यायः ॥58॥

Thus ends the fifty-eighth discourse named “Krishna’s marriage to the rest of his five principal wives” in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
