Chapter 53

Krishna carries away Rukmini

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

रुक्मिणीहरणं नाम त्रिपञ्चाशत्तमः अध्यायः

(57 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
वैदर्भ्याः स तु सन्देशं निशम्य यदुनन्दनः ।
प्रगृह्य पाणिना पाणिं प्रहसन्निदमब्रवीत् ॥10.53.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, वैदर्भ्याः of the daughter of Vidarbha, सः He, तु indeed, सन्देशम् the message, निशम्य hearing, यदु-नन्दनः the beloved descendant of Yadu, प्रगृह्य grasping, पाणिना with his hand, पाणिम् the hand, प्रहसन् smiling, इदम् this, अब्रवीत् spoke

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच — यदुनन्दनः सः तु वैदर्भ्याः सन्देशं निशम्य पाणिना पाणिं प्रगृह्य प्रहसन् इदम् अब्रवीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, यदुनन्दनः the beloved descendant of Yadu, सः He, तु indeed, वैदर्भ्याः of the daughter of Vidarbha, सन्देशं the message, निशम्य hearing, पाणिना with his hand, पाणिं the hand, प्रगृह्य grasping, प्रहसन् smiling, इदम् this, अब्रवीत् spoke.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Hearing this message from Rukmini, the daughter of Vidarbha, Lord Krishna, the beloved descendant of Yadu, took the hand of the brahmana in His own hand and spoke to him with a smile.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
तथाहमपि तच्चित्तो निद्रां च न लभे निशि ।
वेदाहं रुक्मिणा द्वेषान्ममोद्वाहो निवारितः ॥10.53.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, तथा in the same way, अहम् I, अपि also, तत्-चित्तः mind fixed on her, निद्राम् sleep, च and, न not, लभे obtain, निशि at night, वेद know, अहम् I, रुक्मिणा by Rukmi, द्वेषात् out of enmity, मम my, उद्वाहः marriage, निवारितः stopped

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच — तथा अहम् अपि तच्चित्तः निशि निद्रां च न लभे। अहम् वेद रुक्मिणा द्वेषात् मम उद्वाहः निवारितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, तथा अहम् in the same way I, अपि also, तच्चित्तः mind fixed on her, निशि at night, निद्रां sleep, च and, न लभे do not obtain. अहम् वेद I know, रुक्मिणा by Rukmi, द्वेषात् out of enmity, मम my, उद्वाहः marriage, निवारितः has been stopped.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord said, Just like her, my mind is also constantly fixed on her, and I cannot find any sleep at night. I am well aware that Rukmi has stopped my marriage to her solely out of enmity toward me.

तामानयिष्य उन्मथ्य राजन्यापसदान् मृधे ।
मत्परामनवद्याङ्गीमेधसोऽग्निशिखामिव ॥10.53.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her, आनयिष्ये I will bring, उन्मथ्य crushing, राजन्य-अपसदान् the degraded kings, मृधे in battle, मत्-पराम् dedicated to Me, अनवद्या-अङ्गीम् of faultless limbs, एधसः from firewood, अग्नि-शिखाम् a flame of fire, इव like

Anvaya: एधसः अग्निशिखाम् इव मत्पराम् अनवद्याङ्गीं तां मृधे राजन्यापसदान् उन्मथ्य आनयिष्ये।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एधसः From firewood, अग्निशिखाम् a flame of fire, इव like, मत्पराम् dedicated to Me, अनवद्याङ्गीं of faultless limbs, तां her, मृधे in battle, राजन्यापसदान् the degraded kings, उन्मथ्य crushing, आनयिष्ये I will bring.

Simple Meaning: Just as one extracts a brilliant flame of fire from firewood, I will rescue her, the princess of unquestionable beauty, and who is entirely dedicated to Me, by thoroughly crushing those degraded kings in battle.

श्री शुक उवाच
उद्वाहर्क्षं च विज्ञाय रुक्मिण्या मधुसूदनः ।
रथः संयुज्यतामाशु दारुकेत्याह सारथिम् ॥10.53.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, उद्वाह-ऋक्षम् the marriage star, च and, विज्ञाय knowing, रुक्मिण्याः of Rukmini, मधुसूदनः the killer of Madhu, रथः the chariot, संयुज्यताम् let it be prepared, आशु quickly, दारुक O Daruka, इति thus, आह said, सारथिम् to the chariot driver

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच — रुक्मिण्याः उद्वाहर्क्षं च विज्ञाय मधुसूदनः सारथिम् आह- दारुक! आशु रथः संयुज्यताम् – इति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, रुक्मिण्याः of Rukmini, उद्वाहर्क्षं the marriage star, च and, विज्ञाय knowing, मधुसूदनः the killer of Madhu, सारथिम् to the chariot driver, आह said, दारुक O Daruka, आशु quickly, रथः the chariot, संयुज्यताम् let it be prepared, इति thus.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said, Having ascertained the exact time of Rukmini's marriage under the auspicious star, Krishna, the killer of Madhu, ordered His chariot driver, Daruka, and said, “O Daruka, prepare the chariot immediately.”

स चाश्वैः शैब्यसुग्रीवमेघपुष्पबलाहकैः ।
युक्तं रथमुपानीय तस्थौ प्राञ्जलिरग्रतः ॥10.53.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, च and, अश्वैः with horses, शैव्य-सुग्रीव-मेघपुष्प-बलाहकैः named Shaibya, Sugriva, Meghapushpa, and Balahaka, युक्तम् yoked, रथम् chariot, उपानीय bringing forward, तस्थौ stood, प्राञ्जलिः with folded hands, अग्रतः in front

Anvaya: सः च शैब्यसुग्रीवमेघपुष्पबलाहकैः अश्वैः युक्तं रथम् उपानीय प्राञ्जलिः अग्रतः तस्थौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः च And He (Daruka), शैब्यसुग्रीवमेघपुष्पबलाहकैः अश्वैः with horses named Shaibya, Sugriva, Meghapushpa, and Balahaka, युक्तं रथम् yoked chariot, उपानीय bringing forward, प्राञ्जलिः with folded hands, अग्रतः तस्थौ stood in front.

Simple Meaning: And Daruka, having brought forward the chariot yoked with the four famous horses named Shaibya, Sugriva, Meghapushpa, and Balahaka, stood before the Lord with folded hands.

आरुह्य स्यन्दनं शौरिर्द्विजमारोप्य तूर्णगैः ।
आनर्त्तादेकरात्रेण विदर्भानगमत् हयैः ॥10.53.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: आरुह्य mounting, स्यन्दनम् the chariot, शौरिः Shouri - grandson of Shura (Krishna), द्विजम् the brahmana, आरोप्य causing to ascend, तूर्ण-गैः swiftly moving, आनर्त्तात् from Anarta, एक-रात्रेण in a single night, विदर्भान् to Vidarbha, अगमत् reached, हयैः by the horses

Anvaya: शौरिः स्यन्दनम् आरुह्य द्विजम् आरोप्य तूर्णगैः हयैः एकरात्रेण आनर्त्तात् विदर्भान् अगमत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शौरिः Krishna, स्यन्दनम् आरुह्य mounting the chariot, द्विजम् आरोप्य helping the brahmana to ascend, तूर्णगैः हयैः with swiftly moving horses, एकरात्रेण in a single night, आनर्त्तात् विदर्भान् from Anarta to Vidarbha, अगमत् reached.

Simple Meaning: Mounting His chariot and helping the brahmana ascend as well, Krishna traveled from the kingdom of Anarta to Vidarbha in a single night, carried by His swiftly moving horses.

राजा स कुण्डिनपतिः पुत्रस्नेहवशं गतः ।
शिशुपालाय स्वां कन्यां दास्यन् कर्माण्यकारयत् ॥10.53.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजा the king, सः he, कुण्डिन-पतिः the lord of Kundina, पुत्र-स्नेह-वशम् under the control of affection for his son, गतः gone, शिशुपालाय unto Shishupala, स्वाम् his own, कन्याम् daughter, दास्यन् intending to give, कर्माणि ceremonies, अकारयत् caused to be performed

Anvaya: सः कुण्डिनपतिः राजा पुत्रस्नेहवशं गतः शिशुपालाय स्वां कन्यां दास्यन् कर्माणि अकारयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः कुण्डिनपतिः राजा He, the lord of Kundina, the king, पुत्रस्नेहवशं गतः going under the control of affection for his son, शिशुपालाय unto Shishupala, स्वां कन्यां his own daughter, दास्यन् intending to give, कर्माणि auspicious ceremonies, अकारयत् caused to be performed.

Simple Meaning: King Bhishmaka, the lord of Kundina, fell under the control of affection for his son Rukmi and arranged to give his daughter in marriage to Shishupala. He therefore ordered the customary wedding ceremonies to be performed.

पुरं सम्मृष्टसंसिक्तमार्गरथ्याचतुष्पथम् ।
चित्रध्वजपताकाभिस्तोरणैः समलङ्कृतम् ॥10.53.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुरम् the city, सम्मृष्ट-संसिक्त-मार्ग-रथ्या-चतुष्पथम् with swept and sprinkled main roads, streets, and intersections, चित्र-ध्वज-पताकाभिः with multicolored banners and flags, तोरणैः with triumphal arches, समलङ्कृतम् beautifully decorated

Anvaya: सम्मृष्टसंसिक्तमार्गरथ्याचतुष्पथं चित्रध्वजपताकाभिः तोरणैः समलङ्कृतं पुरम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सम्मृष्टसंसिक्तमार्गरथ्याचतुष्पथं Swept and sprinkled main roads, streets, and intersections, चित्रध्वजपताकाभिः with multicolored banners and flags, तोरणैः with triumphal arches, समलङ्कृतं beautifully decorated, पुरम् the city...

Simple Meaning: The entire city was beautifully decorated for the occasion. All the main roads, side streets, and intersections were thoroughly swept and sprinkled with water, and the city was adorned with multicolored banners, flags, and grand triumphal arches.

स्रग्गन्धमाल्याभरणैर्विरजोऽम्बरभूषितैः ।
जुष्टं स्त्रीपुरुषैः श्रीमद्गृहैरगुरुधूपितैः ॥10.53.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्रक्-गन्ध-माल्य-आभरणैः with flower garlands, aromatic pastes, flower wreaths, and ornaments, विरजः-अम्बर-भूषितैः dressed in spotless garments, जुष्टम् filled, स्त्री-पुरुषैः with men and women, श्रीमत्-गृहैः with opulent houses, अगुरु-धूपितैः scented with aguru incense

Anvaya: विरजोऽम्बरभूषितैः स्रग्गन्धमाल्याभरणैः स्त्रीपुरुषैः जुष्टम् अगुरुधूपितैः श्रीमद्गृहैः (च तत् पुरम् आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विरजोऽम्बरभूषितैः Dressed in spotless garments, स्रग्गन्धमाल्याभरणैः with flower garlands, aromatic pastes, flower wreaths, and ornaments, स्त्रीपुरुषैः with men and women, जुष्टम् filled, अगुरुधूपितैः scented with aguru incense, श्रीमद्गृहैः with opulent houses, च and, (तत् पुरम्) that city, (आसीत्) was.

Simple Meaning: The city was filled with men and women who were beautifully arrayed in spotless garments, flower garlands, aromatic pastes, and fine ornaments. The opulent houses throughout the city were also heavily scented with the fragrance of burning aguru incense.

पितृन् देवान् समभ्यर्च्य विप्रांश्च विधिवन्नृप ।
भोजयित्वा यथान्यायं वाचयामास मङ्गलम् ॥10.53.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: पितॄन् to the ancestors, देवान् to the demigods, समभ्यर्च्य worshiping properly, विप्रान् to the brahmanas, च and, विधिवत् according to regulations, नृप O King, भोजयित्वा having fed, यथा-न्यायम् in accordance with proper etiquette, वाचयामास caused to be chanted, मङ्गलम् auspicious mantras

Anvaya: नृप! पितॄन् देवान् विप्रान् च विधिवत् समभ्यर्च्य यथान्यायं भोजयित्वा मङ्गलं वाचयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King, पितॄन् to the ancestors, देवान् to the demigods, विप्रान् to the brahmanas, च and, विधिवत् according to regulations, समभ्यर्च्य worshiping properly, यथान्यायं in accordance with proper etiquette, भोजयित्वा having fed, मङ्गलं auspicious mantras, वाचयामास caused to be chanted.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, after properly worshiping the ancestors, gods, and brahmanas according to scriptural regulations, King Bhishmaka fed them sumptuously in accordance with proper etiquette and had them chant the traditional auspicious mantras.

सुस्नातां सुदतीं कन्यां कृतकौतुकमङ्गलाम् ।
अहतांशुकयुग्मेन भूषितां भूषणोत्तमैः॥10.53.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: सु-स्नाताम् well-bathed, सु-दतीम् possessing beautiful teeth, कन्याम् the maiden, कृत-कौतुक-मङ्गलाम् for whom the auspicious marriage-thread ceremony had been performed, अहत-अंशुक-युग्मेन with a pair of unwashed new garments, भूषिताम् adorned, भूषण-उत्तमैः with the finest ornaments

Anvaya: सुस्नातां सुदतीं कृतकौतुकमङ्गलाम् अहतांशुकयुग्मेन भूषणोत्तमैः भूषितां कन्याम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सुस्नातां Well-bathed, सुदतीं possessing beautiful teeth, कृतकौतुकमङ्गलाम् for whom the auspicious marriage-thread ceremony had been performed, अहतांशुकयुग्मेन with a pair of unwashed new garments, भूषणोत्तमैः with the finest ornaments, भूषितां adorned, कन्याम् the maiden…

Simple Meaning: The young princess, who possessed beautiful teeth, was carefully bathed and adorned for the wedding. The priests performed the auspicious ceremony of tying the marriage thread on her wrist, and she was dressed in a pair of brand-new garments and decorated with the finest jewelry.

चक्रुः सामर्ग्यजुर्मन्त्रैर्वध्वा रक्षां द्विजोत्तमाः ।
पुरोहितोऽथर्वविद् वै जुहाव ग्रहशान्तये ॥10.53.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: चक्रुः performed, साम-ऋक्-यजुः-मन्त्रैः with the mantras of the Sama, Rig, and Yajur Vedas, वध्वाः of the bride, रक्षाम् protection, द्विज-उत्तमाः the best of the brahmanas, पुरोहितः the family priest, अथर्व-वित् expert in the Atharva Veda, वै indeed, जुहाव offered oblations, ग्रह-शान्तये for pacifying the planets

Anvaya: द्विजोत्तमाः सामर्ग्यजुर्मन्त्रैः वध्वाः रक्षां चक्रुः, अथर्ववित् पुरोहितः वै ग्रहशान्तये जुहाव।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: द्विजोत्तमाः The best of the brahmanas, सामर्ग्यजुर्मन्त्रैः with the mantras of the Sama, Rig, and Yajur Vedas, वध्वाः of the bride, रक्षां protection, चक्रुः performed. अथर्ववित् Expert in the Atharva Veda, पुरोहितः the family priest, वै indeed, ग्रहशान्तये for pacifying the planets, जुहाव offered oblations.

Simple Meaning: The best of the brahmanas chanted protective mantras from the Rig, Sama, and Yajur Vedas to invoke protection for the bride, while the family priest, who was an expert in the Atharva Veda, offered oblations in the sacred fire to pacify the planetary influences.

हिरण्यरूप्यवासांसि तिलांश्च गुडमिश्रितान् ।
प्रादाद् धेनूश्च विप्रेभ्यो राजा विधिविदां वरः॥10.53.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: हिरण्य-रूप्य-वासांसि gold, silver, and clothing, तिलान् sesame seeds, च and, गुड-मिश्रितान् mixed with jaggery, प्रादात् gave, धेनूः cows, च and, विप्रेभ्यः to the brahmanas, राजा the king, विधि-विदाम् वरः the best among those who know Vedic rituals

Anvaya: विधिविदां वरः राजा विप्रेभ्यः हिरण्यरूप्यवासांसि गुडमिश्रितान् तिलां च धेनूः च प्रादात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विधिविदां वरः The best among those who know rituals, राजा the king, विप्रेभ्यः to the brahmanas, हिरण्यरूप्यवासांसि gold, silver, and clothing, गुडमिश्रितान् तिलां sesame seeds mixed with jaggery, च and, धेनूः cows, च and, प्रादात् gave.

Simple Meaning: King Bhishmaka, who was most expert in the knowledge of Vedic rituals, then gifted gold, silver, fine clothing, cows, and sesame seeds mixed with jaggery to the brahmanas.

एवं चेदिपती राजा दमघोषः सुताय वै ।
कारयामास मन्त्रज्ञैः सर्वमभ्युदयोचितम् ॥10.53.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this way, चेदि-पतिः the lord of Chedi, राजा the king, दमघोषः Damaghosha, सुताय for his son, वै indeed, कारयामास caused to be performed, मन्त्र-ज्ञैः by those expert in Vedic mantras, सर्वम् all, अभ्युदय-उचितम् suitable for prosperity (and the wedding)

Anvaya: एवं चेदिपतिः राजा दमघोषः वै सुताय मन्त्रज्ञैः सर्वम् अभ्युदयोचितं कारयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवं In this way, चेदिपतिः राजा दमघोषः the lord of Chedi, the king, Damaghosha, वै indeed, सुताय for his son, मन्त्रज्ञैः by those expert in Vedic mantras, सर्वम् all, अभ्युदयोचितं suitable for prosperity, कारयामास caused to be performed.

Simple Meaning: In the same manner, King Damaghosha, the lord of Chedi, employed brahmanas expert in chanting Vedic mantras to perform all the auspicious rituals necessary to ensure the prosperity and success of his son Shishupala's upcoming wedding.

Anvaya: (सः) मदच्युद्भिः गजानीकैः हेममालिभिः स्यन्दनैः पत्त्यश्वसङ्कुलैः सैन्यैः परीतः कुण्डिनं ययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सः) He (Damaghosha), मदच्युद्भिः dripping with rut-fluid, गजानीकैः by regiments of elephants, हेममालिभिः adorned with golden garlands, स्यन्दनैः by chariots, पत्त्यश्वसङ्कुलैः crowded with infantry and cavalry, सैन्यैः by armies, परीतः surrounded, कुण्डिनं to the city of Kundina, ययौ went.

Simple Meaning: Surrounded by a massive army crowded with infantry, cavalry, chariots adorned with golden garlands, and majestic elephants dripping with rutting-juice, King Damaghosha traveled with his son to the city of Kundina.

Simple Meaning: King Bhishmaka, the lord of Vidarbha, came forward to meet the arriving groom's party. After honoring King Damaghosha and his entourage appropriately, he joyfully escorted them to a separate, lavishly prepared palace residence.

तत्र शाल्वो जरासन्धो दन्तवक्त्रो विदूरथः ।
आजग्मुश्चैद्यपक्षीयाः पौण्ड्रकाद्याः सहस्रशः ॥10.53.17॥

Anvaya: तत्र चैद्यपक्षीयाः शाल्वः जरासन्धः दन्तवक्त्रः विदूरथः पौण्ड्रकाद्याः (च) सहस्रशः आजग्मुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत्र There, चैद्यपक्षीयाः those belonging to the faction of the King of Chedi, शाल्वः Shalva, जरासन्धः Jarasandha, दन्तवक्त्रः Dantavakra, विदूरथः Viduratha, पौण्ड्रकाद्याः Paundraka and others, सहस्रशः by the thousands, आजग्मुः arrived.

Simple Meaning: To that wedding, thousands of kings who allied themselves with Shishupala's faction arrived, including powerful rulers like Shalva, Jarasandha, Dantavakra, Viduratha, and Paundraka.

कृष्णरामद्विषो यत्ताः कन्यां चैद्याय साधितुम् ।
यद्यागत्य हरेत् कृष्णो रामाद्यैर्यदुभिर्वृतः ॥10.53.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृष्ण-राम-द्विषः the enemies of Krishna and Balarama, यत्ताः alert (fully prepared/united), कन्याम् the maiden, चैद्याय for Shishupala (the prince of Chedi), साधितुम् to secure, यदि if, आगत्य having come, हरेत् should carry away, कृष्णः Krishna, राम-आद्यैः by Balarama and others, यदुभिः by the Yadus, वृतः accompanied

Anvaya: कृष्णरामद्विषः चैद्याय कन्यां साधितुं यत्ताः (आसन्), यदि रामाद्यैः यदुभिः वृतः कृष्णः आगत्य (कन्यां) हरेत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्णरामद्विषः The enemies of Krishna and Balarama, चैद्याय कन्यां साधितुं to secure the maiden for Shishupala, यत्ताः (आसन्) were alert and prepared, यदि if, रामाद्यैः यदुभिः वृतः कृष्णः Krishna accompanied by Balarama and other Yadus, आगत्य having come, हरेत् should carry away (Rukmini).

Simple Meaning: The kings who were inimical to Krishna and Balarama joined forces and remained highly alert. They had resolved that if Krishna arrived accompanied by Balarama and the other Yadus to carry off the bride, they would fight together to secure the maiden for Shishupala.

योत्स्यामः संहतास्तेन इति निश्चितमानसाः ।
आजग्मुर्भूभुजः सर्वे समग्रबलवाहनाः ॥10.53.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: योत्स्यामः we shall fight, संहताः united, तेन with Him (Krishna), इति thus, निश्चित-मानसाः with determined minds, आजग्मुः arrived, भू-भुजः the kings (earth-protectors), सर्वे all, समग्र-बल-वाहनाः with their entire armies and vehicles

Anvaya: "संहताः (वयं) तेन योत्स्यामः" इति निश्चितमानसाः सर्वे भूभुजः समग्रबलवाहनाः (सन्तः) आजग्मुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: संहताः United, (वयं) we, तेन with Him, योत्स्यामः shall fight, इति thus, निश्चितमानसाः with determined minds, सर्वे भूभुजः all the kings, समग्रबलवाहनाः possessing their entire armies and vehicles, आजग्मुः arrived.

Simple Meaning: With minds firmly resolved that they would join forces and fight Krishna if necessary, all these adversarial kings arrived at the wedding accompanied by their entire armies, weapons, and military vehicles.

श्रुत्वैतद् भगवान् रामो विपक्षीयनृपोद्यमम् ।
कृष्णं चैकं गतं हर्तुं कन्यां कलहशङ्कितः ॥10.53.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रुत्वा having heard, एतत् this, भगवान् Lord, रामः Lord Balarama, विपक्षीय-नृप-उद्यमम् the frantic military preparations of the rival kings, कृष्णम् Krishna, च and, एकम् alone, गतम् gone, हर्तुम् to carry away, कन्याम् the maiden, कलह-शङ्कितः fearing a major conflict

Anvaya: भगवान् रामः एतत् विपक्षीयनृपोद्यमं, च कन्यां हर्तुं एकं गतं कृष्णं श्रुत्वा कलहशङ्कितः (सन्)...

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवान् रामः Lord Balarama, एतत् विपक्षीयनृपोद्यमं this military mobilization of the rival kings, च and, कन्यां the maiden, हर्तुं to carry away, एकं alone, गतं gone, कृष्णं Krishna, श्रुत्वा having heard, कलहशङ्कितः anxious about a fierce clash, (सन्) being…

Simple Meaning: When Lord Balarama heard about the massive military mobilization of the rival kings, and that Krishna had set off alone to carry away the princess, He became highly anxious about the impending conflict.

बलेन महता सार्धं भ्रातृस्नेहपरिप्लुतः ।
त्वरितः कुण्डिनं प्रागाद् गजाश्वरथपत्तिभिः ॥10.53.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: बलेन with an army, महता massive, सार्धम् together, भ्रातृ-स्नेह-परिप्लुतः overwhelmed by affection for His brother, त्वरितः swiftly, कुण्डिनम् to the city of Kundina, प्रागात् set out/arrived, गज-अश्व-रथ-पत्तिभिः consisting of elephants, cavalry, chariots, and infantry

Anvaya: भ्रातृस्नेहपरिप्लुतः (भगवान् रामः) गजाश्वरथपत्तिभिः महता बलेन सार्धं त्वरितः कुण्डिनं प्रागात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भ्रातृस्नेहपरिप्लुतः Overwhelmed by affection for His brother, (Lord Balarama), गजाश्वरथपत्तिभिः महता बलेन सार्धं together with a massive army, consisting of elephants, horses, chariots, and infantry, त्वरितः swiftly, कुण्डिनं to Kundina, प्रागात् went.

Simple Meaning: Deeply overwhelmed by affection for His younger brother and anxious for His safety, Lord Balarama quickly gathered a massive army—consisting of infantry, cavalry, chariots, and war elephants—and set out at once for the city of Kundina.

भीष्मकन्या वारारोहा काङ्क्षन्त्यागमनं हरेः ।
प्रत्यापत्तिमपश्यन्ती द्विजस्याचिन्तयत्तदा ॥10.53.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: भीष्म-कन्या the daughter of Bhishmaka (Rukmini), वर-आरोहा a woman of beautiful hips (or excellent form), काङ्क्षन्ती longing for, आगमनम् the arrival, हरेः of Hari (Krishna), प्रत्यापत्तिम् the return, अपश्यन्ती not seeing, द्विजस्य of the brahmana messenger, अचिन्तयत् thought, तदा then

Anvaya: हरेः आगमनं काङ्क्षन्ती वरारोहा भीष्मकन्या द्विजस्य प्रत्यापत्तिम् अपश्यन्ती तदा अचिन्तयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: हरेः आगमनं काङ्क्षन्ती Longing for the arrival of Hari, वरारोहा भीष्मकन्या the beautiful daughter of Bhishmaka, द्विजस्य प्रत्यापत्तिम् अपश्यन्ती not seeing the return of the brahmana, तदा अचिन्तयत् then, anxiously thought.

Simple Meaning: The beautiful princess Rukmini, daughter of King Bhishmaka, was intensely longing for Lord Krishna's arrival. When she saw that the brahmana messenger she had sent had still not returned, she began to think anxiously.

अहो त्रियामान्तरित उद्वाहो मेऽल्पराधसः ।
नागच्छत्यरविन्दाक्षो नाहं वेद्म्यत्र कारणम् ।
सोऽपि नावर्ततेऽद्यापि मत्सन्देशहरो द्विजः ॥10.53.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: अहो alas!, त्रि-यामा-अन्तरितः separated by only three yamas (nine hours/one night), उद्वाहः the marriage, मे of me, अल्प-राधसः who am unfortunate (having little merit), न आगच्छति does not come, अरविन्द-अक्षः the lotus-eyed Lord, न अहम् वेद्मि I do not know, अत्र here, कारणम् the reason, सः अपि even he, न आवर्तते does not return, अद्य अपि even today, मत्-सन्देश-हरः the bearer of my message, द्विजः the brahmana

Anvaya: अहो! अल्पराधसः मे उद्वाहः त्रियामान्तरितः (अस्ति)। अरविन्दाक्षः न आगच्छति, अत्र कारणम् अहं न वेद्मि। मत्सन्देशहरः सः द्विजः अपि अद्य अपि न आवर्तते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अहो Alas!, अल्पराधसः मे Of me who is unfortunate, उद्वाहः the marriage, त्रियामान्तरितः is separated by only one night. अरविन्दाक्षः न आगच्छति The lotus-eyed Lord does not come. अत्र कारणम् अहं न वेद्मि Here, the reason, I do not know. मत्सन्देशहरः सः द्विजः अपि The bearer of my message, that brahmana also, अद्य अपि न आवर्तते even now does not return.

Simple Meaning: Rukmini thought to herself: Alas! Unfortunate as I am, my wedding is only one night away. Still, the lotus-eyed Lord has not arrived, and I do not know the reason for His delay. Even the brahmana carrying my secret message has not yet returned.

अपि मय्यनवद्यात्मा दृष्ट्वा किञ्चिज्जुगुप्सितम् ।
मत्पाणिग्रहणे नूनं नायाति हि कृतोद्यमः ॥10.53.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: अपि perhaps, मयि in me, अनवद्य-आत्मा He whose mind/character is faultless (Krishna), दृष्ट्वा having seen/noticed, किञ्चित् some, जुगुप्सितम् flawed trait (unpraiseworthy quality), मत्-पाणि-ग्रहणे for taking my hand in marriage, नूनम् surely, न आयाति does not come, हि indeed, कृत-उद्यमः though He had initially made an effort

Anvaya: अपि नूनम् अनवद्यात्मा मयि किञ्चित् जुगुप्सितं दृष्ट्वा (सः) कृतोद्यमः मत्पाणिग्रहणे न हि आयाति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अपि नूनम् Perhaps surely, अनवद्यात्मा He of faultless character, मयि in me, किञ्चित् जुगुप्सितं some flawed trait, दृष्ट्वा having seen, (सः) कृतोद्यमः He, having made an effort/preparation, मत्पाणिग्रहणे for taking my hand in marriage, न हि आयाति does not come indeed.

Simple Meaning: Perhaps the faultless Lord, upon reflection, has found some flaw in my character. Surely that is why, despite initially preparing to come, He does not arrive to claim my hand in marriage.

दुर्भगाया न मे धाता नानुकूलो महेश्वरः ।
देवी वा विमुखा गौरी रुद्राणी गिरिजा सती ॥10.53.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: दुर्भगायाः of an unfortunate woman, न not, मे my, धाता the creator (Brahma), न not, अनुकूलः favorable, महेश्वरः the great Lord Shiva, देवी the goddess, वा or, विमुखा turned away/displeased, गौरी Gauri, रुद्राणी the wife of Rudra, गिरिजा the daughter of the mountain, सती the chaste one

Anvaya: दुर्भगायाः मे धाता अनुकूलः न (अस्ति), महेश्वरः (अपि) न (अनुकूलः), वा सती गिरिजा रुद्राणी देवी गौरी विमुखा (अस्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दुर्भगायाः Of an unfortunate woman, मे of me, धाता the creator, अनुकूलः favorable, न (अस्ति) is not, महेश्वरः (अपि) न Lord Shiva also, is not (favorable). वा Or, सती गिरिजा The chaste daughter of the mountain, रुद्राणी wife of Rudra, देवी गौरी goddess Gauri, विमुखा is turned away.

Simple Meaning: I think that Creator Brahma is not favorable to an unfortunate girl like me, nor is the great Lord Shiva. Perhaps the chaste Goddess Gauri—wife of Rudra, the daughter of Himalaya—is also displeased and has turned her face away from me.

एवं चिन्तयती बाला गोविन्दहृतमानसा ।
न्यमीलयत कालज्ञा नेत्रे चाश्रुकलाकुले ॥10.53.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this way, चिन्तयती deeply thinking, बाला the young maiden (Rukmini), गोविन्द-हृत-मानसा whose mind had been stolen by Govinda (Krishna), न्यमीलयत closed, काल-ज्ञा aware of the passing time, नेत्रे her two eyes, च and, अश्रु-कला-आकुले filled with tears

Anvaya: एवं चिन्तयती गोविन्दहृतमानसा कालज्ञा बाला अश्रुकलाकुले नेत्रे च न्यमीलयत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवं In this way, चिन्तयती thinking, गोविन्दहृतमानसा she whose heart was stolen by Govinda, कालज्ञा aware of the passing of time, बाला the young maiden, अश्रुकलाकुले नेत्रे her eyes filled with tears, च and, न्यमीलयत closed.

Simple Meaning: Absorbed in these anxious thoughts, the young princess Rukmini—whose heart was entirely stolen by Govinda—became painfully aware of how little time was left before her wedding. Overwhelmed, she closed her eyes, which were now brimming with tears.

एवं वध्वाः प्रतीक्षन्त्या गोविन्दागमनं नृप ।

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this way, वध्वाः of the bride (Rukmini), प्रतीक्षन्त्याः who was waiting for, गोविन्द-आगमनम् the arrival of Govinda, नृप O King (Parikshit), वाम left, ऊरुः thigh, भुजः arm, नेत्रम् eye, अस्फुरन् twitched/throbbed, प्रिय-भाषिणः indicating something pleasant (or foretelling auspicious news)

 यत+वाच् a. restraining one's speech, observing silence, reticent; see वाग्यत.

 यत+व्रत a. {1} observing vows. {2} keeping to one's engagements or promised observances.

Anvaya: (सा) यतवाक् मातृभिः सार्धं सखीभिः परिवारिता सन्नद्धैः उद्यतायुधैः शूरैः राजभटैः गुप्ता (च आसीत्)। मृदङ्गशङ्खपणवाः तूर्यभेर्यः च जघ्निरे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सा) She, यतवाक् maintaining silence, मातृभिः with her mothers, सार्धं together, सखीभिः by her female companions, परिवारिता surrounded, सन्नद्धैः wearing armor, उद्यतायुधैः with raised weapons, शूरैः heroic, राजभटैः by the royal soldiers, गुप्ता protected, (च) and, (आसीत्) was. मृदङ्गशङ्खपणवाः Mridangas conch shells and panavas, तूर्यभेर्यः turyas and bheris, च and, जघ्निरे were sounded.

Simple Meaning: Maintaining absolute silence, Rukmini was accompanied by her mothers and surrounded by her close female companions. She was heavily guarded by brave royal soldiers who wore protective armor and carried raised weapons, while a grand procession of drums, conchshells, and various musical instruments resounded all around.

नानोपहारबलिभिर्वारमुख्याः सहस्रशः ।
स्रग्गन्धवस्त्राभरणैः र्द्विजपत्न्यः स्वलङ्कृताः ॥10.53.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: नाना-उपहार-बलिभिः with various items of presentation and offerings, वार-मुख्याः prominent courtesans, सहस्रशः by the thousands, स्रग्-गन्ध-वस्त्र-आभरणैः with flower garlands fragrant substances garments and ornaments, द्विज-पत्न्यः the wives of the brahmanas, सु-अलङ्कृताः beautifully adorned

Anvaya: नानापहारबलिभिः सहस्रशः वारमुख्याः स्रग्गन्धवस्त्राभरणैः स्वलङ्कृताः द्विजपत्न्यः (वधूं परिवार्य जग्मुः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नानापहारबलिभिः With various items of presentation and offerings, सहस्रशः वारमुख्याः thousands of prominent courtesans, स्रग्गन्धवस्त्राभरणैः with flower garlands fragrant substances garments and ornaments, स्वलङ्कृताः द्विजपत्न्यः the beautifully adorned wives of the brahmanas, (वधूं परिवार्य जग्मुः) surrounding the bride, went.

Simple Meaning: Accompanying her in the procession were thousands of prominent courtesans carrying various presentations and offerings, along with the wives of the brahmanas who were beautifully adorned with flower garlands, fragrant perfumes, fine garments, and jewelry.

गायन्त्यश्च स्तुवन्तश्च गायका वाद्यवादकाः ।
परिवार्य वधूं जग्मुः सूतमागधवन्दिनः॥10.53.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: गायन्त्यः singing, च and, स्तुवन्तः offering praises, च and, गायकाः singers, वाद्य-वादकाः musicians playing instruments, परिवार्य surrounding, वधूम् the bride, जग्मुः proceeded, सूत-मागध-वन्दिनः the chroniclers bards and panegyrists

Anvaya: गायन्त्यः स्तुवन्तः च गायकाः वाद्यवादकाः सूतमागधवन्दिनः च वधूं परिवार्य जग्मुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गायन्त्यः Singing, स्तुवन्तः च and offering praises, गायकाः singers, वाद्यवादकाः musicians playing instruments, सूतमागधवन्दिनः the chroniclers bards and panegyrists, च and, वधूं the bride, परिवार्य surrounding, जग्मुः proceeded.

Simple Meaning: Singing and offering dynamic praises, vocalists, instrumental musicians, chroniclers, bards, and panegyrists all moved forward together, completely surrounding the young bride as the procession advanced.

आसाद्य देवीसदनं धौतपादकराम्बुजा ।
उपस्पृश्य शुचिः शान्ता प्रविवेशाम्बिकान्तिकम् ॥10.53.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: आसाद्य reaching, देवी-सदनम् the temple of the Goddess, धौत-पाद-कर-अम्बुजा she whose lotus feet and hands were washed, उपस्पृश्य sipping water for purification, शुचिः pure, शान्ता peaceful, प्रविवेश entered, अम्बिका-अन्तिकम् the presence of Goddess Ambika

Anvaya: देवीसदनम् आसाद्य धौतपादकराम्बुजा उपस्पृश्य शुचिः शान्ता (सा) अम्बिकान्तिकं प्रविवेश।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: देवीसदनं The temple of the Goddess, आसाद्य reaching, धौतपादकराम्बुजा she whose lotus feet and hands were washed, उपस्पृश्य sipping water for purification, शुचिः pure, शान्ता peaceful, (सा) she, अम्बिकान्तिकं to the presence of Goddess Ambika, प्रविवेश entered.

Simple Meaning: Upon reaching the temple of the Goddess, Rukmini washed her lotus hands and feet, sipped water for inner purification, and then, feeling completely pure and peaceful, entered the sacred presence of Goddess Ambika.

तां वै प्रवयसो बालां विधिज्ञा विप्रयोषितः।
भवानीं वन्दयाञ्चक्रुर्भवपत्नीं भवान्विताम् ॥10.53.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her (Rukmini), वै indeed, प्रवयसः elderly, बालाम् the young girl, विधि-ज्ञाः who knew the scriptural rituals, विप्र-योषितः the wives of the brahmanas, भवानीम् Goddess Bhavani, वन्दयाञ्चक्रुः made her bow down, भव-पत्नीम् the wife of Lord Bhava (Shiva), भव-अन्विताम् accompanied by her husband Shiva

Anvaya: विधिज्ञाः प्रवयसः विप्रयोषितः वै बालां तां भवान्वितां भवपत्नीं भवानीं वन्दयाञ्चक्रुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विधिज्ञाः Who knew the scriptural rituals, प्रवयसः विप्रयोषितः the elderly wives of the brahmanas, वै indeed, बालां young girl, तां her, भवान्वितां भवपत्नीं भवानीं accompanied by her husband Shiva, to the wife of Lord Bhava, Goddess Bhavani, वन्दयाञ्चक्रुः made her bow down.

Simple Meaning: The elderly wives of the brahmanas, who were knowledgeable about the rituals, guided the young princess Rukmini. They had her bow down in reverence before Goddess Bhavani, the consort of Lord Shiva, who is accompanied by her husband.

नमस्ये त्वाम्बिकेऽभीक्ष्णं स्वसन्तानयुतां शिवाम् ।
भूयात् पतिर्मे भगवान् कृष्णस्तदनुमोदताम् ॥10.53.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: नमस्ये I offer my obeisances, त्वाम् unto you, अम्बिके O Mother Ambika, अभीक्ष्णम् repeatedly, स्व-सन्तान-युताम् who is accompanied by your own children, शिवाम् the auspicious goddess, भूयात् may He become, पतिः the husband, मे my, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, कृष्णः Krishna, तत् that, अनुमोदताम् please grant your blessings to

Anvaya: अम्बिके! स्वसन्तानयुतां शिवां त्वाम् अभीक्ष्णं नमस्ये। भगवान् कृष्णः मे पतिः भूयात् – तत् अनुमोदताम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अम्बिके O Mother Ambika, स्वसन्तानयुतां Who are accompanied by your own children, शिवां the auspicious goddess, त्वाम् unto you, अभीक्ष्णं repeatedly, नमस्ये I offer my obeisances. भगवान् कृष्णः The Supreme Lord Krishna, मे पतिः my husband, भूयात् may He become, तत् that, अनुमोदताम् please grant your blessings.

Simple Meaning: O Mother Ambika, You who are accompanied by your children and are auspicious, I offer my respectful obeisances unto you repeatedly. Please grant your blessings and allow the Supreme Lord Krishna to become my husband.

अद्भिर्गन्धाक्षतैर्धूपैर्वासः स्रङ्गमाल्यभूषणैः ।
नानोपहारबलिभिः प्रदीपावलिभिः पृथक् ॥10.53.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: अद्भिः with water, गन्ध-अक्षतैः with fragrant substances and unbroken grains of rice, धूपैः with incense, वासः with garments, स्रक्-माल्य-भूषणैः with flower wreaths, garlands and ornaments, नाना-उपहार-बलिभिः with various kinds of gifts and offerings, प्रदीप-आवलिभिः with rows of lamps, पृथक् separately

Anvaya: (ताः विप्रयोषितः) अद्भिः गन्धक्षतैः धूपैः वासः स्रङ्गमाल्यभूषणैः नानाोपहारबलिभिः प्रदीपावलिभिः च पृथक् (देवीं समपूजयन)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (ताः विप्रयोषितः) Those wives of the brahmanas, अद्भिः with water, गन्धक्षतैः with fragrant substances and unbroken grains of rice, धूपैः with incense, वासः with garments, स्रङ्गमाल्यभूषणैः with flower wreaths garlands and ornaments, नानाोपहारबलिभिः with various kinds of gifts and offerings, प्रदीपावलिभिः with rows of lamps, च and, पृथक् separately, (देवीं समपूजयन) worshiped the Goddess.

Simple Meaning: The brahmana wives then worshiped the Goddess with great care, presenting each item separately. They made offerings of pure water, fragrant perfumes, unbroken grains of rice, aromatic incense, fine garments, flower wreaths, jewelry, various gifts, and rows of glowing lamps.

विप्रस्त्रियः पतिमतीस्तथा तैः समपूजयत् ।
लवणापूपताम्बूलकण्ठसूत्रफलेक्षुभिः ॥10.53.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: विप्र-स्त्रियः the brahmana women, पतिमतीः whose husbands are alive, तथा in the same way, तैः with those same items, समपूजयत् she worshiped, लवण-अपूप-ताम्बूल-कण्ठसूत्र-फल-इक्षुभिः with salt, cakes, betel nut, necklaces, fruits and sugar cane

Anvaya: तथा (सा) पतिमतीः विप्रस्त्रियः तैः (च) लवणापूपताम्बूलकण्ठसूत्रफलेक्षुभिः समपूजयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तथा In the same way, (सा) she, पतिमतीः whose husbands are alive, विप्रस्त्रियः the brahmana women, तैः with those same items, (च) and, लवणापूपताम्बूलकण्ठसूत्रफलेक्षुभिः with salt, cakes, betel nut, necklaces, fruits and sugar cane, समपूजयत् worshiped.

Simple Meaning: In the same systematic way, Rukmini honored the married brahmana women whose husbands were alive. She worshiped them with the same sacred items used for the deity, offering them special gifts of salt, savory cakes, betel nut, necklaces, fresh fruits, and sugar cane.

तस्यै स्त्रियस्ताः प्रददुः शेषां युयुजुराशिषः ।
ताभ्यो देव्यै नमश्चक्रे शेषां च जगृहे वधूः ॥10.53.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्यै unto her (Rukmini), स्त्रियः the ladies, ताः they, प्रददुः gave, शेषाम् the remnants of the offerings, युयुजुः offered, आशिषः blessings, ताभ्यः unto them, देव्यै unto the Goddess, नमः-चक्रे she bowed down, शेषाम् the prasada remnants, च and, जगृहे accepted, वधूः the bride

Anvaya: ताः स्त्रियः तस्यै शेषां प्रददुः आशिषः युयुजुः (च)। वधूः ताभ्यः देव्यै (च) नमश्चक्रे शेषां च जगृहे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ताः स्त्रियः Those ladies, तस्यै unto her, शेषां प्रददुः gave the remnants of the offerings, आशिषः (च) युयुजुः and, offered blessings. वधूः The bride, ताभ्यः unto them, देव्यै (च) and, unto the Goddess, नमश्चक्रे bowed down, शेषां च and, the prasada remnants, जगृहे accepted.

Simple Meaning: Those respectable ladies then handed the remnants of the sacred offerings to Rukmini and gave her their heartfelt blessings. The young bride bowed down in reverence to the ladies and to Goddess Ambika, and then respectfully accepted the prasada remnants.

मुनिव्रतमथ त्यक्त्वा निश्चक्रामाम्बिकागृहात् ।
प्रगृह्य पाणिना भृत्यां रत्नमुद्रोपशोभिना ॥10.53.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: मुनि-व्रतम् her vow of silence (like a sage), अथ then, त्यक्त्वा breaking, निश्चक्राम she came out, अम्बिका-गृहात् from the temple of Ambika, प्रगृह्य holding, पाणिना with her hand, भृत्याम् a female servant, रत्न-मुद्रा-उपशोभिना which was beautifully adorned with a gem-set ring

Anvaya: अथ (सा) मुनिव्रतं त्यक्त्वा रत्नमुद्रोपशोभिना पाणिना भृत्यां प्रगृह्य अम्बिकागृहात् निश्चक्राम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ Then, (सा) she, मुनिव्रतं her vow of silence, त्यक्त्वा breaking, रत्नमुद्रोपशोभिना which was beautifully adorned with a gem-set ring, पाणिना with her hand, भृत्यां a female servant, प्रगृह्य holding, अम्बिकागृहात् from the temple of Ambika, निश्चक्राम came out.

Simple Meaning: Then, breaking her sage-like vow of silence, Rukmini stepped out of the temple of Goddess Ambika. As she left, she held the hand of a female attendant with her own hand, which was beautifully adorned with a radiant, gem-set ring.

तां देवमायामिव वीरमोहिनीं सुमध्यमां कुण्डलमण्डिताननाम् ।
श्यामां नितम्बार्पितरत्नमेखलां व्यञ्जत्स्तनीं कुन्तलशङ्कितेक्षणाम् ॥10.53.51॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her (Rukmini), देव-मायाम् इव like the illusory potency of the Supreme Lord, वीर-मोहिनीम् who could bewilder even the greatest heroes, सु-मध्यमाम् possessing a beautiful slender waist, कुण्डल-मण्डित-आननाम् whose face was adorned with swinging earrings, श्यामाम् youthful and radiant (or of flawless complexion), नितम्ब-अर्पित-रत्न-मेखलाम् with a gemstone belt resting upon her shapely hips, व्यञ्जत्-स्तनीम् whose developing breasts were becoming apparent, कुन्तल-शङ्कित-ईक्षणाम् whose glances shyly peering seemed as if they were afraid of the the locks of her hair

Anvaya: देवमायाम् इव वीरमोहिनीं सुमध्यमां कुण्डलमण्डिताननां श्यामां नितम्बार्पितरत्नमेखलां व्यञ्जत्स्तनीं कुन्तलशङ्कितेक्षणां तां (राजानः ददृशुः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: देवमायाम् इव Like the internal/illusory potency of the Lord, वीरमोहिनीं bewildering to heroes, सुमध्यमां possessing a beautiful slender waist, कुण्डलमण्डिताननां whose face was adorned with earrings, श्यामां youthful and radiant, नितम्बार्पितरत्नमेखलां with a gemstone belt resting upon her hips, व्यञ्जत्स्तनीं whose breasts were becoming apparent, कुन्तलशङ्कितेक्षणां whose glances were peering as though they were afraid of her locks of hair, तां her, (राजानः ददृशुः) the assembled kings saw.

Simple Meaning: As she stepped outside, the assembled kings beheld her looking like the enchanting cosmic potency of the Lord, capable of mesmerizing the bravest of heroes. She possessed a beautifully slender waist, and her face was exquisitely framed by gleaming earrings. Radiant in her youth, she wore a girdle of precious gems resting upon her hips, and her shy, captivating glances peeked out from amidst the dark locks of hair falling across her face.

शुचिस्मितां बिम्बफलाधरद्युतिशोणायमानद्विजकुन्दकुड्मलाम् ।
पदा चलन्तीं कलहंसगामिनीं शिञ्जत्कलानूपुरधामशोभिना ।
विलोक्य वीरा मुमुहुः समागता यशस्विनस्तत्कृतहृच्छयार्दिताः ॥10.53.52॥

Word-by-word meaning: शुचि-स्मिताम् possessing a pure and brilliant smile, बिम्ब-फल-अधर-द्युति-शोणायमान-द्विज-कुन्द-कुड्मलाम् whose teeth, resembling jasmine buds, were reddened by the radiant glow of her lips that were red like bimba fruits, पदा on foot, चलन्तीम् walking, कल-हंस-गामिनीम् moving with the graceful gait of a royal swan, शिञ्जत्-कला-नूपुर-धाम-शोभिना beautifully resplendent with the sound and luster of her tinkling ankle bells, विलोक्य seeing, वीराः the heroes (assembled kings), मुमुहुः fainted (or became completely bewildered), समागताः who had gathered there, यशस्विनः famous, तत्-कृत-हृच्छय-अर्दिताः afflicted by the desire/love created by her

Anvaya: शुचिस्मितां बिम्बफलाधरद्युतिशोणायमानद्विजकुन्दकुड्मलां शिञ्जत्कलानूपुरधामशोभिना पदा चलन्तीं कलहंसगामिनीं तां विलोक्य समागताः यशस्विनः वीराः तत्कृतहृच्छयार्दिताः मुमुहुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शुचिस्मितां Possessing a pure smile, बिम्बफलाधरद्युतिशोणायमानद्विजकुन्दकुड्मलां whose teeth like jasmine buds were reddened by the glow of her bimba-fruit lips, शिञ्जत्कलानूपुरधामशोभिना पदा चलन्तीं walking on foot, beautifully resplendent with the tinkling sound and luster of ankle bells, कलहंसगामिनीं moving with the gait of a royal swan, तां her, विलोक्य seeing, समागताः who had gathered there, यशस्विनः वीराः famous heroes, तत्कृतहृच्छयार्दिताः afflicted by the desire created by her, मुमुहुः became bewildered.

Simple Meaning: Her smile was pure and radiant, and her teeth, looking like delicate jasmine buds, reflected the deep crimson glow of her bimba-fruit lips. As she walked on foot with the elegant, fluid grace of a royal swan, the sweet tinkling and brilliant luster of her ankle bells filled the air. Beholding her exquisite beauty, the famous, heroic kings gathered there were completely overwhelmed, falling into a state of deep bewilderment as love for her pierced their hearts.

यां वीक्ष्य ते नृपतयस्तदुदारहासव्रीडावलोकहृतचेतस उज्झितास्त्राः।
पेतुः क्षितौ गजरथाश्वगता विमूढा यात्राच्छलेन हरयेऽर्पयतीं स्वशोभाम् ॥10.53.53॥

Word-by-word meaning: याम् whom (Rukmini), वीक्ष्य seeing, ते those, नृपतयः kings, तत्-उदार-हास-व्रीडा-अवलोक-हृत-चेतसः whose minds were completely stolen by her broad smiles and shy glances, उज्झित-अस्त्राः dropping their weapons, पेतुः fell down, क्षितौ onto the ground, गज-रथ-अश्व-गताः though seated on elephants, chariots, and horses, विमूढाः completely bewildered, यात्रा-छलेन under the pretext of the procession, हरये unto Hari (Krishna), अर्पयतीम् offering, स्व-शोभाम् her own exquisite beauty

Anvaya: यात्राच्छलेन हरये स्वशोभाम् अर्पयतीं यां वीक्ष्य गजरथाश्वगताः ते नृपतयः तदुदारहासव्रीडावलोकहृतचेतसः उज्झितास्त्राः विमूढाः (सन्तः) क्षितौ पेतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यात्राच्छलेन Under the pretext of the procession, हरये unto Lord Hari, स्वशोभाम् her own exquisite beauty, अर्पयतीं offering, यां whom, वीक्ष्य seeing, गजरथाश्वगताः seated on elephants, chariots, and horses, ते those, नृपतयः kings, तदुदारहासव्रीडावलोकहृतचेतसः whose minds were stolen by her broad smiles and shy glances, उज्झितास्त्राः dropping their weapons, विमूढाः completely bewildered, क्षितौ on the ground, पेतुः fell.

Simple Meaning: Under the pretext of a temple procession, Rukmini was moving forward to offer her matchless grace and beauty exclusively to Lord Hari. Those kings mounted upon their elephants, chariots, and horses, were so utterly stunned by the sight of her that their weapons slipped from their hands. Their minds stolen by her generous smiles and shy, downward glances, they fell completely senseless to the ground.

सैवं शनैश्चलयती चलपद्मकोशौ प्राप्तिं तदा भगवतः प्रसमीक्षमाणा ।
उत्सार्य वामकरजैरलकानपाङ्गैः प्राप्तान् ह्रियैक्षत नृपान् ददृशेऽच्युतं सा ॥10.53.54॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Rukmini), एवम् in this way, शनैः slowly, चलयती moving, चल-पद्म-कोशौ her feet which resembled trembling lotus buds, प्राप्तिम् the arrival, तदा then, भगवतः of the Supreme Lord, प्रसमीक्षमाणा thoroughly looking for, उत्सार्य brushing aside, वाम-कर-जैः with the nails of her left hand, अलकान् the curls of hair, अपाङ्गैः with side-long glances, प्राप्तान् assembled there, ह्रिया out of shyness, ऐक्षत she looked at, नृपान् the kings, ददृशे she saw, अच्युतम् Lord Achyuta (Krishna), सा she

Anvaya: सा एवं चलपद्मकोशौ शनैः चलयती, तदा भगवतः प्राप्तिं प्रसमीक्षमाणा, वामकरजैः अलकान् उत्सार्य, प्राप्तान् नृपान् ह्रिया अपाङ्गैः ऐक्षत, (तदा) सा अच्युतं ददृशे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सा She, एवं in this way, चलपद्मकोशौ her feet resembling trembling lotus buds, शनैः चलयती moving slowly, तदा then, भगवतः प्राप्तिं the arrival of the Supreme Lord, प्रसमीक्षमाणा thoroughly looking for, वामकरजैः with the nails of her left hand, अलकान् उत्सार्य brushing aside the curls of hair, प्राप्तान् नृपान् the assembled kings, ह्रिया out of shyness, अपाङ्गैः ऐक्षत looked at with side-long glances, (तदा) then, सा अच्युतं ददृशे she saw Lord Achyuta.

Simple Meaning: Moving her feet—which looked like two delicate, trembling lotus buds—step by step, Rukmini scanned the crowd, eagerly searching for the arrival of the Supreme Lord. Using the nails of her left hand to gently brush aside the dark curls of hair falling over her eyes, she cast shy, side-long glances at the assembled kings, and right then, her eyes met Lord Achyuta.

तां राजकन्यां रथमारुरुक्षतीं जहार कृष्णो द्विषतां समीक्षताम् ।
रथं समारोप्य सुपर्णलक्षणं राजन्यचक्रं परिभूय माधवः ॥10.53.55॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her, राज-कन्याम् the royal princess (Rukmini), रथम् the chariot, आरुरुक्षतीम् who was just about to mount, जहार snatched away, कृष्णः Lord Krishna, द्विषताम् of the enemies, समीक्षताम् while they were watching, रथम् onto the chariot, समारोप्य lifting and placing, सुपर्ण-लक्षणम् which bore the emblem of Garuda, राजन्य-चक्रम् the circle of kings, परिभूय disregarding or overpowering, माधवः Lord Madhava (Krishna)

Anvaya: माधवः कृष्णः द्विषतां समीक्षतां राजन्यचक्रं परिभूय रथम् आरुरुक्षतीं तां राजकन्यां सुपर्णलक्षणं रथं समारोप्य जहार।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: माधवः कृष्णः Lord Madhava, Krishna, द्विषतां the enemies, समीक्षतां while they were watching, राजन्यचक्रं परिभूय disregarding (or overpowering) the circle of kings, रथम् आरुरुक्षतीं who was just about to mount her chariot, तां राजकन्यां that royal princess, सुपर्णलक्षणं रथं onto His chariot marked with the emblem of Garuda, समारोप्य lifting and placing, जहार snatched away.

Simple Meaning: Just as the royal princess Rukmini was about to step onto her own chariot to return, Lord Krishna (Madhava) boldly stepped in. Completely disregarding the assembled circle of rival kings, and right before the very eyes of His watching enemies, He lifted her onto His own chariot—which proudly flew the flag of Garuda—and swiftly carried her away.

ततो ययौ रामपुरोगमैः शनैः ।
श‍ृगालमध्यादिव भागहृद् हरिः ॥10.53.56॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, ययौ he proceeded, राम-पुरोगमैः with Balarama at the lead, शनैः slowly (or steadily), श‍ृगाल-मध्यात् from among jackals, इव like, भाग-हृत् one who takes his share (the lion), हरिः lion

Anvaya: ततः रामपुरोगमैः (हरिः) श‍ृगालमध्यात् भागहृत् हरिः इव शनैः ययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः Then, रामपुरोगमैः with Balarama leading the way, (हरिः) Hari (Krishna), श‍ृगालमध्यात् from among the jackals, भागहृत् one who claims his share, हरिः the lion, इव like, शनैः steadily/confidently, ययौ proceeded.

Simple Meaning: Then, with Lord Balarama leading the way, Hari proceeded confidently and steadily, just as a lion carries away its rightful share of prey from the midst of a pack of cowering jackals.

तं मानिनः स्वाभिभवं यशःक्षयं परे जरासन्धवशा न सेहिरे ।
अहो धिगस्मान् यश आत्तधन्वनां गोपैर्हृतं केसरिणां मृगैरिव ॥10.53.57॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् that (act), मानिनः the proud kings, स्व-अभिभवम् their own humiliation, यशः-क्षयम् the destruction of their fame, परे the opposing, जरासन्ध-वशाः those under the leadership of Jarasandha, न did not, सेहिरे tolerate, अहो alas, धिक् fie upon, अस्मान् us, यशः the fame (or honor), आत्त-धन्वनाम् of those wielding bows (mighty archers), गोपैः by the cowherds, हृतम् stolen, केसरिणाम् of lions, मृगैः by deer (or lesser animals), इव like

Anvaya: जरासन्धवशाः मानिनः परे (राजानः) तं स्वाभिभवं यशःक्षयं न सेहिरे। "अहो अस्मान् धिक्! आत्तधन्वनां (अस्माकम्) यशः, मृगैः केसरिणां (यशः) इव, गोपैः हृतम्।"

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जरासन्धवशाः मानिनः परे The followers of Jarasandha, the proud opposing (kings), तं स्वाभिभवं that humiliation of themselves, यशःक्षयं the destruction of their fame, न सेहिरे did not tolerate. अहो अस्मान् धिक् Alas, fie upon us! आत्तधन्वनां Of those wielding bows, यशः the fame, मृगैः by deer, केसरिणां of lions, इव like, गोपैः by cowherds, हृतम् has been stolen.

Simple Meaning: The proud rival kings, who were under the leadership of Jarasandha, could not tolerate this humiliating defeat and the ruin of their reputation. They exclaimed in frustration, "Alas! Fie upon us! Though we are mighty archers, our honor has been snatched away by mere cowherd boys, just as the majestic glory of lions might be stolen by timid deer!"

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “रुक्मिणीहरणं नाम” त्रिपञ्चाशत्तमः अध्यायः ॥53॥

Thus ends the fifty-third discourse named,”Krishna carries away Rukmini” in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
