Lord Rudra saved from Vrikasura

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

वृकासुरात् रुद्रमोक्षणं नाम अष्टाशीतितमः अध्यायः

(40 Shlokas)

राजा उवाच
देवासुरमनुष्येषु ये भजन्त्यशिवं शिवम् ।
प्रायस्ते धनिनो भोजा न तु लक्ष्म्याः पतिं हरिम् ॥10.88.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजा उवाच King Parikshit said, देव-असुर-मनुष्येषु among devas, asuras, and human beings, ये who, भजन्ति worship, अशिवम् who appears to be associated with inauspicious, शिवम् Lord Shiva, प्रायः mostly/generally, ते they, धनिनः wealthy/opulent, भोजाः prosperous/enjoying material power, न not, तु indeed, लक्ष्म्याः पतिम् the husband of the Goddess of Fortune, हरिम् Lord Hari

Anvaya: राजा उवाच— देवसुरमनुष्येषु ये अशिवं शिवं भजन्ति, ते प्रायः धनिनः भोजाः (भवन्ति), तु लक्ष्म्याः पतिं हरिं न।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजा उवाच King Parikshit said— देवसुरमनुष्येषु among all classes of beings—devas, asuras, and human beings, ये those who, अशिवं शिवं Lord Shiva, who embraces fierce and austere external forms, भजन्ति worship, ते प्रायः धनिनः भोजाः they are generally endowed with wealth, and enjoyment; तु whereas, लक्ष्म्याः पतिं हरिं the Supreme Lord who is the ultimate master and shelter of the Goddess of Fortune (Lakshmi), Lord Hari, न (भजन्ति) they seldom worship for material gains.

Simple Meaning: King Parikshit said: Among devas, asuras, and human beings, those who worship Lord Shiva—who appears in fierce or inauspicious external forms—usually enjoy wealth and sense gratification. Yet, those who worship Lord Hari, the husband of the Goddess of Fortune, often do not attain such conspicuous material opulence.

एतद् वेदितुमिच्छामः सन्देहोऽत्र महान् हि नः ।
विरुद्धशीलयोः प्रभ्वोर्विरुद्धा भजतां गतिः ॥10.88.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतत् this, वेदितुम् इच्छामः we wish to know, सन्देहः doubt, अत्र here, महान् great, हि indeed, नः our, विरुद्ध-शीलयोः of two opposite natures, प्रभ्वोः of the two lords, विरुद्धा contrary/contradictory, भजताम् of those who worship, गतिः destination/result

Anvaya: एतत् वेदितुम् इच्छामः, अत्र नः महान् सन्देहः हि— विरुद्धशीलयोः प्रभ्वोः भजतां गतिः विरुद्धा (वर्तते)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एतत् वेदितुम् इच्छामः We wish to know this— अत्र नः महान् सन्देहः हि since we harbor a great doubt here regarding— विरुद्धशीलयोः प्रभ्वोः the two lords of opposing natures (Lord Shiva and Lord Hari), भजतां गतिः विरुद्धा (वर्तते) that the spiritual destinations or material results obtained by their respective worshippers are also contradictory.

Simple Meaning: We wish to know about this, for we harbor a great doubt: Since these two lords possess completely opposite natures, the results attained by their respective worshippers are likewise contradictory.

श्री शुक उवाच
शिवः शक्तियुतः शश्वत् त्रिलिङ्गो गुणसंवृतः ।
वैकारिकस्तैजसश्च तामसश्चेत्यहं त्रिधा ॥10.88.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, शिवः Lord Shiva, शक्ति-युतः associated with his potent energy, शश्वत् always, त्रि-लिङ्गः manifesting in three forms, गुण-संवृतः covered by the qualities of material nature, वैकारिकः sattvika or originating from goodness, तैजसः rajasa or originating from passion, च and, तामसः tamasa or originating from ignorance, च and, इति thus, अहम् the ego, त्रिधा in three parts

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— शिवः शश्वत् शक्तियुतः, त्रिलिङ्गः, गुणसंवृतः (अस्ति)। अहम् इति वैकारिकः तैजसः च तामसः च त्रिधा (वर्तते)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said— शिवः शश्वत् शक्तियुतः Lord Shiva is always united with his creative energy, त्रिलिङ्गः appearing in three distinct manifestations, गुणसंवृतः covered by the three qualities of material nature. अहम् इति वैकारिकः तैजसः च तामसः च त्रिधा (वर्तते) The false ego thus transforms into three functional aspects: sattvika (goodness), rajasa (passion), and tamasa (ignorance).

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Lord Shiva is always connected with his material energy. He is enveloped by the three modes of nature and appears in three distinct aspects through the false ego—sattvika, rajasa, and tamasa.

ततो विकारा अभवन् षोडशामीषु कञ्चन ।
उपधावन् विभूतीनां सर्वासामश्नुते गतिम् ॥10.88.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः from that ego, विकाराः modifications/transforms, अभवन् came into being, षोडश sixteen, अमीषु among these, कञ्चन any one, उपधावन् worshipping/approaching, विभूतीनाम् of opulences, सर्वासाम् of all, अश्नुते obtains/attains, गतिम् result/destination

Anvaya: ततः षोडश विकाराः अभवन्। अमीषु कञ्चन उपधावन् (जनः) सर्वासां विभूतीनां गतिम् अश्नुते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः From that three-fold false ego, षोडश विकाराः अभवन् sixteen modifications were generated. अमीषु कञ्चन उपधावन् (जनः) A person who worships any of these modifications, सर्वासां विभूतीनां गतिम् अश्नुते attains the destination of possessing all types of material opulences.

Simple Meaning: From the false ego arose the sixteen material modifications. Anyone who worships any of these elements or their presiding deities attains the full enjoyment of all material opulences.

हरिर्हि निर्गुणः साक्षात् पुरुषः प्रकृतेः परः ।
स सर्वदृगुपद्रष्टा तं भजन् निर्गुणो भवेत् ॥10.88.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: हरिः Lord Hari, हि indeed, निर्गुणः untainted by the material qualities, साक्षात् directly, पुरुषः the Supreme Person, प्रकृतेः from material nature, परः transcendental, सः He, सर्व-दृक् all-seeing, उपद्रष्टा the witness, तम् Him, भजन् worshipping, निर्गुणः free from material modes, भवेत् becomes

Anvaya: हरिः हि साक्षात् प्रकृतेः परः निर्गुणः पुरुषः (अस्ति)। सः सर्वदृक् उपद्रष्टा (वर्तते), तं भजन् (जनः) निर्गुणः भवेत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: हरिः हि साक्षात् Lord Hari is indeed directly, प्रकृतेः परः beyond material nature, निर्गुणः completely untouched by the material modes, पुरुषः (अस्ति) the Supreme Person. सः सर्वदृक् उपद्रष्टा (वर्तते) He is the all-seeing witness, तं भजन् (जनः) निर्गुणः भवेत् and by worshipping Him, a person becomes liberated from the material qualities.

Simple Meaning: Lord Hari is directly the Supreme Soul, completely beyond material nature and untouched by its modes. He is the all-seeing witness of all things, and one who worships Him becomes free from the influence of the material qualities.

निवृत्तेष्वश्वमेधेषु राजा युष्मत्पितामहः ।
शृण्वन् भगवतो धर्मानपृच्छदिदमच्युतम् ॥10.88.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: निवृत्तेषु having been completed, अश्वमेधेषु the horse sacrifice, राजा King, युष्मत्-पितामहः your grandfather, शृण्वन् hearing, भगवतः from the great devotee, धर्मान् religious principles, अपृच्छत् asked, इदम् this, अच्युतम् regarding Lord Achyuta

Anvaya: अश्वमेधेषु निवृत्तेषु युष्मत्पितामहः राजा भगवतः धर्मान् शृण्वन् अच्युतम् इदम् अपृच्छत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अश्वमेधेषु निवृत्तेषु After the completion of the Ashvamedha Yagna, युष्मत्पितामहः राजा your grandfather King Yudhishthira, भगवतः from the Lord, धर्मान् शृण्वन् while listening about Dharma, अच्युतम् इदम् अपृच्छत् asked this same question to Lord Achyuta.

Simple Meaning: Upon completing his Ashwamedha Yagna, your grandfather King Yudhishthira asked this very same question to Lord Achyuta while listening to the discourses on Dharma.

स आह भगवांस्तस्मै प्रीतः शुश्रूषवे प्रभुः ।
नृणां निःश्रेयसार्थाय योऽवतीर्णो यदोः कुले ॥10.88.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः He (Krishna), आह spoke, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, तस्मै to him (Yudhishthira), प्रीतः pleased, शुश्रूषवे who was eager to hear, प्रभुः the master, नृणाम् of human beings, निःश्रेयस-अर्थाय for the ultimate benefit, यः who, अवतीर्णः descended, यदोः of Yadu, कुले in the dynasty

Anvaya: नृणां निःश्रेयसार्थाय यः यदोः कुले अवतीर्णः, सः भगवान् प्रभुः प्रीतः (सन्) शुश्रूषवे तस्मै आह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृणां निःश्रेयसार्थाय यः यदोः कुले अवतीर्णः He who descended into the dynasty of Yadu for the ultimate benefit of all human beings, सः भगवान् प्रभुः प्रीतः (सन्) शुश्रूषवे तस्मै आह that Supreme Lord and master Krishna, being pleased, spoke to King Yudhishthira who was so eager to hear.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord Krishna, who had descended into the Yadu dynasty for the ultimate welfare of all human beings, was greatly pleased with King Yudhishthira for his eagerness to hear, and spoke the following to him.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
यस्याहमनुगृह्णामि हरिष्ये तद्धनं शनैः ।
ततोऽधनं त्यजन्त्यस्य स्वजना दुःखदुःखितम् ॥10.88.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, यस्य whom, अहम् I, अनुगृह्णामि favor/bless, हरिष्ये I take away, तत्-धनम् his wealth, शनैः gradually, ततः then, अधनम् penniless, त्यजन्ति abandon, अस्य his, स्व-जनाः relatives and friends, दुःख-दुःखितम् suffering one misery after another

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच— यस्य अहम् अनुगृह्णामि, (तस्य) तत् धनं शनैः हरिष्ये। ततः अधनं दुःखदुःखितम् अस्य स्वजनाः त्यजन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said— यस्य अहम् अनुगृह्णामि onto whomever I bestow my true grace, (तस्य) तत् धनं शनैः हरिष्ये I gradually take away all his material wealth; ततः अधनं दुःखदुःखितम् अस्य स्वजनाः त्यजन्ति thereafter, when he becomes completely destitute and overwhelmed by continuous sorrows, his own relatives and friends abandon him.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord said: If I bestow My grace upon someone, I gradually take away his material wealth. When he becomes penniless and suffers one distress after another, his own family and friends abandon him.

स यदा वितथोद्योगो निर्विण्णः स्याद् धनेहया ।
मत्परैः कृतमैत्रस्य करिष्ये मदनुग्रहम् ॥10.88.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, यदा when, वितथ-उद्योगः whose endeavors have failed, निर्विण्णः disgusted/frustrated, स्यात् becomes, धन-इहया with the desire for wealth, मत्-परैः with My devotees, कृत-मैत्रस्य of one who has made friendship, करिष्ये I bestow, मत्-अनुग्रहम् My mercy/grace

Anvaya: सः यदा धनेहया वितथोद्योगः निर्विण्णः स्यात्, मत्परैः कृतमैत्रस्य (तस्य) मदनुग्रहं करिष्ये।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः यदा When he, धनेहया वितथोद्योगः with endeavors to accumulate wealth, निर्विण्णः स्यात् is frustrated, मत्परैः कृतमैत्रस्य (तस्य) to him who cultivates friendship with My devotees, मदनुग्रहं करिष्ये I bestow My mercy.

Simple Meaning: When his efforts to gain wealth prove futile and he becomes completely disgusted with material pursuits, he makes friends with My devotees. At that time, I bestow My true grace upon him.

तद् ब्रह्म परमं सूक्ष्मं चिन्मात्रं सदनन्तकम् ।
अतो मां सुदुराराध्यं हित्वान्यान् भजते जनः ॥10.88.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that, ब्रह्म Absolute Truth, परमम् supreme, सूक्ष्मम् subtle, चित्-मात्रम् pure consciousness, सत् pure existence, अनन्तकम् endless/unlimited, अतः therefore, माम् Me, सुदुराराध्यम् very difficult to worship, हित्वा abandoning, अन्यान् others, भजते worship, जनः people

Anvaya: तत् परमं सूक्ष्मं चिन्मात्रं सत् अनन्तकं ब्रह्म (वर्तते)। अतः सुदुराराध्यं मां हित्वा जनः अन्यान् भजते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत् परमं सूक्ष्मं चिन्मात्रं सत् अनन्तकं ब्रह्म (वर्तते) That Supreme Absolute Truth is subtle, pure consciousness, eternal existence, and unlimited. अतः सुदुराराध्यं मां हित्वा जनः अन्यान् भजते Therefore, because I am difficult to comprehend, people abandon Me and worship other deities instead.

Simple Meaning: My form as the Absolute Truth is supremely subtle, pure consciousness, eternal, and unlimited. Because I am difficult to comprehend, people, giving up on Me, worship other deities instead.

ततस्त आषुतोषेभ्यो लब्धराज्यश्रियोद्धताः ।
मत्ताः प्रमत्ता वरदान् विस्मरन्त्यवजानते ॥10.88.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, ते they, आशु-तोषेभ्यः from those deities who are quickly pleased, लब्ध-राज्य-श्रिया-उद्धताः arrogant upon receiving kingdom and opulence, मत्ताः intoxicated, प्रमत्ताः careless/deluded, वर-दान् those who granted the boons, विस्मरन्ति forget, अवजानते insult/disrespect

Anvaya: ततः ते आशुतोषेभ्यः लब्धराज्यश्रियोद्धताः मत्ताः प्रमत्ताः (सन्तः) वरदान् विस्मरन्ति अवजानते (च)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः ते Then they, आशुतोषेभ्यः लब्धराज्यश्रियोद्धताः from deities who are easily pleased, having obtained kingdom and material opulences, मत्ताः प्रमत्ताः (सन्तः) becoming intoxicated with pride and careless, वरदान् the granters of boons, विस्मरन्ति अवजानते (च) forget and disrespect them.

Simple Meaning: Upon receiving kingdoms and lavish opulences from deities who are quickly pleased, people become proud, intoxicated, and delusional. In their arrogance, they forget the very deities who granted them boons and even begin to disrespect them.

श्री शुक उवाच
शापप्रसादयोरीशा ब्रह्मविष्णुशिवादयः ।
सद्यः शापप्रसादोऽङ्ग शिवो ब्रह्मा न चाच्युतः ॥10.88.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, शाप-प्रसादयोः in cursing and bestowing grace, ईशाः capable lords, ब्रह्म-विष्णु-शिव-आदयः Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva and others, सद्यः immediately, शाप-प्रसादः whose curse and grace, अङ्ग my dear Parikshit, शिवः Lord Shiva, ब्रह्मा Lord Brahma, न not, च and, अच्युतः Lord Achyuta (Vishnu)

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— अङ्ग! ब्रह्मविष्णुशिवादयः शापप्रसादयोः ईशाः (सन्ति)। शिवः ब्रह्मा (च) सद्यः शापप्रसादौ (स्तः), अच्युतः तु न।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said— अङ्ग my dear King, ब्रह्मविष्णुशिवादयः शापप्रसादयोः ईशाः (सन्ति) Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva, and others are all capable of bestowing curses and blessings. शिवः ब्रह्मा (च) सद्यः शापप्रसादौ (स्तः) Shiva and Brahma grant their curses and blessings very quickly, अच्युतः तु न whereas Achyuta (Vishnu) does not act in such an immediate or impulsive manner.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: My dear King, Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva, and other devas are all capable of pronouncing curses and bestowing blessings. Shiva and Brahma are quick to bestow both curses and boons, but Achyuta (Vishnu) is not so.

अत्र चोदाहरन्तीममितिहासं पुरातनम् ।
वृकासुराय गिरिशो वरं दत्त्वाऽऽप सङ्कटम् ॥10.88.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: अत्र here, च and, उदाहरण्ति they cite, इमम् this, इतिहासम् historical account, पुरातनम् ancient, वृक-असुराय to Vrikasura, गिरिशः Girisha - Shiva, वरम् a boon, दत्त्वा having given, आप obtained/faced, सङ्कटम् great danger/crisis

Anvaya: च अत्र पुरातनम् इतिहासम् उदाहरन्ति— गिरिशः वृकासुराय वरं दत्त्वा सङ्कटम् आप।

हतः को नु महत्स्वीश जन्तुर्वै कृतकिल्बिषः ।
क्षेमी स्यात् किमु विश्वेशे कृतागस्को जगद्गुरौ ॥10.88.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: हतः destroyed/slain, कः who, नु indeed, महत्सु among the great/exalted souls, ईश O Lord, जन्तुः living entity/person, वै indeed, कृत-किल्बिषः who has committed an offense, क्षेमी safe/unharmed, स्यात् can be, किम् उत how much less, विश्व-ईशे toward the Lord of the universe, कृत-आगस्कः who has committed an offense, जगद्-गुरौ toward the spiritual master of all worlds

Anvaya: …हतः। ईश! महत्सु कृतकिल्बिषः जन्तुः कः नु क्षेमी स्यात्? किम् उत विश्वेशे जगद्गुरौ?

Literal meaning of the anvaya:  …हतः destroyed. ईश O Lord, महत्सु कृतकिल्बिषः जन्तुः कः what living entity who has committed a grave offense against great souls,  नु क्षेमी स्यात् can ever remain safe? विश्वेशे जगद्गुरौ कृतागस्कः (जन्तुः) किम् उत How much less can anyone remain safe who has committed an offense against the Supreme Lord of the universe and the spiritual preceptor of all worlds?

Simple Meaning: … destroyed. O Lord, what living being who commits an offense against great souls can ever remain safe from destruction? How much less, then, can anyone survive who dares to commit an offense against the Supreme Lord and universal preceptor of all worlds?”

य एवमव्याकृतशक्त्युदन्वतः परस्य साक्षात् परमात्मनो हरेः ।
गिरित्रमोक्षं कथयेच्छृणोति वा विमुच्यते संसृतिभिस्तथारिभिः ॥10.88.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः who, एवम् thus, अव्याकृत-शक्ति-उदन्वतः of the ocean of unmanifest material energy, परस्य of the Supreme, साक्षात् directly, परमात्मनः of the Supreme Soul, हरेः of Lord Hari, गिरित्र-मोक्षम् the liberation and rescue of Lord Shiva, कथयेत् narrates, शृणोति hears, वा or, विमुच्यते becomes completely liberated, संसृतिभिः from the repeated cycles of material birth and death (samsara), तथा and, अरिभिः from all enemies

गिरित्र गिरि—त्र/ mfn. protecting mountains (Rudra-Shiva),  src. Kosha, Ashadhyayi

Anvaya: यः अव्याकृतशक्त्युदन्वतः परस्य साक्षात् परमात्मनः हरेः गिरित्रमोक्षम् एवं कथयेत् शृणोति वा, (सः) संसृतिभिः तथा अरिभिः विमुच्यते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः Whoever, अव्याकृतशक्त्युदन्वतः परस्य of the Supreme Lord who is far beyond the great ocean of unmanifested material energy, साक्षात् परमात्मनः हरेः who is directly the Supreme Soul and Lord Hari, गिरित्रमोक्षम् this sacred narration of the rescue of Lord Shiva, एवं कथयेत् शृणोति वा narrates or hears with devotion, (सः) he, संसृतिभिः तथा अरिभिः विमुच्यते from the repeating cycle of material existence as well as from all the enemies, is liberated.

Simple Meaning: Whoever narrates or hears this glorious account of the rescue of Lord Shiva by the Supreme Lord Hari—who is beyond the ocean of unmanifested material energy—becomes liberated from the cycle of material birth and death, as well as from all enemies.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “वृकासुरात् रुद्रमोक्षणं” नाम अष्टाशीतितमः अध्यायः ॥88॥

Thus ends the eighty-eighth discourse named “Lord Rudra saved from Vrikasura”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
