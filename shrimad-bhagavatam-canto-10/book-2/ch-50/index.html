श्री शुक उवाच
अस्तिः प्राप्तिश्च कंसस्य महिष्यौ भरतर्षभ ।
मृते भर्तरि दुःखार्ते ईयतुः स्म पितुर्गृहान् ॥10.50.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अस्तिः Asti, प्राप्तिः Prapti, च and, कंसस्य of Kamsa, महिष्यौ the two queens, भरतर्षभ O best of the Bharatas (King Parikshit), मृते having died, भर्तरि their husband, दुःख-आर्ते distressed with grief, ईयतुः स्म went, पितुः of their father, गृहान् to the home

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच—भरतर्षभ! कंसस्य महिष्यौ अस्तिः प्राप्तिः च भर्तरि मृते दुःखार्ते (सत्यौ) पितुः गृहान् ईयतुः स्म।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, भरतर्षभ O best of the Bharatas, कंसस्य of Kamsa, महिष्यौ the two queens, अस्तिः प्राप्तिः च Asti and Prapti, भर्तरि मृते their husband having died, दुःखार्ते overcome with grief, पितुः of their father, गृहान् to the home, ईयतुः स्म went.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O best of the Bharatas, after Kamsa was slain, his two queens, Asti and Prapti, became utterly overwhelmed with grief. Deeply distressed by the death of their husband, they left Mathura and returned to the home of their father.

पित्रे मगधराजाय जरासन्धाय दुःखिते ।
वेदयाञ्चक्रतुः सर्वमात्मवैधव्यकारणम् ॥10.50.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: पित्रे to their father, मगध-राजाय the king of Magadha, जरासन्धाय to Jarasandha, दुःखिते the two sorrowful (wives of Kamsa), वेदयाञ्चक्रतुः reported/informed, सर्वम् all, आत्म-वैधव्य-कारणम् the cause of their own widowhood

Anvaya: दुःखिते (ते) पित्रे मगधराजाय जरासन्धाय आत्मवैधव्यकारणं सर्वं  वेदयाञ्चक्रतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दुःखिते The two sorrowful wives, पित्रे to their father, मगधराजाय to the king of Magadha, जरासन्धाय to Jarasandha, आत्मवैधव्यकारणं the cause of their own widowhood, सर्वं everything, वेदयाञ्चक्रतुः reported.

Simple Meaning: Overcome with sorrow, the two widowed queens related everything to their father, Jarasandha, the powerful king of Magadha, explaining in detail how Kamsa was killed and the circumstances that led to their tragic widowhood.

स तदप्रियमाकर्ण्य शोकामर्षयुतो नृप ।
अयादवीं महीं कर्तुं चक्रे परममुद्यमम् ॥10.50.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Jarasandha), तत् that, अप्रियम् unpleasant news, आकर्ण्य hearing, शोक-अमर्ष-युतः filled with grief and anger, नृप O King (Parikshit), अ-यादवीम् devoid of the Yadus, महीम् the earth, कर्तुम् to make, चक्रे made, परमम् supreme, उद्यमम् effort/determination

Anvaya: नृप! सः तत् अप्रियम् आकर्ण्य शोकामर्षयुतः (सन्) महीम् अयादवीं कर्तुं परमम् उद्यमं चक्रे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King, सः he, तत् अप्रियम् that unpleasant news, आकर्ण्य hearing, शोकामर्षयुतः filled with grief and rage, महीम् the earth, अयादवीं devoid of Yadus, कर्तुं to make, परमम् उद्यमं supreme effort/resolve, चक्रे made.

Simple Meaning: O King, upon hearing this highly unwelcome and agonizing news, Jarasandha was consumed by intense grief and raging fury. He instantly vowed to rid the entire earth of the Yadu dynasty and began making massive, supreme preparations for war.

अक्षौहिणीभिर्विंशत्या तिसृभिश्चापि संवृतः ।
यदुराजधानीं मथुरां न्यरुणत् सर्वतोदिशम् ॥10.50.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: अक्षौहिणीभिः with military divisions (akshauhinis), विंशत्या by twenty, तिसृभिः by three, च and, अपि also, संवृतः surrounded/accompanied, यदु-राजधानीम् the capital city of the Yadus, मथुराम् Mathura, न्यरुणत् besieged/blockaded, सर्वतः-दिशम् from all directions

Anvaya: (सः) विंशत्या तिसृभिः च अपि अक्षौहिणीभिः संवृतः (सन्) यदुराजधानीं मथुरां सर्वतोदिशं न्यरुणत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सः) He, विंशत्या तिसृभिः च अपि by twenty and three also, अक्षौहिणीभिः military divisions, संवृतः accompanied, यदुराजधानीं the Yadu capital, मथुरां Mathura, सर्वतोदिशं from all directions, न्यरुणत् besieged.

Simple Meaning: Accompanied by a colossal army of twenty-three akshauhinis (military divisions), Jarasandha marched upon Mathura, the capital of the Yadus, and completely besieged it from all directions.

Note: The strength of an Aksauhini has been specified as follows :

खबाणाग्निनवव्योमचन्द्रसंख्याः पदातयः ।

खेन्दुषट्शरषट्संख्या अश्वा नागा रथा अपि ॥

खाद्रिनागेन्दुदृक्संख्या इत्येषाक्षौहिणी स्मृता ॥

This verse is a mnemonic used to remember the composition of an Akshauhini army.

The words encode numbers through the bhuta-sankhya system, where objects represent digits.

Decoding

पदातयः (infantry):

Reading the digits in reverse order gives: 1,09,350 infantry

अश्वाः (horses):

Reversed: 65,610 horses

नागाः (elephants): They are equal in number to the chariots in an Akshauhini: 21,870 elephants

रथाः (chariots):

Reversed: 21,870 chariots

Therefore, one Akshauhini consists of:

Unit

Number

Chariots (रथाः)

21,870

Elephants (नागाः)

21,870

Horses (अश्वाः)

65,610

Infantry (पदातयः)

109,350

Total strength : 21,870 + 21,870 + 65,610 + 109,350 = 218,700 units

Hence: इत्येषा अक्षौहिणी स्मृता — "Such is remembered as an Akshauhini army."

Akshauhini consists of 1,09,350 infantry, 65,610 horses, 21,870 elephants and an equal number of chariots.

निरीक्ष्य तद्बलं कृष्ण उद्वेलमिव सागरम् ।
स्वपुरं तेन संरुद्धं स्वजनं च भयाकुलम् ॥10.50.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: निरीक्ष्य seeing/observing, तत्-बलम् that army, कृष्णः Krishna, उद्वेलम् overflowing its shores, इव just like, सागरम् an ocean, स्व-पुरम् His own city (Mathura), तेन by that army, संरुद्धम् besieged/blockaded, स्व-जनम् His own people/kinsmen, च and, भय-आकुलम् overwhelmed with fear

Anvaya: कृष्णः उद्वेलं सागरम् इव तद्बलं, तेन संरुद्धं स्वपुरं, भयाकुलं स्वजनं च निरीक्ष्य…

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्णः Krishna, उद्वेलं सागरम् इव like an ocean overflowing its shores, तद्बलं that army, तेन संरुद्धं besieged by it, स्वपुरं His own city, भयाकुलं overwhelmed with fear, स्वजनं च and His own people, निरीक्ष्य observing…

Simple Meaning: Seeing Jarasandha's colossal army resembling a raging ocean overflowing its shores, and observing His own capital city completely blockaded and His people overwhelmed with panic and fear, Lord Krishna began to contemplate His next course of action... (Note: This verse forms a continuous sentence with the subsequent verses).

चिन्तयामास भगवान् हरिः कारणमानुषः ।
तद्देशकालानुगुणं स्वावतारप्रयोजनम् ॥10.50.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: चिन्तयामास thought/reflected, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, हरिः Hari, कारण-मानुषः who had assumed a human form for a specific purpose, तत्-देश-काल-अनुगुणम् appropriate for that specific time and place, स्व-अवतार-प्रयोजनम् the purpose of His own descent/incarnation.

Anvaya: कारणमानुषः भगवान् हरिः तद्देशकालानुगुणं स्वावतारप्रयोजनं चिन्तयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कारणमानुषः Having assumed a human form for a specific mission, भगवान् हरिः the Supreme Lord Hari, तद्देशकालानुगुणं appropriate for that place and time, स्वावतारप्रयोजनं the purpose of His own incarnation, चिन्तयामास carefully considered.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord Hari, who had specifically assumed a human-like form for a divine mission, began to reflect deeply. He contemplated how to fulfill the ultimate purpose of His incarnation in a manner perfectly suited to the immediate crisis, time, and location.

हनिष्यामि बलं ह्येतद् भुवि भारं समाहितम् ।
मागधेन समानीतं वश्यानां सर्वभूभुजाम् ॥10.50.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: हनिष्यामि I shall destroy, बलम् the army, हि indeed, एतत् this, भुवि on the earth, भारम् a heavy burden, समाहितम् assembled, मागधेन by the King of Magadha (Jarasandha), समानीतम् brought together, वश्यानाम् who are under his control/subservient, सर्व-भूभुजाम् of all the kings

Anvaya: मागधेन समानीतं वश्यानां सर्वभूभुजाम् एतत् बलं हि भुवि समाहितं भारं हनिष्यामि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मागधेन By the King of Magadha, समानीतं brought together, वश्यानां of those under his control, सर्वभूभुजाम् of all the kings, एतत् बलं this army, हि indeed, भुवि on the earth, समाहितं भारं an assembled burden, हनिष्यामि I shall destroy.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna thought to Himself: I shall completely destroy this colossal army brought together by Jarasandha, which consists of the combined forces of all the vassal kings under his control. This massive gathering of unrighteous military power has become an immense burden upon the earth, and its elimination is necessary.

अक्षौहिणीभिः संख्यातं भटाश्वरथकुञ्जरैः ।
मागधस्तु न हन्तव्यो भूयः कर्ता बलोद्यमम् ॥10.50.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: अक्षौहिणीभिः in terms of akshauhinis, संख्यातम् numbered/counted, भट-अश्व-रथ-कुञ्जरैः consisting of infantry soldiers, horses, chariots, and elephants, मागधः the king of Magadha (Jarasandha), तु but, न not, हन्तव्यः should be killed, भूयः again, कर्ता will make, बल-उद्यमम् mobilization of forces/effort to assemble an army

Anvaya: भटाश्वरथकुञ्जरैः अक्षौहिणीभिः संख्यातम् (एतत् बलम् अहं हनिष्यामि), मागधः तु न हन्तव्यः, (यतः सः) भूयः बलोद्यमं कर्ता।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भटाश्वरथकुञ्जरैः Consisting of foot soldiers, horses, chariots, and elephants, अक्षौहिणीभिः संख्यातम् numbered in military divisions, (एतत् बलम् अहं हनिष्यामि) this army I shall destroy. मागधः तु But the king of Magadha, न हन्तव्यः should not be slain, (यतः) because, (सः) he, भूयः again, बलोद्यमं mobilization of forces, कर्ता will make.

Simple Meaning: I will destroy this entire army, which is measured in massive akshauhinis and filled with countless foot soldiers, horses, chariots, and elephants. However, Jarasandha himself must not be killed. He must be left alive so that he will gather and bring forth new armies of unrighteous kings again and again.

एतदर्थोऽवतारोऽयं भूभारहरणाय मे ।
संरक्षणाय साधूनां कृतोऽन्येषां वधाय च ॥10.50.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतत्-अर्थः for this purpose/reason, अवतारः descent/incarnation, अयम् this, भू-भार-हरणाय for removing the burden of the earth, मे My, संरक्षणाय for the protection, साधूनाम् of the holy/pious devotees, कृतः is made/undertaken, अन्येषाम् of others (the unrighteous ones), वधाय for the destruction, च and

Anvaya: मे अयम् अवतारः भूभारहरणाय, साधूनां संरक्षणाय अन्येषां वधाय च एतदर्थः कृतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मे My, अयम् अवतारः this incarnation, भूभारहरणाय for relieving the burden of the earth, साधूनां संरक्षणाय for the protection of the virtuous, अन्येषां वधाय च and for the destruction of the others, एतदर्थः for this very purpose, कृतः has been undertaken.

Simple Meaning: This descent of Mine into the material world has been undertaken for this very purpose—to relieve the heavy burden of the earth, to fully protect the virtuous devotees, and to eradicate the unrighteous elements who disrupt cosmic order.

अन्योऽपि धर्मरक्षायै देहः संभ्रियते मया ।
विरामायाप्यधर्मस्य काले प्रभवतः क्वचित् ॥10.50.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्यः other, अपि also, धर्म-रक्षायै for the protection of dharma/righteousness, देहः body/manifestation, संभ्रियते is assumed/maintained, मया by Me, विरामाय for the cessation/stopping, अपि also, अधर्मस्य of unrighteousness, काले in course of time, प्रभवतः which manifests/predominates, क्वचित् sometimes/wherever

Anvaya: मया धर्मरक्षायै क्वचित् काले प्रभवतः अधर्मस्य विरामाय अपि अन्यः अपि देहः संभ्रियते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मया By Me, धर्मरक्षायै for the protection of righteousness, क्वचित् काले at certain times, प्रभवतः prevailing, अधर्मस्य of unrighteousness, विरामाय for the cessation, अपि also, अन्यः अपि another also, देहः body/form, संभ्रियते is assumed.

Simple Meaning: Whenever and wherever unrighteousness begins to dominate in the course of time, I assume various other transcendental forms and incarnations as well, to safeguard the principles of eternal virtue.

एवं ध्यायति गोविन्द आकाशात् सूर्यवर्चसौ ।
रथावुपस्थितौ सद्यः ससूतौ सपरिच्छदौ ॥10.50.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, ध्यायति while reflecting/thinking, गोविन्दे Lord Govinda, आकाशात् from the sky, सूर्य-वर्चसौ having the brilliance of the sun, रथौ two chariots, उपस्थितौ appeared, सद्यः instantly, स-सूतौ along with their charioteers, स-परिच्छदौ fully equipped with weapons and accessories

Anvaya: गोविन्दे एवं ध्यायति (सति), आकाशात् सूर्यवर्चसौ ससूतौ सपरिच्छदौ द्वौ रथौ सद्यः उपस्थितौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गोविन्दे While Govinda, एवं thus, ध्यायति was contemplating, आकाशात् from the sky, सूर्यवर्चसौ with the brilliance of the sun, ससूतौ along with charioteers, सपरिच्छदौ fully equipped with paraphernalia, द्वौ रथौ two chariots, सद्यः instantly, उपस्थितौ appeared.

Simple Meaning: While Govinda was contemplating the situation in this way, two celestial chariots as brilliant as the sun suddenly descended from the sky. They arrived fully equipped with weapons, battle gear, and their respective charioteers.

द‍ृष्ट्वा तानि हृषीकेशः सङ्कर्षणमथाब्रवीत् ॥10.50.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: आयुधानि weapons, च and, दिव्यानि celestial/divine, पुराणानि ancient/eternal, यदृच्छया of their own accord/by supreme will, दृष्ट्वा seeing, तानि them, हृषीकेशः the Lord of the senses (Krishna), सङ्कर्षणम् to Balarama, अथ then, अब्रवीत् spoke

Anvaya: अथ तानि दिव्यानि पुराणानि आयुधानि च यदृच्छया (उपस्थितानि) दृष्ट्वा हृषीकेशः सङ्कर्षणम् अब्रवीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ Then, तानि those, दिव्यानि celestial, पुराणानि ancient, आयुधानि weapons, च and, यदृच्छया by their own accord, (उपस्थितानी) arrived, दृष्ट्वा seeing, हृषीकेशः Lord Hrishikesha, सङ्कर्षणम् to Lord Sankarshana, अब्रवीत् spoke.

Simple Meaning: Seeing those divine, eternal weapons manifest there of their own accord, Lord Hrishikesha (Krishna) then turned and spoke to Lord Sankarshana (Balarama).

पश्यार्य व्यसनं प्राप्तं यदूनां त्वावतां प्रभो ।
एष ते रथ आयातो दयितान्यायुधानि च ॥10.50.13॥

तस्मादद्य विधास्यामो दुर्गं द्विपददुर्गमम् ।
तत्र ज्ञातीन् समाधाय यवनं घातयामहे ॥10.50.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मात् therefore, अद्य today, विधास्यामः we shall construct, दुर्गम् a fortress, द्विपद-दुर्गमम् inaccessible to human beings, तत्र there, ज्ञातीन् our relatives, समाधाय placing, यवनम् the Yavana king Kalayavana, घातयामहे we shall cause to be killed

Anvaya: तस्मात् अद्य द्विपददुर्गमं दुर्गं विधास्यामः। तत्र ज्ञातीन् समाधाय यवनं घातयामहे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तस्मात् Therefore, अद्य today, द्विपददुर्गमं inaccessible to human beings, दुर्गं a fortress, विधास्यामः we shall construct. तत्र There, ज्ञातीन् our relatives, समाधाय placing, यवनं the Yavana king, घातयामहे we shall cause to be killed.

Simple Meaning: Therefore, today we should construct a fortress that is completely inaccessible to human enemies. After safely placing our relatives inside that fortress, we will arrange for the destruction of the Yavana king.

इति सम्मन्त्र्य भगवान् दुर्गं द्वादशयोजनम् ।
अन्तःसमुद्रे नगरं कृत्स्नाद्भुतमचीकरत् ॥10.50.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, सम्मन्त्र्य consulting, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, दुर्गम् a fortress, द्वादश-योजनम् measuring twelve yojanas, अन्तःसमुद्रे within the ocean, नगरम् a city, कृत्स्न-अद्भुतम् completely wonderful, अचीकरत् had constructed

Anvaya: इति सम्मन्त्र्य भगवान् अन्तःसमुद्रे द्वादशयोजनं कृत्स्नाद्भुतं दुर्गं नगरम् अचीकरत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति Thus, सम्मन्त्र्य consulting, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, अन्तःसमुद्रे within the ocean, द्वादशयोजनं measuring twelve yojanas, कृत्स्नाद्भुतं completely wonderful, दुर्गं a fortress, नगरम् a city, अचीकरत् had constructed.

Simple Meaning: Having thus consulted with Balarama, the Supreme Lord had a completely wonderful fortress city measuring twelve yojanas constructed inside the ocean.

दृश्यते यत्र हि त्वाष्ट्रं विज्ञानं शिल्पनैपुणम् ।
रथ्याचत्वरवीथीभिर्यथावास्तु विनिर्मितम् ॥10.50.51॥

Word-by-word meaning: दृश्यते is seen, यत्र wherein, हि indeed, त्वाष्ट्रम् of Tvashta (Vishvakarma), विज्ञानम् the specialized knowledge, शिल्प-नैपुणम् the architectural expertise, रथ्या-चत्वर-वीथीभिः with main roads courtyards and residential streets, यथा-वास्तु according to the principles of vastu architecture, विनिर्मितम् constructed

Anvaya: यत्र हि त्वाष्ट्रं विज्ञानं शिल्पनैपुणं दृश्यते, रथ्याचत्वरवीथीभिः यथावास्तु विनिर्मितम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यत्र Wherein, हि indeed, त्वाष्ट्रं of Vishvakarma, विज्ञानं the specialized knowledge, शिल्पनैपुणं and architectural expertise, दृश्यते is seen, रथ्याचत्वरवीथीभिः with main roads courtyards and residential streets, यथावास्तु according to the principles of vastu architecture, विनिर्मितम् constructed.

Simple Meaning: In that city, the extraordinary architectural expertise and scientific knowledge of Vishvakarma was fully displayed. It was constructed with wide main roads, open courtyards, and residential streets laid out perfectly according to the sacred principles of vastu design.

सुरद्रुमलतोद्यानविचित्रोपवनान्वितम् ।
हेमशृङ्गैर्दिविस्पृग्भिः स्फाटिकाट्टालगोपुरैः ॥10.50.52॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुर-द्रुम-लता-उद्यान-विचित्र-उपवन-अन्वितम् filled with gardens of heavenly trees and creepers along with wonderful orchards, हेम-शृङ्गैः with golden peaks, दिवि-स्पृग्भिः which touched the sky, स्फाटिक-अट्टाल-गोपुरैः with watchtowers and gateways made of crystal

Anvaya: सुरद्रुमलतोद्यानविचित्रोपवनान्वितं दिविस्पृग्भिः हेमशृङ्गैः स्फाटिकाट्टालगोपुरैः (च तत् नगरं निर्मितम्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सुरद्रुमलतोद्यानविचित्रोपवनान्वितं Filled with gardens of heavenly trees and creepers along with wonderful orchards, दिविस्पृग्भिः which touched the sky, हेमशृङ्गैः with golden peaks, स्फाटिकाट्टालगोपुरैः with watchtowers and gateways made of crystal (that city was constructed).

Simple Meaning: That city was filled with beautiful gardens of celestial trees and creepers along with wonderful orchards. It featured sky scraping golden peaks and splendid watchtowers and gateways built entirely of crystal.

राजतारकुटैः कोष्ठैर्हेमकुम्भैरलङ्कृतैः ।
रत्नकूटैगृहैर्हैमैर्महामरकतस्थलैः ॥10.50.53॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजत-आरकुटैः with silver and brass, कोष्ठैः with storehouses, हेम-कुम्भैः with golden waterpots, अलङ्कृतैः decorated, रत्न-कूटैः with jeweled peaks, गृहैः with houses, हैमैः made of gold, महा-मरकत-स्थलैः with floors made of great emeralds

Anvaya: राजतारकुटैः कोष्ठैः हेमकुम्भैः अलङ्कृतैः रत्नकूटैः हैमैः गृहैः महामरकतस्थलैः (च तत् नगरम् व्यरोचत)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजतारकुटैः With silver and brass, कोष्ठैः storehouses, हेमकुम्भैः with golden waterpots, अलङ्कृतैः decorated, रत्नकूटैः with jeweled peaks, हैमैः made of gold, गृहैः houses, महामरकतस्थलैः with floors made of great emeralds (that city shone beautifully).

Simple Meaning: That city shone beautifully with storehouses made of silver and brass, decorated with golden waterpots, as well as golden palaces featuring jeweled peaks and magnificent floors crafted from pure emeralds.

वास्तोष्पतीनां च गृहैर्वलभीभिश्च निर्मितम् ।
चातुर्वर्ण्यजनाकीर्णं यदुदेवगृहोल्लसत् ॥10.50.54॥

Word-by-word meaning: वास्तोः पतीनाम् of the demigods presiding over architecture, च and, गृहैः with the residential palaces, वलभीभिः with balconies and sloping roofs, च and, निर्मितम् constructed, चातुर्वर्ण्य-जन-आकीर्णम् crowded with people of the four social classes, यदु-देव-गृह-उल्लसत् resplendent with the palaces of the chiefs of the Yadus

Anvaya: वास्तोष्पतीनां च वलभीभिः गृहैः च निर्मितम्। चातुर्वर्ण्यजनाकीर्णं यदुदेवगृहोल्लसत् (तत् नगरम् आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वास्तोष्पतीनां Of the presiding demigods presiding over architecture, च and, वलभीभिः with balconies, गृहैः with the residential palaces,च and, निर्मितम् was constructed. चातुर्वर्ण्यजनाकीर्णं Crowded with people of the four social classes, यदुदेवगृहोल्लसत् resplendent with the palace of Lord Krishna the chief of the Yadus.

Simple Meaning: The city was built with assembly halls and residential palaces belonging to the presiding demigods of architecture, complete with elegant balconies. It was densely populated by citizens of all four social orders and shone resplendently with the magnificent palaces of the chiefs of the Yadus.

सुधर्मां पारिजातं च महेन्द्रः प्राहिणोद्धरेः ।
यत्र चावस्थितो मर्त्यो मर्त्यधर्मैर्न युज्यते ॥10.50.55॥

Word-by-word meaning: सुधर्माम् the Sudharma assembly hall, पारिजातम् the Parijata tree, च and, महेन्द्रः Indra, प्राहिणोत् sent, हरेः for Krishna, यत्र wherein, च and, अवस्थितः staying, मर्त्यः a mortal human being, मर्त्य-धर्मैः with the limitations of mortal life, न not, युज्यते is affected

Anvaya: महेन्द्रः हरेः सुधर्मां पारिजातं च प्राहिणोत् यत्र च अवस्थितो मर्त्यो मर्त्यधर्मैः न युज्यते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महेन्द्रः Indra, हरेः for Krishna, सुधर्मां the Sudharma assembly hall, पारिजातं the Parijata tree, च and, प्राहिणोत् sent, यत्र wherein, च and, अवस्थितः staying, मर्त्यः a mortal human being, मर्त्यधर्मैः with the limitations of mortal life, न not, युज्यते is affected.

Simple Meaning: Indra, sent the famous Sudharma assembly hall and the celestial Parijata tree to Krishna. Remaining within the proximity of that assembly hall and the Parijata tree, a mortal human being is never affected by the inevitable limitations of mortal life.

श्यामैककर्णान् वरुणो हयाञ्छुक्लान् मनोजवान् ।
अष्टौ निधिपतिः कोशान् लोकपालो निजोदयान् ॥10.50.56॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्याम-एक-कर्णान् having only one ear black, वरुणः Varuna, हयान् horses, शुक्लान् white, मनोजवान् as swift as the mind, अष्टौ eight, निधि-पतिः the lord of treasures Kubera, कोशान् treasures, लोक-पालः the planetary rulers, निज-उदयान् their own respective opulences

Anvaya: वरुणः श्यामैककर्णान् मनोजवान् शुक्लान् हयान्, निधिपतिः अष्टौ कोशान् लोकपालः निजोदयान् (हरेः प्राहिणवन्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वरुणः Varuna, श्यामैककर्णान् having only one ear black, मनोजवान् as swift as the mind, शुक्लान् white, हयान् horses, निधिपतिः the lord of treasures Kubera, अष्टौ eight, कोशान् treasures, लोकपालः the planetary rulers, निजोदयान् their own respective opulences (for Lord Krishna sent).

Simple Meaning: Varuna presented Krishna with white horses that were as swift as the mind and had only one ear colored black. Kubera, the lord of treasures, offered the eight mystic treasures, and the various planetary rulers brought their own respective opulences.

यद् यद् भगवता दत्तमाधिपत्यं स्वसिद्धये ।
सर्वं प्रत्यर्पयामासुर्हरौ भूमिगते नृप ॥10.50.57॥

Word-by-word meaning: यत् यत् whatever, भगवता by the Supreme Lord, दत्तम् was given, आधिपत्यम् sovereignty, स्व-सिद्धये for the perfection of their own duties, सर्वम् all, प्रत्यर्पयामासुः they returned, हरौ when Lord Krishna, भूमि-गते appeared on the earth, नृप O King

Anvaya: नृप! भगवता स्वसिद्धये यत् यत् आधिपत्यं दत्तं हरौ भूमिगते (ते) सर्वं प्रत्यर्पयामासुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King, भगवता by the Supreme Lord, स्वसिद्धये for the perfection of their own duties, यत् यत् whatever, आधिपत्यं sovereignty, दत्तं was given, हरौ when Lord Krishna, भूमिगते appeared on the earth, ते they, सर्वं all, प्रत्यर्पयामासुः returned.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, whatever positions of sovereignty and cosmic powers the Supreme Lord had previously given to the demigods for the execution of their duties, they returned all of those opulences to Him when He descended to the earth.

तत्र योगप्रभावेण नीत्वा सर्वजनं हरिः ।
प्रजापालेन रामेण कृष्णः समनुमन्त्रितः ।
निर्जगाम पुरद्वारात् पद्ममाली निरायुधः ॥10.50.58॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there, योग-प्रभावेण by the power of His yoga, नीत्वा having brought, सर्वजनम् all the citizens, हरिः Lord Hari, प्रजा-पालेन with the protector of the citizens, रामेण Balarama, कृष्णः Lord Krishna, समनुमन्त्रितः after consulting, निर्जगाम went out, पुर-द्वारात् from the city gate, पद्ममाली wearing a garland of lotuses, निरायुधः completely unarmed

Anvaya: तत्र हरिः कृष्णः योगप्रभावेण सर्वजनं नीत्वा, प्रजापालेन रामेण समनुमन्त्रितः, पद्ममाली निरायुधः पुरद्वारात् निर्जगाम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत्र There, हरिः कृष्णः Lord Hari Krishna, योगप्रभावेण by the power of His mystic yoga, सर्वजनं all the citizens, नीत्वा having brought, प्रजापालेन with the protector of the citizens, रामेण Balarama, समनुमन्त्रितः after consulting, पद्ममाली wearing a garland of lotuses, निरायुधः completely unarmed, पुरद्वारात् from the city gate, निर्जगाम went out.

Simple Meaning: There, after transferring all the citizens to that ocean fortress by the power of His yoga and consulting with Balarama, the protector of the people, Lord Krishna walked out of the city gate completely unarmed, wearing only a garland of lotuses.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “कृष्णजरासन्धयुद्धं तथा कृष्णस्य द्वारकापुरीस्थापनं नाम”  पञ्चाशत्तमः अध्यायः ॥50॥

Thus ends the fiftieth discourse named “Battle between Krishna and Jarasandha. Krishna establishes the City of Dwaraka”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
