श्री शुक उवाच
स इत्थं द्विजमुख्येन सह सङ्कथयन् हरिः ।
सर्वभूतमनोऽभिज्ञ: स्मयमान उवाच तम् ॥10.81.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, सः He, इत्थम् thus, द्विज-मुख्येन with the foremost of brahmanas (Sudama), सह together, सङ्कथयन् conversing, हरिः Lord Hari, सर्व-भूत-मनः-अभिज्ञः who fully knows the minds and hearts of all living beings, स्मयमानः smiling gently, उवाच spoke, तम् unto him

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच — सर्वभूतमनोऽभिज्ञः सः हरिः इत्थं द्विजमुख्येन सह सङ्कथयन् स्मयमानः तम् उवाच।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, सर्वभूतमनोऽभिज्ञः knowing the innermost thoughts of all creatures, सः हरिः Hari, इत्थं in this manner, द्विजमुख्येन सह with the exalted brahmana, सङ्कथयन् conversing intimately, स्मयमानः with a gentle smile, तम् unto him, उवाच spoke.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Lord Hari, who knows the innermost thoughts of all living beings, continued conversing in this manner with the noble brahmana Sudama. Smiling gently, the Lord then addressed him.

ब्रह्मण्यो ब्राह्मणं कृष्णो भगवान् प्रहसन् प्रियम् ।
प्रेम्णा निरीक्षणेनैव प्रेक्षन् खलु सतां गतिः ॥10.81.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्रह्मण्यः respectful to the brahmanas, ब्राह्मणम् unto the brahmana Sudama, कृष्णः Krishna, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, प्रहसन् smiling, प्रियम् His dear friend, प्रेम्णा with love, निरीक्षणेन with glance, एव indeed, प्रेक्षन् looking at, खलु surely, सताम् गतिः the ultimate shelter of all saintly souls

Anvaya: (अथ) सतां गतिः ब्रह्मण्यः भगवान् कृष्णः प्रियं ब्राह्मणं प्रेम्णा निरीक्षणेन एव प्रेक्षन् प्रहसन् (उवाच)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (अथ) Then, सतां गतिः the supreme refuge of noble souls, ब्रह्मण्यः who holds brahmanas in high devotion, भगवान् कृष्णः Lord Krishna, प्रियं His beloved, ब्राह्मणं brahmana, प्रेम्णा with affection, निरीक्षणेन एव with His glance, प्रेक्षन् gazing upon, प्रहसन् smiling, (उवाच) spoke.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna—the ultimate refuge of all saintly souls and the protector of brahmanas—looked upon His dear friend Sudama with a glance and smiling, spoke to him.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
किमुपायनमानीतं ब्रह्मन् मे भवता गृहात् ।
अण्वप्युपाहृतं भक्तैः प्रेम्णा भूर्येव मे भवेत् ।
भूर्यप्यभक्तोपहृतं न मे तोषाय कल्पते ॥10.81.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, किम् what, उपायनम् gift/present, आनीतम् brought, ब्रह्मन् O brahmana, मे for Me, भवता by you, गृहात् from your home, अणु small/minute, अपि even, उपाहृतम् offered, भक्तैः by devotees, प्रेम्णा with love, भूरि abundant/immense, एव indeed, मे for Me, भवेत् becomes, भूरि abundant, अपि even, अभक्त-उपहृतम् offered by non-devotees, न not, मे My, तोषाय for satisfaction, कल्पते serves

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच — ब्रह्मन्! भवता गृहात् मे किम् उपायनम् आनीतम्? भक्तैः प्रेम्णा उपाहृतम् अणु अपि मे भूरि एव भवेत्, अभक्तोपहृतं भूरि अपि मे तोषाय न कल्पते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, (हे) ब्रह्मन् O learned brahmana, भवता by you, गृहात् from home, मे for Me, किम् what, उपायनम् present, आनीतम् has been brought? भक्तैः By devotees, प्रेम्णा with pure love, उपाहृतम् offered, अणु tiny, अपि even, मे for Me, भूरि great, एव indeed, भवेत् becomes; अभक्तोपहृतं offered by a non-devotee, भूरि substantial, अपि even, मे My, तोषाय for gratification, न not, कल्पते suffices.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord said: O holy brahmana, what present have you brought for Me from your home? Even the smallest offering presented by My devotees with love becomes immense to Me, whereas even lavish gifts offered by non-devotees fail to bring Me satisfaction.

पत्रं पुष्पं फलं तोयं यो मे भक्त्या प्रयच्छति ।
तदहं भक्त्युपहृतमश्नामि प्रयतात्मनः ॥10.81.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: पत्रम् a leaf, पुष्पम् a flower, फलम् a fruit, तोयम् water, यः whoever, मे unto Me, भक्त्या with devotion, प्रयच्छति offers, तत् that, अहम् I, भक्ति-उपहृतम् offered with devotion, अश्नामि accept/eat, प्रयत-आत्मनः from one of pure heart

Anvaya: यः मे भक्त्या पत्रं पुष्पं फलं (वा) तोयं प्रयच्छति, प्रयतात्मनः भक्त्युपहृतं तत् अहम् अश्नामि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः Whoever, मे unto Me, भक्त्या with loving devotion, पत्रं a leaf, पुष्पं a flower, फलं a fruit, (वा) or, तोयं water, प्रयच्छति offers; प्रयतात्मनः of that pure-minded devotee, भक्त्युपहृतं presented with devotion, तत् that gift, अहम् I, अश्नामि accept.

Simple Meaning: Whoever offers Me with devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or even water—I accept that offering from that pure-hearted devotee.

इत्युक्तोऽपि द्विजस्तस्मै व्रीडितः पतये श्रियः ।
पृथुकप्रसृतिं राजन् न प्रायच्छदवाङ्मुखः ॥10.81.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति उक्तः spoken to in this manner, अपि even though, द्विजः the brahmana (Sudama), तस्मै unto Him, व्रीडितः feeling bashful/embarrassed, पतये unto the Master, श्रियः of the Goddess of Fortune (Lakshmi), पृथुक-प्रसृतिम् a handful of flattened rice, राजन् O King (Parikshit), न did not, प्रायच्छत् offer/present, अवाङ्मुखः hanging his head down in embarrassment

Anvaya: राजन्! इति उक्तः अपि श्रियः पतये तस्मै व्रीडितः अवाङ्मुखः द्विजः पृथुकप्रसृतिं न प्रायच्छत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King Parikshit, इति उक्तः addressed in this way, अपि even, श्रियः पतये unto the Lord of the Goddess of Wealth, तस्मै unto Him, व्रीडितः feeling embarrassed, अवाङ्मुखः with his head bowed down, द्विजः the brahmana, पृथुकप्रसृतिं handful of beaten rice, न not, प्रायच्छत् did give.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, even though Krishna addressed him so warmly, Sudama felt embarrassed in the presence of the Master of Goddess Lakshmi. Hanging his head in bashfulness, he could not bring himself to offer that handful of flattened rice.

सर्वभूतात्मदृक् साक्षात् तस्यागमनकारणम् ।
विज्ञायाचिन्तयन्नायं श्रीकामो माभजत् पुरा ॥10.81.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: सर्व-भूत-आत्म-दृक् seeing the Soul within all living beings, साक्षात् directly, तस्य his (Sudama's), आगमन-कारणम् cause of arrival, विज्ञाय knowing, अचिन्तयत् thought, न not, अयम् this (friend), श्री-कामः desiring wealth, मा unto Me, अभजत् worshipped, पुरा in the past

Anvaya: सर्वभूतात्मदृक् (भगवान् कृष्णः) तस्य आगमनकारणं साक्षात् विज्ञाय अचिन्तयत् — पुरा अयं श्रीकामः (सन्) मा न अभजत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सर्वभूतात्मदृक् Seeing directly into the hearts of all living beings, (भगवान् कृष्णः Lord Krishna), तस्य his, आगमनकारणं reason for coming, साक्षात् directly, विज्ञाय having known, अचिन्तयत् reflected within Himself: पुरा in the past, अयं this friend, श्रीकामः desiring wealth or material opulence, मा unto Me, न did not, अभजत् worship.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna, who directly sees the innermost thoughts of all living beings, understood the exact reason for Sudama's arrival. The Lord thought to Himself: "In the past, when we lived together, my dear friend never worshipped or served Me out of a desire for material wealth.

पत्न्याः पतिव्रतायास्तु सखा प्रियचिकीर्षया ।
प्राप्तो मामस्य दास्यामि सम्पदोऽमर्त्यदुर्लभाः ॥10.81.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: पत्न्याः of his wife, पतिव्रतायाः who is devoted to her husband, तु indeed, सखा My friend, प्रिय-चिकीर्षया with the desire to please her, प्राप्तः has come, माम् unto Me, अस्य for him, दास्यामि I shall bestow, सम्पदः riches/opulences, अमर्त्य-दुर्लभाः rare even for the immortal devas

Anvaya: तु पतिव्रतायाः पत्न्याः प्रियचिकीर्षया सखा मां प्राप्तः, (अतः) अस्य अमर्त्यदुर्लभाः सम्पदः दास्यामि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तु Indeed, पतिव्रतायाः of his devoted, पत्न्याः wife, प्रियचिकीर्षया with the desire to please her, सखा friend, मां unto Me, प्राप्तः has arrived; (अतः) therefore, अस्य for him, अमर्त्यदुर्लभाः extremely rare even for the immortals, सम्पदः opulences, दास्यामि I will grant.

Simple Meaning: "My friend has come to Me simply out of a desire to fulfill the wish of his devoted wife. Therefore, I shall grant him immense riches and opulences that are rare even for the immortal devas to attain."

इत्थं विचिन्त्य वसनाच्चीरबद्धान्द्विजन्मनः ।
स्वयं जहार किमिदमिति पृथुकतण्डुलान् ॥10.81.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: इत्थम् thus, विचिन्त्य thinking, वसनात् from the garment, चीर-बद्धान् tied in a strip of cloth, द्वि-जन्मनः of the brahmana, स्वयम् Himself, जहार snatched away, किम् what, इदम् this, इति saying, पृथुक-तण्डुलान् the flattened rice grains

Anvaya: इत्थं विचिन्त्य किम् इदम् इति (उक्त्वा) वसनात् चीरबद्धान् पृथुकतण्डुलान् स्वयं जहार।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इत्थं विचिन्त्य Thus thinking, किम् इदम् इति (उक्त्वा) saying what is this, वसनात् चीरबद्धान् tied in a strip of cloth from the garment, पृथुकतण्डुलान् the flattened rice grains, स्वयं जहार Himself snatched away.

Simple Meaning: Thinking thus and asking what is this, Krishna Himself snatched away the flattened rice grains that were tied in a strip of cloth in Sudama’s garment.

नन्वेत्दुपनीतं मे परमप्रीणनं सखे ।
तर्पयन्त्यङ्ग मां विश्वमेते पृथुकतण्डुलाः ॥10.81.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: ननु indeed, एतत् this, उपनीतम् brought, मे for Me, परम-प्रीणनम् giving supreme pleasure, सखे O friend, तर्पयन्ति satisfy, अङ्ग My dear, माम् Me, विश्वम् the entire universe, एते these, पृथुक-तण्डुलान् flattened rice grains

Anvaya: सखे! ननु एतत् मे उपनीतं परमप्रीणनम्। अङ्ग! एते पृथुकतण्डुलाः मां विश्वं तर्पयन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सखे O friend, ननु एतत् indeed this, मे उपनीतं brought for Me, परमप्रीणनम् gives supreme pleasure. अङ्ग My dear friend, एते पृथुकतण्डुलाः these flattened rice grains, मां विश्वं तर्पयन्ति satisfy Me and the whole universe.

Simple Meaning: "O friend, indeed this item brought for Me gives Me supreme pleasure. My dear friend, these flattened rice grains will satisfy not only Me, but also the entire universe."

इति मुष्टिं सकृज्जग्ध्वा द्वितीयां जग्धुमाददे ।
तावच्छ्रीर्जगृहे हस्तं तत्परा परमेष्ठिनः ॥10.81.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, मुष्टिम् a handful, सकृत् once, जग्ध्वा having eaten, द्वितीयाम् a second handful, जग्धुम् to eat, आददे took up, तावत् then, श्रीः Lakshmi Devi, जगृहे held, हस्तम् His hand, तत्-परा devoted to Him, परमेष्ठिनः of the supreme Lord

Anvaya: इति सकृत् मुष्टिं जग्ध्वा द्वितीयां जग्धुम् आददे, तावत् तत्परा श्रीः परमेष्ठिनः हस्तं जगृहे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति सकृत् मुष्टिं जग्ध्वा Having thus eaten one handful, द्वितीयां जग्धुम् आददे He took up a second handful to eat, तावत् तत्परा श्रीः then the devoted Lakshmi Devi, परमेष्ठिनः हस्तं जगृहे held the hand of the supreme Lord.

Simple Meaning: Having thus eaten one handful of the flattened rice, Krishna took up a second handful to eat. At that moment, Lakshmi Devi, who is completely devoted to Him, held His hand to stop Him.

एतावतालं विश्वात्मन् सर्वसम्पत्समृद्धये ।
अस्मिँल्लोकेऽथवामुष्मिन् पुंसस्त्वत्तोषकारणम् ॥10.81.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतावत् with this much, अलम् sufficient, विश्व-आत्मन् O Soul of the universe, सर्व-सम्पत्-समृद्धये for the attainment of all opulences, अस्मिन् in this, लोके world, अथवा or, अमुष्मिन् in the next world, पुंसः for a person, त्वत्-तोष-कारणम् the cause of Your satisfaction

Anvaya: विश्वात्मन्! अस्मिन् लोके अथवा अमुष्मिन् पुंसः सर्वसम्पत्समृद्धये एतावत् अलम्, (यतः) त्वत्तोषकारणम् (सर्वं भवति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विश्वात्मन् O Soul of the universe, अस्मिन् लोके अथवा अमुष्मिन् in this world or in the next, पुंसः सर्वसम्पत्समृद्धये for a person to attain all opulences, एतावत् अलम् this much is sufficient, (यतः) त्वत्तोषकारणम् because pleasing You is the cause of all attainment.

Simple Meaning: "O Soul of the universe, this much is more than sufficient for a person to attain all forms of opulence and prosperity in this world as well as in the next world, because pleasing You is the sole cause of all perfection and good fortune."

ब्राह्मणस्तां तु रजनीमुषित्वाच्युतमन्दिरे ।
भुक्त्वा पीत्वा सुखं मेने आत्मानं स्वर्गतं यथा ॥10.81.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्राह्मणः the brahmana, ताम् that, तु indeed, रजनीम् night, उषित्वा having stayed, अच्युत-मन्दिरे in the palace of Krishna, भुक्त्वा having eaten, पीत्वा having drunk, सुखम् comfortably, मेने felt, आत्मानम् himself, स्वर्ग-तम् gone to heaven, यथा as if

Anvaya: ब्राह्मणः तु अच्युतमन्दिरे तां रजनीम् उषित्वा भुक्त्वा पीत्वा सुखं आत्मानं स्वर्गतं यथा मेने।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्राह्मणः तु The brahmana indeed, अच्युतमन्दिरे तां रजनीम् उषित्वा having stayed that night in the palace of Krishna, भुक्त्वा पीत्वा having eaten and drunk, सुखम् comfortably, आत्मानं स्वर्गतं यथा मेने felt as if he had gone to heaven.

Simple Meaning: The brahmana Sudama spent that night comfortably in the palace of Krishna. After eating and drinking well, he felt as though he had been elevated to heaven itself.

श्वोभूते विश्वभावेन स्वसुखेनाभिवन्दितः ।
जगाम स्वालयं तात पथ्यनुव्रज्य नन्दितः ॥10.81.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्वः भूते at dawn, विश्व-भावेन by the creator of the universe, स्व-सुखेन by His own supreme bliss, अभिवन्दितः revered, जगाम went, स्व-आलयम् to his own home, तात my dear, पथि along the path, अनुव्रज्य following behind, नन्दितः delighted

Anvaya: तात! श्वोभूते स्वसुखेन विश्वभावेन अभिवन्दितः पथि अनुव्रज्य नन्दितः (सः द्विजः) स्वालयं जगाम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तात My dear, श्वोभूते at dawn, स्वसुखेन विश्वभावेन अभिवन्दितः honoured by the blissfully self-satisfied creator of the universe, पथि अनुव्रज्य नन्दितः delighted by being escorted along the path, स्वालयं जगाम he departed for his home.

Simple Meaning: My dear King Parikshit, the next morning, Sudama was respectfully honoured by Krishna, the blissfully self-satisfied creator of the universe. Being escorting a short distance along his path and feeling immense delight, he departed for his home.

स चालब्ध्वा धनं कृष्णान्न तु याचितवान् स्वयम् ।
स्वगृहान् व्रीडितोऽगच्छन्महद्दर्शननिर्वृतः ॥10.81.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, च and, अलब्ध्वा without receiving, धनम् wealth, कृष्णात् from Sri Krishna, न not, तु indeed, याचितवान् asked, स्वयम् himself, स्व-गृहान् to his own house, व्रीडितः feeling ashamed, अगच्छत् went, महत्-दर्शन-निर्वृतः blissfully satisfied by seeing the Great Lord

Anvaya: सः कृष्णात् धनम् अलब्ध्वा स्वयं न तु याचितवान्, महद्दर्शननिर्वृतः व्रीडितः स्वगृहान् अगच्छत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He, कृष्णात् धनम् अलब्ध्वा without receiving wealth from Sri Krishna, स्वयं न तु याचितवान् indeed did not ask Himself, महद्दर्शननिर्वृतः blissfully satisfied by seeing the Great Lord, व्रीडितः feeling ashamed, स्वगृहान् अगच्छत् went towards his home.

Simple Meaning: Even though he received no material wealth from Krishna, he had not asked for anything himself. Feeling a bit ashamed for not asking on behalf of his wife yet deeply satisfied and peaceful from having seen the Great Lord Krishna, he returned home.

अहो ब्रह्मण्यदेवस्य दृष्टा ब्रह्मण्यता मया ।
यद् दरिद्रतमो लक्ष्मीमाष्लिष्टो विभ्रतोरसि ॥10.81.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: अहो oh, ब्रह्मण्य-देवस्य of the benefactor of the brahmanas, दृष्टा seen, ब्रह्मण्यता devotion to brahmanas, मया by me, यत् because, दरिद्र-तमः extremely poor, लक्ष्मीम् Lakshmi Devi, आश्लिष्टः embraced, विभ्रता by Him who bears, उरसि on His chest

Anvaya: अहो! मया ब्रह्मण्यदेवस्य ब्रह्मण्यता दृष्टा, यत् लक्ष्मीम् उरसि विभ्रता दरिद्रतमः (अहम्) आश्लिष्टः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अहो Oh, मया by me, ब्रह्मण्यदेवस्य ब्रह्मण्यता दृष्टा the devotion to brahmanas of the Lord, who is the friend of brahmanas has been witnessed, यत् because, लक्ष्मीम् उरसि विभ्रता by Him who carries Lakshmi Devi on His chest, दरिद्रतमः an extremely poor person like me, आश्लिष्टः was embraced.

Simple Meaning: “Oh, today I have personally witnessed the deep devotion and reverence of Lord Krishna towards the brahmanas. Though He carries Lakshmi Devi upon His chest, He lovingly embraced an extremely poor person like me.

क्वाहं दरिद्रः पापीयान् क्व कृष्णः श्रीनिकेतनः ।
ब्रह्मबन्धुरिति स्माहं बाहुभ्यां परिरम्भितः ॥10.81.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्व where, अहम् I, दरिद्रः poor, पापीयान् sinful, क्व where, कृष्णः Krishna, श्री-निकेतनः the abode of Lakshmi Devi, ब्रह्म-बन्धुः a brahmana, इति thus, स्म indeed, अहम् I, बाहुभ्याम् with His two arms, परिरम्भितः embraced

Anvaya: क्व दरिद्रः पापीयान् अहं, क्व श्रीनिकेतनः कृष्णः, (अहं) ब्रह्मबन्धुः इति बाहुभ्यां परिरम्भितः स्म।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: क्व दरिद्रः पापीयान् अहं Where am I, a poor and sinful person, क्व श्रीनिकेतनः कृष्णः and where is Krishna, the abode of Lakshmi Devi; अहं ब्रह्मबन्धुः इति knowing me to be a brahmana, बाहुभ्यां परिरम्भितः स्म I was embraced by Him with His two arms.

Simple Meaning: “Where am I, an impoverished and sinful person, and where is Krishna, the supreme abode of Lakshmi Devi? Yet, knowing me to be a brahmana, He lovingly embraced me with His two arms.

निवासितः प्रियाजुष्टे पर्यङ्के भ्रातरो यथा ।
महिष्या वीजितः श्रान्तो वालव्यजनहस्तया ॥10.81.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: निवासितः seated, प्रिया-जुष्टे enjoyed by His beloved, पर्यङ्के on the bedstead, भ्रातरः a brother, यथा like, महिष्या by the queen, वीजितः fanned, श्रान्तः exhausted, वाल-व्यजन-हस्तया holding a chamara fan in her hand

Anvaya: भ्रातरः यथा प्रियाजुष्टे पर्यङ्के निवासितः, श्रान्तः (अहं) वालव्यजनहस्तया महिष्या वीजितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भ्रातरः यथा Like a brother, प्रियाजुष्टे पर्यङ्के निवासितः seated on the bedstead enjoyed by His beloved, श्रान्तः being exhausted, वालव्यजनहस्तया महिष्या वीजितः fanned by the queen holding a chamara fan in her hand.

Simple Meaning: “He seated me on the very bedstead used by His beloved queen, just as if I were His own brother. And because I was exhausted, Queen Rukmini herself fanned me with a chamara fan in her hand.

शुश्रूषया परमया पादसंवाहनादिभिः ।
पूजितो देवदेवेन विप्रदेवेन देववत् ॥10.81.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: शुश्रूषया with loving service, परमया supreme, पाद-संवाहन-आदिभिः by massaging the feet and other attentions, पूजितः worshipped, देव-देवेन by the Lord of devas, विप्र-देवेन who honours the brahmanas, देव-वत् like a deva

Anvaya: विप्रदेवेन देवदेवेन परमया शुश्रूषया पादसंवाहनादिभिः देववत् पूजितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विप्रदेवेन By the worshipper of brahmanas, देवदेवेन by the Lord of devas, परमया शुश्रूषया with supreme service, पादसंवाहनादिभिः by massaging the feet and other acts, देववत् पूजितः I was worshipped like a deva.

Simple Meaning: “By Krishna, who is the Lord of all devas and the reverential worshipper of brahmanas, I was worshipped like a deva through supreme loving service, including the massaging of my feet and other acts of respect.

स्वर्गापवर्गयोः पुंसां रसायां भुवि सम्पदाम् ।
सर्वासामपि सिद्धीनां मूलं तच्चरणार्चनम् ॥10.81.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्वर्ग-अपवर्गयोः of heavenly enjoyment and liberation, पुंसाम् for men, रसायाम् in the nether regions, भुवि on the earth, सम्पदाम् of opulences, सर्वासाम् of all, अपि even, सिद्धीनाम् of mystic attainments, मूलम् the root cause, तत्-चरण-अर्चनम् the worship of His lotus feet

Anvaya: पुंसां स्वर्गापवर्गयोः रसायां भुवि सम्पदां सर्वासाम् अपि सिद्धीनां मूलं तच्चरणार्चनम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पुंसां For men, स्वर्गापवर्गयोः of heaven and liberation, रसायां भुवि सम्पदां of riches on earth and in the nether regions, सर्वासाम् अपि सिद्धीनां and of all perfections, मूलं तच्चरणार्चनम् the worship of His lotus feet is the root cause.

Simple Meaning: “Worship of Krishna lotus feet is the fundamental root cause for human beings to attain heavenly pleasures, liberation, material opulences on earth and in the nether worlds, as well as all spiritual and mystic perfections.

अधनोऽयं धनं प्राप्य माद्यन्नुच्चैर्न मां स्मरेत् ।
इति कारुणिको नूनं धनं मेऽभूरि नाददात् ॥10.81.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: अधनः poor, अयम् this person, धनम् wealth, प्राप्य obtaining, माद्यन् becoming intoxicated, उच्चैः greatly, न not, माम् Me, स्मरेत् would remember, इति thinking thus, कारुणिकः the merciful Lord, नूनम् surely, धनम् wealth, मे to me, अभूरि small amount, न not, आददात् did give

Anvaya: अयम् अधनः धनं प्राप्य उच्चैः माद्यन् मां न स्मरेत् – इति कारुणिकः नूनं मे अभूरि धनं न आददात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अयम् अधनः This poor man, धनं प्राप्य obtaining wealth, उच्चैः माद्यन् becoming very intoxicated, मां न स्मरेत् would not remember Me, इति thinking thus, कारुणिकः the merciful Lord, नूनं surely, मे to me, अभूरि धनं न आददात् did not give even a small amount of wealth.

Simple Meaning: “If this poor man receives wealth, he might become overly intoxicated by it and forget Me, thinking thus the merciful Lord out of His compassion surely did not grant me even a small amount of wealth.”

इति तच्चिन्तयन्नन्तः प्राप्तो निजगृहान्तिकम् ।
सूर्यानलेन्दुसङ्काशैर्विमानैः सर्वतो वृतम् ॥10.81.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, तत्-चिन्तयन् thinking of that, अन्तः within his mind, प्राप्तः arrived, निज-गृह-अन्तिकम् near his own home, सूर्य-अनल-इन्दु-सङ्काशैः shining like the sun, fire and moon, विमानैः with palaces, सर्वतः on all sides, वृतम् surrounded

Anvaya: इति अन्तः तच्चिन्तयन् सूर्यानलेन्दुसङ्काशैः विमानैः सर्वतः वृतं निजगृहान्तिकं प्राप्तः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति अन्तः तच्चिन्तयन् Thus contemplating within his mind, सूर्यानलेन्दुसङ्काशैः विमानैः with palaces glittering like the sun, fire and moon, सर्वतः वृतं surrounded on all sides, निजगृहान्तिकं near his own home, प्राप्तः he arrived.

Simple Meaning: While contemplating thus within his mind, Sudama arrived near his home and was astonished to find the entire area surrounded by magnificent palaces shining brilliantly like the sun, fire, and moon.

विचित्रोपवनोद्यानैः कूजद्द्विजकुलाकुलैः ।
प्रोत्फुल्लकुमुदाम्भोजकह्लारोत्पलवारिभिः ॥10.81.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: विचित्र-उपवन-उद्यानैः with wonderful gardens and orchards, कूजत्-द्विज-कुल-आकुलैः filled with swarms of chirping birds, प्रोत्फुल्ल-कुमुद-अम्भोज-कह्लार-उत्पल-वारिभिः with bodies of water containing blossomed lilies, and red and blue lotuses

Anvaya: (सः गृहं) विचित्रोपवनोद्यानैः कूजद्द्विजकुलाकुलैः प्रोत्फुल्लकुमुदाम्भोजकह्लारोत्पलवारिभिः (वृतं ददर्श)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सः गृहं) He saw his home, विचित्रोपवनोद्यानैः with wonderful small gardens and large orchards, कूजद्द्विजकुलाकुलैः crowded with swarms of chirping birds, प्रोत्फुल्लकुमुदाम्भोजकह्लारोत्पलवारिभिः with waters filled with blooming lilies, and red and blue lotus flowers.

Simple Meaning: He saw that his residence was surrounded by magnificent gardens and orchards filled with swarms of chirping birds, as well as ponds filled with fully blossomed lilies, and red and blue lotuses.

जुष्टं स्वलङ्कृतैः पुम्भिः स्त्रीभिश्च हरिणाक्षिभिः ।
किमिदं कस्य वा स्थानं कथं तदिदमित्यभूत् ॥10.81.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: जुष्टम् inhabited, सु-अलङ्कृतैः well ornamented, पुम्भिः by men, स्त्रीभिः by women, च and, हरिण-अक्षिभिः having doe-like eyes, किम् what, इदम् this, कस्य whose, वा or, स्थानम् place, कथम् how, तत्-इदम् that this, इति thus, अभूत् became

Anvaya: स्वलङ्कृतैः पुम्भिः हरिणाक्षिभिः स्त्रीभिः च जुष्टम्, इदं किं, कस्य वा स्थानं, तत् इदं कथम् अभूत् इति (सः अचिन्तयत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: स्वलङ्कृतैः पुम्भिः By beautifully adorned men, हरिणाक्षिभिः स्त्रीभिः च and doe-eyed women, जुष्टम् filled, इदं किं what is this, कस्य वा स्थानं whose place is this, तत् इदं कथम् अभूत् how did this become like this, इति (सः अचिन्तयत्) thus he thought.

Simple Meaning: Seeing the place populated by beautifully adorned men and doe-eyed women, Sudama thought, "What is this? Whose place is this? How could this place have transformed like this?"

एवं मीमांसमानं तं नरा नार्योऽमरप्रभाः ।
प्रत्यगृह्णन् महाभागं गीतवाद्येन भूयसा ॥10.81.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, मीमांसमानम् deliberating, तम् him, नराः men, नार्यः women, अमर-प्रभाः effulgent like the immortals, प्रत्यगृह्णन् welcomed, महा-भागम् the most fortunate one, गीत-वाद्येन with vocal and instrumental music, भूयसा resounding

भूयसा -  भूयस् सकारान्तः, पुंलिङ्गः (अतिशयेन बहु, प्रचुरम्) 3.1

Anvaya: एवं मीमांसमानं तं महाभागम् अमरप्रभाः नराः नार्यः भूयसा गीतवाद्येन प्रत्यगृह्णन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवं मीमांसमानम् As he was thus deliberating, तं महाभागम् that greatly fortunate brahmana, अमरप्रभाः नराः नार्यः men and women resplendent like the immortals, भूयसा गीतवाद्येन प्रत्यगृह्णन् welcomed him with loud vocal and instrumental music.

Simple Meaning: As the greatly fortunate Sudama was thus pondering, men and women who possessed divine effulgence like the devas came forward to welcome him with loud, festive vocal and instrumental music.

पतिमागतमाकर्ण्य पत्न्युद्-हर्षातिसम्भ्रमा ।
निश्चकाम गृहात्तूर्णं रूपिणी श्रीरिवालयात् ॥10.81.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: पतिम् husband, आगतम् arrived, आकर्ण्य hearing, पत्नी wife, उद्-हर्ष-अति-सम्भ्रमा experiencing intense joy and great excitement, निश्चकाम came out, गृहात् from the house, तूर्णम् quickly, रूपिणी personified, श्रीः Lakshmi Devi, इव as if, आलयात् from her abode

Anvaya: पतिम् आगतम् आकर्ण्य उद्वहर्षातिसम्भ्रमा पत्नी रूपिणी श्रीः आलयात् इव गृहात् तूर्णं निश्चकाम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पतिम् आगतम् आकर्ण्य Hearing her husband arrived, उद्वहर्षातिसम्भ्रमा पत्नी the wife overflowing with intense joy and eagerness, रूपिणी श्रीः आलयात् इव like Lakshmi Devi personified leaving her abode, गृहात् तूर्णं निश्चकाम quickly came out from the house.

Simple Meaning: Hearing that her husband had arrived, his wife, overflowing with joy and excitement, hurried out of the house to meet him, looking just like Lakshmi Devi emerging from her abode.

पतिव्रता पतिं दृष्ट्वा प्रेमोत्कण्ठाश्रुलोचना ।
मीलिताक्ष्यनमद्बुद्ध्या मनसा परिषस्वजे ॥10.81.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: पति-व्रता the chaste, devoted wife, पतिम् her husband, दृष्ट्वा seeing, प्रेम-उत्कण्ठा-अश्रु-लोचना her eyes filled with tears of love and longing, मीलित-अक्षी with closed eyes, अनमत् bowed down, बुद्ध्या with her intelligence, मनसा in her mind, परिषस्वजे embraced him

Anvaya: पतिव्रता पतिं दृष्ट्वा प्रेमोत्कण्ठाश्रुलोचना मीलिताक्षी (सती) बुद्ध्या अनमत्, मनसा परिषस्वजे (च)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पतिव्रता The devoted wife, पतिं दृष्ट्वा seeing her husband, प्रेमोत्कण्ठाश्रुलोचना with eyes full of tears born of love and longing, मीलिताक्षी with eyes closed, बुद्ध्या अनमत् bowed down in her mind, मनसा परिषस्वजे and embraced him in her heart.

Simple Meaning: Upon seeing her husband, the devoted wife’s eyes filled with tears of love and longing. Closing her eyes, she bowed down to him with reverence in her mind and mentally embraced him.

पत्नीं वीक्ष्य विस्फुरन्तीं देवीं वैमानिकीमिव ।
दासीनां निष्ककण्ठीनां मध्ये भान्तीं स विस्मितः ॥10.81.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: पत्नीम् wife, वीक्ष्य seeing, विस्फुरन्तीम् shining brightly, देवीम् a lady, वैमानिकीम् heavenly, इव like, दासीनाम् of maidservants, निष्क-कण्ठीनाम् wearing golden neck ornaments, मध्ये in the midst, भान्तीम् glowing, सः he, विस्मितः was astonished

Simple Meaning: Seeing the extraordinary grandeur of all kinds of wealth present there, the unperturbed brahmana Sudama began to reflect upon his own unexpected prosperity.

नूनं बतैतन्मम दुर्भगस्य शश्वद्दरिद्रस्य समृद्धिहेतुः ।
महाविभूतेरवलोकतोऽन्यो नैवोपपद्येत यदूत्तमस्य ॥10.81.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: नूनम् certainly, बत indeed, एतत् this, मम my, दुर्भगस्य of the unfortunate one, शश्वत्-दरिद्रस्य of the perpetually poor, समृद्धि-हेतुः cause of prosperity, महा-विभूतेः of the possessor of great opulence, अवलोकतः from the glance, अन्यः other, न not, एव indeed, उपपद्येत can be accounted for, यदु-उत्तमस्य of the best of the Yadus

Anvaya: बत मम दुर्भगस्य शश्वद्दरिद्रस्य एतत् समृद्धिहेतुः महाविभूतेः यदूत्तमस्य अवलोकतः अन्यः न एव उपपद्येत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बत मम दुर्भगस्य शश्वद्दरिद्रस्य Indeed for me who am unfortunate and perpetually poor, एतत् समृद्धिहेतुः the cause of this prosperity, महाविभूतेः यदूत्तमस्य अवलोकतः अन्यः other than the glance of the best of Yadus who possesses great opulence, न एव उपपद्येत cannot be accounted for.

Simple Meaning: Indeed for an unfortunate and perpetually poor person like me, this sudden prosperity cannot be attributed to anything other than the merciful glance of Lord Krishna, the supreme master of all opulence and the best of the Yadus.

नन्वब्रुवाणो दिशते समक्षं याचिष्ण्वे भूर्यपि भूरिभोजः ।
पर्जन्यवत्तत् स्वयमीक्षमाणो दाशार्हकाणामृषभः सखा मे ॥10.81.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: ननु surely, अब्रुवाणः without speaking, दिशते gives, समक्षम् directly, याचिष्णवे to the beggar, भूरि extensive wealth, अपि even, भूरि-भोजः enjoyer of abundant wealth, पर्जन्य-वत् like a rain cloud, तत् that, स्वयम् himself, ईक्षमाणः considering, दाशार्हकाणाम् of the Dasharhas, वृषभः the chief, सखा friend, मे my

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भूरिभोजः The enjoyer of abundant wealth, दाशार्हकाणां वृषभः the chief of the Dasharhas, मे सखा my friend, पर्जन्यवत् स्वयम् ईक्षमाणः like a rain cloud Himself watching, अब्रुवाणः without speaking, समक्षं याचिष्णवे भूरि अपि दिशते gives vast abundance directly to a beggar.

Simple Meaning: My friend Krishna, the noble leader of the Dasharha clan and the enjoyer of abundant wealth, bestows vast abundance directly upon a beggar like a rain cloud showering water on its own accord, without even declaring what he has given.

किञ्चित्करोत्युर्वपि यत् स्वदत्तं सुहृत्कृतं फल्ग्वपि भूरिकारी ।
मयोपनीतं पृथुकैकमुष्टिं प्रत्यग्रहीत् प्रीतियुतो महात्मा ॥10.81.35॥

Word-by-word meaning: किञ्चित् minimal, करोति makes, उरु great, अपि even, यत् which, स्व-दत्तम् given by Himself, सुहृत्-कृतम् done for a friend, फल्गु insignificant, अपि even, भूरि-कारी making it huge, मया by me, उपनीतम् brought, पृथुक-एक-मुष्टिम् a single handful of flattened rice, प्रत्यग्रहीत् accepted, प्रीति-युतः with great affection, महा-आत्मा the noble soul

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भूरिकारी महात्मा The noble soul who bestows great gifts, स्वदत्तम् उरु अपि किञ्चित् करोति considers even the vast things given by Himself to be minimal, फल्गु सुहृत्कृतम् अपि भूरि करोति while making even an insignificant deed done by a friend seem huge. मया उपनीतं पृथुकैकमुष्टिं A single handful of flattened rice offered by me, प्रीतियुतः प्रत्यग्रहीत् he accepted with great affection.

Simple Meaning: That noble Lord Krishna, who bestows immense treasures, considers even His own large gifts to be insignificant, yet He magnifies even a tiny offering made by a friend. Thus He accepted with great affection a handful of flattened rice brought by me.

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विषज्जतः गुणालयेन महानुभावेन तस्य एव मे For me who am attached to that exalted Lord who is the reservoir of all virtues, जन्मनि जन्मनि पुनः सौहृदसख्यमैत्री दास्यं may there be life after life affectionate companionship and servitude to him, तत्पुरुषप्रसङ्गः च स्यात् and association with his saintly devotees.

Simple Meaning: May I develop deep affection, friendship, and eternal servitude life after life toward Lord Krishna, who is the abode of all divine qualities and exalted glory, and may I always be blessed with the association of his saintly devotees.

भक्ताय चित्रा भगवान् हि सम्पदो राज्यं विभूतीर्न समर्थयत्यजः ।

Simple Meaning: The wise and unborn Lord Krishna, observing how wealth breeds pride and causes the downfall of men, does not usually grant kingdoms, immense wealth, or great power to a devotee who lacks spiritual understanding.

Simple Meaning: Firmly determined in this way with his reasoning and deeply devoted to Lord Janardana, Sudama enjoyed the opulences of material life together with his wife without being overly attached, always prepared to renounce them.

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तस्य देवदेवस्य यज्ञपतेः प्रभोः हरेः For that Supreme Master Lord Hari who is the Lord of lords and the lord of sacrifice, ब्राह्मणाः वै प्रभवः दैवम् (च भवन्ति) brahmanas are indeed worshipable. तेभ्यः परं न विद्यते Higher than them there is none.

Simple Meaning: For Lord Hari, the supreme master and Lord of lords who is the ultimate deity of all sacrifices, the brahmanas are indeed worshipable. Indeed, there is no entity superior to the brahmanas in His eyes.

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this way, सः he, विप्रः the learned brahmana, भगवत्-सुहृत् the friend of the Supreme Lord, तदा then, दृष्ट्वा seeing, स्व-भृत्यैः by his own devotees, अजितम् the unconquerable Lord, पराजितम् conquered, तत्-ध्यान-वेग-उद्ग्रथित-आत्म-बन्धनः who cut all worldly bonds by the intensity of meditation upon him, तत्-धाम the supreme abode of the Lord, लेभे attained, अचिरतः shortly, सताम् गतिम् the destination of saintly souls

Anvaya: तदा एवं भगवत्सुहृत् सः विप्रः स्वभृत्यैः पराजितम् अजितं दृष्ट्वा तद्ध्यानवेगोद्ग्रथितात्मबन्धनः अचिरतः सतां गतिं तद्धाम लेभे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तदा एवं भगवत्सुहृत् सः विप्रः Then in this way that learned brahmana who was the friend of the Supreme Lord, स्वभृत्यैः पराजितम् अजितं दृष्ट्वा seeing the unconquerable Lord conquered by his own devotees, तद्ध्यानवेगोद्ग्रथितात्मबन्धनः cutting the ties of his worldly bondage through intense meditation upon him, अचिरतः सतां गतिं तद्धाम लेभे shortly attained the supreme abode of the Lord which is the destination of saintly souls.

Simple Meaning: Thus seeing how the unconquerable Supreme Lord is conquered by the love of his own devotees, Sudama, the dear friend of the Lord, dissolved all material attachments through constant absorption in Krishna. Soon thereafter, he attained the eternal abode of the Lord, which is the ultimate destination of all saintly souls.

एतद् ब्रह्मण्यदेवस्य श्रुत्वा ब्रह्मण्यतां नरः ।
लब्धभावो भगवति कर्मबन्धाद् विमुच्यते ॥10.81.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतत् this, ब्रह्मण्य-देवस्य of the lord of brahmanas, श्रुत्वा hearing, ब्रह्मण्यताम् devotion to brahmanas, नरः a person, लब्ध-भावः attaining pure love, भगवति unto the Supreme Lord, कर्म-बन्धात् from the bondage of karma, विमुच्यते is completely freed

Anvaya: ब्रह्मण्यदेवस्य एतां ब्रह्मण्यतां श्रुत्वा भगवति लब्धभावः नरः कर्मबन्धात् विमुच्यते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्रह्मण्यदेवस्य एतां ब्रह्मण्यतां श्रुत्वा Hearing this devotion of the Lord of brahmanas toward the brahmanas, भगवति लब्धभावः नरः a person attaining loving devotion unto the Supreme Lord, कर्मबन्धात् विमुच्यते is freed from the bondage of karma.

Simple Meaning: Any person who hears this narration of Lord Krishna's deep devotion toward the brahmanas develops pure loving devotion for the Supreme Lord and is liberated from the bondage of karmic actions.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “पृथुकोपाख्यानं कृष्णानुग्रहः च​ नाम​” एकाशीतितमः अध्यायः ॥81॥

Thus ends the eighty-first discourse named “The story of flattened rice taken by Sudama as a present for Krishna. Krishna blesses Sudama”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
