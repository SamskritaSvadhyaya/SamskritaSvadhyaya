Chapter 56

The story of the Syamantaka gem; Krishna weds Jambavati and Satyabhama

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

स्यमन्तकमण्युपाख्यानं कृष्णस्य जाम्बवतीसत्यभामापरिणयश्च नाम​ षट्पञ्चाशत्तमः अध्यायः

(45 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
सत्राजितः स्वतनयां कृष्णाय कृतकिल्बिषः ।
स्यमन्तकेन मणिना स्वयमुद्यम्य दत्तवान् ॥10.56.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, सत्राजितः Satrajit, स्व-तनयाम् his own daughter, कृष्णाय to Krishna, कृत-किल्बिषः having committed an offense, स्यमन्तकेन मणिना along with the Syamantaka jewel, स्वयम् himself, उद्यम्य coming forward, दत्तवान् gave

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच – कृतकिल्बिषः सत्राजितः स्वतनयां स्यमन्तकेन मणिना स्वयम् उद्यम्य कृष्णाय दत्तवान्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, कृतकिल्बिषः सत्राजितः Satrajit having committed an offense, स्वतनयां स्यमन्तकेन मणिना his own daughter along with the Syamantaka jewel, स्वयम् उद्यम्य coming forward himself, कृष्णाय दत्तवान् gave to Krishna.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Having committed an offense against Lord Krishna, Satrajit stepped forward on his own accord to present the Lord with his daughter along with the Syamantaka jewel.

राजा उवाच
सत्राजितः किमकरोद् ब्रह्मन् कृष्णस्य किल्बिषम् ।
स्यमन्तकः कुतस्तस्य कस्माद् दत्ता सुता हरेः ॥10.56.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजा उवाच the King said, सत्राजितः Satrajit, किम् अकरोत् what did he do, ब्रह्मन् O brahmana, कृष्णस्य to Krishna, किल्बिषम् offense, स्यमन्तकः the Syamantaka jewel, कुतः from where, तस्य his, कस्मात् why, दत्ता was given, सुता the daughter, हरेः to Hari

Anvaya: राजा उवाच — ब्रह्मन्! सत्राजितः कृष्णस्य किं किल्बिषम् अकरोत्? तस्य स्यमन्तकः कुतः? कस्मात् सुता हरेः दत्ता?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजा उवाच The King said, ब्रह्मन्! सत्राजितः कृष्णस्य किं किल्बिषम् अकरोत् O brahmana! what offense did Satrajit commit against Krishna? तस्य स्यमन्तकः कुतः From where did he get the Syamantaka jewel? कस्मात् सुता हरेः दत्ता Why was his daughter given to Hari?

Simple Meaning: King Parikshit asked: O brahmana, what offense did Satrajit commit against Krishna? Where did he obtain the Syamantaka jewel, and why did he decide to give his daughter in marriage to Hari?

श्री शुक उवाच
आसीत् सत्राजितः सूर्यो भक्तस्य परमः सखा ।
प्रीतस्तस्मै मणिं प्रादात् सूर्यस्तुष्टः स्यमन्तकम् ॥10.56.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, आसीत् there was, सत्राजितः of Satrajit, सूर्यः the sun-god, भक्तस्य of his devotee, परमः a supreme, सखा friend, प्रीतः pleased, तस्मै to him, मणिम् the jewel, प्रादात् gave, सूर्यः the sun-god, तुष्टः satisfied, स्यमन्तकम् Syamantaka

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच — सूर्यः भक्तस्य सत्राजितः परमः सखा आसीत्। तुष्टः प्रीतः सूर्यः तस्मै स्यमन्तकं मणिं प्रादात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, सूर्यः भक्तस्य सत्राजितः परमः सखा आसीत् The sun-god was a supreme friend of his devotee Satrajit. तुष्टः प्रीतः सूर्यः तस्मै स्यमन्तकं मणिं प्रादात् The satisfied and pleased sun-god gave the Syamantaka jewel to him.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: The sun-god was a great friend to his devoted follower, Satrajit. Being thoroughly pleased and satisfied with Satrajit's devotion, the sun-god presented him with the Syamantaka jewel.

स तं बिभ्रन् मणिं कण्ठे भ्राजमानो यथा रविः ।
प्रविष्टो द्वारकां राजंस्तेजसा नोपलक्षितः ॥10.56.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, तम् that, बिभ्रन् wearing, मणिम् jewel, कण्ठे on his neck, भ्राजमानः shining, यथा as, रविः the sun, प्रविष्टः entered, द्वारकाम् Dwaraka, राजन् O King, तेजसा by the brilliance, न उपलक्षितः could not be recognized

Anvaya: राजन्! सः कण्ठे तं मणिं बिभ्रन् रविः यथा भ्राजमानः द्वारकां प्रविष्टः। तेजसा न उपलक्षितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन्! O King, सः कण्ठे तं मणिं बिभ्रन् He wearing that jewel on his neck, रविः यथा भ्राजमानः shining like the sun, द्वारकां प्रविष्टः he entered Dwaraka. तेजसा न उपलक्षितः He could not be recognized because of the brilliance.

Simple Meaning: O King, when Satrajit entered the city of Dwaraka wearing the jewel on his neck, he shone as brightly as the sun-god himself. Because of his dazzling brilliance, the residents of the city could not identify who he actually was.

तं विलोक्य जना दूरात्तेजसा मुष्टदृष्टयः ।
दीव्यतेऽक्षैर्भगवते शशंसुः सूर्यशङ्किताः ॥10.56.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him, विलोक्य seeing, जनाः the people, दूरात् from a distance, तेजसा by the brilliance, मुष्ट-दृष्टयः blinded in their vision, दीव्यते who was playing, अक्षैः with dice, भगवते to the Supreme Lord, शशंसुः they reported, सूर्य-शङ्किताः mistaking him for the sun-god

Anvaya: दूरात् तं विलोक्य तेजसा मुष्टदृष्टयः सूर्यशङ्किताः जनाः अक्षैः दीव्यते भगवते शशंसुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दूरात् तं विलोक्य Seeing him from a distance, तेजसा मुष्टदृष्टयः सूर्यशङ्किताः जनाः the people whose vision was blinded by the brilliance and mistaking him for the sun-god, अक्षैः दीव्यते भगवते शशंसुः reported to the Supreme Lord who was playing with dice.

Simple Meaning: Seeing Satrajit approach from a distance, the residents of Dwaraka were completely blinded by his dazzling effulgence. Mistaking him for the sun-god himself, they went to Lord Krishna, who was playing a game of dice at the time, and reported the arrival.

नारायण नमस्तेऽस्तु शङ्खचक्रगदाधर ।
दामोदरारविन्दाक्ष गोविन्द यदुनन्दन ॥10.56.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: नारायण O Narayana, नमः obeisances, ते to You, अस्तु let there be, शङ्ख-चक्र-गदा-धर O holder of the conchshell disc and club, दामोदर O Damodara, अरविन्द-अक्ष O lotus eyed one, गोविन्द O Govinda, यदु-नन्दन O beloved descendant of Yadu

Anvaya: नारायण! शङ्खचक्रगदाधर! दामोदर! अरविन्दाक्ष! गोविन्द! यदुनन्दन! ते नमः अस्तु।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नारायण! O Narayana, शङ्खचक्रगदाधर! O holder of the conch shell disc and club, दामोदर! O Damodara, अरविन्दाक्ष! O lotus eyed one, गोविन्द! O Govinda,  यदुनन्दन! O beloved descendant of Yadu, ते नमः अस्तु let there be obeisances to You.

Simple Meaning: O Narayana, O holder of the conch, disc, and club! O Damodara, lotus eyed Lord Govinda, the joy of the Yadu dynasty! We offer our respectful obeisances unto You.

एष आयाति सविता त्वां दिदृक्षुर्जगत्पते ।
मुष्णन् गभस्तिचक्रेण नृणां चक्षूंषि तिग्मगुः ॥10.56.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: एषः this, आयाति is coming, सविता the sun-god, त्वाम् You, दिदृक्षुः desiring to see, जगत्-पते O Lord of the universe, मुष्णन् blinding, गभस्ति-चक्रेण with the wheel of his rays, नृणाम् of men, चक्षूंषि the eyes, तिग्म-गुः of fiery rays

Anvaya: जगत्पते! एषः तिग्मगुः सविता गभस्तिचक्रेण नृणां चक्षूंषि मुष्णन् त्वां दिदृक्षुः आयाति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जगत्पते! O Lord of the universe!, एषः तिग्मगुः सविता This sun-god of fiery rays, गभस्तिचक्रेण नृणां चक्षूंषि मुष्णन् blinding the eyes of men with the wheel of his rays, त्वां दिदृक्षुः आयाति is coming desiring to see You.

Simple Meaning: O Lord of the universe! This must be the sun-god himself approaching with his fiery rays. He is completely blinding the eyes of everyone with his dazzling effulgence, and he is clearly coming here out of a desire to see You.

नन्वन्विच्छन्ति ते मार्गं त्रिलोक्यां विबुधर्षभाः ।
ज्ञात्वाद्य गूढं यदुषु द्रष्टुं त्वां यात्यजः प्रभो ॥10.56.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: ननु certainly, अन्विच्छन्ति search for, ते Your, मार्गम् path, त्रि-लोक्याम् within the three worlds, विबुध-ऋषभाः the foremost of the demigods, ज्ञात्वा having known, अद्य today, गूढम् hidden, यदुषु among the Yadus, द्रष्टुम् to see, त्वाम् You, याति comes, अजः the unborn sun-god, प्रभो O Lord

Anvaya: प्रभो! विबुधर्षभाः त्रिलोक्यां ते मार्गम् अन्विच्छन्ति ननु। अद्य यदुषु गूढं त्वां ज्ञात्वा अजः (त्वां) द्रष्टुं याति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रभो! O Lord!, विबुधर्षभाः The foremost of the demigods, त्रिलोक्यां within the three worlds, ते मार्गम् अन्विच्छन्ति ननु certainly search for Your path. अद्य यदुषु गूढं त्वां ज्ञात्वा Today having known You are hidden among the Yadus, अजः (त्वां) द्रष्टुं याति the unborn sun-god comes to see You.

Simple Meaning: O Lord, the greatest of the demigods are constantly searching throughout the three worlds to find Your path. Having discovered that You are currently living incognito among the members of the Yadu dynasty, the unborn sun-god has come here today to see You.

श्री शुक उवाच
निशम्य बालवचनं प्रहस्याम्बुजलोचनः ।
प्राह नासौ रविर्देवः सत्राजिन्मणिना ज्वलन् ॥10.56.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, निशम्य having heard, बाल-वचनम् the words of the innocent people, प्रहस्य laughing, अम्बुज-लोचनः the lotus eyed Lord, प्राह said, न not, असौ this, रविः देवः the sun-god, सत्राजित् Satrajit, मणिना by the jewel, ज्वलन् glowing

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच — अम्बुजलोचनः बालवचनं निशम्य प्रहस्य प्राह – असौ रविः देवः न। मणिना ज्वलन् सत्राजित्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अम्बुजलोचनः बालवचनं निशम्य प्रहस्य प्राह the lotus eyed Lord having heard the words of the innocent people laughing said, असौ रविः देवः न This is not the sun-god. मणिना ज्वलन् सत्राजित् But Satrajit glowing by his jewel.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Hearing the innocent words of the residents of Dwaraka, the lotus-eyed Lord Krishna laughed and told them, "This is not the sun-god. It is actually Satrajit, who is glowing brilliantly because of his jewel."

सत्राजित् स्वगृहं श्रीमत् कृतकौतुकमङ्गलम् ।
प्रविश्य देवसदने मणिं विप्रैर्न्यवेशयत् ॥10.56.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: सत्राजित् Satrajit, स्व-गृहम् his own home, श्रीमत् opulent, कृत-कौतुक-मङ्गलम् where auspicious rituals had been performed, प्रविश्य having entered, देव-सदने in the temple room, मणिम् the jewel, विप्रैः with the help of brahmanas, न्यवेशयत् installed

Anvaya: सत्राजित् कृतकौतुकमङ्गलं श्रीमत् स्वगृहं प्रविश्य देवसदने विप्रैः मणिं न्यवेशयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सत्राजित् कृतकौतुकमङ्गलं श्रीमत् स्वगृहं प्रविश्य Satrajit having entered his own opulent home where auspicious rituals had been performed, देवसदने विप्रैः मणिं न्यवेशयत् installed the jewel in the temple room with the help of brahmanas.

Simple Meaning: Satrajit entered his opulent home, where celebratory and auspicious rituals had been performed. Then, with the assistance of learned brahmanas, he carefully installed the jewel inside the family temple room.

दिने दिने स्वर्णभारानष्टौ स सृजति प्रभो ।
दुर्भिक्षमार्यरिष्टानि सर्पाधिव्याधयोऽशुभाः ।
न सन्ति मायिनस्तत्र यत्रास्तेऽभ्यर्चितो मणिः ॥10.56.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: दिने दिने day after day, स्वर्ण-भारान् bhara measures of gold, अष्टौ eight, सः it, सृजति produces, प्रभो O Lord (Parikshit), दुर्भिक्षम् famine, मारि pestilence, अरिष्टानि misfortunes, सर्प-आधि-व्याधयः snakes, mental anxiety and physical disease, अशुभाः inauspicious signs, न not, सन्ति exist, मायिनः deceitful persons, तत्र there, यत्र where, आस्ते stays, अभ्यर्चितः properly worshiped, मणिः the jewel

Anvaya: प्रभो! सः दिने दिने अष्टौ स्वर्णभारान् सृजति। यत्र अभ्यर्चितः मणिः आस्ते तत्र दुर्भिक्षं मारि अरिष्टानि सर्पाधिव्याधयः अशुभाः मायिनः च न सन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रभो! O Lord (Parikshit), सः दिने दिने अष्टौ स्वर्णभारान् सृजति it produces eight bhara measures of gold day after day, यत्र अभ्यर्चितः मणिः आस्ते तत्र दुर्भिक्षं मारि अरिष्टानि सर्पाधिव्याधयः अशुभाः मायिनः च न सन्ति where the properly worshiped jewel stays there famine, pestilence, misfortunes, snakes, mental anxiety, physical disease, inauspicious signs and deceitful persons do not exist.

Simple Meaning: O Lord Parikshit! Day after day that jewel produces eight bhara measures of gold. Furthermore, wherever that jewel is kept and properly worshiped, there can be no famine, pestilence, misfortune, snake bites, mental anxiety, physical disease, inauspicious omens, or deceitful persons.

स याचितो मणिं क्वापि यदुराजाय शौरिणा ।
नैवार्थकामुकः प्रादाद् याच्ञाभङ्गमतर्कयन् ॥10.56.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, याचितः being requested, मणिम् the jewel, क्वापि at one time, यदु-राजाय for the king of the Yadus, शौरिणा by Krishna, न not, एव certainly, अर्थ-कामुकः greedy for wealth, प्रादात् gave, याच्ञा-भङ्गम् the offence of refusing a request, अतर्कयन् without considering

Anvaya: शौरिणा क्वापि यदुराजाय सः मणिं याचितः। अर्थकामुकः याच्ञाभङ्गम् अतर्कयन् न एव प्रादात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शौरिणा क्वापि यदुराजाय सः मणिं याचितः When Krishna at one time requested him for the jewel on behalf of the king of the Yadus. अर्थकामुकः याच्ञाभङ्गम् अतर्कयन् न एव प्रादात् Being greedy for wealth and without considering the offence of refusing a request, he certainly did not give it.

Simple Meaning: When Krishna at one time requested Satrajit to give the jewel to Ugrasena, the king of the Yadus, Satrajit refused. Blinded by greed for wealth, he failed to consider the consequences of denying a request made by the Lord.

तमेकदा मणिं कण्ठे प्रतिमुच्य महाप्रभम् ।
प्रसेनो हयमारुह्य मृगयां व्यचरद् वने ॥10.56.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् that, एकदा once, मणिम् the jewel, कण्ठे on his neck, प्रतिमुच्य fastening, महा-प्रभम् brilliantly shining, प्रसेनः Prasena, हयम् a horse, आरुह्य mounting, मृगयाम् hunting, व्यचरत् roamed, वने in the forest

Anvaya: एकदा प्रसेनः महाप्रभं तं मणिं कण्ठे प्रतिमुच्य हयम् आरुह्य वने मृगयां व्यचरत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एकदा प्रसेनः महाप्रभं तं मणिं कण्ठे प्रतिमुच्य Once Prasena fastening that brilliantly shining jewel on his neck, हयम् आरुह्य वने मृगयां व्यचरत् mounting a horse roamed in the forest for hunting.

Simple Meaning: Once, Satrajit's brother Prasena fastened that brilliantly shining jewel around his neck, mounted his horse, and went hunting in the forest.

प्रसेनं सहयं हत्वा मणिमाच्छिद्य केसरी ।
गिरिं विशञ्जाम्बवता निहतो मणिमिच्छता ॥10.56.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रसेनम् Prasena, स-हयम् along with his horse, हत्वा having killed, मणिम् the jewel, आच्छिद्य snatching away, केसरी a lion, गिरिम् the mountain, विशन् entering, जाम्बवता by Jambavan, निहतः was killed, मणिम् the jewel, इच्छता desiring

Anvaya: केसरी सहयं प्रसेनं हत्वा मणिम् आच्छिद्य गिरिं विशन् मणिम् इच्छता जाम्बवता निहतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: केसरी सहयं प्रसेनं हत्वा A lion having killed Prasena along with his horse, मणिम् आच्छिद्य गिरिं विशन् snatching away the jewel while entering the mountain, मणिम् इच्छता जाम्बवता निहतः was killed by Jambavan who desired the jewel.

Simple Meaning: A lion killed Prasena and his horse and took the jewel. As the lion was entering a mountain cave with the jewel, Jambavan, who desired the jewel, killed the lion.

सोऽपि चक्रे कुमारस्य मणिं क्रीडनकं बिले ।
अपश्यन् भ्रातरं भ्राता सत्राजित् पर्यतप्यत ॥10.56.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Jambavan), अपि also, चक्रे made, कुमारस्य of his child, मणिम् the jewel, क्रीडनकम् a toy, बिले in the cave, अपश्यन् not seeing, भ्रातरम् his brother, भ्राता the brother, सत्राजित् Satrajit, पर्यतप्यत lamented

Anvaya: सः अपि बिले कुमारस्य मणिं क्रीडनकं चक्रे। भ्रातरम् अपश्यन् भ्राता सत्राजित् पर्यतप्यत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः अपि बिले कुमारस्य मणिं क्रीडनकं चक्रे He also made the jewel a toy for his child in the cave. भ्रातरम् अपश्यन् भ्राता सत्राजित् पर्यतप्यत Not seeing his brother, the brother Satrajit lamented.

Simple Meaning: Jambavan then took the jewel into his cave and gave it to his young child to play with as a toy. Meanwhile, back in the city, Satrajit was deeply distressed and lamented because his brother Prasena had not returned.

प्रायः कृष्णेन निहतो मणिग्रीवो वनं गतः ।
भ्राता ममेति तच्छ्रुत्वा कर्णे कर्णेऽजपञ्जनाः ॥10.56.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रायः probably, कृष्णेन by Krishna, निहतः killed, मणि-ग्रीवः wearing the jewel on his neck, वनम् to the forest, गतः went, भ्राता brother, मम my, इति thus, तत् that, श्रुत्वा hearing, कर्णे कर्णे in every ear, अजपन् whispered, जनाः the people

Anvaya: मणिग्रीवः वनं गतः मम भ्राता प्रायः कृष्णेन निहतः इति (सत्राजित् अवदत्)। तत् श्रुत्वा जनाः कर्णे कर्णे अजपन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मणिग्रीवः वनं गतः Wearing the jewel on his neck who went to the forest, मम भ्राता my brother, प्रायः कृष्णेन निहतः was probably killed by Krishna, इति thus, तत् श्रुत्वा hearing that, जनाः कर्णे कर्णे अजपन् the people whispered from ear to ear.

Simple Meaning: Satrajit said, "My brother went into the forest wearing the jewel on his neck, and he has probably been killed by Krishna." Hearing this rumor, people in the city began whispering the gossip from ear to ear.

भगवांस्तदुपश्रुत्य दुर्यशो लिप्तमात्मनि ।
मार्ष्टुं प्रसेनपदवीमन्वपद्यत नागरैः ॥10.56.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: भगवान् the Supreme Lord, तत् that, उपश्रुत्य hearing, दुर्यशः infamy, लिप्तम् smeared, आत्मनि on Himself, मार्ष्टुम् to wipe out, प्रसेन-पदवीम् the path taken by Prasena, अन्वपद्यत followed, नागरैः along with the citizens

Anvaya: भगवान् आत्मनि लिप्तं तत् दुर्यशः मार्ष्टुं नागरैः सह प्रसेनपदवीम् अन्वपद्यत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवान् The Supreme Lord Krishna, आत्मनि लिप्तं तत् दुर्यशः that infamy smeared on Himself, मार्ष्टुं to wipe it out, नागरैः सह along with the citizens, प्रसेनपदवीम् अन्वपद्यत followed the path taken by Prasena

Simple Meaning: When the Supreme Lord Krishna heard about this defamatory rumor circulating against Him, He set out into the forest along with the citizens to follow the path Prasena had taken, in order to clear His name.

हतं प्रसेनमश्वं च वीक्ष्य केसरिणा वने ।
तं चाद्रिपृष्ठे निहतमृक्षेण ददृशुर्जनाः ॥10.56.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: हतम् killed, प्रसेनम् Prasena, अश्वम् horse, च and, वीक्ष्य seeing, केसरिणा by a lion, वने in the forest, तम् that (lion), च and, अद्रि-पृष्ठे on the mountain side, निहतम् killed, ऋक्षेण by a bear, ददृशुः saw, जनाः the people

Anvaya: जनाः वने केसरिणा हतं प्रसेनम् अश्वं च वीक्ष्य अद्रिपृष्ठे ऋक्षेण निहतं तं च ददृशुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जनाः वने केसरिणा हतं प्रसेनम् अश्वं च वीक्ष्य The people seeing Prasena and his horse killed by a lion in the forest, अद्रिपृष्ठे ऋक्षेण निहतं तं च ददृशुः saw that lion also killed by a bear on the mountain side.

Simple Meaning: Deep in the forest, the citizens discovered the bodies of Prasena and his horse, both killed by a lion. Following the trail further up the mountain side, they also found the body of that very lion, which had been killed by a bear.

ऋक्षराजबिलं भीममन्धेन तमसाऽऽवृतम् ।
एको विवेश भगवानवस्थाप्य बहिः प्रजाः ॥10.56.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऋक्ष-राज-बिलम् the cave of the king of the bears, भीमम् terrifying, अन्धेन blinding, तमसा by darkness, आवृतम् covered, एकः alone, विवेश entered, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, अवस्थाप्य leaving behind, बहिः outside, प्रजाः the citizens

Anvaya: भगवान् भीमम् अन्धेन तमसा आवृतं ऋक्षराजबिलं बहिः प्रजाः अवस्थाप्य एकः विवेश।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवान् The Supreme Lord, भीमम् अन्धेन तमसा आवृतं ऋक्षराजबिलं the terrifying cave of the king of the bears which was covered by blinding darkness, बहिः प्रजाः अवस्थाप्य leaving the citizens behind outside, एकः विवेश entered alone.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord Krishna left the citizens waiting outside and entered the terrifying cave of Jambavan, the king of the bears, entirely alone. The vast cave was completely enveloped in blinding darkness.

तत्र दृष्ट्वा मणिश्रेष्ठं बालक्रीडनकं कृतम् ।
हर्तुं कृतमतिस्तस्मिन्नवतस्थेऽर्भकान्तिके ॥10.56.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there, दृष्ट्वा seeing, मणि-श्रेष्ठम् the best of jewels, बाल-क्रीडनकम् a child's toy, कृतम् made, हर्तुम् to take away, कृत-मतिः having resolved, तस्मिन् there, अवतस्थे stood, अर्भक-अन्तिके near the child

Anvaya: तत्र मणिश्रेष्ठं बालक्रीडनकं कृतं दृष्ट्वा (भगवान्) हर्तुं कृतमतिः (सन्) तस्मिन् अर्भकान्तिके अवतस्थे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत्र मणिश्रेष्ठं बालक्रीडनकं कृतं दृष्ट्वा (भगवान्) Seeing there the best of jewels made into a child’s toy, हर्तुं कृतमतिः (सन्) (the Lord) having resolved to take it away, तस्मिन् अर्भकान्तिके अवतस्थे stood there near the child.

Simple Meaning: Inside the cave, Krishna saw that the magnificent jewel had been turned into a toy for a child. Determined to take it back to clear His name, He approached and stood beside the child.

तमपूर्वं नरं दृष्ट्वा धात्री चुक्रोश भीतवत् ।
तच्छ्रुत्वाभ्यद्रवत् क्रुद्धो जाम्बवान् बलिनां वरः ॥10.56.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him, अपूर्वम् unprecedented, नरम् man, दृष्ट्वा seeing, धात्री the nurse, चुक्रोश screamed, भीत-वत् out of fear, तत् that, श्रुत्वा hearing, अभ्यद्रवत् rushed forward, क्रुद्धः angered, जाम्बवान् Jambavan, बलिनाम् of the strong, वरः the foremost

Anvaya: तम् अपूर्वं नरं दृष्ट्वा धात्री भीतवत् चुक्रोश। तत् श्रुत्वा बलिनां वरः जाम्बवान् क्रुद्धः अभ्यद्रवत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तम् अपूर्वं नरं दृष्ट्वा Seeing that unprecedented man, धात्री भीतवत् चुक्रोश the nurse screamed out of fear. तत् श्रुत्वा Hearing that, बलिनां वरः जाम्बवान् the foremost of the strong Jambavan, क्रुद्धः अभ्यद्रवत् rushed forward angered.

Simple Meaning: Seeing an unfamiliar man standing there, the child's nurse screamed in fear. Hearing her cries, Jambavan, who was the strongest of the strong, became furious and rushed forward to attack.

स वै भगवता तेन युयुधे स्वामिनाऽऽत्मनः ।
पुरुषं प्राकृतं मत्वा कुपितो नानुभाववित् ॥10.56.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Jambavan), वै indeed, भगवता with the Supreme Lord, तेन with Him, युयुधे fought, स्वामिना with his master, आत्मनः of his own, पुरुषम् a man, प्राकृतम् ordinary, मत्वा thinking, कुपितः angered, न not, अनुभाव-वित् knowing His true power

Anvaya: सः वै आत्मनः स्वामिना तेन भगवता सह पुरुषं प्राकृतं मत्वा कुपितः (सन्) युयुधे, अनुभाववित् न (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः वै आत्मनः स्वामिना तेन भगवता सह He indeed fought with that Supreme Lord who was his own master, पुरुषं प्राकृतं मत्वा कुपितः (सन्) युयुधे thinking Him to be an ordinary man and being angered, अनुभाववित् न (आसीत्) he did not know His true power.

Simple Meaning: Angered and unaware of Krishna's divine majesty, Jambavan mistook Him for an ordinary human being. Thus, he fiercely fought against the Supreme Lord, who was actually his very own worshipable master from his previous life.

द्वन्द्वयुद्धं सुतुमुलमुभयोर्विजिगीषतोः ।
आयुधाश्मद्रुमैर्दोर्भिः क्रव्यार्थे श्येनयोरिव ॥10.56.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्वन्द्व-युद्धम् a duel, सु-तुमुलम् very fierce, उभयोः of both, विजिगीषतोः desiring victory, आयुध-अश्म-द्रुमैः with weapons, stones and trees, दोर्भिः with bare arms, क्रव्य-अर्थे for the sake of flesh, श्येनयोः of two hawks, इव like

Anvaya: विजिगीषतोः उभयोः आयुधाश्मद्रुमैः दोर्भिः च क्रव्यार्थे श्येनयोः इव सुतुमुलं द्वन्द्वयुद्धम् (अभवत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विजिगीषतोः उभयोः Of both who were desiring victory, आयुधाश्मद्रुमैः दोर्भिः च with weapons, stones, trees and bare arms, क्रव्यार्थे श्येनयोः इव like two hawks for the sake of flesh, सुतुमुलं द्वन्द्वयुद्धम् (अभवत्) a very fierce duel took place.

Simple Meaning: Desiring victory, the two engaged in a fierce duel. They fought using weapons, stones, trees, and finally their bare arms, resembling two powerful hawks fighting savagely over a piece of flesh.

आसीत्तदष्टाविंशाहमितरेतरमुष्टिभिः ।
वज्रनिष्पेषपरुषैरविश्रममहर्निशम् ॥10.56.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: आसीत् was, तत् that (fight), अष्टाविंश-अहम् for twenty eight days, इतरेतर-मुष्टिभिः with each others fists, वज्र-निष्पेष-परुषैः harsh like the striking of thunderbolts, अविश्रमम् without rest, अहर्-निशम् day and night

Anvaya: तत् (युद्धं) वज्रनिष्पेषपरुषैः इतरेतरमुष्टिभिः अहर्निशम् अविश्रमम् अष्टाविंशाहम् आसीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत् (युद्धं) वज्रनिष्पेषपरुषैः इतरेतरमुष्टिभिः That fight with each others fists which were harsh like the striking of thunderbolts, अहर्निशम् अविश्रमम् अष्टाविंशाहम् आसीत् went on day and night without rest for twenty eight days.

Simple Meaning: That furious duel, fought day and night without a single moment's rest, continued for twenty eight days, with both of them striking each other with fists as hard as devastating thunderbolts.

कृष्णमुष्टिविनिष्पातनिष्पिष्टाङ्गोरुबन्धनः ।
क्षीणसत्त्वः स्विन्नगात्रस्तमाहातीव विस्मितः ॥10.56.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृष्ण-मुष्टि-विनिष्पात-निष्पिष्ट-अङ्ग-उरु-बन्धनः his limbs and joints shattered by the falling blows of Krishnas fists, क्षीण-सत्त्वः depleted of strength, स्विन्न-गात्रः his body perspiring, तम् to Him (Krishna), आह spoke, अतीव extremely, विस्मितः astonished

Anvaya: कृष्णमुष्टिविनिष्पातनिष्पिष्टाङ्गोरुबन्धनः क्षीणसत्त्वः स्विन्नगात्रः अतीव विस्मितः (सन्) तम् आह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्णमुष्टिविनिष्पातनिष्पिष्टाङ्गोरुबन्धनः His limbs and joints shattered by the falling blows of Krishnas fists, क्षीणसत्त्वः स्विन्नगात्रः depleted of strength and his body perspiring, अतीव विस्मितः (सन्) being extremely astonished, तम् आह he spoke to Him.

Simple Meaning: With his muscles and joints shattered by the heavy blows of Krishna's fists, his physical strength completely depleted, and his body drenched in sweat, Jambavan became extremely astonished and spoke to the Lord.

जाने त्वां सर्वभूतानां प्राण ओजः सहो बलम् ।
विष्णुं पुराणपुरुषं प्रभविष्णुमधीश्वरम् ॥10.56.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: जाने I know, त्वाम् You, सर्व-भूतानाम् of all living entities, प्राणः the life air, ओजः the sensory power, सहः the mental strength, बलम् the physical power, विष्णुम् Lord Vishnu, पुराण-पुरुषम् the primeval person, प्रभविष्णुम् the all powerful, अधीश्वरम् the supreme controller

Anvaya: सर्वभूतानां प्राणः ओजः सहः बलं पुराणपुरुषं प्रभविष्णुम् अधीश्वरं विष्णुं त्वां जाने।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सर्वभूतानां Of all living entities, प्राणः ओजः सहः बलं पुराणपुरुषं प्रभविष्णुम् अधीश्वरं विष्णुं the life air, sensory power, mental strength, and physical power and the primeval person, the all powerful, supreme controller, Lord Vishnu, त्वां जाने I know You to be.

Simple Meaning: I now recognize You. You are the life force, sensory power, mental strength, and physical prowess of all living beings. You are none other than Lord Vishnu, the primeval person, the all powerful supreme controller.

त्वं हि विश्वसृजां स्रष्टा सृज्यानामपि यच्च सत् ।
कालः कलयतामीशः पर आत्मा तथाऽऽत्मनाम् ॥10.56.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वम् You, हि indeed, विश्व-सृजाम् of the creators of the universe, स्रष्टा the creator, सृज्यानाम् of the created things, अपि also, यत् which, च and, सत् the essential reality, कालः time, कलयताम् of those who subdues, ईशः the lord, परः the supreme, आत्मा soul, तथा also, आत्मनाम् of all individual souls

Anvaya: त्वं हि विश्वसृजां स्रष्टा सृज्यानाम् अपि यत् सत् (तत् त्वं), कलयतां कालः (त्वं), तथा आत्मनाम् ईशः परः आत्मा (त्वं हि)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: त्वं हि विश्वसृजां स्रष्टा You indeed are the creator of the creators of the universe, सृज्यानाम् अपि यत् सत् (तत् त्वं) and whatever is the essential reality among created things that also is You, कलयतां कालः (त्वं) of those who subdues You are time, तथा आत्मनाम् ईशः परः आत्मा (त्वं हि) as well as of all individual souls You indeed are the lord and supreme soul.

Simple Meaning: You are indeed the creator of the creators of this universe, and You are the essential reality of everything that is created. Among all subduing forces, You are almighty time, and You are the supreme lord and super soul of all individual living beings.

यस्येषदुत्कलितरोषकटाक्षमोक्षैर्वर्त्मादिशत् क्षुभितनक्रतिमिङ्गिलोऽब्धिः ।

Anvaya: यस्य ईषदुत्कलितरोषकटाक्षमोक्षैः क्षुभितनक्रतिमिङ्गिलः अब्धिः वर्त्म आदिशत्, (यस्य) सेतुः कृतः, लङ्का उज्ज्वलिता, च रक्षःशिरांसि इषुक्षतानि भुवि पेतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यस्य ईषदुत्कलितरोषकटाक्षमोक्षैः By the casting of whose glances of slightly arisen anger, क्षुभितनक्रतिमिङ्गिलः अब्धिः वर्त्म आदिशत् the ocean with its agitated crocodiles and timingila fish showed the path, (यस्य) सेतुः कृतः by whom a bridge was built, लङ्का उज्ज्वलिता Lanka was burned, च रक्षःशिरांसि इषुक्षतानि भुवि पेतुः and the heads of the rakshasas severed by His arrows fell on the ground.

Simple Meaning: By the mere casting of His slightly angry glances, the ocean became highly agitated, throwing its crocodiles and timingila fish into a frenzy, and submissively showed Him a path, a bridge was built over that ocean, Lanka was burned, and the heads of the rakshasas severed by His arrows fell to the ground.

Simple Meaning: O lord of the bears, we have come to this cave here for the sake of the jewel, so that I may clear myself of a false accusation by using this very jewel.

Simple Meaning: Addressed in this manner, Jambavan joyfully presented his own maiden daughter, Jambavati, along with the jewel, to Krishna as a respectful offering.

Anvaya: किं कृत्वा मह्यं साधु स्यात्, यथा जनः अदीर्घदर्शनं क्षुद्रं मूढं द्रविणलोलुपं (मां) न शपेत् वा।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: किं कृत्वा मह्यं साधु स्यात् What can I do so that there will be a good outcome for me, यथा जनः and how the people, अदीर्घदर्शनं क्षुद्रं मूढं द्रविणलोलुपं (मां) न शपेत् वा will not curse or condemn me who am short sighted, petty, foolish, and greedy for wealth.

Simple Meaning: What can I do now to achieve my real well-being, and how can I prevent the public from cursing me for being a shortsighted, petty, and foolish man who is greedily attached to wealth?

दास्ये दुहितरं तस्मै स्त्रीरत्नं रत्नमेव च ।
उपायोऽयं समीचीनस्तस्य शान्तिर्न चान्यथा ॥10.56.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: दास्ये I shall give, दुहितरम् my daughter, तस्मै to Him, स्त्री-रत्नम् a jewel of a woman, रत्नम् the jewel, एव indeed, च and, उपायः means, अयम् this, समीचीनः appropriate, तस्य of this, शान्तिः pacification, न not, च and, अन्यथा otherwise

Anvaya: तस्मै स्त्रीरत्नं दुहितरं रत्नम् एव च दास्ये, अयम् उपायः समीचीनः (यतः) तस्य शान्तिः (भवति) अन्यथा न च।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तस्मै स्त्रीरत्नं दुहितरं रत्नम् एव च दास्ये I shall give to Him my daughter who is a jewel among women as well as the Syamantaka jewel itself, अयम् उपायः समीचीनः this is the most appropriate means, (यतः) तस्य शान्तिः (भवति) अन्यथा न च because by this His pacification will be achieved and not in any other way.

Simple Meaning: I shall give my daughter, who is a jewel among women, along with the Syamantaka jewel itself to Him. This is the only appropriate way to satisfy Him and gain peace of mind, for there is no other way.

एवं व्यवसितो बुद्ध्या सत्राजित् स्वसुतां शुभाम् ।
मणिं च स्वयमुद्यम्य कृष्णायोपजहार ह ॥10.56.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, व्यवसितः having resolved, बुद्ध्या by his intelligence, सत्राजित् Satrajit, स्व-सुताम् his own daughter, शुभाम् auspicious, मणिम् the jewel, च and, स्वयम् उद्यम्य of his own accord taking initiative, कृष्णाय to Krishna, उपजहार ह presented indeed

Anvaya: एवं बुद्ध्या व्यवसितः सत्राजित् स्वयम् उद्यम्य शुभां स्वसुतां मणिं च कृष्णाय उपजहार ह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवं बुद्ध्या व्यवसितः सत्राजित् Thus having resolved with his intelligence, Satrajit, स्वयम् उद्यम्य taking the initiative of his own accord, शुभां स्वसुतां मणिं च कृष्णाय उपजहार ह presented his auspicious daughter and the jewel to Krishna.

Simple Meaning: Having resolved his mind in this way, Satrajit took the initiative of his own accord and presented his auspicious daughter, Satyabhama, along with the Syamantaka jewel, to Krishna.

तां सत्यभामां भगवानुपयेमे यथाविधि ।
बहुभिर्याचितां शीलरूपौदार्यगुणान्विताम् ॥10.56.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her, सत्यभामाम् Satyabhama, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, उपयेमे married, यथा-विधि according to Vedic rites, बहुभिः by many men, याचिताम् who had been sought after, शील-रूप-औदार्य-गुण-अन्विताम् endowed with the qualities of good character, beauty, and generosity

Anvaya: भगवान् बहुभिः याचितां शीलरूपौदार्यगुणान्वितां तां सत्यभामां यथाविधि उपयेमे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवान् The Supreme Lord, बहुभिः याचितां who had been sought after by many suitors, शीलरूपौदार्यगुणान्वितां तां सत्यभामां and who was endowed with outstanding character, beauty, and generosity, that Satyabhama, यथाविधि उपयेमे married according to proper Vedic rites.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord married Satyabhama in accordance with prescribed Vedic rites. She was a woman of outstanding character, beauty, and generosity, who had previously been eagerly sought after by many suitors.

भगवानाह न मणिं प्रतीच्छामो वयं नृप ।
तवास्तां देवभक्तस्य वयं च फलभागिनः ॥10.56.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: भगवान् the Supreme Lord, आह said, न not, मणिम् the jewel, प्रतीच्छामः desire to accept, वयम् we, नृप O King, तव your, आस्ताम् let it remain, देव-भक्तस्य of the devotee of Sun god, वयम् we, च and, फल-भागिनः the beneficiary of the results

Anvaya: भगवान् आह — नृप! वयं मणिं न प्रतीच्छामः, देवभक्तस्य तव (एव अयम्) आस्तां, वयं च फलभागिनः (भविष्यामः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवान् आह The Supreme Lord said, नृप O King, वयं मणिं न प्रतीच्छामः we do not wish to accept the jewel, देवभक्तस्य तव (एव अयम्) आस्तां let it remain with you who are a devotee of the Sun god, वयं च फलभागिनः (भविष्यामः) and we shall also be the beneficiary of its results.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord said, "O King, we do not wish to take the jewel. Let it remain in your possession, since you are a devoted worshipper of the Sun god. We shall simply enjoy its beneficial results."

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंसायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “स्यमन्तकमण्युपाख्यानं कृष्णस्य जाम्बवतीसत्यभामापरिणयश्च​ नाम​” षट्पञ्चाशत्तमः अध्यायः ॥56॥

Thus ends the fifty-sixth discourse named “The story of the Syamantaka gem; Krishna weds Jambavati and Satyabhama” in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
