Shrimad Bhagavatam - Book 2

Canto 10

Chapter 79

Balvala killed and an account of the pilgrimage of Balarama

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

बल्वलवधः बलरामतीर्थयात्रानिरूपणं च नाम​ एकोनाशीतितमः अध्यायः

(34 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
ततः पर्वण्युपावृत्ते प्रचण्डः पांसुवर्षणः ।
भीमो वायुरभूद् राजन् पूयगन्धस्तु सर्वशः ॥10.79.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, ततः then, पर्वणि upon the arrival of the full moon day, उपावृत्ते having arrived, प्रचण्डः fierce, पांसु-वर्षणः showering dust, भीमः terrifying, वायुः wind, अभूत् arose, राजन् O King (Parikshit), पूय-गन्धः carrying the stench of pus, तु indeed, सर्वशः everywhere

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच – राजन्! ततः पर्वणि उपावृत्ते प्रचण्डः पांसुवर्षणः पूयगन्धः तु सर्वशः भीमः वायुः अभूत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, राजन् O King, ततः पर्वणि उपावृत्ते then when the full moon day arrived, प्रचण्डः पांसुवर्षणः showering fierce dust, पूयगन्धः तु सर्वशः and carrying the smell of pus everywhere, भीमः वायुः अभूत् a terrifying wind arose.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King Parikshit! When the full moon day arrived a terrifying and fierce wind arose, showering dust everywhere and spreading the foul stench of pus in all directions.

ततोऽमेध्यमयं वर्षं बल्वलेन विनिर्मितम् ।
अभवद् यज्ञशालायां सोऽन्वदृश्यत शूलधृक् ॥10.79.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, अमेध्य-मयम् consisting of filthy, impure matters, वर्षम् a shower, बल्वलेन by Balvala, विनिर्मितम् produced, अभवत् took place, यज्ञ-शालायाम् in the sacrificial arena, सः he (Balvala), अन्वदृश्यत appeared, शूल-धृक holding a trident

Anvaya: ततः यज्ञशालायां बल्वलेन विनिर्मितम् अमेध्यमयं वर्षम् अभवत्। सः शूलधृक् अन्वदृश्यत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः Then, यज्ञशालायां in the sacrificial hall, बल्वलेन विनिर्मितम् produced by Balvala, अमेध्यमयं वर्षम् अभवत् a shower composed of filthy foul matters occurred. सः शूलधृक् अन्वदृश्यत He appeared holding a trident in his hand.

Simple Meaning: Then inside the sacrificial hall a horrific shower of filthy, impure substances produced by Balvala began to rain down, and soon the asura himself appeared before them carrying a trident.

तं विलोक्य बृहत्कायं भिन्नाञ्जनचयोपमम् ।
तप्तताम्रशिखाश्मश्रुं दंष्ट्रोग्रभ्रुकुटीमुखम् ॥10.79.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him (Balvala), विलोक्य seeing, बृहत्-कायम् having a massive body, भिन्न-अञ्जन-चय-उपमम् resembling a mountain of crushed kohl, तप्त-ताम्र-शिखा-श्मश्रुम् having hair on his head and facial beard like molten copper, दंष्ट्रा-उग्र-भ्रुकुटी-मुखम् having a fierce face with prominent fangs and terrifying knitted brows

Anvaya: बृहत्कायं भिन्नाञ्जनचयोपमं तप्तताम्रशिखाश्मश्रुं दंष्ट्रोग्रभ्रुकुटीमुखं तं विलोक्य…

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बृहत्कायं Having a massive body, भिन्नाञ्जनचयोपमं resembling a heap of crushed black kohl, तप्तताम्रशिखाश्मश्रुं with hair and beard glowing like molten copper, दंष्ट्रोग्रभ्रुकुटीमुखं with a fierce face adorned with sharp fangs and knitted eyebrows, तं विलोक्य seeing him…

Simple Meaning: Seeing the massive Balvala whose colossal dark body resembled a mountain of crushed kohl, his head hair and beard shining like molten copper and his terrifying face featured prominent fangs and fiercely knitted eyebrows…

सस्मार मुसलं रामः परसैन्यविदारणम् ।
हलं च दैत्यदमनं ते तूर्णमुपतस्थतुः ॥10.79.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: सस्मार remembered, मुसलम् the pestle (named Saunanda), रामः Balarama, पर-सैन्य-विदारणम् the tearing apart of enemy armies, हलम् the plough (named Samvartaka), च and, दैत्य-दमनम् the subduer of daityas, ते both of those weapons, तूर्णम् immediately, उपतस्थतुः appeared before Him

Anvaya: रामः परसैन्यविदारणं मुसलं दैत्यदमनं हलं च सस्मार ते तूर्णम् उपतस्थतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रामः Balarama, परसैन्यविदारणं मुसलं remembered His pestle capable of tearing apart opposing armies, दैत्यदमनं हलं च and His plough capable of subduing daityas, ते तूर्णम् उपतस्थतुः both of those weapons immediately appeared before Him.

Simple Meaning: Balarama remembered His famous weapons His pestle the destroyer of the enemy armies and His plough the subduer of all the daityas and as soon as He thought of them both weapons immediately appeared in His presence.

तमाकृष्य हलाग्रेण बल्वलं गगनेचरम् ।
मुसलेनाहनत् क्रुद्धो मूर्ध्नि ब्रह्मद्रुहं बलः ॥10.78.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him (Balvala), आकृष्य dragging down, हल-आग्रेण with the tip of His plough, बल्वलम् Balvala, गगने चरम् flying in the sky, मुसलेन with His pestle, आहनत् struck, क्रुद्धः enraged, मूर्ध्नि on the head, ब्रह्म-द्रुहम् the enemy of the brahmanas, बलः Balarama

Anvaya: क्रुद्धः बलः गगने चरं ब्रह्मद्रुहं तं बल्वलं हलाग्रेण आकृष्य मुसलेन मूर्ध्नि आहनत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: क्रुद्धः बलः Enraged Balarama, गगने चरं ब्रह्मद्रुहं तं बल्वलं dragging that Balvala the enemy of the brahmanas who was flying through the sky, हलाग्रेण आकृष्य pulling him down with the tip of His plough, मुसलेन मूर्ध्नि आहनत् struck him on the head with His pestle.

Simple Meaning: Enraged Balarama hooked Balvala who was roaming in the sky—the enemy of the brahmanas—with the tip of His plough, dragged him down, and struck him on the head with His pestle.

सोऽपतद् भुवि निर्भिन्नललाटोऽसृक् समुत्सृजन् ।
मुञ्चन्नार्तस्वरं शैलो यथा वज्रहतोऽरुणः ॥10.79.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Balvala), अपतत् fell, भुवि upon the ground, निर्भिन्न-ललाटः with his forehead split open, असृक् blood, समुत्सृजन् gushing out, मुञ्चन् emitting, आर्त-स्वरम् a cry of agony, शैलः a mountain, यथा just like, वज्र-हतः struck by the thunderbolt of Indra, अरुणः red with red chalk or minerals

Anvaya: निर्भिन्नललाटः असृक् समुत्सृजन् आर्तस्वरं मुञ्चन् सः वज्रहतः अरुणः शैलः यथा भुवि अपतत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: निर्भिन्नललाटः With his forehead split open, असृक् समुत्सृजन् gushing out blood, आर्तस्वरं मुञ्चन् emitting a cry of agony, सः he, वज्रहतः अरुणः शैलः यथा like a red mountain struck by a thunderbolt, भुवि अपतत् fell upon the ground.

Simple Meaning: With his forehead split open and blood gushing out profusely, he uttered a dreadful cry of agony and collapsed to the ground, looking like a massive red mountain struck and shattered by Indra's thunderbolt.

संस्तुत्य मुनयो रामं प्रयुज्यावितथाशिषः ।
अभ्यषिञ्चन् महाभागा वृत्रघ्नं विबुधा यथा ॥10.79.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: संस्तुत्य praising, मुनयः the sages, रामम् Balarama, प्रयुज्य bestowing, अवितथ-आशिषः infallible blessings, अभ्यषिञ्चन् performed ritual bathing, महा-भागाः the highly fortunate ones, वृत्र-घ्नम् the killer of Vritra - Indra, विबुधाः the devas, यथा just as

Anvaya: महाभागाः मुनयः रामं संस्तुत्य अवितथाः आशिषः प्रयुज्य – विबुधाः वृत्रघ्नं यथा – अभ्यषिञ्चन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महाभागाः मुनयः The most fortunate sages, रामं संस्तुत्य having praised Lord Balarama, अवितथाः आशिषः प्रयुज्य and bestowing infallible blessings upon Him, विबुधाः वृत्रघ्नं यथा just as the devas, for Indra after he killed Vritra, अभ्यषिञ्चन् so did they perform the sacred bath for Balarama.

Simple Meaning: The greatly fortunate sages praised Balarama and bestowed infallible blessings upon Him. They then performed a ceremonial bathing ritual for Him, just as the devas had once performed for Indra after the destruction of the asura Vritra.

वैजयन्तीं ददुर्मालां श्रीधामाम्लानपङ्कजाम् ।
रामाय वाससी दिव्ये दिव्यान्याभरणानि च ॥10.79.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: वैजयन्तीम् the Vaijayanti, ददुः they gave, मालाम् garland, श्रीधाम-अम्लान-पङ्कजाम् made of unfading lotuses which are the abode of Lakshmi, रामाय unto Balarama, वाससी two garments, दिव्ये divine, दिव्यानि divine, आभरणानि ornaments, च and

Anvaya: श्रीधामाम्लानपङ्कजां वैजयन्तीं मालां दिव्ये वाससी दिव्यानि आभरणानि च रामाय ददुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्रीधामाम्लानपङ्कजां वैजयन्तीं मालां The Vaijayanti garland which is the abode of goddess Lakshmi and made of unfading lotus flowers, दिव्ये वाससी two divine garments, दिव्यानि आभरणानि च and celestial ornaments, रामाय ददुः they presented unto Balarama.

Simple Meaning: The sages presented Balarama with the famous Vaijayanti garland—an abode of goddess Lakshmi woven from unfading lotuses—along with two splendid divine garments and exquisite celestial ornaments.

अथ तैरभ्यनुज्ञातः कौशिकीमेत्य ब्राह्मणैः ।
स्नात्वा सरोवरमगाद् यतः सरयुरास्रवत् ॥10.79.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथ then, तैः by those, अभ्यनुज्ञातः permitted, कौशिकीम् to the river Kaushiki, एत्य having gone, ब्राह्मणैः by the brahmanas, स्नात्वा having bathed, सरोवरम् to the lake, अगात् went, यतः from where, सरयुः the river Sarayu, आस्रवत् flows out

Anvaya: अथ तैः ब्राह्मणैः अभ्यनुज्ञातः कौशिकीम् एत्य स्नात्वा यतः सरयुः आस्रवत् तत् सरोवरम् अगात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ Then, तैः ब्राह्मणैः अभ्यनुज्ञातः permitted by those brahmanas, कौशिकीम् एत्य having reached the Kaushiki river, स्नात्वा and having bathed there, यतः सरयुः आस्रवत् from where the Sarayu river flows out, तत् सरोवरम् अगात् He went to that sacred lake.

Simple Meaning: Then with the permission of those brahmanas Lord Balarama set out on His pilgrimage He first went to the river Kaushiki and bathed there and then proceeded to the sacred lake from which the river Sarayu flows.

अनुस्रोतेन सरयूं प्रयागमुपगम्य सः ।
स्नात्वा सन्तर्प्य देवादीन् जगाम पुलहाश्रमम् ॥10.79.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: अनुस्रोतेन following the downstream course, सरयूम् along the river Sarayu, प्रयागम् to Prayaga, उपगम्य having arrived, सः He (Balarama), स्नात्वा having bathed, सन्तर्प्य offering oblations, देव-आदीन् to the devas and ancestral forefathers, जगाम went, पुलह-आश्रमम् to the hermitage of Sage Pulaha

Anvaya: सः सरयूम् अनुस्रोतेन प्रयागम् उपगम्य स्नात्वा देवादीन् सन्तर्प्य पुलहाश्रमं जगाम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He, सरयूम् अनुस्रोतेन following the downstream course of the Sarayu river, प्रयागम् उपगम्य having arrived at Prayaga, स्नात्वा having bathed in the holy waters, देवादीन् सन्तर्प्य satisfying the devas and ancestors with offerings, पुलहाश्रमं जगाम proceeded to the hermitage of Sage Pulaha.

Simple Meaning: Following the downstream course of the river Sarayu, Balarama arrived at Prayaga. There He bathed, offered sacred oblations to satisfy the devas and forefathers, and then proceeded on His journey to the sacred hermitage of Sage Pulaha.

गोमतीं गन्डकीं स्नात्वा विपाशां शोण आप्लुतः ।
गयां गत्वा पितृनिष्ट्वा गङ्गासागरसङ्गमे ॥10.79.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: गोमतीम् in Gomati, गण्डकीम् in Gandaki, स्नात्वा having bathed, विपाशाम् in Vipasha (Beas), शोणे in Shona, आप्लुतः taking a holy dip, गयाम् to Gaya, गत्वा having gone, पितॄन् the ancestors, इष्ट्वा having worshipped, गङ्गा-सागर-सङ्गमे at the confluence of the Ganga and the ocean

Anvaya: गोमतीं गण्डकीं विपाशां स्नात्वा शोणे आप्लुतः गयां गत्वा पितॄन् इष्ट्वा गङ्गासागरसङ्गमे…

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गोमतीं गण्डकीं विपाशां स्नात्वा Having bathed in the rivers Gomati Gandaki and Vipasha, शोणे आप्लुतः taking a holy dip in the river Shona, गयां गत्वा having gone to Gaya, पितॄन् इष्ट्वा having worshipped the forefathers, गङ्गासागरसङ्गमे at the confluence of the sacred river Ganga and the ocean…

Simple Meaning: Having bathed in the Gomati, Gandaki and Vipasha rivers and taking a holy dip in the Shona river, He then visited Gaya where He performed sacred rites to worship His ancestors. At the holy confluence of the Ganga and the ocean…

उपस्पृश्य महेन्द्राद्रौ रामं दृष्ट्वाभिवाद्य च ।
सप्तगोदावरीं वेणां पम्पां भीमरथीं ततः ॥10.79.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: उपस्पृश्य touching the sacred water, महेन्द्र-आद्रौ on Mount Mahendra, रामम् Lord Parasurama, दृष्ट्वा having seen, अभिवाद्य offering respects, च and, सप्त-गोदावरीम् at the seven branches of Godavari, वेणाम् at the river Vena, पम्पाम् at the river Pampa, भीमरथीम् at the river Bhimarathi, ततः then

Anvaya: …उपस्पृश्य महेन्द्राद्रौ रामं दृष्ट्वा अभिवाद्य च ततः सप्तगोदावरीं वेणां पम्पां भीमरथीम्…

Literal meaning of the anvaya: …उपस्पृश्य touching the sacred waters, महेन्द्राद्रौ रामं दृष्ट्वा अभिवाद्य च at Mount Mahendra seeing Lord Parasurama and offering respects unto Him, ततः then, सप्तगोदावरीं वेणां पम्पां भीमरथीम् to the seven branches of Godavari, the Vena river, the Pampa river and the Bhimarathi river…

Simple Meaning: …touching the sacred waters, at Mount Mahendra, Balarama met Lord Parasurama and offered Him respectful obeisances. He then continued His pilgrimage and bathed in the seven branches of the Godavari river, the Vena river, the Pampa lake and the Bhimarathi river.

स्कन्दं दृष्ट्वा ययौ रामः श्रीशैलं गिरिशालयम् ।
द्रविडेषु महापुण्यं दृष्ट्वाद्रिं वेङ्कटं प्रभुः ॥10.79.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्कन्दम् Lord Skanda (Kartikeya), दृष्ट्वा having visited, ययौ went, रामः Balarama, श्रीशैलम् to Shri Shaila, गिरिश-आलयम् the abode of Lord Shiva, द्रविडेषु in the Dravida country, महा-पुण्यम् supremely holy, दृष्ट्वा having visited, अद्रिम् the hill, वेङ्कटम् Venkata, प्रभुः the Lord Balarama

Anvaya: प्रभुः रामः स्कन्दं दृष्ट्वा गिरिशालयं श्रीशैलं ययौ, द्रविडेषु महापुण्यं वेङ्कटम् अद्रिं दृष्ट्वा…

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रभुः रामः Lord Balarama, स्कन्दं दृष्ट्वा having visited Lord Skanda, गिरिशालयं श्रीशैलं ययौ went to Shri Shaila the abode of Lord Shiva, द्रविडेषु in the Dravida region, महापुण्यं वेङ्कटम् अद्रिं दृष्ट्वा having visited the supremely holy Venkata hill…

Simple Meaning: Lord Balarama then visited the sacred site of Lord Skanda and proceeded to Shri Shaila the abode of Lord Shiva. Having visited the supremely holy Venkata hill in the Dravida region…

कामकोष्णीं पुरीं काञ्चीं कावेरीं च सरिद्वराम् ।
श्रीरङ्गाख्यं महापुण्यं यत्र सन्निहितो हरिः ॥10.79.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: कामकोष्णीम् to Kamakoshni, पुरीम् काञ्चीम् to the city of Kanchi, कावेरीम् to the river Kaveri, च and, सरित्-वराम् the best of rivers, श्रीरङ्ग-आख्याम् named Shriranga, महा-पुण्यम् supremely holy, यत्र where, सन्निहितः manifests in presence, हरिः Lord Hari

Anvaya: (रामः) कामकोष्णीं काञ्चीं पुरीं, सरिद्वरां कावेरीं च महापुण्यं श्रीरङ्गाख्यं यत्र हरिः सन्निहितः (ययौ)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (रामः) Lord Balarama, कामकोष्णीं to Kamakoshni, काञ्चीं पुरीं to the city of Kanchi, सरिद्वरां कावेरीं च and to the Kaveri river the best among rivers, महापुण्यं श्रीरङ्गाख्यं and to the supremely holy place named Shriranga, यत्र हरिः सन्निहितः where Lord Hari is eternally present, (ययौ) went.

Simple Meaning: Lord Balarama then traveled to Kamakoshni and the holy city of Kanchi. He visited the Kaveri, the finest of rivers and proceeded to the supremely sacred place known as Shriranga where Lord Hari eternally dwells.

ऋषभाद्रिं हरेः क्षेत्रं दक्षिणां मथुरां तथा ।
सामुद्रं सेतुमगमन्महापातकनाशनम् ॥10.79.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऋषभ-अद्रिम् to the Rishabha mountain, हरेः क्षेत्रम् the sacred abode of Lord Hari, दक्षिणाम् मथुराम् to Southern Mathura (Madurai), तथा as well as, सामुद्रम् of the ocean, सेतुम् to the bridge (Rameswaram), अगमत् went, महा-पातक-नाशनम् which destroys the greatest of sins

Anvaya: (रामः) हरेः क्षेत्रम् ऋषभाद्रिं  तथा दक्षिणां मथुरां महापातकनाशनं सामुद्रं सेतुम् अगमत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (रामः) Lord Balarama, हरेः क्षेत्रम् ऋषभाद्रिं to the Rishabha mountain the sacred abode of Lord Hari, तथा दक्षिणां मथुरां and to Southern Mathura, महापातकनाशनं सामुद्रं सेतुम् अगमत् went to the bridge on the ocean (Rameshwaram) which destroys the greatest of sins.

Simple Meaning: Lord Balarama then visited Rishabhadri mountain, a sacred abode of Lord Hari and proceeded to Southern Mathura (Madurai). He then traveled to Rameshwaram, which possesses the power to destroy the greatest of sins.

तत्रायुतमदाद् धेनूर्ब्राह्मणेभ्यो हलायुधः ।
कृतमालां ताम्रपर्णीं मलयं च कुलाचलम् ॥10.79.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there, अयुतम् ten thousand, अदात् gave in charity, धेनूः cows, ब्राह्मणेभ्यः unto the brahmanas, हल-आयुधः Balarama who carries the plough as His weapon, कृतमालाम् at the river Kritamala, ताम्रपर्णीम् at the river Tamraparni, मलयम् at the Malaya mountain, च and, कुल-अचलम् the great principal mountain

Anvaya: हलायुधः तत्र ब्राह्मणेभ्यः अयुतं धेनूः अदात्। (ततः) कृतमालां ताम्रपर्णीं कुलाचलं मलयं च (अगमत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: हलायुधः Balarama whose weapon is the plough, तत्र there, ब्राह्मणेभ्यः unto the brahmanas, अयुतं धेनूः अदात् gave ten thousand cows in charity. (ततः) Then, कृतमालां to the Kritamala river, ताम्रपर्णीं to the Tamraparni river, कुलाचलं मलयं च and to the principal mountain Malaya, (अगमत्) He proceeded.

Simple Meaning: There Balarama donated ten thousand cows in charity to the brahmanas. He then continued His pilgrimage visiting the sacred rivers Kritamala and Tamraparni as well as the great Malaya mountain range.

तत्रागस्त्यं समासीनं नमस्कृत्याभिवाद्य च ।
योजितस्तेन चाशीर्भिरनुज्ञातो गतोऽर्णवम् ।
दक्षिणं तत्र कन्याख्यां दुर्गां देवीं ददर्श सः ॥10.79.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there at the Malaya mountain, अगस्त्यम् Sage Agastya, समासीनम् sitting comfortably, नमस्कृत्य bowing down, अभिवाद्य offering respectful greetings, च and, योजितः blessed, तेन by him (Agastya), च and, आशीर्भिः with blessings, अनुज्ञातः permitted, गतः went, अर्णवम् to the ocean, दक्षिणम् southern, तत्र there, कन्या-आख्याम् named Kanya (Kanyakumari), दुर्गाम् देवीम् Goddess Durga, ददर्श saw, सः He (Lord Balarama)

Anvaya: तत्र समासीनम् अगस्त्यं नमस्कृत्य अभिवाद्य च तेन आशीर्भिः योजितः अनुज्ञातः च सः दक्षिणम् अर्णवं गतः। तत्र कन्याख्यां दुर्गां देवीं ददर्श।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत्र There, समासीनम् अगस्त्यं नमस्कृत्य अभिवाद्य च bowing down and offering respects to Sage Agastya who was seated comfortably, तेन आशीर्भिः योजितः blessed by him with benedictions, अनुज्ञातः च and permitted by him, सः He, दक्षिणम् अर्णवं गतः went to the southern ocean. तत्र There, कन्याख्यां दुर्गां देवीं ददर्श He beheld Goddess Durga known as Kanya.

Simple Meaning: There on the Malaya mountain Lord Balarama bowed down and offered His respects to Sage Agastya who was sitting comfortably. Sage Agastya bestowed His heartfelt blessings upon Him and permitted Him to continue His journey. Lord Balarama then traveled to the southern ocean where He visited and beheld Goddess Durga known as Kanyakumari.

ततः फाल्गुनमासाद्य पञ्चाप्सरसमुत्तमम् ।
विष्णुः सन्निहितो यत्र स्नात्वास्पर्शद् गवायुतम् ॥10.79.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, फाल्गुनम् to Phalguna (Ananta Padmanabha or Phalguna Kshetra), आसाद्य having reached, पञ्च-अप्सरसं उत्तमम् the supreme Panch Apsaras, विष्णुः Lord Vishnu, सन्निहितः is manifested, यत्र where, स्नात्वा having bathed, अस्पर्शत् touched and (gave away), गो-अयुतम् ten thousand cows

Anvaya: ततः फाल्गुनम् आसाद्य यत्र विष्णुः सन्निहितः। तम् उत्तमं पञ्चाप्सरसं स्नात्वा गवायुतम् अस्पर्शत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः Then, फाल्गुनम् आसाद्य having reached Phalguna kshetra, यत्र विष्णुः सन्निहितः where Lord Vishnu is directly present. तम् उत्तमं पञ्चाप्सरसं In that supreme Panch Apsaras sacred lake, स्नात्वा having bathed, गवायुतम् अस्पर्शत् He touched and gave away ten thousand cows in charity.

Simple Meaning: Continuing His pilgrimage Lord Balarama reached the sacred site of Phalguna and visited the supreme holy lake known as Pancha Apsaras where Lord Vishnu is eternally present. After taking a holy bath in its waters He gave ten thousand cows in charity.

ततोऽभिव्रज्य भगवान् केरलांस्तु त्रिगर्तकान् ।
गोकर्णाख्यं शिवक्षेत्रं सान्निध्यं यत्र धूर्जटेः ॥10.79.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, अभिव्रज्य having traveled, भगवान् the Supreme Lord Balarama, केरलान् through the Kerala region, तु indeed, त्रिगर्तकान् through the Trigarta region, गोकर्ण-आख्याम् named Gokarna, शिव-क्षेत्रम् the sacred place of Lord Shiva, सान्निध्यम् constant presence, यत्र where, धूर्जटेः of the one with dusty and matted hair

धूर्जटिः - धूर् – dust or small particles. जटि –matted hair or locks.

Anvaya: ततः भगवान् केरलान् तु त्रिगर्तकान् अभिव्रज्य यत्र धूर्जटेः सान्निध्यं तत् गोकर्णाख्यं शिवक्षेत्रम् (अगमत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः Then, भगवान् the Supreme Lord Balarama, केरलान् तु त्रिगर्तकान् अभिव्रज्य having traveled through the Kerala and Trigarta territories, यत्र where, धूर्जटेः सान्निध्यं the personal presence of Lord Shiva is eternally manifest, तत् गोकर्णाख्यं शिवक्षेत्रम् (अगमत्) He arrived at that sacred abode of Lord Shiva known as Gokarna.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord Balarama then traveled onward through the regions of Kerala and Trigarta. He proceeded to Gokarna, the holy abode of Lord Shiva where He is eternally present.

आर्यां द्वैपायनीं दृष्ट्वा शूर्पारकमगाद् बलः ।
तापीं पयोष्णीं निर्विन्ध्यामुपस्पृश्याथ दण्डकम् ॥10.70.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: आर्याम् the goddess Arya (Goddess Durga/Parvati), द्वैपायनीम् at the island shrine, दृष्ट्वा having visited, शूर्पारकमः to Shurparaka, अगात् went, बलः Balarama, तापीम् in the river Tapi, पयोष्णीम् in the river Payoshni, निर्विन्ध्याम् in the river Nirvindhya, उपस्पृश्य having bathed, अथ then, दण्डकम् to the Dandaka forest

Anvaya: बलः द्वैपायनीम् आर्यां दृष्ट्वा शूर्पारकम् अगात् अथ तापीं पयोष्णीं निर्विन्ध्याम् उपस्पृश्य दण्डकम्…

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बलः Balarama, द्वैपायनीम् आर्यां दृष्ट्वा having visited goddess Arya on the island shrine, शूर्पारकम् अगात् went to Shurparaka, अथ then, तापीं पयोष्णीं निर्विन्ध्याम् उपस्पृश्य having bathed in the rivers Tapi, Payoshni and Nirvindhya, दण्डकम् (प्रविश्य) entering the Dandaka forest…

Simple Meaning: Balarama visited goddess Arya on the island shrine and then proceeded to Shurparaka. After taking holy baths in the Tapi, Payoshni and Nirvindhya rivers He entered the Dandaka forest.

प्रविश्य रेवामगमद् यत्र माहिष्मती पुरी ।
मनुतीर्थमुपस्पृश्य प्रभासं पुनरागमत् ॥10.79.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रविश्य having entered, रेवाम् to the river Reva, अगमत् went, यत्र where, माहिष्मती पुरी the city of Mahishmati, मनु-तीर्थम् at Manu-tirtha, उपस्पृश्य having bathed, प्रभासम् to Prabhasa, पुनः again, आगमत् returned

Anvaya: …प्रविश्य (बलः) रेवाम् आगमत्।  यत्र माहिष्मती पुरी (तत्र) मनुतीर्थम् उपस्पृश्य पुनः प्रभासम् आगमत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: …प्रविश्य entering (Dandaka), रेवाम् अगमत् entered the river Reva. यत्र माहिष्मती पुरी Where the city of Mahishmati is located, मनुतीर्थम् उपस्पृश्य having bathed at Manu-tirtha, पुनः प्रभासम् आगमत् returned again to Prabhasa.

Simple Meaning: Lord Balarama entering Dankada bathed in the river Reva. He then visited the city of Mahishmati. After taking a holy bath at Manu-tirtha, He completed His circuitous pilgrimage and returned once again to Prabhasa.

श्रुत्वा द्विजैः कथ्यमानं कुरुपाण्डवसंयुगे ।
सर्वराजन्यनिधनं भारं मेने हृतं भुवः ॥10.79.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रुत्वा having heard, द्विजैः by the brahmanas, कथ्यमानम् being described, कुरु-पाण्डव-संयुगे in the battle between the Kurus and Pandavas, सर्व-राजन्य-निधनम् the total destruction of all the kings, भारम् the burden, मेने He considered, हृतम् removed, भुवः of the earth

Anvaya: कुरुपाण्डवसंयुगे सर्वराजन्यनिधनं द्विजैः कथ्यमानं श्रुत्वा (रामः) भुवः भारं हृतं मेने।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कुरुपाण्डवसंयुगे In the war between the Kurus and Pandavas, सर्वराजन्यनिधनं the annihilation of all the royal warriors, द्विजैः कथ्यमानं being narrated by the brahmanas, श्रुत्वा having heard, (रामः) Lord Balarama, भुवः भारं हृतं मेने considered the heavy burden of the earth to have been removed.

Simple Meaning: While at Prabhasa, Balarama heard the brahmanas describing the tragic war between the Kurus and Pandavas and the total destruction of all the participating kings. Hearing this He thought that the heavy burden bearing down upon the earth had finally been relieved.

स भीमदुर्योधनयोर्गदाभ्यां युध्यतोर्मृधे।
वारयिष्यन् विनशनं जगाम यदुनन्दनः ॥10.79.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः He, भीम-दुर्योधनयोः between Bhima and Duryodhana, गदाभ्याम् with maces, युध्यतोः fighting, मृधे in the battle, वारयिष्यन् intending to stop, विनशनम् destruction, जगाम went, यदु-नन्दनः Lord Balarama the delight of the Yadus

Anvaya: सः यदुनन्दनः मृधे गदाभ्यां युध्यतोः भीमदुर्योधनयोः विनशनं वारयिष्यन् जगाम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः यदुनन्दनः That Lord Balarama the delight of the Yadu dynasty, मृधे in battle, गदाभ्यां with maces, युध्यतोः fighting, भीमदुर्योधनयोः between Bhima and Duryodhana, विनशनं destruction, वारयिष्यन् intending to prevent or stop, जगाम proceeded.

Simple Meaning: Desiring to prevent the duel between Bhima and Duryodhana who were locked in fierce combat with maces on the battlefield, Lord Balarama the delight of the Yadu dynasty proceeded to Kurukshetra.

युधिष्ठिरस्तु तं दृष्ट्वा यमौ कृष्णार्जुनावपि ।
अभिवाद्याभवंस्तूष्णीं किं विवक्षुरिहागतः ॥10.79.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: युधिष्ठिरः Yudhishthira, तु indeed, तम् Him (Lord Balarama), दृष्ट्वा having seen, यमौ the twins Nakula and Sahadeva, कृष्ण-अर्जुनौ Krishna and Arjuna, अपि as well, अभिवाद्य offering obeisances, अभवन् remained, तूष्णीम् silent, किम् what, विवक्षुः desiring to say, इह here, आगतः has come

Anvaya: तु युधिष्ठिरः यमौ कृष्णार्जुनौ अपि तं दृष्ट्वा अभिवाद्य 'इह किं विवक्षुः आगतः' (इति विचारयन्तः) तूष्णीम् अभवन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तु Indeed, युधिष्ठिरः Yudhishthira, यमौ the twins - Nakula and Sahadeva, कृष्णार्जुनौ अपि and Krishna and Arjuna as well, तं दृष्ट्वा having seen Him, अभिवाद्य offering respectful greetings, 'इह किं विवक्षुः आगतः' wondering what He desires to say in coming here, तूष्णीम् अभवन् remained silent.

Simple Meaning: Upon seeing Balarama arrive, Yudhishthira, the twin brothers Nakula and Sahadeva, as well as Krishna and Arjuna offered Him their respectful obeisances They then stood in silence wondering what He intended to say by arriving at the battlefield at that crucial moment.

गदापाणी उभौ दृष्ट्वा संरब्धौ विजयैषिणौ ।
मण्डलानि विचित्राणि चरन्ताविदमब्रवीत् ॥10.79.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: गदा-पाणी carrying maces in their hands, उभौ both Bhima and Duryodhana, दृष्ट्वा having seen, संरब्धौ enraged, विजय-एषिणौ desirous of victory, मण्डलानि intricate circular footwork positions, विचित्राणि various and wonderful, चरन्तौ executing, इदम् these words, अब्रवीत् spoke

Anvaya: (रामः) गदापाणी संरब्धौ विजयैषिणौ विचित्राणि मण्डलानि चरन्तौ उभौ दृष्ट्वा इदम् अब्रवीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (रामः) Lord Balarama, गदापाणी holding maces in their hands, संरब्धौ deeply enraged, विजयैषिणौ desirous of victory, विचित्राणि मण्डलानि चरन्तौ executing various wonderful circular movements, उभौ दृष्ट्वा having seen both of them, इदम् अब्रवीत् spoke these words.

Simple Meaning: Seeing both Bhima and Duryodhana maces in hand, enraged and determined on victory as they circled each other with complex and skillful footwork, Balarama addressed them with the following words.

युवां तुल्यबलौ वीरौ हे राजन् हे वृकोदर ।
एकं प्राणाधिकं मन्ये उतैकं शिक्षयाधिकम् ॥10.79.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: युवाम् both of you, तुल्य-बलौ of equal strength, वीरौ O heroes, हे राजन् O King Duryodhana, हे वृकोदर O Bhima of ravenous appetite, एकम् one of you (Bhima), प्राण-अधिकम् superior in physical power and vitality, मन्ये I consider, उत and on the other hand, एकम् one of you (Duryodhana), शिक्षया-अधिकम् superior in technical skill and training

Anvaya: हे राजन्! हे वृकोदर! युवां तुल्यबलौ वीरौ (अस्थः)। एकं प्राणाधिकं मन्ये, उत एकं शिक्षयाधिकं (मन्ये)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: हे राजन् O King Duryodhana, हे वृकोदर O Bhimasena, युवां both of you, तुल्यबलौ वीरौ are heroes of equal prowess. एकं प्राणाधिकं मन्ये I consider one of you superior in raw physical strength, उत and, एकं शिक्षयाधिकं (मन्ये) I consider the other superior in technical training and technique.

Simple Meaning: O King Duryodhana! O Bhimasena! Both of you are heroes of equal prowess. I consider Bhima to be superior in raw strength and physical power while I consider Duryodhana to be superior in technical skill and training.

तस्मादेकतरस्येह युवयोः समवीर्ययोः ।
न लक्ष्यते जयोऽन्यो वा विरमत्वफलो रणः ॥10.79.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मात् therefore, एकतरस्य of either of the two, इह in this battle, युवयोः of both of you, सम-वीर्ययोः who are equal in prowess, न not, लक्ष्यते is foreseen or observed, जयः victory, अन्यः defeat or loss, वा or, विरमतु let it cease, अफलः fruitless, रणः combat

Anvaya: तस्मात् समवीर्ययोः युवयोः इह एकतरस्य जयः अन्यः वा न लक्ष्यते, (अतः) अफलः रणः विरमतु।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तस्मात् Therefore, समवीर्ययोः युवयोः of both of you who are equal in strength, इह in this contest, एकतरस्य for either one, जयः victory, अन्यः वा or defeat, न लक्ष्यते is not indicated, (अतः) therefore, अफलः रणः this fruitless battle, विरमतु let it cease.

Simple Meaning: Therefore, since both of you are equal in prowess, neither decisive victory nor defeat can be foreseen for either of you in this contest. Let this fruitless duel be brought to an end.

न तद्वाक्यं जगृहतुर्बद्धवैरौ नृपार्थवत् ।
अनुस्मरन्तावन्योन्यं दुरुक्तं दुष्कृतानि च ॥10.79.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, तत् that, वाक्यम् word of advice, जगृहतुः accepted, बद्ध-वैरौ rooted in deep enmity, नृप O King Parikshit, अर्थवत् beneficial, अनुस्मरन्तौ remembering, अन्योन्यम् each other's, दुरुक्तम् harsh words, दुष्कृतानि malicious acts, च and

Anvaya: नृप! बद्धवैरौ अन्योन्यं दुरुक्तं दुष्कृतानि च अनुस्मरन्तौ (तौ) तत् अर्थवत् वाक्यं न जगृहतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya:  नृप O King Parikshit, बद्धवैरौ established in deep-seated hatred, अन्योन्यं each other's, दुरुक्तं harsh insults, दुष्कृतानि च and evil deeds, अनुस्मरन्तौ remembering, (तौ) both of them, तत् अर्थवत् वाक्यं that beneficial advice, न जगृहतुः did not accept.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit! Locked in mutual hatred both Bhima and Duryodhana recalled each other's past insults and malicious acts. Consequently neither of them accepted Balarama's words of advice.

दिष्टं तदनुमन्वानो रामो द्वारवतीं ययौ ।
उग्रसेनादिभिः प्रीतैर्ज्ञातिभिः समुपागतः ॥10.79.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: दिष्टम् destiny or divine ordinance, तत् that, अनुमन्वानः accepting, रामः Balarama, द्वारवतीम् to Dvaraka, ययौ departed, उग्रसेन-आदिभिः headed by King Ugrasena, प्रीतैः joyfully, ज्ञातिभिः by His relatives, समुपागतः was warmly received

Anvaya: तत् दिष्टम् अनुमन्वानः रामः द्वारवतीं ययौ। प्रीतैः उग्रसेनादिभिः ज्ञातिभिः समुपागतः (च अभवत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत् दिष्टम् अनुमन्वानः Accepting that inevitable decree of destiny, रामः Lord Balarama, द्वारवतीं ययौ departed for Dwaraka. प्रीतैः उग्रसेनादिभिः ज्ञातिभिः समुपागतः (च अभवत्) And was joyfully welcomed by His relatives headed by King Ugrasena.

Simple Meaning: Recognizing that their conflict was driven by inescapable destiny, Balarama turned away from the battlefield and returned to Dwaraka. Upon His arrival He was received with great joy and affection by King Ugrasena and His other kinsmen.

तं पुनर्नैमिषं प्राप्तमृषयोऽयाजयन् मुदा ।
क्रत्वङ्गं क्रतुभिः सर्वैर्निवृत्ताखिलविग्रहम् ॥10.79.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् Him (Lord Balarama), पुनः again, नैमिषम् to Naimisharanya, प्राप्तम् who had arrived, ऋषयः the sages, अयाजयन् performed sacrifices for Him, मुदा joyfully, क्रतु-अङ्गम् who is the personification of sacrifice, क्रतुभिः with various sacrifices, सर्वैः all kinds of, निवृत्त-अखिल-विग्रहम् who had ceased all fighting and dualities

Anvaya: सर्वैः क्रतुभिः क्रत्वङ्गं निवृत्ताखिलविग्रहं तं पुनः नैमिषं प्राप्तम् ऋषयः मुदा अयाजयन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सर्वैः क्रतुभिः With all kinds of Vedic sacrifices, क्रत्वङ्गं who is the very embodiment and limbs of sacrifice, निवृत्ताखिलविग्रहं who has completely turned away from all conflict, तं Him, पुनः again, नैमिषं प्राप्तम् who arrived at Naimisharanya, ऋषयः the sages, मुदा अयाजयन् joyfully conducted sacrifices for Him.

Simple Meaning: When Balarama who is the personification of Vedic sacrifice and completely aloof from all earthly conflict arrived once again at Naimisharanya, the assembled sages joyfully performed all types of sacred sacrifices on His behalf.

तेभ्यो विशुद्धविज्ञानं भगवान् व्यतरद् विभुः ।
येनैवात्मन्यदो विश्वमात्मानं विश्वगं विदुः ॥10.79.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: तेभ्यः to those sages, विशुद्ध-विज्ञानम् pure transcendental knowledge, भगवान् the Supreme Lord Balarama, व्यतरत् imparted, विभुः the all-powerful Lord, येन by which knowledge, एव indeed, आत्मनि within the Supreme Self, अदः this, विश्वम् universe, आत्मानम् the Supreme Lord, विश्व-गम् pervasive throughout the universe, विदुः they realized

Anvaya: विभुः भगवान् तेभ्यः विशुद्धविज्ञानं व्यतरत्, येन एव अदः विश्वम् आत्मनि आत्मानं विश्वगं (च) विदुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विभुः भगवान् The all-powerful Supreme Lord Balarama, तेभ्यः to those sages, विशुद्धविज्ञानं व्यतरत् imparted pure transcendent realization, येन एव by which alone, अदः विश्वम् आत्मनि (स्थितम्) this entire universe as resting within the Self, आत्मानं (च) विश्वगं विदुः and the Self as pervading all creation, they came to realize.

Simple Meaning: The all-powerful Supreme Lord Balarama imparted pure transcendental knowledge unto the sages of Naimisharanya. Through this divine realization they clearly understood how this entire universe resides within the Supreme Lord and how the Supreme Lord pervades every part of creation.

स्वपत्न्यावभृथस्नातो ज्ञातिबन्धुसुहृद्वृतः ।
रेजे स्वज्योत्स्नयेवेन्दुः सुवासाः सुष्ठ्वलङ्कृतः ॥10.79.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्व-पत्न्या with His own wife (Revati), अवभृथ-स्नातः having performed the final purifying bath (avabhritha) of the sacrifice, ज्ञाति-बन्धु-सुहृद्-वृतः surrounded by His relatives, family members, and well-wishing friends, रेजे shone resplendently, स्व-ज्योत्स्नया with its own light, इव like, इन्दुः the full moon, सु-वासाः wearing elegant garments, सुष्ठु-अलङ्कृतः magnificently adorned with ornaments

Anvaya: स्वपत्न्या अवभृथस्नातः ज्ञातिबन्धुसुहृद्वृतः सुवासाः सुष्ठ्वलङ्कृतः (सः) स्वज्योत्स्नया इन्दुः इव रेजे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: स्वपत्न्या अवभृथस्नातः Having taken the ceremonial concluding bath of the sacrifice alongside His wife, ज्ञातिबन्धुसुहृद्वृतः surrounded by His kinsmen, relatives, and beloved friends, सुवासाः wearing exquisite attire, सुष्ठ्वलङ्कृतः beautifully adorned with fine ornaments, (सः) He, स्वज्योत्स्नया इन्दुः इव रेजे shone brightly like the full moon surrounded by its own luminous moonlight.

Simple Meaning: Having taken the ceremonial final bath of the sacrifice along with His wife Revati, Lord Balarama stood beautifully attired and magnificently adorned with fine ornaments. Surrounded by His kinsmen, relatives, and dear friends, He shone with brilliant splendor, looking just like the full moon illuminated by its own radiance.

ईदृग्विधान्यसंख्यानि बलस्य बलशालिनः ।
अनन्तस्याप्रमेयस्य मायामर्त्यस्य सन्ति हि ॥10.79.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: ईदृग्विधानि of such nature, असंख्यानि innumerable, बलस्य of Lord Balarama, बल-शालिनः who is endowed with supreme strength, अनन्तस्य of the unlimited Lord, अप्रमेयस्य of the immeasurable Lord, माया-मर्त्यस्य who assumes a human form through His divine potency, सन्ति are indeed, हि certainly

Anvaya: बलशालिनः अनन्तस्य अप्रमेयस्य मायामर्त्यस्य बलस्य ईदृग्विधानि असंख्यानि (चरितानि) सन्ति हि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बलशालिनः Of the supremely powerful, अनन्तस्य of the limitless, अप्रमेयस्य of the incomprehensible, मायामर्त्यस्य of Him who appears as a mortal through His divine potency, बलस्य of Lord Balarama, ईदृग्विधानि असंख्यानि (चरितानि) innumerable pastimes of this nature, सन्ति हि indeed exist.

Simple Meaning: Innumerable pastimes of this sacred nature belong to Lord Balarama who possesses unlimited strength and immeasurable glory. Though He is the eternal infinite Lord, He plays the role of a human being through His divine potency.

योऽनुस्मरेत रामस्य कर्माण्यद्भुतकर्मणः ।
सायं प्रातरनन्तस्य विष्णोः स दयितो भवेत् ॥10.79.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः whoever, अनुस्मरेत remembers or meditates upon, रामस्य of Lord Balarama, कर्माणि the glorious activities and deeds, अद्भुत-कर्मणः of Him whose deeds are wondrous, सायम् in the evening, प्रातः in the morning, अनन्तस्य of the infinite Lord, विष्णोः of Lord Vishnu, सः he, दयितः dear and beloved, भवेत् becomes

Anvaya: यः सायं प्रातः अद्भुतकर्मणः अनन्तस्य रामस्य कर्माणि अनुस्मरेत, सः विष्णोः दयितः भवेत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः Whoever, सायं प्रातः evening and morning, अद्भुतकर्मणः of Him who performs wondrous deeds, अनन्तस्य of the unlimited, रामस्य of Lord Balarama, कर्माणि deeds, अनुस्मरेत meditates upon or remembers, सः he, विष्णोः to Lord Vishnu, दयितः भवेत् becomes exceedingly dear.

Simple Meaning: Whoever regularly remembers and meditates upon the wondrous deeds of the infinite Lord Balarama both in the morning and in the evening, becomes deeply beloved of Lord Vishnu.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “बल्वलवधः बलरामतीर्थयात्रानिरूपणं च नाम​” एकोनाशीतितमः अध्यायः ॥79॥

Thus ends the seventy-ninth discourse named “Balvala killed and an account of the pilgrimage of Balarama”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
