श्री शुक उवाच
तं विलोक्य विनिष्क्रान्तमुज्जिहानमिवोडुपम् ।
दर्शनीयतमं श्यामं पीतकौशेयवाससम् ॥10.51.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, तम् Him (Krishna), विलोक्य seeing, विनिष्क्रान्तम् walking out, उज्जिहानम् rising, इव like, उडुपम् the moon, दर्शनीयतमम् most beautiful to behold, श्यामम् dark-complexioned, पीत-कौशेय-वाससम् wearing yellow silk garments

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— उज्जिहानम् उडुपम् इव विनिष्क्रान्तं दर्शनीयतमं श्यामं पीतकौशेयवाससं तं विलोक्य…

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, उज्जिहानम् Rising, उडुपम् moon, इव like, विनिष्क्रान्तं walking out, दर्शनीयतमं most beautiful to behold, श्यामं dark-complexioned, पीतकौशेयवाससं wearing yellow silk garments, तं Him, विलोक्य seeing…

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Seeing Him walk out of the city like the rising moon—exceedingly beautiful to behold, dark-complexioned, and dressed in brilliant yellow silk garments…

श्रीवत्सवक्षसं भ्राजत्कौस्तुभामुक्तकन्धरम् ।
पृथुदीर्घचतुर्बाहुं नवकञ्जारुणेक्षणम् ॥10.51.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रीवत्स-वक्षसम् bearing the Shrivatsa mark on His chest, भ्राजत्-कौस्तुभ-आमुक्त-कन्धरम् whose neck was adorned with the brilliant Kaustubha gem, पृथु-दीर्घ-चतुर्-बाहुम् having four broad and long arms, नव-कञ्ज-अरुण-ईक्षणम् having eyes as reddish as a newly bloomed lotus

Anvaya: श्रीवत्सवक्षसं भ्राजत्कौस्तुभामुक्तकन्धरं पृथुदीर्घचतुर्बाहुं नवकञ्जारुणेक्षणम्…

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्रीवत्सवक्षसं Bearing the Shrivatsa mark on His chest, भ्राजत्कौस्तुभामुक्तकन्धरं whose neck was adorned with the brilliant Kaustubha gem, पृथुदीर्घचतुर्बाहुं having four broad and long arms, नवकञ्जारुणेक्षणम् having eyes as reddish as a newly bloomed lotus…

Simple Meaning: Bearing the mark of Shrivatsa on His chest, with His neck adorned by the brilliant Kaustubha gem, He possessed four broad and long arms, and His eyes were as beautifully reddish as a newly bloomed lotus.

नित्यप्रमुदितं श्रीमत्सुकपोलं शुचिस्मितम् ।
मुखारविन्दं बिभ्राणं स्फुरन्मकरकुण्डलम् ॥10.51.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: नित्य-प्रमुदितम् always cheerful, श्रीमत्-सु-कपोलम् having beautiful and glorious cheeks, शुचि-स्मितम् possessing a pure smile, मुख-अरविन्दम् a lotus-like face, बिभ्राणम् bearing, स्फुरत्-मकर-कुण्डलम् with glittering shark-shaped earrings

Anvaya: नित्यप्रमुदितं श्रीमत्सुकपोलं शुचिस्मितं स्फुरन्मकरकुण्डलं मुखारविन्दं बिभ्राणम्…

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नित्यप्रमुदितं Always cheerful, श्रीमत्सुकपोलं having beautiful and glorious cheeks, शुचिस्मितं possessing a pure smile, स्फुरन्मकरकुण्डलं with glittering shark-shaped earrings, मुखारविन्दं a lotus-like face, बिभ्राणम् bearing…

Simple Meaning: He bore a lotus-like face that was always cheerful, with beautiful glorious cheeks, a pure smile, and glittering shark-shaped earrings.

वासुदेवो ह्ययमिति पुमाञ्छ्रीवत्सलाञ्छनः ।
चतुर्भुजोऽरविन्दाक्षो वनमाल्यतिसुन्दरः ॥10.51.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: वासुदेवः Vasudeva, हि indeed, अयम् this, इति thus, पुमान् the person, श्रीवत्स-लाञ्छनः bearing the symbol of Shrivatsa, चतुर्-भुजः four-armed, अरविन्द-अक्षः lotus-eyed, वन-माली wearing a forest garland, अति-सुन्दरः extraordinarily beautiful

Anvaya: श्रीवत्सलाञ्छनः चतुर्भुजः अरविन्दाक्षः वनमाली अतिसुन्दरः अयं पुमान् हि वासुदेवः इति…

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्रीवत्सलाञ्छनः Bearing the symbol of Shrivatsa, चतुर्भुजः four-armed, अरविन्दाक्षः lotus-eyed, वनमाली अतिसुन्दरः wearing a forest garland and extraordinarily beautiful, अयं this, पुमान् person, हि indeed, वासुदेवः Vasudeva, इति thus…

Simple Meaning: Bearing the symbol of Shrivatsa, four-armed, lotus-eyed, wearing a forest garland, and extraordinarily beautiful—this person must indeed be Vasudeva.

लक्षणैर्नारदप्रोक्तैर्नान्यो भवितुमर्हति ।
निरायुधश्चलन् पद्भ्यां योत्स्येऽनेन निरायुधः॥10.51.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: लक्षणैः by the characteristics, नारद-प्रोक्तैः described by Narada, न not, अन्यः anyone else, भवितुम् to be, अर्हति can, निरायुधः unarmed, चलन् walking, पद्भ्याम् on foot, योत्स्ये I shall fight, अनेन with Him, निरायुधः unarmed

Anvaya: नारदप्रोक्तैः लक्षणैः अन्यः न भवितुम् अर्हति। निरायुधः पद्भ्यां चलन् अनेन निरायुधः योत्स्ये।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नारदप्रोक्तैः Described by Narada, लक्षणैः by the characteristics, अन्यः anyone else, न भवितुम् अर्हति cannot be. निरायुधः Unarmed, पद्भ्यां on foot, चलन् walking, अनेन with Him, निरायुधः unarmed, योत्स्ये I shall fight.

Simple Meaning: Judging by the characteristics described by Narada, this person cannot be anyone else but Vasudeva. Since He is walking on foot and unarmed, I shall also fight with Him without any weapons.

इति निश्चित्य यवनः प्राद्रवन्तं पराङ्मुखम् ।
अन्वधावज्जिघृक्षुस्तं दुरापमपि योगिनाम् ॥10.51.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, निश्चित्य having decided, यवनः the Yavana (Kalayavana), प्राद्रवन्तम् fleeing, पराङ्मुखम् with His back turned, अन्वधावत् ran after, जिघृक्षुः desiring to capture, तम् Him, दुरापम् difficult to attain, अपि even, योगिनाम् for yogis

Anvaya: इति निश्चित्य यवनः पराङ्मुखं प्राद्रवन्तं योगिनाम् अपि दुरापं तं जिघृक्षुः अन्वधावत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति निश्चित्य Thus, having decided, यवनः the Yavana, पराङ्मुखं with His back turned, प्राद्रवन्तं fleeing, योगिनाम् for yogis, अपि even, दुरापं difficult to attain, तं Him, जिघृक्षुः desiring to capture, अन्वधावत् ran after.

Simple Meaning: Having thus decided, the Yavana ran after Him, who was fleeing with His back turned. He desired to capture Him who is difficult to attain even for the greatest yogis.

हस्तप्राप्तमिवात्मानं हरिणा स पदे पदे ।
नीतो दर्शयता दूरं यवनेशोऽद्रिकन्दरम् ॥10.51.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: हस्त-प्राप्तम् within the reach of his hand, इव as if, आत्मानम् Himself, हरिणा by Lord Hari, सः he (Kalayavana), पदे पदे at every step, नीतः was led, दर्शयता showing, दूरम् far away, यवन-ईशः the lord of the Yavanas, अद्रि-कन्दरम् to a mountain cave

Anvaya: हरिणा पदे पदे आत्मानं हस्तप्राप्तम् इव दर्शयता सः यवनेशः दूरम् अद्रिकन्दरं नीतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: हरिणा By Lord Hari, पदे पदे at every step, आत्मानं Himself, हस्तप्राप्तम् within the reach of his hand, इव as if, दर्शयता showing, सः he, यवनेशः the lord of the Yavanas, दूरम् far away, अद्रिकन्दरं to a mountain cave, नीतः was led.

Simple Meaning: By showing Himself at every step as if He were within the reach of his hand, Lord Hari led the lord of the Yavanas far away to a mountain cave.

पलायनं यदुकुले जातस्य तव नोचितम् ।
इति क्षिपन्ननुगतो नैनं प्रापाहताशुभः ॥10.51.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: पलायनम् fleeing, यदु-कुले in the Yadu dynasty, जातस्य of one born, तव Your, न not, उचितम् proper, इति thus, क्षिपन् taunting, अनुगतः following, न not, एनम् Him, प्राप attained/reached, अहत-अशुभः one whose sins were not destroyed

Anvaya: यदुकुले जातस्य तव पलायनं न उचितम् — इति क्षिपन् अनुगतः अहताशुभः एनं न प्राप।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यदुकुले जातस्य Of the one born In the Yadu dynasty, तव Your, पलायनं fleeing, न उचितम् is not proper, इति क्षिपन् thus, taunting, अनुगतः of the one following, अहताशुभः the one whose sins were not destroyed, एनं Him, न प्राप did not reach.

Simple Meaning: Taunting Him by saying, "It is not proper for one born in the Yadu dynasty to flee," he followed Him, but because his sins were not yet destroyed, he could not reach Him.

एवं क्षिप्तोऽपि भगवान् प्राविशद् गिरिकन्दरम् ।
सोऽपि प्रविष्टस्तत्रान्यं शयानं ददृशे नरम् ॥10.51.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, क्षिप्तः taunted, अपि even though, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, प्राविशत् entered, गिरि-कन्दरम् a mountain cave, सः he (Kalayavana), अपि also, प्रविष्टः entering, तत्र there, अन्यम् another, शयानम् lying down/sleeping, ददृशे saw, नरम् a man

Anvaya: एवं क्षिप्तः अपि भगवान् गिरिकन्दरं प्राविशत्। सः अपि प्रविष्टः तत्र अन्यं शयानं नरं ददृशे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवं Thus, क्षिप्तः taunted, अपि even though, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, गिरिकन्दरं a mountain cave, प्राविशत् entered. सः He, अपि also, प्रविष्टः entering, तत्र there, अन्यं another, शयानं sleeping, नरं man, ददृशे saw.

Simple Meaning: Even though he was taunted in this manner, the Supreme Lord entered the mountain cave. Entering after Him, the Yavana also saw another man lying asleep there.

नन्वसौ दूरमानीय शेते मामिह साधुवत् ।
इति मत्वाच्युतं मूढस्तं पदा समताडयत् ॥10.51.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: ननु certainly, असौ this person, दूरम् far away, आनीय having led, शेते is sleeping, माम् me, इह here, साधु-वत् like a saintly person, इति thus, मत्वा thinking, अच्युतम् Lord Achyuta (Krishna), मूढः the foolish one, तम् him (the sleeping man), पदा with his foot, समताडयत् kicked

Anvaya: ननु असौ मां दूरम् आनीय इह साधुवत् शेते – इति मूढः तम् अच्युतं मत्वा पदा समताडयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ननु Certainly, असौ this person, मां me, दूरम् far away, आनीय having led, इह here, साधुवत् like a saintly person, शेते is sleeping. इति Thus, मूढः the foolish one, तम् him (the sleeping person), अच्युतं मत्वा thinking to be Lord Achyuta, पदा with his foot, समताडयत् kicked.

Simple Meaning: Thinking, "Certainly this person has led me far away and is now sleeping here like a saintly man," the foolish Yavana mistook the sleeping man for Lord Achyuta and kicked him with his foot.

स उत्थाय चिरं सुप्तः शनैरुन्मील्य लोचने ।
दिशो विलोकयन् पार्श्वे तमद्राक्षीदवस्थितम् ॥10.51.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, उत्थाय having arisen, चिरम् for a long time, सुप्तः sleeping, शनैः slowly, उन्मील्य opening, लोचने his eyes, दिशः the directions, विलोकयन् looking around, पार्श्वे by his side, तम् him (Kalayavana), अद्राक्षीत् saw, अवस्थितम् standing

Anvaya: चिरं सुप्तः सः उत्थाय शनैः लोचने उन्मील्य दिशः विलोकयन् पार्श्वे अवस्थितं तम् अद्राक्षीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: चिरं सुप्तः One who was sleeping for a long time, सः he, उत्थाय having arisen, शनैः slowly, लोचने eyes, उन्मील्य opening, दिशः the directions, विलोकयन् looking around, पार्श्वे by his side, अवस्थितं standing, तम् him, अद्राक्षीत् saw.

Simple Meaning: Having been asleep for a long time, he rose and slowly opened his eyes. Looking around in all directions, he saw the Yavana standing by his side.

स तावत्तस्य रुष्टस्य दृष्टिपातेन भारत ।
देहजेनाग्निना दग्धो भस्मसादभवत् क्षणात् ॥10.51.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Kalayavana), तावत् then, तस्य of him (the sleeping person), रुष्टस्य who was angry, दृष्टि-पातेन by the falling of his glance, भारत O descendant of Bharata (Parikshit), देह-जेन born from the body, अग्निना by fire, दग्धः burned, भस्मसात् the state of being ash, अभवत् became, क्षणात् in a moment

भस्मसात्  भस्मसात् अव्य. - भस्मन्+साति राख की स्थिति में src. Kosha Ashtadhyayi

Anvaya: भारत! तस्य रुष्टस्य दृष्टिपातेन तावत् देहजेन अग्निना सः दग्धः क्षणात् भस्मसात् अभवत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भारत O descendant of Bharata, तस्य रुष्टस्य Of him who was angry, दृष्टिपातेन by the glance, तावत् then, देहजेन born from the body, अग्निना by fire, सः दग्धः he burned, क्षणात् in a moment, भस्मसात् अभवत् became reduced to ashes.

Simple Meaning: O descendant of Bharata, by the glance of that angry man, Kalayavana was instantly burned by a fire born from the man's own body and was reduced to ashes in a single moment.

राजा उवाच
को नाम स पुमान् ब्रह्मन् कस्य किंवीर्य एव च ।
कस्माद् गुहां गतः शिश्ये किन्तेजो यवनार्दनः॥10.51.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजा उवाच the King (Parikshit) said, कः who, नाम by name, सः that, पुमान् man, ब्रह्मन् O Brahmana (Shukadeva), कस्य whose (son), किम् वीर्यः of what prowess, एव indeed, च and, कस्मात् for what reason, गुहाम् the cave, गतः entering, शिश्ये lay down to sleep, किम् तेजः of what power, यवन-अर्दनः the destroyer of the Yavana

Anvaya: राजा उवाच— ब्रह्मन्! यवनार्दनः सः पुमान् कः नाम कस्य च किं वीर्य एव कस्मात् गुहां गतः शिश्ये? किं तेजः?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजा उवाच The King said, ब्रह्मन् O Brahmana, यवनार्दनः The destroyer of the Yavana, सः पुमान् that man, कः नाम who by name?, कस्य Whose son?, च and, किं वीर्य एव Of what prowess indeed?, कस्मात् For what reason, गुहां the cave, गतः entering, शिश्ये lay down to sleep?, किं तेजः Of what power?

Simple Meaning: King Parikshit said: O Brahmana, The man who killed Kalayavana, what was his name? Whose son was he? What was his prowess? For what reason did he enter that cave to sleep? And of what power was he?

श्री शुक उवाच
स इक्ष्वाकुकुले जातो मान्धातृतनयो महान् ।
मुचुकुन्द इति ख्यातो ब्रह्मण्यः सत्यसङ्गरः ॥10.51.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, सः he, इक्ष्वाकु-कुले in the dynasty of Ikshvaku, जातः born, मान्धातृ-तनयः the son of Mandhata, महान् great, मुचुकुन्दः Muchukunda, इति as, ख्यातः famous, ब्रह्मण्यः devoted to the Brahmanas, सत्य-सङ्गरः true to his vow

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— सः इक्ष्वाकुकुले जातः मान्धातृतनयः महान् मुचुकुन्दः इति ख्यातः, ब्रह्मण्यः सत्यसङ्गरः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, सः He, इक्ष्वाकुकुले in the dynasty of Ikshvaku, जातः born, मान्धातृतनयः the son of Mandhata, महान् great, मुचुकुन्दः Muchukunda, इति as, ख्यातः famous, ब्रह्मण्यः devoted to the Brahmanas, सत्यसङ्गरः true to his vow.

प्रार्थितः प्रचुरं पूर्वं त्वयाहं भक्तवत्सलः ॥10.51.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः अहम् I that very person, तव your, अनुग्रह-अर्थम् for the sake of showing mercy, गुहाम् cave, एताम् this, उपागतः have approached, प्रार्थना-कृतः requested, प्रचुरम् extensively, पूर्वम् in the past, त्वया by you, अहम् I, भक्त-वत्सलः who is affectionate to his devotees

Anvaya: पूर्वं त्वया अहं प्रचुरं प्रार्थनाकृतः। सः भक्तवत्सलः अहं तव अनुग्रहार्थं एतां गुहाम् उपागतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पूर्वं In the past, त्वया by you, अहं I, प्रचुरं प्रार्थनाकृतः (was) extensively requested. सः भक्तवत्सलः अहं That very person, who is affectionate to his devotees, I, तव अनुग्रहार्थं for the sake of showing mercy to you, एतां गुहाम् this cave, उपागतः have approached.

Simple Meaning: In the past, you prayed to me extensively. I, who am always affectionate to my devotees, have come to this cave to show you my mercy.

वरान् वृणीष्व राजर्षे सर्वान् कामान् ददामि ते ।
मां प्रपन्नो जनः कश्चिन्न भूयोऽर्हति शोचितुम् ॥10.51.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: वरान् benedictions, वृणीष्व please choose, राज-ऋषे O saintly king, सर्वान् all, कामान् desires, ददामि I give, ते to you, माम् unto me, प्रपन्नः surrendered, जनः a person, कश्चित् any, न not, भूयः again, अर्हति deserves, शोचितुम् to lament

Anvaya: राजर्षे! वरान् वृणीष्व। ते सर्वान् कामान् ददामि। मां प्रपन्नः कश्चित् जनः भूयः शोचितुं न अर्हति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजर्षे O saintly king, वरान् वृणीष्व Please choose your benedictions. ते To you, सर्वान् कामान् all desires, ददामि I give. मां Unto me, प्रपन्नः surrendered, कश्चित् जनः any a person, भूयः again, शोचितुं न अर्हति does not deserve to lament.

Simple Meaning: O saintly king, please choose whatever benedictions you desire. I shall grant them all to you. Anyone who has fully surrendered unto me never has to lament again.

श्री शुक उवाच
इत्युक्तस्तं प्रणम्याह मुचुकुन्दो मुदान्वितः ।
ज्ञात्वा नारायणं देवं गर्गवाक्यमनुस्मरन् ॥10.51.45॥

Word-by-word meaning श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति thus, उक्तः addressed, तम् to Him, प्रणम्य bowing down, आह said, मुचुकुन्दः Muchukunda, मुद्-अन्वितः filled with joy, ज्ञात्वा recognizing, नारायणम् Narayana, देवम् the Supreme Lord, गर्ग-वाक्यम् the words of Garga Muni, अनुस्मरन् remembering

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच — इति उक्तः मुचुकुन्दः गर्गवाक्यम् अनुस्मरन् देवं नारायणं ज्ञात्वा मुदान्वितः तं प्रणम्य आह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति उक्तः Thus addressed, मुचुकुन्दः Muchukunda, गर्गवाक्यम् अनुस्मरन् remembering the words of Garga Muni, देवं नारायणं ज्ञात्वा recognizing the Supreme Lord, Narayana, मुदान्वितः filled with joy, तं प्रणम्य bowing down to Him, आह said.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Thus addressed, Muchukunda remembered the prophetic words of the sage Garga Muni. Recognizing the visitor to be the Supreme Lord Narayana, the king was filled with joy. He bowed down before him and spoke as follows.

मुचुकुन्द उवाच
विमोहितोऽयं जन ईश मायया त्वदीयया त्वां न भजत्यनर्थदृक् ।
सुखाय दुःखप्रभवेषु सज्जते गृहेषु योषित् पुरुषश्च वञ्चितः ॥10.51.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: मुचुकुन्दः उवाच Muchukunda said, विमोहितः deluded, अयम् this, जनः person, ईश O Lord, मायया by the illusory energy, त्वदीयया your, त्वाम् you, न not, भजति worships, अनर्थ-दृक् seeing things that are meaningless, सुखाय for the sake of happiness, दुःख-प्रभवेषु in sources of misery, सज्जते becomes attached, गृहेषु in household life, योषित् a woman, पुरुषः a man, च and, वञ्चितः cheated

Anvaya: मुचुकुन्दः उवाच—ईश! त्वदीयया मायया विमोहितः अनर्थदृक् अयं जनः त्वां न भजति। वञ्चितः पुरुषः योषित् च सुखाय दुःखप्रभवेषु गृहेषु सज्जते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मुचुकुन्दः उवाच Muchukunda said, ईश O Lord, त्वदीयया मायया by Your the illusory energy, विमोहितः deluded, अनर्थदृक् seeing things that are meaningless, अयं जनः this person, त्वां न भजति do not worship You. वञ्चितः पुरुषः योषित् च Cheated, man and  woman, सुखाय for the sake of happiness, दुःखप्रभवेषु गृहेषु in sources of misery, the household life, सज्जते becomes attached.

Simple Meaning: Muchukunda said: O Lord, this world of people is completely deluded by your illusory energy. Distorted in their vision, they do not worship you, but instead, being cheated, men and women become attached to household life, seeking happiness in things that are actually the very sources of misery.

लब्ध्वा जनो दुर्लभमत्र मानुषं कथञ्चिदव्यङ्गमयत्नतोऽनघ ।
पादारविन्दं न भजत्यसन्मतिर्गृहान्धकूपे पतितो यथा पशुः॥10.51.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: लब्ध्वा having attained, जनः a person, दुर्लभम् rare, अत्र in this world, मानुषम् human birth, कथञ्चित् somehow or other, अव्यङ्गम् with unimpaired senses, अयत्नतः effortlessly, अनघ O sinless one, पाद-अरविन्दम् the lotus feet, न not, भजति worships, असत्-मतिः whose intelligence is contaminated by material desires, गृह-अन्ध-कूपे into the blind well of household life, पतितः fallen, यथा just like, पशुः an animal

Anvaya: अनघ! अत्र कथञ्चित् अयत्नतः दुर्लभम् अव्यङ्गं मानुषं लब्ध्वा असन्मतिः जनः गृहान्धकूपे पतितः पशुः यथा पादारविन्दं न भजति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अनघ O sinless one, अत्र In this world, कथञ्चित् somehow or other, अयत्नतः effortlessly, दुर्लभम् rare, अव्यङ्गं with unimpaired senses, मानुषं human birth, लब्ध्वा having attained, असन्मतिः जनः a person whose intelligence is contaminated by material desires, गृहान्धकूपे into the blind well of household life, पतितः fallen, पशुः an animal, यथा just like, पादारविन्दं the lotus feet, न भजति does not worship.

Simple Meaning: O sinless one, after somehow or other effortlessly attaining this rare human birth with sound senses, a person whose intelligence is contaminated by material desires still fails to worship your lotus feet. Instead, like a foolish animal, he falls directly into the blind well of household life.

ममैष कालोऽजित निष्फलो गतो राज्यश्रियोन्नद्धमदस्य भूपतेः ।
मर्त्यात्मबुद्धेः सुतदारकोशभूष्वासज्जमानस्य दुरन्तचिन्तया ॥10.51.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: मम my, एषः this, कालः time, अजित O unconquerable one, निष्फलः fruitlessly, गतः passed, राज्य-श्रिया-उन्नद्ध-मदस्य of one whose pride was swollen by royal opulence, भूपतेः of a king, मर्त्य-आत्म-बुद्धेः possessing the mentality of identifying the mortal body as the self, सुत-दार-कोश-भूषु in sons, wife, treasury and land, आसज्जमानस्य of one who is constantly becoming attached, दुरन्त-चिन्तया with endless anxiety

Anvaya: अजित! राज्यश्रियोन्नद्धमदस्य मर्त्यात्मबुद्धेः सुतदारकोशभूषु दुरन्तचिन्तया आसज्जमानस्य भूपतेः मम एषः कालः निष्फलः गतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अजित O unconquerable one, राज्यश्रियोन्नद्धमदस्य Of one whose pride was swollen by royal opulence, मर्त्यात्मबुद्धेः possessing the mentality of identifying the mortal body as the self, सुतदारकोशभूषु in sons, wife, treasury and land, दुरन्तचिन्तया with endless anxiety, आसज्जमानस्य of one who is constantly becoming attached, भूपतेः of a king, मम my, एषः कालः this time, निष्फलः गतः has passed fruitlessly.

Simple Meaning: O unconquerable Lord, my time has passed completely fruitlessly. I was a king whose pride was swollen by royal opulence, maintaining the illusion that this mortal body is the self. Full of endless anxiety, I remained constantly attached to my children, my wife, my treasury, and my land.

कलेवरेऽस्मिन् घटकुड्यसन्निभे निरूढमानो नरदेव इत्यहम् ।
वृतो रथेभाश्वपदात्यनीकपैर्गां पर्यटंस्त्वागणयन् सुदुर्मदः ॥10.51.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: कलेवरे in the body, अस्मिन् this, घट-कुड्य-सन्निभे which is just like a clay pot or a wall, निरूढ-मानः possessing deep-rooted pride, नर-देवः a god among men, इति as, अहम् I, वृतः surrounded, रथ-इभ-अश्व-पदाति-अनीक-पैः by commanders of chariots, elephants, horses and infantry, गामम् the earth, पर्यटन् traveling around, त्वा you, अगणयन् not caring for, सु-दुर्मदः greatly deluded by pride

Anvaya: अस्मिन् घटकुड्यसन्निभे कलेवरे नरदेवः इति निरूढमानः रथेभाश्वपदात्यनीकपैः वृतः गां पर्यटन् सुदुर्मदः अहं त्वा अगणयन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अस्मिन् घटकुड्यसन्निभे कलेवरे In this body which is just like a clay pot or a wall, नरदेवः a god among men, इति as, निरूढमानः possessing deep-rooted pride, रथेभाश्वपदात्यनीकपैः वृतः surrounded by commanders of chariots, elephants, horses and infantry, गां पर्यटन् traveling around the earth, सुदुर्मदः अहं greatly deluded by pride, I, त्वा you, अगणयन् did not care for.

Simple Meaning: Possessing deep-rooted pride in this body, which is actually no better than a clay pot or a physical wall, I thought of myself as a god among men. Surrounded by my commanders of chariots, elephants, horses, and infantry, I traveled around the earth, completely deluded by pride and not caring for you at all.

प्रमत्तमुच्चैरितिकृत्यचिन्तया प्रवृद्धलोभं विषयेषु लालसम् ।
त्वमप्रमत्तः सहसाभिपद्यसे क्षुल्लेलिहानोऽहिरिवाखुमन्तकः ॥10.51.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रमत्तम् completely careless, उच्चैः greatly, इति-कृत्य-चिन्तया with thoughts of what is to be done, प्रवृद्ध-लोभम् whose greed is overgrown, विषयेषु in sense objects, लालसम् craving, त्वम् you, अप्रमत्तः being vigilant, सहसा suddenly, अभिपद्यसे approach, क्षुत्-लेलिहानः licking its lips with hunger, अहिः a snake, इव like, आखुम् a mouse, अन्तकः death

Anvaya: इतिकृत्यचिन्तया उच्चैः प्रमत्तं प्रवृद्धलोभं विषयेषु लालसम् अप्रमत्तः त्वम् अन्तकः – क्षुल्लेलिहानः अहिः आखुम् इव – सहसा अभिपद्यसे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इतिकृत्यचिन्तया With thoughts of what is to be done, उच्चैः greatly, प्रमत्तं completely careless, प्रवृद्धलोभं whose greed is overgrown, विषयेषु लालसम् craving in sense objects, अप्रमत्तः being vigilant, त्वम् अन्तकः you as death, क्षुल्लेलिहानः अहिः आखुम् इव like a snake licking its lips with hunger (towards) a mouse, सहसा अभिपद्यसे suddenly approach.

Simple Meaning: A person remains completely careless, overwhelmed by endless thoughts of what he must do, driven by overgrown greed, and craving sense objects. Yet, just as a hungry snake licking its lips suddenly pounces upon a mouse, you approach that person unexpectedly as a vigilant, inescapable death.

पुरा रथैर्हेमपरिष्कृतैश्चरन् मतङ्गजैर्वा नरदेवसंज्ञितः ।
स एव कालेन दुरत्ययेन ते कलेवरो विट्कृमिभस्मसंज्ञितः॥10.51.51॥

Word-by-word meaning: पुरा in the past, रथैः on chariots, हेम-परिष्कृतैः adorned with gold, चरन् moving about, मतङ्गजैः on majestic elephants, वा or, नर-देव-संज्ञितः known by the title king, सः that, एव indeed, कालेन by time, दुरत्ययेन insurmountable, ते your, कलेवरः the body, विट्-कृमि-भस्म-संज्ञितः known as feces worms or ashes

Anvaya: पुरा हेमपरिष्कृतैः रथैः मतङ्गजैः वा चरन् नरदेवसंज्ञितः सः एव कलेवरः ते दुरत्ययेन कालेन विट्कृमिभस्मसंज्ञितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पुरा In the past, हेमपरिष्कृतैः adorned with gold, रथैः on chariots, मतङ्गजैः on majestic elephants, वा or, चरन् moving about, नरदेवसंज्ञितः known by the title king, सः that, एव indeed, कलेवरः the body, ते your, दुरत्ययेन insurmountable, कालेन by Time, विट्कृमिभस्मसंज्ञितः known as feces worms or ashes.

Simple Meaning: In the past, that very body moved about on gold-adorned chariots or majestic elephants and was honored with the title of king. Yet, by the influence of Your insurmountable Time, that same body eventually receives the name of either feces, worms, or ashes.

निर्जित्य दिक्चक्रमभूतविग्रहो वरासनस्थः समराजवन्दितः ।
गृहेषु मैथुन्यसुखेषु योषितां क्रीडामृगः पूरुष ईश नीयते ॥10.51.52॥

Word-by-word meaning: निर्जित्य having conquered, दिक्-चक्रम् the entire circle of directions, अभूत-विग्रहः without any opposition, वर-आसन-स्थः seated upon an exalted throne, सम-राज-वन्दितः praised by equal kings, गृहेषु in houses, मैथुन्य-सुखेषु in the pleasures of sexual intercourse, योषिताम् of women, क्रीडा-मृगः a pet animal or toy, पूरुषः a man, ईश O Lord, नीयते is led around

Anvaya: ईश! दिक्चक्रं निर्जित्य अभूतविग्रहः वरासनस्थः समराजवन्दितः पूरुषः योषितां मैथुन्यसुखेषु गृहेषु क्रीडामृगः नीयते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ईश O Lord, दिक्चक्रं The entire circle of directions, निर्जित्य having conquered, अभूतविग्रहः without any opposition, वरासनस्थः seated upon an exalted throne, समराजवन्दितः praised by equal kings, पूरुषः a man, योषितां of women, मैथुन्यसुखेषु गृहेषु in houses of the pleasures of sexual intercourse, क्रीडामृगः a pet animal or toy, नीयते is led around.

Simple Meaning: O Lord, even after a man has conquered all directions without any opposition, sits upon an exalted throne, and is praised by rival kings, he is still led to the houses of the women for the sake of sexual pleasures as a pet animal.

करोति कर्माणि तपस्सुनिष्ठितो निवृत्तभोगस्तदपेक्षया ददत् ।
पुनश्च भूयासमहं स्वराडिति प्रवृद्धतर्षो न सुखाय कल्पते ॥10.51.53॥

Word-by-word meaning: करोति he performs, कर्माणि ritual activities, तपः-सुनिष्ठितः situated firmly in austerities, निवृत्त-भोगः having renounced sense enjoyment, तत्-अपेक्षया with the expectation of that reward, ददत् giving charity, पुनः च and again, भूयासम् may become, अहम् I, स्व-राट् a sovereign ruler, इति thus, प्रवृद्ध-तर्षः whose desires are overgrown, न not, सुखाय for real happiness, कल्पते is fit

Anvaya: तपस्सुनिष्ठितः निवृत्तभोगः ददत् कर्माणि करोति। तदपेक्षया अहं पुनः च स्वराट् भूयासम् — इति प्रवृद्धतर्षः सुखाय न कल्पते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तपस्सुनिष्ठितः Situated firmly in austerities, निवृत्तभोगः having renounced sense enjoyment, ददत् giving charity, कर्माणि ritual activities, करोति he performs. तदपेक्षया With the expectation of that reward, अहं I, पुनः च and again, स्वराट् a sovereign ruler, भूयासम् may become, इति thus, प्रवृद्धतर्षः whose desires are overgrown, सुखाय for real happiness, न कल्पते is not fit.

Simple Meaning: A person may practice severe austerities, renounce immediate sense enjoyments, give generously in charity, and perform Vedic rituals, but he does all this with the expectation of future rewards, thinking, may I become a sovereign ruler in my next life. Driven by such overgrown desires, he never achieves real happiness.

भवापवर्गो भ्रमतो यदा भवेज्जनस्य तर्ह्यच्युत सत्समागमः ।
सत्सङ्गमो यर्हि तदैव सद्गतौ परावरेशे त्वयि जायते मतिः ॥10.51.54॥

Word-by-word meaning: भव-अपवर्गः liberation from the cycle of birth and death, भ्रमतः of one who is wandering, यदा when, भवेत् occurs, जनस्य of a living being, तर्हि then, अच्युत O infallible Lord, सत्-समागमः association with saintly devotees, सत्-सङ्गमः saintly association, यर्हि when, तदा एव at that very moment, सत्-गतौ the ultimate goal of the righteous, पर-अवर-ईशे in the Lord of the superior and the inferior, त्वयि unto you, जायते awakens, मतिः attraction or intelligence

Anvaya: अच्युत! भ्रमतः जनस्य यदा भवापवर्गः भवेत् तर्हि सत्समागमः (भवति)। यर्हि सत्सङ्गमः तदा एव सद्गतौ परावरेशे त्वयि मतिः जायते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अच्युत O infallible Lord, भ्रमतः जनस्य Of the wandering living being, यदा when, भवापवर्गः liberation from the cycle of birth and death, भवेत् occurs, तर्हि then, सत्समागमः association with saintly devotees (happens). यर्हि When, सत्सङ्गमः saintly association (occurs), तदा एव at that very moment, सद्गतौ परावरेशे त्वयि in You, who are the ultimate goal of the righteous, the Lord of the superior and inferior, मतिः जायते devotion/attraction, awakens.

Simple Meaning: O infallible Lord (Achyuta), when a soul wandering through the cycle of cosmic existence finally becomes eligible for liberation from material life, he obtains the association of Your pure devotees. And the moment he gains that saintly association, a deep devotion awakens within his heart for You—the ultimate shelter of the righteous and the Supreme Master of all causes, both high and low.

मन्ये ममानुग्रह ईश ते कृतो राज्यानुबन्धापगमो यदृच्छया ।
यः प्रार्थ्यते साधुभिरेकचर्यया वनं विविक्षद्भिरखण्डभूमिपैः ॥10.51.55॥

Word-by-word meaning: मन्ये I consider, मम my, अनुग्रहः mercy, ईश O Lord, ते by you, कृतः bestowed, राज्य-अनुबन्ध-अपगमः the removal of the shackles of kingship, यदृच्छया spontaneously or by divine arrangement, यः which, प्रार्थ्यते is prayed for, साधुभिः by saintly persons, एक-चर्यया with solitary vows, वनम् the forest, विविक्षद्भिः who desire to enter, अखण्ड-भूमि-पैः by rulers of the undivided earth

Anvaya: ईश! यदृच्छया राज्यानुबन्धापगमः ते कृतः मम अनुग्रहः मन्ये, यः वनं विविक्षद्भिः एकचर्यया साधुभिः अखण्डभूमिपैः प्रार्थ्यते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ईश O Lord, यदृच्छया Spontaneously/by divine arrangement, राज्यानुबन्धापगमः the removal of shackles of kingship, ते कृतः मम अनुग्रहः is mercy bestowed by you on me, मन्ये I consider, यः which, वनं विविक्षद्भिः who desire to enter the forest, एकचर्यया with solitary vows, साधुभिः by saintly persons, अखण्डभूमिपैः by rulers of the undivided earth, प्रार्थ्यते is prayed for.

Simple Meaning: O Lord, I consider this sudden, unexpected loss of my kingdom and royal attachments to be your supreme mercy upon me. This renunciation is the very boon that emperors of the entire undivided earth pray for when they wish to take up vows of solitude and retire to the forest for spiritual realization.

न कामयेऽन्यं तव पादसेवनादकिञ्चनप्रार्थ्यतमाद् वरं विभो ।
आराध्य कस्त्वां ह्यपवर्गदं हरे वृणीत आर्यो वरमात्मबन्धनम् ॥10.51.56॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, कामये I desire, अन्यम् another, तव your, पाद-सेवनात् than the service of the lotus feet, अकिञ्चन-प्रार्थ्य-तमात् which is the highest object of prayer for those who possess nothing, वरम् a boon, विभो O almighty Lord, आराध्य having worshiped, कः who, त्वाम् you, हि indeed, अपवर्ग-दम् the bestower of liberation, हरे O Lord Hari, वृणीत would choose, आर्यः a wise or noble person, वरम् a boon, आत्म-बन्धनम् which causes the bondage of the self

Anvaya: विभो! अकिञ्चनप्रार्थ्यतमात् तव पादसेवनात् अन्यं वरं न कामये। हरे अपवर्गदं त्वाम् आराध्य कः हि आर्यः आत्मबन्धनं वरं वृणीत?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विभो O almighty Lord, अकिञ्चनप्रार्थ्यतमात् Which is the highest object of prayer for those who possess nothing, तव पादसेवनात् than the service of Your lotus feet, अन्यं वरं another, boon, न कामये I do not desire. हरे O Lord Hari, अपवर्गदं त्वाम् The bestower of liberation, You, आराध्य having worshiped, कः हि आर्यः who, indeed, a wise person, आत्मबन्धनं वरं a boon which causes the bondage of the self, वृणीत would choose?

Simple Meaning: O almighty Lord, I do not desire any boon other than the service of your lotus feet, which is the most cherished treasure prayed for by saintly souls who possess nothing material. O Lord Hari, having worshiped you—the ultimate giver of liberation—what wise person would ever choose a material boon that only binds the soul to this worldly existence?

तस्माद् विसृज्याशिष ईश सर्वतो रजस्तमःसत्त्वगुणानुबन्धनाः ।
निरञ्जनं निर्गुणमद्वयं परं त्वां ज्ञप्तिमात्रं पुरुषं व्रजाम्यहम् ॥10.51.57॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मात् therefore, विसृज्य completely casting aside, आशिषः material desires or blessings, ईश O Lord, सर्वतः from all angles, रजः-तमः-सत्त्व-गुण-अनुबन्धनाः which bind one to the modes of passion ignorance and goodness, निरञ्जनम् untainted, निर्गुणम् free from material qualities, अद्वयम् non-dual or without a second, परम् supreme, त्वाम् unto you, ज्ञप्ति-मात्रम् pure consciousness, पुरुषम् the Supreme Person, व्रजामि approach or take shelter, अहम् I

Anvaya: ईश! तस्मात् सर्वतः रजस्तमःसत्त्वगुणानुबन्धनाः आशिषः विसृज्य अहं निरञ्जनं निर्गुणम् अद्वयं परं ज्ञप्तिमात्रं पुरुषं त्वां व्रजामि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ईश O Lord, तस्मात् therefore, सर्वतः from all angles, रजस्तमःसत्त्वगुणानुबन्धनाः which bind one to the modes of passion, ignorance, and goodness, आशिषः material desires/blessings, विसृज्य completely casting aside, अहं I, निरञ्जनं untainted, निर्गुणम् free from material qualities, अद्वयं non-dual, परं supreme, ज्ञप्तिमात्रं pure consciousness, पुरुषं the Supreme Person, त्वां unto you, व्रजामि approach for shelter.

Simple Meaning: Therefore, O Lord, completely casting aside all material desires and blessings—which only serve to bind the soul to the material modes of goodness, passion, and ignorance—I take exclusive shelter of you. You are the untainted, non-dual Supreme Person, completely free from material qualities, and the very embodiment of pure consciousness.

चिरमिह वृजिनार्तस्तप्यमानोऽनुतापैरवितृषषडमित्रोऽलब्धशान्तिः कथञ्चित् ।
शरणद समुपेतस्त्वत्पदाब्जं परात्मन्नभयमृतमशोकं पाहि माऽऽपन्नमीश ॥10.51.58॥

Word-by-word meaning: चिरम् for a long time, इह in this material world, वृजिन-आर्तः distressed by sins or miseries, तप्यमानः burning, अनुतापैः with remorses, अवितृष-षड्-अमित्रः whose six enemies (the mind and five senses) are never satisfied, अलब्ध-शान्तिः unable to find peace, कथञ्चित् somehow or other, शरण-द O giver of shelter, समुपेतः have approached, त्वत्-पद-अब्जम् your lotus feet, पर-आत्मन् O Supreme Soul, अभयम् fearless, ऋतम् immortal/truth, अशोकम् free from sorrow, पाहि please protect, माम् me, आपन्नम् surrendered/distressed, ईश O Lord

Anvaya: ईश शरणद परात्मन्! इह चिरं वृजिनार्तः अनुतापैः तप्यमानः अवितृषषडमित्रः अलब्धशान्तिः (अहं) कथञ्चित् अभयम् ऋतम् अशोकं त्वत्पदाब्जं समुपेतः, आपन्नं मा पाहि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ईश O Lord, शरणद O giver of shelter, परात्मन् O Supreme Soul! इह In this material world, चिरं for a long time, वृजिनार्तः distressed by miseries, अनुतापैः with remorses, तप्यमानः burning, अवितृषषडमित्रः whose six enemies (the senses) are never satisfied, अलब्धशान्तिः unable to find peace, कथञ्चित् somehow or other, अभयम् fearless, ऋतम् immortal, अशोकं free from sorrow, त्वत्पदाब्जं your lotus feet, समुपेतः have approached. आपन्नं Surrendered/distressed, मा me, पाहि please protect.

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Lord, O giver of shelter, for a very long time in this material world, I have been tormented by my sins and burning with remorse. My six internal enemies—the mind and the five senses—could never be satisfied, and I found absolutely no peace. Somehow or other, I have now come before your lotus feet, which are completely fearless, immortal, and free from all sorrow. O Master, I am fully surrendered and distressed; please protect me.

श्री भगवान उवाच
सार्वभौम महाराज मतिस्ते विमलोर्जिता ।
वरैः प्रलोभितस्यापि न कामैर्विहता यतः ॥10.51.59॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, सार्वभौम O emperor, महा-राज O great king, मतिः mind/intelligence, ते your, विमला pure, ऊर्जिता powerful or steady, वरैः by boons, प्रलोभितस्य अपि although tempted, न not, कामैः by material desires, विहता corrupted or broken, यतः because

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच—सार्वभौम महाराज! ते मतिः विमलोर्जिता (अस्ति), यतः वरैः प्रलोभितस्य अपि (ते मतिः) कामैः न विहता।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, सार्वभौम O sovereign ruler of the earth, महाराज O great king! ते मतिः Your mind, विमला ऊर्जिता is pure and powerful, यतः because, वरैः by choices of boons, प्रलोभितस्य अपि although tempted, (ते मतिः) your intelligence, कामैः by material desires, न विहता was not corrupted.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord said: O sovereign emperor, O great king, your mind is pure, unblemished, and highly potent. Even though I tempted you with the choice of any boon, your clarity was never shaken or corrupted by material cravings.

प्रलोभितो वरैर्यत्त्वमप्रमादाय विद्धि तत् ।
न धीर्मय्येकभक्तानामाशीर्भिर्भिद्यते क्वचित् ॥10.51.60॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रलोभितः tempted, वरैः by boons, यत् because, त्वम् you, अप्रमादाय to test your freedom from illusion/carelessness, विद्धि please know, तत् that, न never, धीः the intelligence, मयि in me, एक-भक्तानाम् of those who are single-mindedly devoted, आशीर्भिः by material blessings or desires, भिद्यते is deviated or broken, क्वचित् at any time

Anvaya: यत् त्वं वरैः प्रलोभितः, तत् अप्रमादाय (इति) विद्धि। मयि एकभक्तानां धीः आशीर्भिः क्वचित् न भिद्यते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यत् That, त्वं वरैः प्रलोभितः you were tempted by boons, तत् so that, अप्रमादाय to verify your vigilance against illusion, विद्धि know. मयि In me, एकभक्तानां of those who are single-mindedly devoted, धीः the intelligence, आशीर्भिः by material benedictions, क्वचित् at any time, न भिद्यते is never deviated.

Simple Meaning: Please know that my tempting you with boons was only meant to demonstrate your absolute vigilance against illusion. The focused intelligence of those who are single-mindedly devoted to me is never shaken or pulled away by material allurements.

युञ्जानानामभक्तानां प्राणायामादिभिर्मनः ।
अक्षीणवासनं राजन् दृश्यते पुनरुत्थितम् ॥10.51.61॥

Word-by-word meaning: युञ्जानानाम् of those practicing yoga, अभक्तानाम् who are non-devotees, प्राणायाम-आदिभिः by breath control and other processes, मनः the mind, अक्षीण-वासनम् in which material desires are not completely destroyed, राजन् O king, दृश्यते is seen, पुनः again, उत्थितम् rising up or becoming agitated

Anvaya: राजन्! प्राणायामादिभिः मनः युञ्जानानाम् अभक्तानाम् अक्षीणवासनं (मनः) पुनः उत्थितं दृश्यते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O king! प्राणायामादिभिः By breath control and other such practices, मनः the mind, युञ्जानानाम् of those trying to engage or control, अभक्तानाम् who are non-devotees, अक्षीणवासनं whose material desires are not destroyed, पुनः again, उत्थितं pulled back up (to sense objects), दृश्यते is observed.

Simple Meaning: O King, non-devotees who try to control their minds through practices like pranayama (breath control) never truly erase their latent material desires. Consequently, despite their intense discipline, their minds are eventually observed to rise up and succumb to worldly attachments once again.

विचरस्व महीं कामं मय्यवेशितमानसः।
अस्त्वेव नित्यदा तुभ्यं भक्तिर्मय्यनपायिनी ॥10.51.62॥

Word-by-word meaning: विचरस्व please wander, महीम् the earth, कामम् at your will or freely, मयि in me, आवेशित-मानसः with a mind fully absorbed, अस्तु एव let there certainly be, नित्यदा always, तुभ्यम् unto you, भक्तिः devotion, मयि unto me, अनपायिनी unswerving or undying

Anvaya: मयि आवेशितमानसः महीं कामं विचरस्व। मयि तुभ्यं नित्यदा अनपायिनी भक्तिः अस्तु एव।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मयि In me, आवेशितमानसः with a mind fully absorbed, महीं the earth, कामं विचरस्व freely, please wander. मयि In me, तुभ्यं unto you, नित्यदा अनपायिनी भक्तिः always, unswerving devotion, अस्तु एव let there certainly be.

Simple Meaning: With your mind fully absorbed in me, you may now wander the earth freely at your own will. Rest assured that you will always possess absolute, unswerving devotion to me.

क्षात्रधर्मस्थितो जन्तून् न्यवधीर्मृगयादिभिः ।
समाहितस्तत्तपसा जह्यघं मदुपाश्रितः ॥10.51.63॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्षात्र-धर्म-स्थितः remaining in the duty of a warrior king, जन्तून् living beings, न्यवधीः you killed, मृगया-आदिभिः through hunting and other actions, समाहितः with a focused mind, तत् that, तपसा by austerity (of devotion/penance), जहि destroy, अघम् sin, मत्-उपाश्रितः taking exclusive shelter of me

Anvaya: क्षात्रधर्मस्थितः (त्वं) मृगयादिभिः जन्तून् न्यवधीः, मदुपाश्रितः समाहितः (सन्) तपसा तत् अघं जहि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: क्षात्रधर्मस्थितः Remaining in the duties of a kshatriya, मृगयादिभिः through hunting and other such activities, जन्तून् living beings, न्यवधीः you have killed; मदुपाश्रितः Taking complete shelter of me, समाहितः being fully focused, तपसा by penance/austerity, तत् अघं that sin, जहि destroy.

Simple Meaning: Because you were acting in the role of a kshatriya king, you killed many living beings through hunting and warfare. Now, by taking exclusive shelter of me and remaining fully focused in deep spiritual austerity, you can completely dissolve and wipe away all those past sins.

जन्मन्यनन्तरे राजन् सर्वभूतसुहृत्तमः ।
भूत्वा द्विजवरस्त्वं वै मामुपैष्यसि केवलम् ॥10.51.64॥

Word-by-word meaning: जन्मनि in the birth, अनन्तरे next, राजन् O king, सर्व-भूत-सुहृत्-तमः the best well-wishing friend of all living entities, भूत्वा becoming, द्विज-वरः an exalted brahmana, त्वम् you, वै indeed, माम् me, उपैष्यसि you will attain, केवलम् exclusively

Anvaya: राजन्! त्वम् अनन्तरे जन्मनि सर्वभूतसुहृत्तमः द्विजवरः भूत्वा वै मां केवलम् उपैष्यसि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O king! त्वम् You, अनन्तरे जन्मनि in the next birth, सर्वभूतसुहृत्तमः the best well-wishing friend of all living entities, द्विजवरः an exalted brahmana, भूत्वा becoming, वै indeed, मां me, केवलम् alone, उपैष्यसि will attain.

Simple Meaning: O King, in your next life, you will take birth as an exalted brahmana who is a true, deeply compassionate friend to all living beings. Through that life of pure spiritual focus, you will certainly come to me alone and attain my eternal abode.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “कालयवनदहनं मुचुकुन्दोपाख्यानं स्तुतिश्च नाम​” एकपञ्चाशत्तमः अध्यायः ॥51॥

Thus ends the fifty-first discourse named “Burning of Kalayavana, Muchukunda’s story and Muchukunda’s Eulogy of the Lord”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
