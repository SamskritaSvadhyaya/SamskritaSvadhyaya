Chapter 68

The triumph of Sankarshana (Balarama) in the form of dragging Hastinapura and Samba’s wedding

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

हास्तिनपुरकर्षणरूपसङ्कर्षणविजयः साम्बविवाहश्च नाम अष्टषष्टितमः अध्यायः

(54 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
दुर्योधनसुतां राजन् लक्ष्मणां समितिञ्जय ।
स्वयंवरस्थामहरत् साम्बो जाम्बवतीसुतः ॥10.68.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, दुर्योधन-सुताम् the daughter of Duryodhana, राजन् O King (Parikshit), लक्ष्मणाम् named Lakshmana, समितिञ्जयः victorious in battle, स्वयंवर-स्थाम् present at her swayamvara ceremony, अहरत् carried away/kidnapped, साम्बः Samba, जाम्बवती-सुतः the son of Jambavati

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच – राजन्! समितिञ्जयः जाम्बवतीसुतः साम्बः स्वयंवरस्थां दुर्योधनसुतां लक्ष्मणाम् अहरत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, राजन् O King, समितिञ्जयः जाम्बवतीसुतः साम्बः Samba the victorious son of Jambavati, स्वयंवरस्थां दुर्योधनसुतां लक्ष्मणाम् अहरत् carried off Duryodhana's daughter Lakshmana from her swayamvara assembly.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King Parikshit, Samba, the ever-victorious son of Jambavati, went to the swayamvara assembly of Duryodhana's daughter Lakshmana and abducted her right from the middle of the gathering.

कौरवाः कुपिता ऊचुर्दुर्विनीतोऽयमर्भकः ।
कदर्थीकृत्य नः कन्यामकामामहरद् बलात् ॥10.68.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: कौरवाः the Kauravas, कुपिताः enraged, ऊचुः said, दुर्विनीतः ill-mannered/insolent, अयम् this, अर्भकः boy, कदर्थी-कृत्य disrespecting/disregarding, नः us, कन्याम् the maiden, अकामाम् unwilling, अहरत् abducted, बलात् by force

Anvaya: कुपिताः कौरवाः ऊचुः – अयम् अर्भकः दुर्विनीतः। नः कदर्थीकृत्य अकामां कन्यां बलात् अहरत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कुपिताः कौरवाः ऊचुः The enraged Kauravas said, अयम् अर्भकः दुर्विनीतः this boy is insolent. नः कदर्थीकृत्य Disrespecting us, अकामां कन्यां an unwilling maiden, बलात् अहरत् has forcibly abducted.

Simple Meaning: The enraged Kauravas declared: This boy is ill-mannered. Insulting us he has forcibly abducted the young princess against her will.

बध्नीतेमं दुर्विनीतं किं करिष्यन्ति वृष्णयः ।
येऽस्मत्प्रसादोपचितां दत्तां नो भुञ्जते महीम् ॥10.68.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: बध्नीत arrest/bind, इमम् this, दुर्विनीतम् ill-mannered/insolent one, किम् what, करिष्यन्ति will do, वृष्णयः the Vrishnis, ये who, अस्मत्-प्रसाद-उपचिताम् acquired through our favor, दत्ताम् given, नः by us, भुञ्जते enjoy, महीम् the earth

Anvaya: इमं दुर्विनीतं बध्नीत। वृष्णयः किं करिष्यन्ति, ये नः दत्ताम् अस्मत्प्रसादोपचितां महीं भुञ्जते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इमं दुर्विनीतं बध्नीत Bind this insolent boy. वृष्णयः किं करिष्यन्ति What can the Vrishnis do, ये नः दत्ताम् अस्मत्प्रसादोपचितां महीं भुञ्जते who enjoy the earth given by us through our royal favor.

Simple Meaning: Seize and bind this insolent youth! What can the Vrishnis possibly do to stop us? After all, they rule their lands only by our grace and through the royal territories we granted them.

निगृहीतं सुतं श्रुत्वा यद्येष्यन्तीह वृष्णयः ।
भग्नदर्पाः शमं यान्ति प्राणा इव सुसंयताः ॥10.68.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: निगृहीतम् arrested/restrained, सुतम् their son, श्रुत्वा hearing, यदि if, एष्यन्ति will come, इह here, वृष्णयः the Vrishnis, भग्न-दर्पाः their pride broken, शमम् peace/tranquility, यान्ति will attain, प्राणाः the senses/vital airs, इव like, सु-संयताः strictly controlled

Anvaya: सुतं निगृहीतं श्रुत्वा यदि वृष्णयः इह एष्यन्ति, (तर्हि) सुसंयताः प्राणाः इव भग्नदर्पाः शमं यान्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सुतं निगृहीतं श्रुत्वा Hearing their son arrested, यदि वृष्णयः इह एष्यन्ति if the Vrishnis come here, (तर्हि) सुसंयताः प्राणाः इव then like strictly controlled senses, भग्नदर्पाः शमं यान्ति having their pride shattered, they will become subdued and quiet.

Simple Meaning: If the Vrishnis come here after hearing that their son has been arrested, their pride will be completely shattered and they will be forced to submit, just as the turbulent senses naturally grow quiet and subdued when strictly controlled.

इति कर्णः शलो भूरिर्यज्ञकेतुः सुयोधनः ।
साम्बमारेभिरे बद्धुं कुरुवृद्धानुमोदिताः ॥10.68.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, कर्णः Karna, शलः Shala, भूरिः Bhurishravas, यज्ञकेतुः Yajnaketu, सुयोधनः Suyodhana (Duryodhana), साम्बम् Samba, आरेभिरे prepared/began, बद्धुम् to bind/capture, कुरु-वृद्ध-अनुमोदिताः sanctioned/approved by the elders of the Kuru dynasty

Anvaya: इति कुरुवृद्धानुमोदिताः कर्णः शलः भूरिः यज्ञकेतुः सुयोधनः साम्बं बद्धुम् आरेभिरे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति Thus, कुरुवृद्धानुमोदिताः encouraged by the Kuru elders, कर्णः शलः भूरिः यज्ञकेतुः सुयोधनः Karna, Shala, Bhurishravas, Yajnaketu and Suyodhana, साम्बं बद्धुम् आरेभिरे prepared to capture Samba.

Simple Meaning: Having received the approval of the Kuru elders, Karna, Shala, Bhurishravas, Yajnaketu, and Duryodhana joined together and set out to capture Samba.

दृष्ट्वानुधावतः साम्बो धार्तराष्ट्रान् महारथः ।
प्रगृह्य रुचिरं चापं तस्थौ सिंह इवैकलः ॥10.68.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: दृष्ट्वा seeing, अनुधावतः pursuing/running after him, साम्बः Samba, धार्तराष्ट्रान् the sons of Dhritarashtra (the Kauravas), महा-रथः a great chariot warrior, प्रगृह्य grasping/taking up, रुचिरम् splendid/beautiful, चापम् bow, तस्थौ stood firm, सिंहः a lion, इव like, एकलः alone

Anvaya: महारथः साम्बः अनुधावतः धार्तराष्ट्रान् दृष्ट्वा रुचिरं चापं प्रगृह्य एकलः सिंहः इव तस्थौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महारथः साम्बः The great chariot warrior Samba, अनुधावतः धार्तराष्ट्रान् दृष्ट्वा seeing the sons of Dhritarashtra pursuing him, रुचिरं चापं प्रगृह्य grasping his splendid bow, एकलः सिंहः इव तस्थौ stood his ground alone like a lion.

Simple Meaning: Seeing the Kauravas pursuing him in rage, the great chariot warrior Samba grasped his magnificent bow and stood his ground alone to face them, just like a solitary lion turning to face a pack of attackers.

तं ते जिघृक्षवः क्रुद्धास्तिष्ठ तिष्ठेति भाषिणः ।
आसाद्य धन्विनो बाणैः कर्णाग्रण्य समाकिरन् ॥10.68.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him, ते they (the Kaurava warriors), जिघृक्षवः desiring to capture, क्रुद्धाः enraged, तिष्ठ तिष्ठ इति Stop! Stop!, भाषिणः shouting, आसाद्य approaching, धन्विनः archers, बाणैः with arrows, कर्ण-अग्रण्यः led by Karna, समाकिरन् showered/covered

Anvaya: जिघृक्षवः क्रुद्धाः “तिष्ठ तिष्ठ” इति भाषिणः ते कर्णाग्रण्यः धन्विनः तम् आसाद्य बाणैः समाकिरन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जिघृक्षवः क्रुद्धाः Desiring to capture him and enraged, “तिष्ठ तिष्ठ” इति भाषिणः shouting Stop! Stop!, ते कर्णाग्रण्यः धन्विनः those archers led by Karna, तम् आसाद्य approaching him, बाणैः समाकिरन् showered him with arrows.

Simple Meaning: Furious and eager to capture Samba, those master archers led by Karna shouted Stop! Stop! as they closed in and showered him from all sides with a barrage of arrows.

सोऽपविद्धः कुरुश्रेष्ठ कुरुभिर्यदुनन्दनः ।
नामृष्यत्तदचिन्त्यार्भः सिंह क्षुद्रमृगैरिव ॥10.68.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Samba), अपविद्धः struck/attacked, कुरु-श्रेष्ठ O best of the Kurus (King Parikshit), कुरुभिः by the Kurus, यदु-नन्दनः the descendant of the Yadu dynasty, न अमृष्यत् could not tolerate, तत् that, अचिन्त्य-अर्भः son of the incomprehensible Krishna, सिंहः a lion, क्षुद्र-मृगेः by small animals, इव as/like

Anvaya: कुरुश्रेष्ठ! सः यदुनन्दनः अचिन्त्यार्भः कुरुभिः अपविद्धः तत् क्षुद्रमृगैः सिंहः इव न अमृष्यत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कुरुश्रेष्ठ O best of the Kurus, सः यदुनन्दनः he, the descendant of Yadu, अचिन्त्यार्भः the son of the incomprehensible Krishna, कुरुभिः अपविद्धः being attacked by the Kurus, तत् न अमृष्यत् could not tolerate that, क्षुद्रमृगैः सिंहः इव just as a lion does not tolerate an attack by small animals.

Simple Meaning: O best of the Kurus, that scion of the Yadu dynasty, Samba, the son of the incomprehensible Krishna, could not tolerate being attacked by the Kurus, just as a majestic lion does not tolerate being provoked by weak, little animals.

विस्फूर्ज्य रुचिरं चापं सर्वान् विव्याध सायकैः ।
कर्णादीन् षड्रथान् वीरास्तावद्भिर्युगपत् पृथक् ॥10.68.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: विस्फूर्ज्य twanging/stretching, रुचिरम् splendid/beautiful, चापम् bow, सर्वान् all, विव्याध pierced, सायकैः with arrows, कर्ण-आदीन् Karna and others, षट्-रथान् six chariot warriors, वीरान् brave, तावद्भिः with as many, युगपत् simultaneously, पृथक् individually

Anvaya: वीरः रुचिरं चापं विस्फूर्ज्य कर्णादीन् षड्रथान् सर्वान् तावद्भिः सायकैः युगपत् पृथक् विव्याध।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वीरः The heroic youth, रुचिरं चापं विस्फूर्ज्य twanging his splendid bow, कर्णादीन् षड्रथान् सर्वान् all six chariot warriors beginning with Karna, तावद्भिः सायकैः युगपत् पृथक् विव्याध pierced each individually and simultaneously with an equal number of arrows.

Simple Meaning: Twanging his splendid bow, that heroic youth Samba pierced all six great chariot warriors, including Karna, striking each of them individually at the exact same moment with an equal number of arrows.

चतुर्भिश्चतुरो वाहानेकैकेन च सारथीन् ।
रथिनश्च महेष्वासांस्तस्य तत्तेऽभ्यपूजयन् ॥10.68.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: चतुर्भिः with four, चतुरः four, वाहान् horses, एकेन with one, च and, सारथीन् charioteers, रथिनः chariot warriors, च and, महा-इष्वासान् great bowmen, तस्य his, तत् that (feat), ते they, अभ्यपूजयन् praised/admired

Anvaya: चतुर्भिः चतुरः वाहान् एकेन सारथीन् च महेष्वासान् रथिनः च (विव्याध)। ते तस्य तत् अभ्यपूजयन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: चतुर्भिः चतुरः वाहान् With four arrows he struck their four horses, एकेन सारथीन् च and with one arrow each their charioteers, महेष्वासान् रथिनः च and also those great bowmen chariot warriors. ते तस्य तत् अभ्यपूजयन् They praised that extraordinary feat of his.

Simple Meaning: Samba struck each of their four horses with four arrows, their charioteers with one arrow each, and similarly pierced those great chariot warriors themselves. Even though they were his enemies, they marveled at and admired his incredible skill.

तं तु ते विरथं चक्रुश्चत्वारश्चतुरो हयान् ।
एकस्तु सारथिं जघ्ने चिच्छेदान्यः शरासनम् ॥10.68.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him (Samba), तु but, ते they (the Kaurava warriors), विरथम् deprived of his chariot, चक्रुः made, चत्वारः four (warriors), चतुरः four, हयान् horses, एकः one, तु on the other hand, सारथिम् charioteer, जघ्ने killed, चिच्छेद cut/broke, अन्यः another, शरासनम् bow

Anvaya: ते तु तं विरथं चक्रुः। चत्वारः चतुरः हयान् (जघ्नुः)। एकः तु सारथिं जघ्ने, अन्यः शरासनं चिच्छेद।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ते तु तं विरथं चक्रुः But they made him deprived of his chariot. चत्वारः चतुरः हयान् Four of them killed his four horses. एकः तु सारथिं जघ्ने While one killed his charioteer, अन्यः शरासनं चिच्छेद and another broke his bow.

Simple Meaning: However, the Kaurava warriors combined their efforts to deprive Samba of his chariot. Four of them struck down his four horses, one killed his charioteer, and another cut down his bow.

तं बद्ध्वा विरथीकृत्य कृच्छ्रेण कुरवो युधि ।
कुमारं स्वस्य कन्या च स्वपुरं जयिनोऽविशन् ॥10.68.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him (Samba), बद्ध्वा having bound/tied up, विरथीकृत्य after making him chariot-less, कृच्छ्रेण with great difficulty, कुरवः the Kurus, युधि in battle, कुमारम् the youth, स्वस्य their own, कन्याम् daughter (Laksmana), च and, स्व-पुरम् their own city (Hastinapura), जयिनः victorious, अविशन् entered

Anvaya: कुरवः युधि कृच्छ्रेण तं कुमारं विरथीकृत्य बद्ध्वा च जयिनः स्वस्य कन्यां स्वपुरम् अविशन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कुरवः युधि कृच्छ्रेण The Kurus in battle with great difficulty, तं कुमारं विरथीकृत्य बद्ध्वा च after rendering that youth chariot-less and binding him, जयिनः स्वस्य कन्यां स्वपुरम् अविशन् entered their own city as victors along with their daughter.

Simple Meaning: Having managed with great difficulty to disarm and tie up the valiant youth Samba in battle, the victorious Kurus returned to their capital city Hastinapura, taking him and their daughter Laksmana with them.

तच्छ्रुत्वा नारदोक्तेन राजन् सञ्जातमन्यवः ।
कुरून् प्रत्युद्यमं चक्रुरुग्रसेनप्रचोदिताः ॥10.68.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that (news), श्रुत्वा hearing, नारद-उक्तेन spoken by Narada, राजन् O King (Parikshit), सञ्जात-मन्यवः filled with anger, कुरून् the Kurus, प्रति उद्यमम् preparation to retaliate, चक्रुः made, उग्रसेन-प्रचोदिताः urged by King Ugrasena

Anvaya: राजन्! नारदोक्तेन तत् श्रुत्वा सञ्जातमन्यवः उग्रसेनप्रचोदिताः कुरून् प्रति उद्यमं चक्रुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, नारदोक्तेन तत् श्रुत्वा hearing that news spoken by Narada, सञ्जातमन्यवः filled with intense anger, उग्रसेनप्रचोदिताः urged by King Ugrasena, कुरून् प्रति उद्यमं चक्रुः made preparations to march against the Kurus.

Simple Meaning: O King, upon hearing this news from Narada Muni, the Yadus were filled with rage. Urged on by King Ugrasena, they immediately began preparing to march against the Kurus in battle.

सान्तयित्वा तु तान् रामः सन्नद्धान् वृष्णिपुङ्गवान् ।
नैच्छत् कुरूणां वृष्णीनां कलिं कलिमलापहः ॥10.68.14॥

Simple Meaning: Balarama, who removes all the impurities caused by conflict, did not wish for a destructive conflict to break out between the Kurus and the Vrishnis. Pacifying the prominent Vrishni warriors who were arrayed and eager for war…

Anvaya: रामः गजाह्वयं गत्वा बाह्योपवनम् आस्थितः बुभुत्सया उद्धवं धृतराष्ट्रं प्रेषयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रामः गजाह्वयं गत्वा Balarama having arrived at Hastinapura, बाह्योपवनम् आस्थितः stationed Himself in a garden outside the city, बुभुत्सया उद्धवं धृतराष्ट्रं प्रेषयामास and sent Uddhava to King Dhritarashtra to ascertain their motives.

Simple Meaning: Upon reaching Hastinapura, Balarama halted in a garden outside the city gates. Wishing to learn what the Kauravas intended to do, He dispatched Uddhava as an envoy to King Dhritarashtra.

Anvaya: सः अम्बिकापुत्रं भीष्मं द्रोणं बाह्लिकं दुर्योधनं च विधिवत् अभिवन्द्य रामम् आगतम् अब्रवीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He, अम्बिकापुत्रं भीष्मं द्रोणं बाह्लिकं दुर्योधनं च विधिवत् अभिवन्द्य offering proper respectful greetings to Dhritarashtra, Bhishma, Drona, Bahlika, and Duryodhana, रामम् आगतम् अब्रवीत् informed them that Balarama had arrived.

Simple Meaning: Having arrived at the assembly, Uddhava respectfully paid his homage according to tradition to Dhritarashtra, Bhishma, Drona, Bahlika, and Duryodhana, and then informed them that Balarama had come and was waiting nearby.

तेऽतिप्रीतास्तमाकर्ण्य प्राप्तं रामं सुहृत्तमम् ।

Anvaya: ते अतिप्रीताः सुहृत्तमं रामं प्राप्तम् आकर्ण्य सर्वे मङ्गलपाणयः तम् अर्चयित्वा अभिययुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ते अतिप्रीताः They were delighted beyond measure, सुहृत्तमं रामं प्राप्तम् आकर्ण्य hearing that their most beloved well-wisher Balarama had arrived, सर्वे मङ्गलपाणयः all of them holding auspicious items in their hands, तम् अर्चयित्वा after honouring Uddhava, अभिययुः went forth.

Simple Meaning: Overjoyed to learn that their dear friend and supreme well-wisher Balarama had arrived, all the Kaurava leaders took auspicious offerings in their hands and went out together to greet and honor Him.

तं सङ्गम्य यथान्यायं गामर्घ्यं च निवेदयन् ।
तेषां ये तत्प्रभावज्ञाः प्रणेमुः शिरसा बलम् ॥10.68.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् Him (Balarama), सङ्गम्य meeting, यथा-न्यायम् according to custom/etiquette, गाम् a cow, अर्घ्यम् respectful water offering, च and, निवेदयन् offered, तेषाम् of them (the Kauravas), ये those who, तत्-प्रभाव-ज्ञाः aware of His power/prowess, प्रणेमुः bowed down, शिरसा with their heads, बलम् to Balarama

Anvaya: तं यथान्यायं सङ्गम्य गाम् अर्घ्यं च निवेदयन् तेषां ये तत्प्रभावज्ञाः शिरसा बलं प्रणेमुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तं यथान्यायं सङ्गम्य Meeting Him in accordance with proper protocol, गाम् अर्घ्यं च निवेदयन् offering Him a cow and respectful water offerings, तेषां ये तत्प्रभावज्ञाः those among them who knew His supreme power, शिरसा बलं प्रणेमुः bowed down with their heads before Balarama.

Simple Meaning: Greeting Balarama according to prescribed etiquette, they offered Him respectful gifts, including water for washing and a cow. Those among the Kauravas who knew His true divine power bowed down before Him with their heads to the ground.

बन्धून् कुशलिनः श्रुत्वा पृष्ट्वा शिवमनामयम् ।

उग्रसेनः क्षितीशेशो यत् व आज्ञापयत् प्रभुः ।
तदव्यग्रधियः श्रुत्वा कुरुध्वं मा विलम्बितम् ॥10.68.21॥

Literal meaning of the anvaya: क्षितीशेशः प्रभुः उग्रसेनः Lord Ugrasena the ruler over kings, वः यत् आज्ञापयत् what he commands unto you, तत् अव्यग्रधियः श्रुत्वा hearing that with attentive minds, मा विलम्बितं कुरुध्वम् execute it without any delay.

Simple Meaning: Balarama said: Lord Ugrasena, the sovereign ruler of kings, has issued a command to you. Hear his order with focused, undivided attention and execute it immediately without any delay.

यद् यूयं बहवस्त्वेकं जित्वाधर्मेण धार्मिकम् ।
अबध्नीताथ तन्मृष्ये बन्धूनामैक्यकाम्यया ॥10.68.22॥

Simple Meaning: Lord Balarama continued: Many of you united to unrighteously win over and bind a single righteous young man, Samba. Nevertheless, out of a desire to preserve peace and unity among our related families, I am overlooking and tolerating this offense.

वीर्यशौर्यबलोन्नद्धमात्मशक्तिसमं वचः ।

Anvaya: प्रकोपिताः कुरवः वीर्यशौर्यबलोन्नद्धम् आत्मशक्तिसमं बलदेवस्य वचः निशम्य ऊचुः।

Simple Meaning: Hearing Lord Baladeva's words, which were infused with immense prowess, heroism, and power reflecting His divine strength, the Kurus became deeply enraged and replied to Him.

अहो महच्चित्रमिदं कालगत्या दुरत्यया ।
आरुरुक्षत्युपानद् वै शिरो मुकुटसेवितम् ॥10.68.24॥

Simple Meaning: The Kurus arrogantly said: Oh, how extraordinary and strange are the effects of inescapable time! Now a mere shoe wishes to climb up and place itself upon a head crowned with royal majesty.

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यौनेन सम्बद्धाः Connected to us through marriage, सहशय्यासनाशनाः sharing our beds, seats, and meals, अस्मद्दत्तनृपासनाः having been granted royal thrones by us, एते वृष्णयः तुल्यतां नीताः these Vrishnis have now been brought to a position of equality with us.

Simple Meaning: Connected to us through marriage alliances, these Vrishnis have enjoyed sharing our resting quarters, seating, and meals. It is only because we granted them royal thrones that they have now been elevated to a status of equality with us.

अलं यदूनां नरदेवलाञ्छनैर्दातुः प्रतीपैः फणिनामिवामृतम् ।

Anvaya: दातुः प्रतीपैः फणिनाम् अमृतम् इव यदूनां नरदेवलाञ्छनैः अलम्। ये यादवाः अस्मत्प्रसादोपचिताः अद्य गतत्रपाः (सन्तः) आज्ञापयन्ति बत!

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दातुः प्रतीपैः फणिनाम् अमृतम् इव Like milk/nectar given to poisonous serpents who act hostilely toward the giver, यदूनां नरदेवलाञ्छनैः अलम् these royal insignia given to the Yadus are a waste. ये यादवाः These Yadavas who, अस्मत्प्रसादोपचिताः having been elevated purely by our grace, अद्य गतत्रपाः आज्ञापयन्ति बत alas, have today become so shameless as to issue orders to us!

Simple Meaning: The Kurus said: Feeding nectar to poisonous snakes only makes them attack the one who fed them—similarly, bestowing royal insignia upon the Yadus has proven to be a complete waste! शशंस सर्वं यदुपुङ्गवानां मध्ये सभायां कुरुषु स्वचेष्टितम् ॥10.68.53॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, प्रविष्टः having entered, स्व-पुरम् His own city (Dwaraka), हलायुधः Lord Balarama (wielder of the plough), समेत्य meeting, बन्धून् His relatives, अनुरक्त-चेतसः whose hearts were filled with deep affection, शशंस narrated/described, सर्वम् everything, यदु-पुङ्गवानाम् of the noble Yadavas, मध्ये in the midst, सभायाम् in the assembly hall, कुरुषु regarding the Kurus, स्व-चेष्टितम् His own actions/deeds

Anvaya: ततः स्वपुरं प्रविष्टः हलायुधः अनुरक्तचेतसः बन्धून् समेत्य सभायां यदुपुङ्गवानां मध्ये कुरुषु स्वचेष्टितं सर्वं शशंस।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः Then, स्वपुरं प्रविष्टः having returned to His own city Dwaraka, हलायुधः Lord Balarama, अनुरक्तचेतसः बन्धून् समेत्य meeting His loving and affectionate relatives, सभायां in the royal assembly hall, यदुपुङ्गवानां मध्ये in the presence of the foremost Yadava heroes, कुरुषु स्वचेष्टितं सर्वं शशंस recounted in detail all His actions regarding the Kurus.

Simple Meaning: Upon returning to His city of Dwaraka, Lord Balarama met with His loving family and relatives. In the royal assembly hall, before the gathered leaders of the Yadava clan, He recounted everything that had transpired in Hastinapura and how He had dealt with the Kurus.

अद्यापि च पुरं ह्येतत् सूचयद् रामविक्रमम् ।
समुन्नतं दक्षिणतो गङ्गायामनुदृश्यते ॥10.68.54॥

Word-by-word meaning: अद्य अपि even today, च and, पुरम् city, हि indeed, एतत् this, सूचयत् indicating/showing, राम-विक्रमम् the prowess of Lord Balarama, समुन्नतम् elevated/tilted upwards, दक्षिणतः on the southern side, गङ्गायाम् along the river Ganges, अनुदृश्यते is seen

Anvaya: रामविक्रमं सूचयत् एतत् पुरं हि अद्यापि च गङ्गायां दक्षिणतः समुन्नतम् अनुदृश्यते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रामविक्रमं सूचयत् Indicating the extraordinary valor of Lord Balarama, एतत् पुरं हि this very city of Hastinapura indeed, अद्यापि च even today, गङ्गायां दक्षिणतः along the Ganges on its southern bank, समुन्नतम् tilted high upwards, अनुदृश्यते is clearly seen.

Simple Meaning: Even to this day, the city of Hastinapura stands as a visible testament to the immense prowess of Lord Balarama—for it can still be seen tilted high on its southern side along the bank of the Ganges, right where He dragged it with His plough.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे  “हास्तिनपुरकर्षणरूपसङ्कर्षणविजयः साम्बविवाहश्च नाम” अष्टषष्टितमः अध्यायः ॥68॥

Thus ends the sixty-eighth discourse named “The triumph of Sankarshana (Balarama) in the form of dragging Hastinapura and Samba’s wedding”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
