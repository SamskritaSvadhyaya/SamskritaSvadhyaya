Shrimad Bhagavatam - Book 2

Canto 10

Chapter 55

The story of Pradyumna’s birth and the destruction of the asura Shambara

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

प्रद्युम्नोत्पत्तिनिरूपणं शम्भरासुरवधः च नाम​ पञ्चपञ्चाशत्तमः अध्यायः

(40 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
कामस्तु वासुदेवांशो दग्धः प्राग् रुद्रमन्युना ।
देहोपपत्तये भूयस्तमेव प्रत्यपद्यत ॥10.55.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, कामः Kamadeva, तु indeed, वासुदेव-अंशः a plenary portion of Vasudeva, दग्धः burnt, प्राक् previously, रुद्र-मन्युना by the anger of Rudra, देह-उपपत्तये to obtain a body, भूयः again, तम् Him, एव alone, प्रत्यपद्यत took shelter of

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— प्राक् रुद्रमन्युना दग्धः वासुदेवांशः कामः तु भूयः देहोपपत्तये तम् एव प्रत्यपद्यत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, प्राक् previously, रुद्रमन्युना दग्धः burnt by the anger of Rudra, वासुदेवांशः कामः तु the plenary portion of Vasudeva, Kamadeva indeed, भूयः again, देहोपपत्तये to obtain a body, तम् एव Him alone, प्रत्यपद्यत took shelter of.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Kamadeva, who is a plenary portion of Lord Vasudeva, had previously been burnt to ashes by the anger of Rudra. To obtain a new physical body, he again took shelter of that very same Lord.

स एव जातो वैदर्भ्यां कृष्णवीर्यसमुद्भवः ।
प्रद्युम्न इति विख्यातः सर्वतोऽनवमः पितुः॥10.55.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, एव indeed, जातः was born, वैदर्भ्याम् in the daughter of Vidarbha, कृष्ण-वीर्य-समुद्भवः generated by the potent semen of Krishna, प्रद्युम्नः Pradyumna, इति thus, विख्यातः famous, सर्वतः in all respects, अनवमः not inferior, पितुः to his father

Anvaya: सः एव कृष्णवीर्यसमुद्भवः वैदर्भ्यां जातः। प्रद्युम्नः इति विख्यातः पितुः सर्वतः अनवमः ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He, एव indeed, कृष्णवीर्यसमुद्भवः generated by the potent semen of Krishna, वैदर्भ्यां in the daughter of Vidarbha, जातः was born. प्रद्युम्नः इति विख्यातः Famous as Pradyumna, पितुः सर्वतः अनवमः (he) was not inferior in any respect to his father

Simple Meaning: Generated by the potent energy of Krishna, he was born from the womb of Rukmini, the princess of Vidarbha. Famous by the name Pradyumna, he was in no way inferior to his father.

तं शम्बरः कामरूपी हृत्वा तोकमनिर्दशम् ।
स विदित्वाऽऽत्मनः शत्रुं प्रास्योदन्वत्यगाद् गृहम् ॥10.55.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him, शम्बरः Shambara, काम-रूपी capable of assuming any form at will, हृत्वा kidnapping, तोकम् the infant, अनिर्दशम् not even ten days old, सः he, विदित्वा knowing, आत्मनः his own, शत्रुम् enemy, प्रास्य throwing, उदन्वति into the ocean, अगात् went, गृहम् home

Anvaya: कामरूपी शम्बरः तम् अनिर्दशं तोकं हृत्वा आत्मनः शत्रुं विदित्वा उदन्वति प्रास्य गृहम् अगात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कामरूपी शम्बरः Capable of assuming any form at will, Shambara, तम् अनिर्दशं तोकं him, the infant who was not even ten days old, हृत्वा kidnapping, आत्मनः शत्रुं विदित्वा knowing to be his own enemy, उदन्वति प्रास्य throwing into the ocean, गृहम् अगात् went to his home.

Simple Meaning: The asura Shambara, who could assume any form at will, kidnapped the infant before he was even ten days old. Knowing the child to be his own enemy, Shambara threw him into the ocean and returned home.

तं निर्जगार बलवान् मीनः सोऽप्यपरैः सह ।
वृतो जालेन महता गृहीतो मत्स्यजीविभिः ॥10.55.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him, निर्जगार swallowed, बलवान् powerful, मीनः a fish, सः that fish, अपि also, अपरैः with others, सह along, वृतः trapped, जालेन by a net, महता large, गृहीतः was caught, मत्स्य-जीविभिः by people who made their livelihood by catching fish (fishermen)

Anvaya: बलवान् मीनः तं निर्जगार। सः अपि अपरैः सह महता जालेन वृतः मत्स्यजीविभिः गृहीतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बलवान् मीनः A powerful fish, तं निर्जगार swallowed him. सः अपि अपरैः सह That fish also along with others, महता जालेन वृतः trapped by a large net, मत्स्यजीविभिः गृहीतः was caught by fishermen.

Simple Meaning: A powerful fish swallowed the infant. Later, along with other fish, that very same fish was trapped in a huge net and caught by fishermen.

तं शम्बराय कैवर्ता उपाजह्रुरुपायनम् ।
सूदा महानसं नीत्वावद्यन् स्वधितिनाद्भुतम् ॥10.55.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् that fish, शम्बराय to Shambara, कैवर्ताः the fishermen, उपाजह्रुः presented, उपायनम् as a gift, सूदाः the cooks, महानसम् to the kitchen, नीत्वा bringing, अवद्यन् cut it open, स्वधितिना with a knife, अद्भुतम् wondrous

Anvaya: अद्भुतं तं कैवर्ताः उपायनं शम्बराय उपाजह्रुः सूदाः महानसं नीत्वा स्वधितिना अवद्यन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अद्भुतं तं That wondrous fish, कैवर्ताः the fishermen, उपायनं as a gift, शम्बराय उपाजह्रुः presented to Shambara. सूदाः The cooks, महानसं to the kitchen, नीत्वा bringing, स्वधितिना अवद्यन् cut it open with a knife.

Simple Meaning: The fishermen presented that extraordinary fish as a gift to Shambara. The cooks brought it into the kitchen and cut it open with a knife.

दृष्ट्वा तदुदरे बालं मायावत्यै न्यवेदयन् ।
नारदोऽकथयत् सर्वं तस्याः शङ्कितचेतसः ।
बालस्य तत्त्वमुत्पत्तिं मत्स्योदरनिवेशनम् ॥10.55.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: दृष्ट्वा having seen, तत्-उदरे in its belly, बालम् the child, मायावत्यै to Mayavati, न्यवेदयन् they presented, नारदः Narada, अकथयत् explained, सर्वम् everything, तस्याः to her, शङ्कित-चेतसः whose mind was full of doubts, बालस्य of the child, तत्त्वम् the true identity, उत्पत्तिम् the origin, मत्स्य-उदर-निवेशनम् the placement inside the belly of the fish

Anvaya: तदुदरे बालं दृष्ट्वा (सूदाः) मायावत्यै न्यवेदयन् शङ्कितचेतसः तस्याः नारदः बालस्य तत्त्वम् उत्पत्तिं मत्स्योदरनिवेशनं सर्वम् अकथयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तदुदरे In its belly, बालं the child, दृष्ट्वा having seen, मायावत्यै न्यवेदयन् they presented to Mayavati. शङ्कितचेतसः तस्याः Whose mind was full of doubts, to her, नारदः Narada, बालस्य तत्त्वम् उत्पत्तिं the true origin of the child, मत्स्योदरनिवेशनं the placement inside the belly of the fish, सर्वम् अकथयत् explained everything.

Simple Meaning: Seeing the child in the belly of the fish, the cooks brought him to Mayavati. To resolve the doubts in her mind, Narada Muni arrived and explained everything to her: the child's true identity, his birth, and how he ended up inside the belly of the fish.

सा च कामस्य वै पत्नी रतिर्नाम यशस्विनी ।
पत्युर्निर्दग्धदेहस्य देहोत्पत्तिं प्रतीक्षती ॥10.55.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she, च and, कामस्य of Kamadeva, वै indeed, पत्नी the wife, रतिः Rati, नाम named, यशस्विनी famous, पत्युः of her husband, निर्दग्ध-देहस्य whose body was burnt to ashes, देह-उत्पत्तिम् the birth of a new body, प्रतीक्षती waiting for

Anvaya: सा च वै कामस्य पत्नी यशस्विनी रतिः नाम (या) निर्दग्धदेहस्य पत्युः देहोत्पत्तिं प्रतीक्षती (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सा च वै And She indeed, कामस्य यशस्विनी पत्नी (was) the famous wife of Kamadeva, रतिः नाम named Rati, (या) निर्दग्धदेहस्य पत्युः who, of her husband whose body was burnt to ashes, देहोत्पत्तिं प्रतीक्षती waiting for the birth of a new body.

Simple Meaning: She was indeed the famous wife of Kamadeva, named Rati. She had been waiting for her husband, whose body had been burnt to ashes, to obtain a new physical body.

निरूपिता शम्बरेण सा सूपौदनसाधने ।
कामदेवं शिशुं बुद्ध्वा चक्रे स्नेहं तदार्भके ॥10.55.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: निरूपिता appointed, शम्बरेण by Shambara, सा she, सूप-ओदन-साधने to prepare soup and rice, काम-देवम् Kamadeva, शिशुम् the infant, बुद्ध्वा understanding, चक्रे felt, स्नेहम् affection, तदा then, आर्भके for the child

Anvaya: शम्बरेण सूपौदनसाधने निरूपिता सा शिशुं कामदेवं बुद्ध्वा तदा आर्भके स्नेहं चक्रे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शम्बरेण By Shambara, सूपौदनसाधने to prepare soup and rice, निरूपिता appointed, सा she, शिशुं the infant, कामदेवं बुद्ध्वा understanding as Kamadeva, तदा then, आर्भके स्नेहं चक्रे felt affection for the child.

Simple Meaning: Appointed by Shambara to supervise the preparation of soup and rice, she understood that this infant was actually Kamadeva. Consequently, she immediately felt deep affection for the child.

नातिदीर्घेण कालेन स कार्ष्णी रूढयौवनः ।
जनयामास नारीणां वीक्षन्तीणां च विभ्रमम् ॥10.55.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: न अति-दीर्घेण by not very long, कालेन time, सः he, कार्ष्णीः the son of Krishna, रूढ-यौवनः developed youthfulness, जनयामास generated, नारीणाम् of women, वीक्षन्तीनाम् who saw him, च and, विभ्रमम् romantic bewilderment

Anvaya: न अतिदीर्घेण कालेन रूढयौवनः सः कार्ष्णीः वीक्षन्तीणां नारीणां च विभ्रमं जनयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: न अतिदीर्घेण कालेन After not a very long time, रूढयौवनः सः कार्ष्णीः he, the son of Krishna developed youthfulness, वीक्षन्तीणां नारीणां women whoever saw him, च and, विभ्रमं romantic bewilderment, जनयामास generated.

Simple Meaning: Within a short time, that son of Krishna attained full youthfulness. He aroused romantic bewilderment in the minds of all the women whoever saw him.

सा तं पतिं पद्मदलायतेक्षणं प्रलम्बबाहुं नरलोकसुन्दरम् ।
सव्रीडहासोत्तभितभ्रुवेक्षती प्रीत्योपतस्थे रतिरङ्ग सौरतैः ॥10.55.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she, तम् him, पतिम् her husband, पद्म-दल-आयत-ईक्षणम् whose eyes were as broad as lotus petals, प्रलम्ब-बाहुम् possessed of long arms, नर-लोक-सुन्दरम् the most handsome man in human society, स-व्रीड-हास-उत्तभित-भ्रू-ईक्षती looking with bashful smiles and raised eyebrows, प्रीत्या with affection, उपतस्थे approached, रतिः Rati, अङ्ग O King, सौरतैः with amorous expressions

Anvaya: अङ्ग! सा रतिः पद्मदलायतेक्षणं प्रलम्बबाहुं नरलोकसुन्दरं तं पतिं सव्रीडहासोत्तभितभ्रुवेक्षती प्रीत्या सौरतैः उपतस्थे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अङ्ग O King, सा रतिः That Rati, पद्मदलायतेक्षणं whose eyes were as broad as lotus petals, प्रलम्बबाहुं possessed of long arms, नरलोकसुन्दरं the most handsome man in human society, तं him, पतिं as her husband, सव्रीडहासोत्तभितभ्रुवेक्षती looking with bashful smiles and raised eyebrows, प्रीत्या with affection, सौरतैः with amorous expressions, उपतस्थे approached.

Simple Meaning: O King! Rati approached him, her husband, who had long arms and eyes as wide as lotus petals, and who was the most handsome man in human society. Looking at him with bashful smiles and raised eyebrows, she expressed her affection through amorous gestures.

तामाह भगवान् कार्ष्णिर्मातस्ते मतिरन्यथा ।
मातृभावमतिक्रम्य वर्तसे कामिनी यथा ॥10.55.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् to her, आह said, भगवान् Lord, कार्ष्णिः the son of Krishna, मातः O mother, ते your, मतिः mentality, अन्यथा perverse, मातृ-भावम् maternal attitude, अतिक्रम्य transgressing, वर्तसे you are acting, कामिनी a lusty woman, यथा like

Anvaya: भगवान् कार्ष्णिः ताम् आह—मातः ते मतिः अन्यथा (अस्ति)। मातृभावम् अतिक्रम्य कामिनी यथा वर्तसे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवान् कार्ष्णिः The Lord, the son of Krishna, ताम् आह said to her, मातः O mother, ते मतिः your mentality, अन्यथा (is) perverse. मातृभावम् अतिक्रम्य Transgressing maternal attitude, कामिनी यथा like a lusty woman, वर्तसे you are acting.

Simple Meaning: Lord Pradyumna, the son of Krishna, said to her: O mother, your mentality is improper. Transgressing the appropriate maternal attitude, you are acting like a woman full of lust.

रतिः उवाच
भवान् नारायणसुतः शम्बरेणाहृतो गृहात् ।
अहं तेऽधिकृता पत्नी रतिः कामो भवान् प्रभो ॥10.55.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: रतिः उवाच Rati said, भवान् you, नारायण-सुतः the son of Narayana, शम्बरेण by Shambara, आहृतः stolen, गृहात् from the home, अहम् I, ते your, अधिकृता lawfully wedded, पत्नी wife, रतिः Rati, कामः Kamadeva, भवान् you, प्रभो O master

Anvaya: रतिः उवाच—भवान् नारायणसुतः शम्बरेण गृहात् आहृतः। प्रभो! अहं ते अधिकृता पत्नी रतिः, भवान् (च) कामः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रतिः उवाच Rati said, भवान् नारायणसुतः You the son of Narayana, शम्बरेण By Shambara, गृहात् आहृतः (are) stolen from the home. प्रभो O master, अहं I am, ते अधिकृता पत्नी रतिः your lawfully wedded wife, Rati, भवान् कामः you are Kamadeva.

Simple Meaning: Rati said: You are the son of Lord Narayana, and you were stolen from your home by Shambara. O master, I am your lawfully wedded wife, Rati, and you are my husband, Kamadeva.

एष त्वानिर्दशं सिन्धावक्षिपच्छम्बरोऽसुरः ।
मत्स्योऽग्रसीत्तदुदरादिह प्राप्तो भवान् प्रभो ॥10.55.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: एषः this, त्वा you, अनिर्दशम् not even ten days old, सिन्धौ into the ocean, अक्षिपत् threw, शम्बरः Shambara, असुरः the asura, मत्स्यः a fish, अग्रसीत् swallowed, तत्-उदरात् from its belly, इह here, प्राप्तः obtained/reached, भवान् you, प्रभो O master

Anvaya: प्रभो! एषः असुरः शम्बरः अनिर्दशं त्वा सिन्धौ अक्षिपत्। मत्स्यः अग्रसीत्। तदुदरात् भवान् इह प्राप्तः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रभो O master, एषः असुरः शम्बरः This asura Shambara, अनिर्दशं त्वा you who were not even ten days old, सिन्धौ अक्षिपत् threw into the ocean. मत्स्यः अग्रसीत् A fish swallowed (you). तदुदरात् From its belly, भवान् इह प्राप्तः you reached here.

Simple Meaning: O master, when you were not even ten days old, this asura Shambara threw you into the ocean. A fish swallowed you, and from the belly of that fish you have reached here.

तमिमं जहि दुर्धर्षं दुर्जयं शत्रुमात्मनः ।
मायाशतविदं त्वं च मायाभिर्मोहनादिभिः ॥10.55.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् इमम् this very, जहि kill, दुर्धर्षम् invincible, दुर्जयम् unconquerable, शत्रुम् the enemy, आत्मनः of your own, माया-शत-विदम् who knows hundreds of magic illusions, त्वम् you, च and, मायाभिः with magic illusions, मोहन-आदिभिः such as those that bewilder

Anvaya: त्वम् आत्मनः शत्रुं दुर्धर्षं दुर्जयं मायाशतविदं तम् इमं मोहनादिभिः मायाभिः च जहि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: त्वम् You, आत्मनः शत्रुं your own enemy, दुर्धर्षं दुर्जयं मायाशतविदं invincible, unconquerable, who knows hundreds of magic illusions, तम् इमं this very person, मोहनादिभिः such as those that bewilder, मायाभिः च and with magic illusions, जहि kill.

Simple Meaning: Therefore, you must kill this very enemy of yours, who is otherwise invincible, unconquerable, and a master of hundreds of magic illusions. You should slay him by employing your own mystic powers, such as those that cause utter bewilderment.

परिशोचति ते माता कुररीव गतप्रजा ।
पुत्रस्नेहाकुला दीना विवत्सा गौरिवातुरा ॥10.55.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: परिशोचति laments, ते your, माता mother, कुररी like an osprey bird, इव as if, गत-प्रजा whose child is lost, पुत्र-स्नेह-आकुला overwhelmed with affection for her son, दीना wretched, विवत्सा whose calf is taken away, गौः a cow, इव like, आतुरा distressed

Anvaya: गतप्रजा कुररी इव, पुत्रस्नेहाकुला दीना विवत्सा गौः इव आतुरा ते माता परिशोचति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गतप्रजा कुररी इव Like an osprey bird whose child is lost, पुत्रस्नेहाकुला overwhelmed with affection for her son, दीना विवत्सा गौः इव wretched like a cow whose calf is taken away, आतुरा ते माता your distressed mother, परिशोचति laments.

Simple Meaning: Your mother laments for you like an osprey bird that has lost its offspring. Overwhelmed with affection for her son, she is as wretched and deeply distressed as a cow deprived of her young calf.

प्रभाष्यैवं ददौ विद्यां प्रद्युम्नाय महात्मने ।
मायावती महामायां सर्वमायाविनाशिनीम् ॥10.55.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रभाष्य speaking, एवम् thus, ददौ gave, विद्याम् the mystic knowledge, प्रद्युम्नाय to Pradyumna, महा-आत्मने the great soul, मायावती Mayavati, महा-मायाम् Mahamaya, सर्व-माया-विनाशिनीम् which can destroy all magic illusions

Anvaya: एवं प्रभाष्य मायावती महात्मने प्रद्युम्नाय सर्वमायाविनाशिनीं महामायां विद्यां ददौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवं प्रभाष्य Thus speaking, मायावती Mayavati, महात्मने प्रद्युम्नाय to the great soul Pradyumna, सर्वमायाविनाशिनीं which can destroy all magic illusions, महामायां विद्यां Mahamaya mystic knowledge, ददौ gave.

Simple Meaning: Having spoken thus, Mayavati gave the great soul Pradyumna the mystic knowledge known as Mahamaya, which is capable of destroying all magic illusions.

स च शम्बरमभ्येत्य संयुगाय समाह्वयत् ।
अविषह्यैस्तमाक्षेपैः क्षिपन् सञ्जनयन् कलिम् ॥10.55.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, च and, शम्बरम् Shambara, अभ्येत्य approaching, संयुगाय for battle, समाह्वयत् challenged, अविषह्यैः with intolerable, तम् him, आक्षेपैः insults, क्षिपन् reviling, सञ्जनयन् generating, कलिम् a fight

Anvaya: सः च शम्बरम् अभ्येत्य अविषह्यैः आक्षेपैः तं क्षिपन् कलिं सञ्जनयन् संयुगाय समाह्वयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः च And He, शम्बरम् अभ्येत्य approaching Shambara, अविषह्यैः आक्षेपैः with intolerable insults, तं क्षिपन् reviling him, कलिं सञ्जनयन् generating a fight, संयुगाय समाह्वयत् challenged for battle.

Simple Meaning: Approaching Shambara, Pradyumna challenged him to battle. He reviled the asura with intolerable insults, deliberately provoking a fight.

सोऽधिक्षिप्तो दुर्वचोभिः पादाहत इवोरगः ।
निश्चक्राम गदापाणिरमर्षात्तम्रलोचनः ॥10.55.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, अधिक्षिप्तः insulted, दुर्वचोभिः by harsh words, पाद-आहतः kicked by a foot, इव like, उरगः a serpent, निश्चक्राम came out, गदा-पाणिः with a club in his hand, अमर्षात् out of anger, ताम्र-लोचनः whose eyes were red like copper

Anvaya: दुर्वचोभिः अधिक्षिप्तः पादाहतः उरगः इव अमर्षात् ताम्रलोचनः गदापाणिः (सन्) सः निश्चक्राम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दुर्वचोभिः अधिक्षिप्तः Insulted by harsh words, पादाहतः उरगः इव like a serpent kicked by a foot, अमर्षात् ताम्रलोचनः with eyes becoming red like copper out of anger, गदापाणिः with a club in his hand, सः निश्चक्राम he came out.

Simple Meaning: Insulted by those harsh words, Shambara became like a serpent kicked by someone's foot. He emerged from his palace with his eyes burning copper red with rage, wielding a club in his hand.

गदामाविध्य तरसा प्रद्युम्नाय महात्मने ।
प्रक्षिप्य व्यनदन्नादं वज्रनिष्पेषनिष्ठुरम् ॥10.55.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: गदाम् club, आविध्य whirling, तरसा with great speed, प्रद्युम्नाय at Pradyumna, महा-आत्मने the great soul, प्रक्षिप्य hurling, व्यनदत् roared, नादम् a sound, वज्र-निष्पेष-निष्ठुरम् as harsh as the crash of a thunderbolt

Anvaya: महात्मने प्रद्युम्नाय तरसा गदाम् आविध्य प्रक्षिप्य वज्रनिष्पेषनिष्ठुरं नादं व्यनदत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महात्मने प्रद्युम्नाय At the great soul Pradyumna, तरसा गदाम् आविध्य swiftly whirling the club, प्रक्षिप्य and hurling it, वज्रनिष्पेषनिष्ठुरं नादं व्यनदत् he roared a sound as harsh as the crash of a thunderbolt.

Simple Meaning: Swiftly whirling his club, the asura hurled it at the great soul Pradyumna and let out a terrifying roar that was as harsh and deafening as the crash of a thunderbolt.

तामापतन्तीं भगवान् प्रद्युम्नो गदया गदाम् ।
अपास्य शत्रवे क्रुद्धः प्राहिणोत् स्वगदां नृप ॥10.55.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् that, आपतन्तीम् flying towards him, भगवान् the opulent Lord, प्रद्युम्नः Pradyumna, गदया with his club, गदाम् club, अपास्य striking aside, शत्रवे at the enemy, क्रुद्धः angry, प्राहिणोत् hurled, स्व-गदाम् his own club, नृप O King

Anvaya: नृप! भगवान् प्रद्युम्नः आपतन्तीं तां गदां गदया अपास्य क्रुद्धः (सन्) शत्रवे स्वगदां प्राहिणोत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King, भगवान् प्रद्युम्नः the opulent Lord Pradyumna, आपतन्तीं तां गदां that incoming club, गदया अपास्य striking aside with his own club, क्रुद्धः (सन्) becoming angry, शत्रवे स्वगदां प्राहिणोत् hurled his own club at the enemy.

Simple Meaning: O King, the opulent Lord Pradyumna easily struck aside the incoming club with his own. Getting angry, he then hurled his own club forcefully at the enemy.

स च मायां समाश्रित्य दैतेयीं मयदर्शिताम् ।
मुमुचेऽस्त्रमयं वर्षं कार्ष्णौ वैहायसोऽसुरः ॥10.55.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, च and, मायाम् mystic power, समाश्रित्य taking shelter of, दैतेयीम् belonging to the Daityas, मय-दर्शिताम् revealed by Maya Danava, मुमुचे showered, अस्त्र-मयम् consisting of weapons, वर्षम् a rain, कार्ष्णौ on the son of Krishna, वैहायसः remaining in the sky, असुरः the asura

Anvaya: वैहायसः सः असुरः च मयदर्शितां दैतेयीं मायां समाश्रित्य कार्ष्णौ अस्त्रमयं वर्षं मुमुचे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वैहायसः सः असुरः च And that asura staying in the sky, मयदर्शितां दैतेयीं मायां समाश्रित्य taking shelter of the Daitya mystic power revealed by Maya Danava, कार्ष्णौ अस्त्रमयं वर्षं मुमुचे showered a rain consisting of weapons on the son of Krishna.

Simple Meaning: Taking to the sky, that asura resorted to the deceptive Daitya mystic power originally revealed by Maya Danava. From midair, he began showering a dense rain of lethal weapons down upon the son of Krishna.

बाध्यमानोऽस्त्रवर्षेण रौक्मिणेयो महारथः ।
सत्त्वात्मिकां महाविद्यां सर्वमायोपमर्दिनीम् ॥10.55.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: बाध्यमानः being harassed, अस्त्र-वर्षेण by the rain of weapons, रौक्मिणेयः the son of Rukmini, महा-रथः the great chariot fighter, सत्त्व-आत्मिकाम् full of the mode of goodness, महा-विद्याम् the great mystic science, सर्व-माया-उपमर्दिनीम् which destroys all deceptive illusions

Anvaya: अस्त्रवर्षेण बाध्यमानः महारथः रौक्मिणेयः सर्वमायोपमर्दिनीं सत्त्वात्मिकां महाविद्याम् (सस्मार)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अस्त्रवर्षेण बाध्यमानः Being harassed by the rain of weapons, महारथः रौक्मिणेयः the great chariot fighter who is the son of Rukmini, सर्वमायोपमर्दिनीं सत्त्वात्मिकां महाविद्याम् invoked the great mystic science full of the mode of goodness which destroys all deceptive illusions.

Simple Meaning: Being harassed by that fierce rain of weapons, the great chariot warrior Pradyumna, the son of Rukmini, invoked a supreme mystic science rooted in pure goodness, which can instantly destroy all forms of deceptive illusions.

ततो गौह्यकगान्धर्वपैशाचोरगराक्षसीः ।
प्रायुङ्क्त शतशो दैत्यः कार्ष्णिर्व्यधमयत् स ताः ॥10.55.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, गौह्यक-गान्धर्व-पैशाच-उरग-राक्षसीः belonging to the Guhyakas (Yakshas) and Gandharvas and Pisachas and Uragas (Nagas) and Rakshasas, प्रायुङ्क्त employed, शतशः by the hundreds, दैत्यः the Daitya, कार्ष्णिः the son of Krishna, व्यधमयत् destroyed, सः he, ताः them

Anvaya: ततः दैत्यः शतशः गौह्यकगान्धर्वपैशाचोरगराक्षसीः (मायाः) प्रायुङ्क्त। सः कार्ष्णिः ताः व्यधमयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः दैत्यः शतशः Then the Daitya by the hundreds, गौह्यकगान्धर्वपैशाचोरगराक्षसीः प्रायुङ्क्त employed illusions belonging to the Guhyakas, Gandharvas, Pisachas, Uragas and Rakshasas. सः कार्ष्णिः ताः व्यधमयत् But he, the son of Krishna, destroyed them all.

Simple Meaning: Then the Daitya employed hundreds of other mystic illusions belonging to the Guhyakas (Yakshas), Gandharvas, Pisachas, Uragas (Nagas), and Rakshasas. However, Pradyumna, the son of Krishna, completely destroyed every single one of them.

निशातमसिमुद्यम्य सकिरीटं सकुण्डलम् ।
शम्बरस्य शिरः कायात् ताम्रश्मश्र्वोजसाहरत् ॥10.55.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: निशातम् a sharp, असिम् sword, उद्यम्य wielding, स-किरीटम् along with the helmet, स-कुण्डलम् along with the earrings, शम्बरस्य of Shambara, शिरः the head, कायात् from the body, ताम्र-श्मश्रु with a copper colored beard, ओजसा with great force, अहरत् severed

श्मश्रु,  उकारान्तः, नपुंसकलिङ्गः, घिसंज्ञकः, प्रथमैकवचनम् द्वितीयैकवचनम् src. Shabdapatha, Ashtadhyayi . in this context it is प्रथमैकवचनम्

Anvaya: निशातम् असिम् उद्यम्य (सः) शम्बरस्य सकिरीटं सकुण्डलं ताम्रश्मश्रु शिरः ओजसा कायात् अहरत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: निशातम् असिम् उद्यम्य Wielding a sharp sword, शम्बरस्य सकिरीटं सकुण्डलं ताम्रश्मश्रु शिरः Shambaras head which was complete with a helmet and earrings and a copper colored beard, ओजसा कायात् अहरत् he severed with great force from the body.

Simple Meaning: Wielding a razor sharp sword, Pradyumna used great force to sever Shambara's head from his body, striking it off complete with its helmet, earrings, and copper colored beard.

आकीर्यमाणो दिविजैः स्तुवद्भिः कुसुमोत्करैः ।
भार्ययाम्बरचारिण्या पुरं नीतो विहायसा ॥10.55.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: आकीर्यमाणः being showered, दिविजैः by the demigods, स्तुवद्भिः who were offering prayers, कुसुम-उत्करैः with heaps of flowers, भार्यया by his wife, अम्बर-चारिण्या who travels through the sky, पुरम् to the city, नीतः was brought, विहायसा through the aerial path

Anvaya: स्तुवद्भिः दिविजैः कुसुमोत्करैः आकीर्यमाणः (सः) अम्बरचारिण्या भार्यया विहायसा पुरं नीतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: स्तुवद्भिः दिविजैः By the praising demigods, कुसुमोत्करैः आकीर्यमाणः being showered with heaps of flowers, अम्बरचारिण्या भार्यया by his sky traveling wife, विहायसा पुरं नीतः he was brought through the aerial path to the city.

Simple Meaning: While being praised by the demigods and showered with heaps of celestial flowers, Pradyumna was carried through the sky back to his city Dwaraka by his wife, who possessed the power to travel through the air (because she was an apsara).

अन्तःपुरवरं राजन् ललनाशतसङ्कुलम् ।
विवेश पत्न्या गगनाद् विद्युतेव बलाहकः ॥10.55.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्तःपुर-वरम् the most excellent inner palace, राजन् O King, ललना-शत-सङ्कुलम् crowded with hundreds of women, विवेश entered, पत्न्या with his wife, गगनात् from the sky, विद्युता with lightning, इव like, बलाहकः a rain cloud

Anvaya: राजन्! (सः) गगनात् विद्युता बलाहकः इव पत्न्या ललनाशतसङ्कुलम् अन्तःपुरवरं विवेश।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, गगनात् From the sky, विद्युता बलाहकः इव like a rain cloud with lightning, पत्न्या with his wife, ललनाशतसङ्कुलम् अन्तःपुरवरं विवेश he entered the most excellent inner palace crowded with hundreds of women.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit! Descending from the sky with his wife, Pradyumna entered his magnificent inner palace, which was crowded with hundreds of women, appearing just like a dark rain cloud accompanied by a brilliant flash of lightning.

तं दृष्ट्वा जलदश्यामं पीतकौशेयवाससम् ।
प्रलम्बबाहुं ताम्राक्षं सुस्मितं रुचिराननम् ॥10.55.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him, दृष्ट्वा seeing, जलद-श्यामम् dark like a rain cloud, पीत-कौशेय-वाससम् wearing yellow silk garments, प्रलम्ब-बाहुम् possessed of long arms, ताम्र-अक्षम् with copper red eyes, सु-स्मितम् beautifully smiling, रुचिर-आननम् with an attractive face

Anvaya: जलदश्यामं पीतकौशेयवाससं प्रलम्बबाहुं ताम्राक्षं सुस्मितं रुचिराननं तं दृष्ट्वा…

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जलदश्यामं पीतकौशेयवाससं Dark like a rain cloud and wearing yellow silk garments, प्रलम्बबाहुं ताम्राक्षं with long arms and copper red eyes, सुस्मितं रुचिराननं तं दृष्ट्वा seeing him who had a beautiful smile and an attractive face…

Simple Meaning: Seeing him who was dark like a rain cloud, dressed in yellow silk garments, possessed of long arms, copper red eyes, a beautiful smile, and a highly attractive face…

स्वलङ्कृतमुखाम्भोजं नीलवक्रालकालिभिः ।
कृष्णं मत्वा स्त्रियो ह्रीता निलिल्युस्तत्र तत्र ह ॥10.55.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: सु-अलङ्कृत-मुख-अम्भोजम् whose lotus face was beautifully adorned, नील-वक्र-अलक-अलिभिः by swarms of dark curly hair locks, कृष्णम् Krishna, मत्वा thinking him to be, स्त्रियः the women, ह्रीताः feeling bashful, निलिल्युः hid themselves, तत्र तत्र here and there, ह indeed

Anvaya: नीलवक्रालकालिभिः स्वलङ्कृतमुखाम्भोजं तं कृष्णं मत्वा स्त्रियः ह्रीताः (सन्) तत्र तत्र निलिल्युः ह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नीलवक्रालकालिभिः स्वलङ्कृतमुखाम्भोजं तं Him, whose lotus face was beautifully adorned by locks of dark curly hair like a swarm of bees, कृष्णं मत्वा thinking to be Krishna, स्त्रियः ह्रीताः (सन्) the women became bashful, तत्र तत्र निलिल्युः ह and indeed hid themselves here and there.

Simple Meaning: Seeing his lotus face beautifully adorned by locks of dark curly hair that resembled a swarm of bees, the women mistook him for Krishna. Overcome with bashfulness, they immediately ran and hid themselves here and there.

अवधार्य शनैरीषद्वैलक्षण्येन योषितः ।
उपजग्मुः प्रमुदिताः सस्त्रीरत्नं सुविस्मिताः ॥10.55.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: अवधार्य understanding, शनैः gradually, ईषत्-वैलक्षण्येन by slight differences in characteristics, योषितः the women, उपजग्मुः approached him, प्रमुदिताः being very glad, स-स्त्री-रत्नम् along with that jewel among women, सु-विस्मिताः greatly astonished

Anvaya: शनैः ईषद्वैलक्षण्येन अवधार्य प्रमुदिताः सुविस्मिताः योषितः सस्त्रीरत्नं (तम्) उपजग्मुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शनैः ईषद्वैलक्षण्येन अवधार्य Gradually understanding by slight differences in his characteristics, प्रमुदिताः सुविस्मिताः योषितः the very glad and greatly astonished women, सस्त्रीरत्नम् उपजग्मुः approached him who was along with that jewel among women.

Simple Meaning: Gradually noticing slight differences in his appearance and characteristics, the women realized he was not Krishna. Feeling both immensely glad and deeply astonished, they came forward to approach him and his wife, who was a jewel among women.

अथ तत्रासितापाङ्गी वैदर्भी वल्गुभाषिणी ।
अस्मरत् स्वसुतं नष्टं स्नेहस्नुतपयोधरा ॥10.55.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथ then, तत्र there, असित-अपाङ्गी having dark corners of her eyes, वैदर्भी Rukmini daughter of the King of Vidarbha, वल्गु-भाषिणी of sweet speech, अस्मरत् remembered, स्व-सुतम् her own son, नष्टम् lost, स्नेह-स्नुत-पयोधरा whose breasts wet with milk out of maternal affection

Anvaya: अथ तत्र असितापाङ्गी वल्गुभाषिणी स्नेहस्नुतपयोधरा वैदर्भी नष्टं स्वसुतम् अस्मरत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ तत्र Then there, असितापाङ्गी वल्गुभाषिणी the sweet speaking Rukmini who had dark corners of her eyes, स्नेहस्नुतपयोधरा वैदर्भी and whose breasts became wet with milk due to maternal affection, नष्टं स्वसुतम् अस्मरत् remembered her own lost son.

Simple Meaning: Then and there, the sweet speaking Rukmini, whose eyes had beautiful dark corners, remembered her own lost son, and out of deep maternal affection, her breasts automatically became wet with milk.

को न्वयं नरवैदूर्यः कस्य वा कमलेक्षणः ।
धृतः कया वा जठरे केयं लब्धा त्वनेन वा ॥10.55.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: कः who, नु indeed, अयम् this, नर-वैदूर्यः jewel among men, कस्य whose, वा or, कमल-ईक्षणः lotus eyed one, धृतः carried, कया by which woman, वा or, जठरे in the womb, का who, इयम् this woman, लब्धा obtained, तु and, अनेन by him, वा or

Anvaya: अयं कमलेक्षणः नरवैदूर्यः कः नु? कस्य वा? कया वा जठरे धृतः? अनेन वा इयं का लब्धा?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अयं कमलेक्षणः नरवैदूर्यः कः नु This lotus eyed jewel among men who indeed is he? कस्य वा Whose son is he? कया वा जठरे धृतः Or by which woman was he carried in the womb? अनेन वा इयं का लब्धा and who is this woman obtained by him?

Simple Meaning: Who indeed is this lotus eyed jewel among men, and whose son is he? What fortunate woman carried him in her womb, and who is this beautiful lady that he has obtained as his companion?

मम चाप्यात्मजो नष्टो नीतो यः सूतिकागृहात् ।
एतत्तुल्यवयोरूपो यदि जीवति कुत्रचित् ॥10.55.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: मम my, च and, अपि also, आत्म-जः son, नष्टः lost, नीतः stolen, यः who, सूतिका-गृहात् from the maternity room, एतत्-तुल्य-वयो-रूपः of an age and appearance equal to this one, यदि if, जीवति he lives, कुत्रचित् somewhere

Anvaya: मम च अपि यः आत्मजः सूतिकागृहात् नीतः नष्टः, यदि (सः) कुत्रचित् जीवति (तर्हि) एतत्तुल्यवयोरूपः (स्यात्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मम च अपि यः आत्मजः And my own son who, सूतिकागृहात् नीतः नष्टः was stolen from the maternity room and lost, यदि कुत्रचित् जीवति if he is living somewhere, एतत्तुल्यवयोरूपः he would be of an age and appearance exactly equal to this one.

Simple Meaning: My own son was lost, stolen right from the maternity room. If he is still alive somewhere today, he would be of the exact same age and handsome appearance as this young man.

कथं त्वनेन संप्राप्तं सारूप्यं शार्ङ्गधन्वनः ।
आकृत्यावयवैर्गत्या स्वरहासावलोकनैः ॥10.55.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: कथम् how, तु indeed, अनेन by this person, संप्राप्तम् attained, सारूप्यम् resemblance in form, शार्ङ्ग-धन्वनः of Krishna who wields the Sharnga bow, आकृत्या by his stature, अवयवैः by his limbs, गत्या by his gait, स्वर-हास-अवलोकनैः by his voice and smile and glance

Anvaya: अनेन आकृत्या अवयवैः गत्या स्वरहासावलोकनैः शार्ङ्गधन्वनः सारूप्यं कथं तु संप्राप्तम्?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अनेन आकृत्या अवयवैः गत्या By this person through his stature, limbs, and gait, स्वरहासावलोकनैः and through his voice, smile, and glance, शार्ङ्गधन्वनः सारूप्यं कथं तु संप्राप्तम् how indeed has such a resemblance in form to the wielder of the Sharnga bow been attained.

Simple Meaning: How indeed has this young man attained such an exact resemblance to Krishna, the wielder of the Sharnga bow? His stature, limbs, gait, voice, smile, and glance are all identical to the Lord's.

स एव वा भवेन्नूनं यो मे गर्भे धृतोऽर्भकः ।
अमुष्मिन् प्रीतिरधिका वामः स्फुरति मे भुजः ॥10.55.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, एव indeed, वा or, भवेत् must be, नूनम् surely, यः who, मे my, गर्भे in the womb, धृतः was carried, अर्भकः the child, अमुष्मिन् toward him, प्रीतिः affection, अधिका excessive, वामः left, स्फुरति is twitching, मे my, भुजः arm

Anvaya: यः अर्भकः मे गर्भे धृतः नूनं सः एव वा भवेत्। अमुष्मिन् अधिका प्रीतिः। मे वामः भुजः स्फुरति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः अर्भकः मे गर्भे धृतः The child who was carried in my womb, नूनं सः एव वा भवेत् surely it must be he indeed. अमुष्मिन् अधिका प्रीतिः Toward him there is excessive affection. मे वामः भुजः स्फुरति My left arm is twitching.

Simple Meaning: He must surely be the very child I carried in my womb. I feel an overwhelming maternal affection toward him, and my left arm is twitching, indicating good fortune.

एवं मीमांसमानायां वैदर्भ्यां देवकीसुतः ।
देवक्यानकदुन्दुभ्यामुत्तमश्लोक आगमत् ॥10.55.35॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, मीमांसमानायाम् while speculating, वैदर्भ्याम् the daughter of Vidarbha, देवकी-सुतः the son of Devaki, देवकी-आनकदुन्दुभ्याम् with Devaki and Anakadundubhi, उत्तम-श्लोकः the Lord praised by choice verses, आगमत् arrived

Anvaya: वैदर्भ्याम् एवं मीमांसमानायां देवकीसुतः उत्तमश्लोकः देवक्यानकदुन्दुभ्याम् (सह) आगमत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वैदर्भ्याम् एवं मीमांसमानायां While the daughter of Vidarbha was thus speculating, देवकीसुतः उत्तमश्लोकः the son of Devaki who is the Lord praised by choice verses, देवक्यानकदुन्दुभ्याम् आगमत् arrived together with Devaki and Anakadundubhi (Vasudeva).

Simple Meaning: While Rukmini was thus pondering and speculating, the son of Devaki, Lord Krishna, who is glorified by choice verses, arrived there accompanied by Devaki and Anakadundubhi (Vasudeva).

विज्ञातर्थोऽपि भगवांस्तूष्णीमास जनार्दनः।
नारदोऽकथयत् सर्वं शम्बराहरणादिकम् ॥10.55.36॥

Word-by-word meaning: विज्ञात-अर्थः knowing the matter, अपि although, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, तूष्णीम् silent, आस remained, जनार्दनः Janardana, नारदः Narada, अकथयत् explained, सर्वम् everything, शम्भर-आहरण-आदिकम् beginning with the kidnapping by Shambara

Anvaya: भगवान् जनार्दनः विज्ञातार्थः अपि तूष्णीम् आस। नारदः शम्बराहरणादिकं सर्वम् अकथयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवान् जनार्दनः विज्ञातार्थः अपि Although the Supreme Lord Janardana fully knew the matter, तूष्णीम् आस he remained silent. नारदः शम्बराहरणादिकं सर्वम् अकथयत् Sage Narada explained everything beginning with the kidnapping of the child by Shambara.

Simple Meaning: Although the Supreme Lord Janardana knew everything perfectly well, He chose to remain silent. Instead, the sage Narada stepped forward and explained the entire history, beginning with how the infant had been kidnapped by Shambara.

तच्छ्रुत्वा महदाश्चर्यं कृष्णान्तःपुरयोषितः ।
अभ्यनन्दन् बहूनब्दान् नष्टं मृतमिवागतम् ॥10.55.37॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that, श्रुत्वा having heard, महत्-आश्चर्यम् great wonder, कृष्ण-अन्तःपुर-योषितः the women of the inner palace of Krishna, अभ्यनन्दन् welcomed joyfully, बहून् अब्दान् for many years, नष्टम् lost, मृतम् dead, इव as if, आगतम् returned

Anvaya: कृष्णान्तःपुरयोषितः तत् महदाश्चर्यं श्रुत्वा बहून् अब्दान् नष्टं मृतम् आगतम् इव अभ्यनन्दन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्णान्तःपुरयोषितः तत् महदाश्चर्यं श्रुत्वा The women of the inner palace of Krishna having heard that great wonder, बहून् अब्दान् नष्टं the one who was lost was many years, मृतम् आगतम् इव अभ्यनन्दन् welcomed him joyfully as if he had returned from the dead.

Simple Meaning: Having heard that wonderful account, the women of Lord Krishna's palace joyfully welcomed Pradyumna. He had been lost to them for many years, and they celebrated his return as if he had come back from the dead.

देवकी वसुदेवश्च कृष्णरामौ तथा स्त्रियः ।
दम्पती तौ परिष्वज्य रुक्मिणी च ययुर्मुदम् ॥10.55.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: देवकी Devaki, वसुदेवः Vasudeva, च and, कृष्ण-रामौ Krishna and Balarama, तथा as well as, स्त्रियः the women, दम्पती the couple, तौ those two, परिष्वज्य having embraced, रुक्मिणी Rukmini, च and, ययुः attained, मुदम् happiness

Anvaya: देवकी वसुदेवः च कृष्णरामौ तथा स्त्रियः रुक्मिणी च तौ दम्पती परिष्वज्य मुदं ययुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: देवकी वसुदेवः च Devaki and Vasudeva, कृष्णरामौ तथा Krishna and Balarama, स्त्रियः रुक्मिणी च as well as the women and Rukmini, तौ दम्पती परिष्वज्य having embraced those two as a couple, मुदं ययुः attained happiness.

Simple Meaning: Devaki, Vasudeva, Krishna, Balarama, Rukmini, and all the women of the palace affectionately embraced the young couple, Pradyumna and Mayavati. Seeing them together filled everyone with immense joy.

नष्टं प्रद्युम्नमायातमाकर्ण्य द्वारकौकसः ।
अहो मृत इवायातो बालो दिष्ट्येति हाब्रुवन् ॥10.55.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: नष्टम् lost, प्रद्युम्नम् Pradyumna, आयातम् returned, आकर्ण्य hearing, द्वारका-ओकसः the residents of Dwaraka, अहो oh, मृतः dead, इव as if, आयातः returned, बालः the boy, दिष्ट्या by good fortune, इति thus, ह indeed, अब्रुवन् spoke

Anvaya: नष्टं प्रद्युम्नम् आयातम् आकर्ण्य द्वारकौकसः –अहो! दिष्ट्या बालः मृतः आयातः इव – इति ह अब्रुवन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नष्टं प्रद्युम्नम् आयातम् आकर्ण्य द्वारकौकसः Hearing that the lost Pradyumna had returned, the residents of Dwaraka, अहो! दिष्ट्या बालः मृतः आयातः इव  Oh! By good fortune, the boy has returned as if from dead, इति ह अब्रुवन् thus they spoke indeed.

Simple Meaning: When the residents of Dwaraka heard that the long-lost Pradyumna had finally come back, they were filled with amazement and exclaimed to one another that by wonderful good fortune, the boy had returned to them as if rising from the dead.

यं वै मुहुः पितृसरूपनिजेशभावास्तन्मातरो यदभजन् रहरूढभावाः ।
चित्रं न तत् खलु रमास्पदबिम्बबिम्बे कामे स्मरेऽक्षिविषये किमुतान्यनार्यः ॥10.55.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: यम् whom, वै indeed, मुहुः repeatedly, पितृ-सरूप-निज-ईश-भावाः seeing him as the master who has the same appearance as his father, तत्-मातरः his mothers, यत् which, अभजन् felt, रहः-रूढ-भावाः attraction arising in secret, चित्रम् surprising, न not, तत् that, खलु certainly, रमा-आस्पद-बिम्ब-बिम्बे in the reflection of the original form who is the abode of Goddess Rama, कामे Kamadeva, स्मरे Smara, अक्षि-विषये before their eyes, किम् उत what to speak of, अन्य-नार्यः other women

Anvaya: यं वै पितृसरूपनिजेशभावाः तन्मातरः मुहुः रहःरूढभावाः यत् अभजन् तत् चित्रं न खलु। रमास्पदबिम्बबिम्बे कामे स्मरे अक्षिविषये अन्यनार्यः किमुत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यं वै पितृसरूपनिजेशभावाः तन्मातरः मुहुः रहःरूढभावाः यत् अभजन् Toward whom indeed his own mothers seeing him as having the exact appearance of his father repeatedly felt attraction arising in secret, तत् चित्रं न खलु that is certainly not surprising. रमास्पदबिम्बबिम्बे कामे स्मरे अक्षिविषये अन्यनार्यः किमुत When Kamadeva Smara who is the exact reflection of the original form of the Lord who is the abode of Goddess Rama was right before their eyes, what to speak of the reaction of other women.

Simple Meaning: It is not surprising at all that Pradyumna's own mothers felt a deep attraction toward him in secret, mistaking him for his father Krishna because of their identical appearance. Since he was Kamadeva himself and a perfect reflection of Lord Krishna, the abode of Goddess Lakshmi, it is no wonder that any other woman would lose her composure upon seeing him before her eyes.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंसायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “प्रद्युम्नोत्पत्तिनिरूपणं शम्भरासुरवधः च नाम​” पञ्चपञ्चाशत्तमः अध्यायः ॥55॥

Thus ends the fifty-fifth discourse named “The story of Pradyumna’s birth and the destruction of the asura Shambara” in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
