Chapter 66

Krishna kills Paundraka and the others

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

पौण्ड्रकादिवधो नाम षट्षष्टितमः अध्यायः

(43 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
नन्दव्रजं गते रामे करूषाधिपतिर्नृप ।
वासुदेवोऽहमित्यज्ञो दूतं कृष्णाय प्राहिणोत् ॥10.66.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, नन्द-व्रजम् to Nanda's Vraja, गते having gone, रामे Balarama, करूष-अधिपतिः the ruler of Karusha (Paundraka), नृप O King (Parikshit), वासुदेवः अहम् "I am Vasudeva", इति thus, अज्ञः the foolish / ignorant one, दूतम् a messenger, कृष्णाय to Krishna, प्राहिणोत् dispatched / sent

Anvaya: श्री शुक उवाच— नृप! रामे नन्दव्रजं गते (सति), "अहं वासुदेवः" इति अज्ञः करूषाधिपतिः कृष्णाय दूतं प्राहिणोत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, नृप O King Parikshit, रामे Balarama, नन्दव्रजं to Nanda's Vraja, गते having departed, "अहं वासुदेवः I am Vasudeva (the Supreme Lord)," इति thinking thus, अज्ञः करूषाधिपतिः the ignorant the ruler of Karusha - Paundraka, कृष्णाय to Krishna, दूतं a messenger, प्राहिणोत् dispatched.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King Parikshit, while Balarama was away visiting Nanda's Vraja, the foolish king of Karusha—Paundraka—imagined himself to be the real Vasudeva and declared “I am Vasudeva”. In his delusion, he sent a messenger to Krishna.

त्वं वासुदेवो भगवानवतीर्णो जगत्पतिः।
इति प्रस्तोभितो बालैर्मेन आत्मानमच्युतम् ॥10.66.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वम् You, वासुदेवः Vasudeva, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, अवतीर्णः incarnated / descended, जगत्-पतिः Master of the universe, इति thus, प्रस्तोभितः flattered / praised falsely, बालैः by foolish people / children, मेने thought / believed, आत्मानम् himself, अच्युतम् to be the infallible Achyuta (Krishna)

Anvaya: "त्वं भगवान् जगत्पतिः वासुदेवः अवतीर्णः (असि)" इति बालैः प्रस्तोभितः (सः) आत्मानम् अच्युतं मेने।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: "त्वं You, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, जगत्पतिः Master of the world, वासुदेवः Vasudeva, अवतीर्णः (असि) have descended," इति thus, बालैः by foolish childish flatterers, प्रस्तोभितः being praised, (सः) he (Paundraka), आत्मानम् himself, अच्युतं to be the infallible Supreme Lord Krishna, मेने imagined/believed.

Simple Meaning: Flattered by foolish people who told him, "You are the Supreme Lord Vasudeva, the Master of the universe who has descended to earth," the foolish Paundraka actually began to believe that he was the infallible Lord Krishna Himself.

दूतं च प्राहिणोन्मन्दः कृष्णायाव्यक्तवर्त्मने ।
द्वारकायां यथा बालो नृपो बालकृतोऽबुधः ॥10.66.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: दूतम् a messenger, च and, प्राहिणोत् sent, मन्दः the dull-witted / foolish, कृष्णाय to Krishna, अव्यक्त-वर्त्मने whose ways/path are unmanifest (unknowable), द्वारकायाम् in Dwaraka, यथा like, बालः a child, नृपः a king, बाल-कृतः made into a king by children, अबुधः ignorant / senseless

Anvaya: (सः) बालकृतः बालः नृपः इव अबुधः मन्दः द्वारकायाम् अव्यक्तवर्त्मने कृष्णाय दूतं प्राहिणोत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सः) He, बालकृतः made a king by other children, बालः नृपः इव like a child playing king, अबुधः senseless, मन्दः dull-witted Paundraka, द्वारकायाम् residing in Dwaraka, अव्यक्तवर्त्मने whose nature and path are beyond comprehension, कृष्णाय unto Lord Krishna, दूतं a messenger, प्राहिणोत् sent.

Simple Meaning: Just like a child made into a pretend king by other playing children, that senseless and foolish Paundraka sent a messenger to Dwaraka to deliver a message to Krishna, whose true nature is unmanifest and far beyond comprehension.

दूतस्तु द्वारकामेत्य सभायामास्थितं प्रभुम् ।
कृष्णं कमलपत्राक्षं राजसन्देशमब्रवीत् ॥10.66.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: दूतः the messenger, तु indeed, द्वारकाम् to Dwaraka, एत्य having arrived, सभायाम् in the assembly hall (Sudharma), आस्थितम् seated, प्रभुम् the Supreme Lord, कृष्णम् Krishna, कमल-पत्र-अक्षम् with lotus-petal eyes, राज-सन्देशम् the king's (Paundraka's) message, अब्रवीत् spoke / delivered

Anvaya: दूतः तु द्वारकाम् एत्य सभायाम् आस्थितं कमलपत्राक्षं प्रभुं कृष्णं राजसन्देशम् अब्रवीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दूतः तु The envoy, द्वारकाम् at Dwaraka, एत्य arriving, सभायाम् in the royal assembly, आस्थितं seated in state, कमलपत्राक्षं whose eyes resemble lotus petals, प्रभुं कृष्णं the Supreme Master, Krishna, राजसन्देशम् the message of King Paundraka, अब्रवीत् delivered.

Simple Meaning: Arriving in Dwaraka, the envoy went to the royal assembly hall where Lord Krishna—the lotus-eyed one—was seated. There, the messenger delivered the message sent by King Paundraka.

वासुदेवोऽवतीर्णोऽहमेक एव न चापरः ।
भूतानामनुकम्पार्थं त्वं तु मिथ्याभिधां त्यज ॥10.66.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: वासुदेवः Vasudeva (the original Supreme Lord), अवतीर्णः descended / incarnated, अहम् I, एकः only one, एव indeed, न not, च and, अपरः any other, भूतानाम् towards living beings, अनुकम्पा-अर्थम् out of compassion / mercy, त्वम् you, तु however, मिथ्या-अभिधाम् the false title/name, त्यज abandon / give up

Anvaya: भूतानाम् अनुकम्पार्थम् अहम् एकः एव वासुदेवः अवतीर्णः, अपरः न च। त्वं तु (मदीयां) मिथ्याभिधां त्यज।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भूतानाम् For all living entities, अनुकम्पार्थम् for the sake of showing mercy, अहम् I, एकः एव the one and only, वासुदेवः Vasudeva, अवतीर्णः have descended to earth, अपरः any second person, न च does not exist. त्वं You, तु on your part, (मदीयां) my, मिथ्याभिधां false title ("Vasudeva"), त्यज must abandon.

Simple Meaning: The messenger delivered Paundraka's message: "I am the one and only Vasudeva, descended to earth out of compassion for all living beings; there is no other Vasudeva! Therefore, you must give up the false title and name 'Vasudeva' that you have assumed!"

यानि त्वमस्मच्चिह्नानि मौढ्याद् बिभर्षि सात्वत ।
त्यक्त्वैहि मां त्वं शरणं नो चेद् देहि ममाहवम् ॥10.66.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: यानि which, त्वम् you, अस्मत्-चिह्नानि my divine symbols / insignias (Sudarshana, Panchajanya, etc.), मौढ्यात् out of foolishness / delusion, बिभर्षि are carrying / wearing, सात्वत O descendent of Satvata (Krishna), त्यक्त्वा giving them up, एहि come, माम् to me, त्वम् you, शरणम् for refuge, नो चेत् or else, देहि give, मम to me, आहवम् battle / war

Anvaya: सात्वत! मौढ्यात् त्वं यानि अस्मच्चिह्नानि बिभर्षि, (तानि) त्यक्त्वा त्वं मम शरणम् एहि, नो चेत् मम आहवं देहि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सात्वत O Krishna of the Satvata clan, मौढ्यात् due to sheer delusion, त्वं you, यानि which, अस्मच्चिह्नानि my personal symbols and insignia, बिभर्षि are wearing, (तानि) options/symbols, त्यक्त्वा giving up, त्वं you, मम my, शरणम् under the shelter, एहि must come; नो चेत् otherwise, मम to me, आहवं fight/combat, देहि offer.

Simple Meaning: Paundraka's message continued: "O Satvata! Out of sheer foolishness you have been sporting my sacred symbols and weapons! Renounce them immediately and surrender at my feet for refuge. If you refuse, then prepare to meet me in battle!"

श्री शुक उवाच
कत्थनं तदुपाकर्ण्य पौण्ड्रकस्याल्पमेधसः ।
उग्रसेनादयः सभ्या उच्चकैर्जहसुस्तदा ॥10.66.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, कत्थनम् boastful words / arrogant foolish talk, तत् that, उपाकर्ण्य hearing, पौण्ड्रकस्य of Paundraka, अल्प-मेधसः of tiny intellect / foolish, उग्रसेन-आदयः King Ugrasena and others, सभ्याः the members of the assembly, उच्चकैः loudly, जहसुः laughed, तदा then

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— अल्पमेधसः पौण्ड्रकस्य तत् कत्थनम् उपाकर्ण्य तदा उग्रसेनादयः सभ्याः उच्चकैः जहसुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अल्पमेधसः of the feeble-minded, पौण्ड्रकस्य of Paundraka, तत् that, कत्थनम् boastful speech, उपाकर्ण्य having heard, तदा at that moment, उग्रसेनादयः Ugrasena and the others, सभ्याः the royal assembly members, उच्चकैः uproariously, जहसुः burst out laughing.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King, upon hearing this boastful and arrogant message from the foolish, feeble-minded Paundraka, King Ugrasena and all the other members present in the assembly burst into hearty, loud laughter.

उवाच दूतं भगवान् परिहासकथामनु ।
उत्स्रक्ष्ये मूढ चिह्नानि यैस्त्वमेवं विकत्थसे ॥10.66.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: उवाच spoke, दूतम् to the messenger, भगवान् the Supreme Lord (Krishna), परिहास-कथाम् funny/amusing talk, अनु following (after listening to), उत्स्रक्ष्ये I shall yield / discharge / give up, मूढ O fool, चिह्नानि the symbols / insignia, यैः about which, त्वम् you, एवम् thus, विकत्थसे boast / brag

Anvaya: भगवान् परिहासकथाम् अनु दूतम् उवाच— " मूढ! यैः त्वम् एवं विकत्थसे, (तानि) चिह्नानि उत्स्रक्ष्ये।"

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवान् The Supreme Lord Krishna, परिहासकथाम् अनु after the general laughter and joking, दूतम् to the messenger, उवाच spoke: मूढ O foolish one! यैः By whom (being encouraged), त्वम् you, एवं विकत्थसे in this way, brag and boast, (तानि) चिह्नानि those symbols, उत्स्रक्ष्ये I shall release / hurl (at them).

Simple Meaning: After the laughing and joking in the assembly subsided, the Supreme Lord Krishna said to the messenger: "O fool! By whom you are encouraged, I will release those symbols on them only.”

मुखं तदपिधायाज्ञ कङ्कगृध्रवटैर्वृतः ।
शयिष्यसे हतस्तत्र भविता शरणं शुनाम् ॥10.66.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: मुखम् face, तत् that, अपिधाय being covered, अज्ञ O ignorant fool, कङ्क-गृध्र-वटैः by herons, vultures, and kites (or other birds of prey), वृतः surrounded, शयिष्यसे you (your master Paundraka) shall lie dead, हतः slain, तत्र there (on the battlefield), भविता will become, शरणम् a refuge / food, शुनाम् for dogs

Anvaya: अज्ञ! हतः (सन्) कङ्कगृध्रवटैः वृतः तत् मुखम् अपिधाय तत्र शयिष्यसे, (त्वं च) शुनां शरणं भविता।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अज्ञ O ignorant envoy (and through you, Paundraka)! हतः Slain in battle, कङ्कगृध्रवटैः by herons, vultures, and hawks, वृतः surrounded, तत् मुखम् that face of yours, अपिधाय covered (by your fallen arm/hands or by pecking birds), तत्र there on the battlefield, शयिष्यसे you will lie dead;  (त्वं च) and you, शुनां of dogs, शरणं the shelter and sustenance (food), भविता shall become.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna continued His message to the foolish envoy: "O ignorant fool! When I strike your master down on the battlefield, he will lie dead surrounded by vultures, herons, and kites, his face covered up in defeat. He will become food and shelter for stray dogs!"

इति दूतस्तदाक्षेपं स्वामिने सर्वमाहरत् ।
कृष्णोऽपि रथमास्थाय काशीमुपजगाम ह ॥10.66.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, दूतः the messenger, तत्-आक्षेपम् that taunt / sharp reprimand, स्वामिने to his master (Paundraka), सर्वम् everything, आहरत् related / reported, कृष्णः Lord Krishna, अपि also, रथम् His chariot, आस्थाय mounting, काशीम् to Kashi (Varanasi), उपजगाम ह proceeded / marched

Anvaya: इति दूतः स्वामिने तत् सर्वम् आक्षेपम् आहरत्। कृष्णः अपि रथम् आस्थाय काशीम् उपजगाम ह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति दूतः Thus, the messenger, स्वामिने to his master Paundraka, तत् सर्वम् आक्षेपम् that entire stinging reply and rebuke, आहरत् reported. कृष्णः अपि Krishna also, रथम् His chariot, आस्थाय mounting, काशीम् towards Kashi (where Paundraka was staying), उपजगाम ह proceeded.

Simple Meaning: The messenger returned to his master Paundraka and delivered Krishna’s stinging reply in full. Meanwhile, Lord Krishna mounted His chariot and immediately set out toward the city of Kashi where Paundraka was staying.

पौण्ड्रकोऽपि तदुद्योगमुपलभ्य महारथः ।
अक्षौहिणीभ्यां संयुक्तो निश्चक्राम पुराद्द्रुतम् ॥10.66.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: पौण्ड्रकः Paundraka, अपि also, तत्-उद्योगम् His (Krishna's) preparation/march, उपलभ्य learning of, महा-रथः a great chariot warrior, अक्षौहिणीभ्याम् with two akshauhini divisions (of armies), संयुक्तः accompanied, निश्चक्राम marched out, पुरात् from the city (Kashi), द्रुतम् swiftly

Anvaya: महारथः पौण्ड्रकः अपि तदुद्योगम् उपलभ्य अक्षौहिणीभ्यां संयुक्तः पुरात् द्रुतं निश्चक्राम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महारथः पौण्ड्रकः अपि The great chariot warrior, Paundraka also, तदुद्योगम् Krishna's military advance, उपलभ्य learning about, अक्षौहिणीभ्यां with two full akshauhini divisions, संयुक्तः equipped and joined, पुरात् out of the city of Kashi, द्रुतं hurriedly, निश्चक्राम marched forth.

Simple Meaning: Upon learning of Krishna’s march toward him, the mighty Paundraka swiftly mobilized his forces. Accompanied by two full akshauhini divisions of troops, he marched out of the city of Kashi to confront Krishna.

तस्य काशिपतिर्मित्रं पार्ष्णिग्राहोऽन्वयान्नृप ।
अक्षौहिणीभिस्तिसृभिरपश्यत् पौण्ड्रकं हरिः ॥10.66.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य his (Paundraka's), काशि-पतिः the King of Kashi, मित्रम् friend, पार्ष्णि-ग्राहः rear guard / ally supporting from behind, अन्वयात् followed along, नृप O King (Parikshit), अक्षौहिणीभिः with akshauhini divisions, तिसृभिः three, अपश्यत् saw, पौण्ड्रकम् Paundraka, हरिः Hari (Krishna)

Anvaya: नृप! तस्य मित्रं काशिपतिः पार्ष्णिग्राहः तिसृभिः अक्षौहिणीभिः अन्वयात्। हरिः पौण्ड्रकम् अपश्यत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King Parikshit, तस्य his, मित्रं friend, काशिपतिः the ruler of Kashi, पार्ष्णिग्राहः as an ally backing him from behind, तिसृभिः अक्षौहिणीभिः with three akshauhini divisions of troops, अन्वयात् followed behind. हरिः Krishna, पौण्ड्रकम् Paundraka, अपश्यत् spotted / came face to face with.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, Paundraka's ally and friend, the King of Kashi, followed behind him as a rear-guard with three additional akshauhini army divisions. Then Hari came face to face with Paundraka.

शङ्खार्यसिगदाशार्ङ्गश्रीवत्साद्युपलक्षितम् ।
बिभ्राणं कौस्तुभमणिं वनमालाविभूषितम् ॥10.66.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: शङ्ख-अरि-असि-गदा-शार्ङ्ग-श्रीवत्स-आदि-उपलक्षितम् adorned with the conch (Panchajanya), disc (Sudarshana), sword (Nandaka), mace (Kaumodaki), bow (Sharnga), the Shrivatsa mark, and other features, बिभ्राणम् wearing / bearing, कौस्तुभ-मणिम् the jewel Kaustubha, वन-माला-विभूषितम् decorated with a garland of forest flowers

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सर्वस्य अन्तर्बहिः साक्षी The witness of the inside and outside of everything, विभुः the all-powerful Lord Krishna, कृत्यां माहेश्वरीं विज्ञाय recognizing that entity as created through Maheshwara's ritual, तद्विघातार्थं for its destruction, पार्श्वस्थं चक्रम् आदिशत् commanded His Sudarshana Chakra stationed at His side.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna, the all-powerful Supreme Lord who witnesses the inner and outer workings of all beings, recognized that fiery demon as a creation born of Lord Shiva's worship. To destroy it, He commanded His divine Sudarshana Chakra, which was standing by His side.

तत् सूर्यकोटिप्रतिमं सुदर्शनं जाज्वल्यमानं प्रलयनलप्रभम् ।
स्वतेजसा खं ककुभोऽथ रोदसी चक्रं मुकुन्दास्त्रमथाग्निमार्दयत् ॥10.66.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that, सूर्य-कोटि-प्रतिमम् equal to tens of millions of suns, सुदर्शनम् Sudarshana, जाज्वल्यमानम् blazing intensely, प्रलय-अनल-प्रभम् with radiance like the fire of cosmic dissolution, स्व-तेजसा by its own effulgence, खम् the sky, ककुभः all directions, अथ and then, रोदसी heaven and earth, चक्रम् the disc, मुकुन्द-अस्त्रम् weapon of Lord Mukunda (Krishna), अथ then, अग्निम् the fire, आर्दयत् overwhelmed / tormented / repelled

Anvaya: अथ सूर्यकोटिप्रतिमं जाज्वल्यमानं प्रलयनलप्रभं तत् मुकुन्दास्त्रं सुदर्शनं चक्रं स्वतेजसा खं ककुभः रोदसी च (आपूर्य) अग्निम् आर्दयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ Then, सूर्यकोटिप्रतिमं equal in brilliance to ten million suns, जाज्वल्यमानं blazing furiously, प्रलयनलप्रभं having the glow of the final apocalyptic fire, तत् मुकुन्दास्त्रं सुदर्शनं चक्रं that Sudarshana Chakra, the weapon of Lord Mukunda, स्वतेजसा by its own power and light, खं ककुभः रोदसी filling the sky, directions, and the earth and heaven, अग्निम् आर्दयत् struck down and overpowered the fiery creature.

Simple Meaning: Then Sudarshana, the divine disc weapon of Lord Mukunda, blazing intensely with a brilliance equal to tens of millions of suns and shining like the cosmic fire of dissolution, illuminated the sky, all directions, and heaven and earth with its effulgence, completely overpowering and driving back the fiery creature.

कृत्यानलः प्रतिहतः स रथाङ्गपाणेरस्त्रौजसा स नृप भग्नमुखो निवृत्तः ।
वाराणसीं परिसमेत्य सुदक्षिणं तं सर्त्विग्जनं समदहत् स्वकृतोऽभिचारः ॥10.66.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृत्या-अनलः the fiery creature born of black magic, प्रतिहतः repulsed / repelled, सः that, रथाङ्ग-पाणेः of Lord Krishna (who holds the chariot wheel/disc in His hand), अस्त्र-ओजसा by the power of the weapon, सः he, नृप O King (Parikshit), भग्न-मुखः with a crushed / broken face (humbled), निवृत्तः turned back, वाराणसीम् to Varanasi (Kashi), परिसमेत्य returning / surrounding, सुदक्षिणम् Sudakshina, तम् him, स-ऋत्विग्-जनम् along with his priests, समदहत् completely burnt to ashes, स्व-कृतः done unto oneself, अभिचारः ritual of black magic

Anvaya: नृप! रथाङ्गपाणेः अस्त्रौजसा प्रतिहतः सः कृत्यानलः भग्नमुखः निवृत्तः (सन्) वाराणसीं परिसमेत्य सुदक्षिणम् ऋत्विग्जनसहितं तं समदहत्; अभिचारः स्वकृतः (भवति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King Parikshit! रथाङ्गपाणेः अस्त्रौजसा प्रतिहतः repelled by the power of the weapon of Lord Krishna, सः कृत्यानलः that fiery creature, भग्नमुखः with face crushed and humiliated, निवृत्तः turned back, वाराणसीं परिसमेत्य returning to Varanasi, सुदक्षिणम् ऋत्विग्जनसहितं तं Sudakshina along with his officiating priests, समदहत् completely consumed in flames; (for) अभिचारः स्वकृतः black magic ultimately turns back onto its creator.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, repelled and humiliated by the power of Lord Krishna’s divine weapon, that fiery creature turned back. Returning to Varanasi, the black magic entity completely burned to ashes Sudakshina and all his officiating priests—for evil rituals directed at the innocent ultimately recoil upon their creator.

चक्रं च विष्णोस्तदनुप्रविष्टं वाराणसीं साट्टसभालयापणाम् ।
सगोपुराट्टालककोष्ठसङ्कुलां सकोशहस्त्यश्वरथान्नशालाम् ॥10.66.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: चक्रम् disc / weapon, च and, विष्णोः of Lord Vishnu (Shri Krishna), तत्-अनुप्रविष्टम् following right behind that (fiery demon), वाराणसीम् into Varanasi, सा-अट्ट-सभा-आलय-आपणाम् along with its elevated watchtowers/palaces, assembly halls, residences, and market streets, स-गोपुर-अट्टालक-कोष्ठ-सङ्कुलाम् filled with principal gates, towers, and storehouses/granaries, स-कोश-हस्ति-अश्व-रथ-अन्न-शालाम् having treasuries, stalls for elephants, horses, and chariots, and grain storehouses

Anvaya: विष्णोः चक्रं च तदनुप्रविष्टं (सत्) साट्टसभालयापणां सगोपुराट्टालककोष्ठसङ्कुलां सकोशहस्त्यश्वरथान्नशालां वाराणसीं (प्रविवेश)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विष्णोः चक्रं The disc weapon of Lord Vishnu, च and, तदनुप्रविष्टं following right behind that fire, साट्टसभालयापणां (Varanasi) having elevated balconies/palaces, assembly halls, dwellings, and market shops, सगोपुराट्टालककोष्ठसङ्कुलां filled with main gateways, towers, and granaries, सकोशहस्त्यश्वरथान्नशालां complete with treasuries, elephant stables, horse stables, chariot sheds, and grain houses, वाराणसीं (प्रविवेश) entered Varanasi.

Simple Meaning: Following closely behind the fleeing fiery creature, Lord Vishnu's divine Sudarshana Chakra entered Varanasi—a city crowded with grand gateways, watchtowers, granaries, assembly halls, residences, markets, treasuries, and quarters for elephants, horses, chariots, and stored grain.

दग्ध्वा वाराणसीं सर्वां विष्णोश्चक्रं सुदर्शनम् ।
भूयः पार्श्वमुपातिष्ठत् कृष्णस्याक्लिष्टकर्मणः ॥10.66.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: दग्ध्वा having burned, वाराणसीम् the city of Varanasi, सर्वाम् entire / completely, विष्णोः of Lord Vishnu, चक्रम् disc / weapon, सुदर्शनम् Sudarshana, भूयः again, पार्श्वम् to the side, उपातिष्ठत् returned / stood beside, कृष्णस्य of Krishna, अक्लिष्ट-कर्मणः who performs effortless and flawless deeds

Anvaya: विष्णोः सुदर्शनं चक्रं सर्वां वाराणसीं दग्ध्वा भूयः अक्लिष्टकर्मणः कृष्णस्य पार्श्वम् उपातिष्ठत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विष्णोः सुदर्शनं चक्रं The Sudarshana disc of Lord Vishnu, सर्वां वाराणसीं दग्ध्वा having burned down the whole of Varanasi, भूयः again, अक्लिष्टकर्मणः कृष्णस्य पार्श्वम् to the side of Krishna, who acts with complete ease, उपातिष्ठत् returned and took its place.

Simple Meaning: Having completely reduced the entire city of Varanasi to ashes, Lord Vishnu's divine Sudarshana Chakra returned to the side of Krishna, who performs all His heroic and miraculous deeds effortlessly.

य एतच्छ्रावयेन्मर्त्य उत्तमश्लोकविक्रमम् ।
समाहितो वा शृणुयात् सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥10.66.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः whoever, एतत् this, श्रावयेत् recites / causes to hear, मर्त्यः mortal, उत्तम-श्लोक-विक्रमम् the heroic deeds of the Lord praised in sublime verses (Shri Krishna), समाहितः with focused attention / devotion, वा or, शृणुयात् listens to, सर्व-पापैः from all sins, प्रमुच्यते becomes completely liberated

Anvaya: यः मर्त्यः समाहितः (सन्) एतत् उत्तमश्लोकविक्रमं श्रावयेत् शृणुयात् वा, (सः) सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः मर्त्यः Whichever mortal, समाहितः with a focused and devout mind, एतत् उत्तमश्लोकविक्रमं these heroic pastimes of Lord Krishna (who is celebrated in glorious hymns), श्रावयेत् recites to others, शृणुयात् वा or listens to, सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते is fully freed from all sins.

Simple Meaning: Any mortal who, with fixed concentration and devotion, either recites or listens to these glorious pastimes of Lord Krishna—the Lord of noble renown—is completely freed from all sins.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे  “पौण्ड्रकादिवधो नाम” षट्षष्टितमः अध्यायः ॥66॥

Thus ends the sixty-sixth discourse named “Krishna kills Paundraka and the others.”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
