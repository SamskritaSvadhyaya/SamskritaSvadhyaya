Shrimad Bhagavatam - Book 2

Canto 10

Chapter 73

Krishna blesses the liberated kings and returns to Indraprastha

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

कृष्णस्य मुक्तनृपानुग्रहः इन्द्रप्रस्थपुनरागमनं च नाम त्रिसप्ततितमः अध्यायः

(35 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
अयुते द्वे शतान्यष्टौ लीलया युधि निर्जिताः ।
ते निर्गता गिरिद्रोण्यां मलिना मलवाससः ॥10.73.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अयुते द्वे twenty thousand, शतानि अष्टौ eight hundred, लीलया effortlessly, युधि in battle, निर्जिताः conquered/defeated, ते those (kings), निर्गताः emerged/came out, गिरि-द्रोण्याम् from the mountain fortress/prison, मलिनाः unwashed/emaciated, मल-वाससः wearing dirty, worn-out clothes

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— (जरसन्धेन​) युधि लीलया निर्जिताः अयुते द्वे शतान्यष्टौ ते (राजानः) मलिनाः मलवाससः (सन्तः) गिरिद्रोण्याः निर्गताः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said: (जरसन्धेन)​ युधि लीलया निर्जिताः Those who had been easily defeated in battle by Jarasandha, अयुते द्वे शतान्यष्टौ ते राजानः those twenty thousand eight hundred kings, मलिनाः मलवाससः emaciated and clad in filthy garments, गिरिद्रोण्याः निर्गताः emerged from the mountain caves.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: The twenty thousand eight hundred kings whom Jarasandha had effortlessly defeated in battle and locked away emerged at last from the dark mountain fortress. Starved, emaciated, and dressed in tattered, dirty clothes, they stepped out into the light of freedom.

क्षुत्क्षामाः शुष्कवदनाः संरोधपरिकर्शिताः ।
ददृशुस्ते घनश्यामं पीतकौशेयवाससम् ॥10.73.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्षुत्-क्षामाः emaciated by hunger, शुष्क-वदनाः with dry faces, संरोध-परिकर्शिताः worn out by imprisonment, ददृशुः saw, ते they, घन-श्यामम् Krishna - dark like a rain cloud, पीत-कौशेय-वाससम् wearing yellow silk garments

Anvaya: ते क्षुत्क्षामाः शुष्कवदनाः संरोधपरिकर्शिताः घनश्यामं पीतकौशेयवाससं (कृष्णं) ददृशुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ते They, क्षुत्क्षामाः शुष्कवदनाः संरोधपरिकर्शिताः emaciated by hunger with dry faces and worn out by imprisonment, घनश्यामं पीतकौशेयवाससं (कृष्णं) Krishna who was dark as a rain cloud wearing yellow silk garments, ददृशुः saw.

Simple Meaning: Weakened by hunger, with dried-up faces and utterly exhausted from long captivity, those liberated kings beheld Krishna, who possessed a deep blue complexion like a storm cloud and was clad in shining yellow silk robes.

श्रीवत्साङ्कं चतुर्बाहुं पद्मगर्भारुणेक्षणम् ।
चारुप्रसन्नवदनं स्फुरन्मकरकुण्डलम् ॥10.73.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रीवत्स-अङ्कम् bearing the mark of Shrivatsa, चतुः-बाहुम् having four arms, पद्म-गर्भ-अरुण-ईक्षणम् with eyes red like the whorl of a lotus, चारु-प्रसन्न-वदनम् with a beautiful and cheerful face, स्फुरत्-मकर-कुण्डलम् with glittering shark shaped earrings

Anvaya: (ते) श्रीवत्साङ्कं चतुर्बाहुं पद्मगर्भारुणेक्षणं चारुप्रसन्नवदनं स्फुरन्मकरकुण्डलं (कृष्णं ददृशुः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (ते) They (beheld Krishna), श्रीवत्साङ्कं चतुर्बाहुं who bore the Shrivatsa mark and possessed four arms, पद्मगर्भारुणेक्षणं with reddish eyes like the inner whorl of a lotus, चारुप्रसन्नवदनं possessed a beautiful cheerful face, स्फुरन्मकरकुण्डलं and wore gleaming shark shaped earrings.

Simple Meaning: They gazed upon Krishna, who bore the sacred Shrivatsa mark upon His chest and possessed four splendid arms. His tender eyes glowed red like the heart of a lotus flower, His countenance was filled with radiant grace, and wore glittering shark-shaped earrings.

पद्महस्तं गदाशङ्खरथाङ्गैरुपलक्षितम् ।
किरीटहारकटककटिसूत्राङ्गदाचितम् ॥10.73.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: पद्म-हस्तम् holding a lotus in His hand, गदा-शङ्ख-रथ-अङ्गैः उपलक्षणिम् marked by the mace, conch shell and disc, किरीट-हार-कटक-कटिसूत्र-अङ्गद-आचितम् adorned with a crown necklace bracelets waist chain and armlets

Anvaya: (ते) पद्महस्तं गदाशङ्खरथाङ्गैः उपलक्षितं किरीटहारकटककटिसूत्राङ्गदाचितं (कृष्णं ददृशुः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (ते) They (beheld Krishna), पद्महस्तं गदाशङ्खरथाङ्गैः उपलक्षितं who holds a lotus in His hand and is distinguished by His mace conch shell and disc, किरीटहारकटककटिसूत्राङ्गदाचितम् and who is beautifully adorned with a crown necklace bracelets waist chain and armlets.

Simple Meaning: The kings beheld Krishna holding a lotus blossom and wielding His iconic emblems—the mace, conch, and disc. He shone brilliantly, fully adorned with a magnificent crown, sparkling necklaces, fine bracelets, an ornate waist chain, and armlets.

भ्राजद्वरमणिग्रीवं निवीतं वनमालया ।
पिबन्त इव चक्षुर्भ्यां लिहन्त इव जिह्वया ॥10.73.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: भ्राजत्-वर-मणि-ग्रीवम् with a neck glowing with a brilliant jewel (Kaustubha), निवीतम् encircled, वन-मालया by a garland of forest flowers, पिबन्तः drinking, इव as if, चक्षुर्भ्याम् with their eyes, लिहन्तः licking, इव as if, जिह्वया with their tongues

Anvaya: (ते) भ्राजद्वरमणिग्रीवं वनमालया निवीतं (कृष्णं) चक्षुर्भ्यां पिबन्तः इव जिह्वया लिहन्तः इव (ददृशुः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (ते) They (beheld Krishna), भ्राजद्वरमणिग्रीवं वनमालया निवीतं whose neck shone with a priceless jewel (Kaustubha) and who was adorned with a forest garland, चक्षुर्भ्यां पिबन्तः इव as if they were drinking Him with their eyes, जिह्वया लिहन्तः इव and as if licking Him with their tongues.

Simple Meaning: His neck shone brightly with the splendor of Kaustubha gem, and He wore a vibrant garland of forest flowers. The kings gazed upon Krishna with such intense absorption that it seemed as though they were drinking Him in with their eyes and relishing Him with their tongues.

जिघ्रन्तं इव नासाभ्यां रम्भन्त इव बाहुभिः ।
प्रणेमुर्हतपाप्मानो मूर्धभिः पादयोर्हरेः ॥10.73.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: जिघ्रन्तः smelling, इव as if, नासाभ्याम् with their nostrils, रम्भन्तः embracing, इव as if, बाहुभिः with their arms, प्रणेमुः bowed down, हत-पाप्मानः cleansed of all sins, मूर्धभिः with their heads, पादयोः at the feet, हरेः of Lord Hari

Anvaya: (ते राजानः कृष्णं) नासाभ्यां जिघ्रन्तः इव, बाहुभिः रम्भन्तः इव (ददृशुः)। हतपाप्मानः मूर्धभिः हरेः पादयोः प्रणेमुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (ते राजानः) (Those kings beheld Krishna), नासाभ्यां जिघ्रन्तः इव as if smelling Him with their nostrils, बाहुभिः रम्भन्तः इव and as if embracing Him with their arms. हतपाप्मानः Cleansed of all sins (those kings), मूर्धभिः हरेः पादयोः प्रणेमुः bowed down with their heads at the feet of Lord Hari.

Simple Meaning: Those kings beheld Krishna as though they were inhaling His heavenly fragrance through their nostrils and wrapping Him in a warm embrace with their arms. Cleansed of all their past sins by His divine sight, the kings bowed their heads reverently at the lotus feet of Lord Hari.

कृष्णसन्दर्शनाह्लादध्वस्तसंरोधनक्लमाः ।
प्रशशंसुर्हृषीकेशं गीर्भिः प्राञ्जलयो नृपाः॥10.73.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृष्ण-सन्दर्शन-आह्लाद-ध्वस्त-संरोधन-क्लमाः whose exhaustion from captivity was removed by the joy of seeing Krishna, प्रशशंसुः praised, हृषीकेशम् Hrishikesha, गीर्भिः with words, प्राञ्जलयः with folded hands, नृपाः the kings

Anvaya: कृष्णसन्दर्शनाह्लादध्वस्तसंरोधनक्लमाः नृपाः प्राञ्जलयः गीर्भिः हृषीकेशं प्रशशंसुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्णसन्दर्शनाह्लादध्वस्तसंरोधनक्लमाः नृपाः The kings whose suffering and weariness from imprisonment were completely destroyed by the joy of seeing Krishna, प्राञ्जलयः गीर्भिः with folded hands and heartfelt words, हृषीकेशं प्रशशंसुः praised Hrishikesha.

Simple Meaning: The overwhelming bliss of seeing Krishna instantly dispelled all the physical weariness and mental pain the kings had suffered during their long captivity. Standing before Him with hands folded in reverence, they began to offer Him heartfelt prayers of praise.

राजानः ऊचुः
नमस्ते देवदेवेश प्रपन्नार्तिहराव्यय ।
प्रपन्नान् पाहि नः कृष्ण निर्विण्णान् घोरसंसृतेः ॥10.73.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजानः ऊचुः the kings said, नमः-ते bow to You, देव-देव-ईश O Lord of the devas of devas, प्रपन्न-आर्ति-हर-अव्यय O infallible reliever of the distress of those who surrender, प्रपन्नान् who have surrendered, पाहि protect, नः us, कृष्ण O Krishna, निर्विण्णान् who are disgusted, घोर-संसृतेः with terrible material existence

Anvaya: राजानः ऊचुः— देवदेवेश! प्रपन्नार्तिहराव्यय! कृष्ण! ते नमः! घोरसंसृतेः निर्विण्णान् प्रपन्नान् नः पाहि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजानः ऊचुः The kings said: देवदेवेश! प्रपन्नार्तिहराव्यय! कृष्ण O Lord of the devas of devas! O infallible reliever of the suffering of the surrendered! O Krishna, ते नमः obeisances unto You! घोरसंसृतेः निर्विण्णान् Weary of this dreadful material existence, प्रपन्नान् नः who have surrendered unto You - us, पाहि Protect.

Simple Meaning: The kings said: O Lord of all devas, O infallible Krishna who dispels the misery of all who take refuge in You! we bow down to You. Exhausted and disillusioned by the relentless cycle of material suffering, we place ourselves entirely under Your shelter. Please grant us Your divine protection.

नैनं नाथान्वसूयामो मागधं मधुसूदन ।
अनुग्रहो यद् भवतो राज्ञां राज्यच्युतिर्विभो ॥10.73.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, एनम् him (Jarasandha), नाथ O Master, अन्वसूयामः we blame, मागधम् the king of Magadha (Jarasandha), मधुसूदन O Madhusudana, अनुग्रहः a blessing, यत् which, भवतः of You, राज्ञाम् for kings, राज्य-च्युतिः fall from royalty, विभो O Almighty One

Anvaya: नाथ! मधुसूदन! विभो! (वयम्) एनं मागधं न असूयामः, यत् राज्ञां राज्यच्युतिः भवतः अनुग्रहः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नाथ! मधुसूदन! विभो! O Master! O Madhusudana! O Almighty One!, (वयम्) एनं मागधं न असूयामः we do not blame this King of Magadha, यत् राज्ञां राज्यच्युतिः because the loss of a kingdom for kings, भवतः अनुग्रहः is Your grace.

Simple Meaning: O Lord Madhusudana, Almighty Master, we hold no grudge against Jarasandha, the ruler of Magadha. Indeed, we now realize that losing our kingdoms was actually Your grace.

राज्यैश्वर्यमदोन्नद्धो न श्रेयो विन्दते नृपः ।
त्वन्मायामोहितोऽनित्या मन्यते सम्पदोऽचलाः ॥10.73.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: राज्य-ऐश्वर्य-मद-उन्नद्धः blinded by pride of royal wealth, न not, श्रेयः ultimate good, विन्दते finds, नृपः a king, त्वत्-माया-मोहितः deluded by Your illusory power, अ-नित्याः temporary, मन्यते considers, सम्पदः opulences, अ-चलाः eternal

Anvaya: राज्यैश्वर्यमदोन्नद्धः त्वन्मायामोहितः नृपः अनित्याः सम्पदः अचलाः मन्यते, (अतः) श्रेयः न विन्दते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राज्यैश्वर्यमदोन्नद्धः त्वन्मायामोहितः नृपः A king who is puffed up with pride from royal wealth and deluded by Your illusory power, अनित्याः सम्पदः transient fortunes, अचलाः मन्यते considers to be permanent, (अतः) श्रेयः न विन्दते and therefore does not attain the ultimate spiritual good.

Simple Meaning: A ruler blinded by the intoxicating pride of royal power and material wealth becomes completely bewitched by Your illusory energy. Deluded into thinking that fleeting worldly riches and positions are eternal, such a person fails to seek or attain true, lasting spiritual wellbeing.

मृगतृष्णां यथा बाला मन्यन्त उदकाशयम् ।
एवं वैकारिकीं मायामयुक्ता वस्तु चक्षते ॥10.73.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: मृगतृष्णाम् a mirage, यथा as, बालाः foolish children, मन्यन्ते consider, उदक-आशयम् a body of water, एवम् similarly, वैकारिकीम् subject to transformation, मायाम् the illusory energy, अयुक्ताः untaught or unintelligent people, वस्तु as reality, चक्षते perceive

Anvaya: यथा बालाः मृगतृष्णाम् उदकाशयं मन्यन्ते, एवम् अयुक्ताः वैकारिकीं मायां वस्तु चक्षते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यथा बालाः मृगतृष्णांम् उदकाशयं मन्यन्ते Just as naive children mistake a mirage for a real pool of water, एवम् अयुक्ताः वैकारिकीं मायां वस्तु चक्षते so do ignorant people perceive this constantly changing material illusion as the absolute reality.

Simple Meaning: Just as foolish children chase a desert mirage thinking it to be a reservoir of fresh water, people lacking spiritual discrimination take this temporary and ever-changing material realm to be permanent reality, pursuing its false promises of happiness.

वयं पुरा श्रीमदनष्टदृष्टयो जिगीषयास्या इतरेतरस्पृधः ।
घ्नन्तः प्रजाः स्वा अतिनिर्घृणाः प्रभो मृत्युं परस्त्वाविगणय्य दुर्मदाः ॥10.73.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: वयम् we, पुरा in the past, श्री-मद-अनष्ट-दृष्टयः whose vision was blinded by the intoxication of royal opulence, जिगीषया with the desire to conquer, अस्याः this (earth), इतर-इतर-स्पृधः competing with one another, घ्नन्तः killing, प्रजाः subjects, स्वाः our own, अति-निर्घृणाः extremely cruel, प्रभो O Lord, मृत्युम् death, पुरः in front of us, त्वा You, अविगणय्य disregarding, दुर्-मदाः infatuated by false pride

Anvaya: प्रभो! पुरा श्रीमदानष्टदृष्टयः अतिनिर्घृणाः दुर्मदाः अस्याः जिगीषया इतरेतरस्पृधः स्वाः प्रजाः घ्नन्तः वयं पुरः (स्थितं) मृत्युं त्वा अविगणय्य (अचराम)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रभो O Lord, पुरा in the past, श्रीमदानष्टदृष्टयः अतिनिर्घृणाः दुर्मदाः blinded by wealth and infatuated with pride and utterly ruthless, अस्याः जिगीषया इतरेतरस्पृधः स्वाः प्रजाः घ्नन्तः driven by rivalry to conquer this earth, slaughtering our own subjects, वयं पुरः मृत्युं त्वा अविगणय्य we ignored You standing right before us as death.

Simple Meaning: O Lord, in the past, our wealth and status completely blinded our judgment, filling us with ruthless arrogance. Driven by fierce rivalries to conquer this earth, we showed no mercy and even slaughtered our own people, foolishly ignoring You, who were standing right before us in the form of inevitable death.

त एव कृष्णाद्य गभीररंहसा दुरन्तवीर्येण विचालिताः श्रियः ।
कालेन तन्वा भवतोऽनुकम्पया विनष्टदर्पाश्चरणौ स्मराम ते ॥10.73.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: ते those, एव indeed, कृष्ण O Krishna, अद्य now, गभीर-रंहसा of irresistible velocity, दुरन्त-वीर्येण of endless power, विचालिताः removed/shattered, श्रियः opulences, कालेन by time, तन्वा which is a form, भवतः of You, अनुकम्पया by the mercy, विनष्ट-दर्पाः whose pride has been destroyed, चरणौ two lotus feet, स्मरामः we remember, ते Your

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्ण O Krishna, भवतः अनुकम्पया by Your mercy, गभीररंहसा दुरन्तवीर्येण of irresistible speed and unyielding power, तन्वा कालेन through time which is Your form, विचालिताः श्रियः our fortunes have been swept away, विनष्टदर्पाः and with pride thoroughly destroyed, ते एव (वयम्) those same we, अद्य ते चरणौ स्मरामः now remember Your lotus feet.

Simple Meaning: O Krishna, through Your divine mercy acting as relentless and all-powerful time, our royal power and wealth have been swept away. Now that our pride has been completely shattered, those same arrogant rulers that we once were have finally come to remember and meditate upon Your lotus feet.

अथो न राज्यं मृगतृष्णिरूपितं देहेन शश्वत् पतता रुजां भुवा ।

Word-by-word meaning: अथः therefore, न not, राज्यम् kingdom, मृगतृष्णि-रूपितम् like a mirage, देहेन with this body, शश्वत् constantly, पतता decaying/falling, रुजाम् of diseases, भुवा an abode, उपासितव्यम् to be pursued/enjoyed, स्पृहयामहे we desire, विभो O Almighty Lord, क्रिया-फलम् the fruit of ritualistic activities, प्रेत्य after death, च and, कर्ण-रोचनम् pleasing to the ears

रुजाम् - रुज् जकारान्तः, स्त्रीलिङ्गः 6.3

भुवा - भू ऊकारान्तः, स्त्रीलिङ्गः, 3.1

Anvaya: विभो! अथः शश्वत् पतता रुजां भुवा देहेन उपासितव्यं मृगतृष्णिरूपितं राज्यं च प्रेत्य कर्णरोचनं क्रियाफलं (वयं) न स्पृहयामहे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विभो O Almighty Lord, अथः therefore, शश्वत् पतता through the ever decaying, रुजां भुवा and a repository of diseases, देहेन this body, उपासितव्यं मृगतृष्णिरूपितं राज्यं the enjoyment of the mirage-like kingdom, च प्रेत्य and after death, कर्णरोचनं which is pleasing to the ear, क्रियाफलं the results of the actions, (वयं) न स्पृहयामहे we do not long for.

Simple Meaning: O Almighty Lord, knowing that this physical body is constantly decaying and is a vessel for sickness, we no longer desire the enjoyment of the kingdom that is as unreal as a mirage. Nor do we crave those ear-pleasing rewards of the actions after death.

तं नः समादिशोपायं येन ते चरणाब्जयोः ।
स्मृतिर्यथा न विरमेदपि संसरतामिह ॥10.73.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् that, नः to us, समादिश instruct, उपायम् means/path, येन by which, ते Your, चरण-अब्जयोः of the two lotus feet, स्मृतिः remembrance, यथा so that, न not, विरमेत् may cease, अपि even, संसरताम् wandering in the world, इह in this material existence

Anvaya: इह संसरताम् अपि नः ते चरणाब्जयोः स्मृतिः यथा न विरमेत्, तम् उपायं नः समादिश।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इह संसरताम् अपि नः Even as we continue wandering in this material world, ते चरणाब्जयोः स्मृतिः यथा न विरमेत् so that our remembrance of Your lotus feet may never cease, तम् उपायं नः समादिश please instruct us in that path.

Simple Meaning: We ask only that You teach us the way by which our devotion and remembrance of Your lotus feet will never falter, even as we continue to journey through this material world.

कृष्णाय वासुदेवाय हरये परमात्मने ।
प्रणतक्लेशनाशाय गोविन्दाय नमो नमः ॥10.73.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृष्णाय unto Krishna, वासुदेवाय unto the son of Vasudeva, हरये unto Hari, परमात्मने unto the Supreme Soul, प्रणत-क्लेश-नाशाय unto the destroyer of the distress of those who surrender, गोविन्दाय unto Govinda, नमः नमः repeated obeisances

Anvaya: कृष्णाय वासुदेवाय हरये परमात्मने प्रणतक्लेशनाशाय गोविन्दाय नमो नमः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्णाय वासुदेवाय हरये परमात्मने प्रणतक्लेशनाशाय गोविन्दाय Unto Krishna, Vasudeva, Hari, the Supreme Soul, the destroyer of the suffering of those who bow down, and Govinda, नमो नमः repeated bowings again and again.

Simple Meaning: We offer our repeated and humble obeisances time and again unto Krishna, the son of Vasudeva, the Supreme Soul who removes all grief and removes the afflictions of all who surrender to Him, the divine protector Govinda.

श्री शुक उवाच
संस्तूयमानो भगवान् राजभिर्मुक्तबन्धनैः ।
तानाह करुणस्तात शरण्यः श्लक्ष्णया गिरा ॥10.73.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, संस्तूयमानः being praised, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, राजभिः by the kings, मुक्त-बन्धनैः freed from captivity, तान् आह spoken to them, करुणः compassionate, तात O dear king, शरण्यः the giver of refuge, श्लक्ष्णया with gentle, गिरा words

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— तात! मुक्तबन्धनैः राजभिः संस्तूयमानः करुणः शरण्यः भगवान् तान् श्लक्ष्णया गिरा आह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said: तात O dear Parikshit, मुक्तबन्धनैः राजभिः संस्तूयमानः while being glorified by the kings released from prison, करुणः शरण्यः भगवान् the compassionate Lord Krishna who gives shelter to all, तान् श्लक्ष्णया गिरा आह spoke to them with gentle words.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O dear Parikshit, as the kings freed from imprisonment offered their heartfelt prayers, the compassionate Lord Krishna, who is the ultimate refuge for all souls, addressed them with sweet and comforting words.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
अद्यप्रभृति वो भूपा मय्यात्मन्यखिलेश्वरे ।
सुदृढा जायते भक्तिर्बाढमाशंसितं तथा ॥10.73.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, अद्य-प्रभृति from this day forward, वः for all of you, भूपाः O kings, मयि in Me, आत्मनि the Supreme Soul, अखिल-ईश्वरे the Lord of all, सुदृढा firm, जायते is born, भक्तिः devotion, बाढम् certainly, आशंसितम् desired, तथा as

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच— भूपाः! मयि आखिलेश्वरे आत्मनि वः सुदृढा भक्तिः अद्यप्रभृति जायते, तथा आशंसितं बाढम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said: भूपाः O kings, मयि आखिलेश्वरे आत्मनि unto Me who am the Lord of all and the Supreme Self, वः सुदृढा भक्तिः अद्यप्रभृति जायते from today steady devotion is established in you, तथा आशंसितं बाढम् and as you have desired it is surely granted.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord said: O kings, from this day forth, your faith and devotion unto Me, the Supreme Soul and Master of all creation, shall remain unwavering. As you have prayed, your pure devotion is surely fulfilled.

दिष्ट्या व्यवसितं भूपा भवन्त ऋतभाषिणः ।
श्रियैश्वर्यमदोन्नाहं पश्य उन्मादकं नृणाम् ॥10.73.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: दिष्ट्या by good fortune, व्यवसितम् resolve, भूपाः O kings, भवन्तः you all, ऋत-भाषिणः speakers of truth, श्री-ऐश्वर्य-मद-उन्नाहम् the swelling intoxication of wealth and power, पश्ये I see, उन्मादकम् driving mad, नृणाम् human beings

Anvaya: भूपाः! भवन्तः ऋतभाषिणः, दिष्ट्या (भवद्भिः) व्यवसितम् । श्रियैश्वर्यमदोन्नाहं नृणाम् उन्मादकं पश्ये।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भूपाः O kings, भवन्तः ऋतभाषिणः you speak the truth, दिष्ट्या (भवद्भिः) व्यवसितम् fortunately this correct realization has been reached by you. श्रियैश्वर्यमदोन्नाहं नृणाम् उन्मादकं पश्ये I observe that the swelling pride born of wealth and sovereignty drives men to madness.

Simple Meaning: O kings, what you have spoken is true, and it is fortunate that you have reached this wisdom. Indeed, I see how the pride of power and worldly riches blinds human beings and drives them to delusion.

हैहयो नहुषो वेनो रावणो नरकोऽपरे ।
श्रीमदाद् भ्रंशिताः स्थानाद् देवदैत्यनरेश्वराः ॥10.73.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: हैहयः Kartavirya Arjuna (king of the Haihayas), नहुषः King Nahusha, वेनः King Vena, रावणः Ravana, नरकः Narakasura, अपरे and others, श्री-मदात् from the pride of wealth and power, भ्रंशिताः fallen, स्थानात् from their positions, देव-दैत्य-नर-ईश्वराः rulers among devas, daityas, and human beings

Anvaya: हैहयः नहुषः वेनः रावणः नरकः अपरे च देवदैत्यनरेश्वराः श्रीमदात् स्थानात् भ्रंशिताः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: हैहयः नहुषः वेनः रावणः नरकः अपरे च देवदैत्यनरेश्वराः Haihaya, Nahusha, Vena, Ravana, Narakasura, and other sovereign rulers among devas, daityas, and humans, श्रीमदात् स्थानात् भ्रंशिताः were cast down from their elevated positions due to the pride of wealth and power.

Simple Meaning: Great rulers—whether among devas, daityas, or humans, such as Kartavirya Arjuna, Nahusha, Vena, Ravana, and Narakasura—were all deposed from their exalted status solely because of the pride born of material wealth and power.

Note: Haihaya stole the precious cow belonging to Jamadagni, the father of Lord Parasurama. In anger, Parasurama killed Haihaya and his arrogant sons.

Nahusha became extremely proud when he was temporarily appointed as Indra. Driven by this pride, he ordered several brahmanas to carry him in a palanquin so that he could approach Sachi, the chaste wife of Indra, for an illicit encounter. Because of his arrogance and misconduct, the brahmanas caused him to fall from his exalted position and become an old man.

King Vena was similarly arrogant. When he insulted the brahmanas, they killed him through the power of their incantations of the syllable “hum”.

Ravana, the powerful king of the raksasas, driven by lust, abducted Sita. As a result, her husband, Rama, killed him.

Naraka, a ruler of the daityas, dared to steal the earrings of Mother Aditi and was likewise killed for his offense.

These are the examples of the powerful rulers who have lost their exalted positions when they become intoxicated by pride in their wealth, power, and worldly accomplishments.

Anvaya: भवन्तः देहाद्युत्पाद्यम् अन्तवत् एतत् विज्ञाय, युक्ताः अध्वरैः मां यजन्तः धर्मेण प्रजाः रक्षथ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भवन्तः You all, देहाद्युत्पाद्यम् अन्तवत् the physical body and other created things are perishable, एतत् विज्ञाय realizing this, युक्ताः अध्वरैः मां यजन्तः steadfastly worship Me through sacrifices, धर्मेण प्रजाः रक्षथ and protect your subjects with righteousness.

Simple Meaning: Understanding that the material body and everything produced in this world are temporary, remain steadfast in worshiping Me through noble duties and sacrifices, and govern your subjects in accordance with righteousness.

सन्तवन्तः प्रजातन्तून् सुखं दुःखं भवाभवौ ।

Simple Meaning: Continue your royal dynasties by raising virtuous descendants. Endure calmly whatever destiny brings—whether pleasure or pain, prosperity or adversity—and live out your lives with your minds firmly fixed on Me.

उदासीनाश्च देहादावात्मारामा धृतव्रताः ।
मय्यावेश्य मनः सम्यङ् मामन्ते ब्रह्म यास्यथ ॥10.78.23॥

Simple Meaning: Remaining detached from physical attachments, taking inner joy in the Self, and staying firm in your spiritual vows, fix your minds entirely on Me. By doing so, at the end of your lives, you shall attain Me, the Supreme Absolute Truth.

Simple Meaning: O Parikshit, Lord Krishna instructed Sahadeva to honor the released kings with lavish royal hospitality, presenting them with majestic garments, precious ornaments, flower garlands, and aromatic sandal paste fit for monarchs.

भोजयित्वा वरान्नेन सुस्नातान् समलङ्कृतान् ।
भोगैश्च विविधैर्युक्तांस्ताम्बूलाद्यैर्नृपोचितैः ॥10.73.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: भोजयित्वा having fed, वर-अन्नेन with choice, exquisite food, सु-स्नातान् those who were well-bathed, समलङ्कृतान् beautifully adorned, भोगैः with luxuries, च and, विविधैः of various kinds, युक्तान् provided with, ताम्बूल-आद्यैः betel nut and other refreshments, नृप-उचितैः suitable for kings

Anvaya: (सहदेवः) सुस्नातान् समलङ्कृतान् (तान्) वरान्नेन ताम्बूलाद्यैः विविधैः नृपोचितैः भोगैः च युक्तान् भोजयित्वा (पूजयामास)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सहदेवः) Sahadeva, सुस्नातान् समलङ्कृतान् (तान्) those kings who were well-bathed and finely adorned, वरान्नेन with choice exquisite food, ताम्बूलाद्यैः विविधैः नृपोचितैः भोगैः च युक्तान् along with betel nut and various luxuries fit for royalty, भोजयित्वा having fed and served them, (पूजयामास) honored them.

Simple Meaning: Sahadeva served those newly bathed and elegantly dressed kings a sumptuous feast of exquisite food, along with betel nut and various royal luxuries suited to their noble status.

ते पूजिता मुकुन्देन राजानो मृष्टकुण्डलाः ।
विरेजुर्मोचिताः क्लेशात् प्रावृडन्ते यथा ग्रहाः ॥10.73.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: ते those, पूजिताः honored, मुकुन्देन by Mukunda (Krishna), राजानः kings, मृष्ट-कुण्डलाः wearing polished earrings, विरेजुः shone brightly, मोचिताः released, क्लेशात् from suffering/imprisonment, प्रावृत्-अन्ते at the end of the rainy season, यथा like, ग्रहाः planets/stars

Anvaya: मुकुन्देन पूजिताः क्लेशात् मोचिताः मृष्टकुण्डलाः ते राजानः प्रावृडन्ते ग्रहाः यथा विरेजुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मुकुन्देन पूजिताः Honored by Mukunda, क्लेशात् मोचिताः released from their suffering, मृष्टकुण्डलाः wearing gleaming earrings, ते राजानः those kings, प्रावृडन्ते ग्रहाः यथा like the planets at the end of the rainy season, विरेजुः shone radiantly.

Simple Meaning: Honored by Mukunda and freed from their long suffering, the kings adorned themselves with polished, gleaming earrings. Radiant and transformed, they shone brilliantly, just as stars and planets shine clearly in the night sky at the close of the monsoon season.

रथान् सदश्वानारोप्य मणिकाञ्चनभूषितान् ।
प्रीणय्य सूनृतैर्वाक्यैः स्वदेशान् प्रत्ययापयत् ॥10.73.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: रथान् on chariots, सत्-अश्वान् equipped with excellent horses, आरोप्य causing them to ascend, मणि-काञ्चन-भूषितान् decorated with jewels and gold, प्रीणय्य pleasing them, सूनृतैः with truthful and pleasing, वाक्यैः words, स्व-देशान् to their respective lands, प्रति-अयापयत् sent them back

Anvaya: (भगवान्) मणिकाञ्चनभूषितान् सदश्वान् रथान् (तान्) आरोप्य, सूनृतैः वाक्यैः प्रीणय्य स्वदेशान् प्रत्ययापयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (भगवान्) Lord Krishna, मणिकाञ्चनभूषितान् सदश्वान् रथान् (तान्) आरोप्य placing them on chariots adorned with jewels and gold and yoked with fine horses, सूनृतैः वाक्यैः प्रीणय्य gladdening them with affectionate and truthful words, स्वदेशान् प्रत्ययापयत् sent them back to their respective kingdoms.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna seated the kings on splendid chariots adorned with gold and precious gems and yoked with magnificent horses. Pleasing them with warm, truthful, and comforting words, He sent them back honorably to their home kingdoms.

त एवं मोचिताः कृच्छ्रात् कृष्णेन सुमहात्मना ।
ययुस्तमेव ध्यायन्तः कृतानि च जगत्पतेः ॥10.73.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: ते they (the kings), एवम् thus, मोचिताः released, कृच्छ्रात् from severe distress/imprisonment, कृष्णेन by Krishna, सु-महात्मना by the supreme high-souled Lord, ययुः went/departed, तम् एव upon Him alone, ध्यायन्तः meditating, कृतानि actions/pastimes, च and, जगत्-पतेः of the Lord of the universe

Anvaya: सुमहात्मना कृष्णेन एवं कृच्छ्रात् मोचिताः ते जगत्पतेः कृतानि तमेव च ध्यायन्तः ययुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सुमहात्मना कृष्णेन एवं कृच्छ्रात् मोचिताः Thus freed from their great calamity by the supreme high-souled Krishna, ते जगत्पतेः कृतानि तमेव च ध्यायन्तः ययुः those kings departed, meditating continuously upon Him alone and upon the wondrous deeds performed by the Lord of the universe.

Simple Meaning: Having been liberated from their deep misery by the noble Krishna, the kings returned to their kingdoms, their minds fully absorbed in continuous meditation upon Him and the glorious deeds of the Supreme Lord of the universe.

जगदुः प्रकृतिभ्यस्ते महापुरुषचेष्टितम् ।
यथान्वशासद् भगवांस्तथा चक्रुरतन्द्रिताः ॥10.73.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: जगदुः they recounted/narrated, प्रकृतिभ्यः unto their subjects/citizens, ते they (the kings), महापुरुष-चेष्टितम् the deeds and activities of the Supreme Personality, यथा as, अन्वशासत् instructed/directed, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, तथा so/in that manner, चक्रुः they acted, अतन्द्रिताः unwearied/tireless, vigilant

Anvaya: ते प्रकृतिभ्यः महापुरुषचेष्टितं जगदुः, भगवान् यथा अन्वशासत् तथा अतन्द्रिताः चक्रुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ते प्रकृतिभ्यः महापुरुषचेष्टितं जगदुः They narrated unto their citizens the wondrous deeds of the Supreme Personality, भगवान् यथा अन्वशासत् तथा अतन्द्रिताः चक्रुः and exactly as the Supreme Lord had instructed, so they carried out their duties tirelessly and with utter vigilance.

Simple Meaning: Upon returning to their kingdoms, the kings recounted the glorious deeds of Lord Krishna to all their subjects. Following the righteous advice of the Supreme Lord, they ruled their realms with utmost diligence, dedication, and detachment.

जरासन्धं घातयित्वा भीमसेनेन केशवः ।
पार्थाभ्यां संयुतः प्रायात् सहदेवेन पूजितः ॥10.73.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: जरासन्धम् Jarasandha, घातयित्वा having caused to be killed, भीमसेनेन through Bhimasena, केशवः Keshava (Krishna), पार्थाभ्याम् with the two sons of Pritha (Arjuna and Bhima), संयुतः accompanied, प्रायात् departed, सहदेवेन by Sahadeva (son of Jarasandha), पूजितः worshiped/honored

Anvaya: केशवः भीमसेनेन जरासन्धं घातयित्वा सहदेवेन पूजितः पार्थाभ्यां संयुतः प्रायात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: केशवः Keshava (Krishna), भीमसेनेन जरासन्धं घातयित्वा having caused Jarasandha to be slain by Bhimasena, सहदेवेन पूजितः honored and worshiped by Sahadeva (Jarasandha's son), पार्थाभ्यां संयुतः प्रायात् departed accompanied by the two sons of Pritha.

Simple Meaning: Having arranged for the slaying of Jarasandha through Bhimasena, Lord Krishna was reverently worshiped by Sahadeva, Jarasandha's son. He then departed from Magadha in the company of Bhima and Arjuna.

गत्वा ते खाण्डवप्रस्थं शङ्खान् दध्मुर्जितारयः ।
हर्षयन्तः स्वसुहृदो दुर्हृदां चासुखावहाः ॥10.73.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: गत्वा having reached, ते they, खाण्डवप्रस्थम् Khandavaprastha (Indraprastha), शङ्खान् conch shells, दध्मुः blew, जित-अरयः having conquered their enemies, हर्षयन्तः bringing joy, स्व-सुहृदः to their friends, दुर्हृदाम् to their enemies, च and, असुख-आवहाः bringing distress/grief

Anvaya: जितारयः ते खाण्डवप्रस्थं गत्वा स्वसुहृदः हर्षयन्तः दुर्हृदां च असुखावहाः शङ्खान् दध्मुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जितारयः ते Having conquered their enemies, खाण्डवप्रस्थं गत्वा upon arriving at Khandavaprastha, स्वसुहृदः हर्षयन्तः causing delight to their well-wishers, दुर्हृदां च असुखावहाः and causing sorrow unto their foes, शङ्खान् दध्मुः they blew their conch shells.

Simple Meaning: Having vanquished their enemy Jarasandha, Krishna, Bhima, and Arjuna arrived at Indraprastha. They sounded their conch shells, filling the hearts of their loved ones with immense joy while striking grief into the minds of their adversaries.

तच्छ्रुत्वा प्रीतमनस इन्द्रप्रस्थनिवासिनः ।
मेनिरे मागधं शान्तं राजा चाप्तमनोरथः ॥10.73.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that, श्रुत्वा hearing, प्रीत-मनसः joyful at heart, इन्द्रप्रस्थ-निवासिनः the residents of Indraprastha, मेनिरे considered, मागधम् Jarasandha (the king of Magadha), शान्तम् dead/vanquished, राजा the king (Yudhisthira), च and, आप्त-मनोरथः having fulfilled his desire

Anvaya: तत् श्रुत्वा इन्द्रप्रस्थनिवासिनः प्रीतमनसः (सन्तः) मागधं शान्तं मेनिरे, राजा च आप्तमनोरथः (अभवत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत् श्रुत्वा Hearing that news, इन्द्रप्रस्थनिवासिनः प्रीतमनसः the residents of Indraprastha with joyful hearts, मागधं शान्तं मेनिरे understood that the king of Magadha was slain, राजा च आप्तमनोरथः and King Yudhisthira realized his desire was fulfilled.

Simple Meaning: Upon hearing the booming sound of the conch shells, the citizens of Indraprastha rejoiced, realizing that Jarasandha had been defeated. King Yudhisthira felt deeply satisfied, knowing the main obstacle to performing the Rajasuya sacrifice had been removed.

अभिवन्द्याथ राजानं भीमार्जुनजनार्दनाः ।
सर्वमाश्रावयाञ्चक्रुरात्मना यदनुष्ठितम् ॥10.73.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: अभिवन्द्य having bowed respectfully/greeted, अथ then, राजानम् unto King Yudhisthira, भीम-अर्जुन-जनार्दनाः Bhima, Arjuna, and Janardana (Krishna), सर्वम् everything, आश्रावयाञ्चक्रुः reported/narrated, आत्मना by themselves, यत् which, अनुष्ठितम् was accomplished

Anvaya: अथ भीमार्जुनजनार्दनाः राजानम् अभिवन्द्य आत्मना यत् अनुष्ठितं (तत्) सर्वम् आश्रावयाञ्चक्रुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ Then, भीमार्जुनजनार्दनाः Bhima, Arjuna, and Janardana, राजानम् अभिवन्द्य having greeted King Yudhisthira, आत्मना यत् अनुष्ठितं (तत्) सर्वम् आश्रावयाञ्चक्रुः reported everything that had been accomplished by them.

Simple Meaning: Upon their arrival, Bhima, Arjuna, and Krishna greeted King Yudhisthira and recounted everything they had accomplished in Magadha.

निशम्य धर्मराजस्तत् केशवेनानुकम्पितम् ।
आनन्दाश्रुकलान् मुञ्चन् प्रेम्णा नोवाच किञ्चन ॥10.73.35॥

Word-by-word meaning: निशम्य hearing, धर्मराजः King Yudhisthira, तत् that, केशवेन by Keshava (Krishna), अनुकम्पितम् compassion/grace extended, आनन्द-अश्रु-कलान् tear-drops of joy, मुञ्चन् shedding, प्रेम्णा due to divine love, न उवाच did not speak, किञ्चन anything

Anvaya: धर्मराजः केशवेन तत् अनुकम्पितं निशम्य, आनन्दाश्रुकलान् मुञ्चन्, प्रेम्णा किञ्चन न उवाच।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: धर्मराजः King Yudhisthira, केशवेन तत् अनुकम्पितं निशम्य hearing of that extraordinary grace extended by Lord Keshava, आनन्दाश्रुकलान् मुञ्चन् shedding tears of pure joy, प्रेम्णा किञ्चन न उवाच could not say anything owing to intense love.

Simple Meaning: Hearing how Lord Krishna had compassionately orchestrated the defeat of Jarasandha and the release of the captive kings, King Yudhisthira was overcome with intense devotion. Shedding tears of joy, his throat choked with emotion, he could not utter a single word.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “कृष्णस्य मुक्तनृपानुग्रहः इन्द्रप्रस्थपुनरागमनं च नाम​” त्रिसप्ततितमः अध्यायः ॥73॥

Thus ends the seventy-third discourse named “Krishna blesses the liberated kings and returns to Indraprastha”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
