दशमः स्कन्धः

(45 Shlokas)

राजा उवाच
भगवन् यानि चान्यानि मुकुन्दस्य महात्मनः ।
वीर्याण्यनन्तवीर्यस्य श्रोतुमिच्छामहे प्रभो ॥10.80.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजा उवाच King Parikshit said, भगवन् O holy sage (Shuka), यानि which, च and, अन्यानि other, मुकुन्दस्य of Mukunda (Krishna), महात्मनः of the great-souled Lord, वीर्याणि heroic deeds and glorious exploits, अनन्त-वीर्यस्य of Him whose prowess is infinite, श्रोतुम् to hear, इच्छामहे we desire, प्रभो O master

Anvaya: राजा उवाच — भगवन्! प्रभो! महात्मनः अनन्तवीर्यस्य मुकुन्दस्य यानि च अन्यानि वीर्याणि (सन्ति, तानि) श्रोतुम् इच्छामहे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजा उवाच King Parikshit said, भगवन् O venerable sage, प्रभो O master, महात्मनः Of the supreme spirit, अनन्तवीर्यस्य of Him whose heroic prowess is boundless, मुकुन्दस्य of Mukunda, यानि च अन्यानि वीर्याणि (सन्ति) whatever other heroic deeds exist, (तानि) those, श्रोतुम् इच्छामहे we earnestly desire to hear.

Simple Meaning: King Parikshit said: O holy master, We earnestly desire to hear about whatever other heroic deeds and pastimes remain to be told about the great-souled Mukunda, who possesses infinite prowess and boundless glory.

को नु श्रुत्वासकृद् ब्रह्मन्नुत्तमश्लोकसत्कथाः ।
विरमेत विशेषज्ञो विषन्नः काममार्गणैः ॥10.80.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: कः who, नु indeed, श्रुत्वा having heard, असकृत् repeatedly, ब्रह्मन् O learned brahmana, उत्तम-श्लोक-सत्-कथाः the transcendental stories of the Lord Krishna who is praised by glorious words, विरमेत could ever desist or turn away, विशेषज्ञः one who knows the true value of things, विषण्णः who is distressed/exhausted, काम-मार्गणैः by the sharp arrows of material desires

Anvaya: ब्रह्मन्! काममार्गणैः विषण्णः विशेषज्ञः असकृत् उत्तमश्लोकसत्कथाः श्रुत्वा कः नु विरमेत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्रह्मन् O learned sage, काममार्गणैः by the arrows of lustful desires, विषण्णः being tormented, विशेषज्ञः a true connoisseur (of devotional nectar), असकृत् repeatedly, उत्तमश्लोकसत्कथाः the pure transcendental topics of the Lord who is praised with choice poetry, श्रुत्वा having heard, कः नु विरमेत who indeed could ever become satiated or turn away?

Simple Meaning: O learned sage, even a person who is severely tormented by the arrows of material desire, once he understands the true value of life and hears the sublime topics of the Supreme Lord repeatedly, who among such connoisseurs could ever cease hearing them?

सा वाग् यया तस्य गुणान् गृणीते करौ च तत्कर्मकरौ मनश्च ।
स्मरेद् वसन्तं स्थिरजङ्गमेषु शृणोति तत्पुण्यकथाः स कर्णः ॥10.80.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा that alone, वाक् speech or faculty of tongue, यया by which, तस्य His (Lord Krishna's), गुणान् qualities and transcendental virtues, गृणीते praises or chants, करौ two hands, च and, तत्-कर्म-करौ performing His service and work, मनः the mind, च and, स्मरेत् meditates upon, वसन्तम् Him dwelling or residing, स्थिर-जङ्गमेषु in all moving and non-moving living beings, शृणोति hears, तत्-पुण्य-कथाः His sanctifying pastimes, सः that alone, कर्णः ear

Anvaya: यया तस्य गुणान् गृणीते सा वाक्, तत्कर्मकरौ (यौ, तौ) करौ च, स्थिरजङ्गमेषु वसन्तं (तं) स्मरेत् (तत्) मनः च, (यः) तत्पुण्यकथाः शृणोति सः कर्णः (एव)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यया By which, तस्य गुणान् गृणीते His transcendental qualities are praised, सा वाक् that alone is true speech; (यौ) which, तत्कर्मकरौ perform His work, (तौ) those two, करौ च are true hands; स्थिरजङ्गमेषु in all stationary and moving beings, वसन्तं (तं) Him dwelling, स्मरेत् meditates upon, (तत्) मनः च that alone is a true mind; (यः) which, तत्पुण्यकथाः शृणोति hears His holy pastimes, सः कर्णः (एव) that alone is a true ear.

Simple Meaning: That alone is true speech which praises the transcendental virtues of the Lord; those alone are true hands which engage in His service; that alone is a true mind which remembers Him residing within all moving and unmoving creatures; and that alone is a true ear which hears His sacred, purifying pastimes.

शिरस्तु तस्योभयलिङ्गमानमेत्तदेव यत् पश्यति तद् हि चक्षुः ।
अङ्गानि विष्णोरथ तज्जनानां पादोदकं यानि भजन्ति नित्यम् ॥10.80.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: शिरः head, तु indeed, तस्य His (Lord Krishna's), उभय-लिङ्गम् both types of forms (the moving and non-moving manifestation or the Deity and His devotees), आनमेत् bows down to, तत् alone, एव indeed, यत् which, पश्यति beholds, तत् that, हि certainly, चक्षुः eye, अङ्गानि limbs of the body, विष्णोः of Lord Vishnu, अथ and, तत्-जनानाम् of His devotees, पाद-उदकम् the holy water that has washed the feet, यानि which, भजन्ति honor or bathe in, नित्यम् regularly

Anvaya: तस्य उभयलिङ्गं यत् आनमेत् तत् एव शिरः, (तस्य उभयलिङ्गं) यत् पश्यति तत् हि चक्षुः, अङ्गानि (तानि एव) यानि नित्यं विष्णोः अथ तज्जनानां पादोदकं भजन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तस्य His, उभयलिङ्गं both manifestations (His Deity form and His devotees), यत् आनमेत् which bows before, तत् एव शिरः that alone is a true head; (तस्य उभयलिङ्गं) His forms, यत् पश्यति which beholds, तत् हि चक्षुः that indeed is a true eye; अङ्गानि (तानि एव) those alone are true limbs of the body, यानि which, नित्यं regularly, विष्णोः of Lord Vishnu, अथ and, तज्जनानां of His devotees, पादोदकं the water that washed the feet, भजन्ति honor or contact.

Simple Meaning: That alone is a true head which bows down before both manifestations of the Lord—His Deity form and His devotees. That alone is a true eye which beholds those holy manifestations. And those alone are true limbs of the body which regularly are sprinkled by the sacred water that washed the feet of Lord Vishnu and His devotees.

सूत उवाच
विष्णुरातेन सम्पृष्टो भगवान् बादरायणिः ।
वासुदेवे भगवति निमग्नहृदयोऽब्रवीत् ॥10.80.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: सूतः उवाच Suta said, विष्णु-रातेन by King Parikshit (who was protected by Lord Vishnu), सम्पृष्टः thoroughly questioned, भगवान् the divine sage, बादरायणिः the son of Badarayana/Vyasa - Shuka, वासुदेवे in Lord Vasudeva, भगवति the Supreme Lord, निमग्न-हृदयः whose heart was completely absorbed, अब्रवीत् spoke

Anvaya: सूतः उवाच — विष्णुरातेन सम्पृष्टः वासुदेवे भगवति निमग्नहृदयः भगवान् बादरायणिः अब्रवीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सूतः उवाच Suta said, विष्णुरातेन सम्पृष्टः having been earnestly questioned by King Parikshit, वासुदेवे भगवति in the Supreme Lord Vasudeva, निमग्नहृदयः whose mind and heart were deeply merged, भगवान् बादरायणिः the venerable Sukadeva Gosvami, अब्रवीत् spoke.

Simple Meaning: Suta said: Thus deeply questioned by King Parikshit, the revered Shuka, whose heart was completely immersed in loving remembrance of the Supreme Lord Vasudeva, began to reply.

श्री शुक उवाच
कृष्णस्यासीत् सखा कश्चिद् ब्राह्मणो ब्रह्मवित्तमः ।
विरक्त इन्द्रियार्थेषु प्रशानतात्मा जितेन्द्रियः ॥10.80.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, कृष्णस्य of Krishna, आसीत् there was, सखा a companion or friend, कश्चित् a certain, ब्राह्मणः learned brahmana (Sudama), ब्रह्म-विद्-तमः foremost among knowers of the Supreme Brahman, विरक्तः completely detached, इन्द्रिय-अर्थेषु from objects of sense gratification, प्रशान्त-आत्मा whose mind was deeply peaceful, जित-इन्द्रियः who had conquered his senses

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच — इन्द्रियार्थेषु विरक्तः प्रशान्तात्मा जितेन्द्रियः ब्रह्मवित्तमः कश्चित् ब्राह्मणः कृष्णस्य सखा आसीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इन्द्रियार्थेषु विरक्तः totally dispassionate toward worldly sense objects, प्रशान्तात्मा possessed of a perfectly tranquil mind, जितेन्द्रियः having subdued all his senses, ब्रह्मवित्तमः the most eminent among those who know Brahman, कश्चित् ब्राह्मणः a certain saintly brahmana, कृष्णस्य सखा आसीत् was a dear friend of Krishna.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Krishna had a dear brahmana friend (named Sudama) who was most eminent among those realized in the knowledge of Brahman. He was completely detached from material sense pleasures, possessed a deeply peaceful mind, and maintained perfect control over his senses.

यदृच्छयोपपन्नेन वर्तमानो गृहाश्रमी ।
तस्य भार्या कुचैलस्य क्षुत्क्षामा च तथाविधा ॥10.80.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: यदृच्छया by chance or providence, उपपन्नेन with whatever was obtained, वर्तमानः maintaining his livelihood, गृह-आश्रमी a householder, तस्य his, भार्या wife, कु-चैलस्य who was poorly dressed (wearing worn garments), क्षुत्-क्षामा emaciated by hunger, च and, तथा-विधा in a similar condition (wearing shabby attire)

Anvaya: (सः) यदृच्छया उपपन्नेन वर्तमानः गृहाश्रमी (आसीत्)। कुचैलस्य तस्य भार्या क्षुत्क्षामा तथाविधा च (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सः) He, यदृच्छया उपपन्नेन with whatever arrived unasked by fate, वर्तमानः maintaining his sustenance, गृहाश्रमी (आसीत्) was a householder. कुचैलस्य तस्य Of him who was poorly clad, भार्या the wife, क्षुत्क्षामा emaciated through hunger, तथाविधा च and in a similar shabby condition, (आसीत्) was.

Simple Meaning: Living as a householder, Sudama maintained his life solely on whatever came to him unasked by divine providence. Poorly attired himself, his faithful wife was similarly clad in worn garments and severely emaciated due to constant hunger.

पतिव्रता पतिं प्राह म्लायता वदनेन सा ।
दरिद्रा सीदमाना सा वेपमानाभिगम्य च ॥10.80.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: पति-व्रता devoted to her husband, पतिम् to her husband, प्राह spoke, म्लायता with a withered or pale, वदनेन face, सा she, दरिद्रा destitute, सीदमाना suffering or feeling distressed, सा she, वेपमाना trembling (from hunger and weakness), अभिगम्य approaching, च and

Anvaya: दरिद्रा सीदमाना सा पतिव्रता म्लायता वदनेन वेपमाना (च) पतिम् अभिगम्य प्राह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दरिद्रा Poor and destitute, सीदमाना suffering in misery, सा पतिव्रता that chaste and devoted wife, म्लायता वदनेन with a pale face, वेपमाना (च) and trembling, पतिम् अभिगम्य approaching her husband, प्राह spoke.

Simple Meaning: Suffering in extreme poverty and trembling from weakness, that chaste and devoted wife approached her husband. With a pale and withered face, she spoke to him as follows.

ननु ब्रह्मन् भगवतः सखा साक्षाच्छ्रियः पतिः ।
ब्रह्मण्यश्च शरण्यश्च भगवान् सात्वतर्षभः ॥10.80.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: ननु is it not so, ब्रह्मन् O learned brahmana, भगवतः of the Supreme Lord, सखा the intimate friend, साक्षात् directly, श्रियः पतिः the Lord and husband of the goddess of fortune, ब्रह्मण्यः devotedly compassionate to the brahmanas, च and, शरण्यः the ultimate refuge of all, च and, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, सात्वत-ऋषभः the chief leader of the Satvata race (the Yadus)

Anvaya: ब्रह्मन्! (तव) सखा साक्षात् श्रियः पतिः ब्रह्मण्यः शरण्यः सात्वतर्षभः भगवान् (अस्ति) ननु।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्रह्मन् O saintly husband, (तव) सखा your intimate companion, साक्षात् श्रियः पतिः directly the consort of the goddess of fortune Goddess Lakshmi, ब्रह्मण्यः devoted to the well-being of the brahmanas, शरण्यः the shelter of all surrendered souls, सात्वतर्षभः the supreme leader among the Satvatas, भगवान् (अस्ति) ननु is the Supreme Lord, is He not?

Simple Meaning: The noble wife said: O holy husband, is not your dear friend Krishna the Supreme Lord Himself? He is the direct consort of the goddess of fortune, devoted to the brahmanas, the supreme shelter for all souls, and the glorious hero of the Yadu dynasty.

तमुपैहि महाभाग साधूनां च परायणम् ।
दास्यति द्रविणं भूरि सीदते ते कुटुम्बिने ॥10.80.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् Him (Lord Krishna), उपैहि approach or go unto, महाभाग O fortunate one, साधूनाम् of saintly persons, च and, परायणम् the ultimate refuge, दास्यति He will bestow, द्रविणम् wealth, भूरि abundant, सीदते who is suffering, ते to you, कुटुम्बिने a family man with dependents

Anvaya: महाभाग! साधूनां परायणं तम् उपैहि, (सः) सीदते कुटुम्बिने ते भूरि द्रविणं दास्यति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महाभाग O highly fortunate one, साधूनां परायणं the supreme shelter of all holy sages, तम् उपैहि approach Him; (सः) He, सीदते कुटुम्बिने ते to you who are suffering along with your household, भूरि द्रविणं abundant wealth, दास्यति will surely give.

Simple Meaning: O fortunate one, please go to Him, for He is the ultimate refuge of all saintly persons. When He sees you suffering to maintain your family, He will surely bestow abundant wealth upon you.

आस्तेऽधुना द्वारवत्यां भोजवृष्ण्यन्धकेश्वरः ।
स्मरतः पादकमलमात्मानमपि यच्छति ।
किंन्वर्थकामान् भजतो नात्यभीष्टाञ्जगद्गुरुः ॥10.80.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: आस्ते resides, अधुना now, द्वारवत्याम् in Dwaraka, भोज-वृष्णि-अन्धक-ईश्वरः the ruler of the Bhojas, Vrishnis, and Andhakas, स्मरतः to one who remembers, पाद-कमलम् His lotus feet, आत्मानम् Himself, अपि even, यच्छति bestows, किम् नु what then, अर्थ-कामान् material wealth and worldly desires, भजतः to one who worships Him, न not, अति-अभीष्टान् most cherished, जगत्-गुरुः the spiritual master of the universe

Anvaya: अधुना भोजवृष्ण्यन्धकेश्वरः (सः) द्वारवत्याम् आस्ते। (यः) पादकमलं स्मरतः आत्मानम् अपि यच्छति, (सः) जगद्गुरुः भजतः न अत्यभीष्टान् अर्थकामान् किं नु (यच्छति)?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अधुना Now, भोजवृष्ण्यन्धकेश्वरः the ruler of the Bhoja, Vrishni, and Andhaka clans, (सः) He, द्वारवत्याम् आस्ते resides in Dwaraka. (यः) He who, पादकमलं स्मरतः to one who simply remembers His lotus feet, आत्मानम् अपि यच्छति bestows even Himself, (सः) जगद्गुरुः that master of the universe, भजतः to one who worships Him, न अत्यभीष्टान् which are not highly desirable, अर्थकामान् material wealth and desires, किं नु (यच्छति) will He not grant?

Simple Meaning: He who is the ruler of the Bhoja, Vrishni, and Andhaka dynasties currently resides in Dwaraka. He bestows even Himself upon anyone who simply remembers His lotus feet, then what doubt can there be that this spiritual master of the universe will grant wealth and basic needs, which are not even desirable, to one who worships Him?

स एवं भार्यया विप्रो बहुशः प्रार्थितो मृदु ।
अयं हि परमो लाभ उत्तमश्लोकदर्शनम् ॥10.80.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Sudama), एवम् in this manner, भार्यया by his wife, विप्रः the brahmana, बहुशः repeatedly, प्रार्थितः begged/requested, मृदु gently, अयम् this, हि indeed, परमः supreme, लाभः gain/attainment, उत्तम-श्लोक-दर्शनम् seeing the Lord who is praised by choice poetry (Lord Krishna)

Anvaya: सः विप्रः भार्यया एवं बहुशः मृदु प्रार्थितः (अमन्यत) — अयम् उत्तमश्लोकदर्शनं हि परमः लाभः।

Word-by-word meaning: वयम् we, भृशम् severely, तत्र there in that forest, महा-अनिल-अम्बुभिः by violent winds and rain, निहन्यमानाः being battered/struck, मुहुः repeatedly, अम्बु-सम्प्लवे in the deluge of waters, दिशः directions, अविदन्तः not knowing/losing orientation, अथ then, परस्परम् each other, वने in the forest, गृहीत-हस्ताः holding each other's hands, परिबभ्रिम wandered about, आतुराः bewildered

Anvaya: तत्र अम्बुसम्प्लवे महानिलाम्बुभिः भृशं निहन्यमानाः दिशः अविदन्तः वने आतुराः (वयम्) परस्परं गृहीतहस्ताः अथ मुहुः परिबभ्रिम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत्र There in that place, अम्बुसम्प्लवे in the flood of waters, महानिलाम्बुभिः by fierce gales and torrential rains, भृशं severely, निहन्यमानाः being relentlessly tormented, दिशः directions, अविदन्तः unable to discern, वने in the wild woods, आतुराः distressed, (वयम्) we, परस्परं each other's, गृहीतहस्ताः hands held, अथ then, मुहुः repeatedly, परिबभ्रिम wandered around.

Simple Meaning: Battered relentlessly by the fierce winds and heavy deluge, and unable to distinguish any direction in the pitch darkness, we wandered through the flooded forest in great distress, holding onto each other's hands.

एतद् विदित्वा उदिते रवौ सान्दीपनिर्गुरुः ।
अन्वेषमाणो नः शिष्यानाचार्योऽपश्यदातुरान् ॥10.80.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतत् this (condition), विदित्वा knowing/realizing, उदिते having risen, रवौ the sun, सान्दीपनिः Sandipani, गुरुः our spiritual master, अन्वेषमाणः searching for, नः us, शिष्यान् his disciples, आचार्यः the teacher, अपश्यत् saw, आतुरान् distressed/suffering

Anvaya: रवौ उदिते एतत् विदित्वा नः शिष्यान् अन्वेषमाणः गुरुः आचार्यः सान्दीपनिः आतुरान् अपश्यत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रवौ उदिते When the sun had risen, एतत् this plight, विदित्वा having realized, नः us, शिष्यान् his students, अन्वेषमाणः searching everywhere for, गुरुः आचार्यः सान्दीपनिः the spiritual guide and preceptor, Sandipani, आतुरान् in a suffering state, अपश्यत् beheld.

Simple Meaning: At sunrise, when our spiritual master Sandipani realized what had happened during the night, he set out to search for us, his disciples. Upon finding us in the forest, he saw how much we had suffered from the storm.

अहो हे पुत्रका यूयमस्मदर्थेऽतिदुःखिताः ।
आत्मा वै प्राणिनां प्रेष्ठस्तमनादृत्य मत्पराः ॥10.80.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: अहो oh, हे पुत्रकाः O dear sons, यूयम् you, अस्मत्-अर्थे for my sake, अति-दुःखिताः suffered great hardship, आत्मा the self/life, वै indeed, प्राणिनाम् for all living beings, प्रेष्ठः most dear, तम् that self, अनादृत्य disregarding, मत्-पराः devoted to me

Anvaya: अहो हे पुत्रकाः! यूयम् अस्मदर्थे अतिदुःखिताः। प्राणिनां हि आत्मा प्रेष्ठः (भवति), तम् अनादृत्य (यूयं) मत्पराः (जाताः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अहो हे पुत्रकाः Oh my dear children, यूयम् you, अस्मदर्थे for my sake, अतिदुःखिताः suffered severe distress. प्राणिनां For living beings, हि surely, आत्मा one's own self, प्रेष्ठः is most cherished, तम् that self, अनादृत्य disregarding, (यूयं) you, मत्पराः devoted to me as your supreme refuge, (जाताः) have become.

Simple Meaning: Guru Sandipani said: Oh, my dear children! You have undergone such severe hardship for my sake. To every living creature, one's own life is most precious, yet you disregarded your own physical well-being out of devotion to me.

एतदेव हि सच्छिष्यैः कर्तव्यं गुरुनिष्कृतम् ।
यद् वै विशुद्धभावेन सर्वार्थात्मार्पणं गुरौ ॥10.80.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतत् this, एव alone, हि indeed, सत्-शिष्यैः by noble disciples, कर्तव्यम् ought to be done, गुरु-निष्कृतम् repaying the debt to the spiritual teacher, यत् which, वै surely, विशुद्ध-भावेन with pure devotion, सर्व-अर्थ-आत्मा-अर्पणम् offering all one's possessions and one's very self, गुरौ unto the guru

Anvaya: सच्छिष्यैः गुरुनिष्कृतम् एतत् एव हि कर्तव्यं, यत् विशुद्धभावेन गुरौ सर्वार्थात्मार्पणम् (क्रियते)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सच्छिष्यैः By virtuous disciples, गुरुनिष्कृतम् the discharging of duty or repayment owed to the spiritual teacher, एतत् एव हि this alone, कर्तव्यं should be done, यत् which is, विशुद्धभावेन with a pure heart, गुरौ unto the guru, सर्वार्थात्मार्पणम् dedication of all wealth, resources, and one's very self.

Simple Meaning: This alone is the true way for worthy disciples to express their gratitude to their spiritual master: to offer all their possessions and dedicate their very self unto the guru with pure and unblemished devotion.

तुष्टोऽहं भो द्विजश्रेष्ठाः सत्याः सन्तु मनोरथाः ।
छन्दांस्ययातयामानि भवन्त्विह परत्र च ॥10.80.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: तुष्टः pleased, अहम् I, भो O, द्विज-श्रेष्ठाः O best among the twice-born brahmanas, सत्याः true/fulfilled, सन्तु may become, मनोरथाः all desires of the mind, छन्दांसि the Vedas/teachings learned by you, अ-यात-यामानि never losing their potency/freshness, भवन्तु may remain, इह in this life, परत्र च and in the next world

Anvaya: भो द्विजश्रेष्ठाः! अहं तुष्टः (अस्मि), (युष्माकं) मनोरथाः सत्याः सन्तु, छन्दांसि इह परत्र च अयातयामानि भवन्तु।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भो द्विजश्रेष्ठाः O best among the twice-born, अहं I, तुष्टः pleased, (अस्मि) am; (युष्माकं) your, मनोरथाः heart's desires, सत्याः fulfilled, सन्तु may be; छन्दांसि the Vedas, इह in this present world, परत्र च and in the hereafter, अयातयामानि ever-vibrant and un-aging in power, भवन्तु may remain.

Simple Meaning: Guru Sandipani continued: O noble brahmanas, I am deeply pleased with you. May all the desires of your hearts be fulfilled, and may all the Vedas and knowledge you have learned from me never lose their freshness and potency, remaining ever-vibrant for you in this world and the next.

इत्थं विधान्यनेकानि वसतां गुरुवेश्मसु ।
गुरोरनुग्रहेणैव पुमान् पूर्णः प्रशान्तये ॥10.80.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: इत्थम् विधानि of such a nature, अनेकानि many, वसताम् while residing, गुरु-वेश्मसु in the residence of the guru, गुरोः of the guru, अनुग्रहेण by the grace, एव indeed, पुमान् a person, पूर्णः complete or fulfilled, प्रशान्तये for supreme peace

Anvaya: गुरुवेश्मसु वसतां (नः) इत्थंविधानि अनेकानि (वृत्तान्तानि अभवन्), गुरोः अनुग्रहेण एव पुमान् प्रशान्तये पूर्णः (भवति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गुरुवेश्मसु In the home of the spiritual teacher, वसतां while living, (नः) for us, इत्थंविधानि of this kind, अनेकानि many, (वृत्तान्तानि अभवन्) incidents took place; गुरोः of the spiritual preceptor, अनुग्रहेण by the mercy, एव alone, पुमान् a human soul, प्रशान्तये for attaining perfect peace, पूर्णः fully accomplished, (भवति) becomes.

Simple Meaning: Krishna concluded: Many such memorable events occurred while we were living at our guru’s hermitage. Indeed, it is solely through the grace of the spiritual preceptor that a person becomes fulfilled and attains ultimate peace.

ब्राह्मण उवाच
किमस्माभिरनिर्वृत्तं देवदेव जगद्गुरो ।
भवता सत्यकामेन येषां वासो गुरावभूत् ॥10.80.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्राह्मणः उवाच the brahmana Sudama said, किम् what, अस्माभिः by us, अनिर्वृत्तम् unachieved/unfulfilled, देव-देव O Lord of lords, जगत्-गुरो O spiritual master of the universe, भवता with You, सत्य-कामेन whose desires are ever truthful, येशाम् of whom, वासः residence, गुरौ in the gurukula, अभूत् took place

Anvaya: ब्राह्मणः उवाच — देवदेव! जगद्गुरो! सत्यकामेन भवता (सह) येषां गुरौ वासः अभूत्, अस्माभिः किम् अनिर्वृत्तम् (अस्ति)?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्राह्मणः उवाच The brahmana Sudama said, देवदेव O Lord of lords, जगद्गुरो O preceptor of the entire universe, सत्यकामेन whose desires are realized, भवता (सह) along with You, येषां whose, गुरौ in the house of the guru, वासः residence together, अभूत् occurred, अस्माभिः by us, किम् what, अनिर्वृत्तम् remains unfulfilled?

Simple Meaning: The brahmana Sudama said: O Lord of lords! O Guru of the entire universe! What could possibly remain unachieved for us, who were fortunate enough to live together in the gurukula with You— whose every desire is eternally fulfilled?

यस्यच्छन्दोमयं ब्रह्म देह आवपनं विभो ।
श्रेयसां तस्य गुरुषु वासोऽत्यन्तविडम्बनम् ॥10.80.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: यस्य whose, छन्दः-मयम् embodying the Vedas, ब्रह्म the sacred sound of Brahman, देहः body, आवपनम् the dwelling place or field of origin, विभो O all-pervading Lord, श्रेयसाम् of all blessings and ultimate good, तस्य His, गुरुषु residing under spiritual teachers, वासः living, अत्यन्त-विडम्बनम् pure play/sublime imitation

Anvaya: विभो! यस्य छन्दोमयं ब्रह्म देहः श्रेयसां (च) आवपनम् (अस्ति), तस्य गुरुषु वासः अत्यन्तविडम्बनम् (एव)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विभो O all-pervading Lord, यस्य whose, देहः divine form, छन्दोमयं embodiment of the Vedas, ब्रह्म the Absolute Truth, श्रेयसां of all ultimate auspiciousness, (च) and, आवपनम् the source or abode, (अस्ति) is, तस्य His, गुरुषु under the guidance of spiritual masters, वासः living as a student, अत्यन्तविडम्बनम् (एव) is purely a divine pastime.

Simple Meaning: Sudama continued: O all-pervading Lord, Your divine body is the source of all Vedic knowledge and the ultimate abode of all blessings. For You, to live as a student under a spiritual master, was simply a wonderful pastime.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “सुदामचरितं नाम​” अशीतितमः अध्यायः ॥80॥

Thus ends the eightieth discourse named “The story of Sudama”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
