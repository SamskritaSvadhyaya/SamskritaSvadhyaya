श्री शुक उवाच
इत्थं सोऽनुगृहीतोऽङ्ग कृष्णेनेक्ष्वाकुनन्दनः ।
तं परिक्रम्य सन्नम्य निश्चक्राम गुहामुखात् ॥10.52.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इत्थम् in this manner, सः he, अनुगृहीतः favored, अङ्ग O King, कृष्णेन by Krishna, इक्ष्वाकु-नन्दनः the descendant of Ikshvaku (Muchukunda), तम् Him, परिक्रम्य circumambulating, सन्नम्य bowing down, निश्चक्राम came out, गुहा-मुखात् from the mouth of the cave

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच – अङ्ग! इत्थं कृष्णेन अनुगृहीतः सः इक्ष्वाकुनन्दनः तं परिक्रम्य सन्नम्य गुहामुखात् निश्चक्राम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, अङ्ग O King, इत्थं in this manner, कृष्णेन by Krishna, अनुगृहीतः favored, सः इक्ष्वाकुनन्दनः he, the descendant of Ikshvaku, तं Him, परिक्रम्य circumambulating, सन्नम्य bowing down, गुहामुखात् from the mouth of the cave, निश्चक्राम came out.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King, having been favored by Krishna in this manner, Muchukunda, the descendant of Ikshvaku, circumambulated Him, bowed down to Him, and then emerged from the mouth of the cave.

स वीक्ष्य क्षुल्लकान् मर्त्यान् पशून् वीरुद्वनस्पतीन् ।
मत्वा कलियुगं प्राप्तं जगाम दिशमुत्तराम् ॥10.52.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, वीक्ष्य observing, क्षुल्लकान् diminutive, मर्त्यान् human beings, पशून् animals, वीरुत्-वनस्पतीन् creepers and trees, मत्वा understanding, कलि-युगम् the age of Kali, प्राप्तम् arrived, जगाम went, दिशम् direction, उत्तराम् northern

Anvaya: सः क्षुल्लकान् मर्त्यान् पशून् वीरुद्वनस्पतीन् वीक्ष्य कलियुगं प्राप्तं मत्वा उत्तरां दिशं जगाम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He, क्षुल्लकान् diminutive, मर्त्यान् human beings, पशून् animals, वीरुद्वनस्पतीन् creepers and trees, वीक्ष्य observing, कलियुगं the age of Kali, प्राप्तं arrived, मत्वा understanding, उत्तरां दिशं to the northern direction, जगाम went.

Simple Meaning: Observing that all human beings, animals, creepers, and trees had become significantly stunted in size, and understanding that the age of Kali had arrived, he departed in the northern direction.

तपःश्रद्धायुतो धीरो निःसङ्गो मुक्तसंशयः ।
समाधाय मनः कृष्णे प्राविशद् गन्धमादनम् ॥10.52.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तपः-श्रद्धा-युतः endowed with austerity and faith, धीरः sober, निःसङ्गः free from attachment, मुक्त-संशयः freed from doubts, समाधाय fixing, मनः the mind, कृष्णे on Krishna, प्राविशत् entered, गन्ध-मादनम् Mount Gandhamadana

Anvaya: तपःश्रद्धायुतः धीरः निःसङ्गः मुक्तसंशयः मनः कृष्णे समाधाय गन्धमादनं प्राविशत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तपःश्रद्धायुतः Endowed with austerity and faith, धीरः sober, निःसङ्गः free from attachment, मुक्तसंशयः freed from doubts, मनः the mind, कृष्णे on Krishna, समाधाय fixing, गन्धमादनं Mount Gandhamadana, प्राविशत् entered.

Simple Meaning: Filled with austerity and faith, calm, detached, and free from all doubts, he absorbed his mind fully in Krishna and entered Mount Gandhamadana.

बदर्याश्रममासाद्य नरनारायणालयम् ।
सर्वद्वन्द्वसहः शान्तस्तपसाऽऽराधयत् हरिम् ॥10.52.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: बदरी-आश्रमम् Badarika-ashrama, आसाद्य reaching, नर-नारायण-आलयम् the abode of Nara-Narayana, सर्व-द्वन्द्व-सहः tolerating all dualities, शान्तः peaceful, तपसा by austerity, आराधयत् worshiped, हरिम् Hari

Anvaya: नरनारायणालयं बदर्याश्रमम् आसाद्य सर्वद्वन्द्वसहः शान्तः तपसा हरिम् आराधयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नरनारायणालयं The abode of Nara-Narayana, बदर्याश्रमम् Badarika-ashrama, आसाद्य reaching, सर्वद्वन्द्वसहः tolerating all dualities, शान्तः peaceful, तपसा by austerity, हरिम् Hari, आराधयत् worshiped.

Simple Meaning: Reaching Badarika-ashrama, the sacred abode of Nara-Narayana, he remained perfectly peaceful while enduring all dualities like heat and cold, and worshiped Lord Hari through austerities.

भगवान् पुनराव्रज्य पुरीं यवनवेष्टिताम् ।
हत्वा म्लेच्छबलं निन्ये तदीयं द्वारकां धनम् ॥10.52.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: भगवान् the Supreme Lord, पुनः again, आव्रज्य returning, पुरीम् to the city, यवन-वेष्टिताम् surrounded by the Yavanas, हत्वा killing, म्लेच्छ-बलम् the barbarian army, निन्ये brought, तदीयम् their, द्वारकाम् to Dwaraka, धनम् wealth

Anvaya: भगवान् पुनः यवनवेष्टितां पुरीम् आव्रज्य म्लेच्छबलं हत्वा तदीयं धनं द्वारकां निन्ये।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवान् The Supreme Lord, पुनः again, यवनवेष्टितां surrounded by the Yavanas, पुरीम् to the city, आव्रज्य returning, म्लेच्छबलं the barbarian army, हत्वा killing, तदीयं their, धनं wealth, द्वारकां to Dwaraka, निन्ये brought.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord then returned to the city of Mathura which was surrounded by the Yavanas, destroyed the barbarian army, and transported all their riches back to Dwaraka.

नीयमाने धने गोभिर्नृभिश्चाच्युतचोदितैः ।
आजगाम जरासन्धस्त्रयोविंशत्यनीकपः ॥10.52.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: नीयमाने being carried, धने the wealth, गोभिः by oxen, नृभिः by men, च and, अच्युत-चोदितैः directed by Lord Achyuta, आजगाम arrived, जरासन्धः Jarasandha, त्रयोविंशति-अनीक-पः the commander of twenty three armies

Anvaya: अच्युतचोदितैः नृभिः गोभिः च धने नीयमाने त्रयोविंशत्यनीकपः जरासन्धः आजगाम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अच्युतचोदितैः Directed by Lord Achyuta, नृभिः by men, गोभिः by oxen, च and, धने the wealth, नीयमाने being carried, त्रयोविंशत्यनीकपः the commander of twenty three armies, जरासन्धः Jarasandha, आजगाम arrived.

Simple Meaning: While that wealth was being carried away by men and oxen under the direction of Lord Achyuta, Jarasandha arrived leading an army of twenty three military divisions (Aksauhinis).

विलोक्य वेगरभसं रिपुसैन्यस्य माधवौ ।
मनुष्यचेष्टामापन्नौ राजन् दुद्रुवतुर्द्रुतम् ॥10.52.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: विलोक्य seeing, वेग-रभसम् the furious onslaught, रिपु-सैन्यस्य of the enemy army, माधवौ the two descendants of Madhu (Krishna and Balarama), मनुष्य-चेष्टाम् human-like behavior, आपन्नौ assuming, राजन् O King, दुद्रुवतुः ran away, द्रुतम् swiftly

Anvaya: राजन्! रिपुसैन्यस्य वेगरभसं विलोक्य मनुष्यचेष्टाम् आपन्नौ माधवौ द्रुतं दुद्रुवतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, रिपुसैन्यस्य वेगरभसं The furious onslaught of the enemy army, विलोक्य seeing, मनुष्यचेष्टाम् human-like behavior, आपन्नौ assuming, माधवौ the two descendants of Madhu, द्रुतं दुद्रुवतुः swiftly ran away.

Simple Meaning: O King, seeing the furious onslaught of the enemy army, Krishna and Balarama adopting human-like behaviour, fled swiftly.

विहाय वित्तं प्रचुरमभीतौ भीरुभीतवत् ।
पद्भ्यां पद्मपलाशाभ्यां चेरतुर्बहुयोजनम् ॥10.52.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: विहाय leaving behind, वित्तम् wealth, प्रचुरम् abundant, अभीतौ although fearless, भीरु-भीत-वत् terrified like cowards, पद्भ्याम् with feet, पद्म-पलाशाभ्याम् resembling lotus petals, चेरतुः they walked, बहु-योजनम् many yojanas

Anvaya: प्रचुरं वित्तं विहाय अभीतौ भीरुभीतवत् पद्मपलाशाभ्यां पद्भ्यां बहुयोजनं चेरतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रचुरं वित्तं Abundant wealth, विहाय leaving behind, अभीतौ although fearless, भीरुभीतवत् terrified like cowards, पद्मपलाशाभ्यां resembling lotus petals, पद्भ्यां with feet, बहुयोजनं many yojanas, चेरतुः they walked.

Simple Meaning: Leaving behind the abundant wealth, the two Lords, though completely fearless, acted as if terrified like cowards and walked for many miles on feet as tender as lotus petals.

पलायमानौ तौ दृष्ट्वा मागधः प्रहसन् बली ।
अन्वधावद् रथानीकैरीशयोरप्रमाणवित् ॥10.52.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: पलायमानौ fleeing, तौ the two of Them, दृष्ट्वा seeing, मागधः the King of Magadha (Jarasandha), प्रहसन् laughing loudly, बली powerful, अन्वधावत् pursued, रथ-अनीकैः with chariots and armies, ईशयोः of the two Lords, अप्रमाण-वित् not knowing the boundless power

Anvaya: पलायमानौ तौ दृष्ट्वा बली मागधः ईशयोः अप्रमाणवित् प्रहसन् रथानीकैः अन्वधावत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पलायमानौ Fleeing, तौ the two of Them, दृष्ट्वा seeing, बली powerful, मागधः the King of Magadha, ईशयोः of the two Lords, अप्रमाणवित् not knowing the boundless power, प्रहसन् laughing loudly, रथानीकैः with chariots and armies, अन्वधावत् pursued.

Simple Meaning: Seeing Them flee, the powerful Jarasandha laughed loudly and pursued them with his legions of chariots, entirely unaware of the boundless power of the two Lords.

प्रद्रुत्य दूरं संश्रान्तौ तुङ्गमारुहतां गिरिम् ।
प्रवर्षणाख्यं भगवान् नित्यदा यत्र वर्षति ॥10.52.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रद्रुत्य running, दूरम् a long distance, संश्रान्तौ appearing fatigued, तुङ्गम् high, आरुहताम् they climbed, गिरिम् a mountain, प्रवर्षण-आख्यम् named Pravarshana, भगवान् the lord of rain (Indra), नित्यदा always, यत्र where, वर्षति rains

Anvaya: दूरं प्रद्रुत्य संश्रान्तौ तौ प्रवर्षणाख्यं तुङ्गं गिरिम् आरुहतां यत्र भगवान् नित्यदा वर्षति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दूरं A long distance, प्रद्रुत्य running, संश्रान्तौ appearing fatigued, तौ the two of Them, प्रवर्षणाख्यं named Pravarshana, तुङ्गं high, गिरिम् mountain, आरुहतां climbed, यत्र where, भगवान् the lord of rain, नित्यदा always, वर्षति rains.

Simple Meaning: Having run a long distance and appearing fatigued, the two Lords climbed a high mountain named Pravarshana, where Indra, the lord of rain, pours down constant showers.

गिरौ निलीनावाज्ञाय नाधिगम्य पदं नृप ।
ददाह गिरिमेधोभिः समन्तादग्निमुत्सृजन् ॥10.52.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: गिरौ on the mountain, निलीनौ hidden, आज्ञाय knowing, न not, अधिगम्य finding, पदम् track/footprint, नृप O King, ददाह he burned, गिरिम् the mountain, एधोभिः with firewood, समन्तात् on all sides, अग्निम् fire, उत्सृजन् setting

Anvaya: नृप! गिरौ निलीनौ आज्ञाय, पदं न अधिगम्य, समन्तात् एधोभिः अग्निम् उत्सृजन् गिरिं ददाह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King, गिरौ On the mountain, निलीनौ hidden, आज्ञाय knowing, पदं track, न अधिगम्य not finding, समन्तात् on all sides, एधोभिः with firewood, अग्निम् fire, उत्सृजन् setting, गिरिं the mountain, ददाह he burned.

Simple Meaning: O King, knowing that They were hidden on the mountain but unable to find Their footprints, Jarasandha surrounded the mountain with firewood on all sides, set it on fire, and burned it.

तत उत्पत्य तरसा दह्यमानतटादुभौ ।
दशैकयोजनोत्तुङ्गान्निपेततुरधो भुवि ॥10.52.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, उत्पत्य leaping, तरसा with great speed, दह्यमान-तटात् from the burning peak, उभौ both of Them, दश-एक-योजन-उत्तुङ्गात् from eleven yojanas high, निपेततुः they jumped down, अधः downward, भुवि onto the earth

Anvaya: ततः उभौ दह्यमानतटात् तरसा उत्पत्य दशैकयोजनोत्तुङ्गात् अधः भुवि निपेततुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः Then, उभौ both of Them, दह्यमानतटात् from the burning peak, तरसा with great speed, उत्पत्य leaping, दशैकयोजनोत्तुङ्गात् from eleven yojanas high, अधः downward, भुवि onto the earth, निपेततुः they jumped down.

Simple Meaning: Then, both of Them leaped with great speed from the burning peak of the mountain, which was eleven yojanas high, and jumped down onto the earth below.

अलक्ष्यमाणौ रिपुणा सानुगेन यदूत्तमौ ।
स्वपुरं पुनरायातौ समुद्रपरिखां नृप ॥10.52.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: अलक्ष्यमाणौ undetected, रिपुणा by the enemy, स-अनुगेन along with his followers, यदु-उत्तमौ the two best of the Yadus, स्व-पुरम् to Their own city, पुनः again, आयातौ returned, समुद्र-परिखाम् which has the ocean as its moat, नृप O King

Anvaya: नृप! सानुगेन रिपुणा अलक्ष्यमाणौ यदूत्तमौ समुद्रपरिखां स्वपुरं पुनः आयातौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King, सानुगेन along with his followers, रिपुणा by the enemy, अलक्ष्यमाणौ undetected, यदूत्तमौ the two best of the Yadus, समुद्रपरिखां which has the ocean as its moat, स्वपुरं to Their own city, पुनः again, आयातौ returned.

Simple Meaning: O King, remaining completely undetected by the enemy Jarasandha and his followers, the two foremost heroes of the Yadu dynasty returned to Their own capital city of Dwaraka, which was surrounded by the ocean as its moat.

सोऽपि दग्धाविति मृषा मन्वानो बलकेशवौ ।
बलमाकृष्य सुमहन्मगधान् मागधो ययौ ॥10.52.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, अपि also, दग्धौ burned to death, इति thus, मृषा falsely, मन्वानः believing, बल-केशवौ Balarama and Kesava, बलम् military strength, आकृष्य withdrawing, सु-महत् very large, मगधान् to the Magadha province, मागधः the King of Magadha, ययौ went

Anvaya: सः मागधः अपि बलकेशवौ दग्धौ इति मृषा मन्वानः सुमहत् बलम् आकृष्य मगधान् ययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He, मागधः the King of Magadha, अपि also, बलकेशवौ Balarama and Kesava, दग्धौ burned to death, इति thus, मृषा falsely, मन्वानः believing, सुमहत् very large, बलम् military strength, आकृष्य withdrawing, मगधान् to the Magadha province, ययौ went.

Simple Meaning: Falsely believing that Balarama and Kesava had been burned to death in the fire, the King of Magadha withdrew his massive army and returned to his own kingdom of Magadha.

आनर्त्ताधिपतिः श्रीमान् रैवतो रेवतीं सुताम् ।
ब्रह्मणा चोदितः प्रादाद् बलायेति पुरोदितम् ॥10.52.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: आनर्त्त-अधिपतिः the ruler of Anarta, श्रीमान् opulent, रैवतः Raivata, रेवतीम् Revati, सुताम् daughter, ब्रह्मणा by Lord Brahma, चोदितः instructed, प्रादात् gave in marriage, बलाय to Lord Balarama, इति thus, पुरो-दितम् previously described

Anvaya: श्रीमान् आनर्त्ताधिपतिः रैवतः ब्रह्मणा चोदितः सुतां रेवतीं बलाय प्रादात् इति पुरोदितम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्रीमान् आनर्त्ताधिपतिः रैवतः The opulent ruler of Anarta, Raivata, ब्रह्मणा by Lord Brahma, चोदितः instructed, सुतां रेवतीं daughter Revati, बलाय to Balarama, प्रादात् gave in marriage, इति thus, पुरोदितम् previously described.

Simple Meaning: The opulent King Raivata, the ruler of Anarta, gave his daughter Revati in marriage to Balarama under the instruction of Lord Brahma, as has been previously described.

Note: This marriage was briefly mentioned in the Ninth Canto of the Bhagavatam, Third Chapter, shlokas 33-36.

भगवानपि गोविन्द उपयेमे कुरुद्वह ।
वैदर्भीं भीष्मकसुतां श्रियो मात्रां स्वयंवरे ॥10.52.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: भगवान् the Supreme Lord, अपि also, गोविन्दः Govinda, उपयेमे married, कुरु-द्वह O best of the Kurus, वैदर्भीम् the princess of Vidarbha, भीष्मक-सुताम् the daughter of Bhishmaka, श्रियः of the goddess of fortune, मात्राम् a direct expansion, स्वयंवरे in a swayamvara ceremony

Anvaya: कुरुद्वह! भगवान् गोविन्दः अपि स्वयंवरे श्रियः मात्रां भीष्मकसुतां वैदर्भीम् उपयेमे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कुरुद्वह O best of the Kurus, भगवान् गोविन्दः the Supreme Lord, Govinda, अपि also, स्वयंवरे in a swayamvara ceremony, श्रियः of the goddess of fortune, मात्रां a direct expansion, भीष्मकसुतां the daughter of Bhishmaka, वैदर्भीम् the princess of Vidarbha, उपयेमे married.

Simple Meaning: O best of the Kurus, the Supreme Lord Govinda also married the princess of Vidarbha, Rukmini, who was the daughter of King Bhishmaka and a direct expansion of the goddess of fortune herself, winning her in a swayamvara assembly.

प्रमथ्य तरसा राज्ञः शाल्वादींश्चैद्यपक्षगान् ।
पश्यतां सर्वलोकानां तार्क्ष्यपुत्रः सुधामिव ॥10.52.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रमथ्य defeating, तरसा with great force, राज्ञः kings, शाल्वा-दीन् Shalva and others, चैद्य-पक्ष-गान् who took the side of the King of Chedi (Shishupala), पश्यताम् while looking on, सर्व-लोकानाम् all the people, तार्क्ष्य-पुत्रः the son of Tarkshya - another name of the sage Kashyapa) (Garuda), सुधाम् nectar, इव like

राज्ञः राजन्,  नकारान्तः, पुंलिङ्गः, अन्नन्तः, द्वितीयाबहुवचनम्, पञ्चम्येकवचनम् , षष्ठ्येकवचनम्

In this context, राज्ञः is द्वितीयाबहुवचनम्

Anvaya: चैद्यपक्षगान् शाल्वादीन् राज्ञः तरसा प्रमथ्य सर्वलोकानां पश्यतां तार्क्ष्यपुत्रः सुधाम् इव (ताम् उपयेमे)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: चैद्यपक्षगान् Who took the side of the King of Chedi, शाल्वादीन् Shalva and others, राज्ञः kings, तरसा with great force, प्रमथ्य defeating, सर्वलोकानां of all the people, पश्यतां while looking on, तार्क्ष्यपुत्रः the son of Tarkshya, सुधाम् nectar, इव like (He married her).

Simple Meaning: Defeating Shalva and the other kings who took the side of Shishupala with great force, the Lord carried her away and married her while all the world looked on, just as Garuda once carried away the celestial nectar.

राजा उवाच
भगवान् भीष्मकसुतां रुक्मीणीं रुचिराननाम् ।
राक्षसेन विधानेन उपयेम इति श्रुतम् ॥10.52.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजा उवाच the King (Parikshit) said, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, भीष्मक-सुताम् the daughter of Bhishmaka, रुक्मिणीम् Rukmini, रुचिर-आननाम् possessing a beautiful face, राक्षसेन by the Rakshasa, विधानेन style, उपयेमे married, इति thus, श्रुतम् I have heard

Anvaya: राजा उवाच — भगवान् रुचिराननां भीष्मकसुतां रुक्मिणीं राक्षसेन विधानेन उपयेमे इति श्रुतम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजा उवाच The King said, भगवान् The Supreme Lord, रुचिराननां भीष्मकसुतां रुक्मिणीं possessing a beautiful face, the daughter of Bhishmaka, Rukmini, राक्षसेन विधानेन by the Rakshasa style, उपयेमे married, इति thus, श्रुतम् I have heard.

Simple Meaning: King Parikshit said: I have heard that the Supreme Lord married Rukmini, the beautiful-faced daughter of King Bhishmaka, by kidnapping her in the Rakshasa style.

भगवञ्श्रोतुमिच्छामि कृष्णस्यामिततेजसः ।
यथा मागधशाल्वादीन् जित्वा कन्यामुपाहरत् ॥10.52.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: भगवन् O most powerful sage, श्रोतुम् to hear, इच्छामि I wish, कृष्णस्य of Krishna, अमित-तेजसः of immeasurable prowess, यथा how, मागध-शाल्व-आदीन् Jarasandha Shalva and others, जित्वा defeating, कन्याम् the maiden, उपाहरत् carried away

Anvaya: भगवन्! अमिततेजसः कृष्णस्य (चरितं) श्रोतुम् इच्छामि, यथा मागधशाल्वादीन् जित्वा कन्याम् उपाहरत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवन् O most powerful sage, अमिततेजसः Of immeasurable prowess, कृष्णस्य of Krishna, श्रोतुम् इच्छामि I wish to hear, यथा how, मागधशाल्वादीन् Jarasandha Shalva and others, जित्वा defeating, कन्याम् the maiden, उपाहरत् carried away.

Simple Meaning: O holy sage, I wish to hear about the pastimes of Krishna, whose prowess is immeasurable, and learn how He defeated Jarasandha, Shalva, and their allies to carry away the young maiden.

ब्रह्मन् कृष्णकथा पुण्या माध्वीर्लोकमलापहाः ।
को नु तृप्येत शृण्वानः श्रुतज्ञो नित्यनूतनाः ॥10.52.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्रह्मन् O brahmana, कृष्ण-कथाः the narrations about Krishna, पुण्याः purifying, माध्वीः sweet like honey, लोक-मल-अपहाः destroying the dirt of the world, कः who, नु indeed, तृप्येत can be satiated, शृण्वानः hearing, श्रुत-ज्ञः one who knows the value of hearing, नित्य-नूतनाः always fresh

Anvaya: ब्रह्मन्! पुण्याः लोकमलापहाः माध्वीः नित्यनूतनाः कृष्णकथाः शृण्वानः श्रुतज्ञः कः नु तृप्येत?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्रह्मन् O brahmana, पुण्याः Purifying, लोकमलापहाः destroying the dirt of the world, माध्वीः sweet like honey, नित्यनूतनाः always fresh, कृष्णकथाः the narrations about Krishna, शृण्वानः hearing, श्रुतज्ञः one who knows the value of hearing, कः who, नु indeed, तृप्येत can be satiated.

Simple Meaning: O holy brahmana, the narrations about Krishna are purifying, sweet like honey, and completely destroy the contamination of this world. One who knows the true value of hearing could ever be satiated by listening to these descriptions, which always feel completely fresh and new?

श्री शुक उवाच
राजाऽऽसीद् भीष्मको नाम विदर्भाधिपतिर्महान् ।
तस्य पञ्चाभवन् पुत्राः कन्यैका च वरानना ॥10.52.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, राजा king, आसीत् there was, भीष्मकः Bhishmaka, नाम by name, विदर्भ-अधिपतिः the ruler of Vidarbha, महान् great, तस्य of him, पञ्च five, अभवन् there were, पुत्राः sons, कन्या daughter, एका one, च and, वर-आनना of beautiful face

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच - महान् विदर्भाधिपतिः भीष्मकः नाम राजा आसीत्। तस्य पञ्च पुत्राः एका वरानना कन्या च अभवन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, महान् विदर्भाधिपतिः The great ruler of Vidarbha, भीष्मकः नाम Bhishmaka by name, राजा आसीत् there was a king. तस्य Of him, पञ्च पुत्राः five sons, एका वरानना कन्या च and one beautiful daughter, अभवन् there were.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: There was a great king named Bhishmaka who was the ruler of Vidarbha. He had five sons and one beautiful daughter.

रुक्म्यग्रजो रुक्मरथो रुक्मबाहुरनन्तरः ।
रुक्म्केशो रुक्ममाली रुक्मिण्येषां स्वसा सती ॥10.52.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: रुक्मी Rukmi, अग्रजः the first-born, रुक्मरथः Rukmaratha, रुक्मबाहुः Rukmabahu, अनन्तरः following next, रुक्मकेशः Rukmakesha, रुक्ममाली Rukmamali, रुक्मिणी Rukmini, एषाम् their, स्वसा sister, सती virtuous

Anvaya: अग्रजः रुक्मी, अनन्तरः रुक्मरथः, रुक्मबाहुः, रुक्मकेशः, रुक्ममाली (च अभवन्)। एषां स्वसा सती रुक्मिणी (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अग्रजः रुक्मी The first-born Rukmi, अनन्तरः following next, रुक्मरथः Rukmaratha, रुक्मबाहुः Rukmabahu, रुक्मकेशः Rukmakesha, रुक्ममाली Rukmamali, (च अभवन्) and there were. एषाम् Their, स्वसा sister, सती virtuous, रुक्मिणी Rukmini, (आसीत्) there was.

Simple Meaning: Rukmi was the eldest brother, followed next by Rukmaratha, Rukmabahu, Rukmakesha, and Rukmamali. Their sister was the virtuous Rukmini.

सोपश्रुत्य मुकुन्दस्य रूपवीर्यगुणश्रियः ।
गृहागतैर्गीयमानास्तं मेने सदृशं पतिम् ॥10.52.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she, उपश्रुत्य hearing about, मुकुन्दस्य of Mukunda, रूप-वीर्य-गुण-श्रियः the beauty prowess qualities and opulence, गृह-आगतैः by those who visited her home, गीयमानाः being sung, तम् Him, मेने she considered, सदृशम् a suitable, पतिम् husband

Anvaya: सा गृहागतैः गीयमानाः मुकुन्दस्य रूपवीर्यगुणश्रियः उपश्रुत्य तं सदृशं पतिं मेने।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सा She, गृहागतैः by those who visited her home, गीयमानाः being sung, मुकुन्दस्य of Mukunda, रूपवीर्यगुणश्रियः the beauty, prowess, qualities and opulence, उपश्रुत्य hearing about, तं Him, सदृशं पतिं a suitable, husband, मेने considered.

Simple Meaning: Hearing the descriptions of Mukundas beauty, prowess, transcendental qualities, and opulence being sung by guests who visited her home, Rukmini considered Him to be the suitable husband for her.

तां बुद्धिलक्षणौदार्यरूपशीलगुणाश्रयाम् ।
कृष्णश्च सदृशीं भार्यां समुद्वोढुं मनो दधे ॥10.52.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her, बुद्धि-लक्षण-औदार्य-रूप-शील-गुण-आश्रयाम् the abode of intelligence auspicious bodily features magnanimity beauty character and good qualities, कृष्णः Krishna, च also, सदृशीम् suitable, भार्याम् wife, समुद्वोढुम् to marry, मनः mind, दधे fixed

Anvaya: बुद्धिलक्षणौदार्यरूपशीलगुणाश्रयां तां सदृशीं भार्यां समुद्वोढुं कृष्णः च मनः दधे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बुद्धिलक्षणौदार्यरूपशीलगुणाश्रयां तां The abode of intelligence auspicious bodily features magnanimity beauty character and good qualities, her, सदृशीं भार्यां suitable wife, समुद्वोढुं to marry, कृष्णः Krishna, च also, मनः mind, दधे fixed.

Simple Meaning: Krishna also fixed His mind on marrying her, recognizing that she possessed intelligence, auspicious bodily features, magnanimity, beauty, proper behavior, and other excellent qualities that made her an ideally suitable wife for Him.

बन्धूनामिच्छतां दातुं कृष्णाय भगिनीं नृप ।
ततो निवार्य कृष्णद्विड् रुक्मी चैद्यममन्यत ॥10.52.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: बन्धूनाम् of the relatives, इच्छताम् desiring, दातुम् to give, कृष्णाय to Krishna, भगिनीम् sister, नृप O king, ततः from that, निवार्य stopping them, कृष्ण-द्विट् the enemy of Krishna, रुक्मी Rukmi, चैद्यम् the king of Chedi (Shishupala), अमन्यत thought of (as the groom)

Anvaya: नृप! बन्धूनां कृष्णाय भगिनीं दातुम् इच्छताम् अपि, कृष्णद्विट् रुक्मी ततः निवार्य चैद्यम् अमन्यत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O king, बन्धूनां Of the relatives, कृष्णाय to Krishna, भगिनीं sister, दातुम् इच्छताम् desiring to give, अपि even though, कृष्णद्विट् रुक्मी the enemy of Krishna, Rukmi, ततः निवार्य stopping them from that, चैद्यम् king of Chedi - Shishupala, अमन्यत thought of (as the groom).

Simple Meaning: O King, even though all her relatives desired to give their daughter to Krishna, the envious Rukmi, who hated Krishna, stopped them and instead chose Shishupala, the king of Chedi, to be her husband.

तदवेत्यासितापाङ्गी वैदर्भी दुर्मना भृशम् ।
विचिन्त्याप्तं द्विजं कञ्चित् कृष्णाय प्राहिणोद् द्रुतम् ॥10.52.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that, अवेत्य learning of, असित-अपाङ्गी the dark eyed princess, वैदर्भी Rukmini - the daughter of king of Vidarbha (Bhishmaka), दुर्मनाः heavy-hearted, भृशम् extremely, विचिन्त्य thinking, आप्तम् trustworthy, द्विजम् brahmana, कञ्चित् a certain, कृष्णाय to Krishna, प्राहिणोत् sent, द्रुतम् quickly

Anvaya: असितापाङ्गी वैदर्भी तत् अवेत्य भृशं दुर्मना (सती), विचिन्त्य कञ्चित् आप्तं द्विजं कृष्णाय द्रुतं प्राहिणोत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: असितापाङ्गी वैदर्भी The dark eyed princess, Rukmini, तत् अवेत्य learning of that, भृशं दुर्मना (becoming) extremely heavy-hearted, विचिन्त्य thinking, कञ्चित् आप्तं द्विजं a certain trustworthy brahmana, कृष्णाय to Krishna, द्रुतं प्राहिणोत् quickly sent.

Simple Meaning: Learning of this decision, the dark eyed princess Rukmini became extremely heavy-hearted. After deep consideration, she immediately sent a trustworthy brahmana to Krishna.

द्वारकां स समभ्येत्य प्रतीहारैः प्रवेशितः ।
अपश्यदाद्यं पुरुषमासीनं काञ्चनासने ॥10.52.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्वारकाम् Dwaraka, सः he, समभ्येत्य arriving at, प्रतीहारैः by the gatekeepers, प्रवेशितः brought inside, अपश्यत् saw, आद्यम् the Primeval, पुरुषम् Personality, आसीनम् sitting, काञ्चन-आसने on a golden throne

Anvaya: सः द्वारकां समभ्येत्य प्रतीहारैः प्रवेशितः काञ्चनासने आसीनम् आद्यं पुरुषम् अपश्यत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He, द्वारकां Dwaraka, समभ्येत्य arriving at, प्रतीहारैः by the gatekeepers, प्रवेशितः brought inside, काञ्चनासने on a golden throne, आसीनम् sitting, आद्यं पुरुषम् the Primeval Personality, अपश्यत् saw.

Simple Meaning: Upon reaching Dwaraka, the brahmana was escorted inside by the gatekeepers and saw the Primeval Personality sitting on a golden throne.

दृष्ट्वा ब्रह्मण्यदेवस्तमवरुह्य निजासनात् ।
उपवेश्यार्हयाञ्चक्रे यथाऽऽत्मानं दिवौकसः ॥10.52.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: दृष्ट्वा seeing, ब्रह्मण्य-देवः the Lord who is worshipful of the brahmanas, तम् him, अवरुह्य descending, निज-आसनात् from His own throne, उपवेश्य seating him, अर्हयाञ्चक्रे worshiped, यथा as, आत्मानम् Himself, दिवौकसः the gods

Anvaya: ब्रह्मण्यदेवः तं दृष्ट्वा निजासनात् अवरुह्य उपवेश्य, दिवौकसः आत्मानं यथा अर्हयाञ्चक्रे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्रह्मण्यदेवः The Lord who is worshipful of the brahmanas, तं him, दृष्ट्वा seeing, निजासनात् from His own throne, अवरुह्य descending, उपवेश्य seating him, दिवौकसः आत्मानं यथा as the demigods would worship Himself, अर्हयाञ्चक्रे worshiped.

Simple Meaning: Seeing the brahmana arrive, Krishna, who is worshipful of the brahmanas, immediately descended from His throne. He seated the guest respectfully and worshiped him in the same way the gods worship Him.

पाणिनाभिमृशन् पादावव्यग्रस्तमपृच्छत ॥10.52.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him, भुक्तवन्तम् having eaten, विश्रान्तम् rested, उपगम्य approaching, सताम् of the saintly devotees, गतिः the ultimate destination, पाणिना with His hands, अभिमृशन् massaging, पादौ feet, अव्यग्रः calmly, तम् him, अपृच्छत He inquired

Anvaya: सतां गतिः भुक्तवन्तं विश्रान्तं तम् उपगम्य पाणिना पादौ अभिमृशन् अव्यग्रः तम् अपृच्छत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सतां गतिः The ultimate destination of the saintly devotees, भुक्तवन्तं having eaten, विश्रान्तं rested, तम् him, उपगम्य approaching, पाणिना with His hands, पादौ feet, अभिमृशन् massaging, अव्यग्रः calmly, तम् him, अपृच्छत He inquired.

Simple Meaning: When the brahmana had eaten and fully rested, Krishna, the ultimate shelter of all saintly devotees, approached him. Gently massaging the brahmanas feet with His own hands, the Lord calmly began to question him.

कच्चिद् द्विजवरश्रेष्ठ धर्मस्ते वृद्धसम्मतः ।
वर्तते नातिकृच्छ्रेण संतुष्टमनसः सदा ॥10.52.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: कच्चित् whether, द्विज-वर-श्रेष्ठ O best of the excellent brahmanas, धर्मः the religious duties, ते your, वृद्ध-सम्मतः approved by the elders, वर्तते is executed, न at not, अति-कृच्छ्रेण with extreme difficulty, संतुष्ट-मनसः of one whose mind is satisfied, सदा always

Anvaya: द्विजवरश्रेष्ठ! ते वृद्धसम्मतः धर्मः सदा संतुष्टमनसः कच्चित् न अतिकृच्छ्रेण वर्तते?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: द्विजवरश्रेष्ठ O best of the excellent brahmanas, ते Your, वृद्धसम्मतः approved by the elders, धर्मः religious duties, सदा always, संतुष्टमनसः of one whose mind is satisfied, कच्चित् whether, न अतिकृच्छ्रेण without extreme difficulty, वर्तते is executed.

Simple Meaning: O best among the excellent brahmanas, are your religious practices, which are approved by the elders, being carried out without extreme difficulty? And is your mind always maintained in a state of peaceful satisfaction?

सन्तुष्टो यर्हि वर्तते ब्राह्मणो येन केनचित् ।
अहीयमानः स्वाद्धर्मात् स ह्यस्याखिलकामधुक् ॥10.52.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: सन्तुष्टः satisfied, यर्हि when, वर्तते remains, ब्राह्मणः a brahmana, येन केनचित् with whatever comes by itself, अहीयमानः not falling away, स्वात् धर्मात् from his own religious duty, सः that very satisfaction, हि indeed, अस्य for him, अखिल-काम-धुक् fulfills all desires

Anvaya: यर्हि ब्राह्मणः स्वात् धर्मात् अहीयमानः येन केनचित् सन्तुष्टः वर्तते, सः हि अस्य अखिलकामधुक् (भवति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यर्हि When, ब्राह्मणः a brahmana, स्वात् धर्मात् from his own religious duty, अहीयमानः not falling away, येन केनचित् with whatever comes by itself, सन्तुष्टः satisfied, वर्तते remains, सः that very satisfaction, हि indeed, अस्य for him, अखिलकामधुक् fulfills all desires, भवति becomes.

Simple Meaning: When a brahmana remains steadfast in his prescribed religious duties and stays satisfied with whatever comes his way by chance, that satisfaction itself becomes like a wish-fulfilling cow, granting him all perfection.

असन्तुष्टोऽसकृल्लोकानाप्नोत्यपि सुरेश्वरः ।
अकिञ्चनोऽपि संतुष्टः शेते सर्वाङ्गविज्वरः ॥10.52.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: असन्तुष्टः dissatisfied (person), असकृत् repeatedly, लोकान् worlds, आप्नोति achieves, अपि even though, सुर-ईश्वरः the ruler of the demigods, अकिञ्चनः possessing nothing, अपि even though, संतुष्टः satisfied, शेते sleeps peacefully, सर्व-अङ्ग-विज्वरः completely free from the fever of anxiety

Anvaya: असन्तुष्टः सुरेश्वरः अपि असकृत् लोकान् आप्नोति। अकिञ्चनः अपि संतुष्टः सर्वाङ्गविज्वरः शेते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: असन्तुष्टः A dissatisfied person, सुरेश्वरः अपि even though if he (becomes) the ruler of the demigods, असकृत् repeatedly, लोकान् worlds, आप्नोति achieves. अकिञ्चनः Possessing nothing, अपि even though, संतुष्टः satisfied, सर्वाङ्गविज्वरः completely free from the fever of anxiety, शेते sleeps peacefully.

Simple Meaning: A dissatisfied person, even if he is a ruler of demigods, will keep wandering one world from the other. On the other hand, even if a person possesses absolutely nothing, if he is satisfied, he sleeps peacefully, completely free from the burning fever of material anxiety.

विप्रान् स्वलाभसंतुष्टान् साधून् भूतसुहृत्तमान् ।
निरहङ्कारिणः शान्तान् नमस्ये शिरसासकृत् ॥10.52.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: विप्रान् the brahmanas, स्व-लाभ-संतुष्टान् satisfied with whatever is obtained by their own destiny, साधून् saintly, भूत-सुहृत्-तमान् the best well-wishers of all living entities, निरहङ्कारिणः free from false ego, शान्तान् peaceful, नमस्ये I bow down, शिरसा with my head, असकृत् repeatedly

Anvaya: स्वलाभसंतुष्टान् साधून् भूतसुहृत्तमान् निरहङ्कारिणः शान्तान् विप्रान् असकृत् शिरसा नमस्ये।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: स्वलाभसंतुष्टान् Satisfied with whatever is obtained by their own destiny, साधून् saintly, भूतसुहृत्तमान् the best well-wishers of all living entities, निरहङ्कारिणः free from false ego, शान्तान् peaceful, विप्रान् to the brahmanas, असकृत् repeatedly, शिरसा with my head, नमस्ये I bow down.

Simple Meaning: I repeatedly bow my head to those brahmanas who are always satisfied with whatever comes by destiny, who are saintly, free from false ego, peaceful, and the best well-wishers of all living entities.

कच्चित् वः कुशलं ब्रह्मन् राजतो यस्य हि प्रजाः ।
सुखं वसन्ति विषये पाल्यमानाः स मे प्रियः ॥10.52.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: कच्चित् whether, वः of you, कुशलम् welfare, ब्रह्मन् O brahmana, राजतः from the king, यस्य of whom, हि indeed, प्रजाः the subjects, सुखम् happily, वसन्ति reside, विषये in the kingdom, पाल्यमानाः being protected, सः that king, मे to Me, प्रियः dear

Anvaya: ब्रह्मन्! वः राजतः कुशलं कच्चित्? यस्य विषये प्रजाः पाल्यमानाः सुखं वसन्ति, सः हि मे प्रियः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्रह्मन् O brahmana, वः राजतः कुशलं कच्चित् Whether You all are well under your king? यस्य Of whom, विषये in the kingdom, प्रजाः the subjects, पाल्यमानाः (are) being protected, सुखं happily, वसन्ति reside, सः that king, हि indeed, मे to Me, प्रियः dear.

Simple Meaning: O brahmana, is everything well with you under the rule of your king? A king in whose realm the subjects live happily while being well protected is very dear to Me.

यतस्त्वमागतो दुर्गं निस्तीर्येह यदिच्छया ।
सर्वं नो ब्रूह्यगुह्यं चेत् किं कार्यं करवाम ते ॥10.52.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: यतः from where, त्वम् you, आगतः have come, दुर्गम् the difficult path, निस्तीर्य crossing over, इह here, यत्-इच्छया by whatever desire, सर्वम् all, नः to us, ब्रूहि please tell, अगुह्यम् not confidential, चेत् if, किम् what, कार्यम् work, करवाम we can do, ते for you

Anvaya: यदिच्छया त्वं यतः दुर्गं निस्तीर्य इह आगतः, अगुह्यं चेत् सर्वं नः ब्रूहि। ते किं कार्यं करवाम?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यदिच्छया By whatever desire, त्वं you, यतः from where, दुर्गं the difficult path, निस्तीर्य crossing over, इह आगतः have come here, अगुह्यं चेत् if it is not confidential, नः to us, ब्रूहि please tell. ते किं कार्यं करवाम For you, what work can we do?

Simple Meaning: Please tell us where you have come from, crossing over such a difficult path to reach here, and what desire has brought you. If it is not a secret, please share it with us. What work can we perform for you?

एवं सम्पृष्टसम्प्रश्नो ब्राह्मणः परमेष्ठिना ।
लीलागृहीतदेहेन तस्मै सर्वमवर्णयत् ॥10.52.36॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, सम्पृष्ट-सम्प्रश्नः having been fully questioned, ब्राह्मणः the brahmana, परमेष्ठिना by the Supreme Lord, लीला-गृहीत-देहेन who has assumed a body for His pastimes, तस्मै to Him, सर्वम् everything, अवर्णयत् described

Anvaya: लीलागृहीतदेहेन परमेष्ठिना एवं सम्पृष्टसम्प्रश्नः ब्राह्मणः तस्मै सर्वम् अवर्णयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: लीलागृहीतदेहेन By Him who has assumed a body for His pastimes, परमेष्ठिना by the Supreme Lord, एवं thus, सम्पृष्टसम्प्रश्नः having been fully questioned, ब्राह्मणः the brahmana, तस्मै to Him, सर्वम् everything, अवर्णयत् described.

Simple Meaning: Having been fully questioned in this manner by the Supreme Lord, who had assumed a humanlike body to perform His pastimes, the brahmana described everything to Him.

रुक्मिणी उवाच
श्रुत्वा गुणान् भुवनसुन्दर शृण्वतां ते निर्विश्य कर्णविवरैर्हरतोऽङ्गतापम् ।
रूपं दृशां दृशिमतामखिलार्थलाभं त्वय्यच्युताविशति चित्तमपत्रपं मे ॥10.52.37॥

Word-by-word meaning: रुक्मिणी उवाच Rukmini said, श्रुत्वा hearing, गुणान् the qualities, भुवन-सुन्दर O beauty of the worlds, शृण्वताम् of those who hear, ते Your, निर्विश्य entering, कर्ण-विवरैः through the openings of the ears, हरतः removing, अङ्ग-तापम् the distress of the body, रूपम् the beauty, दृशाम् of the eyes, दृशिमताम् of those who have eyes, अखिल-अर्थ-लाभम् the attainment of all desires, त्वयि in You, अच्युत O infallible Lord, अविशति enters, चित्तम् the mind, अपत्रपम् shameless, मे my

Anvaya: रुक्मिणी उवाच —- भुवनसुन्दर! कर्णविवरैः निर्विश्य शृण्वताम् अङ्गतापं हरतः ते गुणान् श्रुत्वा, दृशिमतां दृशाम् अखिलार्थलाभं रूपं (च श्रुत्वा), अच्युत! मे चित्तम् अपत्रपं (सत्) त्वयि अविशति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रुक्मिणी उवाच Rukmini said, भुवनसुन्दर O beauty of the worlds, कर्णविवरैः through the openings of the ears, निर्विश्य entering, शृण्वताम् of those who hear, अङ्गतापं the distress of the body, हरतः removing, ते Your, गुणान् qualities, श्रुत्वा hearing, दृशिमतां of those who have eyes, दृशाम् for the eyes, अखिलार्थलाभं the attainment of all desires, रूपं beauty, (च श्रुत्वा) and hearing about, अच्युत O infallible Lord, मे my, चित्तम् mind, अपत्रपं shameless, (सत्) becoming, त्वयि in You, अविशति enters.

Simple Meaning: Rukmini said: O beauty of the worlds, having heard of Your transcendental qualities, which enter through the ears of those who listen and remove all bodily distress, and having heard of Your beauty, which fulfills every desire of those who possess eyes, my mind has shamelessly abandoned all modesty and entered into You, O infallible Lord.

का त्वा मुकुन्द महती कुलशीलरूपविद्यावयोद्रविणधामभिरात्मतुल्यम् ।
धीरा पतिं कुलवती न वृणीत कन्या काले नृसिंह नरलोकमनोऽभिरामम् ॥10.52.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: का who, त्वा You, मुकुन्द O Mukunda, महती noble, कुल-शील-रूप-विद्या-वयः-द्रविण-धामभिः by lineage, character, beauty, education, age, wealth and influence, आत्म-तुल्यम् equal to Himself, धीरा intelligent, पतिम् as husband, कुल-वती of good family, न not, वृणीत would choose, कन्या a maiden, काले at the proper time, नृ-सिंह O lion among men, नर-लोक-मनः-अभिरामम् the delight of the minds of mankind

Anvaya: मुकुन्द! नृसिंह! काले कुलशीलरूपविद्यावयोद्रविणधामभिः आत्मतुल्यं नरलोकमनोऽभिरामं त्वा महती धीरा कुलवती का कन्या पतिं न वृणीत?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मुकुन्द O Mukunda, नृसिंह O lion among men, काले at the proper time, कुलशीलरूपविद्यावयोद्रविणधामभिः by lineage, character, beauty, education, age, wealth and influence, आत्मतुल्यं equal to Himself, नरलोकमनोऽभिरामं the delight of the minds of mankind, त्वा You, महती धीरा कुलवती noble, intelligent, of good family, का कन्या what maiden, पतिं as husband, न वृणीत would not choose.

Simple Meaning: O Mukunda, O lion among men, what intelligent maiden of noble birth and good family would not choose You as her husband when the time is right? You are unmatched in lineage, character, beauty, education, youth, wealth, and influence, and You are the ultimate delight of all human minds.

तन्मे भवान् खलु वृतः पतिरङ्ग जायामात्मार्पितश्च भवतोऽत्र विभो विधेहि ।
मा वीरभागमभिमर्शतु चैद्य आराद् गोमायुवन्मृगपतेर्बलिमम्बुजाक्ष ॥10.52.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् therefore, मे by me, भवान् You, खलु indeed, वृतः chosen, पतिः husband, अङ्ग O dear one, जायाम् wife, आत्म-अर्पितः offered my soul, च and, भवतः of You, अत्र here, विभो O all-powerful Lord, विधेहि please make, मा do not, वीर-भागम् the share of a hero, अभिमर्शतु touch, चैद्यः Shishupala, आरात् from a distance, गोमायु-वत् like a jackal, मृग-पतेः of the king of animals, बलिम् the prey, अम्बुज-अक्ष O lotus-eyed one

Anvaya: अङ्ग! विभो! तत् मे भवान् खलु पतिः वृतः। भवतः अत्र आत्मार्पितः च, (मां) जायां विधेहि। अम्बुजाक्ष! वीरभागं चैद्यः आरात् मा अभिमर्शतु, मृगपतेः बलिं गोमायुवत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अङ्ग O dear one, विभो O all-powerful Lord, तत् therefore, मे by me, भवान् You, खलु indeed, पतिः husband, वृतः chosen.  भवतः अत्र To You, here, आत्मार्पितः offered my soul, च and, मां जायां me as Your wife, विधेहि please make. अम्बुजाक्ष O lotus-eyed one, वीरभागं the share of a hero, चैद्यः Shishupala, आरात् from a distance, मा अभिमर्शतु let him not touch, मृगपतेः बलिं the prey of the king of animals, गोमायुवत् like a jackal.

Simple Meaning: Therefore, O dear and all-powerful Lord, I have chosen You as my husband and have surrendered my soul to You. Please accept me as Your wife. O lotus-eyed one, let not Shishupala touch the share of a hero from afar, just as a jackal should never touch the prey belonging to the lion.

पूर्तेष्टदत्तनियमव्रतदेवविप्रगुर्वर्चनादिभिरलं भगवान् परेशः ।
आराधितो यदि गदाग्रज एत्य पाणिं गृह्णातु मे न दमघोषसुतादयोऽन्ये ॥10.52.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: पूर्त-इष्ट-दत्त-नियम-व्रत-देव-विप्र-गुरु-अर्चन-आदिभिः by public works, sacrificial offerings, charity, rituals, vows, and worship of demigods, brahmanas, and spiritual masters, अलम् sufficiently, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, पर-ईशः the supreme controller, आराधितः worshipped, यदि if, गद-अग्रजः elder brother of Gada - Krishna, एत्य coming, पाणिम् hand, गृह्णातु let Him take, मे my, न not, दमघोष-सुत-आदयः the son of Damaghosha (Shishupala) and others, अन्ये else

Anvaya: यदि पूर्तेष्टदत्तनियमव्रतदेवविप्रगुर्वर्चनादिभिः परम् ईशः भगवान् अलम् आराधितः, (तर्हि) गदाग्रजः एत्य मे पाणिं गृह्णातु, दमघोषसुतादयः अन्ये न।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यदि If, पूर्तेष्टदत्तनियमव्रतदेवविप्रगुर्वर्चनादिभिः by public works, sacrificial offerings, charity, rituals, vows, and worship of demigods, brahmanas, and spiritual masters, परम् ईशः the supreme controller, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, अलम् sufficiently, आराधितः has been worshipped, (तर्हि) then, गदाग्रजः Krishna, एत्य coming, मे my, पाणिं hand, गृह्णातु let Him take, दमघोषसुतादयः the son of Damaghosha and others, अन्ये else, न not.

Simple Meaning: If I have sufficiently worshipped the Supreme Lord, the supreme controller, through pious works, sacrifices, charity, rituals, vows, and the worship of demigods, brahmanas, and spiritual masters, then let Krishna come and take my hand. Let no one else, such as Shishupala the son of Damaghosha and others, ever touch me.

श्वोभाविनि त्वमजितोद्वहने विदर्भान् गुप्तः समेत्य पृतनापतिभिः परीतः ।
निर्मथ्य चैद्यमगधेन्द्रबलं प्रसह्य मां राक्षसेन विधिनोद्वह वीर्यशुल्काम् ॥10.52.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्वः भाविनि happening tomorrow, त्वम् You, अजित O unconquerable one, उद्वहने for the marriage, विदर्भान् to Vidarbha, गुप्तः concealed, समेत्य arriving, पृतना-पतिभिः with military commanders, परीतः surrounded, निर्मथ्य crushing, चैद्य-मगधेन्द्र-बलम् the army of Shishupala and Jarasandha, प्रसह्य by force, माम् me, राक्षसेन विधिना by the Rakshasa ritual, उद्वह marry, वीर्य-शुल्काम् one whose bride price is prowess

Anvaya: अजित! श्वः भाविनि उद्वहने त्वं पृतनापतिभिः परीतः गुप्तः विदर्भान् समेत्य चैद्यमगधेन्द्रबलं निर्मथ्य प्रसह्य वीर्यशुल्कां मां राक्षसेन विधिना उद्वह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अजित O unconquerable one, श्वः भाविनि happening tomorrow, उद्वहने for the marriage, त्वं You, पृतनापतिभिः with military commanders, परीतः surrounded, गुप्तः concealed, विदर्भान् to Vidarbha, समेत्य arriving, चैद्यमगधेन्द्रबलं the army of Shishupala and Jarasandha, निर्मथ्य crushing, प्रसह्य by force, वीर्यशुल्कां price of valor, मां me, राक्षसेन विधिना by the Rakshasa ritual, उद्वह marry.

Simple Meaning: O unconquerable one, since my marriage is scheduled for tomorrow, You should arrive secretly in Vidarbha surrounded by Your military commanders. Then, after crushing the combined forces of Shishupala and Jarashandha, claim me by force through the Rakshasa ritual of marriage, taking me as Your bride won by Your own prowess.

अन्तःपुरान्तरचरीमनिहत्य बन्धूंस्त्वामुद्वहे कथमिति प्रवदाम्युपायम् ।
पूर्वेद्युरस्ति महती कुलदेवियात्रा यस्यां बहिर्नववधूर्गिंरिजामुपेयात् ॥10.52.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्तःपुर-अन्तर-चरीम् who moves within the inner chambers of the palace, अनिहत्य without killing, बन्धून् relatives, त्वाम् You, उद्वहे I may marry, कथम् how, इति thus, प्रवदामि I am suggesting, उपायम् a means, पूर्वेद्युः on the previous day, अस्ति there is, महती a grand, कुल-देवि-यात्रा procession for the family deity, यस्याम् in which, बहिः outside, नव-वधूः the new bride, गिरिजाम् Goddess Girija, उपेयात् should go to visit

Anvaya: अन्तःपुरान्तरचरीं बन्धून् अनिहत्य त्वां कथम् उद्वहे – इति (चेत्) उपायं प्रवदामि। पूर्वेद्युः महती कुलदेवियात्रा अस्ति यस्यां नववधूः बहिः गिरिजाम् उपेयात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अन्तःपुरान्तरचरीं Who moves within the inner chambers of the palace, बन्धून् relatives, अनिहत्य without killing, त्वां कथम् उद्वहे how can I marry you, इति thus, (चेत्) if You wonder, उपायं a means, प्रवदामि I am suggesting. पूर्वेद्युः On the previous day, महती a grand, कुलदेवियात्रा procession for the family deity, अस्ति there is, यस्यां in which, नववधूः the new bride, बहिः outside, गिरिजाम् Goddess Girija, उपेयात् should go to visit.

Simple Meaning: If You are wondering how You can marry me without killing my relatives since I stay inside the inner palace chambers, I am suggesting a way. On the day before the wedding, there is a grand procession for our family deity, during which the new bride must go outside the palace to visit Goddess Girija.

यस्याङ्घ्रिपङ्कजरजः स्नपनं महन्तो वाञ्छन्त्युमापतिरिवात्मतमोऽपहत्यै ।
यर्ह्यम्बुजाक्ष न लभेय भवत्प्रसादं जह्यामसून् व्रतकृशाञ्छतजन्मभिः स्यात् ॥10.52.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: यस्य whose, अङ्घ्री-पङ्कज-रजः of the dust of the lotus feet, स्नपनम् the bathing, महन्तः great souls, वाञ्छन्ति desire, उमा-पतिः Lord Shiva, इव like, आत्म-तमः the ignorance of the soul, अपहत्यै for destroying, यर्हि if, अम्बुज-अक्ष O lotus-eyed one, न not, लभेय I should obtain, भवत्-प्रसादम् Your mercy, जह्याम् I shall give up, असून् life, व्रत-कृशान् emaciated by vows, शत-जन्मभिः after hundreds of births, स्यात् it may be

Anvaya: अम्बुजाक्ष! उमापतिः इव महन्तः आत्मतमः अपहत्यै यस्य अङ्घ्रिपङ्कजरजः स्नपनं वाञ्छन्ति, यर्हि भवत्प्रसादं न लभेय (तर्हि) व्रतकृशान् असून् जह्याम्, शतजन्मभिः (भवत्प्रसादं) स्यात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अम्बुजाक्ष O lotus-eyed one, उमापतिः Lord Shiva, इव like, महन्तः great souls, आत्मतमः अपहत्यै for destroying the ignorance of the soul, यस्य अङ्घ्रिपङ्कजरजः स्नपनं bathing in the dust of whose lotus feet, वाञ्छन्ति desire, यर्हि if, भवत्प्रसादं Your mercy, न लभेय I should not obtain, (तर्हि) then, व्रतकृशान् emaciated by vows, असून् life, जह्याम् I shall give up, शतजन्मभिः after hundreds of births, (भवत्प्रसादः) Your mercy, स्यात् it may be attained.

Simple Meaning: O lotus-eyed one, great souls like Lord Shiva desire to bathe in the dust of Your lotus feet to destroy the darkness of ignorance within themselves. If I fail to obtain Your mercy, I will give up this life, which has been emaciated by rigid vows. Perhaps then, after hundreds of births, I may finally attain Your grace.

ब्राह्मण उवाच
इत्येते गुह्यसन्देशा यदुदेव मयाऽऽहृताः ।
विमृश्य कर्तुं यच्चात्र क्रियतां तदनन्तरम् ॥10.52.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्राह्मणः उवाच the brahmana said, इति thus, एते these, गुह्य-सन्देशाः confidential messages, यदु-देव O Lord of the Yadus, मया by me, आहृताः brought, विमृश्य considering, कर्तुम् to do, यत् what, च and, अत्र here, क्रियताम् please do, तत् that, अनन्तरम् immediately after

Anvaya: ब्राह्मणः उवाच — यदुदेव! मया इति एते गुह्यसन्देशाः आहृताः। अत्र यत् कर्तुं (योग्यम् अस्ति) विमृश्य, तत् अनन्तरं क्रियताम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्राह्मणः उवाच The brahmana said, यदुदेव O Lord of the Yadus, मया by me, इति thus, एते these, गुह्यसन्देशाः confidential messages, आहृताः have been brought. अत्र Here, यत् what, कर्तुं to do, (योग्यम् अस्ति) is appropriate, विमृश्य considering, तत् that, अनन्तरं immediately after, क्रियताम् please do.

Simple Meaning: The brahmana said, O Lord of the Yadus, these are the confidential messages I have brought to You. Considering the situation, please deliberate on what needs to be done, and then carry it out immediately.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “द्वारकाप्रवेशः बलरामविवाहः रुक्मिणीसन्देशः च​ नाम​” द्विपञ्चाशत्तमः अध्यायः ॥52॥

Thus ends the fifty-second discourse named “Krishna's entry to Dwaraka; Balarama's marriage; Rukmini sends a Brahmana with a message to Krishna”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
