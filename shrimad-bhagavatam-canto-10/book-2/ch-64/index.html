श्री शुक उवाच
एकदोपवनं राजन् जग्मुर्यदुकुमारकाः ।
विहर्तुं साम्बप्रद्युम्नचारुभानुगदादयः ॥10.64.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, एकदा once, उपवनम् to a garden, राजन् O King Parikshit, जग्मुः went, यदु-कुमारकाः the young princes of the Yadu dynasty, विहर्तुम् to play, साम्ब-प्रद्युम्न-चारु-भानु-गद-आदयः Samba, Pradyumna, Charu, Bhanu, Gada and others

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— राजन्! एकदा साम्बप्रद्युम्नचारुभानुगदादयः यदुकुमारकाः विहर्तुम् उपवनं जग्मुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, हे राजन् O King, एकदा once, साम्बप्रद्युम्नचारुभानुगदादयः यदुकुमारकाः the Yadu princes - Samba, Pradyumna, Charu, Bhanu, Gada and others, विहर्तुम् उपवनं जग्मुः went to a garden to play.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King Parikshit, once the young princes of the Yadu dynasty—including Samba, Pradyumna, Charu, Bhanu, Gada, and others—went to a garden outside the city to play and sporting about.

क्रीडित्वा सुचिरं तत्र विचिन्वन्तः पिपासिताः ।
जलं निरुदके कूपे ददृशुः सत्त्वमद्भुतम् ॥10.64.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: क्रीडित्वा having played, सुचिरम् for a long time, तत्र there, विचिन्वन्तः searching, पिपासिताः feeling thirsty, जलम् water, निरुदके dry, कूपे in a well, ददृशुः they saw, सत्त्वम् a living creature, अद्भुतम् extraordinary/amazing

Anvaya: तत्र सुचिरं क्रीडित्वा पिपासिताः (ते) जलं विचिन्वन्तः निरुदके कूपे अद्भुतं सत्त्वं ददृशुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत्र सुचिरं क्रीडित्वा Having played there for a long time, पिपासिताः feeling thirsty, जलं विचिन्वन्तः searching for water, निरुदके कूपे in a dry well, अद्भुतं सत्त्वं ददृशुः they saw an astonishing creature.

Simple Meaning: After sporting there for a long time, the princes became thirsty and began searching for water. Looking inside a dry well, they discovered a truly extraordinary and wondrous living creature.

कृकलासं गिरिनिभं वीक्ष्य विस्मितमानसाः ।
तस्य चोद्धरणे यत्नं चक्रुस्ते कृपयान्विताः ॥10.64.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृकलासम् a chameleon, गिरि-निभम् huge like a mountain, वीक्ष्य seeing, विस्मित-मानसाः with minds filled with wonder, तस्य its, च and, उद्धरणे in lifting out, यत्नम् effort, चक्रुः made, ते they, कृपा-अन्विताः moved by compassion

Anvaya: गिरिनिभं कृकलासं वीक्ष्य विस्मितमानसाः ते कृपयान्विताः च तस्य उद्धरणे यत्नं चक्रुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गिरिनिभं कृकलासं वीक्ष्य Seeing a chameleon as huge as a mountain, विस्मितमानसाः with amazed minds, ते कृपयान्विताः च and moved by compassion, तस्य उद्धरणे in rescuing it, यत्नं चक्रुः made an effort.

Simple Meaning: Seeing a giant chameleon that was as immense as a mountain, the Yadu princes were filled with amazement. Moved by deep compassion for the trapped creature, they made an effort to pull it out of the well.

चर्मजैस्तान्तवैः पाशैर्बद्ध्वा पतितमर्भकाः ।
नाशक्नुवन् समुद्धर्तुं कृष्णायाचख्युरुत्सुकाः ॥10.64.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: चर्मजैः made of leather, तान्तवैः made of woven thread or cloth, पाशैः with ropes, बद्ध्वा having tied, पतितम् the fallen one, अर्भकाः the young boys, न not, अशक्नुवन् were able, समुद्धर्तुम् to lift up completely, कृष्णाय unto Krishna, आचक्ष्युः they reported, उत्सुकाः with eagerness

Anvaya: अर्भकाः चर्मजैः तान्तवैः पाशैः पतितं बद्ध्वा समुद्धर्तुं न अशक्नुवन्, (ततः) उत्सुकाः कृष्णाय आचक्ष्युः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अर्भकाः The young boys, चर्मजैः तान्तवैः पाशैः with leather straps and woven ropes, पतितं बद्ध्वा having tied the fallen creature, समुद्धर्तुं न अशक्नुवन् were unable to pull it out. ततः उत्सुकाः Then eagerly, कृष्णाय आचक्ष्युः reported the matter unto Krishna.

Simple Meaning: The young princes tied straps of leather and woven ropes around the trapped creature, but try as they might, they could not pull it out of the well. Surprised and excited, they eagerly went to Krishna and reported the whole incident to Him.

तत्रागत्यारविन्दाक्षो भगवान् विश्वभावनः ।
वीक्ष्योज्जहार वामेन तं करेण स लीलया ॥10.64.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there, आगत्य having arrived, अरविन्द-अक्षः lotus-eyed, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, विश्व-भावनः the creator/sustainer of the universe, वीक्ष्य looking at it, उज्जहार lifted up, वामेन with His left, तम् him (the chameleon), करेण hand, सः He, लीलया effortlessly/playfully

Anvaya: तत्र आगत्य अरविन्दाक्षः विश्वभावनः भगवान् (कृष्णः) तं वीक्ष्य सः लीलया वामेन करेण उज्जहार।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत्र आगत्य Having arrived there, अरविन्दाक्षः the lotus-eyed, विश्वभावनः भगवान् श्रीकृष्णः Supreme Lord Krishna, the maintainer of the universe, तं वीक्ष्य seeing him, सः लीलया He effortlessly, वामेन करेण with His left hand, उज्जहार lifted him up.

Simple Meaning: Arriving at the well, the lotus-eyed Krishna—the creator and maintainer of the entire universe—looked upon the trapped creature. Then, with supreme ease, He playfully extended His left hand and lifted the giant lizard right out of the pit.

स उत्तमश्लोककराभिमृष्टो विहाय सद्यः कृकलासरूपम् ।
सन्तप्तचामीकरचारुवर्णः स्वर्ग्यद्भुतालङ्करणाम्बरस्रक् ॥10.64.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he, उत्तमश्लोक-कर-अभिमृष्टः touched by the hand of the Supreme Lord (whose glories are praised in sublime poetry), विहाय leaving behind, सद्यः instantly, कृकलास-रूपम् the body/form of a chameleon, सन्तप्त-चामीकर-चारु-वर्णः possessing a beautiful complexion like molten gold, स्वर्ग्य-अद्भुत-अलङ्करण-अम्बर-स्रक् wearing celestial, wondrous ornaments, garments, and flower garlands

Anvaya: उत्तमश्लोककराभिमृष्टः सः कृकलासरूपं सद्यः विहाय सन्तप्तचामीकरचारुवर्णः स्वर्ग्यद्भुतालङ्करणाम्बरस्रक् (अभवत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: उत्तमश्लोककराभिमृष्टः Touched by the hand of the Supreme Lord Krishna, सः he, कृकलासरूपं the form of a chameleon, सद्यः विहाय instantly discarding, सन्तप्तचामीकरचारुवर्णः (became a divine personality) possessing a brilliant hue like burnished gold, स्वर्ग्यद्भुतालङ्करणाम्बरस्रक् adorned with heavenly and marvellous ornaments, robes, and garlands.

Simple Meaning: As soon as he was touched by the divine hand of Lord Krishna, the creature instantly shed his lizard form. He was immediately transformed into a glorious, heavenly being whose body shone with the radiant brilliance of molten gold, beautifully adorned with exquisite celestial robes, marvelous ornaments, and divine flower garlands.

पप्रच्छविद्वानपि तन्निदानं जनेषु विख्यायितुं मुकुन्दः।
कस्त्वं महाभागवरेण्यरूपो देवोत्तमं त्वां गणयामि नूनम् ॥10.64.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: पप्रच्छ asked, विद्वान् though knowing everything, अपि even, तत्-निदानम् the cause/reason for that, जनेषु among the people, विख्यायितुम् to make known, मुकुन्दः Mukunda (Krishna), कः who, त्वम् you, महा-भाग O fortunate one, वरेण्य-रूपः of exquisite/preeminent form, देव-उत्तमम् the best of demigods, त्वाम् you, गणयामि I consider, नूनम् surely

Anvaya: विद्वान् अपि मुकुन्दः जनेषु तत्-निदानं विख्यायितुं पप्रच्छ— महाभाग! वरेण्यरूपः त्वं कः (असि)? त्वां नूनं देवोत्तमं गणयामि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विद्वान् अपि Even though fully all-knowing, मुकुन्दः Mukunda, जनेषु तत्-निदानं विख्यायितुं to make the cause of this known among the people, पप्रच्छ asked— महाभाग O most fortunate one, वरेण्यरूपः possessing an exquisite form, त्वं कः (असि) who are you, त्वां नूनं देवोत्तमं गणयामि I surely consider you to be an exalted demigod.

Simple Meaning: Although Mukunda is fully omniscient and knew everything, He asked the being about his background in order to reveal the cause of his condition to the people present. He said: "O most fortunate one, possessing such a splendid form, who are you? I certainly consider you to be one of the most exalted demigods."

दशामिमां वा कतमेन कर्मणा सम्प्रापितोऽस्यतदर्हः सुभद्र ।
आत्मानमाख्याहि विवित्सतां नो यन्मन्यसे नः क्षममत्र वक्तुम् ॥10.64.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: दशाम् condition/state, इमाम् this, वा or, कतमेन by what, कर्मणा action/deed, सम्प्रापितः brought to/attained, असि you are, अतत्-अर्हः not deserving such a fate, सुभद्र O auspicious one, आत्मानम् yourself, आख्याहि tell/explain, विवित्सताम् desirous of knowing, नः to us, यत् which, मन्यसे you think, नः to us, क्षमम् fit/appropriate, अत्र here, वक्तुम् to tell

Anvaya: सुभद्र! अतदर्हः (त्वं) कतमेन कर्मणा वा इमां दशां सम्प्रापितः असि? नः विवित्सताम् आत्मानम् आख्याहि, यत् अत्र नः वक्तुं क्षमं मन्यसे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सुभद्र O auspicious one! अतदर्हः (त्वं) you who do not deserve this, कतमेन कर्मणा वा or by what deed, इमां दशां सम्प्रापितः असि have been brought to this miserable condition? नः विवित्सताम् To us who are curious to know, आत्मानम् आख्याहि describe yourself, यत् which, अत्र here, नः वक्तुं to speak to us, क्षमं मन्यसे you consider appropriate.

Simple Meaning: O auspicious one, you clearly did not deserve such a terrible fate. By what past action were you brought to this wretched condition? We are very eager to know your story, so please tell us about yourself—that is, if you feel it is appropriate to share it with us.

श्री शुक उवाच
इति स्म राजा सम्पृष्टः कृष्णेनानन्तमूर्तिना ।
माधवं प्रणिपत्याह किरीटेनार्कवर्चसा ॥10.64.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति thus, स्म indeed, राजा King Nriga, सम्पृष्टः questioned, कृष्णेन by Krishna, अनन्त-मूर्तिना whose forms/manifestations are unlimited, माधवम् unto Madhava (Krishna), प्रणिपत्य bowing down, आह spoke, किरीटेन with his crown, अर्क-वर्चसा brilliant like the sun

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच—इति अनन्तमूर्तिना कृष्णेन सम्पृष्टः राजा अर्कवर्चसा किरीटेन माधवं प्रणिपत्य आह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति Thus, अनन्तमूर्तिना कृष्णेन सम्पृष्टः questioned by Lord Krishna of infinite forms, राजा King Nriga, अर्कवर्चसा किरीटेन with his crown shining like the sun, माधवं प्रणिपत्य bowing down to Madhava, आह spoke.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O King Parikshit, thus questioned by Krishna, whose divine forms and expansions are limitless, King Nriga bowed his head low before Madhava—his brilliant crown shining like the sun—and spoke as follows.

नृग उवाच
नृगो नाम नरेन्द्रोऽहमिक्ष्वाकुतनयः प्रभो ।
दानिष्वाख्यायमानेषु यदि ते कर्णमस्पृशम् ॥10.64.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: नृगः Nriga, नाम named, नरेन्द्रः ruler of men/king, अहम् I, इक्ष्वाकु-तनयः the son of Ikshvaku, प्रभो O Lord, दानिषु among generous donors, आख्यायमानेषु while listing/mentioning, यदि if, ते Your, कर्णम् ears, अस्पृशम् I may have touched (i.e., if my name has reached Your ears)

Anvaya: नृगः उवाच— प्रभो! अहम् इक्ष्वाकुतनयः नृगः नाम नरेन्द्रः (अस्मि)। दानिषु आख्यायमानेषु (मम नाम) यदि ते कर्णम् अस्पृशम् (स्यात्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृगः उवाच King Nriga said, हे प्रभो O Lord!, अहम् I, इक्ष्वाकुतनयः son of King Ikshvaku, नृगः नाम नरेन्द्रः (अस्मि) a king named Nriga (am). दानिषु आख्यायमानेषु When generous givers are named, मम नाम my name, यदि if, ते कर्णम् अस्पृशम् has touched Your ears.

Simple Meaning: King Nriga said: O Supreme Lord, I am a king named Nriga, the son of King Ikshvaku. Perhaps my name has previously reached Your ears when lists of charitable and generous donors were mentioned in Your presence.

किं नु तेऽविदितं नाथ सर्वभूतात्मसाक्षिणः।
कालेनाव्याहतदृशो वक्ष्येऽथापि तवाज्ञया ॥10.64.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: किम् what, नु indeed, ते to You, अविदितम् unknown, नाथ O Lord, सर्व-भूत-आत्म-साक्षिणः the witness of the minds and souls of all living beings, कालेन by time, अव्याहत-दृशः whose vision/knowledge is unhindered or unobstructed, वक्ष्ये I shall speak, अथापि nevertheless, तव by Your, आज्ञया order/command

Anvaya: नाथ! सर्वभूतात्मसाक्षिणः कालेन अव्याहतदृशः ते किं नु अविदितम्? अथापि तव आज्ञया वक्ष्ये।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नाथ O Lord! सर्वभूतात्मसाक्षिणः You who are the witness of the innermost self of all beings, कालेन अव्याहतदृशः whose vision is never obstructed by time, ते किं नु अविदितम् what indeed is unknown to You? अथापि Nevertheless, तव आज्ञया by Your command, वक्ष्ये I shall speak.

Simple Meaning: O Lord! You are the inner witness dwelling within the hearts of all living beings, and Your vision is never dimmed or obstructed by past, present, or future time. What could possibly be unknown to You? Nevertheless, in obedience to Your command, I shall share my story.

यावत्यः सिकता भूमेर्यावत्यो दिवि तारकाः ।
यावत्यो वर्षधाराश्च तावतीरददां स्म गाः ॥10.64.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: यावत्यः as many as, सिकताः grains of sand, भूमेः of the earth, यावत्यः as many as, दिवि in the sky, तारकाः stars, यावत्यः as many as, वर्ष-धाराः drops of rain, च and, तावतीः so many, अददाम् I gave away in charity, स्म indeed, गाः cows

Anvaya: भूमेः यावत्यः सिकताः, दिवि यावत्यः तारकाः, यावत्यः वर्षधाराः च (सन्ति), तावतीः गाः (अहम्) अददां स्म।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भूमेः यावत्यः सिकताः As many grains of sand as there are on the earth, दिवि यावत्यः तारकाः as many stars as there are in the sky, यावत्यः वर्षधाराः च and as many drops in a shower of rain, तावतीः गाः so many cows, अहम् अददां स्म I gave away in charity.

Simple Meaning: King Nriga continued: O Lord, I gave in charity a number of cows equal to the total count of sand grains on the earth, stars in the night sky, and raindrops falling in a torrential downpour.

न्यायार्जिता रूप्यखुराः सवत्सा दुकूलमालाभरणा ददावहम् ॥10.64.13॥

Simple Meaning: Yamaraja said: O King, do you wish to suffer the results of your sin first, or enjoy the fruits of your good karma? Truly, I see no end to the pious merits you have earned through your boundless charity, nor to the resplendent heavenly worlds awaiting you.

राजानो राजकुल्याश्च तावतोऽब्दान्निरङ्कुशाः ।
कुम्भीपाकेषु पच्यन्ते ब्रह्मदायापहारिणः ॥10.64.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजानः kings, राज-कुल्याः members of the royal family/royal descendants, च and, तावतः for that many, अब्दान् years, निरङ्कुशाः unrestrained/arrogant, कुम्भीपाकेषु in Kumbhipaka (oil cauldrons), पच्यन्ते are boiled/cooked, ब्रह्म-दाय-अपहारिणः those who steal the property given to brahmanas

Anvaya: ब्रह्मदायापहारिणः निरङ्कुशाः राजानः राजकुल्याः च तावतः अब्दान् कुम्भीपाकेषु पच्यन्ते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ब्रह्मदायापहारिणः Those who steal or usurp property given to brahmanas, निरङ्कुशाः unrestrained, राजानः kings, राजकुल्याः च and royal family members, तावतः अब्दान् for that many years (as there are dust particles soaked by tears), कुम्भीपाकेषु in Kumbhipaka (oil cauldrons), पच्यन्ते are cooked.

Simple Meaning:  “For as many dust particles as are soaked by the tears of weeping brahmanas whose livelihood has been stolen, for that precise number of years will those arrogant, unchecked kings and their royal relatives be boiled in the terrifying hellish cauldrons of Kumbhipaka.

स्वदत्तं परदत्तं वा ब्रह्मवृत्तिं हरेच्चयः ।
षष्टिवर्षसहस्राणि विष्ठायां जायते कृमिः ॥10.64.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्व-दत्तम् given by oneself, पर-दत्तम् given by others, वा or, ब्रह्म-वृत्तिम् livelihood or property of a brahmana, हरेत् steals/usurps, यः who, षष्टि-वर्ष-सहस्राणि for sixty thousand years, विष्ठायाम् in stool/feces, जायते is born, कृमिः as a worm

Anvaya: यः स्वदत्तं परदत्तं वा ब्रह्मवृत्तिं हरेत्, (सः) षष्टिवर्षसहस्राणि विष्ठायां कृमिः जायते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः Whoever, स्वदत्तं property given to a brahmana by himself, परदत्तं वा or property given by others, ब्रह्मवृत्तिं the maintenance or land of a brahmana, हरेत् steals or takes away, सः that person, षष्टिवर्षसहस्राणि for sixty thousand years, विष्ठायां in stool, कृमिः as a worm, जायते is born.

Simple Meaning: “Anyone who steals or takes away a brahmana's property—whether it was donated by himself or by someone else—will be reborn as a worm in stool for sixty thousand years.

न मे ब्रह्मधनं भूयाद् यद् गृद्ध्वाल्पायुषो नराः ।
पराजिताश्च्युता राज्याद् भवन्त्युद्वेजिनोऽहयः ॥10.64.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, मे my, ब्रह्म-धनम् property of a brahmana, भूयात् may it be, यत् which, गृद्ध्वा by greedily coveting/taking, अल्प-आयुषः short-lived, नराः men, पराजिताः defeated, च्युताः fallen, राज्यात् from their kingdom, भवन्ति become, उद्वेजिनः sources of terror/tormenting, अहयः like poisonous snakes

Anvaya: यत् गृद्ध्वा नराः अल्पायुषः पराजिताः राज्यात् च्युताः अहयः (इव) उद्वेजिनः भवन्ति, (तत्) ब्रह्मधनं मे न भूयात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यत् गृद्ध्वा By greedily taking which, नराः men, अल्पायुषः short-lived, पराजिताः defeated, राज्यात् च्युताः fallen from their royal positions, अहयः (इव) like venomous serpents, उद्वेजिनः anxiety-ridden and causing terror to others, भवन्ति become, (तत्) ब्रह्मधनं that property of a brahmana, मे for me, न भूयात् may it never be desired.

Simple Meaning: ”May I never desire to usurp the property of a brahmana! By coveting and seizing a brahmana's wealth, men become short-lived, suffer defeat, fall from their kingdoms, and live full of anxiety, bringing distress to others just like venomous snakes.

विप्रं कृतागसमपि नैव द्रुह्यत मामकाः ।
घ्नन्तं बहु शपन्तं वा नमस्कुरुत नित्यशः ॥10.64.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: विप्रम् a brahmana, कृत-आगसम् even one who has committed an offense/wrong, अपि even, न not, एव indeed, द्रुह्यत harm or show malice towards, मामकाः O My followers/My people, घ्नन्तम् even if he strikes, बहु sharply, शपन्तम् curses, वा or, नमस्कुरुत bow down to him, नित्यशः always/at all times

Anvaya: मामकाः! कृतागसम् अपि विप्रं नैव द्रुह्यत। (सः) घ्नन्तं बहु शपन्तं वा (अपि) नित्यशः नमस्कुरुत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मामकाः O My relatives and followers, कृतागसम् अपि even if he has committed an offense, विप्रं a brahmana, नैव द्रुह्यत do not ever harm or harbor ill will. सः Even if he, घ्नन्तं physically attacks, बहु शपन्तं वा or utters many curses, नित्यशः at all times, नमस्कुरुत offer him your humble obeisances.

Simple Meaning: "O My beloved followers, never harm or harbor malice against a brahmana, even if he commits a fault or offense against you. Even if a brahmana physically strikes you or utters harsh curses upon you, always bow down and offer him your respectful salutations.

यथाहं प्रणमे विप्राननुकालं समाहितः ।
तथा नमत यूयं च योऽन्यथा मे स दण्डभाक् ॥10.64.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: यथा just as, अहम् I, प्रणमे bow down, विप्रान् to the brahmanas, अनुकालम् constantly/regularly, समाहितः with focused attention, तथा in the same way, नमत bow down, यूयम् you all, च and, यः whoever, अन्यथा otherwise/acts contrary to this, मे My, सः he, दण्ड-भाक् subject to punishment

Anvaya: अहं यथा समाहितः अनुकालं विप्रान् प्रणमे, यूयं च तथा नमत। यः अन्यथा (करोति), सः मे दण्डभाक् (भवति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अहं I, यथा just as, समाहितः with full concentration and devotion, अनुकालं at all times, विप्रान् unto the brahmanas, प्रणमे offer My respectful bows, यूयं च and you all, तथा in that very same manner, नमत should bow down. यः Whoever, अन्यथा acts otherwise or disobeys, सः that person, मे My, दण्डभाक् subject to chastisement and punishment, (भवति) becomes.

Simple Meaning: "Just as I constantly and devotedly bow down before the brahmanas with deep reverence, all of you should do the same. Whoever acts contrary to this principle shall be subject to My punishment.

ब्राह्मणार्थो ह्यपहृतो हर्तारं पातयत्यधः ।
अजानन्तमपि ह्येनं नृगं ब्राह्मणगौरिव ॥10.64.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: ब्राह्मण-अर्थः the property of a brahmana, हि indeed, अपहृतः stolen or taken away, हर्तारम् the taker/usurper, पातयति causes to fall, अधः down into hell, अजानन्तम् ignorant or unaware of the offense, अपि even, हि indeed, एनम् him, नृगम् King Nriga, ब्राह्मण-गौः the brahmana's cow, इव just like

Anvaya: हि अजानन्तम् अपि एनं नृगं ब्राह्मणगौः इव, अपहृतः ब्राह्मणार्थः हर्तारम् अधः पातयति हि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: हि For indeed, अजानन्तम् अपि even if completely unaware of the mistake, एनं नृगं King Nriga, ब्राह्मणगौः इव just like the brahmana's cow did to him, अपहृतः stolen or wrongly taken, ब्राह्मणार्थः the wealth or property of a brahmana, हर्तारम् the one who takes it, अधः down into lower realms, पातयति causes to fall.

Simple Meaning: "For indeed, usurping a brahmana's property drags the taker down to hellish existence, even if the mistake was made unknowingly—just as happened to King Nriga simply on account of a brahmana's cow."

एवं विश्राव्य भगवान् मुकुन्दो द्वारकौकसः ।
पावनः सर्वलोकानां विवेश निजमन्दिरम् ॥10.64.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, विश्राव्य having instructed/addressed, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, मुकुन्दः Mukunda (the giver of liberation), द्वारका-ओकसः the residents of Dwaraka, पावनः the purifier, सर्व-लोकानाम् of all the worlds, विवेश entered, निज-मन्दिरम् His own palace

Anvaya: सर्वलोकानां पावनः भगवान् मुकुन्दः द्वारकौकसः एवं विश्राव्य निजमन्दिरं विवेश।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सर्वलोकानां Of all the planetary systems, पावनः the sanctifier, भगवान् मुकुन्दः the Supreme Lord Mukunda, द्वारकौकसः to the inhabitants of Dwaraka, एवं thus, विश्राव्य having clearly spoken, निजमन्दिरं His own personal residence, विवेश entered.

Simple Meaning: Having thus instructed the citizens of Dwaraka, the Supreme Lord Mukunda, who is the ultimate purifier of all the worlds, returned and entered His own palace.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे  “नृगोपाख्यानं नाम” चतुःषष्टितमः अध्यायः ॥64॥

Thus ends the sixty-fourth discourse named “The story of King Nriga”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
