अध्यायः ६५ · Balarama visits Vraja. Diverts Yamuna from her course

ॐ

Shrimad Bhagavatam - Book 2 Chapter 65

॥ॐ॥

वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।

Shrimad Bhagavatam - Book 2

Canto 10

Chapter 65

Balarama visits Vraja. Diverts Yamuna from her course.

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

बलरामव्रजगमनं यमुनाकर्षणं च नाम पञ्चषष्टितमः अध्यायः

(32 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
बलभद्रः कुरुश्रेष्ठ भगवान् रथमास्थितः ।
सुहृद्दिदृक्षुरुत्कण्ठः प्रययौ नन्दगोकुलम् ॥10.65.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, बलभद्रः Balarama, कुरु-श्रेष्ठ O best of the Kurus (King Parikshit), भगवान् the Supreme Lord, रथम् His chariot, आस्थितः mounted/seated upon, सुहृत्-दिदृक्षुः eager to see His dear friends and relatives, उत्कण्ठः longing deeply, प्रययौ departed for, नन्द-गोकुलम् the Gokula of Nanda

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— कुरुश्रेष्ठ! भगवान् बलभद्रः सुहृद्दिदृक्षुः उत्कण्ठः रथम् आस्थितः नन्दगोकुलं प्रययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, कुरुश्रेष्ठ O best of the Kurus, भगवान् बलभद्रः the Supreme Lord Balarama, सुहृद्दिदृक्षुः desiring to see His well-wishing friends, उत्कण्ठः deeply eager, रथम् on His chariot, आस्थितः seated, नन्दगोकुलं toward Nanda's Gokula, प्रययौ set out.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: O best of the Kurus (King Parikshit), the Supreme Lord Balarama, deeply longing and eager to see His dear friends and loved ones, mounted His chariot and set out for Nanda’s Gokula.

परिष्वक्तश्चिरोत्कण्ठैर्गोपैर्गोपीभिरेव च ।
रामोऽभिवाद्य पितरावाशीर्भिरभिनन्दितः ॥10.65.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: परिष्वक्तः embraced, चिर-उत्कण्ठैः who had been longing for a very long time, गोपैः by the gopas, गोपीभिः by the gopis, एव indeed, च and, रामः Balarama, अभिवाद्य paying respects to/bowing down to, पितरौ His parents (Nanda and Yashoda), आशिर्भिः with blessings, अभिनन्दितः was greeted with joy

Anvaya: रामः चिरोत्कण्ठैः गोपैः गोपीभिः एव च परिष्वक्तः, पितरौ अभिवाद्य आशीर्भिः अभिनन्दितः (अभवत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रामः Balarama, चिरोत्कण्ठैः who had been eagerly longing for a long time, गोपैः by the cowherd men, गोपीभिः एव च and also by the cowherd women, परिष्वक्तः was warmly embraced. पितरौ To his parents, अभिवाद्य having offered respects to, आशीर्भिः with heartfelt blessings, अभिनन्दितः was welcomed joyfully.

Simple Meaning: Balarama was lovingly embraced by the gopas and gopis, who had been eagerly longing to see Him for so long. After bowing down and paying His respectful obeisances to His parents, Nanda and Yashoda, He was joyfully received and showered with their blessings.

चिरं नः पाहि दाशार्ह सानुजो जगदीश्वरः ।
इत्यारोप्याङ्कमालिङ्ग्य नेत्रैः सिषिचतुर्जलैः ॥10.65.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: चिरम् for a long time, नः us, पाहि please protect, दाशार्ह O descendant of Dasharha (Balarama), स-अनुजः along with Your younger brother (Krishna), जगत्-ईश्वरः the Lord of the universe, इति thus saying, आरोप्य placing/lifting Him, अङ्कम् onto their laps, आलिङ्ग्य embracing Him, नेत्रैः with their eyes, सिषिचतुः they both showered/drenched Him, जलैः with tear-drops

Anvaya: दाशार्ह! जगदीश्वरः सानुजः (त्वं), नः चिरं पाहि इति (उक्त्वा, रामम्) अङ्कम् आरोप्य आलिङ्ग्य नेत्रैः जलैः सिषिचतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दाशार्ह O Balarama, जगदीश्वरः The Lord of the universe, सानुजः (त्वं) You, along with Your younger brother, नः us, चिरं पाहि for a long time, please protect, इति उक्त्वा thus, having said, रामम् Lord Balarama, अङ्कम् onto their laps, आरोप्य lifting up, आलिङ्ग्य embracing Him, नेत्रैः जलैः with tears from their eyes, सिषिचतुः they both drenched Him.

Simple Meaning: Nanda and Yashoda lifted Balarama onto their laps, embraced Him, and prayed, "O Balarama! May the Lord of the universe, You and Your younger brother Krishna protect us forever." Saying this, they bathed Him in the tears flowing continuously from their eyes.

गोपवृद्धांश्च विधिवद् यविष्ठैरभिवन्दितः ।
यथावयो यथासख्यं यथासम्बन्धमात्मनः ॥10.65.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: गोप-वृद्धान् the elderly gopas, च and, विधिवत् according to proper etiquette/custom, यविष्ठैः by the younger ones, अभिवन्दितः offered respectful greetings, यथा-वयः according to age, यथा-सख्यम् according to friendship, यथा-सम्बन्धम् according to relationship, आत्मनः of Himself

Anvaya: (रामः) गोपवृद्धान् च विधिवत् (अभिवन्दितवान्), यविष्ठैः अभिवन्दितः (अभवत्), आत्मनः यथावयः यथासख्यं यथासम्बन्धं च (सकलैः सम्भावितः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (रामः) Balarama, गोपवृद्धान् च the elderly cowherd men, विधिवत् according to prescribed etiquette, अभिवन्दितवान् respected and paid obeisances to, यविष्ठैः by the younger cowherd boys, अभिवन्दितः was offered respectful greetings, आत्मनः of Himself, यथावयः according to age, यथासख्यं according to ties of friendship, यथासम्बन्धं च and according to family relationship, (सम्भावितः) was greeted appropriately.

Simple Meaning: Balarama paid His respects to the elderly gopas according to proper custom, and received respectful greetings from those younger than Himself. He then warmly greeted everyone based on their age, friendship, and personal relationship with Him.

समुपेत्याथ गोपालान् हास्यहस्तग्रहादिभिः ।
विश्रान्तं सुखमासीनं पप्रच्छुः पर्युपागताः ॥10.65.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: समुपेत्य approaching, अथ then, गोपालान् the cowherd men/friends, हास्य-हस्त-ग्रह-आदिभिः with laughter, holding hands, and other affectionate gestures, विश्रान्तम् who was fully rested, सुखम् comfortably, आसीनम् seated, पप्रच्छुः they asked/inquired, पर्युपागताः surrounding Him closely

Anvaya: अथ हास्यहस्तग्रहादिभिः गोपालान् समुपेत्य, विश्रान्तं सुखम् आसीनं (तं रामं) पर्युपागताः (गोपालाः) पप्रच्छुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ Then, हास्यहस्तग्रहादिभिः with joking laughter, holding hands, and warm exchanges, गोपालान् the gopas, समुपेत्य approaching, विश्रान्तम् when He was well-rested, सुखम् comfortably, आसीनं seated, (तं रामं) Him ( Balarama), पर्युपागताः surrounding Him on all sides, (गोपालाः) the gopas, पप्रच्छुः inquired.

Simple Meaning: After approaching His gopa friends with laughter, affectionate handshakes, and warm greetings, Balarama rested comfortably. As He sat at ease, the gopas gathered closely around Him and began to ask Him questions.

पृष्टाश्चानामयं स्वेषु प्रेमगद्गदया गिरा ।
कृष्णे कमलपत्राक्षे संन्यस्ताखिलराधसः ॥10.65.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: पृष्टाः being asked, च and, अनामयम् about their welfare, स्वेषु regarding their own family members and loved ones, प्रेम-गद्गदया choked with love, गिरा with a voice, कृष्णे in Krishna, कमल-पत्र-आक्षे who has eyes like lotus petals, संन्यस्त-अखिल-राधसः who had surrendered all their possessions and wealth

Anvaya: कमलपत्राक्षे कृष्णे संन्यस्ताखिलराधसः (गोपालाः, रामेण) स्वेषु अनामयं पृष्टाः च प्रेमगद्गदया गिरा (ऊचुः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कमलपत्राक्षे In the lotus-petal-eyed, कृष्णे Krishna, संन्यस्ताखिलराधसः who had completely dedicated all their wealth and belongings, (गोपालाः) the gopas, (रामेण) by Balarama, स्वेषु regarding their own well-being, अनामयम् about their health and welfare, पृष्टाः being inquired, च and, प्रेमगद्गदया choked with deep love, गिरा with words, (ऊचुः) spoke.

Simple Meaning: The cowherd men had surrendered their entire lives, possessions, and wealth unto the lotus-eyed Krishna. When Balarama affectionately inquired about their personal welfare and the well-being of their families, their voices choked with divine love as they began to speak.

कच्चिन्नो बान्धवा राम सर्वे कुशलमासते ।
कच्चित् स्मरथ नो राम यूयं दारसुतान्विताः ॥10.65.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: कच्चित् whether/is it that, नः our, बान्धवाः relatives and friends, राम O Balarama, सर्वे all, कुशलम् in good health and well-being, आसते are, कच्चित् whether, स्मरथ you all remember, नः us, राम O Balarama, यूयम् you, दार-सुत-अन्विताः along with your wives and children

Anvaya: राम! नः सर्वे बान्धवाः कच्चित् कुशलम् आसते? राम! दारसुतान्विताः यूयं कच्चित् नः स्मरथ?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राम O Balarama, नः our, सर्वे all, बान्धवाः relatives, कच्चित् whether, कुशलम् in good health and safety, आसते are abiding. राम O Balarama, दारसुतान्विताः along with your wives and sons, यूयं you all, कच्चित् whether, नः us, स्मरथ do remember.

Simple Meaning: The gopas said: "O Balarama! Are all our relatives and friends living happily and in good health? O Balarama, now that you and Krishna are settled with your wives and children, do you all ever remember us?"

दिष्ट्या कंसो हतः पापो दिष्ट्या मुक्ताः सुहृज्जनाः ।
निहत्य निर्जित्य रिपून् दिष्ट्या दुर्गं समाश्रिताः ॥10.65.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: दिष्ट्या by good fortune, कंसः Kamsa, हतः was killed, पापः the sinful one, दिष्ट्या by good fortune, मुक्ताः were freed, सुहृत्-जनाः our dear friends and relatives, निहत्य having killed, निर्जित्य having conquered, रिपून् enemies, दिष्ट्या by good fortune, दुर्गम् a fortress/secure city (Dwaraka), समाश्रिताः have taken refuge in

Anvaya: पापः कंसः दिष्ट्या हतः, सुहृज्जनाः दिष्ट्या मुक्ताः, (यूयं च) रिपून् निहत्य निर्जित्य दिष्ट्या दुर्गं समाश्रिताः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पापः कंसः The sinful, Kamsa, दिष्ट्या हतः by good fortune, has been slain, सुहृज्जनाः our dear relatives, दिष्ट्या मुक्ताः by good fortune, have been liberated from captivity, यूयं च and you all, रिपून् enemies, निहत्य having struck down, निर्जित्य and vanquished, दिष्ट्या by good fortune, दुर्गं समाश्रिताः in a secure fortress, have taken shelter.

Simple Meaning: The gopas continued: By supreme good fortune, the sinful Kamsa has been killed and all our dear relatives have been freed from prison. By wonderful good fortune, after defeating and eliminating your enemies, you all have now taken residence in the safe fortress of Dwaraka.

गोप्यो हसन्तः पप्रच्छू रामसन्दर्शनादृताः ।
कच्चिदास्ते सुखं कृष्णः पुरस्त्रीजनवल्लभः ॥10.65.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: गोप्यः the gopis, हसन्तः smiling/laughing, पप्रच्छुः asked, राम-सन्दर्शन-आदृताः feeling honored and delighted by the sight of Balarama, कच्चित् whether, आस्ते lives, सुखम् happily, कृष्णः Krishna, पुर-स्त्री-जन-वल्लभः the darling/beloved of the city women

Anvaya: रामसन्दर्शनादृताः गोप्यः हसन्तः पप्रच्छुः— पुरस्त्रीजनवल्लभः कृष्णः कच्चित् सुखम् आस्ते?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रामसन्दर्शनादृताः Delighted by seeing Balarama, गोप्यः the cowherd women, हसन्तः smiling, पप्रच्छुः inquired, पुरस्त्रीजनवल्लभः the beloved of the city women, कृष्णः Krishna, कच्चित् whether, सुखम् happily, आस्ते is residing.

Simple Meaning: The gopis, feeling deeply honored and happy to see Balarama, asked Him with gentle smiles: "Is Krishna, who is now the darling of the city women, living happily?"

कच्चित् स्मरति वा बन्धून् पितरं मातरं च सः ।
अप्यसौ मातरं द्रष्टुं सकृदप्यागमिष्यति ।
अपि वा स्मरतेऽस्माकमनुसेवां महाभुजः ॥10.65.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: कच्चित् whether, स्मरति remembers, वा or, बन्धून् relatives and friends, पितरम् father, मातरम् mother, च and, सः He (Krishna), अपि whether, असौ He, मातरम् His mother, द्रष्टुम् to see, सकृत् once, अपि even, आगमिष्यति will come, अपि whether, वा or, स्मरते remembers, अस्माकम् our, अनुसेवाम् loving service, महा-भुजः the mighty-armed Lord

Anvaya: सः बन्धून् पितरं मातरं च कच्चित् स्मरति वा? असौ मातरं द्रष्टुं सकृत् अपि आगमिष्यति अपि? महाभुजः अस्माकम् अनुसेवां स्मरते वा अपि?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He, बन्धून् relatives and friends, पितरं मातरं च father and mother, कच्चित् whether, स्मरति वा remembers at all, असौ He, मातरं His mother, द्रष्टुं to see, सकृत् अपि even once, आगमिष्यति अपि will come back? महाभुजः The mighty-armed Lord, अस्माकम् our, अनुसेवां loving service, स्मरते वा अपि does He remember at all.

Simple Meaning: The gopis asked: "Does Krishna ever remember His friends, relatives, and His father and mother here in Vraja? Will the mighty-armed Lord come back even once to see His mother Yashoda? Does He ever remember our affectionate service to Him?"

मातरं पितरं भ्रातृन् पतीन् पुत्रान् स्वसृरपि ।
यदर्थे जहिम दाशार्ह दुस्त्यजान् स्वजनान् प्रभो ॥10.65.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: मातरम् mother, पितरम् father, भ्रातृन् brothers, पतीन् husbands, पुत्रान् children/sons, स्वसृः sisters, अपि also, यत्-अर्थे for whose sake, जहिम we abandoned/gave up, दाशार्ह O descendant of Dasharha (Balarama), दुस्त्यजान् which are so difficult to abandon, स्व-जनान् our own relatives and family members, प्रभो O Lord

Anvaya: प्रभो! दाशार्ह! यदर्थे (वयं) दुस्त्यजान् स्वजनान् मातरं पितरं भ्रातृन् पतीन् पुत्रान् स्वसृः अपि जहिम...

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रभो O Lord, दाशार्ह O Balarama, यदर्थे for whose sake, (वयं) we, दुस्त्यजान् which are extremely hard to renounce, स्वजनान् our own family members, मातरं mother, पितरं father, भ्रातृन् brothers, पतीन् husbands, पुत्रान् children, स्वसृः अपि and even sisters, जहिम have completely abandoned...

Simple Meaning: The gopis continued: "O Lord, O Balarama! For Krishna's sake, we gave up our mothers, fathers, brothers, husbands, children, and sisters—our own family members whom it is otherwise impossible to renounce."

ता नः सद्यः परित्यज्य गतः संछिन्नसौहृदः ।
कथं नु तादृशं स्त्रीभिर्न श्रद्धीयेत भाषितम् ॥10.65.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताः us (who abandoned everything for Him), नः us, सद्यः suddenly/abruptly, परित्यज्य having deserted/left, गतः left, संछिन्न-सौहृदः breaking all ties of affection/breaking the bond of friendship, कथम् how, नु indeed, तादृशम् such a person's, स्त्रीभिः by women, न श्रद्धीयेत should not be believed, भाषितम् spoken words

Anvaya: ताः नः सद्यः परित्यज्य संछिन्नसौहृदः (सः) गतः। तादृशं भाषितं स्त्रीभिः कथं नु न श्रद्धीयेत?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ताः नः Us who sacrificed everything, सद्यः suddenly, परित्यज्य having abandoned, संछिन्नसौहृदः severing all affection, सः He, गतः has gone away. तादृशं Such a person's, भाषितं words, स्त्रीभिः by women, कथं नु why indeed, न श्रद्धीयेत should not be trusted/believed.

Simple Meaning: "Yet Krishna suddenly abandoned us and broke off all affectionate ties to go away. Knowing how easily He breaks His word, how could any woman ever trust what He says again?"

कथं नु गृह्णन्त्यनवस्थितात्मनो वचः कृतघ्नस्य बुधाः पुरस्त्रियः ।
गृह्णन्ति वै चित्रकथस्य सुन्दरस्मितावलोकोच्छ्वसितस्मरातुराः ॥10.65.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: कथम् how, नु indeed, गृह्णन्ति accept/trust, अनवस्थित-आत्मनः of one who is fickle-minded or unstable, वचः words, कृतघ्नस्य of an ungrateful person, बुधाः wise or intelligent, पुर-स्त्रियः the city women, गृह्णन्ति they accept, वै indeed, चित्र-कथस्य of one whose talk is charming/colorful, सुन्दर-स्मित-अवलोक-उच्छ्वसित-स्मर-आतुराः agitated by Cupid/lust awakened by His beautiful smiles and glances

Anvaya: अनवस्थितात्मनः कृतघ्नस्य वचः बुधाः पुरस्त्रियः कथं नु गृह्णन्ति? सुन्दरस्मितावलोकोच्छ्वसितस्मरातुराः चित्रकथस्य (तस्य वचः) वै गृह्णन्ति!

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अनवस्थितात्मनः Of a fickle-minded person, कृतघ्नस्य of an ungrateful man, वचः the words, बुधाः wise, पुरस्त्रियः city women, कथं नु how indeed, गृह्णन्ति do they accept/trust? सुन्दरस्मितावलोकोच्छ्वसितस्मरातुराः Being deeply agitated by lust/desire stirred up by His beautiful smile and seductive glances, चित्रकथस्य of Him whose speech is so colorful and charming, तस्य वचः His words, वै indeed, गृह्णन्ति they accept!

Simple Meaning: The gopis continued: "How can those city women—who are supposed to be educated and wise—put any trust in the words of someone so fickle-minded and ungrateful? Ah, but seeing His enchanting smiles and alluring glances, their hearts are filled with romantic longing, and so they fall for His sweet, deceptive talk!"

किं नस्तत्कथया गोप्यः कथाः कथयतापराः ।
यात्यस्माभिर्विना कालो यदि तस्य तथैव नः ॥10.65.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: किम् what use is, नः to us, तत्-कथया with talk of Him (Krishna), गोप्यः O fellow gopis, कथाः topics/talks, कथयत speak, अपराः other, याति passes, अस्माभिः विना without us, कालः time, यदि if, तस्य His, तथा एव in the same way, नः ours

Anvaya: गोप्यः! नः तत्कथया किम्? अपराः कथाः कथयत। यदि तस्य अस्माभिः विना कालः याति, (तर्हि) नः (अपि) तथैव (याति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गोप्यः O gopis, नः for us, तत्कथया with talk about Him, किम् what purpose is served? अपराः other, कथाः stories, कथयत please speak. यदि If, तस्य His, अस्माभिः विना without us, कालः time, याति passes, तर्हि then, नः our time, अपि also, तथैव in the exact same manner, याति passes.

Simple Meaning: The gopis then said to one another: "O gopis, what is the point of talking about Krishna anymore? Let us talk about other things instead! If He can pass His time without us, we can certainly pass our time without Him as well."

इति प्रहसितं शौरेर्जल्पितं चारु वीक्षितम् ।
गतिं प्रेमपरिष्वङ्गं स्मरन्त्यो रुरुदुः स्त्रियः ॥10.65.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, प्रहसितम् laughter, शौरेः of Krishna (the descendant of Shura), जल्पितम् charming talk/conversation, चारु beautiful, वीक्षितम् glances/looks, गतिम् movement/walking posture, प्रेम-परिष्वङ्गम् loving embrace, स्मरन्त्यः remembering, रुरुदुः wept/cried, स्त्रियः the gopis/women of Vraja

Anvaya: इति शौरेः प्रहसितं जल्पितं चारु वीक्षितं गतिं प्रेमपरिष्वङ्गं (च) स्मरन्त्यः स्त्रियः रुरुदुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति Thus, शौरेः of Krishna, प्रहसितं laughter, जल्पितं playful conversation, चारु वीक्षितं beautiful glances, गतिं gait, प्रेमपरिष्वङ्गं (च) and loving embrace, स्मरन्त्यः remembering, स्त्रियः रुरुदुः the gopis wept aloud.

Simple Meaning: Even as they tried to resolve not to speak of Him, the gopis remembered Krishna’s playful laughter, His sweet talk, His beautiful glances, His graceful walk, and His affectionate embraces—and remembering all these, they broke down and wept bitterly.

सङ्कर्षणस्ताः कृष्णस्य सन्देशैर्हृदयङ्गमैः ।
सान्त्वयामास भगवान् नानानुनयकोविदः ॥10.65.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: सङ्कर्षणः Balarama (Sankarshana), ताः those gopis, कृष्णस्य of Krishna, सन्देशैः with messages, हृदयङ्गमैः touching to the heart/deeply moving, सान्त्वयामास comforted/consoled, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, नाना-अनुनय-कोविदः who is expert in various ways of pacifying and appeasing

Anvaya: नानानुनयकोविदः भगवान् सङ्कर्षणः कृष्णस्य हृदयङ्गमैः सन्देशैः ताः सान्त्वयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नानानुनयकोविदः Expert in various methods of conciliation and pacification, भगवान् सङ्कर्षणः the Supreme Lord Balarama, कृष्णस्य of Krishna, हृदयङ्गमैः सन्देशैः with deeply touching messages, ताः those gopis, सान्त्वयामास comforted and pacified.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord Balarama, who is immensely skilled in the art of loving conciliation, comforted the gopis by delivering to them the deeply touching and sweet messages sent directly by Krishna.

द्वौ मासौ तत्र चावात्सीन्मधुं माधवमेव च ।
रामः क्षपासु भगवान् गोपीनां रतिमावहन् ॥10.65.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्वौ two, मासौ months, तत्र there (in Vrindavan), च and, अवात्सीत् resided/stayed, मधुम् Chaitra (the month of Spring), माधवम् month following Chaitra (Vaishakha), एव indeed, च and, रामः Balarama, क्षपासु during the nights, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, गोपीनाम् to the gopis, रतिम् delight/divine bliss, आवहन् bringing/granting

Anvaya: भगवान् रामः क्षपासु गोपीनां रतिम् आवहन् तत्र मधुं माधवम् एव च द्वौ मासौ अवात्सीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवान् रामः The Supreme Lord Balarama, क्षपासु during the nights, गोपीनां to the gopis, रतिम् romantic delight and spiritual joy, आवहन् bestowing, तत्र there in Vraja, मधुं Chaitra, माधवम् एव च and Vaishakha, द्वौ मासौ two months, अवात्सीत् resided,

Simple Meaning: Lord Balarama stayed in Vrindavan for two full spring months—Madhu (Chaitra) and Madhava (Vaishakha). During the nights, the Supreme Lord brought immense joy and love to the hearts of the gopis.

पूर्णचन्द्रकलामृष्टे कौमुदीगन्धवायुना ।
यमुनोपवने रेमे सेविते स्त्रीगणैर्वृतः ॥10.65.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: पूर्ण-चन्द्र-कला-मृष्टे brightened by the rays of the full moon, कौमुदी-गन्ध-वायुना with the moonlight and wind bearing the fragrance of night-blooming flowers, यमुना-उपवने in the garden along the banks of the Yamuna, रेमे enjoyed/sported, सेविते well-served/pleasant, स्त्री-गणैः by groups of women (the gopis), वृतः surrounded

Anvaya: (भगवान् रामः) पूर्णचन्द्रकलामृष्टे कौमुदीगन्धवायुना सेविते यमुनोपवने स्त्रीगणैः वृतः रेमे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (भगवान् रामः) Lord Balarama, पूर्णचन्द्रकलामृष्टे which was illuminated by the soft beams of the full moon, कौमुदीगन्धवायुना by gentle breezes carrying the fragrance of night-blooming lotuses, सेविते rendered delightful, यमुनोपवने in the grove on the banks of the Yamuna, स्त्रीगणैः by surrounding groups of young women, वृतः accompanied, रेमे sported and enjoyed.

Simple Meaning: Illuminated by the soft light of the full moon and cooled by night breezes carrying the sweet scent of lotuses, the garden along the Yamuna was exquisite. There, surrounded by the gopis, Lord Balarama enjoyed His pastimes during those moonlit nights.

वरुणप्रेषिता देवी वारुणी वृक्षकोटरात् ।
पतन्ती तद् वनं सर्वं स्वगन्धेनाध्यवासयत् ॥10.65.19॥

Word-by-word meaning: वरुण-प्रेषिता sent by Varuna (the god of waters), देवी the divine goddess, वारुणी Varuni (the liquid honey-nectar/celestial beverage), वृक्ष-कोटरात् from the hollow of a tree, पतन्ती flowing/cascading down, तत् that, वनम् forest, सर्वम् entire, स्व-गन्धेन with her own fragrance, अध्यवासयत् filled/scented thoroughly

Anvaya: वरुणप्रेषिता देवी वारुणी वृक्षकोटरात् पतन्ती स्वगन्धेन तत् सर्वं वनम् अध्यवासयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वरुणप्रेषिता Sent by Varuna, देवी वारुणी the divine goddess Varuni, वृक्षकोटरात् from the hollow of a tree trunk, पतन्ती cascading down, स्वगन्धेन with her enchanting fragrance, तत् सर्वं वनम् that whole forest, अध्यवासयत् filled with rich perfume.

Simple Meaning: Sent by Varuna, the god of waters, the divine celestial nectar-beverage, Goddess Varuni, began flowing down from the hollow of a tree. As she trickled down, her intoxicating fragrance filled the entire forest.

तं गन्धं मधुधाराया वायुनोपहृतं बलः ।
आघ्रायोपगतस्तत्र ललनाभिः समं पपौ ॥10.65.20॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् that, गन्धम् aroma/fragrance, मधु-धारायाः of the stream of honey-nectar, वायुना by the breeze, उपहृतम् carried over, बलः Lord Balarama, आघ्राय smelling/catching the scent of, उपगतः approaching, तत्र there, ललनाभिः समम् together with the young women (the gopis), पपौ drank

Anvaya: वायुना उपहृतं मधुधारायाः तं गन्धम् आघ्राय बलः तत्र उपगतः (तथा) ललनाभिः समं (तां वारुणीं) पपौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वायुना By the breeze, उपहृतम् brought, मधुधारायाः of the stream of honeyed nectar, तं गन्धम् that fragrance, आघ्राय smelling, बलः Balarama, तत्र to that place, उपगतः approached, तथा and, ललनाभिः समं accompanied by the young women, (तां वारुणीं) that Varuni, पपौ drank.

Simple Meaning: Catching the sweet fragrance of that streaming nectar carried to Him by the gentle breeze, Balarama followed the scent to the spot. There, in the company of the young women of Vraja, He drank that divine beverage.

उपगीयमानचरितो वनिताभिर्हलायुधः ।
वनेषु व्यचरत् क्षीबो मदविह्वललोचनः ॥10.65.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: उपगीयमान-चरितः His glorious deeds being sung aloud, वनिताभिः by the women (the gopis), हलायुधः Balarama (the wielder of the plough-weapon), वनेषु through the groves of the forest, व्यचरत् wandered/roamed, क्षीबः intoxicated (with divine ecstasy and the sweet beverage), मद-विह्वल-लोचनः with eyes rolling/expressive with intoxicated joy

Anvaya: वनिताभिः उपगीयमानचरितः हलायुधः क्षीबः मदविह्वललोचनः (सन्) वनेषु व्यचरत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वनिताभिः By the beautiful young women, उपगीयमानचरितः His heroic deeds being celebrated in song, हलायुधः Balarama, क्षीबः intoxicated, मदविह्वललोचनः with eyes gently rolling in ecstatic intoxication, वनेषु throughout the woodland groves, व्यचरत् wandered freely.

Simple Meaning: As the young women sang sweet songs praising His heroic deeds, Lord Balarama—His eyes rolling with divine bliss and intoxication—roamed happily through the forest groves.

स्रग्व्येककुण्डलो मत्तो वैजयन्त्या च मालया ।
बिभ्रत् स्मितमुखाम्भोजं स्वेदप्रालेयभूषितम् ॥10.65.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्रग्वी adorned with a flower garland, एक-कुण्डलः wearing a single earring, मत्तः intoxicated with ecstatic joy, वैजयन्त्या using the famous Vaijayanti, च and, मालया garland, बिभ्रत् bearing/displaying, स्मित-मुख-अम्भोजम् a lotus-like face filled with a gentle smile, स्वेद-प्रालेय-भूषितम् decorated with drops of perspiration like dew

Anvaya: (हलायुधः) स्रग्वी, वैजयन्त्या मालया बिभ्रत् एककुण्डलः, मत्तः, (तथा) स्वेदप्रालेयभूषितं स्मितमुखाम्भोजं (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (हलायुधः) Balarama, स्रग्वी adorned with flower wreaths, वैजयन्त्या मालया बिभ्रत् shining with the Vaijayanti garland, एककुण्डलः wearing a single ear-ring, मत्तः blissfully intoxicated, तथा and, स्वेदप्रालेयभूषितं स्मितमुखाम्भोजं His smiling lotus face was embellished with perspiration droplets like fine dew.

Simple Meaning: Wearing the Vaijayanti garland, adornments of flowers, and a single ear-ring, Balarama looked splendid in His state of blissful intoxication. His lotus-like face was beautifully adorned with a smile and glistening droplets of perspiration, like dew upon a lotus flower.

स आजुहाव यमुनां जलक्रीडार्थमीश्वरः ।
निजं वाक्यमनादृत्य मत्त इत्यापगां बलः ।
अनागतां हलाग्रेण कुपितो विचकर्ष ह ॥10.65.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः He (Balarama), आजुहाव called/summoned, यमुनाम् the river Yamuna, जल-क्रीडा-अर्थम् for the purpose of sporting in the water, ईश्वरः the Supreme Lord/Master, निजम् Her own (or His), वाक्यम् order/words, अनादृत्य disregarding/ignoring, मत्तः (thinking) "He is intoxicated", इति thus, आपगाम् the river, बलः Balarama, अनागताम् who did not come, हल-आग्रेण with the tip of His plough, कुपितः becoming angry, विचकर्ष dragged, ह indeed

Anvaya: सः ईश्वरः बलः जलक्रीडार्थं यमुनाम् आजुहाव। "मत्तः" इति निजं वाक्यम् अनादृत्य अनागताम् आपगां कुपितः (सन्) हलाग्रेण विचकर्ष ह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः ईश्वरः बलः He, the almighty Lord Balarama, जलक्रीडार्थं for playing in the waters, यमुनाम् the river Yamuna, आजुहाव called out to. "मत्तः" इति "He is intoxicated," thinking thus, निजं वाक्यम् अनादृत्य His command, disregarding, अनागताम् आपगां who failed to appear, the river, कुपितः becoming enraged, हलाग्रेण विचकर्ष ह with the point of His plough weapon, dragged forcibly.

Simple Meaning: Lord Balarama summoned the river Yamuna so He could play in her waters. Thinking that Balarama was intoxicated, Yamuna ignored His command and did not come. Incensed by her disregard, the Lord hooked the river with the tip of His plough and began dragging her toward Him.

पापे त्वं मामवज्ञाय यन्नायासि मयाऽऽहुता ।
नेष्ये त्वां लाङ्गलाग्रेण शतधा कामचारिणीम् ॥10.65.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: पापे O sinful one, त्वम् you, माम् Me, अवज्ञाय disregarding/disrespecting, यत् because, न आयासि do not come, मया by Me, आहूता called, नेष्ये I shall take, त्वाम् you, लाङ्गल-आग्रेण with the tip of My plough, शतधा into a hundred streams, काम-चारिणीम् flowing according to your own whims and desires

Anvaya: पापे! मया आहूता (अपि) यत् माम् अवज्ञाय त्वं न आयासि, (तस्मात्) कामचारिणीं त्वां लाङ्गलाग्रेण शतधा नेष्ये!

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पापे O sinful one, मया by Me, आहूता summoned, अपि even though, यत् because, माम् Me, अवज्ञाय disregarding, त्वं you, न आयासि do not come, (तस्मात्) therefore, कामचारिणीं त्वां flowing arbitrarily according to your own will, you, लाङ्गलाग्रेण with the point of My plough, शतधा into a hundred splintered channels, नेष्ये I shall drag and divide.

Simple Meaning: Lord Balarama addressed Yamuna: "O sinful one! Even after I called you, you disregarded Me and refused to come! Because you think you can flow wherever you please according to your own whims, I will use the tip of My plough to drag you and break you into a hundred separate streams!"

एवं निर्भर्त्सिता भीता यमुना यदुनन्दनम् ।
उवाच चकिता वाचं पतिता पादयोर्नृप ॥10.65.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus, निर्भर्त्सिता scolded/rebuked, भीता terrified, यमुना the river Yamuna, यदु-नन्दनम् to the delight of the Yadu dynasty (Balarama), उवाच spoke, चकिता trembling/frightened, वाचम् words, पतिता fallen, पादयोः at His feet, नृप O King (Parikshit)

Anvaya: नृप! एवं निर्भर्त्सिता भीता यमुना चकिता (सती) पादयोः पतिता यदुनन्दनं वाचम् उवाच।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King Parikshit, एवं in this manner, निर्भर्त्सिता severely chastised, भीता यमुना the terrified Yamuna river, चकिता trembling in fear, पादयोः at His lotus feet, पतिता falling prostrate, यदुनन्दनं to Lord Balarama, वाचम् these words, उवाच spoke.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, thus severely scolded, the river Yamuna became terrified. Trembling with fear, she threw herself down at the feet of Balarama and addressed Him with these words.

राम राम महाबाहो न जाने तव विक्रमम् ।
यस्यैकांशेन विधृता जगती जगतः पते ॥10.65.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: राम O Rama (Balarama), राम O Rama, महा-बाहो O mighty-armed one, न जाने I did not know/comprehend, तव Your, विक्रमम् prowess/true power, यस्य of whom, एक-अंशेन by a single plenary portion (Ananta Sesha), विधृता is sustained/supported, जगती the entire earth/world, जगतः पते O Master of the universe

Anvaya: राम! राम! महाबाहो! जगतः पते! यस्य एकांशेन जगती विधृता (अस्ति), (तस्य) तव विक्रमम् (अहं) न जाने।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राम O Rama! राम O Rama! महाबाहो O mighty-armed Lord! जगतः पते O Lord of the universe! यस्य of whom, एकांशेन by a single expansion/portion, जगती the entire earth, विधृता is held up and sustained, (तस्य) तव of him, Your, विक्रमम् prowess, (अहं) I, न जाने did not realize.

Simple Meaning: Goddess Yamuna prayed: "O Rama! O mighty-armed Lord of the universe! Forgive me! I failed to comprehend Your true power—that You are the Supreme Lord who upholds the entire universe on the head of just one of Your plenary expansions, Ananta Sesha."

परं भावं भगवतो भगवन् मामजानतीम् ।
मोक्तुमर्हसि विश्वात्मन् प्रपन्नां भक्तवत्सल ॥10.65.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: परम् supreme, भावम् nature/glory, भगवतः of the Supreme Lord, भगवन् O Lord, माम् me, अजानतीम् ignorant/unknowing, मोक्तुम् अर्हसि You should forgive/release, विश्वात्मन् O Soul of the universe, प्रपन्नाम् surrendered, भक्त-वत्सल O You who are affectionate to Your devotees

Anvaya: भगवन्! विश्वात्मन्! भक्तवत्सल! भगवतः तव परं भावम् अजानतीं प्रपन्नां मां मोक्तुम् अर्हसि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवन् O Lord, विश्वात्मन् O Soul of the universe, भक्तवत्सल O Lover of Your devotees, भगवतः तव of Your divine Lordship, परं supreme, भावम् status and transcendental nature, अजानतीं not knowing, प्रपन्नां who has surrendered, मां me, मोक्तुम् अर्हसि You ought to forgive/pardon.

Simple Meaning: Yamuna continued her prayers: "O Supreme Lord! O Soul of the entire universe! O Lover of Your devotees! I was completely ignorant of Your supreme power and divinity. Please forgive me, for I have now surrendered unto You!"

ततो व्यमुञ्चद् यमुनां याचितो भगवान् बलः ।
विजगाह जलं स्त्रीभिः करेणुभिरिवेभराट् ॥10.65.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then/thereupon, व्यमुञ्चत् released/let go of, यमुनाम् the river Yamuna, याचितः being prayed to/begged for mercy, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, बलः Balarama, विजगाह entered/plunged into, जलम् the water, स्त्रीभिः along with the women (the gopis), करेणुभिः with female elephants, इव like, इभ-राट् a king among elephants (a majestic bull elephant)

Anvaya: ततः याचितः भगवान् बलः यमुनां व्यमुञ्चत्, (ततः) करेणुभिः इभराट् इव स्त्रीभिः जलं विजगाह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः Thereupon, याचितः being humbly implored, भगवान् बलः the Supreme Lord Balarama, यमुनां the Yamuna, व्यमुञ्चत् released. ततः Then, करेणुभिः with female elephants, इभराट् इव like a king of elephants, स्त्रीभिः accompanied by the women, जलं into the water, विजगाह He plunged and disported.

Simple Meaning: Thus humbly prayed to and appeased, the Supreme Lord Balarama released the Yamuna. Then, just as a majestic king of elephants enters the water surrounded by female elephants, Balarama entered the river Yamuna accompanied by the young women of Vraja to play in the waters.

कामं विहृत्य सलिलादुत्तीर्णायासिताम्बरे ।
भूषणानि महार्हाणि ददौ कान्तिः शुभां स्रजम् ॥10.65.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: कामम् to His full heart's content, विहृत्य having sported/played, सलिलात् out of the water, उत्तीर्णाय to Him who had emerged, असित-अम्बरे  a pair of blue garments/dark robes, भूषणानि ornaments, महार्हाणि very valuable/priceless, ददौ presented/gave, कान्तिः the goddess of beauty (Lakshmi), शुभाम् auspicious, स्रजम् flower garland

Anvaya: कामं विहृत्य सलिलात् उत्तीर्णाय (बलाय) कान्तिः असिताम्बरे महार्हाणि भूषणानि शुभां स्रजं (च) ददौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कामं To His heart's desire, विहृत्य having disported in the water, सलिलात् from the stream, उत्तीर्णाय to Him who stepped out, कान्तिः Kanti (the goddess of beauty), असिताम्बरे a pair of blue garments, महार्हाणि भूषणानि priceless ornaments, शुभां स्रजं auspicious garland of flowers, ददौ bestowed upon Him.

Simple Meaning: After sporting in the Yamuna to His full satisfaction, Lord Balarama stepped out of the water. Thereupon, Kanti, the goddess of beauty, presented Him with  a pair of dark blue garments, priceless glittering ornaments, and an auspicious flower garland.

वसित्वा वाससी नीले मालामामुच्य काञ्चनीम् ।
रेजे स्वलङ्कृतो लिप्तो माहेन्द्र इव वारणः ॥10.65.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: वसित्वा having donned/put on, वाससी two garments, नीले blue, मालाम् a necklace/garland, आमुच्य wearing/putting on, काञ्चनीम् golden, रेजे shone brilliantly, सु-अलङ्कृतः gorgeously adorned, लिप्तः anointed (with fragrant sandalwood paste), माहेन्द्रः of Lord Indra, इव like, वारणः the majestic royal elephant (Airavata)

Anvaya: नीले वाससी वसित्वा, काञ्चनीं मालाम् आमुच्य, स्वलङ्कृतः लिप्तः (बलः) माहेन्द्रः वारणः इव रेजे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नीले वाससी A pair of dark blue garments, वसित्वा having dressed in, काञ्चनीं मालाम् the golden necklace, आमुच्य wearing, स्वलङ्कृतः splendidly ornamented, लिप्तः anointed with fragrant paste, बलः Balarama, माहेन्द्रः belonging to Indra, वारणः इव like the royal elephant Airavata, रेजे shone with resplendent beauty.

Simple Meaning: Dressed in those pair of dark blue garments, wearing a golden necklace, anointed with fragrant pastes, and exquisitely adorned with fine ornaments, Lord Balarama shone brilliantly, looking just like Airavata, the majestic royal elephant of King Indra.

अद्यापि दृश्यते राजन् यमुनाऽऽकृष्टवर्त्मना ।
बलस्यानन्तवीर्यस्य वीर्यं सूचयतीव हि ॥10.65.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: अद्य अपि even today, दृश्यते is seen, राजन् O King (Parikshit), यमुना the river Yamuna, आकृष्ट-वर्त्मना by her path being pulled/deflected, बलस्य of Lord Balarama, अनन्त-वीर्यस्य of infinite prowess/unlimited potency, वीर्यम् strength/heroism, सूचयन्ती इव as if indicating/declaring, हि indeed

Anvaya: राजन्! अद्यापि आकृष्टवर्त्मना (प्रवहन्ती) यमुना अनन्तवीर्यस्य बलस्य वीर्यं सूचयन्ती इव दृश्यते हि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, अद्य अपि even today, आकृष्टवर्त्मना through her path altered by being pulled, यमुना the river Yamuna, अनन्तवीर्यस्य बलस्य of unlimited power, Lord Balarama, वीर्यं the extraordinary prowess, सूचयन्ती इव as if indicating and proclaiming, दृश्यते हि is indeed seen.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, even to this day, the altered course of the Yamuna river clearly displays where she was pulled by the plough, standing as a living testimony to the infinite power and prowess of Lord Balarama.

एवं सर्वा निशा याता एकैव रमतो व्रजे ।
रामस्याक्षिप्तचित्तस्य माधुर्यैर्व्रजयोषिताम् ॥10.65.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this manner, सर्वाः all, निशाः the nights, याताः passed, एक इव like a single (night), रमतः while sporting/enjoying, व्रजे in Vraja, रामस्य for Balarama, आक्षिप्त-चित्तस्य whose heart was captivated/charmed, माधुर्यैः by the sweet charms, व्रज-योषिताम् of the women of Vraja (the gopis)

Anvaya: व्रजयोषितां माधुर्यैः आक्षिप्तचित्तस्य व्रजे रमतः रामस्य एवं सर्वाः निशाः एका इव याताः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: व्रजयोषितां Of the young women of Vraja, माधुर्यैः by the exquisite sweetness and charm, आक्षिप्तचित्तस्य whose mind was deeply enchanted, व्रजे in Vraja, रमतः sporting happily, रामस्य for Balarama, एवं in this way, सर्वाः निशाः all the nights, एका इव as if a single night, याताः passed away.

Simple Meaning: In this manner, with His heart completely captivated by the charms and sweetness of the young women of Vraja, Balarama spent His time enjoying happily. For Him, that whole sequence of nights passed as swiftly as if it were just a single night.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे  “बलरामव्रजगमनं यमुनाकर्षणं च नाम” पञ्चषष्टितमः अध्यायः ॥65॥

Thus ends the sixty-fifth discourse named “Balarama visits Vraja. Diverts Yamuna from her course.”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
