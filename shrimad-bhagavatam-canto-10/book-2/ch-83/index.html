Conversation between Krishna's wives and Draupadi

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

कृष्णपत्नीनां द्रौपद्याः च संवादः नाम त्र्यशीतितमः अध्यायः

(43 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
तथानुगृह्य भगवान् गोपीनां स गुरुर्गतिः ।
युधिष्ठिरमथापृच्छत् सर्वांश्च सुहृदोऽव्ययम् ॥10.83.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, तथा in this way, अनुगृह्य having bestowed His grace and mercy, भगवान् the Supreme Lord Krishna, गोपीनाम् upon the gopis, सः He, गुरुः their supreme spiritual master, गतिः their ultimate goal and refuge, युधिष्ठिरम् Yudhishthira, अथ then, आपृच्छत् inquired after, सर्वान् all, च and, सुहृदः friends and well-wishers, अव्ययम् their health and imperishable well-being

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच – गोपीनां गुरुः गतिः भगवान् तथा अनुगृह्य अथ युधिष्ठिरं सर्वान् सुहृदः च अव्ययम् आपृच्छत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, गोपीनां गुरुः गतिः the supreme master and shelter of the gopis, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, तथा अनुगृह्य having thus favored them with His grace, अथ then, युधिष्ठिरं Yudhishthira, सर्वान् सुहृदः च and all His other friends, अव्ययम् आपृच्छत् inquired after their welfare and well-being.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Having thus bestowed His divine grace upon the gopis, Lord Krishna—who is their ultimate spiritual guide and supreme goal—then approached Yudhishthira and all His other dear friends and relatives to inquire warmly after their health and well-being.

त एवं लोकनाथेन परिपृष्टाः सुसत्कृताः ।
प्रत्यूचुर्हृष्टमनसस्तत्पादेक्षाहतांहसः ॥10.83.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: ते they King Yudhishthira and the other kings and relatives, एवम् in this manner, लोक-नाथेन by the Lord of the universe Krishna, परिपृष्टाः warmly inquired after, सु-सत्कृताः honored with great reverence, प्रत्यूचुः replied, हृष्ट-मनसः with joyful hearts, तत्-पाद-ईक्षा-आहत-अंहसः whose sins were eradicated simply by looking upon His lotus feet

Anvaya: एवं लोकनाथेन परिपृष्टाः सुसत्कृताः (च) तत्पादेक्षाहतांहसः ते हृष्टमनसः प्रत्यूचुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवं In this manner, लोकनाथेन by the Master of the world Krishna, परिपृष्टाः inquired after, सुसत्कृताः च and greatly honored, तत्पादेक्षाहतांहसः whose impurities were washed away by viewing His lotus feet, ते constraint-free kings, हृष्टमनसः with gladdened minds, प्रत्यूचुः replied.

Simple Meaning: Having been thus warmly greeted, inquired after, and honored by Lord Krishna, the Lord of the universe, Yudhishthira and the assembled kings felt their hearts overflow with joy. Just by beholding His sacred lotus feet, all their sins were destroyed, and they replied to Him with deep reverence.

कुतोऽशिवं त्वच्चरणाम्बुजासवं महन्मनस्तो मुखनिःसृतं क्वचित् ।
पिबन्ति ये कर्णपुटैरलं प्रभो देहम्भृतां देहकृदस्मृतिच्छिदम् ॥10.83.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: कुतः how, अशिवम् inauspiciousness/ill fortune, त्वत्-चरण-अम्बुज-आसवम् the sweet nectar of Your lotus feet, महत्-मनस्तः from the minds of great exalted devotees, मुख-निःसृतम् flowing out from their mouths, क्वचित् anytime or anywhere, पिबन्ति drink, ये who, कर्ण-पुटैः through the cups of their ears, अलम् fully to their satisfaction, प्रभो O Lord, देह-भृताम् for embodied living beings, देह-कृत्-अस्मृति-छिदम् that which cuts asunder the ignorance and forgetfulness caused by the material body and its creator

Anvaya: प्रभो! महन्मनस्तः मुखनिःसृतं देहम्भृतां देहकृदस्मृतिच्छिदं त्वच्चरणाम्बुजासवं ये कर्णपुटैः अलं पिबन्ति, तेषां कुतः क्वचित् अशिवं (भवति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रभो O Lord, महन्मनस्तः originating in the hearts of exalted souls, मुखनिःसृतं flowing through their mouths, देहम्भृतां for embodied beings, देहकृदस्मृतिच्छिदं destroying the body-consciousness and forgetfulness of self, त्वच्चरणाम्बुजासवं the nectar of Your lotus feet, ये कर्णपुटैः अलं पिबन्ति who drink fully through the cups of their ears, तेषां for them, कुतः क्वचित् अशिवं whence can any inauspiciousness arise anywhere.

Simple Meaning: The kings replied: O Lord! How could any inauspiciousness or misfortune ever touch those who drink to their heart's content, through the cups of their ears, the divine nectar of Your lotus feet—a nectar that flows from the hearts and mouths of great saintly devotees and completely severs the deep-rooted ignorance and body-consciousness of living entities?

हित्वाऽऽत्मधामविधुतात्मकृतत्र्यवस्थमानन्दसम्प्लवमखण्डमकुण्ठबोधम् ।
कालोपसृष्टनिगमावन आत्तयोगमायाकृतिं परमहंसगतिं नताः स्म ॥10.86.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: हित्वा having set aside, आत्म-धाम Your own abode, विधुत-आत्म-कृत-त्रि-अवस्थम् wherein the three material states (of waking, dreaming, and sleep) created by Your illusion are dispelled, आनन्द-सम्प्लवम् an overflowing ocean of bliss, अखण्डम् infinite, अकुण्ठ-बोधम् unobstructed knowledge, काल-उपसृष्ट-निगम-आवने for the protection of the Vedas threatened by the influence of Time, आत्त-योगमाया-आकृतिम् who has assumed a form through Your internal divine potency Yogamaya, परमहंस-गतिम् the ultimate goal and refuge of the highest self-realized souls Paramahamsas, नताः स्म we offer our humble obeisances

Anvaya: विधुतात्मकृतत्र्यवस्थम् आनन्दसम्प्लवम् अखण्डम् अकुण्ठबोधम् आत्मधाम हित्वा कालोपसृष्टनिगमावने आत्तयोगमायाकृतिं परमहंसगतिं (त्वां) नताः स्म।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विधुतात्मकृतत्र्यवस्थम् Where the three states of material existence are dispelled, आनन्दसम्प्लवम् an ocean of pure bliss, अखण्डम् undivided, अकुण्ठबोधम् of unobstructed knowledge, आत्मधाम Your divine abode, हित्वा leaving aside, कालोपसृष्टनिगमावने to protect the Vedas when threatened by time, आत्तयोगमायाकृतिं assuming a form through Your divine potency Yogamaya, परमहंसगतिं the ultimate destination of Paramahamsas, (त्वां) unto You, नताः स्म we offer our respectful obeisances.

Simple Meaning: You reside in Your supreme abode, which is free from the three material states of consciousness (waking, dreaming, and deep sleep)—an abode of undivided, perfect wisdom and an ocean of endless bliss. Yet, to protect the sacred Vedas whenever they are threatened by the degrading influence of time, You descend into this world, assuming a form through Your internal potency Yogamaya. We bow down unto You, the ultimate refuge of all self-realized Paramahamsas.

ऋषि उवाच
इत्युत्तमश्लोकशिखामणिं जनेष्वभिष्टुवत्स्वन्धककौरवस्त्रियः ।
समेत्य गोविन्दकथा मिथोऽगृणंस्त्रिलोकगीताः शृणु वर्णयामि ते ॥10.83.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऋषिः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति in this manner, उत्तमश्लोक-शिखा-मणिम् Lord Krishna the jewel among those praised with supreme poetry, जनेषु when the people, अभिष्टुवत्सु were offering prayers of praise, अन्धक-कौरव-स्त्रियः the women of the Andhaka and Kaurava dynasties, समेत्य coming together, गोविन्द-कथा talks and stories about Govinda, मिथः mutually among themselves, अगृणन् sang/recounted, त्रि-लोक-गीताः sung throughout the three worlds, शृणु listen, वर्णयामि I shall describe, ते unto you

Anvaya: ऋषिः उवाच — जनेषु इति उत्तमश्लोकशिखामणिम् अभिष्टुवत्सु अन्धककौरवस्त्रियः समेत्य त्रिलोकगीताः गोविन्दकथाः मिथः अगृणन्, (ताः) ते वर्णयामि शृणु।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ऋषिः उवाच The sage Shuka said, जनेषु while the people, इति thus, उत्तमश्लोकशिखामणिम् the crest jewel among all worshipful beings, अभिष्टुवत्सु were praising, अन्धककौरवस्त्रियः the noble ladies of the Andhaka and Kaurava clans, समेत्य gathering together, त्रिलोकगीताः praised across the three worlds, गोविन्दकथाः the narrations of Lord Govinda, मिथः अगृणन् recounted to one another, (ताः) those conversations, ते unto you, वर्णयामि I shall describe, शृणु hear.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: While the assembled kings and devotees were thus glorifying Lord Krishna, the crest jewel of all worshipful personalities, the noble women of the Andhaka and Kaurava dynasties gathered together. They shared and discussed the divine pastimes of Lord Govinda, which are sung throughout all three worlds. Now listen as I describe these conversations to you.

द्रौपदी उवाच
हे वैदर्भ्यच्युतो भद्रे हे जाम्बवति कौसले ।
हे सत्यभामे कालिन्दि शैब्ये रोहिणि लक्ष्मणे ॥10.83.6॥

हे कृष्णपत्न्य एतन्नो ब्रूत वो भगवान् स्वयम् ।
उपयेमे यथा लोकमनुकुर्वन् स्वमायया ॥10.83.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्रौपदी उवाच Draupadi said, हे वैदर्भि O daughter of Vidarbha Rukmini, अच्युतः Krishna, भद्रे O Bhadra, हे जाम्बवति O Jambavati, कौसले O daughter of Kosala Satya, हे सत्यभामे O Satyabhama, कालिन्दि O Kalindi, शैब्ये O daughter of Shaibya - Mitravinda, रोहिणि O Rohini (one of the sixteen thousand queens married after the killing of Narakasura), लक्ष्मणे O Lakshmana, हे कृष्ण-पत्न्यः O wives of Krishna, एतत् this, नः unto us, ब्रूत please tell, वः you, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, स्वयम् Himself, उपयेमे married, यथा in what manner, लोकम् the ways of the ordinary world, अनुकुर्वन् imitating, स्व-मायया by His own divine Maya potency

Anvaya: द्रौपदी उवाच — हे वैदर्भि, हे भद्रे, हे जाम्बवति, हे कौसले, हे सत्यभामे, कालिन्दि, शैब्ये, रोहिणि, लक्ष्मणे, हे कृष्णपत्न्यः, भगवान् अच्युतः स्वयं स्वमायया लोकम् अनुकुर्वन् यथा वः उपयेमे, एतत् नः ब्रूत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: द्रौपदी उवाच Draupadi said, हे वैदर्भि O Rukmini, हे भद्रे O Bhadra, हे जाम्बवति O Jambavati, हे कौसले O Satyama, हे सत्यभामे O Satyabhama, कालिन्दि O Kalindi, शैब्ये O Mitravinda, रोहिणि O Rohini, लक्ष्मणे O Lakshmana, हे कृष्णपत्न्यः O queens of Krishna, भगवान् अच्युतः स्वयं the Supreme Lord Achyuta Himself, स्वमायया through His internal power, लोकम् अनुकुर्वन् imitating human customs, यथा in what manner, वः उपयेमे married you, एतत् this, नः ब्रूत please recount to us.

Simple Meaning: Draupadi said: O noble queens—Rukmini, Bhadra, Jambavati, Satya, Satyabhama, Kalindi, Mitravinda, Rohini, Lakshmana, and O wives of Krishna! Please recount to us how the Supreme Lord Achyuta Himself, while humanly imitating the customs and ways of the world through His own divine Maya potency, came to wed each of you.

रुक्मिणी उवाच
चैद्याय मार्पयितुमुद्यतकार्मुकेषु राजस्वजेयभटशेखरिताङ्घ्रिरेणुः ।
निन्ये मृगेन्द्र इव भागमजावियूथात् तच्छ्रीनिकेतचरणोऽस्तु ममार्चनाय ॥10.83.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: रुक्मिणी उवाच Rukmini said, चैद्याय unto Shishupala - the king of Chedi, मा me, अर्पयितुम् to hand over, उद्यत-कार्मुकेषु with bows raised and ready for battle, राजसु among all the kings, अजेय-भट-शेखरित-अङ्घ्रि-रेणुः He whose foot-dust was borne as a crown upon the heads of invincible warrior kings, निन्ये carried away, मृगेन्द्रः a lion, इव just as, भागम् his rightful share of prey, अजा-अवि-यूथात् from a herd of goats and sheep, तत् His, श्री-निकेत-चरणः feet which are the supreme abode of the goddess of fortune, अस्तु may they be, मम for my, अर्चनाय constant worship and devotion

Anvaya: रुक्मिणी उवाच — चैद्याय मा अर्पयितुम् उद्यतकार्मुकेषु राजसु (सत्सु), अजेयभटशेखरिताङ्घ्रिरेणुः (सः) अजावियूथात् भागं मृगेन्द्रः इव (मा) निन्ये, तत् श्रीनिकेतचरणः मम अर्चनाय अस्तु।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रुक्मिणी उवाच Rukmini said, चैद्याय मा अर्पयितुम् to offer me unto Shishupala, उद्यतकार्मुकेषु राजसु with kings having raised their bows in opposition, अजेयभटशेखरिताङ्घ्रिरेणुः He whose foot-dust is accepted on the heads of unconquerable warriors, अजावियूथात् भागं मृगेन्द्रः इव just as a lion carries off its rightful prey from a herd of goats and sheep, मा निन्ये carried me away, तत् श्रीनिकेतचरणः those lotus feet which are the dwelling place of Goddess Lakshmi, मम अर्चनाय अस्तु may ever be the object of my worship.

Simple Meaning: Rukmini said: When my brother and the rival kings raised their bows, determined to hand me over to Shishupala, Krishna —whose foot-dust is worn on the heads of even the most invincible warriors— carried me away in the presence of all those kings, just as a lion fearlessly claims his rightful share from amidst a flock of goats and sheep. May those lotus feet of His, which are the eternal abode of Goddess Lakshmi, forever remain the object of my worship.

सत्यभामा उवाच
यो मे सनाभिवधतप्तहृदा ततेनलिप्ताभिशापमपमार्ष्टुमुपाजहार ।
जित्वर्क्षराजमथ रत्नमदात् स तेन भीतः पितादिशत मां प्रभवेऽपि दत्ताम् ॥10.83.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: सत्यभामा उवाच Satyabhama said, यः who (Krishna), मे my, स-नाभि-वध-तप्त-हृदा with a heart grieving and tormented over the death of his brother (Prasena), ततेन by my father (Satrajit), लिप्त-अभिशापम् the false accusation cast upon Him, अपमार्ष्टुम् to erase, उपाजहार retrieved (the Syamantaka jewel), जित्वा having defeated, ऋक्ष-राजम् the king of the bears - Jambavan, अथ then, रत्नम् the jewel, अदात् gave back, सः he Satrajit, तेन by Him (by Krishna), भीतः terrified, पिता my father, आदिशत offered, माम् me, प्रभवे unto the Lord, अपि even though, दत्ताम् previously betrothed to others

Anvaya: सत्यभामा उवाच — मे सनाभिवधतप्तहृदा ततेन लिप्ताभिशापम् अपमार्ष्टुं यः ऋक्षराजं जित्वा रत्नम् उपाजहार, अथ (तस्मै एव) अदात्। तेन भीतः पिता (मां) प्रभवे आदिशत, (अहं) दत्ताम् अपि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सत्यभामा उवाच Satyabhama said, मे सनाभिवधतप्तहृदा by my father whose heart was agonized over his brother's killing, ततेन by father Satrajit, लिप्ताभिशापम् the false accusation leveled against Him, अपमार्ष्टुं in order to remove, यः ऋक्षराजं जित्वा who, having vanquished Jambavan the bear-king, रत्नम् उपाजहार recovered the jewel, अथ and then, (तस्मै एव) अदात् gave it back to my father. तेन भीतः पिता Being afraid of having falsely accused Him, my father, प्रभवे आदिशत unto the Supreme Lord, presented me in marriage, दत्ताम् अपि even though I had already been promised to other suitors.

Simple Meaning: Satyabhama said: My father, Satrajit, grieved deeply over the death of his brother Prasena and falsely accused Krishna of stealing the precious Syamantaka jewel. To clear His name and wipe away this false stigma, Krishna defeated Jambavan, the king of the bears, and retrieved the gem. When Krishna returned the jewel to my father, my father was overcome with fear and shame for having falsely accused the Lord. To appease Him and make amends, he offered me to the Supreme Lord in marriage, even though I had previously been promised to other suitors.

जाम्बवति उवाच
प्राज्ञाय देहकृदमुं निजनाथदेवं सीतापतिं त्रिणवाहान्यमुनाभ्ययुध्यत् ।
ज्ञात्वा परीक्षित उपाहरदर्हणं मां पादौ प्रगृह्य मणिनाहममुष्य दासी ॥10.83.10॥

My father, upon being tested, realizing who He was, grasped His feet in reverence and presented the jewel along with me as an offering of homage. I am His maidservant.

मित्रविन्दा उवाच

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मित्रविन्दा उवाच Mitravinda said, श्वयूथगम् आत्मबलिं His offering surrounded by a pack of dogs, द्विपारिः इव like a lion, यः Who, स्वयंवरे at the swayamvara ceremony, उपेत्य coming, भूपान् the kings, मे अपकुरुतः भ्रातृन् च and my opposing brothers, विजित्य defeating, मां me, श्रिया ओकः स्वपुरं to His city which is the abode of fortune, निन्ये carried away, तस्य His, अङ्घ्र्यवनेजनत्वं the service of washing His feet, मे अनुभवम् अस्तु may be my constant experience.

Simple Meaning: Mitravinda said: Just as a lion claims his rightfully hunted prey from amidst a pack of dogs, He came to my swayamvara assembly, defeated the gathered kings along with my brothers who opposed Him, and carried me away to His city, the residence of the goddess of fortune. May it be my eternal privilege to serve at His lotus feet and wash them.

सत्या उवाच

Simple Meaning: Satya said: To test the valor of royal suitors seeking my hand, my father set up seven fierce bulls endowed with tremendous strength, vigor, and razor-sharp horns. Literal meaning of the anvaya: इत्थं In this way, यः Who, पथि on the way back, राजन्यान् the opposing kings, निर्जित्य defeating, चतुरङ्गिणीं a complete fourfold army, दासीभिः along with maidservants, वीर्यशुल्कां मां me won as a prize for His prowess,  निन्ये carried off, मे my, तद्दास्यम् servitude to Him, अस्तु may be my fortune.

Simple Meaning: Bhadra continued: As I wander through different life forms according to my past karma, my only desire is that birth after birth I may be blessed with the contact of His lotus feet, for that alone bestows the ultimate spiritual welfare upon the soul.

श्रुत्वैतत् सर्वतो भूपा आययुर्मत्पितुः पुरम् ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एतत् श्रुत्वा Hearing this news, सर्वास्त्रशस्त्रतत्त्वज्ञाः expert in the science of all missiles and weapons, सोपाध्यायाः along with their martial teachers, भूपाः the kings, सहस्रशः by the thousands, सर्वतः from all quarters, मत्पितुः my father's, पुरम् to the capital city, आययुः arrived.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: Hearing about this challenge, thousands of kings came from all directions to my father's capital city. They were all master archers expert in the science of missiles and weapons, and they brought along their teachers.

पित्रा सम्पूजिताः सर्वे यथावीर्यं यथावयः ।
आददुः सशरं चापं वेद्धुं पर्षदि मद्धियः ॥10.83.21॥

Word-by-word meaning: पित्रा by my father, सम्पूजिताः duly honored, सर्वे all, यथा-वीर्यम् according to their prowess, यथा-वयः according to their age, आददुः took up, स-शरम् along with an arrow, चापम् the bow, वेद्धुम् to pierce the target, पर्षदि in the assembly, मत्-धियः their minds set on winning me

Anvaya: पित्रा यथावयः यथावीर्यं सम्पूजिताः सर्वे मद्धियः पर्षदि वेद्धुं सशरं चापम् आददुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पित्रा By my father, यथावयः according to their age, यथावीर्यं and according to their strength, सम्पूजिताः being properly worshiped and honored, सर्वे all those kings, मद्धियः with their minds focused on obtaining me, पर्षदि in that assembly, वेद्धुं in order to pierce the target, सशरं चापम् along with an arrow, the bow, आददुः took hold of.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: My father honored all the assembled kings properly according to their age and prowess. Then, with their hearts set on winning my hand, they each stepped forward in the assembly and picked up the bow along with an arrow to pierce the target.

आदाय व्यसृजन् केचित् सज्यं कर्तुमनीश्वराः ।
आकोटिः ज्यां समुत्कृष्य पेतुरेकेऽमुना हताः ॥10.83.22॥

Word-by-word meaning: आदाय picking up, व्यसृजन् dropped, केचित् some, स-ज्यम् strung, कर्तुम् to make, अनीश्वराः unable, आ-कोटिः up to the end, ज्याम् the bowstring, समुत्कृष्य pulling back, पेतुः fell, एके some, अमुना by that bow, हताः struck

Anvaya: केचित् (चापम्) आदाय सज्यं कर्तुम् अनीश्वराः (सन्तः) व्यसृजन्, एके ज्याम् आकोटिः समुत्कृष्य अमुना हताः पेतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: केचित् Some of the kings, (चापम्) आदाय picking up the bow, सज्यं कर्तुम् अनीश्वराः being unable to string it, व्यसृजन् dropped it. एके While others, ज्याम् the bowstring, आकोटिः all the way to the tip of the bow, समुत्कृष्य pulling, अमुना हताः being struck by that bow recoil, पेतुः fell to the ground.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: Some of the kings picked up the bow but dropped it, unable even to string it. Others managed to pull the string toward the tip, but the powerful bow recoiled and struck them, knocking them to the ground.

सज्यं कृत्वापरे वीरा मागधाम्बष्ठचेदिपाः ।
भीमो दुर्योधनः कर्णो नाविन्दंस्तदवस्थितिम् ॥10.83.23॥

Word-by-word meaning: स-ज्यम् strung, कृत्वा having made, अपरे other, वीराः heroes, मागध-अम्बष्ठ-चेदि-पाः the kings of Magadha, Ambastha and Chedi, भीमः Bhima, दुर्योधनः Duryodhana, कर्णः Karna, न did not, अविन्दन् find, तत्-अवस्थितिम् its location (the location of the fish target in the reflection)

Anvaya: अपरे वीराः मागधाम्बष्ठचेदिपाः भीमः दुर्योधनः कर्णः (चापं) सज्यं कृत्वा तदवस्थितिं न अविन्दन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अपरे वीराः Other heroic warriors, मागधाम्बष्ठचेदिपाः the rulers of Magadha, Ambastha and Chedi, भीमः Bhima, दुर्योधनः Duryodhana, कर्णः and Karna, (चापं) सज्यं कृत्वा having successfully strung the bow, तदवस्थितिं the position of the target fish, न अविन्दन् could not locate in the reflection.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: Other great heroic warriors—including the kings of Magadha, Ambastha, and Chedi, along with Bhima, Duryodhana, and Karna—did manage to string the bow. However, they were unable to locate the target's position in the water's reflection.

मत्स्याभासं जले वीक्ष्य ज्ञात्वा च तदवस्थितिम् ।
पार्थो यत्तोऽसृजत् बाणं नाच्छिनत् पस्पृशे परम् ॥10.83.24॥

Word-by-word meaning: मत्स्य-आभासम् the reflection of the fish, जले in the water, वीक्ष्य having seen, ज्ञात्वा having understood, च and, तत्-अवस्थितिम् its exact position, पार्थः Arjuna, यत्तः with great concentration, असृजत् released, बाणम् the arrow, न did not, अच्छिनत् cut/pierce, पस्पृशे only touched, परम् merely

Anvaya: पार्थः जले मत्स्याभासं वीक्ष्य तदवस्थितिं ज्ञात्वा च यत्तः बाणम् असृजत्, (तत्) न अच्छिनत्, परं पस्पृशे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पार्थः Arjuna, जले in the water, मत्स्याभासं the reflection of the fish, वीक्ष्य looking at, तदवस्थितिं its position, ज्ञात्वा च and understanding, यत्तः बाणम् असृजत् carefully, shot the arrow, (तत्) it, न अच्छिनत् did not pierce, परं पस्पृशे merely grazed it.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: Arjuna looked at the reflection of the fish in the water and accurately determined its position. With full concentration, he shot his arrow at the target, but the arrow did not pierce the fish; it merely grazed it.

राजन्येषु निवृत्तेषु भग्नमानेषु मानिषु ।
भगवान् धनुरादाय् सज्यं कृत्वाथ लीलया ॥10.83.25॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजन्याेषु the kings, निवृत्तेषु having desisted, भग्न-मानेषु their pride broken, मानिषु proud men, भगवान् the Supreme Lord Krishna, धनुः the bow, आदाय taking up, स-ज्यम् strung, कृत्वा having made, अथ then, लीलया effortlessly as play

Anvaya: मानिषु राजन्याेषु भग्नमानेषु निवृत्तेषु (सत्सु), अथ भगवान् धनुः आदाय लीलया सज्यं कृत्वा…

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मानिषु When the proud, राजन्याेषु heroic kings, भग्नमानेषु with their vanity shattered, निवृत्तेषु desisted from trying, अथ then, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, धनुः आदाय taking the bow, लीलया playfully, सज्यं कृत्वा stringing it…

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: When all those proud kings had failed and retired with their vanity shattered, the Supreme Lord Krishna picked up the bow and effortlessly strung it as if playing a game.

तस्मिन् सन्धाय विशिखं मत्स्यं वीक्ष्य सकृज्जले ।
छित्त्वेषुणापातयत्तं सूर्ये चाभिजिति स्थिते ॥10.83.26॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मिन् onto that bow, सन्धाय fitting, विशिखम् an arrow, मत्स्यम् the fish, वीक्ष्य looking at, सकृत् once, जले in the water, छित्त्वा cutting through, इषुणा with the arrow, आपातयत् brought down, तम् it, सूर्ये when the sun, च and, अभिजिति in the Abhijit muhurta, स्थिते was situated

Anvaya: (भगवान्) तस्मिन् विशिखं सन्धाय जले सकृत् मत्स्यं वीक्ष्य सूर्ये अभिजिति स्थिते च इषुणा तं छित्त्वा आपातयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (भगवान्) Lord Krishna, तस्मिन् on that bow, विशिखं an arrow, सन्धाय placing, जले in the reflection of the water, सकृत् only once, मत्स्यं at the fish target, वीक्ष्य looking, सूर्ये when the sun, अभिजिति स्थिते was positioned in the auspicious Abhijit constellation, च and, इषुणा with His arrow, तं the fish, छित्त्वा piercing, आपातयत् brought it down.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: Fitting an arrow onto the bow, Lord Krishna looked at the reflection of the fish in the water just once. Then, at midday during the auspicious Abhijit muhurta, He shot His arrow, pierced the target, and brought the fish down.

दिवि दुन्दुभयो नेदुर्जयशबदयुता भुवि ।
देवाश्च कुसुमासारान् मुमुचुर्हर्षविह्वलाः ॥10.83.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: दिवि in the heavens, दुन्दुभयः kettle-drums, नेदुः resounded, जय-शब्द-युताः accompanied by shouts of victory, भुवि on the earth, देवाः the devas, च and, कुसुम-आसारान् showers of flowers, मुमुचुः released, हर्ष-विह्वलाः overwhelmed with joy

Anvaya: दिवि दुन्दुभयः नेदुः, भुवि जयशब्दयुताः (अभवन्), देवाः च हर्षविह्वलाः कुसुमासारान् मुमुचुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दिवि In the heavens, दुन्दुभयः divine drums, नेदुः sounded, भुवि on the earth, जयशब्दयुताः accompanied by cries of victory, देवाः च and the devas, हर्षविह्वलाः overcome with delight, कुसुमासारान् torrents of blossoms, मुमुचुः showered down.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: Celestial drums resounded in the heavens, and shouts of victory echoed across the earth. Overwhelmed with delight, the devas showered blossoms down upon the scene.

तद् रङ्गमाविशमहं कलनूपुराभ्यां पद्भ्यां प्रगृह्य कनकोज्ज्वलरत्नमालाम् ।
नूत्ने निवीय परिधाय च कौशिकाग्र्ये सव्रीडहासवदना कबरीधृतस्रक् ॥10.83.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that, रङ्गम् assembly arena, आविशम् entered, अहम् I, कल-नूपुराभ्याम् with softly tinkling anklets, पद्भ्याम् with my feet, प्रगृह्य clutching, कनक-उज्ज्वल-रत्न-मालाम् a necklace of lustrous gems set in gold, नूत्ने brand-new, निवीय wrapping around the waist, परिधाय and wearing, च and, कौशिक-अग्र्ये exquisite silk garments, स-व्रीड-हास-वदना with a face bearing a shy smile, कबरी-धृत-स्रक् with a garland adornment in my woven hair

Anvaya: अहं नूत्ने कौशिकाग्र्ये निवीय परिधाय च कबरीधृतस्रक् सव्रीडहासवदना कलनूपुराभ्यां पद्भ्यां कनकोज्ज्वलरत्नमालां प्रगृह्य तत् रङ्गम् आविशम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अहं I, नूत्ने new, कौशिकाग्र्ये finest silk robes, निवीय wrapping, परिधाय च and donning, कबरीधृतस्रक् with flowers woven into my braided hair, सव्रीडहासवदना with a face adorned with a bashful smile, कलनूपुराभ्यां featuring softly tinkling anklets, पद्भ्यां with my feet, कनकोज्ज्वलरत्नमालां holding a necklace of radiant jewels set in gold, प्रगृह्य carrying in my hands, तत् that, रङ्गम् marriage arena, आविशम् stepped into.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: Attired in the finest brand-new silk garments, with flowers woven in my hair and a bashful smile illuminating my face, I stepped into the assembly arena. As my anklets tinkled softly with each step, I carried in my hands a brilliant garland of jewels set in gold.

उन्नीय वक्त्रमुरुकुन्तलकुण्डलत्विड् गण्डस्थलं शिशिरहासकटाक्षमोक्षैः ।
राज्ञो निरीक्ष्य परितः शनकैर्मुरारेरंसेऽनुरक्तहृदया निदधे स्वमालाम् ॥10.83.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: उन्नीय lifting up, वक्त्रम् my face, उरु-कुन्तल-कुण्डल-त्विड् glowing with abundant hair locks and shining earrings, गण्ड-स्थलम् my cheeks, शिशिर-हास-कटाक्ष-मोक्षैः with cool smile and side-glances, राज्ञः the kings, निरीक्ष्य looking around at, परितः all around, शनकैः slowly, मुरारेः of Lord Murari (Krishna), असे on the shoulder, अनुरक्त-हृदया my heart deeply attached to Him, निदधे I placed, स्व-मालाम् my garland

Anvaya: (अहम्) उरुकुन्तलकुण्डलत्विड् गण्डस्थलं वक्त्रम् उन्नीय शिशिरहासकटाक्षमोक्षैः परितः राज्ञः निरीक्ष्य शनकैः अनुरक्तहृदया मुरारेः असे स्वमालां निदधे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अहम् I, उरुकुन्तलकुण्डलत्विड् गण्डस्थलं my face whose cheeks were glowing with abundant locks of hair and shining earrings, वक्त्रम् face, उन्नीय lifting up, शिशिरहासकटाक्षमोक्षैः with a cool smile and side-glances, परितः all around, राज्ञः at the assembled kings, निरीक्ष्य looking, शनकैः slowly, अनुरक्तहृदया with a heart deeply attached in love, मुरारेः of Lord Murari, असे upon the shoulder, स्वमालां my own garland, निदधे placed.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: Raising my face, which was illuminated by abundant hair locks and glinting earrings resting against my cheeks, I glanced around at the assembled kings with a cool smile and side-glances. Then, with my heart completely attached to Lord Murari in deep love, I slowly placed my garland around His shoulder.

तावन्मृदङ्गपटहाः शङ्खभेर्यानकादयः ।
निनेदुर्नटनर्तक्यो ननृतुर्गायका जगुः ॥10.83.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: तावत् at that moment, मृदङ्ग-पटहाः mridanga drums and pataha drums, शङ्ख-भेरी-आनकादयः conch shells kettledrums anaka drums and other instruments, निनेदुः resounded, नट-नर्तक्यः actors and female dancers, ननृतुः danced, गायकाः singers, जगुः sang

Anvaya: तावत् मृदङ्गपटहाः शङ्खभेर्यानकादयः निनेदुः, नटनर्तक्यः ननृतुः, गायकाः जगुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तावत् Right at that moment, मृदङ्गपटहाः mridanga and pataha drums, शङ्खभेर्यानकादयः conch shells, kettledrums, anaka drums, and other instruments, निनेदुः resounded loudly, नटनर्तक्यः male actors and female dancers, ननृतुः danced, गायकाः singers, जगुः sang songs of joy.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: Immediately upon my placing the garland on Him, musical instruments including mridanga drums, drums, conchshells, kettledrums, and anaka drums resounded all around. Dramatic performers and female dancers began to dance, while royal singers sang praises in high joy.

एवं वृते भगवति मयेशे नृपयूथपाः ।
न सेहिरे याज्ञसेनि स्पर्धन्तो हृच्छयातुराः ॥10.83.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this manner, वृते being chosen, भगवति the Supreme Lord, मया by me, ईशे the Supreme Master, नृप-यूथपाः the prominent leaders among the kings, न did not, सेहिरे tolerate, याज्ञसेनि O Draupadi (daughter of Yagnasena - Drupada), स्पर्धन्तः competing, हृच्छय-आतुराः afflicted with lusty desires

Anvaya: याज्ञसेनि! मया ईशे भगवति एवं वृते (सति) हृच्छयातुराः स्पर्धन्तः नृपयूथपाः न सेहिरे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: याज्ञसेनि O Draupadi - daughter of Yagnasena, मया by me, ईशे the Supreme Controller, भगवति the Supreme Lord Krishna, एवं in this way, वृते being accepted as husband, हृच्छयातुराः sickened with worldly lust, स्पर्धन्तः competing, नृपयूथपाः the principal kings, न सेहिरे could not tolerate it.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmana continued: O Draupadi, when I had thus chosen the Supreme Lord Krishna as my husband, those arrogant kings—who were already burning with lust and rivalrous ambition—could not tolerate what had transpired.

मां तावद् रथमारोप्य हयरत्नचतुष्टयम् ।
शार्ङ्गमुद्यम्य सन्नद्धस्तस्थावाजौ चतुर्भुजः ॥10.83.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: माम् me, तावत् thereupon, रथम् onto His chariot, आरोप्य placing, हय-रत्न-चतुष्टयम् drawn by four jewels among horses, शार्ङ्गम् His Sharnga bow, उद्यम्य raising, सन्नद्धः armored, तस्थौ stood ready, आजौ on the battlefield, चतुर्भुजः four-armed

Anvaya: (भगवान्) चतुर्भुजः हयरत्नचतुष्टयं रथं माम् आरोप्य तावत् सन्नद्धः शार्ङ्गम् उद्यम्य आजौ तस्थौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (भगवान्) चतुर्भुजः Lord Krishna who has four arms, हयरत्नचतुष्टयं pulled by four excellent horses, रथं onto His chariot, माम् me, आरोप्य having seated, तावत् then, सन्नद्धः equipped in armor, शार्ङ्गम् His bow Sharnga, उद्यम्य raising aloft, आजौ in the battleground, तस्थौ stood.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: Placing me upon His chariot, which was yoked to four splendid horses, Lord Krishna, who has four-arms, donned His armor, raised His bow Sharnga, and stood on the battlefield, ready to face the opposition.

दारुकश्चोदयामास काञ्चनोपस्करं रथम् ।
मिषतां भूभुजां राज्ञि मृगाणां मृगराडिव ॥10.83.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: दारुकः Daruka, च and, चोदयामास drove, काञ्चन-उपस्करम् furnished with gold ornaments, रथम् the chariot, मिषताम् while looking on, भूभुजाम् of the kings, राज्ञि O Queen, मृगाणाम् among beasts, मृग-राट् इव like the king of beasts (a lion)

Anvaya: राज्ञि! दारुकः मिषतां भूभुजां (मध्यतः) मृगाणां मृगराट् इव काञ्चनोपस्करं रथं चोदयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राज्ञि O Queen, दारुकः Daruka, मिषतां looking helplessly on, भूभुजां of those kings, middle of, मृगाणां among animals, मृगराट् इव like a lion, काञ्चनोपस्करं adorned with gold fittings, रथं the chariot, चोदयामास drove forward.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: O Queen Draupadi, just as a lion moves fearlessly among animals, Daruka drove Lord Krishna's gold-ornamented chariot right through the midst of those kings while they looked on helplessly.

तेऽन्वसज्जन्त राजन्या निषेद्धुं पथि केचन ।
संयत्ता उद्धृतेष्वासा ग्रामसिंहा यथा हरिम् ॥10.83.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: ते those, अन्वसज्जन्त pursued, राजन्याः kings, निषेद्धुम् to stop, पथि on the road, केचन some, संयत्ताः prepared for battle, उद्धृत-इष्वासः with uplifted bows, ग्राम-सिंहाः village dogs, यथा just as, हरिम् a lion

Anvaya: केचन राजन्याः पथि निषेद्धुं संयत्ताः उद्धृतेष्वासः (सन्तः) ग्रामसिंहाः हरिं यथा ते अन्वसज्जन्त।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: केचन राजन्याः Some of the kings, पथि on the path, निषेद्धुं to obstruct and stop Him, संयत्ताः fully prepared, उद्धृतेष्वासः with weapons and bows raised, ग्रामसिंहाः village dogs, हरिं a lion, यथा just as, ते they, अन्वसज्जन्त gave chase behind Him.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: Some of those rival kings armored themselves and gave chase on the road to block His path, raising their weapons and drawn bows like village dogs trying to check a lion.

ते शार्ङ्गच्युतबाणौघैः कृत्तबाह्वङ्घ्रिकन्धराः ।
निपेतुः प्रधने केचिदेके सन्त्यज्य दुद्रुवुः ॥10.83.35॥

Word-by-word meaning: ते they, शार्ङ्ग-च्युत-बाण-ओघैः by the torrents of arrows shot from His Sharnga bow, कृत्त-बाहु-अङ्घ्रि-कन्धराः having their arms legs and necks severed, निपेतुः fell down, प्रधने in the battle, केचित् some, एके others, सन्त्यज्य abandoning the fight, दुद्रुवुः fled away

Anvaya: ते शार्ङ्गच्युतबाणौघैः कृत्तबाह्वङ्घ्रिकन्धराः प्रधने निपेतुः, केचित् एके (युद्धं) सन्त्यज्य दुद्रुवुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ते Those kings, शार्ङ्गच्युतबाणौघैः by the shower of arrows discharged from His Sharnga bow, कृत्तबाह्वङ्घ्रिकन्धराः with severed arms legs and necks, प्रधने on the battle site, निपेतुः fell dead, केचित् एके while some others, (युद्धं) the battle, सन्त्यज्य abandoning, दुद्रुवुः fled.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: As Lord Krishna released a relentless torrent of arrows from His Sharnga bow, many of those attacking kings had their arms, legs, and necks severed and fell dead on the battlefield. The remaining kings abandoned the fight altogether and fled for their lives.

ततः पुरीं यदुपतिरत्यलङ्कृतां रविच्छदध्वजपटचित्रतोरणां ।
कुशस्थलीं दिवि भुवि चाभिसंस्तुतां समाविशत्तरणिरिव स्वकेतनम् ॥10.83.36॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, पुरीम् city, यदु-पतिः the Lord of the Yadus (Krishna), अति-अलङ्कृताम् richly decorated, रवि-छद-ध्वज-पट-चित्र-तोरणाम् adorned with banners blocking out the sun and colorful archways, कुशस्थलीम् Kushasthali (Dwaraka), दिवि in heaven, भुवि on earth, च and, अभिसंस्तुताम् highly praised, समाविशत् entered, तरणिः the sun, इव like, स्व-केतनम् its own abode

Anvaya: ततः यदुपतिः अत्यलङ्कृतां रविच्छदध्वजपटचित्रतोरणां दिवि भुवि च अभिसंस्तुतां कुशस्थलीं पुरीं तरणिः स्वकेतनम् इव समाविशत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः Then, यदुपतिः the Supreme Lord of the Yadus Krishna, अत्यलङ्कृतां exceptionally decorated, रविच्छदध्वजपटचित्रतोरणां featuring archways with colorful banners and flags that screened the sun, दिवि भुवि च in the celestial realms as well as on earth, अभिसंस्तुतां universally glorified, कुशस्थलीं पुरीं the city of Kushasthali, तरणिः the sun, स्वकेतनम् इव just as it enters its own house or constellation, समाविशत् entered triumphantly.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: Then Lord Krishna, the Master of the Yadus, entered the grand city of Kushasthali (Dwaraka) which is celebrated across heaven and earth. The city was gorgeously decorated with colorful archways and sweeping banners that screened the sunlight, and the Lord entered it triumphantly, just as the sun enters its own zodiacal abode.

पिता मे पूजयामास सुहृत्सम्बन्धिबान्धवान् ।
महार्हवासोऽलङ्कारैः शय्यासनपरिच्छदैः ॥10.83.37॥

Word-by-word meaning: पिता father, मे my, पूजयामास worshipped and honored, सुहृत्-सम्बन्धि-बान्धवान् friends relatives and kinsmen, महार्ह-वासः-अलङ्कारैः with costly garments and ornaments, शय्या-आसन-परिच्छदैः with beds, furniture and royal paraphernalia

Anvaya: मे पिता सुहृत्सम्बन्धिबान्धवान् महार्हवासोऽलङ्कारैः शय्यासनपरिच्छदैः पूजयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मे पिता My father, सुहृत्सम्बन्धिबान्धवान् the friends, relatives, and kinsmen of the Lord, महार्हवासोऽलङ्कारैः with valuable clothes and jewelries, शय्यासनपरिच्छदैः along with beds, seats, and personal paraphernalia, पूजयामास honored.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: My father honored his friends, relatives, and kinsmen with costly garments, precious ornaments, beds, seats, and fine royal furnishings.

दासीभिः सर्वसम्पद्भिर्भटेभरथवाजिभिः ।
आयुधानि महार्हाणि ददौ पूर्णस्य भक्तितः ॥10.83.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: दासीभिः with maidservants, सर्व-सम्पद्भिः with all kinds of opulences and wealth, भट-इभ-रथ-वाजिभिः with soldiers, elephants, chariots and horses, आयुधानि weapons, महार्हाणि very costly, ददौ he gave, पूर्णस्य to the self-sufficient Lord, भक्तितः out of devotion

Anvaya: (मे पिता) दासीभिः सर्वसम्पद्भिः भटेभरथवाजिभिः महार्हाणि आयुधानि च भक्तितः पूर्णस्य (हरये) ददौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (मे पिता) My father, दासीभिः with maidservants, सर्वसम्पद्भिः along with all forms of royal wealth, भटेभरथवाजिभिः with foot soldiers, elephants, chariots, and steeds, महार्हाणि आयुधानि च and highly valuable arms, भक्तितः out of devotion, पूर्णस्य to the inherently complete Lord Krishna, ददौ presented.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa continued: Out of pure devotion, my father presented all these gifts to Lord Krishna—who is eternally self-sufficient and complete in Himself—giving maidservants, vast wealth, infantry, elephants, chariots, horses, and priceless weapons as part of the royal dowry.

आत्मारामस्य तस्येमा वयं वै गृहदासिकाः ।
सर्वसङ्गनिवृत्त्याद्धा तपसा च बभूविम ॥10.83.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: आत्म-रामस्य of Him who is completely self-satisfied within Himself, तस्य of that Lord, इमाः these, वयम् we, वै indeed, गृह-दासिकाः household maidservants, सर्व-सङ्ग-निवृत्त्या by complete freedom from all worldly attachments, अद्धा directly, तपसा by penance and austerity, च and, बभूविम have become

बभूविम अस् , कर्तरि लिट्लकारः (परस्मैपदम्) , उत्तमपुरुषः , बहुवचनम् src. Dhatupatha, Ashtadhyayi

Anvaya: सर्वसङ्गनिवृत्त्या तपसा च वयम् आत्मारामस्य तस्य (कृष्णस्य) इमाः गृहदासिकाः अद्धा बभूविम।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सर्वसङ्गनिवृत्त्या Through total detachment from mundane inclinations, तपसा च and through austerity, वयम् we, आत्मारामस्य of Him who is fully self-sufficient and delights in the Self, तस्य of that Lord, इमाः these women, गृहदासिकाः humble household maidservants, अद्धा directly, बभूविम have become.

Simple Meaning: Lakshmanaa concluded her testimony to Draupadi: Thus, by completely relinquishing all worldly attachments and undergoing devotional austerity, we have been blessed to become the humble maidservants in the home of Lord Krishna, who is inherently self-satisfied and complete within Himself.

महिष्यः ऊचुः
भौमं निहत्य सगणं युधि तेन रुद्धा ज्ञात्वाथ नः क्षितिजये जितराजकन्याः ।
निर्मुच्य संसृतिविमोक्षमनुस्मरन्तीः पादाम्बुजं परिणिनाय य आप्तकामः ॥10.83.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: महिष्यः ऊचुः the other queens said, भौमम् Narakasura (Bhaumasura), निहत्य having slain, स-गणम् along with his horde, युधि in battle, तेन by him, रुद्धाः imprisoned, ज्ञात्वा learning about, अथ then, नः us, क्षितिजये during his conquest of the earth, जित-राज-कन्याः royal princesses captured from defeated kings, निर्मुच्य releasing, संसृति-विमोक्षम् the liberator from the cycle of birth and death, अनुस्मरन्तीः constantly remembering, पाद-अम्बुजम् His lotus feet, परिणिनाय wedded, यः who, आप्त-कामः though possessing all desires fulfilled

Anvaya: महिष्यः ऊचुः— यः आप्तकामः (अपि) युधि सगणं भौमं निहत्य, क्षितिजये तेन रुद्धाः नः जितराजकन्याः ज्ञात्वा, निर्मुच्य संसृतिविमोक्षं (तस्य) पादाम्बुजम् अनुस्मरन्तीः (अस्मान्) परिणिनाय।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महिष्यः ऊचुः The remaining queens said, यः He who, आप्तकामः (अपि) although fully self-satisfied and having all desires fulfilled, युधि in battle, सगणं भौमं Narakasura along with his associates, निहत्य after killing, क्षितिजये during his world conquest, तेन रुद्धाः imprisoned by Narakasura, नः us, जितराजकन्याः princesses captured from conquered rulers, ज्ञात्वा learning, निर्मुच्य releasing us from captivity, संसृतिविमोक्षं the granter of liberation from worldly existence, (तस्य) पादाम्बुजम् His lotus feet, अनुस्मरन्तीः us who were constantly meditating upon Him, (अस्मान्) us, परिणिनाय accepted as His wives.

Simple Meaning: The remaining queens said: After Lord Krishna killed Narakasura and his army in battle, He learned about us—the thousands of royal princesses whom Narakasura had captured during his conquests and kept in captivity. Upon setting us free, He accepted us as His wives because we were constantly meditating upon His lotus feet, the ultimate source of liberation from material existence—even though He is eternally self-satisfied and needs nothing.

न वयं साध्वि साम्राज्यं स्वाराज्यं भौज्यमप्युत ।
वैराज्यं पारमेष्ठ्यं च आनन्त्यं वा हरेः परम् ॥10.83.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, वयम् we, साध्वि O chaste Draupadi, साम्राज्यम् sovereignty over the earth, स्वाराज्यम् lordship over heaven, भौज्यम् enjoying vast royal luxuries, अपि uta or even, वैराज्यम् ruler over mystic powers, पारमेष्ठ्यम् the exaltation of Lord Brahma, च and, आनन्त्यम् liberation or immortality, वा or, हरेः of Lord Hari, परम् supreme devotion

Anvaya: साध्वि! वयं साम्राज्यं स्वाराज्यं भौज्यं वैराज्यं पारमेष्ठ्यं च आनन्त्यं वा हरेः परम् (पदम्) अपि न (वाञ्छामः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: साध्वि O virtuous lady, वयं we, साम्राज्यं earthly empire, स्वाराज्यं heavenly lordship, भौज्यं sensory luxuries, वैराज्यं ruler over mystic domains, पारमेष्ठ्यं position of Brahma, च and, आनन्त्यं वा or liberation, हरेः of Lord Hari, परम् ultimate status, अपि even, न do not desire.

Simple Meaning: The queens continued: O chaste Draupadi, we do not desire earthly empire, heavenly lordship, royal opulences, mystic powers, the exalted seat of Lord Brahma, or even liberation itself, or even The Lord's abode.

कामायामह एतस्य श्रीमत्पादरजः श्रियः ।
कुचकुङ्कुमगन्धाढ्यं मूर्ध्ना वोढुं गदाभृतः ॥10.83.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: कामयामहे we desire, एतस्य His, श्रीमत्-पाद-रजः the glorious dust of His lotus feet, श्रियः of Goddess Lakshmi, कुच-कुङ्कुम-गन्ध-आढ्यम् enriched with the fragrance of the saffron powder from her bosom, मूर्ध्ना on our heads, वोढुम् to bear, गदा-भृतः of the wielder of the mace (Lord Krishna)

Anvaya: (वयं) श्रियः कुचकुङ्कुमगन्धाढ्यं गदाभृतः एतस्य श्रीमत्पादरजः मूर्ध्ना वोढुं कामयामहे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (वयं) We, श्रियः of Goddess Lakshmi, कुचकुङ्कुमगन्धाढ्यं rich with the fragrance of saffron from the chest, गदाभृतः of Lord Krishna who wields the mace, एतस्य His, श्रीमत्पादरजः divine foot-dust, मूर्ध्ना upon our heads, वोढुं to carry, कामयामहे desire.

Simple Meaning: The queens continued: Our only desire is to forever carry upon our heads the glorious dust of the lotus feet of Lord Krishna, wielder of the mace—that divine foot-dust enriched by the fragrant saffron from Goddess Lakshmi’s bosom.

व्रजस्त्रियो यद् वाञ्छन्ति पुलिन्द्यस्तृणवीरुधः ।
गावश्चारयतो गोपाः पादस्पर्शं महात्मनः ॥10.83.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: व्रज-स्त्रियः the women of Vraja (the gopis), यत् which, वाञ्छन्ति long for, पुलिन्द्यः the Pulinda tribal women, तृण-वीरुधः the grass and creeping plants (of Vrindavan), गावः the cows, चारयतः while pasturing, गोपाः the cowherd boys, पाद-स्पर्शम् the touch of the feet, महात्मनः of the glorious Supreme Soul (Lord Krishna)

Anvaya: (वयं) व्रजस्त्रियः पुलिन्द्यः तृणवीरुधः गावः चारयतः गोपाः (च) महात्मनः पादस्पर्शं यत् वाञ्छन्ति (तत् एव वाञ्छामः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (वयं) We, व्रजस्त्रियः the damsels of Vraja, पुलिन्द्यः the maidens of the Pulinda tribe, तृणवीरुधः and the blades of grass and creepers of Vrindavan, गावः चारयतः गोपाः the cow-herd boys tending the cows, महात्मनः of the elevated Supreme Spirit Lord Krishna, पादस्पर्शं touch of the lotus feet, यत् वाञ्छन्ति which long for, (तत् एव वाञ्छामः) that same touch of the lotus-feet, we desire.

Simple Meaning: The queens concluded: We long only for the sacred touch of the lotus feet of the Supreme Lord Krishna—the very same touch cherished by the gopis of Vraja, the Pulinda maidens, the cowherd boys who roamed with Him while pasturing the cows, and even the plants and blades of grass of Vrindavan.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “कृष्णपत्नीनां द्रौपद्याः च संवादः नाम” त्र्यशीतितमः अध्यायः ॥83॥

Thus ends the eighty-third discourse named “Conversation between Krishna's wives and Draupadi”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
