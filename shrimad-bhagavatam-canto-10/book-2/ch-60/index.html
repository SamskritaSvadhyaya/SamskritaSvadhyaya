A dialogue between Krishna and Rukmini

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

कृष्णरुक्मिणीसंवादः नाम​​ षष्टित्तमः अध्यायः

(59 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
कर्हिचित् सुखमासीनं स्वतल्पस्थं जगद्गुरुम् ।
पतिं पर्यचरद् भैष्मी व्यजनेन सखीजनैः ॥10.60.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, कर्हिचित् once, सुखम् comfortably, आसीनम् seated, स्व-तल्प-स्थम् resting on His own bed, जगत्-गुरुम् the teacher/master of the universe, पतिम् her husband, पर्यचरत् served, भैष्मी Rukmini (the daughter of Bhishmaka), व्यजनेन with a fan, सखी-जनैः along with her maidservants

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— कर्हिचित् स्वतल्पस्थं सुखम् आसीनं जगद्गुरुं पतिं भैष्मी सखीजनैः व्यजनेन पर्यचरत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, कर्हिचित् स्वतल्पस्थं सुखम् आसीनं जगद्गुरुं पतिं once, her husband, the master of the universe, who was comfortably seated on His bed, भैष्मी सखीजनैः व्यजनेन पर्यचरत् Rukmini, along with her maidservants, served with a fan.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Once, as Krishna—the master of the universe—was seated comfortably upon His bed in her palace, Rukmini, the daughter of Bhishmaka, accompanied by her maidservants, personally rendered service to her husband by gently fanning Him.

यस्त्वेतल्लीलया विश्वं सृजत्यत्त्यवतीश्वरः ।
स हि जातः स्वसेतूनां गोपीथाय यदुष्वजः ॥10.60.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः who, तु indeed, एतत् this, लीलया effortlessly/by playful pastime, विश्वम् universe, सृजति creates, अत्ति destroys, अवति protects, ईश्वरः the Supreme Controller, सः He, हि indeed, जातः born/manifested, स्व-सेतूनाम् of His own principles/religious laws, गोपीथाय for the protection, यदुषु among the Yadus, अजः the unborn Lord

Anvaya: यः ईश्वरः तु लीलया एतत् विश्वं सृजति अवति अत्ति, सः अजः हि स्वसेतूनां गोपीथाय यदुषु जातः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यः ईश्वरः तु लीलया एतत् विश्वं सृजति अवति अत्ति He, the Supreme Lord, who indeed effortlessly creates, protects, and destroys this universe, सः अजः हि स्वसेतूनां गोपीथाय यदुषु जातः that unborn Lord has indeed appeared among the Yadus for the protection of His own laws.

Simple Meaning: Although He is the unborn Supreme Lord who effortlessly creates, sustains, and dissolves this entire cosmos as a mere pastime, He took birth among the Yadu dynasty for the purpose of preserving and protecting the eternal principles of righteousness.

तस्मिन्नन्तर्गृहे भ्राजन्मुक्तादामविलम्बिना ।
विराजिते वितानेन दीपैर्मणिमयैरपि ॥10.60.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मिन् in that, अन्तः-गृहे inner chamber / bedchamber, भ्राजत्-मुक्ता-दाम-विलम्बिना hanging with brilliant strands of pearls, विराजिते splendidly adorned, वितानेन with a canopy, दीपैः with lamps, मणि-मयैः made of precious jewels, अपि as well

Anvaya: तस्मिन् अन्तःगृहे भ्राजन्मुक्तादामविलम्बिना वितानेन मणिमयैः दीपैः अपि विराजिते (सति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तस्मिन् अन्तःगृहे In that inner chamber, भ्राजन्मुक्तादामविलम्बिना वितानेन adorned with a canopy from which hung glittering strands of pearls, मणिमयैः दीपैः अपि विराजिते and also illuminated by lamps made of precious jewels...

Simple Meaning: The inner bedchamber was splendidly adorned with a grand canopy decorated with dangling, brilliant strands of pearls, and it was brightly illuminated by exquisite lamps fashioned from precious jewels.

मल्लिकादामभिः पुष्पैर्द्विरेफकुलनादितैः ।
जालरन्ध्रप्रविष्टैश्च गोभिश्चन्द्रमसोऽमलैः ॥10.60.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: मल्लिका-दामभिः with garlands of jasmine, पुष्पैः with flowers, द्विरेफ-कुल-नादितैः echoing with the humming of swarms of bees, जालरन्ध्र-प्रविष्टैः entering through the openings of the latticed windows, च and, गोभिः rays, चन्द्रमसः of the moon, अमलैः pure/spotless

Anvaya: (तस्मिन् अन्तःगृहे) द्विरेफकुलनादितैः मल्लिकादामभिः पुष्पैः (च), जालरन्ध्रप्रविष्टैः चन्द्रमसः अमलैः गोभिः च (विराजिते सति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (तस्मिन् अन्तःगृहे) In that inner chamber, द्विरेफकुलनादितैः मल्लिकादामभिः पुष्पैः (च) adorned with flowers and jasmine garlands resounding with the humming of bee swarms, जालरन्ध्रप्रविष्टैः चन्द्रमसः अमलैः गोभिः च and illuminated by the pure rays of the moon entering through the openings of the latticed windows...

Simple Meaning: The inner chamber was beautifully adorned with fragrant flowers and jasmine garlands that buzzed with swarms of humming bees, and it was soft-lit by the spotless, pure rays of the moon streaming in through the latticed windows.

पारिजातवनामोदवायुनोद्यानशालिना ।
धूपैरगुरुजै राजन् जालरन्ध्रविनिर्गतैः ॥10.60.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: पारिजात-वन-आमोद-वायुना with breeze carrying the fragrance of Parijata forests, उद्यान-शालिना rich with surrounding gardens, धूपैः with incense smoke, अगुरु-जैः arising from aguru (aloeswood), राजन् O King (Parikshit), जालरन्ध्र-विनिर्गतैः wafting out through the openings of the latticed windows

Anvaya: राजन्! पारिजातवनामोदवायुना उद्यानशालिना जालरन्ध्रविनिर्गतैः अगुरुजैः धूपैः च (तस्मिन् अन्तःगृहे विराजिते सति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King Parikshit, पारिजातवनामोदवायुना उद्यानशालिना in that chamber rendered delightful by gardens with breezes carrying the fragrance of Parijata groves, जालरन्ध्रविनिर्गतैः अगुरुजैः धूपैः च and filled with aguru incense smoke wafting out through the openings of the latticed windows...

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, the palace was further enriched by surrounding gardens from which gentle breezes wafted the divine fragrance of Parijata groves. Inside the inner chamber, aromatic incense made of aguru drifted through the room, its fragrant smoke softly escaping through the latticed windows.

पयःफेननिभे शुभ्रे पर्यङ्के कशिपूत्तमे ।
उपतस्थे सुखासीनं जगतामीश्वरं पतिम् ॥10.60.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: पयः-फेन-निभे resembling the foam of milk, शुभ्रे dazzling white, पर्यङ्के on a bedstead, कशिपु-उत्तमे on the finest soft mattress, उपतस्थे served / attended upon, सुख-आसीनम् comfortably seated, जगताम् of all the worlds, ईश्वरम् the Supreme Controller, पतिम् her husband

Anvaya: (सा भैष्मी) पयःफेननिभे शुभ्रे कशिपूत्तमे पर्यङ्के सुखासीनं जगताम् ईश्वरं पतिम् उपतस्थे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सा भैष्मी) That Queen Rukmini, पयःफेननिभे शुभ्रे कशिपूत्तमे पर्यङ्के on a dazzling white, finest mattress resembling the foam of milk on a bedstead, सुखासीनं जगताम् ईश्वरं पतिम् उपतस्थे served her husband, the Supreme Controller of all the worlds, who was seated comfortably.

Simple Meaning: On a dazzling white bed fitted with the softest mattress, as light and gentle as milk foam, sat the Supreme Lord of all the worlds. Queen Rukmini personally attended upon her husband, who was resting there in complete comfort.

वालव्यजनमादाय रत्नदण्डं सखीकरात् ।
तेन वीजयती देवी उपासाञ्चक्र ईश्वरम् ॥10.60.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: वाल-व्यजनम् a fan made of yak-tail hair (chamar), आदाय taking, रत्न-दण्डम् with a handle made of jewels, सखी-करात् from the hand of her maidservant, तेन with that, वीजयती fanning, देवी Queen Rukmini, उपासाञ्चक्रे served / worshipped, ईश्वरम् the Supreme Lord

Anvaya: देवी सखीकरात् रत्नदण्डं वालव्यजनम् आदाय तेन वीजयती ईश्वरम् उपासाञ्चक्रे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: देवी सखीकरात् रत्नदण्डं वालव्यजनम् आदाय Queen Rukmini, having taken from the hand of her maidservant the yak-tail fan with a jeweled handle, तेन वीजयती ईश्वरम् उपासाञ्चक्रे fanning Him with it, served the Supreme Lord.

Simple Meaning: Taking the jewel-handled yak-tail fan (chamar) from the hand of her maidservant, Queen Rukmini began to fan the Supreme Lord, lovingly rendering personal service to Him.

सोपाच्युतं क्वणयती मणिनूपुराभ्यां रेजेऽङ्गुलीयवलयव्यजनाग्रहस्ता ।
वस्त्रान्तगूढकुचकुङ्कुमशोणहारभासा नितम्बधृतया च परार्ध्यकाञ्च्या ॥10.60.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Rukmini), उप-अच्युतम् near Lord Achyuta, क्वणयती making a tinkling sound, मणि-नूपुराभ्याम् with her gem-studded anklets, रेजे shone brilliantly, अङ्गुलीय-वलय-व्यजन-अग्र-हस्ता her hands adorned with rings and bangles holding the fan handle, वस्त्र-अन्त-गूढ-कुच-कुङ्कुम-शोण-हार-भासा with the luster of her necklace, made reddish by the saffron on her breasts that were partially hidden under her garment, नितम्ब-धृतया worn on her hips, च and, परार्ध्य-काञ्च्या with an invaluable golden waist-belt

Anvaya: सा मणिनूपुराभ्यां क्वणयती, अङ्गुलीयवलयव्यजनाग्रहस्ता, वस्त्रान्तगूढकुचकुङ्कुमशोणहारभासा, नितम्बधृतया परार्ध्यकाञ्च्या च उपाच्युतं रेजे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सा मणिनूपुराभ्यां क्वणयती She, tinkling with her gem-studded anklets, अङ्गुलीयवलयव्यजनाग्रहस्ता her forehands adorned with rings and bangles while holding the fan handle, वस्त्रान्तगूढकुचकुङ्कुमशोणहारभासा with the brilliance of her necklace, which was tinted red from the saffron on her breasts partially concealed beneath her garment, नितम्बधृतया परार्ध्यकाञ्च्या च and with the priceless belt worn around her hips, उपाच्युतं रेजे shone exquisitely beside Lord Achyuta.

Simple Meaning: Standing beside Lord Achyuta, Queen Rukmini looked exquisitely beautiful. Her gem-encrusted anklets tinkled softly as she moved, and her hands—adorned with sparkling rings and bangles—gracefully held the handle of the fan. The necklace around her neck glowed with a reddish hue from the saffron paste on her breasts, which were softly veiled under her garment, and a priceless golden belt encircled her hips, enhancing her radiance.

तां रूपिणीं श्रियमनन्यगतिं निरीक्ष्य या लीलया धृततनोरनुरूपरूपा ।
प्रीतः स्मयन्नलककुण्डलनिष्ककण्ठवक्त्रोल्लसत्स्मितसुधां हरिराबभाषे ॥10.60.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her (Rukmini), रूपिणीम् personified, श्रियम् the Goddess of Fortune (Lakshmi), अनन्य-गतिम् having no other refuge / devoted solely to Him, निरीक्ष्य beholding, या who, लीलया for His pastimes, धृत-तनोः of Him who assumed a humanlike form, अनुरूप-रूपा possessed a form matching His own, प्रीतः pleased, स्मयन् smiling, अलक-कुण्डल-निष्क-कण्ठ-वक्त्र-उल्लसत्-स्मित-सुधाम् the nectar of her smile illuminating her face, which was framed by curling locks of hair, swinging earrings, and a gold locket on her neck, हरिः Lord Hari, आबभाषे addressed

Anvaya: या लीलया धृततनोः (तस्य) अनुरूपरूपा, अलककुण्डलनिष्ककण्ठवक्त्रोल्लसत्स्मितसुधाम् अनन्यगतिं रूपिणीं श्रियं तां निरीक्ष्य प्रीतः स्मयन् हरिः आबभाषे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: या लीलया धृततनोः तस्य अनुरूपरूपा She who possessed a form perfectly matching His as He assumed a body for His divine pastimes, अलककुण्डलनिष्ककण्ठवक्त्रोल्लसत्स्मितसुधाम् with the nectar of a smile illuminating her face, which was adorned with curling hair, earrings, and a golden necklace, अनन्यगतिं रूपिणीं श्रियं तां who was Goddess Lakshmi personified and single-mindedly devoted to Him, निरीक्ष्य beholding her, प्रीतः स्मयन् हरिः pleased and smiling, Lord Hari, आबभाषे spoke.

Simple Meaning: Queen Rukmini was none other than Goddess Lakshmi personified, having manifested a divine form that perfectly matched Lord Hari's as He enacted His pastimes on Earth, and her heart was devoted exclusively to Him. With the nectar of that brilliant smile illuminating her face—framed by soft curling locks, swinging earrings, and a golden locket around her neck—the Lord beholding her with deep affection, smiled and began to speak to her.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
राजपुत्रीप्सिता भूपैर्लोकपालविभूतिभिः ।
महानुभावैः श्रीमद्भी रूपौदार्यबलोर्जितैः ॥10.60.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, राज-पुत्रि O princess, ईप्सिता desired, भूपैः by kings, लोक-पाल-विभूतिभिः possessing the opulences of the guardians of the worlds, महा-अनुभावैः greatly powerful, श्रीमद्भिः wealthy and majestic, रूप-औदार्य-बल-ऊर्जितैः endowed with great beauty, magnanimity, and strength

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच—राजपुत्रि! (त्वं) लोकपालविभूतिभिः महानुभावैः श्रीमद्भिः रूपौदार्यबलोर्जितैः भूपैः ईप्सिता (आसीः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, राजपुत्रि O princess, (त्वं) You लोकपालविभूतिभिः who possessed the opulences of world guardians, महानुभावैः श्रीमद्भिः who were greatly powerful, wealthy, रूपौदार्यबलोर्जितैः and full of beauty, generosity, and strength, भूपैः by kings, ईप्सिता (आसीः) were desired.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord said: O Princess! You were sought after in marriage by great kings who commanded the vast opulences of the guardians of the worlds—men who were mighty, powerful, immensely wealthy, and renowned for their physical beauty, magnanimity, and supreme prowess.

तान् प्राप्तानर्थिनो हित्वा चैद्यादीन् स्मरदुर्मदान् ।
दत्ता भ्रात्रा स्वपित्रा च कस्मान्नो ववृषेऽसमान् ॥10.60.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: तान् those, प्राप्तान् arrived, अर्थिनः suitors, हित्वा rejecting, चैद्य-आदीन् King of Chedi (Shishupala) and others, स्मर-दुर्मदान् intoxicated with love, दत्ता given in marriage, भ्रात्रा by your brother, स्व-पित्रा by your father, च and, कस्मात् why, नः us, ववृषे chose, असमान् unequaled/mismatched

Anvaya: भ्रात्रा स्वपित्रा च दत्ता स्मरदुर्मदान् चैद्यादीन् प्राप्तान् तान् अर्थिनः हित्वा कस्मात् असमान् नः ववृषे?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भ्रात्रा स्वपित्रा च दत्ता Although given in marriage by your brother and your father, स्मरदुर्मदान् चैद्यादीन् प्राप्तान् तान् अर्थिनः हित्वा rejecting those arrived suitors headed by Sisupala who were intoxicated with love, कस्मात् असमान् नः ववृषे why did you choose us who are mismatched to you?

Simple Meaning: Even though your father and brother had offered your hand in marriage to those illustrious suitors like Shishupala and others—who had arrived eagerly, intoxicated with love—you rejected them all. Why, O Princess, did you choose us instead, when we are so completely mismatched to you?

राजभ्यो बिभ्यतः सुभ्रूः समुद्रं शरणं गतान् ।
बलवद्भिः कृतद्वेषान् प्रायस्त्यक्तनृपासनान् ॥10.60.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजभ्यः from the kings, बिभ्यतः being fearful, सु-भ्रूः O lady with beautiful eyebrows, समुद्रम् the ocean, शरणम् refuge/shelter, गतान् gone to, बलवद्भिः with the powerful, कृत-द्वेषान् having created enmity, प्रायः mostly, त्यक्त-नृप-आसनान् abandoned the royal thrones

Anvaya: सुभ्रूः! (वयं) राजभ्यः बिभ्यतः समुद्रं शरणं गतान्, बलवद्भिः कृतद्वेषान्, प्रायः त्यक्तनृपासनान् (स्मः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सुभ्रूः O lady with beautiful eyebrows, (वयं) राजभ्यः बिभ्यतः समुद्रं शरणं गतान् we, fearful of those kings, took shelter in the ocean, बलवद्भिः कृतद्वेषान् created enmity with the powerful, त्यक्तनृपासनान् (च) and have abandoned all royal thrones.

Simple Meaning: O fair lady with beautiful eyebrows! We were so terrified of those mighty kings that we fled and took refuge inside the sea. We have incurred the wrath of the powerful, and for all practical purposes, we have given up any claim to a royal throne.

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भूमन् O infinite Lord, त्वत्पादपद्ममकरन्दजुषां मुनीनां of the sages who relish the honey of Your lotus feet, वर्त्म path, अस्फुटम् is mysterious, (अतः एव) therefore, नृपशुभिः by animalistic men, दुर्विभाव्यं ननु is indeed hard to understand. यस्मात् Because, ईश्वरस्य तव Your (activity) as the Supreme Lord, अलौकिकम् इव is supernatural, अथः and, ये भवन्तम् अनु those who follow You, (तेषाम् अपि ईहितम् अलौकिकम् एव) their actions too are supernatural.

Simple Meaning: O infinite Lord! The path of the great sages who drink the sweet nectar of Your lotus feet is subtle and mysterious, making it impossible for animalistic human beings to truly comprehend. This is because Your actions as the Supreme Sovereign are entirely transcendent, and so are the deeds of those devoted souls who follow in Your footsteps.

निष्किञ्चनो ननु भवान् न यतोऽस्ति किञ्चिद् यस्मै बलिं बलिभुजोऽपि हरन्त्यजाद्याः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भवान् Your Lordship, निष्किञ्चनः ननु is indeed called possessor of nothing, यतः because, (तव परं) superior to You, किञ्चित् न अस्ति nothing exists, यस्मै to whom, अजाद्याः बलिभुजः अपि even the demigods like Lord Brahma who accept sacrifices, बलिं हरन्ति offer tribute. आढ्यतान्धाः People blinded by wealth, असुतृपः who live only to satisfy their senses, त्वा अन्तकं न विदन्ति do not know You as their destruction. बलिभुजाम् अपि Even to those demigods, भवान् प्रेष्ठः You are most dear, ते अपि and they also, तुभ्यम् are dear to You.

Simple Meaning: You are indeed called possessor of nothing because there is no object or wealth superior to You, and even great exalted gods like Lord Brahma, who receive sacrificial offerings from others, bring their own tributes to You. Materialistic people blinded by pride of wealth and obsessed with sense pleasure do not understand You, as their death itself. But to those great gods who accept offerings, You are the ultimate beloved, and they are equally dear to You.

त्वं वै समस्तपुरुषार्थमयः फलात्मा यद्वाञ्छया सुमतयो विसृजन्ति कृत्स्नम् ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विभो O almighty Lord, त्वं वै You indeed, समस्तपुरुषार्थमयः फलात्मा are the personification of all human pursuits and the ultimate reward itself, यद्वाञ्छया desiring whom, सुमतयः intelligent persons, कृत्स्नं विसृजन्ति discard everything else. तेषां भवतः च समाजः Association between them and You, समुचितः is fitting, रतयोः सुखदुःखिनोः of those attached to worldly pleasures and suffering, पुंसः स्त्रियाः च and of a ordinary man and woman, न it is not so.

Simple Meaning: O all-powerful Lord! You are the true embodiment of all spiritual goals and the ultimate reward of life. Out of intense desire to attain You, truly wise souls give up every worldly attachment. Thus, You are the perfect companion for such detached saints, not for ordinary men and women who are bound by mutual attachment and driven by the endless cycle of pleasure and pain.

त्वं न्यस्तदण्डमुनिभिर्गदितानुभाव आत्माऽऽत्मदश्च जगतामिति मे वृतोऽसि ।

Anvaya: न्यस्तदण्डमुनिभिः गदितानुभावः जगताम् आत्मा आत्मदः च, इति त्वं मे वृतः असि। भवद्भ्रुवः उदीरितकालवेगध्वस्ताशिषः अब्जभवनाकपतीन् हित्वा अन्ये (नृपाः) कुतः?

Simple Meaning: You are the Supreme Soul of the entire universe who bestows Your very self upon Your devotees, and Your glories are constantly sung by peaceful sages who have renounced all worldly passions. Understanding this, I chose You alone as my husband. When I have already turned away from exaltations like Lord Brahma and King Indra—whose worldly powers are destroyed in an instant by the mere movement of Your eyebrow—why would I ever consider any ordinary king?

जाड्यं वचस्तव गदाग्रज यस्तु भूपान् विद्राव्य शार्ङ्गनिनदेन जहर्थ मां त्वम् ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गदाग्रज O elder brother of Gada, ईश O Lord, यः त्वं You who, शार्ङ्गनिनदेन with the roaring sound of Your Sharnga bow, भूपान् those kings, विद्राव्य driving away, पशून् small animals, सिंहः a lion, स्वभागं स्वबलिं यथा just like its own portion and hunt, मां जहर्थ carried me off, तव Your, “तेभ्यः भयात् out of fear of those kings, उदधिं शरणं प्रपन्नः took shelter in the ocean”, इति वचः this statement, तु जाड्यम् is simply laughter inducing silliness.

Simple Meaning: O Lord Krishna, elder brother of Gada! When You drove away all those rival kings with the roaring twang of Your Sharnga bow and carried me off just as a lion boldly claims its own hunt by scaring off small animals, You proved Your supreme valor. Therefore, Your claim that You fled to the ocean out of fear of those kings is nothing more than lighthearted, humorous silliness.

यद्वाञ्छया नृपशिखामणयोऽङ्गवैन्यजायन्तनाहुषगयादय ऐकपत्यम् ।

Anvaya: अम्बुजाक्ष! यद्वाञ्छया अङ्गवैन्यजायन्तनाहुषगयादयः नृपशिखामणयः ऐकपत्यं राज्यं विसृज्य वनं विविशुः, ते तव अनुपदवीम् इह आस्थिताः सीदन्ति किम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अम्बुजाक्ष O lotus eyed Lord, यद्वाञ्छया desiring whose lotus feet, अङ्गवैन्यजायन्तनाहुषगयादयः Anga, Vainya, Yayati, Nahusha Gaya and other, नृपशिखामणयः crest jewels of monarchs, ऐकपत्यं राज्यं absolute rulership over the kingdom, विसृज्य leaving behind, वनं विविशुः retired to the forest, ते they, तव अनुपदवीम् Your path, इह in this world, आस्थिताः following, सीदन्ति किम् do they undergo misery.

Simple Meaning: O lotus eyed Lord! Great saintly kings like Anga, Prithu, son of Vena, Yayati, Nahusha, Gaya, and many other exalted monarchs willingly renounced their undisputed supreme sovereignty over the entire world and entered the forest just to attain Your grace. They dedicated themselves entirely to following Your divine path—could such great souls ever be considered miserable or fallen?

Simple Meaning: O Lord! Having once smelled the sweet, intoxicating fragrance of Your lotus feet—which are gloriously sung by great saints, bestow supreme liberation upon humanity, and serve as the eternal abode of Goddess Lakshmi—what woman who truly understands the ultimate purpose of life would ever reject You, the reservoir of all divine qualities, to seek refuge in an ordinary mortal man who is constantly tormented by immense fear and death?

As I wander through endless cycles of birth and death, may Your divine lotus feet become my everlasting refuge, for they completely destroy all material illusion and bestow themselves upon any devotee who sincerely serves You.

यत्कर्णमूलमरिकर्षण नोपयायाद् युष्मत्कथा मृडविरिञ्चसभासु गीता ॥10.60.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्याः for that woman, स्युः let them be, अच्युत O infallible Lord, नृपाः kings, भवता by You, उपदिष्टाः suggested, स्त्रीणाम् of women, गृहेषु in the homes, खर-गो-श्व-बिडाल-भृत्याः beasts of burden like donkeys cows dogs cats and servants, यत् whose, कर्ण-मूलम् ears, अरि-कर्षण O subduer of enemies, न not, उपयायात् has reached, युष्मत्-कथा Your narrations, मृड-विरिञ्च-सभासु in the assemblies of Lord Shiva and Lord Brahma, गीता sung

Anvaya: अच्युत! अरिकर्षण! मृडविरिञ्चसभासु गीता युष्मत्कथा यस्याः कर्णमूलं न उपयायात्, तस्याः स्त्रीणां गृहेषु खरगोश्वबिडालभृत्याः भवता उपदिष्टाः नृपाः (पतयः) स्युः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अच्युत O infallible Lord, अरिकर्षण O subduer of enemies, मृडविरिञ्चसभासु in the assemblies of Lord Shiva and Brahma, गीता sung, युष्मत्कथा Your divine topics, यस्याः whose, कर्णमूलं ears, न उपयायात् have not reached, तस्याः for such a woman, स्त्रीणां गृहेषु in the homes of women, खरगोश्वबिडालभृत्याः like donkeys cows dogs cats and slaves, भवता उपदिष्टाः recommended by You, नृपाः kings, (पतयः) स्युः may become husbands.

Simple Meaning: O infallible Lord Krishna! O subduer of all enemies! Those kings You suggested for me—who act inside a woman's household merely like donkeys, cows, dogs, cats, and subservient slaves—are indeed suitable husbands only for a woman who has never heard Your transcendent glories, which are lovingly sung in the exalted assemblies of Lord Shiva and Lord Brahma.

त्वक्श्मश्रुरोमनखकेशपिनद्धमन्तर्मांसास्थिरक्तकृमिविट्कफपित्तवातम् ।
जीवच्छवं भजति कान्तमतिर्विमूढा या ते पदाब्जमकरन्दमजिघ्रती स्त्री ॥10.60.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वक्-श्मश्रु-रोम-नख-केश-पिनद्धम् covered with skin, beard, body, hair, nails and hair on head, अन्तः inside, मांस-अस्थि-रक्त-कृमि-विट्-कफ-पित्त-वातम् filled with flesh, bones, blood, worms, feces, mucus, bile and air, जीवत्-शवम् a living corpse, भजति worships, कान्त-मतिः regarding as beloved, विमूढा utterly bewildered, या who, ते Your, पद-अब्ज-मकरन्दम् the honey of the lotus feet, अजिघ्रती not smelling, स्त्री a woman

Anvaya: या स्त्री ते पदाब्जमकरन्दम् अजिघ्रती, विमूढा (सा) त्वक्श्मश्रुरोमनखकेशपिनद्धम् अन्तः मांसास्थिरक्तकृमिविट्कफपित्तवातं जीवच्छवं कान्तमतिः (सती) भजति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: या स्त्री Which woman, ते Your, पदाब्जमकरन्दम् nectar of lotus feet, अजिघ्रती does not smell, विमूढा (सा) foolish woman, त्वक्श्मश्रुरोमनखकेशपिनद्धम् covered externally with skin facial hair body hair nails and head hair, अन्तः internally filled with, मांसास्थिरक्तकृमिविट्कफपित्तवातं flesh bone blood worms stool phlegm bile and wind, जीवच्छवं a living corpse, कान्तमतिः considering as her charming lover, भजति serves.

Simple Meaning: Any foolish woman who has never inhaled the divine honey of Your lotus feet worships a mere living corpse as her beloved husband—a body that is wrapped externally in skin, facial hair, body hair, nails, and head hair, and filled inside with nothing but flesh, bones, blood, worms, feces, mucus, bile, and air.

अस्त्वम्बुजाक्ष मम ते चरणानुराग आत्मन् रतस्य मयि चानतिरिक्तदृष्टेः ।
यर्ह्यस्य वृद्धय उपात्तरजोऽतिमात्रो मामीक्षसे तदु ह नः परमानुकम्पा ॥10.60.46॥

Word-by-word meaning: अस्तु let there be, अम्बुज-अक्ष O lotus eyed Lord, मम my, ते unto You, चरण-अनुरागः devotion to the lotus feet, आत्मन् O Supreme Soul, रतस्य who are satisfied in Yourself, मयि towards me, च and, अनतिरिक्त-दृष्टेः whose vision sees no distinction, यर्हि when, अस्य of this universe, वृद्धये for the creation or growth, उपात्त-रजः assuming the quality of passion, अतिमात्रः excessively, माम् at me, ईक्षसे You glance, तत् that, उ indeed, ह certainly, नः for us, परम-अनुकम्पा supreme favor

Anvaya: अम्बुजाक्ष! आत्मन्! मयि च अनतिरिक्तदृष्टेः स्वयं रतस्य ते (चरणेषु) मम चरणानुरागः अस्तु। यर्हि अस्य वृद्धये अतिमात्रः उपात्तरजः (त्वं) माम् ईक्षसे, तत् ह उ नः परमानुकम्पा।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अम्बुजाक्ष O lotus eyed Lord, आत्मन् O Supreme Soul, मयि च towards me as well, अनतिरिक्तदृष्टेः (You) who look without any distinction, स्वयं रतस्य who are self satisfied, ते Your, (चरणेषु) at the feet, मम my, चरणानुरागः constant loving attachment, अस्तु may remain. यर्हि When, अस्य for the growth of this world, अतिमात्रः excessively, उपात्तरजः accepting the quality of passion, (त्वं) You, माम् at me, ईक्षसे glance, तत् ह उ that alone indeed, नः for us, परमानुकम्पा is the ultimate mercy.

Simple Meaning: O lotus eyed Lord! O Supreme Soul! You are completely self satisfied and look upon me without any distinction (You look upon me as an ordinary person). May I always possess unshakeable loving devotion for Your divine lotus feet. And whenever You assume the mode of passion - Rajoguna - to expand this universe and glance lovingly upon me, we consider that tender gaze alone to be Your greatest blessing and ultimate favor.

नैवालीकमहं मन्ये वचस्ते मधुसूदन ।
अम्बाया इव हि प्रायः कन्यायाः स्याद् रतिः क्वचित् ॥10.60.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, एव certainly, अलीकम् false, अहम् I, मन्ये think, वचः statement, ते Your, मधुसूदन O killer of the Madhu asura, अम्बायाः of princess Amba, इव like, हि indeed, प्रायः generally, कन्यायाः of an unmarried girl, स्यात् may be, रतिः affection or attraction, क्वचित् sometimes

Anvaya: मधुसूदन! अहं ते वचः अलीकं न एव मन्ये, हि प्रायः अम्बायाः इव कन्यायाः क्वचित् (अन्येन पुंसा सह) रतिः स्यात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मधुसूदन O slayer of the Madhu asura, अहं I, ते वचः Your words, अलीकं false, न एव मन्ये do not consider at all, हि because, प्रायः generally, अम्बायाः इव like Princess Amba, कन्यायाः of an unmarried girl, क्वचित् sometimes, (अन्येन पुंसा सह with another man) रतिः attraction, स्यात् may occur.

Simple Meaning: O Lord Krishna, slayer of the Madhu asura! (When You ask me to choose another partner even now) I do not consider Your words to be false at all. Just as Princess Amba, the daughter of King of Kashi, a maiden might sometimes feel attraction for somebody else other than the one by whom they have been won.

व्यूढायाश्चापि पुंश्चल्या मनोऽभ्येति नवं नवम् ।
बुधोऽसतीं न बिभृयात् तां बिभ्रदुभयच्युतः ॥10.60.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: व्यूढायाः of a married woman, च and, अपि even, पुंश्चल्याः of an unchaste woman, मनः mind, अभ्येति wanders toward, नवम् new, नवम् new lover, बुधः a wise man, असतीम् an unchaste woman, न not, बिभृयात् should maintain, ताम् her, बिभ्रत् maintaining, उभय-च्युतः fallen from both worlds

Anvaya: व्यूढायाः पुंश्चल्याः अपि मनः नवं नवं (पुंसम्) अभ्येति। बुधः असतीं न बिभृयात्। तां बिभ्रत् उभयच्युतः (भवति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: व्यूढायाः पुंश्चल्याः अपि Even of a married unchaste woman, मनः mind, नवं नवं (पुंसम्) toward new and new lovers, अभ्येति goes. बुधः A wise man, असतीं an unchaste woman, न बिभृयात् should not support. तां बिभ्रत् Maintaining her, उभयच्युतः (भवति) becomes fallen from both this world and the next.

Simple Meaning: Even when legally married, an unchaste woman continually lets her mind wander toward new lovers. Therefore, a wise man should never maintain an unfaithful wife, for anyone who supports such a woman falls from righteousness and forfeits happiness in both this life and the next.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
साध्व्येतच्छ्रोतुकामैस्त्वं राजपुत्रि प्रलम्भिता ।
मयोदितं यदन्वात्थ सर्वं तत् सत्यमेव हि ॥10.60.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, साध्वि O chaste lady, एतत् this, श्रोतु-कामैः by desiring to hear, त्वम् you, राज-पुत्रि O princess, प्रलम्भिता teased, मया by Me, उदितम् spoken, यत् which, अन्वात्थ you have explained, सर्वम् all, तत् that, सत्यम् true, एव indeed, हि certainly

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच— साध्वि राजपुत्रि! एतत् श्रोतुकामैः त्वं प्रलम्भिता। मया उदितं यत् अन्वात्थ तत् सर्वं सत्यम् एव हि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, साध्वि O virtuous lady, राजपुत्रि O princess, एतत् this, श्रोतुकामैः desiring to hear, त्वं you, प्रलम्भिता were teased. मया उदितं What was spoken by Me, यत् अन्वात्थ as you have explained, तत् सर्वं all that, सत्यम् एव हि is completely true indeed.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord Krishna said: O virtuous princess! I teased you only because I desired to hear these wonderful words from you. Everything you have spoken in response to My words is absolutely true in every respect.

यान् यान् कामयसे कामान् मय्यकामाय भामिनि ।
सन्ति ह्येकान्तभक्तायास्तव कल्याणि नित्यदा ॥10.60.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: यान् यान् whatever, कामयसे you desire, कामान् desires, मयि in Me, अकामाय for freedom from material desires, भामिनि O beautiful lady, सन्ति exist, हि indeed, एकान्त-भक्तायाः of an unalloyed devotee, तव your, कल्याणि O auspicious one, नित्यदा always

Anvaya: भामिनि! कल्याणि! अकामाय मयि एकान्तभक्तायाः तव यान् यान् कामान् कामयसे, (ते सर्वे) नित्यदा सन्ति हि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भामिनि O beautiful lady, कल्याणि O auspicious one,अकामाय For freedom from selfish material desires, मयि in Me, एकान्तभक्तायाः of an unalloyed devotee, तव your, यान् यान् कामान् whatever desires, कामयसे you may wish for, (ते सर्वे) all of them, नित्यदा सन्ति हि are indeed eternally present.

Simple Meaning: O beautiful and auspicious lady! For freedom from selfish material desires, whatever pure desires you - who are My pure, unalloyed devotee - may ever have are already eternally fulfilled.

उपलब्धं पतिप्रेम पातिव्रत्यं च तेऽनघे ।
यद्वाक्यैश्चाल्यमानाया न धीर्मय्यपकर्षिता ॥10.60.51॥

Word-by-word meaning: उपलब्धम् attained, पति-प्रेम devotion to the husband, पातिव्रत्यम् chastity, च and, ते your, अनघे O sinless lady, यत् because, वाक्यैः by statements, चाल्यमानायाः being tried to be shaken, न not, धीः mind, मयि in Me, अपकर्षिता turned away

Anvaya: अनघे! ते पतिप्रेम पातिव्रत्यं च उपलब्धम्। यत् वाक्यैः चाल्यमानायाः (अपि) मयि धीः न अपकर्षिता।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अनघे O sinless one, ते your, पतिप्रेम love for husband, पातिव्रत्यं च and marital fidelity, उपलब्धम् has been attained. यत् Because, वाक्यैः by My teasing words, चाल्यमानायाः being shaken, (अपि) even though, मयि in Me, धीः your mind, न अपकर्षिता was not turned away.

Simple Meaning: O sinless Rukmini! Deep love for your husband and unshakeable chastity has been attained by you. Because even when I tried to disturb your mind with provocative words, your devotion to Me could not be shaken or turned away in the slightest.

ये मां भजन्ति दाम्पत्ये तपसा व्रतचर्यया ।
कामात्मानोऽपवर्गेशं मोहिता मम मायया ॥10.60.52॥

Word-by-word meaning: ये who, माम् Me, भजन्ति worship, दाम्पत्ये in conjugal life, तपसा with penance, व्रत-चर्यया by observance of vows, काम-आत्मानः full of material desires, अपवर्ग-ईशम् the Lord of liberation, मोहिताः bewildered, मम My, मायया by illusory energy

Anvaya: ये कामात्मानः अपवर्गेशं मां तपसा व्रतचर्यया दाम्पत्ये भजन्ति, (ते) मम मायया मोहिताः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ये Those who, कामात्मानः filled with material desires, अपवर्गेशं मां Me who am the Lord of liberation, तपसा व्रतचर्यया with penance and observance of vows, दाम्पत्ये for the sake of conjugal happiness, भजन्ति worship, (ते) मम मायया they, by My illusory energy, मोहिताः are bewildered.

Simple Meaning: Those who are full of material desires worship Me, the very Lord of liberation, through penance and strict vows merely to gain conjugal happiness and worldly family life, they bewildered by My illusory energy

मां प्राप्य मानिन्यपवर्गसम्पदं वाञ्छन्ति ये सम्पद एव तत्पतिम् ।
ते मन्दभाग्या निरयेऽपि ये नृणां मात्रात्मकत्वान्निरयः सुसङ्गमः ॥10.60.53॥

Word-by-word meaning: माम् Me, प्राप्य having attained, मानिनि O proud lady, अपवर्ग-सम्पदम् the ultimate wealth of liberation, वाञ्छन्ति desire, ये who, सम्पदः material riches, एव only, तत्-पतिम् the master of that liberation, ते they, मन्द-भाग्याः unfortunate, निरये in hell, अपि even, ये which, नृणाम् for human beings, मात्रात्मकत्वात् due to sense gratification, निरयः hell, सु-सङ्गमः easily available

Anvaya: मानिनि! अपवर्गसम्पदं तत्पतिं मां प्राप्य ये सम्पदः एव वाञ्छन्ति ते मन्दभाग्याः। ये नृणां मात्रात्मकत्वात् निरये अपि (सन्ति), निरयः (तु) सुसङ्गमः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मानिनि O proud lady, अपवर्गसम्पदं तत्पतिं मां Me who am the bestowal of liberation and its supreme master, प्राप्य having reached, ये who, सम्पदः एव material opulences alone, वाञ्छन्ति desire, ते मन्दभाग्याः they are unfortunate. ये नृणां Because for those human beings, मात्रात्मकत्वात् due to obsession with sense enjoyment, निरये अपि even in hell, निरयः sense gratification which is like hell, सुसङ्गमः is easily attainable.

Simple Meaning: O proud lady, those who reach Me, the supreme controller who bestows the ultimate wealth of liberation, yet desire only fleeting material riches are truly unfortunate. Such sense gratification is easily obtained even in hell, where living beings naturally sink due to their attachment to sense enjoyment.

दिष्ट्या गृहेश्वर्यसकृन्मयि त्वया कृतानुवृत्तिर्भवमोचनी खलैः ।
सुदुष्करासौ सुतरां दुराशिषो ह्यसुम्भराया निकृतिञ्जुषः स्त्रियाः ॥10.60.54॥

Word-by-word meaning: दिष्ट्या fortunately, गृह-ईश्वरि O mistress of the home, असकृत् repeatedly, मयि toward Me, त्वया by you, कृता executed, अनुवृत्तिः devotion, भव-मोचनी liberating from material existence, खलैः by envious people, सु-दुष्करा extremely difficult to perform, असौ this, सुतराम् especially, दुराशिषः for a woman with dark desires, हि indeed, असुम्-भरायाः who lives only to nourish her own body, निकृतिम्-जुषः who practices deceitfulness, स्त्रियाः for a woman

Anvaya: गृहेश्वरि! दिष्ट्या भवमोचनी मयि अनुवृत्तिः त्वया असकृत् कृता। असौ दुराशिषः असुम्भरायाः स्त्रियाः निकृतिञ्जुषः खलैः सुतरां सुदुष्करा हि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गृहेश्वरि O mistress of the home, दिष्ट्या By good fortune, भवमोचनी which releases one from material rebirth, मयि अनुवृत्तिः this devotion to Me, त्वया by you, असकृत् कृता has been repeatedly offered. असौ This (devotion), दुराशिषः for one with selfish motives, असुम्भरायाः focused on feeding her own physical body, स्त्रियाः for such a woman, निकृतिञ्जुषः addicted to deceit, खलैः by malicious people, सुतरां सुदुष्करा हि indeed, it is exceedingly difficult to perform.

Simple Meaning: O mistress of the home, it is by pure good fortune that you have rendered Me such constant devotion which frees one from the cycle of birth and death. Such selfless devotion is impossible for selfish women who care only for feeding their own bodies and by wicked people practicing deceitful ways.

न त्वादृशीं प्रणयिनीं गृहिणीं गृहेषु पश्यामि मानिनि यथा स्वविवाहकाले ।
प्राप्तान् नृपानवगणय्य रहोहरो मे प्रस्थापितो द्विज उपश्रुतसत्कथस्य ॥10.60.55॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, त्वादृशीम् like you, प्रणयिनीम् loving, गृहिणीम् housewife, गृहेषु in households, पश्यामि I see, मानिनि O proud lady, यथा as, स्व-विवाह-काले at the time of your own marriage, प्राप्तान् assembled, नृपान् kings, अवगणय्य disregarding, रहः-हरः secret messenger, मे My, प्रस्थापितः sent, द्विजः brahmana, उपश्रुत-सत्कथस्य whose noble glories you had heard

Anvaya: मानिनि! गृहेषु त्वादृशीं प्रणयिनीं गृहिणीं न पश्यामि यथा स्वविवाहकाले प्राप्तान् नृपान् अवगणय्य उपश्रुतसत्कथस्य मे रहःहरः द्विजः प्रस्थापितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मानिनि O proud lady,  गृहेषु In any household, त्वादृशीं like you, प्रणयिनीं loving, गृहिणीं wife, न पश्यामि I do not see, यथा just as, स्वविवाहकाले at the time of your marriage, प्राप्तान् नृपान् assembled kings, अवगणय्य disregarding, उपश्रुतसत्कथस्य मे to Me, whose glorious tales you had heard, रहःहरः the carrier of confidential message, द्विजः a brahmana, प्रस्थापितः was sent.

Simple Meaning: O proud lady, I do not see anywhere in any household a loving wife to match you. At the time of your marriage, simply having heard My glories, you disregarded all the powerful kings who had assembled for you and sent a confidential message to Me through a trusted brahmana.

भ्रातुर्विरूपकरणं युधि निर्जितस्य प्रोद्वाहपर्वणि च तद्वधमक्षगोष्ठ्याम् ।
दुःखं समुत्थमसहोऽस्मदयोगभीत्या नैवाब्रवीः किमपि तेन वयं जितास्ते॥10.60.56॥

Word-by-word meaning: भ्रातुः of your brother, विरूप-करणम् disfigurement, युधि in battle, निर्जितस्य who was defeated, प्रोद्वाह-पर्वणि on the occasion of the marriage ceremony, च and, तत्-वधम् his killing, अक्ष-गोष्ठ्याम् in the gambling hall, दुःखम् grief, समुत्थम् arisen, असहः unbearable, अस्मत्-अयोग-भीत्या out of fear of separation from Us, न not, एव indeed, अब्रवीः you spoke, किम् अपि anything at all, तेन by that, वयम् We, जिताः conquered, ते by you

Anvaya: युधि निर्जितस्य भ्रातुः विरूपकरणं, प्रोद्वाहपर्वणि अक्षगोष्ठ्यां तद्वधं च समुत्थम् असहः दुःखं अस्मदयोगभीत्या किमपि न एव अब्रवीः, तेन वयं ते जिताः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: युधि निर्जितस्य भ्रातुः Of your brother who was defeated in battle, विरूपकरणं the disfigurement, प्रोद्वाहपर्वणि on the occasion of the wedding, अक्षगोष्ठ्यां in the gambling room, तद्वधं च and his slaying, समुत्थम् असहः दुःखं the unbearable grief that arose, अस्मदयोगभीत्या out of fear of separation from Us, किमपि न एव अब्रवीः you did not utter a single word. तेन By this conduct, वयं We, ते जिताः have been conquered by you.

Simple Meaning: You suffered unbearable grief when your brother Rukmi was defeated in battle and disfigured, and later killed in the gambling hall during the wedding celebrations (of Aniruddha). Yet out of fear that speaking up might cause separation between Us, you remained completely silent and said nothing. By this selfless devotion, you have completely conquered Me.

Note: Killing of Rukmi is narrated in a later chapter.

दूतस्त्वयाऽऽत्मलभने सुविविक्तमन्त्रः प्रस्थापितो मयि चिरायति शून्यमेतत् ।
मत्वा जिहास इदमङ्गमनन्ययोग्यं तिष्ठेत तत्त्वयि वयं प्रतिनन्दयामः ॥10.60.57॥

Word-by-word meaning: दूतः messenger, त्वया by you, आत्म-लभने to obtain Me, सु-विविक्त-मन्त्रः a very well worded confidential message, प्रस्थापितः was sent, मयि Me, चिरायति delaying, शून्यम् empty, एतत् this world, मत्वा considering, जिहासः desiring to abandon, इदम् this, अङ्गम् body, अनन्य-योग्यम् fit for no one else, तिष्ठेत may it remain, तत् that, त्वयि in you, वयम् We, प्रतिनन्दयामः express gratitude

Anvaya: आत्मलभने सुविविक्तमन्त्रः दूतः त्वया प्रस्थापितः। मयि चिरायति एतत् शून्यं मत्वा अनन्ययोग्यं इदम् अङ्गं जिहासः (असीत्)। तत् त्वयि तिष्ठेत, वयं प्रतिनन्दयामः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: आत्मलभने To attain Me, सुविविक्तमन्त्रः with a very well worded secret message, दूतः a messenger, त्वया by you, प्रस्थापितः was sent. मयि चिरायति When I delayed, एतत् शून्यं this world as empty, मत्वा considering, अनन्ययोग्यं fit for no other husband, इदम् अङ्गं this body, जिहासः desiring to give up, (असीत्) you were. तत् That love, त्वयि तिष्ठेत let it remain in you. वयं प्रतिनन्दयामः We can only express Our joy and admiration for it.

Simple Meaning: You sent a messenger with a very well worded confidential message to attain Me as your husband. When I delayed in coming, you viewed this entire world as vacant and were ready to give up your body, which you deemed fit for no other husband. May that pure and unswerving love ever reside in you; We can only offer Our heartfelt appreciation and gratitude for it.

श्री शुक उवाच
एवं सौरतसंलापैर्भगवाञ्जगदीश्वरः ।
स्वरतो रमया रेमे नरलोकं विडम्बयन् ॥10.60.58॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, एवम् in this manner, सौरत-संलापैः with intimate loving conversations, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, जगत-ईश्वरः the Lord of the universe, स्व-रतः satisfied within Himself, रमया with Goddess Rama or Rukmini, रेमे enjoyed, नर-लोकम् human society, विडम्बयन् imitating

Anvaya: श्रीशुक उवाच - स्वरतः जगदीश्वरः भगवान् एवं सौरतसंलापैः नरलोकं विडम्बयन् रमया रेमे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुक उवाच Shri Shuka said, स्वरतः Self satisfied, जगदीश्वरः भगवान् the Supreme Lord of the universe, एवं in this way, सौरतसंलापैः through intimate loving talks, नरलोकं विडम्बयन् imitating the ways of human society, रमया with Goddess Rukmini, रेमे enjoyed.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: In this manner, the Supreme Lord, the master of the entire universe, who is fully self satisfied within Himself, enjoyed intimate loving exchanges with Goddess Rukmini, perfectly playing the role of a human husband in ordinary society.

तथान्यासामपि विभुर्गृहेषु गृहवानिव ।
आस्थितो गृहमेधीयान् धर्माँल्लोकगुरुर्हरिः ॥10.60.59॥

Word-by-word meaning: तथा similarly, अनासाम् of other wives, अपि also, विभुः the all powerful Lord, गृहेषु in the homes, गृह-वान् like a householder, इव as if, आस्थितः established, गृह-मेधीयान् relating to householders, धर्मान् duties, लोक-गुरुः the spiritual master of all worlds, हरिः Lord Hari

Anvaya: तथा लोकगुरुः विभुः हरिः अन्यासाम् अपि गृहेषु गृहवान् इव आस्थितः (सन्) गृहमेधीयान् धर्मान् (आचरति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तथा Similarly, लोकगुरुः विभुः हरिः Lord Hari the all powerful spiritual master of the universe, अन्यासाम् अपि गृहेषु in the homes of His other wives as well, गृहवान् इव like an ordinary householder, आस्थितः being situated, गृहमेधीयान् धर्मान् the duties of household life, (आचरति) executed.

Simple Meaning: In the same way, Lord Hari, the all powerful Lord and spiritual teacher of the entire universe, lived in the palaces of His other queens as if He were an ordinary householder, meticulously performing all duties appropriate for household life.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे  “कृष्णरुक्मिणीसंवादः नाम​​” षष्टित्तमः अध्यायः ॥60॥

Thus ends the sixtieth discourse named “A dialogue between Krishna and Rukmini”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
