Discomfiture of Shishupala's allies and Rukmi, and the wedding of Krishna and Rukmini

श्री शुक उवाच
इति सर्वे सुसंरब्धा वाहनारुह्य दंशिताः ।
स्वैः स्वैर्बलैः परिक्रान्ता अन्वीयुर्धृतकार्मुकाः ॥10.54.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति thus, सर्वे all of them, सु-संरब्धाः highly enraged, वाहनान् the vehicles, आरुह्य mounting, दंशिताः clad in armor, स्वैः स्वैः by their own respective, बलैः by armies, परिक्रान्ताः surrounded, अन्वीयुः pursued (Him), धृत-कार्मुकाः wielding their bows

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच—इति सर्वे सुसंरब्धाः दंशिताः धृतकार्मुकाः वाहनान् आरुह्य स्वैः स्वैः बलैः परिक्रान्ताः (अच्युतम्) अन्वीयुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इति thus, सर्वे all of them, सुसंरब्धाः highly enraged, दंशिताः clad in armor, धृतकार्मुकाः wielding their bows, वाहनान् onto their vehicles, आरुह्य mounting, स्वैः स्वैः बलैः by their own respective armies, परिक्रान्ताः surrounded, अन्वीयुः pursued.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Burning with rage, all those kings put on their heavy armor, grasped their bows, and mounted their chariots. Surrounded by their respective armies, they set out in hot pursuit of Krishna.

तानापतत आलोक्य यादवानीकयूथपाः ।
तस्थुस्तत्संमुखा राजन्विस्फूर्ज्य स्वधनूंषि ते ॥10.54.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: तान् them, आपततः charging forward, आलोक्य seeing, यादव-अनीक-यूथ-पाः the commanders of the Yadu army, तस्थुः stood (their ground), तत्-संमुखाः facing them, राजन् O King (Parikshit), विस्फूर्ज्य twanging, स्व-धनूंषि their own bows, ते they

Anvaya: राजन्! यादवानीकयूथपाः ते तान् आपततः आलोक्य स्वधनूंषि विस्फूर्ज्य तत्संमुखाः तस्थुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King, यादवानीकयूथपाः ते Those commanders of the Yadu army, तान् them, आपततः charging forward, आलोक्य seeing, स्वधनूंषि their own bows, विस्फूर्ज्य twanging loudly, तत्संमुखाः facing them, तस्थुः stood.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, seeing the enemy forces charging furiously toward them, the valiant commanders of the Yadu army stood their ground. Stringing their bows and twanging them loudly, they turned to face the oncoming onslaught head-on.

अश्वपृष्ठे गजस्कन्धे रथोपस्थे च कोविदाः ।
मुमुचुः शरवर्षाणि मेघा अद्रिष्वपो यथा ॥10.54.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: अश्व-पृष्ठे on the backs of horses, गज-स्कन्धे on the shoulders (necks) of elephants, रथ-उपस्थे on the seats of chariots, च and, कोविदाः experts (the enemy warriors), मुमुचुः released, शर-वर्षाणि showers of arrows, मेघाः clouds, अद्रिषु upon mountains, अपः water, यथा just as

Anvaya: अश्वपृष्ठे गजस्कन्धे रथोपस्थे च कोविदाः (ते) मेघाः अद्रिषु अपः यथा, शरवर्षाणि मुमुचुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अश्वपृष्ठे On the backs of horses, गजस्कन्धे on the shoulders of elephants, रथोपस्थे and on the seats of chariots, कोविदाः experts, (ते) those (warriors), मेघाः clouds, अद्रिषु upon mountains, अपः water, यथा just as,  शरवर्षाणि showers of arrows, मुमुचुः released.

Simple Meaning: Expert in fighting from the backs of horses, the necks of elephants, and the decks of chariots, the enemy warriors unleashed a relentless downpour of arrows upon the Yadu forces—just as massive storm clouds release torrential sheets of rain upon mountain peaks.

पत्युर्बलं शरासारैश्छन्नं वीक्ष्य सुमध्यमा ।
सव्रीडमैक्षत्तद्वक्त्रं भयविह्वललोचना ॥10.54.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: पत्युः of her husband (Krishna), बलम् the army, शर-आसारैः by torrential downpours of arrows, छन्नम् covered over, वीक्ष्य seeing, सु-मध्यमा the slender-waisted princess (Rukmini), स-व्रीडम् with shyness (or anxiety), ऐक्षत looked at, तत्-वक्त्रम् His face, भय-विह्वल-लोचना her eyes overwhelmed with fear

Anvaya: सुमध्यमा (रुक्मिणी) पत्युः बलं शरासारैः छन्नं वीक्ष्य भयविह्वललोचना (सती) सव्रीडं तद्वक्त्रम् ऐक्षत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सुमध्यमा The slender-waisted one, पत्युः of her lord, बलं the army, शरासारैः by torrential rains of arrows, छन्नं covered, वीक्ष्य seeing, भयविह्वललोचना her eyes overwhelmed with fear, सव्रीडं anxiously/shyly, तद्वक्त्रम् His face, ऐक्षत looked at.

Simple Meaning: Seeing her husband's army completely obscured by the enemy's torrential downpour of arrows, the slender-waisted Rukmini grew deeply anxious. With eyes wide and trembling with fear, she looked up into the face of Krishna.

प्रहस्य भगवानाह मा स्म भैर्वामलोचने ।
विनङ्क्ष्यत्यधुनैवैतत् तावकैः शात्रवं बलम् ॥10.54.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रहस्य laughing, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, आह said, मा स्म भैः do not fear, वाम-लोचने O lovely-eyed one (with beautiful, trembling eyes), विनङ्क्ष्यति will be destroyed, अधुना एव right now, एतत् this, तावकैः by your (servants/soldiers), शात्रवम् enemy, बलम् army

Anvaya: भगवान् प्रहस्य आह—वामलोचने! मा स्म भैः, एतत् शात्रवं बलं तावकैः अधुना एव विनङ्क्ष्यति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवान् The Supreme Lord, प्रहस्य laughing, आह said, वामलोचने O beautiful-eyed one, मा स्म भैः do not fear, एतत् शात्रवं बलं this enemy army, तावकैः by your own (soldiers), अधुना एव right now, विनङ्क्ष्यति will be destroyed.

Simple Meaning: Seeing her distress, the Supreme Lord laughed and reassured her, saying, "O beautiful, lovely-eyed one, do not be afraid! This entire enemy host will be completely wiped out right now by your own soldiers."

तेषां तद्विक्रमं वीरा गदसङ्कर्षणादयः ।
अमृष्यमाणा नाराचैर्जघ्नुर्हयगजान् रथान् ॥10.54.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: तेषाम् of them (the enemy kings), तत्-विक्रमम् that display of prowess, वीराः the heroic warriors, गद-सङ्कर्षण-आदयः Gada, Sankarshana (Balarama), and others, अमृष्यमाणाः unable to tolerate, नाराचैः with iron arrows, जघ्नुः struck down (or destroyed), हय-गजान् the horses and elephants, रथान् the chariots

Anvaya: गदसङ्कर्षणादयः वीराः तेषां तद्विक्रमम् अमृष्यमाणाः नाराचैः हयगजान् रथान् (च) जघ्नुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गदसङ्कर्षणादयः Gada, Sankarshana, and others, वीराः heroes, तेषां their, तद्विक्रमम् that prowess, अमृष्यमाणाः unable to tolerate, नाराचैः with iron arrows, हयगजान् horses and elephants, रथान् chariots, जघ्नुः destroyed.

Simple Meaning: Unable to tolerate this aggressive display of prowess by the enemy kings, the heroic Yadu warriors—led by Gada, Balarama, and others—retaliated fiercely. Unleashing flights of deadly iron arrows, they began smashing the enemy's chariots and cutting down their horses and elephants.

पेतुः शिरांसि रथिनामश्विनां गजिनां भुवि ।
सकुण्डलकीरीटानि सोष्णीषाणि च कोटिशः ॥10.54.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: पेतुः fell, शिरांसि the heads, रथिनाम् of the chariot-warriors, अश्विनाम् of the horse-riders, गजिनाम् of those riding elephants, भुवि onto the ground, स-कुण्डल-किरीटानि along with their earrings and crowns, स-उष्णीषाणि along with their turbans, च and, कोटिशः by the millions

Anvaya: भुवि रथिनाम् अश्विनां गजिनां च सकुण्डलकीरीटानि सोष्णीषाणि शिरांसि कोटिशः पेतुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भुवि Onto the ground, रथिनाम् of the chariot-warriors, अश्विनां of the horse-riders, गजिनां and of the elephant-riders, सकुण्डलकीरीटानि along with earrings and crowns, सोष्णीषाणि along with turbans, शिरांसि the heads, कोटिशः by the millions, पेतुः fell.

Simple Meaning: Severed by the millions, the heads of chariot-warriors, cavalrymen, and elephant-riders came raining down onto the battlefield. Stripped from their bodies in an instant, the heads fell to the earth still adorned with brilliant earrings, regal crowns, and splendid turbans.

हस्ताः सासिगदेष्वासाः करभा ऊरवोऽङ्घ्रयः ।
अश्वाश्वतरनागोष्ट्रखरमर्त्यशिरांसि च ॥10.54.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: हस्ताः hands, स-असि-गद-इष्वासाः still holding swords, clubs, and bows, करभाः forearms (or hands shaped like an elephant's trunk), ऊरवः thighs, अङ्घ्रयः feet/legs, अश्व-अश्वतर-नाग-उष्ट्र-खर-मर्त्य-शिरांसि the heads of horses, mules, elephants, camels, donkeys, and men, च and

Anvaya: सासिगदेष्वासाः हस्ताः, करभाः, ऊरवः, अङ्घ्रयः, अश्वाश्वतरनागोष्ट्रखरमर्त्यशिरांसि च (भुवि पेतुः)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सासिगदेष्वासाः Along with swords, clubs, and bows, हस्ताः hands, करभाः forearms, ऊरवः thighs, अङ्घ्रयः legs, अश्वाश्वतरनागोष्ट्रखरमर्त्यशिरांसि the heads of horses, mules, elephants, camels, donkeys, and humans, च and, (भुवि पेतुः) fell upon the ground.

Simple Meaning: The ground became covered with severed hands still tightly gripping swords, clubs, and bows, alongside severed forearms, thighs, and legs. Scattered everywhere among them were the severed heads of horses, mules, elephants, camels, donkeys, and human warriors.

हन्यमानबलानीका वृष्णिभिर्जयकाङ्क्षिभिः ।
राजानो विमुखा जग्मुर्जरासन्धपुरःसरा ॥10.54.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: हन्यमान-बल-अनीकाः whose armies and divisions were being slaughtered, वृष्णिभिः by the Vrishnis (Yadavas), जय-काङ्क्षिभिः who were desiring victory, राजानः the kings, विमुखाः turning their faces away (retreating), जग्मुः fled, जरासन्ध-पुरःसराः led by Jarasandha

Anvaya: जयकाङ्क्षिभिः वृष्णिभिः हन्यमानबलानीकाः जरासन्धपुरःसराः राजानः विमुखाः (सन्तः) जग्मुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जयकाङ्क्षिभिः By those desiring victory, वृष्णिभिः by the Vrishnis, हन्यमानबलानीकाः whose armies and divisions were being slaughtered, जरासन्धपुरःसराः led by Jarasandha, राजानः the kings, विमुखाः turning away, जग्मुः fled.

Simple Meaning: As their armies and divisions were ruthlessly slaughtered by the victory-seeking Vrishni warriors, the enemy kings, led by Jarasandha, turned their backs on the battlefield and fled for their lives.

शिशुपालं समभ्येत्य हृतदारमिवातुरम् ।
नष्टत्विषं गतोत्साहं शुष्यद्वदनमब्रुवन् ॥10.54.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: शिशुपालम् Shishupala, समभ्येत्य approaching, हृत-दारम् whose wife had been stolen, इव like, आतुरम् distressed/suffering, नष्ट-त्विषम् whose luster was completely gone, गत-उत्साहम् devoid of enthusiasm, शुष्यत्-वदनम् whose mouth and face were drying up, अब्रुवन् they (Jarasandha and the other kings) spoke

Anvaya: (ते राजानः) हृतदारम् इव आतुरं नष्टत्विषं गतोत्साहं शुष्यद्वदनं शिशुपालं समभ्येत्य अब्रुवन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (ते राजानः) Those kings, हृतदारम् whose wife has been stolen, इव like, आतुरं distressed, नष्टत्विषं whose luster was gone, गतोत्साहं devoid of enthusiasm, शुष्यद्वदनं whose face was drying up, शिशुपालं Shishupala, समभ्येत्य approaching, अब्रुवन् spoke.

Simple Meaning: Approaching Shishupala—who was utterly devastated like a man whose bride had just been snatched away, his royal luster gone, his enthusiasm broken, and his mouth completely dry from grief—Jarasandha and the surviving kings began to speak to him as follows.

भो भोः पुरुषशार्दूल दौर्मनस्यमिदं त्यज ।
न प्रियाप्रिययो राजन् निष्ठा देहिषु दृश्यते ॥10.54.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: भोः भोः O, O!, पुरुष-शार्दूल O tiger among men, दौर्मनस्यम् this dejection (or depression), इदम् this, त्यज give up, न not, प्रिय-अप्रिययोः of happiness and distress (or the desirable and undesirable), राजन् O King, निष्ठा a permanent state (or certainty), देहिषु in embodied living beings, दृश्यते is seen

Anvaya: भोः भोः पुरुषशार्दूल! इदं दौर्मनस्यं त्यज। राजन्! देहिषु प्रियाप्रिययोः निष्ठा न दृश्यते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भोः भोः O, O!, पुरुषशार्दूल O tiger among men, इदं दौर्मनस्यं this dejection, त्यज give up. राजन् O King, देहिषु among embodied beings, प्रियाप्रिययोः of the pleasant and unpleasant, निष्ठा a permanent, unchanging state, न दृश्यते is not seen.

Simple Meaning: "O tiger among men! Shake off this deep dejection. O King, for all embodied souls in this world, there is no permanent state of either joy or sorrow."

यथा दारुमयी योषिन्नृत्यते कुहकेच्छया ।
एवमीश्वरतन्त्रोऽयमीहते सुखदुःखयोः ॥10.54.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: यथा just as, दारु-मयी made of wood, योषित् a woman (a puppet), नृत्यते is made to dance, कुहक-इच्छया by the will of the puppeteer (magician), एवम् in this very way, ईश्वर-तन्त्रः dependent on the will of the Supreme Controller (or Fate), अयम् this (world of living beings), ईहते acts/strives, सुख-दुःखयोः in happiness and distress

Anvaya: यथा दारुमयी योषित् कुहकेच्छया नृत्यते, एवम् ईश्वरतन्त्रः अयं (जीवः) सुखदुःखयोः ईहते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यथा Just as, दारुमयी made of wood, योषित् a puppet doll, कुहकेच्छया by the desire of the puppeteer, नृत्यते is made to dance, एवम् in the same way, ईश्वरतन्त्रः controlled by the Supreme Lord (or destiny), अयं this (embodied soul), सुखदुःखयोः amid happiness and distress, ईहते acts.

Simple Meaning: "Just as a wooden puppet is made to dance entirely by the sweet will of the puppeteer, so this world of living beings, completely dependent on the absolute control of the Supreme Lord, is moved to act through alternating conditions of happiness and distress.

शौरेः सप्तदशाहं वै संयुगानि पराजितः ।
त्रयोविंशतिभिः सैन्यैर्जिग्य एकमहं परम् ॥10.54.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: शौरेः with Krishna (the descendant of Shura), सप्तदश seventeen times, अहम् I, वै indeed, संयुगानि in battles, पराजितः defeated, त्रयोविंशतिभिः with twenty-three, सैन्यैः by military divisions (Akshauhinis), जिग्ये won/conquered, एकम् once, अहम् I, परम् however

Anvaya: शौरेः सप्तदश त्रयोविंशतिभिः सैन्यैः अहं (जरासन्धः) संयुगानि पराजितः वै, परं एकम् अहं जिग्ये।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: शौरेः With Shauri (Krishna), सप्तदश seventeen times, त्रयोविंशतिभिः with twenty-three, सैन्यैः military divisions, अहं I, संयुगानि in battles, पराजितः was defeated, वै indeed, परं Only, एकम् once, अहं I, जिग्ये won.

Simple Meaning: "Look at my own example: I marched against Krishna with twenty-three full Akshauhini divisions and was soundly defeated by Him in battle seventeen times. Only once have I won (in the last battle)!"

तथाप्यहं न शोचामि न प्रहृष्यामि कर्हिचित् ।
कालेन दैवयुक्तेन जानन् विद्रावितं जगत् ॥10.54.14॥

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तथापि Even so, दैवयुक्तेन coupled with destiny, कालेन by Time, जगत् the world, विद्रावितं driven/impelled, जानन् knowing, अहं I, कर्हिचित् ever, न शोचामि do not lament, न प्रहृष्यामि and not rejoice.

Simple Meaning: "Even so, I never lament in defeat nor do I rejoice in victory. For I know well that this entire universe is relentlessly driven and swept along by the supreme power of Time, aligned with destiny.

Simple Meaning: As Rukmi came hurtling toward Him, Krishna used His arrows to effortlessly shatter both Rukmi's sword and shield into tiny pieces, as small as sesame seeds. Simple Meaning: Lord Balarama explains the absolute oneness of existence: "There is only one Supreme Soul residing within all living entities. It is only the spiritually blind who perceive people as separate, distinct entities, just as the single sky appears divided when viewed through different windows, or the one sun appears as thousands of different reflections in separate pots of water.”

Simple Meaning: Lord Balarama continues his metaphysical analysis: "This physical body—which is born, perishes, and is merely a combination of physical matter, life-airs, and the fluctuating modes of nature—is falsely superimposed upon the pure soul through sheer spiritual ignorance. It is this false identification with the temporary body that traps the conscious soul, forcing it to undergo the painful cycle of repeated birth and death.”

Simple Meaning: Lord Balarama delivers the ultimate conclusion of His instruction: "O virtuous lady, the eternal soul can never truly be joined with or separated from the temporary body and its worldly relationships. The soul is both the foundational cause and the ultimate witness of the body, remaining entirely independent of it. This is just like the sun: although it creates the eye's power to see and illuminates physical forms, the sun itself is never bound by or tied to the eyes or the objects it reveals.”

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जन्मादयः Birth, growth, and decay, तु indeed, देहस्य of the physical frame, विक्रियाः modifications, आत्मनः of the internal soul, क्वचित् ever, न are not—यथा just as, कलानाम् of the changing phases, इन्दोः of the moon, न एव absolutely not; अस्य its (the soul's), मृतिः death/disappearance, कुहूः the Amavasya (new-moon night), इव like, हि certainly (is).

Simple Meaning: Lord Balarama concludes this sequence of instructions with a striking analogy: "Birth, growth, decay, and death are transformations that belong exclusively to the physical body; they never touch the eternal soul. This is exactly like the phases of the moon: it is the individual digits of light that appear, change, and vanish, while the moon itself remains constant. What we call 'death' for the soul is merely like the new-moon night (kuhu)—the soul is simply hidden from our material sight, not destroyed.”

Simple Meaning: Lord Balarama uses the classic analogy of a dream to explain worldly suffering: "When a person is asleep and dreaming, they experience a whole world around them—they see themselves, interact with objects, and feel genuine joy or terror, even though none of those things are actually there. Word-by-word meaning: प्राण-अवशेषः with only his life-breath remaining (barely alive), उत्सृष्टः released/abandoned, द्विड्भिः by his enemies (Krishna and Balarama), हत-बल-प्रभः stripped of his physical strength and bodily luster, स्मरन् constantly remembering, विरूप-करणम् his disfigurement (the shaving of his hair and mustache), वितथ-आत्म-मनोरथः his personal desires and ambitions ruined

Anvaya: द्विड्भिः उत्सृष्टः, प्राणावशेषः, हतबलप्रभः, विरूपकरणं स्मरन्, वितथात्ममनोरथः (रुक्मी अतिष्ठत)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: द्विड्भिः By his adversaries, उत्सृष्टः let go, प्राणावशेषः left with nothing but his bare breath, हतबलप्रभः robbed of his might and splendor, विरूपकरणं the humiliating disfigurement, स्मरन् brooding over, वितथात्ममनोरथः with all his cherished desires completely shattered, (रुक्मी) Rukmi (remained).

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka now shifts the focus back to Rukmini's defeated brother: Released by his adversaries with nothing but his bare life remaining, Rukmi stood there utterly ruined. Stripped of his military might, his royal prestige, and his physical luster, he could do nothing but brood bitterly over his humiliating disfigurement, his grand ambitions completely shattered.

चक्रे भोजकटं नाम निवासाय महत् पुरम् ।
अहत्वा दुर्मतिं कृष्णमप्रत्यूह्य यवीयसीम् ।
कुण्डिनं न प्रवेक्ष्यामीत्युक्त्वा तत्रावसद् रुषा ॥10.54.52॥

Word-by-word meaning: चक्रे he built/created, भोजकटम् Bhojakata, नाम named, निवासाय for his residence, महत् a great, पुरम् city, अहत्वा without killing, दुर्मतिम् the wicked-minded, कृष्णम् Krishna, अप्रत्यूह्य without bringing back, यवीयसीम् my younger sister (Rukmini), कुण्डिनम् into Kundina (his capital city), न never, प्रवेक्ष्यामि I shall enter, इति thus, उक्त्वा having vowed, तत्र there, अवसत् he lived, रुषा with deep anger/wrath

Anvaya: 'दुर्मतिं कृष्णम् अहत्वा यवीयसीम् अप्रत्यूह्य च कुण्डिनं न प्रवेक्ष्यामि' इति उक्त्वा रुषा निवासाय भोजकटं नाम महत् पुरं चक्रे, तत्र अवसत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: 'दुर्मतिं कृष्णम् The evil-minded, Krishna, अहत्वा without slaying, यवीयसीम् my younger sister, अप्रत्यूह्य च and without rescuing/taking back, कुण्डिनं the capital city of Kundina, न प्रवेक्ष्यामि I shall not enter,' इति thus, उक्त्वा having sworn, रुषा filled with rage, निवासाय for his dwelling, भोजकटं नाम named Bhojakata, महत् पुरं a large city, चक्रे he constructed, तत्र and there, अवसत् he resided.

Simple Meaning: Driven by intense humiliation and rage, Rukmi took a solemn vow: "I will never enter my capital city of Kundina again until I have slain the wicked Krishna and brought my younger sister back!" Remaining right where he was defeated, he constructed a grand city named Bhojakata, and made it his permanent residence.

भगवान् भीष्मकसुतामेवं निर्जित्य भूमिपान् ।
पुरमानीय विधिवदुपयेमे कुरूद्वह ॥10.54.53॥

Word-by-word meaning: भगवान् the Supreme Lord (Krishna), भीष्मक-सुताम् the daughter of Bhishmaka (Rukmini), एवम् in this manner, निर्जित्य having thoroughly defeated, भूमि-पान् the kings/rulers of the earth, पुरम् to His city (Dwarka), आनीय having brought, विधिवत् according to prescribed Vedic rites, उपयेमे married her, कुरु-उद्वह O best of the Kurus (Parikshit)

Anvaya: कुरूद्वह! एवं भगवान् भूमिपान् निर्जित्य भीष्मकसुतां पुरम् आनीय विधिवत् उपयेमे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कुरूद्वह O prominent leader of the Kurus (King Parikshit)!, एवं in this way, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, भूमिपान् the rival kings, निर्जित्य having utterly vanquished, भीष्मकसुतां the daughter of King Bhishmaka, पुरम् to His own capital city, आनीय having escorted, विधिवत् in accordance with formal scriptural injunctions, उपयेमे accepted her hand in marriage.

Simple Meaning: O best of the Kuru dynasty! Having thoroughly defeated all the opposing kings in this manner, the Supreme Lord Krishna escorted Rukmini, the daughter of King Bhishmaka, to His capital city of Dwaraka and married her in full accordance with the sacred Vedic rites.

तदा महोत्सवो नृणां यदुपुर्यां गृहे गृहे ।
अभूदनन्यभावानां कृष्णे यदुपतौ नृप ॥10.54.54॥

Word-by-word meaning: तदा then/at that time, महोत्सवः a grand festival, नृणाम् of the people, यदु-पुर्याम् in the city of the Yadus (Dwaraka), गृहे गृहे in every single home, अभूत् there was, अनन्य-भावानाम् of those possessing unalloyed/exclusive devotion, कृष्णे towards Krishna, यदु-पतौ towards the Lord of the Yadus, नृप O King (Parikshit)

Anvaya: नृप! तदा यदुपतौ कृष्णे अनन्यभावानां नृणां यदुपुर्यां गृहे गृहे महोत्सवः अभूत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King!, तदा at that time, यदुपतौ कृष्णे towards the master of the Yadu dynasty, Krishna, अनन्यभावानां नृणां of the citizens, who possessed undivided love and devotion, यदुपुर्यां inside the city of the Yadus, गृहे गृहे in house after house, महोत्सवः a massive celebration, अभूत् took place.

Simple Meaning: O King! Because the citizens of Dwarka possessed pure, unalloyed devotion toward Krishna, the master of the Yadus, a spectacular, joyous festival erupted in every single household across the city to celebrate the arrival of the Lord and His new bride.

नरा नार्यश्च मुदिताः प्रमृष्टमणिकुण्डलाः ।
पारिबर्हमुपाजह्रुर्वरयोश्चित्रवाससोः ॥10.54.55॥

Word-by-word meaning: नराः the men, नार्यः the women, च and, मुदिताः filled with joy, प्रमृष्ट-मणि-कुण्डलाः wearing polished jeweled earrings, पारिबर्हम् wedding gifts/presents, उपाजह्रुः brought forward, वरयोः for the newly wed couple, चित्र-वाससोः who were dressed in exquisite, colorful garments

Anvaya: प्रमृष्टमणिकुण्डलाः मुदिताः नराः नार्यः च चित्रवाससोः वरयोः पारिबर्हम् उपाजह्रुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रमृष्टमणिकुण्डलाः Wearing brilliantly polished gemstone earrings, मुदिताः jubilant, नराः नार्यः च men and women, चित्रवाससोः वरयोः for the bride and groom dressed in splendid, multicolored wedding attire, पारिबर्हम् abundant bridal gifts, उपाजह्रुः presented.

Simple Meaning: Dressed in their finest attire and wearing brilliantly polished jeweled earrings, the joyful men and women of the city came forward to offer beautiful wedding gifts to the newlywed couple, who looked magnificent in their colorful, exquisite garments.

सा वृष्णिपुर्युत्तभितेन्द्रकेतुभिर्विचित्रमाल्याम्बररत्नतोरणैः ।
बभौ प्रतिद्वार्युपक्लृप्तमङ्गलैरापूर्णकुम्भागुरुधूपदीपकैः ॥10.54.56॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा that, वृष्णि-पुरी the city of the Vrishnis (Dwarka), उत्तभित-इन्द्र-केतुभिः with raised victory flags, विचित्र-माल्य-अम्बर-रत्न-तोरणैः with decorative archways made of colorful garlands, fine cloth, and precious gems, बभौ shone brilliantly, प्रति-द्वारि at every single doorway/gate, उपक्लृप्त-मङ्गलैः with auspicious arrangements set up, आपूर्ण-कुम्भ-अगुरु-धूप-दीपकैः with overflowing water-pots, fragrant aguru incense, and shining lamps

Anvaya: उत्तभितेन्द्रकेतुभिः विचित्रमाल्याम्बररत्नतोरणैः प्रतिद्वारि उपक्लृप्तमङ्गलैः आपूर्णकुम्भागुरुधूपदीपकैः च सा वृष्णिपुरी बभौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: उत्तभितेन्द्रकेतुभिः By high-raised banners of victory, विचित्रमाल्याम्बररत्नतोरणैः by decorative archways made of multicolored garlands, fabrics, and jewels, प्रतिद्वारि at every single threshold, उपक्लृप्तमङ्गलैः by arranged auspicious items, आपूर्णकुम्भागुरुधूपदीपकैः च and by completely filled water-pitchers, aromatic aloe wood incense, and bright lamps, सा वृष्णिपुरी that capital city of the Vrishnis, बभौ radiated splendor.

Simple Meaning: That capital city of the Vrishnis (Dwaraka) shone with splendor. Tall, majestic victory banners fluttered in the air, and gates were adorned with archways woven from exquisite textiles, jewels, and multi-colored flowers. At every single doorway, auspicious offerings were meticulously arranged—including overflowing water-jugs, fragrant burning aguru incense, and brightly glowing lamps.

सिक्तमार्गां मदच्युद्भिराहूतप्रेष्ठभूभुजाम् ।
गजैर्द्वास्सु परामृष्टरम्भापूगोपशोभिता ॥10.54.57॥

Word-by-word meaning: सिक्त-मार्गाम् whose streets were sprinkled/moistened, मद-च्युद्भिः by the exuding rut (ichor juice), आहूत-प्रेष्ठ-भू-भुजाम् of the invited, beloved allied kings, गजैः by the elephants, द्वास्सु at the entrance gates, परामृष्ट-रम्भा-पूग-उपशोभिता decorated with freshly cut banana trees and betel-nut trees (laden with fruit)

Anvaya: (सा वृष्णिपुरी) आहूतप्रेष्ठभूभुजां मदच्युद्भिः गजैः सिक्तमार्गां द्वास्सु परामृष्टरम्भापूगोपशोभिता (च बभौ)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सा वृष्णिपुरी That city of the Vrishnis) आहूतप्रेष्ठभूभुजां of the invited and deeply beloved royal guests, मदच्युद्भिः emitting fragrant ichor, गजैः by the elephants, सिक्तमार्गां having its avenues sprinkled and moistened, द्वास्सु and at every entranceway, परामृष्टरम्भापूगोपशोभिता adorned with beautifully arranged banana stalks and betel-nut trees laden with fruit, (च बभौ and radiated splendor).

Simple Meaning: The royal avenues of the city were completely moistened and cooled by the fragrant ichor dripping from the magnificent elephants of the beloved, invited kings. Furthermore, every entranceway and portal was exquisitely adorned with freshly cut banana trees and fruit-laden betel-nut trees, adding to the city's festive grandeur.

कुरुसृञ्जयकैकेयविदर्भयदुकुन्तयः ।
मिथो मुमुदिरे तस्मिन् सम्भ्रमात् परिधावताम् ॥10.54.58॥

Word-by-word meaning: कुरु-सृञ्जय-कैकेय-विदर्भ-यदु-कुन्तयः the members of the Kuru, Srinjaya, Kaikeya, Vidarbha, Yadu, and Kunti dynasties, मिथः among one another, मुमुदिरे rejoiced/felt supreme delight, तस्मिन् in that (wedding festival), सम्भ्रमात् out of joyful excitement/haste, परिधावताम् of those who were running about here and there

Anvaya: तस्मिन् (महोत्सवे) सम्भ्रमात् परिधावतां कुरुसृञ्जयकैकेयविदर्भयदुकुन्तयः मिथः मुमुदिरे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तस्मिन् In that grand celebration, सम्भ्रमात् due to ecstatic eagerness, परिधावतां of the people rushing to and fro, कुरुसृञ्जयकैकेयविदर्भयदुकुन्तयः the royals and citizens of the Kuru, Srinjaya, Kaikeya, Vidarbha, Yadu, and Kunti clans, मिथः mutually/with one another, मुमुदिरे rejoiced exceedingly.

Simple Meaning: During that grand wedding celebration, as people eagerly rushed about here and there in festive excitement, the gathered members of the Kuru, Srinjaya, Kaikeya, Vidarbha, Yadu, and Kunti dynasties all mingled and rejoiced together in pure bliss.

रुक्मिण्या हरणं श्रुत्वा गीयमानं ततस्ततः ।
राजानो राजकन्याश्च बभूवुर्भृशविस्मिताः ॥10.54.59॥

Word-by-word meaning: रुक्मिण्याः of Rukmini, हरणम् the abduction/carrying away, श्रुत्वा having heard, गीयमानम् being sung/praised, ततः ततः here and there (everywhere), राजानः the kings, राज-कन्याः च and the princesses, बभूवुः became, भृश-विस्मिताः utterly astonished/struck with wonder

Anvaya: ततस्ततः गीयमानं रुक्मिण्याः हरणं श्रुत्वा राजानः राजकन्याः च भृशविस्मिताः बभूवुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततस्ततः Everywhere, गीयमानं being sung in celebratory verses, रुक्मिण्याः हरणं the carrying away of Rukmini, श्रुत्वा having listened to, राजानः the ruling kings, राजकन्याः च and the young royal princesses, भृशविस्मिताः completely filled with amazement, बभूवुः became.

Simple Meaning: As the thrilling account of Rukmini’s carrying away by Krishna was sung by bards and celebrated everywhere, all the kings and princesses across the lands who heard it were struck with utter amazement and wonder.

द्वारकायामभूद् राजन् महामोदः पुरौकसाम् ।
रुक्मिण्या रमयोपेतं दृष्ट्वा कृष्णं श्रियः पतिम् ॥10.54.60॥

Word-by-word meaning: द्वारकायाम् in the city of Dwarka, अभूत् there arose, राजन् O King (Parikshit), महा-मोदः immense joy/exultation, पुर-ओकसाम् of the citizens/inhabitants of the city, रुक्मिण्या with Rukmini, रमया उपेतम् comparing to Rama (the Goddess of Fortune), दृष्ट्वा having seen, कृष्णम् Krishna, श्रियः पतिम् the Lord of Lakshmi (the Lord of all Fortune)

Anvaya: राजन्! रमया रुक्मिण्या उपेतं श्रियः पतिं कृष्णं दृष्ट्वा द्वारकायां पुरौकसां महामोदः अभूत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King!, रुक्मिण्या रमया उपेतं comparing Rukmini to the Goddess of Fortune, श्रियः पतिं कृष्णं and the eternal husband of Lakshmi, Krishna, दृष्ट्वा having gazed upon, द्वारकायां inside Dwarka, पुरौकसां of the inhabitants of the city, महामोदः a state of supreme, ecstatic joy, अभूत् manifested.

Simple Meaning: O King! When the citizens of Dwarka gazed upon Krishna, the eternal Lord of Fortune, accompanied by His new bride Rukmini—who is none other than Goddess Lakshmi (Rama) herself—an absolute wave of supreme, ecstatic joy swept through every single inhabitant of the city.

कृष्णरुक्मिणीउद्वाहः च​ नाम​” चतुःपञ्चाशत्तमः अध्यायः ॥54॥

Thus ends the fifty-fourth discourse, named, “Discomfiture of Shishupala's allies and Rukmi, and the wedding of Krishna and Rukmini” in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
