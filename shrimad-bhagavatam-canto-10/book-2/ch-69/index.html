Chapter 69

A glimpse into the household life of Krishna by Narada

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

नारदेन कृष्णगार्हस्थ्यदर्शनं नाम एकोनसप्ततितमः अध्यायः

(45 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
नरकं निहतं श्रुत्वा तथोद्वाहं च योषिताम् ।
कृष्ण्नैकेन बह्नीनां तद् दिदृक्षुः स्म नारदः ॥10.69.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, नरकम् Narakasura, निहतम् killed, श्रुत्वा hearing, तथा as well as, उद्वाहम् the marriage, च and, योषिताम् of the women/princesses, कृष्णेन एकेन by Krishna alone, बह्वीनाम् of many, तत् that, दिदृक्षुः स्म desire to see, नारदः Sage Narada

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच – नारदः नरकं निहतं तथा एकेन कृष्णेन बह्वीनां योषिताम् उद्वाहं च श्रुत्वा तत् दिदृक्षुः स्म।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said: नारदः Sage Narada, नरकं निहतं Narakasura slain, तथा and also, एकेन कृष्णेन by Krishna alone, बह्वीनां योषिताम् of many princesses, उद्वाहं च the simultaneous marriage, श्रुत्वा having heard, तत् दिदृक्षुः स्म became desirous of witnessing that.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: When Sage Narada heard that Krishna had slain Narakasura and single-handedly married thousands of princesses all at the same time, he was filled with wonder and wished to witness this extraordinary feat with his own eyes.

चित्रं बतैतदेकेन वपुषा युगपत् पृथक् ।
गृहेषु द्व्यष्टसाहस्रं स्त्रिय एक उदावहत् ॥10.69.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: चित्रम् wonderful/astonishing, बत indeed, एतत् this, एकेन with a single, वपुषा form/body, युगपत् simultaneously, पृथक् separately, गृहेषु in houses/palaces, द्वि-अष्ट-साहस्रम् sixteen thousand (two times eight thousand), स्त्रीः women, एकः one (Krishna), उदावहत् married

Anvaya: बत!  एतत् चित्रम्। एकेन वपुषा एकः (कृष्णः) द्व्यष्टसाहस्रं स्त्रियः पृथक् गृहेषु युगपत् उदावहत्,।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बत Ah, indeed! एतत् चित्रम् this is truly wondrous. एकेन वपुषा With a single form, एकः (कृष्णः) one single Krishna, द्व्यष्टसाहस्रं स्त्रियः sixteen thousand women, पृथक् गृहेषु in separate individual homes, युगपत् उदावहत् married simultaneously.

Simple Meaning: Sage Narada marveled: "How astonishing! This is truly a marvel! With a single body, how could Krishna marry sixteen thousand women simultaneously in sixteen thousand separate palaces?"

इत्युत्सुको द्वारवतीं देवर्षिर्द्रष्टुमागमत् ।
पुष्पितोपवनारामद्विजालिकुलनादिताम् ॥10.69.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, उत्सुकः eager/curious, द्वारवतीम् to Dwaraka, देव-ऋषिः the divine sage (Narada), द्रष्टुम् to see, आगमत् came, पुष्पित-उपवन-आराम-द्विज-अलि-कुल-नादिताम् echoing with the sounds of swarms of birds and bees in flowering gardens and parks

Anvaya: इति उत्सुकः देवर्षिः पुष्पितोपवनारामद्विजालिकुलनादितां द्वारवतीं द्रष्टुम् आगमत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति उत्सुकः Thus eager with wonder, देवर्षिः the celestial sage Narada, पुष्पितोपवनारामद्विजालिकुलनादितां resonating with flocks of birds and swarms of bees in blooming gardens and orchards, द्वारवतीं द्रष्टुम् आगमत् arrived to view the city of Dwaraka.

Simple Meaning: Filled with wonder and curiosity, the celestial sage Narada traveled to the city of Dwaraka. The entire city resounded with the sweet humming of bees and the melodious chirping of birds flitting through lush, blooming orchards and gardens.

उत्फुल्लेन्दीवराम्भोजकह्लार कुमुदोत्पलैः ।
छुरितेषु सरस्सूच्चैः कूजितां हंससारसैः ॥10.69.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: उत्फुल्ल-इन्दीवर-अम्भोज-कह्लार-कुमुद-उत्पलैः with fully blown blue lotuses, pink lotuses, white lotuses, water lilies, and red lotuses, छुरितेषु in the filled/adorned, सरस्सु in lakes/ponds, उच्चैः loudly, कूजिताम् filled with the warbling, हंस-सारसैः by swans and cranes

Anvaya: उत्फुल्लेन्दीवराम्भोजकह्लारकुमुदोत्पलैः छुरितेषु सरस्सु हंससारसैः उच्चैः कूजितां (द्वारवतीं देवर्षिः ददर्श)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: उत्फुल्लेन्दीवराम्भोजकह्लारकुमुदोत्पलैः With fully open blue, pink, and white lotuses and water lilies, छुरितेषु filled and decorated, सरस्सु in the lakes, हंससारसैः by swans and cranes, उच्चैः कूजितां loudly reverberating with sweet bird calls, (द्वारवतीं देवर्षिः ददर्श Narada beheld the city of Dwaraka).

Simple Meaning: The lakes of Dwaraka were blanketed with fully blossomed blue, pink, and white lotuses and lilies, and resounded with the joyful, melodious calls of swans and cranes.

प्रासादलक्षैर्नवभिर्जुष्टां स्फाटिकराजतैः ।
महामरकतप्रख्यैः स्वर्णरत्नपरिच्छदैः ॥69.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रासाद-लक्षैः with lakhs of palaces, नवभिः nine (nine hundred thousand palaces), जुष्टाम् endowed/adorned with, स्फाटिक-राजतैः built of crystal and silver, महा-मरकत-प्रख्यैः resplendent with precious emeralds, स्वर्ण-रत्न-परिच्छदैः with furnishings made of gold and jewels

Anvaya: स्फाटिकराजतैः महामरकतप्रख्यैः स्वर्णरत्नपरिच्छदैः नवभिः प्रासादलक्षैः जुष्टाम् (द्वारवतीं ददर्श)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: स्फाटिकराजतैः Built with pure crystal and silver, महामरकतप्रख्यैः resplendent with precious emeralds, स्वर्णरत्नपरिच्छदैः equipped with opulent furnishings of gold and gems, नवभिः प्रासादलक्षैः with nine lakh (nine hundred thousand) palaces, जुष्टाम् adorned, (द्वारवतीं ददर्श Narada saw Dwaraka).

Simple Meaning: Sage Narada saw Dwaraka filled with nine hundred thousand magnificent palaces crafted from crystal and silver, gleaming brightly with emeralds, and furnished inside with exquisite gold and jewels.

विभक्तरथ्यापथचत्वरापणैः शालासभाभी रुचिरां सुरालयैः ।
संसिक्तमार्गाङ्गणवीथिदेहलीं पतत्पताकाध्वजवारितातपाम् ॥69.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: विभक्त-रथ्या-पथ-चत्वर-आपणैः with systematically laid out main roads, lanes, crossroads, and marketplaces, शाला-सभाभिः with residential halls and public assembly houses, रुचिराम् beautiful, सुर-आलयैः with temples of devas, संसिक्त-मार्ग-अङ्गण-वीथि-देहलीम् with sprinkled/watered main roads, courtyards, side lanes, and doorsteps, पतत्-पताका-ध्वज-वारित-आतपाम् with swaying banners and flags blocking the heat of the sun

Anvaya: विभक्तरथ्यापथचत्वरापणैः शालासभाभिः सुरालयैः रुचिरां, संसिक्तमार्गाङ्गणवीथिदेहलीं, पतत्पताकाध्वजवारितातपाम् (द्वारवतीं ददर्श)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: विभक्तरथ्यापथचत्वरापणैः With neatly divided avenues, streets, town squares, and markets, शालासभाभिः सुरालयैः with mansions, assembly halls, and temples, रुचिरां adorned and picturesque, संसिक्तमार्गाङ्गणवीथिदेहलीं whose roads, courtyards, lanes, and thresholds were freshly sprinkled with water, पतत्पताकाध्वजवारितातपाम् whose sunlit heat was warded off by fluttering banners and tall flags, (द्वारवतीं ददर्श Narada saw Dwaraka).

Simple Meaning: Dwaraka was beautifully designed with well-planned avenues, broad streets, public squares, bustling marketplaces, grand assembly halls, and magnificent temples. Its roads, courtyards, and doorways were constantly washed and sprinkled with water, while flags and banners fluttered overhead, sheltering the city from the fierce heat of the sun.

तस्यामन्तःपुरं श्रीमदर्चितं सर्वधिष्ण्यपैः ।
हरेः स्वकौशलं यत्र त्वष्ट्रा कार्त्स्न्येन दर्शितम् ॥10.69.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्याम् in that city (Dwaraka), अन्तःपुरम् the inner private quarters/palace compound, श्रीमत् glorious/opulent, अर्चितम् worshipped/revered, सर्व-धिष्ण्य-पैः by all the guardians of planets/universes, हरेः of Lord Hari, स्व-कौशलम् his own craftsmanship/skill, यत्र wherein, त्वष्ट्रा by Vishvakarma (the architect of the devas), कार्त्स्न्येन in its entirety, दर्शितम् was displayed

त्वष्ट्रा  - त्वष्टृ ऋकारान्तः  पुंलिङ्गः  3.1  (देवशिल्पिः)

Anvaya: तस्यां सर्वधिष्ण्यपैः अर्चितं श्रीमत् हरेः अन्तःपुरं (वर्तते), यत्र त्वष्ट्रा स्वकौशलं कार्त्स्न्येन दर्शितम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तस्यां In that city of Dwaraka, सर्वधिष्ण्यपैः अर्चितं worshipped and admired by all the universal guardians, श्रीमत् magnificent, हरेः अन्तःपुरं the inner residential palace complex of Lord Hari (stood), यत्र wherein, त्वष्ट्रा by Vishvakarma, स्वकौशलं his own supreme architectural craftsmanship, कार्त्स्न्येन in all its fullness, दर्शितम् was exhibited.

Simple Meaning: Within that magnificent city stood Lord Hari's private palace quarters, revered even by the great guardians of the universe. In constructing these palaces, Vishvakarma, the divine architect, had displayed the absolute pinnacle of his art and skill.

तत्र षोडशभिः सद्मसहस्रैः समलङ्कृतम् ।
विवेशैकतमं शौरेः पत्नीनां भवनं महत् ॥10.69.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there, षोडशभिः with sixteen, सद्म-सहस्रैः with thousands of palaces (sixteen thousand palaces), समलङ्कृतम् beautifully adorned, विवेश entered, एकतमम् one among them, शौरेः of Shauri (Krishna), पत्नीनाम् of the wives, भवनम् palace, महत् grand/magnificent

Anvaya: तत्र शौरेः पत्नीनां षोडशभिः सद्मसहस्रैः समलङ्कृतम् एकतमं महत् भवनं विवेश।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत्र There in Dwaraka, शौरेः पत्नीनां of the wives of Krishna, षोडशभिः सद्मसहस्रैः with sixteen thousand palaces, समलङ्कृतं beautifully adorned, एकतमं महत् भवनं one magnificent palace, विवेश Narada entered.

Simple Meaning: There, of the sixteen thousand palaces of the wives of Krishna, Narada entered one of the magnificent palaces.

विष्टब्धं विद्रुमस्तम्भैर्वैदूर्यफलकोत्तमैः ।
इन्द्रनीलमयैः कुड्यैर्जगत्या चाहतत्विषा ॥10.69.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: विष्टब्धम् supported, विद्रुम-स्तम्भैः with pillars made of coral, वैदूर्य-फलक-उत्तमैः with superior floor planks of lapis lazuli, इन्द्रनील-मयैः made of sapphires, कुड्यैः with walls, जगत्या with floors/foundations, च and, आहत-त्विषा possessing brilliant radiance/luster

Anvaya: (तत् भवनम्) विद्रुमस्तम्भैः वैदूर्यफलकोत्तमैः इन्द्रनीलमयैः कुड्यैः आहतत्विषा जगत्या च विष्टब्धम् (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (तत् भवनम् That palace), विद्रुमस्तम्भैः with coral pillars, वैदूर्यफलकोत्तमैः with the finest lapis lazuli floor blocks, इन्द्रनीलमयैः कुड्यैः with walls built of sapphire, आहतत्विषा जगत्या च and with a glowing foundation floor of unfading brilliance, विष्टब्धम् was structurally held up and adorned.

Simple Meaning: That grand palace was supported by columns of red coral and featured fine lapis lazuli pavements. Its walls were inlaid with brilliant blue sapphires, and its glowing floors radiated an intense, dazzling light.

वितानैर्निर्मितैस्त्वष्ट्रा मुक्तादामविलम्बिभिः ।
दान्तैरासनपर्यङ्कैर्मण्युत्तमपरिष्कृतैः ॥10.69.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: वितानैः with canopies, निर्मितैः constructed, त्वष्ट्रा by Vishvakarma, मुक्ता-दाम-विलम्बिभिः hanging with pearl garlands, दान्तैः made of ivory, आसन-पर्यङ्कैः with seats and beds, मणि-उत्तम-परिष्कृतैः inlaid with the finest gems

Anvaya: (तत् भवनम्) त्वष्ट्रा निर्मितैः मुक्तादामविलम्बिभिः वितानैः, मण्युत्तमपरिष्कृतैः दान्तैः आसनपर्यङ्कैः (च समन्वितम् आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (तत् भवनम् That palace), त्वष्ट्रा निर्मितैः crafted by Vishvakarma, मुक्तादामविलम्बिभिः from which strands of pearls hung down, वितानैः with canopies, मण्युत्तमपरिष्कृतैः beautifully decorated with precious jewels, दान्तैः आसनपर्यङ्कैः and with ivory seats and couches, (समन्वितम् आसीत् was richly furnished).

Simple Meaning: The palace was adorned with elegant canopies designed by Vishvakarma from which hung strands of lustrous pearls. It was furnished with exquisite couches and seats carved from pure ivory and inlaid with the finest gems.

दासीभिर्निष्ककण्ठीभिः सुवासोभिरलङ्कृतम् ।
पुम्भिः सकञ्चुकोष्णीषसुवस्त्रमणिकुण्डलैः ॥10.69.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: दासीभिः by maidservants, निष्क-कण्ठीभिः wearing golden necklaces, सु-वासोभिः dressed in fine garments, अलङ्कृतम् adorned, पुम्भिः by male guards/attendants, स-कञ्चुक-उष्णीष-सुवस्त्र-मणि-कुण्डलैः wearing jackets, turbans, fine clothes, and jeweled earrings

Anvaya: (तत् भवनम्) निष्ककण्ठीभिः सुवासोभिः दासीभिः, सकञ्चुकोष्णीषसुवस्त्रमणिकुण्डलैः पुम्भिः (च) अलङ्कृतम् (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (तत् भवनम् That palace), निष्ककण्ठीभिः wearing gold lockets around their necks, सुवासोभिः attired in beautiful dresses, दासीभिः by female attendants, सकञ्चुकोष्णीषसुवस्त्रमणिकुण्डलैः adorned with doublets, turbans, elegant garments, and gem-studded earrings, पुम्भिः by male guards, (च) अलङ्कृतम् was gracefully attended and embellished.

Simple Meaning: That residence was served by beautiful maidservants dressed in fine garments and wearing golden necklaces. It was also guarded and attended by handsome male servants elegantly attired in turbans, jackets, fine clothes, and brilliant jeweled earrings.

रत्नदीपनिकरद्युतिभिर्निरस्तध्वान्तं विचित्रवलभीषु शिखण्डिनोङ्ग ।
नृत्यन्ति यत्र विहीतागुरुधूपमक्षैर्निर्यान्तमीक्ष्य घनबुद्धयः उन्नदन्तः ॥10.69.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: रत्न-दीप-निकर-द्युतिभिः by the brilliant radiance of arrayed jewel lamps, निरस्त-ध्वान्तम् dispelled darkness, विचित्र-वलभीषु on the colorful rooftops/turrets, शिखण्डिनः peacocks, अङ्ग O my dear (King Parikshit), नृत्यन्ति dance, यत्र where, विहीत-अगरु-धूपम् released aloe incense smoke, अक्षैः through the lattice windows, निर्यान्तम् coming out, ईक्ष्य seeing, घन-बुद्धयः thinking it to be rain clouds, उन्नदन्तः crying aloud/screaming with joy

Anvaya: अङ्ग! यत्र रत्नदीपनिकरद्युतिभिः निरस्तध्वान्तं (भवनम् आसीत्), अक्षैः निर्यान्तं विहीतागरुधूपम् ईक्ष्य घनबुद्धयः शिखण्डिनः विचित्रवलभीषु उन्नदन्तः नृत्यन्ति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अङ्ग O King, यत्र where (in that palace), रत्नदीपनिकरद्युतिभिः by the glowing beams of clusters of gem-embedded lamps, निरस्तध्वान्तं darkness was completely driven away. अक्षैः Through the latticed window openings, निर्यान्तं drifting outwards, विहीतागरुधूपम् the rich incense smoke of aguru, ईक्ष्य seeing, घनबुद्धयः mistaking it to be dark monsoon clouds, शिखण्डिनः peacocks, विचित्रवलभीषु on the uniquely ornamented rooftops, उन्नदन्तः calling out with excitement, नृत्यन्ति were dancing.

Simple Meaning: O King, the darkness inside that palace was completely dispelled by the brilliant glow of jewel-studded lamps. Anyone who faithfully sings, hears, or simply rejoices in these divine pastimes will surely attain pure devotion unto the Supreme Lord, who is Himself the ultimate path to complete liberation."

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “नारदेन कृष्णगार्हस्थ्यदर्शनं नाम” एकोनसप्ततितमः अध्यायः ॥69॥

Thus ends the sixty-ninth discourse named “A glimpse into the household life of Krishna by Narada”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
