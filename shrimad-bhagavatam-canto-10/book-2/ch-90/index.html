Krishna’s pastimes with His wives, their lamentations on separation from Krishna and an account of Krishna’s descendants

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

 कृष्णस्य पत्नीभिः सह क्रिडाः, तद्विरहे तासां विलापाः, कृष्णवंशजवर्णनं च नाम नवतितमः अध्यायः

(50 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
सुखं स्वपुर्यां निवसन् द्वारकायां श्रियः पतिः ।
सर्वसंपत्समृद्धायां जुष्टायां वृष्णिपुङ्गवैः ॥10.90.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, सुखम् happily/comfortably, स्व-पुर्याम् in His own city, निवसन् residing, द्वारकायाम् in Dwaraka, श्रियः-पतिः the Lord of the goddess of fortune (Shri), सर्व-संपत्-समृद्धायाम् fully enriched with all opulences, जुष्टायाम् inhabited/adorned, वृष्णि-पुङ्गवैः by the most eminent heroes of the Vrishni dynasty

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— श्रियः पतिः (भगवान् कृष्णः) सर्वसंपत्समृद्धायां वृष्णिपुङ्गवैः जुष्टायां स्वपुर्यां द्वारकायां सुखं निवसन् …

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said: श्रियः पतिः The Supreme Lord of the goddess of fortune, सर्वसंपत्समृद्धायां in that city which was completely enriched with all celestial and earthly opulences, वृष्णिपुङ्गवैः जुष्टायां inhabited by the most noble heroes among the Vrishnis, स्वपुर्यां द्वारकायां in His own capital city of Dwarka, सुखं निवसन् residing happily…

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Lord Krishna, the supreme Master of the goddess of fortune, resided happily in His own capital city of Dwaraka, which was enriched with every divine opulence and home to the most eminent heroes of the Vrishni dynasty.

स्त्रीभिश्चोत्तमवेषाभिर्नवयौवनकान्तिभिः ।
कन्दुकादिभिर्हर्म्येषु क्रीडन्तीभिस्तडिद्द्युभिः॥10.90.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्त्रीभिः by women, च and, उत्तम-वेषाभिः dressed in magnificent attire, नव-यौवन-कान्तिभिः radiant with the charm of fresh youthfulness, कन्दुक-आदिभिः with balls and other playthings, हर्म्येषु on the palatial rooftops, क्रीडन्तीभिः playing/sporting, तडित्-द्युभिः effulgent like flashes of lightning

Anvaya: (सा द्वारका) उत्तमवेषाभिः नवयौवनकान्तिभिः तडिद्द्युभिः हर्म्येषु कन्दुकादिभिः क्रीडन्तीभिः स्त्रीभिः शोभिता आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सा द्वारका) That city of Dwaraka, उत्तमवेषाभिः dressed in exquisite garments, नवयौवनकान्तिभिः possessing the beauty of prime youth, तडिद्द्युभिः gleaming like flashes of lightning, हर्म्येषु on the upper balconies of the palaces, कन्दुकादिभिः with balls and other playful objects, क्रीडन्तीभिः disporting, स्त्रीभिः by women, (शोभिता आसीत्) was splendidly adorned.

Simple Meaning: That city of Dwaraka was splendidly adorned with women dressed in magnificent attire and radiant with the charm of fresh youthfulness, who, gleaming like flashes of lightning, played with balls and other playthings upon the palatial balconies.

नित्यं संकुलमार्गायां मदच्युद्भिर्मतङ्गजैः ।
स्वलङ्कृतैर्भटैरश्वै रथैश्च कनकोज्ज्वलैः ॥10.90.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: नित्यम् constantly/perpetually, संकुल-मार्गायाम् in the densely crowded royal avenues, मद-च्युद्भिः exuding temporal juice (in state of rut), मतङ्गजैः by mighty elephants, सु-अलङ्कृतैः exquisitely adorned, भटैः by brave warriors, अश्वैः by horses, रथैः by chariots, च and, कनक-उज्ज्वलैः glowing brightly with gold

Anvaya: (सा द्वारका) संकुलमार्गायां नित्यं मदच्युद्भिः मतङ्गजैः स्वलङ्कृतैः भटैः अश्वैः कनकोज्ज्वलैः रथैः च (शोभिता आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सा द्वारका) That city of Dwarka, संकुलमार्गायां नित्यं in the densely crowded royal avenues, मदच्युद्भिः मतङ्गजैः by grand elephants exuding temporal juice, स्वलङ्कृतैः भटैः by beautifully decorated heroic warriors, अश्वैः by fine horses, कनकोज्ज्वलैः रथैः च and by chariots gleaming with gold, (शोभिता आसीत्) was adorned.

Simple Meaning: The royal avenues of Dwaraka were perpetually bustling with activity, filled with grand elephants in rut, exquisitely adorned warriors, fine horses, and chariots gleaming brightly with gold.

उद्यानोपवनाढ्यायां पुष्पितद्रुमराजिषु ।
निर्विशद्भृङ्गविहगैर्नादितायां समन्ततः ॥10.90.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: उद्यान-उपवन-आढ्यायाम् in abundantly enriched with magnificent public gardens and private pleasure groves, पुष्पित-द्रुम-राजिषु in the lines of trees filled with blossoming flowers, निर्विशत्-भृङ्ग-विहगैः by swarming bees and birds feeding on nectar, नादितायाम् resounding with harmonious sounds, समन्ततः all around/on every side

Anvaya: (सा द्वारका) उद्यानोपवनाढ्यायां पुष्पितद्रुमराजिषु निर्विशद्भृङ्गविहगैः समन्ततः नादिता (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सा द्वारका) That city of Dwarka, उद्यानोपवनाढ्यायां rich with extensive public parks and royal groves, पुष्पितद्रुमराजिषु amidst rows of trees laden with vibrant blossoms, निर्विशद्भृङ्गविहगैः by honeybees and birds delighting in the floral nectar, समन्ततः on all sides, नादिता (आसीत्) was filled with joyful resounding sounds.

Simple Meaning: Dwaraka was abundantly enriched with magnificent gardens and private groves. On every side, it resounded with the sweet melodies of birds and the humming of bees delighting in the nectar of trees laden with fresh blossoms.

रेमे षोडशसाहस्रपत्नीनामेकवल्लभः ।
तावद्विचित्ररूपोसौ तद्गृहेषु महर्द्द्धिषु ॥10.90.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: रेमे enjoyed/sported, षोडश-साहस्र-पत्नीनाम् of His sixteen thousand wives, एक-वल्लभः the sole beloved husband, तावत् just as many, विचित्र-रूपः possessing wonderfully unique forms, असौ He (Krishna), तत्-गृहेषु in their palaces, महा-ऋद्धिषु filled with great divine opulence

Anvaya: असौ एकवल्लभः महर्द्धिषु तद्गृहेषु तावद्विचित्ररूपः (सन्) षोडशसाहस्रपत्नीनां (सहितः) रेमे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: असौ Krishna, एकवल्लभः the single beloved of all, महर्द्धिषु in those palaces endowed with boundless opulence, तावद्विचित्ररूपः (सन्) assuming just as many wonderfully distinct individual forms (sixteen thousand forms simultaneously), षोडशसाहस्रपत्नीनां (सहितः) in the company of His sixteen thousand queens, रेमे enjoyed pastimes.

Simple Meaning: As the sole beloved husband of His sixteen thousand wives, Lord Krishna expanded Himself simultaneously into just as many distinct, wonderful forms. In each of their opulently appointed palaces, He enjoyed divine pastimes in their company.

प्रोत्फुल्लोत्पलकह्लारकुमुदाम्भोजरेणुभिः ।
वासितामलतोयेषु कूजद्द्विजकुलेषु च ॥10.90.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रोत्फुल्ल-उत्पल-कह्लार-कुमुद-अम्भोज-रेणुभिः by the pollen of fully blossomed blue lotuses, night-blooming red water lilies, white water lilies, and day-blooming lotuses, वासित-अमल-तोयेषु in the crystal-clear waters scented by pollen, कूजत्-द्विज-कुलेषु filled with the melodious chirping of flocks of birds, च and

Anvaya: प्रोत्फुल्लोत्पलकह्लारकुमुदाम्भोजरेणुभिः वासितामलतोयेषु कूजद्द्विजकुलेषु च…

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रोत्फुल्लोत्पलकह्लारकुमुदाम्भोजरेणुभिः वासितामलतोयेषु In lakes whose clear waters were richly scented with the fragrant pollen of fully bloomed blue lotuses, red water lilies, white lilies, and day lotuses, कूजद्द्विजकुलेषु च and resounded with the harmonious warbling of various species of birds…

Simple Meaning: In the lakes of crystal-clear water, deliciously scented with the pollen of fully blossomed blue, red, and white lotuses, and filled with the melodious chirping of flocks of birds…

विजहार विगाह्याम्भो हृदिनीषु महोदयः ।
कुचकुङ्कुमलिप्ताङ्ग: परिरब्धश्च योषिताम् ॥10.90.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: विजहार sported, विगाह्य entering/diving into, अम्भः the waters, हृदिनीषु in the lakes, महोदयः Lord Krishna (possessing supreme glory and opulence), कुच-कुङ्कुम-लिप्त-अङ्गः His body smeared with the saffron paste from the breasts, परिरब्धः embraced, च and, योषिताम् by the queens/women

Anvaya: …महोदयः (भगवान् कृष्णः) योषितां परिरब्धः कुचकुङ्कुमलिप्ताङ्गः (सन्) हृदिनीषु अम्भः विगाह्य विजहार।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: …महोदयः Lord Krishna, possessing supreme glory and boundless opulence, योषितां परिरब्धः being tenderly embraced by His queens, कुचकुङ्कुमलिप्ताङ्गः (सन्) His divine body smeared with the red saffron paste from their breasts, हृदिनीषु in the lakes, अम्भः विगाह्य diving into the cool waters, विजहार sported.

Simple Meaning: … Lord Krishna, who possessed supreme glory and opulence, entered the waters of the lakes to sport with His queens. As they embraced Him in the water, His body became smeared with the saffron paste from their breasts.

उपगीयमानो गन्धर्वैर्मृदङ्गपणवानकान् ।
वादयद्भिर्मुदा वीणां सूतमागधवन्दिभिः ॥10.90.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: उपगीयमानः being sung about/extolled in song, गन्धर्वैः by the celestial singers (Gandharvas), मृदङ्ग-पणव-आनकान् classical drums such as mridangas, panavas, and anakas, वादयद्भिः playing upon, मुदा with great joy, वीणाम् the lute/veena, सूत-मागध-वन्दिभिः by the professional bards, chroniclers, and reciters of praise

Anvaya: (हृदिनीषु विजहरन् सः) मुदा मृदङ्गपणवानकान् वीणां वादयद्भिः गन्धर्वैः सूतमागधवन्दिभिः च उपगीयमानः (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (हृदिनीषु विजहरन् सः) As Krishna sported in the waters, मुदा with great delight, मृदङ्गपणवानकान् वीणां वादयद्भिः playing expertly upon mridanga drums, panava drums, anaka kettle-drums, and the veena, गन्धर्वैः by the Gandharvas, सूतमागधवन्दिभिः च and by the professional bards, heralds, and panegyrics, उपगीयमानः (आसीत्) He was praised.

Simple Meaning: As Krishna sported in the waters, He was joyfully glorified in song by Gandharvas, bards, chroniclers, and heralds, who played upon mridangas, panavas, anakas, and veenas.

सिच्यमानोऽच्युतस्ताभिर्हसन्तीभिः स्म रेचकैः ।
प्रतिसिञ्चन् विचिक्रीडे यक्षीभिर्यक्षराडिव ॥10.90.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: सिच्यमानः being sprayed/squirted with water, अच्युतः the infallible Lord Krishna, ताभिः by those queens, हसन्तीभिः who were laughing merrily, स्म (past tense marker), रेचकैः with water syringes/pumps, प्रतिसिञ्चन् spraying them back in return, विचिक्रीडे sported playfully, यक्षीभिः with celestial Yakshinis, यक्ष-राट्-इव like the king of the Yakshas - Kubera

Anvaya: हसन्तीभिः ताभिः रेचकैः सिच्यमानः अच्युतः प्रतिसिञ्चन् यक्षीभिः यक्षराट् इव विचिक्रीडे स्म।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: हसन्तीभिः By those laughing queens, ताभिः with them, रेचकैः through water-syringes, सिच्यमानः being continuously sprayed with streams of water, अच्युतः the infallible Lord Krishna, प्रतिसिञ्चन् spraying water back at them in return, यक्षीभिः with Yaksha maidens, यक्षराट् इव like Kubera the king of the Yakshas, विचिक्रीडे स्म sported delightfully.

Simple Meaning: As the laughing queens sprayed Him with streams of water using syringes, the infallible Lord Krishna sprayed them back in return, sporting joyfully in the water like Kubera surrounded by celestial Yaksha maidens.

ताः क्लिन्नवस्त्रविवृतोरुकुचप्रदेशाः सिञ्चन्त्य उद्धृतबृहत्कबरप्रसूनाः ।
कान्तं स्म रेचकजिहीरषयोपगुह्य जातस्मरोत्सवलसद्वदना विरेजुः ॥10.90.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताः those queens, क्लिन्न-वस्त्र-विवृत-उरु-कुच-प्रदेशाः whose thighs and breasts were revealed through their wet garments, सिञ्चन्त्यः spraying water, उद्धृत-बृहत्-कबर-प्रसूनाः the flower garlands dislodged/falling out from their thick braids, कान्तम् their beloved husband Krishna, स्म (past tense marker), रेचक-जिहीर्षया with the desire to seize His water syringe, उपगुह्य embracing, जात-स्मर-उत्सव-लसत्-वदनाः whose faces beamed with the bliss of awakening conjugal love, विरेजुः shone brilliantly/appeared splendid

Anvaya: क्लिन्नवस्त्रविवृतोरुकुचप्रदेशाः उद्धृतबृहत्कबरप्रसूनाः सिञ्चन्त्यः ताः रेचकजिहीर्षया कान्तम् उपगुह्य जातस्मरोत्सवलसद्वदनाः विरेजुः स्म।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: क्लिन्नवस्त्रविवृतोरुकुचप्रदेशाः Whose garments had become wet, partially revealing their thighs and breasts, उद्धृतबृहत्कबरप्रसूनाः from whose thick loosened braids of hair the decorative flowers were slipping away, सिञ्चन्त्यः spraying water, ताः those queens, रेचकजिहीर्षया out of a playful desire to snatch away the water syringe from His hand, कान्तम् their beloved husband Krishna, उपगुह्य embracing, जातस्मरोत्सवलसद्वदनाः their faces glowing with the joy of awakening passion, विरेजुः स्म shone.

Simple Meaning: Their garments soaked with water revealed their thighs and breasts, and flowers slipped from their loosened hair as they were sprayed with water by Krishna. Desiring to snatch away His water syringe, the queens embraced their beloved Krishna, their faces beaming radiantly with the bliss of awakening love.

कृष्णस्तु तत्स्तनविषज्जितकुङ्कुमस्रक्क्रीडाभिषङ्गधुतकुन्तलवृन्दबन्धः ।
सिञ्चन् मुहुर्युवतिभिः प्रतिषिच्यमानो रेमे करेणुभिरिवेभपतिः परीतः ॥10.90.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: कृष्णः Lord Krishna, तु indeed/but, तत्-स्तन-विषिञ्चित-कुङ्कुम-स्रक् whose garland was stained with the saffron paste from their breasts, क्रीडा-अभिशङ्ग-धुत-कुन्तल-वृन्द-बन्धः whose bound lock of hair was loosened and disheveled by the momentum of playful sport, सिञ्चन् spraying water, मुहुः repeatedly, युवतिभिः by the young women, प्रतिषिच्यमानः being sprayed in return, रेमे sported happily, करेणुभिः with female elephants, इव like, इभ-पतिः a king of elephants (a lordly bull elephant), परीतः surrounded

Anvaya: कृष्णः तु तत्स्तनविषञ्चितकुङ्कुमस्रक् क्रीडाभिशङ्गधुतकुन्तलवृन्दबन्धः (सन्) युवतिभिः मुहुः प्रतिषिच्यमानः (ताः) सिञ्चन् करेणुभिः परीतः इभपतिः इव रेमे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्णः तु Lord Krishna indeed, तत्स्तनविषञ्चितकुङ्कुमस्रक् His flower garland soaked and stained with the saffron paste from the breasts of the queens, क्रीडाभिशङ्गधुतकुन्तलवृन्दबन्धः His neatly tied mass of hair loosened and shaken by the vigorous activity of water play, युवतिभिः by those young women, मुहुः repeatedly, प्रतिषिच्यमानः being sprayed back with water, (ताः) सिञ्चन् while Himself spraying water back upon them, करेणुभिः परीतः surrounded by female elephants, इभपतिः इव like a mighty king of elephants, रेमे enjoyed pastimes.

Simple Meaning: Lord Krishna's garland became stained with the red saffron paste from the queens' breasts, and His bound locks of hair loosened in the momentum of play. Repeatedly spraying water and being sprayed in return by the young women, He sported happily like a lordly bull elephant surrounded by female elephants.

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महिष्यः ऊचुः The queens said, सखि कुररि O friend kurari bird, रात्र्यां at night, जगति स्वपिति when the entire world is asleep, (सति) and even, गुप्तबोधः ईश्वरः the Supreme Controller whose consciousness is merged in sleep, (अपि स्वपिति) is sleeping, त्वं वीतनिद्रा (असि) you have lost your sleep, न शेषे you do not rest, विलपसि you cry aloud in sorrow. नलिननयनहासोदारलीलेक्षितेन By the magnanimous playful glances accompanied by the smile of lotus-eyed Krishna, वयम् इव just like us, तव चेतः your heart, कच्चित् can it be that, गाढनिर्भिन्नम् is deeply pierced through.

Simple Meaning: The queens said: O friend kurari bird, it is night and the whole world is asleep, and even the Supreme Lord, whose awareness is merged in deep repose, sleeps. Yet you are bereft of sleep, crying out in sorrow and unable to rest. Can it be that, just like us, your heart has been deeply pierced by the lotus-eyed Lord's magnanimous smiles and playful glances?

नेत्रे निमीलयसि नक्तमदृष्टबन्धुस्त्वं रोरवीषि करुणं बत चक्रवाकि ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: चक्रवाकि O female chakravaka bird, त्वं you, नक्तं at night, नेत्रे eyes, निमीलयसि close, अदृष्टबन्धुः separated from your beloved mate, करुणं plaintively, रोरवीषि cry aloud again and again. वयम् इव Just like us, दास्यं गता have you fallen into a state of devoted servitude? अच्युतपादजुष्टां स्रजं A flower garland touched by the lotus feet of Lord Achyuta, कबरेण with your hair/feathers, वोढुं to wear, किं वा or perhaps, स्पृहयसे do you yearn for?

Simple Meaning: O female chakravaka bird, why do you close your eyes at night and cry out so piteously, separated from your mate? Have you, like us, fallen into devoted servitude to the Lord? Or do you perhaps yearn to wear in your hair a flower garland that has been blessed by the lotus feet of Lord Achyuta?

Simple Meaning: O grand ocean, you stay awake perpetually, unable to find sleep, groaning and roaring without pause. Could it be that Mukunda has stolen away your inner composure just as He did ours, causing you to fall into this same insurmountable plight?

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इन्दो O moon, त्वं you, बलवता by a powerful, यक्ष्मणा consumption/emaciation, गृहीत: असि are afflicted, क्षीणः having become withered and thin, निजदीधितिभिः by your own cooling rays, तमः the darkness, न क्षिणोषि you no longer dispel. वयम् इव Just like us, मुकुन्दगदितानि the sweet words spoken by Mukunda, विस्मृत्य having forgotten, स्थगितगीः with your speech muted, त्वम् you, नः to us, उपलक्ष्यसे appear, कच्चित् could it be so?

Simple Meaning: O moon, you are afflicted by severe emaciation, just as if consumed by a wasting disease, and so dwindled that you can no longer dispel the darkness with your rays. Just like us, having forgotten the sweet words of Mukunda, your voice seems to be muted as you appear before us—could this be true?

Simple Meaning: While engaged in everyday activities like resting, sitting, walking, talking, playing, or bathing, the Vrishnis were so completely absorbed in the thought of Lord Krishna that they remained entirely oblivious to their own physical bodies.

तीर्थं चक्रे नृपोनं यदजनि यदुषु स्वःसरित्पादशौचं विद्विट्स्निग्धाः स्वरूपं ययुरजितपरा श्रीर्यदर्थेऽन्ययत्नः ।
यन्नामामङ्गलघ्नं श्रुतमथ गदितं यत्कृतो गोत्रधर्मः कृष्णस्यैतन्न चित्रं क्षितिभरहरणं कालचक्रायुधस्य ॥10.90.47॥

Word-by-word meaning: तीर्थम् a holy place of pilgrimage, चक्रे made/created, नृप O king, ऊनम् reducing, यत् which, अजनि was born, यदुषु in the Yadu dynasty, स्वः-सरित्-पाद-शौचम् whose feet are washed by the heavenly river (Ganga), विद्विट्-स्निग्धाः even enemies and affectionate devotees alike, स्वरूपम् His own eternal spiritual nature/supreme abode, ययुः attained, अजित-परा who is devoted to the one who is Unconquered, श्रीः the Goddess of fortune (Lakshmi), यत्-अर्थे for the sake of whom, अन्य-यत्नः all other endeavors (are undertaken by others), यत्-नाम the name of whom, अमङ्गल-घ्नम् destroys all inauspiciousness, श्रुतम् when heard, अथ and also, गदितम् when chanted, यत्-कृतः established by whom, गोत्र-धर्मः the sacred duties of the lineage/moral order, कृष्णस्य of Krishna, एतत् this, न not, चित्रम् surprising/wonderful, क्षिति-भर-हरणम् the removal of the earth's burden, काल-चक्रेण with His weapon of the wheel of time (Sudarshana), आयुधस्य of Him who bears weapons

Anvaya: नृप! यत् यदुषु अजनि स्वःसरित्पादशौचं तीर्थम् ऊनं चक्रे; विद्विट्स्निग्धाः स्वरूपं ययुः; यत्-अर्थे अन्ययत्नः श्रीः अजितपरा; यन्नाम श्रुतं गदितम् अमङ्गलघ्नम्, यत्कृतः गोत्रधर्मः; क्षितिभरहरणं कालचक्रायुधस्य कृष्णस्य एतत् न चित्रम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप! O King! यत् यदुषु अजनि When Krishna was born amongst the Yadus, स्वःसरित्पादशौचं तीर्थम् ऊनं चक्रे He reduced the sanctity of the water which washes His feet; विद्विट्स्निग्धाः स्वरूपं ययुः the enemies and the devotees attained His form; यत्-अर्थे श्रीः अजितपरा अन्ययत्नः for whose sake, Goddess Lakshmi is devoted, the others’ strive to attain her; यन्नाम श्रुतं गदितम् अमङ्गलघ्नम् Whose name when heard and spoken of, destroys all inauspiciousness; यत्कृतः गोत्रधर्मः for Whose sake are the rishis and dharma; कालचक्रायुधस्य कृष्णस्य of Krishna who bears Sudarshana chakra - the wheel of time as His weapon, क्षितिभरहरणं to reduce the burden of the earth, एतत् न चित्रम् all this is not extraordinary.

Simple Meaning: O King! When Krishna was born amongst the Yadus, He reduced the sanctity of the water which washes His feet; the enemies and the devotees attained His form; Goddess Lakshmi is devoted, the others’ strive to attain Goddess Lakshmi, she herself strives for His sake; Whose name when heard and spoken of, destroys all inauspiciousness; for Whose sake are the rishis and dharma; of Krishna who bears Sudarshana chakra - the wheel of time as His weapon, to reduce the burden of the earth, all this is not extraordinary.

जयति जननिवासो देवकीजन्मवादो यदुवरपर्षत्स्वैर्दोर्भिरस्यन्नधर्मम् ।
स्थिरचरवृजिनघ्नः सुस्मितश्रीमुखेन व्रजपुरवनितानां वर्धयन् कामदेवम् ॥10.90.48॥

Word-by-word meaning: जयति all glories to/victorious is, जन-निवासः the ultimate shelter of all living beings (or He who dwells within the hearts of all), देवकी-जन्म-वादः the famous on account of taking birth from Devaki (though unborn), यदु-वर-पर्षत्-स्वैः by the members of the assembly of the noble Yadus, दोर्भिः by His own mighty arms, अस्यन् destroying/casting away, अधर्मम् unrighteousness, स्थिर-चर-वृजिन-घ्नः the destroyer of the sins and sufferings of all moving and non-moving beings, सु-स्मित-श्री-मुखेन by His beautifully smiling, glorious face, व्रज-पुर-वनितानाम् of the women of Vraja (the gopis), वर्धयन् increasing, काम-देवम् lust/transcendental loving desire.

Anvaya: जननिवासः देवकीजन्मवादः, यदुवरपर्षत्स्वैः दोर्भिः अधर्मम् अस्यन्, स्थिरचरवृजिनघ्नः, सुस्मितश्रीमुखेन व्रजपुरवनितानां कामदेवं वर्धयन् (कृष्णः) जयति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जननिवासः He who is the eternal abode and inner dweller of all living entities; देवकीजन्मवादः yet it is said that He took birth from Devaki; यदुवरपर्षत्स्वैः surrounded by the elite assembly of the supreme Yadu heroes, दोर्भिः by His own powerful arms; अधर्मम् अस्यन् all unrighteousness, vanquishing, स्थिरचरवृजिनघ्नः the vanquisher of all distress and evil for both stationary and moving creatures, सुस्मितश्रीमुखेन by His enchanting, gloriously smiling face, व्रजपुरवनितानां of the maidens of Vraja, कामदेवं वर्धयन् increasing the love, (कृष्णः Lord Krishna), जयति reigns supreme in all victory and glory.

Simple Meaning: All glories unto Lord Shri Krishna! He is the ultimate refuge of all living beings, yet He is famously celebrated as the son of Devaki. Surrounded by the noble assembly of Yadu heroes, He vanquished unrighteousness with His own mighty arms. He removes the sins and sufferings of all moving and non-moving creatures, and with His charming, radiantly smiling face, He continuously rekindles and expands the ecstatic divine love of the maidens of Vraja.

इत्थं परस्य निजवर्त्मरिरक्षयाऽऽत्तलीलातनोस्तदनुरूपविडम्बनानि ।
कर्माणि कर्मकषणानि यदूत्तमस्य श्रूयादमुष्य पदयोरनुवृत्तिमिच्छन् ॥10.90.49॥

Word-by-word meaning: इत्थम् in this manner, परस्य of the Supreme Lord, निज-वर्त्म-रिरक्षया with the desire to protect His own path (the path of righteousness and devotion), आत्त-लीला-तनोः who assumed a body for His divine pastimes, तत्-अनुरूप-विडम्बनानि imitations/enactments suitable to that human-like role, कर्माणि activities/deeds, कर्म-कषणानि which destroy the bonds of karma, यदु-उत्तमस्य of the best of the Yadus (Lord Krishna), श्रूयात् should hear, अमुष्य of Him, पदयोः of the lotus feet, अनुवृत्तिम् service/continuous devotion, इच्छन् one who desires

Anvaya: निजवर्त्मरिरक्षया आत्तलीलातनोः परस्य यदूत्तमस्य इत्थं तदनुरूपविडम्बनानि कर्मकषणानि कर्माणि अमुष्य पदयोः अनुवृत्तिम् इच्छन् (जनः) श्रूयात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: निजवर्त्मरिरक्षया Out of a desire to protect and preserve His own sacred path of righteousness, आत्तलीलातनोः who assumed a bodily form specifically for conducting divine pastimes, परस्य of the Supreme Lord, यदूत्तमस्य of Shri Krishna, the crest-jewel of the Yadu dynasty, इत्थं thus, तदनुरूपविडम्बनानि activities that perfectly imitated and enacted human-like behavior, कर्मकषणानि which completely eradicate all reaction and bondage of karma, कर्माणि transcendental deeds, अमुष्य पदयोः at the lotus feet of that Supreme Lord, अनुवृत्तिम् devotion, इच्छन् desiring, (जनः) a person, श्रूयात् should hear.

Simple Meaning: To protect the path of righteousness, He assumed a divine form for His pastimes and enacted human-like roles, performing activities that completely destroy all karmic bondage. One who desires continuous loving service at the lotus feet of Lord Krishna should regularly hear the transcendental deeds of the Supreme Lord, the best of the Yadus.

मर्त्यस्तयानुसवमेधितया मुकुन्दश्रीमत्कथाश्रवणकीर्तनचिन्तयैति ।
तद्धाम दुस्तरकृतान्तजवापवर्गं ग्रामाद् वनं क्षितिभुजोऽपि ययुर्यदर्थाः ॥10.90.50॥

Word-by-word meaning: मर्त्यः a mortal human being, तया by that, अनुसवम् constantly/at every moment, एधितया which is fully developed and enhanced, मुकुन्द-श्रीमत्-कथा-श्रवण-कीर्तन-चिन्तया by hearing, chanting, and meditating upon the glorious pastimes of Lord Mukunda, एति attains, तत्-धाम that supreme eternal abode, दुस्तर-कृतान्त-जव-अपवर्गम् which is free from the insurmountable speed of death/time, ग्रामात् from household/worldly life, वनम् to the forest (renounced order), क्षिति-भुजः great kings, अपि even, ययुः went, यत्-अर्थाः for the sake of attaining which

Anvaya: अनुसवम् एधितया मुकुन्दश्रीमत्कथाश्रवणकीर्तनचिन्तया मर्त्यः दुस्तरकृतान्तजवापवर्गं तद्धाम एति, यदर्थाः क्षितिभुजः अपि ग्रामात् वनं ययुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अनुसवम् Continuously at every moment, एधितया through ever-increasing, मुकुन्दश्रीमत्कथाश्रवणकीर्तनचिन्तया devotion born of hearing, chanting, and meditating upon the glorious narratives of Mukunda (Lord Krishna), मर्त्यः a mortal being, दुस्तरकृतान्तजवापवर्गं which brings total liberation from the insurmountable, relentless speed of death, तद्धाम that supreme divine realm, एति reaches/attains; यदर्थाः for the ultimate realization of which, क्षितिभुजः अपि even great sovereign kings, ग्रामात् leaving behind worldly life and possessions, वनम् to the forest, ययुः retired.

Simple Meaning: By constantly hearing, chanting, and meditating upon the glorious pastimes of Lord Mukunda with ever-increasing devotion, a mortal human being attains His supreme abode—a realm entirely beyond the insurmountable and relentless speed of time and death. It is for the sake of attaining this supreme goal that even great kings abandoned their worldly kingdoms and retired to the forest.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “कृष्णस्य पत्नीभिः सह क्रिडाः, तद्विरहे तासां विलापाः, कृष्णवंशजवर्णनं च” नाम नवतितमः अध्यायः ॥90॥

इति दशमस्कन्धोत्तरार्धः सम्पूर्णः

Thus ends the ninetieth discourse named “Krishna’s pastimes with His wives, their lamentations on separation from Krishna and an account of Krishna’s descendants”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
