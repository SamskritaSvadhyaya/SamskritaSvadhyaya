Chapter 59

Krishna takes away Parijata tree by force from heaven, kills Narakasura - the son of Mother Earth. Description of how Krishna behaves like an ordinary householder in each of His palaces.

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

नरकासुरवधः पारिजातापहरणं कृष्णस्य गृहस्थलीलावर्णनं च नाम​​ एकोनषष्टित्तमः अध्यायः

(45 Shlokas)

राजा उवाच
यथा हतो भगवता भौमो येन च ताः स्त्रियः ।
निरुद्धा एतदाचक्ष्व विक्रमं शार्ङ्गधन्वनः ॥10.59.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजा उवाच the king said, यथा how, हतः killed, भगवता by the Supreme Lord, भौमः Bhauma, येन by whom, च and, ताः those, स्त्रियः women, निरुद्धाः confined, एतत् this, आचक्ष्व please tell, विक्रमम् prowess, शार्ङ्ग-धन्वनः of the wielder of the Sharnga bow

Anvaya: राजा उवाच — येन च ताः स्त्रियः निरुद्धाः, भगवता भौमः यथा हतः, शार्ङ्गधन्वनः तत् विक्रमम् आचक्ष्व।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजा उवाच The king said, येन च ताः स्त्रियः निरुद्धाः By whom those women were confined, भगवता भौमः यथा हतः how that Bhauma was killed by the Lord, शार्ङ्गधन्वनः तत् विक्रमम् आचक्ष्व please recount those valiant deeds of the wielder of the Sharnga bow.

Simple Meaning: King Pariksit said: Please describe to me the valiant deeds of Lord Krishna, the wielder of the Sharnga bow. Tell me how He killed the asura Bhauma (Narakasura) and rescued all those women whom Bhauma had kept in captivity.

श्री शुक उवाच
इन्द्रेण हृतच्छत्रेण हृतकुण्डलबन्धुना ।
हृतामराद्रिस्थानेन ज्ञापितो भौमचेष्टितम् ।
सभार्यो गरुडारूढः प्राग्ज्योतिषपुरं ययौ ॥10.59.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, इन्द्रेण by Indra, हृत-छत्रेण whose royal umbrella had been stolen, हृत-कुण्डल-बन्धुना whose mother's earrings had been stolen, हृत-अमर-अद्रि-स्थानेन whose location on the celestial mountain Mandara had been taken away, ज्ञापितः informed, भौम-चेष्टितम् about the misdeeds of Bhauma, स-भार्यः along with His wife, गरुड-आरूढः mounted on Garuda, प्राग्ज्योतिष-पुरम् to the city of Pragjyotisha, ययौ went

Anvaya: श्रीशुकः उवाच — हृतछत्रेण हृतकुण्डलबन्धुना हृतामराद्रिस्थानेन इन्द्रेण भौमचेष्टितं ज्ञापितः सभार्यः गरुडारूढः (भगवान्) प्राग्ज्योतिषपुरं ययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, हृतछत्रेण हृतकुण्डलबन्धुना हृतामराद्रिस्थानेन इन्द्रेण भौमचेष्टितं ज्ञापितः Informed of Bhauma's misdeeds by Indra, whose royal umbrella and mother's earrings had been stolen and whose dwelling on the celestial mountain had been usurped, सभार्यः गरुडारूढः (भगवान्) प्राग्ज्योतिषपुरं ययौ the Lord along with His wife mounted on Garuda went to the city of Pragjyotisha.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Indra came to Lord Krishna and reported how the asura Bhauma had stolen the royal umbrella of Varuna, the earrings of his mother Aditi, and usurped the abode of the devas on Mount Mandara. Upon learning of Bhauma's misdeeds, the Lord mounted Garuda along with His wife Satyabhama and set out for Pragjotishapura.

गिरिदुर्गैः शस्त्रदुर्गैर्जलाग्न्यनिलदुर्गमम् ।
मुरपाशायुतैर्घोरैर्दृढैः सर्वत आवृतम् ॥10.59.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: गिरि-दुर्गैः surrounded by mountain fortifications, शस्त्र-दुर्गैः surrounded by weapon fortifications, जल-अग्नि-अनिल-दुर्गमम् protected by trenches of water, fire, and wind, मुर-पाश-अयुतैः with tens of thousands of wire snares set by the asura Mura, घोरैः terrible, दृढैः formidable, सर्वतः on all sides, आवृतम् enclosed

Anvaya: (तत् प्राग्ज्योतिषपुरं) गिरिदुर्गैः शस्त्रदुर्गैः जलाग्न्यनिलदुर्गमं, घोरैः दृढैः मुरपाशायुतैः (च) सर्वतः आवृतम् (आसीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (तत् प्राग्ज्योतिषपुरं) गिरिदुर्गैः शस्त्रदुर्गैः जलाग्न्यनिलदुर्गमं That city of Pragjyotisha was fortified by mountains and weapons and made inaccessible by water, fire, and wind, घोरैः दृढैः मुरपाशायुतैः च सर्वतः आवृतम् and was enclosed on all sides by tens of thousands of terrible and formidable wire snares set by Mura.

Simple Meaning: That city of Pragjotisha was surrounded on all sides by mountain defenses and weapon fortifications, made inaccessible by barriers of water, fire, and wind, and enclosed everywhere by tens of thousands of terrible, unbreakable wire snares set by the asura Mura.

गदया निर्विभेदाद्रीन् शस्त्रदुर्गाणि सायकैः ।
चक्रेणाग्निं जलं वायुं मुरपाशांस्तथासिना ॥10.59.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: गदया with His (Kaumodaki) mace, निर्विभेद broke to pieces, अद्रीन् the mountain fortifications, शस्त्र-दुर्गाणि the weapon fortifications, सायकैः with arrows, चक्रेण with His (Sudarshana) disc, अग्निम् fire, जलम् water, वायुम् wind, मुर-पाशान् the wire snares of Mura, तथा and, असिना with His (Nandaki) sword

Anvaya: (भगवान्) गदया अद्रीन् निर्विभेद, सायकैः शस्त्रदुर्गाणि, चक्रेण अग्निं जलं वायुं (तथा) असिना मुरपाशान् (निर्विभेद)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (भगवान्) गदया अद्रीन् निर्विभेद The Lord with His mace (known as Kaumodaki) broke to pieces the mountain fortifications, सायकैः शस्त्रदुर्गाणि चक्रेण अग्निं जलं वायुं तथा असिना मुरपाशान् with arrows the weapon fortifications, with His disc (known as Sudarshana) the fire, water, and wind, and with His sword (known as Nandaki) the wire snares of Mura.

Simple Meaning: Krishna shattered the mountain fortifications with His Kaumodaki mace, destroyed the weapon barriers with His arrows, neutralized the defenses of fire, water, and wind using His Sudarshana disc, and cut through the terrible wire snares of Mura with His Nandaki sword.

शङ्खनादेन यन्त्राणि हृदयानि मनस्विनाम् ।
प्राकारं गदया गुर्व्या निर्बिभेद गदाधरः ॥10.59.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: शङ्ख-नादेन by the sound of His conch shell (Panchajanya), यन्त्राणि the military siege engines, हृदयानि the hearts, मनस्विनाम् of the courageous warriors, प्राकारम् the surrounding wall, गदया with His mace, गुर्व्या heavy, निर्बिभेद smashed to pieces, गदाधरः Krishna, the wielder of the mace

Anvaya: गदाधरः शङ्खनादेन यन्त्राणि मनस्विनां हृदयानि (च), गुर्व्या गदया प्राकारं (च) निर्बिभेद।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गदाधरः शङ्खनादेन यन्त्राणि मनस्विनां हृदयानि Lord Krishna, the wielder of the mace, by the sound of His conch shell the military engines and the hearts of the courageous warriors, गुर्व्या गदया प्राकारं (च) निर्बिभेद and with His heavy mace smashed the surrounding wall to pieces.

Simple Meaning: With the resounding blast of His conch shell Panchajanya, Krishna, the wielder of the mace, shattered the enemy's military machinery and struck fear into the hearts of even the most valiant warriors. He then smashed the city's massive outer wall to pieces using His heavy mace.

पाञ्चजन्यध्वनिं श्रुत्वा युगान्ताशनिभीषणम् ।
मुरः शयान उत्तस्थौ दैत्यः पञ्चशिरा जलात् ॥10.59.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: पाञ्चजन्य-ध्वनिम् the sound of the Panchajanya conch shell, श्रुत्वा hearing, युग-अन्त-अशनि-भीषणम् terrifying like the lightning bolt at the end of an eon, मुरः Mura, शयानः sleeping, उत्तस्थौ arose, दैत्यः the asura, पञ्च-शिराः having five heads, जलात् from the water

Anvaya: युगान्ताशनिभीषणं पाञ्चजन्यध्वनिं श्रुत्वा, जलात् शयानः पञ्चशिराः मुरः दैत्यः उत्तस्थौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: युगान्ताशनिभीषणं पाञ्चजन्यध्वनिं श्रुत्वा Hearing the sound of the Panchajanya conch shell which was terrifying like the lightning bolt at the end of an eon, जलात् शयानः पञ्चशिराः मुरः दैत्यः उत्तस्थौ the five-headed asura Mura who was sleeping in the water arose.

Simple Meaning: Hearing the thunderous sound of Krishna's Panchajanya conchshell, which reverberated like the terrifying cosmic lightning at the end of an eon, the five-headed asura Mura woke up and emerged from the depths of the water where he had been sleeping.

त्रिशूलमुद्यम्य सुदुर्निरीक्षणो युगान्तसूर्यानलरोचिरुल्बणः ।
ग्रसंस्त्रिलोकीमिव पञ्चभिर्मुखैरभ्यद्रवत्तार्क्ष्यसुतं यथोरगः ॥10.59.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्रिशूलम् his trident, उद्यम्य lifting up, सु-दुर्निरीक्षणः very difficult to look at, युग-अन्त-सूर्य-अनल-रोचिः blazing like the sun and fire at the end of an eon, उल्बणः fierce, ग्रसन् devouring, त्रि-लोकीम् the three worlds, इव as if, पञ्चभिः with five, मुखैः mouths, अभ्यद्रवत् rushed toward, तार्क्ष्य-सुतम् Garuda, the son of Tarkshya, यथा like, उरगः a serpent

Anvaya: सुदुर्निरीक्षणः युगान्तसूर्यानलरोचिः उल्बणः (सः मुरः) पञ्चभिः मुखैः त्रिलोकीं ग्रसन् इव त्रिशूलम् उद्यम्य, उरगः तार्क्ष्यसुतं यथा, (कृष्णम्) अभ्यद्रवत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सुदुर्निरीक्षणः युगान्तसूर्यानलरोचिः उल्बणः Very difficult to look at, blazing like the sun and fire at the end of an eon, and fierce, पञ्चभिः मुखैः त्रिलोकीं ग्रसन् इव त्रिशूलम् उद्यम्य as if devouring the three worlds with his five mouths and lifting up his trident, उरगः तार्क्ष्यसुतं यथा अभ्यद्रवत् he rushed forward just as a serpent rushes toward Garuda.

Simple Meaning: Dazzling and fierce, glowing like the blazing sun and apocalyptic fire at the end of an eon, the terrible asura Mura rushed at Krishna with his trident raised. Opening his five mouths as if to swallow all three worlds at once, he charged forward like an enraged serpent attacking Garuda.

आविध्य शूलं तरसा गरुत्मते निरस्य वक्त्रैर्व्यनदत् स पञ्चभिः ।
स रोदसी सर्वदिशोऽन्तरं महानापूरयन्नण्डकटाहमावृणोत ॥10.59.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: आविध्य brandishing, शूलम् trident, तरसा with force, गरुत्मते at Garuda, निरस्य threw, वक्त्रैः with mouths, व्यनदत् roared, सः he, पञ्चभिः five, सः that, रोदसी heaven and earth, सर्व-दिशः all directions, अन्तरम् space between, महान् great sound, आपूरयन् filling, अण्ड-कटाहम् the entire cosmic sphere, आवृणोत् covered

Anvaya: सः तरसा शूलम् आविध्य गरुत्मते निरस्य पञ्चभिः वक्त्रैः व्यनदत्, (तस्य) सः महान् (नादः) रोदसी सर्वदिशः अन्तरम् आपूरयन् अण्डकटाहम् आवृणोत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः तरसा शूलम् आविध्य गरुत्मते निरस्य He forcefully brandished his trident and hurled it at Garuda, पञ्चभिः वक्त्रैः व्यनदत् and roared with his five mouths, तस्य सः महान् रोदसी सर्वदिशः अन्तरम् आपूरयन् and that great sound filling heaven, earth, all directions, and the space between, अण्डकटाहम् आवृणोत् covered the entire cosmic sphere.

Simple Meaning: Brandishing his trident with immense force, Mura hurled it straight at Garuda and unleashed a roar from all five of his mouths. That deafening roar filled heaven, earth, all directions, and the space between them, reverberating throughout the entire cosmic shell.

तदापतद् वै त्रिशिखं गरुत्मते हरिः शराभ्यामभिनत्त्रिधौजसा ।
मुखेषु तं चापि शरैरताडयत् तस्मै गदां सोऽपि रुषा व्यमुञ्चत ॥10.59.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that, आपतत् flying toward, वै indeed, त्रि-शिखम् three-pointed trident, गरुत्मते at Garuda, हरिः Hari, शराभ्याम् with two arrows, अभिनत् split, त्रिधा into three pieces, ओजसा with great power, मुखेषु in the mouths, तम् him, च and, अपि also, शरैः with arrows, अताडयत् struck, तस्मै at Him, गदाम् a club, सः he, अपि also, रुषा in anger, व्यमुञ्चत hurled

Anvaya: (अथ) गरुत्मते आपतत् वै तत् त्रिशिखं हरिः ओजसा शराभ्यां त्रिधा अभिनत्, तं च अपि मुखेषु शरैः अताडयत्। सः अपि रुषा तस्मै गदां व्यमुञ्चत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ गरुत्मते आपतत् वै तत् त्रिशिखं Now that three-pointed trident flying indeed toward Garuda, हरिः ओजसा शराभ्यां त्रिधा अभिनत् Hari with great power split into three pieces with two arrows, तं च अपि मुखेषु शरैः अताडयत् and struck him also in his mouths with arrows. सः अपि रुषा तस्मै गदां व्यमुञ्चत He also in anger hurled a club at Him.

Simple Meaning: Seeing the three-pointed trident flying toward Garuda, Hari powerfully split it into three pieces with two arrows. He then struck Mura in all five of his mouths with a barrage of arrows. Enraged by this counterattack, the asura hurled a club at the Lord.

तामापतन्तीं गदया गदां मृधे गदाग्रजो निर्बिभिदे सहस्रधा ।
उद्यम्य बाहूनभिधावतोऽजितः शिरांसि चक्रेण जहार लीलया ॥10.59.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् that, आपतन्तीम् flying towards, गदया with His club, गदाम् club, मृधे in battle, गद-अग्रजः the elder brother of Gada, Krishna, निर्बिभिदे shattered, सहस्रधा into a thousand pieces, उद्यम्य raising, बाहून् arms, अभिधावतः running towards Him, अजितः the unconquerable Lord, शिरांसि heads, चक्रेण with His Sudarshana disc, जहार severed, लीलया effortlessly

Anvaya: मृधे आपतन्तीं तां गदां गदाग्रजः गदया सहस्रधा निर्बिभिदे। बाहून् उद्यम्य अभिधावतः (तस्य) शिरांसि अजितः चक्रेण लीलया जहार।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मृधे आपतन्तीं तां गदां In the battle, that club which was flying towards Him, गदाग्रजः गदया सहस्रधा निर्बिभिदे Krishna, the elder brother of Gada shattered into a thousand pieces with His club. बाहून् उद्यम्य अभिधावतः तस्य शिरांसि अजितः चक्रेण लीलया जहार The unconquerable Lord effortlessly severed with His disc the heads of him who was running towards Him with raised arms.

Simple Meaning: As the asura's club came flying toward Him in battle, Krishna shattered it into a thousand pieces with His own heavy club. When Mura then raised his arms and charged directly at Him in fury, the invincible Lord effortlessly severed all five of the asura's heads with His Sudarshana disc.

व्यसुः पपाताम्भसि कृत्तशीर्षो निकृत्तशृङ्गोऽद्रिरिवेन्द्रतेजसा ।
तस्यात्मजाः सप्त पितुर्वधातुराः प्रतिक्रियामर्षजुषः समुद्यताः ॥10.59.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: व्यसुः lifeless, पपात fell, अम्भसि into the water, कृत्त-शीर्षः with severed heads, निकृत्त-शृङ्गः with severed peaks, अद्रिः a mountain, इव like, इन्द्र-तेजसा by the power of Indra, तस्य his, आत्मजाः sons, सप्त seven, पितुः of their father, वध-आतुराः distraught over the killing, प्रतिक्रिया-अमर्ष-जुषः filled with fury to retaliate, समुद्यताः prepared for battle

Anvaya: (एवं) कृत्तशीर्षः (मुरः) इन्द्रतेजसा निकृत्तशृङ्गः अद्रिः इव व्यसुः अम्भसि पपात। पितुः वधातुराः प्रतिक्रियामर्षजुषः तस्य सप्त आत्मजाः समुद्यताः (अभवन्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवं कृत्तशीर्षः Thus, with severed heads, इन्द्रतेजसा निकृत्तशृङ्गः अद्रिः इव like a mountain with severed peaks by the power of Indra, व्यसुः अम्भसि पपात he fell lifeless into the water. पितुः वधातुराः Distraught over the killing of their father, प्रतिक्रियामर्षजुषः and filled with fury to retaliate, तस्य सप्त आत्मजाः समुद्यताः his seven sons prepared for battle.

Simple Meaning: With his heads severed, Mura fell lifeless into the water like a mountain whose peaks had been struck down by Indra's thunderbolt. Grief-stricken and furious over their father's death, his seven sons immediately rose up and prepared for battle to seek revenge against Krishna.

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ताम्रः अन्तरिक्षः श्रवणः विभावसुः वसुः नभस्वान् सप्तमः अरुणः च Tamra, Antariksa, Sravana, Vibhavasu, Vasu, Nabhasvan, and the seventh Aruna, चमूपतिं पीठं पुरस्कृत्य putting the commander in chief Pitha in front, भौमप्रयुक्ताः धृतायुधाः मृधे निरगन् dispatched by Bhauma and brandishing weapons went forth into battle.

Simple Meaning: The seven sons of Mura—Tamra, Antariksa, Sravana, Vibhavasu, Vasu, Nabhasvan, and Aruna—were ordered into battle by Bhauma. Placing their general Pitha at their head, they marched forth brandishing their weapons.

Simple Meaning: Confronting the unconquerable Krishna, those fierce warriors unleashed a volley of arrows, swords, clubs, pikes, lances, and tridents in fury. However, the Supreme Lord, whose prowess is absolute and unfailing, countered by shooting His own arrows, effortlessly slicing that barrage of weapons into tiny pieces as small as sesame seeds.

Simple Meaning: Krishna dispatched those asura commanders headed by Pitha to the realm of Yamaraja, severing their heads, thighs, arms, feet, and armor. Seeing his finest generals annihilated in this manner by the disc and arrows of Achyuta, Narakasura, the son of Bhumi, could not tolerate it. He marched out to battle surrounded by massive elephants born of the ocean, all dripping with rut-juice.

दृष्ट्वा सभार्यं गरुडोपरि स्थितं सूर्योपरिष्टात् सतडिद्घनं यथा ।

Simple Meaning: Seeing Krishna seated on Garuda with His wife Satyabhama, resembling a dark cloud with flashing lightning hovering above the sun, Narakasura launched the deadly Shataghni weapon at Him. At the same moment, all his attending warriors unleashed a fierce, simultaneous attack upon the Lord.

Simple Meaning: Right then and there, Lord Krishna, the elder brother of Gada, unleashed a volley of sharp, beautifully feathered arrows upon Bhauma's forces. In an instant, He decimated the enemy army, slaying their war horses and elephants while leaving the battlefield strewn with severed arms, legs, necks, and trunks.

Anvaya: वितथोद्यमः भौमः अच्युतं हन्तुं शूलम् आददे, (परन्तु) तद्विसर्गात् पूर्वम् एव हरिः क्षुरनेमिना चक्रेण गजस्थस्य नरकस्य शिरः अपाहरत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: वितथोद्यमः भौमः Bhauma whose effort was futile, अच्युतं हन्तुं शूलम् आददे took up his trident to kill Lord Achyuta, परन्तु तद्विसर्गात् पूर्वम् एव but even before its release, हरिः क्षुरनेमिना चक्रेण गजस्थस्य नरकस्य शिरः अपाहरत् Lord Hari severed with His razor-sharp disc the head of Narakasura seated on his elephant.

Simple Meaning: Seeing his previous attack fail, Bhauma (Narakasura) raised his trident in a futile attempt to kill Lord Achyuta. However, before he could even hurl the weapon, Lord Hari released His razor-sharp Sudarshana disc and severed Narakasura's head while he was still seated atop his elephant.

सकुण्डलं चारुकिरीटभूषणं बभौ पृथिव्यां पतितं समुज्ज्वलत् ।
हाहेति साध्वित्यृषयः सुरेश्वरा माल्यैर्मुकुन्दं विकिरन्त ईडिरे ॥10.59.22॥

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सकुण्डलं चारुकिरीटभूषणं Decorated with earrings and a beautiful crown, समुज्ज्वलत् पृथिव्यां पतितं बभौ that brilliantly glowing head shone as it fell to the earth. हा हा इति (His relatives) exclaimed “Alas Alas!”. “साधु” इति ऋषयः सुरेश्वराः The sages and chief gods exclaiming “well done”, माल्यैः मुकुन्दं विकिरन्तः ईडिरे showered Lord Mukunda with flower garlands and praised Him.

Simple Meaning: Adorned with glowing earrings and a beautiful crown, Narakasura's head shone brightly as it fell to the earth. Seeing this, the sages and chief demigods cried out “alas alas” at the tragedy of the asura's demise and “well done” in praise of the Lord. They showered Lord Mukunda with flower garlands and offered prayers to Him.

ततश्च भूः कृष्णमुपेत्य कुण्डले प्रतप्तजाम्बूनदरत्नभास्वरे ।

Anvaya: ततः च भूः कृष्णम् उपेत्य प्रतप्तजाम्बूनदरत्नभास्वरे कुण्डले सवैजयन्त्या वनमालया प्राचेतसं छत्रम् अथः महामणिम् अर्पयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः च भूः कृष्णम् उपेत्य then Mother Earth approaching Krishna, प्रतप्तजाम्बूनदरत्नभास्वरे कुण्डले the two earrings shining with pure burnished gold and jewels, सवैजयन्त्या वनमालया along with a flower garland containing the Vaijayanti, प्राचेतसं छत्रम् the umbrella of Varuna, अथः महामणिम् and also the great jewel, अर्पयत् presented.

Simple Meaning: Thereafter Mother Earth approached Krishna and presented Him with the two earrings that shone brightly with pure burnished gold and jewels. She also gave Him the flower garland containing the Vaijayanti garland, Varuna's umbrella, and the crest of Mount Mandara, known by the name of Mahamani or Maniparvata (so-called because of its abounding in precious gems).

अस्तौषीदथ विश्वेशं देवी देववरार्चितम् ।

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, then Goddess Earth bowed down with folded hands and, with a mind full of devotion, offered prayers to Krishna, the Master of the universe who is worshipped by the greatest demigods.

भूमिः उवाच
नमस्ते देवदेवेश शङ्खचक्रगदाधर ।

Simple Meaning: Obeisances unto You unto Him who has a lotus in the navel, obeisances unto You who wear a garland of lotus flowers. Obeisances unto You whose eyes are like lotuses, and obeisances unto You whose feet are like lotus.

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अस्य जनयित्रे अजाय अनन्तशक्तये ब्रह्मणे Unto the unborn creator of this universe, the Supreme Absolute possessed of unlimited power, परावरात्मन् भूतात्मन् परमात्मन् ते नमः अस्तु O Soul of the high and low, O Soul of all living beings, O Supreme Soul, let my obeisances be unto You.

Simple Meaning: I offer my obeisances unto You, who are unborn yet the creator of this entire universe, the Supreme Brahman possessed of infinite power. O Soul of all that is high and low, O Inmost Soul of all created beings, O Supreme Lord, my humble bow unto You.

त्वं वै सिसृक्षू रज उत्कटं प्रभो तमो निरोधाय बिभर्ष्यसंवृतः ।
स्थानाय सत्त्वं जगतो जगत्पते कालः प्रधानं पुरुषो भवान् परः ॥10.59.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वम् You, वै indeed, सिसृक्षुः desiring to create, रजः the mode of passion, उत्कटम् prominent, प्रभो O Lord, तमः the mode of ignorance, निरोधाय for destruction, बिभर्षि You assume, असंवृतः uncovered, स्थानाय for maintenance, सत्त्वम् the mode of goodness, जगतः of the universe, जगत्-पते O Master of the universe, कालः time, प्रधानम् unmanifest material nature, पुरुषः the cosmic Person, भवान् Your Lordship, परः transcendental

Anvaya: जगत्पते! प्रभो! त्वं वै सिसृक्षुः उत्कटं रजः (बिभर्षि), निरोधाय तमः (बिभर्षि), जगतः स्थानाय सत्त्वं बिभर्षि, असंवृतः भवान् कालः प्रधानं पुरुषः परः (च अस्ति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जगत्पते! प्रभो! O Lord, O Master of the universe, त्वं वै सिसृक्षुः उत्कटं रजः (बिभर्षि) desiring to create You assume prominent mode of passion - Rajas, निरोधाय तमः (बिभर्षि) for dissolution (You assume) mode of ignorance - Tamas, जगतः स्थानाय सत्त्वं बिभर्षि and for maintenance of the universe (You assume) mode of goodness - Satva. असंवृतः भवान् कालः प्रधानं पुरुषः परः Remaining unattached, You are Time, unmanifest nature, the cosmic Person, and transcendental to all.

Simple Meaning: O Lord, O Master of the universe! When You desire to create, You assume the prominent mode of passion (Rajas); when You dissolve the universe, You assume the mode of ignorance (Tamas); and for its maintenance, You assume the mode of goodness (Satva). Yet You remain completely unattached and untouched by these modes. You are time, unmanifest nature, the cosmic Person, and the Supreme Truth beyond them all.

कर्ता महानित्यखिलं चराचरं त्वय्यद्वितीये भगवन्नयं भ्रमः ॥10.59.30॥

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भगवन्! O Supreme Lord, अहं पयः ज्योतिः अथ अनिलः नभः मात्राणि देवाः मनः इन्द्रियाणि कर्ता महान् इति myself (earth), water, fire, air, ether, sense objects, demigods, mind, senses, false ego, and the mahat-tattva, अद्वितीये त्वयि अखिलं चराचरम् अयं भ्रमः in You who are one without a second, all this moving and nonmoving universe appears as an illusion.

Simple Meaning: O Supreme Lord! Earth, water, fire, air, ether, the sense objects, demigods, mind, senses, false ego, and the mahat-tattva—indeed, this entire moving and nonmoving creation—is merely an illusory appearance superimposed upon You, who are one without a second.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Thus requested by Goddess Earth with words full of humble devotion, the Supreme Lord granted protection and fearlessness to Narakasura's son. He then entered Narakasura's palace, which was filled with all forms of royal wealth and opulence.

तत्र राजन्यकन्यानां षट्सहस्राधिकायुतम् ।
भौमाहृतानां विक्रम्य राजभ्यो ददृशे हरिः ॥10.59.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत्र there, राजन्य-कन्यानाम् of princess maidens, षट्-सहस्र-अधिक-अयुतम् sixteen thousand, भौम-आहृतानाम् abducted by Bhauma, विक्रम्य by prowess, राजभ्यः from kings, ददृशे saw, हरिः Hari

Anvaya: तत्र हरिः राजभ्यः विक्रम्य भौमाहृतानां षट्सहस्राधिकायुतं राजन्यकन्यानां ददृशे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत्र हरिः There Hari, राजभ्यः विक्रम्य through his prowess from various kings, भौमाहृतानां षट्सहस्राधिकायुतं राजन्यकन्यानां sixteen thousand princess maidens abducted by Bhauma, ददृशे saw.

Simple Meaning: Inside the palace, Lord Hari saw sixteen thousand princesses whom Bhauma (Narakasura) had forcibly abducted from various kings by demonstrating his military power.

तं प्रविष्टं स्त्रियो वीक्ष्य नरवीरं विमोहिताः ।
मनसा वव्रिरेऽभीष्टं पतिं दैवोपसादितम् ॥10.59.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् Him, प्रविष्टम् who had entered, स्त्रियः the women, वीक्ष्य seeing, नर-वीरम् the hero among men, विमोहिताः enchanted, मनसा with their minds, वव्रिरे chose, अभीष्टम् desired, पतिम् husband, दैव-उपसादितम् brought by destiny

Anvaya: स्त्रियः प्रविष्टं तं नरवीरं वीक्ष्य विमोहिताः (सत्यः), दैवोपसादितम् अभीष्टं पतिं मनसा वव्रिरे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: स्त्रियः प्रविष्टं तं नरवीरं वीक्ष्य विमोहिताः The women seeing Him, that hero among men, enter, became enchanted, दैवोपसादितम् अभीष्टं पतिं मनसा वव्रिरे and chose Him with their minds as their desired husband brought to them by destiny.

Simple Meaning: As those princesses saw Him—the hero among men—enter their quarters, they were instantly captivated by His beauty. Recognizing Him as the desired husband brought to them by divine providence, they each chose Him as their husband within their hearts.

भूयात् पतिरयं मह्यं धाता तदनुमोदताम् ।
इति सर्वाः पृथक् कृष्णे भावेन हृदयं दधुः ॥10.59.35॥

Word-by-word meaning: भूयात् may He become, पतिः husband, अयम् this, मह्यम् my, धाता Providence, तत् that, अनुमोदताम् may grant, इति thus, सर्वाः all, पृथक् individually, कृष्णे on Krishna, भावेन with deep devotion, हृदयम् heart, दधुः fixed

Anvaya: अयं मह्यं पतिः भूयात्, धाता तत् अनुमोदताम्, इति सर्वाः पृथक् भावेन कृष्णे हृदयं दधुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अयं मह्यं पतिः भूयात् May He become my husband, धाता तत् अनुमोदताम् and may Providence grant that, इति सर्वाः पृथक् भावेन कृष्णे हृदयं दधुः thus each of all those maidens individually fixed her heart with deep devotion on Krishna.

Simple Meaning: Each princess thought within her heart, "May this Supreme Lord become my husband, and may Providence grant my wish." With deep devotion, every one of them individually fixed her heart upon Krishna.

ताः प्राहिणोद् द्वारवतीं सुमृष्टविरजोऽम्बराः ।
नरयानैर्महाकोशान् रथाश्वान् द्रविणं महत् ॥10.59.36॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताः those maidens, प्राहिणोत् He dispatched, द्वारवतीम् to Dwaraka, सु-मृष्ट-विरजः-अम्बराः dressed in spotless clean garments, नर-यानैः in palanquins, महा-कोशान् vast treasures, रथ-अश्वान् chariots and horses, द्रविणम् wealth, महत् immense

Anvaya: (भगवान्) सुमृष्टविरजोम्बराः ताः नरयानैः, महाकोशान् रथाश्वान् महत् द्रविणं (च) द्वारवतीं प्राहिणोत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सुमृष्टविरजोम्बराः ताः नरयानैः Those maidens dressed in spotless clean garments in palanquins, महाकोशान् रथाश्वान् महत् द्रविणं द्वारवतीं प्राहिणोत् as well as vast treasures, chariots, horses, and immense wealth, He dispatched to Dwaraka.

Simple Meaning: Krishna arranged for the princesses, dressed in fine spotless garments, to be sent to Dwaraka in palanquins. Along with them, He dispatched vast treasures, chariots, horses, and immense riches captured from Narakasura.

ऐरावतकुलेभांश्च चतुर्दन्तांस्तरस्विनः ।
पाण्डुरांश्च चतुःषष्टिं प्रेषयामास केशवः ॥10.59.37॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऐरावत-कुल-इभान् elephants of Airavata's family, च and, चतुः-दन्तान् four-tusked, तरस्विनः swift and powerful, पाण्डुरान् white, च and, चतुः-षष्टिम् sixty-four, प्रेषयामास sent, केशवः Keshava

Anvaya: केशवः चतुर्दन्तान् तरस्विनः पाण्डुरान् चतुःषष्टिम् ऐरावतकुलेभान् च (द्वारवतीं) प्रेषयामास।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: केशवः चतुर्दन्तान् तरस्विनः पाण्डुरान् चतुःषष्टिम् ऐरावतकुलेभान् च प्रेषयामास Keshava sent sixty-four four-tusked, swift and powerful, white elephants belonging to the family of Airavata.

Simple Meaning: Lord Keshava also dispatched sixty-four swift and powerful white elephants to Dwaraka. These majestic beasts belonged to the noble lineage of Airavata and possessed four tusks each.

गत्वा सुरेन्द्रभवनं दत्त्वादित्यै च कुण्डले ।
पूजितस्त्रिदशेन्द्रेण सहेन्द्राण्या च सप्रियः ॥10.59.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: गत्वा having gone, सुर-इन्द्र-भवनम् to the abode of the king of demigods (Indra), दत्त्वा having given, अदित्यै unto Aditi, च and, कुण्डले the two earrings, पूजितः worshipped, त्रिदश-इन्द्रेण by the king of the gods, सह इन्द्राण्या along with Indrani, च and, स-प्रियः along with His beloved (Satyabhama)

Anvaya: सप्रियः (भगवान्) सुरेन्द्रभवनं गत्वा अदित्यै कुण्डले दत्त्वा च, इन्द्राण्या सह त्रिदशेन्द्रेण पूजितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सप्रियः सुरेन्द्रभवनं गत्वा अदित्यै कुण्डले दत्त्वा च Accompanied by His beloved Satyabhama, having gone to the abode of Indra and returned the two earrings to Aditi, इन्द्राण्या सह त्रिदशेन्द्रेण पूजितः He was worshipped by Indra together with his wife Indrani.

Simple Meaning: Accompanied by His beloved wife Satyabhama, Krishna went to the abode of Indra and returned the celestial earrings to Mother Aditi. There, the Lord was worshiped with great honor by Indra along with his wife Indrani.

चोदितो भार्ययोत्पाट्य पारिजातं गरुत्मति ।
आरोप्य सेन्द्रान् विबुधान् निर्जित्योपानयत् पुरम् ॥10.59.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: चोदितः prompted, भार्यया by His wife, उत्पाट्य uprooting, पारिजातम् the Parijata tree, गरुत्मति on Garuda, आरोप्य placing, स-इन्द्रान् including Indra, विबुधान् the demigods, निर्जित्य defeating, उपानयत् brought, पुरम् to His city

Anvaya: भार्यया चोदितः (भगवान्) पारिजातम् उत्पाट्य गरुत्मति आरोप्य, सेन्द्रान् विबुधान् निर्जित्य पुरम् उपानयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भार्यया चोदितः Prompted by His wife, पारिजातम् उत्पाट्य uprooting the Parijata tree, गरुत्मति आरोप्य and placing it on Garuda, सेन्द्रान् विबुधान् निर्जित्य having defeated the demigods including Indra, पुरम् उपानयत् He brought it to His city.

Simple Meaning: Prompted by His wife Satyabhama, Krishna uprooted the celestial Parijata tree and placed it upon Garuda. When Indra and the demigods opposed Him to retain the tree, the Lord defeated them and brought the Parijata tree to His city of Dwaraka.

स्थापितः सत्यभामाया गृहोद्यानोपशोभनः ।
अन्वगुर्भ्रमराः स्वर्गात् तद्गन्धासवलम्पटाः ॥10.59.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: स्थापितः planted, सत्यभामायाः of Satyabhama, गृह-उद्यान-उपशोभनः beautifying the garden of her house, अन्वगुः followed, भ्रमराः the bees, स्वर्गात् from heaven, तत्-गन्ध-आसव-लम्पटाः greedy for its fragrance and sweet nectar

Anvaya: (सः पारिजातः) सत्यभामायाः गृहोद्यानोपशोभनः स्थापितः, तद्गन्धासवलम्पटाः भ्रमराः स्वर्गात् अन्वगुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (सः पारिजातः) सत्यभामायाः गृहोद्यानोपशोभनः स्थापितः That Parijata tree was planted beautifying the house garden of Satyabhama. तद्गन्धासवलम्पटाः भ्रमराः स्वर्गात् अन्वगुः The bees, greedy for its fragrance and nectar, followed it from heaven.

Simple Meaning: The Parijata tree was planted in the garden of Satyabhama's residence, enhancing its beauty immensely. Attracted by the tree's enchanting fragrance and sweet nectar, the heavenly bees flew all the way down from the celestial realm to remain near it.

ययाच आनम्य किरीटकोटिभिः पादौ स्पृशन्नच्युतमर्थसाधनम् ।
सिद्धार्थ एतेन विगृह्यते महानहो सुराणां च तमो धिगाढ्यताम् ॥10.59.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: ययाच begged, आनम्य bowing down, किरीट-कोटिभिः with the tips of his crown, पादौ feet, स्पृशन् touching, अच्युतम् Lord Achyuta, अर्थ-साधनम् the accomplisher of his goals, सिद्ध-अर्थः whose purpose was fulfilled, एतेन with Him, विगृह्यते fights, महान् the great one (Indra), अहो alas, सुराणाम् of the demigods, च and, तमः ignorance, धिक् fie upon, आढ्यताम् opulence

Anvaya: (यः इन्द्रः) अर्थसाधनम् अच्युतम् आनम्य किरीटकोटिभिः पादौ स्पृशन् ययाच, सिद्धार्थः (सः) एतेन महान् विगृह्यते, अहो! सुराणां तमः, आढ्यतां धिक्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (यः इन्द्रः) अर्थसाधनम् अच्युतम् आनम्य किरीटकोटिभिः पादौ स्पृशन् ययाच That Indra who, bowing down and touching the feet of Lord Achyuta with the tips of his crown, begged the accomplisher of his goals, सिद्धार्थः (सः) एतेन महान् विगृह्यते having fulfilled his purpose, that great one now fights with Him, अहो! सुराणां तमः, आढ्यतां धिक् alas, the ignorance of the demigods, fie upon opulence.

Simple Meaning: Even after bowing down and touching Lord Achyuta's feet with the tips of his crown to beg for the fulfillment of his goal, Indra turned around and fought with the Lord once his purpose was achieved. Alas, how great is the ignorance of even the elevated demigods! Fie upon material opulence, which so easily intoxicates the mind.

अथो मुहूर्त एकस्मिन् नानागारेषु ताः स्त्रियः ।
यथोपयेमे भगवांस्तावद्रूपधरोऽव्ययः ॥10.59.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: अथ thereupon, मुहूर्ते एकस्मिन् in a single auspicious moment, नाना-आगारेषु in separate palaces, ताः स्त्रियः those women, यथा properly according to Vedic rites, उपयेमे married, भगवान् the Supreme Lord, तावत्-रूप-धरः assuming as many forms as there were brides, अव्ययः the imperishable Lord

Anvaya: अथ अव्ययः भगवान् तावद्रूपधरः (सन्) एकस्मिन् मुहूर्ते नानागारेषु ताः स्त्रियः यथा उपयेमे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अथ अव्ययः भगवान् Thereupon the imperishable Supreme Lord, तावद्रूपधरः assuming as many distinct forms as there were brides, एकस्मिन् मुहूर्ते at one and the same auspicious moment, नानागारेषु in separate palaces, ताः स्त्रियः यथा उपयेमे married those women according to proper rites.

Simple Meaning: Thereafter, at one and the same auspicious moment, the imperishable Supreme Lord expanded Himself into as many forms as there were brides. Entering their individual, magnificent palaces simultaneously, He married each of those princesses with proper Vedic rites.

गृहेषु तासामनपाय्यतर्क्यकृन्निरस्तसाम्यातिशयेष्ववस्थितः ।
रेमे रमाभिर्निजकामसंप्लुतो यथेतरो गार्हकमेधिकांश्चरन् ॥10.59.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: गृहेषु in the palaces, तासाम् of those princesses, अनपायी never leaving, अतर्क्य-कृत् performing incomprehensible deeds, निरस्त-साम्य-अतिशयेषु in residences without equal or superior, अवस्थितः remaining continuously, रेमे He enjoyed, रमाभिः with those opulent queens like Goddess Lakshmi, निज-काम-संप्लुतः completely self-fulfilled in His own desires, यथा like, इतरः an ordinary person, गार्हक-मेधिकान् household duties, चरन् performing

Anvaya: अनपायी अतर्क्यकृत् (भगवान्) निरस्तसाम्यातिशयेषु तासां गृहेषु अवस्थितः, निजकामसंप्लुतः (अपि) इतरः यथा गार्हकमेधिकान् चरन् रमाभिः रेमे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अनपायी अतर्क्यकृत् Never leaving and performing incomprehensible deeds, (भगवान्) निरस्तसाम्यातिशयेषु तासां गृहेषु अवस्थितः the Supreme Lord resided in those palaces of theirs which were without equal or superior. निजकामसंप्लुतः Although completely self-fulfilled in His own desires, इतरः यथा  like an ordinary man, गार्हकमेधिकान् चरन् performing household duties, रमाभिः रेमे He enjoyed with those queens who were like Lakshmi.

Simple Meaning: Residing continuously in each of their peerless and incomparable palaces, the Supreme Lord performed extraordinary, incomprehensible deeds. Although He is entirely self-fulfilled and beyond all desires, He enjoyed the company of those magnificent queens—who were expansions of the Goddess of Fortune—while executing the day-to-day duties of a householder just like an ordinary person.

इत्थं रमापतिमवाप्य पतिं स्त्रियस्ता ब्रह्मादयोऽपि न विदुः पदवीं यदीयाम् ।
भेजुर्मुदाविरतमेधितयानुरागहासावलोकनवसङ्गमजल्पलज्जाः॥10.59.44॥

Word-by-word meaning: इत्थम् thus, रमा-पतिम् the husband of Goddess Lakshmi, अवाप्य having obtained, पतिम् as husband, स्त्रियः women, ताः those, ब्रह्मा-दयः demigods headed by Brahma, अपि even, न do not, विदुः know, पदवीम् the supreme state or position, यदीयाम् whose, भेजुः they served, मुदा with joy, अविरत-एधितया constantly increasing, अनुराग-हास-अवलोक-नव-सङ्गम-जल्प-लज्जाः through affectionate smiles, glances, intimate association, conversations, and shy bashfulness

Anvaya: ताः स्त्रियः, ब्रह्मादयः अपि यदीयां पदवीं न विदुः, इत्थं तं रमापतिं पतिम् अवाप्य अविरतैधितया मुदा अनुरागहासावलोकनवसङ्गमजल्पलज्जाः (तं) भेजुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ताः स्त्रियः Those women, ब्रह्मादयः अपि यदीयां पदवीं न विदुः whose supreme position even demigods headed by Brahma do not comprehend, इत्थं तं रमापतिं पतिम् अवाप्य thus, having obtained as husband, that Lord of Lakshmi, अविरतैधितया मुदा अनुरागहासावलोकनवसङ्गमजल्पलज्जाः भेजुः served Him with constantly increasing joy through affectionate smiles, glances, intimate association, loving conversations, and shy bashfulness.

Simple Meaning: Having obtained as their husband the Lord of Lakshmi—whose true glory and position remain incomprehensible even to exalted demigods like Brahma—those princesses served Him with ever-increasing joy. Expressing their devotion through affectionate smiles, loving glances, intimate companionship, sweet conversations, and charming bashfulness, they rendered constant service to the Supreme Lord.

प्रत्युद्गमासनवरार्हणपादशौचताम्बूलविश्रमणवीजनगन्धमाल्यैः।
केशप्रसारशयनस्नपनोपहार्यैर्दासीशता अपि विभोर्विदधुः स्म दास्यम् ॥10.59.45॥

Word-by-word meaning: प्रत्युद्गम-आसन-वर-अर्हण-पाद-शौच-ताम्बूल-विश्रमण-वीजन-गन्ध-माल्यैः by going out to receive Him, offering excellent seats, worshiping Him, washing His feet, presenting betel nut, resting and massaging His feet, fanning Him, offering fragrant pastes and flower garlands, केश-प्रसार-शयन-स्नपन-उपहार्यैः dressing His hair, arranging His bed, bathing Him, and bringing food and gifts, दासी-शताः hundreds of maidservants, अपि although, विभोः of the almighty Lord, विदधुः स्म performed, दास्यम् personal service themselves

Anvaya: (ताः स्त्रियः) दासीशताः अपि प्रत्युद्गमासनवरार्हणपादशौचताम्बूलविश्रमणवीजनगन्धमाल्यैः केशप्रसारशयनस्नपनोपहार्यैः विभोः दास्यं विदधुः स्म।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: (ताः स्त्रियः) दासीशताः अपि Those queens, although having hundreds of maidservants, प्रत्युद्गमासनवरार्हणपादशौचताम्बूलविश्रमणवीजनगन्धमाल्यैः by going out to meet Him, offering excellent seats, worshiping, washing feet, betel nut, resting, fanning, fragrant pastes, garlands, केशप्रसारशयनस्नपनोपहार्यैः dressing hair, bedding, bathing, and offering presentations, विभोः दास्यं विदधुः स्म performed personal service of the almighty Lord themselves.

Simple Meaning: Although each of these queens possessed hundreds of maidservants at her command, they preferred to personally serve the Lord themselves. They personally greeted Him upon His arrival, offered Him excellent seats, worshiped Him, washed His feet, offered betel nut, massaged His feet while He rested, fanned Him, presented sandalwood paste and flower garlands, dressed His hair, prepared His bed, bathed Him, and served Him exquisite meals and gifts.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “नरकासुरवधः पारिजातापहरणं कृष्णस्य गृहस्थलीलावर्णनं च नाम”​​ एकोनषष्टित्तमः अध्यायः  ॥59॥

Thus ends the fifty-ninth discourse named “Krishna takes away Parijata tree by force from heaven, kills Narakasura - the son of Mother Earth. Description of how Krishna behaves like an ordinary householder in each of His palaces.” in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
