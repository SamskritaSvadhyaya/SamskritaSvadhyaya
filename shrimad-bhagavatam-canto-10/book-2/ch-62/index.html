Shrimad Bhagavatam - Book 2

Canto 10

Chapter 62

Meeting of Usha and Aniruddha

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

 उषा-अनिरुद्धमिलनः नाम द्विषष्टितमः अध्यायः

(33 Shlokas)

राजा उवाच
बाणस्य तनयामूषामुपयेमे यदूत्तमः ।
तत्र युद्धमभूद् घोरं हरिशङ्करर्योमहत् ।
एतत् सर्वं महायोगिन् समाख्यातुं त्वमर्हसि ॥10.62.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: राजा उवाच King Parikshit said, बाणस्य of Bana, तनयाम् daughter, ऊषाम् Usha, उपयेमे married, यदु-उत्तमः Aniruddha (the best of the Yadus), तत्र there, युद्धम् fight/battle, अभूत् took place, घोरम् terrible, हरि-शङ्करयोः between Lord Hari (Krishna) and Lord Shankara (Shiva), महत् great, एतत् all this, सर्वम् completely, महायोगिन् O great yogi (Shri Shuka), समाख्यातुम् to explain in detail, त्वम् you, अर्हसि ought to

Anvaya: राजा उवाच— महायोगिन्! यदूत्तमः बाणस्य तनयाम् ऊषाम् उपयेमे, तत्र हरिशङ्करयोः घोरं महत् युद्धम् अभूत्, त्वम् एतत् सर्वं समाख्यातुम् अर्हसि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजा उवाच King Parikshit said: महायोगिन् O great yogi Shuka, यदूत्तमः बाणस्य तनयाम् ऊषाम् उपयेमे the best among the Yadus (Aniruddha) married Usha, the daughter of Bana; तत्र हरिशङ्करयोः घोरं महत् युद्धम् अभूत् in that connection a terrible and great battle took place between Lord Hari and Lord Shankara. त्वम् एतत् सर्वं समाख्यातुम् अर्हसि You ought to explain all this in full detail to me.

Simple Meaning: King Parikshit said: O great yogi Shuka, I have heard that Aniruddha, the best of the Yadus, married Usha, the daughter of Bana, and that a terrible, historic battle broke out between Lord Hari and Lord Shankara. Please explain this entire incident to me in detail!

श्री शुक उवाच
बाणः पुत्रशतज्येष्ठो बलेरासीन्महात्मनः ।
येन वामनरूपाय हरयेऽदायि मेदिनी ॥10.62.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, बाणः Bana, पुत्र-शत-ज्येष्ठः the eldest among one hundred sons, बलेः of Bali, आसीत् was, महा-आत्मनः of the great soul, येन by whom, वामन-रूपाय to the one in the form of Vamana, हरये to Lord Hari, अदायि was given, मेदिनी the earth

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— महात्मनः बलेः पुत्रशतज्येष्ठः बाणः आसीत्, येन वामनरूपाय हरये मेदिनी अदायि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said: महात्मनः बलेः पुत्रशतज्येष्ठः बाणः आसीत् Bana was the eldest among the hundred sons of the great soul King Bali, येन वामनरूपाय हरये मेदिनी अदायि by whom the earth was given in charity to Lord Hari in the form of Vamana.

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: Bana was the eldest of the one hundred sons born to the noble and great soul King Bali, who had previously given the entire earth as a gift to Lord Hari when He appeared in the form of Vamana.

तस्यौरसः सुतो बाणः शिवभक्तिरतः सदा ।
मान्यो वदान्यो धीमांश्च सत्यसन्धो दृढव्रतः ॥10.62.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य of him, औरसः legitimate/own, सुतः son, बाणः Bana, शिव-भक्ति-रतः always devoted to the worship of Lord Shiva, सदा always, मान्यः respectable/revered, वदान्यः generous, धीमान् wise, च and, सत्य-सन्धः truthful to his word, दृढ-व्रतः firm in his vows

Anvaya: तस्य औरसः सुतः बाणः सदा शिवभक्तिरतः, मान्यः, वदान्यः, धीमान्, सत्यसन्धः, दृढव्रतः च आसीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तस्य औरसः सुतः बाणः His own legitimate son Bana, सदा शिवभक्तिरतः was always devoted to Lord Shiva, मान्यः वदान्यः धीमान् सत्यसन्धः दृढव्रतः च आसीत् and was respected by all, generous, wise, truthful to his word, and firm in his vows.

Simple Meaning: Bali’s legitimate son was Bana, who was always deeply devoted to Lord Shiva. He was highly respected, generous, intelligent, truthful to his word, and firm in his vows.

शोणिताख्ये पुरे रम्ये स राज्यमकरोत् पुरा ।
तस्य शम्भोः प्रसादेन किङ्करा इव तेऽमराः ।
सहस्रबाहुर्वाद्येन ताण्डवेऽतोषयन्मृडम् ॥10.62.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: शोणित-आख्ये named Shonita, पुरे in the city, रम्ये beautiful, सः he (Bana), राज्यम् kingdom/rule, अकुरुत् made/ruled, पुरा in the past, तस्य of him, शम्भोः of Lord Shiva, प्रसादेन by the mercy/grace, किङ्कराः इव like servants, ते those, अमराः demigods/celestials, सहस्र-बाहुः having one thousand arms, वाद्येन with musical instruments, ताण्डवे during the Tandava dance, अतोषयत् satisfied/pleased, मृडम् Lord Shiva

Anvaya: पुरा सः रम्ये शोणिताख्ये पुरे राज्यम् अकरोत्। तस्य शम्भोः प्रसादेन ते अमराः किङ्कराः इव (आसन्)। सहस्रबाहुः ताण्डवे वाद्येन मृडम् अतोषयत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: पुरा सः रम्ये शोणिताख्ये पुरे राज्यम् अकरोत् In the past he ruled his kingdom in the beautiful city named Shonita. तस्य शम्भोः प्रसादेन ते अमराः किङ्कराः इव By the grace of Lord Shiva upon him even the demigods served him like menial servants. सहस्रबाहुः ताण्डवे वाद्येन मृडम् अतोषयत् Bana possessing one thousand arms satisfied Lord Shiva during His Tandava dance by playing musical instruments.

Simple Meaning: In the past, Bana ruled over his kingdom in the beautiful city named Shonitapura. By the special grace of Lord Shiva, even the demigods attended to him like humble servants. Possessing one thousand arms, Bana once greatly pleased Lord Shiva during Tandava dance by playing various musical instruments simultaneously to accompany Him.

भगवान् सर्वभूतेशः शरण्यो भक्तवत्सलः ।
वरेणच्छन्दयामास स तं वव्रे पुराधिपम् ॥10.62.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: भगवान् the Supreme Lord Shiva, सर्व-भूत-ईशः the master of all living beings, शरण्यः the shelter/refuge, भक्त-वत्सलः compassionate to His devotees, वरेण with a boon, छन्दयामास requested/offered, सः he (Lord Shiva), तम् him (Bana), वव्रे chose/made, पुर-अधिपम् as the protector of his city

Anvaya: सर्वभूतेशः शरण्यः भक्तवत्सलः भगवान् तं वरेण छन्दयामास, सः पुराधिपं वव्रे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सर्वभूतेशः शरण्यः भक्तवत्सलः भगवान् तं वरेण छन्दयामास Lord Shiva the master of all beings the refuge for all and affectionate toward His devotees offered Bana a boon. सः पुराधिपं वव्रे Bana requested Lord Shiva to become the guardian protector of his city.

Simple Meaning: Lord Shiva, who is the ruler of all created beings, the ultimate shelter, and deeply affectionate to His devotees, offered Bana a boon. Bana chose to ask Lord Shiva Himself to become the guardian protector of his city.

स एकदाऽऽह गिरिशं पार्श्वस्थं वीर्यदुर्मदः ।
किरीटेनार्कवर्णेन संस्पृशंस्तत्पदाम्बुजम् ॥10.62.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Bana), एकदा once, आह spoken/said, गिरिशम् to Lord Shiva (the lord of the mountain), पार्श्व-स्थम् standing nearby, वीर्य-दुर्मदः intoxicated with pride of his strength, किरीटेन with his crown, अर्क-वर्णेन effulgent like the sun, संस्पृशन् touching, तत्-पद-अम्बुजम् His lotus feet

Anvaya: एकदा वीर्यदुर्मदः सः अर्कवर्णेन किरीटेन तत्पदाम्बुजं संस्पृशन् पार्श्वस्थं गिरिशम् आह।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एकदा वीर्यदुर्मदः सः Once he who was intoxicated with pride of his immense physical strength, अर्कवर्णेन किरीटेन तत्पदाम्बुजं संस्पृशन् touching Lord Shiva's lotus feet with his sun like brilliant crown, पार्श्वस्थं गिरिशम् आह spoke to Lord Shiva who was standing nearby.

Simple Meaning: Once, Bana, puffed up with extreme pride in his own physical strength, touched the lotus feet of Lord Shiva with his brilliant sun-like crown and spoke to the Lord who was standing near him.

नमस्ये त्वां महादेव लोकानां गुरुमीश्वरम् ।
पुंसामपूर्णकामानां कामपूरामराङ्घ्रिपम् ॥10.62.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: नमस्ये I bow down/pay homage, त्वाम् to You, महा-देव O Great God (Lord Shiva), लोकानाम् of all the worlds, गुरुम् the master/spiritual teacher, ईश्वरम् the supreme controller, पुंसाम् of human beings, अपूर्ण-कामानाम् whose desires are unfulfilled, काम-पूर-अमर-अङ्घ्रिपम् wish-fulfilling tree whose roots are divine/immortal

Anvaya: महादेव! लोकानां गुरुम् ईश्वरम् अपूर्णकामानां पुंसां कामपूरामराङ्घ्रिपं त्वां नमस्ये।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महादेव O Great Lord Shiva, लोकानां गुरुम् ईश्वरम् the supreme controller and teacher of all the worlds, अपूर्णकामानां पुंसां कामपूरामराङ्घ्रिपं who is like a wish fulfilling tree for persons whose desires are unfulfilled, त्वां नमस्ये I offer my respectful obeisances unto You.

Simple Meaning: Bana said: O Mahadeva, You are the supreme lord and master of all the worlds. You are like a heavenly tree that fulfills every wish of people whose desires remain unsatisfied. I bow down to You in deep reverence.

दोः सहस्रं त्वया दत्तं परं भाराय मेऽभवत् ।
त्रिलोक्यां प्रतियोद्धारं न लभे त्वदृते समम् ॥10..62.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: दोः arms, सहस्रम् one thousand, त्वया by You, दत्तम् given, परम् only, भाराय for a burden, मे to me, अभवत् has become, त्रि-लोक्याम् in the three worlds, प्रति-योद्धारम् a rival fighter/opponent, न not, लभे I find, त्वद्-ऋते except You, समम् equal match

Anvaya: त्वया दत्तं सहस्रं दोः मे परं भाराय अभवत्। त्रिलोक्यां त्वदृते समं प्रतियोद्धारं न लभे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: त्वया दत्तं सहस्रं दोः मे परं भाराय अभवत् The thousand arms given by You have become a heavy burden to me. त्रिलोक्यां त्वदृते समं प्रतियोद्धारं न लभे In all the three worlds I cannot find any equal opponent to fight with me except You.

Simple Meaning: Bana said: The thousand arms You bestowed upon me have become a burden to me now. In all the three worlds, I am unable to find an equal opponent to engage in combat with me, except for You.

कण्डूत्या निभृतैर्दोर्भिर्युयुत्सुर्दिग्गजानहम् ।
आद्यायां चूर्णयन्नद्रीन् भीतास्तेऽपि प्रदुद्रुवुः ॥10.62.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: कण्डूत्या by itching, निभृतैः filled/swollen, दोर्भिः with arms, युयुत्सुः desiring to fight, दिग्-गजान् the directional elephants, अहम् I, आद्यायाम् originally/at first, चूर्णयन् pulverizing/crushing, अद्रीन् mountains, भीताः terrified, ते they, अपि even, प्रदुद्रुवुः fled away

Anvaya: कण्डूत्या निभृतैः दोर्भिः युयुत्सुः अहम् अद्रीन् चूर्णयन् आद्यायां दिग्गजान् (अभ्यागच्छं), ते अपि भीताः प्रदुद्रुवुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कण्डूत्या निभृतैः दोर्भिः युयुत्सुः अहम् I who was eager to fight with my arms itching with energy, अद्रीन् चूर्णयन् crushing mountains to powder, आद्यायां दिग्गजान् initially went after the great directional elephants, ते अपि भीताः प्रदुद्रुवुः but even they became terrified and ran away.

Simple Meaning: Bana said: My arms were itching with so much strength and eagerness to fight that I went around crushing giant mountains to dust. Then I went to fight the great directional elephants, but even they became terrified of me and fled in panic!

तच्छ्रुत्वा भगवान् क्रुद्धः केतुस्ते भज्यते यदा ।
त्वद्दर्पघ्नं भवेन्मूढ संयुगं मत्समेन ते ॥10.62.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: तत् that, श्रुत्वा hearing, भगवान् Lord Shiva, क्रुद्धः angry, केतुः banner/flag, ते your, भज्यते will be broken, यदा when, त्वत्-दर्प-घ्नम् the destroyer of your pride, भवेत् will take place, मूढ O fool, संयुगम् battle, मत्-समेन with my equal, ते for you

Anvaya: तत् श्रुत्वा भगवान् क्रुद्धः (आसीत्), (सः उवाच)— मूढ! यदा ते केतुः भज्यते, (तदा) मत्समेन ते त्वद्दर्पघ्नं संयुगं भवेत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तत् श्रुत्वा भगवान् क्रुद्धः Hearing this Lord Shiva became angry, मूढ! यदा ते केतुः भज्यते O fool! when your battle flag is broken, मत्समेन ते त्वद्दर्पघ्नं संयुगं भवेत् then a fight with someone equal to Me will take place for you that will destroy your pride.

Simple Meaning: Hearing Bana speak so arrogantly, Lord Shiva became furious and replied: O fool! When the flag on your chariot gets broken, a battle with someone equal to Me will take place, and that fight will thoroughly crush your senseless pride!

इत्युक्तः कुमतिर्हृष्टः स्वगृहं प्राविशन्नृप ।
प्रतीक्षन् गिरिशादेशं स्ववीर्यनशनं कुधीः ॥10.62.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: इति thus, उक्तः spoken to, कु-मतिः foolish/wicked-minded, हृष्टः delighted, स्व-गृहम् his own house, प्राविशत् entered, नृप O King (Parikshit), प्रतीक्षन् waiting for, गिरिश-आदेशम् Lord Shiva's word, स्व-वीर्य-नशनम् the destruction of his own strength, कु-धीः dull-witted

Anvaya: नृप! इति उक्तः कुमतिः कुधीः स्ववीर्यनशनं गिरिशादेशं प्रतीक्षन् हृष्टः स्वगृहं प्राविशत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King Parikshit, इति उक्तः कुमतिः कुधीः thus spoken to that fool of foolish mind, स्ववीर्यनशनं गिरिशादेशं प्रतीक्षन् waiting for the prediction of Lord Shiva that would destroy his own strength, हृष्टः स्वगृहं प्राविशत् entered his house filled with joy.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, upon hearing this from Lord Shiva, that foolish and foolishly-minded Bana felt delighted instead of sorrowful. Eagerly waiting for the fulfillment of Lord Shiva's statement which promised a battle that would destroy his immense power, he returned happy to his palace.

तस्योषा नाम दुहिता स्वप्ने प्राद्युम्निना रतिम् ।
कन्यालभत कान्तेन प्रागदृष्टश्रुतेन सा ॥10.62.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य of him (Bana), ऊषा Usha, नाम named, दुहिता daughter, स्वप्ने in a dream, प्राद्युम्निना with the son of Pradyumna (Aniruddha), रतिम् intimate union/joyful association, कन्या the maiden, अलभत obtained, कान्तेन with her lover, प्राक्-अदृष्ट-श्रुतेन who was previously neither seen nor heard of, सा she

Anvaya: तस्य दुहिता ऊषा नाम। सा कन्या स्वप्ने प्रागदृष्टश्रुतेन कान्तेन प्राद्युम्निना रतिम् अलभत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तस्य दुहिता ऊषा नाम His daughter was named Usha. सा कन्या That young maiden, स्वप्ने प्रागदृष्टश्रुतेन कान्तेन प्राद्युम्निना रतिम् अलभत obtained intimate joy in a dream with her lover the son of Pradyumna, Aniruddha whom she had never seen or heard of before.

Simple Meaning: Bana had a daughter named Usha. That young princess enjoyed an intimate romantic meeting in a dream with her lover Aniruddha, the son of Pradyumna, even though she had never seen or heard of him before in her waking life.

सा तत्र तमपश्यन्ती क्वासि कान्तेति वादिनी ।
सखीनां मध्य उत्तस्थौ विह्वला व्रीडिता भृशम् ॥10.62.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: सा she (Usha), तत्र there, तम् him (Aniruddha), अपश्यन्ती not seeing, क्व where, असि are you, कान्त O lover, इति thus, वादिनी speaking/crying out, सखीनाम् of her female companions, मध्ये in the midst, उत्तस्थौ stood up/awoke, विह्वला agitated/distressed, व्रीडिता embarrassed/ashamed, भृशम् greatly

Anvaya: सा तत्र तम् अपश्यन्ती, “कान्त! क्व असि?” इति वादिनी सखीनां मध्ये भृशं विह्वला व्रीडिता च उत्तस्थौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सा तत्र तम् अपश्यन्ती She not seeing him there in her dream upon waking, “कान्त! क्व असि?” इति वादिनी crying out, “Where are you, O dear?”, सखीनां मध्ये भृशं विह्वला व्रीडिता च उत्तस्थौ woke up in the midst of her female companions feeling deeply agitated and greatly embarrassed.

Simple Meaning: Upon waking up, Usha did not find her beloved lying beside her. Crying out, "O my dear, where are you?", she suddenly sat up in the middle of her female friends, feeling deeply distressed and thoroughly embarrassed for having blurted those words out loud.

बाणस्य मन्त्री कुम्भाण्डश्चित्रलेखा च तत्सुता ।
सख्यपृच्छत् सखीमूषां कौतूहलसमन्विता ॥10.62.14॥

Anvaya: बाणस्य मन्त्री कुम्भाण्डः (आसीत्), तत्सुता चित्रलेखा च (आसीत्)। कौतूहलसमन्विता सखी (चित्रलेखा) सखीम् ऊषाम् अप्रच्छत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बाणस्य मन्त्री कुम्भाण्डः (आसीत्) Bana had a minister named Kumbhanda. तत्सुता चित्रलेखा च And his daughter was Chitralekha. कौतूहलसमन्विता सखी सखीम् ऊषाम् अप्रच्छत् Filled with curiosity, the friend (Chitralekha) asked her friend Usha.

Simple Meaning: Bana had a minister named Kumbhanda, whose daughter was Chitralekha. Chitralekha was a close friend and companion of Usha. Filled with curiosity upon seeing Usha so distressed and embarrassed, Chitralekha questioned her friend.

कं त्वं मृगयसे सुभ्रूः कीदृशस्ते मनोरथः।
हस्तग्राहं न तेऽद्यापि राजपुत्र्युपलक्षये ॥10.62.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: कम् whom, त्वम् you, मृगयसे are looking for, सु-भ्रूः O beautiful-eyebrowed one, कीदृशः of what kind, ते your, मनोरथः desire/heart's wish, हस्त-ग्राहम् taking of hand/husband, न not, ते your, अद्य today, अपि even, राज-पुत्रि O princess, उपलक्षये I see/observe

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सुभ्रूः O lady with beautiful eyebrows, त्वं कं मृगयसे whom are you searching for, ते मनोरथः कीदृशः what is your desire, राजपुत्रि O princess, अद्यापि ते हस्तग्राहं न उपलक्षये even today I do not see anyone who has held your hand in marriage.

Simple Meaning: Chitralekha said: O girl with beautiful eyebrows, whom are you searching for so desperately? What is this intense desire in your heart? O princess, I do not see any husband as of yet who has taken your hand in marriage!

ऊषा उवाच
दृष्टः कश्चिन्नरः स्वप्ने श्यामः कमललोचनः ।
पीतवासा बृहद्बाहुर्योषितां हृदयङ्गमः ॥10.62.16॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऊषा उवाच Usha said, दृष्टः seen, कश्चित् a certain, नरः man, स्वप्ने in a dream, श्यामः dark-complexioned, कमल-लोचनः lotus-eyed, पीत-वासाः wearing yellow garments, बृहत्-बाहुः long-armed, योषिताम् of women, हृदयङ्गमः heart-stealing/captivating to the mind

Anvaya: ऊषा उवाच— स्वप्ने श्यामः कमललोचनः पीतवासाः बृहद्बाहुः योषितां हृदयङ्गमः कश्चित् नरः दृष्टः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ऊषा उवाच Usha said: स्वप्ने In the dream, श्यामः dark-complexioned, कमललोचनः who had lotus-like eyes, पीतवासाः wore yellow garments, बृहद्बाहुः possessed long arms, योषितां हृदयङ्गमः and was captivating to the hearts of women, कश्चित् नरः a certain man, दृष्टः was seen.

Simple Meaning: Usha said: In my dream I saw an extraordinary man with a dark complexion, lotus-like eyes, yellow garments, and long arms. He was so incredibly handsome that he could steal the heart of any woman!

तमहं मृगये कान्तं पाययित्वाधरं मधु ।
क्वापि यातः स्पृहयतीं क्षिप्त्वा मां वृजिनार्णवे ॥10.62.17॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him, अहम् I, मृगये am searching for, कान्तम् lover, पाययित्वा having made me drink, अधरम् of his lips, मधु the nectar, क्व अपि somewhere, यातः gone, स्पृहयतीम् longing/desiring for him, क्षिप्त्वा throwing, माम् me, वृजिन-अर्णवे into an ocean of sorrow

Anvaya: अहं तं कान्तं  मृगये, यः (मह्यम्) अधरं मधु पाययित्वा स्पृहयतीं मां वृजिनार्णवे क्षिप्त्वा क्वापि यातः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अहं तं कान्तं मृगये I am searching for that lover, यः अधरं मधु पाययित्वा who made me drink the nectar of his lips, स्पृहयतीं मां वृजिनार्णवे क्षिप्त्वा क्वापि यातः and throwing me who longs for him into an ocean of sorrow has gone away somewhere.

Simple Meaning: I am searching for that beloved lover of mine. After making me taste the sweet nectar of his lips, he abandoned me here while I was still longing for him, throwing me into a deep ocean of grief, and went away somewhere unknown!

चित्रलेखा उवाच
व्यसनं तेऽपकर्षामि त्रिलोक्यां यदि भाव्यते ।
तमानेष्ये नरं यस्ते मनोहर्ता तमादिश ॥10.62.18॥

Word-by-word meaning: चित्रलेखा उवाच Chitralekha said, व्यसनम् distress/sorrow, ते your, अपकर्षामि I shall remove, त्रि-लोक्याम् in the three worlds, यदि if, भाव्यते exists/can be found, तम् him, अनेष्ये I will bring, नरम् man, यः who, ते your, मनः-हर्ता heart-stealer, तम् him, आदिश point out/identify

Anvaya: चित्रलेखा उवाच— ते व्यसनम् अपकर्षामि, यः ते मनोहर्ता (अस्ति), सः नरः यदि त्रिलोक्यां भाव्यते (तर्हि) तम् अनेष्ये, तम् आदिश।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: चित्रलेखा उवाच Chitralekha said: ते व्यसनम् अपकर्षामि I shall remove your sorrow, यः ते मनोहर्ता सः नरः यदि त्रिलोक्यां भाव्यते picture or person who stole your heart if that man exists anywhere within the three worlds, तम् अनेष्ये I will bring him to you, तम् आदिश please describe or point him out.

Simple Meaning: Chitralekha said: I shall dispel your sorrow! If the man who stole your heart exists anywhere in the three worlds, I will fetch him and bring him right to you. Just point him out to me or describe what he looks like!

इत्युक्त्वा देवगन्धर्वसिद्धचारणपन्नगान् ।

Anvaya: इति उक्त्वा देवगन्धर्वसिद्धचारणपन्नगान् दैत्यविद्याधरान् यक्षान् मनुजान् च यथालिखत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: इति उक्त्वा Having spoken thus, देवगन्धर्वसिद्धचारणपन्नगान् of demigods, gandharvas, siddhas, charanas, and serpent-creatures, दैत्यविद्याधरान् of daityas and vidyadharas, यक्षान् मनुजान् च यथालिखत् of yakshas and humans, she accurately drew pictures.

Simple Meaning: Having spoken thus to reassure Usha, Chitralekha took her drawing materials and accurately drew portraits of demigods, demigods, gandharvas, siddhas, charanas, and serpent-creatures, daityas, vidyadharas, yakshas, and various prominent men.

मनुजेषु च सा वृष्णीन् शूरमानकदुन्दुभिम् ।
व्यलिखद् रामकृष्णौ च प्रद्युम्नं वीक्ष्य लज्जिता ॥10.62.20॥

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सा मनुजेषु च वृष्णीन् शूरम् आनकदुन्दुभिं रामकृष्णौ च व्यलिखत् And among humans she drew the Vrishni heroes, Shura, Anakadundubhi, Balarama and Krishna, प्रद्युम्नं वीक्ष्य लज्जिता and upon seeing the portrait of Pradyumna, Usha became bashful.

Simple Meaning: Among the humans, Chitralekha drew the prominent heroes of the Vrishni dynasty: King Shura, Vasudeva, Balarama, and Krishna. When Chitralekha drew Pradyumna, Usha looked at his portrait and became noticeably bashful and shy, recognizing her dream-lover's close family likeness.

अनिरुद्धं विलिखितं वीक्ष्योषावाङ्मुखी ह्रिया ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महीपते O King Parikshit, विलिखितम् अनिरुद्धं वीक्ष्य seeing Aniruddha drawn in the picture, ऊषा ह्रिया अवाङ्मुखी (भूत्वा) Usha bowing her head down out of modesty, स्मयमाना 'सः असौ असौ' इति प्राह smiling shyly said "He is that one! That one!"

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, the moment Usha saw the portrait of Aniruddha, she lowered her head in deep shyness. Smiling softly with bashfulness, she pointed at the picture and exclaimed, "He is the one! That's him, that's him!"

चित्रलेखा तमाज्ञाय पौत्रं कृष्णस्य योगिनी ।

Anvaya: राजन्! योगिनी चित्रलेखा तं कृष्णस्य पौत्रम् आज्ञाय विहायसा कृष्णपालितां द्वारकां ययौ।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजन् O King Parikshit, योगिनी चित्रलेखा Chitralekha, who possessed yogic powers, तं कृष्णस्य पौत्रम् आज्ञाय recognizing him to be the grandson of Krishna, विहायसा कृष्णपालितां द्वारकां ययौ traveled through the sky to Dwaraka which was protected by Krishna.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit, Chitralekha—who was endowed with extraordinary yogic powers—instantly identified the man as Aniruddha, the grandson of Krishna. She immediately took to the sky and flew through the air to the city of Dwaraka, which was guarded and protected by Krishna.

तत्र सुप्तं सुपर्यङ्के प्राद्युम्निं योगमास्थिता ।

Simple Meaning: Employing her yogic powers, Chitralekha went there and took Aniruddha, the son of Pradyumna, while he was fast asleep on a luxurious bed. She transported him back to the city of Shonitapura and presented her dear beloved to her friend Usha.

सा च तं सुन्दरवरं विलोक्य मुदितानना ।

परार्ध्यवासः स्रग्गन्धधूपदीपासनादिभिः ।

गूढः कन्यापुरे शश्वत् प्रवृद्धस्नेहया तया ।

Word-by-word meaning: गूढः hidden, कन्या-पुरे in the residence of the maiden, शश्वत् continuously, प्रवृद्ध-स्नेहया by her whose love was greatly increasing, तया by her, न not, अहः-गणान् the passing of many days, सः he Aniruddha, बुबुधे was aware of, ऊषया by Usha, अपहृत-इन्द्रियः whose senses were completely captivated

Anvaya: प्रवृद्धस्नेहया तया ऊषया अपहृतेन्द्रियः गूढः कन्यापुरे शश्वत् सः अहर्गणान् न बुबुधे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रवृद्धस्नेहया तया ऊषया अपहृतेन्द्रियः Captivated in his senses by Usha whose deep affection was constantly growing, गूढः कन्यापुरे शश्वत् hidden continuously within the residence of the maiden, सः अहर्गणान् न बुबुधे he was not even aware of the passing of many days.

Simple Meaning: Hidden away continuously in the inner quarters of the maiden, Aniruddha was completely captivated by Usha and her rapidly growing love for him. He was so deeply absorbed in her company that he did not even notice the passing of many days.

तां तथा यदुवीरेण भुज्यमानां हतव्रताम् ।
हेतुभिर्लक्षयाञ्चक्रुराप्रीतां दुरवच्छदैः ॥10.62.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: ताम् her (Usha), तथा in that manner, यदु-वीरेण by the hero of the Yadu dynasty Aniruddha, भुज्यमानाम् being enjoyed, हत-व्रताम् who had broken her vow of maidenhood, हेतुभिः by distinct signs, लक्षयाञ्चक्रुः they noticed or observed, आप्रीताम् completely satisfied, दुरवच्छदैः which could not be concealed

Anvaya: यदुवीरेण तथा भुज्यमानां हतव्रताम् आप्रीतां तां दुरवच्छदैः हेतुभिः लक्षयाञ्चक्रुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यदुवीरेण तथा भुज्यमानां Being enjoyed in that way by the hero of the Yadu dynasty, हतव्रताम् आप्रीतां तां her who had lost her vow of being a maiden and was thoroughly satisfied, दुरवच्छदैः हेतुभिः लक्षयाञ्चक्रुः they noticed through clear signs that were impossible to conceal.

Simple Meaning: As she enjoyed secret loving encounters with the hero of the Yadu family, Usha had broken her vow of maidenhood. The female attendants and guards in her palace soon noticed this change in her, observing distinct signs of her deep physical satisfaction and romantic happiness that could not be hidden.

भटा आवेदयाञ्चक्रू राजंस्ते दुहितुर्वयम् ।
विचेष्टितं लक्षयामः कन्यायाः कुलदूषणम् ॥10.62.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: भटाः the guards, आवेदयाञ्चक्रूः reported, राजन् O King Bana, ते to you, दुहितुः of your daughter, वयम् we, विचेष्टितम् conduct or behavior, लक्षयामः we observe, कन्यायाः of the unmarried girl, कुल-दूषणम् bringing dishonor to the family

Anvaya: भटाः आवेदयाञ्चक्रुः – राजन्! ते कन्यायाः दुहितुः कुलदूषणं विचेष्टितं वयं लक्षयामः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: भटाः आवेदयाञ्चक्रुः The guards reported, राजन्! ते कन्यायाः दुहितुः O King! we observe of your unmarried daughter, कुलदूषणं विचेष्टितं वयं लक्षयामः conduct that brings dishonor to the family.

Simple Meaning: The guards went to King Bana and reported to him saying O King, we have been observing the conduct of your unmarried daughter, and it appears to be bringing dishonor and disgrace to the family lineage.

अनपायिभिरस्माभिर्गुप्तायाश्च गृहे प्रभो ।
कन्याया दूषणं पुम्भिर्दुष्प्रेक्षाया न विद्महे ॥10.62.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: अनपायिभिः by those who do not leave their post, अस्माभिः by us, गुप्तायाः of her who is guarded, च and, गृहे inside the palace, प्रभो O master, कन्यायाः of the daughter, दूषणम् defilement or dishonor, पुम्भिः by men, दुष्प्रेक्षायाः of her who is not visible to men, न not, विद्महे we understand

Anvaya: प्रभो! अनपायिभिः अस्माभिः गुप्तायाः गृहे दुष्प्रेक्षायाः कन्यायाः पुम्भिः दूषणं न विद्महे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: प्रभो O master, अनपायिभिः अस्माभिः गुप्तायाः guarded by us who never abandon our post, गृहे दुष्प्रेक्षायाः कन्यायाः of the daughter within the house who is not visible to outside men, पुम्भिः दूषणं न विद्महे we do not understand how this defilement by men has occurred.

Simple Meaning: O Lord, we constantly guard her palace without ever leaving our posts, and she is completely hidden away from the sight of any outside men. Therefore, we are entirely at a loss to understand how your daughter could have been defiled by a man.

ततः प्रव्यथितो बाणो दुहितुः श्रुतदूषणः ।
त्वरितः कन्यकागारं प्राप्तोऽद्राक्षीद् यदूद्वहम् ॥10.62.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, प्रव्यथितः deeply agitated or distressed, बाणः Bana, दुहितुः of his daughter, श्रुत-दूषणः having heard of the dishonor, त्वरितः rapidly or in haste, कन्यका-आगारम् to the residence of the unmarried daughter, प्राप्तः arrived, अद्राक्षीत् he saw, यदु-उद्वहम् the prominent hero of the Yadu dynasty - Aniruddha

Anvaya: ततः दुहितुः श्रुतदूषणः प्रव्यथितः बाणः त्वरितः कन्यकागारं प्राप्तः यदूद्वहम् अद्राक्षीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः दुहितुः श्रुतदूषणः प्रव्यथितः बाणः Then Bana having heard of the dishonor of his daughter became deeply distressed, त्वरितः कन्यकागारं प्राप्तः and rushing in haste to the maiden residential quarters, यदूद्वहम् अद्राक्षीत् saw the prominent hero of the Yadu dynasty.

Simple Meaning: Upon hearing about the dishonor of his daughter, Bana became deeply agitated and distressed. He rushed in great haste to her private palace quarters and there he saw the eminent hero of the Yadu dynasty, Aniruddha.

कामात्मजं तं भुवनैकसुन्दरं श्यामं पिशङ्गाम्बरमम्बुजेक्षणम् ।
बृहद्भुजं कुण्डलकुन्तलत्विषा स्मितावलोकेन च मण्डिताननम् ॥10.62.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: काम-आत्मजम् the son of Pradyumna (who was the incarnation of Kamadeva ) - Aniruddha, तम् him, भुवन-एक-सुन्दरम् the most beautiful person in the entire world, श्यामम् dark complexioned, पिशङ्ग-अम्बरम् wearing yellow garments, अम्बुज-ईक्षणम् lotus-eyed, बृहद्-भुजम् possessing long powerful arms, कुण्डल-कुन्तल-त्विषा with the brilliance of his earrings and curling locks of hair, स्मित-अवलोकेन with a smiling glance, च and, मण्डित-आननम् whose face was beautifully adorned

Anvaya: बाणः कामात्मजं भुवनैकसुन्दरं श्यामं पिशङ्गाम्बरम् अम्बुजेक्षणं बृहद्भुजं कुण्डलकुन्तलत्विषा स्मितावलोकेन च मण्डिताननं तम् (अद्राक्षीत्)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: बाणः कामात्मजं भुवनैकसुन्दरं Bana saw the son of Kamadeva Pradyumna who was the most beautiful person in the entire world, श्यामं पिशङ्गाम्बरम् अम्बुजेक्षणं dark complexioned wearing yellow silk garments and having lotus eyes, बृहद्भुजं कुण्डलकुन्तलत्विषा possessing long arms and with the luster of his earrings and curly hair, स्मितावलोकेन च मण्डिताननं तम् (अद्राक्षीत्) and whose face was beautifully adorned with a gentle smiling glance.

Simple Meaning: Bana saw Aniruddha, the son of Pradyumna. He was incomparably beautiful, dark-complexioned, and clad in vibrant yellow garments. With lotus-like eyes and long, powerful arms, his handsome face was brilliantly adorned by glowing earrings, fine curling hair, and an enchanting, gentle smile.

दीव्यन्तमक्षैः प्रिययाभिनृम्णया तदङ्गसङ्गस्तनकुङ्कुमस्रजम् ।
बाह्वोर्दधानं मधुमल्लिकाश्रितां तस्याग्र आसीनमवेक्ष्य विस्मितः ॥10.62.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: दीव्यन्तम् playing, अक्षैः with dice, प्रियया with his beloved Usha, अभिनृम्णया who was extremely charming, तत्-अङ्ग-सङ्ग-स्तन-कुङ्कुम-स्रजम् carrying the garland smeared with saffron from her breasts due to contact with her body, बाह्वोः on both his arms, दधानम् wearing, मधु-मल्लिका-श्रिताम् frequented by bees attracted to the jasmine flowers, तस्याः of her, अग्रे in front, आसीनम् seated, अवेक्ष्य seeing, विस्मितः astonished

Anvaya: अभिनृम्णया प्रियया अक्षैः दीव्यन्तं, मधुमल्लिकाश्रितां तदङ्गसङ्गस्तनकुङ्कुमस्रजं बाह्वोः दधानं, तस्याः अग्रे आसीनं, अवेक्ष्य विस्मितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अभिनृम्णया प्रियया अक्षैः दीव्यन्तं Playing dice with his extremely charming beloved Usha, मधुमल्लिकाश्रितां तदङ्गसङ्गस्तनकुङ्कुमस्रजं बाह्वोः दधानं wearing on his arms a jasmine garland smeared with saffron from her body through close contact and which was surrounded by bees attracted to the fresh jasmine blossoms, तस्याः अग्रे आसीनम् sitting right in front of her, अवेक्ष्य विस्मितः seeing him, Bana was utterly astonished.

Simple Meaning: Bana was utterly astonished to see Aniruddha sitting right in front of his extremely charming daughter Usha, playing a game of dice with her. Aniruddha was wearing a garland of jasmine flowers on his arms that was covered in red saffron from contact with her body, and bees were swarming around the fragrant blossoms.

स तं प्रविष्टं वृतमाततायिभिर्भटैरनीकैरवलोक्य माधवः ।
उद्यम्य मौर्वं परिघं व्यवस्थितो यथान्तको दण्डधरो जिघांसया ॥10.62.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: सः he (Aniruddha), तम् him (Bana), प्रविष्टम् entered, वृत्तम् surrounded, आततायिभिः with weapon-wielding attackers, भटैः by guards, अनीकैः with armed troops, अवलोक्य seeing, माधवः the descendant of Madhu (Aniruddha), उद्यम्य raising up, मौर्वम् made of iron, परिघम् club, व्यवस्थितः stood ready, यथा like, अन्तकः the god of death Yama, दण्ड-धरः holding his rod of punishment, जिघांसया with the desire to kill

Anvaya: सः माधवः आततायिभिः भटैः अनीकैः वृतं प्रविष्टं तं अवलोक्य, दण्डधरः अन्तकः जिघांसया यथा, मौर्वं परिघम् उद्यम्य व्यवस्थितः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः माधवः That descendant of Madhu Aniruddha, आततायिभिः भटैः अनीकैः वृतं प्रविष्टं तं अवलोक्य seeing Bana enter surrounded by weapon-wielding guards and troops, दण्डधरः अन्तकः जिघांसया यथा just like Yama holding his club with the intent to kill, मौर्वं परिघम् उद्यम्य व्यवस्थितः stood ready raising up his heavy iron club.

Simple Meaning: Seeing Bana enter surrounded by a large contingent of armed guards and attacking soldiers, Aniruddha stood his ground. He raised his formidable iron club and prepared to fight, looking just like Yama, the god of death, holding his staff of punishment ready to destroy his foes.

जिघृक्षया तान् परितः प्रसर्पतः शुनो यथा सूकरयूथपोऽहनत् ।
ते हन्यमाना भवनाद् विनिर्गता निर्भिन्नमूर्धोरुभुजाः प्रदुद्रुवुः ॥10.62.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: जिघृक्षया with the desire to capture him, तान् them, परितः all around, प्रसर्पतः advancing or closing in, शुनः dogs, यथा as, सूकर-यूथपः the leader of a boar herd, अहनत् he struck down, ते they, हन्यमानाः being struck, भवनात् from the palace, विनिर्गताः escaping, निर्भिन्न-मूर्ध-ऊरु-भुजाः with broken heads thighs and arms, प्रदुद्रुवुः fled in panic

Anvaya: जिघृक्षया परितः प्रसर्पतः तान् सूकरयूथपः शुनः यथा अहनत्। हन्यमानाः ते निर्भिन्नमूर्धोरुभुजाः भवनात् विनिर्गताः प्रदुद्रुवुः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: जिघृक्षया परितः प्रसर्पतः तान् As they advanced on all sides with the desire to capture him, सूकरयूथपः शुनः यथा अहनत् he struck them down just as the leader of a boar herd strikes down dogs. हन्यमानाः ते निर्भिन्नमूर्धोरुभुजाः Being beaten severely with their heads thighs and arms shattered, भवनात् विनिर्गताः प्रदुद्रुवुः they escaped from the palace and fled in panic.

Simple Meaning: As the guards closed in from all sides attempting to capture him, Aniruddha struck them down forcefully, just like the leader of a boar pack attacking a pack of dogs. Beaten black and blue with their heads, thighs, and arms broken, the guards ran out of the palace and fled in panic.

तं नागपाशैर्बलिनन्दनो बली घ्नन्तं स्वसैन्यं कुपितो बबन्ध ह ।
ऊषा भृशं शोकविषादविह्वला बद्धं निशम्याश्रुकलाक्ष्यरौदिषीत् ॥10.62.35॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him Aniruddha, नाग-पाशैः with serpent ropes, बलि-नन्दनः the son of Bali - Bana, बली powerful, घ्नन्तम् who was striking down, स्व-सैन्यम् his own army, कुपितः enraged, बबन्ध bound, ह indeed, ऊषा Usha, भृशम् greatly, शोक-विषाद-विह्वला overwhelmed with grief and sorrow, बद्धम् bound, निशम्य seeing, अश्रु-कला-अक्षी with eyes full of tears, अरौदिषीत् wept

Anvaya: स्वसैन्यं घ्नन्तं तं बली बलिनन्दनः कुपितः नागपाशैः बबन्ध ह, बद्धं निशम्य ऊषा भृशं शोकविषादविह्वला अश्रुकलाक्षी अरौदिषीत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: स्वसैन्यं घ्नन्तं तं बली बलिनन्दनः कुपितः As he was striking down his army the powerful son of Bali - Bana became enraged, नागपाशैः बबन्ध ह and bound him tightly with serpent ropes. बद्धं निशम्य ऊषा भृशं शोकविषादविह्वला Seeing him bound Usha became overwhelmed with grief and sorrow, अश्रुकलाक्षी अरौदिषीत् and wept with her eyes brimming with tears.

Simple Meaning: As Aniruddha continued to slaughter his forces, the powerful Bana, son of Bali, became furious and bound him tightly with magical snake weapons. Seeing her beloved bound and helpless, Usha was utterly overwhelmed with sorrow and grief, weeping bitterly as tears filled her eyes.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे  “उषा-अनिरुद्धमिलनः नाम​​” द्विषष्टितमः अध्यायः ॥62॥

Thus ends the sixty-second discourse named “Meeting of Usha and Aniruddha”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.

॥ सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥
