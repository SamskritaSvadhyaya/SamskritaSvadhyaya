Canto 10

Chapter 78

Deliverance of Dantavaktra and Viduratha

Romaharshana killed by Balarama

श्रीमद्भागवतमहापुराणम्

दशमः स्कन्धः

दन्तवक्रविदूरथोद्धारः बलरामेण रोमहर्षणवधः च नाम​ अष्टसप्ततितमः अध्यायः

(40 Shlokas)

श्री शुक उवाच
शिशुपालस्य शाल्वस्य पौण्ड्रकस्यापि दुर्मतिः ।
परलोकगतानां च कुर्वन् पारोक्ष्यसौहृदम् ॥10.78.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said, शिशुपालस्य of Shishupala, शाल्वस्य of Shalva, पौण्ड्रकस्य of Paundraka, अपि also, दुर्मतिः the wicked-minded (Dantavakra), परलोक-गतानाम् who had departed to the next world, च and, कुर्वन् displaying/demonstrating, पारोक्ष्य-सौहृदम् friendship for those who are absent (deceased)

Anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच— दुर्मतिः (दन्तवक्त्रः) परलोकगतानां शिशुपालस्य शाल्वस्य पौण्ड्रकस्य च अपि पारोक्ष्यसौहृदं कुर्वन्...

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री शुकः उवाच Shri Shuka said: दुर्मतिः the evil-minded Dantavakra, परलोकगतानां who had gone to the other world, शिशुपालस्य of Shishupala, शाल्वस्य of Shalva, पौण्ड्रकस्य च अपि and also of Paundraka, पारोक्ष्यसौहृदं fidelity and affection toward absent friends, कुर्वन् demonstrating...

Simple Meaning: Shri Shuka said: The foolish and wicked-minded Dantavakra came forward to demonstrate his loyalty and friendship toward his deceased allies—Shishupala, Shalva, and Paundraka—who had all been dispatched to the next world by Krishna.

एकः पदाति संक्रुद्धो गदापाणिः प्रकम्पयन् ।
पद्भ्यामिमां महाराज महासत्त्वो व्यदृश्यत ॥10.78.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: एकः alone, पदातिः on foot, संक्रुद्धः extremely enraged, गदा-पाणिः mace in hand, प्रकम्पयन् causing to tremble, पद्भ्याम् with his feet, इमाम this (earth), महाराज O Great King (Parikshit), महा-सत्त्वः possessing immense physical strength, व्यदृश्यत appeared

Anvaya: महाराज! महासत्त्वः संक्रुद्धः गदापाणिः एकः पदातिः पद्भ्याम् इमां प्रकम्पयन् व्यदृश्यत।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: महाराज O Great King! महासत्त्वः possessing formidable prowess, संक्रुद्धः burning with wrath, गदापाणिः clutching a mace in his hand, एकः alone, पदातिः marching on foot, पद्भ्याम् with the force of his footfalls, इमां this earth, प्रकम्पयन् making shake, व्यदृश्यत appeared on the battleground.

Simple Meaning: O Great King Parikshit! Possessing immense bodily power and burning with rage, Dantavakra alone appeared on foot, mace in hand, causing the earth itself to tremble beneath his heavy strides as he charged.

तं तथाऽऽयान्तमालोक्य गदामादाय सत्वरः ।
अवप्लुत्य रथात् कृष्णः सिन्धुं वेलेव प्रत्यधात् ॥10.78.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् him (Dantavakra), तथा in that manner, आयान्तम् approaching, आलोक्य seeing, गदाम् His mace, आदाय taking, सत्वरः quickly, अवप्लुत्य leaping down, रथात् from His chariot, कृष्णः Krishna, सिन्धुम् the ocean, वेला the shore/coastal barrier, इव like, प्रत्यधात् blocked/confronted

Anvaya: तथा आयान्तं तम् आलोक्य, कृष्णः सत्वरः गदाम् आदाय रथात् अवप्लुत्य, वेला सिन्धुम् इव (तं) प्रत्यधात्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तथा आयान्तं Approaching in that terrifying manner, तम् him (Dantavakra), आलोक्य observing, कृष्णः Krishna, सत्वरः swiftly, गदाम् His mace (Kaumodaki), आदाय taking hold of, रथात् from His chariot, अवप्लुत्य leaping down, वेला a shore, सिन्धुम् इव like blocking the ocean, (तं) him, प्रत्यधात् stood before Him to halt his advance.

Simple Meaning: Seeing Dantavakra charge in such a furious manner, Krishna quickly grabbed His mace and leaped down from His chariot. He stood firm to block his assault, just as a shore restrains the surging waves of the ocean.

गदामुद्यम्य कारूषो मुकुन्दं प्राह दुर्मदः ।
दिष्ट्या दिष्ट्या भवानद्य मम दृष्टि पथं गतः ॥10.78.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: गदाम् mace, उद्यम्य raising high, कारूषः Dantavakra (the king of Karusha), मुकुन्दम् to Mukunda (Lord Krishna), प्राह spoke, दुर्मदः arrogant, दिष्ट्या by good fortune, दिष्ट्या by supreme luck, भवान् You, अद्य today, मम my, दृष्टि-पथम् path of vision, गतः has come

Anvaya: गदाम् उद्यम्य दुर्मदः कारूषः मुकुन्दं प्राह— दिष्ट्या दिष्ट्या भवान् अद्य मम दृष्टिपथं गतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गदाम् His mace, उद्यम्य raising aloft, दुर्मदः arrogant, कारूषः the ruler of Karusha (Dantavakra), मुकुन्दं to Mukunda, प्राह spoke; दिष्ट्या दिष्ट्या by absolute stroke of luck, भवान् You, अद्य today, मम my, दृष्टिपथं path of vision, गतः have entered.

Simple Meaning: Raising his heavy mace, the arrogant Dantavakra, king of Karusha, insolently addressed Mukunda: "By sheer luck! By great good fortune, You have finally crossed my path today!"

त्वं मातुलेयो नः कृष्ण मित्रध्रुङ्मां जिघांससि ।
अतस्त्वां गदया मन्द हनिष्ये वज्रकल्पया ॥10.78.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: त्वम् You, मातुलेयः mother's brother's son (maternal cousin), नः our, कृष्ण O Krishna, मित्र-ध्रुक् traitor to friends, माम् me, जिघांससि You wish to kill, अतः therefore, त्वाम् You, गदया with my mace, मन्द O foolish one, हनिष्ये I shall strike down, वज्र-कल्पया which is hard as a thunderbolt

Anvaya: कृष्ण! त्वं नः मातुलेयः (अपि) मित्रध्रुक् मां जिघांससि, अतः हे मन्द! वज्रकल्पया गदया त्वां हनिष्ये।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: कृष्ण O Krishna! त्वं You, नः our, मातुलेयः maternal cousin, (अपि) even though, मित्रध्रुक् a betrayer of friends, मां me, जिघांससि seek to destroy; अतः therefore, मन्द O fool, वज्रकल्पया as devastating as a thunderbolt, गदया with my mace, त्वां You, हनिष्ये I will slay.

Simple Meaning: Dantavakra continued: "O Krishna, although You are our cousin, You are a treacherous betrayer of Your own friends who now wants to kill me too. Therefore, You fool, I will slay You today with my mace, which strikes as hard as a thunderbolt!"

Note: Shrutadeva (mother of Dantavakra), and Shrutasrava (mother of Shishupala) were Vasudeva’s sisters. Therefore Dantavakra and Shishupala were material cousins to Krishna.

तर्ह्यानृण्यमुपैम्यज्ञ मित्राणां मित्रवत्सलः ।
बन्धुरूपमरिं हत्वा व्याधिं देहचरं यथा ॥10.78.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: तर्हि then/therefore, आनृण्यम् freedom from debt, उपैमि I shall attain, अज्ञ O ignorant one, मित्राणाम् of my friends, मित्र-वत्सलः devoted to my friends, बन्धु-रूपम् disguising as a relative, अरिम् enemy, हत्वा by killing, व्याधिम् a disease, देह-चरम् dwelling inside the body, यथा just as

Anvaya: अज्ञ! मित्रवत्सलः (अहं) देहचरं व्याधिं यथा बन्धुरूपम् अरिं (त्वां) हत्वा मित्राणाम् आनृण्यम् उपैमि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अज्ञ O foolish one! मित्रवत्सलः being loyal to my allies, (अहं) I, देहचरं dwelling within the physical frame, व्याधिं a chronic disease, यथा just like, बन्धुरूपम् masquerading in the guise of a relative, अरिं an enemy, (त्वां) You, हत्वा by destroying, मित्राणाम् of my fallen friends, आनृण्यम् freedom from moral obligation/debt, उपैमि shall achieve.

Simple Meaning: "O foolish one! Devoted as I am to my friends, I shall discharge my debt to them by destroying You—an enemy hiding under the guise of a relative—just as one eradicates a deadly disease that infects the body from within!"

एवं रूक्षैस्तुदन् वाक्यैः कृष्णं तोत्त्रैरिव द्विपम् ।
गदयाताडयन्मूर्ध्नि सिंहवद् व्यनदच्च सः ॥10.78.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this way, रूक्षैः with harsh, तुदन् piercing, वाक्यैः with words, कृष्णम् Krishna, तोत्त्रैः with sharp goads, इव like, द्विपम् an elephant, गदया with his mace, अताडयत् struck, मूर्ध्नि on the head, सिंह-वत् like a lion, व्यनदत् roared, च and, सः he (Dantavakra)

Anvaya: सः तोत्त्रैः द्विपम् इव रूक्षैः वाक्यैः कृष्णं तुदन्, गदया मूर्ध्नि अताडयत्, सिंहवत् व्यनदत् च।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सः He (Dantavakra), तोत्त्रैः with sharp iron goads, द्विपम् इव like an elephant, रूक्षैः abrasive, वाक्यैः with words, कृष्णं Krishna, तुदन् piercing/taunting, गदया with his heavy mace, मूर्ध्नि on the head, अताडयत् struck, सिंहवत् like a lion, व्यनदत् च and roared loudly.

Simple Meaning: Piercing Krishna with harsh words just as an elephant driver goads a wild elephant, Dantavakra struck Him on the head with his heavy mace and let out a ferocious, lion-like roar.

गदयाभिहतोऽप्याजौ न चचाल यदूद्वहः ।
कृष्णोऽपि तमहन् गुर्व्या कौमोदक्या स्तनान्तरे ॥10.78.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: गदया by the mace, अभिहतः struck, अपि even though, आजौ in the battlefield, न not, चचाल moved/flinched, यदूद्वहः the chief of the Yadus (Lord Krishna), कृष्णः Krishna, अपि in return, तम् him (Dantavakra), अहन् struck, गुर्व्या with His heavy, कौमोदक्या mace named Kaumodaki, स्तन-अन्तरे in the center of the chest

Anvaya: आजौ गदया अभिहतः अपि यदूद्वहः न चचाल। कृष्णः अपि गुर्व्या कौमोदक्या तं स्तनान्तरे अहन्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: आजौ In the arena of battle, गदया अभिहतः अपि by the blow of the mace, even though struck violently, यदूद्वहः न चचाल the eminent leader of the Yadu dynasty did not budge an inch; कृष्णः अपि Krishna, also, गुर्व्या कौमोदक्या with His massive mace Kaumodaki, तं him (Dantavakra), स्तनान्तरे right in the middle of the chest, अहन् struck forcefully.

Simple Meaning: Even when struck full force on the head by Dantavakra's mace, the noble chief of the Yadus did not flinch or yield an inch on the battlefield. In return, Krishna struck Dantavakra squarely in the center of the chest with His massive, formidable mace Kaumodaki.

गदानिर्भिन्नहृदय उद्वमन् रुधिरं मुखात् ।
प्रसार्य केशबाह्वङ्घ्रीन् धरण्यां न्यपतद् व्यसुः ॥10.78.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: गदा-निर्भिन्न-हृदयः whose chest/heart was shattered by the mace, उद्वमन् vomiting, रुधिरम् blood, मुखात् from his mouth, प्रसार्य sprawling/stretching out, केश-बाहु-अङ्घ्रीन् hair, arms, and legs, धरण्याम् upon the earth, न्यपतत् fell down, व्यसुः lifeless

Anvaya: गदानिर्भिन्नहृदयः मुखात् रुधिरम् उद्वमन्, केशबाह्वङ्घ्रीन् प्रसार्य व्यसुः (सन्) धरण्यां न्यपतत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: गदानिर्भिन्नहृदयः With his heart shattered by the impact of Kaumodaki, मुखात् from his mouth, रुधिरम् crimson blood, उद्वमन् gushing forth, केशबाह्वङ्घ्रीन् his disheveled hair, arms, and legs, प्रसार्य wildly sprawling, व्यसुः (सन्) becoming devoid of life, धरण्यां onto the ground, न्यपतत् collapsed.

Simple Meaning: His heart shattered by the crushing blow of Krishna's mace Kaumodaki, Dantavakra vomited blood from his mouth and collapsed lifeless onto the earth, his hair, arms, and legs sprawling in all directions.

ततः सूक्ष्मतरं ज्योतिः कृष्णमाविशदद्भुतम् ।
पश्यतां सर्वभूतानां यथा चैद्यवधे नृप ॥10.78.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, सूक्ष्मतरम् exceedingly subtle, ज्योतिः light/effulgence, कृष्णम् Lord Krishna, आविशत् entered, अद्भुतम् wonderful, पश्यताम् as they watched, सर्व-भूतानाम् all living entities, यथा just as, चैद्य-वधे at the time of the killing of Shishupala (the King of Chedi), नृप O King (Parikshit)

Anvaya: नृप! ततः सर्वभूतानां पश्यतां यथा चैद्यवधे (तथा) अद्भुतं सूक्ष्मतरं ज्योतिः कृष्णम् आविशत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: नृप O King Parikshit! ततः then, सर्वभूतानां of all gathered beings, पश्यतां while looking on, यथा as occurred, चैद्यवधे during the slaying of Shishupala, (तथा) so too, अद्भुतं wondrous, सूक्ष्मतरं extremely subtle, ज्योतिः effulgent spark of light, कृष्णम् into Krishna, आविशत् merged.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit! Then, before the eyes of all living beings present, an exceedingly subtle and wondrous light emerged from Dantavakra's body and merged directly into Krishna, just as it had occurred during the slaying of Shishupala.

Note: Jaya and Vijaya, the gatekeepers of Vaikuntha (the abode of Lord Vishnu), took three births on Earth as adversaries of Vishnu due to a curse by the Four Kumaras - Sanaka, Sanandana, Sanatana and Sanat Kumara.

When the Kumaras cursed them for blocking their entry, Lord Vishnu offered them a choice: seven lifetimes as devout worshippers or three lifetimes as His fierce enemies. Preferring a quicker return to their Lord, they chose the three births as adversaries

विदूरथस्तु तद्भ्राता भ्रातृशोकपरिप्लुतः ।
आगच्छदसिचर्मभ्यामुच्छ्वसस्तज्जिघांसया ॥10.78.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: विदूरथः Viduratha, तु but, तत्-भ्राता his (Dantavakra's) brother, भ्रातृ-शोक-परिप्लुतः overwhelmed with grief for his brother, आगच्छत् arrived/charged forward, असि-चर्मभ्याम् equipped with sword and shield, उच्छ्वसन् heavy-breathing/panting with rage, तत्-जिघांसया with the desire to kill Him (Krishna)

Anvaya: तु तद्भ्राता विदूरथः भ्रातृशोकपरिप्लुतः असिचर्मभ्याम् उच्छ्वसन् तत्जिघांसया आगच्छत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: तु But, तद्भ्राता his brother, विदूरथः Viduratha, भ्रातृशोकपरिप्लुतः drowned in intense sorrow over his brother's death, असिचर्मभ्याम् armed with a sword and target shield, उच्छ्वसन् breathing heavily in wild anger, तत्जिघांसया out of an eager desire to destroy Him (Krishna), आगच्छत् rushed to the battleground.

Simple Meaning: However, Dantavakra's brother Viduratha, overcome with immense grief and rage over his brother's death, came charging forward. Armed with a sword and shield and breathing heavily in anger, he sought to kill Krishna.

तस्य चापततः कृष्णश्चक्रेण क्षुरनेमिना ।
शिरो जहार राजेन्द्र सकिरीटं सकुण्डलम् ॥10.78.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्य of him (Viduratha), च and, आपततः as he was charging forward, कृष्णः Krishna, चक्रेण with His discus, क्षुर-नेमिना having a razor-sharp rim, शिरः the head, जहार severed/cut off, राज-इन्द्र O Chief of Kings (Parikshit), स-किरीटम् together with the crown, स-कुण्डलम् together with the earrings

Anvaya: राजेन्द्र! आपततः तस्य सकिरीटं सकुण्डलं च शिरः क्षुरनेमिना चक्रेण कृष्णः जहार।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: राजेन्द्र O Sovereign of Kings! आपततः as he rushed furiously, तस्य of him (Viduratha), सकिरीटं सकुण्डलं च शिरः the head, adorned with a royal helmet and glittering earrings, क्षुरनेमिना possessing an edge as sharp as a razor, चक्रेण with His Sudarshana discus, कृष्णः Lord Krishna, जहार sliced off.

Simple Meaning: O King Parikshit! As Viduratha rushed forward to attack, Krishna used His razor-sharp Sudarshana Chakra to instantly slice off his head, complete with its brilliant crown and dangling earrings.

एवं सौभं च शाल्वं च दन्तवक्त्रं सहानुजम् ।
हत्वा दुर्विषहानन्यैरीडितः सुरमानवैः ॥10.78.13॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् in this manner, सौभम् the Saubha airship, च and, शाल्वम् Shalva, च and, दन्तवक्त्रम् Dantavakra, स-अनुजम् along with his younger brother (Viduratha), हत्वा having slain, दुर्विषहान् invincible/unconquerable, अन्यैः by others, ईडितः praised/worshiped, सुर-मानवैः by devas and human beings

Simple Meaning: Having thus destroyed the Saubha airship and slain Shalva, Dantavakra, and his younger brother Viduratha—all of whom were invincible to any other warrior—Lord Krishna was magnificently praised and glorified by both the devas and humans alike.

एवं योगेश्वरः कृष्णो भगवाञ्जगदीश्वरः ।

Anvaya: एवं योगेश्वरः भगवान् जगदीश्वरः सः कृष्णः पशुदृष्टीनां ‘निर्जितः’, ‘जयति’ इति ईयते।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एवं In this way, योगेश्वरः the Lord of all mystic power, भगवान् the Supreme Lord possessing all opulences, जगदीश्वरः the Supreme Ruler of the cosmos, सः कृष्णः Krishna, पशुदृष्टीनां to those who possess the vision of beasts, 'निर्जितः' as having suffered defeat, 'जयति' as attaining victory, इति thus, ईयते is regarded.

Simple Meaning: In this manner, Lord Krishna—the Supreme Lord of yoga and Ruler of the universe—is seen by those with ignorant, animal-like vision as if He experiences defeat or achieves victory. (In truth, being the transcendental Lord, He is beyond all material duality.)

श्रुत्वा युद्धोद्यमं रामः कुरूणां सह पाण्डवैः ।

Anvaya: रामः कुरूणां पाण्डवैः सह युद्धोद्यमं श्रुत्वा, मध्यस्थः (सन्) तीर्थाभिषेकव्याजेन प्रययौ किल।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: रामः Balarama, कुरूणां of the Kauravas, पाण्डवैः सह with the sons of Pandu, युद्धोद्यमं the active preparations for war, श्रुत्वा having heard, मध्यस्थः (सन्) remaining impartial, तीर्थाभिषेकव्याजेन on the pretext of taking sacred baths at holy pilgrimage sites, प्रययौ departed, किल indeed.

Simple Meaning: Upon hearing that the Kauravas and Pandavas were preparing for war, Balarama chose to remain neutral. Under the guise of taking sacred baths at holy pilgrimage sites, He departed on a pilgrimage tour.

एतदर्थो हि लोकेऽस्मिन्नवतारो मया कृतः ।
वध्या मे धर्मध्वजिनस्ते हि पातकिनोऽधिकाः ॥10.78.27॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतत् अर्थः for this purpose, हि indeed, लोके in this world, अस्मिन् in this, अवतारः incarnation, मया by Me, कृतः made, वध्याः punishable by death, मे by Me, धर्म-ध्वजिनः hypocrites wearing the flag of dharma, ते they, हि because, पातकिनः sinners, अधिकाः worst

Anvaya: अस्मिन् लोके मया अवतारः एतदर्थः हि कृतः। धर्मध्वजिनः मे वध्याः। ते अधिकाः पातकिनः हि।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: अस्मिन् लोके मया अवतारः एतदर्थः हि कृतः My incarnation in this world has been taken for this very purpose, धर्मध्वजिनः मे वध्याः Hypocrites wearing the banner of dharma are to be killed by Me. ते अधिकाः पातकिनः हि Because they are the worst kind of sinners.

Simple Meaning: I have incarnated in this world for this exact purpose to punish those who hypocritically parade as religious leaders. Because such deceitful people are the worst sinners of all.

एतावदुक्त्वा भगवान् निवृत्तोऽसद्वधादपि ।
भावित्वात्तं कुशाग्रेण करस्थेनाहनत् प्रभुः ॥10.78.28॥

Word-by-word meaning: एतावत् this much, उक्त्वा having spoken, भगवान् the Supreme Lord (Balarama), निवृत्तः refrained, असत्-वधात् from killing the unrighteous, अपि although, भावित्वात् due to inevitability, तम् him (Romaharshana), कुश-अग्रेण with the tip of a blade of kusha grass, कर-स्थेन held in His hand, आहनत् struck, प्रभुः the almighty Lord

Anvaya: एतावत् उक्त्वा असद्वधात् निवृत्तः अपि भगवान् प्रभुः भावित्वात् करस्थेन कुशाग्रेण तम् आहनत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एतावत् उक्त्वा Having spoken this much, असद्वधात् निवृत्तः अपि although usually abstaining from killing the unworthy, भगवान् प्रभुः the supreme almighty Lord Balarama, भावित्वात् due to destiny, करस्थेन कुशाग्रेण with the tip of a kusha grass held in His hand, तम् आहनत् struck him.

Simple Meaning: Having said this, even though Lord Balarama had refrained from killing unrighteous people, the almighty Lord struck Romaharshana with the tip of a blade of kusha grass held in His hand due to inevitable destiny.

हाहेति वादिनः सर्वे मुनयः खिन्नमानसाः।
ऊचुः सङ्कर्षणं देवमधर्मस्ते कृतः प्रभो ॥10.78.29॥

Word-by-word meaning: हा हा इति Alas Alas, वादिनः exclaiming, सर्वे all, मुनयः sages, खिन्न-मानसाः with distressed minds, ऊचुः said, सङ्कर्षणम् to Sankarshana, देवम् to the deva, अधर्मः unrighteous action, ते by You, कृतः committed, प्रभो O Lord

Anvaya: सर्वे मुनयः खिन्नमानसाः “हा हा” इति वादिनः देवं सङ्कर्षणम् ऊचुः – प्रभो ते अधर्मः कृतः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: सर्वे मुनयः खिन्नमानसाः All the sages with distressed minds, हा हा इति वादिनः exclaiming Alas Alas, देवं सङ्कर्षणम् ऊचुः said to the deva Sankarshana, प्रभो! ते अधर्मः कृतः O Lord! an unrighteous act has been committed by You.

Simple Meaning: All the sages present became deeply distressed in their minds and exclaimed Alas Alas They addressed the deva Sankarshana saying, O Lord! You have committed an unrighteous act.

अस्य ब्रह्मासनं दत्तमस्माभिर्यदुनन्दन ।
आयुश्चात्माक्लमं तावद् यावत् सत्रं समाप्यते ॥10.78.30॥

Word-by-word meaning: अस्य to him (Romaharshana), ब्रह्म-आसनम् the seat of the speaker of scriptures, दत्तम् given, अस्माभिः by us, यदु-नन्दन O delight of the Yadus (Balarama), आयुः long life, च and, आत्म-अक्लमम् freedom from physical distress, तावत् for so long, यावत् as long as, सत्रम् the sacrifice, समाप्यते is completed

Anvaya: यदुनन्दन! अस्माभिः अस्य ब्रह्मासनं, यावत् सत्रं समाप्यते तावत् आयुः आत्माक्लमं च दत्तम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: यदुनन्दन O delight of the Yadus, अस्माभिः अस्य ब्रह्मासनं by us the master seat of sacred discourses, यावत् सत्रं समाप्यते तावत् as long as this sacrifice is being completed for that long, आयुः आत्माक्लमं च दत्तम् long life and freedom from bodily fatigue were also granted.

Simple Meaning: The sages said, O delight of the Yadus! we had granted him the master seat of scriptures as well as long life and freedom from physical distress for as long as this sacrificial session continues.

अजानतैवाचरितस्त्वया ब्रह्मवधो यथा ।
योगेश्वरस्य भवतो नाम्नायोऽपि नियामकः ॥10.78.31॥

Word-by-word meaning: अजानता unknowingly, एव indeed, आचरितः committed, त्वया by You, ब्रह्म-वधः killing of a brahmana, यथा just like, योगेश्वरस्य of the Master of Yoga, भवतः You, न not, आम्नायः scriptural injunction, अपि even, नियामकः controlling ruler

Anvaya: योगेश्वरस्य भवतः त्वया अजानता एव ब्रह्मवधः आचरितः यथा आम्नायः अपि भवतः न नियामकः।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: योगेश्वरस्य भवतः Of You the Master of Yoga, त्वया अजानता एव ब्रह्मवधः आचरितः by You unknowingly indeed the killing of a brahmana was committed, यथा आम्नायः अपि भवतः न नियामकः although even scriptural injunctions are not controlling regulators for You.

Simple Meaning: O Master of Yoga even though You are beyond all scriptural rules and restrictions You have unknowingly committed the sin of killing a brahmana.

यद्येतद् ब्रह्महत्यायाः पावनं लोकपावन ।
चरिष्यति भवाँल्लोकसङ्ग्रहोऽनन्यचोदितः ॥10.78.32॥

Word-by-word meaning: यदि if, एतत् this, ब्रह्म-हत्यायाः for the sin of killing a brahmana, पावनम् purification, लोक-पावन O purifier of the entire world, चरिष्यति will perform, भवान् You, लोक-सङ्ग्रहः setting an example for the world, अनन्य-चोदितः uninfluenced by anyone else

Anvaya: लोकपावन! अनन्यचोदितः भवान् यदि एतत् ब्रह्महत्यायाः पावनं चरिष्यति, लोकसङ्ग्रहः (भविष्यति)।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: लोकपावन O purifier of the world, अनन्यचोदितः भवान् Being uninfluenced by any other authority You, यदि एतत् ब्रह्महत्यायाः पावनं चरिष्यति if You will perform this penance of atonement for killing a brahmana, लोकसङ्ग्रहः (भविष्यति) it will be an example for the society.

Simple Meaning: O purifier of the universe! If, without being prompted by anyone, You perform the penance for this sin of killing a brahmana, then it will be an example for the society.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
करिष्ये वधनिर्वेशं लोकानुग्रहकाम्यया ।
नियमः प्रथमे कल्पे यावान् स तु विधीयताम् ॥10.78.33॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, करिष्ये I shall perform, वध-निर्वेशम् atonement for the killing, लोक-अनुग्रह-काम्यया out of desire to bestow grace upon the world, नियमः rule, प्रथमे in the first, कल्पे prescription, यावान् whatever, सः that, तु indeed, विधीयताम् let it be prescribed

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच – लोकानुग्रहकाम्यया वधनिर्वेशं करिष्ये। प्रथमे कल्पे यावान् नियमः सः तु विधीयताम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, लोकानुग्रहकाम्यया Out of a desire to show mercy to the world, वधनिर्वेशं करिष्ये I shall perform atonement for this killing. प्रथमे कल्पे यावान् नियमः Whatever rule is prescribed as the primary option in scriptural law, सः तु विधीयताम् let that indeed be prescribed by you all.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord said: Out of a desire to bestow grace upon the world and set an ideal example I shall perform atonement for this killing Please prescribe for Me whatever primary vow or discipline is specified in scriptural law for such an act.

दीर्घमायुर्बतैतस्य सत्त्वमिन्द्रियमेव च ।
आशासितं यत्तद्ब्रूत साधये योगमायया ॥10.78.34॥

Word-by-word meaning: दीर्घम् long, आयुः lifespan, बत certainly, एतस्य of his, सत्त्वम् strength, इन्द्रियम् sensory power, एव indeed, च and, आशासितम् desired for, यत् which, तत् that, ब्रूत please tell, साधये I shall accomplish, योग-मायया by Yoga-maya

Anvaya: एतस्य दीर्घम् आयुः सत्त्वम् इन्द्रियम् एव च यत् आशासितं तत् ब्रूत बत योगमायया तत् साधये।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: एतस्य दीर्घम् आयुः सत्त्वम् इन्द्रियम् एव च यत् आशासितं His long life, strength and sensory vigor, whatever is desired for him, तत् ब्रूत please tell that, बत certainly, योगमायया तत् साधये I shall fulfill that using Yoga-maya.

Simple Meaning: If long life, strength and sensory power, whatever you desire for him, please tell me. I shall surely fulfill through My Yog-maya.

ऋषयः ऊचुः
अस्त्रस्य तव वीर्यस्य मृत्योरस्माकमेव च ।
यथा भवेद् वचः सत्यं तथा राम विधीयताम् ॥10.78.35॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऋषयः ऊचुः the sages said, अस्त्रस्य of the weapon (the kusha grass), तव Your, वीर्यस्य of the prowess, मृत्योः of death, अस्माकम् of us, एव indeed, च and, यथा so that, भवेत् may become, वचः word, सत्यम् true, तथा in that manner, राम O Balarama, विधीयताम् let it be arranged

Anvaya: ऋषयः ऊचुः – राम! तव अस्त्रस्य वीर्यस्य, मृत्योः, अस्माकम् एव च वचः यथा सत्यं भवेत्, तथा विधीयताम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ऋषयः ऊचुः The sages said, राम O Balarama, तव अस्त्रस्य वीर्यस्य of the power of Your weapon, मृत्योः of the inevitability of death, अस्माकम् एव च वचः and of our words, यथा सत्यं भवेत् so that all may remain true, तथा विधीयताम् please act in such a manner.

Simple Meaning: The sages said: O Lord Balarama, please arrange things in such a way that the power of Your weapon (the kusha grass used to slay Romaharshana), the certainty of death and the truth of our spoken words all remain intact and fulfilled.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
आत्मा वै पुत्र उत्पन्न इति वेदानुशासनम् ।
तस्मादस्य भवेद् वक्ता आयुरिन्द्रियसत्त्ववान् ॥10.78.36॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, आत्मा one's own self, वै indeed, पुत्रः as the son, उत्पन्नः is born, इति thus, वेद-अनुशासनम् the injunction of the Vedas, तस्मात् therefore, अस्य his, भवेत् shall be, वक्ता speaker, आयुः-इन्द्रिय-सत्त्व-वान् possessing his lifespan sensory power and strength

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच – आत्मा वै पुत्रः उत्पन्नः इति वेदानुशासनम्। तस्मात् अस्य पुत्रः आयुरिन्द्रियसत्त्ववान् वक्ता भवेत्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच The Supreme Lord said, आत्मा वै पुत्रः उत्पन्नः इति वेदानुशासनम् one is indeed born as one's own self according to the Vedic injunctions. तस्मात् अस्य पुत्रः आयुरिन्द्रियसत्त्ववान् वक्ता भवेत् Therefore his son shall become the speaker endowed with his lifespan sensory power and physical strength.

Simple Meaning: The Supreme Lord Balarama said: The Vedic scriptures declare that a person is reborn as his own son Therefore let his son become the speaker of the scriptures endowed with all the long life sensory vigor and strength that you granted to Romaharshana.

किं वः कामो मुनिश्रेष्ठा ब्रूताहं करवाण्यथ ।
अजानतस्त्वपचितिं यथा मे चिन्त्यतां बुधाः ॥10.78.37॥

Word-by-word meaning: किम् what, वः your, कामः desire, मुनि-श्रेष्ठः O best among sages, ब्रूत tell, अहम् I, करवाणि shall do, अथ furthermore, अजानतः of one acting unknowingly, तु indeed, अपचितिम् atonement, यथा how, मे for Me, चिन्त्यताम् let it be considered, बुधाः O wise ones

Anvaya: मुनिश्रेष्ठाः! वः कामः किं ब्रूत। अहम् अथ करवाणि। बुधाः! अजानतः मे अपचितिः यथा तु चिन्त्यताम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: मुनिश्रेष्ठाः O best among sages, वः कामः किं ब्रूत tell Me what your desire is, अहम् अथ करवाणि I shall fulfill it for you, बुधाः अजानतः मे अपचितिः यथा तु चिन्त्यताम् O wise ones please deliberate on what atonement is appropriate for Me since I acted unknowingly.

Simple Meaning: O best among sages! Tell Me what your desire is so that I may fulfill it. O wise ones! Please determine what atonement is appropriate for Me since I committed this act without knowing.

ऋषयः ऊचुः
इल्वलस्य सुतो घोरो बल्वलो नाम दानवः ।
स दूषयति नः सत्रमेत्य पर्वणि पर्वणि ॥10.78.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: ऋषयः ऊचुः the sages said, इल्वलस्य of Ilvala, सुतः son, घोरः terrible, बल्वलः Balvala, नाम named, दानवः danava, सः he, दूषयति pollutes, नः our, सत्रम् sacrifice, एत्य coming, पर्वणि पर्वणि at every change of the moon

Anvaya: ऋषयः ऊचुः – इल्वलस्य घोरः सुतः बल्वलः नाम दानवः। सः पर्वणि पर्वणि एत्य नः सत्रं दूषयति।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ऋषयः ऊचुः The sages said, इल्वलस्य घोरः सुतः बल्वलः नाम दानवः the terrible son of Ilvala a danava named Balvala. सः पर्वणि पर्वणि एत्य नः सत्रं दूषयति He comes at every change of the moon and pollutes our sacrificial arena.

Simple Meaning: The sages said: A terrible danava named Balvala who is the son of Ilvala comes here on every sacred full moon and new moon day to pollute our sacrificial arena.

तं पापं जहि दाशार्ह तन्नः शुश्रूषणं परम् ।
पूयशोणितविण्मूत्रसुरामांसाभिवर्षिणम् ॥10.78.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: तम् that, पापम् sinful, जहि kill, दाशार्ह O descendant of Dasharha (Balarama), तत् that, नः to us, शुश्रूषणम् service, परम् highest, पूय-शोणित-विट्-मूत्र-सुरा-मांस-अभिवर्षिणम् raining pus, blood, stool, urine, liquor and flesh

Anvaya: दाशार्ह! पूयशोणितविण्मूत्रसुरामांसाभिवर्षिणं तं पापं जहि, तत् नः परं शुश्रूषणम्।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: दाशार्ह O descendant of Dasharha, पूयशोणितविण्मूत्रसुरामांसाभिवर्षिणं who rains down pus, blood, stool, urine, liquor and flesh, तं पापं जहि kill that sinful creature, तत् नः परं शुश्रूषणम् that will be the highest service unto us.

Simple Meaning: The sages said: O descendant of Dasharha! please kill that sinful creature who rains down pus, blood, stool, urine, liquor and flesh onto our sacrificial arena That will be the highest service You can render to us.

ततश्च भारतं वर्षं परीत्य सुसमाहितः।
चरित्वा द्वादश मासांस्तीर्थस्नायी विशुद्ध्यसे ॥10.78.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: ततः then, च and, भारतम् वर्षम् the land of Bharata, परीत्य traveling all around, सुसमाहितः with great concentration and devotion, चरित्वा touring, द्वादश twelve, मासान् months, तीर्थ-स्नायी bathing in holy places, विशुद्ध्यसे You will be purified

Anvaya: ततः च भारतं वर्षं सुसमाहितः परीत्य द्वादश मासान् तीर्थस्नायी चरित्वा विशुद्ध्यसे।

Literal meaning of the anvaya: ततः च भारतं वर्षं सुसमाहितः परीत्य And then traveling all around Bharata-varsha with great devotion, द्वादश मासान् तीर्थस्नायी चरित्वा bathing in various holy places for twelve months, विशुद्ध्यसे You will be fully purified.

Simple Meaning: After killing that asura You should travel all around Bharata-varsha with great concentration and devotion bathing in various holy places over the course of twelve months to complete Your purification.

इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे पारमहंस्यां संहितायां दशमस्कन्धे उत्तरार्धे “दन्तवक्रविदूरथोद्धारः बलरामेण रोमहर्षणवधः च नाम​” अष्टसप्ततितमः अध्यायः ॥78॥

Thus ends the seventy-eighth discourse named “Deliverance of Dantavaktra and Viduratha; Romaharshana killed by Balarama”, in the latter half of Book Ten of the great and glorious Bhagavata-Purana.
