॥ॐ श्री परमात्मने नमः॥

॥श्रीमद्भगवद्गीता॥

॥कर्म​योगो नाम अथ तृतीयोऽध्यायः॥

(43 Shlokas)

अर्जुन उवाच
ज्यायसी चेत्कर्मणस्ते मता बुद्धिर्जनार्दन ।
तत्किं कर्मणि घोरे मां नियोजयसि केशव ॥3.1॥

Word-by-word meaning: अर्जुनः उवाच Arjuna said, ज्यायसी superior/better/greater, चेत् if, कर्मणः than action/work, ते by You/Your, मता considered/thought/deemed, बुद्धिः knowledge/discriminative wisdom/intellect, जनार्दन O Janardana (agitator of the wicked/Krishna), तत् then/therefore, किम् why, कर्मणि in action/deed, घोरे terrible/dreadful/horrible, माम् me, नियोजयसि do You engage/urge/employ, केशव O Keshava (slayer of Keshi/Krishna)

Anvaya: अर्जुनः उवाच — जनार्दन! कर्मणः बुद्धिः ज्यायसी (इति) ते मता (अस्ति) चेत्, तत् केशव! मां घोरे कर्मणि किं नियोजयसि?

Simple meaning: Arjuna said: O Janardana, if You consider knowledge to be superior to action, then O Keshava, why do You urge me to engage in this terrible action (of war)?

व्यामिश्रेणेव वाक्येन बुद्धिं मोहयसीव मे ।
तदेकं वद निश्चित्य येन श्रेयोऽहमाप्‍नुयाम् ॥3.2॥

Word-by-word meaning: व्यामिश्रेण ambiguous/equivocal/mixed/perplexing, इव as it were/as if/seemingly, वाक्येन by speech/words/statement, बुद्धिम् intellect/understanding/mind, मोहयसि You confuse/bewilder/delude, इव as though/as if, मे my, तत् therefore/so, एकम् one/single (path), वद tell/speak, निश्चित्य having decided/with certainty/definitely, येन by which, श्रेयः the highest good/spiritual welfare/bliss, अहम् I, आप्नुयाम् may attain/reach

Anvaya: व्यामिश्रेण वाक्येन इव मे बुद्धिं मोहयसि इव। तत् एकं निश्चित्य वद, येन अहं श्रेयः आप्नुयाम्।

Simple meaning: You are, as it were, confusing my intellect with seemingly ambiguous words; therefore, tell me decisively that one single path by which I may attain the highest good.

श्री भगवान् उवाच
लोकेऽस्मिन्द्विविधा निष्ठा पुरा प्रोक्ता मयानघ ।
ज्ञानयोगेन सांख्यानां कर्मयोगेन योगिनाम् ॥3.3॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, लोके in the world, अस्मिन् this, द्वि-विधा twofold/of two kinds, निष्ठा path/steadfastness/devotion/basis, पुरा formerly/in the beginning/of old, प्रोक्ता declared/spoken/taught, मया by Me, अनघ O sinless one/blameless one (Arjuna), ज्ञान-योगेन through the path of knowledge, सांख्यानाम् for the contemplatives/followers of Sankhya, कर्म-योगेन through the path of selfless action, योगिनाम् for the yogis inclined towards action

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच — अनघ! अस्मिन् लोके द्विविधा निष्ठा पुरा मया प्रोक्ता — सांख्यानां ज्ञानयोगेन, योगिनां कर्मयोगेन।

Simple meaning: The Supreme Lord said: O sinless one, in this world a twofold path was declared by Me in the beginning—the path of knowledge for the contemplatives, and the path of selfless action for the yogis inclined towards action.

न कर्मणामनारम्भान्नैष्कर्म्य पुरुषोऽश्न‍ुते ।
न च सन्न्यसनादेव सिद्धिं समधिगच्छति ॥3.4॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, कर्मणाम् of actions/duties, अनारम्भात् by non-commencement/abstention/avoidance, नैष्कर्म्यम् freedom from action/transcendence of karma/actionlessness, पुरुषः a person/man, अश्नुते attains/enjoys/reaches, न not, च and, सन्न्यसनात् by mere renunciation (of external action), एव alone/merely, सिद्धिम् perfection/spiritual fulfillment/liberation, समधिगच्छति attains/achieves

Anvaya: पुरुषः कर्मणाम् अनारम्भात् नैष्कर्म्यं न अश्नुते, न च सन्न्यसनात् एव सिद्धिं समधिगच्छति।

Simple meaning: A person does not attain freedom from action merely by abstaining from work, nor does one achieve spiritual perfection simply by outward renunciation of action.

न हि कश्चित्क्षणमपि जातु तिष्ठत्यकर्मकृत् ।
कार्यते ह्यवशः कर्म सर्वः प्रकृतिजैर्गुणैः ॥3.5॥

Word-by-word meaning: न not, हि indeed/certainly/for, कश्चित् anyone/anybody, क्षणम् a moment/instant, अपि even, जातु ever/at any time, तिष्ठति remains/stays/abides, अकर्म-कृत् without performing action/inactive, कार्यते is made to act/is compelled to perform, हि indeed/because, अवशः helplessly/involuntarily/under compulsion, कर्म action/work, सर्वः everyone/every being, प्रकृति-जैः born of Prakriti (material nature), गुणैः by the gunas (modes/qualities of nature: sattva, rajas, tamas)

Anvaya: हि कश्चित् जातु क्षणम् अपि अकर्मकृत् न तिष्ठति; हि सर्वः प्रकृतिजैः गुणैः अवशः कर्म कार्यते।

Simple meaning: Indeed, no one can ever remain without performing action even for a single moment; for everyone is helplessly driven to act by the qualities born of material nature.

कर्मेन्द्रियाणि संयम्य य आस्ते मनसा स्मरन् ।
इन्द्रियार्थान्विमूढात्मा मिथ्याचारः स उच्यते ॥3.6॥

Word-by-word meaning: कर्म-इन्द्रियाणि the organs of action (hands, feet, speech, etc.), संयम्य having restrained/controlled/curbed, यः who, आस्ते sits/remains, मनसा with the mind, स्मरन् remembering/dwelling upon/brooding over, इन्द्रिय-अर्थान् the objects of the senses, विमूढ-आत्मा deluded soul/bewildered person, मिथ्या-आचारः hypocrite/person of false conduct, सः he/that person, उच्यते is called/is said to be

Anvaya: यः विमूढात्मा कर्मेन्द्रियाणि संयम्य मनसा इन्द्रियार्थान् स्मरन् आस्ते, सः मिथ्याचारः उच्यते।

Simple meaning: That deluded person who restrains the external organs of action, yet his mind dwells upon the objects of the senses is called a hypocrite.

यस्त्विन्द्रियाणि मनसा नियम्यारभतेऽर्जुन ।
कर्मेन्द्रियैः कर्मयोगमसक्तः स विशिष्यते ॥3.7॥

Word-by-word meaning: यः who, तु but/on the other hand, इन्द्रियाणि the senses, मनसा by/with the mind, नियम्य having controlled/regulated/subdued, आरभते begins/undertakes/performs, अर्जुन O Arjuna, कर्म-इन्द्रियैः with the organs of action, कर्म-योगम् the yoga of selfless action, असक्तः unattached/free from attachment, सः he/that person, विशिष्यते excels/is superior/stands out

Anvaya: अर्जुन! यः तु मनसा इन्द्रियाणि नियम्य, असक्तः (सन्) कर्मेन्द्रियैः कर्मयोगम् आरभते, सः विशिष्यते।

Simple meaning: But, O Arjuna, one who regulates the senses through the mind and, remaining unattached, engages the organs of action in the yoga of selfless work, truly excels.

नियतं कुरु कर्म त्वं कर्म ज्यायो ह्यकर्मणः ।
शरीरयात्रापि च ते न प्रसिद्ध्येदकर्मणः ॥3.8॥

Word-by-word meaning: नियतम् prescribed/obligatory/allotted, कुरु do/perform, कर्म action/duty/work, त्वम् you, कर्म action/work, ज्यायः superior/better, हि indeed/for/because, अकर्मणः than inaction/non-performance of duty, शरीर-यात्रा maintenance of the body/physical sustenance, अपि even, च and/also, ते your/of you, न not, प्रसिद्ध्येत् would be accomplished/possible/maintained, अकर्मणः by inaction/without action

Anvaya: त्वं नियतं कर्म कुरु, हि अकर्मणः कर्म ज्यायः (अस्ति); अकर्मणः ते शरीरयात्रा अपि च न प्रसिद्ध्येत्।

Simple meaning: Perform your prescribed duty, for action is indeed superior to inaction; without action, even the bare maintenance of your physical body would not be possible.

यज्ञार्थात्कर्मणोऽन्यत्र लोकोऽयं कर्मबन्धनः ।
तदर्थं कर्म कौन्तेय मुक्तसङ्गः समाचर ॥3.9॥

Word-by-word meaning: यज्ञ-अर्थात् for the sake of sacrifice/offering (to the Supreme/greater good), कर्मणः than action/work, अन्यत्र otherwise/apart from/except, लोकः world/mankind/people, अयम् this, कर्म-बन्धनः bound by action/in bondage to karma, तत्-अर्थम् for that sake (of sacrifice), कर्म action/duty, कौन्तेय O son of Kunti - Arjuna, मुक्त-सङ्गः free from attachment, समाचर perform well/practice

Anvaya: यज्ञार्थात् कर्मणः अन्यत्र अयं लोकः कर्मबन्धनः (अस्ति)। कौन्तेय! मुक्तसङ्गः (सन्) तदर्थं कर्म समाचर।

Simple meaning: This world is bound by action, except when action is performed for the sake of sacrifice (as an offering to the Divine). Therefore, O son of Kunti, being free from all attachment, perform your duty for the sake of sacrifice (as an offering to the Divine).

सहयज्ञाः प्रजाः सृष्ट्वा पुरोवाच प्रजापतिः ।
अनेन प्रसविष्यध्वमेष वोऽस्त्विष्टकामधुक् ॥3.10॥

Word-by-word meaning: सह-यज्ञाः together with sacrifice (yagnya), प्रजाः living beings/creatures/mankind, सृष्ट्वा having created, पुरा formerly/in the beginning/of old, उवाच said/spoke, प्रजा-पतिः Prajapati (the Lord of Creation/Brahma), अनेन by this (sacrifice), प्रसविष्यध्वम् you shall prosper/multiply/flourish, एषः this (sacrifice), वः to/for you, अस्तु let it be, इष्ट-काम-धुक् the milker/bestower of desired objects (like the wish-fulfilling cow Kamadhenu)

Anvaya: पुरा प्रजापतिः सहयज्ञाः प्रजाः सृष्ट्वा उवाच — "अनेन प्रसविष्यध्वम्, एषः वः इष्टकामधुक् अस्तु।"

Simple meaning: In the beginning, having created mankind together with the spirit of sacrifice, the Lord of Creation - Brahma said: "By this sacrifice you shall prosper; let this be to you the bestower of all your desired needs."

देवान्भावयतातेन ते देवा भावयन्तु वः ।
परस्परं भावयन्तः श्रेयः परमवाप्स्यथ ॥3.11॥

Word-by-word meaning: देवान् the celestial deities/devas/cosmic powers, भावयत cherish/nourish/sustain/honor, अनेन by this (sacrifice), ते those, देवाः deities/devas, भावयन्तु may they nourish/cherish/sustain, वः you, परस्परम् mutually/one another, भावयन्तः nourishing/cherishing/sustaining, श्रेयः the highest good/supreme welfare/spiritual prosperity, परम् supreme/ultimate, अवाप्स्यथ you shall attain/achieve

Anvaya: अनेन देवान् भावयत, ते देवाः वः भावयन्तु; परस्परं भावयन्तः (यूयं) परं श्रेयः अवाप्स्यथ।

Simple meaning: Nourish the celestial powers through this spirit of sacrifice, and may those powers in turn nourish you; thus nourishing one another, you shall attain the supreme good.

इष्टान्भोगान्हि वो देवा दास्यन्ते यज्ञभाविताः ।
तैर्दत्तानप्रदायैभ्यो यो भुङ्क्ते स्तेन एव सः ॥3.12॥

Word-by-word meaning: इष्टान् desired/wished-for, भोगान् enjoyments/objects of enjoyment/necessities, हि indeed/surely/for, वः to/for you, देवाः the celestial deities/devas, दास्यन्ते will grant/will bestow/will give, यज्ञ-भाविताः nourished/honored/propitiated by sacrifice, तैः by them (the deities), दत्तान् given/bestowed, अप्रदाय without offering/without giving back, एभ्यः to them, यः who, भुङ्क्ते enjoys/consumes, स्तेनः a thief, एव indeed/verily, सः he/that person

Anvaya: हि यज्ञभाविताः देवाः वः इष्टान् भोगान् दास्यन्ते; तैः दत्तान् (भोगान्) एभ्यः अप्रदाय यः भुङ्क्ते, सः स्तेनः एव (अस्ति)।

Simple meaning: Indeed, nourished by sacrifice, the devas will bestow upon you the enjoyments you desire; one who enjoys the gifts given by them without offering back to them in return is verily a thief.

यज्ञशिष्टाशिनः सन्तो मुच्यन्ते सर्वकिल्बिषै ।

Word-by-word meaning: यज्ञ-शिष्ट-आशिनः those who eat the remnants/leavings of sacrifice (yagnya), सन्तः the virtuous/righteous/good people, मुच्यन्ते are freed/released/liberated, सर्व-किल्बिषैः from all sins/impurities/faults, भुञ्जते eat/consume/partake of, ते they, तु but/on the other hand, अघम् sin/impurity/evil, पापाः sinful ones/the wicked, ये who, पचन्ति cook/prepare food, आत्म-कारणात् for their own sake/for selfish enjoyment

Anvaya: यज्ञशिष्टाशिनः सन्तः सर्वकिल्बिषैः मुच्यन्ते; ये तु आत्मकारणात् पचन्ति, ते पापाः अघं भुञ्जते।

Simple meaning: The virtuous who partake of the remnants of sacrifice are freed from all sins; on the other hand, those sinful ones who prepare food solely for their own selfish sake verily eat sin.

अन्नाद्भ‍वन्ति भूतानि पर्जन्यादन्नसम्भवः ।
यज्ञा‍द्भ‌वति पर्जन्यो यज्ञः कर्मसमुद्भ‍वः ॥3.14॥

Word-by-word meaning: अन्नात् from food/grains, भवन्ति come into being/arise/exist, भूतानि living beings/creatures, पर्जन्यात् from rain/rain-clouds, अन्न-सम्भवः production/origin of food, यज्ञात् from sacrifice (yagnya), भवति arises/comes into being/is produced, पर्जन्यः rain, यज्ञः sacrifice (yagnya), कर्म-समुद्भवः born of/originating from prescribed action/duty

Anvaya: अन्नात् भूतानि भवन्ति, पर्जन्यात् अन्नसम्भवः (भवति), यज्ञात् पर्जन्यः भवति, यज्ञः कर्मसमुद्भवः (अस्ति)।

Simple meaning: Living beings come into existence from food; food is produced from rain; rain arises from sacrifice (yagnya); and sacrifice is born of prescribed action.

कर्म ब्रह्मोद्भ‍वं विद्धि ब्रह्माक्षरसमुद्भ‍वम् ।
तस्मात्सर्वगतं ब्रह्म नित्यं यज्ञे प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥3.15॥

Word-by-word meaning: कर्म prescribed action/duty, ब्रह्म-उद्भवम् arisen/originated from Brahma (the Vedas/cosmic creator), विद्धि know/understand, ब्रह्म the Vedas (or Brahma), अक्षर-समुद्भवम् born of/originating from the Imperishable Supreme (Akshara/Brahman), तस्मात् therefore/hence, सर्व-गतम् all-pervading/omnipresent, ब्रह्म Brahman (the Absolute Truth/the Vedas), नित्यम् eternally/always, यज्ञे in sacrifice (yagnya), प्रतिष्ठितम् established/situated/abides

Anvaya: कर्म ब्रह्मोद्भवं (विद्धि), ब्रह्म अक्षरसमुद्भवं विद्धि; तस्मात् सर्वगतं ब्रह्म नित्यं यज्ञे प्रतिष्ठितम् (अस्ति)।

Simple meaning: Know that prescribed action arises from the Vedas (or Brahma), and the Vedas originate from the Imperishable Supreme; therefore, the all-pervading Brahman is eternally established in sacrifice (yagnya).

एवं प्रवर्तितं चक्रं नानुवर्तयतीह यः ।

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus/in this manner, प्रवर्तितम् set in motion/revolving/established, चक्रम् wheel/cycle (of creation and sacrifice), न not, अनुवर्तयति follows/keeps turning/conforms to, इह here/in this world, यः who, अघ-आयुः living a sinful life/whose life is spent in sin, इन्द्रिय-आरामः delighting/rejoicing in the senses, मोघम् in vain/uselessly/fruitlessly, पार्थ O Partha (son of Pritha/Arjuna), सः he/that person, जीवति lives

Anvaya: पार्थ! यः इह एवं प्रवर्तितं चक्रं न अनुवर्तयति, सः अघायुः इन्द्रियारामः मोघं जीवति।

Simple meaning: O Partha, one who does not follow the wheel of creation thus set in motion in this world—living a sinful life and delighting solely in the senses—lives in vain.

यस्त्वात्मरतिरेव स्यादात्मतृप्त‍श्च मानवः ।

श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात् ।
स्वधर्मे निधनं श्रेयः परधर्मो भयावहः ॥3.35॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्रेयान् better/superior/preferable, स्व-धर्मः one's own duty/innate nature-aligned duty (svadharma), विगुणः devoid of merit/imperfectly performed/defective, पर-धर्मात् than another's duty (paradharma), सु-अनुष्ठितात् well-performed/thoroughly executed, स्व-धर्मे in one's own duty, निधनम् death/destruction, श्रेयः better/preferable, पर-धर्मः another's duty, भय-आवहः fraught with fear/perilous/dangerous

Anvaya: स्वनुष्ठितात् परधर्मात् विगुणः स्वधर्मः श्रेयान्; स्वधर्मे निधनं श्रेयः, परधर्मः भयावहः (भवति)।

Simple meaning: One's own duty (svadharma), even if performed imperfectly, is superior to another's duty (paradharma) performed well; death in the performance of one's own duty is preferable, whereas following another's duty is fraught with danger.

अर्जुन उवाच
अथ केन प्रयुक्तोऽयं पापं चरति पूरुषः ।
अनिच्छन्नपि वार्ष्णेय बलादिव नियोजितः ॥3.36॥

Word-by-word meaning: अर्जुनः उवाच Arjuna said, अथ now/then/moreover, केन by what/by whom, प्रयुक्तः impelled/driven/instigated, अयम् this, पापम् sin/wrongdoing/evil, चरति commits/practices/performs, पूरुषः person/human being, अनिच्छन् not wishing/unwilling/reluctant, अपि even/though, वार्ष्णेय O Varshneya (descendant of Vrishni/Krishna), बलात् by force/forcibly, इव as if/as though, नियोजितः compelled/constrained/engaged

Anvaya: अर्जुनः उवाच — वार्ष्णेय! अथ अयं पूरुषः अनिच्छन् अपि बलात् नियोजितः इव केन प्रयुक्तः (सन्) पापं चरति?

Simple meaning: Arjuna said: O Varshneya, by what is a person impelled to commit sin, even when unwilling, as if compelled by force?

श्री भगवान् उवाच
काम एष क्रोध एष रजोगुणसमुद्भ‍वः ।
महाशनो महापाप्मा विद्ध्येनमिह वैरिणम् ॥3.37॥

Word-by-word meaning: श्री भगवान् उवाच the Supreme Lord said, कामः desire/lust/craving, एषः this, क्रोधः anger/wrath (which arises when desire is obstructed), एषः this, रजः-गुण-समुद्भवः born of the rajo-guna (the mode of passion/activity), महा-अशनः all-devouring/insatiable/of great appetite, महा-पाप्मा great sinner/greatly sinful/most destructive, विद्धि know/understand, एनम् this, इह here (in this world/in this matter), वैरिणम् enemy/adversary/foe

Anvaya: श्री भगवान् उवाच — रजोगुणसमुद्भवः एषः कामः, एषः क्रोधः (च) महाशनः महापाप्मा (अस्ति); एनम् इह वैरिणं विद्धि।

Simple meaning: The Supreme Lord said: It is desire (kama) and it is anger (krodha), born of the mode of passion (rajo-guna), which is all-devouring and greatly sinful; know this to be the enemy here in this world.

धूमेनाव्रियते वह्निर्यथादर्शो मलेन च ।
यथोल्बेनावृतो गर्भस्तथा तेनेदमावृत्तम् ॥3.38॥

Word-by-word meaning: धूमेन by smoke, आव्रियते is enveloped/covered/obscured, वह्निः fire, यथा just as/as, आदर्शः a mirror, मलेन by dust/dirt/tarnish, च and, यथा just as/as, उल्बेन by the amnion/fetal membrane/womb, आवृतः is enveloped/covered/enclosed, गर्भः the embryo/fetus, तथा so/in the same way, तेन by that (desire/kama), इदम् this (wisdom/knowledge/Self), आवृतम् is enveloped/covered/obscured

Anvaya: यथा धूमेन वह्निः, मलेन आदर्शः च आव्रियते, यथा (च) उल्बेन गर्भः आवृतः (भवति), तथा तेन (कामेन क्रोधेन च​) इदम् (ज्ञानम्) आवृतम् (भवति)।

Simple meaning: Just as fire is enveloped by smoke, a mirror is covered by dust, and an embryo is enclosed within the womb (amnion), so too is this wisdom enveloped by that desire (and anger).

आवृतं ज्ञानमेतेन ज्ञानिनो नित्यवैरिणा ।
कामरूपेण कौन्तेय दुष्पूरेणानलेन च ॥3.39॥

Word-by-word meaning: आवृतम् enveloped/covered/obscured, ज्ञानम् knowledge/wisdom, एतेन by this, ज्ञानिनः of the wise/discerning person, नित्य-वैरिणा by the eternal/constant enemy, काम-रूपेण having the form of desire (kama), कौन्तेय O Kaunteya (son of Kunti/Arjuna), दुष्पूरेण insatiable/difficult to satisfy or fill, अनलेन by fire, च and

Anvaya: कौन्तेय! ज्ञानिनः नित्यवैरिणा दुष्पूरेण अनलेन च कामरूपेण एतेन ज्ञानम् आवृतम् (अस्ति)।

Simple meaning: O Kaunteya, knowledge is enveloped by this eternal enemy of the wise, which takes the form of insatiable desire (kama) and burns like an unquenchable fire.

इन्द्रियाणि मनो बुद्धिरस्याधिष्ठानमुच्यते ।
एतैर्विमोहयत्येष ज्ञानमावृत्य देहिनम् ॥3.40॥

Word-by-word meaning: इन्द्रियाणि the senses, मनः the mind, बुद्धिः the intellect, अस्य of this (desire/kama), अधिष्ठानम् seat/abode/stronghold/base of operation, उच्यते is said to be/is declared, एतैः by means of these, विमोहयति deludes/bewilders/infatuates, एषः this (desire), ज्ञानम् knowledge/wisdom, आवृत्य having enveloped/veiled/obscured, देहिनम् the embodied soul/living being

Anvaya: इन्द्रियाणि मनः बुद्धिः (च) अस्य (कामस्य​) अधिष्ठानम् उच्यते। एषः (कामः) एतैः (इन्द्रियामनःबुद्धिभिः) ज्ञानम् आवृत्य देहिनं विमोहयति।

Simple meaning: The senses, the mind, and the intellect are declared to be the stronghold (seat) of this desire (kama). Through these (the senses, the mind, and the intellect), the desire (kama) veils the wisdom and deludes the embodied soul.

तस्मात्त्वमिन्द्रियाण्यादौ नियम्य भरतर्षभ ।
पाप्मानं प्रजहि ह्येनं ज्ञानविज्ञाननाशनम् ॥3.41॥

Word-by-word meaning: तस्मात् therefore, त्वम् you, इन्द्रियाणि the senses, आदौ in the beginning/first, नियम्य having controlled/regulated/restrained, भरत-ऋषभ O bull/best among the Bharatas (Arjuna), पाप्मानम् the sinful/wicked one, प्रजहि slay/destroy/conquer, हि indeed/certainly, एनम् this (desire/kama), ज्ञान-विज्ञान-नाशनम् the destroyer of spiritual knowledge (gnyana) and realized/experiential wisdom (vignyana)

Anvaya: भरतर्षभ! तस्मात् त्वम् आदौ इन्द्रियाणि नियम्य, ज्ञानविज्ञाननाशनम् एनं पाप्मानं (कामं) हि प्रजहि।

Simple meaning: Therefore, O best among the Bharatas, first bring the senses under control, and decisively slay this sinful desire (kama), which is the destroyer of both scriptural knowledge (gnyana) and direct experiential wisdom (vignyana).

इन्द्रियाणि पराण्याहुरिन्द्रियेभ्यः परं मनः ।
मनसस्तु परा बुद्धिर्यो बुद्धेः परतस्तु सः ॥3.42॥

Word-by-word meaning: इन्द्रियाणि the senses, पराणि superior/higher/subtle (than the physical body), आहुः (the wise) say/declare, इन्द्रियेभ्यः than the senses, परम् superior/higher/subtle, मनः the mind (manas), मनसः than the mind, तु but/and, परा superior/higher/subtle, बुद्धिः the intellect/discriminating faculty (buddhi), यः that which/who, बुद्धेः than the intellect, परतः beyond/higher/superior, तु indeed/but, सः He/That (the Supreme Self/Atman)

Anvaya: इन्द्रियाणि पराणि आहुः। इन्द्रियेभ्यः परं मनः (अस्ति)। मनसः तु परा बुद्धिः (अस्ति)। यः तु बुद्धेः परतः, सः (आत्मा अस्ति)।

Simple meaning: The wise say that the senses are superior (to the physical body); superior than the senses is the mind (manas); superior than the mind is the intellect (buddhi); and that which is beyond even the intellect is He (the Self/Atman).

एवं बुद्धेः परं बुद्ध्वा संस्तभ्यात्मानमात्मना ।
जहि शत्रुं महाबाहो कामरूपं दुरासदम् ॥3.43॥

Word-by-word meaning: एवम् thus/in this manner, बुद्धेः than the intellect (buddhi), परम् superior/beyond/supreme (the Atman), बुद्ध्वा having known/understood/realized, संस्तभ्य having steadied/stabilized/restrained, आत्मानम् the self (the mind/lower self), आत्मना by the Self (by the higher Self/discriminating intellect), जहि slay/conquer/destroy, शत्रुम् the enemy, महा-बाहो O mighty-armed one (Arjuna), काम-रूपम् having the form of desire (kama), दुरासदम् formidable/hard to conquer/elusive/difficult to approach

Anvaya: महाबाहो! एवं बुद्धेः परं बुद्ध्वा, आत्मना आत्मानं संस्तभ्य, कामरूपं दुरासदं शत्रुं जहि।

Simple meaning: Thus, O mighty-armed one, knowing the Self (Atman) to be superior to the intellect, by the Self (Atman - discriminating intellect) steady the self (the senses, the mind and the intellect) and slay this formidable enemy in the form of desire (kama).
