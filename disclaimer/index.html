About this work, and the sources it draws on

ॐ

I started this exercise as a way to self-study - Svadhyava. As I studied the shlokas and the English meanings, it occurred to me to do Padaccheda (Word-by-word meaning), Anvaya (Prose order) and then read the meaning in English. Thus began this journey.

I took the shlokas from

  1. https://archive.org/details/vCpZ_shrimad-bhagavata-mahapurana-english-of-c.-l.-goswami-m.-a-shastri-part-2-gita-press-gorakhpur
  2. https://narayaneeyam-firststep.org/
  3. https://bhaagavatam-firststep.org/
  4. https://archive.org/details/valmiki-ramayana-gita-press-english
  5. https://archive.org/details/raghuvamsha_with_sanjivini_edited_by_mr_kale
  6. https://archive.org/details/shrimad-bhagwat-anvaya-complete-sudarshan-singh-chakra
  7. https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.273609/mode/2up
  8. https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/10/
  9. https://sanskritdocuments.org/sites/valmikiramayan/sundara
  10. https://sanskritdocuments.org/sites/valmikiramayan/kish

I referred to the Sanskrit shlokas and studied the meanings from these sources in good faith for my study.

I used this website for typing out the shlokas. This has been extremely helpful.

  1. https://www.lexilogos.com/keyboard/sanskrit_devanagari.htm

I have used https://ashtadhyayi.com extensively for looking up the meanings of the words, the dhaturoopas and shabdaroopas.

  1. https://ashtadhyayi.com/kosha - for looking up the meanings of the words
  2. https://ashtadhyayi.com/dhatu - for looking up the dhaturoopas
  3. https://ashtadhyayi.com/shabda - for looking up the shabdaroopas

I am sincerely grateful for all the above resources which have been my teachers in my effort to study.

The Word-by-word meaning, Anvaya (Prose order), Literal meaning of the Anvaya (in case of Shrimad Bhagavatam Canto 10) and Simple meaning in English is my own work, cross referenced from these sources. The language is my own.

This being the case, I do understand that this work is by no means complete and there will be errors. This collection will be a work in progress with my sincere attempt to keep the meanings as close to the original text as possible.

I request the users and scholars to keep in mind that this is a work of my own self study which I am sharing so that fellow Sanskrit students desirous of studying our rich texts, benefit from this. Hence the name of the website Svadhyayat Svadhyaparyantam.

The website is created with the help of AI but the content in the website is my own work, by Shri Krishna’s Grace.

Happy Studying!
